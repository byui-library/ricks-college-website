Chemical Engineering


Chemical engineers use chemistry and the discipline of engineering to solve problems. They are responsible for the production of the fuel we burn and the food we eat, for the purification of water and air, and for the recovery and use of the raw materials found in our earth, oceans and in space. The chemical engineer develops economical industrial processes. The  commercial success of a product depends on the chemical engineer to design a pilot plant and subsequent full-scale plant. Chemical engineers work in the production of chemical products. They design equipment and plants and determine and best methods of manufacturing products. Chemical engineers work in areas other than chemical manufacturing or bio- technology. The duties of chemical engineers cut across many fields. They apply principles of chemistry, physics, mathematics, and mechanical and electrical engineering. They often specialize in a particular process such as oxidation, polymerization, pollution control, or the production of  plastics.
