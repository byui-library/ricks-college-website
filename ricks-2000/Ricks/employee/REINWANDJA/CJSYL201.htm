COURSE DESCRIPTION:  This course will emphasize the United States Supreme Court as an American Institution.  The first half of the semester will focus on the creation, history and function of the court, with particular emphasis on the court and political processes.  The second part of the course will focus on the Court as the administrator of the Bill of Rights with emphasis on history and the current revolution in Bill of Rights thinking.

TEXT: No text has been selected as yet, rather we will use the internet as a resource.  A Bill of Rights reader will be available for the second half of the course.

TESTS: A midterm and a final exam may be given. They will cover only the material since the preceding test. Tests will be short essay. Each test will be worth 100 points.  The class will choose between a test or a term paper option.

TERM PAPERS: Two six page text term papers will be required on an approved topic. A style manual will be used. The papers will be due before the midterm and final, dates will be announced. Topics must be pre approved. No papers will be accepted late!  Students should be prepared to give a ten minute oral presentation of their paper.

GRADES: All students can earn an "A". I do not grade on a curve, but upon a standard of excellence.        90%=A,  80%=B,  65%=C,  55%=D.                                                                     

HONOR CODE: All students will be expected to obey the honor and dress and grooming standards while in the classroom.
