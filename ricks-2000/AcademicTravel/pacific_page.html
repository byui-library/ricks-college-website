Pacific Coast Discovery
$965 Total Cost - $150 Non-Refundable Deposit
First summer term: April 29 - June 6
All students must take 6 credits minimum (10 credits maximum)
  • BS 118: Field Biology (4 crs - natural science)
  • BS 290: Special Problems (1-2 crs)
  • Geol 101 & 102: Intro to Geology - lecture & lab (4 crs - natural science)
  • Geol 290: Directed Study (1-2 crs)
  • REL 431: Doctrines of the Gospel (2 crs - religion)
Search the tide pools of the Oregon Pacific coast with Jerry Scrivner of Biology, Steve Hansen of Geology.

The Discovery team will spend two weeks traveling and exploring the tide pools of the Oregon Coast. On the way back, they will spend a week hiking and studying in the Salmon River area. Later they will discover Yellowstone, the Tetons and Island Park. Operating from a base camp not far from the southwest comer of Yellowstone National Park, the adventure will be concluded with experiences both educational and just plain fun.

Enrollment limited to 30 participants
