Personal History and Bibliography

Ideally this assignment should be worked on throughout the course.  As each reference tool is considered make note as to how you might use it to enhance your personal history.  For example, when we cover literary indexes you might decide to add a note to your personal history about a favorite author or poem citing the reference tool(s) used to retrieve the information.

Another example might come from our review of atlases and gazetteers; you could provide detailed information about your hometown or other places that you have lived.  Consider adding information about world events, and leaders, or a favorite area of study.  Include a minimum of  8 sources in your bibliography.

Be creative and have fun with it.  Remember the product you turn in for a grade will not necessarily be the final product but a work in progress that will bless your life and the lives of people who care about you.
