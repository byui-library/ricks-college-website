Ricks College News Release
February 22, 2000

Idaho Falls firm awarded contract for addition to Ricks library

REXBURG, Idaho — Work will begin in March on a major addition and renovation to the David O. McKay Library at Ricks College.

Ormond Builders of Idaho Falls submitted the lowest bid and is being awarded the contract, says Chuck Frost, director of Physical Plant.

The first phase will involve renovation of the vacant Administration Building, which is attached to the present library. When complete, this will become the east wing of the library.

Upon completion of the 47,000 square foot east wing in December 2000, work then will begin on remodeling of the third floor of the main library. During the summer of 2001, additional remodeling will be done on the first and second floors of the main library. The entire library project should be completed by Aug. 24, 2001, for the beginning of school.

The library expansion was made possible by the freeing up of administrative space following completion of the new Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building last year.

Building Chairman Max Checketts says the library expansion will boost the amount of seating for students from about 700 to 1,100 to 1,200 in the library when it’s completed. The number of group study rooms will also increase from about four at present time to about 20.

The library will also feature increased computer access. Frost explains, "This is intended to be a major support to require each student at Ricks to take an Internet class. We are moving the college from a paper-based library to an electronic-based library."

The project involves a new heating, ventilation and air conditioning system, new electrical system, new lighting and new floor. There will also be a major expansion of the Learning Assistance Lab that includes the writing center, reading lab and math lab.

When work begins on the third floor of the existing library in the winter semester of 2001, many of the functions will temporarily be moved to the east wing. On the third floor, more computers and study areas will be added, along with added space for multi-media work.

Work on the first and second floors of the main library will involved limited remodeling, some new light fixtures and some new carpet.

Design work for the project is being done by the architectural firm of Nielsen Bodily & Associates of Idaho Falls.
