Grading Criteria for GS 108A/Ed. 208

 

 

           Attendance to all tutor-training classes is very important.  Each Class missed, permanently drops your final course grade 1/3 of a letter (i.e. A to A-).    If you miss two or more class meetings you automatically fail GS 108/Ed 208 and will be terminated as an employee of the BYU-Idaho Tutoring Center.  There are two sections of this course.  The first section will be held Tuesday from 11:00 - 11:50.  The second section will be held Tuesday from 5:00 - 5:50.  GS 108A/    ED 208 has 150 total possible points and the criteria for achieving these points is as follows:

 

1.      Attend all tutor-training classes listed in the Semester Outline. 

 

2.      Throughout the semester you are required to complete 10 “Application of Tutor Training Cards”, describing how you applied the previous week’s training to your tutoring.

(10 @ 5 pts. each = 50 pts.)

 

3.      Read The ABC’s of Tutoring and turn in the accompanying ABC’s Worksheet, to be completed one week from date of hire.  (25 pts.)

                                   

                        4.  Complete the Study Skills Worksheet in the Study Skills Lab. (25 pts.)

 

                        5.  Attend an end of the semester interview with Brother Teichert. (5 pts.)

 

                        6.  Complete a Tutor Self-Assessment Sheet. (5 pts.)

 

                        7.  Have four of your students complete Tutor Evaluation forms. (5 pts.)

 

8.      Write a case study about one of your tutees by using the following Case Study Guide. (10 pts.)

 

9.      Twice during the course of the semester, observe an experienced tutor in an actual tutoring session.  You will be paid for the time spent observing the tutoring session and an observation handout will be provided to assist you in the observation. (5 pts.)

 

** During the semester there will be three paid General Tutor In-service meetings.  While these In-services are NOT mandatory, it is encouraged that you attend each of these informative meetings pertaining to your job as a tutor.  (The dates and times of these meeting should not conflict with other class times and will be announced throughout the semester.)                       

 

*** In order to certify as a Level I tutor with the College Reading & Learning Association you must complete everything listed above and have tutored a minimum of 25 hours.
