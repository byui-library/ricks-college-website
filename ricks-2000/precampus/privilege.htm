 Students admitted to BYU-Idaho receive both a privilege and a responsibility. Many students desire to attend this institution and have a "BYU-Idaho" experience, unfortunately, BYU-Idaho is not able to accommodate every worthy student who applies for admission. For this reason, students who are admitted receive a special opportunity and privilege. You have been given a special gift, a scholarship of sorts from the Church which enables all LDS members attending BYU-Idaho to have their tuition greatly reduced.  This tuition reduction scholarship has partially been funded by the tithes and offerings of church members throughout the world.

We invite you and your parents to read the following devotional addresses given by Elder David A. Bednar, a member of the Fifth Quorum of the Seventy of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and current President of BYU-Idaho.  These addresses explain the specific privileges and responsibilities of all Brigham Young University-Idaho students.

 

1. September 1, 1998 - "In the Path of Their Duty"

2.  January 5, 1999 - "Your Whole Soul As An Offering Unto Him"
