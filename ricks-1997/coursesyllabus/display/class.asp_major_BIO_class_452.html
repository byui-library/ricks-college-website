Wildland Ecology and Habitats
WILD ECO/HAB/L
CLASS CODE: BIO 452 CREDITS:  4
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This course surveys the regional habitat types, their individual floral components, and the ecological principles that govern them.
DESCRIPTION: The course will review ecological principles and introduce floral species that make up western region wildland habitats. Students will be introduced to these species in laboratory and real life situations. Students will be required to learn and recognize many plant species.
TOPICS: Ecological Succession, Taxonimic Heirarchy, Floral Morphology, Field and Laboratory identification methods.
OBJECTIVES: Assist recognition of major habitat types, their distribution and the individual floral components.
REQUIREMENTS: Plant collection, Field trips, Identification of floral species.

OTHER: $50 Lab fee
