COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 100
Title: A.S.I.D./Portfolio Review Credit: 1
Text: None

Course Objectives:
1. To strengthen, supplement, and enrich course offerings.
2. To provide additional professional points of view.
3. To aid students psychologically in dealing with time management, stress, motivation, professionalism, etc.
4. To increase awareness of the professional world of design.
5. To help students gain new product information.

Description:
Required membership in the student chapter of the American Society of Interior Designers (ASID) with weekly/bi-weekly classes with guest lecturers/presenters.  Each student must attend Portfolio Review each semester

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Attendance at all meetings, events, presentations, and workshops is mandatory.
2. Attendance by each student at an individual portfolio review by the Interior Design faculty is mandatory each semester, with the required professional dress and complete portfolio of work for current semester courses.
