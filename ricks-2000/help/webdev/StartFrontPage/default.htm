This will assist you in starting FrontPage 2000 and creating a shortcut on your desktop.

Starting FrontPage 2000
  1. Click on the Start menu; click on Programs, click on Microsoft FrontPage.
Starting FrontPage 2000 by Creating a Shortcut on the Desktop
  1. Right-click on the desktop, click New, click Shortcut.
  2. Click on Browse. Make sure the C drive is in the Look in: box, double click Program Files, double click on Microsoft Office 2000, double click on Office, double click on Frontpg.
  3. Click Next.
  4. Type an appropriate name for the shortcut.
  5. Click Finish.
