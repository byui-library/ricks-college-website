Ricks College News Release, Feb. 28, 1998

Region 18 Basketball Tournament

to be hosted by Ricks College

Thanks to having the top women's basketball team in the conference, the National Junior College Athletic Association Region 18 joint men's and women's basketball tournament is coming to Rexburg for the first time.

Ricks College, with a women's team that leads the league with a 26-2 season record, will host the tournament March 4-7 in the Hart Gymnasium.

The last time Ricks hosted tournament action was in 1984 when the men's tournament was held and in 1990 when the women's tournament was held in Rexburg, says Ricks Athletic Director Garth Hall.

Because the tournament location was just determined at the end of last week, little lead time has been available for putting together final plans.

Hall says local motels have filled quickly because of the tournament that has been a traditional popular event and brought a lot of spectators to the host community.

He expects a highly competitive, single-elimination tournament with Ricks to do well. "This is one of best junior college tournaments in the country," he says.
Both Dixie College and the College of Southern Utah men's teams are ranked nationally along with the Ricks women's team.

While the Ricks women's team is considered the favorite, the Ricks men's team also has a strong record with 22 wins and 7 losses during regular season play. The men are 12-1 at home, losing in double overtime to Salt Lake Community College.

Eight men's teams and eight women's teams will compete in the four-day event. The men's teams will be from Ricks College; Dixie College in St. George, Utah; the College of Southern Idaho in Twin Falls; Salt Lake Community College in Salt Lake City; Utah Valley State College in Orem, Utah; Snow College in Ephraim, Utah; North Idaho College in Coeur d'Alene; and the College of Eastern Utah College in Price, Utah.

The women's tournament will feature the same teams except possibly Colorado Northwestern Community College from Rangely, Colo., instead of CEU.

Session 1 games on March 4 will feature women's teams at noon and 2 p.m. followed by Session 2 games at 6 and 8 p.m. The Ricks women will play at 8 p.m. Thursday, March 5, will feature men's games in Session 3 at noon and 2 p.m. and Session 4 games 6 and 8 p.m. The Ricks men will play at 8 p.m.
The semi-final games will be held Friday, March 6, with Session 5 games at noon and 2 p.m. and Session 6 games at 6 and 8 p.m. If the Ricks teams make it to the semi-finals, the women will play at 6 p.m. and the men at 8 p.m.
Saturday will feature the women's championship game at 5 p.m. and men's championship game at 7 p.m.

Tickets are now on sale at the Ricks College Ticket Office. Call 356-2230 or 1-800-71 RICKS for more information. Tickets will also be available at the gate. Tickets for students will be available beginning Monday, March 2.
Costs are $6 for a session pass (two games, excluding championship games), $10 for a day pass (four games or championship games), $25 for a tournament pass if purchased by March 2, and $30 for a tournament pass if purchased March 3 or at gate. Student passes are $4 per day (ID required) and children under 6 years are free.

Ricks season ticket holders should reserve early to get priority seating.
