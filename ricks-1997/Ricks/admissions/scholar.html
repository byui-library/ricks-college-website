Scholarships recognize outstanding student performance in many areas. Scholarships vary in amount and duration and are based on individual achievement. Details regarding our scholarship programs are provided below.

We encourage those interested in applying for Ricks College scholarships to use the
on-line Admissions and Scholarship Application.

Academic Scholarships -- Based upon grades and ACT scores.
  • Presidential: two years full tuition.
  • Others: Amounts vary up to full tuition
  • Students who have completed AP or Honors classes will receive special consideration.
Leadership Awards -- Consideration is given to students who have held various school, seminary and civic leadership positions. To receive full consideration, grades must be above average.
Departmental Achievement Awards -- These scholarships are available in varying amounts from the following departments to acknowledge outstanding achievement in a particular area.
Agriculture Business Electronics
Agronomy/Ag. Engineering Home Economics
Animal Science Landscape/Floral
Automotive
Architecture & Construction
Computer Aided Engineering Technologies
(Design/Manufacturing/Welding)
Talent Awards -- Awards in varying amounts are given in Art, Dance, Debate, Drama, Journalism and Music. Supplementary scholarship applications materials are required. The scholarship deadline may be different than the admissions deadline. For example, the Music Department requires all scholarship materials be received by February 1, not February 15. Please contact each department for specific scholarship information.
Sophomore and Continuing Student Awards -- These awards, which vary up to full tuition, are based upon performance and a review by the Scholarship Committee. To be considered, students should carry a minimum of 12 credits each semester of their freshman year. Applicants who have completed 41 or more credits must request special consideration.
Scholarship Application Deadlines -- Academic and Leadership Scholarship consideration will be given on the basis of all application materials being received before the deadline.
  • Fall and Winter Semesters . . . . . . . . . February 15
  • Winter Semester only . . . . . . . . . . . . .October 1
