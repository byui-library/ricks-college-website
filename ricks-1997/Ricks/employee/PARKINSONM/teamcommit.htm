Dear Tennis Enthusiast,

I am very excited to be working with the tennis programs at Ricks College. The past four years some very eager tennis players have been working very hard to get this program off and running. This is our third organized year in a league and we are so excited.

The traveling team will consists of 6 players with one, two or three alternates. We are playing the world team format which has first, second and third singles for men and women, one doubles team for men and women, and one mixed team.

For try-outs, we will divided the candidates into two groups with one of our top players from last year in each group. You will play a 11 point tie breaker with each member of your group. At the end of each match [tie breaker] you will report both of the player's scores. The top 8-10 people according to the points will then go into playing sets for the top 6 positions.

The team will be traveling to Utah for 3 or 4 matches. The first match you play every other school's team in your division. That will be a seeding match. The next match is a team match for the best school out of Ricks, Snow, Salt Lake Community, Utah Valley, College of Eastern Utah and Dixie. The third match is for individual winners in each division. The dates of the matches have not yet been sent to me but check back. I will post them as soon as I get them.

We are trying to set up one or two home matches with some Idaho colleges or Utah Schools. We are also trying to set up an Idaho College tennis tournament in Sun Valley. Once we held an open invitational at Ricks for all high school and college players. That was quite fun. We will see how the year goes?

The team will practice at 3:10 p.m. daily. You will be expected to be there, on time and dressed for a hard workout. We usually practice until 4:30 and then most play a while longer. Friday's are usually traveling but if not, there will not be a formal practice but the day will be used for challenge matches.

After the third weekend in October, the team is officially over. The members may go on and play, practice, or have an intra-team tournament throughout the year. That will be entirely run by the students. After bad weather stops outdoor play, the tennis club can practice indoors Friday and Saturday evenings from 6:15 to 8:00 p.m.

In the spring we host a high school team tournament. We also plan to host a college open tournament in either the fall or spring.

There is a fee to belong to the team. The college charges each member of a sport club $25 to help cover the costs of having sports clubs, insure our rights to practice, and make it more of a commitment on your part. The club will also need a uniform that will run around $25. If you would like a warm-up they are $45 and hats $10. To help provide tennis balls, we are charging a $5 try-out fee. If you are not on the traveling team, there is a $15 fee that includes your t-shirt.

There is also a traveling fee, that will be determined at a later date. It will not be more than $20 a trip which will include your travel and motel. You will be responsible for your food money and spending money.

This program is to be student instigated and basically run by the students. I am here to help assist and organize, but the commitment must come from the students. Each practice will consist of a warm up and then drills on a specific technique of the game. We alternate between working single and double drills. (The practices will be determined by the students leaders. Techniques covered: Serve, net play, overheads, movement, placement, Return of Serve, etc. Ball machines will also be provided.) We usually play a little Olympic or other drill games.

One last bit of very important information. Marie is not being paid for the services rendered with this club. All her time is donated. Why does she donate their time? Number one, because she loves the youth. Number two, because she is past the point of loving the game of tennis to being completely obsessed with the game and wants the youth to be able to enjoy this game while attending Ricks. But volunteer time does come with some limitations. She is in no mood to put up with a lot of complaining and problems. You are seeded according to your win-loss record. You travel according to your attendance and participation at practice. You will uphold all dress and grooming and conduct standards of this college at all times. You must be in good standing with all your professors to travel whether you miss that class or not.
