COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 255
Title: Tailoring Credit: 3
Text: Tailoring Technics by Margaret Komives

Course Objective:
1.  Students are to understand the quality of fashion  fabrics and interfacings used in traditional hand tailored garments.
2. Students will learn hand and machine technics that account for the  superb  workmanship found in traditional tailored garments..
3. Students will experience construction using fusible interfacings for molding and shaping in contemporary tailoring  construction.
4. Special technics of  bound buttonholes, welt and patch pockets and other details found in tailored garments will be taught and duplicated by the students in both samples and garments.

Description: Principles of men’s and women’s tailoring taught.   Traditional and contemporary technics will be used in construction of samples and simple tailored designs. .

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.Completion of one traditionally tailored jacket and one contemporary tailored jacket.
2. Completion of a completely lined skirt or pair  trousers.
3. Completion of buttonhole, pockets and other detail tailoring samples as required.
