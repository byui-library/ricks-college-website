Join the BYU Idaho fencing club
               

 

Join the club:  Joining the BYU Idaho fencing club is as easy as showing up to our next club meeting. 
To get an application to join the fencing club contact Brandon Fenton at 356-3737or just show up to a club meeting.

Where do we meet?:  We meet on the BYU Idaho campus in the Hart Building (Physical Education Facilities).  We are in the dance hall at room #142.

When: We meet every week on Thursdays 7:30 pm and Saturdays at 1:30 pm for 1-1/2 hours.

Cost: The cost is about $30 per semester and should be paid at the BYU Idaho Student Building at the Cashier's offices found in the Spencer W. Kimball Administration Building.

What to Bring: You will need to bring some athletic shoes and some kind of long athletic pants (nylon, sweat pants, or other tough materials).  If you are a BYU Idaho Student you will also want to bring your student ID in case you would like to use the clothes issued by the school.
