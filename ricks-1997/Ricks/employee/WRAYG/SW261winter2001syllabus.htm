Course description:

This course will expose students to the practice of social work by blending values, skills and knowledge needed to be successful in the field of social work. Students will become familiar with the development and background of social work as a profession and gain an understanding of the problem solving process as it relates to the practice of social work. Throughout the semester students will be exposed to the following areas: human interaction and the communication process; how ones influence can produce an effect on another person, group or organization; the process of self-knowledge; social histories and how they are used; skills required in working in a one-to-one situation; and finally, how the environment influences the worker-client interactions.

Exams:

There will be four exams (including the final) throughout the semester. The final will be comprehensive, using material learned and tested on previously. Each exam is equal to 100 points. All exams will be in .

Self-evaluation paper:

October 16 & 18 the self-evaluation papers will be given orally in class and should address the following:

  1. Social work skills you currently possess that will aid you in working with clients, families, groups, etc.
  2. Social work skills needed but that you do not currently possess.
  3. What part of the field of social work you might enjoy the most and the least.
  4. Three pages
  5. This paper is worth 100 points

Skills video:

Early in the course you will choose a partner and together decide on a skill from the inside front cover of the workbook. Each pair will be required to demonstrate the chosen skill on video and each demonstration will be observed and critiqued by the class. The videos are to be turned into the instructor on March 15th to be reviewed and they will be shown on the 17th in class for further evaluation. Each video must be at least five minutes long.
