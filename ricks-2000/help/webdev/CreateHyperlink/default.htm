This will assist you in creating or editing a hyperlink including e-mail.  
A hyperlink can be on text or images.

Create a Text Hyperlink
    1. Begin by highlighting the text you want to become a hyperlink.

    2. While the text is highlighted, click on the Edit menu and then click on Hyperlink (Ctrl+K).

    3. If you are linking to another page of yours, click on the Explorer icon to locate that page.  When you have found the page you need to link to, click on OK.  

    4. If you are linking to someone else's page, such as The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints page, type in the full Internet address in the URL: box.  Example http://www.lds.org

    5. Click OK.  Now your text should have become blue and underlined to indicate that it is a hyperlink.
Create an Image Hyperlink
1. Click on the image you want to make a hyperlink.  

2. Follow steps 3 through 5 of the previous example.

Note: After you complete the steps, there may be a blue line around the image indicating it is a hyperlink.  To remove the blue line but not the hyperlink, right-click on the image, choose either Picture Properties (FrontPage 98 - Image Properties); Click on the Appearance Tab; and make the Border Thickness = 0.
Create an E-mail Hyperlink
1. Either highlight the text or click on the image you want to become a hyperlink to your e-mail address.

2. Click on the Edit menu and then click on Hyperlink (Ctrl+K).

3. Click on the Envelope icon.

4. Type in the e-mail address. Click OK.

5. Click OK.
