The campus is not confined to the south side of Rexburg. The Badger Creek Outdoor Learning Center is located 45 miles east of Rexburg at the base of the Grand Teton Mountains. Throughout the year, students experience such activities as snowshoeing, cross-country skiing, trail riding and backpacking. Badger Creek is run by the Recreation Education Department and provides students with terrific hands-on experience.
