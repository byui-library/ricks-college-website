Introduction to Pathophysiology
INTRO PATHOPHY
CLASS CODE: BIO 280 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An application of basic principles of physiology to pathological conditions. Designed for students of paramedicine and related fields. Does not fulfill requirements for Bachelors of Nursing Degree.
DESCRIPTION: An application of basic principles of physiology to pathological conditions. Designed for students of paramedicine and related fields. Does not fulfill requirements for Bachelors of Nursing Degree.
TOPICS: Etiology, Pathophysiology and treatment of diseases of the major organ systems of the body.
OBJECTIVES: To provide basic principles of pathophysiology to students of paramedicine enabling them to recognize and understand pathological conditions encountered in their profession.
REQUIREMENTS: Grade based on unit exams
