Cancellation Information: Please Read Carefully

Contracts cancelled before the 1st cancellation deadline will incur no termination fee.  Contracts cancelled after the 1st deadline but before the 2nd deadline will incur a $50 termination fee.  Contracts cancelled after the 2nd cancellation deadline will incur a 30% contract price termination fee.  Termination fees can be waived under the following circumstances - leaving to serve a mission, getting married, or graduation.  Proof of a mission call, wedding announcement, or graduation report is required to waive the termination fee.

Cancellation Deadlines
Fall Semester / Fall Block - 1st Deadline June 30, 2nd Deadline July 31 October Block - 1st Deadline Sep. 1, 2nd Deadline Oct. 1
Winter Semester / Winter Block - 1st Deadline Nov. 1, 2nd Deadline Dec. 1 March Block - 1st Deadline Jan. 1, 2nd Deadline Feb. 1
Summer - 30 days before contract begins
