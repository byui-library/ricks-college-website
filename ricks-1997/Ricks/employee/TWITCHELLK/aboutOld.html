Kevin E. Twitchell has a BA in History and Asian Studies from Brigham Young University and an MS in Computer Science from the University of Idaho. He has 20 years of industrial software engineering experience including real-time systems, statistical process control, machine vision and other manufacturing systems, system administration, software quality, software engineering, Web development, and software project management. In the area of Web development, he has managed or lead projects funded in excess of $1.6 million. He has been a member of the University of Idaho adjunct faculty for over six years and has specialized in Internet-related courses. He currently teaches computer science at Ricks College.
