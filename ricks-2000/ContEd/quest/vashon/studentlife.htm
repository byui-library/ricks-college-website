STUDENT LIFE

The 26 Quest students per term are housed in male and female dormitories that were constructed in 1997.  Rooms are shared with one to three other Quest participants depending upon the room configuration.  A staff resident assistant provides leadership and support to student residents.

 
Room and board are provided to students during their stay at Morningside on Vashon. A computer is furnished in each room for word processing and performing other home work assignments.  There are four networked computers in a lab that students may utilize to complete Internet course work, email, and research via the Internet. Other facilities include a laundry in each dorm provided at no charge while residing at Morningside, recreational equipment, and audiovisual equipment.   
At Morningside, it seems easier to concentrate on spiritual matters due to the absence of many of the distractions found on other campuses.   
Learning at Vashon occurs in a traditional classroom, in peer study groups, and one-by-one between faculty, administrators and other students.   Other times the quietness of the beautiful garden property allows students time to learn in solitude.
 

 Quest participants are encouraged to take advantage of the different opportunities that are presented to learn from resident experts.  Everyone from the aquarist at Point Defiance Aquarium to the engineers at K2 can direct and guide students to new interest and perhaps a career.
