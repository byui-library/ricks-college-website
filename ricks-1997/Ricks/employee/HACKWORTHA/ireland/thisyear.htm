Present Rose
    RÓISÍN RYAN EGENTON

       Róisín (23) has always wanted to live and work in  Ireland and this was the primary reason for getting a degree in International Studies. She lives in   Fanwood,  New Jersey with her parents Michael and Katherine, and her brother Cormac. Róisín loves performing and has played the violin since the age of 3. She was Principal of the Manhattan School of Music  Philharmonic and even performed twice in Carnegie
Hall.
