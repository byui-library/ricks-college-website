NEW STUDENTS ARRIVE FOR FINAL SUMMER TERM

The lazy days of summer seem more hectic this week with the arrival of hundreds of new students. Many of the new students are recent graduates from high schools across the country. They have arrived to get a jump start on their education by taking classes during the final five-week term that began Monday. Although official numbers are not available, Assistant Academic Vice President Jim Gee believes there is a record number of students enrolled for the third summer term.

It's expected that even more students will be studying at Ricks in coming summers when the college starts offering upper division classes.

WORK ON TRANSITION TO BYU-IDAHO CONTINUES

"The Spirit of Ricks" will not be lost with the transition from Ricks College to Brigham Young University-Idaho. In speaking to employees on June 29, Ricks President David A. Bednar said, "One of the major challenges is to make sure that as we go through the transition that we preserve and enhance and even substantially improve the 'Spirit of Ricks.' "

He added that Ricks College hasn't changed its name to Brigham Young University-Idaho yet. The exact timing of the name change is unsure, but may happen sometime next year. The name change depends on the institution receiving "candidacy" status to be considered for accreditation as a four-year institution from the Northwest Accreditation Commission.

During the question-and-answer meeting, Community Services Vice President Brent Kinghorn said intercollegiate athletics would likely continue for at least two years. The college will keep commitments that were made to student athletes and other colleges in the conference.

An expanded student activity program will take the place of intercollegiate athletics at BYU-Idaho. Bob Wilkes, Student Life Vice President, said a new committee has been formed to look at the many options for student activities. "Out of this we expect them to have a structure in place that will help us involve great numbers of students in good things. The bottom line is we anticipate that there are going to be a large number of students involved in a broad spectrum of activities that would include athletics, performing arts and academic activities."

The First Presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the Board of Trustees of Ricks College announced that Ricks College will change from its present two-year junior college status to a four-year institution June 21.

Information about the transition is posted on the Ricks College homepage at www.byui.edu. This Internet site will be continually updated as new information becomes available.

AMERICAN FOLK DANCERS PERFORMING IN SPAIN AND PORTUGAL

The American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band are in their second week of a month-long tour of Spain and Portugal. The student performers are goodwill ambassadors for Ricks College, The Church of Jesus-Christ of Latter-day Saints, and the United States of America.

Shows are scheduled for LDS stake activities in the Spanish cities of Barcelona, Madrid, Seville, and Cadiz and the Portugese cities of Setubal, Lisbon, and Porto. The concluding two weeks will put the Americans on center stage of Grupo Folclórico de Barcelinhos, an international folk festival in northern Portugal and western Spain.

"Our Ricks students are always terrific. They are so enthusiastic, charismatic, and such wholesome young people to represent the United States that they really stand out," said artistic director Charles West. "They are not necessarily professionals, but the radiation they display really gets to the audiences. Their smiles and demeanor on stage is outstanding."

NORWEGIAN ORGANIST TO PLAY RICKS RUFFATTI

Eric Gundersen, a Norwegian student who has studied with Ricks College organist Darwin Wolford, will present a recital Friday, July 14, at 7:30 p.m. The free recital will be held in the Barrus Concert Hall where he will play the Ruffatti organ.

Gundersen first came to Rexburg as a senior in high school and graduated from Madison High School in 1997. He served a mission for the LDS Church in Birmingham, England, before coming to Ricks College.
