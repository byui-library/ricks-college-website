Women's Health Issues
WOMEN'S HLTH
CLASS CODE: HS 131W CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HEALTH SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Basic Skills requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This class has been specifically designed to meet the needs of the female students of BYU-Idaho. All the basic elements of a health class are covered but the specific needs of women's health concerns are emphasized.
The class is designed to build on the absolute glory of womanhood, through all the unique experiences that women are to be involved in.
DESCRIPTION: This class has been specifically designed to meet the needs of the female students of BYU-Idaho. All the basic elements of a health class are covered but the specific needs of women's health concerns are emphasized.
The class is designed to build on the absolute glory of womanhood, through all the unique experiences that women are to be involved in.
TOPICS: The class is divided into three areas:
1. Emotional health and well being.
2. Maintaining a healthy lift-style
(Fitness and nutrition)
3. Physical health (the female body)
OBJECTIVES: This course has been designed to offer the women of BYUI with a multidisciplinary view of Women's contemporary health issues including the ability to do the following:
1. Identify types of emotional and physical health care available for Women's needs.
2. Identify individuals and groups at risk for various emotional and physical problems.
3. Gain specific strategies for maintaining a fit and healthy life-style emotionally, physically, and spiritually.
REQUIREMENTS: Class attendance is required.
Assignments will be given each class period.
The class has two options:
1. That you participate in a fitness program 3 - 4 days a week for 12 weeks OR
2. That you write 4 reviews on research articles.
There will be three test throughout the semester.
PREREQUISITES: You must be a WOMAN!!!!!!!
OTHER: This is such a wonderful class that the instructors just love to teach. Hope you can fit it in your schedule!
