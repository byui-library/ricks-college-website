Unit #8 - ELECTROCHEMISTRY

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Indicate on a diagram of an electrochemical cell (either electrolytic or galvanic), the anode, the cathode, and the direction of current flow.
2. Interconvert charge (in coulombs), current (in amperes), and moles of electrons (in faradays).
3. Obtain the standard potential and/or equilibrium constant of a reaction from standard reduction potentials (Appendix J).
4. Use the Nernst equation to obtain reaction potentials at specified non-standard conditions.
5. Predict the sponaneity of a reaction from its reaction potential.
