'A' Student Checklist
Determine your ability to succeed in college by evaluating whether you too can make the following affirmative statements made by 'A' students of BYU-Idaho.  Don't forget to score yourself at the bottom of the page.
Utilize Campus Resources

yesno 1.I am familiar with the FREE services Learning Assistance offers in Tutoring, Writing, Reading, Math, Study Skills, ESL, and Services for Students with Disabilities.
yesno 2.I use the library and it's services.
yesno 3.I use the services available to students on the BYU-Idaho Students web page.
yesno 4.I consult with my academic advisor.
yesno 5.I don’t hesitate to get a tutor for a course even if I am doing well.
Know your Schedule

yesno 1.I know my class schedule, including when and where classes are held and the professor's name.
yesno 2.I know my class syllabi and refer to them often.
Set Academic Goals

yesno 1.I have a specific goal in mind as to why I am attending BYU-Idaho and know what I want to get from my education.
yesno 2.I make goals and work to achieve them.
Time Management

yesno 1.I prioritize activities. I write down all the things I have to do, or want to do, and then decide which is the most important, next important, etc. I then focus on the first item, and get it done. Then do the second item, etc.
yesno 2.I make and use a semester calendar so I can see ahead of time when exams and projects are due. This makes projects easier to complete because I can break them into segments with due dates for each segment.
yesno 3.I make a weekly schedule including specific times to study specific subjects. 
yesno 4.I evaluate how my time is used each week, and make improvements.
yesno 5.I've learned to say "no" when others want me to play before studying.
yesno 6.I use the idle time between classes wisely, even if it’s only 5-10 minutes, by studying notes or flash cards.
Lifestyle Management

 

yesno 1.I eat properly, with five servings of fruits and vegetables daily.
yesno 2.I schedule regular exercise to give my body a break from studying.
yesno 3.I get the proper amount of sleep; I go to bed early.
yesno 4.I think of the time I spend in classes and studying as being equivalent to a full time job. 
yesno 5.I take advantage of all the opportunities available at BYU-Idaho.
yesno 6.I develop friends outside of my apartment.
yesno 7.If there is a conflict with roommates, I sit down and talk it out.
yesno 8.Along with required courses, I take other classes of personal interest.
yesno 9.I try to find something to enjoy about each subject.
yesno 10.I challenge myself to be motivated.
yesno 11.I don’t worry about things that cannot be controlled.
yesno 12.I remember to stay close to the Lord through prayer, scripture study, and church attendance.
yesno 13.I listen 70% of the time and speak only 30% of the time.
yesno 14.I follow the honor code; I always do my own work.
yesno 15.I keep track of my scores in each class.
yesno 16.I keep track of personal disks, papers, homework etc.
yesno 17.I try to help others and find that as I do so, whether academically or otherwise, I also learn and grow.
18.I schedule free time.
Maintaining a Healthy Professor-Student Relationship

yesno 1.I have the mind set that my professors are here to help me succeed and are not adversaries.
yesno 2.I make sure the professor not only knows me but also knows that I am interested in my education.
yesno 3.I try not to be shy.
yesno 4.I arrive early to each class and sit in the front so that I can see the professor easily and so that I have fewer distractions.
yesno 5.I try to develop a friendly relationship with my professors and talk with them regularly.
yesno 6.I make appointments with my professors if something is not clear.
yesno 7.I try to understand the professor’s expectations.
yesno 8.I maintain a positive attitude toward the subjects being studied by focusing on learning and not just on the grade.
yesno 9.I participate in class by asking questions.
yesno 10.I complete all of my readings and assignments on time.
Preparation for Class

yesno 1.I read the assigned chapter(s) before class, and review my class notes within 24 hours of class.
yesno 2.I use the few minutes between classes to review notes from the previous class and to preview the assigned reading.
yesno 3.I finish my homework before going to class and don’t procrastinate.
yesno 4.I go to class with formulated questions I would like to have answered.
Getting the Most out of Class

yesno 1.I try not to schedule back-to-back classes.
yesno 2.I listen for the patterns of organization which the professors use as they lecture, then organize my notes in the same manner.
yesno 3.If the topic or class is not of interest to me, I try to become interested. I try to find a reason to become involved in every discussion or lecture.
yesno 4.If I don’t understand something during class, I jot down questions in the side of my notes and then talk with the teacher about it.
yesno 5.I ask questions; I recognize that I am paying my professors to help me.
yesno 6.I am mentally involved in all class discussions! 
yesno 7.I take notes in class and keep those notes organized. 
yesno 8.I get to know people in each of my classes, especially students who seem to be serious students. I form study groups with them and compare notes.
Getting it to Stick

yesno 1.I study during daylight hours.
yesno 2.I study my hardest or most boring subjects first while I am still fresh.
yesno 3.I have a set study time instead of "Whenever I have time".
yesno 4.I avoid the ineffective marathon study sessions!
yesno 5.I don’t study where I sleep, and don’t sleep where I study.
yesno 6.I find a quiet place to study so I can focus properly.
yesno 7.I study in the same place so my mind can be trained to do a certain thing in a certain place.
yesno 8.If I feel stressed I take a five to ten minute non-TV break from studying and then come back and study a different subject.
yesno 9.To help combat distractions I make a mark on a piece of paper every time my mind wanders or is distracted. After several days of doing so, my concentration deepens and the number of marks begins to decrease.
yesno 10.I use flash cards, which is a good way to learn facts.
yesno 11.I look for application from what I am learning to everyday life.
yesno 12.I try to teach what I learn.
yesno 13.I use mnemonic devices to help me memorize.
yesno 14.I frequently review my notes, so that through repetition it will become permanent.
Reading Text

yesno 1.I've learned how to STUDY a textbook rather than just read it. 
yesno 2.I read my textbook in segments, recite after each section (not after each chapter), then review what I have read when finished.
yesno 3.I write in and mark my text books.
yesno 4.I am not afraid to disagree with a textbook.
yesno 5.I am familiar with my textbook; I study the summary at the end of each chapter first, and write down possible test questions from each paragraph. I take notes on what I have read.
yesno 6.I write down any questions about the assigned reading to ask in class.
yesno 7.I ALWAYS keep up on reading.
yesno 8.I know that if I experience difficulty comprehending what I read that I can get help in the Reading Center.
yesno 9.I keep a dictionary handy for referencing unfamiliar words.
Develop the Art of Questioning

yesno 1.I am not afraid to ask questions in class because chances are someone else will have the same question. I also recognize that asking questions will help the instructor get to know me.
yesno 2.For specific questions I make an appointment and talk to the teacher privately.
yesno 3.I continually ask myself questions while studying and try to find the answer before the study session is over.
yesno 4.I try to find answers to my questions as soon as possible!
yesno 5.I ask classmates questions on unclear points.
Develop Good Study Skills

yesno 1.I know that study habits can be developed and I have used the Study Skills Center.
yesno 2.I know my learning style.
yesno 3.I know how to take effective notes.
How to Take Exams

yesno 1.I learned effective testing strategies in the Study Skills Center. 
yesno 2.I find out what types of tests I will be taking and study appropriately.
yesno 3.I try to predict questions the professor might ask on the test.
yesno 4.I take tests early in the day while I am fresh.
yesno 5.I review all my notes, textbook chapters, homework, and tests!
yesno 6.I begin preparing two to three days before the test, not the night before!
yesno 7.I start preparing for my final exams after the very first class period!
yesno 8.I don’t cram. I know that if I do I will most likely forget everything in two or three days.
yesno 9.I think of tests as a way to demonstrate how much I know. I use the returned test to review the points I missed.
