Athletes of the Year
Mari Burningham Carpenter 
BYU Women's Volleyball

Ricks Coach JoAnn Reeve calls Mari "the best, smartest, and most dominating hitter we've ever had." As a sophomore and team captain at Ricks in 1997 she had 734 kills, averaging over five per game, tops in junior college competition. She was named a NJCAA First Team All-American. Prior to Ricks she had spent one year at UCLA, receiving All-Pac 10 freshmen team Honorable Mention honors. Transferring to Ricks, she reinjured her back early in the season and underwent surgery. A two-sport athlete out of Logan, Utah, Mari joined the BYU women's basketball team under former Ricks Coach Trent Shippen. But volleyball is her first love. As a junior outside hitter at BYU she played for the fifth-best NCAA women's volleyball team in the nation. As a senior last fall her team reached the final 16 nationally with a 28-5 overall record. She was named to the Mountain West Second Team at the end of the season.

Mari is a student/athlete at BYU and a member of the Slate Canyon 5th Ward of the Provo Utah Stake where she is the youth athletics assistant coordinator.

She is married to Matthew Carpenter. They met at Ricks and were both students in a volleyball class. In 1997 Matthew was the assistant volleyball coach to Coach JoAnn Reeve.

Of her time at Ricks, Mari has high praise for several people on campus. She says, "I found myself in an environment that allowed me to succeed and to feel loved and cared about by faculty, staff and administrators."

Mari enjoys fly fishing, reading, writing, sky diving, basketball, listening to music, watching movies, outdoor volleyball, camping and traveling.
