Winter Orientation Overview

Winter Orientation Schedule 

Saturday, January 5, 2002:

Info Fair
9 a.m.-1 p.m. - Manwaring Center West Lounge
Representatives from various areas on campus will be available to answer questions and disseminate information to interested parents and students.


Parents Orientation
10-11 a.m. - Manwaring Center West Ballroom
Parents can learn about the Ricks College experience from Elder Bednar, President of BYU-Idaho and his wife Sister Bednar.


Presidents Reception For Parents
Starts at 11 a.m. - Manwaring Center East Ballroom
Parents of new students are invited to visit with Elder Bednar and Sister Bednar during the President's Reception.  Light refreshments will be served.    Representatives from various areas on campus will also be adjacent to the ballroom in the Manwaring Center West Lounge as part of the Info Fair to answer questions and distribute information to students and interested parents.


New Student Barbecue
3:30 - 5:30 p.m. - Manwaring Center East Ballroom - Check-in begins at 3 p.m.
The New Student Barbecue provides new and transfer students the opportunity to meet other new students, make friends, and feel a part of BYU-Idaho.  Here, new students will meet  other members of their Y-Team, led by Y-Team Leaders who are upperclassmen.  New students will participate in their Y-Team by attending all Orientation activities and events together as a group.   

New Student Commencement
6-7 p.m. - Taylor Chapel
New Student Commencement serves as the official welcome to BYU-Idaho by Elder Bednar and Sister Bednar.  The commencement emphasizes the importance of gaining an education by study and by faith as well as the importance of establishing the goal of graduation from BYU-Idaho.   

First Night Extravaganza
8-11 p.m. - Manwaring Center
The Manwaring Center is transformed on the First Night of every Orientation to Party Central.  New and returning students are invited to particiapte.  The cost is $3 but includes food, drink, dances, inflatables rides, games, free giveaways, and much more.  Don't miss the most exciting event of each semester!!! 


Sunday,  January 6, 2002:
*Attend Church Services (various times)


Monday, January 7, 2002:

Morningside: AArise for Honor"
8-9 a.m. - Taylor Chapel
New students, along with their Y-Teams, are encouraged to wake up early and attend the "Arise For Honor" Morningside which focuses on the tradition of honor at BYU-Idaho.  This student-led presentation is one you won't want to miss.  A free continental breakfast will be served to all in attendance.

Free Continental Breakfast/Campus Tours
9-11 a.m. - Campus Locations
A free continental breakfast will be served to all new students who attend the Arise For Honor event.  Following breakfast new students and their Y-Team will participate in Campus Tours, which includes a tour of campus buildings, services, and traditions, along with other exciting surprises.  Plan on dressing warm, but don't fret about the cold - plenty of hot chocolate will be available to warm you up!

Academic Department Orientations
11 a.m.-12:00 Noon - Various Locations
The Academic Department Orientation meetings provide students the opportunity to learn more about their major and specific class requirements.  Each division/department conducts its own orientation.  Ask your Y-Team Leader where your Department Orientation will take place.  Free lunch will be provided to all new  students who participate in this meeting.

Academic Departmental Luncheon
12-1 p.m. Manwaring Center West Ballroom 
By ticket only.  Go to your departmental meeting to get a ticket!

LDS Humanitarian Service Project
2-2:30 p.m. - Manwaring Center West Ballroom
2:30-4:30 p.m. - Manwaring Center East Ballroom
New students, with their Y-Team, will learn about the importance of service as well as participate in a meaningful service project which will benefit the lives of others.  This year we plan on painting and assembling wooden toys to be delivered to needy children in third world countries.   

Tuesday, January 8, 2002:

First Winter Semester Devotional
2-3 p.m. - Hart Auditorium, also broadcast to Taylor and Kirkham auditoriums.
Attend the first devotional of the semester featuring Elder David A. Bednar, President of BYU-Idaho.


Saturday, January 12, 2002:

New Student Concert/ASBYU Club Dance
Concert - 6:30-8 p.m. / Dance - 8 - 11 p.m. --Hart Auditorium
The office of New Student Orientation, along with ASBYU are sponsoring the New Student Concert which will feature the Seattle-based a cappella group The Coats.  New and returning students are invited to attend the concert.   Tickets for this event are $3.00 with a student ID and $4.00 without and includes both the concert and the ASBYU Club Dance.


Sunday, January 13, 2001:

CES Young Adult Fireside 
6-7 p.m. - Hart Auditorium (also broadcast to the Kirkham, and Taylor buildings).

 
  Winter New Student Orientation will take place Saturday, January 5th and Monday January 7th.
