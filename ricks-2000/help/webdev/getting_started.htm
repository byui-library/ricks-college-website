To get started on a new web page, all requests should be made to Bob Reed.   If you already have a page and need extra help, use Computer Based Training (CBT), the FrontPage 2000 Tutorials, other Tutorials, Web Development Rules, the Other Web Development Sites, or call the students of the Internet Team ext. 2157.

PC users should have FrontPage 2000 installed on their office machines.  If you do not have FrontPage 2000, contact your CCS to install it for you.  If there is question of who your CCS is, contact Doug Thompson ext. 2810.  MAC users that do not already have Dave Access and PageMill installed on their computer need to contact their CCS to install them. 

For support or technical questions contact the students of the Internet Team ext. 2157.  The Internet Team is here to teach and assist you as you create your web page.  If you choose to have an Internet Team member assist you, they will verify which type of computer you have and what software is needed.  (PCs-FrontPage 2000 and Macs-Dave Access and PageMill)  You will be asked to have the appropriate software installed, then contact the Internet Team member to schedule an appointment.   Before the appointment, have an idea of what you would like your web page to include and look like.  At the appointment, they will teach you how to map a drive, use the software, and develop your web page.  They are to be a resource to help and not to do it for you.  You are the one responsible for the upkeep and contents of your web page.

Distributed Data Services (DDS) personnel provide Systems Administration and technical support for all Ricks College Windows NT, Netware,Unix and Web servers.  Other responsibilities include employee and student e-mail, web services, and Internet news services.
