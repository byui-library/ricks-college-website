Student Relations Board

Hi! Welcome to the Student Relations office. My name is Kayla Anderson and I am the Student Relations Officer for the 2000-2001 school year. I am from Fruit Heights Utah, a little town near Salt Lake. I am the oldest of six children, three boys and three girls, just like the Brady Bunch, but cuter!. I love to swim, play soccer, make scrapbooks and read. I am majoring in English, which I might add, is the best major that Ricks (er-BYU Idaho) has to offer. :) This year in the office of Student Relations, I want to provide an opportunity for each and every student to serve; whether it is directly or indirectly. My office is a collaboration of everything that didn’t fit anywhere else. My Board is in charge of Service Week, Fund-raising Week, and Teacher Appreciation Week. These weeks are huge and help is always appreciated. I also help run the Student Solutions Committee, which was formally known as Ombudsman. This committee provides solutions to students problems and concerns that otherwise seem unsolvable. I also help host Christmas Tree Lane, a opportunity for clubs, organizations, and community business to decorate a tree and help add to the spirit of Christmas. Guitars Unplugged also falls under my Board and proves to be one of the best events on campus. Last of all, I help select Student of the Week and other campus recognition. Student Relations is a wonderful opportunity to get involved in many different area’s on campus. If you are interested in apply for the Student Relations Board you can pick up an application in the ASRC offices, on-line or call Kayla Anderson at 356-2273.
