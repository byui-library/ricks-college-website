On-Going Orientation

On-going Orientation is designed to continue the friendships and involvement which began with Campus Orientation.   Y-Team Leaders and new students are strongly  encouraged to continue their association with one another as part of On-Going Orientation.

Y-Team Leaders can submit activity proposals to the Office of New Student Orientation for their group to participate in together.  Activity proposals can cover academic, social, leadership, and spiritual activities.  Funding for each activity will be provided in full or part by the Office of New Student Orientation.
