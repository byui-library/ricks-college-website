COMPUTER ARCHITECTURE

CST - 220     SECTION II      1:00 - 1:50 DAILY

Prerequisites: EET - 151                                                                                 RETURN

Faculty Name: GORDON BLACK

Text: Assembling and Repairing Personal Computers, Prentice Hall, 1997, by Dan L. Beeson

Materials: None

Course Description: Introduction to the world of computer hardware and system timing. Use of simple programs to exercise the hardware and to implement control and I/O operations. Hands on Lab experience provided.

Course Objectives: 1. Become familiar with different types of computer systems.

                                 2. To develop an understanding of the function of each part of the system.

                                 3. Troubleshoot Computer and digital designed systems
