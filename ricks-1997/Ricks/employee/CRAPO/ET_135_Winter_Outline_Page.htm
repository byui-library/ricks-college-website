PREREQUISITES: None.

TEXT: Grob, Bernard. 1997. BASIC ELECTRONICS, 8th Ed. Glencoe. Westerville, Ohio.

COURSE MATERIALS: Text, Scientific Calculator.

COURSE DESCRIPTION: Principles of Basic Electricity and Ohm's Law are introduced. Series, parallel, and series-parallel circuits are applied to fundamental DC and AC circuits. Magnetism, inductors, and capacitors are studied. A short introduction to linear and digital integrated circuits may be included.

STUDENT OBJECTIVES:

1. To be able to analyze circuits and solve problems dealing with basic DC and AC circuits

2. To be able to determine proper component values in DC and AC circuits.
