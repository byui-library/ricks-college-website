Outstanding Teacher Award

Purpose

To publicly honor and recognize those individuals who are considered outstanding teachers, by his/her peers or former students.

Criteria

  1. The nominees must be conidered a truly outstanding teacher by his/her peers adn actively incorporating current teaching methods with an emphasis on building student self-esteem.

  2. The candidate must be a full-time professional teacher with at least 10 years of service in grades preschool through senior high and be currently employed as a teacher.

  3. The nominee must exemplify the highest standards of moral, character, and personal integrity.

  4. Team teachers must be nominated individually.

  5. The nominee must be an alumni of Ricks, completing a minimum of 16 credits, not necessarily a graduate.
