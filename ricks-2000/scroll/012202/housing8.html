Computers in apartment, essential for some

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Many students feel it is essential for them to have computers in each apartment. Computer availability at all hours of the day is important to most college students who work hard in their studies.

“Computers in apartments aren’t necessary, but they are very useful,” Eric Nelson, a sophomore from Omaha, Neb., said.

Some apartments have computers for every roommate, in which case there is no need to share. This can eliminate fighting between roommates that may share a computer.

Apartments with only one computer benefit from the situation as well. Roommates use each others’ computers for school assignments and other things.

“I love having a computer in the apartment. It’s easier to do school work; we can print up papers before we go to class. I use it a lot — about four times a week,” Michele Blatter, a sophomore from Idaho Falls, said.

Computers in apartments may be useful in cases where students do their homework late at night. There are computers available for students in computer labs on some off-campus complexes. The labs are open from early in the morning to curfew at night for student’s use.

Birch Plaza has a computer lab with six stations available for their tenants. Many times this lab is filled with students who are waiting for an available computer.

There are also 1,000 computers accessible to students around campus. Four hundred and fifty of those computers are located in the David O. Mckay Library, according to the library home page.

Even with the large number of computers available, many students still feel that having a computer in their apartment is necessary.

“I strongly agree with having computers in the apartment. It’s easier to do the e-mail thing and write papers,” Josh Wrathall, a sophomore from Rigby, Idaho, said.

The investment in computers has been an expense for many student’s. Some students have found a good deal on old or used computers.

“I got my computer in 1994, and it’s probably worth $500,” Wrathall said.

Computers can be a big investment or a considerably small investment, depending on the money available. Used computers are sold outside the BYU-Idaho Bookstore at times for as low as $100.

Depending on the computer students use, whether it be in the library or in their own apartment, computers are pretty essential in the lives of all college students.
