Summit Expeditions

 Spring Summit is generally in May but with the new 4 year program we may see some changes.  Check with the Department.  

We will have a Winter Summit during the Christmas Break.  Check with Scott Wood for details.

Canyonland Spring Summit

Cost for the trip includes tuition for the credit hours, food, transportation, group gear and river trip. You will be responsible for your own sleeping bag and pad, backpack, clothing and mountain bike.

PHYSICAL CONDITION: It is important that the health and physical condition of each student be generally good. Those people with heart malfunctions, weight problems, or other conditions that might jeopardize their well-being should consult with the program director and team leader before enrolling.

Besides the obvious reasons of "Learning by Doing," being in the great outdoors, and becoming close friends with some fine people, Summit has other advantages:

1. You can accelerate your college program.

2. You can complete classes in a very pleasant atmosphere.

3. You will receive special help from your instructors while you are on Summit.

4. In this day and age, experience is really more valuable than credits. On Summit you get both.

5. You learn about yourself -- strengths/weaknesses and your ability to extend your limits.

Contact either Scott F. Wood or Colleen Masterson at the Recreation Education Department - MC 150, Ext. 2225 for more information. Limit is 12 students. Cost is approximately $850.

 

Winter Summit

Winter summit is in two time segments. The first part will begin with a three day, two night winter camping trip.  You will learn backcountry skiing, and telemark turns.

The second session (after Christmas) will be taught in the Teton Wilderness Area east of Driggs, Idaho. This is a more rigorous trip where we will move camp each day enabling us to see and experience a wide variety of terrain.  Advanced intensive route finding, using maps and compass, avalanche awareness skills such as forecasting, avalanche pits, beacon search and probing will be covered. We will also deal with winter first aid and evacuation.

Cost of trip will be around $350. This includes instruction, ski equipment, food, transportation and four credits.
