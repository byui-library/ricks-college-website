Confucianism

Moral Truth
The great religious leaders of the world such as Mohammed, Confucius, and the Reformers, as well as philosophers including Socrates, Plato, and others, received a portion of God's light. Moral truths were given to them by God to enlighten whole nations and to bring a higher level of understanding to individuals.
Moral Purpose
Consistent with these truths, we believe that God has given and will give to all peoples sufficient knowledge to help them on their way to eternal salvation, either in this life or in the life to come.
Fullness
We also declare that the gospel of Jesus Christ, restored to His Church in our day, provides the only way to mortal life of happiness and a fullness of joy forever. For those who have not received this gospel, the opportunity will come to them in the life hereafter if not in this life.

Li and language
"If terms are not correct, language is not in accordance with the truth of things, affairs cannot be carried out to successTherefore a superior person considers it necessary that the names used are spoken appropriately and that what is spoken is carried out appropriately. The superior person requires that in word and deed there is nothing incorrect."
Li and five constant relationships
1. Parent (loving) and child (respectful)
2. Husband and wife
3. Elder sibling and junior sibling
4. Elder friend and junior friend
5. Ruler and subject
A story about Li
A very elderly woman asked her daughter-in-law (with whom she lived) to fetch her fresh fish during the winter. The young woman went to the frozen river, prostrated herself and bared her breasts upon the ice. She caught the fish that surfaced and fed them to her mother-in-law."
An interpretation
· Ice: Mother-in-law and Daughter-in-law
· Fish: nourishment, attention, kindness
· Breast: intimate maternal nourishment
· The daughter-in-law melted the ice between her and her mother-in-law by loving the elderly woman as mother would her own child. The proper relationship is founded upon love and giving of self.
