Born in Raymond, Alberta in 1941, I graduated from BYU with a masters degree in French and in 1968 came to Ricks College. But for a semester in Paris, France; a semester in Jerusalem, Israel; and a three-year LDS Church mission to Siberia, Russia, I have enjoyed continuous employment at Ricks College. This is home. In 1960 I returned from an LDS mission to France and Belgium. Shortly thereafter married my childhood sweetheart Carol Bingham.

 

 

 


We have 10 children, nine of whom are married and have blessed us with an impressive posterity (soon to be 24 grandchildren and counting.)

Teaching young people is my passion. I love to turn them on to learning--to discovery. I enjoy trout fishing, running, (I’ve run a total of 10 marathons) and travel. My occupation has given me opportunity to travel regularly to Europe, Russia, and the Middle East. Since 1970 I have been organizing and conducting numerous tours to these lands. It’s a hobby I love to do for people.

While teaching at Ricks College, I served for five years as Foreign Language department chair and for eight years, Dean of the Division of Humanities. In July 1999, Carol and I returned from our three-year mission to Siberia where we served and thusly fell in love with the Russian people and our missionaries. We look forward to returning to that country.
