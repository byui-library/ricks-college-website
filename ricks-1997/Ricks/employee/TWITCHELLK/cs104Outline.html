Class
Preparation 		Brigham Young said, "We are prepared for some things, and we receive just as fast as we prepare ourselves." You are expected to study assigned material prior to class.
Homework Homework assignments allow you to further explore the material we discuss in class. Late homework is penalized 10% the first school day and 30% the second school day. Homework is not accepted after two school days late. It is your responsibility to submit homework correctly, including, but not limited to, the correct location and the correct format. Incorrect homework submissions are subject to late penalties, including not being accepted. You may not resubmit homework that has already been graded.
Grading Your grade is the total accumulation of homework points earned plus three points for each class attended. The grade for this class is either "pass" or "fail." A minimum of 91 points are required to pass: this leaves little room for error. While there is no extra credit, students are permitted to make up assignments with a 50% late penalty. No homework shall be accepted after the final date listed in the anticipated schedule below.
Justice
and
Mercy 		Homework assignments are to be completed as scheduled. Do not procrastinate! (See Alma 34:33.) However, mercy is available for a limited number of emergency situations if you discuss your situation with me before (not after) the homework assignment is due. Otherwise, justice prevails. There will be no routine extensions of due dates - be prepared to justify any requests for extensions.
Disclaimer The instructor reserves the right to make changes to this course outline, including the anticipated schedule, as well as to homework assignments. You will be notified in class when changes are made.
