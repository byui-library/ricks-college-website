Question: 

Is seminary graduation a requirement?

 

Answer: 

Seminary attendance and graduation are considered strongly in the admissions process.  If a student has had access to seminary and has not attended or will/has not graduated, their chances of being admitted will be diminished.  If there are legitimate reasons for not graduating, these should be explained.  An admissions counselor and committee will review the reasons.  Students who have served a mission or are high school graduates enrolled in Institute will have their missionary service and Institute involvement considered instead of seminary.
