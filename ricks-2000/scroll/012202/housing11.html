Make your apartment look, feel like a home

by Janielle Gurney
 GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Moving away from home can be difficult for college students. Not only is there a change of living with family to roommates, but there is also the transition from a “home” to an apartment.

“Not seeing your family is the hardest part of moving away from home,” Marcela Galls, a freshman from Winnetca, Calif., said.

BYU-Idaho students try to make their apartments more of a home by putting pictures on the wall, having scheduled chores and using decorations and other items often brought from home.

Marchelle Morley, a sophomore from Midvale, Utah, makes her apartment feel more like a home by putting up decorations and pictures of the Savior on the wall, she said.

Some students feel that the people living in the apartment can make it seem more like a home. Good communication and friendship can have an impact on the overall environment of the apartment.

“It feels more like a home because of good roommates, as well as pictures of family on the wall and pictures of the mission,” Brent Mackey, a sophomore from Detroit, Mich., said.

Equal responsibilities for each roommate can contribute to a homey atmosphere and aid in keeping the apartment clean.

“It feels more like a home when everyone has equal responsibilities,” Cassie Honn, a junior from Emmett, Idaho, said.

The spiritual aspect of love shared between roommates through special experiences can come with a clean home. By allowing the Lord into the home by keeping the apartment clean, students are finding their apartments more peaceful.

“We have apartment prayer every night, including a spiritual thought or message. This brings more harmony to our apartment,” Davida Neet, a sophomore from Las Vegas, said.

Students also feel that having dinner together makes it feel more like home. Nate Stevens, a junior from Idaho Falls, and his roommates eat together on Sunday evenings, and that makes the apartment homey, he said.

Stevens’ first action when he moved in was to “set up stereo systems with surround sound to say this is our pad,” he said.

Students are attempting all different ways of creating a home in their apartment. With the feeling of a home, it can provide a comfortable place for every roommate to live.
