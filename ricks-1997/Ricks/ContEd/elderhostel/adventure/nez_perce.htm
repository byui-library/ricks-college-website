HORSE PACKING IN THE LEMHI MOUNTAINS
Explore the Lemhi Mountains by horseback and travel part of the route Chief Joseph took in his attempt to escape to Canada. Understand the significance of the Nez Perce War of 1877 and Chief Joseph’s promise to “fight no more.” Investigate Indian campsites and a ghost town. Study the environmental sciences of one of Idaho’s scenic areas. Participants should be in good physical condition and be able to ride horseback in mountain terrain for several miles a day at an elevation of 7,000 feet; hostelers should also be comfortable with camping.
