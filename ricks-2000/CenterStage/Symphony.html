The Idaho Falls Symphony was formed in 1949 from a group of musicians gathered to present a performance of Handel's Messiah. Its first symphony concert was on April 26, 1950, under the leadership of Marcell Bird. Professor Mealy conducted until 1960. Among the landmarks of his tenure were appearances by noted artists such as Joseph Schuster and Grant Johnneson, as well as the first annual Youth Concert, which featured young artists Lamar Barrus and Carol Jaeger.

The orchestra moved to its new home, the Civic Auditorium, in 1954 and has been based there since that time. Succeeding conductors have included Robert Lenz, Dr. Lamar Barrus, Dr. Donald McGlothin, Mel Flood, Carl Eberl, and John LoPiccolo. The current conductor is Dr. George Adams.

Over the years this fine volunteer orchestra has brought the finest in symphonic music to Idaho Falls and the eastern Idaho area. The roster of artists who have performed with the orchestra includes names such as Stephanie Chase, Mischa Dichter, Gary Karr, Steven Honigberg, and Hans Pizka.

The orchestra's growth in musical stature and repertoire continues as it concludes its 52nd season. Its mission continues to be the presentation of the best in orchestral music to region audiences, as well as educational outreach to students in area schools.
