Elder Bruce R. McConkie's Ten Points
To Understanding Isaiah

1 - Gain an overall knowledge of the plan of salvation and of God's dealings with his earthly children

2 - Learn the position and destiny of the House of Israel in the Lord's eternal scheme of things

3 - Know the chief doctrines about which Isaiah chose to write

        a - Restoration of the Gospel
        b - Latter-day gathering of Israel
        c - Coming forth of the Book of Mormon
        d - Apostate conditions of the world in the latter-days
        e - Messianic prophecies of the first coming of the Messiah
        f - Second coming of the Messiah and the Millennium
        g -Historical data relative to his own day

4 - Use the Book of Mormon

5 - Use latter-day revelation

6 - Learn how the New Testament interprets Isaiah

7 - Study Isaiah in its Old Testament Context

8 - Learn the manner of Prophesying among the Jews in Isaiah's day

9 - Have the Spirit of Prophecy

10 - Devote ourselves to hard, conscientious study.
