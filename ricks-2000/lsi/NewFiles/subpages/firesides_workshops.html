Firesides

Each semester the marriage enrichment committee sponsors an evening fireside to assist couples. These generally start at 7:00 p.m. and last until 8:30 p.m.

Last fall, for example, Glenn Latham came to Rexburg to give a special presentation on parenting. Brother Latham has lectured extensively on the management of human behavior in home and school settings. He is a popular speaker with BYU Education Week and the Know Your Religion Series. Brother Latham holds a doctorate in education from Utah State University and has authored seven books and numerous articles on how to create "safe" environments in classrooms and families.
