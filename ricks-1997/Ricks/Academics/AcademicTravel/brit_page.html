The Ricks College British Literary Pilgrimage is a concentrated, four-week study abroad program which examines the cultural and historic settings of writers from England, Ireland, Wales, and Scotland. Such settings include writers’ birthplaces, gravesites, homes, churches, hometowns, "stomping grounds," and the like. In addition to visiting literary sites, tour directors teach British literature "on site." Tour participants will also visit France and Belgium, archeological sites, LDS Church history sites, world-class museums, and popular tourist attractions--among other things. Furthermore, tour participants will see at least five plays in England, two of which by the world-renowned Royal Shakespeare Company.
