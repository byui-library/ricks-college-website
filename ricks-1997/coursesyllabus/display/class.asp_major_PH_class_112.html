ELEMENTARY PHYSICS LAB
ELEM PHYS LAB
CLASS CODE: PH 112 CREDITS:  1 FEE:  $5.00
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This lab will give experience at an elementary level in making measurements and analyzing data in several areas of Physics.
DESCRIPTION: An elementary laboratory designed to give students experience in measurement and data analysis at a beginning level in all areas of physics.
TOPICS: Measurements,Relationships & Graphs,Displacement,Speed,Velocity,Acceleration.
Mass,Volume,Density,
Graphing II,
Elasticity & Oscillations,
Ohm's Law,
Series Circuits,Parallel Circuits,
Atomic Spectra,
Brightness,Distance,Cost of Electricity,
Convex Lenses & Images,
Waves & Sound,
Seismology.
OBJECTIVES: The purpose of this lab is to help you think physically by giving you experience making measurements and analyzing data at a beginning level in many areas of physics. A more specific goal is to understand how graphs relate to measurements.
REQUIREMENTS: Student Lab Manual.
