Personal Safety Resources
Afraid to walk alone?  Good!  Recognizing that you can do something about your personal safety Is empowering!
Our department is concerned for your safety as well.  Our Student Security guards insure your personal safety around Ricks College Campus by providing many services such as  Safety Escorts  and Building lock ups. Our Officers Patrol the Campus on foot, on bikes and in vehicles. Our officers also provide self protection seminars so that you can be educated in current crime prevention techniques and the latest in personal protection.

The Safety Escort Program.
This program is designed for those who feel uncomfortable walking alone on the campus day or night. Our guards will walk you from anywhere on the confines of  the Campus. To request the Safety Escort service. call 356-2448;  A uniformed guard will appear shortly.


Student Security guards providing an "Escort".



Building lock ups and let ins.
Denying the opportunity for a thief to steal from the building is extremely important in a campus environment. Our Security Guards lock each building  every night.
Need into a building?  Our Student Security will let you in provided you have proper authorization from the appropriate campus administrator. To see if you qualify, contact our office 356-2448.
