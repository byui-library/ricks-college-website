Course Description


English 107 is designed to teach learning strategies and reading skills necessary to read college texts. The course emphasizes vocabulary, comprehension, and fluency in oral reading. Often, after learning a strategy in class, you will be asked to show mastery of the strategy by using it with a text from one of your other classes. The course concentrates on reading and thinking skills through whole class, group, and individual instruction. You will meet with a personal tutor in the Reading Lab (Clark 329) at least once a week to review and improve the skills you learn in class.


Course Objectives

1. To develop and practice strategies and skills that will transfer across disciplines.

2. To show students how to be active readers.

3. To reinforce the idea that reading is enjoyable.

Attendance Requirements

Attendance is very important. Every two absences will result in a 1/3 of a grade penalty. For example, if your final grade was a B+, but you were absent two times from class, then your grade would be adjusted to a B. If you were absent four times, your grade would be adjusted to a B-, etc.
