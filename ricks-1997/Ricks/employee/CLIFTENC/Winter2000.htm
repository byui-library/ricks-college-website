Library Science 111

COURSE DESCRIPTION: An introductory course on the use of libraries and library materials with an emphasis on the effective use of the Ricks College McKay Library.

OBJECTIVE: Students will be able to locate library reference materials and know their use. They will be able to research a topic and prepare a bibliography of materials on that subject.

TEXT: Marcia Alldredge. Using the David O. McKay Library
