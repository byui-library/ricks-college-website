Ricks beats Western Montana in home opener

by Nathan Hathaway
hat97003@byui.edu
Scroll Staff

Aaron Bowman went the humble route.

His 30 points in the Vikings’ 91-75 win over Western Montana College Thursday weren’t anything to balk at, of course, but he wanted to talk about Ricks’ collective effort.

When Bowman was asked about his hot hand, he simply said, “No, I don’t want to talk about that. It’s all about the team. If it wasn’t for the team, none of that would happen. If it wasn’t for our big guys and our guys setting picks, none of that scoring would have been possible.”

But Ricks Coach Clyde Nelson had no problem talking about Bowman's scoring.

“I don’t know that we expect 30 out of him every night,” Nelson said. “He’ll be our scorer and he’ll be the guy that we’ll go to.”

Despite Bowman’s 30 points, Ricks looked rocky at the outset of the game, missing layup after layup.

“I think that’s inexperience. You get a little scared,” Nelson said. “We tried to do things too fast instead of at our own pace.”

By the end it was all smooth sailing for the Vikes.

“I just loved how we battled,” Nelson said. “We just showed a lot of energy tonight and played hard.
Ricks kicked it into gear midway through the first half. Down 26-23, the Vikings went on a 10-0 run capped by a Bowman three-point play. The Bulldogs never regained the lead after the Ricks spurt. The Vikings took a 49-42 lead into the locker room at halftime.

The Vikings put the game away coming out of the half. Center Aaron McKee sparked a 14-3 Viking surge to put the game out of Western Montana’s reach. Twice in the second half Ricks jumped out to a 21-point lead.

Quite a difference from when the teams matched up last Tuesday and WMC beat Ricks by 26.

“You could see a huge difference between how we played them up there and how we played them down here,” Nelson said.

The Vikings shot 56 percent from the field for the game, including 40 percent from 3-point range. The Bulldogs made a meager 38 percent of their field goals and 26 percent of their three-pointers.

Viking guard Adam Hiatt added 24 points to Bowman’s 30 and Jeremy Thomas chipped in with 15 for Ricks. Cesario Chipepo led Western Montana with 17 points. Richard Griffin scored 11 for the Bulldogs and pulled down 14 rebounds, 11 of them offensive.
