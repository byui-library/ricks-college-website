Survey of Accounting
SURVEY OF ACCT
CLASS CODE: ACCTG 180 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: ACCOUNTING
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: For nonbusiness majors and certain specialized business programs. This is an introduction to financial and managerial accounting principles with exposure to basic accounting statements, processes, and management applications. Transfers as elective credit only.
DESCRIPTION: This course is designed to introduce students to both financial and managerial accounting principles with exposure to basic accounting statements, processes, and management applications. This course is designed mainly for nonbusiness majors with certain specialized business and information systems programs.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: The main objective of the course is to help students develop the skills and confidence necessary to apply accounting concepts and techniques in analyzing, planning, and controlling operations of various kinds of organizations.
REQUIREMENTS: Prior to each class meeting, students are expected to study the chapter material assigned for the day and complete the given assignment. The instructor will normally devote a part of each class meeting to lecture and demonstration of selected items from the assigned materials, the objective being to clarify and elaborate on the selected items because of their special relevance or difficulty. Students are encouraged to ask questions and otherwise enter fully into class discussions.
