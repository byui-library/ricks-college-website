POINTS TO PONDER
  • What is electromagnetic radiation?   How is it related to energy emitted by the sun?  What is the relationship between temperature of an emitting body and wavelength?  What is the electromagnetic spectrum and how is it related to various energy wavelengths?
  • What is the "solar constant?"  What is its numerical value?
  • What is "insolation?"  What is significant about the angle of the sun's rays?  What determines the angle of the sun above the horizon?  If you are given date and a location could you calculate the "solar angle"?  Explain the changing pattern of daily insolation through the year at different latitudes.  click here for sample problems.
  • How does latitude affect the amount of insolation we receive?  What is the pattern of seasonal insolation receipt at the following locations at the different seasons of the year?  Equator.  Tropic of Cancer or Capricorn.  The Arctic or Antarctic Circle.  The North or South Pole.
  • What are the world latitude zones and why are they significant?
  • What are the major gases and other components of the atmosphere?  What role does water vapor have?  What are hygroscopic (condensation) nuclei?  Why are they significant?  Where do they come from?  What is happening to ozone in our atmosphere and why does it matter?
  • What is sensible heat and sensible heat transfer?  What is latent heat and how does it transfer energy across the earth?
  • What is the solar constant?  How is it related to the earth's "albedo"?   What is the "greenhouse effect?"  Why is it significant to us?  What is counterradiation (reradiation)?
  • Can you sketch and explain the diagram for the global energy balance?  What is "net radiation balance" and how does it relate to latitude and the "global energy balance"?
