Parking Overnight
Overnight parking on campus streets and parking lots, as well as city streets is prohibited from November 1 to March 1 every year.  This regulation is necessary to facilitate snow removal.  Vehicles parked in violation of this regulation will be cited regardless of weather conditions and towed at the owner’s expense when impeding snow removal equipment.
A few designated lots are exempt from this regulation.  They are the lots used by the on campus residence halls; Biddulph Lot, Taylor Lot (general parking area only), Pioneer Lot (between the girls on-campus residence halls), as well as the North Stadium Lot, and the Manwaring East Lot (general parking area only).
Contact Campus Police at Kimball 150 or 356-2448 any time day or night, for additional information.

Parking Permits
A Ricks College Parking Permit is required to park in all on-campus lots.  General Parking Permits are available at Campus Police, Kimball 150 for a $5.00 fee and are good for the academic year in lots designated “General Parking.”  Faculty and Staff parking lots are reserved and require a Faculty/Staff Permit.  Permits and parking regulations can be obtained at the Campus Police office or call 356-2448 if you have questions.

Payment Deadlines
Each student is responsible to meet the various payment deadlines which apply.  Failure to meet these deadlines will result in additional service charges and a hold on transcripts and registration.  Payments must be in the Cashier’s Office by these deadlines.  Details about various deadlines are found in the catalog, class schedule, contracts, fee and schedule forms, etc.  Contact the Accounting Services office, 130 Kimball. Bldg, 356-1050, for additional information.

Payroll Office
All hiring of student employees must be processed through the Student Employment Office located in Suite 244 in Kimball 244.  This process must be completed before any student works for any department.  Pay periods end on the 15th and the last day of each month.  Paydays are on the 8th and 23rd of each month unless it falls on a weekend, and then they will be paid the preceding Friday.  Notifications are usually picked up by the student at the office where they turn in their time cards.  Any questions ask your work supervisor.  No checks are made for students.  They can either go to Cashier’s and pick up cash or sign up for direct deposit.  Direct deposit is encouraged.  It is safe, convenient and we can do a direct deposit to any bank or financial institution in the United States.  Direct any other questions to the Payroll Office located in Kimball 130 or call 356-1063.

Plagiarism
Academic dishonesty is defined by Ricks as an unacceptable act by any student, such as plagiarism or falsifying documents, that would gain that student an advantage in admission, grading, graduating from the college, or qualifying for entrance into any academic program.
Academic dishonesty and plagiarism undermine the foundation of a gospel centered educational experience.  Honesty in test taking and writing assignments should be a fundamental commitment of each student.  Every member of the campus community should be united in their effort as a community to help one another maintain their commitment and maintain their personal integrity without fail.
Plagiarism is defined as the attempt to pass off as ones own the unacknowledged and extensive appropriation of material which is not original to the student.  It can consist of material taken from the text of a book, of an electronic publication, or of a fellow students work.   Refer to the catalog under Academic Honesty for information in regards to possible penalties.

Planetarium
The Planetarium is located in room 107 of the main foyer in the Romney Science Building.  Planetarium programs are shown on Thursday nights at 7 p.m. and are changed monthly.  The cost is $1 for students and $1.25 for the public.  Private shows can be scheduled by calling the Scheduling Office at  356-2248.

Police
Law enforcement and emergency services for the campus are provided by the Ricks College Division of the Rexburg City Police.  Members of the division are duly sworn municipal police officers.  All violations of the law and requests for emergency services occurring on campus should be reported directly to the Campus Police by dialing 356-2448 or 911 for emergencies.  The division office is located in Kimball, Room 150.  Although Campus Division Officers have city wide jurisdiction, their specific area of responsibility is the campus of Ricks College.  Off-campus housing units are not college property; therefore, all requests for police service off campus should be referred to the Rexburg Police Department by calling 359-3000.

Post Office
The U.S. Post Office is located one-half block north of the campus at 140 South Center Street.  A full range of postal services is available.  Some tips to assist you in sending and receiving mail are as follows:
1.  Use correct and proper addressing and return address procedures with the proper two letter state  abbreviation and zip code.
2.  Use proper packaging materials for all parcels.
3.  Provide all persons with whom you will correspond with your current, correct, and complete address.  Check with your apartment manager or head resident for the proper address.  (The City of Rexburg has a single digit street number system.  For example, 1st West cannot be used as 100 West.  This will create mail receipt problems).
4.  Learn and follow your apartment complexes procedure for sending and receiving mail.
5.  Residents of off-campus housing units will need to file a change of address with the local post office at least two or three days before moving.  On-campus residents will need to leave their forwarding address with their head resident, otherwise their mail will be returned to sender.  All students residing in a unit which has a head resident or manager should provide that person with their proper forwarding address.  Stamps are available at the Cashier's Office and Manwaring Center stamp machine.

Postal Services
Ricks College has its own campus mail service for the purpose of delivering CAMPUS MAIL to Ricks College departments.  Postal Services also delivers the U.S. Mail to campus residences, as well as Ricks College departments.

Posters
Prior to being displayed or circulated on campus, posters must be approved at the Manwaring Center Information Desk and stamped with the "Approved For Campus Display" stamp.  They must be no larger than 14" x 22", well done, and be of poster board quality (no flyers) not of a commercial nature, and meet the standards of decency of Ricks College.  They must pertain to the college or relate to a college-sponsored activity or event.  (Go to the Manwaring Center Information Desk to request an exception to the rule).  To find out where authorized areas for hanging posters are, pick up the brochure, Approved Placement For Posters On Campus, at the  Information Desk.

Probation--Academic
You should check with your faculty advisor or the Registrar's Office on how to avoid academic probation or suspension.

Probation--Behavioral
Violations of the Honor Code, dress and grooming standards, housing regulations or criminal law may result in disciplinary action being taken through the Dean of Students Office.  Disciplinary action can range from a warning to dismissal, depending on the nature and seriousness of the violation.  Probation, restrictions, and stipulations are to be used to strengthen a student's commitment during the probationary period.  For example, probation may make you ineligible to hold a campus job or to participate with an athletic team or performing group.  Additionally, academic loads may be reduced, fines may be levied, curfews established, or restrictions placed on visits to other apartments.  Terms of the probation are established between you and the Dean of Students or one of the assistant deans.
See Honor Code in the Standards Section.

Public Expression
Application forms are required for permission to conduct any public expression in the form of any appropriate rallies or gatherings on campus.  The forms are available at the Public Relations Office and must be submitted at least five days in advance of any public expression event.  All applications will be reviewed and applicants informed of approval, approval with conditions, or disapproval within five (5) working days.  (Requests which require approval by the Executive Committee and the Board of Trustees must be submitted at least two months prior to the event.)

Public Relations
The Public Relations Department is responsible for communicating the programs and services of Ricks College to the general public.  It serves as a liaison between the college and civic and service organizations, chambers of commerce, schools, churches, and other public groups who can benefit from college programs, facilities, and resources.  Another important function of this office is to be an integral part of the internal communications program of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Publications
The Scroll comes out each Tuesday and is a tabloid newspaper produced as a laboratory experience for journalism majors.  During the Summer Terms it is published on Thursdays.  In addition to the publications for current students, the Summit magazine is published two times a year and mailed to more than 95,000 Ricks College alumni.  Several departments on campus also have their own periodic newsletters they send to former students.

Publicity (unauthorized)
No flyers, handbills, or commercial advertising may  be posted anywhere on campus, (campus includes all college property and buildings, including men's and women's housing) or placed on any vehicle parked on campus property.  Commercial advertising may be done through the Scroll (college newspaper).  All publicity hung on campus must display the "Approved for Campus Display Date" stamp.  Any publicity hung without this stamp of approval will be removed.  Go to the Manwaring Center Information Desk for approval.

Repeating Classes
You may repeat classes in which you have previously earned a poor grade.  In calculating GPA's, the first grade will not be included.  The first grade is not taken off the transcript, but is marked as "repeated," and is no longer calculated into your GPA.  It is the second grade which counts.  Updating your transcript for repeated courses is automatic; you need not request the change.  Go to the Registrar's Office if you have further questions.

For the prevention of accidents and the protection of students, faculty, staff, community patrons and college property, rollerblading and skateboarding are prohibited on campus sidewalks and parking lots.  Violators are subject to prosecution under Rexburg City Ordinance #759.
