Executive Seminar Writing Guide

After attending the Business Division Executive Seminar you are to write a paper that contains the following elements:

  1. Speaker Name:
  2. Presentation Date:
  3. Introductory Paragraph: Outlines the topic along with a short biography of the speaker.
  4. Content Paragraph: Outlines the content of the presentation, including: major concepts, facts, and themes.
  5. Summary Paragraph: Personal observations about the presentation. Include benefits to you personally and your future professional endeavors.

Mechanical Requirments:

  • Format: Computer generated, single-spaced, one page maximum
  • Grading: Content, grammar, spelling, punctuation

Submission:

The final paper can be submitted via print (handed in to me) or email (attachment).
