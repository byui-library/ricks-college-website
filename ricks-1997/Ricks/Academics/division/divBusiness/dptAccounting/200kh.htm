Prerequisites: None.

Course Description and Objectives: This course is designed to introduce students to both financial and managerial accounting principles with exposure to basic accounting statements, processes, and management applications. It should transfer to most institutions, but may only transfer as elective credit. This course is designed mainly for nonbusiness majors and certain specialized business programs. The main objective of the course is to help students develop the vocabulary, understanding, skills and confidence necessary to apply accounting concepts and techniques in analyzing, planning, and controlling the operations of a business.

Course Routine: Students are expected to study and complete the homework before class. We will spend the class period demonstrating accounting concepts as well as going over the homework. Be careful not to fall behind in your work.

Ricks College Code of Honor: All students must fulfill their commitment to abide by the Code of Honor, including complete honesty in the course and having an appearance which follows the college's standards of dress and grooming. Cheating on an exam or quiz would result in an an "F" as a student's final grade in the course. Sharing information about exams or quizzes after one has taken the quiz or exam is considered cheating. Studying together and getting help on homework (other than straight copying) is fair and encouraged.

Evaluation: There will generally be a quiz or exam at the end of each chapter. A student's final letter grade will be based on quizzes, exams, homework, and attendance.

Attendance - Attendance is required. Each student is allowed one absence and one tardy without any penalty. After that each absence will be penalized 3 points and each tardy 1 point. However, there is no penalty for absences due to sickness or official school business if the teacher has been notified before class starts. One can do this by phoning extension 1485 and leaving a message. Leaving class early is considered the same as being tardy. Absences due to some personal circumstances may also be excused, but one would need to check with the teacher beforehand.

Quizzes - You cannot make up a quiz (even for sickness), however, your lowest quiz score will automatically be dropped. If a quiz is to be missed due to school business, it can be taken early (but never late).

Exams - All exams and quizzes will be given in the testing center. The Testing Center will be open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. with the last test given out at 9 p.m. There are no grade improvement projects available. If you have a bad exam you need to do better on the others to get a good grade. If you score below 65% visit with the teacher immediately in order to have the chance to bring your exam score up to 65%. Any exam missed would need to be made up immediately and could be subject to a point penalty.

Homework - Assignments are due at the beginning of class. Half credit for homework not more than a week late can be received by checking with the instructor to see which problems need to be completed. If homework is late due to illness, full credit can still be earned. To receive credit for late homework (even that due to illness) you must bring it in to the instructor's office and check it yourself against the answer book.

Disability - Students with a disability may be entitled to special consideration. It is the student's responsibility to talk to the teacher about this matter during the first five days of class.
