REVIEW FOR TEST #1 RENAISSANCE

 

Why a renaissance?   institutions of Middle Ages begin to weaken (chivalry, two classes, Church)

            Roman empire dies / no central authority except in Church, but it is corrupt

            changes in lifestyle: feudalism ends / new plow / horse collar / mills / more food

            guild system outdated / rise of middle class / more $$ / plague

Rise of: individualism / secularism / humanism / cities / use of the mind: reason

1453 Turks capture Constantinople / Greeks flee west to Italy / take books with them

Ren. starts in Italy: weak pope (Avignon, France for 70 yrs) / strong trade / love of past / many Greek expatriates

Ficino starts worship of Nature / Beauty / heaven, leads to Platonic Academy (Florence) 1462

            is now OK to study everything  / secular universities / seek perfection in this world, not heaven

            (Dante breaks with church tradition and writes Divine Comedy in vernacular, not Latin)

Petrarch first humanist / crowned “poet of the realm”

Science develops: Ptolemy(geocentric) / Copernicus(heliocentric) / Galileo(gravity-Pisa / planets)

            Descartes (God is a machine) / dualism / measured vs. nonmeasured qualities

            new discoveries: printing 1454 (Gutenberg) / gunpowder (China and Marco Polo)

            new sail shape / new world / new wealth / Columbus 1492 / Line of Demarcation 1494

1517 Reformation:      Luther / 95 thesis on door / half Europe is Protestant in 40 years / indulgences

            Calvin / faith / capitalism / The Elect / Geneva / work ethic / making $$ is OK=a healthy soul

            Henry VIII / splits from Church / Act of Supremacy 1534 / Sir Thomas More won’t take oath

Counter-Reformation: Council of Trent 1545-63 / Inquisition, very harsh (especially in Spain)

Results: strong nationalistic movement

            more education / individuals read Bible / money is power / competition is “normal”

            rising middle class / Man is the measure of all things (Protagorus)

Ren Art: Early 1400 / High 1500-1520 / Mannerism 1520-1600 / Sistine Chapel 1508-12

            major “guide” is “reason” / “virtu” desired

            Donatello’s David is first full nude since Roman days

            David is best symbol of Ren. = youth / vitality / wisdom / action / Florence’s symbol

            discovery of linear perspective / ability to impose reason and order on Nature

            circle & square are perfect shapes

Ren Music: moves from North ($$ from textiles) to South / best composers from Flanders (Holland)

            text, not music, determines whether sacred or secular

            Machaut changes music; uses popular (secular) melodies, not just church melodies

            Josquin is “Prince”  /  creates international style / creates new form called “motet”

            Council of Trent wishes to remove all music from church, but Palestrina saves it

            madrigals - unique to England, fun, secular

Ren Literature: More—Utopia, but at a price / caste system / slaves / mercenaries / almost medieval

            Erasmus—pure thinker / wouldn't take sides / Praise of Folly

            Cervantes—last of the previous age / crusades / Don Quixote / dream-ideal vs. reality.

            Machiavelli—cold realist / sees Man as an animal / is amoral / The Prince 1513

            Shakespeare—his vision evolves / Tempest is his final answer / analyze Man / What IS Man? the best, the worst? Is man good for what he IS? or what he can BECOME?

Conclusions:   role of artist: to interpret the world around us

                        to adjust us to the world by satire, wit or cold observation

                        We are then free to make our own choices, but must live with them.
