Resource Center

Pamphlets, brochures, and other materials are available for married students through the John L. Clarke Resource Center at the Leadership & Service Institute’s Office in Manwaring Center #380. These include free publications on marriage, parenting, and family finances.

The resource area includes thirty publications of the Church, Brigham Young University, and other reputable sources that are free to students. The resource area also houses a collection of Ensign articles on a variety of marital and family issues in addition to a bibliography of books that can be checked out at the Mckay Library.

A pass for free yogurt is given to all married students on their initial visit to encourage students to use these resources.
