Over the past decade, the Rexburg, Idaho Family History Center has been extracting pre-1930 marriage information from Idaho counties. Better than 99% of the entries are included in this file. Also, many pre-1900 marriages records from nearly every county in Arizona and Nevada are here; plus selected counties for California, Eastern Oregon and Utah.  This file contains approximately 150,000 marriage records and may not be comprehensive for the time period and/or locality described.

Each entry may contain the following:

-name of each spouse
-marriage date and place
-county and state in which the marriage was recorded (list of counties and years covered).
-residency of bride and groom
-miscellaneous comments


Students in the Special Collections area of the Ricks College McKay Library in Rexburg, Idaho have typed the resulting manuscript information into an electronic database. This internet file represents a preliminary effort to share the results even though the marriage list is incomplete. As time allows additional marriages will be entered and new editions will be published. Special thanks is made to the administrators and staff of the McKay Library for their assistance and support particularly Bruce Bills, Tom Liau, Gale Reeser and Mat Miles. Also, appreciation is extended to the present and past supervisors the Arthur Porter Special Collections area, namely Barbara Jardine, Ruth Walker Hammar and Verna Hatch.

Blaine R. Bake, editor
