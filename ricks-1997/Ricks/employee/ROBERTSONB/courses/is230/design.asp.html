Design Considerations

The considerations of designing documents (print or electronic) are:

  • What is your message?
  • Who is your audience?
  • How will the document be formatted?

Apply design elements:

  1. Emphasis (what is important?)
  2. Contrast (differentiate between the big ideas and details)
  3. Balance (visually appealing)
  4. Alignment (increasing readability)
  5. Repitition (say it [the important stuff] again, Sam) - my apologies to Humphrey Bogart
  6. Flow (make it easy to follow)
