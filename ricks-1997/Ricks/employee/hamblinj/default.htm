Favorite Recipe: Rhubarb Cheesecake      Yield 12 Servings

Crust: 
1 cup flour
1/4 cup sugar
1/2 cup butter

Rhubarb Layer:
3 cups sliced rhubarb(cut into 1/2 inch pieces)
1/2 cup sugar
1 tablespoon flour

Cream Layer:
12 ounces cream cheese, softened
1/2 cup sugar
2 eggs

Topping:
1 cup sour cream
2 tablespoons sugar
1 teaspoon vanilla extract

1. For crust, mix flour, sugar, and butter then put into a 10-inch spring form pan, quiche dish, or pie plate. Set aside.
2. Combine rhubarb, sugar, and flour; toss lightly and pour into crust. Bake at 375 F for about 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, prepare cream layer by beating together cream cheese and sugar until fluffy. Beat in eggs one at a time, them pour over rhubarb layer.
4. Bake at 350 F for about 30 minutes or until almost set.
5. Combine topping ingredients.
6. Allow the cheesecake to cool slightly, the spread on the topping.
7. Chill
Taken from "A Taste of the Country" cookbook.
