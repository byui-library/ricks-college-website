COURSE SYLLABUS

 Department: Home Economics
 Course#: ID 102
 Title: Color  Credit: 2
 Text: 
 
 Course Objective:

The objective of this class is to prepare the design student to use color intelligently with the other principles and elements of design.  Also, to introduce the design student to the way that color affects the human physiology and psyche.  Color has a gestalt all its own that we react to both positively and negatively.  After this class, one will never look at color the same again.
 
 Description: Topics Covered,

Introduce color theory by using the color wheel to execute color experiments
Introduction of major color theorist, Munsell, Ittens, ect.
Color in relation to the principles and elements design
Psychological and physiological effects of color
Color and the human environment

 Special Requirements:

Create a color design book demonstrating the elements and principles of design and color theory
Experiments
Do a research project on a specific human environment and develop a color scheme based on know properties of color.
