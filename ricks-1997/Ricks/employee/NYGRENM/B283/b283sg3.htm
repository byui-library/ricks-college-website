STUDY GUIDE #3

Entrepreneurship & Small Business Management

Part 3 Exam, Chapter 12-15

TRUE/FALSE

  1. The product line continuum (P.L.C.) is a valuable tool for managing the product mix.

  2. Considering the business analysis stage of the product development process, a product that is completely new to a small firm should not be added to the product mix unless it has at least a family relationship to existing products.

  3. There are six categories of product strategy alternatives for small businesses.

  4. According to the Nutrition Labeling and Education Act of 1990, food products addressed must have a standard nutrition label, listing the amount of calories, fat, salt, and nutrients contained.

  5. Packaging is a significant tool for increasing the value of the total product by creating a distinctive impression.

  6. Markup pricing is a cost-plus method of pricing that arrives at a markup percentage high enough to cover operating expenses, subsequent price reductions, and desired profits levels.

  7. The primary purposes of the Equal Credit Opportunity Act are to inform consumers about terms of a credit agreement and to require creditors to specify how finance charges are computed.

  8. In many lines of business, trade credit terms are so firmly set by tradition that a unique policy is difficult for a small firm to implement.

  9. Bank credit cards are widely accepted by retailers who desire to offer credit but do not have their own credit cards.

  10. An installment account is a typical trade credit agreement.

  11. During sales presentations, a salesperson should never admit product weaknesses to potential customers.

  12. In relationship selling, a salesperson must exhibit professional etiquette in all contacts with customers, since this will determine the success of the sale.

  13. The type of advertising used should be based on the nature of the business, industry practice, available media, and the firm's objectives.

  14. Regular contacts with news media are required if publicity programs are to be effective.

  15. The majority of small business advertising is institutional advertising.
