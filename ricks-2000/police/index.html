Welcome to the Ricks College Division of the Rexburg Police Department


    Law enforcement and emergency services for the Campus are provided by the Ricks College Division of the Rexburg City Police Department. The division is comprised of 10  duly sworn municipal police officers and 5 civilian employees to meet it's law enforcement needs. The department also employs 15 - 20 college students to provide critical services such as Lock up, Student Escorts, Event Security, Parking Enforcement and other necessary services that assists in making the Ricks Campus a safer environment.  All violations of the law and request for emergency services occurring on campus should be reported directly to the Campus Division  by dialing 356-2448 or 911 for emergencies. The division office is located in room 150 of the Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building.
