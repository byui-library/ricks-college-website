READING LAB



The Reading Lab helps students with basic skills as well as with skills to study college textbooks. Each student receives individual diagnosis, advising and specific instruction. Most instruction is one-on-one with a tutor, supplemented by small group meetings and regular class work. Students may choose to register for credit or to work without credit.

Some of the topics for study are phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, organizing ideas, inference, rapid reading, study systems with practice reading in biology, social science, humanities, and scriptures. Reading classes are listed under English in the catalog.

WRITING CENTER

The Writing Center gives students the chance to work with qualified, peer writing assistants to learn to appreciate writing as a central means of discovering what they think and why they think it. Writing assistants analyze student drafts to discern and teach principles of the writing/thinking process. Together, students and assistants explore principles related to such things as: writing more engaging introductions; developing intriguing ideas through detailed, concrete discussion; discovering significant problems through insightful questioning; and representing effective reasoning on the page by drawing upon personal and textual resources. Because the Center teaches writing as "rewriting," students should view the Center as a place where they can get help on a paper from its earliest to its final stages. Grammatical and mechanical errors are dealt with while students learn a process of rewriting focused upon vividly representing the relationship between what they think and the authoritative events and texts that influence their thinking.

STUDY SKILLS LAB

The purpose of the Study Skills program is to provide assistance to students in developing, or improving, more effective study techniques which will lead to a successful academic career.

The program offers three options:

1. Class (P 101) 2 hrs per/wk (block)

2. Lab study for credit

3. Lab study without credit

The Class:

P 101 is offered each block and meets two hours per week for 8 weeks. You will find it in catalog listed as Psychology 101.

The Lab:

Students may enroll in the study skills modules listed below by filling out an add/drop card in the Study Skills Lab. Students are limited to studying one module at a time and two per block. Enrollment for credit must begin before the 12th week of the semester.

TUTORING CENTER

The Tutoring Center offers free individual and group tutoring to all students who need help beyond their regular classroom instruction. Trained student tutors are proficient in a variety of subjects and are prepared to help students strengthen their academic background, understand class material and improve general learning and study skills.

Emphasis is placed on the needs of each individual student and on flexibility in meeting those needs.
