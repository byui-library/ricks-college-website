Checklist for New Hires

1. Go to the Personnel Services office to fill out:

  • I-9 Form
  • W-4 Form
  • Information Sheet
  • Direct Deposit Form (if you want your paycheck electronically deposited to your bank account). Bring in a cancelled check if you are interested in direct deposit.

2. Go to the ID Center in the Manwaring Center to obtain a BYU-Idaho photo ID.

3. Attend the "Orientation Meeting" conducted by Personnel Services to fill out insurance forms, Code of Honor Agreement, Conflict of Interest Statement, etc.

4. Where applicable, go to the Accounting Office to sign up for an American Express card.

5. Where applicable, go to the Accounting Office to obtain a departmental phone card (which will be charged to the department that you will be working in).

6. If interested, go to the Accounting Office to obtain a personal phone card (which will be charged to your personal account at the university).

7. Where applicable, go to the Accounting Office to get clearance to sign Check Requests and other related financial forms.

8. Go to the Campus Police office to pick up a faculty/staff parking sticker.

9. If interested, go the Hart Gym to rent a locker and exercise equipment.
