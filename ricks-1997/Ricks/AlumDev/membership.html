Membership in the Alumni Association includes:

  1. Persons who have attended Ricks College as full time
    students and accumulated a minimum of 16 credits.

  2. Faculty, administrators and staff who did not attend
    Ricks College, but have completed 10 continuous years of service to the school, are eligible to be members of the association.

   Benefits of Membership:

   1.  Semi-annual Summit Magazine
   2.  Assistance with Reunions
   3.  Weekly Inside Ricks sent via E-mail
   4.  Annual Alumni Tours

  If you have any questions concerning the above topics,
  and they are not listed elsewhere in our web page,
  please contact us!  We will be glad to answer your
  questions.
