The Ricks College Viking fight song is played each time the Viking teams enter the field or court or when a boost in spirit or effort is needed.  It is traditional to stand, clap, and sing as the spirit team leads out.  The words are:


Sail for the goal you Vikings
and always fight with the warrior's might.
We will be true to our school and faithful to the teams who keep the blue and white still flying high.

Fight!  Fight!  Fight!

Sail for the goal you Vikings,
Your course will remain forever true,
and your foe, will, bow before your might and give the glory to you!
