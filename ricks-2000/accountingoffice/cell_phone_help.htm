Voice mail | Call Forwarding

 

USING VOICE MAIL

  •   Accessing your voice mail box – You can reach your voice mail box from either your cellular phone or via a conventional land line phone. In either case, dial your own cell phone number to access your mail box.


  •   Reaching the system menu – If you dial from your cell phone you will immediately be connected to the voice mail users menu. If you call via a land line, you will hear the greeting for incoming calls. Press the "*" key to access the menu.


  •   Password – The first time you access your mail box, you will be asked for a password. The default password is "9999". You will be required to change the password during the system set-up.
  •   System set-up – The system will guide you through a process which requires you to do 3 things:
  •  Record your name to identify the mailbox.
  •  Record a greeting for those who reach your voice mail
  •  Create a new password to replace the default.
  •   Programming phone to access voice mail – Your cell phone number can be programmed into the voice mail feature on your cell phone. After you do this you can access voice mail by pressing and holding the "1" key on your phone. See your owner’s manual for instructions on programming the voice mail access option for your phone.

CALL FORWARDING

  •   Air time charges – Remember that you will be charged for air time and any applicable long distance for calls forwarded through the Edge Wireless system.


  •   To activate forwarding – From your cellular phone dial *71. Then dial the area code and phone number you want your calls forwarded to and press the "send" button. You will hear 3 short tones to confirm that you calls have been forwarded.


  •   To deactivate forwarding – From your cellular phone dial *710 and press the "send" button. You will hear 3 short tones to confirm that your call forwarding has been canceled.
