Phase III--Financial Plan

Standards:  Computer generated, 3 pages + a cover page
Grading:  Content, Application to the text, grammar, spelling, and punctuation

Cover page:  Name, Company, B101, Section, Date

Page #1:  Prepare a Summer 2002 projected income statement for your business.  Include all the revenues you would expect to receive matched against all the expenses you would incur.   (Click here for an example of an projected income statement for a business with a good as the primary product.  Click here for an example of an projected income statement for a business with a service primary product.)

Page #2:  Prepare a Summer 2002 projected balance sheet for your business as of the end of August, with the balance sheet accounts (assets, liabilities, and owners equity).  Make sure your balance sheet equation is in balance and the accounts have reasonable balances.  Use at least 10 different accounts, including at least one liability account (refer to page 639 for an example).  

At the bottom of page #2 tell me how much you or other owners are going to put into the company to get it started--either in cash and/or equipment and/or supplies.

Page #3:  Prepare one journal page of five typical entries your business would most likely use to get started in the first month of business.  Use at least five different accounts.  Be sure to use double entry accounting as we did in class. Examples
