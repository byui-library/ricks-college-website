Choose cakes that bring tiers to your eyes

by Elizabeth Hatchett
HAT01004@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Wedding cakes have been a tradition since the Roman era through the centuries to become an expected item at a wedding.

The style of the traditional cake evolved in the Victorian era. The traditional wedding cake includes butter or sponge cake with white frosting.

Today, people want their cakes to reflect their personalities. They want it to be unique and set apart from the traditional wedding cake. The bride and her dress are the focal point of the wedding, but the cake is the centerpiece of the reception.

The design of the cake is often determined by the couple. Layers can be stacked on each other. Columns that look like pillars or champagne glasses can be used to separate each tier. A flat cake in a specific design may also be a beautiful selection.

Chances are the cake will be carefully crafted and designed. The common medium for decorating is sugarpaste frosting. Small roses and petals can be placed strategically around the cake surrounded by vines. There can be sugared fruit, fresh flowers, fondant icing and marzipan.

The cake can be personalized with family heirlooms like a grandmother’s cake topper or a mother’s cake plate. People can also decorate the area around the cake with heirloom items such as tablecloths, linens or pictures.

Cake toppers, which are traditional, are optional and can reflect the personalities of the bride and groom. Look around for toppers that are unusual or eye-catching. Talk to the cake designer to see what type he or she would suggest and for the appropriate size. Some couples have chosen favorite collectibles while others have hired artists to design a topper specifically for the occasion.

The flavor of the wedding cake is another place to add a personal touch. For many, it does not matter if the cake looks good if it does not taste good.

The most popular wedding cake flavors include lemon, vanilla, chocolate, yellow, spice and carrot. The flavor of the wedding cake is limited only by the imagination of the couple.

Filling can also be placed in between each of the layers to fill space. Filling selections include anything from jam to cream, frosting, icing, chocolate mousse, fruit, buttercream, curd or cream cheese. It can be one or a combination of flavors.

The icing is the cakes base color on top of which additional decorations may be placed. The most common types of icing are buttercream, royal and fondant.

Buttercream frosting is creamy and soft. Because of its softness the frosting is not versatile. Some bakers are able to spread the frosting over the cake so that it looks like fondant frosting.

Royal icing has a sleeker look. The frosting dries hard and is crunchy. It is also durable, which makes it perfect for details such as latticework, flowers or writing.

Fondant frosting is rolled into a sheet, wrapped over the cake and then trimmed. It can also be molded into shapes such as flowers or ribbons.

Today, wedding cakes can be anything the couple imagines. One can have a three- tiered white traditional cake or a cake in the shape of a temple, or even a cake in the shape of a present. The options are as vast and unique as each individual couple.
