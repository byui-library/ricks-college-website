Primary Children's Medical Center, located in Salt Lake City, Utah, is the pediatric referral center for the entire Intermountain West equipped to care for children and adolescents with complex illnesses and injuries. Owned by Intermountain Health Care, a non-profit health care system, it is also associated with the University of Utah School of Medicine Department of Pediatrics. Primary Children's provides the finest medical care in an atmosphere of love and concern.

Funds generated from the Guitars Unplugged show and associated sales will be donated to Primary Children's Medical Center Foundation. Primary Children's Medical Center Foundation supports the hospital's charity mission by providing more than $3 million to help pay for hospital care of children with no other means of payment.
