SHE-WARRIORS

The Ricks College Vikings might not like it, but they thrive on the edge. Twice this weekend they held on to one point leads resulting in two big conference wins.

The first win was against Utah Valley 57-56. The game was tied 27-27 at the half and Ricks built up a 10-point lead only to lose it with less than two minutes to go, 56-52. Then the sophomores took over. Post player Mandie Little nailed a bucket creating a chance for the Vikings. Lindsay Johnson, having shot only 33 percent all night redeemed herself, hitting the 3-pointer that gave the Vikings the one point lead.

MEN DROP TWO CONFERENCE ROAD GAMES, FALL TO SIXTH PLACE IN SWAC STANDING

Ricks College men's basketball lives and dies by the 3-point line. This weekend they died.

After a hard-fought game of changing leads, Ricks and Utah Valley were tied 68-68 with six seconds left to go. Each team threw up a three pointer.

UVCS's went in and Ricks' rimmed out at the buzzer to give the Bruins the 71-68 win.

After clawing back from a 36-28 halftime deficit, the Vikings didn't score a single point in the last 2:15 of the game.
