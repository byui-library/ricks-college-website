Adding a Numbered List
  1. Open the page you want to add the numbered list.
  2. Select the Menu "Format:Bullets and Numbered list..."
  3. Select either a bulleted list or numbered list.
  4. Pressing "Enter" will start a new line with the next number.
  5. If you press "Shift-Enter," you will get a new line without a new number.  
    For Example, this line was created with a "Shift-Enter."

Deciding on the Amount of Personal Information

 The amount of personal information to use is a personal question.  In general we would suggest that you do not provide too much information about you or your family that might make you in some way vulnerable or unprofessional.  Don't give out more information than you feel comfortable making public. In general that would probably include:
  • your home phone
  • your home address
  • your children's names
  • your bank accounts
  • personal wills
  • license plate numbers
  • social security numbers
  • pictures (consider what information they convey)
  • or any information that may be considered vital or confidential.

Deciding on an HTML Editor

 At this time, many HTML editors are being used across campus. Many are available on the Internet.  Some are free, others shareware, and others commercial software. You are free to use which ever editor you wish.

On campus at this time, we have decided to support FrontPage, due to its ability to be useful for the novice and expert.

At Distributed Data Services, we may not be able to answer all question about any HTML editor, but we do claim to be able to answer almost any FrontPage question in a timely manner.

Easily Spellchecking Documents

 Press F7 to use the Spell Check
Press Shift-F7 to use the Thesaurus

Many people forget to spell check their work. Make it a habit to press F7 before closing your document.

Making Pages Viewable on the Library's Special Web Browser

 Library Web Browser Restrictions
  • No use of search engines is allowed from the browser.
  • Email other than that provided by Ricks College to students is not allowed from the browser.
  • The browser does not provide a URL/location/address bar, so web address can not be entered. If faculty have off campus sites that they want students to access, they must include links to those sites in their pages, or provide the address to Bruce Bills or Matt Miles for inclusion in the electronic reserve.
