Spain adopted for Lent
Mexico received from 16th century missionaries
Aztecs had similar custom.
Religious symbolism: Satan = pinata; treasures = temptations; blind fold = blind faith; stick = virtue; goodies = rewards. (research December 14, 1998 by a colleague)
