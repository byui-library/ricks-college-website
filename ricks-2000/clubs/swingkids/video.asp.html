Club Scrapbook

This is a bunch of instructional videos that David S. and Caroline S. made some time ago.  Anybody can use them if they promise to follow a couple of rules:

1.  Be Nice!

Don't crash our servers.  The school will pull the plug on this really quick if you're opening multiple download sessions, or doing silly things.  Please help us make these available to everyone.

2.  Don't Steal

You can download these for your use and show them to your friends.  However, please don't put them on your website.  If you'd like, you can provide your visitors with a link to this page.

3.  Don't Whine

These were filmed 8 months ago, and in some cases the way we dance now is very different (thanks Tonya/Joon).  There are lots of styles, and what you think is "wrong" may just be another style.  We made up some of these names, and there will be some errors I'm sure.  Feel free to let us know about those, but don't be critical.  If all else fails, refer back to rule #1.  Thanks again Dave and Caroline for your hard work!
