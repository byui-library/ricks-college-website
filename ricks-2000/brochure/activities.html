Our unprecedented Student Activities Program meets the needs and interests of a broad and diverse student body. When fully operational, the program will have an array of year-round activities in three areas: Service and Social, Visual and Performing Arts, and Physical Activities. Each area is structured to give students numerous opportunities for active involvement at various levels of interest and commitment—including leisure, instructional, recreational, and competitive.

In the expanded Activities Program, students are participants not just spectators. Innovative programs, such as outdoor recreation, help students develop character and enhance leadership skills as they teach, coach, and mentor one another.

Although we will discontinue intercollegiate athletics by the end of the 2001-2002 school year, highly competitive athletics within the institution will involve a higher percentage of students. This new program broadens the access of competitive sports and goes beyond “intramurals.” The competitive athletics program will include structured coaching, regular practices, full uniforms, and equipment—a level formerly characteristic of only varsity athletics.
