BRIGHAM YOUNG UNIVERSITY - IDAHO

 Located in the heart of Idaho, Sun Valley/ Ketchum is a year-round recreational resort. Because of its picturesque setting and mild climate, movie stars and celebrities frequently visit; the late Ernest Hemingway made the area his home. At 5,900 feet, Sun Valley lies 175 miles west of Brigham Young University - Idaho. Lodge provides double occupancy, limited singles, extra charge. Private bath. Classes and meals are at the lodge. No alcohol allowed.

"THE CALL OF THE WILD" :INVESTIGATING THE GRAY WOLF
Starting with only 15 wolves relocated from Canada, the Idaho Recovery Program now has approximately 115 wolves and 12 known wolf packs in Idaho.  Learn about the gray wolf's struggles and success for existence in today's society.

THE MYTH AND MYSTIQUE OF THE OLD WEST
Focus on the development of the Northwest Idaho territory, and Sun Valley area.  Study early explorers, mountain men, native tribes, fur trappers, and the history that surrounds them.  Visit the Sun Valley Lodge and ore wagons.

"GUESS WHO'S COMING TO DINNER?  THE BIRDS!"
August through October is Sun Valley's major migration time for songbirds, loons, and ducks.  Learn to identify the many varieties of birds.  Obtain information on nesting boxes and your local bird's favorite cuisine.
