The Music...

In addition to preparing for concerts and an upcoming tour, Collegiate Singers has been recording many of their pieces for the release of their second CD. Their first CD was This Holy Night, a Christmas album featuring spiritual hymns of praise centered around the birth of Christ. Their second album will include songs from many different styles.
