Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts

One of the most stunning buildings on campus, the Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts is the home of the Music Department. It also houses a 700-seat concert hall, a recital hall, a 500-seat drama theater, practice rooms, classrooms and areas for art exhibits. Its most prized addition is the 4,000-pipe Ruffatti organ, second largest in the LDS Church.
