Ricks College Technology Training


Textbooks, blackboards, computer programs, the Internet, libraries:  these are technologies, or tools, that assist us in providing a quality experience for the students of Ricks College.

"TechSmart" is the name of a new campus program designed to assist Ricks College faculty and staff in using available technology to become more efficient and effective in their work.

One of the services of TechSmart is a full offering of workshops for faculty and staff development.  With offerings from beginning word processing to writing course material for the Internet, TechSmart is designed to assist all Ricks College employees in utilizing available tools and techniques.

All workshops are stand alone; you don't have to attend a series of workshops in order to benefit from them.

Registration is not required, and drop-ins are welcome.   However, to better help us plan for the workshops, and to guarantee yourself a seat, it is preferred that you sign up. In each view - by Topic and by Calendar - click the button to guarantee yourself a seat in a workshop.

Click the button to find out more about a Workshop on a particular day.

The red numbers indicate the number of individuals signed up, and the number of available seats (i.e. 12/20).  Workshops with few sign ups will likely be canceled, and those signed up will be given an option for individual tutoring.
