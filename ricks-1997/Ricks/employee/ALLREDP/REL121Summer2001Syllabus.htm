­Assignments:

Reflection Essay--You will type a one page (single-spaced) essay that must address your impressions of each quote in “The Art of Gospel Scholarship” and the article “Questions, But No Doubts” by Richard Neitzel Holzapfel (click here) or at my home page under “The Art of Gospel Scholarship.”  If you have already taken a class from Br. Allred, there is an alternative assignment in the instructions for this assignment.  This is due on the due date or you will incur a 50% drop in grade for the assignment (5 pts).

Plan of Salvation Overview—You will work through an overview of the plan of salvation (click here) or available on my homepage under "Plan of Salvation Overview & Quiz” (if you have already taken a class from Br. Allred, there is an alternative assignment under this link on the homepage).    Do this together with another student or small group.  Print it out and take the quiz as you go through the overview.  Bring the Overview and your filled out Quiz to class on the due date.  We will correct it in class as part of that day’s discussion.  This is due on the due date or you will incur a 50% drop in grade for this assignment (5 pts).

 

Reading/Journal--You will read each day’s assignment, then you will select a specific verse (or verses, but NOT more than 3-5) that YOU feel is significant and write YOUR feelings, impressions, questions, experiences, testimony, etc. about this verse(es).  Each entry must be at least a half page typed, or a full page hand-written.  This exercise is designed to (1) help you process what you read and (2) help you develop a habit of recording what you learn so as to encourage the Lord to give you more gospel understanding.  Again, this is NOT A SUMMARY, rather it should be A DETAILED PERSONAL STUDY of something that intrigued you from the reading.  You will report this on each day’s roll; if you are absent you will not be able to record this (excepting documented school-excused absences) (50 pts; 1 for reading & 1 for journal).

 

General Conference Talk—You will write a 3-4 page a doctrinal talk (single-spaced).  It must have 5 scripture references from the Book of Mormon covered in this class.  You must also have 5 quotes from General Authorities/Officers from Conference or the Ensign.  You may use www.lds.org to search the Scriptures, Conferences and Ensigns.  You must also have a personal experience (meaning YOUR own) that relates to your subject.  It must be subject (e.g., “The Doctrine of Repentance in the Book of Mormon”).  Your paper must follow the sample talk on my homepage under “Sample Talk Format” or (click here).  This is due on the due date or you will incur a 50% drop in grade for this assignment (20 pts).

 

Final Exam—You will be accountable for the major people, places, events and doctrines in this half of the Book of Mormon.  The test will be available on the Blackboard (link on Allred’s homepage or click here) and will be T/F and Multiple Choice questions.  The test will be closed book with no notes.  Available June 5 (Tues) from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (20 pts).
