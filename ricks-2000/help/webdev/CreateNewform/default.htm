This is the process to create a Html form in FrontPage 2000.

Creating a HTML form
  1. Create a New Page

  2. Open the Insert Menu at the top of the page left click on Form then left click on Form

  3. Now if you press enter a couple of times your Form should look like the picture below

  4. Now you can add a Form Element
    Open the Insert Menu at the top of the page left click on Form then left click on the Form Element you want

  5. And now you have a Text Box
