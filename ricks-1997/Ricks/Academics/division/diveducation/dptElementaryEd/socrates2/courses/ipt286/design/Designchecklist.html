Multimedia Design Guidelines

From Univ. of Michigan

This document is not intended to be a definitive guideline to the multimedia design process. Rather, it is meant as a checklist of design issues to be aware of during your project. Think about each of these items before you start developing and plan accordingly.


1. Think about the use


Who is the user?
Who is the audience?
What is the environment the software will be used in?
What are the human constraints?
What are the technical constraints?


2. Identify the subject of your project


Mission statement (What is your goal(s)?)
What is included?
What is excluded?
Logistical constraints (money, time, personnel )
Technical constraints (disk space, network speed, monitor size)


3. Decide how your information will be structured


What is the most appropriate way to present the subject?
Slide show? continous animation? guided path? reference?
Consider using a presentational metaphor (map, tape player, game, etc. )
Determine software structure (multi-stack vs. single stack )


4. Create a timeline


Elements
Content
References
Media capture/creation
Authoring
Create project calendar
Speed, quality and cost: choose any two
Critical paths
Count on technical problems (estimate x 3)
Schedule resources (people and machines)!
Create milestones
Think about slippage plans
Reduce features first, content second
Allocate tasks and resources
Who will do what ?
