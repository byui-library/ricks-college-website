Unit #6 - IONIC EQUILIBRIA III: THE SOLUBILITY PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Calculate Ksp values given solubilities, and calculate solubilities from Ksp values.
2. Determine whether a precipitate will form in a given mixture.
3. Use the dissociation constant, Kd, of a given complex ion to calculate the concentrations of the ion and its components at equilibrium.
4. Combine the solubility product of a solid with the dissociation constant of a complex ion to determine the solubility of that solid in a solution containing a complexing agent.
