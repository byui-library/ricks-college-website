172nd Annual General Conference

Conference attendees rise to sing “How Firm a Foundation” during the Sunday afternoon session.
James Ricks / Scroll
The hubbub of the Olympics has been gone from Utah for over a month, but Salt Lake City filled with people from all over the world again Saturday morning as the 172nd Annual General Conference of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints commenced.

President Gordon B. Hinckley commented on the success of the Olympics and the community’s volunteer efforts during the Winter Games. During this time, the Church received attention from citizens, media and government leaders from all over the world...[more] April 9

President Packer: Children used as teaching tool

PRESIDENT PACKER
“Children are the past, the present and the future all blended into one,” President Boyd K. Packer said. “Every time a child is born, the world is renewed in innocence.”

“Many years ago in Cuzco, [Peru], I [was in] a sacrament meeting in a long narrow room with a door which opened to the street,” President Packer said. “It was night and it was very cold.”
