Some Support Comes from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

Members of the Church voluntarily help build the Lord’s kingdom on earth  by paying tithes and offerings. This money supports the Church and many of its programs. But these sacred funds do not financially support every aspect of all the programs.

This annual budget does not account for:

  • Contingencies,
  • Additional scholarships or grants, or
  • Unexpected opportunities to advance Ricks College or benefit the Church.

Giving to Ricks College now is more important than ever because of the enormous demands being placed on the Church’s monetary resources. Funding for projects outside the Church’s basic budgeted structure must come from private sources.

As Ricks College’s sponsor, the Church provides an annual budget to the college which:

  • Pays for faculty and staff
  • Pays for the operation of the college's physical facilities,
  • Pays two-thirds of a student’s tuition.

Your gift to Ricks College helps maintain its kingdom building role by:

  • Giving more deserving and needy students an educational opportunity
  • Providing quality teaching that is spiritually strengthening and academically challenging
  • Supporting new building and beautification projects
  • Enriching existing programs
  • Ensuring the College’s preparedness for future opportunities

All of this helps build the character necessary to match the challenges of our time and make better the world around us.

You can help by acting now to keep vital opportunities from being at risk and help assure the success of Ricks College’s significant role well into the next century.
