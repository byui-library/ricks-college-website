Engr 201 Engineering Mechanics -Statics   3
Prerequisite: Concurrent registration in Math 162, Physics 121
Three instruction hours per week; two hours problem-solving laboratory
Equilibrium force systems; particle and rigid body statics; forces and moments; free body
diagrams; centroids; trusses and frames; structures and machines; friction. Vector analysis used.
