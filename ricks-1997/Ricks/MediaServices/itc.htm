What is the ITC?

  The Instructional Technology Center is a new campus facility where faculty may receive production assistance and/or training as they develop technology based curriculum materials.
  At the ITC, one-on-one tutoring and consultation are provided to faculty in the areas of instructional design, electronic presentations - including PowerPoint, digital imaging, web page creation and other areas of multimedia development and authoring.
