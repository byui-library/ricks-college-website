The teachings of the apostles and prophets have been made available in many forms.  I would encourage you to consistently read the Semi-annual conference reports and the writings and teachings of the First Presidency and the Twelve.  If something is mentioned in class you would like to read I will be glad to furnish you a reference.  Below the linked articles, you will find a list of significant doctrinal messages and their references.   Each reference to an "Ensign" may be located on the Church's website, LDS.org. I hope you will find time to read some of them. The following articles are available to assist you in your understanding of the Scriptures and particularly, the importance of reading and understanding the Book of Mormon.  These articles have been selected to offer classroom support and are not intended for further publication.
