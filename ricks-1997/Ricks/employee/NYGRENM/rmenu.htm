Career Advising Center

Are you wondering how you will spend the next 40 years of your life?  If you have made a decision, perhaps it would be good to verify it by a few simple exercises prior to spending a lot of time, energy, and money preparing for it?  If you have not decided--decide no to decide.  Do you have an educational plan for  after you leave Ricks?  Not just a plan to go to "XYZU" but do you know what is required for admittance or specific courses you can get out of the way here at Ricks?  Save time & thousands of dollars by planning now!

Leadership & Service Institute

Unique to Ricks College is the Leadership & Service Institute.  In a mountain lodge with a panoramic view of the Teton mountains (near Victor, Idaho) you go with a small group of other Ricks students for one evening or on an overnight weekend retreat to learn and apply principles of leadership & service that will help you in every area of your life.  Make sure you include the TMLI a part of your Ricks College Education.

Placement Center

Some of you will leave Ricks and go directly into the Job Market.  All of you will want to develop a resume as you seek for employment, to assist you thru college, seek research grants, or apply for internships.  Use the "resource" of the Placement Center (both here and at your transfer school) to help you design and refine your resume.  Learn about and begin NOW to apply the power of 'networking'.  Check internships Possibilities, summer employment, etc.

Associated Student of Ricks College

One of the marked characteristics of successful people is that they are 'Joiners'.  Studies are so strong in this area of "joinerism" that most major employers require their employees to join associations, committees, service organizations, and community related programs.  You will do well to develop a 'Joiner' mentality now.  It will bless you and in turn bless others!  Join and be active in a CLUB or SERVICE GROUP on campus.  There are nearly 100 different clubs on campus.  you may also volunteer to be on an ASRC Committee.
