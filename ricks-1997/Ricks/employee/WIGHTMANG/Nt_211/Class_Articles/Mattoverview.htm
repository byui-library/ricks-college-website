The Three Fold Division of Matthew

I.    Prologue:  The Birth of Christ Narrative  (Matt. 1-2)

II.    The Main Body:  The Ministry of Christ  (Matt. 3-25)
       1.   The New Kingdom introduced
               A.  Narrative:  3:1 - 4:25
                    B.  Discourse:  5:1 - 7:29

           2.    The New Kingdom extended under Jesus' authority
               A.  Narrative:  8:1 - 10:4
                    B.  Discourse:  10:5 - 11:1

           3.    Reaction to the New Kingdom
               A.  Narrative:  11:2 - 12:50
                    B.  Discourse:  13:1-53

           4.    The preeminence of the apostles
               A.  Narrative:  13:54 - 17:27
                    B.  Discourse:  18:1 - 19:2

           5.    The Kingdom then and the last days
                    A.  Narrative:  19:3 - 22:46
                    B.  Discourse:  23:1 - 26:2

III.    The Climax:  The Death and Resurrection of Christ (Matt. 26-28)
