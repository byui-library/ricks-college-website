It's As Simple As 1,2,3

When we think of what's for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, we don't think about good things as far as taking care of our bodies. Very rarely do we try to balance our food choices. With that in mind, here are a few practical tips on how to understand the basic dietary guidelines each of us should follow.

  • Guideline 1:
  • Eat a variety of foods, there is no need to eat the same things every day. Don't forget the legume family such as black-eyed peas, kidney beans, black beans and chick peas.
  • Guideline 2:
  • Balance the foods that you eat with physical activity--maintain or improve your weight. There is no need to feel guilty if you indulge--just remember all foods can fit. Keep your body in motion and it will help burn off those extra calories.
  • Guideline 3:
  • Choose a diet with plenty of grain products, fresh vegetables and fruits. Look at what you are having or preparing with each meal and decide if you have chosen at least one serving of a grain product, one fresh vegetable and one fresh fruit today.
  • Guideline 4:
  • Choose a diet low in fat, saturated fat and cholesterol. Select chicken, turkey, fish or lean red meat whenever possible. Limit added fats such as butter and margarine.
  • Guideline 5:
  • Choose a diet moderate in salt and sodium. Look for alternative spices to flavor your food.
  • Guideline 6:
  • Look for natural alternatives to satisfy your sweet tooth, try berries, cantaloupes, strawberries or a mixed fruit cup.

Remember that you are responsible for your health and your eating habits. After all, you are what you eat. Remember it's as simple as 1, 2, 3.
