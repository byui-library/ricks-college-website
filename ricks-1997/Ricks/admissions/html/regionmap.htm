REGIONAL MAP AND DIRECTIONS


AIR TRAVEL

If you are flying, the closest commercial airport is in Idaho Falls, 25 miles south of Rexburg. SkyWest, a Delta connection, and Horizon Air, are the major providers. The college offers year-round shuttle service from the Idaho Falls airport. The cost is  $25.  Special discount rates are offered at the beginning and end of each semester.

If you are flying to Salt Lake City, Ricks provides a shuttle service at the beginning and end of the fall and winter semesters, the Thanksgiving holiday, and for the October and April LDS general conferences. The costs is $40 one way at the beginning of the fall semester and the end of the winter semester. The cost for all other times is $30 one way and $50 round trip.


SHUTTLE SERVICE

Please call the Ricks College Public Relations Office at (208) 356-1150 for information on shuttle service from Idaho Falls and Salt Lake City.  More information regarding student travel and transportation can be found at the Public Relations Web Site.  

SLASH (Salt Lake Airport Shuttle Hop) also provides commercial service from Salt Lake City to Rexburg. They can be reached at (208) 359-3174 or on the web at   http://www.srv.net/~slash/Index.htm

Trailways Salt Lake Express provides daily express transportation between Ricks College and the Salt Lake Airport or downtown Salt Lake City. They offer 3 convenient round trips a day with departures from Ricks College at 5:00 a.m., 8:10 a.m. and 10:40 a.m. Shuttles arrive back at Ricks College at 4:00 p.m., 7:00 p.m. and 10:00 p.m.  Frequent specials offered, call or visit the web site for pricing information. Call (208) 656-8824 or (800) 356-9796 or visit our web site at http://www.gotrailways.com
