PREREQUISITE: Completion of Chem 105

 

TEXT: Biology, Campbell and Reece, 6th edition

COURSE OBJECTIVES: Through this class, the students will gain a knowledge of the basic concepts of biochemistry, cell biology, genetics, and evolution and an appreciation for the nature and complexity of life.

COURSE REQUIREMENTS:

1. Honor Code: All students attending Ricks College have agreed in writing to adhere to the Honor Code. The students' dress, grooming, and conduct will show integrity in honoring their pledge.

2. Attendance: Roll will not be taken in class. This class covers a large amount of material, and absences will definitely affect your performance on the unit exams.

3. Exams: Six unit exams worth 100 points each will be given during this course. Each exam will cover the information in that unit. A comprehensive final exam worth 200 points will be given during finals week.

GRADING

Grading: Your grade will be based on performance of lecture exams and your lab score. The units exams and the final will make up 75% of your grade. The remaining 25% will come from your lab score.

NO PROJECTS OR PAPERS ARE ACCEPTED FOR EXTRA CREDIT!!!
