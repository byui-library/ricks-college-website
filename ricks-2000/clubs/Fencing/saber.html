Saber Fencing
Target: The saber target is similar to the foil target because it is restricted to the upper body. It does however include the head and arms down to the wrist.
Scoring:A point can be scored when the tip lands on the target area just like with the other two weapons. You can also score a point when the blade lands on the target area or also the your opponent is slashed with the blade or tip.
Types of touches:On target and off target apply in saber fencing like they do in foil fencing. Unlike foil fencing though an off target touch does not stop action. The two fencers continue fencing until a point is scored when they return to the middle of the fencing strip.
Defense and offense:Offensive actions include lunges, jumping lunges, thrust, and disengage. The thrust is the simplest of the attacks and can be very fast. The lunge and the jumping lunge when combined with either the disengage or simple thrust can be a fast attack that covers a large amount of distance. With saber however you are not allowed to cross your legs when moving forward.

The main defense is the parry. There are two main parries, the simple parry and circular parries. The simple parries can be used against simple attacks to avoid being touched as well gain the opportunity to get a point. The circular parries move in a circular pattern to protect your target area.
