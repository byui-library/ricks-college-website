 BYU-Idaho Business Management  Our Mission
  • The Business Management Department’s mission is to create a learning environment for students to acquire the knowledge, skills, and experiences necessary to make an immediate contribution in the workplace and to rapidly grow into leadership positions in their organizations.

BYU-Idaho Business Management Our Strategy
  • We will do this by partnering with industry and non-profit organizations to provide.
    •  mentoring.
    •  seminars.
    • consulting  and service learning projects and.
    • internships.
  •  to allow the student to apply what they have learned via case study and classroom discussion.

BYU-Idaho Business Management Our Vision
  • Graduating students will emerge with an innovative and entrepreneurial spirit, technological and global literacy and key teamwork, communication and problem solving skills.
  • Our students will bring honor to the school through their integrity and their success.

New Venture
Integrated Business Core
  • We view an entrepreneurial spirit as one of the keys to success in today’s business world - whether you work for a large corporation or start your own business.
    • All businesses need innovators who can help them expand by identifying and pursuing growth opportunities - in marketing, finance or any field.
    • A student who has experienced first-hand what it means to launch a new venture will be a valuable addition to any organization.
35
New Venture IBC
  • Students in the New Venture IBC will form companies, write a business plan, and actually launch the business, all in one semester.
  • The students will have the same classmates for four different courses.
36
Integrated Business Core               Team Learning Concept
  • In addition to the great experience of starting a business in the New Venture Creation course, the Marketing, Finance and Organizational Behavior courses will utilize the Team Learning concept benefits:
    • Classroom time for the three subjects will be primarily filled with small group learning activities and exercises designed to help everyone in the class grasp key concepts covered in the textbook.  Lectures will be minimized.

Integrated Business Core:
Flexible Scheduling
  • Instead of having each class meet 50 minutes every MWF, on a given day you may spend up to three hours on exercises relating to one aspect of marketing, finance or organizational behavior - learned just in time to apply it to your business plan or execution.

B499 – Senior Capstone Experience
  • All seniors must complete a three – credit capstone experience. Several options will be available:
    • B499A:  Senior Consulting Projects: be a part of a team coached by a faculty advisor that fulfills a needed project for a local, regional or national business client.  Projects can me finance or marketing specific, or more general in nature.
    • B499B:  Business Strategy Class:  ties it all together with heavy emphasis on cases that integrate finance, marketing and other business disciplines. Includes a major team project
    • B499C: E-Business Leadership: serve in one of the top management positions for one of our E-Businesses (pre-requisite: B450 and instructor approval.

Areas of Emphasis

    • Finance
    • Marketing

46
 Finance Emphasis
  • The Finance emphasis.
    •  familiarizes students with the elements of financial management.
    • provides entry-level skills for careers in a wide variety of financial areas such as banking, corporate finance, insurance, real estate, investments or financial planning.
47
Finance Emphasis
  • Emphasis Requirements:
      • Complete all pre-business and business core classes.
      • Complete the following required emphasis courses:
        • Advanced Corporate Finance.
        • Investments.
        • Money and Banking.
      • Choose two additional emphasis electives from the following:
        • Managerial Economics               - Financial Planning.
        • Advanced Investments               -  Taxation of Business Entities. -   Real Estate.
        • Individual taxation.
        • Insurance and Risk Management.
      • Complete two enrichment electives from the list.
48
Marketing Emphasis
  • The Marketing emphasis.
    •  prepares students for entry-level marketing management responsibilities,
    •  particularly in business-to-business sales and marketing, e-commerce, marketing research, retailing and marketing communications.
    • Stresses both domestic and international marketing in an entrepreneurial or corporate setting.
