Chapter 21

 BAROQUE ART

 

“barocco” is a Spanish shell—ugly and irregular. Term of derision; was used to describe Caravaggio's work.

Rome is the center for this movement.

          Is supported by the Catholic church to show wealth of church.

Art reflects the times (kings) and BIGGER = BETTER.

Since water is favorite subject, movement is in everything.

Aware of the many sides of Man—that not all men see things or believe the same way.

 

CARAVAGGIO  1571 - 1610

forerunner of Rembrandt, Rubens, and Velasquez

lived a wild life—killed a man in a brawl, wounded too

killed a man in a tennis match

knighted to the Order of Malta

jailed over a dispute, flees to Sicily

another knife fight/face slashed beyond recognition

receives a Papal pardon

returns to Rome but is mistakenly thrown in jail, his belongings stay on ship, which leaves.

          He chases ship along coast, catches a fever, and dies.

STYLE:

use of common man/dirty feet/ugly people/ragged clothes

dark shadows and high key lighting

will influence Rembrandt’s chiaroscuro

 

RUBENS  1577 - 1640

diplomat, rich, famous, intellectual, known in courts all over Europe

painter for the Church and rich patrons

Council of Trent (remember Palestrina) declares art is to used to promote the faith

                   Church pours in money

                   Church dictates the subject matter

                   asked to stress the sensual (glory/spendor) to

                             answer doubts of the faithful (both Heaven

                             and Earth are beautiful)

STYLE:

the best colorist of his day

perfect nudes with perfect skin

          no one has any imperfections (nor does Church)

          fat equated with health and wealth

production line technique, many helpers

sold paintings by the amount he painted, by size, by weight, by total square feet.

 

BERNINI  1592 - 1680

the Rubens of sculpture

worked and lived in Rome

many religious and secular works

          St. Peter's canopy, sunburst, colonnade

          David

          bust of Louis XIV

          Rape of Persephone

          Apollo and Daphne

          St. Theresa - shows God's love piercing her heart

 

REMBRANDT  1606 - 69

lived in Holland where the rich are not just princes

          men of great wealth from shipping

          Dutch “fleet” ship changes the world

          group portraits of rich men are very popular

represents the most liberal views of the Protestants yet done at a time when Holland was not sponsoring biblical subjects (Dutch Reformed Church)

over one third of his works come from bible (over 2000)

very religious mother/ schooling with bible emphasis

is a great psychologist/painted you are you really are

painted questions, not answers (as did Rubens)

was not always popular with peers or officials, but didn't pander to public opinion

marries Saskia in 1634, have 4 children but only 1 lives(Titus) and Saskia dies in childbirth (1642)

 

Night Watch  in 1642

original is 13 x 16’

done for 18 patrons; the other 16 are just added for effect

whole career goes down from there, dies a pauper but story is apocryphal

painting tastes move to bright colors (Van Dyke)
