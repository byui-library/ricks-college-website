Welcome to the Ricks College Student-Life Pathway.  Pathway is your entry to the many programs and services offered by the college, as well as a means of personal preparation for a Ricks College learning experience.

With Pathway you can get acquainted with Ricks College by learning how to access resources, get involved, plan your academic course, and get answers to your questions.  In short, Pathway provides you with the tools necessary to access the information you need to succeed here at Ricks.
