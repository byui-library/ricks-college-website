Professional Writing and Editing
PROF WRTG/EDIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 452 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Builds expertise for developing, designing and editing professional documents. Provides students both advanced instruction and opportunities for "real-world" applications. (Intended as the culminating class experience for English/Professional Writing majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Builds expertise for developing, designing and editing professional documents. Provides students both advanced instruction and opportunities for "real-world" applications. Intended as the culminating class experience for English/Professional Writing majors.
TOPICS: Advanced editing; document design; current usage; style.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Learning to edit and prepare documents for publication. (2) Understanding contemporary usage. (3) Improving style. (4) Practicing skills needed for professional editors and writers.
REQUIREMENTS: Complete readings, exercises, assignments, presentations, various in-class and out-of-class projects. Pass an in-class editing test.
PREREQUISITES: Either English 312 or 316.
