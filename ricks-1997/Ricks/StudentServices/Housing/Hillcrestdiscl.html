The Ricks College Home Page contains hyperlinks to home pages of some College-approved housing units. Being "approved" merely means that the unit meets several basic minimum standards with respect to physical facilities, and on-site management. They also observe and enforce the Ricks College Code of Honor. These units are privately owned and operated.

Enjoying approved status does not mean that Ricks College endorses or approves the proposed contractual or financial arrangements you make with an off-campus unit. Housing contracts are between you and the housing unit. Ricks College urges you to make a personal on-site visit to the unit to determine if it meets your particular needs and preferences. Remember, price may or may not be reflective of quality.
