Course Description:

This course provides students with opportunities to critically analyze leadership and management in nursing. Nursing delivery systems, the role and challenges of nurse management, and provider relationships and interactions are explored in depth. Students become familiar with strategies to facilitate professional practice and effectively deal with human resource functions in health care organizations.

Pre-requisite: Nursing 316 and 327
