Social Dance 180
Teach correct principles and they govern themselves!
The main overall goal of the class is to teach a student the skills of participating at a Church sponsored dance, and understanding their responsibility in bringing the spirit of the Lord to the dance. D & C 88: 36-39.

Objectives
At the end of the class the student should be able to do the following;

1. Dance and verbally explain the following basics;
o 6 Directions, forward, backward, left turn, right turn, sideways left, sideways right.
o 8 Positions, closed, semi-open (promenade), left-side, right-side, open(RL & LR), full open, two hand, and sweetheart.
o 8 Components that can be done in any Rhythm; walk, rock, arch (loop), wheel, pivot, hesitate, chassé, and grapevine (cross).
o Proper Closed Position
o Rules of Walking.
o How the upper body moves in relation to the feet.
The following Square Dance terms; Circle R, L, Do Sa Do, Swing, Promenade, Allemande L, Star, R and L Grand, Pass Thru, Courtesy Turn, Ladies Chain, R & L Thru.

2. Dance these couple dances, Walk, Cha Cha, Waltz, Swing (single rhythm), Foxtrot, Country two-step, and Polka. Other couple dances are; Teton Mt. Stomp, Bingo Waltz, Jump Polka, Broken Ankle, Salty Dog Rag.

3. Dance 3 line dances and non-partner folk dances; MaNavu, Little Bird, Hashual, etc.

4. Recognize and explain the criteria of choosing music in the following areas;
o Danceable Rhythms-Why is a Cha Cha a Cha Cha, etc. how to hear the rhythm.
o Moral Judgment of Music-What's in the music that leads us away from the spirit of the Lord?

5. Recognize and explain the proper etiquette and responsibilities of an uplifting dance, such as; Asking to dance, escorting on and off floor, no hats, floor is for dancing sides are for standing, proper lighting, what's the proper behavior.
