General Secondary Methods
GEN SEC MTHDS
CLASS CODE: SECED 405 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: SECONDARY EDUATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Prepares prospective Secondary Educators in general instructional strategies appropriate for grades 6-12. Also assists in developing skills in the use of assessment, the construction of assessment devices, the statistical analysis of test results, and the appraisal of educational research.

TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: This course will only be offered during secondary education major's initial four weeks of the student teaching semester. The majority of instructional, planning and assessment skills taught in the class will directly be tied to student's upcoming student teaching assignments.
REQUIREMENTS:
PREREQUISITES: Restricted to Secondary Education majors during their student teaching year. Exceptions will be handled on a case by case basis.
