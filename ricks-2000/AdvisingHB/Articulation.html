ARTICULATION AGREEMENT
BYU/RICKS COLLEGE
June 21, 1996

The 1996-97 articulation agreement is designed to be used by students who transfer to BYU without receiving an Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree from Ricks College. The agreement lists those courses taught at Ricks College that fill general education (GE) requirements at BYU. The required number of courses in each category is shown in parentheses. Courses with a grade of "D+" or below will not fill GE requirements. Courses will be evaluated on a course-by-course basis according to the list below. The official BYU general education program for students who transfer from Ricks College is the GE program in place at BYU at the time of their initial enrollment at Ricks College, as long as that initial enrollment is within five years of their BYU enrollment.

Ricks students should be aware that they can fill their BYU General Education (GE) requirements, with the exception of Advanced Writing, Advanced Math or Foreign Language, and Religion, by completing the Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree at Ricks College.

RELIGION:
1. All students must complete the entire Book of Mormon sequence. In addition, students transferring with less than 96.0 semester hours of credit must complete one course in the Doctrine and Covenants. Transfers with less than 64.0 semester hours of college credit must complete one course in the New Testament as well as the course in the Doctrine and Covenants. These courses can be completed at an LDS Institute of Religion, or one of the other Church Educational System schools, or at BYU.

ADMISSIONS PROCEDURES AND TRANSFER INFORMATION
Students applying to transfer to BYU should have completed a minimum of 32.0 semester hours of credit. We must receive an official transcript from each institution attended. Students requesting to transfer before completing 32.0 semester hours of college credit must also submit an official high school transcript, and the American College Test (ACT) results. To be competitive, a student should have both a cumulative grade point average and a general education grade point average above the 3.5 (B) level. At least 50 percent of the hours completed should be in the general education subjects (i.e., English, mathematics, history, science, foreign language). Students who transfer to BYU must be able to graduate in the normal time allotted to BYU students who are in the same major and the same class standing (i.e., sophomore, junior, etc.).

ADVANCED PLACEMENT, CLEP, AND MILITARY CREDIT AT BYU

Students with a score of 3, 4, or 5 on an advanced placement exam may receive credit at BYU for each examination. BYU will award 3.0 semester hours of credit through the CLEP examination in each of the five general areas (English, natural sciences, mathematics, humanities, social sciences) in which a student scores 610 or higher in each exam.

Students who have been on active duty in the military service for more than one year may receive 2.0 semester hours of credit in P.E. and 4.0 semester hours of Military Science credit. Credit may be given for completion of special military schools. A student desiring such credit should contact the Transfer Evaluation Office (B-238 ASB, 801-378-3641) at BYU Provo Campus.
