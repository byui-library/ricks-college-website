WORKSHOPS

FOR OYA

 

 

Horseback Riding

The basic techniques will be covered. This will include safety, grooming, bridling, saddling, 

mounting, and riding. After the group is ready, they will go on a horse ride around the 

BYU-Idaho property.

 

Canoeing

Under the direction of the staff, the participants will learn how to get in and out of a canoe, the 

various strokes, parts of the canoe, safety, water rescue, etc. Toward the end of the 

workshop, there may be some free time to paddle or be involved in a competition course.

 

Ropes Course - A/ Inclined/Horizontal Log

This is a high element ropes activity. The participants have the opportunity of walking up an 

inclined log. Upon reaching the top, they step onto the horizontal log and walk across. The 

participants are then lowered to the ground using a belay system. Helmet, harness, and 

climbing ropes will be used.

 

Ropes Course - B/ Climbing Tower

This high element ropes activity consists of climbing the face of a 35-foot high wall. The 

participant will learn climbing techniques to aid in their ascent. Helmet, harness, and climbing 

ropes will be used.

 

Ropes Course - C/ Lobster Course 

This is a high element ropes activity consisting of a number of challenging bridges and

traverses. The course is located 30 ft. above ground and can be physically exerting. Helmet, 

harness, and climbing ropes will be used.

 


Ropes Course - D/ Power Pole

This is a high ropes activity which involve a great deal of commitment. Participants will have 

the challenge of climbing a vertical pole and standing on top, afterwards jumping out to touch 

a hanging tennis ball.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be used.

 

Outdoor Survival

Those engaging in this workshop will learn skills and gain a better understanding of how to 

make their outings safer and to prepare them for emergencies. Topics could include rules for 

preparedness, keys to survival, inventory, making fires, shelters, obtaining water, signals, food, 

essentials to take, etc.

 

Initiative Course #1

This course has approximately ten events which will challenge the thinking of those involved. 

There are various obstacles, tasks, and problems to solve, sometimes using equipment, while 

other times not being able to speak to one another. This is an excellent workshop for building 

teamwork, unity, trust, self esteem, communication, etc.

 

Initiative Course #2

This is similar to Course #1 except there are different situations to solve, obstacles, problems, 

and equipment.  

 

Map and Compass

The basic fundamentals of map and compass reading will be given. Participants will learn the 

parts of a compass and how to take a bearing. After that, they will find their way around an 

orienteering course.

 

Black Powder Rifles

Starting from the time the lead is melted, the participants will learn the various steps from 

making the lead bullets to actually firing them. Safety is stressed in the process and nobody is 

allowed to fire at the targets until a staff member has given the firing cap to the individual. 

 

Leatherwork

Participants will become acquainted with the various leatherwork tools, design a pattern of 

their choice, and make a project to take home.

 

Candle Making

Wax is melted and color is added to give variety. After that, individuals can dip leaves or 

twigs, make candles, design wax sculptures, or other things that come to mind.
