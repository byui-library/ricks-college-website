Technical Reference Sources

This page was last updated on 06/15/01

[Home]

Semiconductor Manufacturers    These are links to the home pages of many semiconductor manufacturers.  They offer product information, software, etc.   Much of it is free.

Online Data Books     These links skip the usual corporate home pages and take you directly to the data sheets of their products.  There is a lot of information here.

Philips Data Sheets    One stop shopping for most of the digital logic data sheets you'll need!

Jameco    This is a fast, inexpensive source for parts to use in your projects.  I usually check this catalog first.

Digi-Key    This parts catalog is more complete than Jameco, but also a little more expensive.  I  use this source a lot, too.

JDR    Here is another catalog.   This one has more computer products than the other two.

Research a Topic    Here is a good starting point for your term paper.  Use this link if the college computer you are on has a browser that does not allow you to type in a URL.
