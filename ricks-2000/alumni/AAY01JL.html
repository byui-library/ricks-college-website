2001 Alumni Athlete of the Year
Jared Lee '99 
FOOTBALL
Jared was one of only 16 players nationwide honored last season as a National College Scholar-Athlete at BYU. A starter on the Cougar football team since the fifth game of his junior year, Jared maintained a 3.96 grade-point average as a history major. As a junior he led BYU with 96 tackles, including 67 unassisted tackles, as a safety. As a senior he ranked second on the team with 71 tackles, including 41 unassisted and 30 assisted takedowns. Former BYU head coach LaVell Edwards called Jared, "the epitme of a student-athlete." Jared has triumphed despite personal problems that include a severe leg injury as a young boy of 12, and a paralysis that nearly cost him his life. Jared played football at Ricks in 1995, served a Church mission to Italy, and returned in 1998 and All-American honors.
