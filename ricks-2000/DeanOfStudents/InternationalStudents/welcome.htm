Welcome!!                    

Brigham Young University-Idaho serves as home to almost 300 international students representing about 40 different countries. Students are attracted to Brigham Young University-Idaho for a variety of reasons. Strong and accredited academic programs and excellent campus facilities makes BYU-I an excellent choice for getting a high quality education. BYU-I also offers a unique spiritual environment. BYU-I is affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  As a result, an unusual blend of academic excellence and increased spiritual awareness exists among the students and faculty.  For students who have a desire for a quality education in an atmosphere consistent with their moral and religious values, BYU-I is a perfect match.

The International Student Office is located in the Dean of Students Office, in Room 230 of the Kimball Administration Building.  All international students are required to process into the school through the International Student Office, where the international student advisor is available to assist international students regarding student visas, campus employment, travel, personal, cultural, and other related issues and problems. The International Student Office is a great resource center with ability to answer most any question related to international studies.  You can also get information about the International Student Association and its activities from the International Student Advisor.
