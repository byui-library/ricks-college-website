Off-Campus Housing Requirements

APARTMENT FACILITY REQUIREMENTS

Minimum Specifications Off-Campus Housing 	

The landlord agrees to maintain both the interior and exterior of the 
property and any furnishings or appliances provided in a safe and operable
condition and in compliance with all minimum specifications established by
Ricks College.  Where necessary a written notification of needed repairs 
or maintenance should be given to the landlord.  Serious violations of 
minimum specifications should be brought to the attention of the Housing 
Office.

1. Space and room arrangements:
(a) Every dwelling unit occupied by students shall contain adequate study 
    facilities.  (It is recommended that this include one study desk for each 
    two students, or six square feet of desk or table area per student).
(b) Every room occupied for sleeping purposes shall contain at least 50 
    square feet of floor space per student, not including closet space.
(c) Not more than four students shall sleep in a single bedroom.

2.  Apartment furnishings include the following:
(a) There shall be one straight back study chair for each student.
(b) There shall be one reasonably sized dresser or chest of drawers space 
    per student.
(c) There shall be adequate book shelves.
(d) There shall be adequate mirrors in the apartment.

3.  Beds and bedding:
(a) A separate bed shall be provided for each student.
(b) Beds shall be clean, sanitary, in a good state of repair, and equipped
    with mattress cover.
(c) Sleeping facilities for single students are not allowed to be set up 
    in the living room areas.

4.  Light and ventilation:
(a) Every room shall contain at least two separate electrical outlets.
(b) Every room shall have adequate light and ventilation.
(c) Each window should have screens, curtains, or drapes as well as an 
    inside lock.

5.  Bathroom Facilities
(a) Every bathroom shall have an outside window or exhaust fan .
(b) There shall be at least one toilet, wash basin, and bathtub or shower 
    in good working condition for each six students.
(c) All bathroom facilities shall be so located within the dwelling as to 
    be reasonably accessible to all persons sharing such facilities.
(d) As a safety precaution, there shall be no portable electric heating 
    devices.

6.  Heat and Water:
(a) Every dwelling shall have heating facilities and equipment that is 
    capable of maintaining a room temperature of 70oF at a point 3 feet 
    above the floor in all habitable rooms.
(b) Comfortable temperatures shall be maintained in all rooms occupied by 
    students.
(c) Hot water shall be available at all times.

7.  Closet and storage space:
(a) There shall be closet space at least 22 inches deep and three feet in 
    width, or the equivalent, provided for each student occupying the bedroom.
(b) There shall be storage space for bags and trunks belonging to the 
    students.

8.  Cooking and eating apartment requirements:
(a) The apartment shall contain a kitchen sink that is connected to hot 
    water and cold water, and is also properly connected to the sewer 
    drain.
(b) The apartment shall have an electric or gas stove with an oven in good
    operating condition.
(c) The apartment shall have a refrigerator in good operating condition 
    for each 6 students.	
(d) Floors and walls shall be in a good state of repair and shall be 
    finished so that they are sanitary and can be easily kept clean.
(e) There shall be adequate garbage storage and disposal facilities.

9.  Basement apartment and sleeping rooms:
(a) Floors and walls shall be adequately sealed against dampness.	
(b) The living quarters must be separated by a wall from the furnace room,
    coal bin, and laundry room.

10.  Safety:
(a) Adequate exit facilities shall be provided (at all times).
(b) Doorways and aisles shall be at least 28 inches wide, and shall be 
    kept unobstructed and well lighted at all times.
(c) Each dwelling unit housing more than ten students shall have two 
    separate exits.
(d) No accumulation of rubbish or combustibles shall be permitted in the 
    building, and no combustibles shall be stored within 12 inches of the 
    furnace.
(e) Electrical wiring shall not be hung on nails or run under rugs and 
    carpets.  There shall not be more than two appliances, etc., plugged 
    into each outlet.
(f) All apartments shall have outside doors with a substantial lock, and 
    a key shall be issued to each student occupying the apartment.
(g) Each apartment must have fire extinguishers that are functional.  
    Fire extinguishers will be located in the kitchen area.  Smoke alarms 
    are also required in each apartment located in the hallway by the 
    bedrooms.
(h) A written certificate of occupancy and business license must be 
    obtained from the City of Rexburg.
(i) Electrical outlets in the bathrooms must be GFI protected as required 
    by the Uniform Building Code.
(j) Landlords shall make arrangements to reasonably remove snow and ice 
    from stairways, walkways and parking lots.

11.  Appearance of apartment:
(a) Rooms occupied by students shall be in a good state of maintenance 
    with walls and ceilings preferably light in color, especially basement
    rooms and study rooms.
(b) An approved student apartment shall present a neat, attractive 
    appearance.
(c) The grounds shall be reasonably maintained and free from offensive and
    unsightly debris.
(d) The outside of the unit shall be in good repair.

12.  Parking:
(a) Parking shall comply with city ordinances.  Rexburg City ordinances 
    require the landlord to furnish sufficient parking for automobiles 
    owned by tenants.

13.  Inspection:
(a) Each apartment unit will be inspected by Ricks College before initial 
    approval and periodically to assure compliance with all approval 
    guidelines.

14.  Hotels, Motels, and Studio Apartments
(a) Hotels, motels, and studio apartments are not approved housing for 
    single students.  
    (See Section 3c above).
