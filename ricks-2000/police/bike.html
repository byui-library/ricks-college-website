Police Bike Patrol

Police bike patrol is a successful way to bring Police Officers closer to their community. On Rick's campus, it is also becoming the best way for us to repond to problems.

Other advantages of the bike patrol:

* Saves literally thousands of dollars that would be spent on vehicle maintenance and gas.
* Brings Officers closer to the community. Feel free to stop one and ask for directions or any other questions. Sorry, we don't give rides!!
* Bike patrol helps us to keep a safe environment on inner Campus.
* Finally, it keeps your Police Officers in shape, so they are better able to serve you in time of need.
