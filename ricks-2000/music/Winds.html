Winds


At Brigham Young University-Idaho you will have the opportunity to participate in some of the finest ensembles in the intermountain West.  These include:

Symphonic Band    Under the Direction of Kendell Nielsen, the Symphonic Band is an audition band welcoming interested instrumentalists.  The band performs a Fall Concert and a Spring Gala Concert for the University and the community.  The Symphonic Band specializes in the basics of musical expression using a wide range of great band literature.  Its emphasis is performance.  The Symphonic Band is renowned throughout the intermountain West and is the foundation of an exceptional Wind Program at Brigham Young University-Idaho.

Concert Band    The Concert Band is designed to provide a challenging experience for band students who possess basic technique while focusing on the improvement of ensemble playing skills.

Chamber Winds/Brass    Conducted by Mark Watkins and Kendell Nielsen, the Chamber Winds consists of 14 - 18 select woodwind and brass players.  Under near professional conditions, this distinctive group of instrumentalists is challenged with the finest music of the chamber wind repertoire.  Members of the Chamber Winds are also given performance opportunities in diverse settings, from the opera/musical orchestra to smaller chamber groups.  Additionally, the Chamber Winds functions as a touring group in combination with the Chamber Orchestra.  Performing under such distinct circumstances provides students a most rich and rewarding musical experience.

Viking Pep Band    Under the leadership of Jim Brague, the Pep Band uses 70 musicians for football season and 20 musicians for basketball season to bring popular music to BYU-Idaho athletic events.  The football band plays for all home games and attends any invitational bowl games.  The basketball band members are selected from the football band and play for all home basketball games and the district tournament when either men's or women's teams are participating.  These are auditioned groups, and scholarships are available for each sport.

Sound Alliance Jazz Ensemble    Directed by Wilson Brown, the Sound Alliance is a jazz ensemble of 20 musicians who study and perform different jazz styles of today--traditional and straight-ahead jazz, bebop, rock, Latin, etc. -- with emphasis on both group precision and improvisation.  Concerts, jazz festivals,  and tours are part of its curriculum, and it serves as the feature band for periodic large-scale stage shows.  Membership is by audition.

Jazz Lab Ensemble    Directed by Wilson Brown, this group is a preparatory jazz ensemble of 20 musicians who focus on developing basic styles and techniques of jazz performance.  They perform fall and winter semesters on campus.  Membership is by audition. 

Jazz Combos    Under the direction of Wilson Brown these small groups of instrumentalists specialize in improvising in various jazz styles.  They perform fall and winter semesters.  The top group(s) may compete in jazz festivals.  Membership is by audition.

ORCHESTRA


Symphony Orchestra    Under the baton of Ted Ashton, regular performances of the Symphony Orchestra feature major works from the 19th and 20th century standard repertoire as well as biennial premieres of newly commissioned sacred LDS oratorios.    Featured guest soloists range from our own freshman and sophomore students in the annual Concerto Concert to international concert artists such as Benny Kim, Dmitri Berlinsky, and even the likes of PDQ Bach!  

Chamber Orchestra    Conducted by Robert Tueller, the Chamber Orchestra consists of twenty select string players, chosen from membership of the Symphony Orchestra to form the core of this ensemble which specializes in music of the 17th and 18th centuries.  Designed to provide a near "professional" experience for advanced students, regular performances include operas, musicals, chamber music, string orchestra concerts, and full chamber performances.  Many of these concerts include woodwind and brass musicians drawn from the select Chamber Winds.  The Chamber Orchestra and Chamber Winds present a biennial concert tour during the winter semester.
