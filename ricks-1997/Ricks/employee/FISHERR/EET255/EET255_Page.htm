Course Description:

Introduction to system controllers (state machines & ROM controllers), microprocessors, and microcontrollers.  Processor architecture and operation: control unit, datapath, and instruction sets.  Processor systems: direct memory access, interrupts, memory, and I/O.  Applications of system controllers, microprocessors, and microcontrollers.  Students must design, build, and demonstrate a microcontroller project that meets given specifications.  A student presentation is required.  Computers are used to write and assemble code, emulate processors, program devices, and write reports.  Laboratory exercises are included. 

At the conclusion of this course students  will be able to analyze the operation of a wide variety of microprocessors, and other intelligent circuits.  They will also be able to design simple control circuits and interface external components to them.

Required Software: 

WinCUPL: Compiler for Programmable Logic Devices (Freeware version).  The serial number you should enter is 66999998.  Download WinZip if you do not have it already.  Read the CUPL Tutorial.

Digital Works: Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software to design and simulate logic circuits (Freeware version).  Read a short tutorial called Getting Started with Digital Works.

CircuitMaker: This is similar to Digital Works (above), but also lets you work with analog circuits.  This program makes it easy to include schematic diagrams in your lab reports.  (Free student version.)

MPLAB: FREE Development environment for PIC microcontrollers from MicroChip.
