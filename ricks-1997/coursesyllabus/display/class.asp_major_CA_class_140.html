Confectionary
CONFECTIONARY
CLASS CODE: CA 140 CREDITS:  1 FEE:  $20.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Practical work in the preparation of candy and chocolate products.
DESCRIPTION: This course is designed to introduce the student to basic confectionery techniques through application. A brief lecture and/or demonstration will take place at the beginning of each meeting to offer the students some of the theoretical knowledge necessary for practical application. Lab Fee: $20
TOPICS: Tempering Chocolate; The Art of the Plated Dessert Truffles & Chocolate Candies; Marzipan; Filigree;
Sugar & Caramels; Dipped Cherries; Nut Brittles & Butter Mints; Pulled Sugar; Fudge, Nougat, and Divinity; Tuile Cookies; Fruit Candies.
OBJECTIVES: 1. Correctly identify and use candy making equipment, as well as tell the advantages of one type, or piece, of equipment over another.
2. Properly make truffles, caramels, brittles, butter mints, and various other candies and chocolates.
3. Assemble marzipan, chocolate, sugar, and other listed garnishes.
4. Learn proper candy storage techniques.
5. Understand sugar and chocolate; where they come from, how heat, water, and other conditions may affect them.
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Final Examination: This exam will be based upon the weekly quizzes given in class.
2. Project: On the last day of class the students will prepare a plated dessert presentation. The purpose of the assignment is for the students to understand how a simple or plain dessert can be dressed up by using some of the techniques we learned in class.
3. Competencies: Each student is responsible to keep and turn in this competency sheet by the last day of class. The student is also responsible to have the instructor sign off their competencies. They are gimmee points if you just REMEMBER.
4. Attendance/Participation: In order to receive the full benefits of the class, all students will be required to be in class. In addition, the students are expected to participate fully without disrupting class
5. Quizzes: Will be offered throughout the course of the semester.

PREREQUISITES: Culinary Arts Major
