COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 240
Title: Space Planning I–Introduction to Interior Design Credit: 4
Text: Designing Interiors, Kilmer & Kilmer

Course Objectives:
 1. To provide a fundamental background in the principles and elements of design, proxemics, human factors, lifestyle and personality theories.
 2. To introduce the student to programming, conceptualizing, problem solving, space planning, and furniture layout.
 3. To introduce the student to specification of furniture, lighting, interior finishes and supporting schedules.
4. To cultivate an understanding of interior systems and finish materials through lecture and studio experience.
 5. To acquaint the student with the historical background of the design profession, architecture and furniture styles.
6. To introduce the student to research.
 7. To provide opportunities for group interaction through creativity exercises and small studio assignments.
8. To promote professional presentation skills in oral, written and graphic formats.

Description:
A foundation in space planning and interior design with an introduction to basic design theories, proxemics, programming, furnishings and furniture arrangement, materials and finishes, architectural systems, color and lighting, and historical architecture and furniture.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Oral and written interior design profession report.
2. Multiple studio and creativity assignments completed in class.
3. Multiple studio and lecture assignments.
4. Final studio project.
5. Four exams.
