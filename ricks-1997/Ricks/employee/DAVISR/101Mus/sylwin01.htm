CLASS REQUIREMENTS

 

1. QUIZZES: There will be preparation quizzes (10 pts each) in class. No make-ups unless cleared with me in advance. Your lowest score will be automatically dropped.

 

2. EXAMS: Your exams will consist of two parts: a written major exam, taken in the Testing Center on dates indicated on the sypllabus, and a Picture Quiz, taken in class. Exams count 100 points and all are comprehensive. Picture quizzes vary, but are usually about 45 points. None of these will be dropped.

 

3. GRADES: An ongoing printout showing your FINAL GRADE TO DATE will be posted in the hall. Grades are based on total points, with a 90, 80, 70, etc. cut-off. On the printout your lowest quiz score will be automatically dropped (all your scores will still appear on the printout but the lowest number will not be included in the total).

 

4. EXTRA CREDIT: There is none.

 

5. WANTS:  Please bear with me on three things: no food in class, no baseball caps, and I like to start on time, not five minutes after the bell.
