Diva returns to stage

by Aubrey Oman
OMA00001@BYUI.EDU
Special Sections editor

Celine Dion sings during the taping of “A New Day Has Come,” a musical special March 3 at the Kodak Theater in Los Angeles. This is the first performance for Dion after a two-year hiatus. AP Photo Archive
The stadium is dark and all that can be heard is the cheering of 90,000 fans. A single spotlight breaks through the darkness and illuminates the stage. Within moments, a golden voice breaks through the crowd’s cheers. “‘Cause I’m your lady, and you are my man...”

Celine Dion’s voice rang through the past decade with worldwide tours and record-breaking sales. She sold more than 140 million albums, making her the top-selling female artist of all time, according to 20/20.

But the story for Dion, 32, has changed a bit. Now a mother, her music and perspective on life has changed. Her new song, “A New Day Has Come,” relates Dion’s feelings as she was finally able to have her child, Rene Charles Angelil.

“Where there was weakness, I found my strength — all in the eyes of a boy,” according to the song’s lyrics.

“There’s specific songs that I can sing to him [Rene Charles], and other things that I can’t because he cries,” Dion said in a recent interview with Barbara Walters. “When I sing with all my heart, and my soul lullabies, he cries.”

Dion is best known for her Titanic smash hit “My Heart Will Go On.” The soundtrack became the first predominantly instrumental disc to top the charts in the SoundScan era. In fact, the album knocked Dion’s studio album “Let’s Talk About Love” out of the No. 1 Billboard slot.

“I love ballads. They touch me. I’m a very romantic person. I’m just sharing what I’ve received all my life-just giving it back,” Dion said in an E! interview about the Titanic theme song.

As a French Canadian, Dion’s singing roots reach back past her English albums. As a 12-year-old in a family of 13 musical siblings in Montreal, she dreamt of being a singer. That is when she met Rene Angelil. He mortgaged his house to pay for the five- octave soprano to break her debut album into the music business.

In 1990, Dion decided to make her American premier. She learned English in only two months.

The relationship between Dion and Angelil soon grew from professional to romantic. When Dion was 20, they began dating and married six years later. Angelil was 46 years old.

“I think that’s the most important thing in life, to share your happiness and be with the person you love. If my career was to stop tomorrow, even right now, I would still be the happiest person on Earth because I have found the man of my life,” Dion said.

The successful pop star will officially begin her consecutive concerts in March 2003. Dion signed a three-year contract for $100 million to perform at Caesar’s Palace in Las Vegas five nights a week.
