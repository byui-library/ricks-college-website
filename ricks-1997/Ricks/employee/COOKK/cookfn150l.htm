COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 150L
Title: Food Service Management I Lab Credit: 1
Text: West &Wood’s Introduction to Foodservice, Payne-Palacio, 1997

Course Objective:
At the completion of this lab the student should be able to:
- describe the organization chart and management levels of Ricks College foodservice
- describe factors involved in a successful catering office
- demonstrate how to organize and conduct a food catering event
- demonstrate how to determine purchasing needs and describe the process of purchasing,
   receiving and storing food items
- follow production sheets in preparing foods for service
- standardize, adjust and cost-out recipes
- describe factors that affect a foodservice cost control

Description:
Food preparation experience in all areas of food service.  Lab rotations at the Ricks College Foodservices include purchasing, receiving, storage of food items, preparation and serving, catering, and cost-control management.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Standardizing recipes, Expanding and costing out recipes
Evaluation of food and labor costs
Paper describing the food purchasing, receiving and storage process at Ricks College
