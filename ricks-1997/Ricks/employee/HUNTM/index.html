“The Fam” : a reflective thinking exercise

 

I call this family photograph a reflective thinking exercise because the picture has been doctored. Can you detect the evidence tampering?  Although it shows the whole family at my oldest son’s wedding reception in May 2002, at least one of my children was actually not present for the photograph, and I am clearly not as old as I look. What do you think—can you always trust photographic evidence?
