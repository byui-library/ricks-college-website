"W" Grades – and What They Mean at BYU-Idaho

A "W" grade is placed on your academic record (transcript) anytime you drop a class after the 10th day in a semester (or after the 5th day in a block or summer term) for any reason.

"W" grades are NOT calculated into your grade point average (GPA).

"W" grades do NOT disappear if you re-take the class at a later date.

"W" grades DO count against you in the 75% rule. The 75% satisfactory progress policy states that you must complete at least 75% of the credits for which you register each semester. "F" and "W" grades are non-progress grades.

For example, if you register for 16 credits and later drop 4 credits, your progress rate is 75% (12 divided by 16 = 75) and you are considered to be making satisfactory progress; however, if you drop or fail a total of 5 credits, your progress rate drops to 69% is lower than 75%, you will be placed on academic probation when final grades were processed for that summer.

In the next summer, if your progress rate was 75% or higher, you would be removed from academic probation; if it were lower than 75% you would be placed on academic suspension.

FINANCIAL AID: If you are receiving Federal Financial Aid, you must check with that office to determine how "W" grades affect your eligibility.
