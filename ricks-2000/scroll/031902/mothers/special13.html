Women spruce up their style

by Katie McMurray
MCM00003@BYUI.EDU
Arts editor

Accessories — Most girls do not leave home without them. They can be worn or carried in hand bags and purses of many different sizes and with many different material prints.

“I think that there are accessories to fit every person’s personality. It’s fun to find the accessory that fits you,” Nicole Bigler, a sophomore from Farmington, Utah, said.

An accessory is a little something extra that women add to their appearance. Although not meant to be essential, many women agree there are some items that are necessary to have at all times. Such items could include lotion, chapstick, mints and a cell phone. Others add rings, bracelets, necklaces and watches to complete their ensemble.

“I really like watches because I hate not knowing what time it is,” Bigler said. “I also like necklaces because there are so many to choose from.”

“My favorite accessory would have to be my rings. I wear two, and they both have very special meaning for me,” Ashley Blackburn, a freshman from Crofton, Md., said. “One was a gift from my parents after they took a trip to Mexico, and the other was my Grandma’s wedding ring. Both help to remind me of my family.”

Rings carry a variety of different meanings, including those of sentimental value. There are wedding rings, mood rings, engagement rings, promise rings, CTR rings, etc. Each accessory fits the style of the girl and her preferences.

“[My favorite accessories are] my rings because my grandma gave them to me. They have sentimental value,” Julie Bell, a sophomore from South Lake Tahoe, Calif., said.

“The accessories [women] wear indicate their personality,” Allison Barrus, a freshman from Yucca Valley, Calif., said.

Girls choose their accessories in order to be unique, in order to be different from other women.

“I like my rolled penny bracelet. I think it’s unique. I haven’t seen anybody else with it,” Barrus said.

“My favorite accessory is a rubber band because you can do anything with it. It’s universal,” Julia Sawada, a sophomore from Kailua, Hawaii, said.

On the other hand, some think certain women go overboard with their fashions.

“I think women wear too much junk — their makeup, big earrings and hair stuff,” Gail Washburg, a freshman from El Dorado Hills, Calif., said.

“I’m all about probability, so when I see girls carrying around trendy little purses or wearing really flashy jewelery, it just makes me wonder,” Blackburn said.

Whether a woman wears one simple ring or an entire outfit with a handbag, each accessory defines who she is.
