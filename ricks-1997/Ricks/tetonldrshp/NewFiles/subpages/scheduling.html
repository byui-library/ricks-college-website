Scheduling

The Teton Lodge Facilities can be scheduled for Ricks College groups on a first-come, first-served basis starting:

April 1st for the Fall semester.

November 1st for the Winter semester Spring

(we do not have access to the facilities between June 1st and August 20th).

Each group must agree to use the facilities in accordance with the Institute’s mission and must follow the guidelines explained on the application form. The facilities are NOT available for social or recreational activities.

Different application forms are available depending on your group:

1) Recognized ASRC Club (the advisor must approve the retreat, sign the application form, and attend the retreat-- He or she is responsible for the group activities)

2) Campus Church Group (the presiding priesthood officer should schedule the retreat)

3) Campus Department (a coordinating employee can reserve the facilities for administrative, staff, or faculty training)

If you have any questions about the Teton Lodge Facilities or want to check available dates, contact Van Crawford at 356-2598 or email: crawfordv@ricks.edu.
