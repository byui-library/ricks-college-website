Clean checks vital to apartments

by Tammy Walquist
WAL01018@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

An important event takes place in each apartment complex at least twice a month: clean check.

There are a variety of reasons why clean check is given each week.

One is to keep the apartments nice.

“Clean check ensures that apartments stay nicely cleaned and uncluttered,” Cindy Sharp, manager of Colonial Apartments, said. “It also makes it possible so that people can be happy.”

Clean check is also important because it follows the guidelines set by the school.

“It’s important because it is conducive with what the school wants,” Andy Clark, manager of Carousel Court, said. “[Clean apartments] add to the learning process [by] creating a good environment.”

Clean check also allows managers the opportunity to check the condition of each apartment.

“It allows us the chance to get into apartments and get things fixed now instead of later,” Clark said.

With clean check, there is a list of chores which must be done each week. Some of these chores include cleaning the bathrooms, vacuuming the floor, making sure the kitchen is cleaned up, cleaning out the fridge, cleaning the windows and, in some apartments, cleaning the baseboards is required.

Weekly clean check can take anywhere from half an hour to three hours to prepare for, Maria Reftivo, a sophomore from Ontario, Canada, said.

In some complexes, the resident assistants do the clean check. In others, the managers do the checking.

The Colonial House is one such complex.

Some of the things the managers check for include cleaned bathrooms and kitchens,, cleaned fridge, the microwave wiped out and the oven scrubbed, Sharp said.

Most of the time there is no problem with the students cleaning the apartment.

“We give the [students] a list, and [they are] good at doing everything,” Sharp said.

Students do not find clean check to be a bother either. Knowing that it is coming and being able to plan for it helps out. Plus, when each member of the apartment helps, it makes the cleaning go faster, Denice Crofutt, a junior from Redding, Calif., said.

Some students also see having a regular clean check as useful.

“There are a lot of reasons why we keep our apartment clean, but [one of] the main reasons is for visitors, so we don’t look like slobs,” Brian Hone, a freshman from Shelley, Idaho, said. “Once-a-week clean checks [are better] because then we can stay clean. Once-a- month checks, it would build up.”

However, some students would enjoy having clean check less often.

“I would prefer once a month because it makes us be less babied,” Reftivo said. “We don’t have to [be] check[ed on] each week.”

The majority of clean checks are held on a certain day of the week. This helps people remember that they are coming up.

“The [students] always know when [clean check] is going to be, so it’s easier to prepare,” Clark said.

Even though clean checks are not easy to prepare for, they are useful.

“I think they’re great. I love them,” Sharp said.

It also adds to a student’s learning experience to have an orderly apartment.

“I think it’s just important so the [students] can be in a nice clean environment while they are going to school,” Clark said.
