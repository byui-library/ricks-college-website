HOW TO OBTAIN A RICKS ID CARD

 

Student ID cards are issued at the ID Center which is located next to the Ticket Office in the Manwaring Center. Office hours are from 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. Monday through Friday, except on Tuesday when the ID Center closes at 2 p.m. for the weekly devotional. A photo ID is required before a card will be issued (driver's license, passport, etc.). A nonstudent spouse of a registered student can be issued a Ricks ID. Students taking classes through Continuing Education are not eligible to receive a Ricks ID card.
