Internet Scavenger Hunt

Can you use the WWW to find these items? Try your hand, learn and have fun.

Directions: Save a copy of this page to your personal disk or H:\ drive. Open it and save it as a Word document.Add your name to the document and then, as you search the internet and find the answers to these questions copy the URL from the page where you find the information, along with the answer, and record it on this page. When you are done, send me an e-mail, with this page as an attachment, for grading.

1. What is the difference between an meta-search engine and a search engine?

2. What are the projected world-wide revenues for e-commerce transactions for the year 2000?

3. What is the difference between the Internet and an Intranet?

4. What is a "Hit" to a web server? What four web servers can handle the most hits simultaneously (list the name and number)?

5. What is the difference between an html editor and a text editor?

6. What does MODEM stand for, what does it do? What is the difference between a DCE and DTE connection?

7. Find a corporate web site that has a posted Privacy policy. In one paragraph summarize it.

8. Visit the W3C website and summarize the Design Principles that guide the W3C's?

9. Where you can download graphics, sound and backgrounds for inclusion on a web page without copyright violation?

10. Where can you find JavaScripts that you can copy for use in your own web site without copyright violation?

Bonus: What is your hobby or special interest? Find a website that expands your knowledge of the hobby or gives you information about how to do it better.
