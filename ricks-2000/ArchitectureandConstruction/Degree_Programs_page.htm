Degree Programs

Associate in Architectural Drafting Technology (Career & Transfer) (047)

A two-year specialized program for those individuals who desire to enter the architectural drafting job market at the completion of the program. This program can also be tailored for transfer to universities or colleges in the area of Construction Management.
Advisor:  Garth J. Jensen


Associate in Carpentry & Building Construction (Career) (219)

 A two-year specialized program for those individuals who desire to enter the construction industry upon graduation. Graduates with this degree and field experience can expect to fill such construction positions as carpenters, construction superintendents, general contractors and other positions in the construction industry. 
Advisor:  Rudy Puzey


Associate in Arts & Sciences with a major in Technology Education (276)
A program that prepares industrial/technology/vocational teachers for junior high/middle school, high school, and post-high-school programs. Additional education is required to prepare for teacher education in college or university. This is a transfer program designed to complete all general education requirements for transfer to a four-year university to complete a Bachelor of Science degree in Technology Education.  
Advisor:  Rudy Puzey




Associate in Arts & Sciences with a major in Pre-Architecture (Transfer) (293)

A rigorous, college level, transfer oriented program. This transfer program is designed to meet many of the first two-year core requirements for an architectural curriculum. To accomplish this objective the student must consult his advisor to tailor the transfer curriculum to meet the core requirements of the students selected college of architecture.   Advisor:  William Barnhill




Associate in Arts & Science with a major in Construction Management (Transfer) (303)
A transfer program that provides basic construction classes and allows students to complete all of the General Education requirements for transfer to a four-year university to complete a Bachelor of Science degree in Construction Management. Advisor:  Brian Blaylock
