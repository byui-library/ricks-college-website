Assignments for Sharing the Gospel
Purposes and Grading Criteria





1. Why I want to Serve paper

Purpose: To give the student an opportunity to prayerfully look at their motivations in sharing the gospel. It is hoped that the student will take some time to ponder what the gospel means to them and why they are going to share it with others. Lesson 7 in the Missionary Preparation manual should be read prior to doing this assignment.

Grading Criteria: The assignment is to write a two page (1.5 line spacing) paper that would outline some of the thoughts and feelings of the student. To receive full credit the paper must be the full two pages and must have been proof read (we have spell checkers for a reason). I will deduct some point if it is obvious that the paper was thrown together without any fore or after thought (this is usually shown by huge spelling and grammatical errors). This paper is worth 100 points.
 

2 & 3. Doctrinal Papers

Purpose: To give the student the opportunity to better understand two selected doctrines of the gospel which are introduced in the discussions thus helping the student be better prepared to share these doctrines. It is hoped that the student will choose a doctrine that he/she might not understand as well as he/she would like.

Grading Criteria: To receive full credit the paper must be two pages (1.5 line spacing), proof read and must also include at least four scriptures and two references from General Authorities to develop your paper. Please document the scriptures and GA quotes on a separate works cited page. Each paper is worth 100 points.
 

3. Book of Mormon Paper

Purpose: This semester each student is to read the Book of Mormon for ½ an hour each day. The purpose of this paper is to first report how the student did on their reading and to then discuss the experience they had reading this semester. Each student will be expected to answer the following two questions in their paper: How has this assignment affected my life this semester? How will this better prepare me to share the gospel?

Grading Criteria: In the heading the student will report how they did (by percentage) in reading every day for ½ an hour. The student will receive full credit if they then write a two page, proof read paper which answers the questions listed above.

Scripture Tests

Purpose: These test are to provide an opportunity for the student to familiarize themselves with some of the basic scriptures used in teaching the discussions. By design the tests are not to be a major portion of the grade (they account for less than 15% of the grade) but are provided to assist in learning the doctrines taught by the discussions. A major portion of each test will allow the student to match the scripture reference up with the descriptive phrase used on the green sheet. There are also some questions that will require the student to pick one scripture from a list of three or four that would be best used in the situation described. The following is a breakdown of the scriptures covered by each test.
