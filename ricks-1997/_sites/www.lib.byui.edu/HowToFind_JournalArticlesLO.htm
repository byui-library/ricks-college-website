How to Find Journals (Library Owned)

The McKay Library subscribes to approximately 800 journals and magazines. Journals are generally more scholarly in their content than are magazines. These are located in the Periodicals area on the first floor in the West Wing and are placed on the shelves in alphabetical order by title. All titles are kept for five years, and some for ten or more. See the David O. McKay Serials books that are placed around the Library for a list of holdings, or use the Library Catalog and enter the subject or keyword followed by the collection code"per" which indicates periodicals. An example would be "nursing per" without the quotation marks. This would show journals and magazines related to the field of nursing. See also Microfiche/film Sources
