Histology with Lab
HISTOLOGY/LAB
CLASS CODE: BIO 380 CREDITS:  4
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Microscopic anatomy of cells and tissues and their relationship to the function of the cell.
DESCRIPTION: A microscopic study of the anatomy of the cells that constitute the majors systems and organs of the body. Exposure to the techniques and protocols for creating histological specimens and slides.
TOPICS: Microscopic identification of the organs and systems of the body. Students will study the histological staining techniques used for light microscope, immunohistochemistry, cytoarchitecture, SEM and TEM.
OBJECTIVES: Equip students with the requisite skills needed to identify the tissues of the body and the techniques for preparing these tissues.
REQUIREMENTS:
PREREQUISITES: BIO 200 or BIO 264 & 265
