Purpose: This course is designed as an introduction to classical mechanics.  Integral to this course are systematic problem solving techniques necessary for quantitative thinking.

Prerequisite: (minimum) concurrent registration in Calculus I.

 

Tests: There are four scheduled tests (closed book) throughout the semester in the Testing Center.  The tests will normally be at the Testing Center for 3 days with each test being worth 200 points.  If there is a problem in taking the test during the scheduled time, see me before the test begins. Otherwise, if you see me after the test is over then I will allow you to take the test late but with a 20-point penalty.

The Final Exam, which will be taken in the Testing Center, is a comprehensive examination of all the chapters studied throughout the semester and is worth 200 points.

  NOTE: The lowest test or exam score will be dropped, giving

  a total of 800 possible points.  DO NOT DISCUSS the test with

  anyone until after the test closes!

Homework: Homework is assigned from the Problems in your textbook and is due at the beginning of class when we start a new chapter (see class schedule for exact due dates).

 ** late homework is given 3/4 credit for one day late, 1/2 credit      for up to two weeks late, and zero credit after two weeks **

All problems after chapter one will use the 3D BE SNUB outline as discussed in class, otherwise you will not receive full credit.  You may work together in groups, but everyone needs to do his/her own work.

 

Standards: Follow Them!
