COURSE SYLLABUS

 Department: Home Economics
 Course#: ID 236
 Title: Presentation I: Perpective For Designers  Credit: 3
 Text:
 
 Course Objective:

Introduce the design student to two dimensional drawing and design
Help the student gain competency with one and two point perspective in preparation to making
perspective drawings, presentations, the development of design concepts and quick sketches for clients.
Reinforce elements and principles of design
 
 Description: Topics Covered:

Interpretive Construction drawing
One point perspective
Two point perspective
Project presentation including presentation boards and oral communication

 Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)

Create a perspective book
Two finished one point perspective interior drawings
Two finished two point perspective interior drawings
One rendered floor plan
