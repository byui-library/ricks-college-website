Request An Application

Students may now apply for admissions and scholarships on the Internet. We would encourage you to use the on-line application. Ricks College has a secured server, which guarantees the privacy of information sent to us electronically. Applications received by the Internet will be processed within one to two working days.

If you would prefer to use a paper application, know that it will take up to two weeks to process your file. A paper application may be requested by e-mail, written request or by telephone. Please include your mailing address and the term you wish to enroll. To request an application by e-mail click here .
