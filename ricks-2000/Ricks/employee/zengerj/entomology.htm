Below are links to a wide variety of insect related information arranged under the headings of Youth Entomology, Reference Sites and Organizations.

 

 

Youth entomology

A Bugs Life Web site

The official site of the Disney Film. Includes video clips and some games and activities. http://disney.go.com/disneyvideos/masterpiece/shelves/bugs/index.html

 

AES Bug Club

An online bug club that includes excellent information about insect activities and the care of insect pets. http://www.ex.ac.uk/bugclub/

 

Alien Empire

The companion Web site of the 6 hour PBS insect documentary of the same name.  Provides some interesting multimedia education tools, teacher resources, and activities. http://www.wnet.org/nature/alienempire/index.html

 

Banph

A fun and extensive online story for older children about the adventures of an ant named Banph.  http://www.banph.com/

 

City Bugs

A youth entomology site from UC Berkely that provides resources for identifying insects, activities and teacher resources. http://www.cnr.berkeley.edu/citybugs/  

 

Florida 4-H Bug Club

This site includes a weekly quiz contest with prizes, information about activities and events, and places to post your insect artwork, photographs and poems. A Spanish version is also available. http://bugweb.entnem.ufl.edu/bugclub/

 

Insect ABCs

An alphabetical look at the world of insects from Cornell University.  http://cornell-iowa.edu/biology/insects/index.html

 

Insecta Inspecta

Information about a wide variety of specific insects.  http://www.insecta-inspecta.com/

 

Mississippi State 4-H Entomology Web site.  This web site contains information about the excellent youth entomology program at Mississippi State University including activities, contest rules, and photographs from their annual bug camp.  http://www.ext.msstate.edu/anr/entpath/4-h/ent4-h/4-hentom.html

 

Using Live Insects In Elementary Classrooms

This is a University of Arizona Web site that has lesson plans for elementary school teachers and information on rearing insects. http://insected.arizona.edu/uli.htm

Reference Sites

Insect Management Guides

Information on managing a wide range of insect pests from the University of Florida.. http://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/MENU_IG

 

Entomology Index of Internet Resources

The single best source for all types of entomology information.  http://www.ent.iastate.edu/List/

 

Collecting and Preserving Insects and Mites

Detailed instructions from the USDA on how to collect and preserve insects.  http://www2.sel.barc.usda.gov/selhome/collpres.htm

 

Database of IPM Resources

The place to look for information regarding the Integrated Pest Management of insect pests. http://ippc.orst.edu/dir/

 

Food Defect Action Levels

An interesting site showing the amount of insect and other contaminants the FDA allows to be present in our food. http://vm.cfsan.fda.gov/~dms/dalbook.html

Organizations

Entomology Institutions

A complete list of all entomology institutions found on the Iowa State Entomology Index. http://www.ent.iastate.edu/List/institutions.html

 

Entomological Society of America

The largest entomology organization in the world. Includes information about meetings, publications, and an online entomology bookstore. http://www.entsoc.org/

 

Florida Entomological Society

An organization comprised of over 1,000 entomologists in the Southeastern United States and Caribbean. Includes access to electronic versions of peer reviewed journal The Florida Entomologist.  http://www.flaentsoc.org/

 

Southeastern Branch of the ESA

Information about the upcoming meeting of the Southeaster Branch of the Entomological society of America. http://www.gaentsoc.org/seblocal.html
