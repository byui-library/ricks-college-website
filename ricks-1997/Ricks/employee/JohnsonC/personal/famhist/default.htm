Search Engines:  Used to find web sites containing particular words.
www.dogpile.com Meta Search Engine:  Searches several search engines (including Yahoo)
www.yahoo.com Yahoo is one search engine.
Use it to look up information on a specific area or subject.  This got me excited about new possibilities for family history research on the web.
For example:  Choose "U.S. States" under the regional heading by clicking on the highlighted text "U.S. States."  From there, Choose a state such as "Illinois."  After that choose "Arts and Humanities" and after that click on "History."  Finally, you can choose "Genealogy."   This gives a list of several genealogical resources for that state.
Here is another example:  To find links for Danish genealogy, I clicked on the following sequence of words:  under Regional, "Countries" > "Denmark" > "Arts and Humanities" > "Humanitites" and then scrolling down the page I found "genealogy" and a couple of good links.
