Ricks College Bookstore       Check Cashing


Checks may be cashed for up to $25.00 over the amount of purchase.  A current Student ID** must be presented.  Local address and telephone number must be clearly written on all checks.  Payroll checks or large personal checks may be cashed at the Cashier's Office in the Administration Building.

**During the first week of each semester, a drivers license or other picture ID can be used in the place of current student ID.
