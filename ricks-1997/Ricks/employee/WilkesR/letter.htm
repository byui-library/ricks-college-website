Vice President of Student Life: Robert M. Wilkes
A Letter To Students

Dear Student:

Welcome to Ricks College's web site. We are very pleased that you have an interest in pursuing your educational goals here.

Many resources are available to assist you in having a successful experience. You will learn about some that will be particularly helpful in meeting your individual needs and maximizing your opportunities. Among the most important of these would be your Academic Advisor. We strongly recommend that you develop and maintain a close working relationship with him/her.

As an institution we have collectively observed thousands of students over many years. Should you be admitted, you would be wise to follow a few simple guidelines. Among them are the following:

- Faithful adherence to the Honor Code will lift you to a higher level of performance in all aspects of your life.

- Compliance with established policies, procedures, and regulations will enhance your freedom of choice and increase the range of positive experiences you will have.

- Being an informed student will significantly reduce your number of missed opportunities. When uncertain of any issue, seek assistance from college personnel. You will also find that other students are often able to provide informed answers to your questions.

- Seeking service opportunities will enrich your education and contribute to your sense of gratitude and appreciation for your own circumstances. Make yourself available to serve through ASRC offices, the Leadership and Service Institute, student wards and stakes, as well as informal, spur of the moment, occasions that may arise.

- Participating in a wide range of campus, community, and ecclesiastical activities will compliment your in-class experiences. Make it a point to spend weekends on campus where you can be involved in school activities and appropriate Sabbath Day observance. A significant part of a complete Ricks College Experience occurs on Saturday and Sunday. Excessive weekend travel also exposes you to an increased and unnecessary risk of accident and injury.

Thank you again for your interest in being part of this unique student body. We confidently anticipate your contribution to both the social and learning environments we enjoy. Please contact the Coordinator of New Student Orientation by calling (208) 356-2393 if we can be of any help.

Sincerely,

Robert M. Wilkes
Student Life Vice President
