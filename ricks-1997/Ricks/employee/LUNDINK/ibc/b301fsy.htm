Course Description:

This introductory course in financial management provides a basic foundation for other courses in finance and business management.  Topics covered in this course include financial statement analysis, financial forecasting, leverage, working capital management, capital structure, capital budgeting, short and long-term financial management, sources of financing, time value of money, and cost of capital. 

 

Course Objectives:  Students will develop an understanding of and ability to work with fundamental financial concepts.  Basic mathematics of finance and use of a business calculator will be taught in the course.  Students will learn real-world applications of core financial principles.

NOTES: 

 

Study Habits:  This class is a fairly intensive review of financial principles, including several quantitative subjects.  Students who are most successful in this class tend to be those who are disciplined in their approach; this includes doing the reading and homework problems prior to class and coming to class prepared to ask questions that will assist the student in further understanding the material.  Students who fall behind in their reading or who do not put forth consistent effort in doing the homework assignments will likely struggle with the material. 

 

Exams:  Comprised of multiple choice and essay / problem questions.  Exams will cover key terms and review questions from each chapter.  Exams also will include key concepts covered in classroom discussions.  Most exams will be held in the testing center. 

 

Final Exam:  Will cover material that was discussed throughout the semester in the classroom as well as material in the text.  Further detail will be given in class.

 

Homework Exercises:  Homework problems have been assigned for most of the chapters that will be covered.  The homework assignments are due at the BEGINNING of class.  They are worth a total of 50 points (5 points each), and will be graded on completeness of each answer, as well as neatness of presentation.  If more than 10 homework assignments are given, only the 10 highest homework scores will be counted in the total score.  Some of the homework solutions will be reviewed in class; an effort will be made to post all homework solutions in an easily accessible location (instructor will have more detail on this during class).

 

Cases:  Five group case analyses will be counted in the score for the class for a total of 50 points (10 points each).  If more than 5 cases are given, only your highest 5 case scores will be counted in the total score.  Cases will be completed as group projects in class.  The cases will be handed out in class on the day they are to be worked on.  The cases will be graded on several criteria, including proper application of concepts, correct computations, issue identification, and individual input.  Group case analyses will be timed exercises.

 

Spreadsheet Exercises:  Two spreadsheet exercises will be required during the semester.  They are worth a total of 50 points (25 points each).  Students will be required to complete the spreadsheet exercises on their own time and will need access to a computer with Microsoft’s Excel spreadsheet program.  Computers are available in the business computer labs and the library.  Further details on the exercises will be provided in class.
