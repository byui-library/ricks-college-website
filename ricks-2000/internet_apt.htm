Off-Campus Internet Access

Many students who come to BYU-Idaho desire to sign up for Internet access in their apartments. Since this service is not provided by BYU-Idaho, it is necessary to sign up through an Internet Service Provider (ISP). We recommend that students sign up with an ISP that filters out undesirable content, i.e., pornography, violence, etc. Filtering services cannot guarantee 100 percent effectiveness, but they can help prevent students from accessing inappropriate web sites.

BYU-Idaho students are expected to refrain from viewing any media which compromise their commitment to the BYU-Idaho Honor Code, and infractions can lead to dismissal.
