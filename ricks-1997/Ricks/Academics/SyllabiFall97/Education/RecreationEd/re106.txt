Purpose:
     The purpose of this class is twofold.  One is to learn 
to ride a mountain bike in a manner that is fun, safe, and 
challenging while minimizing damage to the environment, to
yourself, and to others.  This involves riding technique, 
trail etiquette and minimum impact riding.  The second purpose 
deals with mountain bike and your body maintenance so that
optimum fun and safety can be enjoyed.
     Perhaps another purpose of equal importance is the 
development of an appreciation for the natural world and the 
wide variety of challenges and opportunities for growth it offers.

Requirements:
	Attend class each week ready to ride.  Be on time.  
We will meet at Hart 118 at the beginning of class.  You can 
wear modest biking shorts to class.  Each student must have
his/her own helmet, and that helmet must be worn while on your 
bike!!
	Read assignments from text.
	Be willing to exert or extend yourself physically and 
emotionally.
	Write a two page paper on your personal perception 
of mountain biking (why you like to do it.  What are your 
concerns or fears.  What are your goals.  Has mountain biking
changed the way you view things.  Why or why not. Etc)
	In compliance with applicable disability law, qualified 
students with a disability may be entitled to "reasonable 
accommodation."  It is the student's responsibility to disclose 
to the teacher any special need she/he may have before the end 
of the first week of class.
