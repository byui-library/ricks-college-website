POWERSTUDY OF THE SCRIPTURES

PROMISES INHERENT IN POWERSTUDY:
* Receive Revelation * Understand what you study * Remember what you study * Love to study the scriptures

ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS OF POWERSTUDY:
    * A prayer for understanding and revelation. Ask in faith and the Lord will keep his promise.
    * Paper in a 3-ring binder (or Planner or Scripture Journal), pen, scripture markers, and the scriptures.
    * Time and setting for pondering where you can receive impressions and think without disruptions.
    * A Purpose! Begin by having a reason to study! Consider these questions:

-What does this teach me about God?
-How does He deal with us?
-What gospel principle is here?
-How does this relate to me?
-Will this help me with a problem?
-Why is this story here?
-What does the Lord want me to do?
THE PROCESS OF POWER STUDY:
    1) 1st Page:
        * Write the reference in the upper right-hand corner.
        * Write this questions at the top: "Why am I studying this chapter?"
        * Write your answers to this question before you go on. This step gives you a purpose in studying!
    2) Continue your study by:
        * Write "A," circle it, and then write two parentheses ( ).
        * After the parentheses, write the first major theme found in the chapter heading. These major themes were identified by Elder McConkie.
        * In the parentheses, write the verses where this major theme is found in the scriptures. (Except for the Doctrine and Covenants, you have to search for where the verses begin and end for that idea.
        * Begin your reading, taking time to reflect and ponder on what you read. As you come to an idea you want to remember, write it down as "A-1." If you find a question about something you want to answer, write a "?" by the idea. Ponder the answer or go find the answer! If some idea or insight comes powerfully into your mind, write a "*" by it in the margin. This is how you capture the ideas that are significant to you! There are no "correct" ideas to write as A-1, A-2, etc. These are YOUR ideas which you receive through revelation or ideas you want to remember.
        * If you come to any words or themes you don't understand, take the time to check some of the references in the Topical Guide, the Bible Dictionary, or a good commentary.
        * After finishing the verses that cover the first main idea, go on to the second idea by repeating the above process. This next main idea is labeled "B." Continue on through "C," "D," etc.
        * REMEMBER: the main purpose of Power Study is to take the time to understand what you read, to ponder the scriptures, and to capture your impressions and ideas that have come during your study.

POWER STUDY is like planting your time of study (a seed) in fertile soil rather than rocky, thorny, or roadside soils; it's drinking from the living waters of revelation as you fill the reservoir of understanding; it helps you receive and capture the other half of the revelation that was begun with the prophet's original words. When you want to speak to God, you pray; when you want God to speak to you, you POWER STUDY the scriptures.

Student Concerns and Ideas For Personal Modifications:

"I don't like all of the writing. Can't I just read?"
* Suggestion: No, you can't just read. That's the problem with most gospel study! Having a pencil and paper handy to capture insights that come to you or ideas you want to remember is an essential to effective gospel study. You can "just read" any of the chapters which are not assigned to be Power Studied. But in the Power Study chapters, you need to apply yourself for some heavy-duty discovery!

"I like to take notes, but not in the same format as you've suggested. Do I have any flexibility with how I take notes?"
* Suggestion: If you have found a method of capturing ideas as you study, you may use your own personal process. To keep your efforts in scripture study as meaningful as the other students, please include two of the most important aspects of Power Study into your own ideas. These two ideas are: 1) Remember to identify a purpose for your study before you actually start, and 2) Keep your notes in such a way that you remember significant ideas and capture new insights to come to you.

What PowerStudy Notes Might Look Like

1 Nephi 17

Why Am I Studying This Chapter? (I wrote this before I began my study.)

1) How does the Lord motivate people to do His work?
2) How does the righteous protect themselves from the wicked?
3) What revelation does the Lord want me to receive while I read this chapter?
4) What were God's dealings with Nephi, and do they parallel how He would deal with me?

A (1-16) Nephi is commanded to build a ship. (McConkie's summary statement.)
        A-1 Nephi had a good attitude even though they had afflictions.

* A good attitude sees God's blessings in the midst of trails.

V. 3 A-2 This is a repeat of Nephi's earlier statement in 1 Ne 3:7. It shows his trust that God will show a way.

        A-3 If God wants to talk to me, He will send me to a temple (mountain, in Nephi's day.)

V. 7 A-4 After Nephi went there, he prayed, asking God what he should learn. Do I ask my Father what I should learn when I go to a temple.

       A-5 Nephi asked God how to make a ship; I would ask God how I can make a righteous family. Both answers are the same: After God's manner!

       A-6 Nephi asked where he would find the ore wherewith to make tools. I ask: "Where do I go to find the insights and concepts wherewith I can make the tools of parenting (i.e. discipline.)

       A-7 Four blessings if we keep the commandments:
            * We will have LIGHT.
            *We will PREPARE THE WAY.
            * We will be LED TO A PROMISE LAND (i.e. Patriarchal blessings.)
            * We will KNOW - HAVE A TESTIMONY OF GOD.

B (17-22) His Brethren oppose him. (McConkie's summary statement.)

       B-1 Laman is like the "Doubters" around us:
            * They put their trust in statistics which say things are pretty grim.
            * My own feelings of inadequacy.
            * People who hold you back or mock you when you try to do better.

V. 18 B-2 Why people doubt or complain:
            * They are lazy.
            * They don't believe in revelation.
            * They don't have faith in Christ!

         B-3 Satan rejoices in God's sorrows.

         B-4 Satan tries to portray revelation as the foolish imagination of a person's heart.

V. 20 B-5 Satan wants you to stay within your comfort zones. To leave a comfort zone gives you new strength, power, and growth.
