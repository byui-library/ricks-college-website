You will need the following if you are staying in women's on-campus cooking style housing:

 
  • Bedding - standard twin sheets, blankets, pillow, pillowcases
  • Towels/wash cloths
  • Dishes, pans, kitchen utensils, toaster, a hot-air popcorn popper, ect.
  • Iron
  • Alarm clock
  • Flashlight - use of matches or candles are prohibited
  • Soap and other toiletries
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Laundry soap
  • Toilet paper
  • Hangers
  • Personal Items
