Teton Lodge Facilities

Several buildings in the Teton Basin near Victor, Idaho are available for use by Ricks College student groups and departments. The property owner generously makes these facilities leadership available for training between September and May of each year. Below is a description of what each building offers:

Except for the barn, all of the facilities can be used for overnight retreats--usually on Friday nights. Participants should bring their own bed roll/sleeping bag, pillow, and bathroom amenities. In most buildings foam pads are available for sleeping on the floor.

Lodge
The lodge can accommodate up to 60 people for training. There are three levels to the lodge, which include a basement, main floor and loft. The lodge has bathroom and shower facilities for both men and women. It is equipped with an industrial-sized kitchen; freezer and refrigeration; paper plates, cups, plastic silverwarde, napkins; TV/VCR; overhead projector; flip chart; markers; paper; pencils; firewood; and a piano.

Barn
The barn is located approximately 50 yards South of the lodge. The barn is a rustic facility that can accommodate up to 250 people. Large conferences and workshops are usually held in this building. It is also a suitable location for team building activities for groups of 20-40 people. The barn is equipped teambuilding activities along and 250 chairs.

Homestead Cabin
The homestead is a beautiful, rustic cabin just a 1/4 of a mile West of the Teton Lodge. The cabin can accommodate up to 10 people comfortably and up to 15 if necessary. It is equipped with two kitchens (refrigeration, stove & oven in each); paper plates, cups, and napkins; plastic silverware; and a small TV/VCR.

Bunk Houses
Two new "bunk house" cabins have been made available this year. Both are constructed essentially the same, with one main room in the middle and a bedroom on each side of the main room. The main room in each cabin can accommodate up to 20 people for training. Each side room is equipped with bathroom facilities and a shower. There is no kitchen, cooking, or refrigeration equipment at these facilities. Prepared food is necessary for groups using these buildings (limited access during the Winter months).

Quickwater Ranch Facilities

Lodge

This lodge can accommodate up to forty people for training. It includes a kitchen, dining area, and training room, two lofts, and one bathroom. We have some paper supplies. No media equipment is provided.
