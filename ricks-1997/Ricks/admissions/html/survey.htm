ALTERNATE TRACKS

In an effort to make Ricks College available to all worthy and qualified students who wish to attend, we are considering the implementation of non-traditional enrollment tracks.  These tracks would differ from the traditional Fall/Winter semester sequence.  For example, some students would attend Summer/Fall, but not Winter and other students would attend Winter/Summer, but not Fall.  Most students, however, will be admitted to the traditional Fall/Winter track.
