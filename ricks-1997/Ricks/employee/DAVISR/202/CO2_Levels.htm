The percentage of Carbon Dioxide in the atmosphere is so small it's usually listed with the trace gasses. As you can
see, it makes up only one third of one percent of the air we breathe; it's so small it doesn't even show up on the graph.
So why all the fuss??
CO2 levels in our atmosphere are the lowest they have ever been, not the highest. One hundred million years
ago the levels were six times this high, and we had plants everywhere. Merely doubling today's levels will make plants
grow 15 times faster and use less water. And that's bad?
Let's enjoy some increases in levels. It's for our good.
