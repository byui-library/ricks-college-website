Course Description:

This 3 semester credit hour course is a study of concepts, strategies, tools and ethical issues involved in the use of the Internet. Focuses on design and construction of Home Pages including HTML. Includes Web Server management concepts and development of Internet and Intranet web sites and servers.

Materials:

  • Sklar, J. (2000). Principles of Web Design. Cambridge, MA.: Course Technologies.
  • Backup storage media (A zip disk is recommended or a CD-RW drive (at home)).

Assignments:

Each student in the course will:

Become knowledgeable about key design issues pertinent to web-based documents and demonstrate that knowledge by building websites.
Conduct a written design and operations review of two websites. A list of potential sites will be provided that the student can choose from but the review need not be limited to suggested sites. A total of 20 design errors and solutions must be identified per site as well as 5 elements of proper design and justification per site.
Build a personal website demonstrating the ability to implement design elements effectively.
With a team, develop a site for a hypothetical business: At Home Site.
(A retirement and assisted living facility.)
Develop a web policy that demonstrates an understanding of and application of  copyright law and privacy issues. This policy must be a part of the business web site.
Demonstrate a knowledge of basic issues of web server administration.

Engage in career-oriented readings to become more knowledgeable in the IT field:

  •    There's No Place Like Home
  •    Global Web Sites Prove Challenging
  •    The Evolving IT Worker
  •    Wanted: IT Workers of All Age

Class Policies

Attendance
Attendance is strongly encouraged. Materials, processes and techniques will be covered in class that may not be found in class resources. Therefore, it is to your benefit to attend consistently. Remember it is your responsibility to fulfill all assignments and other coursework, regardless of attendance, by the date and time specified. This policy also pertains to school excused absences. If you are gone for a school activity you must present your excused absence form 5 school days in advance. Your work will be due 5 school days after your return if your absence is more than 5 days, or 2 days after your return if the trip is less than 5 days.

Computer Ethics
The use of the school's network, systems and data impose responsibilities and obligations upon the students to use these computer components in an ethical manner and in accordance with the guidelines found in the Honor Code. Students are to access only data and files that are owned by them, or the instructor for their use has given explicit authorization. Copyrighted material must be used in accordance with copyright law and applicable license agreements followed. Attempting to circumvent or subvert system security measures or engaging in any activity that causes harm to systems or to any information stored thereon, such as creating or propagating viruses, disrupting services, or damaging files are considered inappropriate use. Violators are subject to disciplinary actions as prescribed by school policy and may also be prosecuted under applicable local, state, or federal civil or criminal law.

Computer Use
During class presentations you are to use only those applications being studied as part of the specified curriculum. E-mail, Internet and Instant Messaging use are prohibited unless being used for class assignments. Violators of this policy will be asked to close the application or leave class. Please do not disrupt class by using these other applications during class time.
