ONLINE CLASSES - Are they for you?

Where can I find a list of available classes? Page 7 in the Class Schedule.

Where can I get information about how I sign on, take tests, get going???  INFO PAGE

How hard? Just like a regular class -- you read the book, you take a test, you move on, BUT, if you're not the motivated type this class will be harder, not easier, because there's no one to push you. And since there's no class discussion it might be harder to understand or remember a point. Lots of people start with the best of intensions but not everyone finishes because they don't have the drive. That's the hard part of an online course.

How long will it take? Each course lasts 15 weeks (like a regular semester), but the 15 week window starts when you register, and runs on right through holidays, whatever. You can start at any time, not just at the start of a regular semester.

Can I go as fast as I want? Yes, yes, yes. One of the real advantages of an online class is that you don't have to wait for anyone. It's all at your own pace. If you're the self-motivated type and want to shorten your educational time then online classes can be a real asset.

How do I do classwork? All your assignments are done online; you will never set foot in a classroom. That's how students in Florida or Hong Kong can take a class from Ricks. Each class offered will have a syllabus which lists the assignments, texts, exams, etc. Texts can be purchased directly from the bookstore or ordered directly from Amazon.com. Some courses use just one text, some have several; some use a CD for music, some allow you to download audio and video files; it all varies with the class being taken.

How do I hand in my work? Most assignments will be submitted online. Papers, etc. can be emailed directly to the teacher as an attachment.

Exams and quizzes: Quizzes are taken anytime you want, anywhere you happen to be. But doesn't this mean you could cheat and just copy your answers right from your text; no one would be watching?? Sure does, but remember you still have to take your Midterm and Final in a proctored location, i.e., you have to taken them in front of someone. To take either exam you must notify the Testing Center here on campus of the date and time you wish to take your test. They will then "open" the exam during a two-hour window and it will appear on your normal class site. You must have made arrangements beforehand to take this exam in a "controlled" location - - at some university testing center, a bishop's home, etc.

Grades: Can I find out how I'm doing? Yes, grades as posted online as the term progresses. You can see how you've done on quizzes and exams.

Feedback: Does anyone know I'm out there? Yes, you will be in constant contact with your faculty member via email. Write him/her often, whenever you have a question. It's almost better than being in a classroom because you can ask questions one-on-one and get an answer.

Announcements: Is there some special location where all items pertaining to my course will be found? Yes, the main interface for online classes is Blackboard, a program which allows the teacher to post messages for the group or for specific individuals. You can also create study groups or chat areas.
