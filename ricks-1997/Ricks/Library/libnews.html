What's New at the McKay Library?

The primary purpose of this page is to keep faculty and staff informed as to new resources - electronic or otherwise - available at the David O. McKay Library. We frequently set up trial subscriptions to various online databases, and this is one way to make those easily accessible. We also hope to provide lists of recent additions (books, periodicals, videos, etc.) to the Library collection. We expect the information to change frequently, so stop in on a regular basis! And if you have any questions or suggestions, please let us know.
