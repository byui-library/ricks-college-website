The Do's Of The Word Of Wisdom

The Lord has given us a marvelous health and nutritional program in the Word of Wisdom Ashowing forth the order and will of God in the temporal salvation of all saints in the last days.   " This revelation was partly given as a warning to the saints Ain consequence of the evils and designs, which do and will exist in the hearts of conspiring men in the last days."

This reminds me of the television and print advertisements used to promote the purchase of cigarettes in the 1940's and 1950's. Advertising statements such as, "4 out of 5 doctors choose Camel" presented with a man attired in a white physician's coat, and "Phillip-Morris the cigarette that takes the fear our of smoking... offering you this record of safety. For your pleasure, For your protection, try a carton." Of course, this ad has a beautiful woman portraying the image of health and beauty.

Of course, alcoholic beverages continue to enjoy similar advertising portraying it as fun and the socially accepted thing to do with no mention of the horrible toll it can demand on the person, their family, and society.

We are familiar with these "Don'ts" of the Word of Wisdom, but how completely do we understand and follow the "Do's" of the Word of Wisdom? The dietary recommendations contained in that inspired revelation given to the prophet Joseph in 1833 provide excellent guidelines to help preserve our health, so we can better serve Him, our families and ourselves.

(Note: In a 1851 General Conference the guidelines to abstain from alcohol, tobacco, coffee and tea were adopted as commandment.)

  • Verses 5 and 6 tells us that we should not consume wine or strong drink, but rather "pure wine of the grape of the vine...". In the July 29th Faculty/Staff Bulletin you can read about some of the recent research findings of the heart healthy benefits of purple grape juice - the pure wine of the grape of the vine.
  • Verses 10 and 11 discuss how the Lord has placed herbs here "for the constitution, nature, and use of man ... to be used with prudence and thanksgiving". There is a growing number of physicians and medical researchers in this country and abroad who have turned to studying the medicinal and health promoting benefits of a wide variety of herbs - testing them in the same scientific models as the pharmacological agents. When proven safe and effective these herbs may offer health-promoting attributes in addition to those of vitamins and minerals.
  • Verses 11 and 16 mentions the importance of "every fruit in the season thereof" and the "fruit of the vine; that yieldeth fruit, whether in the ground or above the ground."
  • Verses 12 and 13 establish restrictions on the consumption of "flesh of the beasts and of the fowls of the air", that "they are to be used sparingly."
  • Verses 14 and 16 state that "All grain is ordained for the use of man... to be the staff of life..."

After years and years of research and millions of dollars spent the U.S. government developed and introduced the Food Guide Pyramid. This was designed to illustrate graphically the importance of and preference for grains (6-11 servings per day), vegetables (3-5 servings per day) and fruits (2-4 servings per day) compared to meat, dairy products, and other foods. These recommendations give scientific validation to the inspired direction the Lord has given us in the Word of Wisdom.. I also believe personally that contained in the promise of the Word of Wisdom is the recommendation to engage in exercise wherein it states that through obedience to these principles we "shall run and not be weary, and shall walk and not faint."

We can experience greater health and receive the promised blessings by also following the "Do's" of Word of Wisdom..
