Prerequisites: Generally, students planning to transfer to a four year school should have attained Sophomore status. No previous accounting courses are required to take this course.

Course Description: This course is a foundation course for students majoring in accounting, business management, and computer information systems. Topics range from the accounting cycle, merchandising, and inventories to owner's equity, and the amortization of bond premiums and discounts. For more specifics see the schedule on the next page. This is a rigorous course that transfers to most other universities.

Objectives:

(1) To develop a working vocabulary of accounting terminology.
(2) To learn and understand accounting practices and concepts and to apply these to specific problems and transactions in developing financial reports for sole proprietorships and corporations.
(3) To understand, use, and interpret financial statements.
(4) To provide the foundation for more advanced work in accounting and business related subjects.

Attendance - Attendance is required. There is no penalty for absences due to sickness or official school business if the teacher has been notified before class starts. Absences due to some personal circumstances may also be excused, but one would need to check with the teacher beforehand. One can do this by phoning extension 1485 and leaving a message. The message should indicate why you're absent, your name, and your phone number. Unexcused absences would be penalized 3 points. For being tardy 1 or 2 points would be subtracted from a students score. Leaving class early is considered the same as being tardy. Everyone will be allowed one free absence without any penalty.

Quizzes - All of the quizzes will be given in the Test Center. You cannot make up a quiz (even for sickness), however, your lowest quiz score will automatically be dropped. If a quiz is to be missed due to school business, it can be taken early (but never late). On Monday through Fridays the Test Center is open from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. with the last quiz given out at 9 pm. On Saturdays the Test Center is open from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. with the last quiz given out at 3 p.m.

Homework - Assignments are due at the beginning of class. One can receive half credit for homework that is late. Check with the teacher to see what assignments should be completed for late credit. If homework is late due to illness, full credit can still be earned. To receive credit for late homework (even that due to illness) you must bring it in to the instructor's office and check it yourself against the answer book. Tutors are available in the Accounting Lab.

Accounting Lab - Tutoring is available in Smith 383 on Mondays and Fridays from 4 to 6 p.m. On Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays the lab is open from 4 to 7 p.m. This is a great place to work on one's homework and obtain help if needed.

Annual Report Project - Two students will work together as a team. It will be graded on both English and content. It should be typed.

Exams - Exams will be given in the test center. There are no grade improvement projects available. If you have a bad exam you need to do better on the others to get a good grade. Any exam missed would need to be made up immediately and could be subject to a point penalty. Final Exam Schedule: The final exam is on Tuesday, December 14th.

Disability: Students with a disability may be entitled to special consideration. It is the student's responsibility to talk to the teacher about this matter during the first week of class.
