Coed-Soccer

1. We will follow and use National Federation rules.

2. Games will consist of 2 twenty-four minute halves with continuous running clock.  There will be a 5 minute half-time.

3. You may begin the game with 8 players, but 4 must be girls.  Players can play only on one team.

4. If the score is tied after time has expired, then we will have a shoot-out until someone wins.

5. No jewelry is allowed to be worn on the playing field, including baseball caps.

6. When slide-tackling, if you hit the person before the ball it is an immediate red card/ejection.
