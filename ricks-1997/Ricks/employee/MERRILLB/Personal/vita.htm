Non-career Interests and Activities

After family, which is my highest interest and priority, and church, my interests seem boundless. Mathematics, the natural and social sciences, art, music, history, literature–they are all immensely absorbing. Yet all of these one might consider career interests, along with philosophy. For it is my greatest pleasure to be a teacher of students, and the richer one’s understanding, the richer is one’s teaching. I also have the weekly pleasure of contributing to a renaissance music ensemble my meager skills on the alto recorder.
