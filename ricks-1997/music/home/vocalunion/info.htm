About Vocal Union...

     

    In only two years of existence, Vocal Union has become an ambassador of goodwill and quality performance.  This is an ensemble of mixed voices specializing in popular styles of vocal jazz, musical theatre, and "swing era" tunes. Vocal Union rehearses five hours a week over a three day period: M W from 2:00-3:50 and F from 2:00-2:50. On many occasions, Vocal Union combines with Sound Alliance Jazz Band and other ensembles in entertaining performances of contemporary music.

    Each year this ensemble participates in many on- and off-campus performances. Tours are planned for each year with extended travel on a biennial basis.

    Concurrent vocal instruction is recommended. Music majors should consult with their academic advisor or area coordinator before considering this ensemble. Vocal Union is directed by Kevin and Nori Brower of the BYU-Idaho Music Department.
