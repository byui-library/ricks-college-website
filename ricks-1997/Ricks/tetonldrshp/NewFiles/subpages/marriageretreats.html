Marriage Enrichment Retreats

Marriage enrichment retreats are designed specifically to assist newly wed students at Ricks College. The overnight experience permits couples to break from their hectic schedules and consider how they can enhance their relationship.
Experienced couples selected from Ricks College faculty present material that incorporates gospel principles and emphasizes the importance of righteous leadership in the home.
The retreats leave at 5:00 p.m. on Friday and return between 1:00 and 3:00 p.m. on Saturday. The cost is $3 and includes transportation and three meals. Housing in provided at the lodge and in additional cabins on the property.
Recently married couples can sign up for the retreats at the Institute’s office in the Manwaring Center, Room 380, starting two weeks before the scheduled date (on a first come-first served basis). One spouse must be a Ricks College student. Registration is limited to couples who have not previously participated.
