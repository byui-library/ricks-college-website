General Art 3-D
GENERAL ART-3D
CLASS CODE: ART 103 CREDITS:  2 FEE:  $25.00
DIVISION: PERFORMING AND VISUAL ARTS
DEPARTMENT: ART
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A studio experience exploring the principles and practices of sculpture in clay and a variety of other materials. Designed for non-art majors.
DESCRIPTION: Assignments may include a replica of an ancient ceramic piece, a response to a contemporary ceramic piece, construction of a notebook/sketchbook, wire and/or paper sculpture, a construction using found materials, a major conceptual piece involving any materials.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. To give non-art majors a studio art experience in sculpture.
2. To explore the ways in which ideas and purposes may be translated into three-dimensional form.
3. To expose students to a variety of materials and methods of work.
4. To acquaint students with principles of art and judgment.
REQUIREMENTS:
PREREQUISITES: Art Major status.
OTHER:
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2001
