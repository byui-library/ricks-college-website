The experience begins with a four day Church I-listorv tour of Independence, Mo., Liberty Jail, Far West Temple site, Nauvoo, and Carthage jail.

As we arrive in Chicago, there will be a three week experience working with Inner City, at risk youth. These are either members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints or are in some way connected with the Church. The age group of these youth are from 10 - 16. There is a program for both the young men and young ladies, The BYU-Idaho participants in the program will be involved in one-on-one experiences with the youth as well as leading them in small group structured experiences.

In addition, the BYU-Idaho student will be actively involved in many "big city" experiences: visit the University of Chicago; bus tour of inner city, business, and upperclass Chicago areas-,
