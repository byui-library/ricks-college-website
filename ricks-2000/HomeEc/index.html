Home and Family Education is . . . 

an integrative profession which provides a holistic approach to the study of home and family. Our mission is to assist individuals in their quest for perfection and eternal life, and to maintain the tradition that the home is the most effective institution for impacting upon the well being of the family unit. This department strives to impart philosophical knowledge, build practical skills, and enhance professional and occupational training in the subject matter areas that most impact the institution of home and family.
