Chapter 13 -- Volume Changes in Concrete

Chapter Review

  • Define volume change.
  • Why do cracks develop?
  • Concrete expands slightly as temperatures rise and contracts as temperatures fall. Concrete also expands slightly with a gain in moisture and contracts with a loss of moisture.
  • As drying takes place in concrete, the surface dries and shrinks faster than the inner concrete, causing tensile stress and possible cracks. Major random cracks may develop if joints are not properly provided. Refer to 9-2, page 114, for maximum joint spacing.
