Campus Services Tour

There is a vast array of offices and services available to assist and serve students.  You will want to familiarize yourself with the following services, their location, and function.  Services are listed in alphabetical order with hypertext links to their websites on the campus intranet.

ASBYU-I (MC 394, 356-2270) - ASBYU-I stands for Associated Students of Brigham Young University - Idaho.  ASBYU-I oversees the majority of student activities at the university.

Athletics (HRT 264, 356-2104) - Intercollegiate athletics at BYU-Idaho.   Admission is free for all students but tickets are required for most events.  Tickets can be obtained from the Ticket Office.

ATM's - Automated Teller Machines are located on the first floors of the Manwaring Center and Kimball Buildings.   Operated by Beehive Federal Credit Union, the ATM's are available to anyone who has an ATM card associated with the Plus System / Handibank / VISA / Cirrus Networks.   There is no fee charged for using these ATM's.

Bookstore (MC, 356-2211) - The BYU-Idaho Bookstore offers a wide selection of merchandise to meet your academic and personal needs.  Reserve textbooks online, check cashing, textbook buyback, and more.

Bowling (MC 140, 356-2221) - Open bowling is available for students with activity cards.  Campus groups may schedule the Bowling Center for private parties.

Campus Police (KIM 150, 356-2448) - PARKING PERMITS, campus safety, campus crime statistics, and more.

Career & Academic Advising Center (KIM 250, 356-1167) - Offer students assistance through academic advising and career exploration.

Cashier's Office (KIM 130, 356-1080) -  All payments for loans, registration, tuition and fees, and housing may be made at the Cashier's office.  In addition, personal checks may be cashed for up to $200.00 per student/day.  Student ID is required. 

Catalog - The BYU-Idaho catalog is the official publication listing all requirements for admission as well as detailed information on all class offerings and associate degrees available.

Center Stage Entertainment Series (c/o Ticket Office, MC 162, 356-2230) - Performing Arts cultural series at BYU-Idaho.  Tickets are required for these events and can be purchased through the BYU-I Ticket Office. 

Computer Lab (MCK 111, 356-2361) - Located in the McKay Library, the lab has approximately 150 IBM compatible computers and 16 Macintosh computers, all computers print to a Hewlett Packard laser printer.  There are also an additional 90 research stations available. 

Continuing Education (ASB 103, 356-1040) - The Division of Continuing Education is designed to serve your needs whether you are an on-campus or community student.  It offers special credit, and non-credit courses as well as special educational programs such as Know Your Religion, EFY, Education Week, and more.

Copy Spot Centers (MCK 223, 356-2382, and ASB 121, 356-2300) - Copy Spot Centers with full copy services are available at these two locations on campus.   

Counseling Center (KIM 270, 356-1100) - The Counseling Center helps students maintain balance in their lives by identifying and resolving emotional problems that interfere with effective spiritual, intellectual, social, or physical functioning.   Counseling services are free and complete confidentiality is maintained.

Dean of Students Office (KIM 230, 356-1120) - The Dean of Students Office assists students with questions regarding the Honor Code as well as handles all disciplinary procedures associated with Honor Code infractions.  This office also oversees international student advising, withdrawing from the university, and assisting with personal concerns.

Development & Alumni Relations (KIM 220, 356-1128) - The BYU-Idaho Development and Alumni Relations Office provide services for alumni, friends, businesses, parents, and students associated with BYU-I.

Disability Services (MCK 123, 356-1158) - The office of Services for Students with a Disability provides academic accommodations for all qualified students who have documented learning, emotional, and/or physical disabilities and have need for accommodation.

E-Mail Account - Access your student e-mail account.

E-Mail Addresses - Campus e-mail addresses and telephone numbers for campus.

Financial Aid (KIM 100, 356-1013) -  The Financial Aid office assists students in obtaining financial aid, mainly in terms of Federal Pell Grants, Stafford loans, and a limited number of other types of aid. 

Food Services (MC 246, 356-2251) - BYU-Idaho Food Services offers a wide variety of quality food at reasonable prices.

Game Center & Internet Room (MC 160, 356-2238) - The game room offers video games, poole, air hockey and foosball as well as internet computer connections. 

Health Center (CLK 120, 356-1300) - The Health Center provides health care assistance to BYU-Idaho Students.

Honors Program (TAY 240C, 356-1253) -

Housing Office (KIM 195, 356-1045) - The Housing Office provides service to both on and off-campus students by answering questions in regards to contracts, disputes, and the availability of housing.

ID Center (MC 162, 356-1012) - Student ID cards are issued at the ID Center.

Information Desk (MC 2nd Floor, 356-2200) - The Information Desk, located on the 2nd floor of the Manwaring Student Center provides information by phone and to walk-up patrons on:  campus activities and events, academic information, university ward and church information, and general BYU-Idaho information. 

International Student Services (KIM 226, 356-1120) The Dean of Students Office provides international student advising, including:   Immigration and Naturalization Service advising covering employment eligibility, financial aid, adjustment issues, transferring and more.

Intramurals (HRT 223, 356-2115) - The Intramural Office offers more than 30 sports including individual, team, and co-ed competition. 

Leadership & Service Institute (MC 380, 356-2018) - The Leadership & Service Institute provides three program services for students at BYU-Idaho:  (1) Training Retreats, (2) Volunteer Center, and (3) Marriage Enrichment.

Learning Assistance Labs (MCK 127, 356-1158) - The Learning Assistance Labs offers free assistance to students with the goal of mastering basic academic skills.  Services include:  Reading Lab, Writing Center, Math Learning Lab, Study Skills Lab, and Tutoring Center.

Library (MCK, 356-2351). The David O. McKay Library is available for study as well as for checkout of books, records, tapes, videos and any other necessary equipment for viewing or listening to any of these materials.  Computer resources are also located within the Library.

Lost & Found (MC 160, 356-2238) - Lost and found items can be claimed and/or turned into the Lost & Found Center.   Items will be held at least 60 days before being sold.

Manwaring Student Center (MC) - The Manwaring Center is the student activities enter on campus and houses a bowling center, game room, snack bars, movie theater, ticket office, ID center, bookstore, ballrooms, conference rooms, ASBYUI offices and other facilities. 

Married Student Association - (356-1120). The Married Student Association provides services and activities designed for married students.

Media Services (MCK 328, 356-1200) -

New Student Orientation (MC 250, 356-2393) - The New Student Orientation Office assists incoming students by providing programs and activities designed to orient students to campus and the many resources available to assist them in their studies while here at BYU-I.

Outdoor Equipment Rental (HRT 118B, 356-2100) - Rent equipment for outdoor activities. 

Parking Permits (see campus police).

Physical Education/Recreation

Planetarium (ROM 107, 356-2242) - The Planetarium sponsors weekly programs shown on Thursday nights at 7 p.m., and are changed monthly.  The cost is $1.00 for students and $1.25 for the public. 

Public Relations (KIM 226, 356-1150) - The Public Relations Department serves as a link between BYU-Idaho and the students, employees, outside community, and members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints around the world.

Student Handbook - You can access the 2000-2001 Student Handbook to look up the answer to any questions you may have regarding school policies, services, etc.   Scroll down on the Dean of Students' website to find the link to the book.

Rixida (MC 372, 356-2295) - Rixida is the BYU-Idaho yearbook, now published on CD-Rom.

Safety Office (PPLT 136, 356-2457) - The Safety Office oversees safety awareness for all campus employees and students.

Scheduling Office (MC 207, 356-2248) - Campus facilities scheduling for on-campus and off-campus use.

The Scroll (SPO 160, 356-2900) - The Scroll is the college newspaper. 

Stitchin' Post (CLK 390, 356-1340) - A student-based sewing service, assists students with clothes repair, hemming, etc.

Student Alumni Association - The Student Alumni Association is a great way to get involved at at BYU-Idaho.  

Student Employment / Career Placement Center (KIM 244, 356-2170) - The Student Employment and Career Placement Center assist students in locating both on-campus and off-campus positions.  Students or alumni who are looking for full-time careers after graduation or are looking for internships, cooperative work, or summer work related to their careers are also assisted through the Placement Center.

Student Transportation (c/o Public Relations KIM 226, 356-1150) - The public relations department offers information regarding shuttle services to and from the Salt Lake city and Idaho Falls airports.

Study Skills Lab (MCK 232, 356-1162) - Assistance is available to students in the following study skills:  concentration and memorization, note taking and listening, thinking skills, textbook study methods, time management, test taking, and studying chemistry. 

Telephone Directory Service/Telephone Services (PPLT 129, 356-2411) - The BYU-Idaho main campus number is 356-2011.   Directory information is 356-2411.  BYU-I Telephone office provides directory information for:  campus offices, faculty, administrators, staff, and currently enrolled students.

Testing Services (MCK 14B, 356-1206) - The Testing Center provides a centralized location for student examinations.

Ticket Office (MC 148, 356-2230) - Tickets for sporting and cultural events.

Travel Bulletin Board (MC 1st Floor) - Located across from the Game Room, the Travel Bulletin Board is used to list information for students who want a ride or who are willing to take riders to specific destinations.   Information must be written on the blue or pink "Ride Cards" only and posted at the Travel Board.  Cards are available at the Manwaring Center Information Desk.  There is also an online version.

Travel Study/Academic Travel  - Want to study in Europe for a summer, Russia, or experience the Mormon-American tour.  Check out the various student travel destinations available to BYU-Idaho students.

Tutoring Center - (MC 164, 356-1158) The Tutoring Center provides help for students beyond their regular classroom instruction. 

Women's Organization (MC 394, 356-2270) - Women at BYU-I are automatically members of the Associated Women Students (AWS).  AWS sponsors various activities, including Women's Week, held once a year. 

Writing Center (CLK 327, 356-1189) - The Writing Center is a free service for students who would like extra help with their writing skills.
