Summer in the Snow
Ricks College Entertainment Series 
Michael McLean
Thursday, June 7, 7:30 p.m. 
Hart Auditorium

Composer and singer Michael McLean will entertain during Education Week at Ricks College. He will be joined by singers Katie Thompson (vocalist on "Safe Harbors" and a favorite as Connie Lou in the national tour of the Forgotten Carols) and his son Jeff at the concert.

"We'll be doing some of the songs we performed together at the Women's Conference at BYU last year, some wonderful new songs, some old favorites and some brand new songs that I just finished writing," he says. "I believe so strongly that sometimes a song can tell us the truth the only way our hearts will hear it. I'm hoping that the program will give some hope, some insight, some inspiration and some entertainment for those that attend."

The concert is open to the public and you do not need to be registered for Education Week to attend this special evening of music. Michael has been entertaining audiences for more than two decades with his uplifting, contemporary music that touches the soul. His annual "Forgotten Carols" plays to sellout crowds at Christmastime. Come hear some of his old standbys, as well as some of his latest works. Cost is $8 and $6.
