World Literature
WORLD LIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 362 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Surveys contemporary and/or classical world literature with an emphasis on literary and cultural backgrounds.
(Recommended for English, Anthropology, Foreign Language, History, Humanities, and International Studies majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Surveys contemporary and/or classical world literature with an emphasis on literary and cultural backgrounds. (Recommended for English, Anthropology, Foreign Language, History, Humanities, and International Studies majors.
TOPICS: Literary study: To find meanings in human experience; understanding of peoples and ideas; introduce students to the broadest possible range of literature; influential literature from varied ethnicities, cultures, religions, historical periods, and philosophies; appreciation and enjoyment of great literature; reading, thinking, writing, and discussion skills.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Reading texts skillfully. (2) Recognizing distinctive characteristics among varied literature. (3) Analyzing, interpreting and evaluating literary qualities, philosophical ideas, and historical implications of texts. (4) Assessing the influence of literature on people and of context on literature. (5) Expressing ideas in thoughtful speech and writing.
REQUIREMENTS: Readings. Papers. Oral presentations. Research projects.
