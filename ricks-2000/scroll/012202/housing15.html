Crews maintain campus

by Alex Shafer Jr.
SHA01019@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Maintenance is a vital job for housing units, and it requires a large staff of many divisions.

On campus, maintenance is divided into three staffs: one for the Hyrum Manwaring Student Center, one that operates out of the Housing Office and one for the Physical Plant.

Maintenance for the campus and its grounds are paid for by the general fund, while the MC and Housing Office fund cleaning and some maintenance from their own budgets.

The crew run from the Housing Office is made up of three full-time employees and 20 students.

They respond to problems such as leaky toilets and problems with appliances. This division also includes the custodial crew, who cleans the men’s bathrooms three times a day and the public areas of the women’s dorms twice a day.

All calls are responded to within 24 hours or sooner for an emergency. Over each break, up to 60 students are hired to handle additional responsibilities like carpet cleaning and other cleaning that is not possible during the school year.

Whenever there is a more serious problem for on-campus housing, the Physical Plant is called. It is a large division which consists of three separate groups: central shops, which covers electrical, mechanical and heating problems; custodial, which is responsible for all academic buildings on campus; and support facilities, which takes care of the grounds as well as all campus vehicles.

The grounds crew is responsible for snow removal and begins at 4 in the morning each day it snows.

“We try to keep going as long as we can until 5,” Bob Michaels, head of the grounds crew, said.

The grounds crew is made up of Michaels, three full-time employees and students. Students start working at 4:30 a.m. and work 20 hours per week around classes.

Off campus, each housing unit is responsible for its own maintenance.

Melissa Wade, the owner of the Baronnessa women’s housing complex, has her husband take care of the maintenance.

Other housing units such as American Manor and Arcadia are run by the same owner and are taken care of by a single crew.
