Officials seize $250,000 in narcotics

ST.ANTHONY, Idaho (AP) — Officers are calling a drug bust this week the biggest in Fremont County history.

Officers seized three pounds of methamphetamine and one-half pound of cocaine Wednesday night from a residence within four blocks of the Fremont County Courthouse.

Officers arrested one man and have a warrant out on another suspect, Sheriff Tom Stegelmeier said.

The suspect in custody has been charged, Stegelmeier said, but the name has not be released in order to give officials additional time for the investigation.

Stegelmeier estimates the street value of the drugs seized to be about $250,000.

The bust is part of a drug investigation in Fremont County that has been going on for the past year, Stegelmeier said.

“I know the detectives feel pretty good about this one,” Stegelmeier said. “The drug task force put in a lot of hours on this one. We were pretty ecstatic last night — with good reason.”

The Fremont County Sheriff’s Office and the St. Anthony Police Department cooperated in the investigation, along with officers and deputies from Madison, Teton and Bonneville Counties and the Rexburg Police Department.

“There’s just no way we could do it by ourselves,” Stegelmeier said. “We need the cooperation of all these counties and cities.”
