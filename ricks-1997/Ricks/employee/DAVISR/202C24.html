to present the symbol rather than the thing

NOTES ON ALSO SPRACH: (1896)

He was not trying to set Nietzsche's philosophy to music.

"I meant to convey the development of the human race from its origin, through the various phases of its evolution, religious and scientific, up to Neitzsche's idea of the superman."

Zarathustra was a philosopher who left the world and lived by himself. Years later he decided he needed to enlighten Man, so he went down from his mountain and spent time with the common people. His journey is listed in the movements of the tone poem:

The inhabitants of the unseen world

Great longing

Joys and Passion

The Grave Song

The Convalescent--the Waltz

The Night-Wanderer's Song

When it ends he has taught Man enough that Man can learn for himself, and Zarathustra returns to the mountains. The final chords in CM are Nature, and Bm, Man. When the piece starts, Nature is strongest, but as it ends Man has learned enough that He now speaks on equal footing with Nature. He dominates, but Nature is still hovering in the background.
