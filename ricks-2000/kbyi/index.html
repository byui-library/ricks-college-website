KBYI Radio - FM 100.5

KBYI is owned and operated by Brigham Young University - Idaho and broadcasts at a power of 100,000 watts to Southeastern Idaho and parts of Wyoming and Montana. Programming sources include National Public Radio, Public Radio International, and locally produced news and public affairs programming. Devotionals from Brigham Young University - Idaho are aired live at 2:00 PM on Tuesday and repeated that evening at 9:00. Viking Football and Basketball games are also aired live. Classical music, and jazz and folk music can be heard along with news and information 24 hours each day.
