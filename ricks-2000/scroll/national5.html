Eye witness in New York City

by Carol Salomonson
 New York City resident


I went out for a walk with Ilana to get food. The air smells a little funny to me and made my head hurt. Someone from the ward warned me to go to a hospital if I start to get nauseated or sick.

Pretty much everything is closed. The stores, the restaurants, everything. Probably one out of five restaurants are open, and even then, they're short staffed, or don't have everything on the menu. I'm fine though, I ate well today.

I don't know if there will be school tomorrow. All the public schools are closed, the catholic ones, and NYU. But that's all I know.

I saw my Boss out my window earlier, and he was mad that I hadn't opened the store, but then I convinced him that America was under attack and NO business was open - he hadn't heard anything about it at all until 3pm when I told him! He has this minimal apartment with no phone, no TV, no radio, nothing. So it took an hour to convince him America was under attack. Even now, I don't think he gets it.

It's so quiet outside, I saw people crying. I've heard stories indirectly from people that were down there. A guy I know saw the first plane hit - he's been throwing up and in shock all day. My friendsÕ sistersÕ friend was in the building on the 34th floor and was able to get out. Everyone is running from dust clouds in panic. Everyone's sad outside. Most bars are open - imagine that.
