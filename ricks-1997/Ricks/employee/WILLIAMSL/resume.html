Lane D. Williams

EDUCATION

Granted the Kiplinger Fellowship, given to only eight journalists each year. I taught beginning classes in graphic communication, did a major reporting project on drug development in the United States and completed a major research project analyzing more than 300 American newspapers on the World Wide Web. For three years, I designed the Kiplinger Program report.

--Brigham Young University, Provo, UT - B.A.. in Journalism, 1989
Graduated cum laude as BYU's top journalism student. Received a four-year academic scholarship and two journalism scholarships. Served as editor and news editor of the campus newspaper. I worked in college at The Spanish Fork Press, a weekly newspaper in Utah.

--NICAR, Columbia, MO - Completed Missouri's NICAR Boot Camp, 1999.
I have subsequently completed a significant project on useful Web sites for journalists for this project.

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
