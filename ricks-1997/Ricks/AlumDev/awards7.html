Distinguished Emeritus Service Award

Edna Stowell Taylor of Rexburg, Idaho

Taylor attended Ricks from 1931 to 1933 majoring in education.  She is the granddaughter of the school's first principal, Jacob Spori.

 

Bill and Verna Egbert of Ogden, Utah

Bill, who retired in 1933 as an obstetrician and gynecologist practicing in the Ogden area, attended Ricks in 1946-47 and again in 1949-50 following a mission to the Southern States Mission.  He was raised in Lincoln, near Idaho Falls, and married Verna McDonald, a native of Ammon, Idaho.
