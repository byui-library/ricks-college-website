Linear Regression on the TI-85
  1. Press the STAT button.
  2. Choose the EDIT menu item (F1).
  3. Choose the lists to edit:  xStat and yStat work great.  Just press return twice to select these.
  4. If the lists are not empty, press the CLRxy menu option (F5).
  5. Enter your data.  Note that you enter the first x-value then the corresponding y-value.  You then enter the next x-value and its corresponding y-value.   Continue on until you have entered the entire data set.
  6. Check your data!  Remember, GIGO.  A calculator always does what it is told.  You (or the calculator programmer) get to claim any mistakes.
  7. Now select the CALC menu option (2nd F1).
  8. Press ENTER twice to select the lists xStat and yStat.
  9. Now choose the LINR menu option (F2) for linear regression.
  10. The values of a and b in the equation y=a+bx are given.  Also given is corr, the correlation coefficient, and n, the number of data points.
