GENERAL STUDIES

Students who have not selected a major may graduate with a general studies degree. Although students must graduate from one of the academic divisions, the requirements are the same for each of the nine divisions. The nine divisions and their codes are as follows:

Agriculture 281
Engineering & Technology 282
Performing & Fine Arts 283
Humanities 284
Natural Science 285
Religious & Family Living 286
Behavioral & Social Sciences 287
Business 294
Education 295

Students may also graduate with a General Studies degree in the following four departments:
General Studies-Advised in Education 300 General Studies-Advised in Pre Interior Design 268
General Studies-Advised in Pre Nursing 115
General Studies-Advised in Pre Physical Therapy 320

This degree allows students to explore the curriculum and test many different interests while at the same time getting their Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree.

The following courses are offered by different departments across campus. These courses can serve as an additional resource as you explore your interests and ultimately choose a major
