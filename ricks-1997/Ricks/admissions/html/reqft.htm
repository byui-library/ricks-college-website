FAST TRACK REQUIREMENTS

In order to participate in the Fast Track program, some preparation is required.  Following is a list of what you should be working on to prepare to enroll in Fast Track. 

1.  Earn College Credit While In High School.

College credit can be earned before high school graduation in several different ways, including: 

  • Attending Ricks summer school between your junior and senior year of high school.
  • Enroll in BYU or Ricks correspondence classes.
  • Earn concurrent credit from your local college.
  • Pass an Advanced Placement (AP) exam with a score of three or higher. 

2.  Select a Fast Track Major

Students interested in any one of our 51 Fast Track majors can participate in the Fast Track program.  Not all majors offered by Ricks College qualify for the program.  Please review the Fast Track Majors list to see if the major in which you are interested is available for the program. 

3.  Contact your Fast Track Advisor

After you are accepted to Ricks as a full-time, regular student and have arrived on campus, you will need to meet with your Fast Track Advisor.   Your advisor will assist you in outlining your schedule from your first day of classes to graduation.  He or she will also provide you with a Fast Track contract.   You may, however, contact your Fast Track advisor to assist you in planning your schedule prior to arriving on campus.

Please note that no special application is required to participate in Fast Track.  Your Fast Track Advisor will provide with the necessary paper work.   

4.  Sign and Turn In Your Fast Track Contract

Once you have outlined your schedule for one full calendar year with your advisor, simply sign your contract and turn it in to the Admissions Office located in suite 120 in the Kimball Building. 

5.  Collect One-Half Tuition Scholarship for any summer terms attended. 

As both an incentive and reward for participation in Fast Track, all participants will receive a one-half tuition scholarship for any summer terms attended.  This scholarship is in addition to any other scholarship for which you might qualify, up to full tuition. 

6.  Attend Ricks for One Consecutive Year

A typical Fast Track Student will start school at Ricks as a full-time student the summer following high school graduation.  He or she would attend one or two summer terms, Fall Semester, Winter Semester, and up to three Summer Terms the following summer.
