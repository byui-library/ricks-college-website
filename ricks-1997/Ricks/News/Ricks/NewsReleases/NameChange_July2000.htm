Ricks College News Release
July 12, 2000

Ricks College hasn’t changed
its name to BYU-Idaho yet

REXBURG, Idaho — Ricks College hasn’t changed its name to Brigham Young University-Idaho yet.

The exact timing of the name change is unsure, but may happen sometime next year. The name change depends on the institution receiving “candidacy” status to be considered for accreditation as a four-year institution from the Northwest Accreditation Commission.

Ricks officials are in the initial stages of preparing a prospectus for the accreditation commission informing it of the college’s specific plans to become a four-year institution. After the prospectus is filed, the Northwest Commission will respond to the college’s request for candidacy as a four-year institution.

"Only at the point of being accepted as a candidate will it be appropriate for us to begin using the name of Brigham Young University-Idaho," Ricks President David A. Bednar says.

He stresses that four-year programs will not be added for the 2000-2001 academic year. The college is hopeful that the junior year of the first four-year programs will be phased in beginning in the fall semester of 2001.

The First Presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the Board of Trustees of Ricks College announced that Ricks College will change from its present two-year junior college status to a four-year institution June 21.

In making the announcement, LDS Church President Gordon B. Hinckley said the new name of BYU-Idaho would give the school immediate national and international recognition.

The new four-year institution will operate on an expanded year-round basis, incorporating innovative calendaring and scheduling while also taking advantage of advancements in technology that will enable the four-year institution to serve more students.

Information about the transition is posted on the Ricks College homepage at www.ricks.edu. This Internet site will be continually updated as new information becomes available.
