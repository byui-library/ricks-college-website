Course Objective/Description: Introductory to diesel repair is a class where students will learn how diesel engines and fuel systems operate. Engine mechanical and fuel system repairs will be performed. However, engine four cycle theory, diesel fuel and fuel systems will be the focus of this class.

Current diesel applications on today’s light pick-ups will be the emphasis. This class will be more of a familiarization type of course as compared to any other class in the automotive department. It is not intended that this class be a complete, in depth diesel repair course.

                    -Industrial diesels will not be worked on due to lack of room and tools that relate to them.
                    -There will be many demonstrations and visitors that will compliment class instruction.
                    -When live work is arranged for, the student will be expected to cooperate with the
                        customer and finish the job.
