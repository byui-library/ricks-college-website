Application # 1

         

 

 

Problem: 

A Texas-based oil company, Harken Energy Corporation, wants to drill off Costa Rica’s Caribbean Coast against the wishes of environmentalists, tourists, and civic groups who oppose the plan.  They argue that all of the factors have not been considered, especially the economic impact and the damage that will occur to the marine ecosystems along the coast should an oil spill occur.  The environmentalists are particularly concerned about the impact on the three endangered species of sea turtles that migrate through the area. 

Theoretical Perspective: 

Looking at the problem form the functionalist perspective, the Caribbean Coast is an ecosystem that exists in a delicate balance.  An oil spill could have a very detrimental effect upon the fragile relationship.  It would be dangerous for the endangered species of turtles that use the coast as a migratory path.  It would not only be harmful to the plant and animal life, it would cause dysfunction in the Costa Rican economy.  There would be a devastating impact on tourism, which is an important source of income for the country. 

Possible Solution: 

Since there is such a demand for oil in today’s world, a possible solution would involve letting the company drill with the stipulation that they would clean up a possible oil spill and provide appropriate compensation for declining Costa Rican tourism.  Hopefully this solution would provide effective protection for the environment and the economy of Costa Rica.   

Moral Perspective:

In the scriptures we are commanded to be good stewards over what we have been given on this earth.  Doctrine and Covenants 104:13 it states:  “For it is expedient that I, the Lord, should make every man accountable, as a steward over earthly blessings, which I have made and prepared for my creatures.”  We need to make sure that we use the resources we been have been given in the right way.  In Doctrine and Covenants 82:3 it also explains, “For of him unto whom much is given, much is required.”  We should do our best to conserve our resources and try to maintain the fragile balance of ecosystems around us.
