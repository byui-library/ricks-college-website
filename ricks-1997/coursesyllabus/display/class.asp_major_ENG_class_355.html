Children's Literature
CHILDREN'S LIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 355 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Focuses on comprehension, exposure to, and evaluation of children's books past and present to meet children's needs at school and at home. (Required for Elementary Education majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Focuses on comprehension, exposure to, and evaluation of children's books past and present to meet children's needs at school and at home. (Required for Elementary Education majors.)
TOPICS: Various genres of children's literature; evaluating children's literature; censorship; writing about children's literature; book presentations.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Introducing students to various genres of children's literature including, but not limited to, folktales, historical fiction, contemporary realistic fiction, non-fiction, fantasy, and picture books. (2) Understanding the value of children's literature. (3) Exposing students to key texts in the evolution of children's literature. (4) Evaluating books using accepted criteria for each genre of children's literature. (5) Writing about children's books for
REQUIREMENTS: Books in common as dictated by the teacher; 3,000 words of edited and unedited prose.
