TECHNIQUES OF TEACHING DANCE
DA. 256 Fall of 99, Bro. Oliphant
Kirkham Dance Studio 2:00 PM MWF

Prerequisites: DA 180, 170 Text: Dance A While, Harris, Pittman & Waller
Description: This course is designed to give you ideas, insight and training in the technique's of teaching dance with an emphasis on a uplifting, wholesome, and social perspective. We will work with all kinds of recreational dance, including ballroom, folk, square, fun, etc.
Objectives: To finish the class with the understanding and skill needed to teach social dance to ward and community. To know why and how Satan is using this beautiful medium and how to combat this influence.
PRACTICAL GOALS TO BE REACHED DURING SEMESTER:
Teach Basics of Dance to At Least Two Different Types of People.
Know And Be Able To Teach 10 Folk And Fun Dances.
Know And Be Able To Call And Teach 12 To 18 Of The Square Dance Basics.
Know And Be Able To Call Ahead All The Popular Ballroom Dance Rhythms.
Know And Be Able To Use The Basic Technique's Of Teaching, Such As Calling Ahead, Use Of Voice, Where To Stand, Unit System, Etc.
