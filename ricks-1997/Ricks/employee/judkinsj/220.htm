  • Course Description: This course is about relationships. The text for this course is full of theories and research that will help us understand how we relate to those around us through communication. We will also draw heavily upon other sources to aid in our learning. The purpose of this course is not only to explore information, but to apply it to relationships. As true communication principles are learned and applied, they become an integrated part of the heart mind and soul. The heart’s capacity to love will increase; the mind’s capacity to reason will increase; and the soul’s capacity to receive inspiration will increase.   The course objective, then, is to expand the heart mind and soul in order to maximize interpersonal communication competence.  In order for this course to positively impact lives, we will need to internalize it through thoughtful reading, writing, listening, verbalizing, practicing, and living. Communication competence is not something with which we’re either born or not born. Rather, it is skill that can be learned and developed line upon line throughout a lifetime. It comes from awareness, understanding, caring, active & critical thinking, and consistent practice.
