Guidelines for Selecting Exemplary Employees

The following criteria should be kept in mind as exemplary employee nominations are made.

1. Exemplifies the guiding principle "Every person at BYU-Idaho is a teacher." President Bednar has taught us, "Each interaction with a student or colleague is a potential teaching and learning opportunity."

2. General Attitude.

A. Takes interest and pride in their own specific assignments and BYU-Idaho as a whole.

B. Is cooperative, courteous, friendly, and demonstrates a concern and helpfulness to other employees.

C. Always presents a neat personal appearance.

3. Job Performance.

A. Demonstrates a high degree of skill and proficiency in carrying out assigned tasks.

B. Strives to improve skills and work methods as a means toward greater productivity and efficiency.

C. Is a willing worker, accurate, demonstrates initiative, and does not require constant supervision.

4. "Extra Mile" Activities.

A. Has performed specific and unusual activities worthy of recognition at BYU-Idaho.

B. Volunteers time and expertise to other persons and organizations in the community.


Individuals to be considered for exemplary employee awards may be nominated by the following procedure:

1. Each Fall and Spring, one administrator and two staff employees will be chosen to receive exemplary employee awards.

2. Immediate supervisors or fellow employees may nominate individuals to receive the exemplary employee awards by submitting a memo to their division or department head to forward to the respective vice-president naming the employee(s) and reasons for recommending this award.

3. The President's Council will meet to consider the nominations and select the employees who will be honored.

4. The President will send a letter to the employees inviting them to a devotional luncheon where the employee will receive a plaque and a cash award in recognition of their service to the university and the community. They will also be invited to attend the devotional where they will also be recognized.
