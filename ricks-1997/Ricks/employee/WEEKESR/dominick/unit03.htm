Consolidation:
The Big Radio Groups (See text)

Prior to the Telecommunications Act of 1996, radio group owners were only allowed to own 7 FM and 7 AM stations.

The number of stations a group owns now depends on the size of the radio market.

Telecommunications Act of 1996
Cities with more than 44 stations: can own up to eight stations (no more than five AM or FM).
Cities with 30 to 44 stations: can own up to seven stations (no more than four AM or FM).
Cities with 15 to 30 stations: can own up to six stations.
Cities with up to 15 stations: can own up to five stations (no more than three AM or FM).
