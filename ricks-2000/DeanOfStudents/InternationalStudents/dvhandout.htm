Diversity Immigrant Visa Program
(Green Card Lottery)


Each year, the U.S. Diversity Visa program makes available 55,000 permanent residence visas to persons meeting the eligibility requirements. Applicants for Diversity Visas are initially chosen through a random computer-generated lottery drawing. Visas are distributed among six geographic regions with a greater number of visas going to regions with lower rates of immigration, and no visas going to countries sending more than 50,000 immigrants to the U.S. in the past five years. No one country can receive more than seven percent of the available diversity visas in any one year. More information about the next Diversity Immigration Visa Program may be found at the U.S. Department of State website:  http://www.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2001/index.cfm?docid=4382
