GRIEVANCE POLICY

A student or their representative may petition a decision of the SSD Office by submitting a written statement outlining their situation and the reasons that their position should be considered. The first level of petition should be submitted directly to the Director of Services for Students with a Disability and/or to the immediate supervisor, the Director of the Learning Assistance Labs.
