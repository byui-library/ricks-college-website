Ricks College News Release, September 19, 1997

Enrollment climbs to record 8,277

REXBURG, Idaho—Ricks College experienced its first real growth in several years as its doors were opened to more students.
In an effort to try to accommodate more applicants, Ricks admitted about 250 more students this year.
The official enrollment for fall semester stands at 8,277 students, which is the highest on record at the largest private junior college in the country.
Jim Gee, assistant academic vice president for Support Services, is pleased at the count, saying about 12 more faculty members were hired during the summer to handle the increase in students. The increase also was made possible by more efficient use of classroom space during afternoon and evening hours.
Fall semester enrollment statistics show 2,528 new students. The remainder are either continuing from last year or returning after an absence, such as returned missionaries who are re-entering school.
Students this semester are registered for an average of 15 credit hours, which shows the students are traditional full-time students.
A breakdown in statistics for fall semester shows 2,840 from Idaho, 1,186 from Utah, 774 from California, 630 from Washington and 351 from Oregon.
Fifty-seven percent of the students are women (4,682). There are 2,215 returned LDS Church missionaries (27 percent of the student body), 232 foreign students from 40 countries and 546 married students. Sixty-two percent of the students are either 18 or 19 years old.
