Conjunctions
A conjunction is a word that connects one sentence element to another.
 
Coordinating
A coordinating conjunction joins sentence elements that are EQUAL.  A coordinating conjunction can join adverbs, adjectives, nouns, verbs, phrases, and clauses.  They include: for, and, nor, but or, yet, so.   (FAN BOYS)
 
Subordinating
A subordinating conjunction joins sentence elements that are UNEQUAL.  These conjunctions include words like:  although, if, since, while, when, after, because, and as.
