Beginning French I
BEG SEM FRENCH
CLASS CODE: FR 101 CREDITS:  4
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Acquisition of basic language skills, listening and speaking first, followed by reading and writing.
DESCRIPTION: Speaking and listening skills, leading to class discussion entirely in French by end of semester, reading progressively more difficult texts and writing a relatively error free one page poem, essay or story. May not be repeated for credit.
TOPICS: French Language
OBJECTIVES: At semester's end students should be able to speak at novice-high level, pass a test on grammar concepts in first six chapters of the class manual, demonstrate understanding of basic written texts and write a one page paper in French.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, textbook, workbook, CD listening exercises, quizzes and exams.
PREREQUISITES: This course is for beginners, students with less than a year and a half of high school French.
