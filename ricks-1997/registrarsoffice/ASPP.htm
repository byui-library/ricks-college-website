All BYU-Idaho students have the privilege to appeal any BYU-Idaho academic policy through one of two petition forms. Each form has its own purpose. Please read the descriptions provided on each of the forms so you will know which is appropriate for your particular situation. Each petition form goes before a committee who reviews all applicable information and makes an approve or deny decision regarding the request. The student is notified of the decision through a letter sent by the Registrar's Office.
















*Academic Standards Committee Petition

The Academic Standards Committee Petition is a petition for special consideration of the following policy reasons:
•     Current Semester/Term GPA is less than 1.0
•     Academic Suspension
•     Percent Credit Earned is Below 75%
•     Over 80 Credits Earned
This form may be obtained by clicking the hyperlinked text above. Remember to submit the form to the Registrar's Office by fax (208-496-1035), or mail to our address at:

Registrar's Office
190 Kimball
Rexburg, ID 83460-1640

The Petition for Exception to College Policy form can be obtained through the Registrar's Office.

This three part form is provided by the Registrar's Office, to students who have special circumstances that include but are not limited to:

•     Withdrawing from school or dropping a class after published deadlines for
        non-academic emergencies such as:
                                        •     serious injury or illness
                                        •     death in the family
                                        •     under some circumstances, employment

This form is essentially used for non-academic circumstances that are or were outside the student's control. This form requires that the student, his/her advisor, and the instructor of the class all submit comments for the committee to take into consideration. If the petition form is needed because of a medical type situation, a letter from the doctor is required. If approval is granted, there is a $5.00 fee which is charged to your student account.

Please refer any questions to the Petition Clerk at (208) 496-1009.
