Admissions Deadlines

Admission consideration will be given on the basis of all application materials being received before the deadline.

Applications will not be accepted more than one year prior to the requested semester or term.

All applicants who meet the deadlines below are assured admission consideration based upon space availability.

Fall Semester February 15
Fall Block August 1
Winter Semester October 1
Winter Block December 1
First Summer Term (May) February 15
Second Summer Term (June) February 15
Third Summer Term (July) February 15
