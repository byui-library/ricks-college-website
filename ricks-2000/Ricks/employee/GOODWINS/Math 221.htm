Prerequisite:

 

Successful completion of Math 110 or equivalent.

 

Overview:

 

An exploration of uses and abuses of both descriptive and inferential statistics.  Coverage includes frequency distributions, measures of central tendency and dispersion, elementary probability, central limit theorem, regression and correlation, hypothesis testing and contingency tables.  4.0 credit students will complete a small research project and take a 10 question final exam over additional topics involving one-way ANOVA, two mean / two proportion hypothesis testing and control charts.

 

Materials Needed:

 

Textbook:          Elementary Statistics, Mario F. Triola, Addison-Wesley Longman Publishing, © 2001  

Calculator:         TI-86 will be the instructional calculator of choice.  If you do not have a TI-86 or TI-83 you will be at a serious

disadvantage since we will be using statistical software from the calculator extensively.

How to optimize your Math 221 experience:
