BROADCAST ANNOUNCING
BRDCAST ANNOUN
CLASS CODE: COMM 268 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: COMMUNICATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Introduces students to announcing techniques, including news and commercial reading, voice diction and oral interpretation and provides overview of radio and television station operations. This class is needed to qualify for production or tape-to-on-air work for KBYI, KBYR or the BYU-I television station.
DESCRIPTION: Introduces students to announcing techniques, including news and commercial reading, voice diction and oral interpretation and provides overview of radio and television station operations. This class is needed to qualify for production or tape-to-on-air work for KBYI, KBYR or the BYU-I television station.
TOPICS: Commercials and pronunciation readings, recorded readings, news stories, sports and music shows.
OBJECTIVES: Teach students the basics of radio broadcasting with the opportunity to have assignments that will teach and require the use of production facilities.
