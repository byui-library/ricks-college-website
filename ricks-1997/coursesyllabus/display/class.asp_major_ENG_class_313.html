Advanced Writing for Elementary Education Majors
ADV WRIT ELEM
CLASS CODE: ENG 313 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - English requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes writing proficiency by focusing on the writing process, collaborative writing, and techniques for teaching writing. (Required for Elementary Education majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes writing proficiency by focusing on the writing process, collaborative writing, and techniques for teaching writing. (Required for Elementary Education majors.)
TOPICS: Effective writing processes; rhetorical modes; academic research writing; writing to get a job; strategies for teaching writing to elementary-age students; writing teacher reports.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Improving future teachers' writing proficiency by focusing on the writing process, collaborative writing, researching, and techniques for teaching writing. (2) Acquiring the skills needed to write effective reports, examinations, term papers, and theses in school.
REQUIREMENTS: Academic Research Project: This project includes a research paper proposal, an annotated bibliography, a research paper, a critique of a research paper, and an oral report.

Teacher Reports: a resume and letter of application, four assorted letters illustrating different rhetorical situations, a student observation report, a textbook evaluation, and two personal essays.

Language Arts Strategies: language arts workshops presented in collaboration with a group, and five original ideas that a teacher with little preparation time could take into a classroom.
PREREQUISITES: Elementary Education major, English 111 or 111C, and at least 22 credit hours or sophomore standing.
