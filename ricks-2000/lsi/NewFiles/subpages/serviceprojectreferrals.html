Service Project Referrals

Need a service project? The Institute maintains a list of service projects in the community. Visit our office in Manwaring Center room #380 to see a notebook full of postings. Each referral briefly describes the project and who can be contacted to set-up the service project.

Many of these projects are perfect for church groups or clubs that are looking for a one-time event. Other referrals are helpful for individual students looking for other ways to volunteer.

While BYU-Idaho encourages its students to perform community service, it does not endorse or investigate these service projects. Rather, it is up to the student to determine for himself or herself as to whether or not to volunteer. As a result, BYU-Idaho does not take responsibility for the acts or omissions of either the service providers or of the student volunteers.
