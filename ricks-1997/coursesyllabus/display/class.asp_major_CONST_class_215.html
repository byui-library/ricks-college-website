Construction Estimating
CONST ESTIMATE
CLASS CODE: CONST 215 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: ARCHITECTURE & CONSTRUCTION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This course will focus on interpreting plans and specifications to determine material quantities, labor, overhead and profit on residential and commercial construction projects. Additional topics will include organizing estimates, developing material and labor databases, and computerized estimates.
DESCRIPTION: Interpreting building plans and specifications; estimating material quantities, labor cost, overhead, and profit on residential and commercial structures; bidding procedures.
TOPICS: To help students in interpreting plans and specifications; estimating material quantities, labor, cost, overhead, and profit on residential and commercial structures; bidding procedures, competitive bidding.
OBJECTIVES: The ability to prepare quick and accurate material and labor estimates in one of the most important skills of a successful construction manager. The focus of this course will be on helping students to develop the ability to prepare accurate materials and labor estimate for construction projects. The student will have the opportunity of using building plans and specifications to prepare material and labor quantity takeoffs. They will use those quantity takeoffs to prepare computerized cost estimates. The course will begin with basic residential estimating and then advance into commercial structures.

This class is designed to assist those interested in the construction industry as a profession to gain knowledge and experience in construction estimating. It is an exciting subject and one which takes a great deal of practice in order to become proficient. The consequences of poor estimating can be catastrophic.

Estimating is the most basic of all management functions in the construction industry. Up to this point, most of your classes in the Construction Management Program have dealt with the technology of building and basic business management . We now begin to apply all that you have learned thus far to the management of construction projects and companies.
REQUIREMENTS: The students will be expected to complete a number of practical estimating assignments and activities. Due dates for assignment will be announced in class. Pop Quizzes may be given on the assigned reading material. Pop quizzes cannot be made up and must be taken on the day they are administered unless the student has prearrange their absence with the instructor.

Examinations: A number a practical examination will be given on reading and in-class material. The examinations will be scheduled in the testing center and the student should plan on taking the test by the date scheduled. An examination schedule is provided with the assignment sheet, generally student can take the exam up to on week before the expiration date.

Grading: The following represents an approximate point scale for the final grade in this course.

Exams 200 pts
Estimate Assignments 500pts
Commercial Estimate 100 pts
Total 800 pts
PREREQUISITES: Arch 110, Const 120, Const 220, IS 140
OTHER: A number of computer lab estimating assignments will be given. Some time in class will be given to work on the assignments, however, students will need to schedule time in the computer lab in order to complete these assignments.
