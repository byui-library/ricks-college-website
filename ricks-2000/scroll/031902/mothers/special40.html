Sheila’s mom always takes time to be there

by Marie MacKay
MAC00005@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

That is one main support for guard Sheila Hutchison, a sophomore from Blackfoot, Idaho and member of the Ricks College basketball team is her mother, Bonnie Hutchison, Sheila said.

“She is always there for me,” Sheila said.

Since Sheila was in third grade, she has participated in sports. She remembers when she participated in the annual Hoop Shoot, a free-throw contest for young children, and her mom was there the entire time.

“I remember [my mom] took me there and just sat and watched me and cheered me on,” Sheila said.

Sheila ended up winning the contest for Burley, Idaho, making the most out of 25 free throws in the shortest amount of time.

In high school Sheila participated in volleyball and softball. She is currently on Ricks’ rodeo team aside from being on the women’s basketball team.

Because Bonnie lives in Blackfoot, she is not able to see Sheila all the time, so she calls her daughter on the phone and comes to all of the Ricks College women’s basketball games.

“She is very supportive. She always comes to my games and cheers me on,” Sheila said.

Even in the hardest times of Sheila’s athletic career, her mother has always been there for her.

Last year during a Ricks basketball game, Sheila dove for the ball, and her face hit the floor. She shattered her cheek bone and two bones in her eye socket.

“[My mom] was there for me through it all, and I guess you could say she babied me,” Sheila said.

With three boys and one girl, Bonnie has a busy life, but she is still very positive throughout all of it.

“[I admire] how happy she is all the time. Even if she isn’t happy, she doesn’t show it,” Sheila said. “I love her.”

Before every game, Bonnie wishes Sheila good luck.

With such support from her mother and after winning the Region 18 title Saturday in at Dixie College, Sheila plans to take on the next step in her athletic career and head to the national tournament in Kansas this week.
