Textbook Returns:
A Textbook Refund Policy statement is attached to every sales receipt when purchasing textbooks. We highly recommend that you read the refund policy each term and take note of the deadline dates that are listed. In order to deal with all students on a fair and equal basis we hold very closely to this policy.

The following statements reflect the refund policy:.

  1. A full refund is given only when a cash register receipt is presented. If a refund is authorized without a receipt, a penalty is charged for each book.

  2. Refund Deadlines are established for each term. Full refunds are given until one day after the last day to add or drop classes.

  3. A book purchased new and then returned in used condition will be refunded at the used price throughout the refund time period.

  4. Textbooks purchased outside the refund time period are refundable for only forty-eight hours and only if they were not used while in the possession of the customer.

  5. There are no refunds on textbooks purchased the last two weeks of each term or during final exams, unless they were purchased for use during the next term.

Please keep your sales receipts and refund policy slips throughout each term. They are not needed however, for buyback at the end of the term.
