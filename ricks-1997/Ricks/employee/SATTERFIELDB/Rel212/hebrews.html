The Epistle to the Hebrews Project

 


Choose one of the following research studies relative to The Epistle of Paul the Apostle to the Hebrews. This research paper must be typewritten.
 

1. In connection with Hebrews 3-4, research and write about the rebellion of the children of Israel at the base of Mt. Sinai (Exodus 19-34). What are Paul's points in relation to the rebellion at Mt. Sinai?
 

2. In connection with Hebrews 5,7, research and write about the man, Melchizedek (Alma 13, Genesis 14, and JST Gen. 14:25-40, appendix). What are Paul's points relative to Melchizedek?
 

3. In connection with Hebrews 4:14 through 5:5, 6:19-20, 8:1-6, and 9:1-14, research and write about the dress and duties of the High Priest. You would want to understand the symbolism of the high priest's clothing and the meaning of the ritual performed on the Day of Atonement. The description of the high priest's clothing is found in Exodus 28 and the ritual of the Day of Atonement is found in Leviticus 16. (The Old Testament Student Manual and Gospel Symbolism, by Joseph Fielding McConkie may help you on this.) What is Paul's point with regards to the high priest?
 

4. In connection with Hebrews 8-9, research and write about the layout and symbolism of the Tabernacle (Exodus 25-40). (The Old Testament Student Manual and Gospel Symbolism, by Joseph Fielding McConkie may help you on this.) What is Paul's point in relation to the tabernacle?
 

5. In connection with Hebrews 9-10, research and write about the sacrificial system of the Law of Moses (found in the Book of Leviticus). (The Old Testament Student Manual, Gospel Symbolism, by Joseph Fielding McConkie, and the NIV Study Bible may help you on this.) What is Paul's point in relation to Christ and sacrifices?
 

Each project consists of two parts: (1) Research, (2) What is Paul's point. All research should include scriptural references and a bibliography of works cited.

Criteria of Judgment

This will be judged on how your paper helps someone understand what Paul is saying in Hebrews.
