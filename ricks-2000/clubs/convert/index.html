Mission Statement

 

All are invited to strengthen their testimonies by listening to the stories and testimonies of converts to the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

 

Listen to words and counsel of inspired men and women for all to hear, especially converts.

Build strong friendships based on common goals and beliefs in the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

 Enjoy wholesome activities, service, and recreation that will provide an opportunity to have fun and meet new friends.
