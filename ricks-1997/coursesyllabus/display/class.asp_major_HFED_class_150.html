Practical Homemaking
PRACTICL HMKG
CLASS CODE: HFED 150 CREDITS:  2 FEE:  $35.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A course designed to teach principles relating to various aspects of homemaking including: food preservation, food preparation, nutrition, meal planning, home nursing and health, home/financial management and organization, gardening and basic emergency food storage all within the context of the LDS home. Emphasis is on practical, hands-on learning experiences.
