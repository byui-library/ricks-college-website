As the world's first national park, millions of people flock to Yellowstone each year.  Many spend time visiting the geological wonders of geysers (including Old Faithful), hot springs, mud pots, & fumaroles.  The Washburn Expedition of 1870 camped on the banks of the Madison River in the Park.  According to records, that evening it was suggested that "the whole of it (Yellowstone) ought to be set aside as a great national park." It is at this historic site that hostelers will help rehabilitate the land and preserve this aspirations of Yellowstone born over 125 years ago.

 

Madison Campground Restoration 

Millions of tourists visit Yellowstone National Park each year. Each night, Madison Campground has approximately 500-600 visitors. The impact on the resources has exceeded it capacity to absorb this use unscathed. The National Park Service is understaffed and cannot perform all the desired rehabilitation itself.  They request the help of unselfish volunteer groups to assist with preserving and protecting Yellowstone's fragile natural resources.  

 
  The campground is criss-crossed with "social" trails that increase the  erosion, these trails will need to be rehabilitated and trees and shrubs planted.  Under direct guidance of the park rangers, hostelers will  replanting will be harvested from new growth stands that were reseeded naturally after the 1988 forest fires.
Other tasks may include written documentation that will need to be recorded to aid in futures studies or placing signs at critical areas.  Madison Junction has a diverse and interesting history associated with camping.  The existing campground is located on an archeological site of early Native Americans.  Any artifacts located will be documented according to the park's archeological survey policy.   
  Only hand tools (such as shovels, rakes, hoes, etc.) will be used. All tools will be provided as well as  project.  To enhance the learning process, field trips will also be taken to some of the geological and historic features of the Park. FOR PHYSICALLY FIT HOSTELERS. Elevation is 6,800 ft.
