Ethnic Literature
ETHNIC LIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 363R CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes a literary study of texts produced by ethnic writers or of texts that address ethnic identity. These texts may be in the form of essays, fiction, novels, poems, plays, and/or autobiographies. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes a literary study of texts produced by ethnic writers or of texts that address ethnic identity. These texts may be in the form of essays, fiction, novels, poems, plays, and/or autobiographies. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
TOPICS: Exposure to ethnic literature; analysis and evaluation of the literary qualities of the texts; appreciation and understanding of other cultures to gain respect for citizens of the world.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Analyzing, critical evaluationing, and gaining insight into ethnic literature. (2) Gaining knowledge and appreciation of the craft of ethnic writing. (3) Accepting and learning from the images, symbols, figurative language, plots, and structure of ethnic writings without unfairly imposing our Eurocentric mindset. (4) Learning intensified reading interpretation skills. (5) Learning how to express ideas in analytical essays.
REQUIREMENTS: Readings, analysis papers, research paper, and oral presentations.
