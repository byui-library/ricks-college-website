Other types of aid

Ricks College Legacy Fund

This fund is supported by student donations. The philosophy of this fund  is that the student will repay the amount borrowed  plus a donation so that additional students may be helped. Each applicant will be interviewed by a Financial Aid Administrator. The approval or denial of the application will be by committee action. The committee will consist of two Ricks College administrators and a student representative.   For more information on the Legacy fund Click Here. 

Ricks College Private Grants

Limited private grant funds are available. All students who have submitted information on the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) will be considered. Students need not make specific application. These grants are awarded to students with extreme need or extenuating circumstances. Recipients will be notified by the Financial Aid Office.

Ricks College Summer School Grant

Ricks College also has funds available to assist students with tuition while attending Summer School. Students applying for a Ricks College Summer School Grant must have applied for federal student aid. International students are not required to file for federal aid to be considered. Summer School Grant applications will be available February 1. Recipients will be notified by March 31.

 

Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA)

BIA grants are available to some American INDIAN students. These grants are awarded by the student’s tribal education office or agency office. To be eligible to apply, a student must be registered with a U.S. tribe that has a treaty with the U.S. government.

Eligibility requirements may differ for each tribe; therefore, the student should contact his/her tribe to determine eligibility.

It is the responsibility of the student to keep in contact with his/her tribe and send Ricks College any necessary paperwork such as the Needs Analysis that is required by many tribes. The student must be aware of all deadlines. If you have any questions contact our office.

 

Veterans Benefits

If you are eligible for Veterans Administration Educational Benefits, it is important that you contact the Financial Aid Office  as soon as possible so the necessary paperwork can be processed.  

Continuing students need to contact the financial aid office every semester or term they attend.  The verifying officer will certify the number of credits that the student is taking for that particular semester or term.

If a student is receiving veterans benefits, he/she must notify the financial aid office if he/she:

  • adds or drops classes
  • changes major 
  • withdraws from school
