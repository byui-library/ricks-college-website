VISION COMMITTEE REQUESTS ASSISTANCE IN PLANNING FUTURE

by ANDREA SCOTT
scroll staff

The Rexburg Downtown Vision Committee has asked that Madison County residents, including Ricks College students, complete a survey that looks ahead to the year 2020.

The committee, established to revitalize the city's downtown, asks questions pertaining to shopping, parking and building repair in the area.

Surveys will appear in the Rexburg Standard Journal, the Post Register and intoday's Scroll. Copies are also available from Mitch Neibaur, the committee's chairman, at Anchor Mortgage in Rexburg.

"The main thing is that we hope students will fill it out and let us know what they think," Steve Moser, assistant director of public relations at Ricks College, said.

Once the committee has received a "decent response," the next step it will take is making suggestions to the Rexburg City Council, which will then decide what changes will take place, Moser said.

"We are a fact-finding and suggestion-making committee. The city will have to decide what changes will be implemented," Moser said.

The data will be used to support grant applications and to entice new businesses to the downtown area, among other things, according to the Post Register.

The city wants to make the downtown area more friendly to Madison County residents as well as Ricks students because they will soon "be spending more than two years in Rexburg," Moser said.

Rexburg Mayor Bruce Sutherland formed the committee shortly after Gordon B. Hinckley, president of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, announced the transition of Ricks College to a four-year institute last June.

The committee, which is not affiliated with the recreation center proposal committee, was given the charge to "look ahead to 2020 and what could happen to Rexburg," Moser said.

Ballots can be dropped off at the MC Information Desk, the information desk in the Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building or the public relations office in Kimball Room 226.
