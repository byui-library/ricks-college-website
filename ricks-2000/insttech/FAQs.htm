  The computer will not let me login to BlackBoard.  How come?

The BlackBoard program was having some login problems, so on Saturday, March 10, the system was changed to a different login system.  Instead of having its own unique system, BlackBoard now uses the campus network login.  The login username and password for BlackBoard now are the same as you use to access student e-mail and to login to campus computers.

If you are logging in from off-campus, you may have to enter a "domain."  Try leaving the domain field blank.  If it still doesn't work, type in STUDENTS as the domain.  In Internet Explorer, there will be a separate line for domain. Type STUDENTS into this line.  In Netscape, you enter the domain by typing it in front of the username - - i.e. students\wxy99001

If the program continues to deny you access to BlackBoard, call 356-2361 or 356-1217.
How and when do I access my online class?
-The class schedule shows all online classes as having an arranged time and location, or just "web." Unless specifically arranged by your instructor, there will be no classroom meetings. Access course materials by:
1) Going to the Ricks College homepage - (http://www.ricks.edu)
2) Click "Online Courses"
3) Click "Orientation" - review the information here
4) From the Online Courses homepage, Click "Login to BlackBoard" - or go to the web address - http://courses.ricks.edu.

After entering in your username and password you will see the name of the online class you are registered for. Click on the name to get into the class.

You will also want to view a "course" called - Online Student Information in the BlackBoard program.  This has orientation information that you will need to be able to access all of the pieces of your course, and do well with online learning.

All online classes will be available the first day of the semester you are registered for.  Classes will not be available before that day, unless other arrangements are made.

 
Do I need a textbook for an online class?
How do I get my books for online classes?

- Yes, you do need a textbook (except for CS 100 and HS 131).
You will also need a CD-ROM that accompanies the online course.
The textbook can be purchased through the Ricks College Bookstore just like you purchase textbooks for your other classes.  The CD-ROM must be purchased at the Department of Instructional Technology (McKay Library 321).  Hours 8 am - 5 pm.  They are not available at the bookstore.

 
How long do I have to complete an online class?
- Ricks College online classes are tied to the semesters. You have until the end of the semester to complete the work. All courses have due dates for assignments.

 
How do I register for online classes?
- Register for online classes the same way you register for classroom classes. You can identify an online class because it will have a section number 801 or 802, etc.. Online classes offered through Continuing Education will have a section number 920.

 
Can I take online courses if I am not living in Rexburg?
- As long as you are admitted as a student for the current semester, you can sign up for an online course. If you are not admitted for the current semester, there are 8 online courses that you can take through Continuing Education (call 356-1040).

 
I am on the Fall/Summer or Winter/Summer program. Can I take online classes during the “off” semesters?
- Since you are technically not admitted during your "off" semester, you can sign up for the online courses offered through Continuing Education (356-1040).

 
  I submitted an essay assignment in Perception, but it shows up with a 0% score in the gradebook. How come?

The 0% is only a placeholder. You instructor will insert a grade there as soon as he/she reads the essay and gives it a grade.

 

  I entered the Perception program to review an assignment, then left it to study. When I came back to it, I couldn't access the assignment. Why?

Assignments can only be accessed 1 (one) time. You will only want to access an assignment when you are ready to take it for a grade.

 

  The assignments I need to do in Perception are not showing up. Why?

1) The assignment was previously accessed
You can only access assignments in Perception 1 (one) time. Make sure that you are ready to complete an assignment when you open it.

2) Assignment is past due
With a few exceptions (your instructor will have informed you if you are an exception), assignments are due at noon on the day indicated in the syllabus - and assignments are not available on the computer after this day.

3) Computer crashed
Occasionally if your computer crashes while you are doing an assignment, it will not be available when you try to access it again. If this happens, call 356-1217 or e-mail onlinehelp@ricks.edu, and it will be reset.

 

  Can I take my Online Final test or Midterm test from off-campus?

You can take the Midterm or Final test from off-campus if you arrange for an approved person to administer and proctor the test.  For more information, click here.

 

  When are my assignments due?

Assignments are due at 12:00 Noon on the date indicated in your syllabus. 
(Some courses do not have due dates. Your instructor will inform you if this is the case.)

 

  Occasionally when I login to BlackBoard CourseInfo from off-campus, it doesn't show the classes I am enrolled in.  Why?
- Occasionally some students do not get added into the BlackBoard system for courses they need to access.  To get added, call the Student Help Desk (356-2361).

 

  When I try to login to view my assignments, I cannot get past the login screen.
-1) When it asks for a name, enter your username (i.e. you77091)
-2) When it asks for a password, enter your PIN number
If you have any other difficulties, call the Student Help Desk (356-2361)

 

  How do I add or drop an online class?
- The process for adding and dropping online classes is exactly the same as adding and dropping regular classroom classes.

 

When I try to play the video clips in the McKay library, they don't work.  How come?
Your CD-ROM drive may be set-up incorrectly.  When it asks you for the drive letter of your CD-ROM - click "D"

 
  How do I install the information on my course CD?
- There is no need to install anything from the course CD.  The only contents of the CD are video, audio, and occasionally text clips that go along with the course.  You just have to put the CD into the CD-ROM drive and go through the course on the Internet.  Whenever there is a video or audio button in the course, you can click it to view the video or listen to the audio.  These are coming off of the CD.

 
How do I get the CD-ROM that comes with the course?
- The course CD-ROM can be purchased at the Department of Instructional Technology (McKay Library 321).  Hours 8 am - 5 pm.  They are not available at the bookstore.
