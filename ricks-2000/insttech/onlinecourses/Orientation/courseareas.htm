Getting Onto Your Online Course

Your online course is divided into three areas:

1) Information ABOUT the course
     - syllabus, contact information, what you need to do to succeed, what you will be graded on, etc.

2) The COURSE MATERIALS
     - Textbook reading assignments, supplemental readings and web pages, etc.

3) ASSESSMENT
     - Assignments, Quizzes, Tests

Click "NEXT" to see step-by-step instructions for accessing each of these areas.
