Each spring, over 1000 young students travel to Ricks College to gain knowledge and find that learning can be fun. Public Relations coordinate tours using Ricks students as tour guides. Group tours are free of charge with dates reserved well in advance. Tours are created specifically to meet needs. Because many of the display areas are relatively small, large groups may be divided into smaller numbers to accommodate the space; ideally groups are 25-30 each. Younger students receive complimentary copies of the award-winning color book, "Your Visit to Ricks College."

Key points of interest:

Wildlife Display Center Natural Science Museum
Greenhouses Planetarium
Demonstration Gardens

Please plan ahead to avoid conflicts with other groups who may request the same date.

Walking campus tours for groups are also available for an over all look at our college campus.
