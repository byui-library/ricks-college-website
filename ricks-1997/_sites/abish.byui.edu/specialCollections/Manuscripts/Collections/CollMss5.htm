Scope and Content

The Vardis Fisher Collection is mostly letters and other forms of writing that the Fisher Family composed and sent via mail to communicate with one another. These letters include the personal thoughts, ideas, and feelings of Vardis Fisher himself as well as those of his immediate family. The collection includes three hollinger boxes of correspondence.
