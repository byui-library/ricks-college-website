IDAHO FALLS AIRPORT SHUTTLES

The Idaho Falls Municipal Airport is located approximately 35 miles from campus. If needed, the college will provide a driver and vehicle to pick up students for a fee of $25 per trip. If more than one student is taking advantage of the offer, the fee is split between them with a minimum fee being $8. Advance arrangements for this individualized service are required at least two business days before the shuttle is needed.

Reservations may be made by contacting the Public Relations office during business hours (Monday-Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.) at (208) 356-1150.

At the beginning and end of the semesters and for major holidays, the college offers shuttles at the reduced rate of $8 each way. Following are the schedules for the times when reduced rate shuttles are offered. Only at these times are reservations not required:

Fall - beginning of the fall semester

Thanksgiving
