Purpose of Student Conduct Programs

Ricks College has a dual responsibility to its students.  First, to 
provide a quality education which will prepare them for further 
education, employment,and for their roles as citizens and parents.  
Second, to maintain a wholesome learning environment which will assist in 
building testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ(Ricks College 
Mission Statement). The student in turn has the responsibility of fulfilling 
his or her commitment to live the Code of Honor and comply with the dress 
and grooming standards. The Code of Honor is designed to facilitate both of 
these responsibilities. 

The application of the Code of Honor is essential to fulfillment of the mission
statement. Discipline is one of the means to bring about compliance with the 
code for those who are in violation.  No discipline in college should be 
administered as punishment to the individual.  It should be an educationally
based program to teach the individual and the student body that the rules 
and regulations of the college as well as society must be obeyed and that 
there are consequences for choices that are made.
