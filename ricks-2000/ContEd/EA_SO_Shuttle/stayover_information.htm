STAY OVER HOUSING

INFORMATION

Stay Over Housing is available for OYA participant's ONLY, needing to stay an extra day in order to catch their ride the next day.  We cannot guarantee availability.  It is all depending upon housing availability.

The cost of this housing is $15.00 if reservations are made before two weeks before the program begins.  $20.00 with in two weeks of the program start date.

Thank you for inquiring about Stay Over Housing at BYU-Idaho.  Reservations must be in our office no later than the Thursday preceding the start of your program by 3:00 p.m.  Any received after this time will be handled on a time and space available basis.

Please follow the directions below to make reservations.

Please read through the agreement carefully to ensure you understand the requirements in order to stay in our facilities. We expect those arriving early to observe the dress and conduct standards of BYU-Idaho. Shorts, capris, tank tops, and grubby attire are not allowed on campus. You will be expected to respect the rights and property of others you will be housed with. There will not be any adult supervision for this housing.

If you are flying from Idaho Falls and need transportation to the airport, please go to the "Transportation Information" link at the bottom of the page to make reservations.

If you have any questions feel free to call us (208) 496-1040..

IF YOU MUST CANCEL, REFUNDS ARE ONLY AVAILABLE MINUS A $5.00 SERVICE FEE UNTIL TWO WEEKS PRIOR TO YOUR PROGRAM START DATE AFTER THAT NO REFUNDS ARE AVAILABLE.
