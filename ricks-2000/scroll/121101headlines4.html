Reeve named JC Coach of the Year

Less than a month after the Ricks volleyball team ended its season, the American Volleyball Coaches Association named Ricks Coach JoAnn Reeve the 2001 Tachikara/AVCA Junior College/Community College National Coach of the Year Wednesday. The Vikings posted a 32-14 record in the last year of competitive athletics at Ricks College.

This is the second time Reeve has received a Coach of the Year award. The AVCA honored Reeve as the West Region Coach of the Year in 1996.

In Reeve’s 27 years as coach, all at Ricks, her Vikings have never posted a losing season. Their best record was in 1991, when Ricks went 52-4 (a 92.85 win percentage). The highlight of Reeve’s coaching career came in her second year as coach, in 1974, when the Vikings won the national championship.

Another highlight of Reeve’s career was in 1998 at the NJCAA finals when Reeve was inducted into the NJCAA Volleyball Coaches Association Hall of Fame.
