"Bro. Dave" Duerden came to Ricks College in 1989 from Lafayette, Indiana where he was working on his doctorate in Family Studies at Purdue. He is married and the father of four sons. He goes by "Bro Dave" from his years in child lab preschool programs at BYU-Provo and BYU-Hawaii. It also is easier to say Brother Dave than Brother Duerden, and he likes to think Brother Joseph and Brother Brigham would like it. ;-)

Bro. Dave Duerden has been director of "R" Children, the Ricks College Child Labs, and is currently serving as a member of the Idaho Governor's Coordinating Council for Families and Children. Working with colleagues at BYU-Provo, and with Byron Webster, he has helped pioneer the Family 100 - Family Proclamation class here at BYU-Idaho.

Bro. D has degrees in Child Development and Family Relations from BYU, and was a doctoral candidate in Family Studies at Purdue U.

He has taught child, family and religion courses at BYU, BYU-Hawaii, Purdue and Ricks College-now-BYU-Idaho.
