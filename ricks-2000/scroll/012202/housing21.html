Home buyers should consider a few tips

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Buying a home is a big decision for any first-time buyer. There are many things that should be considered before deciding whether or not to even buy a house and which house to buy if the choice is made.

The first thing to consider is whether an individual can afford a home. Price range can help in eliminating the number of homes that wouldn’t be suitable for that person.

The checklist to cover for a financial picture in buying a house includes: 1) Running a credit report to maker sure there are not any problems for a loan. 2) Making sure the budget can handle the expense of making the down payment and then the following regular payments. 3) Pay off minor debts that may be attached to the budget. This extra money can be used for repairs and maintenance that will be needed for owning a home, according to www.ourfamilyplace.com.

When determining if a budget can allow for payments on a home, multiply the gross monthly income by the qualifying ratio. This gives the dollar amount that a lender would consider acceptable to spend on the regular payments, according to www.housesense.com.

The transition from renting to ownership can be an advantage or disadvantage. If moving is a frequent activity, renting would provide the mobility to make such a task easier. If there is a desire to settle in one place for an extended period of time, owning a home would be advantageous.

Owning a home brings an opportunity for tax benefits. Currently, the interest paid on a mortgage and real estate or property tax are both deductible from your income tax.

Owning can be a challenge for those who are used to renting. The change from calling a manager to fix a leaking faucet to the owner trying to fix it can be a big task. Many owners end up turning to professionals to aid in their home improvement needs.

Although there will be home improvement problems for the owner, there are also the advantages of remodeling and redecorating the home to meet personal desires. This improvement can be exciting and fun for the first-time home owner.

To find a suitable house, many people turn to an agent or realtor.

There are a few items to be careful of when it comes to using another person to help in buying a home, according to www.fool.com.

To find the best agent, ask for referrals from close friends or associates. When an agent is found, interview him or her to get a better idea of the training and background the agent has.

When there is a good rapport between the new agent and buyer, ask to see a house. Find out if the agent’s style is compatible to the buyer’s needs.

After a particular agent is chosen, negotiate a fee. Make sure this fee will be beneficial to the buyer and the agent. The fee can increase the help from an agent or decrease according to the agreement. Make sure both parties understand the agreements made before signing any contracts.

After a home is chosen, a mortgage is needed to make security for a loan. A mortgage is a separate document that you sign when closing for a house.

It pledges your home as security for the loan, according towww.housense.com.

Mortgage lenders generally require buyers to make a down payment for the home.

In past instances, the required down payment was 20 percent of the cost of the home. It is now possible to get a mortgage loan with only 3 percent to 5 percent of a down payment, according to www.housense.com.

With all of the factors present in buying a home, there may be doubts.

The decision can be difficult but may be easier when taken with the right steps.
