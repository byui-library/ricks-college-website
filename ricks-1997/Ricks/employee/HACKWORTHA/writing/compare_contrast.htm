Comparison Contrast
To compare shows how things are alike; to contrast shows how things are different.  Your next writing practive allows you to do this.
 
Follow this guideline -- Pick subjects which:
 
1.  seem to be alike but are, in fact, different.
2.  seem to be different but are, in fact, alike.
 
You can contrast parents, family members, roommates, jobs, schools, coaches, teachers, dates, events, or ideas.  Pick a subject that will complement your life story.
 
Include three pieces of information in your thesis:
 
1.   The names of the people or things being contrasted (A/B)
 
2.   Whether you will stress likenesses or differences
 
3.   The way or ways these people or things are alike or different
 
Example.  Two of my high school teachers, Mr. Garrett and Mr. Stoddard, were different in the ways they treated their students.
 
Example.  Two of my high school seminary teachers, Mr. Hawkes and Mr. Smith, were different in several ways.
 
With the first example, you would list three ways these teachers were different -- three because you will be writing a five paragraph paper -
 
    introduction (1)
    body (3)
    conclusion (1).
 
For example, they were different in their:
 
    1.  teaching methods
    2.  methods of discipline
    3.  manner of dress
 
For this paper, we now have an outline and a thesis-support structure.  Structure your paper like this:
 
Introduction -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Thesis -- teachers were different in several ways.
 
First topic sentence -- One way these teachers differed was in their teaching methods.  For example,  Mr Hawkes  (Discuss Hawkes's method). ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------      ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------    
On the other hand, Mr. Smith ------  (Now discuss Smith's method.) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
Transition and new topic sentence -- Not only were these teachers different in their teaching methods but also in their ways of disciplining the students.   For example Mr. Hawkes was always tolerant and kind with offenders.  One time, Brother Hawkes ---------- (Discuss Hawkes's method of discipline.)  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In contrast to this, however, Mr. Smith was extremely strict.  One day when ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
Finally, these teachers were different in the ways they dressed.  (Discuss Hawkes) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Brother Smith, however, ----- (Discuss Smith) ------------------------------------------------------------------------
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 
Then write a concluding paragraph.  This can be quite short.   Summarize and let the reader know you have finished your paper.            
 
Checklist for Contrast Paper
 
1.   The thesis is the last sentence of the first paragraph.
 
2.   The thesis includes three parts (A/B, alike or different, way or ways).
 
3.   Transitions occur between each paragraph and subjects.
 
4.   At least five sentences introduce the thesis.
 
5.   The paper contains a concluding paragraph which restates the thesis in a new way and signals an end of the paper.
 
6.   The paper is accurate (spelling, punctuation, and grammar).
 
7.   The paper is at least 800 words.
 
8.   Specific examples are given for each point.
 
9.   Awkward sentences and phrases are avoided.
