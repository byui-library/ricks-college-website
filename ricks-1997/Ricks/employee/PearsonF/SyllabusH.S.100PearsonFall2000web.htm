Class Description: Introduction to the medical and allied health careers. There are over 900 different careers in the medical field. This class is a overview of health care in the United States and then a partial discussion of the medical and allied health careers. The class includes three hour of lecture each week and assignment that will help the student looking at one or more of the careers in health care.



Textbook: Introduction to the Medical and Allied Health Careers, Fred R. Pearson, BYUI Press, August 2001



Assignments:

1. Prepare a resume of you education and job experiences.

2. Prepare  written in-depth papers on at least three of the medical or allied health careers. Include a personal interview with a health professional in each of the papers. Deliver findings during a class presentation.

3. Read textbook/ complete textbook assignments.
