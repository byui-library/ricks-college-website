Alumni Awards

Alumni Awards recognize those who have made outstanding accomplishments, provided extraordinary service or have lived exemplary lives.  Nominations for awards can come from any BYU - Idaho/Ricks College Alumnus/Alumna.   Award decisions are made by the Alumni Council. 

If you are interested in making a nomination, please complete the Alumni Award Nomination Form (pdf format).
