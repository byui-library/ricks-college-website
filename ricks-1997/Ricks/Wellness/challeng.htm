RICKS COLLEGE FITNESS CHALLENGE--

"CLIMB THE GRAND TETON"

 

When: January 18-April 9, 1999

Who: Ricks College employees and their spouses

Teams: There will be teams comprised of ten people per team, one being selected as the captain. The ten people could be from the same department, several departments, campus friends, or spouses. If you want to participate but cannot find others, then contact the Wellness Committee and we will help in forming groups.

How does it work: The minutes exercised during the week will be turned into the team captain. The captain in turn will report the minutes to the Wellness Committee. Each minute will be equal to one foot toward "climbing" the Grand Teton (13,770 feet).

How many minutes per week: The program will last 12 weeks. In order to meet the goal, each team will need to accumulate about 1170 minutes per week. This breaks down to each person exercising 39 minutes three times per week. A very realistic goal.

Goal: The goal is to have each team reach the summit by Friday April 9, 1999. It will not be a race to see who is "first, second, or third", but rather a common goal for everyone to reach.

Rewards: Teams who reach the summit by the deadline will be awarded a prize. Special recognition will be given to the team and the individual with the most minutes.

How to enter: Fill out a team roster and send it to the Wellness Committee, 103 ASB before January 8, 1999. Click here to print a team roster or write Larry Wickham at the above address or by calling ext. 1040.

What exercises will count: Any exercise that will help you achieve cardiovascular fitness. To do this, you should exercise at least three times a week for 20-30 minutes, maintaining your target heart rate range for at least 15 minutes. Your target heart range is 65-80 percent of your maximum heart rate. To find your maximum heart range, subtract your age from 220. The target heart range of 65 % can be found by multiplying that number by 0.65. For example, if a 40 year old wants to exercise at 65 percent of his/her maximum heart rate, it would be calculated in the following manner: 220-40=180 x 0.65=117. This means a person should get the pulse rate to 117 beats per minute for at least 15 minutes during the workout.

 

Caution: If you have not been exercising regularly or have health problems, see your doctor before starting an exercise program.
