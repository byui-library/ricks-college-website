Welcome!!

Ricks College serves as home to over 200 international students representing about 40 different countries. Students are attracted to Ricks for a variety of reasons. In addition to having strong and accredited academic programs and excellent campus facilities, Ricks offers a unique spiritual environment. Ricks College is affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. For this reason, an unusual blend of academic excellence and increased spiritual awareness exists at Ricks. For students who have a desire for a quality education in an atmosphere consistent with their moral and religious values, Ricks is a perfect match.

The International Student Office is located in the Dean of Students Office, in Room 230 of the Kimball Administration Building. The international student advisor is available to assist international students regarding student visa, employment, travel, personal, cultural, and other related issues and problems. The International Student Office is a great resource center with ability to answer most any question related to international studies.
