WHAT IS QUEST?

Quest is one answer to a request made by BYU-Idaho President David Bednar to serve more students. By creating Quest, more students are provided with an opportunity for a BYU-Idaho education. The Quest programs have as a focus hands-on or experiential education. Each student gains a better understanding of  the host sites through taking courses that relate to the area. The number of students in each Quest program is limited to 26 participants to give more individualized attention. The Quest programs are not a remedial education experience or for at risk students. Quest programs are serious academic programs for the adventurous student.
