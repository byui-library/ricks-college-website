CLASS REQUIREMENTS:

 

1.QUIZZES: There will be preparation quizzes (10 pts each) in class. These are usually 8 questions of 1 point each, plus 2 points for just signing your name. No make-ups unless cleared with me in advance.

Throughout the semester your lowest quiz score to date is automatically dropped by the computer (it will still show up on the printout, but it won’t be included in your total points). All others are used in tabulating your final score.

A weekly printout showing your FINAL GRADE TO DATE will be posted in the hall. Grades are based on total points and a 90, 80, 70, etc. cut-off.

Quizzes are given right at the start of class. Please don’t be late.

 

2.EXAMS: You will have three major exams plus a final. All count 100 points each and all are comprehensive. No exam score will be dropped. There a study sheet online for each exam. Be sure to keep them to help review for the next exam and the final.

 

3.PAPER: (50 points) 

a. Your paper is due on the date indicated, and will be on the topic below. No late papers will be accepted—NONE.

b. Follow this or lose points: The paper must be 1,300-1,500 words (stay within these limits), in two columns, left and right justified, single-spaced. Use 12 or13 point Times-Roman (this is Times-Roman, 13pt),or Arial (this is Arial, 13pt) or a similar font (no weird fonts please, especially script), and printed on a laser. No dot-matrix will be accepted. Read this paragraph again.

Make your pages look like a magazine page. Justify BOTH right and left sides. Watch paragraphs; don't leave a blank line between unless you're using numbers, and indent each.

c. Use the computer spell check and, although you may be tempted, avoid the thesaurus unless you can show me you use the word in normal conversation. In other words, the paper should sound like you.

d. All direct quotes must be so noted, with quotation marks and a reference in the text, not at the end of the paper.

e. You will graded on everything: content, grammar, spelling, presentation, etc., so be neat, no typos, no last minute corrections in ink, etc.

f. Omit fancy covers, plastic covers, etc. Just a simple sheet of paper with (1)your name (2) section, and (3) row—so I can hand them back.

g. If you have the need you are encouraged to use the Writing Lab in MC E 220.It's easy; just walk in and ask, or call 1189.

h. Unacceptable papers lose two full letter grades, are returned, and must be re-submitted in one week.

TOPIC: After reading The Prince, discuss Machiavelli and his ideas based on examples you have seen in your own life. State your conclusions as to when he is wrong and when he is correct. You are not limited to discussing just one principle. Show me that you have seen Mach alive and well in the real world.

7.HOW TO SUCCEED: This is not an easy class, nor is it intended to be, but you’ll learn a great deal, and will use it the rest of your life.

a. Plan to work and you will be amazed at how much you will learn. Miss several times, skip your reading, fail several quizzes, and you will be amazed how quickly your grade will drop.

b. Study in groups of 2 or 3 individuals. Meet on a regular basis and review the material with each other. Verbalize—that's important—the concepts, asking each other questions. Making the chapter make sense to another person is the best way to have it make sense to you.

c. Please don't quit. I'll help you any way I can. You’re just getting started in life. Ten years from now you’ll have noticed that those who make it, in class, in the mission field, or anywhere else, are those who simply settle down and get to work.
