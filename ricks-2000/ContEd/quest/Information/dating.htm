WHY CAN'T WE DATE OTHER GROUP MEMBERS?

Due to the small number of students in each cohort, dating group members or engaging in intimate behavior such as public displays of affection with group members is not allowed.  The Quest program is based in the theory of experiential education and meeting a variety of new friends is a crucial part of that process.  We ask that each member of the group restrain from dating, pairing off, or engaging in public displays of affection that may cause discomfort to other members of the group and limit friendships from developing.
