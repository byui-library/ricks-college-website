Communication 298 - Broadcast Internship

Prior to registering for this course you must first meet with Brother Weekes.

To complete an internship at a local radio station, you must first complete Communication 265R.

To complete an internship at a local television station, you must first complete Communication 259.

As an intern at a radio or television station, you must work a minimum of sixty hours during a fifteen week semester.  Please keep in mind that most, if not all, internships are for college credit only.  Very few radio or television stations will pay you a salary while interning.

At the end of your internship, you will be required to submit to the instructor a three to five page report on your internship.  This report should include such items as your specific job task and what you liked or disliked about your internship.  I have found in the past that the more meaningful internships occur when a student intern sits down with their supervisor at the station and establish some mutual workable goals.

I will periodically call your supervisor and get a status report of your internship.  Your station supervisor will assign you your letter grade at the end of the semester.

Internships can be rewarding and fun.  They will help you determine early on if broadcasting is really the career for you.  Internships can even open the door to a part or full time job.
