Engineering Courses


Engr 250 Introduction to Environmental Engineering   4
Prerequisite: Math 263, Chem 105, Engr 102 (or equivalent computer/design skills)
An introduction to engineering modeling of environmental topics, including heat and mass transfer;
environmental chemistry; water quality and management; hazardous materials handling; noise
pollution; and selected topics in environmental modeling, monitoring and design. Group design
projects required.
