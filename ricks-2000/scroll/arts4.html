Faculty members to perform piano and flute duo

by Julie Cope
cop00002@byui.edu
Scroll Staff

The Snow Recital Hall will be housing a flute and piano duo Friday at 7:30 p.m., performed by BYU-Idaho faculty members Patricia George and Stephen Allen.

George is an adjunct faculty member who specializes in teaching the flute, which is what she will be performing on.

Allen, who has been with BYU-I for over 15 years, will be spending his evening on the ivory keys.

“[George] and I will be performing a piece that was written for her by her husband,” Allen said. “The piece is spectacular, and she will be performing it again later in the month with the Twin Falls Orchestra.”

The two faculty members will only come together once this semester for an eventful evening of musical enjoyment.

The concert is free and classroom attire is encouraged. This event is also FA 100 approved, filling a recital requirement.
