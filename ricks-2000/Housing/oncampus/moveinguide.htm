What things should I not bring? [Top]
Fireworks or other explosive/flammable materials are not allowed in the residence halls. Small appliances and space heaters are not allowed in the bedrooms, with the exception of personal refrigerators provided by the university.  Incense, candles, or any open flame are not allowed in the residence halls. Weapons including firearms, BB guns, paint-pellet guns, wrist rockets, large residence knives, and ammunition are not allowed in the residence halls.  Skateboards, longboards, and in-line skates are not allowed on campus because of an increase in accidents and damage to university facilities.  Pets (including fish), are not allowed in the residence halls.

 
Will I have a telephone? [Top]
One wall-mounted telephone is provided in each dorm room or apartment.
Can I decorate my room? [Top]
Of course! Each dorm/apartment has bulletin boards you can use to hang up posters, pictures, etc.  You can use white Plastitack (which is provided by your Head Resident) for putting things on the walls. Please do not use tape or other types of adhesives, and make sure your decorations can be easily removed (no paint, wallpaper, contact paper, or decals). Please do not hang items on ceilings either. Due to potential fire hazards, residents may not use light bulbs of more than 75 watts.

Will I need renter's insurance? [Top]
If your parents have homeowners insurance, chances are you are covered under it. If not, you should purchase renter's insurance. University insurance only covers university property, not your personal property.

 
Is there a place for my bike? [Top]
There are bike racks located outside each residence hall.  There is no indoor storage for bikes in the women's dorms during the winter.  Bikes can be stored in the basement of the men's halls during the winter, but storage is very limited.  Bikes are not allowed inside the men's residence halls or women's apartments.
