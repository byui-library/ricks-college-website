Mineralogy & Petrology
MIN & PET
CLASS CODE: GEOL 350 CREDITS:  4 FEE:  $50.00
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: The constitution, origin, and identification of minerals. The genesis, interpretation, and identification of igneous and metamorphic rocks.
DESCRIPTION: This course teaches students the principles of crystallography and crystal chemistry; identification & field relations of minerals and igneous & metamorphic rocks; and the principles and processes that lead to the formation of minerals and igneous and metamorphic rocks.
TOPICS: Structure & Crystal Chemistry of MineralsPhysical Properties of Minerals & Mineral Identification.Optical Mineralogy and Thin Section Petrography.Occurrence & Use of Minerals.Thermodynamics & KineticsRadiometric & Stable Isotope GeochemistryMagma Properties, Generation, Movement, Differentiation, & Emplacement/Eruption.Petrotectonic AssociationsHand Sample and Thin Section Identification of Igneous & Metamorphic RocksMetamorphic Facies & P-T-t pathsMetamorphic Reactions & FluidsMetamorphic Environments & Metamorphism of Pelitic, Calcareous, & Mafic/Ultramafic Rocks
OBJECTIVES: The main objective is for students to be able to identify, understand, and interpret minerals, igneous rocks, and metamorphic rocks. Other objectives include giving students experience in: field geology, computer modeling, the use of mathematics in geology, research, scientific writing and speaking, and the geologic literature.
REQUIREMENTS: This class requires significant preparation for and participation in class. Field trips are also required.
PREREQUISITES: Geol 111 & 112; Chem 105 or 111
