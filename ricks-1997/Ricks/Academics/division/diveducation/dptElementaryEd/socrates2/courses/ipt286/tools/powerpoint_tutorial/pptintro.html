Introduction to Microsoft PowerPoint97

Microsoft PowerPoint is a presentation program designed to allow the user to graphically demonstrate ideas to an audience. The tools available for use in PowerPoint include text, graphics, various chart types, hyperlinks, audio and video. In addition, a variety of special effects are also available to "jazz up" a presentation. This tutorial is intended to introduce you to basic concepts within PowerPoint. As always, the best way to become proficient is to use the product and experiment on your own.
