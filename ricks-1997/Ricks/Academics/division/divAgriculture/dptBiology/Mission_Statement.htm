Our Mission Statement

To build testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ by exposing students to the intricacies of God's creations and instilling in them an appreciation for the beauties and complexities of nature and the interdependence of all life on earth.

To provide a basic framework of biological knowledge with which students can make informed decisions and to foster lifelong learning, which will enhance daily living.

To provide biology majors and those in pre-professional science-related career fields with a well organized and stimulating treatment of current biological information, preparing them for upper division classes in their major fields of study.

To provide effective academic advising in order to help individual students remain focused and on track with their education, while at the same time helping these students realistically evaluate their abilities and make wise career choices.
