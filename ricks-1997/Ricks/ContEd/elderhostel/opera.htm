$340		 Opera, Over Easy: It Can Be for Everyone
Whether you are highbrow or lowbrow, this course will appeal to you. The classes will deal with comic and light opera, and light and heavy tragedy. Featured will be contemporary production such as "Les Miserables," "Phantom of the Opera," "The Magic Flute," "La Boheme," "Othello," and others. Video and audio tapes will enhance the learning experience. Discussions will include "Historical Aspects of Opera," "Symbolism of Music & Drama in Opera," and "How Does Music Carry an Opera."

Final Call! - Theater Life Form Backstage to On Stage
Study the activity behind the scenes in order to put a production on stage. This will include designing and construction of sets, costumes, props, lighting, sound and special effects. Understand a director's concept and examine the actor's method.
