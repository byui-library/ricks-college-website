for Humanitarian Service
Doug and Deanna Andrus

Idaho Falls natives Doug and Deanna Andrus were in the same class at Idaho Falls High School in the late fifties, but they did not start dating until Doug returned from his Church mission to the Eastern States. "We were both dating someone else," Deanna says, but she remembers thinking that his date didn’t seem right for him. "Of course I didn’t know (what was ahead for both of us) at the time." They were married in 1963.

Doug grew up in the trucking business owned by his father. After graduating from Ricks in 1964 he attended BYU and earned a degree in business management. Doug Andrus Distributing has grown from six trucks in 1976 to 187 semis and 280 trailers today covering most of the United States and three provinces in Canada.

Deanna graduated from Ricks with a nursing degree in 1961 and worked at LDS Hospital in Salt Lake City for about a year. She then worked for a private practitioner in Rigby. By the time Doug graduated from BYU in 1967 the couple had three children and Deanna became a fulltime mother.

The Andrus’ have 13 children, 12 of them living, and 28 grandchildren.

Eight of the children are girls and often perform professionally as the Andrus Sisters. The family promotes wholesome entertainment acts through its Double A Family Entertainment entity. The Andrus’ own several rental units which are often used to help those financially disabled. Doug is active in scouting and serves as a vice president over public relations in the Grand Teton Council. He has also organized benefit dinners and auctions to help those in need. Time, money, and equipment has been donated by his company to transport food and clothing to other states as part of overseas relief efforts.

Doug is currently a counselor in the presidency of the Eagle Rock Stake. He has been a bishop’s counselor, ward and stake Young Men’s president and a stake mission president. Deanne is now serving as Relief Society president in her home ward.
