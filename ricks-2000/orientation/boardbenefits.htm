What benefits will I receive from serving on the NSO Board?

 

As a NSO Board Member you will receive many awesome benefits, including:

  • Opportunity to serve by providing event planning, management, and support for all new students attending Orientation, as well as other "On-Going" programs and initiatives sponsored by the Office of New Student Orientation.

  • Meet new people and make new friends.

  • Lots of free food!!!

  • Free NSO Board Polo and name tag.

  • Attendance at Y-Team Conference.  You won't want to miss this, its a blast!

  • Gain leadership skills and experience for your resume.  NSO Board Members may also qualify as ideal candidates when applying for NSO Team positions.

  • Service opportunities.

  • Freed admission in the Y-Team Association, a new student association for Y-Team Leaders.

Make a difference . . . Join the NSO Board today!!!
