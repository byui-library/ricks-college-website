Student Employment & Career Placement

1. Purpose & Content of a Cover Letter

Purpose of Cover Letter
To introduce yourself when you can't do it in person.

Opening Paragraph
 State why your are writing. Specify the name of the position that you are seeking.
Mention how you heard of the opening - use mutually known names.

Body Paragraphs
Explain why you are interested in the job. Specify how your education and work experience would benefit them. If it is an internship, briefly describe how the job would benefit you.

Closing Paragraph
Reiterate why you want the job.
Facilitate the opportunity for an interview.
Thank them.

 

2. Follow-up Letters (thank you letters)

Follow-up Letters (thank you letters)
Thank the interviewer.
Refer to something in the interview that will remind them who you are.
Mention any points that you were not able to bring up during the interview.
Reiterate your eagerness to hear from them concerning their hiring decision.
Letters should be sent immediately after the interview to ensure that it arrives before a decision has been made.
Follow up with a thank you note even if you receive a rejection.

Dear Mr. Smith:

As a college student preparing to graduate with a degree in Retail Management this April, I am writing to apply for the position of Sales Manager at your Pocatello store. I learned of this opportunity through my sister, Jane Smith, who is an Accountant with your company. I am confident that my work experience and education has provided me with the skills required to be a productive employee for ZCMI.

As a Sales Clerk for Payless Drug Store for the past three years, I have learned the skills and responsibilities associated with the retail industry. Working directly with the public to meet their needs has helped me develop valuable interpersonal skills. I also gained valuable experience by working with inventory, stocking, pricing, and record keeping.

My education has provided me with additional training and knowledge of the retail industry. I have successfully completed courses in retail management, marketing, sales, and accounting.

I would like the chance to discuss this employment opportunity by meeting with you for a personal interview at your convenience. I will call on Thursday, December 17 to arrange a date and time. A resume has been enclosed to assist you in evaluating my qualifications. Thank you for your time. I look forward to meeting you.

Sincerely,

 

John Smith
