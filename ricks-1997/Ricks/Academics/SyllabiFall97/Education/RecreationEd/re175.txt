I. COURSE DESCRIPTION:  Introduction to Rock Climbing, basic techniques 
	include free climbing and safety systems.

II. STATEMENT OF PURPOSE:  The purpose of this course is to expose 
	students to the basics of technical rock climbing as a 
	recreational pursuit.  Focus will be directed toward the 
	general principles and techniques of safety systems and 
	leadership as they are generalized to the various levels 
	of rock climbing.  The purpose of this course is not to 
	turn out 5.9 climbers or professional guides, but to develop 
	skills and judgement in individuals so that they may conduct 
	themselves and work with others in a safe mountain experience.

III. REQUIRED PRE-REQUISITE:  RE 175 is a level II course.  Students 
	should have previously completed Backpacking(RE 102) or possess 
	adequate background experience.

IV. COURSE OBJECTIVES:
	A. To provide the student with background knowledge and skill in 
	basic rockcraft techniques through classroom lectures and 
	intensive climbing in the field.
	B. To develop awareness and understanding of the advancement, 
	techniques and ethics involved in modern climbing.
	C. To develop a background in the selection and use of equipment 
	and clothing in relation to basic rockcraft.
	D. To provide a basis of the development of life-long 
	recreational pursuits.

V. FEES AND EQUIPMENT:
	A. Lab fee - $60 will cover transportation, group gear, lab 
	assistants and program materials.
        B. Textbook - How to Rock Climb, John Long  Other books of interest 
	are: Basic & Advanced Rock craft, Royal Robbins, Climbing Ice, Yvon 
	Chouinard, Chouinard Catalog, Great Pacific iron Works, 
	Mountaineering--the Freedom of the Hills, The Seattle Mountaineers, 
	Learning to Rock Climb Michael Loughman.
	C. Personal Gear:
		1.  Rock climbing - wear loose fitting clothing. (i.e. 
		bibs, baggy pants, etc.) Climbing shoes are provided as 
		part of your equipment fee.
		2.   Caving - dress for damp, cold conditions.  Avoid 	
		hypothermia by not wearing cottons.
