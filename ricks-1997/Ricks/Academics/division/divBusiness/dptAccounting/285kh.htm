Required Text: You Name It, Company Practice Set by Saftner and Cranor, copyright 1998 by Irwin/McGraw-Hill. You will also need to purchase two 3½ inch computer disks (double density or high density).

Prerequisites: Successful completion of Accounting 201.

Course Description: This course is a review of Accounting 201. We will use a practice set where the student will journalize, prepare statements, and analyze the results of each month for 6 months. This will be done on the computer. Each month introduces new concepts, and procedures and integrates them with the concepts introduced in earlier months. Over the 6 month period, the business will grow and change from a service business to a merchandising concern. We will also learn concepts related to general ledger software systems.

Objectives:
(1) To review and apply the principles learned in Acct. 201.
(2) To increase one's skill and understanding in using general ledger accounting software.
(3) To increase one's understanding and use of a company's financial statements.

Course Routine: The manual write up work should be completed at home. Part of each class period will be used for the computer part of this course. Some of the computer work will require time outside of class. A part of each period will be used to demonstrate and review the concepts introduced in each chapter.
