Resources available to students:
  • A career library containing career and college transfer information
  • A computerized career decision-making program to assist in making career choices
  • The Academic Advising program:
    • Students are assigned an advisor to assist them in their education.
    • The advisors help students keep on track for graduation.
    • Advisors will receive copies of grades at mid-term and at the end of the semester.
    • After the student applies for graduation the advisor will also complete a graduation
      evaluation.
