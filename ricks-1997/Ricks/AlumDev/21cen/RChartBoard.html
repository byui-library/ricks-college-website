The board of trustees endorses the continuing commitment at Ricks College to magnify its crucial role in the work of the Restoration and to enhance the wonderful light and spirit of its campus. Educational excellence is the standard fostered in all courses and programs in an environment with a strong emphasis on moral and spiritual values.

Educational and extracurricular activities at Ricks College are designed to help fulÞll its mission, which is to:

  • Build testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ and encourage living its principles,
  • Provide a quality education for students of diverse interests and abilities,
  • Prepare students for further education and employment, and for their roles as citizens and parents, and
  • Maintain a wholesome academic, cultural, social, and spiritual environment.

For those who value the distinct contributions of Ricks College, this is an important time to join in support of its vital inþuence in the lives of its students. We extend our appreciation to those who have responded so generously in the past, and we invite your initial or continuing contributions as Ricks College continues to harmonize spiritual and educational excellence during the coming century.
