I.   COURSE DESCRIPTION: Introduction to catching, grooming, saddling 
	and riding horses.  The student will learn to walk trot and canter 
	a horse.  Safety measures are taught each class period.

II.  STATEMENT OF PURPOSE: Completion of course should develop confidence 
	in students in working with horses.

III. PRE-REQUISITE:  Students should have an interest in horses and want 
	to gain and develop skills.  This is a level I course.

IV. OBJECTIVES:
	A. Provide instruction and skill practice in safe horse handling 
	and riding technique.
        B. Develop a foundation of a life long recreational activity.
        C. Develop an awareness of horses and the potential of a life 
	long hobby.

V. FEES AND EQUIPMENT:
	The Fee of $60.00 covers transportation to and from the Livestock 
	Center, horse feed, and use of tack.

	Textbook optional use:  Training for Western Riders, by McArthur.

	Personal Gear:  When going to the Livestock Center, students 
	should wear loose fitting outdoor clothing, riding boots or tennis 
	shoes.  No large lug sole boots.

VI. GRADING CRITERIA:
	A. Class attendance
	B. Working well with horses and other class members.

VI. In compliance with applicable disability law, qualified students with 
	a disability may be entitled to "reasonable accommodation."  It is 
	the student's responsibility to disclose to the teacher any special 
	need she/he may have before the end of the first week of class.
