Playing with Fire

'The Matchmaker' is one hot comedy
By Kristen Whipple, Scroll Staff



POP-UPS-Suprised clerks pay attention to the schemes of the store andtake off to New York footloose and fancy free. They are played by Kyle Scanion and Jeff Anderson.
by Michael LewisPublic Relations

The Ricks College Theater is performing Thorton Wilder's The Matchmaker from April 8-10 and 13-17 at 7:30 p.m. in the Snow Drama Theatre. Tickets are available at the ticket office. The cost is $1 with a student ID and $6 for non-students.

Similar to Hello Dolly (without the music), this comedy tells the story of a miserly merchant named Horace Vandergelder, who decides he wants to get married. He turns to matchmaker, Dolly Levi, who is herself looking for a husband. Dolly begins to do a little scheming.

Also as a subplot, Vandergelder's clerks ditch work and decide to experience life in New York. The catch to their adventure is they nearly run into their boss several times. Dolly somehow becomes involved with Vandergelder's clerks and some young ladies where they all meet at an expensive resturaunt which leads to a whirlwind climax of humor and confusion. How does the plot end? Well that is for the story to tell.

This is the third time that The Matchmaker has been performed at Ricks.

"It's about as funny as a play as you can get," Director Robert Nelson said.

Wilder, an American playwright, has also written several short novels and plays including Our Town, and Skin of our Teeth. Both of these plays won a Pulitzer Prize. Wilder is known for "maintaining that true meaning and beauty are found in ordinary experience."

Of the messages in The Matchmaker one is to live life to the fullest.

"It's important to experience life. Sometimes we get caught up in studying. Live life," Kyle Scanlon, a sophomore from Sun Valley, Idaho, said. Scanlon plays one of Vandergelder's clerks.

Another message of The Matchmaker is to entertain.

"We hope they'll enjoy the story. It's a great story, I hope they'll laugh," Trent Krummenacher, a sophomore from Williams, Ariz., said. Krummenacher plays the part of Vandergelder.

The Matchmaker is the last chance for students enrolled in FA 100 to fulfill their play requirement. But the play is more than just getting FA 100 requirements fulfilled.

"It has been exciting to see them develop their characters and develop the humor. We'll be ready to give everyone a smashing time," Nelson said.
