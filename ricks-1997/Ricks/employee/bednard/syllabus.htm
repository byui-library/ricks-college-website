Course Description

This course focuses upon the calling and functions of the living prophets, emphasizing their teachings through both classic and recent general conference addresses.

 

Value of the Course

Two inspired statements highlight the value and importance of this course.

We believe all that God has revealed, all that He does now reveal, and we believe that He will yet reveal many great and important things pertaining to the Kingdom of God (9th Article of Faith).

Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets (Amos 3:7).

We live at a pivotal time in the history of the world, even the dispensation of the fullness of times. God the Father and His Son continue to reveal their will through prophets to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saint and the people of the earth. This course investigates both the process of revelation and the men called as apostles and prophets through whom revelations come. Striving to understand the revelatory process and hearkening to the teachings of the living prophets are essential spiritual attributes in these the latter days.

 

Course Objectives

• create a learning environment wherein the spirit of the Holy Ghost is present and can teach and testify (D&C 50: 13-14).

• learn the spiritual process for "understanding" (D&C 50: 22; 3 Nephi 17:1-3) and applying the teachings of the living prophets.

• understand and apply the doctrines and principles the Brethren presently are setting before The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the world.

• gain an appreciation for the distinctive qualities and attributes of the men called to be apostles and prophets.

 

Required Text

Teachings of the Living Prophets: Student Manual Religion 333.

 

Course Procedures

Students are expected to (1) read assigned articles before class sessions, (2) come to class spiritually prepared to invite the Holy Ghost, ask questions, and participate in class discussions, and (3) learn a process for and develop the discipline of recording effectively the "thoughts" and "feelings" (D&C 8:2) that come as a result of studying the teachings of the living prophets.

Class attendance is expected, as is your participation in and contributions to class discussions. Note that quality of contribution is important, not the amount of "airtime."

 

Examinations and Grading

Papers, examinations, your personal record, and attendance/participation will be used to determine your overall grade in the class. You will have three major papers during the semester, a mid-term examination, and a comprehensive final examination. Additional and more detailed information about course assignments and grading procedures will be provided during the first class session.
