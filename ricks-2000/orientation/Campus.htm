Campus Orientation

Campus Orientation exists to assist new students in becoming familiar with the spiritual, academic, social, and personal opportunities that constitute a BYU-Idaho learning experience.

An exciting array of events and activities have been planned to welcome you to BYU-Idaho as well as build upon your Pre-Campus Orientation  experience.  By participating in New Student Orientation, you will become familiar with campus, meet new people, make new friends, and better understand the unique environment at BYU-I.
