Tragedy

1. Men in Shakespeare's time believed in Wheel of Fortune (as one man rises, another falls).

2. Tragedy is the fall of a great man, from high position.

3. Tragic hero is so placed in society that his actions involve the well-being of all its members.

4. When it is too late to escape, the victim comes to realize what has happened to him, and dies bitter, burnt-out, and desperate.

5. Dramatic irony: when audience knows more than the players. They know what will happen.
