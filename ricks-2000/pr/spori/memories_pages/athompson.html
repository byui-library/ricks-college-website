Ann Clarke Thompson

When my family moved to Rexburg in 1944, I was 11 years old. My dad was to be the new president of Ricks College.

Dad’s office was in the northwest corner of the second floor of the Spori building where he would spend the next 27 years loving Ricks College.

As a youngster this is the building I came to when I needed to visit with dad and even after I was married to ask his advice and share events in my life. It was in his office in this building that my husband went to ask dad’s permission for us to get married. I was 17, my husband 18, both of us just graduated from high school. Pretty scary, huh? It turned out great.

Even today, so many years later, when I pass by the Spori building I look up at those windows and in my mind I see dad sitting at his desk.”

Ann Clarke Thompson
Rexburg, Idaho
