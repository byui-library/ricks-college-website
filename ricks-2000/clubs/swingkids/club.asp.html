Get Hep to the Club

Hey club members!  This has been such a great year, and it's all you!  We had a great experience at the "day of dance."  Lots of you showed up for the classes, and we saw many new faces.  The Swing Team did a half-time performance at the basketball game, and many of you showed up to participate in that as well.  That brought a lot of exposure to the club.

In the near future we're going to be putting on some other (non-dance) activities, so we can all get to know each other a little better.  Look for an ice skating night (free for club members), and a service activity.  Let us know if you have any good ideas for stuff to do.

If you want to join our club, you need do no more than show up for one of our Wednesday night "meetings" (read dances).  They are every Wednesday in the East Ballroom of the MC.  7:30 to 8:00pm is a lesson, and 8:00 to 10:00pm is a general dance.  You don't need to bring a partner as there are tons of guys/girls there for you to dance with.  The dance costs $2 for students or $3 for non-students.

Join the Club!
Club members get all kinds of benefits! First of all, they get into the Wednesday night dances for free.  Also, the weekend lessons are for members only.   Caroline is in charge of these classes, and if you were anywhere else you'd pay at least $25/lesson for the quality of teaching that she provides. 

The club costs $10/semester.  You can sign up at any Wednesday dance.  Plus, now we can charge it directly to your account if that's what you'd like.

If you'd like one of our spiffy baseball-style swing shirts, let us know.  They cost $9.  The men's are available in S, M, L or XL with either Red or Navy sleeves.  Women's are available in S/M or L/XL with Sky Blue, Pink or Navy sleeves.  Big thanks to Rigby's Geisler Athletics for making them for us.
