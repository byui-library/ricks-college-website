PROPER CARE OF ID CARD

 

  • Don't bend the ID card; card readers cannot read bent or cracked cards.
  • Don't wash your card; doing so could damage the magnetic strip.
  • Keep the back of the card away from the back of other cards with magnetic strip; the magnetic fields can erase each other.
  • Keep you card away from any magnetic fields such as VCR's or stereo speakers.
  • Care for you ID card the same as you would your driver's license, credit cards, or cash
  • Don't use the card as an ice scraper to clean your car windows, it  will break.
