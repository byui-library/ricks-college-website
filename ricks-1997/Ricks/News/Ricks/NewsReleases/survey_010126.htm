Ricks College News Release
January 26, 2001

Ricks undertakes survey on BYU-Idaho transition

REXBURG, Idaho —Ricks College officials are asking area residents, alumni and students to tell them how they feel about the school’s transition to Brigham Young University – Idaho.

The results of the survey will help administrators know what questions and concerns people have about the changes that will occur as the junior college becomes a baccalaurate-granting institution, says Brent Kinghorn, community services vice president.

The survey is designed to see how much people understand about the transition to BYU-Idaho, what benefits they foresee with the proposed changes, and what concerns they have.

To take the survey, log onto the college home page on the Internet at and find the option "Transition Survey."

If you do not have access to the Internet, call the Public Relations Office at (208) 356-1150 and a survey will be mailed.

Poised to be renamed BYU-Idaho later this year, the school will begin some upper division classes in the fall of 2001. BYU-Idaho will be a two-tiered institution that will offer specialized associate degrees and bachelor’s degrees.

The school may see a modest increase in the size of the student body during the next few years.
