ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Ricks College exists to provide an
education consistent with the religious and family values taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The college seeks to maintain a balanced and diversified student body representative of world-wide Church membership.

The mission of the college is to build testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ, provide a quality education in a wholesome environment, and prepare students for further education, employment and their roles as citizens and parents.

In selecting a class of about 4,000 new students, the Admissions Committee considers the following: church, seminary and institute activity (LDS); personal commitment to living Ricks College standards; minimum academic preparation; geographic location; gender; out-of-class activities ; ethnicity; underenrolled programs; and missionary service.

New Freshmen

To be considered for admission, the following materials must be submitted before the deadline. The student is ultimately responsible to see that all application items arrive before the deadline.

Application: Personal Data (Part A), Educational Plans and Scholarships (Part B), High School Data and Student Essays (Part C), Student Commitment and Confidential Report (Part D), Seminary/Institute Evaluation (Part E).

Application Fee: $15 non-refundable application fee. Please do not send cash in the mail.

High School Transcript: Have an official transcript sent to the Admissions Office.
American College Test (ACT)or Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) scores: All applicants must take the ACT/SAT. For specific test dates, check with your high school counselor. Be sure to request your scores be sent to Ricks College.

Transfer Students
To be considered for admission, the following materials must be submitted before the deadline. The student is ultimately responsible to see that all application items arrive before the deadline.

Application: Personal Data (Part A), Educational Plans and Scholarships (Part B), High School Data and Student Essays (Part C), Student Commitment and Confidential Report (Part D), Seminary/Institute Evaluation (Part E).

Application Fee: $15 non-refundable application fee. Do not send cash in the mail.

Transfer Credits: Transfer students must have the Registrars Office of each school attended mail an official transcript to the Admissions Office. If you have taken courses at an LDS Institute of Religion, an Institute transcript must also be sent. All transcripts must be received before admission consideration is given. In cases where applicants are applying with less than 12 semester (18 quarter) hours, a high school transcript and ACT or (SAT) scores must accompany the application.

Former Students
Former Ricks College students who interrupt enrollment by not attending a Fall or Winter Semester must re-apply for admission.

To be considered for admission, the following materials must be submitted before the deadline. The student is ultimately responsible to see that all application items arrive before the deadline.

Application: Personal Data (Part A), Educational Plans and Scholarships (Part B), High School Data and Student Essays (Part C), Student Commitment and Confidential Report (Part D), Seminary/Institute Evaluation (Part E). (Seminary/Institute Evaluation is only required if you have attended another school since last enrolling at Ricks.)

Transfer Credits: If you have attended another school since attending Ricks, please have the Registrars Office of each school mail an official transcript to the Admissions Office. If you have taken courses at an LDS Institute of Religion, an institute transcript must also be sent.

Missionaries: Former Ricks College students who are currently serving a full-time LDS mission or those who have recently returned from a full-time mission (within six months) should use the simplified Former Student Returned Missionary application. These applications can be obtained by contacting the Ricks College Admissions Office at (208) 356-1066

Early Admission
High school students wishing to be considered for admission to Ricks College before high school graduation must:

1. Have a cumulative high school grade point average of 3.0 (B) or higher on all work to date.
2. Be within one semester of completing high school graduation requirements.
3. Receive an unconditional recommendation from both the principal and the parents stating that the student can no longer benefit from a high school education.
4. Complete all application requirements, including ACT scores, before the application deadline.

International Students
In addition to the requirements listed for U.S. citizens, international students must submit the following: International Student Deposit of $2000 U.S. dollars (not required of Canadian students); *U.S. High School Equivalency, international students must submit proof they have completed the equivalent of a U.S. high school education; TOEFL results (Test of English as a Foreign Language), required of students from non-English speaking countries, (a minimum score of 500 is required) or ACT/SAT scores are required of all students from English speaking countries; and Financial Certification and Affidavit of Support.

*A supplemental application (Part F) is required of all international students seeking admission to Ricks College.
