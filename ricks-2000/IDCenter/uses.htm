WHAT YOUR RICKS ID CARD CAN DO FOR YOU

 

It is your:

  • Valid form of identification on campus
  • Library card
  • Debit card for food purchased at the Nordic Landing, Viking Place, and Thor's Court
  • Cafeteria meal card (if applicable)

It is needed to:

  • Take tests in the Testing Center
  • Use the computer labs and for e-mail
  • Use the facilities in the Hart Building
  • Obtain tickets for athletic and cultural events
