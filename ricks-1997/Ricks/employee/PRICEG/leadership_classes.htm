"Membership in the Church is a call to leadership" (President Kimball) and that leadership is, "the ability to encourage the best efforts of others in working toward a desirable goal" (Cited in George L. Cannon, Ensign, Novermber 1991, p. 14; The Teachings of Spencer W. Kimball, p. 322).   "We will find it very difficult to be significant leaders unless we recognize the reality of the perfect leader, Jesus Christ, and let hime be the light by which we see the way!" ("Jesus: The Perfect Leader," Ensign, August 1979, p.7).

Members of the Church can learn to be leaders.  "Contrary to the old saying that leaders are born not made, the art of leading can be taught and it can be mastered" (President Gordon B. Hinckley quoting General Mark W. Clark, cited in TOGBH, p.306).  President N. Eldon Tanner said:  "...Christ is a model of correct leadership; and , as the scriptures record his life and teachings, they become case studies of divine leadership" ("The Message,: New Era, June 1997, p.4.   Cited in Eric Stephan, R. Wayne Pace, The Perfect Leader, p. 3).
