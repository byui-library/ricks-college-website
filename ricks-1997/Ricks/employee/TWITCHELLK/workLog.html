Work Log

Each homework assignment shall include a work log. Each entry in the work log shall provide the following information:

  • Date
    mm/dd/yy
  • Start time
    hh:mm
  • Stop time
    hh:mm
  • Interruption time (in minutes)
    includes time not spent on the task at hand
    record the total time if there were multiple interruptions
    specify the reason for the interruption(s) under Notes
  • Work time (in minutes)
  • Phase
    requirements, design, coding, compiling (including removing syntax errors), debugging (removing semantic errors), testing, documenting (including improving style), research
  • Notes
    examples: took a break, helped another student
  • Assisted
    List name(s) of students to whom you who provided help
  • Assisted by
    List name(s) of lab assistants, lab tutors, private tutors, faculty, other students, etc. who provided help
