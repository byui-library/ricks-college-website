Mixed Flag Football

1. Games will consist of 2 twenty minute halves with continuous running clock.  The clock stops for team and official time-outs, and the last 2 minutes of the second half.  Each team is allowed 2 one minute time-outs per half.  Half time is five minutes long.

2. Teams play with seven players on the field, three of which need to be of the opposite gender.

3. Be on time! Game time is forfeit time, and after one forfeit a team can be dropped out of play and tournament.

4. All players must play in at least 2 regular season games to be eligible for tournament.  Games will be scheduled to play on Wednesday's and Thursday's, and schedules will be posted on the bulletin board every Friday for the upcoming week.  Players may play only on one team.

5. No metal cleats allowed.  Only molded plastic cleats, turf shoes, or regular gym shoes are acceptable for play.

6. Flag football is non-contact.  The offensive player must avoid running through a defensive player and vice-versa.  The charge/block principles of basketball apply.  Offense may block with arms crossed on chest, at side, or behind the back.  No stiff-arming, flag guarding, or hurdling to avoid the pulling of a flag.

7. Tie games will be decided with each team getting four downs from the ten yard line.

8. No jewelry: rings, necklaces, watches, bracelets, etc....

9. No shorts or pants with pockets, belt loop, or drawstrings that are exposed.
