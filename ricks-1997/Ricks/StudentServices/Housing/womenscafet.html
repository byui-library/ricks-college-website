On-Campus Women's Cafeteria Style Housing

The Ricks College Women's housing units are Dorms 5 and 6. Each one houses 210 women with facilities for disabled students.


   Each building has a main lounge
as with all on campus dorms.


   The three bedroom apartments are shared by six women, two per bedroom. The bedrooms contain two beds, two dressers, one desk, an eight-foot closet and some
wall-mounted shelving.

The double-sink bathroom has a tub/shower and toilet, with a floor-to-ceiling storage cabinet. Each apartment also has a MicrofridgeÆ unit with microwave and refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls.

   There is also a coin-operated laundry room in each building with Maytag® washers and dryers.

For information about the meal plans which are available please visit the
Ricks College Food Services
website.
