German "Flavoring Particles"

There is a group of small German words which change the "flavor" or tone of an entire sentence. They are typically found somewhere in the middle of the sentence, most frequently after the conjugated verb. Depending on the text, these words will be referred to variously as "flavoring particles", "Füllwörter", "Würzwörter", "Modalglieder", or simply "adverbs". Unfortunately, many grammar texts don't even discuss them.

Here are a few very general rules:
    1. Flavoring particles change the mood or tone of the whole sentence.
    2. They may have more than one grammatical usage, and they might be translated very differently when used as a flavoring particle than
        otherwise.
    3. Typically the flavoring particle is not stressed or emphasized in spoken German.

Here are some of the more common flavoring particles, along with general translations and a few examples.
aber (but)
    Du bist aber groß geworden.      My, but you've grown!
    Kommst du? Aber ja.                    Are you coming? But of course.
also (thus, therefore, so)
    Sie ist die Mutter meiner Frau. Sie ist also meine Schwiegermutter.
    She is my wife's mother. So, she's my mother-in-law.
auch ([generalization]; whatever)
    Was er auch sagt ist eine Lüge.                                     Whatever he says is a lie.
    So klug er auch sein mag, das wird er nicht wissen.   Smart as he might be, he won't know that.
denn (well, so)
    Was macht ihr denn hier?        So what are you doing here?
    Was ist denn mir dir los?         So what's the matter with you?
    Ist das denn so wichtig?         Well, is that really so important?
doch ([emphasis])
    Du kommst doch morgen, oder?        You are coming tomorrow, aren't you?
    Ich habe aber doch Recht!                 But I really am right in this case!
    Es ist doch wahr!                                  It really is true!!
doch mal ([less emphatic than "doch"])
    Komm doch mal zu Besuch!               Hey, come on and come visit us.
    Geben Sie mir doch mal das Salz!      Why don't you go ahead and give me the salt?
eben ([resignation])
    Das sind eben unsere Schwierigkeiten.  <Sigh> So that's what our problems are.
eigentlich (by the way; as a matter of fact)
    Was sind eigentlich Mormonen?             By the way, just what are Mormons?
    Wann kommt eigentlich dein Bruder?     Oh, when is your brother coming?
etwa ([used when a positive answer is feared])
    Kommt heute etwa der Inspektor?                        Is today the day when the inspector is coming?
    Hast du etwa dein ganzes Geld ausgegeben?    Do you mean to tell me you've spent all your money?
ganz und gar (totally; completely)
    Er ist ganz und gar von Sinnen.              He is totally out of his mind.
immer ([generalization]; ever)
    Kommen Sie, wann immer Sie Zeit haben.      Come whenever you have time.
ja [not emphasized]
    Confirmation of a known fact.
        Laufen wir, es ist ja nicht weit zur Uni.     Let's walk; after all, it isn't far to the university.
    Surprise
        Es schneit ja!                             Wow, it's snowing!
          Du siehst ja ganz blaß aus!      My, but you look very pale!
    Emphasis of a statement
          Ich mag Pizza, ja, ich liebe sie sogar.         I like pizza; in fact, I love it.
ja [emphasized]
    Urgent command ("nur ja" is even more emphatic)
         Sieh den Film ja an!                     Man, you've just got to see that movie!
         Glauben Sie das nur ja nicht!    Don't even think of believing that!
    Warning, threat
         Tue das ja nicht!           Don't you dare do that!
         Komm nur ja nicht zu spät nach Hause!    Don't even think about coming home late!
mal ([polite suggestion])
    Gehen wir mal ins Kino.       How about if we go to the movies?
    Komm mal her.                       Why don't you come here?
nicht ([a question for which a positive answer is desired])
    Ist das Haus nicht schön?   Isn't the house pretty?
    Habe ich nicht Recht?          I'm right, aren't I?
nun (so, well)
    War es nun das beste?         So was it the best?
nur [unemphasized]
    Generalization
        Du bekommst, was du nur wünschen kannst. You get everything you every wish for.
    Resignation, apathy
        Er soll nur kommen.          Go ahead and let him come.
    Encouragement (with imperative)
        (Haben Sie) nur Mut!       Just get your courage up. You can do it!
    "If only", when used with subjunctive
        Wenn er nur käme!           If only he would come!
        Wenn wir nur Geld hätten!   If we only had some money!
    Expressing interest or concern in a question.
        Wo ist er nur?                   I wonder where he is?
        Warum kommt er nur nicht?    I wonder why he isn't coming? (Is there something wrong?)
nur [emphasized]
    Warning, threat
        Komm nur nicht nach Hause!    Don't you dare come home! (or there will be a punishment)
        Geh nur nach Hause!                   You get home right now! (a punishment is waiting for you)
schon
    Limitation, reservation
        Der Film war schon gut, aber ich habe mehr erwartet.   The movie was fine as far as it goes, but I was expecting more.
    A condition (usually used with "auch")
        Wenn ich schon in Deutschland bin, will ich auch Deutsch sprechen.
        As long as I'm in Germany, I want to go ahead and speak German.
    Encouraging command
        Komm schon her!          Come over here; everything's ok.
so (by the way, so)
    Wie geht es so mit deiner Arbeit?    So how's it going at work?
sogar (even)
    Das ist sogar eine Menge wert!       In fact, that's really worth a lot of money.
überhaupt (at all, after all)
    Können Sie überhaupt Fremdsprachen?    Can you speak any foreign languages at all?
übrigens (by the way)
    Die Klasse ist übrigens gar nicht so leicht.   By the way, that class isn't all that easy.
vielleicht ([expressing amazement, including ironically])
    Der Mann hat vielleicht gearbeitet!               Boy if he didn't work like crazy!
    Du hast vielleicht dumme Ideen!                    Man, you have some of the stupidest ideas!
wohl
    Probably
        Du bist wohl böse.      I imagine you're upset about that.
        Er wird wohl heute noch kommen.     He'll probably still arrive yet today.
    Perhaps
        Hast du wohl morgen Zeit?         Do you think you might have time tomorrow?
zwar (although, granted, admittedly)
    Der Hauptsitz der Kirche ist zwar in Amerika, aber sie ist eine weltweite Kirche.
    It is true/although the headquarters of the Church are in America, it is a world-wide church.
    Es schneit zwar, aber es ist nicht kalt.
    Although it's snowing, it's not cold.
