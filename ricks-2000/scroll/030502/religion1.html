President Boyd K. Packer to speak at BYU-Idaho

by Kelly Smurthwaite
SMU01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

President Boyd K. Packer visits with Leon Parson of the art department during his visit to Ricks College in 1995. President Packer also spoke at devotional. photo courtesy Public Relations
President Boyd K. Packer, Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles, will be the March 12 devotional speaker.

Because of the large number of students and members of the community expected to attend, special arrangements for overflow have been made in the Taylor Chapel (Taylor Room 120), the Kirkham Auditorium, the Manwaring Center West Ballroom, Little Theater and Special Events Room.

Students are reminded that they are not to save seats for the devotional.

President Packer has been a devotional speaker before. He spoke at a Ricks College devotional in 1995.

BYU-Idaho art instructor Leon Parson met with President Packer when he last came to Ricks College in 1995.

President Packer met with Parson upon the General Authority’s request. President Packer knew of Parson and enjoyed his artwork.

He wrote Parson a few times to congratulate him on his art accomplishments.

President Packer is an artist himself, Parson said.

Being able to meet President Packer was an experience that had a great impact on him and his art work, Parson said.

“I was honored and I ... wondered why he wanted to meet with me,” Parson said.

“We talked about art and hunting. ... It was good to visit with him,” he said.

President Packer has been involved in many Church Educational Programs.

His career includes service as a supervisor of seminaries and Institutes of Religion for the Church.

He served a four-year term as a city councilman in Brigham City, Utah.

He has also been a member of the Administrative Council of Brigham Young University.

President Packer attended Weber College and received his bachelor and master of science degrees from Utah State University. He also earned an Ed.D. in educational administration from BYU.

During World War II, he was a bomber pilot in the Pacific Theater.

President Packer has served as the president of the New England Mission.

He has also written many books and other published works.

On June 5, 1994, President Packer was set apart as the Acting President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles.

Before his current calling, President Packer served as a member of the Quorum of the Twelve.

He was called to be an apostle on April 9, 1970. Prior to this, he was an Assistant to the Quorum of the Twelve. He had this calling for almost nine years.

He is married to the former Donna Smith. They were married July 27, 1947 in the Logan, Utah Temple. They are the parents of 10 children.
