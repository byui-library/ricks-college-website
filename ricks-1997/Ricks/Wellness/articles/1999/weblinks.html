NUTRITION & EXERCISE-RELATED SITES ON THE WEB

Do you believe everything you read when you stumble upon an interesting-looking Web site? Here are some criteria to help in evaluating Web resources:

Author

1. Who is the author of the Web site?
2. Is the author the original creator of the information?
3. Does he or she list his/her credentials, including occupation, position, years of experience?
4. With this information or lack of it, do you feel this person is qualified to write on the topic?

Source

1. What institution (company, government, university, etc.) or Internet provider supports this information?
2. If it is an institution, is it nationally recognized?
3. Is the site a representative of the institution or a personal home page?
4. Does the author's affiliation with this institution appear to bias the information?

Content

1. When was the information created or last updated?
2. What appears to be the purpose for the information (inform, explain, persuade, etc.)?
3. Are the links accurate?

Conclusion

1. Given all you have determined from the above, is this piece of information appropriate?
2. Would you recommend this site to someone else? Why or why not?
