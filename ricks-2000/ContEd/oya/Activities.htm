ACTIVITIES &

WORKSHOPS

 

The week will be filled with activities- both daytime and nighttime. Participants will be involved in activities such as horseback riding, canoeing, climbing and rappelling, handcart trek, black powder rifles, initiative courses, campfire programs, night walk, plus many other workshops. The focus is on personal growth and development. The spiritual aspect is not overlooked. Speakers, nightly devotionals, solo experiences, and a testimony meeting will also be part of the agenda. Those who do not plan to participate in all the activities should not apply.
