We are A Success . . .

We are a success:

When we have lived well, laughed often and loved much. When we gain the respect of intelligent people, and the love of children. When we fill a niche and accomplish a task. When we leave the world better than we found it, whether by an improved idea, a perfect poem or a rescued soul. We are successful if we never lack appreciation of earth’s beauty or fail to express it. If we look for the best in others, and give the best we have.

Robert Louis Stevenson
