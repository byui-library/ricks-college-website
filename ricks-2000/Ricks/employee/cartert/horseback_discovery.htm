Horseback Discovery

To see more pictures "click" here

Horseback Discovery uses horses as transportation to travel to and study some of the most beautiful biological communities in Eastern Idaho and our surrounding states. Some of the biological communities that will be studied are Yellowstone National Park, Teton National Park, Island Park, Big Hole Mountain Range, Palisades, Cold Desert and the Sand Dunes.

Horseback Discovery is 6 credits. The program will be taught first summer term and first fall block.  There will be two sections, section 1 on Tuesdays and section 2 on Thursdays. Both sections will meet for a common class period on Mondays from 3:00 - 3:50 pm in Benson 226.

Registration Procedure

The registration for classes is taken care of internally by Lark Shattuck of the Biology department. The above classes cannot be registered for by the student using the phone system or the website. All students must carry 6 credit hours.

Continuing BYU-I students must have an ecclesiastical endorsement from their bishop and an academic clearance from their advisor. All fines and bills must be paid before registration for classes can take place.

The $200.00 deposit is generally Non-Refundable and Non-Transferable. Each case will be individually considered.

Special Requirements

Participants in Horseback Discovery are required to have an interview with Bro. Tate Carter prior to being accepted for the program. For the safety of the rider and the horse participants’ weight should not exceed 200 pounds. It is important that the health and physical condition of each participant generally be good. Those persons with heart conditions, weight problems, or other conditions that might limit their participation in the program or endanger their health through participation should seek competent medical advice and must disclose these conditions to the program director. Some conditions may preclude enrollment as determined by the director.

Each student will be required to have medical insurance. Adequate family and group policies are sufficient to meet the requirement. If you do not have personal medical insurance, it is available through the university.
