Course Objectives

  • To survey the administrations of Brigham Young through Gordon B. Hinckely.
  • To become familiar with many of the addresses of the prophets, messages of the First Presidency, and proclamations given during the administrations of Brigham Young to Gordon B. Hincely.


Texts
        The texts required for this class are:

  • The scriptures of the LDS church (Bible, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, Pearl of Great Price).
  • Church History in the Fulness of Times


Grading

A. Church Presidents Project (Due April 13 at class time)

Project Goal: (1)To become familiar with each the life of each prophet. (2) To become familiar with the events and accomplishments of their administration as President of the Church.

Criteria of Judgment:
(1) One to two page biography of each prophet's life (single spaced!)
(2) One to two page summary (single spaced!) of events and accomplishments of each prophet's administration.
(3) Include copy of important talks, proclamations, or First Presidency messages given by prophet or other general authorities during administration.
(4) Include references of materials consulted.
(5) The final project must be bound.
(6) Project will be judged on how well it would help a member of the Church understand the second half of Church History.

A rough draft version of Brigham Young through Lorenzo Snow (unbound) will be due February 24 at class time. This will reflect on your mid term grade.

B. Summary of Important Talks

You will find under the Daily Reading Assignment section various talks, Messages of the First Presidency, etc. We will discuss the talk assigned that day during class. Write a one to two page summary of each. Each summary will be due at the beginning of class. If handed in after class, the summary will be considered late.
