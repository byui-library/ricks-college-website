Ricks College News Release
January 23, 2001

Devotional: Richard N. Holzapfel

REXBURG, Idaho — Richard N. Holzapfel, an associate professor in the Department of LDS Church History and Doctrine at Brigham Young University, spoke about the positive side of the Second Coming of Jesus Christ at Tuesday’s Ricks College devotional.

Holzapfel used as a basis for his remarks a scripture in the Old Testament (Daniel 2:44), which contains a prophecy about a stone being cut out of a mountain and filling the whole earth.

"I used to be pessimistic about the future," he said. "We are seeing the literal fulfillment of this prophecy. The gospel is going to places that when I was your age, I never seriously contemplated."

He told about the humble beginnings of the LDS Church from its organization by the Prophet Joseph Smith to modern times.

"Certainly along this road that the church is going, . . . there will be those moments when it (the work) will stop for a second, there will be some hesitation, because of incidents in history," he said. "Sometimes there are wars that slow down our missionary work, sometimes there are legal problems about opening a temple, but because this is the work of God, the next step will be twice as great. The Church will only get stronger."

Holzapfel called listeners to action. "Let us begin today to resolve again anew," he said. "That we’ll be more honest in our doings, more kind, more cautious, better willing to serve, so that we can help this great mission. We have in our grasp the power to save ourselves souls and our kindred dead, and the world needs that message of salvation and hope."

Next Tuesday’s speaker will be Elder Harold Brown, the director of LDS Church Welfare Services. He will speak at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Devotionals are broadcast live and repeated at 9 p.m. on KRIC-FM.
