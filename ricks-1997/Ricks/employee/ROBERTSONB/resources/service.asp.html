Service Learning @ BYU-Idaho


     The service learning opportunity is a one-credit, Service-Learning Practicum, that is paired with IS 235 (Internet Techniques). It gives a student the opportunity to be involved in a service-learning experience with a non-profit organization that could benefit from volunteer help.

     The Faculty Coordinator of Service-Learning, Br. Doug Ladle, is the instructor of the 1 credit class (GS 290D). The service requirement is 40 hours during the semester. The course is a joint-effort between the university and the class instructor, in conjunction with the students and the receiving organization.

     This experience not only benefits the receiving organiation, but also the student in that it will provide practical experience and can be listed on a resume'. Such opportunities can also provide contacts, recommendations and references that can be invaluable after graduation.
