COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 280
Title: Kitchen Management: Buffet/a la Carte/ Fine Dining Credit: 5
Text: Food for Fifty, Shugart
         Classic American Cooking, California Culinary Academy
         Professional Table Service, Meyer

Course Objective:
The students will apply concepts and skills learned in all prerequisite classes to plan and prepare buffets and a la carte menus for professional service.

Description:
The capstone course for students planning to work in food service.  Weekly luncheons are planned and prepared for the public.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Students must act as executive chef for at least one buffet or one al a carte event.  A complete report of the event is required; including planning sheets, pricing, vendors, and peer evaluations.
