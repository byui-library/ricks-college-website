Geochemistry
GEOCHEMISTRY
CLASS CODE: GEOL 420 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Applying elementary chemical principles to understand geologic processes and materials.
DESCRIPTION: This course teaches students how to apply the principles of chemistry to geologic systems and materials. Both high- and low-temperature systems and materials will be covered.
TOPICS: Geochemical Behavior of the ElementsStable, Radiogenic, & Cosmogenic Isotope GeochemistryTrace Element GeochemistryCrystal ChemistryBiogeochemistryTransport, Advection, & DiffusionGeochemistry of Natural Water SystemsGeochemistry of Sedimentary Rock SystemsGeochemistry of Igneous Rock SystemsGeochemistry of Metamorphic Rock SystemsWhole-Earth & Planetary GeochemistryAnalytical GeochemistryGeochemical Modeling
OBJECTIVES: The main objective is for students to be able to understand and use basic chemical principles in studying natural systems and materials. Other objectives include giving students experience in: experimental geochemistry, numerical modeling of geochemical systems, research, scientific writing and speaking, and the geologic literature.
REQUIREMENTS: This class requires significant preparation for and participation in class. Field trips are also required.
PREREQUISITES: Geol 111, Chem 105, (Chem 106 recommended)
