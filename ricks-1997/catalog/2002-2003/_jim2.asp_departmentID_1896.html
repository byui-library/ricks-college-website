    Department of Physical Education
    The  Department  of  Physical  Education  is  committed  to  serving  a  wide  range  of  special  interests  for  the  general  student  body  as  well  as  faculty  and  staff.    The  well-equipped  Hart  Physical  Education  Building  includes  three  gymnasiums,  four  weight  rooms  with  the  very  latest  in  weight  training  and  fitness  equipment,  a  wrestling  room,  an  Olympic  size  pool,  and  a  dance  studio.

    Course  work  has  been  designed  to  assist  the  undergraduate  planning  to  continue  a  four  year  education,  students  planning  to  transfer  to  another  university  to  major  in  Physical  Education,  students  who  need  to  fill  General  Education  requirements,  and  all  who  desire  to  learn  proper  methods  of  teaching  physical  activities  leading  to  fitness.    The  health  and  fitness  portion  of  the  General  Education  program  is  offered  jointly  with  the  Department  of  Health  Science.

    Course Descriptions

    PE*C 100 Aerobic Fitness (1:0:2)
    Combines exercise and dance to fit three individual fitness levels: beginning, intermediate, advance. Includes kick boxing.
     
    PE*C 101 Water Aerobics (1:0:2)
    Exercise in water for all ability levels.
     
    PE*C 110 Fencing (1:0:2)
    To introduce the student to the sport of fencing through experience with the French foil and introduction to the 'Epee.'
     
    PE*C 115 Military Style Physical Fitness (1:0:0)
    A military-style fitness program open to all students.
     
    PE*C 115 Military Style Physical Fitness (1:0:2)
    A military-style fitness program open to all students.
     
    PE*C 117 Racquetball Beginning (1:0:2)
    Class designed to teach the basic skills, fundamentals, and techniques required to play racquetball effectively.
     
    PE*C 118 Wallyball (1:0:2)
    Designed to introduce and instruct students in basic wallyball skills.
     
    PE*C 119 Racquetball Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: PEC 117 or have played racquetball enough to know the basic rules, strategies, and fundamentals of beginning racquetball.
    An advanced course in the game of racquetball providing instruction in advanced skills and strategies. Higher level competition than beginning racquetball classes.
     
    PE*C 121 Softball (1:0:2)
    This is a block class teaching fundamentals of softball.
     
    PE*C 123 Badminton Beginning (1:0:2)
    Designed to teach the basic fundamentals of badminton to a beginning student.
     
    PE*C 124 Badminton Int (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Beginning Badminton or have played the game enough to know and exhibit the ability to play beginning badminton
    Designed to teach strategies beyond the basic fundamentals of badminton
     
    PE*C 126 Archery Beginning (1:0:2)
    Designed to introduce the sport of archer through hands-on application of using the bow and arrow through lecture, demonstration, and practice of archery skills.
     
    PE*C 127 Archery Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: PEC 126 or previous archery experience and skills.
    Designed to help student learn more advanced techniques through hands-on application of using the bow and arrow.
     
    PE*C 128 Bowling Beginning (1:0:2)
    Fee:  $32.00
    Teaches basic skills, fundamentals and techniques required to bowl effectively.
     
    PE*C 131 Golf (1:0:2)
    Teach basic skills, fundamentals, and techniques of golf.
     
    PE*C 133 Tennis Beginning (1:0:2)
    This is a class for beginning students who do not have the basic skills of playing tennis.
     
    PE*C 134 Tennis Intermediate (1:0:2)
    A class for students who have progressed beyond the skill of beginning tennis
     
    PE*C 139 Cardio Fitness (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: PEC 177
    Consists of four written assignments with no book required but access to a Fitness for Life text recommended. Prepare workout sachedule and report workouts weekly.
     
    PE*C 140 Basketball Beginning (1:0:2)
    A basketball class to instruct students with basic skills of basketball.
     
    PE*C 141 Basketball Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Beginning Basketball PEC 140 or high school basketball
    A basketball class with students who have learned the basic skills.
     
    PE*C 144 Volleyball Beginning (1:0:2)
    This is a class to for students who do not have volleyball skills to learn the basics of volleyball.
     
    PE*C 145 Volleyball Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: 1. Beginning volleyball or adequate basic volleyball skills and knowledge. 2. Must have offensive and defensive knowledge and ability.
    A class for students who know basic skills of volleyball and will provide an opportunity to use these skills to improve their game.
     
    PE*C 147 Soccer Beginning (1:0:2)
    To teach the fundamental skills and drills of Soccer.
     
    PE*C 148 Intermediate Soccer (1:0:0)
    Prerequisite: 1. Must be experienced soccer players. 2. Must have played competitive soccer.
    A class for experienced students who wish to play team soccer.
     
    PE*C 157 Varsity Baseball (1:0:10)
    Prerequisite: Approved by head coach of varsity baseball and can only be added after school has started in each semester.
    Designed to help student develop baseball skills and understand techniques and fundamentals of the game.
     
    PE*C 160 Swimming Beginning (1:0:2)
    Designed for non-swimmers and weak swimmers. The course teaches the six main strokes, basic diving, and safety skills.
     
    PE*C 161 Swimming Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Pass Beginning Swimming class or exhibit basic skills of swimming.
    An intermediate swimming class designed to instruct students beyond the basic swimming skills and strokes.
     
    PE*C 162 Swimming Advanced (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Pass Intermediate Swim Class or exhibit skills of intermediate swimmer.
    An advanced swimming class designed to instruct students beyond intermediate swimming skills and strokes.
     
    PE*C 173 Gymnastics Beginning (1:0:2)
    Designed to introduce coed students to all apparatus and beginnming skills in gymnastics.
     
    PE*C 174 Gymnastics Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Beginning Gymnastics class or basic gymnastics instruction and ability.
    A class to teach students beyond the basic skills of gymnastics.
     
    PE*C 175 Adaptive Physical Education (1:0:2)
    Fullfills GE Basic Skills requirement.
    Prerequisite: An existing Physical Therapy problem.
    A class for students who are unable to participate in regular physical education classes and who need individual instruction.
     
    PE*C 177 Fitness for Living (1:1:1)
    Fullfills GE Basic Skills requirement.
    An individualized approach to fitness with lectures and instruction.
     
    PE*C 178 Weight Training (1:0:2)
    A class to teach students proper weight training techniques.
     
    PE*C 195 Skiing (1:0:5)
    Fee:  $240.00

    PE*C 196 Snowboarding (1:0:2)
    Fee:  $240.00
    Recreational snowboarding, one day a week for ten weeks at Grand targhee Resort with professional instructors
     
    PE*C 204 Aerobic Fitness Teaching Techniques (2:2:1)
    Prerequisite: PEC 100
    Designed to offer information and techniques required to teach at a professional level in the fitness industry.
     
    PE*C 205 Intramural Sports - Administration & Organization (2:2:1)
    A study of the objectives, organization, and supervision of Intramural and recreation sports programs.
     
    PE*C 206 Sports Officiating (2:2:0)
    Rules, techniques, problems, and procedures in officiating football, basketball and other team and individual sports. (fall)
     
    PE*C 207 Sports Offic (2:2:0)
    Rules, techniques, proglems, and procedures in officiating basketball, volleyball and softball. (W)
     
    PE*C 264 Lifeguarding (3:4:0)
    Prerequisite: At least 15 years of age. Able to swim at least 500 yards Good strong breast stroke kick or scissor kick Strong swimming skills.
    Lifeguard training covers CPR, first aid, and rescue techniques needed to become a lifeguard. It also covers job duties and how to handle a water emergency.
     
    PE*C 268 Water Safety Instruction (3:5:0)
    Prerequisite: Intermediate swimming skills.
    This methods course is designed for potential swimming instructors.
     
    PE*C 299 Professional Seminar (1-3:0:0)
    Prerequisite: PEC 350
    Teaching assistant to Physical Education instructor.
     
    PE*C 320 Motor Learning (2:2:0)
    Prerequisite: Class should be taken during sophomore or junior year.
    Motor learning, control, and development in improving skill by the performer.
     
    PE*C 330 Sports Psychology (2:2:0)
    Prerequisite: PEC 350 PEC 333 PEC 334
    Psychological principals of activity in sports and activity.
     
    PE*C 332 Teaching Methods (2:2:1)
    Teaching methods of softball and baseball. Teaching methods of volleyball.
     
    PE*C 333 Teaching Methods (2:2:1)
    Teaching methods of football and wrestling.
     
    PE*C 334 Team Sports (2:2:1)
    Prerequisite: Volleyball prerequisite: Must have PEC 145. Basketball prerequisite: Must have PEC 141.
    Teaching methods of golf and tennis. Teaching methods of basketball.
     
    PE*C 335 Teaching Methods (2:2:1)
    Teaching methods of soccer and track.
     
    PE*C 350 Eval & Adm (3:3:0)
    Lecture class designed to familiarize the student with a wide variety of administrative responsibility.
     
    PE*C 375 Exercise Physiology (2:3:0)
    Prerequisite: It is suggested that you take these classes as prerequisites, but not manditory: BS 264,BS 265, and HS 131

    PE*W 178 Weight Training (1:2:0)
