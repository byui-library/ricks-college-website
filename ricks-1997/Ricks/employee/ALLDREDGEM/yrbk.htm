A yearbook is issued annually to give narrative, statistical, and directory information.
Rixida (OV2 LD4711.R52A3 ) is the yearbook produced to give this type of information about our student body.
Government agencies publish yearbooks.
Organizations print this type of publication.

Statistical Abstract of the United States is a general yearbook which also is called a compendium.
Compendium is a term that may also be used to describe this type of reference tool that gives a brief summary of a larger field. Census Bureau of the U.S. Commerce Department uses this publication to summarize many other statistical publications.

Either use the table of contents or index to find information.
For example, the table of contents will give the page where vital statistics start.
The first pages of each section will give narrative explanations of terminology.
Vital statistics include birth, death, abortion, fetal death, fertility, life expectancy, marriage and divorce numbers.

The index, however, will give the specific table number of a chart by subject.
See also cross references should be consulted, as needed.
In the example, births and birth rates have a separate listing in the index.

When using the tables and charts look for indications of the unit of measurement used.
The number may stand for thousands or millions.
The number could be part of a ratio, as in "per thousand."

The bottom of the table will give the source of the information.
Often the source is a government publication which could be consulted for more recent or more detailed information.
Our library is a selective depository for government documents.
Monthly Vital Statistics is a microfiche with call number HE20.6217:.
Check our catalog for this or other publications.

The United Nations also publishes similar yearbooks that will give statistics from around the world.
Demographic Yearbook will give similar marriage rates and number for various countries.
Remember that as an international organization the information is given in both French and British English.

Explanations are found in footnotes which are at the end of the table.
The table for death rates by cause has an abbreviated mortality number.
The footnote for this column then refers the reader to Technical Notes.
Using the table of contents, Technical Notes for vital statistics are located.
A comparison of abbreviated mortality number and International Classification of Disease number is made.
The World Health Organization uses the International Classification of Diseases (ICD).

The explanation in Statistical Abstract of the United States gives a reference which explains the number system.

    Yearbooks is a broad group of publications.
    More specific or related types of yearbooks include almanacs and directories.

    Almanacs were published originally to give calendar and weather forecast.
    Almanacs now contain a wide variety of facts about places, people, and events.
    Almanacs tend to not be limited to current facts but may include lists of historical events or people.

    HANDBOOKS

    Handbooks are literally small books that can be held in the hand.
    They treat broad subjects briefly.
    A handbook to etiquette will give a brief discussion of broad topics, such as weddings and correspondence.
    Organizations and government agencies publish handbooks.
    Use handbooks to find:

    • how many...
    • how do you...
    • occupations
    • quotations
    • travel
    • what happened...
    • when did...
    • who wrote...
    General handbooks and manuals give the extremes of a subject.
    The best and worst or first of everything.
    They are also called miscellanies.
    They contain trivia or facts that may be difficult to locate elsewhere.
    Titles at our library include:
    • Guinness Book of World Records (Ref AG243.G87)
    • Famous First Facts (Ref AG5.K315)
