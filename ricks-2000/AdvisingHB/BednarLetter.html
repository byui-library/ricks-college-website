Dear Colleagues:

Advising students is an essential activity that supports and enhances the personalized learning climate we seek to foster at Ricks College. I have always considered advising to be a specific type and extension of teaching, a faculty member's primary responsibility. Ultimately, our stewardship as teachers includes both classroom instruction and advising.

The Ricks College Advising Handbook has been compiled to provide an easy-to-use reference book as we perform this important service. Please read carefully and become familiar with the information in this handbook; it will increase your effectiveness as advisors.

Effective advising produces a number of important outcomes with which we should all be familiar:

Increased motivation as students learn from faculty advisors in a one-on-one setting,

Enhanced retention as students identify educational goals and plan appropriately,

Improved vocational and career opportunities, and

Effective and efficient use of time and financial resources as students complete programs of study on time.

Certainly, this important work warrants our best efforts!

We desire to continually improve the advising process at Ricks College and welcome any suggestion you may have. Please share your ideas and suggestions with Jacque Weekes, director of Academic Advisement/Transfer Articulation. Your input is both needed and valued.

I express my appreciation for all you do to make Ricks College the unique and marvelous institution it is. Best wishes to you in this vital phase of our work at Ricks College.

Sincerely,

David A. Bednar
President
