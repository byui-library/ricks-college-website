Repair & Maintenance Welding
GTA WELDING
CLASS CODE: WELD 117 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: MECHANICAL ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Welding techniques, principles, theory, and practices needed for the repair of broken weldable/ repairable components. Processes include TB, SMAW, GTAW, GMAW, AAC, PAC. Study of the physical, mechanical, and chemical properties and composition of the materials to be repaired.
DESCRIPTION: A course in welding techniques, principles, theory, and practices needed for the repair of broken weldable/ repairable components.
TOPICS: Processes used will include TB, SMAW, GTAW, GMAW, AAC, PAC. We will study the physical, mechanical, and chemical properties and composition of the materials to be repaired.
OBJECTIVES: 1. Students will be able to identify different types of metals using standard identification methods: spark test, hardness, etc.
2. Students will be able to select the correct/best repair procedures.
3. Students will be able to make acceptable repairs.
REQUIREMENTS: A. Required Reading: Modern Welding Technology
B. Reference Reading: Metals and How to Weld Them
The Procedure Handbook of Arc Welding
Structural Welding Code Steel ANSI/AWS D1.1-96
C. Tests and Quizzes
1. Written: Tests and quizzes will be given covering reading, lecture/discussion, and lab assignments, and will consist of problem-solving, essay, and multiple choice questions.
2. Skills Tests: Skills tests will consist of problem-solving exercises taken from daily lab assignments.

D. Attendance: Students are responsible for class attendance. No absences, whether approved by the College for participation in College-sponsored activities or necessitated by sickness or other personal emergencies, are “excused” in the sense that the student is relieved from work assigned or carried out by the class during the absence. To ensure continuity, students must make up work missed. In most cases, repeated absences will result in a lower grade or a failure.

E. Homework: On the average, students should plan to devote at least four hours of homework time per week to this course.
PREREQUISITES: Weld 105 or concurrent enrollment
OTHER: Materials: Gloves, helmet with 10 lens, slip joint pliers, tip cleaner (Wypo) standard set, safety glasses, coveralls, ear plugs, silver pencil, notebook, loose leaf binder, pen and pencil, leather boots, cutting goggles (#5 lens).

(Students must wear safety glasses at all times while in lab sessions.)
