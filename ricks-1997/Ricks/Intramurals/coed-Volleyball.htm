Coed-Volleyball

1. The games will start on the hour. The games will consist of the best of 3 games to 15.

2. We will play with teams of four. Two makes and two females at all times. You may have as many alternates as you want. You may play short if you have at least one male and female.

3. General volleyball rules apply.
