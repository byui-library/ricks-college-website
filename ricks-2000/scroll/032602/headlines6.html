The Lighter Side:
90-year-old woman defends herself

STAMPS, Ark. (AP) — The basketball, gardening and power walks paid off for 90-year-old Illa Hooper when she discovered a burglar in her home. Hooper was able to get away from the man after hitting him with her cane.

As it turned out, the 21-year-old suspect was under investigation by the Arkansas State Police in another case.

James Sharp was being held Thursday in the Lafayette County Sheriff’s Office in Lewisville on charges in the burglary and on a charge of rape of a 13-year-old in the state police case.

Hooper, who will turn 91 on May 15, has known Sharp since he was 13, but had not seen him in nearly two years, she said.

“I wish it wouldn’t have happened,” she said. “The man needs help. Although I’m the most tender-hearted thing, I believe he broke man’s law and God’s law.”

Born and raised in Union County, Hooper retired in 1972 after working 41 years as an executive secretary. She leads an active life and loves sports. She was a guard on her high school basketball team.

Weather permitting, Hooper works in her garden. Until health problems interfered, she took “power walks” almost daily at the Stamps City Park.

Hooper, whose friends call her “Miss Ike,” told the Banner-News in Magnolia that on the morning of March 14, a bright light awakened her. At first, she thought it was lightning, but then realized it was a flashlight and a man was in her bedroom.

Hooper grabbed a cane that she keeps at the head of her bed and struck the man two or three times across his back, telling him to get out of her house or she would get her gun and shoot him, she said.

She got around the man and ran from her home. She crossed the highway to a neighbor’s house where she called police.
