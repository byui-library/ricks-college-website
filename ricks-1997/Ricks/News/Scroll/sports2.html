Matadors, like a stroll in park for the Vikings, 56-14
By John Mortensen, Scroll Staff



Ricks receiver Ben Rex hauls in a reception in Ricks' thrashing of Arizona Western Saturday. Rex was one of nine Ricks receivers to catch a pass in the game .

by Brian Passey /scroll staff

 


The Ricks College football team left Arizona Western College without options as the Vikings defeated the Matodors 56-14 Saturday.
Arizona Western successfully ran the option on their opening drive for a touchdown, but that would be all. Ricks answered with six straight touchdowns and held the Matodors to three pass completions.
"After the first drive, we shut them off," Coach Ron Haun said. "We took away what they like to do best."
"(The defense) dug down and did what we had to do," Donny Allred, a sophomore from Garland, Utah, said.
Ricks got big plays from Pat Williams and Tyler Scharman, as well as great play from the offensive line and defense.
Williams, a freshman from Bountiful, Utah, caught three passes for 89 yards and two touchdowns. He also had an 18-yard reverse run for a touchdown in the first quarter.
"Their defensive backs played far off the ball," Williams said. "With one fake, we were able to get open."
Scharman, a sophomore from Salt Lake City, carried the ball eight times for 124 yards and two touchdowns, each from 21 yards out.
Tom Pace, a sophomore from Mesa, Ariz., compiled 144 yards of total offense and scored two touchdowns.
Tight end Spencer Nead, a freshman from Tetonia, Idaho, also caught a 17-yard touchdown pass.
Saturday, the 5-1 Ricks squad will travel to Phoenix Community College.
