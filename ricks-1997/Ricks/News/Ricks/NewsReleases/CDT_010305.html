Ricks College News Release
March 5, 2001

CDT concert opens March 8 at Ricks

REXBURG, Idaho — The Ricks College Contemporary Dance Theatre will present a concert March 8-10 at 7:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium.

Admission is $6 for the general public and $1 for Ricks students.

Ricks faculty member Wendy Bone says the annual showcase will include a variety of modern dances. Along with old favorites such as "Would You Like Butter with That?," a jazzed up version of "Popcorn Popping on the Apricot Tree" and "This Day," the company will perform some new hip-hop and modern dances.

CDT also will perform "Because of Thy Exceeding Faith," a portrayal and dedication to the women of the Book of Mormon. The dance features Sariah, the mother of Nephi; Abish, the inspired Lamanite servant; King Lamoni’s wife, a woman that showed greater faith than all the Nephites; and the mothers of the 2,000 stripling warriors, who "had been taught by their mothers, that if they did not doubt, God would deliver them."(Alma 56: 44-48).
