COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 150
Title: Food Service Management I Credit: 1
Text: West &Wood’s Introduction to Foodservice, Payne-Palacio, 1997

Course Objective:
At the completion of this class the student should be able to:
- Describe types of foodservice operations in commercial and institutional industry
- Describe the marketing channel and the formal and informal purchasing procedures
- State guidelines in the proper management of receiving and storing food products
- Demonstrate how to use tools of management in foodservice production
- Identify factors in maintaining cost control
- Discuss records used for financial management of the foodservice.
-Describe guidelines in facility planning and design

Description:
Concepts of Food Service management: cash operation, purchasing, production, waste control, cost control, and presentation.
