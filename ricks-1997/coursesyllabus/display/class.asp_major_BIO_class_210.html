Plant Classification
PLANT CLASSIF
CLASS CODE: BIO 210 CREDITS:  4 FEE:  $30.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A basic course in vascular plant classification, systematics, and nomenclature, including a survey of common or important vascular plant families, with emphasis on flowering plants and the local flora. Students will also learn the skills necessary for plant identification.
DESCRIPTION: A basic course in vascular plant classification that explores our past and present understanding of plant systematics and the importance of botanical nomenclature. A survey of selected vascular plant families is included, with emphases on flowering plants and the local flora. Students will also learn the skills necessary for plant identification.
TOPICS: Systematics, taxonomy, classification, botanical nomenclature, identification, using keys, vascular plant diversity, herbarium use and collection techniques, nomenclatural types, and vegetative terminology.
OBJECTIVES: Students will gain an understanding and appreciation of the phylogenetic relationships within the plant kingdom, how plants are classified, the skills and techniques needed for plant identification, and the characteristics of various vascular plant families.
REQUIREMENTS: Attend classes and labs, collect and identify local plants, construct an identification key, complete quizzes and exams.
PREREQUISITES: BIO 208 or consent of the instructor
