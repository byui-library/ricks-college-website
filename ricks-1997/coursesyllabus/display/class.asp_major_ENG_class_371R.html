CLASS CODE: ENG 371R CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Focuses on several contemporary creative writers, exploring themes and literary elements. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
DESCRIPTION: Focuses on several contemporary creative writers, exploring themes and literary elements. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
TOPICS: Literary analyses of authors' works; distinctive characteristics of contemporary literature; exploration of contemporary creative writers in relationship to cultural contexts.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Discussing thematic and literary styles of individual authors. (2) Identifying and analyzing distinctive characteristics of contemporary literature.
REQUIREMENTS: Read stories, novels, plays, poems, and essays by contemporary authors. Write analytical essays
including a paper explicating or interpreting a single text and a paper comparing several texts from a single author or a single theme developed by several authors. Participate in class discussion.
