WireFrame  Connecting Bar

"Put a person in darkness and ask him to walk in a straight path and he cannot do it for very long, if at all. We must have the illumination in order to have direction, and the gospel of Jesus Christ gives us illumination and direction, as well as motivation.", Neal A. Maxwell.

Construct a 3-dimensional wireframe model of the connecting bar shown in Fig 7-3-B, page 161 of your text.   Over the past years, there have been some mistakes on the dimensions of this figure.  For consistency across the class, please note the following dimensions:  The thickness of the body of the part is to be 28 mm, the thickness of the tabs on both ends is to be 16 mm.  When creating the model, orient the part such that the longest dimension is horizontal in the front view.

Show ALL lines as object lines on the model. Do NOT add dimensions, hiddenlines, or centerlines. Do NOT place inside a titleblock. Make sure that your name appears someplace on the model.

Place the finished model in the dropbox using the keyword bar after your last and first names. No hard copy is required for this assignment.
