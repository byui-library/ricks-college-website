ENERGY ESSENTIALS

Some time ago someone sent me the following information pertaining to how we might be able to increase our energy. I felt the contents would be helpful, but unfortunately I do not have the source.

EAT RIGHT

Consume a variety of foods so you're getting energy-boosters like magnesium, zinc, iron, B vitamins and the antioxidants beta carotene and vitamins A and E. For quick lifts: Keep lunch light - and snack on raisins, low-fat granola for fig bars later in the afternoon to get fast-fuel carbohydrates.

SLEEP TIGHT

Wake up and go to bed at the same time each day to help your biological clock run smoothly. For a restful night: Avoid caffeine and alcohol, which can disturb sleep...drink a glass of warm milk or listen to relaxing music before bed...keep bedtime reading light.

SEE FRIENDS

Numerous studies suggest friendships boost your health and happiness. Example: People with close relationships live longer and handle personal setbacks better.

KEEP FIT

Exercise strengthens your heart, lung and muscles to help you do more physically. But it also increases the supply of oxygen to your brain, boosting alertness so you can absorb information quicker and perform other mental tasks faster.

GET MOTIVATED

Set frequent goals and reward yourself when you meet them. Your accomplishments will energize you reach higher and higher.
