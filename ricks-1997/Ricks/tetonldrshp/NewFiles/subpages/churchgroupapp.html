The Leadership and Service Institute (LSI) seeks to develop and prepare students for leadership and service in the family, Church, workplace, and community. Our threefold mission is to:

o Promote Leadership and Personal Development
o Instill Life-long Service Values through Participation
o Strengthen Families through Marriage Enrichment

LSI’s primary objective is to serve the students of Ricks College. However, the Teton Lodge facilities are available for church groups on Tuesday evenings only for leadership training. The Teton Lodge is not to be used for social or recreational activities. This facility is made available because of the generosity of its owner and Ricks College donors.

Application

To schedule the lodge return a completed application to Van Crawford in MC 380 (+0790). Reservations are granted on a first-come, first-served basis. Upon approval, a letter of confirmation will be sent to the presiding church officer.
