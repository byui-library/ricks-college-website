* Music Major Admission

All entering freshman are eligible for pre-music major courses.  Course and faculty resources, however,  limit the number of sophomores admitted each year as voice majors.  Admission as a music major and clearance for subsequent courses is contingent on Department of Music review of student's abilities and grades in the following courses:

  • M196 Music Theory I
  • M194 Aural Skills I
  • M156/157 or M118 Piano Skills and Proficiency
  • M149A or 149B Foundations and Explorations of Music
  • M155/160 Applied Music Lessons
  • M3xx Choral Ensemble(s)

Students will be notified by June 1 following successful completion of the classes listed above.

** General Education

Sequence for GE courses is interchangeable but Eng 111, Eng 311, and Hum 202 are prerequisite for Music 302/303/304.  Math 108 or higher is a prerequisite for Physics 167 and 112.

(Return to Top)

Opera Workshop Requirement

Two semesters of M317 Opera Workshop are required for this degree.  Students may elect to fill this by replacing tow semesters of the choral ensemble requirement at any time during the eight semester sequence.  Students may also elect to participate in a choral ensemble and Opera Workshop concurrently.

(Return to Top)

Additional Credits for Graduation

Each baccalaureate at BYU-Idaho requires 120 credits to graduate.  In addition to the 96 credits of GE, Religion, & Music specified in the suggested sequence above and the summary below, the Bachelor of Musical Arts: Voice Emphasis Degree requires 24 credits of a minor or predetermined clusters.  Each student should visit with their advisor to propose a directed program of study for these additional credits.
