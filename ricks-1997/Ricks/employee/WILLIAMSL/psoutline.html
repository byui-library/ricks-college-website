Historic Speeches

OUTLINE INSTRUCTIONS:

Your speech outlines must:
1. Use complete sentences
2. Have separate portions for each of the three main parts, Introduction, Main Body, Conclusion.
3. Have the purpose and structure at the beginning.
4. Say what kind of support each section uses
5. You may use italic notes to yourself to help you remember what to do. Because many use note cards rather than an outline in delivering a speech, this is not required.
6. Make your central idea as one sentence at the beginning of the outline.
7. Include a source list of at least two sources at the end of your outline.
8. Occasionally, single phrases are acceptable, but only as used here, as breakers for your outline or self-evident ideas.
9. Your outline must have at the very top your speech title, which differs from the central idea.

SAMPLE OUTLINE:

CLA: A newly discovered nutrient

Purpose: To inform
Structure: Thematic
Central Idea: Conjugated Linoleic Acid, a newly discovered nutrient, may hold the key to reducing your risk of cancer, to cutting your chances of heart diseases and to increasing your ability to lose weight and keep it off.

INTRODUCTION

I. When you hear beef sizzle on a charcoal grill, you are hearing chemical reactions. (ATTENTION-GETTER, EXAMPLE)

A. Some believe those chemical reactions are harmful.

B. University of Wisconsin's Michael Pariza discovered that good things are there too. (EXAMPLE)

C. The specific nutrient he discovered was CLA-the acronym for congugated linoleic acid. (EXPLANATION)
D. CLA, an essential fat, has been shown in laboratory animals to cut the risk of cancer, to cut the amount of fat deposition on the veins, to cut the amount of fat accumulating on the body, to curb appetite, to potentially curb the effects of osteoporosis and to curb the effects of immune response.(REASON FOR LISTENING, EXPLANATION)

E. CLA is a kind of fat, but a kind you probably want to get more of in your diet. Scientists are discovering that CLA is actually occuring less in our diets than before, so supplements may be important for your diet. (REASON FOR LISTENING, EXPLANATION)

MAIN BODY

I. FATTY ACID EXPLANTION
A. Fat is a way the body stores energy.
B. Fat makes up the membrane of each cell. Everything must pass through fat to get to the interior of the cell. Fat, though you may think it is bad, is often necessary. (COMPARISON/CONTRAST; EXPLANATION)
C. Three acids can't be synthesized, but must be derived from diet. One of them is linoleic acid. These are the essential fatty acids. (EXPLANATION)
D. Congugated linoleic acid is converted from linoleic acid in the stomach of ruminant animals -- those that have multiple stomachs, such as cows and lambs. (EXPLANATION)

E. Dr. Michael Pariza discovered the effects of CLA on cancer in animals in research dating back to 1979. He isolated CLA in 1987.(EXAMPLE)

II. CLA seems to cut the risk of cancer in animals.
A. Scientists spread some known cancer-causing agents on the skin of certain animals. On some they spread CLA, too. Those with CLA were less likely to contract a tumor and had fewer tumors when they did.
GIVE SOURCE IN TALK (AUTHORITATIVE TESTIMONY)
1. Pariza quote from the journal article . . . testimony.

B. They did similar things with breast tumors. This is significant because one in eight American women will likely be diagnosed with a breast tumor at some point in their lives. (EXAMPLE, STATISTIC, GIVE SOURCES)

C. Similar research has been done on colon cancer and other cancer. (EXAMPLE, AUTHORITATIVE TESTIMONY) AGAIN GIVE SOURCES
1. Pariza quote.

D. Though all of these studies have been done in animals, that CLA helps prevent cancer in a variety of different animals and under a variety of circumstances is good evidence it has the same effect in humans. (EXPLANATION)

II. CLA has other important physiological effects.
GIVE SOURCES -- LOOK UP HERE
A. CLA is a saturated fatty acid, usually associated with higher risks of heart disease. In this case, however, it had less risk. An amazing result. (AUTHORITATIVE TESTIMONY, EXAMPLE)

B. CLA-supplemented pigs had lower bodyfat than controls. (EXAMPLE)

C. CLA-supplemented animals had higher bone mass.

D. CLA-supplemented animals ate 30 percent less. (STATISTIC)

E. CLA-supplemented animals ate more during times of illness. (EXAMPLE, EXPLANATION)

III. This nutrient has important, specific results for your health:
REMEMBER THE 3-to-5 second rule!

A. The U.S. Centers for Disease control notes that heart disease is the number one killer in the United States. Indeed, about 50 percent of all deaths in the U.S. are related to the cardiovascular system. Anything to lower that risk is significant. (STATISTIC)

B. Lower bodyfat, and sometimes lower weight, means a person is stronger and looks better. (EXPLANATION)

C. Higher bone mass may mean a lowered risk of a top-ten killer among women, osteoporosis. (EXPLANATION, STATISTIC)

D. Eating less means a great deal for weight management because curbing the appetite means eating fewer calories. (EXPLANATION)

E. Eating less in the animal industry is significant because less feed per animal means more animals eating less food and, hence, more food for humans. (EXPLANATION)

F. When we get sick, our immune system goes through what is called the "catabolic cascade." At times like that, appetite dies, we lose substantial, unhealthy weight. We feel lousy. With CLA, animals eat more, don't lose the weight. This means they feel better when they are sick. It means they don't lose weight, so animal's food processing is more efficient. It means people with diseases like AIDS are less likely to waste away. (EXPANATION, AUTHORITATIVE TESTIMONY)

IV. We get less CLA in our diets than we used to.
A. Ever wondered why your parents weighed less than we do, but they probably eat more meat, and, therefore, more fat? (RHETORICAL QUESTION)

B. In the 1980s, Americans gained, on the average, about eight pounds per person, The Washington Post said. (STATISTIC)

C. One study out of Australia showed that Australian cows, which eat live grasses on their rangeland, have a higher body percentage of CLA than American cows, primarily fed dried, processed grains. (EXPLANATION, EXAMPLE)

D. This means that cows that once fed on the range, no longer can convert as much CLA in their stomachs as before. This means we are more likely to gain fat and more likely to gain weight than our parents, even while consuming less red meat. (EXPLANATION)

V. CLA is available as a nutritional supplement.

A. This nutrient is available at many health food stores around the world. (EXAMPLE).

III. Conclusion -- A POSITIVE VISION OF THE FUTURE

I. CLA may someday be as well known and as vital as vitamin C.

A. Scurvy, which comes from vitamin c deficiency killed thousands through the last century, including many Mormon pioneers struggling across the plains. (EXAMPLE)

B. Could it be that some of our other, modern disease, in some way are like scurvy, based in part on poor nutrition? (RHETORICAL QUESTION)

C. Perhaps so, but as with the discovery of vitamin C, this discovery of the last decade could save millions of lives.

D. Imagine something that made you look better, feel better, live longer and healthier, help solve world hunger problems.

E. Preliminary science on this nutrient promises all those things . . . and more.

F. CLA, like vitamin C before it, could become the greatest discovery in nutrition this century.
