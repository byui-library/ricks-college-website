Psychology Club



  The Ricks College Psychology Department sponsors the Psychology Club for those students who desire to work together to develop leadership capabilities and practices, and to have fun together with other psychology majors.

    The activities of the the club are dependent on its members.

    In the past, they have included such things as the following:
        -An overnight getaway to the Leadership Institute with special lectures and activities.
        -A Christmas collection for gifts for a needy family.
        -Special lectures from professionals in different fields of psychology.
        -Psychology Experiment Replications
        -Socials
        -Creation of Homecoming Floats and other parade entries.
        -Swimming and picnics at Green Canyon
