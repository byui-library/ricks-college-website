CLOTHING &

EQUIPMENT LIST

EACH PARTICIPANT SHOULD PLAN TO BRING

 ONLY THE FOLLOWING EQUIPMENT

NO EXTREME APPEARANCES WILL BE ALLOWED

 

  T Coat

  T Sweater

  T Rain poncho or equivalent

         This is MANDATORY - MUST BE SHOWN AT TIME OF CHECK-IN.

         Please note that having quality rain gear is very important! Cheap items,

         such as garbage bags, do not stand up to the activities we are involved with.

  T Extra Pants NO SHORTS

  T Extra Shirts -- long sleeved or t-shirts NO TANK TOPS

  T Extra Underwear

  T Extra Socks

  T Good Hiking Boots (broken in) and/or tennis shoes (most people prefer both)

         NO SANDALS OR FLIP FLOPS

  T Hat (for sun)

  T Water Bottle This is MANDATORY

  T Insect Repellent

  T *Sleeping Bag

  T *Sleeping Pad

  T Towel

  T Washcloth

  T Soap (Biodegradable)

  T Personal Toiletries

  T Personal Medicine

  T Scriptures (old set)

  T Small Notebook

  T Pencil or Pen

  T Day Pack (used to carry personal items to workshops -- put your name on it)

  T Flashlight (extra batteries)

  T Pocket Knife

Optional:

         T Sunglasses                       T Leather Work Gloves

         T Chapstick                        T Camera

         T Journal                             T Suntan Lotion/Sun Block

 

PLEASE DO NOT BRING: Candy of any kind, Pop, Radios, Walkman, Stereo, etc.

 

*These items can be rented (very limited supply) if you contact our office by a week before

your program starts.

  This clothing and equipment list is not designed to encourage participants to go out and buy new

things.  Please try to use existing clothing, then try to borrow from family or friends, etc.  

Arm/Navy surplus, DI (Deseret Industries) and other thrift stores are excellent places to buy 

outdoor type clothing.

 

Please bring only the items on this list.  Bare feet, midriffs, tank tops (boys and girls), shorts, 

etc. are NOT ALLOWED.  Shirts must be worn at all times. 

BYU-Idaho Dress & Grooming Standards
