EARLY ARRIVAL INFORMATION

 

Housing is available for those needing to arrive a day early for EFY, AFY, OYA, 

(formerly OEFY) Sports Camps, Etc.

 

The cost of this housing is $15.00 if reservations are made before two weeks 

before the program begins.  $20.00 with in two weeks of the program start 

date.  Thank you for inquiring about Early Arrival Housing at BYU-Idaho.

 

Reservations must be made no later than the Thursday preceding the start 

of your program by 3:00 p.m.  Any received after this time will be handled 

on a time and space available basis.

 

Please follow the directions below to make reservations.

 

Please read through the agreement carefully to ensure you understand the 

requirements in order to stay in our facilities. We expect those arriving early to 

observe the dress and conduct standards of BYU-Idaho. Shorts, capris, tank 

tops, and grubby attire are not allowed on campus. You will be expected to 

respect the rights and property of others you will be housed with. There will 

not be any adult supervision for this housing.

 

If you are flying into Idaho Falls and have requested transportation to campus,

please go to the "Transportation Information" link at the bottom of the 

page to make reservations.. If you are coming in on;

S.L.A.S.H. http://www.idahomall.com/slashvan/ , Salt Lake Express 

http://www.saltlakeexpress.com/, or a Greyhound bus, please list arrival 

times. If you are arriving by car, please list that as well. Please also remember 

that because of the Sabbath, check in times at Riviera or Baronnessa 

Apartments is ONLY between 5-6 pm. Check your transportation 

arrangements to be sure they will allow you to arrive within that hour.

 

If you have any questions feel free to call us (208) 496-1040.

 

IF YOU MUST CANCEL, REFUNDS ARE ONLY AVAILABLE MINUS A $5.00 

SERVICE FEE UNTIL TWO WEEKS PRIOR TO YOUR PROGRAM START 

DATE AFTER THAT NO REFUNDS ARE AVAILABLE.
