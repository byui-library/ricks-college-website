ROOMMATE

REQUESTS

 

        To Eliminate problems we have experienced in years past. This

year we will limit the roommate/group request to one person.  If you

are part of a larger group, please choose just one person as a

roommate.  Larger groups may not be together for some limited activities

and housing assignments, but there are several opportunities throughout

the week for being together as a group of friends, cousins, etc.  If per

chance your choice of person to be with in a group is of a different

gender (i.e. your brother or sister) simply list them as roommate

preference and e-mail the address below to ensure you are not mistakenly

housed together.

        When you register and it asks for housing (boys or girls) there 

is a place where you can enter the ID # for who you are requesting.

Or you can send an e-mail with both names and the program you are in

to wyliec@byui.edu
