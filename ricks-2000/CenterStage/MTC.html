Mormon Tabernacle Choir

Saturday, Oct. 27, 2001
6 p.m., Dinner in Manwaring Center
7:30 p.m., Hart Auditorium

Sunday, Oct. 28, 2001
9:30 a.m., Hart Auditorium

The Tabernacle Choir will perform as part of the university's celebration in becoming BYU-Idaho. The Choir will give a full concert on Saturday night, as well as broadcast “Music and the Spoken Word" from BYU-Idaho Sunday morning.

The Tabernacle Choir was formed just a month after the pioneers reached the Valley of the Great Salt Lake in 1847 and since then, the choir has thrilled audiences worldwide and added its name to the pages of musical history. The choir has released more than 150 albums, including five gold and two platinum albums.

Tickets go on sale Oct. 8. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Choir. Concert dress suggested. Free tickets will be distributed for Sunday morning broadcast from the Hart Auditorium. A pre-show, American dinner will be served in the Manwaring Center Special Events Room at 6 p.m. for $10 extra.
