Ricks College News Release
September 28, 2000

LDS educator teaches value of opposition

REXBURG, Idaho — David A. Christensen, a member of the faculty of the Institute of Religion at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City, expounded on the subject of opposition at the Ricks College weekly Tuesday devotional.

He began by quoting scripture for the Book of Mormon that states, "There is opposition in all things."

"I see so many unhappy Latter-day Saints because they don’t get it," Christensen said. "They don’t understand that the opposition that comes into our lives is meant to purify us, enable us and prepare us to be strong and true to the Lord Jesus Christ and Heavenly Father."

He explained that one way he has come to learn about the principle of opposition has been through his 31-year-old handicap and diabetic son, whom he and his wife care for on a daily basis.

He continued by providing three reasons and benefits that former LDS Church President Lorenzo Snow said on why God allows opposition: "Faith may become more perfect. . . confidence will be increased. . . . and the knowledge of the powers of heaven may be strengthened."

He said, "So every time I see somebody having opposition I just think, ‘Will you use this and allow the Lord to bless you now or are you going to fight?’ " Christensen said. "The opposition is not to be feared and must not be feared."

Christensen concluded by exhorting everyone to participate in daily prayer and scripture study. "If you come to the Lord, he will make your burden light. . . and give you joy."

Next Tuesday’s speaker will be Marie Hafen, wife of former Ricks College president Bruce C. Hafen. She will speak at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Devotionals are broadcast live and repeated at 9 p.m. on KRIC-FM.
