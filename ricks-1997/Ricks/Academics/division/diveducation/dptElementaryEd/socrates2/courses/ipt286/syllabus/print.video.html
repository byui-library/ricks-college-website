Print and Video Resources

Available at Ricks College

Return to IS 286 Index Page

These resources are available in the David O. McKay library on the Ricks College campus. Print resources are contained in the general collection, while video resources may be found in the collection of Media Services. Both types of resources may be checked out by current Ricks college students. However, video resources may be viewed free of charge in the Media Services video viewing area, but a fee is assessed if the resource is to be taken from the library. To contact Media Services call (208) 356-3156.
