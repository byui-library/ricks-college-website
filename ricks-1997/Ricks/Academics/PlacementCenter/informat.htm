About Ricks College
 Located in Rexburg, Idaho (city population is approximately 15,000).
Largest private junior college in the U.S. with over 8,000 students.
Students from all 50 states and 40 foreign countries.
Over 300 full-time faculty.
More than 90% of the faculty have a Masters or Doctorate Degree.
Owned and operated by the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
