August 22, 2001

New telephone prefix assigned 

for BYU-Idaho

    
REXBURG, Idaho – Not only does the school have a new name, it will also start using a new telephone prefix beginning in late August.

Gaylen Jensen, director of telecommunication services at Ricks College, says, "We were running out of phone numbers and knew we would need more. Since we are becoming BYU-Idaho, we felt it was the right time to ask for an entire prefix of our own. We had 356 and 359 numbers on campus and also felt it would be good to consolidate them into a single prefix."

All BYU-Idaho numbers beginning with the prefix 356 will be changed to 496 beginning Aug. 27 and the extension will remain the same. For example, campus operator assistance will change Aug. 27 from 356-2411 to 496-2411.

All e-mail fax extensions previously assigned to a 359 prefix will need to be changed completely, both prefix and extension.

For the first month, either prefix 356 or 496 will work. After Oct. 1, a recording will inform callers to use the new 496 prefix. As of Feb. 1, the 356 prefix will no longer work nor will it result in a recording. At that time, 356 and 359 numbers previously used on campus will be turned back to Qwest for reassignment in the Rexburg area.

The university telephone system currently handles more than 2,000 phone devices (telephones, modems, faxes, etc.). The demand for services continues to steadily increase with expanding faculty and student body.

On a daily basis, there are between 2,000 and 3,000 outgoing long distance and nearly 10,000 incoming calls a day when school is in full session. Jensen estimates the volume of outgoing local calls is similar to incoming calls while internal calls has an even higher volume.
