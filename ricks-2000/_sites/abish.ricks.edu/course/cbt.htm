Introduction to CBT

Ricks College has purchased a series of Computer-Based Training materials that you can access through the campus Intranet.  These CBT programs provide a work-at-your-own-pace resource for learning new software, such as Microsoft Windows, Microsoft Office, and Internet applications.  This workshop will show you how to access CBT on the Intranet, how to log-in, and how to register for a course.  Learn about the CBT interface, including information about bookmarking your progress (so you can finish a course later), taking tests, and receiving certification.
