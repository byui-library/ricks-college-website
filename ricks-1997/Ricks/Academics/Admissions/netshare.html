Welcome to your Netshare.

Your Netshare provides you with a personalized workspace for you and your projects, and rich functionality that allows you to manage your content.  You can publish, manage,and share all your documents in a central place.  You can access your Netshare from any machine in the office, at home, or on the road.

The buttons on the left bar launch content management utilities that come with Netshare, in addition to some useful links your administrator has set up for you.  If you like, you can customize the buttons or add additional ones for other frequently used tools or applications.
