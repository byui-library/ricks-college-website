How to Log onto a TEC System

 

Figure 1: Non-Activated System

1)       Activate a Dark Touch Panel Screen by lightly touching center of screen.

 

2)       When screen is active either the BYU-I or system controls will appear

 

3)       If BYU-I enter the current access code (available from Media Services at Ext. 2400)  by pressing the buttons located on the left and right of the display screen.

Figure 2 Activated System

1)       Now that the panel is active, please wait until the “System Warming” message is no longer displayed prior to proceeding

 

2)       When the “System Warming” is no longer displayed then select the desired input by pressing one of the following buttons 1,2,6 or 7 (CPU, VCR, Laptop or External respectively)

 

3)       If volume needs to be adjusted press button number 3 or 8 (Volume Up or Volume down respectively)

 

4)       If you desire to mute or unmute audio then press button number 4 or 9 as desires

 

5)       If you desire to turn the image on or off then press button 5 or 10 as desired.

 

6)       When finished, turn system off by pressing the “System Off” button on the LCD panel.
