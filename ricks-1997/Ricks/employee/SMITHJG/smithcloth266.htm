COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 266
Title: Custom Sewing Business Credit: 2
Text: Sewing for a Profit

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire the knowledge and skills necessary for operating a home based sewing business.  Upon completion of this course the student should be able to:
 1. Demonstrate skills in organizing a business
 2. Apply specific principles of setting up a sewing business plan
 3. Derive solutions to customer relation problems
 4. Locate appropriate resources information for supplies and equipment
 5. List the various ways of determining supply and demand
 6. Know the necessary inventory to run an effect business
 7. List Business guidelines and etiquette rules
 8. List ways to keep abreast of current development, updates, seminars, publications
 9. Know Time managements techniques
 10. List Proper business forms, documents, work orders
 11. Know the records, financial, and legal arrangements which need to be made

Description:
Fundamental principles of starting and operating a sewing business.  Discussion of legal, financial records, and management skills.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Set up a business plan which includes the following sections: business plan (business forms), goals & objectives, legal aspects, promotional, financial aspects, space planning, employees, resources, misc.
