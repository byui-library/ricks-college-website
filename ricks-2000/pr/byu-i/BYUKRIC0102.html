KRIC Radio Report, February 2001

The following was prepared by Mark Bailey, KRIC Radio news director. It includes sound bites that you can click on and hear audio recordings:

More information has been released regarding the BYU-Idaho transition. At a recent meeting the college's Board of Trustees gave its approval that once "candidacy status" is given to Ricks College to change from a two-year to a four-year academic institution is when the school can be known as BYU-Idaho. . . but with qualification. Ricks College President David Bednar explains that there will be a number of things that will still have to be done. . .so the name change won't be made on the day that notification is officially given. . .

[Click here to listen]
"We will have a transition time period, and in fact we will literally refer to as 'BYU-Idaho in transition.' So we will go from Ricks College to 'BYU-Idaho in transition.' And then at a later time, we will officially become BYU-Idaho. Commencement in April will be as Ricks College. Commencement in June will be as Ricks College."

Bednar says from the June commencement to the beginning of fall semester Ricks College will be in transition to BYU-Idaho. He anticipates that when the upper-division classes are taught in the fall is when the school becomes BYU-Idaho. However, he says a number of events during the fall will highlight the transition celebrating the beginning of BYU-Idaho. . .

[Click here to listen]
"In terms of the prospectus that was submitted in early January, the accreditation folks are working on that review now. It is likely that they will respond more quickly than we had anticipated. It is possible, I wouldn't say probable, but it is possible that we could have a response from Northwest as early as the middle of March. Where as originally we had thought that that might be April or May. So they are fast tracking fairly this and moving very quickly. . ."

President Bednar says once candidacy status is given then visually the school can work on logs, symbols and other things that will help with the permanent identification of BYU-Idaho.
