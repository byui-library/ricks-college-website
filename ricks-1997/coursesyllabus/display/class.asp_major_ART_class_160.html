Photography I
PHOTOGRAPHY I
CLASS CODE: ART 160 CREDITS:  3 FEE:  $40.00
DIVISION: PERFORMING AND VISUAL ARTS
DEPARTMENT: ART
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Arts requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Aesthetic principles are discovered using the photographic medium. This class is required of all photography majors.
DESCRIPTION: This course will involve lectures on principles of photography, as well as practical experience in the lab developing film and making black and white prints.
TOPICS: Topics include camera operation, darkroom experience, history of photography, and a critical examination of student photographs.
OBJECTIVES: The objective of the course is to teach students the basic principles of photography. Serves as a foundation for photography majors, and a general education art experience for the student body at large.
REQUIREMENTS: Two periods a week will be spent in lecture and three are spent in lab. Four hours a week outside of class should be spent working on assignments. Grades will be based on quizzes, weekly assignments, and a final portfolio.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: A 35mm SLR camera is used for this course. Limited supply for rent. Photo majors should have own camera.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2001
