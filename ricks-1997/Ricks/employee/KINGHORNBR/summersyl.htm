Grade: While real goal of this class is to help you come closer to Christ through the study of the Book of Mormon there still must be a grade assigned. The grade will be determined according to the following areas:

Daily Preparation: There is no substitute to being in class and letting the Holy Ghost testify of the things that we are discussing in class, especially when they coincide with what you have already prepared through your personal study. Each class period I will have you report to me the level of your preparation which will be broken down into the following categories: attendance, your scripture journal and Book of Mormon reading. For coming to class you earn five points, five points for your journal entry and ten more points if you read the Book of Mormon assignment for a total of 20 points per day.

Attendance: School excused or illnesses verified by a doctor may be made up within three days of the absence. Missing more than eight times overrides all other considerations and a failing grade will be given.

Journal: This scripture journal will provide you with an opportunity to record any of the impressions that you might receive while studying the scriptures. Every week you will turn in a paper based on your journal entries. This one page (single spaced) paper needs to focus on a personal application of the scriptures and your journal entries will be the major source for these papers. Each paper will be worth 20 points.

Tests: I realize that some people do better than others on tests and so I am giving you the option to take both tests (worth 100 points) or to write a paper in place of the tests. The test option will be given in the testing center according to the schedule that is included on this syllabus and will be multiple choice, true/false and matching. The paper will be due the day that the test closes and must be 4 pages in length and will be worth 100 points. I will provide information concerning this option in class.

Book of Mormon Project: There is so much that can be gained from a serious study of the Book of Mormon that it can provide a lifetime of learning. Your assignment is to study something relating to the Book of Mormon and to report that to me. I am leaving this quite wide open to allow you to grow in which ever area you would like, with a few guidelines. Following this paragraph are a few ideas that you could choose from, but again it is up to you what you do. All I ask is that if you are doing something beyond a paper (i.e. an art work, poetry or whatever else you come up with) you clear that with me before beginning. If you do choose to write a paper I would ask that it be at least three pages in length (1.5 line spacing) and that you fully document all of the sources you use, both in and out of the scriptures. This assignment will be worth 200 points.

Book of Mormon character paper

Book of Mormon evidence paper

Doctrinal research paper

Book of Mormon history paper (coming forth of the book)

If you do decided to do something else it is expected that you would put at least as much time and effort into this project as would be put into a research paper.
