Stakes and Wards
BYU-Idaho is affiliated with the
Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Single students are assigned to a ward based on their campus address and the current semester. Students should attend the ward to which they have been assigned.  Exceptions must be approved by the Stake President.

Students living at home are encouraged to attend the campus ward to which they are assigned, but may attend their home ward.

Married students may attend either a University ward or the local ward wherein they reside.
