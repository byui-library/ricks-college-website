Overview

 Students who are non-native speakers of English can receive extra help in the Reading Center. The English as a Second Language (ESL) program, located in the Reading Center, is designed to help students improve their listening, speaking, reading and writing skills. Students can get individual help with such things as pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary building, and listening comprehension. This program is only available to students who have been accepted to BYU-Idaho. 

Individual Instruction

 The one-on-one instruction helps students understand and adjust more quickly to the new culture they have entered. The Reading Center is a place where students can practice, get help, and improve their English under non-threatening and authentic situations. English 108 and 109 are courses especially designed to help non-native speakers of English improve oral and written language. These classes are listed under English in the catalog.
