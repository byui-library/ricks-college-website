Kinghorn on PBS show

by Kelly Smurthwaite
SMU01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Edward Kinghorn of BYU-Idaho’s Psychology Department, appeared on the Idaho Public Telvision’s show Dialogue Jan. 31.

He was featured because of his volunteer work with the Red Cross helping people who were affected by the Sept. 11 terrorist attacks.

Kinghorn spent part of his Christmas break on the East Coast as a mental health volunteer.

Most New Yorkers had coped with the initial shock of the attacks, yet many were still having other problems, he said.

“The horrific grief was over [for the New Yorkers] ... but now many were faced with problems such as ‘how will I pay my bills?’” Kinghorn said.

Kinghorn had the opportunity to visit ground zero on Christmas Eve with a family he had been working with.

The night that Kinghorn felt the most emotion was when he spent an evening with fire fighters.

“I lost it — it was one of the most emotional things I’ve experienced ... but it has been one of the best experiences I’ve had professionally,” he said.

He said this experience has given him a different perspective on life.

“I don’t worry about the things that I used to worry about,” he said.
