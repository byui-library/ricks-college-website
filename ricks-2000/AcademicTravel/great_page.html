Explore the wild and beautiful Great Basin of the Western United States with Gene Weller of Biology, Gene Thompson and David Peck of History.

This discovery group will spend the first three weeks camping, hiking, and exploring in the southern Utah.   The last two weeks will be spent in the Salmon Country of Idaho.  Some of the highlights of this trip include swimming in the Great Salt Lake, viewing the beautiful red rock country of southern Utah, visiting church historic sites and exploring Leman Caves in Nevada.  The objective fo this program is a quest for knowledge and fun using the wilderness as a classroom.

Enrollment limited to 30 participants
