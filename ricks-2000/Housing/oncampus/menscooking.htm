Men's Cooking Style On-Campus Housing

(Available Summer Only)

Ricks Hall
During the summer, men living on campus will be housed in Dorm 4, Ricks Hall.  This is a cooking style residency.  However if a meal plan is desired, please contact Food Services.    See map #4.
Enclosed Courtyard
A large courtyard provides secure exterior entrances into each resident apartment.
Your Own Desk With Lofted Bed
Each two bedroom apartment is shared by four men.  Lofted beds provide a large desk and dresser for each student.
Kitchen
Each apartment has a newly remodeled bright-white kitchen with microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls.
Lounge
Each building has a main lounge with a TV/VCR, snack and beverage machines, and plenty of comfortable chairs and couches for social gatherings.
Laundry Facility
There is also a coin-operated laundry room with Maytag washers and dryers.  One washer load is 75¢ and a dryer will run for 30 minuets for just 25¢.
