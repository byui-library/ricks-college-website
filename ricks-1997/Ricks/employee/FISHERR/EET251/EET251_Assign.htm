IMPORTANT: Schedules, assignments, and policies are subject to change. You will be given advance notification of any changes.

General Assignment Policy:

Late Assignments will almost never be accepted.

There are very few circumstances that merit an exception to this rule.  Broken cars, broken computers, broken clocks, broken hearts, lost keys, lost disks, lost calculators, lost papers, too much other homework, dates, discretionary travel, parties, and forgetfulness do not qualify.  If you are sick, send your assignment with a roommate.

If there is an emergency, e.g., your pregnant wife is in labor, or you are in the hospital, I am willing to negotiate an extension.

Presentation Group Assignments:

The class will be divided into five presentation groups.  Each presentation group will be assigned one of the ten-hour blocks.  The group members will, in effect, become assistant instructors and facilitate the class' learning of the material for that block.  The group may lecture, conduct demonstrations, hold lab exercises, and assign homework problems for the first 9 hours of the block to meet this objective.   The last hour will be spent administering a test that covers the material in that block.  This test must be pre-approved by the instructor.

The presentation group members will be responsible for grading all class assignments, including the test, for "their" block.  A total percentage score for each class member during the block will be submitted to the instructor for recording.

The presentation group members will be ordinary class members during all other blocks.

Block Scores:

The course is divided into six blocks of two chapters each.  All of the assignments in each block, e.g., homework, lab exercises, quizzes, and tests, will be combined to form a total block score.  The lowest of the six block scores will be dropped, and the remaining five scores will be combined to form a total block score.   The total block score will be 50% of your grade. 

Each block will have a test during the last hour of that block.  No assignments for that block will be accepted after the conclusion of that test.  There are no makeup tests!

[Back]

Instructor & Class Evaluations:

At the end of each block, both the instructor and the class members will evaluate the performance of the presentation group that facilitated that block.  These scores will be 45% of your grade.

Questions on the evaluation form include the following:

  1. Was the class given clear expectations on what was to be learned and how it would be evaluated?
  2. Were the assignments relevant to the material?
  3. Were the test and other evaluation procedures appropriate to the material covered?
  4. Was the workload appropriate for a 3 credit course?
  5. Was the presentation group well prepared?
  6. Was the subject matter presented clearly?

Portfolio:

A portfolio of your class work is worth 5% of your grade.  These are collected at the end of the semester and copied for review by the Technology Accreditation Commission of the Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology (TAC/ABET). 

Get a 3-ring binder and make the following divider sections for it:

1.    Exams & Quizzes
2.    Homework
3.    Lab Experiments

Put all your graded assignments in the appropriate section.  

The portfolio is due at 5:00 PM on the last day of class.  It will be returned to you during the final test.  Late portfolios will receive a zero.
