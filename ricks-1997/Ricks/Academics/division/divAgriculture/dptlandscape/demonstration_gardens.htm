Heaven on Campus

    When many students faculty and staff need a break for contemplative meditation, exercise, or just a place to view nature and relax and unwind, they head for the Horticulture Demonstration Garden. This 10 acre garden on the south side of Ricks College campus began in 1977 with the start of the Landscape Horticulture Department. Over the years it has evolved into one of the finest show gardens in the western U. S. 

Uses

    Although used by the entire school and surrounding communities, the garden was originally established to facilitate outdoor teaching for the Landscape Horticulture Department. The entire garden has been designed, constructed, and maintained by faculty, staff and students in the department. Over the years many other campus departments, wards, clubs and community members have used the gardens for many activities. 

 

 

Features

    Some features included in the garden are three water features, a picnic structure, reception area, test gardens, fruit tree orchard, flower beds, native garden, and more. Approximately 10 outdoor wedding receptions are held in the garden each summer. Each year more features are added and the garden continues to grow.
