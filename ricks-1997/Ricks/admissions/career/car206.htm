  Summer		 Associate in Arts and Sciences with a major in Agriculture Systems Technology (206)

The Agriculture Systems Technology program is designed for students planning a career in the rapidly changing field of high-technology and agriculture mechanization. This major provides the student a solid foundation for transfer to a four-year university or preparation to enter the work force directly. Career opportunities include latest agriculture technologies, field testing new ideas, designing equipment, marketing new lines of equipment, education, journalism and much more.
