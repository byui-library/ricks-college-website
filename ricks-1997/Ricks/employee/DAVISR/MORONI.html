JOSEPH SMITH

AND THE

VISITATIONS OF MORONI

by Jerry C. Roundy

"He called me by name, and said unto me that he was a messenger sent from the presence of God to me, and that is name was Moroni."1 Such was the beginning of an intimate relationship between a mortal man and a resurrected being—the culmination of which would bring an unlearned farm boy to the position of a heaven-taught, angel-tutored prophet of God.

After the great and glorious vision of the Father and Son to Joseph Smith in the Spring of 1820, the young prophet-to-be said he was guilty of levity, and often associated with company not consistent with that character "which ought to be maintained by one who was called of God."2 One should not think him guilty of gross sins, but it may be that he had not been as diligent in calling on the Lord as he should have been and the Lord was in no hurry to extend knowledge until supplication was extended.

The First Visit

On the evening of September 21, 1823, after retiring to his bed, Joseph sought the Lord in prayer for forgiveness of his sins and follies. He had full confidence in obtaining a divine manifestation that evening, but the manifestation did not come without extreme pleading. Oliver Cowdery indicates that Joseph prayed until the house became quiet and even after the house became cold, and it was mid-night or thereafter before the Angel made his appearance. Describing the event Joseph said:

While I was thus in the act of calling upon God, I discovered a light appearing in my room, which continued to increase until the room was lighter than at noonday, when immediately a personage appeared at my bedside, standing in the air, for his feet did not touch the floor.

. . . his whole person was glorious beyond description, and his countenance truly like lightning. The room was exceedingly light, but not so very bright as immediately around his person. When I first looked upon him, I was afraid; but the fear soon left me.3

After telling the future prophet his name would be had for good and evil among all nations, the Angel wasted no time beginning his important instructions.

He said there was a book deposited, written upon gold plates, giving an account of the former inhabitants of this continent, and the source from whence they sprang. He also said that the fullness of the everlasting Gospel was contained in it, as delivered by the Savior to the ancient inhabitants.

Also, that there were two stones in silver bows—and these stone fastened to a breastplate, constituted what is called the Urim and Thummin—deposited with the plates; and the possession and use of these stones were what constituted "seers" in ancient or former times; and that God had prepared them for the purpose of translating the book.4

Along with the instructions concerning the gold plates, the Prophet was given a brief sketch of, and shown in vision, the aboriginal inhabitants of ancient America. He later recounted:

I was also informed concerning the aboriginal inhabitants of this country and shown who they were, and from whence they came; a brief sketch of their origin, progress, civilization, laws, governments, of their righteousness and iniquity, and the blessings of God being finally withdrawn from them as a people, was made known unto me.5

What knowledge flowed through the mind of this young seventeen year old lad as that panoramic vision flashed before his eyes. Oliver Cowdery intimates that Joseph was able to behold the ancient inhabitants of this continent in the same way that Moses and other prophets were shown the peoples of the earth.6 Oliver Cowdery wrote:

When God manifests to his servants those things that are to come, or those which have been, he does it by unfolding them by the power of that spirit which comprehends all things, always; . . . you will understand then, by this that while those glorious things were being rehearsed, the vision was also opened, so that our brother was permitted to see and understand much more fully and perfectly than I am able to communicate in writing.7

During his visitation Moroni further instructed the Prophet that

The covenant which God made with ancient Israel was at hand to be fulfilled, that the preparatory work for the second coming of the Messiah was speedily to commence; that the time was at hand for the Gospel in all its fullness to be preached in power, unto all nations that a people might be prepared for the Millennial reign. I was informed that I was chosen to be an instrument in the hands of God to bring about some of His purposes in this glorious dispensation.8

These instructions make it clear that Joseph Smith was truly going to play a lead role in the preparations for the Second Coming of Christ. In light of this, it is natural that Moroni should then commence quoting and explaining Biblical scriptures bearing on future events leading up to the Advent of Christ in the Latter days.

The Angel commenced his instructions by quoting part of the third chapter of Malachi—no doubt the first five verses. The Prophet, himself, doesn't enlarge on the verses quoted, but they speak of a messenger who is to prepare the way before the Savior, and although Christ says the messenger spoken of, was John the Baptist.9 It appears that since Joseph Smith received keys from John, he shares with him the role of a special messenger to prepare the way before the Lord. That this scripture speaks in part of the Second Coming of Christ, is evident from a reading of verses three and four. According to those verses, there will be many unable to stand when he appears, since it will be a time of cleansing and purging. The future cleansing will be by the heavenly messengers sent by the Lord, for the quotation of Moroni differs slightly from the Bible quotation. Moroni says: "For behold, the day cometh that shall burn as an oven, and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly shall burn as stubble; for they that come shall burn them, saith the Lord of Hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch."10 This could only refer to the second coming.

Moroni also quoted the last verses in Malachi differently:

Behold, I will reveal unto you the Priesthood, by the hand of Elijah the Prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.

And he shall plant in the hearts of the children the promises made to the fathers, and the hearts of the children shall turn to their fathers. If it were not so, the whole earth would be utterly wasted at his coming."11

At that time, perhaps Joseph did not fully understand the meaning of Moroni's words, but later after Elijah appeared to him in the Kirtland Temple, April 3, 1836, he was able to write:

The spirit, power, and calling of Elijah is, that ye have power to hold the key of the revelations, ordinances, oracles, powers, and endowments of the fullness of the Melchizedek Priesthood and of the kingdom of God on the earth; and to receive, obtain, and perform all the ordinances belonging to the Kingdom of God, even unto the hearts of the children unto the fathers, even those who are in heaven.12

It is thus by the power of Elijah that the Saints may attain an exaltation within the celestial kingdom.

The angel next quoted the 11th chapter of Isaiah, saying it was about to be fulfilled.13 Because of the length of the chapter, it will not be quoted here. Suffice it to say the entire chapter deals with the Millennial reign of Christ upon the earth. If this was near to fulfillment in Joseph Smith's day, should we not be much more on guard even today.

Moroni next quoted:

For Moses truly said unto the fathers, a prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear in all things whatsoever he shall say unto you,

An it shall come to pass, that every soul which will not hear that prophet, shall be destroyed from among the people.14

The Angel said that the prophet was Christ and had reference to His Second Coming. Surely those who will not hear His voice at that time shall be "destroyed from among the people."15

The last passage of scripture Moroni quoted was Joel 2:28 32.16 I will comment only on verse 28.

And it shall come to pass afterward, that I will pour out my spirit upon all flesh; and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, your old men shall dream dreams, your young men shall see visions.

Some have thought this prophecy has already been fulfilled, but it is yet in the future. This does not mean that the Lord has not poured out the Holy Ghost in some measure upon the Gentiles as foretold by the Book of Mormon, but it is not that to which Joel referred. First, the angel Moroni declared to Joseph that Joel's prophecy "was not yet fulfilled," although the Lord had by then poured out His spirit upon the Gentiles. Second, Joel states plainly that God will pour out His spirit after Israel has been redeemed; and that it will be after the final signs of Christ's coming are manifested—after the sun shall be darkened and the moon turned into blood. Third, the Lord declared in a revelation to Joseph Smith that He would pour out His spirit upon all flesh after He had pruned His vineyard for the last time.17 Evidently there is to be a future outpouring of the Spirit like unto the Day of Pentecost.

The night of September 21-22, 1823, was a night of intense instruction in which Joseph Smith learned three important things; (1) He learned of an ancient record deposited in a hill near to his home, which (2) contained a history of a people who once inhabited the land of America, and (3) he would become an instrument in the hand of the Lord preparing for the second advent of the Savior.

As the Heavenly messenger departed, leaving Joseph to contemplate the glorious visions and instruction, the cock crowed and sunlight began rolling back the shades of night. Morning had come; the visions had occupied the whole night.

That night began a series of meetings between Moroni and Joseph Smith, which documentation numbers close to thirty, and speculation many more. Moroni came often to instruct, edify and chastise. Perhaps there were countless appearances of which Joseph never saw fit to mention or even allude to.

SUBSEQUENT APPEARANCES

OF MORONI

Joseph first sees the Plates

After the three initial appearances during the night of September 21-22, 1823, the young Prophet went to work the next day in the field, but finding himself physically exhausted he was told by his father to return to the house. While attempting to cross the fence, the Angel again appeared to him and told him to tell his father about the proceedings of the previous night, and to then go to the Hill where he would be further instructed. After informing his father of the previous visions, Joseph proceeded to Hill Cumorah and another meeting with Moroni. When he reached the appointed location the past visions were so clear in his mind that he recognized it instantly. Removing the dirt from around the stone, he got a lever and with a little exertion, lifted the lid from the repository. There, as stated by Moroni, were plates, the Urim and Thummim, and the breast plate.

Joseph said further:

The box in which they lay was formed by laying stones together in some kind of cement. In the bottom of the box were laid two stones crossways of the box, and on these stones lay the plates and the other things with them.

I made an attempt to take them out, but was forbidden by the messenger, and was again informed that the time for bringing them forth had not yet arrived, neither would it, until four years from that time; but he told me that I should come to that place precisely in one year from that time, and he would there meet with me, and that I should continue to do so until the time should come for obtaining the plates.18

Upon receiving the plates and other articles, Joseph's first impression was to reach in and take them, but in attempting to do so, he received a severe shock which seemed to paralyze his strength. A second and third attempt resulted in the same, except that they grew more severe. Joseph cried aloud, "Why can I not obtain this Book?" "Because you have not kept the commandments of the Lord," said a voice behind him. Turning he beheld the messenger of the previous night.19

Writing in the Messenger and Advocate, Oliver Cowdery sheds further light upon the visit between Joseph and Moroni. He said that while Joseph was being humbled by the severe shock and chastisement of the Angel

The heavens were opened and the glory of the Lord shone around about and rested upon him. While thus he stood gazing and admiring, the angel said, "Look!" and as he thus spake, he beheld the prince of darkness, surrounded by his innumerable train of associates. All this passed before him, and the heavenly messenger said,

"All this is shown, the good and the evil, the holy and impure, the glory of God, the power of darkness; that ye may know hereafter the two powers and never be influenced or overcome by that wicked one. Behold, whatever entices and leads to good and to do good is of God, and whatever does not is of that wicked one . . . and you may learn from henceforth, that his ways are to destruction, but the way of holiness is peace and rest."20

This revelation was undoubtedly given to lend strength to the young man by showing him both ends of the spectrum—that which he could receive if faithful and that which would be his were he to fail. As Moroni closed his instructions to the future prophet, he told him the record would not be given to him for translation until he had learned to "keep the commands of God"—not only until he was willing but until he was also able to do it.21

Are You Able?

True to the instructions of Moroni, Joseph returned the following year on the appointed date (September 22, 1824) to receive further instructions. At this visitation, he painfully learned that a willingness to keep the commandments of the Lord is not the same as being able to keep them. Joseph went to the hill fully intending to take the plates home with him. This time as he reached in to take the plates, there was no shock and Moroni allowed him to take them from their hiding place. He supposed he was fully capable of keeping the commandments of God. As he removed the plates, he received a strict injunction from Moroni not to lay the plates down or put them out of his hands until he could deposit them in a safe place. But, as he took them out of the repository and turned away, he thought perhaps he should return and cover the box and check to see if, perchance, he had left anything therein. After laying the plates down upon the ground, he returned; and as he turned again to pick up the record, it was gone. In a state of great alarm he commenced to pray, whereupon the Angel appeared to him and chastised him for his neglect. He was told to return to the hiding place where upon he found the stone had been returned to its original position. He was again permitted to remove the cover and therein he beheld the plates. Still convinced he was ready to take the plates from the hill,

He immediately reached forth his hand to take them, but instead of getting them, as he anticipated, he was hurled back upon the ground with great violence. When he recovered, the angel was gone, and he arose and returned to the house, weeping for grief and disappointment.22

After this experience, Joseph was sufficiently humbled and never troubled Moroni again to prematurely allow him to take the plates from their hiding place. Instead, he dutifully returned to the hill on the same date each year until, in the Lord's time, he was able to remove them. By September 22, 1827, three years later, Joseph was sufficiently schooled to where he was not only willing to keep the Lord's commandments, but he was able to keep them.

There may have been other appearances of the Angel to the Prophet during the four years of preparation which we know nothing about. On one occasion, Joseph had gone on an errand to Manchester a short distance from the Smith home. He was late returning home, and as he entered the door, his father began questioning him as to the reasons for his tardiness. Joseph answered, "I have taken the severest chastisement that I have ever had in my life." When his father began to interrogate him as to who had a right to find fault with him, he answered:

Stop, father, stop, it was the angel of the Lord. As I passed by the Hill of Cumorah, where the plates are, the angel met me and said that I had not been engaged enough in the work of the Lord; that the time had come for the record to be e brought forth; and that I must be up and doing and set myself about the things which God had commanded me to do.23

There is nothing in the record by way of instructions received from Moroni during the visits in 1825, 1826, and 1827, except that in the latter meeting the Angel charged that he should be responsible for them and that, "if I should let them go carelessly, or through any neglect of mine, I should be cut off; but that if I would use all my endeavors to preserve them, until he, the messenger, should call for them, they should be protected."24

The Lost Manuscript

The next recorded visit of Moroni came after Joseph had moved to Harmony, Pennsylvania and had given in to the teasings of Martin Harris to take 116 pages of the translated manuscript home to Palmyra to show his wife as an evidence that they were really working on something. Martin bound himself with a sacred covenant that he would only show the manuscript to certain persons, but unfortunately, he broke his covenant and showed the manuscript to others. It disappeared and he was forced to admit he was unable to return it to Joseph. It was around June 14, 1828, when Martin left Harmony for Palmyra with the manuscript promising to return inside of two weeks. Soon after Martin left, Emma gave birth to her first child who lived only a few hours and her life hung in the balance for several days. Recovering from her illness, Emma discerned Joseph was also worried about something other than her health.

Joseph was indeed worried, Martin had not returned and Moroni had appeared and taken back into his custody the plates and the Urim & Thummin. At the time, Joseph had no sure knowledge that the manuscript was lost, but Moroni's actions gave him "grave doubts as to the wisdom of the whole procedure in this thing."25

Joseph made a hurried trip to Palmyra where his worst fears were confirmed—the manuscript was lost. He returned to Harmony greatly troubled in spirit. Greatly humbling himself in prayer, he moved God to compassion and sometime in the month of July 1828, Moroni appeared and handed him the Urim and Thummim. Immediately Joseph inquired, through that instrument concerning his standing before the Lord. He was chastised severely and told "if thou art not aware, thou wilt fall."26

It is evident that Joseph was truly humbled, but the revelation from the Lord gave him hope that he would soon be restored to favor. Soon after this revelation was received, Moroni appeared again, took the Urim and Thummim from him, and again placed him on probation.27

This probation, however, was brief, and a few days after the above visitation, Moroni appeared with a more complete pardon and manifestation of forgiveness of God. He was again allowed to petition the Lord concerning his standing. Section ten of the Doctrine and Covenants was received in which he was told the lost manuscript was in the hands of wicked men who were waiting to see if he would re-translate verbatim from the plates; if he did, they were going to change their copy to make him appear a false prophet. If he didn't translate verbatim they would leave their copy unchanged to produce the same result.28 Joseph, however, was told not to re-translate from the same record, but to translate from the small plates of Nephi which record covered the same period of time. It was Satan and his followers who were frustrated, not Joseph and God.

Finally, on September 22, 1828, he had the joy and satisfaction of being completely restored to God's favor and received again the holy interpreters and the plates.29 Joseph did not, however, begin translating again immediately, but went to work on the farm, and throughout the winter it seems that the translation work did not progress much. In the early spring of 1829, Oliver Cowdery came to Harmony to assist Joseph, and on April 7, 1829, he began his work as a manuensis for the Prophet.

The Three Nephites

It wasn't long before lying tongues aroused persecution to the point where it was impossible for the young men to remain longer in Harmony, Pennsylvania. Since Oliver had become friends with a Whitmer family in Fayette, N.Y., it was determined that he should write and ask permission to come and live with them while completing the translation work. Oliver wrote to David, a member of the Whitmer family about Oliver's age, asking if he could come and transport them from Harmony, Pennsylvania to Fayette, New York, but the letter arrived while David was in the midst of his spring work, and it was determined that he could not leave for at least two weeks.

There were several acres of farm land that had not yet been plowed, harrowed and fertilized in preparation for the spring planting. But one morning as David entered the field to work he found that "between five and seven acres of my land had been plowed during the night." He was puzzled and didn't know who had done the work but "it was done just as I would have done it myself, and the plow was left standing in the furrow."30

The next day while harrowing a field of wheat, David found he was able to accomplish more in a few hours than was usually done in two or three days. Furthermore, when he went the next morning to spread plaster over a field he found that the work had already been done, and done well. His sister, who lived nearby, told him that three strangers had appeared in the field the day before and spread the plaster with great skill—such skill in fact that she and other members of the family had gone out to watch them.31 This apparent Divine help enabled David to start immediately for Harmony to bring the Prophet and Oliver to his home.

A few days later, Joseph suggested to Oliver that David would be arriving in a short while and suggested they go out to meet him. When they met, David says that "Oliver told me that Joseph had informed him when I started from home, where I had stopped the first night, how I read the sign at the tavern; where I stopped the next night, etc.; and that I should be there that day before dinner, and this was why they had come out to meet me, all of which was exactly as Joseph had told Oliver, at which I was greatly astonished."32

An Old Man

The following morning, as the wagon was being loaded in preparation for the return journey, David looked anxiously at each box as it was placed in the vehicle in hopes of seeing the container of the Gold Plates. He was disappointed when he didn't see Joseph load anything into the wagon which resembled the plates—the object of which was his long wagon trip from Fayette to Harmony—but he was told to never mind, that the plates were safe. Apparently, Moroni had appeared and taken the plates into his custody, intending to carry them himself from Harmony to Fayette. According to B.H. Roberts: "The day following David Whitmer's arrival at Harmony the plates were packed and delivered into the care of the angel Moroni by the prophet, that they might be safely conveyed to Fayette."33

Years later David recalled an interesting experience that occurred the next day as he, Joseph and Oliver were riding along in the wagon:

When I was returning to Fayette with Joseph and Oliver, all of us riding in the wagon, Oliver and I on an old fashioned wooden spring seat and Joseph behind us, when traveling along in a clear, open place, a very pleasant, nice looking old man suddenly appeared by the side of our wagon and saluted us with "Good morning; it is very warm," at the same time wiping his face or forehead with his hand. We returned the salutation, and by a sign from Joseph, I invited him to ride if he was going our way. But he said very pleasantly, "No, I am going to Cumorah." This name was somewhat new to me, and I did not know what "Cumorah" meant. We all gazed at him and at each other and as I looked inquiringly of Joseph, the old man instantly disappeared, so that I did not see him again.

He was, I should think, about 5 feet 8 or 9 inches tall, and heavy set, about such a man as James Vaucleave there, but heavier; his face was as large, he was dressed in a suit of brown woolen clothes, his hair and beard were white, like Brother Pratt's, but his beard was not so heavy. I also remember that he had on his back a sort of knapsack with something in it, shaped like a book. It was the messenger who had the plates, who had taken them from Joseph just prior to our starting from Harmony.34

Shortly after arrival in Fayette, Joseph must have met Moroni somewhere in the area around the Whitmer farm where the sacred record was again delivered into his custody, because David says that one day he observed something which led him to believe the plates were concealed in his father's barn. He frankly asked Joseph if his supposition was right and was told that it was.35

David further reported that a few days later, his mother was going to the barn to milk the cows when she was met by the same old man (at least her description of him led David to believe him to be the one and the same) who said to her:

You have been very faithful and diligent in your labors, but you're (sick) and tired because of increase of your toil; it is proper therefore that you should receive a witness that your faith may be strengthened.36

Thereupon the gentleman showed her the plates. The reason for Moroni showing Mary Whitmer the plates is never explained by Church History, but it may be that the added burden of company in the home had caused her to feel her labors were too much. David says his mother was greatly strengthened in her faith and never once complained thereafter of her extra labors.37

The Three Witnesses

During the stay at the Whitmer home while the translation was in progress, Joseph and Oliver discovered from Moroni's abridgement of Ether's record of the Jaredites, who came to the new World from the Tower of Babel, that there were to be three special witnesses to the Book of Mormon. The witnesses chosen were Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer, and Martin Harris.

Retiring into the wilderness the three men, along with Joseph Smith, finding a secluded spot in a grove of trees, knelt in fervent prayer to God for a vision of the plates. When two prayerful attempts brought no answer from the heavens, Martin Harris excused himself saying he believed his presence was the cause of their failure. The remaining three prayed again and the Prophet says the Angel stood before them; and turning the pages of the Plates one by one showed them the engravings on the plate38

In an interview with David Whitmer, in Richmond, Missouri, September 7, 1878, Orson Pratt and Joseph F. Smith learned some interesting details about this vision. In response to a question by Orson Pratt concerning his seeing the plates David replied:

It was in June, 1829—the latter part of the month, and the eight witnesses saw them, I think, the next day or the day after. (i.e., one or two days after). Joseph showed them the plates himself, but the angel showed us (the three witnesses) the plates, as I suppose to fulfill the words of the book itself. Martin Harris was not with us at this time; he obtained a view of them afterwards, (the same day). Joseph, Oliver, and myself were together when I saw them. We not only saw the plates of the Book of Mormon, but also the brass plates, the plates of the Book of Ether, the plates containing the records of the wickedness and secret combinations of the people of the world down to the time of their being engraved, and many other plates. The fact is, it was just as though Joseph, Oliver, and I were sitting just there on a log, when we were overshadowed by a light. It was not like the light of the sun nor like that of a fire, but more glorious and beautiful. It extended away round us, I cannot tell how far, but in the midst of this light about as far off as he sits (pointing to John C. Whitmer, sitting a few feet from him), there appeared as it were, a table with many records or plates upon it, besides the plates of the Book of Mormon, also the Sword of Laban, the directors—i.e., the ball which Lehi had, and the interpreters. I saw them just as plain as I see this bed (striking the bed beside him with his hand), and I heard the voice of the Lord, as distinctly as I ever heard anything in my life, declaring that the records of the plates of the Book of Mormon were translated by the gift and power of God.39

When the vision ended, Joseph sought Martin Harris and found him praying fervently. He joined him in supplication, and the same vision was opened to their view. As Martin beheld the Angel and the ancient Nephite records and relics he cried out, "Tis enough, tis enough; mine eyes have beheld; mine eyes have beheld."40

If Moroni were in custody of the plates at the time he showed them to the three witnesses, he must have come to Joseph to get them, and then he must have appeared again to the Prophet to return them; because the next day or so Joseph himself showed the plates to the eight witnesses, thus making two additional appearances by Moroni of which the record is silent.

The Plates are Returned

When the work of translation was completed, the records was apparently delivered, according to previous arrangements, into the hands of Moroni. Joseph very tersely comments: "When according to arrangements, the messenger called for them, I delivered them up to him; and he has them in his charge until this day, being the second day of May, one thousand eight hundred and thirty-eight."41 We are indebted to Oliver Cowdery, however, for an enlarged account of the return of the plates to Moroni, which took place at the Hill Cumorah. Brigham Young relates the story as it was told to him by Oliver:

When Joseph got the plates, the angel instructed him to carry them back to the Hill Cumorah, which he did. Oliver says that when Joseph and Oliver went there, the hill opened, and they walked into a cave, in which there was a large and spacious room. He says he did not think at the time, whether they had the light of the sun or artificial light; but that it was just as light as day. They laid the plates on a table; it was a large table that stood in the room. Under this table there was a pile of plates as much as two feet high, and there were altogether in this room more plates than probably many wagon loads; they were piled up in the corners and along the walls. The first time they went there the sword of Laban hung upon the wall; but when they went again, it had been taken down and laid upon the table across the gold plates; it was unsheathed, and on it was written these words: "This sword will never be sheathed again until the kingdoms of this world become the kingdoms of our God and his Christ." I tell you this as coming not only from Oliver Cowdery, but also from others who were familiar with it and who understood it just as well as we understand coming to this meeting.42

The relationship which developed between Joseph Smith and Moroni began with Joseph standing in great awe before a celestial being filled with light, but there was also a great deal of respect on the part of Moroni for Joseph Smith. He did not see Joseph as just a humble unlearned farm boy, but saw him as one of the great Council of Gods in the pre-mortal existence. Moroni had been given the call of teacher and confidant to the man entrusted with laying the ground work for the Second Advent of the Savior. Their relationship became more than an angel-mortal relationship—they became friends and companions in a great mission of salvation to mankind. Moroni's position in the grand scheme of things was that of personal instructor to the man who was to become the Prophet of the Dispensation of the Fullness of Times. What a great call to be the one who was to teach and mold the Prophet of God.
