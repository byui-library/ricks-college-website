Health & Wellness

An important part of success in college comes from maintaining an overall sense of health, fitness, and wellness.  BYU-Idaho provides physical fitness facilities, counseling services and an on-campus health center to attend to all your wellness needs.

 

Counseling Services

College students frequently experience stress and other emotional discomforts.  These may range from adjusting to college life to severe feelings of depression or anxiety; from concerns about careers to feelings of low self-esteem.  Help in dealing with these problems is available at the Counseling Center located in the Kimball building (208-356-1100).  Students will receive reliable, professional assistance in an atmosphere of warmth and understanding.  Complete confidentiality is maintained by the counselors.  

 

Health Center

The Student Health Center is located in room 120 of the Clarke Building.  Please call 356-1300 to make an appointment. 

Student Insurance.  BYU-Idaho requires that each student must be covered by health and accident insurance.  Students who register  as a half-time student or more are automatically charged for the BYU-Idaho Student Insurance.  A student may have the BYU-Idaho insurance waived if (1) he or she is covered in the Rexburg area by another health and accident insurance plan, and (2) a completed waiver form has been submitted to the BYU-Idaho Accounting Office before the deadline.  Deadlines for waiving BYU-Idaho Insurance are posted in the BYU-Idaho  Catalog.  For questions regarding student insurance please call 208-356-1039 or visit the BYU-I  Student Insurance web site.

 

Physical Facilities

The Hart Building is the university's center for Physical Fitness.   Opened Monday through Saturday from 6:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., the Hart Building offers student access to the following areas:  swimming pool, racquetball courts, general weight room, nautilus weight room, field house (the field house is used for jogging, walking, tennis, archery, fencing, and soccer), main and auxiliary gyms (the main gym is used for basketball games and devotionals), adaptives and cycle room.  

Check with the Hart Building to see what times are available as "Student Open Times."  Student open times mean that listed facilities are open for student use during that time and cannot be reserved by clubs or other organizations.  

BYU-Idaho requires that all persons using the Hart Building facilities be dressed in BYU-I physical education issue.  This includes all participants in regular physical education classes, intramurals, and free time.  P.E. issue is received in the locker room and returned after use.

Since usage and demand for fitness facilities is high, BYU-Idaho has forged an agreement with the Sports & Fitness Club in Rexburg (51 S. 1st E.) allowing students to use those facilities for free.
