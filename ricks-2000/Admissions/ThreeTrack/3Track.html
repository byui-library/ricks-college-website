THREE-TRACK

We operate on an expanded year-round basis to allow more students the opportunity to attend BYU-Idaho. This creative academic calendar revolves around a three-semester system--fall, winter, and summer. Qualified students are admitted to one of three track options. Competition for the individual tracks is based on the number of application for each track. The tracks are as follows:

 Winter/Summer Track
Admission is less competitve. Students may begin in either summer or winter and must sit out fall semesters.
Summer/Fall Track
Admission is less competitve. Students must begin summer and sit out winter semesters.
Fall/Winter Track
Admission is more competitive. Students may begin in either summer or fall. Summer is optional based on availability.
Fast Track
Students begin the summer after high school graduation. Generally most feasible for students who earn college credit while in high school.
