1. What is Interlibrary Loan (ILL)?

Interlibrary Loan is a service that provides access to materials not owned by the David O'McKay Library. Through agreements, participating libraries share their collections with us. Currently costs for Interlibrary Loans are covered by the library. Our loans are processed through BYU-Provo on a system called Illiad.

2. Please read this carefully before requesting an item through Interlibrary Loan

Before making a request, check the BYU-Idaho Online Catalog to see if we have the item in our library or databases. If it is on our catalog, obtain it here. If you need help please call or visit the Reference Desk in the west wing, ext. 2367 or the Information Desk in the east wing, ext. 2363. If the item is in our library but pages are missing, it is at the bindery, or not on the shelf, please indicate that in the notes section as you order the item from Interlibrary Loan so the order will not be cancelled.

3. Who is eligible to use Interlibrary Loan?

All of BYU-Idaho faculty, administrators, staff, students, visiting scholars, off campus patrons, and some commercial businesses are eligible to request material through Interlibrary Loan.

4. What types of material can I request?

You can request an article, book, book chapter, report, thesis, standards document and a conference paper from libraries who participate with us on ILL. BYU-Provo provides us with materials that they have.

5. How do I make an Interlibrary Loan Request?

Point the mouse to "Request an Item using Interlibrary Loan". If you are a first time user click on register as a new user and fill in the blanks. If you have any problems with this process call the Interlibrary Loan Office at 2357. When you are asked for your email address be sure to put your BYUI email address because that is how we let you know the item you ordered has arrived.

The next time you order an item go in as a registered user.

6. What do I do if I forget my username and password?

Call us at 2357 or come to the Interlibrary Loan Office, McKay Library 164 and we will help you resolve this problem.

7. How can I check on the status of my request?

Contact us in the Interlibrary Loan Office McKay Library 164, call at 2357, or send an email to ill@byui.edu.

8. How long will it take for my request to arrive?

Loans: Most items take an average of 10 days to arrive. Items that have to be requested from other states can take longer.

Articles: Articles from BYU-Provo take an average of 4 days. Articles from other libraries can take 10 days or longer.

9. What if I need a Renewal?

Logon to the Main Menu of Illiad. Under Review Requests click on the "View/Renew Checked Out Items" button. The system will automatically renew your item for two weeks upon approval from the lending library. We will contact you if you need to bring the item back sooner than the two week period.

10. What if the item is overdue?

You are responsible for the return of the material by the due date. To remain in good standing with the lending library they expect the book back on the due date that they set. So please help us remain in good standing by returning the item on the date it is due.

11. How will my article/book be delivered?

Loans: You will be notified by email when items arrive. To receive an email notification, be sure your current email address is on your Illiad registration record. Pick up your items from the Interlibrary Loan Office, McKay Library 164 between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. After 5:00 p.m. check at the Circulation Desk in the East Wing by the Interlibrary Loan Office to pick up your items.

Article Requests: Pick up articles from the Interlibrary Loan Office as described above.

12. What is document delivery?

This is a service provided for faculty, administrators and staff at BYU-Idaho. When an item arrives for you at Interlibrary Loan we deliver it to your mail box on campus and have a secretary sign for it. When you are returning an item and need to have it picked up, please leave it in your mail box and notify us by phone 2357 or email ill@byui.edu that it needs to be picked up.

This service is also provided for the faculty and adminstrators if they need to have an item from the McKay Library delivered or picked up. Please call our office 2357 (not the circulation desk or reference desk) for this service. For videos call Media Services at 2356.
