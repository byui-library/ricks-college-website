VIKING HEADLINES

ACADEMIC NATIONAL CHAMPIONS IN ALL FOUR FALL SPORTS

Viking athletes have proven once again that success in the classroom and success in athletic competition go hand in hand. In unprecedented fashion, Viking teams have won Academic Team of the Year honors for NJCAA fall sports.
At the top of the list again, is the women’s cross country team, winning the academic national championship with a team GPA of 3.83. The men’s cross country team has also won the team academic championship with a 3.35 team GPA. Both the men and the women won the Region 18 and the NJCAA Cross Country Championships.
Keeping pace was the volleyball team. Their academic national crown came as a result of a 3.61 team GPA. It’s the first academic national championship for volleyball. Viking volleyball was 32-14 this past season, finishing second in their division, third in the league, and ranked as high as fourteenth nationally.
The football team repeated as Academic Team of the Year, this time with their best team GPA ever, 3.23. No other NJCAA football team even achieved a 3.0 GPA. Viking football was 10-1 in 2001, co-champions of the WSFL, and finished the season ranked fifth on the final NJCAA poll.
Volleyball coach JoAnn Reeve summed up the feelings of coaches and athletes. "It’s a thrill to win this, but to have all our fall sports achieve Team of the Year honors is terrific. It speaks highly of all those involved in this athletic program."
