Five common myths about exercise.

Myth 1. Exercise makes you tired.

As your body get into shape, most people feel exercising gives them even more energy.

Myth 2. It takes too much time.

Regular exercise does not need to take more than 25 to minutes, three times a week. Once a routine has been established, it becomes part of your life.

Myth 3. All exercise gives you the same benefits.

Only regular, brisk, and sustained exercise can improve the efficiency of your heart and lungs and burn off a lot of calories.

Myth 4. The older you are, the less exercise you need.

As we grow older we generally become less physically active, and therefore need to make sure we are getting enough exercise. Generally, middle-aged and older people benefit from exercise just as young people do. Age should not deter you from exercising. You need to tailor your exercise program to your own fitness level.

Myth 5. You have to be athletic to exercise.

Most brisk exercises do not require any social athletic abilities. Many people who found school sports difficult have discovered these other activities are easy to do and enjoy.
