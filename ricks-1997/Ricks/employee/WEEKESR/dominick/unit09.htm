The World of TV Syndication

•Syndicators are companies that sell programs directly to TV stations and cable services.
•This is why you might see the Rosie O'Donnell Show on the ABC affiliate in Salt Lake City but on the NBC affiliate in southeastern Idaho.

The World of TV Syndication

•Each year, Program Managers from across the nation attend NATPE International (National Association of Television Program Executives). It is here that syndicators attempt to sell their programming to TV stations.
