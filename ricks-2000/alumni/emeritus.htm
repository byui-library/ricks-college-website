Emeritus Club

Emeritus Club members automatically include all those who attended or graduated over 50 years ago, as well as retired faculty, staff and administrators. 

This club offers an association and ongoing relationship with Brigham Young University - Idaho.

An Emeritus Breakfast is held twice a year around Homecoming and April graduation.  The next breakfast will be held on September, 2002 at 7:00a.m. in the Manwaring Center.

If you are not receiving notice about the breakfasts, Contact Us and update your information.

The Emeritus Service Award is presented at this biannual breakfast.  Please refer to alumni Awards for more information about this award.
