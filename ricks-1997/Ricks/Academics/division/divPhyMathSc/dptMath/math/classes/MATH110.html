Math 110 College Algebra

Credits: 3
Prerequisite: Two years of high school algebra or Math 101.
May be taken daily for 10 weeks of MWF for the full semester.Functions, polynomials, theory of equations, exponential and logar4ithmic functions, matrices, determinants, systems of linear equations, sequences and series. (This can be followed in the same semester with Math 111.) Graphing calculator is required. See class schedule.
