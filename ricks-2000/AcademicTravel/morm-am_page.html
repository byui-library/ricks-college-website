Mormon-American Travel Studies
$1,900 (excluding tuition)
Third summer term
A total of 8 credits may be earned
  • Rel 224: The Doctrine and Covenants I (2 religion credits)
  • Rel 225: The Doctrine and Covenants II (2 religion credits)
  • Rel 241: Latter-day Saint History and Doctrine (2 religion credits)
  • Eng 260: American Literature (3 letters credits)
  • AmHer 170: American Heritage (3 social science credits)
  • History 120: United States to 1877(3 social science credits)
Description of program: This annual travel study program is an experience of a lifetime. A rendevouz with the American past and its people, Jefferson, Emerson and the Prophet Joseph. We study American history and literature where it was lived and experienced. we appreciate as never before the Restoration and Mormon heritage in the context of American history, heritage and literature.

This is an integrated program with daily tutelage. We study on site where American History was made, American Literature was written and Mormon History and doctrine may be felt and testimonies strengthened. Enrollment is limited to 40.
