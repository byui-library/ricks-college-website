Ricks College News Release
August 17, 2000

New faculty to start at Ricks College

REXBURG, Idaho — Twenty-three new full-time faculty members will be working at Ricks College when fall semester classes start August 29.

Max Checketts, assistant academic vice president, says the new employees have been hired to fill vacancies mainly caused by retirements. The overall number of full-time faculty members remains steady at approximately 370.

Garth Jensen of Layton, Utah, will teach architecture and construction. Raised in Billings, Mont., and Logan, Utah, he holds a degree in architecture from Montana State University and most recently has worked as a project architect for the LDS Church Temple Construction Department.

Dave Stricklan of Dubois will teach biology. Raised on a ranch near Arco, he holds a master's degree from BYU and worked as a wildlife biologist for the U.S. Forest Service for 12 years.

Travis Wall of Burns, Ore., will teach biology. The Idaho Falls native is a Ricks alumnus and holds a master's degree from Oregon State University where he did research in range ecosystems.

Amy Hanks of Logan, Utah, will teach chemistry. She is a Ricks alumnus and holds a master's degree in chemistry from Utah State University.

Jennifer O'Farrell of Provo, Utah, will teach dance. She holds a degree from the Juilliard School in New York City. She taught for two years at Eastern Connecticut Ballet before pursuing a master's degree at BYU.

Rick Hirschi of Macomb, Ill., will teach economics. Raised on a ranch in Star Valley, Wyo., he studied beef production management at Ricks College and holds a doctorate in agricultural economics from the University of Illinois at Urbana in Champaign, Ill. He has been an assistant professor at Western Illinois University.

Anne Hendricks of Ithaca, N.Y., will teach English. She is a Ricks alumnus and holds a master's degree in English from BYU. She is daughter of former Ricks Counseling Center Director Vance Hendricks and was raised in Rexburg.

Sean Cannon will teach geography. Raised in Orem, Utah, he has been a captain in the U.S. Army and served in various assignments in the eastern United States for the past 13 years. He taught at U.S. Military Academy at West Point for three years and holds a master's degree in geography from the University of Utah.

Janiel Nelson, who was raised in Aberdeen, will teach home economics. The Ricks alumna holds a master's degree in occupational training management from ISU. She has been the associate director and field instructor of the ISU Center for Economic Education.

Shane Cole of Pleasant Grove, Utah, will work as a reference librarian in the McKay Library. He was raised in Twin Falls and most recently worked for a software provider for libraries located in Provo. He holds a master's degree in library science from BYU.

David Oliphant of Pleasant Grove, will teach physics. Born and raised in Pleasant Grove, he holds a master's degree from BYU.

Philip Allred of Idaho Falls will teach religion. Raised in Burley, he has taught LDS Church seminary in Idaho Falls for the past eight years. He holds a master's degree from Notre Dame and is working on his doctorate at ISU.

Stephen Smith of West Lafayette, Ind., will teach sociology. The Arizona native is a doctoral candidate at Purdue University where he is majoring in family studies.

Gary Benson of Granite Falls, Wash., will teach theater. He was raised in Rexburg and West Yellowstone, Mont., is a Ricks alumnus, and is the son of retired theater instructor Lynn Benson. He holds a master of fine arts degree from Mankato State University in theatre.

Lorette Courchaine of Idaho Falls will become a permanent English instructor. She worked at Ricks last year on a one-year contract. She holds a master's degree from the College of William and Mary.

Matthew Babcock of Rexburg, who has taught at Ricks for two years, also will assume a permanent faculty position. The Ricks alumnus lives in Rigby Hall where his wife, Missy, is head resident. He holds a master's from Binghamton University in creative writing and English.

Mark Orchard of Rexburg will become a permanent animal science instructor. Born in Ten Sleep, Wyo., he was raised on a cattle ranch and has manager a cattle ranch in Mud Lake. He holds a bachelor's degree from BYU. He worked at Ricks last year on a one-year contract.

Brian Schaat of Rexburg, who has been an adjunct faculty member, will teach health science full time. Raised in the upper valley, he is a Ricks alumnus and holds a master's degree in physical therapy from ISU.

Kathleen Cook of Rigby, who has been an adjunct faculty member since 1993, will teach home economics full time. Raised in Bountiful, Utah, she has just completed her master's degree in public health from Idaho State University.

Randall Kempton of Rexburg, who has been an adjunct faculty member, will teach vocal music full time. Raised in the Southwest, he is a doctoral candidate at Arizona State University. He was director of choirs at Meridian High School in Provo for four years.

JoAnn Dalling of Idaho Falls will become a full-time nursing instructor. She taught last year on a one-year contract. The Alabama native holds a master's degree in nursing from Idaho State University.

Reese Nelson of Rexburg, the Ricks greenhouse manager, will assume a faculty position in landscape horticulture. He is working on his master's degree at ISU and has worked in landscape production and maintenance at Temple Square in Salt Lake City.

Greg Palmer of Rexburg, who has been working for the LDS Foundation assigned to the Development Office at Ricks College, will teach religion classes as well as serve as director of the college's outreach program. The Rexburg native is a Ricks alumnus and holds a master's degree in international management from the American Graduate School of International Management (Thunderbird).

Kristin Erickson of Kaysville, Utah, will fill a one-year contract in counseling.

Lisa Kurki of Rexburg will fill a one-year contract in English.

Mark Bennion of Missoula, Mont., will fill a one-year contract in English. He is a son of former Ricks President Steven Bennion.

Margaret Parson of Teton will fill a one-year contract in nursing.

Rebecca Foster of Puyallup, Wash., will fill a one-year contract in theater.

Debora Scholes of Rigby will assume an 18-month position in the library.
