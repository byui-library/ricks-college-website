Course Information
COURSE DESCRIPTION: This ensemble provides a challenging performance experience for percussion students regardless of playing ability. Membership in the Percussion Ensemble is open to all interested percussionists.
OBJECTIVES: 1. Help students build skills as percussionists through performance in a musical setting of like instruments outside the traditional band or orchestra format.
2. Develop individual playing skills on a large number of percussion instruments.
3. Acquaint students with percussion ensemble literature.
PREREQUISITES: Attendance at all rehearsals and performances. Students must have own mallets.
OTHER: Piano majors may receive large ensemble credit.
