Student Employment & Career Placement

Email Your Resume to Our Database:

To file your resume with us, simply email a copy to us. When employers are interested in Ricks College alumni, we will contact you. As a reminder, we are not a staffing agency, therefore, please don't expect us to find a job for you. Finding employment is still your responsibility. We will simply forward your information on to interested employers.

Be sure your resume and related materials include the following:

Name, address, telephone number.
Email address if applicable.
College major(s).
Specific area of interest. For example:

Accounting
Agronomy
Business
Design & Drafting
Education
Electronics
English
Nursing
Psychology
Etc...

Please Note: This service is for those looking for full-time career employment. All requests for
student employment go through Student Employment & Career Placement: call (208) 356-2290.
