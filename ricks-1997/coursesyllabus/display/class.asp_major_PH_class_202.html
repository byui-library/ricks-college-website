GENERAL PHYSICS
GEN PHYSICS
CLASS CODE: PH 202 CREDITS:  5
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A continuation of Physics 201. Course is taught at the beginning calculus level and fills 5 semester hours of Physical Science and General Ed. credit.
DESCRIPTION: A continuation of Physics 201.
TOPICS: Topics include electricity and magnetism, optics, relativity, atomic and nuclear physics. These topics are the basis for mosts of our current technologies, particularly in the medical field.
OBJECTIVES: To help you learn to think physically.
REQUIREMENTS: Includes a lab taught either on Tuesday or Thursday from 3-5 p.m. It will be necessary to effectively study at least 2 hours outside of class for each hour in class to earn an average grade.
PREREQUISITES: Physics 201, Math 209 or Math 111
Concurrent Registration: none
Math Level: Extensive use of college algebra, trigonometry, and elementary calculus.
OTHER: Clear thinking is critical to the success in this course. Particularly the concepts of electricity and magnetism requre time to "age" before they have meaning.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1983
