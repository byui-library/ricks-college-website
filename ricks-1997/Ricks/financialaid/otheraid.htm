Ricks College Emergency Loans

Ricks College Emergency Loans may be used only for food, medical and other emergency needs.

Ricks College Emergency Loans for Fall Semester are to be paid by November 25 and winter Semester Loans are due March 25. However, if the student’s enrollment is discontinued, the full amount of the loan becomes due immediately. Contact the Financial Aid Office for more information.

 

Ricks College Perpetual Education Loan Fund

This fund is supported by student donations. The philosophy of this loan fund program is that the student will repay the loan plus a donation so that additional students may be helped. Each loan applicant will be interviewed by a Financial Aid Administrator. The approval or denial of the loan application will be by committee action. The committee will consist of two Ricks College administrators and a student representative.

 

Ricks College Private Grants

Limited private grant funds are available. All students who have submitted information on the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) will be considered. Students need not make specific application. These grants are awarded to students with extreme need or extenuating circumstances. Recipients will be notified by the Financial Aid Office.

Ricks College Summer School Grant

Ricks College also has funds available to assist students with tuition while attending Summer School. Students applying for a Ricks College Summer School Grant must have applied for federal student aid. International students are not required to file for federal aid to be considered. Summer School Grant applications will be available February 1. Recipients will be notified by March 31.

 

Bureau of Indian Affairs (BIA) Grant

BIA grants are available to some American INDIAN students. These grants are awarded by the student’s tribal education office or agency office. To be eligible to apply, a student must be registered with a U.S. tribe that has a treaty with the U.S. government.

Eligibility requirements may differ for each tribe; therefore, the student should contact his/her tribe to determine eligibility.

It is the responsibility of the student to keep in contact with his/her tribe and send Ricks College any necessary paperwork such as the Needs Analysis that is required by many tribes. The student must be aware of all deadlines. If you have any questions about BIA call Kenneth Jackson at (208) 356-2399.
