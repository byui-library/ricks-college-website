Wendy Callahan (80)

When I think of Ricks College, the memory that stands out is that of being in the Spori Building. Besides some enjoyable classes taken there, it’s the steps going from the first floor to the second floor that I think of. They were narrow, a dark brown color, imperfect, and took me to a place where I felt loved working as secretary of the Communication Department. Through the doors of the department I was greeted by my three bosses, my college ‘fathers;’ the wise one, the fun one and the loving one--Lee Gifford, Alan Bossard, and Kay Wilkins. The few hours at work each day ended all too soon, but I knew once I saw those steps the next day, that feeling of peace would return to me because I was going to a place I felt loved and welcome.

Wendy Callahan (80)
Moses Lake, Wash.
