Junior Business Core Requirements
lQ:  
–I haven’t quite finished my pre-business core, so I can’t take B301,321,341, 383.  What junior level business classes can I take? 
lA : 
–IS 350 Technology and E-Business (prerequisite: IS 140)
–Econ 358 - International Economics (prerequisite: Econ 111/112, Math 221)
–English 312 - Advanced Writing for Business (prerequisite  OSM220 or Eng 211,215,216)
–Rel 431 – special sections for business majors
–B 390 – Readings in Ethics and Leadership
–B 361 – Operations Management ( prereq. Math 221 and IS 140)
