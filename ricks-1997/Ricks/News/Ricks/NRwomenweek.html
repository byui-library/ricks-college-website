Ricks College News Release, Feb. 28, 1998

Women's Week March 19-22

Women's Week, sponsored by the Associated Women Students, is an annual event held at Ricks College where student's mothers are invited to visit campus. The purpose is to provide an opportunity for students to strengthen relationships with their mothers by experiencing a weekend of college life together.

A variety of activities are provided to help enrich this relationship, but are not intended to be the main focus of Women's Week.

During Women's Week regular classwork will continue and some professors may allow mothers to attend classes. There will also be events provided for mothers to enjoy while students are in class. Activities are schedules from Thursday evening through Sunday evening.

There is no formal registration or frees required to visit during the weekend. There are, however, various events you may wish to attend. Tickets should be purchased in advanced from the Ricks College Ticket Office.

Tickets will be available beginning Saturday, Feb. 28, at the Manwaring Center and Hart Building ticket offices from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. After Feb. 28 tickets may be purchased from the Manwaring Center Ticket Office from 9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. and Tuesday to 2 p.m. Tickets may also be purchased with a credit card beginning Monday, March 2, by calling 208-356-2230 or 1- 800-717-4257. There will be a $2 charge for all phone calls and students will need to pay full price for all events if ordered by phone. Tickets should be purchased in advance as events sell out quickly and seating at most events is limited. However, standby seating is often available.

Ricks will have charter buses or vans available from the Salt Lake area for $50 round trip. Shuttle service is also available to and from the Idaho Falls airport for $8. Call 208-356-1150.
