CLASS REQUIREMENTS

1. QUIZZES: There will be preparation quizzes (10 pts each) in class. No make-ups unless cleared with me in advance. Your lowest score is  automatically dropped.

2. EXAMS: Your exams will consist of two parts: a written major exam, taken in the Testing Center on dates indicated on the syllabus, and a Picture Quiz, taken in class. Exams count 100 points and all are comprehensive. Picture quizzes vary, but are usually about 45 points. None of these will be dropped.

3. GRADES: An ongoing printout showing your final grade to date is on the bulletin board by the Humanities Office. Grades are based on total points, with a 90, 80, 70, etc. cut-off. Your lowest quiz score is automatically dropped (all your scores will still appear on the printout but the lowest number is not be included in the total).

4. EXTRA CREDIT: There is none.

5. WANTS:  Please bear with me on three things: no food in class, no baseball caps, and I like to start on time, not five minutes after the bell.

          I’ll try to adhere to this schedule, but life being what it is, some things may change. You’ll be informed if they do.
