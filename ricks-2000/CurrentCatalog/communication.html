The Department of Communication provides opportunities for students to study and build skills in human and mass communication and communicative disorders with the ultimate aim of transferring to a university for further study. Communication education at Ricks is enhanced by hands-on opportunities with speech and hearing screening services, two FM radio stations, weekly newspaper, yearbook, video and audio editing labs, internet site development, forensics team, and various other opportunities to apply interpersonal, speech and organizational communication skills.

Communicative Disorders majors are provided a challenging, diverse program and curriculum designed to prepare them for additional studies at the university level.

While the Communication major is primarily geared for students transferring to similar majors on the university level, some students find it a valuable foundation on their way to other areas of study. The Ricks Communication program emphasizes a strong, diverse, common core of studies, enhanced by introductions to specialized career-oriented areas, and capped by practicum or laboratory experiences intended to apply principles learned in classes. Students majoring in Communication have the opportunity to choose from eight areas of emphasis and are allowed, with the aid of their advisors, the flexibility to mix and match courses to best match their interests.

Program Descriptions

Associate in Arts and Sciences with a major in Communicative Disorders (046)

Students in the Communicative Disorders program prepare for further studies in the fields of speech-language pathology or audiology, professions dedicated to assisting individuals with communication difficulties. Major requirements are designed not only to enhance student understanding of human communication and its disorders, but also to help students become more effective communicators themselves. A 3.0 GPA is required for graduation in this major.

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

Credits

Comm 102* Public Speaking 3
Three hours per week

Improving public speaking skills and verbal ability, self-confidence, message structure, presentation strategies and audience adaptation.

Comm 105 Speech and Hearing Therapy 1
Prerequisite: Instructor permission
One hour private therapy per week
Private lesson fee: $50
May be repeated for credit

Individual speech therapy for students who have speech, language or hearing difficulties that interfere with their ability to communicate effectively.

Comm 110 Mass Media and Society 3
Three hours per week
Prerequisite: Suggested completion of Comm 220

Surveys mass communication and related contemporary issues, with particular emphasis on societal impacts of and on the media.

Comm 202 Introduction to Disorders of Communication 3
Three hours per week

Introduces the professions of speech pathology and audiology, examines normal communication, and surveys communicative disorders.

Comm 203 Phonetics: Theory and Application 3
Three hours per week

Transcription and analysis of normal and disordered speech production. (Winter only)

Comm 205 Clinical Preparation in Communicative Disorders 2
Prerequisite: Comm 202 or concurrent enrollment
Two hours per week
For majors only

Preparation for a clinical experience in Communicative Disorders. (Winter only)

Comm 210R Yearbook 1
Prerequisite: Consent of Instructor Four hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised practice in planning and producing the student digital yearbook on CD-ROM.

Comm 211** Writing For Communication Careers 3
Prerequisite: Communication major or Instructor permission
Two class hours plus one lab hour per week
Computer supplies fee: $5

Analysis of audience, purpose, and context; selection and use of appropriate voice, structure, and techniques for effective written communication for mass audiences and other applications.

Comm 212 Editing and Production 3
Prerequisite: Comm 211
Two class hours per week plus one lab hour per week
Computer supplies fee: $5

Training in print media copy editing, layout and design, headline and caption writing, photo editing and sizing, graphic design and publication production.

Comm 213R Campus Newspaper 1
Two hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised work on the campus newspaper in the areas of writing, editing, photography, design and production, advertising sales and newspaper management.

Comm 214 Research and Reporting 3
Prerequisite: Communication major or consent of Instructor
Two class hours plus one lab hour per week
Computer supplies fee: $5

Information gathering strategies and techniques, including library, database and Internet searching and use; source and data analysis. Interviewing and reporting techniques and skills for mass media and marketing.

Comm 215 Photojournalism 3
Three hours per week
Supplies fee: $45, key deposit: $5
Students must supply their own SLR cameras

Examines functions of pictures in the print media, how to maximize their effectiveness, and provides experience in picture taking and darkroom procedures. (Fall only)

Comm 217R New Media Lab 1
Two hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit
Prerequisite: Comm 218 or consent of Instructor

Supervised work developing, editing, and maintaining publicly accessed campus Internet pages.

Comm 218 Internet Communications 3
Non-Communication majors should consult with Instructor before registering for course
One class hour lecture plus two lab hours per week
Computer supplies fee: $5

Examination of the use of computer technology, focusing on the Internet, in gathering, designing and transmitting news and information. Specific training in computer software applications and web page design.

Comm 220* Interpersonal Theory and Practice 3
Three hours per week

Examines basic elements and theory of human communication. Explores varied facets of interpersonal process, with emphasis on improving communication skills. Intended as opening course in sequence for majors but appropriate for students who will only take one communication course.

Comm 235 Introduction to Public Relations 3
Three hours per week

Philosophy and practice of public relations in business, government, education, and other organizations. Study of publics, media, methods, press relations, and publicity.

Comm 240 Small Group Communication 3
Prerequisite: Comm 220
Three class hours per week

How to organize, lead, participate in, and evaluate small group discussions, conferences, and meetings.

Comm 250 Organizational Communication 3
Prerequisite: Comm 220
Three hours per week

Analysis of communication principles and practices in organizations, with a strong theoretical base and an emphasis on improving organizational skills.

Comm 256 Introduction to Broadcasting 2
For Broadcasting majors only
One class hour plus two hours lab per week

Discussion of radio and television as a career. Instruction in basic radio and television production techniques.

Comm 259 Beginning Television Production 3
Prerequisite: Comm 256
Two class hours plus two lab hours per week

Study and practice of television production techniques, with special emphasis on news and commercial material. Involves operation of equipment as well as performance opportunities.

Comm 265R Radio Station Operation 1
Prerequisite: Comm 256 or consent of Instructor
Two hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised work on KWBH-FM student radio station in the area of on-air announcing, production and station operation and management.

Comm 266R FM Radio Station Operation .5-2
Prerequisite: Comm 265R and consent of Instructor
Two hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised work on KRIC-FM involving students selected to serve as staff announcers.

Comm 267R Electronic Field Production 1
Prerequisite: Comm 259 or consent of Instructor
One hour per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Creation and development of advanced video productions.

Comm 271R Forensics 1
Prerequisite: Consent of Instructor
Four hours per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised work with Ricks' intercollegiate speech and debate team.

Comm 275R Communication Practicum 1
Prerequisite: Comm 220 or consent of Instructor
One hour per week plus out-of-class assignments
May be repeated for credit

Supervised work on a variety of communication-oriented projects applying interpersonal, speech and rhetoric, organizational and general communication principles.

Comm 298 Internship (Broadcast) 1-2
Prerequisite: Consent of Instructor
Schedule and term of internship to be arranged

Comm 299 Internship (Journalism) 1-2

On-the-job print journalism training to be arranged through and monitored by Instructor.

SIGN 101 American Sign Language I 3
Three hours per week

For beginning students. Development of basic conversational-level skills, general overview of the deaf culture and current issues of interest to the deaf community.

SIGN 102 American Sign Language II 3
Prerequisite: Sign 101 or consent of Instructor
Three hours per week
