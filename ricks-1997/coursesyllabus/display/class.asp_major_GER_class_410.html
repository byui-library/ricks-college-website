German Literature
GERMAN LIT
CLASS CODE: GER 410 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A cross section of major literary works in German will be discussed, raed, and related to their respective genres and periods.
DESCRIPTION: A panoramic veiw of literature of German nations and cultures. Also, an in-depth study of selected masterpieces.
TOPICS: Students study literary genres including the novel [and corresponding film], novelette, short story, drama and the Holy scriptures.
OBJECTIVES: To instill in students the ability to understand and appreciate artistic expression, thought and world view found in francophone cultural traditions. To give them skills in deep raeding, analysis, and discussion of great works. To increase their ease of expression and accuracy in the target language.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, oral participation, small group work, oral and/ or written reports. Students completing this course should be able to explain, discuss, and give extensive oral and written responses to the assigned works.
PREREQUISITES: German 202 and 321
