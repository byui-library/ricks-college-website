COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 299
Title: Comprehensive Project Credit: 4
Text: Interior Design Reference Manual, Ballast
          Interior Design Practicum Exam Workbook, Henley

Course Objectives:
 1. To review Interior Design major course material in preparation for the NCIDQ examination.
 2. To further develop oral, written and presentation skills through class lecture opportunities.
 3. To develop speed and refine skills in programming, conceptual design, drafting, space planning, and lighting in timed class scenarios.
 4. To provide an opportunity for further research skill development through community design projects.
 5. To provide an opportunity for team approach to design conceptualization, process and solution.

Description:
A course designed to prepare the student for the NCIDQ (National Council for Interior Design Qualification) Examination through lecture review of previous design courses, written exams, completion of timed projects, and research and development of a community project(s).

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Preparation and presentation of a 40-50 minute lecture assigned from the course text.
2. Completion of nine objective quizzes from course text material.
3. 80 hours of individual community design “services” with original project books to substantiate projects completed with “real-life clients”.
4. Completion of four timed projects in class which simulate actual NCIDQ scenarios.
