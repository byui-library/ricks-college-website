Calling Good Evil and Evil Good

Letter of Pastor Joe Wright

Pastor Joe Wright of Witchita Central Christian Church was invited to give the invocation for the Kansas State Legislature a few years ago. No sooner had the guest chaplain concluded his prayer than three representative were on their feet at the microphones protesting is prayer. By the three representatives, the invocation was called gross, divisive, sanctimonious, overbearing, blasphemous, and ignorant. So what had Pastor Wright said that incited the righteous wrath of these representatives? Here is the invocation Pastor Wright gave, and I quote:
 

Heavenly Father, we come before you today to ask your forgiveness and to seek your direction and guidance. We know your Word says, "Woe to those who call evil good." but that is exactly what we have done.

We have lost our spiritual equilibrium and inverted our values.
We confess that we have ridiculed the absolute truth of your Word in the name of moral pluralism.
We have worshiped other gods and called it multiculturalism.
We have endorsed perversion and called it alternative life style.
We have exploited the poor and called it the lottery.
We have neglected the needy and called it self-preservation.
We have rewarded laziness and called it welfare.
In the name of choice, we have killed our unborn.
We have neglected to discipline our children and called it building self esteem.
We have abused power and called it political savvy.
We have coveted our neighbor's possessions and called it taxes.
We have polluted the air with profanity and pornography and called it freedom of expression.
We have ridiculed the time on earth values of our forefathers and called it enlightenment.

Search us, O God, and know our hearts today. Try us and show us any wicked in us. Cleanse us from every sin and set us free. Guide and bless these men and women who have been sent here by the people of Kansas, and who have been ordained by you to govern this great state. Grant them your wisdom to rule. May their decisions direct us to the center of your will. I ask in the name of your Son, the Living Savior, Jesus Christ, Amen.

Observation of my own

Consider just a few ways in which Satan can make evil appear good and good evil: Not long ago a man and a woman living together outside the bonds of marriage would be considered as living in sin--Conveniently we now call this COHABITATION.

Until a few years ago, society considered homosexuality as unnatural, and for the most part, participation was in secret or in the "closet" so to speak. In just a few short years this evil practice has received a new and elevated status. Those who engage in unnatural affections are merely expressing themselves through an ALTERNATIVE LIFESTYLE.

There was a day when it was considered common knowledge that the idler should not eat the bread nor wear the garments of the laborer--and I would add nor sleep in the laborer's home. And yet even this group has been the recipient of increased social status as they are no longer called beggars, or vagrants, but HOMELESS.

This title suggests that they are the victims of society, and while we should be found sympathetic and willing to help, we are not the cause of their idleness. Divorce has become quite fashionable as societal pressure to keep couples together has dwindled. All a couple has to say is we have IRRECONCILABLE DIFFERENCES.

Notice no mention of irreconcilable pride that has resulted in selfishness, self pity, and broken homes. Abortion is conveniently labeled PROCHOICE. Prayer in the schools is an INFRINGEMENT on the rights of a vocal minority.

The production and dissemination of pornography are protected under FREEDOM OF THE PRESS and FREE SPEECH laws. With very few exceptions, no single resource of Satan can take away an individual's freedom more completely than pornography.

Using sexist language is more socially unacceptable and has greater social consequences than immoral behavior. The media every chance it gets, will condone evil and diminish righteousness. Our political leader's infidelity is a nonissue--yet if misuse of public funds is involved-- that is dishonest. We cannot trust them.

Now a little closer to home. I have noticed a similar pattern expressing itself across our campus:

Our students participate in what has become known as a noncommittal makeout. That is to say that because there is little or no respect initially between two individuals it is okay to take advantage of one another. It is only wrong if we think we love each other.

Student cheating is on the increase--it would appear it is only wrong if one gets caught. Talking with a Stake President at BYU, he states: "Our number one problem across campus is students thinking they can engage in premeditated sin." Young adults wishing not to date before their sixteenth birthday, or going steady too early, are having to defend themselves not to the world, but their peers. Their own latter day saint friends are among some of their greatest critics.
