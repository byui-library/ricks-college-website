STUDY GUIDE #4

Entrepreneurship & Small Business Management

Part 4 Exam, Chapter 16-18

TRUE/FALSE

  1. Research has always shown that socially responsible practices have a negative impact on profits.

  2. One study cited in the textbook found entrepreneurs tend to be more narrowly focused on profits and thus less socially responsible then CEOs of large businesses.

  3. Acting in a socially responsible manner can be costly to small businesses.

  4. Codes of ethics should not only express the principles that members of the firm should follow but also give examples of situations likely to be faced.

  5. In most cases, the pressures of environmentalism have contributed to the profitability of small firms.

  6. Bait advertising is considered unethical because the business lures customers with an attractive price only to try to convince them to purchase more expensive products or services.

  7. According to a recent survey, many small business owners experienced ethical problems pertaining to the environment, but very few of them reported ethical problems in relationships with customers.

  8. When a firm reaches Stage 2 (player-coach) in its growth, the entrepreneur withdraws from personal participation in production, selling, writing checks, and record keeping.

  9. Although management consultants can be helpful to business performance, they do not serve small businesses.

  10. Stewardship delegation focuses on results in judging the performance of a subordinate in carrying out assigned tasks.

  11. There are now several hundred  incubators in the United States, but the popularity of these facilities is decreasing.

  12. If staff specialists begin to give orders instead of being helpers, they destroy the unity of command.

  13. Gofer delegation allows the subordinate substantial freedom in carrying out an assignment.

  14. Intermediate supervision, which occurs in Stage 3 of small business growth, involves the use of budgets, personal policies, organizational charts, and computerization.
