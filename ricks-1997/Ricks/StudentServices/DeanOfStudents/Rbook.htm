"R" BOOK
"Your Passport to Success"

The "R"Book is a pamphlet that is made available to all students. This book explains general information about campus and campus life.  The welcomes by President Bednar and Vice President Wilkes are followed by an alphabetical listing of this information

  Welcome   

On behalf of the faculty, staff, and administration of this great institution, I welcome you to Ricks College.  Your decision to join us at Ricks is an expression of trust which we value and take most seriously.  Our primary desire is to assist you in making your experience at Ricks both meaningful and memorable.  We are confident you will contribute to and benefit from the “Spirit of Ricks.”

Ricks College exists to provide an education consistent with the religious and family values taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  The Church leaders who comprise the Board of Trustees of Ricks College have provided a beautiful campus where you can develop spiritually, intellectually, socially, and physically.  A wealth of resources exists at Ricks College, particularly a competent and caring faculty, to help you achieve your personal and career goals.  I encourage you to take full advantage of and magnify the many opportunities for learning provided at this unique institution.

The Honor Code and related standards are essential elements in the learning environment at Ricks College.  The decision you made to comply with and adhere to these guidelines will be a great blessing in your life and for the entire campus community.

Attending devotionals, studying for exams, meeting new people and developing lifelong friendships, serving in church assignments, participating in campus organizations, discussing an issue or a concern with a faculty member who truly cares---all of these activities will be important parts of your Ricks College experience.

We are delighted you chose to attend Ricks College and look forward to working and serving with you this year.  We wish you the very best as you join us at this special school.

 Sincerely,
 
 

David A. Bednar
President


Dear Student:

I am glad you have enrolled at Ricks College.  It is always a thrill to  meet new students and to feel the enthusiasm you bring to our campus.

I hope you will experience great success while you are here.  When one is well informed, the chances of succeeding are great.  Most of the circumstances you face at college can be resolved by following established procedures.  The rules, regulations, policies, and procedures outlined in this booklet are designed to be informative and to provide you with the answers you need to succeed.  That’s why we call it "Your Passport to Success" at Ricks College.  If the answer you seek cannot be found in these pages, please ask your advisor or one of our friendly Ricks College employees.  You will find people at the school eager to accommodate your needs.

I would advise you to get involved.  Experience the growth that comes through participation in activities planned by the school as well as your student ward.

One of the life enriching characteristics you will have the opportunity to establish and/or enhance while at Ricks College will be the ability to make and keep commitments.  That capacity is becoming increasingly rare in the self indulgent world in which we live.  Being able to do so, however, will serve you well as a student and beyond.

I can promise that you will have a great year if you keep yourself informed, become involved, and keep the commitments you’ve made.  Let me know if I can ever be of help.  I would welcome the chance to become personally acquainted with you.

Success to you!

Sincerely,
 
Robert M. Wilkes
Student Life Vice President
