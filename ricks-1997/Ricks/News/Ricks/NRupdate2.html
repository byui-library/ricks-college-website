Ricks Update - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

April 28, 1998

Elder Henry B. Eyring of the Quorum of the Twelve and commissioner of the Church Educational System, spoke to the graduates at the college's 109th graduation ceremonies Saturday morning. A record 2,946 degrees were awarded to 2,874 graduates. The Class of 1998 has 1,153 males, 1,721 females, 1,100 returned missionaries, 458 married students and 78 foreign students. The states with the most graduates are Idaho with 1,100; Utah with 447, California with 231 and Washington with 195. Including these graduates, there are now 122,800 alumni of Ricks College.
Elder Eyring told graduates, families and faculty in a packed Hart Auditorium that they had succeeded at Ricks because of miracles that had happened in their lives. "In every graduate's life there came the help of heaven," he said. "If we had time ... they [the graduates] could tell us story after story. For some, it would be the day when they found just the right reference they needed to write an assigned paper. For another, it would be a sudden flash of insight during a feverish night of preparation for a test in mathematics. Or it might have been when a check arrived just in time to pay a bill."
Elder Eyring said some people call such occurrences "coincidences or simply the result of a natural process. But to the student who felt the miracle, it was more. He or she felt what we all have felt - that quiet assurance that the help came from heaven." He encouraged the graduates to always line themselves up with the purposes of heaven, and when they do, he promised they would always receive help.
Artell G. Chapman, 88, died April 21. He was a professor at Ricks for 39 years teaching in the math and natural sciences areas. The family suggest memorials to the Artell and Verla Chapman Scholarship Fund at Ricks.
President David Bednar will be in Arizona May 11 and 12 to meet with alumni and friends of the college. A fireside will be Tuesday, May 12, at 7 p.m. in the Mesa Central Stake Center, 925 N. Harris. Come join us.
