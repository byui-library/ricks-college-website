Scholarships



  There are a limited number of department scholarships available for Psychology Majors.

Eligibility Requirements:
    1.  Be a full time student in good standing.
    2.  Be a declared psychology major, with the intention of continuing as a major.
    3.  Have completed more than one semester of study at Ricks College.
    4.  Have a specific financial need.
    5.   Submit application by the end of the first week of April of the year in which the scholarship will    
          be awarded.

 Criteria for awarding scholarships:
    1.  College Grade Point Average (GPA)
    2.  Commitment to Psychology as a field
    3.  Financial Need
