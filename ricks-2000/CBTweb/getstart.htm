Getting Started with CBTWeb


CBTWeb™ is a system for deploying CBT Systems™ courseware over an intranet or the Internet. It has been designed to allow students to locate and launch CBT Systems courses easily using their web browsers. It also allows administrators to track student usage of their training library.

CBTWeb is presented like a typical web site, with linked  HTML pages, so that students and administrators can navigate their way around the product easily . Once the student has found a course they want to study, they simply click the LivePlay button. If they choose to play the course, the course immediately starts up on screen.

This page briefly describes CBTWeb. For more detailed information, see the CBTWeb Installation and Administration Guide or the CBTWeb Student’s Guide.

It takes just three steps to get started:

  1. First, you download and install some components needed on your workstation for launching courses. (This is described in the Required components section below).

  2. You can then find a course quickly to take online by viewing the list of courses available from this site page.

    or

    You can go straight to the Courseware page to view information on any course in the CBT Systems courseware library. The Courseware page contains links to Curriculum Planners, which you can use to plan your course of study. Each Curriculum Planner is in turn linked to Course Descriptions, which provide useful information on each course, for example the topics covered and the study time entailed.

  3. When you’ve found a course to launch, simply click the LivePlay button. The course can then be taken online.

The sections that follow provide more detailed instructions on how to complete these steps.

If this is the first time you’ve used CBTWeb, you will need to download and then install the CBTPlayer™.

To download the CBTPlayer, click the appropriate link below:

Windows® 95 and Windows NT® users click here to download the CBTPlayer (32-bit)

Windows® 3.x users click here to download the CBTPlayer (16-bit)

Note: Once you have downloaded the file you should double-click it to start the installation process.

CBTWeb is accessed using your web browser. On the CBTWeb home page, you can click the Courseware link, which will bring you to the CBT Systems courseware library.

On the Courseware page, you can quickly find a course to take online by clicking the list of courses available from this site link.

Alternatively, by clicking one of the hotspots on the graphic, you can navigate through CBTWeb, browsing information on curricula and the courses in each curriculum. You can even find out exactly which topics are covered in a course and the number of study hours it entails. For example, here we have navigated to the Course Description for a course on Internet concepts.

To take a course online, you navigate to its Course Description page and click the LivePlay button. The course starts up, its front screen displays, and you can start working through the units.

Note: If the course has not been made available to you, there will be no LivePlay button on the Course Description page. You can request the course from your training administrator.


When you take a course online, your progress is automatically sent to your training administrator when you exit the course.
