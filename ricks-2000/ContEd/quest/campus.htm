WHERE IS REXBURG?

Rexburg is located in the Upper Snake River Valley and was settled by Mormon pioneers who had the desire to provide an education for their children.
From the humble  beginnings of the Bannock and Ricks Academies, BYU-Idaho has grown and developed into a world class educational institution.  Students in the Quest program will take part of their course work on campus either on the front end or back end of their experience at Vashon Island or Badger Creek.
The campus has grown up over the years with the center of campus now focused around the quad  between the Manwaring Center and the Taylor  building to the South.  The campus environment provides plenty of opportunity for course work, service, and social activities.  Quest students are encouraged to participate in campus wards, service projects and social groups while they are on campus in Rexburg. (Activities)
The newest building on campus the Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building provides students with services from career advising to check cashing. (Campus Map)
Quest students are fully matriculated BYU-Idaho students who have all of the privileges that campus can provide.The up-to-date computer labs on campus can be used by students.  The John Hart Physical Education Building presents students with the chance to engage in intramural sports, personal fitness and recreation.
