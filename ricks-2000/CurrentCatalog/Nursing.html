The Nursing Department offers the following programs:

ASSOCIATE IN ARTS AND SCIENCES DEGREE WITH A MAJOR IN NURSING
This program has been designed to prepare the Graduate to write the national licensing examination for Registered Nurses.

Upon completion of the Nursing curriculum, the Graduate will be prepared to:
1. Use the conceptual model as a guide for holistic nursing care.
2. Administer nursing care in a variety of health care settings required for patients with identified health problems across the life span.
3. Be accountable for their individual nursing practice.
4. Assume responsibility for personal and professional stewardship goals.
5. Be eligible to write professional board exam.

ENTRANCE REQUIREMENTS
Students are accepted into the Nursing program following admission to Ricks College, and review and selection by the Nursing Admissions Council.
The evaluation is based upon:
1. Chemistry requirement met
2. Science classes taken
3. Scholastic Record
4. American College Test (ACT) score
5. TOEFL (see below)
6. Adjusted GPA (per Ricks College Admissions Dept)

Two separate applications are required; one for Ricks College, and one for the nursing program.

The completed nursing application must be on file with the Department of Nursing prior to February 15.

All students with English as a second language will be required to pass the TOEFL Examination with a score of at least 500. Check your local college/university for availability. Ricks College offers the exam by request only.

Clinical experiences are in the communities of Rexburg, Idaho Falls, and Blackfoot. To achieve the best possible clinical education, students are assigned to various facilities throughout these communities. Students are responsible for, and assume the risks of, their own transportation to and from the clinical areas.

Students will be required to participate in two trips; one during their Freshman year, and one in the Sophomore year.
-Freshman Travel Fee: $15, to be assessed during N 100 course
-Sophomore Travel Fee: $25, to be assessed at the time of the trip

PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS
After being accepted to Ricks College and prior to starting Fall Semester, the student must:
1. Receive their confirmation letter to the Nursing program
2. Return signed Acceptance Form by specified date.
3. Provide documentation of current "Healthcare Provider CPR" certification
4. Submit completed health questionnaire with immunization documentation.
5. Provide own transportation to clinicals (see above).

Each nursing student is required to carry student liability insurance against malpractice suits. The insurance fee is $10 per semester. This is paid for by Ricks College.

LPN's accepted into the program may choose to take the NLN Mobility Exam I at their own expense. If they pass with a 75% composite or better, and they pass a required clinical case scenario exam, they will be permitted to waive Nursing 100 and 100A. The student must contact the Department Chair to receive approval prior to beginning the program.
Other Required Costs:
Freshman: $45
Sophomores: $50
Complete the NCLEX Review Course, total cost-$240( $30/selected modules)

TRANSCULTURAL NURSING
One transcultural experience is offered by the department each year. Programs are in Jerusalem, Israel, OR Merida, Mexico. A select number of students may participate. Application for the program occurs during Fall Semester of the Freshman year. The transcultural experience is completed during the Fall Semester of the Sophomore year. Details pertaining to the specific experience, including curriculum recommendations and requirements, will be mailed to each student with their acceptance packet. Prerequisite to the Mexico Program is one semester of collegiate Spanish, or completion of the approved Spanish offered through Ricks College Continuing Education Program.
Contact Sue Towne or Kim Van Wagoner, Chair, (208) 356-1325 for more information.

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

Credits

Nurs 100 Fundamentals 6
Prerequisites: Acceptance into nursing program, completion of prerequisites, as stated in the catalog.

This course offers the student experience in basic nursing assessment and care of the hospitalized patient in acute care settings.
Two hours core-lecture, and nine hours clinical/ lab per week.
Lab fee: $5
Syllabus: $45
Nursing Kit: $125

Nurs 100A Professionalism; Interpersonal Skills 1
Prerequisite: Acceptance into nursing program.

Introduction of concepts related to professionalism including values, leadership, economics, ethics, teaching/learning, legal implications, cultural and spiritual aspects.
Communication and interpersonal skills will be emphasized.
This class will meet one hour per week.

Nurs 101 Gerontology 2
Prerequisites: Nurs 100 and 100A

This course relates to the nursing assessment, care, and common disease processes of the geriatric population. (5 week course)
Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5

Nurs 102 Metabolic/GU 2
Prerequisites: Nurs 100 and 100A

This course incorporates nursing assessment and management of disorders involving the kidney and related structures, liver, gall bladder, and pancreas.
(5 week course)
Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 103 Psychiatric 3
Prerequisites: Nurs 100 and 100A

This course is designed to aid the student in applying the nursing process to the psychological needs of the patient, as well as providing the student with a growing self understanding through the study of the principles of psychiatric nursing.
(5 week course)
Three hours lecture, and 12 hours
clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 104 GI and Cancer 3
Prerequisites: Nurs 100 and 100A,

This course focuses on problems of the gastrointestinal tract, and introduces the student nurse to the care of a person with cancer.
(5 week course)
Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 200 Neuro/Endocrine 4
Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This course focuses on the assessment and care of the person with problems relating to neurological, sensory and endocrine systems and immobility.
(Block course)
Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference and 14 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 201 Cardiovascular and Respiratory 4
Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This course focuses on prevention of illness and patient teaching, as well as covering assessment and nursing care of the person with problems relating to respiratory and cardiovascular systems.
(Block course)
Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference, and 14 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 202 Obstetrics 4
Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This course offers the student experience in the hospital and community settings in assessing and caring for the child bearing family. (Block course)
Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference, and 14 hours clinical per week
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 203 Pediatrics 4
Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This course focuses on the assessment and care of the well and sick infant, child, and adolescent in hospital and community settings.
(Block course)
Three hours lecture and 14 hours clinical per week.
Lab fee: $5
NCLEX Review Fee: $30

Nurs 204 Law & Ethics 1 Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This course focuses on professional accountability and legal aspects.
(Fall Semester)
One hour lecture per week
Lab fee: $5
Syllabus: $50

Nurs 205 Trends and Professionalism 1
Prerequisites: Currently enrolled in the Nursing Program

This class is a continuation of Nurs 204 with a focus on licensure, current trends, professional and educational opportunities.
(Winter Semester)
One hour lecture per week
Graduation Pin Fee: $35

Nurs 290 Special Problems 1-3

This course provides the opportunity for study of pertinent nursing topics. May be repeated for credit.
