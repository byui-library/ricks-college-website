OPEN HOUSE PLANNED FOR NEW RICKS BUILDING

Construction was recently completed on a new, 4,225 square foot building on the Ricks College campus. The building, known as the Radio and Graphic Services Building (RGS), will have an open house for the public Thursday, Dec. 7, from 5-8 p.m. Refreshments will be served and tours of the building will be given. The building is located immediately east of the Kimball Building parking lot, one half block west of the intersection of South Second East Street and Viking Drive.

The building houses KRIC-FM, the college-owned National Public Radio (NPR) station, and Graphic Services, the college's design studio for publications, brochures and logos. Both entities were previously located in the Spori Building which is now being razed. Beginning in January the building will also house KWBH-FM, a low-wattage, laboratory-type radio station operated by radio and communications majors at Ricks College. The building is a one story building with brick veneer and a metal roof. The building was designed by Rulon Nielsen, an architect on the Ricks College Physical Plant staff and was constructed by Vern Clark and Sons Construction of Idaho Falls.


WORLD-RENOWNED CANADIAN BRASS TO PERFORM AT RICKS

 

In the midst of their 30th anniversary touring season, the world-renowned Canadian Brass will stop Wednesday, Dec. 6, at the Hart Auditorium to present an evening of Christmas music mixed with some of their standard repertoire.

Tickets are $8 and $6 for the public and on sale at the Ricks College Ticket Office (208) 356-2230. Tickets can also be ordered online at www.byui.edu/tickets. A special pre-show dinner is planned at 6 p.m. in the Manwaring Center for $10. The Canadian Brass will perform a variety of Christmas selections, along with music by Johann Sebastian Bach, Georg Frideric Handel and George Gershwin.

From their first concerts in Canada in 1970, the quintet is considered to have set a new standard for brass performance. Three decades of recording and performing on major stages around the world in addition to numerous television appearances have met with abundant acclaim and recognition. They are brass quintet-in-residence at the University of Toronto and chamber quintet-in-residence at the Music Academy of the West in Santa Barbara, California. Their more than 50 recordings have created a new musical tradition in brass performance. More information about the ensemble may be found on The Canadian Brass web site at www.canadianbrass.com.


ANNUAL PRESENTATION OF "MESSIAH"

Ricks College will present its annual performance of Handel's "Messiah" Sunday, Dec. 10, at 7 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Free tickets for the "Messiah" are available from the Ticket Office. Only bleacher seats are available, and no children under 6 or babies will be admitted. Call (208) 356-2230 for more information.

The performance will feature various Ricks orchestras and choirs along with student soloists. The choirs include Collegiate Singers, Ricks Men's Choir, Women's Choir, College Choir and Women's Glee, along with the Madison High School Bel Canto from Rexburg. The Ricks College Symphony Orchestra and the Ricks College String Orchestra will play. Highlights will include the combined choirs singing "And the Glory of the Lord," "Hallelujah" and the finale "Worthy Is the Lamb."

RICKS MUSICAL CHRISTMAS USHERS IN THE SEASON

The annual Ricks Musical Christmas will open the Christmas season on Thursday and Friday, Nov. 30 and Dec. 1. The concerts will be held at 6 & 8:30 p.m. each night in the Barrus Concert Hall. Tickets are $2 for the public. Call the Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230 or see www.byui.edu/tickets.

Ricks College choirs that will be performing include the Collegiate Singers, Men's Choir, Women's Choir and the Chamber Orchestra. Ricks faculty member Kevin Brower says the concert has become an annual favorite for students and community alike. "Traditional carols and other holiday favorites set the mood for the story of Christ, giving, and love for all," he says. "The hope of all who participate in Ricks Musical Christmas is to share heartfelt feelings of hope made possible by the Savior's birth, life, death and resurrection."

ASRC PRESENTS WINTERFEST

Snow blankets the Ricks College campus just in time for the Associated Students of Ricks College planned Winterfest activities this week. "The purpose (of the week) is to continue to do the things Ricks College has always done - that is, promote student involvement, friendships and allow them to have fun," said sophomore Marci Clay, ASRC programs vice president from Blue Springs, Mo. Snow sculpture contests are slated for Thursday and will add seasonal touch for visitors to Christmas Tree Lane which opens that same night in the Manwaring Center. The annual tradition will feature more than 40 trees decorated by college and community clubs. The trees will remain up to be enjoyed by the public through Dec. 11. The annual ski swap will be held in the MC West Ballroom from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Friday. The annual Warren Miller ski movie will be shown in the Kirkham Saturday at 7:30 p.m. Movie tickets will be $6 for students and $9 for non-students available at the Ticket Office.
