How to Find Pictures and Posters

Pictures or posters can be located using the Library Catalog from the Library Home Page. Choose the General Search option from the choices at the top of the screen. Scroll to the bottom of the page and use the Limiting Fields and Collections box to select Pictures all floors or Posters all floors. Scroll back up to the top of the screen and enter your subject into the search box. Pictures or posters that match your search topic will be displayed on the screen, along with their call numbers and location in the library. Pictures and posters can also be accessed using the PAC for Windows catalog on the computer desktop. Select the Search Settings box and click on Pictures and Posters, return to the search screen and enter your search topics. Pictures or posters that match your search will be displayed, along with their call numbers.
