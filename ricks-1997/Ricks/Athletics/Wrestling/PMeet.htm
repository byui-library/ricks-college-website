WRESTLING

    Coach: Bob Christensen (18th season 209-69-2)   2001-2002 Dual Record 7-7,   NJCAA #5

    PREVIOUS MEET: 

    January 10-25, 2002
     A January 10th match at Western Wyoming was cancelled.  The Vikings went on the road and recorded a fifth place finish at the Dickinson State Invitational, followed by losses at Montana State Northern, Northwest CC, and Neosho CC. The Vikings bounced back with a win at Yakima, WA, but closed the regular season with a loss at North Idaho.

    January 8, 2002 (in Rexburg)
    Ricks College 31   Lassen College 12
     Ricks College beat 5th ranked Lassen as Steve Babcock (184) recorded a major decision and heavyweight Robert Perkes scored a third period takedown for a decision.  Lassen forfeited at 141, 165, 174, and 197, then won narrow decisions at 125, 133, 149, and 157.

    January 4-5, 2002 (in Washington)
     Returning to action for the new year, the Vikings handily downed Highline CC of Washington, 40-7. Bob Perkes (hwt) and Dave Anderson (174) recorded pins. Pete Mecham (165) took a major decision as the Vikings only losses came at 125 and 197. Moving on to the Pacific Lutheran Open, only B.J. Wright (157) placed, taking second with a 4-1record. Steve Babcock (184) won 3 of 5 matches.

    December 8, 2001
    Ricks College 18   MSU Northern 33 (in Rexburg)
     The defending NAIA champions were too much for the Vikings.  B.J. Wright(149) and Bob Perkes(hwt) recorded pins for Ricks while Jake Wright(157) and Pete Mecham(165) took decisions.  MSU Northern recorded four pins, one technical fall, and one major decision.

    December 6, 2001
    Ricks College 29   NW Wyoming 16 (in Rexburg)
     Ricks got pins from Braden Hansen at 197 at Tyler Price at 125.  Jake Wright added a technical fall.  The shock of the night came when B.J. Wright was pinned by Loren Hutchins at 149.

    December 1, 2001
    Ricks College 23   W. Wyoming 19 (in Rexburg)
     Jake Wright recorded a pin at 157 pounds to lead the Vikings.  Jake's older brother B.J. was also a winner, along with Tyler Price, Pete Mecham, Steve Babcock, and Robert Perkes. 

    November 24, 2001
    Ricks College at Lassen Open (no team scores kept)
     Steve Babcock completed a perfect weekend with three wins and a first place finish at 184 pounds.  Babcock was 6-0 over the two day event.  B.J. Wright, Jake Wright, and Dustin Cooper were 3-1 in the open, each finishing second.  Tyler Price and Jake Summers were fourth place finishers.

    November 23, 2001
    Lassen Duals, Reno, NV
     The Vikings faced three opponents on Friday and beat two of them.  Doug Ireland, B.J. Wright and Bob Perkes each recorded falls in a 34-19 win over Lincoln College.  Dustin Hoopes and Braden Hansen won by fall in a 32-9 victory over Clackamas CC.   Ricks closed the day with a 15-32 loss to a very talented Boise State JV team.  Doug Ireland recorded a 6-3 decision.  Steve Babcock won by forfeit.

    November 17, 2001
    Ricks College at Southern Oregon Open (no team scores kept)
     A lot of wrestling with only one Viking placing.  B.J. Wright went undefeated in five matches to take first place at 149 pounds.  He recorded two falls, one technical fall, and a major decision over the weekend.  Brother Jake Wright had two falls and two decisions but did not place.  Going 3-2 were Jake Summers, Doug Ireland, and Pete Mecham

    November 9, 2001
    Ricks College 9    North Idaho 24 (in Rexburg)
     Top ranked and defending NJCAA champion North Idaho controlled the Vikings with seven wins in ten weights.  For Ricks, brothers B.J. and Jake Wright took decisions at 149 and 157.  B.J. remains unbeaten this season.  Braden Hansen of Ogden was the other winner for Ricks with a decision at 197.  The only fall of the night was at 125 with NIC's Nathan DeLeon pinning Tyler Price. 

    November 2-3, 2001
    JC Invitational, Las Vegas, NV
     Viking wrestlers opened the season at the 2001 JC Invitational in Las Vegas Nevada. Ricks finished third among 32 teams. Individually, B.J. Wright took first place at 149 pounds. Brother Jake Wright was second at 157. Steve Babcock place third at 184 pounds while Tyler Price and Kimball Cluff finished fifth at 125 and 174 respectively.
