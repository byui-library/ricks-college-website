The purpose of the STUDY SKILLS LAB is to help students develop their full academic potential as they learn  proven, effective study techniques using videos, computer programs, audio tapes, and readings. 

When a student enrolls in a module (a self-directed learning experience) he/she receives a packet containing the tasks.  When the student completes each task, a lab assistant  asks open-ended questions or asks to see the completed assignment to ascertain that the student understands the information at least at a "B" level.  The  lab assistant signs off the task, and the student proceed to the next task repeating the process until the module is finished.  The modules are designed to take about 12- 15 hours of work to complete and must be completed within a four-week period.

The lab offers seven modules: concentration and memorization, note taking and listening, thinking skills, textbook study, time management, test taking skills and, studying chemistry.  A student can earn .5 credits per module and may register for them until the 12th week of the semester.  On the day a module is completed, the student may register for another one.

Earning an "A" also requires finishing a teaching assignment.  The student chooses one concept from the module, develops a lesson plan on the pre-approved subject, then teaches the concept to another student.

We also offer worksheets and surveys.  Our SURVEY OF READING AND STUDY EFFICIENCY (SRSE)  will help students understand their strengths and weaknesses in 16 study skill areas, giving specific suggestions on enhancing strengths and eliminating weaknesses. I

The STUDY SKILLS WORKSHEET (SSW) highlights major concepts of each module.  The student earns no college credit for finishing the worksheet but can learn several hints on improving study skills.
