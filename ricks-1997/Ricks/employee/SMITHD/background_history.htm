Background

Doug was born in Shelley Idaho to a family of 9 children. He started working on the family dairy farm and milking cows at the age of five.  Doug attended Ricks for two years, served an LDS mission to the East Central States, graduated from BYU with a Bachelors Degree in Recreation Education and a Minor in Dance. 

One highlight for Doug while attending BYU,  was dancing in Europe for three months with the BYU Folk Dancers on tour.   The following year Doug was chosen as one of eight couples to represent the United States at the prestigious British and World Ballroom formation championships held in Black pool England.  This team was the 1st team from the U.S. ever to win both the British and the World Amateur Formation Titles.

 
After graduating from BYU Doug returned to Idaho where he ran the family farm for six years.   In 1978 Doug accepted an invitation to teach ballroom dance at Ricks College.  Prior to his present assignment, Doug directed the ballroom dance program at Ricks for 18 years.. 

Doug is Married to the former Debra Beardall of Rexburg Idaho. They have combined two families for a total of 11children.

" August '2000"  Doug begins his 23rd year of employment with Ricks College.
