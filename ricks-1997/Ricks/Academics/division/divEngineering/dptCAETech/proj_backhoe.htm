Backhoe Design

A cooperative effort between faculty and students of the Department of Computer Aided Engineering Technologies resulted in the design, simulation, and building of the small backhoe as illustrated in the figures below.  The purpose of the project was to show the steps of the design process from idea to the manufacturing of a product.

The project began with a basic sketch of a backhoe.  Measurements were then made and a scale was established.  From this information a 2-dimensional CAD drawing was created.

The 2-dimensional CAD drawings were transferred to a CNC Plasma Cutter which was used to cut images from sheet metal.
Students spent many hours, welding, grinding, and assembling the backhoe, thus gaining valuable hands-on experience.

 

Concurrently, the 2-dimensional CAD drawings were used to develop a 3-dimensional solids model.  The 3-D model provides a more realistic representation of the backhoe and permits the user to view it from any angle.  At this point it is still very easy to identify problems, such as interferences, etc. and make corrections before the design is finalized.

 

The 3-D model was then rendered, complete with materials and lighting effects, to provide a realistic image of the final product.
