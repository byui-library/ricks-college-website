The Tutoring Center, is located in Clark 329 until October 23 at which time they will move to McKay 272. Their phone number is 356-1158.  They offer free individual and group tutoring to all students who need help beyond their regular classroom instruction. Trained student tutors are proficient in a variety of subjects and are prepared to help students strengthen their academic background, understand class material and improve general learning and study skills.  Help is available through appointments or by drop-in. Emphasis is placed on assessing the needs of each student, and then using flexibility to meet those needs.
