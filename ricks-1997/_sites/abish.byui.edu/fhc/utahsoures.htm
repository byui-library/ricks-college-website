Locating civil marriage records in Utah prior to 1887 can be difficult as historically there wasn't a legal
requirement to record them in any government register.  While our listing is not complete it does
represent nearly all of the records thus far identified as containing early marriages.  If anyone can direct
us to any additional sources we are not aware of it would be appreciated.  In an effort to assist researchers
in finding the original records we have created the following bibliography (list).
