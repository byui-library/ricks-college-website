Create a drawing of the hold-down bracket shown in Fig 9-10-B, page 251 of your text, using PaperSpace as discussed in class.  Begin by creating the object as a solids model.  Use SOLVIEW and SOLDRAW commands as required to create viewports, section, and hidden line views.  Include orthographic projections of the TOP and RIGHT side views.  Replace the FRONT orthographic with a FULL section view.   Also include an ISOMETRIC view of the object at 3/4 scale.  Orthographic views should be shown with hidden lines dashed, while no hidden lines should be shown on the section or isometric views.

When creating the model, orient the part such that longest dimension is horizontal in the front or section view.

Note that the dimensions on this part are METRIC.  To facilitate plotting a metric part create as a metric drawing when you start, or create the part full size, then scale the part when bringing it into paperspace with Soldraw.  Remember 1" = 25.4 mm.

Also note the groove detail.  Dimensions for the groove detail may be found in the appendix on page A-24.
