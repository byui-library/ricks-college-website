InsideOut - Men's A Capella
Saturday, August 11, 7:30 p.m.
Kirkham Auditorium

InsideOut, a six-man, all vocal group from Provo, Utah, has been a big hit a Ricks. With their musical capability, vocal percussion and invigorating performance, InsideOut has produced a show that entertains audiences of all ages. Their show consists of original music and cover tunes, many of which can be found on their CDs, "Innocence" and "Experience." Cost is $5 general admission ; $3 for currently enrolled Ricks students.
