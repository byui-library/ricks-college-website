General Studies Courses

Test Taking Skills: GS-103F

Since Examinations determine to a large extent a student's class grade, this module focuses on methods of studying for objective and essay exams.  A four day preparation schedule is applied to exams taken in the student's classes.

Time Management: GS-103E

Effective time management is the emphasis of this module.  The student determines what his long and short term goals are and plans semester and weekly schedules with both fixed and flexible times.  Learning to set priorities is stressed.

Textbook Study Methods: GS-103D

In this module the student analyzes his current textbook study methods and identifies areas where improvement is needed.  The SQ3R (Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review) method of active study is introduced and the student must apply this to his own texts.

Thinking Skills: GS-103C

Analytical reasoning skills are introduced and applied to verbal reasoning problems, analogies, patterns, and trends, and mathematical word problems.  The areas of application are determined by the student's needs.  The student must also demonstrate his problem solving ability against a computer "game"

Note-taking Skills: GS-103B

This module teaches the student to be an effective listener by introducing methods to screen out classroom distractions, to recognize lecture organization, to concentrate, and to paraphrase lecture material.  Outlining and Cornell methods of notetaking are introduced.  The student must then apply these methods to his present classes and critique his notetaking efforts.

Concentration and Memorization: GS-103A

The student identifies, evaluates and improves his plan of study, then practices methods to improve concentration.  Several techniques of memorization are taught which the student then applies to classwork.

Studying Chemistry: GS-103J

Specific strategies for the study of chemistry are taught in this module.  Among the many techniques, learn how to prepare for chemistry tests, take chemistry notes, generate generic chemistry problems and retain the information in a text.
