RICKS THANKS COMMUNITY FOR SUPPORT

Ricks College President David A. Bednar thanked area business owners and community leaders for their support of Ricks students during Monday's business appreciation luncheon. "You not only supply goods and services, you help teach these young people," he said. "It takes the entire community to create the spirit of Ricks."

Donna Benfield, executive director of the Rexburg Chamber of Commerce and city councilwoman, represented the guests, who were mainly members of the chamber. She said, "As I have watched Ricks College over the years, it has been quite apparent to me that as Ricks College grows, so grows Rexburg. Thank you for being here. We can't imagine Rexburg without Ricks College."

In his remarks, Bednar said the college will keep its 8,600 cap for the foreseeable future. "However, this is not an exact science," he said. "If we err, we would rather have a few hundred more as opposed to less." During the past semester, Ricks had an enrollment of 8,840. He added that sometimes stories circulate that Ricks is raising its enrollment. "If anything changes, we will let you know and we will keep you informed."

Bednar said the college is working hard to develop additional enrollment opportunities that will allow the school to offer all worthy and academically qualified applicants an admission option. During the past month, acceptance letters were sent to applicants seeking to enter for fall 2000 semester. There were about 4,000 available spaces for the more than 6,700 students with completed applications.

However, "ninety-seven percent of all worthy and qualified applicants were offered some type of Ricks experience," he said. Besides the traditional fall-winter track, several other options were offered. Some students were offered admission if they agree to attend summer sessions or agree to sit out either the fall or winter semesters. Another option is the Fast Track program that allows students to complete their education at Ricks in little more than a year. "I believe we will arrive at the day when no worthy, academically qualified student will be denied some type of a Ricks College experience," he said.

2000 ATHLETIC HALL OF FAME INDUCTEES

Two former Viking athletes are to be inducted into the Ricks College Athletic Hall of Fame at the annual Ricks Athletics banquet Wednesday, April 12, 2000, in Rexburg. They are former track and field All-American Georgia Johnson Palmer, and former football All-American Greg Hunter.

GEORGIA JOHNSON PALMER - Track and Field. Running for herself, or as part of a relay team, Georgia knows her way around the track. As a freshman in the spring of 1986, she took All-American honors with a fifth-place finish nationally in the 400-meters. Earlier she had won the regional title in the 400-meters in a school record-setting 58.02 seconds. She also won the 200-meters at the regionals and was a member of the winning 1,600-meter relay team. As a sophomore she repeated for All-American honors placing second nationally in the 3,200-meter relay, running the anchor leg, with teammates Bobbi Hawkins, Paula Egan and Karen Booker. As a member of the 1,600-meter relay she placed fifth nationally. In the regionals, Georgia was a member of the championship 3,200 and 1,600-meter relay teams, the latter covering the course in a school record 3:57.33. During her sophomore season she also won a 400-meter race at a meet in Provo, Utah, in 58.97.

GREG HUNTER - Football. Members of the offensive line in football often toil in anonymity. Greg's talent as a guard, however, was impossible to ignore. Ricks placed 11th nationally in 1985 with a 7-3 record during his freshman year. A year later, with Greg as one of the team captains, Ricks was only four votes short of being named the national junior college champion. The 1986 team never lost a game, but had one tie to mar its season record. The highlight of the year came in a thrilling last-second, come-from-behind Jayhawk Bowl win in Kansas. For his efforts Greg was named a First Team All-American at Ricks. He was a member of the Western States Football League's First Team two years, a rare feat, and was given the Clyde P. Packer Ideal Male Athlete Award at this banquet13 years ago. A returned missionary from the Chile Vina Del Mar Mission, Greg was an offensive starter at Brigham Young University for two years, graduating in 1989.

ACADEMIC NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIPS

Ricks College athletes score high in academic achievement. The football has won the academic national championship with a team GPA 3.12. The men's cross country team also placed first nationally with a team GPA of 3.31. The Women's cross country team was a runner up with a team GPA of 3.628.
