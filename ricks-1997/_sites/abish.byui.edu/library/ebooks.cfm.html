How to Find e-Books

4Literature - Several thousand books including religious and historical documents, children's fairy tales, and books by famous 19th-century American authors.

National Academy Press - Full-text e-books in science, engineering, and health.

netLibrary - About 5,800 titles useful for undergraduate research needs , as well as thousands of literature and history classics.

Overland Trails - (BYU) Collection of digitized journals of people traveling overland to the American West.

Project Gutenberg - No latest bestsellers or modern computer books here, but plenty of classics from the literary canon.

The Making of America - About 8,500 books on American social history from the antebellum period through Reconstruction.

The Online Books Page - About 13,000 books, mostly older titles that are in the public domain.
