CAN I BRING MY CAR TO THE QUEST PROGRAM?

Yes. You can bring your private vehicle with you to the Quest program sites at Vashon Island and Badger Creek.  You will be solely responsible for the expense and operation of your vehicle at all times.  Personal vehicles will not be permitted on Quest sponsored field trips or activities due to the logistical concerns.  Keep in mind that at the Vashon Island site there are hidden costs to operating a vehicle.  Ferry passes cost $11.50 per vehicle based on a round trip, gasoline on the island is about $1.85 a gallon, and seldom is there free parking in Seattle.
