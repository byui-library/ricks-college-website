Ricks College Disruptive Behavior Policy

                Pursuant to our Mission Statement, Ricks college is committed to providing a wholesome academic, cultural, social and spiritual environment for students.  Individuals who disrupt this environment by behavioral action, intimidation, threats or violence whether in Ricks College approved housing or directed towards the institution, employee, student, guest, or other person using Ricks College facilities--in any class, activity, or special event--may be required to leave the institution or its properties when their behavior is such that it results in concern for the individual's welfare, the welfare of others or if the behavior hinders others from being able to perform or be productive in respect to their educational goals.  Such acts or behavior are incompatible with general health and safety practices, the mission of Ricks College, the Code of Honor and the principles of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ.

                In cases involving an individual with a disability, including mental disabilities, this policy will operate to make a determination based upon an individual's behavior rather than upon the individual's status of having a disability.

                When it is determined that an individual is involved in disruptive behavior, that person will receive an appropriate sanction, including, but not limited to, dismissal from enrollment or employment (in accordance with College policy) and/or being banned from the campus of Ricks College.
