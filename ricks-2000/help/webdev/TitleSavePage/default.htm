This will assist you in adding a title and saving your page.

Title Your Page
  1. Right-click on the page you wish to title, choose Page Properties.
     
  2. Be sure the General tab is being viewed.  Highlight the text in the Title: box and type in a appropriate name for your page.
  3. Click OK.
Save Your Page
  1. To save your page the first time, click on the File menu and click Save As.
  2. Be sure that the Save in: box is directed to your share on Joseph not My Webs.  Type an appropriate name for your page with NO spaces in the File name: box.  If this is your home page, your page should be saved as default.
  3. To save your page in the future, click the File menu and Save not Save As.
