Simple HTML Script

<HTML>

<HEAD><TITLE> Title that appears in the Browser Title Bar</TITLE></HEAD>

<H1> Title that appears on your document</H1>

<BR>

<BODY>

<P> This is the first line of text on your page </P>

<P> This is the second line of text on your page </P>

<BR>

<IMG SRC="an_image.jpg">

<P>

<A HREF="mailto:youremailaddress@here">E-MAIL</A>

<P>

<CAPTION> MY TABLE </CAPTION>

<TABLE CELL="1" BORDER="1" WIDTH="300">

<TR><TD COLOR=" FFFFFF">

<FONT COLOR="CC33FF">My Text</FONT></TD>

<TD><FONT COLOR="FF0000">My Second line of text</FONT></TD></TR>

</TABLE>

</BODY>

</HTML>

This or any HTML script can be typed into a text editor such as Wordpad or Word or WordPerfect and saved as a text document. Be sure to include the suffix .htm or .html. Then the document can be opened into a browser to be viewed or loaded to a server to be distributed on the web.
