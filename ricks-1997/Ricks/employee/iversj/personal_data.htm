John J. Ivers, a native of Philadelphia, lived most of his life in the East.  Getting his education did not come easily as he worked full time during eight of the ten years he was in college.  He holds BA and MA degrees in Spanish from Brigham Young University and an Ed.D degree in Curriculum and Teaching from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro.  His undergraduate degree also includes a minor in Anthropology.  During his career, he has taught a variety of subjects.  These include Spanish in the Elementary School, Middle School Spanish, College Spanish (grammar, literature, and phonetics), Introduction to Teacher Education, Educational Foundations, Philosophy of Education, College Survival, Personal Achievement and Seminar in Behavior and Society.  John has been involved in several addtional activities at Ricks College.  For example, from 1990 to 1995 he directed a special course to help Ricks instructors improve their teaching skills.  Over a third of the roughly 340 full time professors at the college went through the program.  Since 1993 he has been the co-director of a program where college students instruct middle school students in several foreign languages.  Since 1999 he has served as the director of the Ricks College Summer Honors Institute.

John came to Ricks in 1989.  In 1996 he received a faculty award for his teaching.  He resides in Rexburg with his wife and three children.
