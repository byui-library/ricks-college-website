BOOKS YOU SHOULD READ

 

This list of 100 titles does not presume to be the “list of all lists.” Rather, it is to be seen as a starting place as you enter a lifetime of reading.  Everyone has to start someplace.  Try these, and remember, “reading is to the mind what exercise is to the body.”
