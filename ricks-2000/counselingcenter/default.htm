Services

The BYU-Idaho Counseling Center, located on the second floor of the Administration Building, provides individual, group, and marital counseling at no charge to the students of the college. Students may receive help for a wide range of emotional problems ranging from adjustment issues, career issues or problems with self-esteem to severe feelings of depression. Counseling is intended primarily for those students who can be helped in a relatively short period of time. It is not intended for those who need long-term therapy. Psychological testing is available when considered appropriate but may result in a financial charge to the student.

In some cases, if evaluation for the possible need of medications is deemed necessary, the physicians at the Student Health Center or a contracted psychiatrist may be utilized.

Staff

The Counseling Center staff is comprised of nine well-trained , experienced, professional therapists with either a doctorate or masters degree in psychology, social work, or counseling. Students are assured of reliable, professional assistance in an atmosphere of warmth and understanding where confidentiality is maintained.
