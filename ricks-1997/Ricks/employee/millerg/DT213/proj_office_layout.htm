DT 213
Office Layout

"Being of good cheer may not halt or deflect the downpour of discouragement which is drenching others, but it is the unfolding of an umbrella and walking together under it.", Neal A. Maxwell.

Create an office layout as described below:

  • Create a simple room consisting of a floor and two walls. Place carpet on the floor and an appropriate paint, paneling, or wallpaper on the walls. Place a chair guard in the middle of each wall.

  • A library of parts created as a result of the previous project is available for your use on Jupiter in the Classes\Dt213\Office Equipment folder. You may use the parts as needed, making changes as desired to the geometry and/or the materials.

  • The furnishings in the office must include all of the following:

    • desk
    file cabinet
    telephone
    picture
    clock
    bookcase (with books)
    computer (with monitor, keyboard, mouse, mousepad)

  • Include several other items from the library (appropriately positioned) to give the office that lived-in, realistic look. You may create additional objects, or download from the internet. Be sure to give proper credit for any objects not created by you in the accompanying document (described below).

  • Show evidence of each of the following material and mapping techniques:

plastic material
glass material
metal material
diffuse map
bump map
standard reflection map
flat mirror reflection map
automatic reflection map
refraction map

  • Include a document with your scene that describes where/how each of the above requirements have been incorporated.

Pay special attention to the selection of materials and lighting to make the entire scene look as realistic as possible. The higher the realism, the higher your grade!

Include a minimum of 2 jpg files of the completed scene at a resolution of 640x480. If you use any bitmaps that are not already in the library, be sure to include a copy of those files.

Place the model, renderings, and document inside a folder in the DropBox when complete using the keyword of Layout before your name.
