Ricks Update - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

May 12, 1998

We have a record 2,170 students attending our first five-week-long term of summer school. This is a 10 percent increase over last year at the same time. We have been working on ways to boost summer enrollment and are excited about the upward trend. We expect increases in summer enrollment in the coming few years as we initiate our Fast Track program. The new program is designed for students desiring to earn an associate degree in quicker than the traditional two years. Students who take college classes during the summer terms in combination with some college-level classes during their high school years will be able to graduate sooner. The program is ultimately designed to allow more students to attend Ricks.

The Viking women have won their 14th consecutive regional track and field title and the men their ninth consecutive. Jason Williams of Meridian, Idaho, and Natalie Gutierrez of Rexburg won Outstanding Athlete awards and Coach Ferron Sonderegger was selected Coach of the Year. They now head to nationals May 14-16 in Edwardsville, Ill.

Coached by Larry Stocking, the women's softball team won its third consecutive Region 18 title. That's quite impressive considering the Ricks program is only three years old! They now head to nationals in Hutchinson, Kansas, May 21-23. The team record is 39-7-1. Freshman pitcher Maryon DeWitt from Show Low, Ariz., won tournament MVP honors.

Thanks to the Internet, students around the world can now bypass the telephones to register for classes at Ricks. While students may still register by calling (208) 356-1006, Registrar Kelly Hymas says the Internet option will likely become more popular. The path to get to the Student Web Access page begins at the Ricks home page at www.ricks.edu. From there go to "College Services" and then to "Registration."

Summer devotionals are held each Tuesday at 9:10 a.m. in the Taylor Chapel. They will be delay broadcast this summer at 2 and 8:30 p.m. on KRIC, 100.5 FM. Speakers will be Sander Larson, Associate Director of Public Relations and soon-to-be president of the Washington Seattle Mission, May 12; Linda Lloyd, Chair of Department of Elementary Education, May 19; Sharon Hanson, Elementary Education Department faculty, May 26; Scott Ferguson, Religion Department faculty, June 9; Van Christman, Biology Department faculty, June 16; Judy Steiner, Learning Assistance staff, June 23; James Smyth, Administrative Vice President, June 30; Bruce Blanchard, new director of Counseling Center, July 7; Gary Purse, Religion Department faculty, July 14; Robert Marrott, Religion Department faculty, August 4.

We made an error in last week's information about Campus Education Week that will be June 4- 6. The activities are limited to people ages 14 and older. For more information, you can check the Ricks home page at www.ricks.edu under Continuing Education or call (208) 356-1040 for a booklet.
