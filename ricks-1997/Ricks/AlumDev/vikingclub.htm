Viking Club membership provides many exclusive benefits, securely positioning you in the middle of the excitement of Ricks College intercollegiate athletic competition. Your annual membership donation helps bring quality athletes to campus, provides scholarships, and helps the athletic department continue the outstanding Viking winning tradition.

Membership options include:

Full Viking $1,000
Golden Viking $500-$999
Viking Couple $200-$499
Century Viking $100-$199

Membership benefits:

  • Program Recognition;
  • Membership Pass;
  • "Viking Vision" Newsletter and e-mails;
  • Invitations to luncheons, banquets and pre & post game functions;
  • Tax Deduction for non-benefit portion of membership;*
  • Membership Plaque;
  • Reserved Parking;
  • Special Travel Opportunities;
  • Seating Priority for football, basketball, and bowl game;
  • Also, each $100 donation entitles the donor to purchase 1 reserved seat in Viking Club sections for football and basketball.

Thor membership $50-99  (This membership includes all the above except: Membership Plaque, Reserved Parking, Special Travel Opportunities, and Seating Priority.)

*Gifts to the Viking Club are generally deductible by the donor, though recent IRS rulings have indicated that the amount deductible is affected by the benefits members receive in return for their contribution.  We recommend you consult your tax advisor concerning the deductibility of your gift.
