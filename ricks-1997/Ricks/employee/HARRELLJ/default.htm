Academic Background

My Conversion

Teaching English at Ricks College gives me the opportunity to study, discuss, and write about the art of words.

As a literature teacher, I am able to work at and grow in my appreciation of well-crafted writing. Fiction, poetry, drama, non-fiction; contemporary and ancient works; studies of religion, art, nature, and humanity; the popular and the recondite--all have a place on my reading list.

As a writing teacher, I can pursue the art of writing with clarity, skill, and imagination. Everything I learn can be my tool in the writing classroom.

The study of writing and literature at Ricks College allows me to blend secular truths and gospel truths in order to forge a better vision of both. My faith asks me to reach, to hope, to be renewed--to repent of yesterday's error and ignorance and embrace tomorrow's richer understanding. As I live to be ruled by God, the light before me increases. 

I am a teacher at Ricks College, but I am also a student--of literature, writing, and life in God's universe.
