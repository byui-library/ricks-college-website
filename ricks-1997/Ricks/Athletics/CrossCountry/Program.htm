2001 Review
    Viking Cross Country

Repetition.   Ricks College Cross Country believes in it. Make it seven straight NJCAA Cross Country Championships for the women.  Make it three in a row for the men. For Coach Doug Stutz, it was a repeat performance as NJCAA Coach of the Year. 

The men were led all year by Ryan Jenks and David Woodbury.  Defending national champion Jeff Davidson battled back from a knee injury to score big at the Region 18 and NJCAA finals.  Those three earned All America honors for 2001.  Brett Larsen turned in a key performance at nationals as Ricks edged Central Arizona by four points for the NJCAA crown.  

With five of seven runners back from last years national championship team, Ricks was the team to beat all year.  They didn't disappoint.  Angie Benson came from almost out of no-where to win the Region 18 title and the NJCAA championship.  Running in her draft were Second Team All Americans Lida Clapier, Kim Conover, Audrey Golightly, and Kathrine Anderson, all running within 35 seconds of each other in the national finals.  Amy Conover earned Third Team honors.

The team once again won the award for best combine score, and Doug Stutz repeated as Coach of the Year.
