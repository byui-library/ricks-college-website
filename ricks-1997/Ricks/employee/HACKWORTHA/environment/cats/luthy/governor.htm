Whereas, the Lyman Creek Grazing Association has successfully designed and implemented a Cooperative Grazing Land Management Plan on 5,455 acres of Public School Endowment land with the cooperation of the Idaho Department of Lands, Idaho Department of Fish and Game, and the National Resource Conservation Service; and,

Whereas, the Association has expended considerable time and money of their own to successfully accomplish the objectives of this cooperative land management plan, as a result of their personal desire to achieve, maintain, and ensure good range and riparian conditions; and,

Whereas, the Association now serves as an outstanding example for other lessees of Idaho Endowment land and all of Idaho’s ranching industry of what can be accomplished once a challenge is accepted and beneficial action is undertaken;

Now, Therefore, BE IT RESOLVED, that the LYMAN CREEK GRAZING ASSOCIATION and the following MEMBERS be duly recognized for their exemplary commitment, persistence and demonstration of outstanding land stewardship.
