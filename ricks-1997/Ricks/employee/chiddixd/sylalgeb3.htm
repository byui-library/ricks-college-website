To calculate your grade:

  1. Take the number of points you have earned
  2. Divide this number by the total number of points possible
  3. Multiply by 100
  4. Locate this number(your percentage) on the grading scale to determine your current grade. Keep proper track of your points and you should know your grade for each class period.

Study Tips
To study a section in the textbook  means to read it, then ponder what you have read, highlight key ideas, make notes to yourself in the margin of the book, and dutifully work through the example problems in the section on a sheet of paper. Be sure you indicated in the book with a big ? those things in the section you didn't understand. Be sure to follow-up, quickly, so that you eliminate all ? (misunderstood items) .  On the Weekly Schedule Web Page you will often see the phrase "Practice Problems".   These problems should be done in a notebook as if you were going to hand them in.   However, practice problems are not to be turned in.  Any assignments to be turned in will be clearly indicated on the Weekly Web Page.  Any multiple page assignments must be stapled.  Failure to staple will result in some point reduction.   I recommend purchasing a small stapler to carry in your backpack.  Some assignments will need to be typed.  Work that is to be turned in should be placed on the desk in front of the room before lecture begins.  There will not be a call to turn in papers.  Work turned in after the lecture begins is considered late and some point reduction will result.  Exams not taken on time will result in significant point reduction.

.Suggested out-of-class study time for this class is 2 hours per day(Monday through Saturday)

I suggest the first hour be devoted to reading, pondering, working through examples, etc. in  the assigned section in the textbook  I suggest that notetaking and comments be written in your textbook. The second hour of the of the study period should be used to work on assigned practice problems. All practice problems assigned in a textbook section should be done in a notebook neatly and completely.  Practice problems are not to be turned in. However, it is important that you do all of them.  On those problems you don't understand, you must  clarify by means of study groups, tutors, or the professor.

This time schedule, while strongly suggested, may need to be altered depending on the individual.situation.
