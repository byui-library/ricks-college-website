KEYBOARD 

Stephen R. Allen, Piano (1986); B.M., Eastman School of Music, 1977; M.M., San Jose State University, 1980; D.M., University of Indiana, 1993. Director of Piano Studies at Brigham Young University-Idaho. Dr. Allen teaches studio piano, piano ensemble, and pedagogy courses.  His solo performances include appearances throughout the United States and Japan.  He is an active chamber musician and member of the Trio Terra Nova, which as performed for the International Double Reed Society.

Wilson Brown, Jazz Piano (1977); B.A., University of Utah, 1958; graduate study, Brigham Young University-Provo. Director of Jazz Studies, the jazz ensemble, jazz combos, and popular music program. He brings the background of a professional career in the music business, including television and recording work. For 11 years he was musical conductor, arranger, and keyboardist for the popular singing group "The Lettermen." Before coming to BYU-I he did free-lance music work in Los Angeles and toured extensively.

Brent M. Jones, Piano (1972); . B.A., M.A. Brigham Young University-Provo, 1970, 1972; D. A., University of Northern Colorado, 1988. Teaches piano, music theory, composition, and keyboard harmony. Dr. Jones has performed or served as  clinician and lecturer in the western states and Mexico. His students have won awards in contests on various levels.

Darwin K. Wolford, Organ (1967); B.S., Utah State University, 1960; M.M., Ph.D. University of Utah, 1963, 1967. Director of Organ Studies and a specialist in composition and church music. A former student of Alexander Schreiner, he has performed organ recitals and given workshops for church organists throughout the western United States. He has many published works by the nation's leading music publishers.

BRASS

Kendell Nielsen, French Horn (1987); B.S., Southern Utah State College, 1976; M.M., Brigham Young University-Provo, 1988. Director of Bands. Coordinator of Brass Studies. Director of Symphonic Band.
