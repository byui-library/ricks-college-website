Waiver of General Education Requirements:
If you have questions about graduation requirements and religion courses, please read the following policy concerning attendance and completion of religion courses at Brigham Young University-Idaho:

RELIGION GRADUATION REQUIREMENT

1. Two hours of religion credit per semester up to 14 credits are required of students of BYU-Idaho for a Bachelors Degree. Two hours of religion credit per semester up to eight credits are required of students for an Associate Degree. There is no differentiation between students who take classes while on campus or on-line courses.

2. All graduates must have completed four hours of credit in Book of Mormon (Rel. 121-122). If these courses transfer from another Church University or Institute as a two credit class, requirement is waived.

TRANSFER OF CREDIT

Institute credit is NOT transferable as religion credit. It can transfer as elective credit when the following conditions are met:

1. Course number is 100 or above

2. Course is equivalent to a religion course taught at BYU-Idaho.

3. Course is taken for two credits.

4. Required paperwork is sent to BYU-Idaho registrar from the LDS Institute of Religion or Church School.

5. Letter grade is given.

In rare cases of hardship Institute credit that meets the above criteria MAY be considered for application to religion credit by use of the Waiver Policy listed below.

MULTIPLE RELIGION CLASSES

No more than two hours of religion credit may be taken per term or semester. The only exception is Book of Mormon for Prospective Missionaries.

SECOND RELIGION CLASS

It is the desire of the Church Board of Education that students be in a religion class each term. However on rare occasion a student may need to take two classes in a given term. Such cases must be cleared through the chairman of the Department of Religious Education.

ATTENDANCE

Each instructor may establish their own criteria for giving credit, but generally lack of attendance would affect a student’s grade in a significant way and could result in no credit for the class, thus requiring the student to retake the class.

WAIVERS

As a rule there is a no waiver policy except when a student has been denied a graduation diploma from BYU-Idaho because the religion requirement was not completed, and reasons for non-completion were beyond the control of the student.

For clarification and/or application for a waiver see the Dean of the Division of Religious and Family Living, 202C Taylor Building, Rexburg, Idaho 83460-1505.
