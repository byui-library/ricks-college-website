Arts General Education Requirement: Class and Lab

To fulfill the Arts GE requirement a student must take any three credit class from the approved GE Arts classes listed in the college catalog, and complete the zero credit FA 100 Lab. Both must be completed for graduation. It is recommended that when possible, a student enroll for both requirements in the same semester.

Time Limit: One semester

Those not completing the FA 100 lab requirement within a one semester period will have to enroll and start over again in a subsequent semester.

Number of Required Events: Seven

The following seven events are required: (1) An Art Exhibit (2) A Play (3) A Choir Concert (4) A Dance (5) A Recital (6) An Opera or Oratorio (7) A Symphonic Band or Orchestra Concert.

Testing: None

Completion of the lab requirement is based only on attendance of the seven events noted above.

How to Proceed:

1. Obtain an approved list of events for the semester.

The events list is available at the beginning of each semester at the following locations: Campus Bookstore, Manwaring Center information desk, Spori Building - Room 270, Snow Building - Room 120, and the above link in red. The list indicates the event, time & date, category it fills, ticket requirement, and dress standard for each event.


2. Select the events you plan to attend.

Make a plan in advance to the entire semester. Some events are less frequent than others.


3. Get tickets where needed.

Tickets are available at the Manwaring Center ticket office approximately two weeks in advance of a performance. Call the ticket office at extension 2230 for prices and schedules.


4. Fill out the scantron sheet.

Each scantron sheet in this packet has the name of a specific requirement printed on it. With a #2 pencil , carefully fill out the appropriate scantron sheet for the event you plan to attend. Fill in all areas at the bottom and top of each sheet. Scantron sheets filled out with pen will be discarded.

5. Attend the event.

ARRIVE EARLY - TICKETS ARE RELEASED 10 MINUTES BEFORE THE PERFORMANCE IS SCHEDULED TO BEGIN. Take the completed scantron sheet with you.


6. Turn in the appropriate scantron sheet.

For performing arts events, give the sheet to the usher at the conclusion of the performance. For art exhibits, fill out the sheet as indicated in the special instructions on the form and turn it in to the Division of Performing and Fine Arts secretary in Spori 270.


7. Check your progress.

Periodically throughout the semester, check your progress on the student directory computers near the Registrar's Office, and on the FA100 web site. When your computer record indicates that you have attended all seven events, the lab requirement is complete.



Suggestions and Questions:

Get started early. Plan your events. There are enough events in each semester to fulfill the seven requirements. These are not, however, evenly distributed throughout the semester. You will want to plan time to study for mid-terms and finals without the pressure of having to attend an FA 100 event to complete your seven. Start attending events early in the semester so there is not a problem at the end of the semester.


Select events from the approved list. Not all performances on campus fulfill the FA 100 requirement. Consult the events list to determine if and for which category a campus event counts. Thursday 2 p.m. Recitals ARE NOT FA 100 EVENTS.



Protect your scantron sheets. Scantron sheets that are mulitated, unidentified, filled out with pen, or incorrectly in any other way, will not be recorded until the responsible student comes to Spori 270 and fills out a corrected form. Turn in your own sheet. Do not turn in sheets for roommates or friends. Do not ask anyone else to turn your sheets in to the usher. It is your responsibility.


Observe the required dress standard for each event. The events sheet indicates appropriate dress for each event. Concert dress means Sunday Dress. Those inappropriately dressed will not be admitted to performances.


What if an event is sold out? Seats are usually available for those without tickets who go to the event early and line up for cancellation tickets.


Can I get credit if I am in a performance? Students in performances can get credit if they turn in their scantron sheet to the usher immediately following the performance.


What if I forget to take my scantron sheet to the event? The usher at the door will validate your ticket or program after the event. On the school day following the event, take your completed scantron sheet and the validated ticket or program to Spori 270.


What if I lose a scantron sheet? Rather than buying a complete packet at the bookstore, you can purchase a single scantron sheets from the Division Office in Spori 270.
