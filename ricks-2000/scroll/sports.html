Vikings lose final two home games

The Vikings submitted to a College of Southern Idaho blowout, losing 79-49 Thursday night and suffered a loss to the Treasure Valley Chukars 65-60 Saturday night during the last ever Ricks College regular season basketball game.

Vikings top in SWAC

After winning both games this weekend, beating College of Southern Idaho 87-75 and Treasure Valley Community College 100-72, the Vikings had done their part to take home a piece of the Scenic West Athletic crown. The only other thing the Vikings needed was for Dixie State College and Salt Lake Community College to each lose one game and Ricks could take home an undisputed crown.

Ricks got half of the help they needed Friday when Dixie beat Salt Lake in overtime. On Saturday, a Dixie loss would have given the Vikings hosting duties for the Region 18 Tournament, but during the TVCC game, an announcement was made that Utah Valley was beating Dixie 30-26. It was not meant to be, however, as Dixie ended up coming from behind to beat UVSC 63-51.

With the win, Ricks and Dixie split the SWAC crown, and because of the tiebreaker situation, the Rebels won the right to host the Region 18 Tournament...[more] March 5
