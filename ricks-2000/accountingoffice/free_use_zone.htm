"Free Use Zone" Billing Explanation

Return to Cell Phone Home

 

We have received many inquiries from employees who have cellular telephone service under the "Free Use Zone" plan. Many of you are questioning why you have been billed for calls that show up on your bill as having been made or received in Rexburg. Hopefully the following information will answer most of your questions:

(1)     The "From City" location that is listed on your bill is based on which Edge Wireless antenna the call is initially connected to.

(2)    There are several Edge Wireless antennas within what the Edge Wireless billing system defines as the "Rexburg Calling Area".

(3)    The "Rexburg Calling Area" is not the same thing as the "BYU-Idaho Free Use Zone". The Free Use Zone is a subset of antennas within the    Rexburg Calling Area.

(4)    The Free Use Zone consists of the following antennas:

Rexburg bench

North Rexburg

Menan Butte (north facing panel only)

(5)    Calls made within the Free Use Zone are NOT listed in detail on your bill. Rather, the total minutes for all calls made or received in the Free Use Zone are shown on a single line near the bottom of the detail listing.

(6)    Other antennas that are within the Rexburg Calling Area but NOT included within the BYU-Idaho Free Use Zone are:

Sugar City

Menan Butte - (east facing panel)

        Calls that originate off of either of these two panels will show as Rexburg on your billing detail and will be charged air time.

(7)    PLEASE NOTE that the only way to be assured of a free call under the Free Use Zone plan is to check the display on your phone prior to making or answering a call. If the display says "RICKS" the call will not be charged air time. If the display says "EDGE WIRELESS" the call will be charged air time.

(8)   PLEASE  NOTE that coverage from individual antennas varies from day to day depending on atmospheric conditions and time of year. This means that a particular spot on the fringe of an antenna’s coverage area today may not be in that antenna’s coverage area tomorrow. For this reason it is vital that you check your phone before connecting a call regardless of where you are when you make the call.

(9)   We have reviewed several of the Rexburg calls for which air time was charged to Free Use Zone users. In each case the billing has been correct because the call was originally connected to one of the Rexburg antennas that was not part of the Free Use Zone.  Furthermore, due to the volume of calls processed each month on the BYU-Idaho account with Edge Wireless, it is not feasible for the Accounting Office to research the originating tower of individual calls.  We can therefore not make adjustments to your account for Rexburg calls listed on bills for phones on the Free Use Zone plan.  If you feel uncomfortable with the charges you incur under the Free Use Zone billing plan, you may change back to the standard rate plan.  Changes in the billing plan can be requested at any time but will not take effect until the start of a new billing cycle (the 26th of each month).  Changes can be made only at the Accounting Office (KIM 130).
