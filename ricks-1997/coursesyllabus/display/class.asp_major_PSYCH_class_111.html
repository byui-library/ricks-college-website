GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Social Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A survey of the various fields of psychology and application of selected psychological principles to life situations.
DESCRIPTION: This is an INTRODUCTORY course in Psychology. A survey of the various fields of psychology and application of selected psychological principles to life situations.
TOPICS: Topics will include the biological basis of behavior, sensation and perception, learning, emotion, memory, social thinking, IQ, personality, therapy, stress, motivation, developmental, and abnormal psychology. Each of these topics is in and of itself an entire semester long course. We will only be scratching the surface of each, and changing directions frequently-hence the emphasis on the word "Introductory".
