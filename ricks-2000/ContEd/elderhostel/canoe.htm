BRIGHAM YOUNG UNIVERSITY - IDAHO CANOEING ELDERHOSTEL

AUGUST 18-24, 2002

 Sunday   and Friday evenings will be accommodated in apartment-style housing adjacent to campus or on campus. Each apartment (three double occupancy bedrooms -- twin beds -- shared common living room) houses maximum of one couple (private bath) or three singles (unlimited, no charge) who share one bathroom.  Part-time camping, Monday thru Friday, is spent in Yellowstone's pristine forests and emerald lakes.  Amazing geysers, hot springs, abundant wildlife and vegetation tantalize the eye.  Bring sleeping bag, ground pad, rentals available, tent provided; Maximum of two per tent, pit toilets.  Light chores expected; No coffee, tea on campus.  No alcohol allowed:  smoking prohibited.  For OUTDOOR, ACTIVE HOSTELERS; canoe five to seven miles per day at 8000 foot elevation.

CANOEING ON LEWIS AND SHOSHONE LAKES: YELLOWSTONE'S HISTORY AND ENVIROMENT
Experience five days canoeing the shores of Lewis and Shoshone lakes in Yellowstone National Park while studying the early history of the park. Instruction will include learning how Yellowstone received its name, Indian occupancy, traders, trappers and mountain men. Observe the destruction caused by the 1988 forest fires and how nature is working in the regeneration processes. A day trip will be taken to a remote geyser basin rarely seen by the average tourist. In this restricted area of Yellowstone Park, learn by experience the importance of minimal impact camping. Food and camping gear will be transported in canoes.
