Ricks College Campus Traffic and Parking Rules and Regulations

Ricks College has established and maintains traffic ways and parking facilities for use by faculty, staff, students and community patrons. All college buildings and grounds are governed by rules and regulations established for the purposes of maintaining safety and order pursuant to Idaho Code 49-222 and Rexburg City Ordinance # 759. Those regulations listed hereafter govern the grounds, traffic ways and parking lots of Ricks College.
 

1.0 Parking permits
 

1.1 Parking permits are required for all vehicles parking in college parking lots. These permits may be obtained from the Parking Services Office located in the Kimball building room 150.
 

1.2 Responsibility for compliance with all parking rules and regulations is assumed by the primary responsible party for a vehicle upon receipt of a Ricks College parking permit. It is the responsibility of the responsible party to obtain and read all such regulations. The individual receiving a permit is responsible for all violations incurred by a vehicle for which the permit is assigned.
 

1.3 Types of permits, fees and periods of validity for permits have been established by the Ricks College administration as described hereafter.
 

A. Administration, Faculty and Staff are entitled to two permits. The first permit is free and the second permit, if needed, is $1.00. No more than two permits will be issued per family. Faculty/Staff permits are valid in reserved and general parking lots. Permits are initially valid for three years and expire on the date printed on the permit.
 

B. General/Students may obtain one permit for vehicles parked on campus. The permit is $5.00. General/Student permits are valid for General/Student parking lots only. The permits are valid for one academic year and expire on the date printed on the permit.
 

C. Community patrons may obtain genealogy, visitor or vendor permits according to need. There is no charge for visitor, vendor and genealogy permits. Community patron permits are valid for the period of time designated by the Parking Services Office. Visitors not directly associated with Ricks College may park in General/Student or Faculty/Staff lots without a parking permit. Citations issued for parking without a permit will be voided. All other parking regulations apply to visitors and vehicles may be impounded for recurring violations.
 

1.4 display of permits should be accomplished according to the following instructions.
 

A. Hanging permits should be hung on the interior rear view mirror post with the lettering facing to the outside of the vehicle. If no rear view mirror is present the permit may be placed on the vehicle dashboard facing up.
 

B. Temporary permits should be attached according to the instructions described for hanging permits (1.4A).
 

D. Motorcycle permits should be placed on the windshield or front forks clearly visible from the front on the vehicle.
 

E. State issued handicap permits should be placed so as to be clearly visible to external inspection.
 

1.5 The Parking Services Office should immediately be notified of lost or stolen permits. A replacement permit may be obtained for $2.00. (Possession of a lost or stolen permit constitutes an offense for which a violator may be criminally prosecuted.)
 

1.6 Parking privileges may be suspended or revoked for misrepresentation, misuse or multiple violations at the discretion of the Parking Services Office.
 

1.7 No refunds of permit fees can be granted and no provision for prorating of fees exists.
 

2.0 Parking regulations
 

2.1 A valid parking permit is required for all full and part-time administrative, faculty and staff personnel and for all full and part-time students to park a motor vehicle in Ricks College parking facilities. Community patrons or other visitors may obtain a parking permit if desired.
 

2.2 A vehicle shall be parked only in the lot/s for which the issued permit is valid.
 

2.3 Reserved spaces are identified below. No unauthorized vehicles are permitted to park in reserved spaces.
 

A. Service spaces are identified by a painted white curb and/or a service sign. Service spaces are reserved for college owned vehicles and/or vendor vehicles.
 

B. Handicap spaces are identified by a painted blue curb and a handicap sign. Only vehicles operated by/or for handicapped individuals and displaying a handicap permit are permitted to park in such spaces.
 

C. Visitor spaces are identified by a painted white curb and a visitor sign. Such spaces are reserved for community patrons. Administrators, faculty, staff and students are not permitted to park in these spaces.
 

D. 30 Minute parking meter spaces are available for use by VISITORS ONLY for a period of 30 minutes. Parking for longer than 30 minutes constitutes a violation.
 

E. No parking zones are identified by painted yellow curbs. No vehicular parking is permitted in such areas.
 

F. Motorcycles may be parked at the ends of parking rows within painted triangular or rectangular areas in either General/Student or Faculty/Staff lots. These areas are reserved for motorcycle parking. Motorcycles may also be parked in regular parking spaces.
 

G. Loading zones are located at designated areas on campus and are intended for loading and unloading purposes. Any vehicle may park up to 20 minutes in a loading zone for the designates purpose. The flashers of any vehicle parking for the purpose should be activated.
 

2.4 Vehicles shall be parked entirely within painted lines when such lines are visible and as nearly in proper position as possible when such lines are not visible.
 

2.5 Vehicles shall not be parked so as to block or otherwise restrict driveways and/or traffic ways.
 

2.6 Automobiles shall not park in motorcycle spaces. (Motorcycles may park in regular parking spaces when no other space is available.
 

2.7 Overnight parking is prohibited between November 1 and March 1 of each calendar year except in the Pioneer, Biddulph and Life Science Addition parking lots.
 

2.8 Recreational vehicles may be parked on a temporary basis with approval of and in a location designated by the Parking Services Office.
 

2.9 Parking or driving on sidewalks or landscaped areas is prohibited except for college owned vehicles operated in the performance of necessary duties. The Campus Police Division shall be notified when driving on the sidewalk is deemed necessary.
 

2.10 Parking within 15 feet of a fire hydrant is prohibited.
 

2.11 Vehicles shall not back into diagonal parking spaces.
 

2.12 Abandoned vehicles are prohibited on campus property and will be removed at owner's expense.
 

2.13 No major repairs shall be made on vehicles parked in college parking lots.
 

2.14 Vehicles larger than a full-sized pick up truck shall not be parked on campus property on a continuing basis.
 

2.15 Exceptions to parking regulations may be granted by the Parking Services Office if deemed necessary.
 

2.16 Additions to the parking regulations may be made at the discretion of the Campus Planning Committee.
 

2.17 Enforcement of parking rules and regulations will be accomplished by the Parking Services personnel and by officers of the Campus Police Division.
 

2.18 Hours of enforcement for parking permit regulations are Monday through Friday from 7:00 A.M. until 5:00 P.M. Handicap spaces, yellow curb/no parking zones and other regulations are enforced 24 hours every day.

3.5 The Taylor Building lot is located immediately west of the John Taylor Religion building. This lot is for General/Student parking.
 

3.6 The Benson   is located North of the Benson Building. This lot is open to Staff and Faculty parking.

3.8 The Hart south parking lot is located at the southwest corner of the Hart physical education building. The lot is reserved for Faculty, Staff and Administrators.
 

3.9 The South Stadium parking lot is located immediately south of the football stadium on the west side of the campus. The lot is open to General/Student parking.

3.11 The Snow building parking lot is located south of the Snow building and east of the football field. The portion of the lot south of the Snow building is reserved for Faculty, Staff and Administrators. The portion of the lot west of the Snow building is open to General/Student parking.
 

4.0 Parking Citations and Fines
 

4.1 Vehicles parked in violation of the campus parking rules and regulations will receive a parking citation.
 

4.2 Parking citations shall be placed on the front windshield or on the driver's side of a vehicle.
 

4.3  The permit holder will remain responsible for payment of the fine.  In the event that a citation is removed from a vehicle without authorization prior to an operator receiving notice or if an operator simply fails to notice a citation which is subsequently lost the fact that a violation occurred is not altered and the permit holder will remain responsible for payment of the fine. (Written notification of fines is distributed by the college accounting office on a monthly basis.)

5.0 Appeal Procedure
 

5.1 Students, visitors, faculty, staff and administrators desiring to appeal a parking citation should contact the Parking Services Office and complete a written appeal form. The appeal will be sent to the Appeal Committee for review and disposition. Appellants will be notified in writing as to the decision of the committee.
 

5.2 Decisions of the Appeal Committee are final.
 

6.0 Towing of vehicles.
 

6.1 Vehicles may be towed at the owner's expense for any of the following reasons.
 

A. Vehicles with 3 or more unresolved citations on file with the parking Services Office.
 

B. Vehicles unlawfully parked in a handicap space.
 

C. Vehicles parked blocking a traffic way or driveway or which constitute an immediate hazard.
 

D. Vehicles which have been abandoned on campus property more than 10 days.
 

7.0 Parking committee
 

7.1 The Campus Planning Committee has been appointed by the Ricks College Administration and charged with the responsibility of establishing and maintaining parking rules and regulations which govern college property.
 

7.2 The committee is chaired by the administrative vice-president and the appointed members are representative of the faculty, staff and administration of Ricks College.
 

7.3 Any parking suggestions may be submitted to the planning committee through the Parking Services Office, a member of the committee or the administrative vice-president.
 

8.0 Waiver of liability.
 

8.1 Any person desiring to use the traffic ways, grounds and/or parking facilities of Ricks College does so at his or her own risk. Ricks college assumes no liability for vehicles parked or driven on college property.
 

9.0 Definitions
 

9.1 Abandoned vehicle- any vehicle left unmoved in a college parking lot for more than 10 consecutive days. (Permission for and location of long term vehicle storage may be obtained through the Parking Services Office.)
 

9.2 Campus-any property owned and maintained by Ricks College.
 

9.3 Campus Police- the division responsible fore police, parking and safety services on the campus of Ricks College. Located in room 150 of the Kimball building. Phone number 356-2448.
 

9.4 Citation, Campus- a notice of parking regulation violation issued by the Parking Services Office or the Campus Police for which a fine is assessed. This is an administrative sanction established by the college which governs college property.
 

9.5 Citation, Municipal- a notice of a municipal parking violation issued by the police department for which a fine is assessed. This is a legal sanction established by ordinance of the City of Rexburg.
 

9.6 Faculty - all full and part-time educators working under contract at Ricks college.
 

9.7 Financial hold- a hold placed on student transcripts which will not allow the release of grades or transcripts until all unsatisfied financial matters are resolved.
 

9.8 Fines- a penalty fee assessed for a parking violation.
 

9.9 Motorcycle- any two or three wheel vehicle propelled by other than human power. Motorcycle parking spaces are designated on campus.
 

9.10 Parking Appeal Committee- A committee consisting of representatives from the Administration and Student body of Ricks College given the responsibility of deterrmining the validity of parking violation appeals.
 

9.11 Parking permit- a decal or permit issued by the Parking Services Office which authorizes a vehicle to be parked in designated areas on the Ricks College campus.
 

9.12 Parking Services Office- an office within the Campus Police Division responsible for management and enforcement of college parking rules and regulations. The office is located in the Kimball building room 150. Phone number 356-2448.
 

9.13 Police- Campus Police personnel are duly commissioned police officers of the City of Rexburg, Idaho. Their office is located in Kimball building room 150. The phone number is 356-2448. Campus Police officers are on duty 24 hours every day.
 

9.14 Resident student- any student living in on-campus college owned housing.
 

9.15 Staff- staff employees are any regular full-time or part-time employees of ricks College. Student employees are not considered staff employees for the purposes of parking regulations.
 

9.16 Student- any individual enrolled in and taking college courses regardless of the numbers of hours carried.
 

9.17 Visitor- a community patron or other party visiting the Ricks College Campus who is not affiliated with the college as faculty, staff, administrator or student.

11.0 Bicycles
 

11.1 Persons riding bicycles on campus sidewalks or in parking lots must exercise due care and reasonable caution at all times so as to prevent any accident or injury to pedestrians or bicyclists.
 

11.2 Bicyclists shall yield the right of way to all pedestrians using sidewalks and parking lots.
 

11.3 Bicyclists shall ride at a reasonable and prudent speed and shall not exceed walking speed when pedestrians are present on sidewalks (Maximum 5 MPH)> During high pedestrian traffic times (ie. During class changes) it is advisable for bicyclists to dismount and walk their bikes.
 

11.4 All bicycles should be parked in bicycle racks provided on campus and should be secured when not in use.
 

11.5 Bicycles may not be parked in buildings or locked to trees, posts, or other fixtures other than designated bicycle racks. Those bicycles found in violation of this section will be subject to impoundment and a $10.00 fine.
 

11.6 Bicycle riders have legal responsibility in the case of any accident involving a pedestrian on a sidewalk.
 

11.7 Registration of bicycles is required. Registration helps identify a bicycle in the event of theft and provides the owner/rider with important safety information. This can be accomplished at the Campus Police Office in the Kimball building room 150 during regular business hours. There is no cost for bicycle registration. Unregistered bicycles shall be subject to a $5.00 fine.
 

11.8 Bicycle use is permitted for transportation purposes only. Entertainment activity, jumping, trick riding, etc. is not permitted.
 

12.0 Skateboards and Roller blades
 

12.1 Skateboards and roller blades are prohibited from use on campus sidewalks, parking lots and grounds as they are primarily recreational devices and the campus is not designed nor intended for such recreational use.
 

12.2 Persons violating these provisions on a recurring basis may be cited under Rexburg City Ordinance # 759, may be banned from access to campus and may be subject to arrest under Idaho trespass laws.
