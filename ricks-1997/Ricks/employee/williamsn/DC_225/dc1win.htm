 Course Objectives:
1. Seek learning in the Doctrine and Covenants by study and by faith
2. Build faith through the personal application of principles and doctrines learned through personal and class scripture study

Grading / Requirements: (100-94=A, 93-90=A-, 89-87=B+, 86-84=B, 83-80=B-,etc)
1. Reading / Expansion (200 Points)
2. Tests (2 @ 100 points)
3. Study Questions (10 @ 300 points)
4. Memorization (50 Points)
5. Extra Project (50 Points)
       TOTAL: 700 POINTS

1. Reading / Expansion: Each class period a 1/2 page expansion of a doctrine taught in the reading block will be required.  A discussion question will also need to be included in that expansion.  A list of expansion ideas will be created.  Different expansions will be expected.  Points will be taken in class and will be all or none. Type one of your expansion ideas on a discussion board and respond to someones expansion on the discussion board.

2. Tests: Two tests, a midterm and a final, will be taken on the technical aspects of the reading, class discussion and lesson material.

3. Study Questions: (SQ) There will be 10 study question assignments that will be posted on the reading block prior to the formal lesson.  The study questions must be completed prior to class in order to receive credit.

4. Memorization: Memorization of the titles for sections 76-138 (list will be provided) is expected.

5. Extra Project: Students will choose a project between 5-10 hours doing something they normally don't do to deepen their understanding of the Doctrine and Covenants.  A one page paper will be handed in at the end of the semester describing what was done, what was learned, and time spent.
