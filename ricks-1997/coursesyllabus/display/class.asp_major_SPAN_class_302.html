Readings in Hispanic Literature for advanced speakers.
HISPANIC LIT
CLASS CODE: SPAN 302 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An exploration of Spanish-language literature across the ages for advanced speakers.
DESCRIPTION: A study of Spanish-language poems, essays, short stories, plays, novels, culture, and language. Practice of language and principles leading to understanding and appreciation of literary genres.
TOPICS: Principles for analyzing literature, study of symbols, language, culture, word histories, historical background, etc., for advanced non-native speakers.
OBJECTIVES: To give students tools and abilities to analyze and enjoy literature on their own. Students completing this course should be able to speak at advanced low level and appreciate varied literary forms.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, oral participation and presentations in class, papers, extensive readings, exams (including short essay).
PREREQUISITES: Entry level for returned missionaries and similarly advanced non-natives who learned Spanish outside of the classroom.
OTHER: Native speakers should take Spanish 321.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2001
