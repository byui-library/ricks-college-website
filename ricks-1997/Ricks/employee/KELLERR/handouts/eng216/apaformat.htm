APA Format

1-Tab indent five spaces.
2-Author's name--reverse order--first name initial--period
3-Date--parenthesis--period
4-Book title--italicized/underlined--first letter only capitalized, all others lower case (capitalize first word only after colon)--period
5-Place of publication--only city name if major city, if not, include state also--colon
6-Publisher--period
7-Call number (applies only to cards
    and not to final reference page)
   
   
  
1-Tab indent five spaces.
2-Author's name--reverse order--first name initial only--period
3-Date--year--month--day--parenthesis--period
4-Article title--first letter capitalized--all other lower case--no quotation marks--period
5-Journal title--italicized--each major word captialized--comma
6-Volume number--underlined--no space
7-Issue number--parenthesis--comma

8-Page numbers of entire article--period
