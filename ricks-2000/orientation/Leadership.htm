Leadership Opportunities

Make a difference, get involved in NSO today!!!

When new students come to BYU-Idaho they often face fear, uncertainty, and sometimes loneliness.  The office of  New Student Orientation sponsors two leadership opportunities for current or returning students to get involved by serving new students to BYU-Idaho.  

Y-Team Leaders:  Y-Teams were designed so that no new students would not have to be alone during the first week of school.  Y-Teams give new students a base from which they can develop close friendships in a small-group setting, ask questions concerning personal and academic adjustments, and begin to learn what the "Spirit of Ricks" is all about. 

Non-freshmen students are invited to serve as Y-Team Leaders to new students starting school at BYU-Idaho.   Y-Team Leaders are paired in groups of two, and trained to provide leadership and guidance to approximately 15-25 incoming students during New Student Orientation. 

More than 160 Y-Team Leaders are needed to serve as peer mentors for incoming students.  Students who are outgoing, love to be involved and have the desire to serve new students should apply today to be on the Y-Team.  Applications will be made available at the Manwaring Center Information Desk beginning January , 2002.

NSO Team Leader - The NSO Team consists of eight students who serve as student leaders given the charge to help implement the various New Student Orientation activities.  NSO Team Leaders will plan and implement the various New Student Orientation events for new students attending BYU-Idaho for Fall and Winter semesters.  Students who are interested in a demanding leadership experience, love planning programs and activities, are committed to Ricks, and have a strong desire to be involved should consider applying for a position on the NSO Team. Applications for the NSO Team 2002 will be available at the Manwaring Center Information Desk beginning Fall 2001 and will be accepted through December 1, 2001.
