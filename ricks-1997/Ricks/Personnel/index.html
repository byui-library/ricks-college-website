Please Note: BYU-Idaho, an equal opportunity employer, is owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  Adherence to personal behavioral principles and standards of the Church is required of each employee of the University. Employees must also observe the BYU-Idaho Code of Honor, including the dress and grooming standards.  All employees accept, as a condition of employment, an annual eligibility review process which confirms compliance with the required Church and University standards. As required by the Immigration Reform and Control Act, all applications selected for employment will be required to be eligible to work in the United States before beginning employment. BYU-Idaho does not sponsor individuals for employment purposes.
