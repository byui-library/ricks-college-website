Creative Writing and Publishing
CREAT WRIT/PUB
CLASS CODE: ENG 418 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Uses workshops, document production, portfolios, professional documentation and editorial applications to prepare students for creative publishing. (Required for English Creative Writing Emphasis.)
DESCRIPTION: Uses workshops, document production, portfolios, professional documentation and editorial applications to prepare students for creative publishing. (Required for English Creative Writing Emphasis.)
TOPICS: Standards and methods of creative writing workshops; professional documentation; creative writing publications and markets; editing and production of a literary magazine.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Generating documents used in professional creative writing, including query letters to publishers, novel proposals, submission letters, applications to MFA programs and submissions to TV and movie production companies. (2) Learning standards and practices of contemporary publishing. (3) Facilitating the production of
Outlet: The BYU-Idaho Student Literary Journal.
REQUIREMENTS: Write and revise poems, stories, essays, and dramatic scenes, workshopping them with peers and the teacher. Create professional documents. Submit an original creative piece to a professional journal or magazine. Produce a portfolio representing a body of creative work produced at BYU-Idaho. Serve on the student editorial board of Outlet.
PREREQUISITES: English 218 or 318R.
