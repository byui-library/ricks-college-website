Idaho Falls Symphony

Saturday, March 9, 2002
7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall

The Idaho Falls Symphony was formed in 1949 from a group of musicians gathered to present a performance of Handel's "Messiah." Over the years this volunteer orchestra has brought the finest in symphonic music to Idaho Falls and eastern Idaho.

Tickets go on sale Feb. 19. Cost $6. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Orchestra/Band. Concert dress required for BYU-Idaho students.
