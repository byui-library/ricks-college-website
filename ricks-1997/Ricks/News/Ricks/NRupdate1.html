Ricks Update - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

April 21, 1998

We're trying something new to reach out to the friends of Ricks College. Wherever you are around the world, now you can keep up with the news from Ricks. Most of you are receiving this weekly newsletter via e-mail. If you know of someone who would like to be added to our mailing list, send an e-mail to Kristine Tighe at ricksupdate@ricks.edu. And if you're wondering how you got on this list and don't care to receive our weekly newsletter, please let us know!

It's a busy week at Ricks! Finals, banquets, graduation, packing and lots of tears as graduates say goodbye. We have 2,874 graduates - a new record number. Graduation activities will start with the Friday, April 24, Alumni Awards noon luncheon in Special Events Room of Manwaring Center. The graduation banquet will be at 5:30 p.m. on Friday for parents and graduates. Saturday will start with the Emeritus Breakfast at 7:30 a.m. in Special Events Room (retired employees and all alumni 1948 or prior are invited). Commencement will be at 9 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium (tickets required). Elder Henry B. Eyring, who served as president of Ricks from 1971-77, will preside and speak. Convocations will follow at 11:30 a.m. and the Spori Scholars luncheon will be at 1 p.m.

Sophomore basketball standout Cady Williams of Sugar City was named as a First Team All- American by the National Junior College Athletic Association. She's the third Lady Viking in Ricks history to achieve this status in basketball.

Our youngest student body president we can recall was recently elected. He is 17-year-old Adam Harrop of Quincy, Ill. He came to Ricks early and plans to serve a mission when he turns 19. He hopes to implement the "Step It Up" program and encourages each students to look at their life and do one more thing to improve.

Sander Larson, associate director of public relations, is leaving Ricks after 23 years to become president of the Washington Seattle Mission. He and his wife, Merlyn, will enter the MTC June 21.

The Ricks College 6th Stake was created at a special multi-stake conference April 19. Elder Harold G. Hillam, who grew up in Sugar City and attended Ricks, presided. New stake presidents are Jud Miller (new Ricks College 6th Stake) and Roger Wright of Idaho Falls replacing Douglas Ladle in the 2nd Stake. There are now six college stakes compared to four Rexburg stakes! We're now at 54 wards and counting. Enrollment is expected to peak at 8,600 students in the fall.

If you have suggestions or need more information, contact Don Sparhawk, Coordinator of Press Relations, Ricks College, Rexburg, ID 83460-4122, call (208) 356-2230 or write sparhawkd@ricks.edu.
