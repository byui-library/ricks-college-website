Housing units required to follow BYU-I Code of Honor

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Off-campus does not mean off duty.

Regardless of location, all on- and off-campus housing units are required to enforce the BYU-Idaho Code of Honor.

An ad hock group of off-campus owners and students wrote in the BYU-I housing Web page that “being approved merely means the unit meets several basic minimums in physical facilities, management and has agreed to enforce the BYU-Idaho Code of Honor. Approved off-campus units and their managers have gained approval status by also agreeing to live by and enforce the Code of Honor.”

Housing unit managers are required to sign a contract with the Housing Office stating that they will abide by the Code of Honor. They are also required to attend training on how to enforce it.

While housing managers are all committed to live by the Code of Honor, many are different in how they choose to abide by it.

“The resident assistant comes by maybe once or twice a week, but we usually are in on time anyway,” Chris Merrill, a sophomore from Rigby, Idaho, who lives in Horizon, said.

Housing units are not required to have RAs but are responsible for enforcing the Code of Honor, Sharon Tucket, housing director, said. Many managers find having RAs is the easiest way to keep track of their tenants.

While many students may think that different housing unit owners view the Code of Honor differently, they are all required to maintain the same level of enforcement.

“The dorms may have the reputation of being a little stricter than off-campus housing,” Deeaura Thompson from the Housing Office said. “But they all have the same rules.”

If housing unit owners are found neglecting their responsibility of enforcement, they are given a simple phone call as a warning. The next step is a mandatory meeting of further training for the manager and owners. During these training sessions, leadership skills and style are taught to help enforce the Code of Honor. If the unit continues to overlook, they will be taken off the approved housing list.
