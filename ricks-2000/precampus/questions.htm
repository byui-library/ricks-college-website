CASHIER'S OFFICE

  1. Q:  What are the cashier office hours?
    A:  We are open at 8:15 a.m. and close at 4:30 p.m. every day.   We are closed during devotionals on Tuesdays at 2 p.m.

  2. Q:  Do you cash out of state checks?
    A:  We will cash any check under $200.00 if made out to "cash" or to the student, with their I.D. card.

  3. Q:  Do I have to stand in line to pay for tuition and other bills?
    A:  NO, you can put the payment in an envelope with your name and social security number, and instructions, and drop it in the cashier's drop box.

  4. Q:  Do I have to have my I.D. card?
    A:  It is possible to make payments without your BYU-Idaho ID card, but a picture ID is necessary if you are receiving any money.  Any transaction can be completed faster and more efficiently if you have your BYU-Idaho ID card out and ready when you reach the cashier window.

  5. Q:  When is payday for student employees?
    A:  Paydays for campus jobs are on the 8th and 23rd of each month, unless that day is on a weekend or holiday.  In that case, payroll will be available on the Friday previous to that date.  Direct deposit to any bank in the U.S. is available through the payroll office.

HONORS PROGRAM

  1. Q:  Why should I join the Honors program?
    A:  It will not only help your thinking but will be a big plus when applying to your next school.

  2. Q:  Are Honors classes harder?
    A:  No.  We try to keep them about the same; the difference being that they are smaller (about half the size of a normal class) and the discussions are fantastic because all the people in the room are smart.

  3. Q:  How do I join?
    A:  There is no formal sign-up: just take any classes you like, but remember that you must have and maintain a 3.5 cumulative GPA.

  4. Q:  What are the course requirements?
    A:   Read your catalog, p. 170, under Honors Program or visit the Honors Program web site.

  5. Q:  How many extra classes will I need to take?
    A:  No "extra" classes at all; almost all of our classes give General Education (GE) credit.

HOUSING

  1. Q:  How do I find out what housing is available for students?
    A:  Please call the Housing Office at (208) 356-1045 (phone prefix will be changing) for mailed listings of available housing.  Also check the Housing Office web site for approved on-campus and off-campus lists, as well as student family housing information.

  2. Q: What does approved housing mean?
    A:  Single students attending BYU-Idaho need to live in approved housing.  This means that the apartments have on-site management and that they have met some minimum facility requirements.  These requirements are very basic so it well for students to look at the apartment to make sure they are satisfied with it.   Traveling to Rexburg is difficult for  some who live far away so those individuals may want to talk to a former student about the apartment they are interested in.

  3. Q:  How do I get my deposit back if I change my mind?
    A:  When a deposit is submitted to a housing complex, it means that the student is committed to stay there and the owner will hold a bed for the student.  When a student desires a change, he/she should review the terms of their security deposit/application fee agreement and contract.  Some agreements state that the deposit is not refundable while others state that a portion of the deposit is refundable within a specific time period.

  4. Q:  How much on-campus housing is available and are freshmen required to live there?
    A:  There are only 1076 beds available for men and women on campus.   Reservations are made on a first-come-first-served basis.  Freshmen are not required to stay on campus although some prefer to do so their first year.  On campus reservations are made through the BYU-Idaho Housing Office or by calling (208) 356-1045. 

  5. Q:  How do I select a roommate?
    A: Off-campus roommate requests are handled by the apartment manager or owner.  On-campus roommate requests may be made at the time of the application process, when returning the personal data sheet which is mailed with the contract, or by a phone call at any time.  The roommate's name and BYU-Idaho student ID number will be needed.  Your roommate will also need to put your name and student number on the application sheet or phone it in, in order for the request to be honored.  Please make sure that all roommates requested the same residence hall.

  6. Q:  When do I find out who my roommate(s) will be?
    A:   Information concerning roommates will be given to you when you check in.

  7. Q:  What are the deadlines to sign up for housing?
    A:   Reservations are made on a first-come-first-serve basis and need to be made separately for each summer term, for fall & winter semesters, for fall block & winter semester, for winter, and winter block.  Students mistakenly think that when they are contracted for summer they are automatically contracted for fall housing.  Some also mistakenly think that if they are contracted for fall-winter they are automatically contracted for the next fall-winter.  On-campus reservations are often made the year prior to arriving at BYU-Idaho.  On-campus cooking-style housing is often full by November the year before the contract begins.   Applications are available at the BYU-Idaho Housing web site, or by calling the Housing Office at (208) 356-1045.   Off-Campus reservations are also made on a first-come-first-serve basis.   Contact the housing complex for details regarding their reservation process.   Some complexes have a web site which can be located by clicking on their unit name at the BYU-Idaho Housing web site.

  8. Q:  Does the BYU-Idaho Housing Office make all housing reservations for all students?
    A:  No.  The Housing Office only makes reservations for students who would like to love on campus.  All off-campus reservations can be made by contacting the individual approved off-campus housing units.

  9. Q:  What is provided in my housing unit?
    A:   A list of provided items and items to bring is available on the BYU-Idaho Housing web site for on-campus housing.  Off-campus units will need to be contacted for this information.

  10. Q:  How do I get a meal plan?
    A:  Any student living on or off-campus may have a meal plan.   Those living on-campus in the non-cooking residence halls automatically have a meal plan with their housing contract.  Others may contact Food Services or by calling (208) 356-2250.

INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

  1. Q:  How do I get my international friend into BYU-Idaho?
    A:      The admission criteria for international students is the same for regular students.  In addition, other admission requirements include:

    -TOEFL score of 500 or above for applicants from non-English speaking countries.
    -Completion of high school or equivalency.
    -Affidavit of support and supporting financial statements.
    -$1,200 deposit (except for Canadian students).

  2. Q:  Are there any financial grants for international students?
    A:      International students are not eligible for U.S. federal student aid. Eligibility for academic and other scholarships require completion of a minimum of 24 credit hours. There are limited grants available for international students who attend summer terms.  Other grants are very limited.

  3. Q:  When can international students work off and on-campus?
    A    :  International students are eligible to work on campus once they are enrolled in BYU-Idaho. They must check with the International Student Office and apply for and receive a United States social security number prior to seeking employment. International students are not allowed to work off-campus unless authorized. There must be a completion of at least two full semesters prior to applying for any of the off campus-work options.

  4. Q:  I want to marry a non U.S. citizen, what do I have to do?
    A    :  Go to the International Student Office in Kimball 230 for assistance.

  5. Q:  What must an international student do when they first arrive?
    A    :  They must go to the International Student Office in Kimball 230 and bring their documents (passport, I-20 and I-94). The I-20 must be signed by the advisor.  Each student will receive information regarding their obligations and requirements to remain in status while studying in the United States.

KBYI RADIO

  1. Q:  What work opportunities are there at KBYI?
    A:      KBYI employs a limited number of students as news producers, copy editors, news readers, and production coordinators. The prerequisite class is Communications 265.

  2. Q:  When are the university devotionals broadcast?
    A    :  BYU-Idaho Devotionals are broadcast live at 2 p.m. on Tuesdays and repeated at 8:30 p.m. that evening. KBYI also broadcasts BYU-Idaho Devotionals from the archives on Sundays at 5 p.m.

  3. Q:  Will KBYI broadcast the ball game tonight?
    A:  KBYI broadcasts Viking Sports live with play-by-play action, interviews with coaches and players, and exciting coverage of each game including advancement to playoffs. All Viking Football and Viking Basketball games are carried, whether at home or on the road.

  4. Q:  How can I get a campus event announced on the radio for publicity?
    A:  KBYI features a community calendar twice each day. Copy must be submitted by mail two weeks in advance of the event.

  5. Q:  What is the format on KBYI?
    A:  KBYI features programming from National Public Radio and Public Radio International, as well as locally produced news and arts entertainment. In-depth news coverage of world, national, and local events is covered by network news and the KBY(I News Team. Classical music is presented daily with air time given to Jazz and Folk music on weekend evenings.
