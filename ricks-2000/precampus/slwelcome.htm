Student Life Welcome

  a       
             
             
   

 




Welcome to BYU-Idaho.  We are excited to have you attend and become part of the BYU-I university community.  You will have many opportunities for learning, growth, and personal development from both your class and out-of-class experiences.  Our faculty are here to assist you to learn all that you can while the university staff will provide you assistance and direction for all your student needs.

Your college years are a wonderful time in life, filled with discovery and growth.   There will be many exciting challenges, transitions, and opportunities associated with starting college.  For most incoming students, college is the first time students leave home and experience "independence."    Most will leave family and friends behind and travel some distance to come to BYU-I.  Some may live relatively close to Rexburg and may only be traveling a few miles to come to college.   Others may be returning from an LDS church mission to start college for their first time or may have transferred from another educational institution.  Regardless of your situation, we welcome you and congratulate you on your decision to attend BYU-Idaho.

It is not uncommon during this new transition in your life to have some fears, concerns, and uncertainties regarding college life and what to expect.  For this reason the office of New Student Orientation was created to assist new students so they did not have to learn by "trial and error."  

Your Y-Team is ready to assist you and make your transition to BYU-Idaho easier by answering your questions, giving you suggestions and guidance about coming to BYU-I, and familiarizing you with campus upon your arrival.  In addition, Pathway will help alleviate your concerns, answer questions you may have, and prepare you for a BYU-Idaho learning experience.

We are excited to have the opportunity to teach, guide, and serve you. 

Sincerely,


Robert M. Wilkes
Student Life Vice President
