APPEALS ARE A SERIOUS MATTER

 

The appeals process is a serious matter! If you feel that you have unjustly received a ticket, this is your forum to voice your argument. However, this is not the avenue for general criticisms, complaints or policy debates.

General Appeals Notices

Ricks College Parking Service allow ONLY ONE APPEAL per Notice of Parking Violation (ticket). This on-line appeal form is considered a written appeal and will be treated as such in all respects.

Written appeals are reviewed by a Hearing Referee. Your letter should contain clear, concise and complete information so the hearing officer can give a fair assessment of your appeal.

If fines and/or fees are found due as a result of the appeal process, they must be paid at Ricks College Accounting Office by the payment due date provided on notice of appeal results.
