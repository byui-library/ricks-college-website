Sacred and Religious Literature
SAC&REL LIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 361 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Examines a variety of sacred and religious texts, explores connections betwen literature and religious ideals, and provides an interpretive framework within which to gain insight into sacred and religious thought.
DESCRIPTION: Examines a variety of sacred and religious texts, explores connections between literature and religious ideals, and provides an interpretive framework within which to gain insight into sacred and religious thought.
TOPICS: Exposure to a range of sacred and religious literature; examination of the way literature explores, dramatizes, and promotes sacred and religious ideals; literary principles for reading sacred and religious texts closely to gain insight into religious thought; critical thinking about sacred/religious texts; theme identification within the literary tradition.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Heightening student consciousness of the sacred. (2) Developing respect for the value of religious literature. (3) Understanding of living a faithful life through analysis of sacred and religious literature. (4) Encouraging responsible discussion connecting religious ideals and literature.
REQUIREMENTS: Explicate a central theme in a religious text demonstrating how it illuminates understanding of a scriptural or cultural text. Summary of a religious text exploring the literary dimensions.
