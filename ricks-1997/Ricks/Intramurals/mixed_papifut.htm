Mixed Papifut

1. Must score in the score box.

2. If ball goes above head, it is a dropped ball and change of possession.

3. No person, defensive or offensive, can go inside the crease except for the goalie.

4. Kick in all out of bounds.

5. One male or female on the field at all times.

6. Games are two 20 minute halves.

7. Subs-out of bounds.

8. Goalie can go anywhere.

9. Must have four to start.  One must be a member of the opposite sex or the game is a forfeit.

10. No off sides.

11. Ties- Go to penalty kicks.  (five kicks)
