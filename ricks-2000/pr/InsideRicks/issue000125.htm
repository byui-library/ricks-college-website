ELDER RICHARD G. SCOTT SPEAKS AT DEVOTIONAL

Elder Richard G. Scott of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles outlined a pathway to greater happiness through understanding and applying principles of truth. He set the stage for his instruction at the devotional on January 18 by inviting the audience to make a written record of impressions they received through the Spirit. "You can learn by what you hear, see, and especially by what you feel as prompted by the Holy Ghost. . . .Write down the important things you learn from the Spirit and continue to use them. . . . Always, day or night, wherever you are, whatever you are doing, seek to recognize and respond to the direction of the Spirit in your life," he said.

Elder Scott opened the assembly to a discussion that focused upon motivations of the students. He directed questions to the students, listened to their responses, and then gave counsel. He told them that their daily decisions should lead to where they want to be. "Be certain that you are not being led 'carefully' from the main track to happiness onto a side track that can, in time, result in loss of that which will be most precious to you." Recognizing worthy goals given by the students in response to his questions, he said that each needs to establish a set of guiding principles for life and then not deviate from them in daily actions. "Exercise faith in Jesus Christ. Faith is abiding trust in the Lord." Elder Scott said, "The greatest motivation in life for me comes from knowing you have a Father in Heaven who loves you very much and wants your eternal happiness. It comes from knowing the Savior and believing that He will help you."

APPLAUSE FOR BALLROOM DANCE COMPANY

The Ricks College Ballroom Dance Company successfully defended their position as cabaret champions at the U.S. Collegiate Dance Board Festival held in Daytona Beach, Florida, earlier this month. Dancers competing represented 35 colleges from throughout the United States, England, and Canada. Ricks College was the only junior college represented. The 31-member team from Ricks won the cabaret formation event, placed second in Latin formation, and third place in the team match competition. Five of the couples won in various individual events.

Ricks College dancers not only excel in their performance, but they also demonstrate that dance can be beautiful and modest. Shawn Fisher, director of the Ballroom Dance Company, said the opportunity to compete is "great publicity for the Church because of our costume standards and the way we dance. We are much more conservative than other groups. The people that come to watch and even other schools really appreciate it."

Members of the Ballroom Dance Company are selected by audition each fall. Students rehearse for approximately six hours a week. The Ricks College Ballroom Dance Company joins with the Contemporary Dance Theater each year to tour for Ricks College and the Church Educational System as Extravadance. The Extravadance program has been enthusiastically accepted by audiences throughout the world. Most recent tours have been to Brazil, the islands of the Pacific, and New Zealand. This year Extravadance is scheduled to tour the Southeastern United States. Information on Extravadance and other Ricks College performance tours is available through PublicRelations@ricks.edu.
