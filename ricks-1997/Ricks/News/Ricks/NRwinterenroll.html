Ricks College News Release, January 23, 1998

Enrollment climbs to record 8,350

Ricks College is continuing to see an increase in enrollment with a record number of students attending winter semester.

The official enrollment for winter semester stands at a record 8,350 students, which is 73 students more than fall semester.

Jim Gee, assistant academic vice president for Support Services, says the administration is pleased with the numbers. "It's a little bit over what we expected, but there were probably more students returning than we thought," he says. "We are now looking forward to 8,600 in the fall."

In an effort to try to accommodate more applicants, Ricks is gradually moving toward 8,600 students, a number it expects to reach in the fall of this year.

The increase during 1997 and 1998 is made possible by additional personnel and more efficient use of classroom space during afternoon and evening hours.
Fall semester enrollment statistics show 763 new students. The remainder are either continuing from last semester or returning after an absence, such as returned missionaries who are re-entering school.

Students this semester are registered for an average of 15 credit hours, which shows the students are traditional full-time students.

A breakdown in statistics for fall semester shows 2,893 from Idaho, 1,158 from Utah, 790 from California, 659 from Washington, 361 from Oregon, 228 from Colorado, 201 from Texas, 181 from Arizona, 178 from Wyoming, 147 from Montana and 120 from Nevada.

Fifty-six percent of the students are women (4,683). There are 2,508 returned LDS Church missionaries (30 percent of the student body), 242 foreign students from 41 countries and 590 married students. Fifty-six percent of the students are either 18 or 19 years old.
