QUEEN OF THE GEMS:  FROM DISCOVERING RARE OPALS TO FINISHED JEWELRY
Rare fire opals are among this "Gem State's" precious minerals. Recognize and gather opals through a hands-on opportunity from an open pit mine, one of the few places left in the world with fire opals. Examine the geological features of the gem's birth.  Learn and develop the fine-tuned skills of grinding the rock, and produce finished jewelry for your private collection. 

WHAT YOU ALWAYS WANTED TO KNOW ABOUT MORMONS, BUT WERE AFRAID TO ASK
Are Mormons Christians? Why are they so interested in genealogy work? Why do they build temples? Why are they called Mormons? What is their history? Why are families so important? Find answers to these questions and others.
