Athletic Hall Of Fame 
Greg Hunter

Members of the offensive line in football often toil in anonymity. Greg's talent as a guard, however, was impossible to ignore. Ricks placed 11th nationally in 1985 with a 7-3 record during his freshman year. A year later, with Greg as one of the team captains, Ricks was only four votes short of being named the national junior college champion. The 1986 team never lost a game, but had one tie to mar its season record. The highlight of the year came in a thrilling last-second, come-from-behind Jayhawk Bowl win in Kansas. For his efforts Greg was named a First Team All-American at Ricks. He was a member of the Western States Football League's First Team two years, a rare feat, and was given the Clyde P. Packer Ideal Male Athlete Award at this banquet13 years ago. A returned missionary from the Chile Vina Del Mar Mission, Greg was an offensive starter at Brigham Young University for two years, graduating in 1989.

Greg has been a Seattle police officer for 10 years.

He graduated from BYU in 1989 with two degrees, one in Spanish and another in American Studies.

Greg makes his home in Edmonds, Wash.

He is a native of Brigham City, Utah

Greg is married to the former Becki Holley and they met when Greg was visiting a friend at Ricks College two weeks after he returned from his mission to Chile (Vina Del Mar) in 1984.  The Hunters have four children: Holley is 11; Joseph is 9, Margaret is 2 and James is going on eight months.

Greg is his ward's Young Men's President.

For hobbies he lists changing diapers, household repairs, yard work. On a more serious note he says he enjoys taking his wife out on the town, playing with the kids and exercising.
