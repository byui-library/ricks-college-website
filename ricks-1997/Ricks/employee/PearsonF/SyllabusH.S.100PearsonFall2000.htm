Class Description: Introduction to the medical and allied health careers. There are over 900 different careers in the medical field. This class is a overview of health care in the United States and a discussion of the many medical and allied health careers. The class includes two hours of lecture each week and assignments that will help the student gain insight into possible health career options..



Textbook: Introduction to the Medical and Allied Health Careers, Fred R. Pearson, Ricks College Press, August 2000



Assignments:

1. Prepare a resume of your education and job experiences.

2. Prepare a written three page double spaced paper on one of the medical or allied health careers. Include a personal interview with a health professional in the paper and be prepared to present this orally to the class on your assigned day.

3. Read textbook and complete assignments at the end of the chapter.

Interview and Oral Presentation



Follow the following outline when preparing and doing the interview.. All of these areas should be discussed as a part of your presentation before the class. 150 points possible for the paper and presentation of this paper to the class.





Provide a clear description of the health career.





What are the responsibilities of this job?





What education and training is needed for obtaining this job?





What special qualities should one have or develop to do this job?





What is the work environment like for this type of job?





What are the different types of instutution or places where someone find work with this job?





What type of advantages do you see with this job.





What are the disadvantages in doing this job.





What kind of career opportunities do you see in doing this job?



What are the advancement possibilities for this job?





Average salaries?





Health care trends that may in the future or are now affecting this career.





Question to the person you are interviewing: "If you were a student looking for a career why would you choose this one"? And would the person being interviewed choose this career again?
