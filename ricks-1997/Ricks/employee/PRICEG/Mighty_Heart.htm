Mighty Heart
Pure Heart - (Spiritual)

 

"Serve [God] with all your heart, might, mind, and strength" (D&C 4:2).  "I applied mine heart to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom, and the reason of things..." (Ecclesiastes 7:25).

     With a MIGHTY HEART, STRONG MIND, and CLEAN HANDS:   "We can live in the world,... without letting the world into us" (John 17:15).  We have the gospel message that can carry men and women buoyantly through the "mist of darkness" (1 Nephi 8:23) to the source of all light.

     "The Lord does not need a society that hides and isolates itself from the world.  Rather,  He needs stalwart individuals and families who live exemplary lives in the world and demonstrate that joy and fulfillment come not of the world but throught the spirit and the doctrine of Jesus Christ." 

Elder M. Russell Ballard
