LOST, STOLEN OR DAMAGED CARDS

 

If your card is lost or stolen, you can purchase a replacement card at the ID Center for a nonrefundable fee of $10. If your card becomes damaged through wear and tear, a new one will be issued at no charge only when the damaged card is returned to the ID Center. If you have a meal plan or have money on your card, it is important that you notify the ID Center promptly if it is lost or stolen so the card can be deactivated. It is against the Code of Honor to be in possession of more than one ID card.
