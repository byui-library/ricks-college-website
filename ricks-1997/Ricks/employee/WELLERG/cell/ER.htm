The purpose of endoplasmic Reticulum (ER) is various.  ER is identified as two types Rough ER and Smooth ER.

This is a transmission electron microscope (TEM) view of cell.  The TEM shows internal structures of  objects.   In this image you can see the Rough RE. Rough ER is covered with ribosomes.  The ribosomes are the dark granules on the ER and in the cytoplasm. Ribosomes are responsible for protein synthesis.   Some proteins are taken inside of the Rough ER and further modified and conducted throughout the cell.

In the TEM image of a cell Smooth ER can be see at the edges of cell surrounding the vacuole.  Smooth ER is involved with synthesis of lipids.
