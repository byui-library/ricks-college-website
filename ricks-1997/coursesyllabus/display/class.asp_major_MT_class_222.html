Engineering Materials
ENGR MATERIALS
CLASS CODE: MT 222 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: MECHANICAL ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Physical properties, composition and constituents of metals and metal alloys. Laboratory work consists of preparation of metallographic samples for microscopic examination and the effects of heat treatment on steels. (F)
DESCRIPTION: This course is intended to introduce technology students to both theory and application of metallurgical principles as they are applied to the design, selection, heat treating, and processing of metals and alloys.
The principles of materials testing, atomic structure, phase diagrams and theories of deformation will be introduces. Principles of heat treating and surface treatments will also be introduced.
TOPICS: Laboratory work consists of preparation of metallographic samples for microscopic examination and also the effects of heat treatment on steels. (Fall)
OBJECTIVES: The student will be introduced to the following metallurgical concepts and will apply this knowledge in the solution of problems and laboratory experiments.
1. Extraction of Metals from Ores
2. Manufacturing and selection of steel
3. Nonferrous metals
4. Mechanical and Physical Properties of Metals
5. Testing of Metals
6. Crystal Structure of Metals
7. Iron-Carbon Phase Diagrams
8. Hardening, Tempering, Annealing etc of Carbon Steels
9. Metallurgy of machining, welding, working og metals
10. Corrosion and other metal failure mechanisms
