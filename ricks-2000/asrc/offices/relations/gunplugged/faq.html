Q: What is Guitars Unplugged?

A: Guitars Unplugged is sponsored by the Associated Students of Ricks College. The show features the talents of student guitarists who are chosen via tryouts.

Q: Where do the ticket sales go?

A: See the funds page.

Q: Where does the show take place?

A: Manwaring Center West Ballroom and the Best of Guitars Unplugged is located in the Hart Auditorium.

Q: When does the show begin?

A: 7:00 P.M.

Q: How much does the show cost?

A: $2 and $3 for Best of Guitars Unplugged

Q: What is the Best of Guitars Unplugged?

A: Best of Guitars Unplugged features the best acts of the Thursday and Friday shows chosen by judges.

Q: When does Guitars Unplugged take place?

A: Guitars Unplugged is showcased each semester including summer shows. You can sign up for tryouts at these times at the ASRC office.
