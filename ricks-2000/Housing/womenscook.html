On-Campus Women's Cooking-Style Housing


The Ricks College Women's Cooking-style housing units are Dorms One through Four (dorms 1-3 in the summer) . Each one houses 120 women.
Each building has a main lounge as with all on campus dorms.
The three bedroom apartments are shared by six women, two per bedroom. The bedrooms contain two beds, two dressers, one desk, an eight-foot closet and some
wall-mounted shelving.
There is also a coin-operated laundry room in each building with Maytag washers and dryers.
The double-sink bathroom has a tub/shower and toilet, with a floor-to-ceiling storage cabinet.
Each apartment also has a newly remodeled bright-white kitchen with microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls
  • You can live in the cooking-style housing and still eat in the Gallery.
  • For information about the meal plans which are available please visit the Ricks College Food Services website.
