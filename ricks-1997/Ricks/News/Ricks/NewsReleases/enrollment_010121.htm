Ricks College News Release
January 21, 2001

Ricks enrollment slightly higher than in fall semester

REXBURG, Idaho — The official winter enrollment count is in at Ricks College and it’s just slightly higher than last semester’s record.

There are 8,955 students registered at the Rexburg college for winter semester. The total is slightly higher than fall’s enrollment of 8,949 and last winter’s enrollment of 8,840.

Attending Ricks this winter are 626 new students and 7,801 continuing students. The remaining 528 are returning after an absence of one or more semesters, including those who are re-entering after serving missions for the LDS Church. All in all, 2,884 of those attending Ricks are returned missionaries.

There are 5,069 females (56 percent) compared to 3,886 male students. Married students total 655.

Full time number of students total 8,441, with 514 attending part time.

Students from all 50 states are represented in the United States. The number breakdown show that more than 200 students come from seven states. Idaho reported 2,948; Utah had 1,336; California with 865; 726 from Washington; Oregon had 388; 276 from Texas; and Colorado had 243.

There are 275 international students representing 48 countries. Of these, 152 come from Canada.

Statistics are based on enrollment after the first 10 days of class.
