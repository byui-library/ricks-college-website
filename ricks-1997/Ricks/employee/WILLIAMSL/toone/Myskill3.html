The Post Game Fiasco . . .

After the game concludes, time is short, so talk to at least the head coach of each team and then try and find a few key players from each team, the more interviews, the better the story will be.

Interviewing: Ask questions that make them avoid cliched-answers. "What were you thinking when you threw that interception?" or "What did coach tell you in that last timeout?" Be creative, funny, friendly and don't be afraid to ask dumb questions. Also, review the stats with them, good questions can come from the stats.

Don't be nervous, but just be yourself ­ athletes and coaches are human too.

Use pen and paper, not recorder, because they take to long to review. When you feel like you got more than enough, get out and get to your pre-designated place to start writing!
