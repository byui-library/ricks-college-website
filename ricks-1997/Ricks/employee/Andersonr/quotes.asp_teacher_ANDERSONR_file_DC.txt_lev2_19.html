Doctrine & Covenants Section 20
Outline of Section 20
20:1-4~~Establish Church
20:5~~First Vision 1820-1823. See D&C 1:30
20:6-7~~Angel Moroni--Preparation 1823-27. See Lucy Mack Smith pp. 82-83
20:8~~Translation of Book of Mormon 1827-1829. See D&C 5:4
20:9~~Contents
20:10~~What it is--Scripture; Angelic confirmation; Witnesses confirm to world
20:11-12~~Purposes:
A. Prove Bible
B. Prove Revelation
C. Prove Priesthood is eternal
D. Jesus is God of Yesterday--Old Testament; Today--Ministry to Second Coming D&C 64:23; Forever--Eternal
20:13~~World Judged by D&C 5:6-10--the above events (witnesses)
20:14~~Blessings-to those who receive these things
20:15~~Cursings-to thos who reject these things
20:16~~Conclusion
20:17-28~~Doctrines established by the above mentioned events (witnesses)
20:29-36~~What it means to man
A. Baptism 37
B. Priesthood duty 38-67
C. Duties after baptism 68-84
Conclusion D&C 21:1-5 Joseph Smith as a Prophet, Seer, and Revelator, etc.
D&C 20
1. Before someone is confirmed a member of the Church, what should the Elders or Priests do? TEACH AND EXPOUND ALL THINGS CONCERNING THE CHURCH OF CHRIST TO THEIR UNDERSTANDING--D&C 20:68
2. The Church of Jesus Christ was organized on:
APRIL 6, 1830--D&C 20:1
3. Why are we asked to sustain people in church offices and callings by raising our right hands?
THE LORD WANTS TO GIVE EVERYBODY THE CHANCE TO VOTE--D&C 20:65
4. Jesus Christ's birthday is:
APRIL 6, 0 --D&C 20:1
5. What office is required to give someone the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands?
ELDER--D&C 20:41,58
6. Explain justification.
THE PROCESS OF BECOMING JUST AND TRUE-A JUST PERSON-RETAINING A REMISSION OF YOUR SINS--D&C 20:30
7. How often do we hold Stake Conference?
EVERY SIX MONTHS--D&C 20:61,81
8. Christ was tempted, but He:
GAVE NO HEED UNTO THEM--D&C 20:22
9. Why do teachers prepare the Sacrament?
IT IS PART OF STRENGTHENING THE CHURCH--D&C 20:53-59
10. How does the Lord feel about the Book of Mormon?
CONTAINS A RECORD OF FALLEN PEOPLE-FULLNESS OF GOSPEL-GIVEN BY INSPIRATION-CONFERRED BY ANGELS-PROVES SCRIPTURES ARE TRUE--D&C 20:9-16
11. Explain sanctification.
BEING FORGIVEN OF SINS-REPENTING AND RECEIVING FORGIVENESS-BECOMING PURE--D&C 20:31
12. What office in the priesthood do you have to hold to bless the Sacrament?
PRIEST--D&C 20:46
13. How often do we hold General Conference?
EVERY SIX MONTHS--D&C 20:61-62,81
14. Why is it so important to pray always anyway? D&C 20:33
D&C 10:5--TO CONQUER SATAN
D&C 19:38--POUR OUT SPIRIT
D&C 31:12--LEST YOU ENTER INTO TEMPTATION
D&C 32:4--UNDERSTANDING
D&C 33:17--BE READY FOR SECOND COMING
D&C 61:39--ENTER NOT INTO TEMPTATION
D&C 88:126--SECOND COMING
D&C 90:24--ALL THINGS SHALL WORK TOGETHER FOR YOUR GOOD
D&C 93:49--OVERCOME SATAN
15. What office must you hold to ordain an Elder?
ELDER--D&C 20:39
16. How can you be saved in the Kingdom of God?
REPENT, BELIEVE IN JESUS CHRIST-WORSHIP THE FATHER-ENDURE TO END
17. Why do we have Stake Conference and General Conference?
TO BE INSTRUCTED BY OUR LEADERS--D&C 20:61-62,81
18. What office is required to baptize someone?
PRIEST--D&C 20:46
19. Why aren't mentally retarded children required to be baptized?
NOT ACCOUNTABLE--D&C 20:71
20. Why don't we baptize children under eight years of age?
NOT ACCOUNTABLE--D&C 20:71
21. Your eight-year-old sister askes you to baptize her. You don't know what to do or what to say. Where can you find out?
--D&C 20: 73
22. You forgot the exact day you were baptized. Where could you find out?
--D&C 20: 83-84
23. Why don't we sprinkle people when they are baptized?
--D&C 20:74
24. How should members demonstrate their worthiness?
GODLY WALK OF CONVERSATION-WORKS-WALK IN HOLINESS--D&C 20:69
25. Why do deacons pass the Sacrament?
--D&C 20:57,59
26. What qualifies a person to be baptized?
--D&C 20:37
27. What office do you have to hold to ordain someone to be a teacher in the Aaronic Priesthood?
PRIEST--D&C 20:48
28. Why do deacons, teachers, and priests go hometeaching?
--D&C 20:47,53-54,57
29. You have been assigned to bless the Sacrament in Sacrament Meeting. You notice that the card that is usually there is gone. You haven't memorized the prayer. What do you do?
--D&C 20:76-79
30. True or False. There are only three set prayers in the Church.
--D&C 20:73,77,79
31. Why do we bless little children in Sacrament Meeting?
--D&C 20:70
32. What happens to a member of the church when he is excommunicated?
--D&C 20:83
