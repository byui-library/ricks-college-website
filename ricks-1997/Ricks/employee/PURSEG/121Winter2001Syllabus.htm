Objective: To increase our understanding of, and our appreciation for, the Book of Mormon. We cannot cover everything in the scripture blocks during our class sessions. We will therefore focus on some of the more important and interesting doctrines, principles, and story points.

Testing: There will be one test accounting for 60% of your grade. The test will be based on the study guide provided via the internet. The test will be available in the testing center from 4/16 through 4/27. If you miss taking the test other than for extreme emergencies, you will receive a 0.

Reading, Marking, and Attendance: Each class period you will mark the roll for reading, marking, and attending, 1,2, or 3 points. These total points account for 40% of your grade. You must mark each term, every time the Lord intervenes (tender mercies watch) on behalf of the people in the Book of Mormon narrative, and, you must write one very good question that you would ask one of the characters in the scripture block.
