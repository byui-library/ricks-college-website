Information for Returned Missionaries

We have created a new class, CS 148 (Computer Science Review), to help returned missionaries get back into the mode of programming. We'll cover the material you need for CS 235. CS 148 is one credit and meets daily for three weeks so that you can take it concurrently with CS 235.

We split CS 171 (Programming II) into two classes, CS 173 (Web Programming) and CS 246 (Advanced Object Oriented Programming). CS 173 has been replaced by CS 273 (Web Engineering). CS 246 has been replaced by CS 145 (Software Design and Development). If you have successfully completed CS 171, you do not need to take CS 145, CS 173, CS 246, or CS 273.

Computer Science Fast Track

Fast track students can major in Computer Science. Fast track allows you to graduate in less calendar time (e.g., between high school and mission). If you are interested in fast track, contact Brother Twitchell (telephone number and e-mail address are at the bottom of this page).

Please note that there are financial incentives for being fast track (one-half tuition scholarship for any summer term attended).
