Creative Writing
CREATIVE WRTG
CLASS CODE: ENG 218 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Focuses on the creation of original works of poetry, drama, fiction and non-fiction. Introductory course for the creative writing emphasis for English majors. (Does not give GE credit in the Letters area of the Arts & Letters requirement nor in the Reading & Writing area of the Basic Skills requirement.)
DESCRIPTION: Focuses on the creation of original works of poetry, drama, fiction and non-fiction. Introductory course for the creative writing emphasis for English majors. (Does not give GE credit in the Letters area of the Arts & Letters requirements nor in the Reading & Writing area of the Basic Skills requirement.)
TOPICS: Basic forms of creative literature; class-based writing workshops; literary terms; basic expectations of the creative publishing market.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Knowing basic terminology. (2) Practicing writing in several creative genres. (3) Developing a heightened aesthetic sense and becoming more appreciative of literary writing. (4) Creating a portfolio as an artifact of the creative writing experience.
REQUIREMENTS: Reading assignments from the works of published authors. Numerous writing assignments. Class-based workshopping assignments.
PREREQUISITES: Completion of English 111 or 111C.
