November 8, 2001

Eliza R. Snow Society invites

new members to join group


REXBURG, Idaho- The Eliza R. Snow Society for the Arts, a Brigham Young University-Idaho support group for the performing and visual arts, is working on broadening its membership.

Last week about 150 members and friends gathered for a dinner at BYU-Idaho that featured a showcase of student talent. The group then attended the Halloween concert presented by the Symphonic Band.

The Snow Society has been established to make it possible for BYU-Idaho students to develop their talents and achieve their full potential in the areas of theatre, dance, music, and the visual arts, says board chairman Vonnie Lu Broulim of Rigby. It also provides opportunities for members to interact and to attend student productions throughout the year.

Contributions are used for scholarships for talented, deserving students, who without financial assistance could not fully participate in tours or other study-related activities designed to help them develop their artistic talents. Funds are also used for specialized equipment.

Bradley Petersen, who is the managing director for the society, explains that people may join at different levels of financial commitment beginning at $50 per year as a friend and running at levels of $100 (contributor), $250 (patron), $500 (master), and $1,000 or more (benefactor).

Members are invited to special receptions and a year-end gala showcasing students. They also may purchase tickets to student performances at BYU-Idaho in advance of the time they go on sale to the general public. Members of the society are also recognized for their generosity in a list of supporters that is published in printed programs at BYU-Idaho productions.

Members of the newly organized board are Debbie Gardner of Idaho Falls; Jay Slaughter of Island Park; L. Sid Jenson of Roberts; Gwen Lee, Rich Ballou, Marsha Sparhawk, Carrie Mills, and Thomas Kennelly, all of Rexburg; Stacy Bauer of Terreton; and Ted Wright of Rigby. Representing the university on the board are Greg Palmer, Robyn Bergstrom, Steve Davis, Matt Geddes, Kristine Tighe, and Petersen.

For more information, contact one of the board members or Petersen at 496-2183.
