TRANSPORTATION

Transportation is provided for all Quest sponsored activities for students on site at Vashon Island or Badger Creek.

Students attending the Vashon Island site who need transportation to and from activities not sponsored by the Quest program will need to utilize their own private vehicle or the public transportation systems of King County (Seattle) and Pierce County (Tacoma).  For further information about bringing your own vehicle click here.   For more information about the public transportation systems available in the Seattle/Tacoma metro area select from the following sites.

King County Transit     Washington State Ferries     Pierce County Transit

Students attending the Badger Creek site who need transportation to and from activities not sponsored by the Quest program will need to utilize their own private vehicles.

Hitchhiking is a dangerous and unpredictable form of commuting.  Quest program participants who are either at the Vashon Island or Badger Creek sites are strictly prohibited from hitchhiking.
