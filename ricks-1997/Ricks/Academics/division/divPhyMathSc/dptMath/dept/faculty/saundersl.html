Beginning 32nd year teaching at Ricks College. Bro. Saunderswas born in Ogden, Utah October 14, 1940. He is the second child and oldest son of Harold and Doris Saunders of Vale, Oregon (they are the parents of one daughter and seven sons). His youngest brother, David, also teaches at Ricks College. Bro. Saunders served a mission in New York. He married Ruth Olsen of Provo, Utah in 1966 and they have six children: Brad, Taunie, Cory, Jon, Julie, and Katie. He graduated from BYU in 1968 with an M.S. degree. He enjoys public speaking, building log furniture and nearly anything else (he built his own home), and he loves sports.
