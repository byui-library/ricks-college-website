New Student Forum Activity:  

Access the New Student Forum discussion board from the "Communications" section or "Discussion Board" section in Blackboard (use the links provided in the "Questions" section of Pathway).

  1. The New Student Forum exists for you to get to know other incoming students, ask questions of new and current students, discuss student service needs, and discuss or share items of interest to you with your fellow students.  
  2. There are two major rules that apply to posting messages on this forum:

    1.  All messages posted must be in line with the standards and principles of the BYU-Idaho Honor Code and the teachings of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. 

    2.  Asking what teachers to take for specific courses is inappropriate in this and other forums.  

  3. You should submit at least one message to the New Student Forum.  However, you are free to submit as many messages as you would like and are encouraged to frequent this site often to see what other students are saying or asking.
