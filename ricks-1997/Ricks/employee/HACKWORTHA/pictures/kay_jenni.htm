Bighole Mountains
My friend, Kay, and my daughter, Jenni, are pictured here.

In the background is the Snake River Valley.  

We have explored so much beautiful country since owning our ATVs.  

People live a lifetime in an area, and never know what is just fifteen miles off the beaten path near their homes.

Few people in my town know about the following places of beauty, just over the hill from their backyards: Hell-hole Canyon, Moody Swamp, Limekiln Canyon, Prospect Peak, Moody Meadow,  Long Hollow Road, Buckskin Morgan Ridge, Hawley Gulch Trail, Wolverine Creek Trail, the Stock Driveway Trail, Castle Lake, Blacktail Pass, and Garns Mountain, just to name a few.

And this is only one direction (east).  Additional adventures spread to the north and west, and they never stop.
