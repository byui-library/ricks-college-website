Electrical & Computer Engineering

Electrical and computer engineers design and develop electrical power generation and distribution systems, world wide communications systems including satellites, radio and television broadcasting and receiving equipment, recording equipment, telephone, Internet, computer, optical fiber cables. Computer engineers work on new ways to build faster, more powerful computers. Just about every new product on the market uses electronics or computer components which are designed by electrical or computer engineers. The medical fields rely heavily on new electronic and computer based equipment ranging from magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) to laser surgery to pacemakers. Electricity and computers have become so extensively used in our society as to make this one of the largest fields on engineering.
