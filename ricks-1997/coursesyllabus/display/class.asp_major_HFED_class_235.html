Children's Clothing
CHILD CLOTH
CLASS CODE: HFED 235 CREDITS:  2 FEE:  $10.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Selection, design and construction of children's clothing using the serger and sewing machine. Projects include bib, booties, sleeper, t-shirt, pants, blessing dress, coat, and mittens. No sewing experience required. Fee for materials is approximate.
DESCRIPTION: Selection, design and construction of children's clothing using the serger and sewing machine. Projects include bib, booties, sleeper, t-shirt, pants, blessing dress, coat, and mittens. No sewing experience required. Materials approximately $50. Lab Fee: $10
TOPICS: Serger, threading serger, serger tension, sewing machine, fabrics, patterns, cutting out, construction of t-shirt, bib, pants, booties, coat, mittens, dress, sleepers
OBJECTIVES: The student will be able to:
1. Thread and adjust the tension of the serger to create a balanced stitch.
2. Construct the following articles of children's clothing: bib, t-shirt, pants, booties, sleepers, coat, mittens and blessing dress.
3. Use quick methods of sewing construction without minimizing the quality of the finished product.
4. Enjoy the art of sewing while developing talents.
REQUIREMENTS: Construct t-shirt, bib, pants, booties, sleepers, coat, mittens, blessing dress
