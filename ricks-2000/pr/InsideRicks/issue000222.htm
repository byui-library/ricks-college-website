DAVID O. MCKAY LIBRARY

Work will begin in March on a major renovation to the David O. McKay Library. Ormond Builders of Idaho Falls submitted the lowest bid and is being awarded the contract, says Chuck Frost, director of Physical Plant. The first phase will involve renovation of the vacant Administration Building, which is attached to the present library. When complete, this will become the additional east wing of the library. Upon completion of the 47,000 square foot east wing in December 2000, work will begin on remodeling of the third floor of the main library. During the summer of 2001, more remodeling will be done on the first and second floors of the main library. The entire library project should be completed by August 24, 2001, for the beginning of school.

The library expansion was made possible by the freeing up of administrative space following completion of the new Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building last year. The project involves a new heating, ventilation and air conditioning system, new electrical system, new lighting and new floor. There will also be a major expansion of the Learning Assistance Labs. Building Chairman Max Checketts says the library expansion will boost the amount of seating for students from about 700 to 1,100-1,200 in the library when it is completed. Group study rooms will also increase from about four at present time to about 20. The library will also feature increased computer access. Frost explains, "This is intended to be a major support to require each student at Ricks to take an Internet class. We are moving the college from a paper-based library to an electronic-based library."

When work begins on the third floor of the existing library in the winter semester of 2001, many of the functions will temporarily be moved to the east wing. More computers and study areas will be added on the third floor along with added space for multi-media work. Work on the first and second floors of the main library will involve limited remodeling, some new light fixtures and some new carpet. Design work for the project is being done by the architectural firm of Nielsen Bodily & Associates of Idaho Falls.

WRESTLERS PLACE THIRD IN NJCAA

The Ricks College wrestling team placed third overall in the National Junior College Athletic Association tournament held in Rochester, Minn., last weekend. All five of the Ricks finalists earned all-American status by finishing in the top eight. James Huml had five first-period pins as he repeated his standing as heavy weight champion and was named the outstanding wrestler of the tournament. Overall first place went to Neosho, Kan., with 142.5 points. Iowa Central took second with 115 points. Ricks racked up 93 points for third place. Completing the list of the top five schools were Lassen, Calif, with 82.5 and Lincoln, Neb., with 79.

RICKS COLLEGE PRESENTS MEN'S WEEK

"Arise . . . my sons and be men" is the theme of Ricks College Men's Week that takes place February 22-27. ASRC Programs Vice President Melissa Merrill says, "This will be a foundation for the Men's Week, and we want to get everyone involved." There are daily noon activities in Manwaring Center. A free talent show will be Thursday at 7:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium. The Men's Week concert will begin at 7:30 p.m. featuring the Hughes Brothers. Saturday in the Hart Gymnasium will feature three-on-three basketball championships at 7 p.m., arm wrestling and slam-dunk championships at 7:30 p.m., and a dance at 8:30 p.m. The week will conclude with a fireside featuring Elder L. Aldin Porter, member of the Presidency of the Seventy, on Sunday, February 27, at 7 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium.

VISITORS WELCOME TO CAMPUS

Many of our alumni and friends welcome the opportunity to come to Ricks College for a visit. For those who now have children of their own who now attending Ricks as students, Women's Week is an excellent opportunity to build memories together. Others find the annual Ricks College Education Week a preferred choice.

With increased enrollment, there is an obvious rise in the number of students who will host parents for Women's Week March 16-19. Over two thousand visitors are expected. Tickets for many Women's Week activities at Ricks College will go on sale Monday, February 28, at 7 a.m. MST. Tickets are limited and will be available over the Internet at http://www.byui.edu/tickets, by phone at (208) 356-2230, or at the Ticket Office in the Manwaring Center. For more specific information, see the Ricks College home page at http://www.byui.edu/WomensWeek. Women's Week is an activity coordinated by the Associated Women Students of Ricks College.

Continuing Education is now accepting registration for Education Week from June 1-3. Education Week has daily devotionals, classes for youth, and evening events. Over 60 presenters will present more than 300 classes on topics including gospel, family, self-improvement, homemaking, emergency preparedness, fine arts, and others. Education Week is calendared to take advantage of the open days between the first and second summer terms. More details are available at http://www.byui.edu/educationweek.
