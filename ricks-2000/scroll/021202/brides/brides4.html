Three Places to Register

Online: Registering online is convenient for both the couple and the guest. Almost every store allows online registration. Easy access to the registry list is given to the guests who may not live near the registered store.

Department Stores: Gifts here are limited because most department stores are specialized. Think about those who will be buying gifts and their financial situation because gifts are more expensive in department stores. If the couple knows exactly what they want and the store offers it, then is the ideal registry.

Retail Stores: These stores offer the most variety. Most items from cookware to hardware tools can be found here. Price ranges are also diverse in these stores. Registering here leaves more options open for those buying gifts.
