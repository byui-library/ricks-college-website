COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 250
Title: Space Planning III–Residential Design Credit: 3
Text: Kitchen Basics, NKBA
         Bathroom Basics, NKBA

Course Objectives:
1. To familiarize students with NKBA instructional materials for both kitchens and baths.
 2. To develop fundamental competency in conceptualizing, problem solving and space planning as related to kitchens and baths, emphasizing NKBA’s 40 Guidelines to Kitchen Planning and 41 Guidelines to Bathroom Planning.
 3. To familiarize students with construction, plumbing and mechanical standards for kitchens and baths.
4. To introduce the student to cabinet construction, installation and specification.
 5. To introduce students to appliances, construction materials and interior finishes for the kitchen and bath.
 6.  To provide opportunities for individual problem solving, group interaction, and a “real world” client opportunity.

Description:
Studio exercises in the planning, design and layout for kitchen and bath.  Prepares the student for NKBA (National Kitchen and Bath Association) certification exams.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Short outlines of each reading assignment from course text(s).
2. Multiple short term assignments to facilitate integration of principle application.
3. Three major projects with oral presentations, including a “real-life” kitchen design experience with a local “client”.
4. Oral presentation to the class of selected kitchen and bath guidelines with appropriate visuals and handouts.
5. Compilation of an Idea File (morgue) of kitchen and bath ideas and information.
