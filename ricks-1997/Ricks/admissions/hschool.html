Question: 

What are the admissions requirements for home schooled students?

 

Answer: 

Home schooled students must meet the same high standards of church and seminary activity.  Home schooled students (except American School, IL) must submit GED scores, in addition to ACT/SAT scores.  Ricks has a GED minimum of 50 with no sub-score below a 45 in order to considered for admission.  Home schooled students must also apply with their age group.  Typically this means at or near their 18th birthday.
