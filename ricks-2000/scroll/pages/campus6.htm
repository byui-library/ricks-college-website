ONLINE RESEARCH SITE PROVIDES SOURCES FOR RESEARCH PAPERS

by BONNIE PONTIUS
scroll staff

Students who feel as though they're being drowned by research papers may now have a life jacket to hang on to.

Questia, an online subscription-based site, offers close to 50,000 books and journals for students to use over the Internet. Questia's goal is to have 250,000 books and journals by 2003.

Students can search through thousands of literary books and journals for information according to topic, author or title. The full text can be viewed and includes automatic footnotes and bibliography created in APA or MLA format.

"We already have thousands of subscribers with tens of thousands enrolled in one of our trial programs. ... We hope to have hundreds of thousands using the service each semester," Page Janco, a Questia representative, said.

Questia has deals with over 200 publishers based on a pay-per-view model.

"This benefits publishers by being able to collect royalties often lost from students copying materials at the traditional library," Janco said. "They also get to post the full texts of their backlist and out-of-print titles as a way of generating additional revenue."

Questia offers different subscription deals depending on need. They offer a yearly subscription, a monthly subscription and a 48-hour subscription for those last-minute term papers.

For a free 24-hour trial visit or more information, visit www.questia.com.
