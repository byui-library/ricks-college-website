ORIENTATION FOR WINTER SEMESTER

About 9,000 students are expected to begin classes at Ricks College in Rexburg when the winter semester classes officially begin Tuesday, Jan. 9. Most returning students are expected to arrive in Rexburg during the weekend or on Monday, and many activities are planned in an effort to orient the new students with the campus.

New student orientation activities begin Saturday, says Allen Jones, the new student orientation coordinator. "We want students to feel more welcomed and comfortable at Ricks and to have parents get a better feel for campus and what their son or daughter will experience here," he says.

Saturday's parent orientation will include the President's Reception from 10 to noon in the Manwaring Center East Ballroom, parent and student information booths from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. in the Manwaring Center West Lounge and a parent orientation assembly at noon. A new student barbecue will be held in the Hart Fieldhouse from 3:30-5:30 p.m. with new student commencement in the Taylor Chapel from 6-7 p.m. A dance will be held from 8:30-11:30 p.m. in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom.

Monday's new student orientation activities include a morningside from 8-9 a.m. in the Taylor Chapel, breakfast and campus tours from 9-9:45 a.m., academic department orientations from 10 a.m. to noon, a service project in the Taylor Chapel and Hart Fieldhouse from 2-4:30 p.m. and R Team breakout discussions from 4:30-5 p.m.

Classes will begin Tuesday morning. Tuesday's opening devotional by President David A. and Susan Bednar will be at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. It also will be carried live on KRIC-FM and rebroadcast at 8:30 p.m.

A new student concert is set for Friday, January 12, from 7:30-9 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium featuring the male a capella group InsideOut and a dance will follow. Cost is $4 for Ricks students and $5 for the general public.

 

INSIDEOUT TO BRING A CAPELLA TO RICKS

A six-man, all-vocal group with roots in the Upper Snake River Valley will perform at Ricks College on Friday, Jan. 12, at 7:30 p.m. InsideOut, with three members who graduated from Ricks, will perform in the Hart Auditorium as part of new student orientation activities. Tickets are $4 for Ricks students and $5 for the general public. They may be purchased from the Ricks College Ticket Office, by calling (208) 356-2230 or on Internet at www.byui.edu/tickets. They also will be available at the door.

Members of InsideOut are Kimball Brown, Ben Haacke and Layne Haacke, who grew up in Sugar City and graduated from Ricks, and Jason Smith from Idaho Falls. Other members are Ryan Hinton of Mesa, Ariz., and Paul Hatch of Bountiful, Utah.

The group was formed in the winter of 1997. It started as a hobby and they performed for friends and the BYU A Capella Club. The group continued to improve and was invited to perform around the BYU campus. As word got out, they began to perform more and more, with concerts throughout Utah, Arizona, Idaho and California.

With their musical capability, vocal percussion and invigorating performance, InsideOut has produced show that entertains both small and large audiences of all ages. Their show consists of original music and cover tunes, many of which can be found on their three CDs, "Innocence" (1999), "Experience" (2000) and "Reverence" (2000). The group has performed numerous times at universities, community events and other venues, including appearances on local network television channels in Arizona, Utah and Idaho, as well as a national cable television appearance.

 

RICKS WINTER ENTERTAINMENT TO RANGE FROM CLASSICAL TO POP

Winter entertainment at Ricks College will range from classical to country and jazz to pop. It includes such names as the Osmond Brothers from Branson, Mo., the Beatles group known as 1964, jazz singer Kevin Mahogany and Emmy-winning actors Roscoe Lee Browne and Anthony Zerbe. The Center Stage Entertainment Series offers something for just about everyone. "Our intent is to bring a variety of very fine, nationally recognized entertainment to campus for both our students and the community at large," says Don Sparhawk, who arranges the series.

Tickets for the first three events go on sale Jan 8. Other tickets will go on sale about three weeks before the event. For more information, call the Ricks Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230. All shows will begin at 7:30 p.m.

The series begins with The American String Quartet from New York City in the Barrus Concert Hall on Wednesday and Thursday, Jan. 17 and 18 with tickets costing $6.

Relive the music of Sixties as the group known as 1964, the No. 1 Beatles show in the world, takes to the stage in the Hart Auditorium on Friday, Jan. 26. Cost for the concert and dance is $8 and $6. To make the evening complete, a British dinner for an extra $10 will be served before the show in the Manwaring Center.

A Shakespearean tale of jealousy, betrayal and repentance will open the winter theater series. The Ricks College Theatre Department will present "The Winter's Tale" in the Kirkham Arena Theater Jan. 18-20, 24-27, 31, Feb. 1-3. Tickets are $6.
