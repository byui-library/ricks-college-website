COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 288
Title: Field Trip Credit: 1-2
Text: None

Course Objectives:
 1. To provide exposure for students to museums, historical and contemporary architecture and furnishings, surface ornamentation, furniture design, and other interior design oriented subjects.
2. To stimulate global understanding of differing cultures through foreign travel.
3. To expose students to architectural building materials and interior finish materials in actual use.
4. To expose students to professional design studios and business procedures.

Description:
Trips taken to enrich the Interior Design Program and background of student.  Credit is dependent upon the length and scope of travel study: from one week to three weeks, depending on destination(s).  Travel may include major market cities, European and other foreign travel.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. A summary paper or specific project binder may be required at the conclusion of the trip as determined by the field trip emphasis(es) and/or interior design course offerings..
