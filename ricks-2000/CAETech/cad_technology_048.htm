Associate in Computer-Aided Design Technology (048)

The major in CAD/CAM Technology, (048), involves graduates in the process of translating creative ideas and concepts into models, computer simulations, or working drawings used to produce new machines or products. Emphasis is placed on traditional design concepts coupled with strong Computer-Aided Design (CAD) skills. The curriculum includes Computer-Aided Design (CAD), Solids Modeling, Computer Rendering and Animation, CAD customization, Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM), and Computer Numerical Control (CNC). This program is designed for immediate employment, as well as transfer to a technology program at a four year university.
