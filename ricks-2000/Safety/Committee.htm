Ricks College Safety Committee

Ricks College is committed to providing a safe work place for all students, staff, faculty, and administrators.  The Ricks College Safety Committee meets regulary to discuss campus safety issues and concerns as well as recommend and develop institutional campus safety policies to the President's Council.

The following list of individuals serve on the Campus Safety Committee.  Please forward any safety concerns and/or issues you may have to your respective departmental representative.
