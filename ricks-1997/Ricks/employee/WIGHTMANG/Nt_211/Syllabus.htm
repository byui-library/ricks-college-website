Course Objective:
1. TAKE RESPONSIBILITY FOR YOUR OWN LEARNING by:
            * abiding the attendance and punctuality requirements established
            * completing assignments, exercises, and tests as required ON TIME.
            * intensively studying from the New Testament  in and out of class
            * being a positive influence on the learning environment which includes:
                        - actively participating in classroom discussions
                        - living life so as to not offend the presence of the spirit (honor code and dress standards)

Required Materials:
    All four standard works of LDS footnoted scriptures.

Grading: (520 points possible)
As this is an academic course for 2 credits, you must be graded on your efforts and your mastery of the content. An "A" will not be given simply because someone is "nice" or has a testimony of the gospel. These two things are of most importance for you but they do not meet the academic standard to receive college credit. Therefore, the "A" grade will be reserved for those who excel far above the average level to make this class more meaningful not only for themselves, but for others. The standard is as follows:

Scripture Reading Summaries --- 43% of overall grade
You will be required to submit a typewritten reading summary at the beginning of each scripture block studied. You can find the guidelines to and an example of the summary on the web page under "class information". There will be 21 summaries and one quiz (each worth 10 points) and they will be due at the beginning of class.

Attendance/Participation --- 19% of overall grade
Not only are you expected to attend, you are expected to be a positive influence on the learning environment through your participation in classroom discussions. My definition of participation is remaining alert and attentive by taking notes, and asking a question or making a comment now and then. If you are shy, it is time to become comfortable being an active participant in society. There are a possible 100 points for attendance and participation.

You will be allowed three excused absences this semester which means you either call or email to let me know why you were gone.   I will determine if the absence is excused, not you.  Use these wisely as each absence thereafter will result in the loss of 10 points no matter what the excuse!   Any un-excused absence results in the loss of 10 points.  Those who choose to attend but not participate will also lose 10 points.
I reserve the right in the end to remove points for semester long silence. I get to know those who participate. If I don't know you by the end of the semester, you will most likely lose points.

Content Mastery --- 19% of overall grade
You are expected to try and master the material you will learn this semester. I feel this is best accomplished through note taking. Take good notes while in class, then learn how to transcribe those notes into your scriptures. Come test time, you will most likely be allowed to use your notes and scriptures on the test. There will only be a final (see end notes) worth 100 pts.

Application Essays --- 19% of overall grade
Two times this semester, you will be required to take something you have learned in class and apply it in your life. Guidelines for the essay are also found on the web page under "class information".  Each essay will be worth 50 points.
