How can I become more active throughout my day?

Take advantage of any opportunity to get up and move around.

  • Use the stairs- up and down
  • Park a few blocks away from the office or store and walk the rest of the way. If you ride public transportation get off a stop or two before and walk the rest of the way.
  • Take an exercise break- get up and stretch, walk around and give your muscles and mind a chance to relax.
  • Instead of eating that extra snack take a stroll around the neighborhood.
