    November 11, 2000   Ricks defeated by CSI 15-8, 5-15, 6-15, 6-15 (Reg 18 Tournament, North Division Final)
    With a trip to the NJCAA tournament on the line, Ricks came out red hot in game one, with a .378 attack percentage.  But the Lady Vikings faded fast, dropping to a -.065 in game two, and -.235 in game four.  Malia Koelliker was consistent at the net with 13 kills, and Jessica Jackson added 9 as the season ends for Ricks.

    November 10, 2000   Ricks defeated North Idaho 15-2, 15-1, 15-6 (Reg 18 Tournament)
    Ricks attack percentage was .333 in the first game, .478 in the second.  The Lady Vikings were balanced in the stats with no one player standing out.

    November 4, 2000  Ricks defeated by Utah Valley 10-15, 12-15, 11-15
    Utah Valley continued to play extremely well.  Ricks played strong in all three games but could not get past the red hot Lady Wolverines.  Brittany Ahlstrom and Jessica Jackson each had 11 kills.  Trish Pettingill had 43 assists.  Ahlstrom, Pettingill, and Sahara Castillo all had double digit digs in the match.

    November 3, 2000  Ricks defeats Salt Lake CC  15-9, 15-3, 15-11
    Brittany Ahlstrohm led the Lady Vikings, hitting for .444 on 16 kills.  Jessica Jackson had 12 kills and Malia Koelliker added 10.  Trish Pettingill  totaled 51 assists.  Ashleyn Sannar had ten digs. 

    October 26, 2000 Ricks defeated Snow 15-5, 15-10, 10-15, 15-1
    Ricks got off to a good start. Brittany Ahlstrom was in form with 12 kills. The defense was superb with 51 digs, ten each from Malia Koelliker and Trish Pettingill.

    October 27, 2000 Ricks defeated Dixie State 15-5, 15-8, 5-15, 15-8
    Malia Koelliker had another good night with 12 kills and four digs. Brittany Ahlstrom had 8 kills and 22 digs. Sahara Castillo added to the defensive show with 13 digs.

    October 28, 2000 Ricks defeated Eastern Utah 15-6, 15-11, 15-6
    Sahara Castillo led the offense with 11 kills. Ricks totaled 62 digs in the game. Jodi Ethington had 3 aces.

    October 21, 2000   Ricks defeated by CSI  7-15, 9-15, 15-12, 15-11, 9-15
    Southern Idaho got off to a quick start with wins in the first two games.  After the break, Ricks came from behind to win game three, then jumped out to a 9-2 lead in game four.  CSI battled back to an 11-11 tie but went down in flames as Jodi Ethington closed the game with four service aces.  Ricks and CSI stayed even in rally scoring in the early going of the final game, but could not control the net as CSI pulled away for the win.  Stephanie Martin led CSI with 20 kills.  Jessica Jackson led Ricks with 15 kills.  Trish Pettingill had 50 assists.  Ethington totaled six aces.

    October 7 Ricks loses at Southern Idaho 11-15, 4-15, 12-15
    Ricks College's seven game winning streak in SWAC play ended Saturday night at CSI. Brittany Ahlstrom and Jessica Jackson recorded 10 kills each, Trish Pettingill had 42 assists, while Malia Koelliker had 10 digs. CSI attack percentage was at .447 in game two.

    October 6 Ricks defeats North Idaho 15-3, 15-3, 15-12
    The Lady Vikings remained unbeaten in conference play with a three game sweep over NIC. Evonne Goodman led the Ricks attack with 11 kills and Amy Welcker added 8. Trish Pettingill had 28 assists and seven digs. Ricks hit .476 of their kill atempts in game two.

    September 30  Ricks over Snow 15-9, 15-5, 15-10
    Ricks completed a three game sweep at home with a 3-0 win over Snow.  Ahlstrom and Jackson led at the net with Amy Welcker coming off the bench for five big kills.   Pettingill had 29 assists.  Jodi Ethington and Malia Koelliker were big on defense with seven digs each.

    September 29  Ricks over Dixie 15-3, 15-13, 15-2
    Ricks team attack percentage was at .462 in game one to Dixie's  -.333.   Ricks was at .400 in game three while Dixie was again at a negative.  Dixie led in game two but couldn't hold it.  Brittany Ahlstrom and Jessica Jackson led at the net, Trish Pettingill had 24 assists, and Sahara Castillo had 13 digs.

    September 28  Ricks over CEU  15-1, 15-8, 15-8
    Ricks rolled quickly through the first game with a .312 attack percentage to CEU's -.034.  CEU  totaled 9 service errors on the night, each coming at critical times.  Ricks had 5 service aces.

    Sept 21-23 at Valley of the Sun Tournament
    Twenty six teams competed at the tournament.  Ricks won six and lost three, all three losses to ranked teams.  The Vikings went down to top ranked and undefeated Miami-Dade 8-15, 10-15, and also lost to Glendale, AZ, and Golden West of California.   Ricks beat nationally ranked Arizona Western.

    Sept 16 vs Salt Lake CC
    Ricks College made quick work of the Lady Bruins with a 3-0 win, scores 15-4, 15-6, 15-7.  Brittany Ahlstrom had twelve kills, Jessica Jackson added 10.  Trish Pettingill had 30 assists.  The Lady Vikings totaled 38 digs.

    Sept 15 vs Westminster College
    Ricks College reserves carried the load against the Griffins of Westminster College, with the Lady Vikings winning in three, 15-7, 15-5, 15-2.  Ashelyn Sannar led a balanced attack with 7 kills.

    Sept 14 vs Utah Valley
    Ricks opened the home part of its schedule with a win over Utah Valley, 16-14, 15-13, 12-15, and 15-13.  Ricks ended the night with  five players in double figures for kills, led by Brittany Ahlstrom with 19.    Trish Pettingill had 61 assists.  Sahara Castillo had 16 digs.  UVSC was led by Fran Pozzi's 26 kills.   Ricks trailed 8-13 in the final game.  Amy Welcker came off the bench to spark the Lady Vikings.

    Sept 7 at North Idaho  -  Sept 8 at Spokane CC(non-conference)
    SWAC PLAY OPENS
    Ricks opened league play with a 3-0 win over North Idaho.  Scores were 15-0, 15-6, 15-3.  The Lady Vikings had an impressive attack percentage of .500 in the first game and .393 in the final.  Brittany Ahlstrom recorded 11 kills and Trish Pettingill had 15 assists.  Pettingill also had 4 service aces.  Jessica Jackson had 3 blocks.  Ricks followed with a 3-0 win at Spokane College the next night.


    UVSC INVITATIONAL, Orem, UT
    The Lady Vikings continue to play outstanding volleyball.  Ricks fought through the first two days of competition with eight consecutive wins, including stunning victories over host Utah Valley, followed by an upset of powerful Southern Idaho.   Ricks moved into Saturday's championship round as the top seed, and opened with a semi-final, straight set win over Spokane CC.  The Lady Vikings then met Southern Idaho and lost in five grueling games to finish in second place.

    CSI INVITATIONAL at Twin Falls, ID
    Ricks College opened the season with six wins in seven tries at the Southern Idaho Invitational in Twin Falls.  The Lady Vikings beat Casper College 3-0, Salt Lake CC 3-0, Sheridan College 3-0, and upset Utah Valley 3-0.  Ricks defeated Kansas City CC 3-1 and Nortwest College 3-2.  The Vikings only loss was to national power CSI 1-3.
