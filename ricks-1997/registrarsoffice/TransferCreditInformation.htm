Procedures For Transfer Credit

 

 

BYU-Idaho accepts all college-level credits from accredited colleges and universities.  If a particular class is not available here it will be counted as an elective.  Students may petition a specific department for the acceptance of credit for certain classes.

 

 

For information regarding Petition Processes, Click Here.

 

 

 

 

College-Level Examination Program
National CLEP

1.  Students may receive University credit for National CLEP exams as specified on the approved list distributed by the Registrar’s Office. Credit will be posted on transcript by Department, Course Number, Course Title, Credits Earned, and GE Credit Assigned.  No letter grade will be assigned.  All classes will be noted as passing (“P” grade).

2.  Acceptance of National CLEP general examination and subject examination will be determined by Academic Council upon the recommendation of departments. Changes in the awarding of National CLEP credit will occur by department recommendation and Academic Council approval.

3.  The Registrar's Office will administer the National CLEP policy.
