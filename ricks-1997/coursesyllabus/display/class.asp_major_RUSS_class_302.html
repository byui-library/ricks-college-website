Third-Year Russian part II
3RD YR RUSS II
CLASS CODE: RUSS 302 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Continuation of Russian 301.
DESCRIPTION: This course will continue in presenting the Russian language to the student with a two-year background in university Russian or its equivalent. Focus in class will be placed on an in-depth review of basic grammar concepts including verb conjugation and aspect and grammatical cases. Homework will mainly consist of written exercises about selected grammar topics.
TOPICS: Basic speaking, listening, writing, and reading skills and knowledge of the grammar to enable the student to participate in an in-depth grammar review throughout the semester. Emphasis will also be placed on Russian word formation, including roots and prefixes.
OBJECTIVES: By the end of the semester students will be able to: 1)More fully understand the concepts of Russian grammar, especially as pertaining to grammatical cases, verbs, and participles; 2) Increase their understanding of Russian word formation and knowledge of roots; 3)Write grammatically correct essays about certain topics; and 4)Read expository and literary excerpts from real Russian sources.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, written homework (using textbook and handouts), quizzes, tests, and final exam.
PREREQUISITES: Russian 301 with a grade of "C" or better or consent of instructor.
