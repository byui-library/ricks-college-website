Inspiration for Student Legacy Fund: 
The Perpetual Emigration Fund, Garden Grove, and Mt. Pisgah

While LDS pioneer history is filled with situations in which early saints helped one another through their mutual hardships, three examples became the inspiration for the name and purpose of the Student Legacy Fund—the Perpetual Emigration Fund, Garden Grove, and Mt. Pisgah. All three were organized by the prophet Brigham Young in an effort to help the saints help each other in their migration to their promised land of Zion in the Salt Lake valley.

Today, enrolled students at Ricks College participate in SLF by donating what they can to help fellow students deal with the many costs of going to college. They may never know the names of those they have helped, but as the recipients of these blessings turn back and give so that others may attend Ricks, the charitable experience of sharing in Christ’s love becomes perpetuated.
