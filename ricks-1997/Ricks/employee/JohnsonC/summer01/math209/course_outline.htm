Course Outline
Math 209 Section 401 – Elementary Calculus
First Summer Term 2001

Course Requirements, Grading and Evaluation:   The grade earned will be computed using the following scales and percentages.
 

Homework 15%:  Homework will be collected at the beginning of class the day after each section is finished.  You will grade your own homework in class based on the honor code.  Each correct problem is worth 2 points.  Each honestly attempted incorrect problem is worth 1 point.  Students are encouraged to collaborate on homework, but plagiarism is forbidden.  Homework appearance must be neat and professional.  Messy or unstapled papers and papers written in ink or torn from a notebook will not be accepted.  Late papers will not be accepted, with one exception.  One assignment may be submitted up to one week late without penalty.  The late assignment must be stapled to the original "Late Assignment Slip" distributed in class.

Quizzes 80%:  There will be five equally-weighted quizzes.  The quizzes will be administered in the classroom.  Unless prior arrangements have been made, missed quizzes will result in a score of zero.

Character Development 5%:  This is a self-evaluation of your progress towards a challenging daily personal goal.  You receive one point for every day in which you achieve your goal.  Submit your score (out of 34) on May 5.  Character development scores may not be submitted late.
