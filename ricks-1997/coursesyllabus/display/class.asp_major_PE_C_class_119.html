Racquetball Intermediate
INTER RCQTBLL
CLASS CODE: PE*C 119 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An advanced course in the game of racquetball providing instruction in advanced skills and strategies. Higher level competition than beginning racquetball classes.
DESCRIPTION: An advanced course in the game of racquetball providing instruction in advanced skills and strategies. Higher level competition than beginning racquetball classes.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. To provide advanced instruction and skill development in techniques and strategies.
2. To provide a consistently higher level of competition than is found in beginning racquetball classes.
3. To learn social etiquette in competitive settings and positive interaction.
4. Develop emotional control to stay calm and efficient while competing.
5. Develop knowledge of rules and terminology of the game.
6. Develop physical skills to be able to enjoy racquetball as a lifetime activity providing cardiovascular benefits.
7. Develop skills to teach different shots, rebounds, serves, and strokes.
8. Ability to organize a tournament.
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Ability to organize a tournament.
2. Exhibit knowledge and skill of rules and fundamentals of softball
3. Attend class and participate.
4. BYU-I clothing required.
5. Racquetball goggles.
PREREQUISITES: PEC 117 or have played racquetball enough to know the basic rules, strategies, and fundamentals of beginning racquetball.
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences allowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
5. P.E. clothing fee: $4.
6. Students must provide their own goggles.
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1991
