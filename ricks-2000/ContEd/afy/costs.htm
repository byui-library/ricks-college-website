COSTS & CHARGES

COST

The cost for 2002 AFY sessions is $305. This includes tuition fees, recreational activities, t-shirt, food, housing, and transportation to and from the week's activities. 

REFUND POLICY

No refunds will be granted after a session begins.

There will be a $25 processing fee for each

refund.  A $100 processing fee will be withheld

from refunds made within two weeks of the start

of the program.

SESSION CHANGE FEE

 

There will be a $25 fee assessed for

changing sessions or programs
