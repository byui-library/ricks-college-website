When are Tryouts?  
Return to top Tryouts are the first two days of school. For fall season 2001 that translates to Wednesday, Sept 5th and Thursday, Sept 6th. Tryouts begin at 5 p.m.
Where are Tryouts?  
Return to top Tryouts are held on the east intramural soccer field for the men and the west intramural soccer field for the women. The intramural fields are located south of campus on 1st West Street. They are the only fields on campus with soccer goals on them. If you can not find the fields, call Peter Stilling at 208-356-2881 (Office), 208-359-9049 (Home), or 208-313-0945 (Cell). You may email him at stillingp@byui.edu  
What is required of me at Fall Tryouts?  
Return to top You will be required to run 2.2 miles on the road.  If you finish behind Coach Stilling then you are invited to come back in the Spring and tryout again.  If you can run 2.2 miles on a track in less than 14 minutes then you will have little to worry about.  If your time is 14-16 minutes then you may want to increase your training regimen.  If your time is 16 minutes or greater, then you had better start training.  After the run, the remaining candidates will then be split into several small teams of 4 v 4 and 5 v 5 and we will play small sided games.  You will be evaluated on your technical, tactical, physical, and social abilities.  You will be further evaluated on your communications, leadership, ability to play both offense and defense, and your desire.
How much time or commitment must I give?
Return to top The club soccer team is very competitive.  As a result of this, the team has extremely high demands of your personal time.
Practices will be held Monday through Thursday, from 5:00 p.m. - 6:30 p.m. (Sometimes until 7 p.m.), Conditioning will be held Monday through Wednesday, 6:00 a.m. - 6:45 a.m. Games will be every Friday evening and every Saturday afternoon. There is 14 regular season matches (7 home, 7 away), so you can count on missing some school on Fridays. The Friday's missed will be excused absences, so you will be allowed to make-up missed assignments, etc.
How much will it cost to play?  
Return to top This varies from season to season based on uniforms, travel, etc. However, each player can count on paying somewhere from $175 to $300 for a season. This will cover your uniform (which you keep), travel, lodging, referees, and food. It will get you the full regular season of 14 games and the post season tournament (3 game minimum).
Does the announcement of Ricks College turning into BYU-Idaho affect soccer?  
Return to top Unknown at this time.  The answer to this question changes quite frequently.  Depending on whom is asked and when they are asked, the answer could be Yes or No.  Regardless of what BYU-Idaho decides to do with soccer, the program will live on with or without the support of the institution.  If I have to run the program privately, legal steps have already been investigated that will allow us to do this.  As long as I am employed here, soccer will continue to be played here.

How competitive are the teams?  
Return to top Don't let the classification of being "Just a Club" fool you. The teams are very competitive and very good. We play tough competition and fair very well against our opponents. The great thing about club soccer is that the athletes play for the love of the game and not for the sake of scholarship. They play because they WANT to play, not because it is their only way through college. In many respects this is what a soccer player should be, someone who CARES about the game simply because the game exists.
Does BYU-Idaho compete in an organized league?  
Yes. The league is called The ICSA (Intermountain Collegiate Soccer Association). Currently it has 16 teams in the league that are divided into 2 conferences. Ricks College plays in the southern conference which is comprised of the 8 teams above. The northern conference is made up of U of Montana, Montana State, Gonzaga B Team, U of Idaho, Washington State U, Walla Walla College, Eastern Washington U, and Flathead Valley CC.

Additionally, the league competes at the National Level in an organization called NIRSA which hosts the NIRSA National Soccer Tournament each year for College Club Soccer. Just as FYI, there is another fraction of college club soccer in the United States called The NACS. They are a smaller organization than NIRSA, yet still exist just the same.
Are scholarships available to play soccer at BYU-Idaho?  
Unfortunately not.  None of the club teams at BYU-Idaho have scholarships available.  There is however opportunity for some financial aid.  The money is very limited and would only cover the cost of your season; such as uniform, food, and travel.  There would be no assistance with tuition, books, lab fees, or campus housing.  Please ask Coach Stilling at stillingp@byui.edu for more information about soccer financial aid.
