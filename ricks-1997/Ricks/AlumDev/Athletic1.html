Athletic Hall Of Fame 
Georgia Johnson Palmer

Running for herself, or as part of a relay team, Georgia knows her way around the track. As a freshman in the spring of 1986, she took All-American honors with a fifth-place finish nationally in the 400-meters. Earlier she had won the regional title in the 400-meters in a school record-setting 58.02 seconds. She also won the 200-meters at the regionals and was a member of the winning 1,600-meter relay team. As a sophomore she repeated for All-American honors placing second nationally in the 3,200-meter relay, running the anchor leg, placing third in the 800-meters and taking fifth as a member of the 1,600-meter relay team. In the regionals, Georgia was a member of the championship 3,200 and 1,600-meter relay teams, the latter covering the course in a school record 3:57.33, and winning the 800-meters. During her sophomore season she also won a 400-meter race at a meet in Provo, Utah, in 58.97.

Following graduation from BYU, Georgia taught elementary education in Utah for seven years but is now a fulltime mother and homemaker in Pleasant Grove, Utah.

She is married to Kirt Palmer, a former wrestler at Ricks in 1982-83 and 1985-86. Kirt owns a stucco company. She says she met Kirt in Brother Jerry Roundy's 'Book of Mormon' class and spent a lot of time in the Hart Building together. "I always did like those wrestlers," she says.

The Palmers have four children: Madison is 9, McKell is 7, Morgan is 4 and Marc is about 20 months old.

As a member of the Manila 4th Ward, Georgia is a Teacher Development Instructor, Primary Achievement Day Leader, and Visiting Teacher in the Relief Society.

She continues to run in local road races and says she is "reluctantly becoming a distance runner." She trains for and competes in half and full marathons. She also enjoys spending time with her family, playing outdoors and helping on her parents' cattle ranch.
