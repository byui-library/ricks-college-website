CONTINUING ECCLESIASTICAL ENDORSEMENT

I. Annual Ecclesiastical Endorsement

A. General

The continued increase in enrollment applications and the implementation of a ceiling on numbers of students being accepted at Ricks College has resulted in an increased focus on student worthiness by the college through the appropriate ecclesiastical leader. At the direction of the Ricks College Board of Trustees, all students attending Ricks College should be interviewed at least once each semester by their ecclesiastical leader to verify their current worthiness and their continued commitment to live the Honor Code and dress and grooming standards. For LDS students this would include their commitment to do their duty in the Church by attending church meetings and abiding by the standards of the Church. LDS students who are not active in the Church, or who do not do their duty in the Church, or who do not keep their commitment to the Honor Code may place their ecclesiastical endorsement to attend Ricks in jeopardy. For non-LDS students the review would focus on their continued commitment to live the Honor Code and dress and grooming standards.

B. Procedure

Every student currently registered at Ricks College and planning to continue with his/her studies beyond the end of the Winter Semester must obtain a continuing ecclesiastical endorsement prior to being permitted to register for any upcoming term or semester. LDS students receive their endorsement from the bishop of the ward they reside in during the winter semester. Non-LDS students may receive their endorsement from the ecclesiastical leader of their faith, an LDS bishop where they reside, or from the Dean of Students.

Endorsement forms will be made available to bishops of campus wards. Students who reside at home or attend church in other than campus wards can obtain a form at the Dean of Students Office.

Endorsements will not be accepted over the phone, and it is the student's responsibility to contact his/her bishop and see that the endorsement is completed. Bishops are asked to submit the completed endorsements to the Dean of Students Office in a timely manner so that students can register.

II. Denial or Withdrawal of Continuing Ecclesiastical Endorsement

A. Certification

The continuing ecclesiastical endorsement is a certification that LDS students are abiding by the Honor Code, the dress and grooming standards, and fulfilling their duty in the Church by attending Church meetings and abiding by the rules and standards of the Church. For non-LDS students, the endorsement certifies that students are abiding by the Honor Code and the dress and grooming standards.

B. Endorsement

An endorsing officer may decline to endorse a student or, once endorsed, may withdraw the endorsement at anytime upon determining that the conditions for the endorsement have not been met. When an endorsement is withdrawn, the affected student will be required to withdraw from the college subject to the student's and college's right to administrative review.

C. Withdrawal of Endorsement

In the event that an endorsement is withdrawn by an LDS bishop, the bishop should advise the student and notify the Dean of Students Office of the action. The decision of a bishop to withdraw a student endorsement may be reviewed by the applicable stake president.

In the event the stake president's decision is adverse to the student, or when the endorsement is withdrawn originally by the stake president, by a non-LDS ecclesiastical officer, or a representative of the Dean's Office, a request for administrative review may be made to the Dean of Students.

D. Grounds for Administrative Review

There are two grounds for administrative review of the denial or withdrawal of a continuing ecclesiastical endorsement:

1. The student claims proof of compliance with the applicable standard of conduct in question.

2. The student believes there exists mitigating factors which, in fairness, should allow for continued enrollment at the college in the absence of a continuing ecclesiastical endorsement, i.e., close to the end of the semester or very near completion of an academic program.

E. Review Process

Should a request for administrative review be presented to the Dean of Students, it will be processed in the following manner:

1. The student will be notified in writing by the Dean of Students that the continuing ecclesiastical endorsement has been withdrawn and of the student's right to administrative review.

2. Upon receipt of a written request for a review to the Dean of Students, the Dean will assign an Assistant Dean to meet with the student to determine the facts in the case according to the student's best knowledge and to establish a time and date for the review before the Dean or other appointed review officer. The student is expected to be completely and totally honest in providing the facts and circumstances surrounding the decision of the ecclesiastical officer(s).

3. The Dean will notify the bishop who withdrew his endorsement or declined to endorse the student of the pending administrative review.

4. The student will be invited to sign a consent form allowing communication between the college and ecclesiastical leaders concerning privileged or confidential information pertaining to the student. If the student declines to sign the consent, the matter will be referred to the Dean or review officer who will make a determination based upon nonprivileged and nonconfidential information available through the review and the investigation by the Assistant Dean. The burden of persuasion shall be upon the student.

5. Following discussion with the Assistant Dean, the student must submit a written statement to the Dean of Students Office summarizing the pertinent facts and circumstances of the case, identifying the names of individuals who will provide information, providing summaries of expected statements at the review, and submitting all documentation that the student intends to introduce.

6. The Assistant Dean will prepare a file of appropriate information furnished by the student, by the ecclesiastical officer(s), and by the Assistant Dean based upon his investigation. This file will be reviewed by the Assistant Dean with the affected student at least two (2) days prior to the review and will be given to the Dean in preparation for the meeting.

F. Administrative Review

The administrative review of a denied or withdrawn ecclesiastical endorsement will be held by the Dean of Students or by a single reviewer appointed by the Dean from among the members of the Student Life Division, i.e., the Assistant Vice President of Student Life; the directors of Campus Police, the Counseling Center, Housing, and Student Health Center. The reviewer will read all documents furnished by the Dean of Students Office and will hear oral statements from the student and from any appropriate individuals as determined by the reviewer and as selected by the student and by the Assistant Dean assigned to the student.

The student and the Assistant Dean may direct questions to the reviewer concerning statements given and will have an opportunity to respond. The reviewer may limit both the statements and the response in the interest of precluding redundancy and respecting relevancy. Written minutes of the review will be kept. The student will be allowed to bring an advisor of his/her choice to the meeting to lend support and counsel, but this individual will not be allowed to act in an advocacy role or to address the review officer. However, a parent of the student will have the right to make a verbal or written recommendation to the reviewer as to the disposition of the case that would be in the best interest of the student. All documents made available by the Dean of Students Office and presented at the review will be returned to the reviewer at the conclusion of the review.

G. Standard of Review

The standard of assessment to be addressed on review will be whether the ecclesiastical officer(s) acted reasonably given the facts and circumstances by denying or withdrawing the ecclesiastical endorsement and/or whether or not there exists sufficient mitigating circumstances to allow for continued enrollment notwithstanding a declined or revoked endorsement.

H. Decisions by Reviewer

The reviewing officer, if not the Dean, will convey his/her decision as a recommendation to the Dean of Students. In every case, the Dean of Students will review the matter with the Vice President of Student Life and the President prior to rendering a decision. However, in cases where an endorsement is denied or withdrawn in conjunction with an LDS student being excommunicated, disfellowshipped, or becoming disaffiliated from the Church, the final determination will be made by the President of the college in accordance with guidelines for enrollment established by the Board of Trustees.

If a decision is made to allow the student to remain enrolled at the college, the student will remain enrolled without an ecclesiastical endorsement. A student allowed to remain enrolled without an endorsement may do so only through the end of the current semester of the affected endorsement and will be placed on a dismissal-withheld status through the Dean of Students Office until completion of the semester. At that time the student will not be permitted to continue unless it has been determined that there exists reasonable cause to allow him to remain enrolled without an ecclesiastical endorsement.

If the decision is to uphold the withdrawal of the endorsement, the student will be expected to leave the college and his/her approved housing unit immediately. Thereafter, the student must qualify for endorsement for succeeding academic terms or semesters.

Any decision made by the Dean of Students, the Vice President of Student Life, or the President of the college will be conveyed to the student in writing and to the following additional individuals as appropriate to the circumstances: (1) ecclesiastical leaders (home and campus), (2) the Director of Housing, and (3) the manager of the student's residential complex.
