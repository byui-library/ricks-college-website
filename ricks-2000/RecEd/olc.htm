BYU Idaho Outdoor Learning Center
(Badger Creek Ranch)

 The BYU Idaho  Outdoor Learning Center (OLC) is a 200 acre facility 45 miles east of campus near the Tetons. This facility is operated in cooperation with the Continuing Education Department for family and youth camps during the summer and for use by academic and student activity programs during the winter.

The OLC offers 10 small sleeping cabins, a lodge, picnic pavilions, horseback riding, canoeing, adventure/challenge ropes course, cross country skiing and sledding.
