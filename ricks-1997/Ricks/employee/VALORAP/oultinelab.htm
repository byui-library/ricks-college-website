Overview

 You must be registered for, or have completed Geography 101 to enroll in this class. 

This lab is designed to help you in your studies in the Geography 101 lecture class. All of the material that we cover in the lab will also be covered in the lectures. The lab assignments allow us to cover selected topics in greater depth thereby giving you a better understanding of the concepts.

Your lab will be taught either by myself, Kinsey Hinman, or Dorian Ducker, both of whom have successfully completed the class and the lab.  The lab assistants wll be available to assist you during the lab period or at other times which they will announce.  I will be present and available during the lab class period as well as during my regular office hours on MWF at 11:00 a.m. and on Thursdays from 1:00 to 2:00 p.m.

This lab should enhance your learning of the materials covered in the Physical Geography class.  We will cover selected materials from the lectures in greater detail.  Some new material will be introduced.  Since there will be simple math problems to solve, you have need for a calculator. 

Since this is a lab,  we encourage you to work in small groups to complete the exercises.  However, we will not tolerate someone simply copying from another.  You should do most of your work in pencil, unless directed otherwise.  This will facilitate corrections of mistakes you might make.

Instruction for lab assignmentss will be given on the dates indicated below.  Completed lab assignments will be due to the lab assistant at the beginning of the following lab period.  Late assignments will be assessed an immediate 10% penalty with an additional 5% penalty each week they are late. 

 

Suggestions

  

It is highly recommended that you use a pencil on your lab assignments, since it will then be easier for you to correct your mistakes, and make it easier for the grader to understand your answer. It is also recommend that you work in small groups of 4-5 people. By working together you can teach each other and thereby learn more. (Understand that I am not suggesting that you copy from each other or that you let someone else copy your work. This is cheating and will not be taken lightly). The more you do your own work, the better you will do in the lecture class.
