Ricks College British Literary Tour 2000

  • Features - the travel-study tour leaves from Salt Lake City Tuesday, April 25 and returns Friday May 19, totaling 25 days of travel.  The tour will visit six countries including England, Whales, Scotland, Ireland, Belgium, and France.  For a itinerary of travel, see the Virtual Tour or the Complete 2000 Itinerary.
  • Attractions include Les Miserables and Phantom of the Opera in London, plus two Shakespeare plays in Stratford.

 

  • Cost - $2950.00 plus tuition (about $82 per credit).  When considering the cost of the trip, subtract the amount needed for one summer term at Ricks (housing, food, entertainment, etc.).  Deduct this amount from the cost of the tour, and the tour becomes extremely cost effective.
  • Credits - Earn up to six credits from the following: English Literature, Introduction to Literature, Shakespeare and Introduction to Drama.
