FLY FISHING ON BLUE-RIBBON TROUT STREAMS 
People have known for years that nothing is quite as sublime as spending time luring a wily, old fish from the depths of a stream with rod, line, and fly. Instruction for women and men, beginner to advanced. Bring your own rod and equipment. Some rentals are available. 

RIVER ECOLOGY: NATURE'S DELICATE BALANCE 
Seek to understand the complex interactions within a stream ecosystem. Consider ways to effectively manage our waterways, and deal with current diseases and other environmental factors. Visit a fish hatchery and wetland refuge. 

LITERARY WORKS OF THE GREAT OUTDOORS 
Enrich your appreciation of nature through short stories, essays, and poems by classic and contemporary writers. A provocative inquiry into the authors' minds and feelings. Reading list will be provided.
