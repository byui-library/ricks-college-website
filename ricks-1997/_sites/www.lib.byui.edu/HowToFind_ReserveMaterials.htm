How to Find Reserve Materials

Items that are available from Electronic Reserve (to be read directly from the computer screen) can be accessed from the Library Home Page. Select Quick Links and click on Electronic Reserve. Scroll down the left side of the screen and click on your instructor's name. Information about the materials on reserve will be displayed on the right side of the screen. Click on the item you wish to view and it will load onto the screen.

Materials in print form that an instructor has placed on Reserve in the library are located at the Reserve Area at the Main Circulation Desk on the first floor of the West Wing. You can Search the Library Catalog for these materials by choosing General Search and limiting the collection to Reserve Area OR ask for an instructor's reserve item at the desk. These are items that can be checked out for two hours at a time, and generally remain available for the entire semester.
