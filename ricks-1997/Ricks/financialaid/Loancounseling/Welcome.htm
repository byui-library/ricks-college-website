Welcome to Ricks College Entrance Loan Counseling!

In order to receive a federal student loan, federal law requires us to give you certain basic information. This self-paced tutorial is designed to give you the information you need to make informed decisions about student borrowing. 

Once you have successfully completed this program, you will be asked to answer some questions about what you have read. If you pass this test, you will be able to print out a counseling form for you to sign. Take this to the Financial Aid Office. If your loan has arrived at Ricks College and you have met all the other requirements, you should be able to go to the Accounting Office to pick up your loan money. However, loan money cannot be disbursed earlier than the first day of classes. 

If you have any questions about this program or about your particular situation, make an appointment with your Financial Aid counselor, or call the Financial Aid Office at 356-1014.
