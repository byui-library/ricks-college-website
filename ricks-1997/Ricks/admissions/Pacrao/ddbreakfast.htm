DIVERSITY DEVELOPMENT BREAKFAST

Diversity Development Advocate Brenda Simmons organized the breakfast as somewhat of a casual event, since most of the business items had already been addressed in the Diversity Development Connection I.  A number of give-aways, the introduction of the Diversity Development Committees – the core committee and the “virtual” committee – and a few testimonials marked the morning event.

Brenda hosted the event, sharing experiences in PACRAO and how she wound up as Advocate.  She attributed much to Janet Ward and Masa Fujitani, both of whom encouraged Brenda to become more involved in the Association.  Brenda thanked Masa and Cliff Ramirez for the bringing the Diversity Development Initiative together, making it easier for her to step in and fill the Advocate’s responsibilities.

As a continuing Committee member, Anne Delfin testified to the growth she has experienced on the Committee and in PACRAO.  She spoke warmly of the Committee and encouraged members, especially new members, as a way of getting more deeply involved with PACRAO and in the profession.

Lastly, Cliff was asked to say a few words to summarize the theme of the morning.  Cliff spoke about the direction of the committee as moving more and more toward inclusiveness, almost becoming a welcoming committee for the Association regardless of people’s backgrounds.  Cliff encouraged members to reach out to one another in a way that did not discriminate, create labels, or set distances between one another.  Cliff then shared “Puppies for Sale,” a short story form Chicken Soup for the Soul, as a way of illustrating his point.
