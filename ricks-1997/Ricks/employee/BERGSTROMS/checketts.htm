Ten Questions About Your Spiritual and Academic Development

Max Checketts

Sacrament Talk

August 27th, 2000

 

1.    Do I read the scriptures daily?  (2 Nephi 32:3)

2.    When I pray do I really communicate with the Lord?  (Alma 34:17-27)

3.    Do I have meaningful fast every month?  (D&C 59:13-23)

4.    Do I go to bed early and arise early?   (D&C 88:124)

5.    Am I a happy person?  (D&C 131:3; D&C 68:6)

6.    Do I work hard?  (D&C 31:5)

7.    Am I more concerned with how I serve than where I serve?  (John 13:1-17)

8.    Do I love everyone, even my enemies?  Do I keep my romantic feelings in their proper perspective?  (John 13:34-35)

      Brother Checketts' three guidelines for dating:

  • "Go with the gang" (i.e., establish friendships; go out in groups when possible)
  • "Too long, so long" (i.e., keep romantic interludes in check and short)
  • "Keep vertical" (i.e., don't get yourself in situations where passions and emotions take over)

9.    Am I a work in progress?  (John 12:4)

10.    Do I share my testimony?  (D&C 60:2)
