Wayne L. Startin

Email: startinw@ricks.edu

Brother Startin is beginning his 17th year at Ricks College. In his other life he was a mathematics instructor and head football and basketball coach at three Salt Lake City area high schools. He was also an assistant football coach at BYU for five years. He is currently an assistant football coach at Ricks College. He thinks Ricks College is the best school in the world. and that the mathematics department is the best department at Ricks. He is a strong student advocate. He believes in perfect attendance, a lot of accountable assignments, and the integreation of technology into the teaching of mathematics. He feels that the concepts of mathematics can be explained and understood by the vast majority of Ricks College students. He is kind and soft-hearted. It is easy to feel comfortable in his classroom even though he expects a great deal from his students. He is married to Susan Kesler from Fillmore, Utah. He is the father of eight children. He loves teaching.
