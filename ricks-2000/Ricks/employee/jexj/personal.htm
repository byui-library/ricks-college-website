Personal

Brother Jerald L. Jex was born in Provo, Utah.  After graduating from BYU, he began his teaching career in 1970 with the seminary and institues.  He has served as a seminary and institute teacher, Principal, Institute Director and now teaches at BYU-Idaho as a professor of Recreation and Leisure Studies, and religion. He is also the Department Chairman for Recreation Leadership.

He has served as President of the BYU-Idaho Faculty Association (formerly Ricks College).  Brother Jex served in the U.S. Coast Guard during the Vietnam era.   He has served in numerous callings in the church, including Bishop, Counselor in Stake Presidency, Mission President in his stake and 4 times as a High Counselor.  He speaks regularly at youth conferences and for the Gospel Know Your Religion series throughout the United States.

He and his wife, Linda Kay Jex are the proud parents of 6 children and 6 grandchildren.
