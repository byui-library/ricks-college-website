John L. Clarke Resource Center

The leadership resource center in our lobby provides numerous books and manuals for student use to get ideas on such topics as team-building, delegation, conducting meetings, ice-breakers, etc. The area has materials for students to use that can increase their leadership abilities and effectiveness.

Free literature on marital and family issues is available to help develop leadership in the home. These resources include publications by the Church, Brigham Young University, and other reputable sources.
