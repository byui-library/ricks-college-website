COURSE OUTLINE French 102

Course Objectives

1. To help you develop basic communicative skills in reading, writing, speaking and listening.

2. To give you the intellectual experience of understanding how French functions as a language. (This will also sharpen your awareness of English.)

3. To introduce you to the French Speaking (Francophone) world and help you meet the peoples and cultures that make up this world.

4. To have fun and help you learn to enjoy and appreciate this beautiful, useful language. "Amusez-vous bien!"

Course Policies and Requirements

1. Textbooks: Deux Mondes 3rd Edition with accompanying workbook.

2. You will need to purchase the accompanying tapes from chapters 7 to 13 (Media Services, 3rd floor library). Since much of our focus is on listening comprehension, these tapes are invaluable. It will also be difficult to pass the listening comprehension portion of each test if you have neglected the tapes.

3. You will prepare a 1 minute "discours" (short talk) to be given in French. Sign-up sheet for specific date will be circulated. This assignment will be worth the same as one exam.(100 pts.)

4. You will be required to complete the exercises in the workbook as homework. Each chapter must be completed, corrected and turned in the day of the test on that chapter. You do the exercises in one color ink first, then correct your work from the answers given in the back in another color ink. This process must be completed in order to receive the full credit. Always study the grammar section in the text (blue pages) that is called to your attention before a written section. You will loose percentage points for failing to complete workbook or for failing to turn it in on time- the same day as the test for that chapter.

5. Other homework may include exercises from the textbook or assignments given by the teacher.

6. If you are absent it is your responsibility to get notes and assignments from classmates. A class roster will be formed with names and addresses of classmates to assist you.

7. There will be a test given at the end of each chapter and a final comprehensive written and oral exam.

8. Attendance and participation are crucial. You may miss four class periods before your grade drops one-half letter grade. Two tardies equal one absence. You will be graded on your in-class participation and daily preparation several times throughout the semester on an unannounced basis. These grades will be averaged together and will be worth the same as one test.
