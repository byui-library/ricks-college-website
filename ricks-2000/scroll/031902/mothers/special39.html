Madarieta’s mom never loses loyalty and faith

by Paul Gardner
GAR01025@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Throughout his 15 years of playing baseball, Jim Madarieta, a sophomore from Boise, and a starting pitcher for the Ricks College baseball team, has had a loyal and faithful fan.

“My mom. ... Ever since I’ve been in Little League, she’s always been there supporting me,” Madarieta said. “We’re really close. She’s my biggest fan. I can never do wrong in her eyes.”

Madarieta’s parents travel from Boise to attend every Ricks baseball game. They also drive to Utah to see the games, even if Madarieta is not pitching.

He played baseball, football and basketball in high school, and his parents attended every game.

Madarieta is on the starting pitching rotation and has one win and one loss this season.

“I pitch once a week. Depending on how well I do is how many innings I pitch. Our starting rotation this year looks pretty strong. We have really good pitching, so that’s going to carry us this year,” he said.

Besides a strong pitching staff and a solid team all around, something else will be carrying the baseball team this year — Madarieta’s mom cheering.

“She’s really loud and energetic,” Madarieta said. “She likes to cheer. Sometimes she embarrasses me. When I was little, she used to always embarrass me because she’d always yell at the ref even when she didn’t know what she was talking about. But now she has learned the rules. I’m always right in her eyes.”

Madarieta plans to transfer this fall to Boise State or the University of Utah, depending on scholarship options. Whatever he does, he can count on his parents

“When you have the support of your parents, it’s a big help,” he said.
