Personal Information

I was born and raised in Susanville, California, the third of six children.  I have two brothers and three sisters.  I attended Lassen High School, went to a year of Lassen Junior College, then served a mission to the North Central States, which included Minnesota, parts of North and South Dakota, Manitoba, and parts of Wisconsin.  Upon returning home I attended Brigham Young University where I received a bachelors and master's degree in Technical and Industrial Education.

I taught in public high schools for five years, then in a California State Prison vocational program for about a year and a half.  I then opened and operated a Shell service station and repair shop for two years until coming to Ricks College in 1976.

I am married to the former ReNee Berry of Provo, Utah, and we are the parents of eight children.  Five of them are married, and we are proud of our six grandchildren.

I have been active in Scouting (our six sons are all Eagle Scouts!), and have held several positions in our wards in Hibbard.

My hobbies are camping, fishing, rebuilding old cars, cannon shoots, and playing the guitar.
