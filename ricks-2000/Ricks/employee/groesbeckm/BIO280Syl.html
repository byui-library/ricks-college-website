PATHOPHYSIOLOGY - BIO 280

Text: Essentials of Pathophysiology:

Concepts and Applications for Health Care Professionals

by Nowak and Handford

Michael D. Groesbeck, Ph.D. BEN 224 Ex: 2035

Course Description: This course is designed for specifically for paramedic students. Its intent is to apply the basic principles of physiology to pathological conditions. Prerequisites for the class are BS264 and BS265 (Anatomy and Physiology I and II). It is expected that you already have a basic understanding of the physiology of the organs systems to be covered. We will not have time to review this material in detail.

Grades will be based solely on the exams that follow each unit. No projects or papers will be accepted for extra credit. There will be 5 exams worth 100 points each and a final worth 200 points. The final will be in two parts, 100 points from the last unit of material and 100 points comprehensive. There are 700 points possible. The highest cumulative score will be scaled to 100% and all other scores adjusted accordingly. Final grades will be assigned according to the following scale:
