Wellness Committee
Mission Statement

The mission of the  Wellness Committee for Ricks College employees and their families is four fold:

  1. Promote wellness principles and guidelines for all Ricks College employees and their families through:

            a. Education

            b. Motivation

           c. Support groups and providing resources.

    2. Utilize facilities and organize personnel at Ricks College to contribute to the wellness movement.

    3. Coordinate our efforts with Deseret Mutual for screening, evaluation, and follow up.

    4. Work with the college administration to insure an ongoing, quality program for employee wellness.
