FROM YELLOWSTONE TO THE MOON AND EVERYTHING IN BETWEEN
This action-packed week will take you on a journey from the famous thermal features and animal habitat of Yellowstone to the unique volcanic landscape of Craters of the Moon, former scientific training ground for U.S. astronauts. Classroom studies will explore volcanic formations, ancient sand dunes, wildlife and birds. The classes will be enhanced while on field trips to see Old Faithful, buffalo, elk, coyotes, geese, swans, cinder and splatter cones and explore lava caves. Campus events will include a low-level initiative course, lawn games, and bonding activities.
