Hart Building Information
Facilities Schedule

1. General Information

a. This schedule is designed to acquaint students, faculty, staff and community patrons with the general schedule for the Hart Building facilities at BYU-Idaho.

b. The facilities will be open 6 a.m. to 10:30 p.m., Monday - Saturday. The facilities will be closed every Sunday, and on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day. The swimming pool opens at 5 a.m. Monday through Friday.

c. The term "Student Open Time" indicates that no special group is scheduled and the facility is open for use by anyone. No organized team or group should plan to take over a given facility for practice during such time.

d. The Field House will be set up for tennis play on Fridays from 6:30 p.m. to 10:30 p.m.

e. All students are entitled to use facilities, clothing and equipment at no cost.

f. Guests are permitted at a cost of $2.00.

g. Exceptions to this schedule, which arise during the semester during special events, will be posted in the dressing rooms. Major repair and maintenance periods will be scheduled during the summer, at which time individual facilities will be closed for short periods of time.

h. All persons using the Hart Building facilities must be dressed in BYU-Idaho physical education clothing or standard clothing issued to varsity athletes. During the daily jogging times in the Field House, patrons may dress in appropriate clothing of their own, if they go directly to the field house from their place of residence and return.

i. On Saturdays, from October 15 to March 31, varsity baseball and track practice will be held in the Field House from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. The rest of the day is student open time.

j. Student reservations for the racquetball and indoor tennis courts may be made in Hart 152 during the posted times.

k. Family Home Evening groups are invited to use the facilities Monday evening. Facilities will be available on a first-come basis. Reservations cannot be made.

l. All facilities are closed on Sundays except for church meetings.

m. Please contact the university scheduling office in Manwaring Center 207, or call ext. 2248, for special scheduling.

2. Dress/Clothing Guidelines

a. A dress and clothing policy for the Hart Building was established primarily as a means of controlling the facility for those authorized to use it. In addition, using a standard clothing issue helps with cleanliness, modesty, and appearance.

b. All persons using the Hart Building facilities must be dressed in BYU-Idaho issued physical education clothing. This includes all participants in regular physical education classes, intramurals and free play time.

c. Athletic clothing issued to varsity athletes may be used by participants in the Hart Building facilities during athletic games, practice sessions, try-outs, meetings, etc. Such clothing can be worn in physical education classes which are directly related to varsity athletic programs, such as athletic conditioning.

d. Building patrons can wear clothing underneath the issued shorts or shirts. They can wear full length sweat pants over the shorts. The shirt must be completely visible with nothing over it. Any of these additional clothing items must be neat, clean and appropriate.

e. There are to be no hats or bandannas worn in the facility. Tank tops or any other immodest type clothing are not allowed in the building at any time.

f. Building personnel do not enforce the dress code during physical education classes or athletic activities. The authority rests with the teacher or coach.

g. During the daily jogging times in the field house, patrons may dress in appropriate clothing of their own if they go directly to the field house from their place of residence and return.
