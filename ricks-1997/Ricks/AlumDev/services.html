1.     Inside Ricks is an informal newsletter sent via e-mail each week.  Through it we share the latest information about Ricks College including upcoming events and a report on recent events.  It's a great way to stay updated.  If you don't currently receive it, contact us at insideRicks@ricks.edu and we'll begin sending it to you.

2.     Area Activities such as reunions and firesides that cater to alumni across the country are directed from the Alumni Office on campus.  Check our Upcoming Events page to find out if we're coming to your area soon.  

3.     Reunions sponsored by the Alumni Office maintain relationships and help keep the college experience unforgettable.  We are glad to help make arrangements for you.  If you are interested in organizing a reunion, please Contact Us. 

4.     Alumni Travel is a way to tour the world with Ricks College alumni, staff and friends.  The Alumni Office organizes area tours in areas such as Church History and the Holy Land.  Special rates and travel packages are included.  Check our Upcoming Events page to learn of currently planned tours.

 5.     Alumni Directory   You can now register on our directory and find old friends and roommates or contact us if you need to update your address; we want to keep you connected to Ricks.

6.     Alumni Council Members in various areas of the United States are involved when a Ricks College function is in their area, such as firesides, performing, groups, and CES programs.  They are a resource to the college and to you, as well as to new and future Ricks College students.  If you live near one of these representatives, call and volunteer your services.
