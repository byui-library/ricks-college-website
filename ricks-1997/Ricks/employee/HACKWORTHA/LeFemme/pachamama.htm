Pachamama

Pachamama is the Andean Goddess Mother Earth. Unlike European notions of God, She is not apart from Earth but the very Earth that gave us life, sustains us and will receive us at the end. There are three rules of Pachamama. First, whenever we are hungry and thirsty, we should remember that Pachamama is hungry and thirsty too; and we should take care of her first. Second, there are times when Pachamama does not want to be touched and we should leave her alone. Third, there are times when Pachamama is sad and wants to be consoled; we should bring her flowers.
