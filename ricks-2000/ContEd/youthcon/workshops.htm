OUTDOOR LEARNING CENTER

WORKSHOPS

 

Horseback Riding

The basic techniques will be covered. This will include safety, grooming, bridling, saddling, 

mounting, and riding. After the group is ready, they will go on a horse ride around the OLC 

property. Additional Charge (2 hours)

 

Canoeing

Under the direction of the staff, the participants will learn how to get in and out of a canoe, the 

various strokes, parts of the canoe, safety, water rescue, etc. Toward the end of the workshop, 

there may be some free time to paddle or be involved in a competition course. (2 hours)

 

Inclined/Horizontal Log

This is a high element ropes activity.  The participants have the opportunity of walking up an

inclined log.  Upon reaching the top they step onto the horizontal log and walk across.  The 

participants are then lowered to the ground using a belay system.  Helmet, harness, and climbing 

ropes will be used. (2 hrs.) .

 

Climbing Tower

This high element ropes activity consists of climbing the face of a 35-foot high wall.  The 

participant will learn climbing techniques to aid in their ascent.  Helmet, harness, and climbing 

ropes will be used.

 

Cargo Net 

This high element ropes activity consists of climbing a 40-foot high cargo net.  This activity

is a tougher challenge than one might assume.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will

be used.

 

Rappel Tower

This high element ropes activity consists of a free rappel of 40 ft. from the top of our rappel 

tower.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be used.  All participants will be using a back-up 

belay system.

 

Giant Swing

This element is another high ropes activity consisting of swinging from a height chosen by the 

participant.  The highest point is 40 ft. above the ground. This is a self-release system.  Helmet, 

harness, and climbing ropes will be used.

 

Zip Line

This high element ropes activity consists of zipping down a cable from atop our zip tower.  The 

participants will be clipped into a seat harness.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be 

used.

 

Power Pole

This is a high ropes activity which involve a great deal of commitment.  Participants will have the 

challenge of climbing a vertical pole and standing on top, afterwards jumping out to touch a 

hanging tennis ball.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be used.

 

Outdoor Survival

Those engaging in this workshop will learn skills and gain a better understanding of how to make 

their outings safer and to prepare them for emergencies. Topics could include rules for 

preparedness, keys to survival, inventory, making fires, shelters, obtaining water, signals, food, 

essentials to take, etc. (2 hours)

 

Initiative Course #1

This course has approximately ten events which will challenge the thinking of those involved. 

There are various obstacles, tasks, and problems to solve, sometimes using equipment, while 

other times not being able to speak to one another. This is an excellent workshop for building 

teamwork, unity, trust, self esteem, communication, etc. (2 hours)

 

Map and Compass

The basic fundamentals of map and compass reading will be given. Participants will learn the 

parts of a compass and how to take a bearing. After that, they will find their way around an 

orienteering course. (2 hours)

 

Black Powder Rifles

Starting from the time the lead is melted, the participants will learn the various steps from making 

the lead bullets to actually firing them. Safety is stressed in the process and nobody is allowed to 

fire at the targets until a staff member has given the firing cap to the individual. (2 hours)

 

Initiative Course #2

This is similar to Course #1 except there are different situations to solve, obstacles, problems, 

and equipment. (2 hours)

 

Leatherwork

Participants will become acquainted with the various leatherwork tools, design a pattern of their

choice, and make a project to take home. (1 hour)

 

Solar Wood Burning

After selecting a pattern, the participants will use a solar wood burner to burn the design onto the 

wood.  Necklaces, paperweights, and wall hangings are among some of the projects that can be 

made to take home. (1 hour)

 

New Games

This workshop has a collection of games and activities where everyone can participate. Some of 

the games can be played competitively with lots of room for skills and strategy. Many of them 

have no real objective other than to have fun, enjoy each other, and build teamwork and 

cooperation. (1-2 hours)

 

Dream Catchers

Participants will learn to weave artificial sinew into a "spinning oval" that looks similar to a 

spider's web. Indians used Dream Catchers to trap bad dreams sent by evil spirits and let the 

good dreams slip down a feather into the mind. (1 hour)

 

Nature Hike

This hike is programmed to help participants become aware of objects in front of them by using 

their many senses. Many look but do not see. This program will help participants see by using 

games or many other outdoor activities. (1 hour)
