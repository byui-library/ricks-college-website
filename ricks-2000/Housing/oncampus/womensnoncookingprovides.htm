Women's Non-Cooking Style Housing Provides:

 
  • Two twin size lofted beds per bedroom
  • A vinyl mattress cover for each bed
  • High efficiency closets with short and long hanging and dresser drawers for each person
  • One desk per student
  • Microfridge (microwave, small refrigerator/freezer)
  • Two loveseats and two overstuffed chairs in the living room
  • One telephone per apartment (long distance cards can be purchased at the Cashiers Office)
  • Marker board by the telephone
  • Ironing board and cover
  • Vacuum
  • Broom and dustpan
  • Two large sponges
  • Toilet brush and plunger
  • TV/VCR in main lounge
  • Piano, TV, and exercise machine in the upper lounge
  • Kitchenette in the upper lounge that can be scheduled with the head resident.  She also has a few dishes that can be checked out.  Use of this kitchenette is intended for occasional use only.  Meals are eaten in the dining hall.
  • Laundry facilities - washers 75 cents per load; dryers 25 cents for 30 minuets
  • Each apartment has three bedrooms, a small living room, and a bathroom with a toilet stall, two sinks, tub/shower, and lots of storage for linens, makeup, personal items, etc.  (These apartments do not have a kitchen.)  There are high efficiency closets in each bedroom including a place to apply makeup and style hair with electrical outlets and mirrors.  There is a pantry above the Microfridge in the living room.  Carpet color is mauve and the living room furniture sets are in three colors - spruce, royal, or burgundy.  Each bedroom has burgundy drapes and the living room has white blinds with a burgundy topper.  We are unable to grant room requests based on color.
