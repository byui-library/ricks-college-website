Religious History Tour
Updated for 2002


Tour Fee of $2,895 includes:
  • Round-trip airfare from Salt Lake City.

  • Daily breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

  • All lodging in Europe.

  • Entrance fees to mandated attractions.

  • Transportation in Europe.

Your tour fee does not include:
  • Books for courses taken.

  • Personal expenses for snacks or souvenirs.

  • Insurance.

  • Passport application fees.

The trip runs during the second block of the Winter Semester.
Students can take 2-3 block, module, and/or internet courses in January/February, then from 7 to 9 credits on tour. In preperation, we will hold classes on the BYU-I campus for 3 weeks in March. The rest of the learning will take place on sight in some of the greatest cities in the World.

  • HUM 201*: Western Culture (3 credits, Fills G.E. Arts requirement)
  • HUM 202*: Western Culture (3 credits, Fills G.E. Arts requirement) 
  • PHIL 203: Hist. of Religious Thought, Ancient (3 credits, Fills G.E. Social Science requirement)
  • PHIL 204: Hist. of Religious Thought, Modern(3 credits, Fills G.E. Social Science requirements)
  • REL 211: New Testament (3 credits, Fills G.E. Religion Requirement)
  • REL 212: New Testament (3 credits, Fills G.E. Religion Requirement)
  • REL 227: Pearl of Great Price (3 credits, Fills G.E. Religion Requirement)
  • REL 352: The Gospel and Christian History (3 credits, Fills G.E. Religion Requirement)


    • *if both Humanities 201 and 202 are taken the GE Arts and Letters requirement is met.
HIGHLIGHTS:
World class museums, the Alps, boat trips on the Seine in Paris, Italian ice cream, stunning castles, beautiful piazzas, a gondola ride in Venice, French bread and cheese, eighteenth-century water parks, fabulous churches, gorgeous parks and unbelievable palaces. These experiences are only the beginning of the rich educational experience that you can share with us.
