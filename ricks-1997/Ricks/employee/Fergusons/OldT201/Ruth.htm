Christ and Boaz

1. Setting- Bethlehem- House of Bread

2. Famine- No Bread

3. Christ- I am the Bread of Life

4. Ruth- Ancestor of Jesus

5. Boaz- Kinsmen- Redeemer- Savior: 

a. Saves the widows 

b. Bought, purchased Ruth 

c. 1 Cor. 6:20; Acts 20:28

6. Boaz- Name means "Come then" a. Isaiah 1:18; Isaiah 63:1-3
 

Note: Ruth is a convert. Because she gave up her former religion, and her former life, to stay with her Mother-in-

law, she had no place to turn. She exemplifies the truth that participation in the kingdom of God is not decided by blood and birth, but by conformity to God's will through the Covenant. It isn't which organization I belong to that will save me in the end, rather it is in the end, whether I have given my full heart the Lord and His kingdom.
