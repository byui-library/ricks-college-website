Study Aids for English 270 Romanticism Test

Even though John Keats (1795-1821) himself said, A>Beauty is truth, truth beauty.=Bthat is all/ Ye know on earth, and all ye need to know.@ [Ode on a Grecian Urn] Keep in mind that Keats died at age 25 before he had to take a test on his own poetry.

Some hints:

  1. Match Poem with Poet--11 questions. Be able to match assigned poems with authors.  They will be taken from the syllabus.
  2. Biographical Notes--28 questions. Some of the information will be from the biographies in the text; therefore, read the biographical information.  Some of the information will be from the poems themselves that indicate something about the author.  Some will be from class lecture.
  3. Match characters with poet.--5 questions.  To get these you will need to know the major characters in the poems.  Be able to match the character with the title, the title with the poet.
  4. The Romantic Period--5 questions.  Know the dates of the Romantic Period, the catalyst for its movement, the prime movers.
  5. Match lines or characters with the Poem--20 questions.  Whereas you do not need to memorize lines, you must be familiar enough with the assigned poems to recognize their themes and key lines lines from those poems.  Read the poems out loud.  Keep both the title and the author in mind as you read them.
  6. Birth and Death Dates--6 questions. Know the birth and death dates of each of the Romantic Poets.  Six poets, six questions.  Get the hint?
  7. Match lines with the poet--25 questions.  To get these questions you will need to be able to match the poems with the poet and the lines with the poem.   Familiarity is the only key.  Read them until they are familiar to you.

Total questions--100.  Each question is worth 1 point.
