ANTHROPOLOGY 290R FIELD TRIP

SPRING BREAK   2003       April 3-7 

This trip will provide anthropology students with an experience in archaeology, culture,  museology, and astronomy emphasizing the Anasazi  or prehistoric Pueblo culture of the American Southwest. 

PREREQUSITE:  Anth 101 or GS.PS230 or current enrollment.

TOUR DIRECTORS: Jack Reinwand, Anthropology, and an invited Professor from a related discipline.   (Ellis Miller, Astronomy, 2002)

ITINERARY:     Evening class periods (Mar 6, 13, 20, and 27; Clark 327, 6:00pm ) will be required to orient students and make assignments for credit. Excuse slips will be available for those who will miss Wednesday classes Apr 3.

Day 1. Travel to Moab, Utah for dinner, then overnight in Cortez, Colo.

Day 2. Tour Anasazi Heritage Museum and Mesa Verde National Park.

Day 3. Travel to and tour Chaco Canyon Nat. Monument and  Hovenweep N. M.

Day 4.  Tour Edge of Cedars State Park and Museum in Blanding, Utah. The museum houses one of the best pottery collections in Utah.   Travel to the Butler Wash Ruin (about thirty rooms and five  kivas). We will also visit the Cave Towers site, a complex of seven stone towers constructed more than 900 years ago. Then travel to  Muley Point, a scenic overlook of the San Juan river.  We will have dinner in historic Bluff, Utah at the Cow Canyon Restaurant which specializes in southwestern and Navajo foods.

Day 5. Tour Arches National Park. Arrive in Rexburg late that evening.

ASSIGNMENT:  A journal will be submitted containing results of outside readings, an entry for each museum or event attended, and a recap essay.  Photos are nice but not required.  The journal is due no later than April 22.

COSTS: Class fee - $160. Covers travel, entrance fees and motels.    Students will be responsible for their own meals and spending money. (As we travel we will try to provide a variety of eating options at each stop, grocery stores, fast foods or restaurants.)

CREDIT: Students must enroll for 2 credits of Anthropology 290R.   Pay student travel fee at Cashier's Office.   Class enrollment may be completed by presenting a cashiers' receipt to Bro. Reinwand, in Clarke 344B.

NOTICE:  This tour includes considerable cross country / off trail hiking.   Physical conditioning is mandatory.  Medical conditions must be reported to tour directors prior to enrollment.
