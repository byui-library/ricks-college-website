Citing Online Sources--MLA
Proquest

  • Name of the author, editor, compiler, or translator of the source (if available and relevant), reversed for alphabetizing and followed by an abbreviation, such as ed., if appropriate 

  • Title of a poem, short story, article, or similar short work within a scholarly project, database, or periodical (in quotation marks); or title of a posting to a discussion list or forum (taken from the subject line and put in quotation marks), followed by the description Online posting

  • Title of a book (underlined) 

  • Name of the editor, compiler, or translator of the text (if relevant and if not cited earlier), preceded by the appropriate abbreviation, such as Ed. 

  • Publication information for any print version of the source 

  • Title of the scholarly project, database, periodical, or professional or personal site (underlined); or, for a professional or personal site with no title, a description such as Home page 

  • Name of the editor of the scholarly project or database (if available) 

  • Version number of the source (if not part of the title) or, for a journal, the volume number, issue number, or other identifying number 

  • Date of electronic publication, of the latest update, or of posting 

  • For a work from a subscription service, the name of the service and--if a library is the subscriber--the name and city (and state abbreviation, if necessary) of the library 

  • For a posting to a discussion list or forum, the name of the list or forum 

  • The number range or total number of pages, paragraphs, or other sections, if they are numbered 

  • Name of any institution or organization sponsoring or associated with the Web site

  • Date when the researcher accessed the source 

  • Electronic address, or URL, of the source (in angle brackets); or, for a subscription service, the URL of the service's main page (if known) or the keyword assigned by the service

MLA Format for ProQuest 

Magazine Format

Author's Last Name, First Name and Initial. "Title of Article." Title of Magazine Day

 Month: Page Range (if known). ProQuest. Library Name, Name of City. Day Month Year of

        Access <http://porquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

Magazine Example

Schaefer, Bradley E. "Meteors That Changed the World." Sky and Telescope Dec. 1998.

        Research Library Periodicals. ProQuest. Ricks Coll. Lib., Warren. 29

        Oct. 1999 <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

Newspaper Format

Author's Last Name, First Name and Initial. "Title of Article." Title of Newspaper Day

           Month Year, Edition: Page number(s) or First Page Number and Plus Sign. Name of

           City.  Day Month Year of Access <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

Newspaper Example

Henneberger, Melinda. "Author Reveals Much about Others and Little of Herself." New

           York Times 6 Dec. 1999, East Coast late ed.: A16. PA Research II Newspapers.

           ProQuest. Ricks Coll. Lib. 6 Dec. 1999 <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

Scholarly Journal Format

Author's Last Name, First Name and Initial. "Title of Article." Title of Journal Volume

        Number (Year): Page Range. Name of Database (if Known). ProQuest. Library Name,

        Name of City. Day Month Year of Access <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

Scholarly Journal Example

Onwuemene, Michael C. "Limits of Transliteration: Nigerian Writers' Endeavors toward a

        National Literary Language." PMLA 114 (1999): 1055-66. PA Research II

        Periodicals. ProQuest. New York Public Lib. 3 Dec. 1999

       <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

The World Book Encyclopedia Format

Author's Last Name, First Name and Initial. "Title of Article." The World Book

         Encyclopedia. Year of Edition. ProQuest. Name of Database. Library Name, Name of

        City Day Month Year of Access <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

The World Book Encyclopedia

Genovese, E. N. "Mars." The World Book Encyclopedia. 1998. ProQuest. Reference.

         Southwest Community Coll. Lib., Warren. 3 Dec. 1999

         <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>.

APA Format

Author's Last Name, First Name and Initial. (Year, Month, Day). Article Title. Publication

         Title, [Page Range or Number of Pages]. Retrieved [month day, year] from [source]

         database ([name of database], [item no.-if applicable]) on the World Wide Web:

          [URL]

Example

Schaefer, Bradley E. (1998, December). Meteors that changed the world. Sky and

           Telescope, [8 pages]. Retrieved October 29, 1998 from ProQuest (Research Library

           Periodicals) on the World Wide Web: <http://proquest.umi.com/pqdweb>
