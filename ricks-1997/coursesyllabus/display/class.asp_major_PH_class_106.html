INTRODUCTORY APPLIED PHYSICS
APPL PHYSICS
CLASS CODE: PH 106 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This course is the second sequence of Applied Physics. Concurrent registration of PH 108 is strongly recommended.
DESCRIPTION: Second of sequence to Applied Physics.
TOPICS: Topics include electricity and magnetism, optics, relativity, atomic and nuclear physics. Astronomy. These areas are important in a wide range of areas including structures, human anatomy, transportation, music, earth science and philosophy.
OBJECTIVES: The purpose of this class is to develop an understanding of the basic principles and concepts of physics.
REQUIREMENTS: Text: Giancoli; Physics 5th Edition.
PREREQUISITES: Prerequisite: Physics 105
Concurrent Registration: Physics 108 recommended.
Math Level: Extensive use of high school Algebra II and Trigonometry.
