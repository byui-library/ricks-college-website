The BYU-Idaho Men's Choir (conducted by Randall Kempton) is an all-male chorus of 75-100 singers.  Our repertoire ranges from Gregorian chant to barbershop, from sacred hymns and spirituals to the best of Broadway and Hollywood, from serious classical music to contemporary rock-a-pella.   The choir performs frequently–in the Barrus concert hall, at campus devotionals, and for special events.  We also travel occasionally off campus; we have sung recently in General Conference, in the Salt Lake Tabernacle, and for the governor at the Idaho State Capitol.  In addition to rehearsing and performing independently, Men's Choir combines regularly with the equally stunning Women's Choir for special rehearsals and performances announced at the beginning of each semester.  

 

Interested in joining the BYU-Idaho Men’s Choir? 

 

Auditions for the 2001-2002 FIRST EVER edition of the Brigham Young University–Idaho Men’s Choir will be held September 1, 3, and 4, 2001, in the Eliza R. Snow Building.  Come early to avoid the rush!  Sign up for a time in the lobby of the Snow building.

 

Please come prepared to sing an art song or a MEMORIZED hymn verse (sing the melody, not the bass or tenor line).  For the purposes of this audition, we would prefer not to hear “pop” songs or “sacred pop.”  An accompanist will be provided.
