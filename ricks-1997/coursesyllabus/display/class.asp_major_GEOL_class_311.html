Structural Geology
STRUCT GEOL
CLASS CODE: GEOL 311 CREDITS:  4 FEE:  $50.00
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Fundamentals of rock deformation and rock structures; Applications to geological engineering, mining geology, and petroleum geology; Introduction to tectonics; Field Trips.
DESCRIPTION: This course teaches students the principles of mineral & rock deformation and the identification, measurement, analysis, and interpretation of structures in thin section, hand sample, and in the field. Emphasis is placed on applications to geologic engineering, mining geology, and petroleum geology. Students are introduced to tectonics and regional deformation.
TOPICS: Stress and StrainMechanisms for Mechanical Deformation of Minerals & RocksBrittle & Ductile StructuresIdentification of Structures (thin section, hand sample, & field-scale)Measurement, Analysis, & Interpretation of Structural DataStructural Applications to Geologic Engineering, Mining Geology, & Petroleum GeologyGeophysical Methods in Structural GeologyTectonics & Regional DeformationRadiometric Dating in Structural Geology
OBJECTIVES: The main objective is for students to be able to identify structures, measure their properties, and analyze and interpret structural data. Other objectives include giving students experience in: field geology, computer modeling, the use of mathematics in geology, research, scientific writing and speaking, and the geologic literature.
REQUIREMENTS: This class requires significant preparation for and participation in class. Field trips are also required.
PREREQUISITES: Geol 111 & 112, Geol 350; Phys 105, 121, or 221
