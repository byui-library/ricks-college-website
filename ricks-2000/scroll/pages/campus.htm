E-COMMERCE PROVIDES REAL EXPERIENCE FOR RICKS STUDENTS

No tests, no textbooks, no assignments. Sounds like a pretty fun class, right? For students in Craig Bell's e-commerce class, this is the usual.

Bell's class is a merger of in-class, traditional learning "meshed" with real-life job experience. The concept of the class is one that has existed in theory alone until recently. Bell's students are in the process of developing an online business, joining the fast-paced dot-com world.

The class concept is simple: using experiential education techniques to teach students about the e-commerce world. Bell has brought together 19 students from varying fields such as graphic design, marketing, advertising, public relations and computer information systems, all of which play a crucial role in the development of the online business.

HUTCH SWAN NAMED MAN OF THE YEAR

The man sitting at the table had a certain aura about him. However, it wasn't one of arrogance or conceit. His attitude was more of humility.

George "Hutch" Swan has every right to be a little cocky. After all, he was recently chosen as Ricks College's Man of the Year. However, he is genuinely modest. In fact, if you talked with him, you'd realize this truly was an honor he did not expect.

"Its kind of neat. I don't know, I try to not let things build up too much in my head," Swan, a sophomore from Twin Falls, Idaho, said.
