Idaho Promise Scholarship

Category B

No application is necessary.  All eligible students attending BYU-Idaho will receive a $250 per semester scholarship.

To qualify, a student must:

  • be a resident of Idaho

  • have completed high school or its equivalent in Idaho

  • have a GPA of 3.0 or an ACT score of 20

  • be entering college for the first time in the 2001-2002 school year or later

  • enroll as a full-time student at an Idaho college or university, and

  • as of July 1 2001, you must be 21 or younger
