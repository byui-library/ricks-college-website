BROWERI@ricks.edu The Director of Distributed Data Services is Ivan J. Brower.  Distributed Data Services is responsible for the NT, NetWare, Unix and Web servers on campus.  Ivan has been at Ricks since February of 1982.  He is married and has six children.  Prior to coming to Ricks, he worked for EG&G in both Idaho Falls and in Las Vegas.  He has an associates degree from Ricks in Mathematics, a B.S. in Zoology and an M.S. in Computer Science from BYU.  Ivan was born in St. Anthony and raised in Blackfoot, Idaho.
