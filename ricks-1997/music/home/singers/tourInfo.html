Tour Information

After graduation, Collegiate Singers will be performing on tour for 3 weeks in May 2002. The group will be stopping in a number of cities from Idaho to Texas. The main performances will be concentrated in the Dallas, Texas area and will consist of formal concerts, firesides, and public school performances.

Tours like the one coming up in May provide the singers with a great service opportunity. Building bridges of understanding and sharing cultural diversity is the main focus of our touring program.
