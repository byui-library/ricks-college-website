Introduction

Daughters of the Pioneers of the Upper Snake River Valley

Histories of Pioneers who settled in the Upper Snake River Valley 1883-1893

Processed by: Blaine Bake

Register created by: John L. Powell

Date: November 5, 2001

Physical Description: 1.6 linear ft.

Agency History

The Daughters of the Pioneers of the Upper Snake River Valley was organized in 1924 and operated until at least 1950, at Rexburg, Idaho. The organization had a constitution and by-laws and operated with the encouragement of the Idaho State Historical Society. At meetings members read histories of pioneers who came into the Upper Snake River country between 1883 and 1893 that they had researched and written. After the histories had been read and accepted one copy was sent to the State Historical Society in Boise and another was kept on file for preservation and reference at the local organization. The organization also remembered the passing of pioneers with flowers.

Scope and Content

The collection is comprised mainly of short historical sketches of pioneers who settled in the Upper Snake River Valley between 1883 and 1893. The histories were written by members of the Daughters of the Pioneers of the Upper Snake River Valley and presented at meetings of the club.

In addition, the collection contains a brief history of the meetings, elections and programs of the Daughters of the Snake River Valley Pioneers from 1924-1950.

Also included (Box 4 fd 50) are newspaper clippings on various subjects and a holograph history of Menan. Box 4 fd 51 contains transcripts of KRXK radio programs "Salute To A Community" each of which consists of a short history of an area town.
