FORMER STUDENT RETURNED MISSIONARY
ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Former Student Returned Missionary -- Former students who are currently serving full-time LDS missions or those who have recently returned from full-time missions (within six months) should use the simplified Former Student Returned Missionary application. These applications can be obtained by contacting the Ricks College Admissions Office.
