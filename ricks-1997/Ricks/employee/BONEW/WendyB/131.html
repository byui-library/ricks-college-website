COURSE DESCRIPTION

A movement class where rhythms, style, and technique of jazz dancing are introduced to the student, including basic jazz terminology.

OBJECTIVES

    1. To introduce jazz dancing as a stylized dance form with an introduction to the basics of dance technique as a foundation.

    2. To demonstrate an understanding of basic dance technique and jazz skills.

    3. To help each student experience the fun of jazz rhythms and motion.

    4. To heighten physical health and well-being through activity and correct principles of training.

REQUIREMENTS

    Professionalism is stressed:
    1. Punctuality
    2. Proper and modest attire
    3. Hair out of face
    4. No chewing gum
    5. Total involvement and attention during class

Attendance/Participation:

    Two (2) absences are allowed without penalty. Three to four (3-4) absences will result in lowering of grade. The 11th absence will result in automatic failing of course. Three (3) tardies equal one absence. Three observations equal one absence. To receive credit for observing you must take in-depth notes on your observations and turn them in to the instructor.

Movement and Technique:

    Performance of dance combinations will be performed periodically throughout the semester for evaluation by the instructor. Evaluations will be directly related to demonstration of jazz rhythms, style, and technique.

Critique:

    A two-page, typewritten critique (not a review) of a live dance performance will be submitted by each student. Due:

    Late papers are not accepted.

Final Exam: During scheduled final time.

    GRADING
    Attendance 40%
    Participation/Attitude 10%
    Achievement/Skill 40%
    Written assignment 10%

    The above schedule, procedures, and requirements In this course are subject to change In the event of extenuating circumstances.


    Students are encouraged to meet with the instructor at any time they feel the need for feedback or other assistance. Periodic feedback will be given, solicited or not, but the student must ensure that it is sufficient for his or her individual needs. It is hoped that this will be a rewarding semester in which the student begins to feel the exciting dynamics which can exist in skillfully relating body and spirit. Come ready to work hard and enjoy!!

    Upon successful completion of DA 131 (Beginning Jazz), DA 240 A and B (Modern Dance Technique I) will be required as preparation for DA 231 (Intermediate Jazz).
