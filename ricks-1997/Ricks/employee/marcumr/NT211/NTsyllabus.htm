NEW TESTAMENT--CHRONOLOGICALLY

DAY ONE--INTRODUCTION and HISTORY AND BACKGROUND OF THE NEW TESTAMENT

DAY TWO

Luke 1:1-4--Purpose of the gospels

Matthew 1:1-17 and Luke 3:23-38--The genealogies of Jesus are given.

St. Luke 5-26--The annunciation of John. Temple at Jerusalem.

Luke 1:26-38--The Annunciation of Mary. Nazareth.

Matthew 1:18-25--Annunciation to Joseph. Nazareth.

Luke 1:39-56--Mary visits Elizabeth. Judaea near Jerusalem and Bethlehem.

Luke 1:57-80--John is born and Zacharias prophesies concerning him. Judaea, near Jerusalem and Bethlehem.

DAY THREE

Luke 2:1-7--Mary and Joseph go to Bethlehem for the census. The Savior is born. Bethlehem.

Luke 2:8-20--Annunciation to the Shepherds. Bethlehem, shepherds field.

Luke 2:21-39--circumcising, presentation at the temple and Witness of Anna and Simeon. Bethlehem, then Jerusalem.

Matthew 2:1-23--The Wisemen, Herod's slaughter of the children, fleeing to Egypt and the return. Jerusalem, Bethlehem, then to Egypt.

Luke 2:40--Jesus' childhood. Nazareth.

JST Matthew 3:24-25

Luke 2:41-52--Jesus at age 12 in the temple. His youth and young manhood. Jerusalem.

DAY FOUR

Matthew 3:1-4--John preaches in the wilderness. Bethabara in the Jordan valley east of Jericho.

JST Luke 3:3-11--The teachings of John.

Matthew 3:5-17--John challenges the Pharisees and Saducees. Jesus receives baptism.

JST John 1:1-34--John the Apostles testimony of Jesus' preexistent life. He gives John the Baptist's witness.

John 1:35-51--John turns his disciples to Christ. Unknown where John was when he wrote his record. John the Baptist is at the Jordan.

Matthew 4:1-11--The temptation of Jesus. Judean wilderness.

John 2:1-11--The Miracle at Cana. Cana in Galilee.

John 2:12--He and his family go back to Capernaum after the wedding. Capernaum on the northern shores of Galilee.

DAY FIVE

John 2:13-Jesus goes to Passover, cleanses the temple for the first time and makes prophecy about the future of His house. Jerusalem.

John 3:1-21--Jesus teaches Nicodemus about the importance of the TWO baptisms. Jerusalem.

John 3:22-36--Jesus stays in the surrounding area. Jesus and his disciples baptize. John bears witness that his time is over. Judaea and Aenon, near Salim.

Matthew 14:3-5--John the Baptist is put in prison. Machaerus, or in the winter palace of Herod Antipas near Jericho.

Matthew 4:12-13--Jesus hears of John's imprisonment. Starts for Capernaum in the Galilee.

John 4:2-54--The Samaritan Woman at the Well. Jacobs well in Samaria.

John 4:43-54--Goes on to Cana in Galilee. Heals Capernaum noblemans son while there.

Luke 4:15-Jesus goes to Nazareth and declares who he is. He is rejected. Nazareth.

Matthew 4:13-16--Jesus goes to Capernaum.

Luke 4:31-44. Jesus Teaches in Capernaum. Heals the sick, teaches with authority.

Luke 5:12-16--Healing of a Leper. Galilee.

Matthew 4:18-25--Begins to call the Twelve. His fame is growing. Galilee, probably at Capernaum or Bethsaida.

Matthew 9:9-13--A feast at Matthews house. (In the harmony this is put latter, After Matthew 9:1-8). Capernaum.

DAY SIX

Matthew 10:1-42. All twelve called and ordained. Given their charge. In Galilee, but exact location is not known.

Matthew 5-Matthew 7. The Sermon on the Mount. Jesus teaches his "disciples" and prepares them to teach the people. Hill north of Capernaum.

Read Matthew 5:1-48.

DAY SEVEN

Matthew 6:1-24

JST Matthew 6:25-27 is added here.

Matthew 6:25-34--Continuation of the sermon.

Matthew 7:1-11.

JST Matthew 7:1-17 (Note the additions to the above)

Matthew 7:12-29--Continuation of the sermon.

DAY EIGHT

Matthew 8:5-13--Healing of the Centurions Servant. Capernaum.

Luke 7:11-17--Jesus heals the son of the Widow of Nain. Nain.

Matthew 8:14-17--Peters Mother-in-law is healed, and others come to receive his blessing. Capernaum.

Matthew 8:18-22--Two disciples, one a former scribe, are taught lessons about the cost of being a disciple. Capernaum.

Matthew 8:23-27--The stilling of the Sea. Sea of Galilee.

Matthew 8:28-34--Casts devils into swine. Geresa or Gedara on the eastern shore of the Sea of Galilee.

DAY NINE

Matthew 9:1-8--Heals a paralytic. Capernaum.

Matthew 9:14-17--Some of John's disciples question Jesus. Galilee.

JST Matthew 9:18-23--The Pharisees ask about their baptism. Galilee.

Matthew 9:18-26--Jesus raises Jairus' daughter from the dead and heals the woman with an issue of blood for 12 years. Capernaum.

Matthew 9:27-31--Two blind men healed. Capernaum.

Matthew 9:32-34--Heals Demoniac, prince of the devils. Capernaum.

Matthew 11:1--Teaches in cities of the Apostles. Galilee.

Luke 7:19-23--John sends disciples unto Jesus so that they may learn for themselves. Jesus pronounces that there has been no greater prophet than John. Galilee.

John 5:1--Jesus goes to Jerusalem for the (second) Feast of the Passover. Jerusalem.

John 5:2-16--Jesus uses healing of the lame man at the pool of Bethesda to teach the higher law of the Sabbath. Jerusalem.

DAY TEN

John 5:17-47--The witness of the Father and of the Son. Jerusalem.

Matthew 9:35-38--Jesus returns to Galilee. His following is growing. He has compassion on them.

Luke 8:1-3--He and the twelve preach harder. Galilee.

Matthew 12:1-21--Teachings on the Sabbath-day. Galilee.

Matthew 12:22-37--Pharisees denounce Jesus as a servant of Beelzebub. Blasphemy against the Holy Ghost. Galilee.

Matthew 12:38--Pharisees seek a sign. Galilee.

Matthew 12:39-45--Sign of Jonah is given. Parable of unclean spirit given as part of answer to earlier denunciation for denying witness. Galilee.

Matthew 12:46-50--Jesus' mother and brethren used to show how important disiciples are to Christ. Galilee.

DAY ELEVEN

Matthew 13:1-53 (and others given)--Parables about the future of the Kingdom. Sea of Galilee.

Matthew 13:3-23--The Sower. In the middle of this parable he explains why he is beginning to use such a method.

Luke 8:16-18--The Candle.

Matthew 13:24-30--The Tares.

The interpretation of the Tares is given in verses 34-43

Matthew 13:31-32--The Mustard Seed.

Matthew 13:33--The Leaven.

Matthew 13:34-43--Explanation of the Tares.

Matthew 13:44--Treasure in the field.

Matthew 13:45-46--Pearl of Great Price.

Matthew 13:47-50--Parable of the Net.

Matthew 13:51-52--Parable of the Householder.

DAY TWELVE

Matthew 13:53-58--Second Rejection at Nazareth

Matthew 14:1-12--The record of Herod's fear that Jesus is John returned from the dead to haunt him. How John died is recorded. (Machaerus).

Matthew 14:13-15--Jesus reacts to the news of John's death. Bethsaida.

Matthew 14:15-21--Feeding of the five thousand. Near Bethsaida.

Matthew 14:22-33--Jesus walks on the water and Peter joins him. Sea of Galilee.

John 6:22-71--Bread of Life Discourse. Capernaum.

John 7:1--Jesus stays away from "Jewry." Galilee.

Matthew 14:34-36--Further Healings. Gennesaret.

Matthew 15:1-20--Jesus' teaches about "traditions." Galilee.

Matthew 15:21-28--A daughter of the Canaanites is healed. Tyre and Sidon, in Northwest Palestine.

Matthew 15:29-31--Further healings. Galilee.

DAY THIRTEEN

Matthew 15:39--Matthew 16:4--Pharisees and Saducees ask a sign. Magdala.

Matthew 16:5-12--Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and Saducees. East side of the Sea of Galilee.

Mark 8:22-26--Healing a blind man by stages.

Matthew 16:13-20--Testimony of Peter and the promise by Jesus to deliver the sealing keys to them. Caesarea Philippi.

Matthew 16:21--Prophecy of coming death and resurrection. Caesarea Philippi.

Matthew 16:22-27--Peter is rebuked for not fully taking up the cross. Caesarea Philippi.

Matthew 17:1-13--Sealing keys restored. Mt. Tabor in Galilee.

DAY FOURTEEN

Matthew 17:14-21--Heals Demoniac child. Galilee.

Matthew 17:22-23--Jesus prophesies about his death and resurrection. Galilee.

Matthew 17:24-27--Tribute money in the mouth of a fish. Capernaum.

Matthew 18:1-6--Who is greatest in the kingdom. Galilee.

Matthew 18:7-35--Teaches about what he means in verse 6 about offending. Then teaches about the worst kind of offense--refusing to forgive.

verses 12-14--Parable of the Lost Sheep.

Verses 15-22--Leadership principles of avoiding offenses.

Verses 23-35--Parable of the Unmerciful Servant.

John 7:2-9--Time of the Feast of Tabernacles nears. Jesus' half brothers challenge him. Galilee, probably at Capernaum.

Luke 9:51-56--Lukes travel narrative as Jesus leaves Galilee for the last time.

Luke 10:1-16--More seventy are appointed. Probably a place along his journey south, possibly Samaria. They are charged and cities who do not accept them and their message are condemned.

Luke 10:17-20--The seventy return. Somewhere in Judaea.

Luke 17:11-19--Ten lepers are healed. Samaria Galilee. (this is listed in the Harmony as happening later, after leaving Perea).

Luke 10:25-37--A "lawyer" (probably a scribe from the temple) challenges Jesus who gives him the parable of the Good Samaritan. Judaea.

DAY FIFTEEN

Luke 10:38-42--At the home of Mary and Martha. Bethany.

Luke 11:1-13--Teaches his disciples more about prayer.

Luke 11:27-28--Hear and keep the word. Judaea.

Luke 12: 29-Repeats teaching on seeking a sign. Judaea.

Luke 12:13-21--Teaches against covetousness. Judaea.

verses 16-21--Parable of the Rich Fool.

Luke 12:22-37--Applies this teaching to his Apostles. Judaea. It is more in depth teaching of what he told them in their original charge.

Luke 13:1-9--Repentance involves change. Judaea.

Luke 13:10-17--Heals on the Sabbath again, is challenged, embarrasses the challengers. Perea.

Luke 13:18-21--Uses parables already taught to emphasize the point. Perea.

Luke 13:22--He takes his message into the villages of the area. Perea.

Luke 13:24-33--How to become saved and afriend warns of Herod's opposition. Perea.

Luke 14:25-35--warning to those who profess to be followers. Perea.

DAY SIXTEEN

Luke 16:1-13--Continues teaching about stewardship. Parable of the unjust steward. Perea.

Luke 16:14-15; 19-31--Jesus is derided by the Pharisees. He denounces them as fitting the parable, then gives them another. Perea.

Parable of the Rich man and Lazarus.

Luke 17:20-33--The Pharisees challenge him again. He gives a warning and culminates it with a fitting parable. Perea.

Luke 18:1-8--Parable of the unjust judge. Perea.

Luke 18:9-18--Parable of hypocrisy. Perea. (this comes later in the harmony).

John 7:10-13--Jesus leaves Perea and goes to Jerusalem to attend the Feast of the Tabernacles.

John 7:14-36--The doctrine of the Father. Jerusalem.

John 7:37-44--Jesus declares himself the living waters. (Partial fulfilment of the Feast of Tabernacles). Some believe, others question and wonder. The spirit works upon all. Jerusalem.

John 7:45-Chief Priests and Pharisees want him out of the way but are thwarted. Nicodemus defends him. Jerusalem.

DAY SEVENTEEN

John 8:1-11--The woman taken in adultery used to try and trap Jesus. Jerusalem.

Luke 8:12-30--He declares he is the light of the world. (Another fulfillment of the Feast of the Tabernacles). Bares testimony of his relationship with the Father. Jerusalem.

Luke 8:31-59--They must continue the work if they wish to be his disiciples and have further truth revealed to them. Discussion of what it means to be Abraham's seed. He declares he is "I AM." Jerusalem.

John 9:1-41--Blind man healed on the Sabbath. Did he or his parents sin? To manifest the works of God (Fulfil the Feast). Leaders refuse to see. Jerusalem.

John 10:1-21--Continues his teaching. (More fulfilment of the Feast). Jerusalem.

Matthew 19:3-12--Teachings on Marriage. The new law repeated. Perea.

Matthew 19:13-Blessedness of little children . Perea.

Matthew 19:16-26--The rich young ruler. Perea.

Matthew 19:27-30; Luke18:28-30; The greater riches. Perea.

Luke 18:28-30--Comparison.

DAY EIGHTEEN

Matthew 20:1-16--Parable--Laborers in the Vineyard--used to illustrate how he would give them the kingdom though others had could have had it. Perea.

John 10:22-39--Feast of the Dedication. Jerusalem.

John 10:40-42--Escapes to Bethabara. Teaches some of Johns followers who believe in him.

John 11:1-7--Jesus is summoned to the aid of Lazarus. Perea, probably Bethabara.

Matthew 20:17-19--Jesus prophesies once again of his coming sacrifice. Leaving Perea for Bethany.

Matthew 20:20-28--Who is greatest among the leaders of the kingdom? Bethabara.

Matthew 20:29-34--Two blind men are healed. Leaving Bethabara.

DAY NINETEEN

Luke 19:1-10--Dinner at the house of Zacchaeus. Outside Jericho.

Luke 19:11-27--Parable of the Pounds. A lesson on salvation. Outside Jericho.

John 11:8-53--Raising of Lazarus from the dead. Bethany.

John 11:47-53--Chief Priests and Pharisees are even more threatened and plot to kill him. Caiaphas unwittingly testifies of the coming sacrifice.

John 11:54--Jesus prepares for his final sacrifice at Ephraim, north of Jerusalem.

John 11:55-57--Time for Passover comes. Many look for Christ, the chief priests and Pharisees look to kill him.

SIXTH DAY BEFORE PASSOVER.

John 12:1-11--Jesus comes to Bethany. Annointed once more by Mary. The use of the ointment challenged by Judas Iscariot )keeper of the bag."

DAY TWENTY

FIFTH DAY BEFORE PASSOVER.

Luke 19:41-44--Jesus laments over Jerusalem. (the harmony has this after the triumphal entry but it seems to fit better here because of verse 41).

Matthew 21:1-11--Prepartions AND the Triumphal entry. Mt. of Olives and Jerusalem.

Luke 19:39-40--Some Pharisees try to stop the proceedings.

John 12:20-53--Some Greeks (defined as either those directly from Greece or those who followed after the Greek culture--therefore these may have been Gentiles) who have come to Passover seek him. It is hard to tell if the teachings that follow are given to them. Nevertheless, he teaches important doctrine on being sent by the Father.

Matthew 21:12-17--Cast out the moneychangers for the second time. Chief Priests and scribes sore displeased but take no action.

DAY TWENTY ONE

FOURTH DAY BEFORE PASSOVER

Matthew 21:18-22; Compare Mark 11:12-14, 20-26--Cursing of the fig tree. Bethany.

Matthew 21:23-46--His authority is challenged by chief priests and elders. He gives them several parables to denounce them. 70

Matthew 22:1-14--Parable of the Wedding of the King's son.

Back to Matthew 21:42-44. He drives home the point.

JST Matthew 21:47-56--Jesus denounces the chief priests and Pharisees.

Matthew 22:15-22--The Pharisees take action, and involve the Herodians.

Matthew 22:23-33--The Saducees make their attempt when the Pharisees fail.

Matthew 22:34-40--The Pharisees continue their efforts.

Matthew 22:41-46--Jesus tires of them and silences them with a doctrinal challenge of his own.

Matthew 23:1-36--Denunication of all those who had challenged him. He emphasizes their hypocrisy's.

Matthew 23:37-39--Jesus Laments the refusal of Jerusalem (probably the leaders he has just denounced) to accept him.

DAY TWENTY TWO

THIRD DAY BEFORE PASSOVER(?)

Matthew24. Please read Joseph SMith Matthew in the Pearl of Great Price.

DAY TWENTY THREE

Matthew 25:1-13--Jesus continues his teaching thorugh use of parables. Parable of the Ten Virgins.

Matthew 25:14-30--Parable of the Talents.

Matthew 25:31-46--Parable of the Sheep and Goats.

TWO DAYS BEFORE PASSOVER

Matthew 26:1-5--Jesus reiterates his coming death and states the method.

Matthew 26:6-13--Jesus annointed again.

Matthew 26:14-16--Judas takes first steps to betrayal.

Matthew 26:17-19--Arrangements for the Feast are made.

DAY TWENTY FOUR

EVE OF PASSOVER

Matthew 26:20-25; Compare to John 13:18-30. The feast begins. Jesus identifies his betrayer.

John 13:18-30

Matthew 26:26-29--Institution of the sacrament.

John 13:2-12--The washing of the Apostles feet.

John 13:31-35--A new commandment is given to the eleven.

John 14:1-14--Jesus comforts the disciples.

John 14:15-31--The comforter.

DAY TWENTY FIVE

John 15:1-8--A parable. The True Vine.

John 15:9-17--Emphasizes the earlier commandment to Love one another.

John 15:18-25--The world's rejection of Christ. They will also hate his followers.

John 15:26-27--The Comforter will be a greater witness, and they must testify as well. It is the only way to overcome the worlds opposition.

John 16:1-6--Warnings to the Apostles.

John 16:7-16--The role of the Comforter is emphasized for the third time.

John 16:17-30--The role of opposition--Joy and Sorrow.

John 16:31-33--Though the comforter will come and good things will happen, the flock will be scattered.

John 17:1-26--Jesus' intercessory prayer.

John 18:1--They go to Gethsemane.

DAY TWENTY SIX

Luke 22:31-34; Compare Matt. 26:33-35; Mark 14:29-31 and John 13:36-38 --Last counsel to Peter.

Matthew 26:36-46; Mark 14:32-42; Luke 22:40-46; John 13:36-38--Gethsemane.

Matthew 26:47-57; Compare Mark 14:43-52, Luke 22:47-54 and John 18:2-13--Judas' betrayal to Jesus arrest.

Matthew 26:57-68--Hearing before the Priests.

Matthew 26:69-75; Compare Mark 14:66-72, Luke 22:55-62 and John 18:15-18, 25-27. Peters denial.

Luke 22:63-65--Jesus is mocked by those who hold him.

Matthew 27:1-2; Luke 22:66-71--Final decision by Caiaphas, the "chief" priests and the elders.

Luke 23:1-6--Brought before Pilate.

Matthew 27:3-10--Judas' remorse and death.

DAY TWENTY SEVEN

Luke 23:7-11--Pilate sends Jesus to Herod.

John 19:13-15--Pilate sits in Judgement. Jewish leaders refuse Jesus as their king.

Luke 23:13-16--Pilate finds no fault in Jesus.

John 19:1-11 Pilate has him scourged with an intent to release him.

Matthew 27:19--Pilates wife sends him a message.

John 19:12--Jewish leaders threaten Pilate.

Mark 15:6-14. In last attempt to keep Jesus alive, Pilate trys to use a tradition, but the leaders thwart his plan and use it against Christ.

Matthew 27:24-26--Pilate washes his hands of the judgement and the people present, convinced by their leaders, accept responsibility.

Matthew 27:32-45 Golgotha.

John 19:25-27. John given direction to take care of Jesus' mother.

Matthew 27:46-Jesus' last hours on the cross. He makes final payment.

John 19:31-37--The soldiers pierce his side with a spear, and remove him from the cross.

Matthew 27:55-56--Followers watch from a distance.

Luke 24:50-56--Compare to the above.

Matthew 27:62-66--Chief Priests and Pharisees seal the tomb.

DAY TWENTY EIGHT

JST Matthew 28:1-3. TWO angels come and roll back the tomb.

John 16:1-8--Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of James come to prepare the body.

John 20:1-2--Mary Magdalene runs ahead of the others and tells Peter and John what has happened.

John 20:11-17--Mary sees the Lord.

Matthew 28:7-9--The other women go to the Apostles, but are met and blessed by the risen Lord.

Luke 24:10-11--The women make their report.

Matthew 28:11-Chief Priests hear and take steps to stop word from spreading.

Luke 24:13-35--Jesus appears to two disciples on the road to Emmaus. They return to Jerusalem and tell the eleven.

Luke 24:36-53--Jesus appears to his disciples in Jerusalem. The first ascension.

John 24:24-31--Thomas was not there. Christ appears unto him.

John 21:1-Jesus appears unto some of the Apostles at the Sea of Galilee.

Matthew 28:16-20--Meeting in Galilee where Jesus reemphasizes his charge to them.

Mark 16:19-20--The Ascension in Galilee.

Acts 1:3-25--The Final Ascension and reorganization of the Council of the Twelve.
