Occupational Outlook Activity Assignment:

  1. Access the Occupational Outlook Handbook
  2. Sample a minimum of  your top three career choices (you may select more if you desire)  Record these top three career choices in your New Student Journal and label it Journal Entry # 5.
  3. Record in your New Student Journal the important information provided to you from the Occupational Outlook Handbook as well as your answers to the questions that follow.  You should include the nature of the work, general description of the working conditions, employment statistics, training or other need qualifications, average salary, etc., as well as any other information of importance to you in your journal.
  4. Compare the three careers you have selected.  Do they still seem interesting to you?  Are they a good match for your interests and abilities?
  5. Has this search helped you in selecting a career?  Did you changed your original career interests or has this assignment confirmed your original career decisions?
