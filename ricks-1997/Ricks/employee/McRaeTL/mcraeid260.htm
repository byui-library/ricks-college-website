COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 260
Title: Contract Design II Credit: 3
Text: Specifications for Commercial Interiors, Reznikoff

Course Objective:
To introduce the student to design for a special population in a large project situation, i.e. elderly, disabled; persons suffering substance abuse, battered women, AIDS, and other healthcare and social issues.  To provide the student with a project that will give in depth experience in problem solution in regards to human factors, and sensitivity to design for the elderly, disabled, or those in need of special healthcare considerations.  To present by lecture pertinent topics relating to design for the above mentioned, i.e. accessibility, psychological effects of color and lighting, ADA requirements, etc.  To broaden understanding of “universal design” in relationship to barrier-free design.  To further develop oral, written, and presentation skills.  To evaluate the student’s competency by project completion, and oral presentation.  To provide the opportunity for team approach to design process and problem solution.  To further the student’s knowledge of ADA requirements, and require its use throughout the assigned project.
 
 Description:
 A course designed to give students advanced experience in contract design and research development; a concentration in healthcare for the disabled, persons with AIDS, persons suffering substance abuse, etc.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
 Each student will complete the assigned design project with accompanying list of requirements.  Each student will sit for a Midterm and a Final exam.  Each student or team will be required to initiate their own research for problem solution.  The student will be required to meet all deadlines as indicated in the course outline, or set by the student team.
