Physical Geography is an exciting topic because we study earth's physical systems which we encounter every day. What is El Nino, or La Nina, and how do they affect our weather? Why is the weather in Idaho different from Oklahoma? Why does Seattle receives lots of rain while it is dry in eastern Idaho. Why is it normally much warmer in Nebraska in the summer than along the coast of Oregon? Why are the winters of Nebraska much colder? Those from the eastern U.S. miss their trees and generally green landscape. (You'll learn that sagebrush also has its own special beauty). Have you ever visited the beautifully colorful desert of southern Utah and Arizona? Contrast the flat mesas of that region with the snow capped alpine regions of Canada & Alaska. Standing at the base of one the mesas is as awe inspiring as standing on a glacier in one of the world's colder regions. Where did the iceberg come from that sank the Titanic? Are there still icebergs in the North Atlantic? Are they a hazard for ships today? As you study for this course you will learn the answer to these questions and many more!
