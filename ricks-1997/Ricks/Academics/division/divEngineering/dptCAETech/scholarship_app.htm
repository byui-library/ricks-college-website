I. Instructions and Guidelines:

Application must be complete and signed, and submitted with all letters of recommendation and transcripts to Dale L. Larsen, Department Chairman, Department of Computer-Aided Engineering Technologies, Austin 171A, Ricks College, Rexburg, ID 83460 no later than March 1 for fall semester or 60 days prior to any other semester.

A. Eligibility

1. Applicant must be accepted to Ricks College, be officially enrolled in one of the department majors, and must maintain enrollment as a department major.
2. Applicant must be actively pursuing a degree (minimum of 12 credits) by enrollment in course work outlined by the Ricks College catalog and by consultation with an advisor.
3. Applicant must maintain academic excellence, GPA of 2.5 or above, during the active period of the scholarship.

B. Requirements

1. Completed (including a recent photograph of yourself) and signed application.
2. Two letters of recommendation from past school teachers, employers, supervisors, or administrators familiar with your ability who can support your scholarship application.
3. Official transcript of grades for prior year of schooling (if currently not enrolled at Ricks).

C. Notification of Scholarship Awards

Applications will be reviewed by the Scholarship committee and scholarship recipients will be notified within 30-90 days of the committee=s receiving the completed application.
