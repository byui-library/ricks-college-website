Sample of one-line footer

Personalizing BYU-Idaho Letterhead

 

 

 

 

Microsoft Word Instructions:

 

·    Change bottom margin to ½” by going to “File,” “Page Setup” on the menu bar.

 

·    Create a footer by selecting “View” on the menu bar, then “Header/Footer.” 

 

·    On the Header/Footer menu that pops up, choose the option “Switch Between Header/Footer” (three icons left of “Close,” verify by moving the mouse across until a tag line appears).

 

·    Center the information within the footer by using Ctrl E.

 

·    Set the footer font size to 8 point.

 

·    Use Times New Roman bold for your name, then hit the space bar four times.

 

·    Change the font to Times New Roman regular italic and type in your title and hit the space bar four times, type in your address and space four times, and type in your phone number and space four times.  (See sample below.)

 

·     When finished, exit footer editing mode by clicking close on the Header/Footer menu.

 

 

 

Sample of two-line footer

Personalizing BYU-Idaho Letterhead

 

 

 

 

Microsoft Word Instructions:

 

·        Change bottom margin to ½” by going to “File,” “Page Setup” on the menu bar.

 

·        Create a footer by selecting “View” on the menu bar, then “Header/Footer.”

 

·        On the Header/Footer menu that pops up, choose the option “Switch Between Header/Footer” (three icons left of “Close,” verify by moving the mouse across until a tag line appears).

 

·        Center the information within the footer by using Ctrl E.

 

·        Set the footer font size to 8 point.

 

·        Use Times New Roman bold for your name, then hit the space bar four times.

 

·        Change the font to Times New Roman regular italic and type in your title and hit the space bar four times, type in your address and space four times.

 

·        Whenever a second line is needed, stay in the same format and begin the second line with the phone number.  Add fax, e-mail, and cell phone information as desired. (See sample below.)

 

·        When finished, exit footer editing mode by clicking “Close” on the Header/Footer menu.
