The purpose of New Student Orientation is to assist new students in becoming familiar with the spiritual, academic, social, and personal learning opportunities associated with BYU-Idaho. 

 

 

Because there are so many aspects to attending BYU-I, a three-phase orientation program has been created.  The three phases of Orientation include:  (1) Pre-Campus Orientation, (2) Campus Orientation, and (3) On-Going Orientation. 

Pre-Campus Orientation:  Pre-Campus Orientation provides an extensive overview of BYU-Idaho and campus life.  Helpful hints, suggestions, and online resources can assist students in preparing for BYU-I before they arrive on campus.  This course is offered as a one credit course and fulfills the online course requirement needed for graduation.  

Campus Orientation:  Campus Orientation provides new students with opportunities to make new friends, learn more about their academic major, meet their academic advisor, and get better acquainted with the campus.  Students who participate in Campus Orientation are sure to have fun, make new friends, receive academic insights and instruction, render service, and be better prepared than their peers to succeed at BYU-I.

Leadership opportunities are available for returning students (sophomores) each year to assist in planning and implementing orientation activities and events.  More than 200 Y-Team Leaders are required for Fall Orientation and 7-10 NSO Team Leaders are also needed for Fall Orientation.

On-Going Orientation:  On-Going Orientation is designed to assist students with learning and life skills.  The New Student Orientation office co-sponsors and/or advertises various seminars, lab sessions, and skill sessions which take place throughout the year.  

Parent Orientation:  An extensive parent orientation program is held for on the first day of Fall, Winter, and 2nd Summer Term Orientation.  Parents of new students are encouraged to attend this event.
