STUDENT POLICY

 

 

All registered students can receive an ID card. A photo ID is required before a card will be issued (driver's license, passport, etc.). To receive the student activity pass, a student must be registered for 11.5 credits. A student carrying 6 to 11 credits can purchase the pass for $25. A nonstudent spouse of a registered student can be issued a free Ricks ID card. A spouse activity pass can be purchased for $25 if the registered students has 6 credit or more. Students taking classes through Continuing Education are not eligible to receive a Ricks ID card. The ID card is for the exclusive use of the student to whom it is issued. The card is invalid if the bearer does not comply with Ricks College standards as printed on the card. The ID card is the property of Ricks College and is not transferable. Its privileges will be canceled if the card is misused. One ID card is intended to last the entire time a student is at Ricks College. A $10 fee will be charged to replace a lost or damaged ID card.
