Past Wildlife Club Events:

26 January 2002- Trip to Jackson Elk Refuge

       

This is us looking at the elk.  It was a really fun day.  We saw the elk herd, along with a pack of wolves feeding on a bull elk.  We also saw several Bighorn sheep on the back side of the refuge.         It was an amazing trip.

9 February 2002- River Wildlife Habitat Work

We traveled to Hibbard to help cut and plant willows along the Henry's Fork of the Snake River to improve wildlife habitat and prevent further erosion along the banks of the river.  We were helping a landowner improve his portion of the river.  The willows that will be planted in the spring, along the river banks will hopefully sprout and provide a soil stabilizing root-system to prevent further erosion of the banks of the river.  It was a really good time.  We appreciate those who helped in this project.  It was great to help in rehabilitating such prime wildlife habitat.

31 March 2002- Birding Trip  Camas National Wildlife Refuge

We went up to Camas National Wildlife Refuge for a morning of birding.  We saw 49 different species of birds.  Some of the birds we saw were life list birds for me.  We saw 13 species of waterfowl.  We saw several species of hawks.  We saw a Northern Shrike, a short-eared owl, and a great horned owl.  It was an enjoyable trip.  Wish you could have been there!
