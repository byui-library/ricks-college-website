THE GOLDEN ERA OF BIG BANDS, JAZZ, AND SWING
Sit back and enjoy the sounds from Big Bands and Jazz groups of the past.  An enlightening look at the influence music had on such a dramatic period.  Hear songs and discuss the musical masters that dominated this Golden Era of music such as Gershwin, Lombardo, Ellington, Miller, Dorsey, and James.  Bring your dancin' shoes and enjoy dance instruction in the evening to your favorite "oldies" of the Big Band and Jazz Era. 

EMPIRE OF THE AIR:  A LOOK AT HISTORY THROUGH THE RADIO
Remember the years when you sat listening to the family radio?  Relive those memorable days during a week long look at radio in the early decades.  Trace historical memories through a study of some of the greatest moments in this past era.
