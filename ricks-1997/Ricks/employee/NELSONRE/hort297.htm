The Practicum class is designed to help students learn practical plant growing, display and sales skills by participating in an Enterprise Project.  Students apply for a particular indoor plant crop (Attached list, page 2).  They are assigned bench space in the greenhouse.  Students order stock, propagate, irrigate, fertilize, and otherwise care for the crop.  Students prepare plants for sale, arrange and display their plants, and help sell them during weekly plant sales.  Plants may also be sold to other retail outlets in the area.

 

Class meeting time:  1:00-1:50 each Thursday.  Students arrange additional time to work on their individual projects.  Students are also responsible to arrange at least one hour every other week to work during Friday plant sales (8 or more hours during the semester).  Your instructors will be available during class time to help you or you may contact them as needed during office hours.  You must meet at least once every two weeks with your assigned instructor/advisor to show your written records and your plant project.  It is the student’s responsibility to arrange for these meetings either during class time or at other arranged times.

 

Course Procedures and Requirements

            1.  The first seven pages of the Enterprise Agreement and Record forms must be completed by the second class period of the semester.  Price charts are included with this syllabus to assist in completing the budget form (page 7).  Greenhouse growing space will be assigned only after the seven pages are completed and signed.  Students may then order stock plants and begin their projects.

            2.  Students arrange their own time to care for their projects during the week.   They are responsible for all cultural practices including daily watering from Monday through Friday.  Projects will be watered on weekends as needed by greenhouse staff.

            3.  Records must be kept daily on time, expenses and income associated with the project (Enterprise Record pages 8 and 9).  These records as well as crop progress must be reviewed individually with your instructor every two weeks.  The student is responsible to arrange meetings.  Part of the course grade will be based upon regularity of meetings.

            4.  Students are responsible for pricing, labeling and preparing plants for plant sales.  Plants should be placed in flats on benches or carts in the headhouse by 9 am of plant sale days.

            5.  In addition to the normal growing and sales activities, students must complete two other activities listed on page 12 of the Enterprise Record during the semester.  These can be arranged for with the greenhouse manager, Bro. Reese Nelson, BEN 271, 356-2014.

            6.  Record pages 8 through 12 must be completed and submitted to the instructor by the last day of class.
