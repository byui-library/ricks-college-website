FLORAL DESIGN MANAGEMENT PROGRAM

Established at Ricks College in the spring of 1987 in response to the need in the floral industry for good, hardworking, honest young people.

The curriculum for the program has evolved over the years into what today provides college students with skills and experiences that open the doors of opportunity in retail floristry, mass marketing, wholesale product distribution, floriculture, and interiorscape design and maintenance. After completion of the first two semesters of the program, as part of the curriculum, each student is required to fulfill a cooperative work experience in the industry. It is required that the student work for several weeks to obtain real world experience. At the same time, the student, the employer, and the faculty supervisor evaluate the student's performance. Upon completion of the work experience, the student enters into an intense five week course of advanced floral design based on the individual needs of the student, and the other students in the class. The curriculum then moves through two more semesters of design, horticulture, communication, and business management skills. If you would like to view the curriculum, you may proceed to the catalog. The course syllabi may also be of interest.
