OSM Degrees Offered

The Office Systems Management department addresses the needs of students who wish to work on computers in the office environment. The following degrees are available:

  • One-year certificate (Major 174)
    (Non Transfer Program)
    This degree would enable a student who wishes to gain skills quickly to qualify for an entry level position in the office.

  • Associate Degree in Office Systems Management (Major 175)
    (Non Transfer Program)
    This degree would prepare a student for immediate entry as a qualified secretary, data typist, receptionist, medical or legal secretary, and possibly an administrative assistant or executive secretary.

  • Associate Degree in Arts and Science (Major 297)
    Those interested in earning an AAS degree and the two-year (175) degree must contact their advisor for help in scheduling the required classes.

  • Associate Degree in Arts and Science with a major in Office Systems Management (Major 314)
    This degree would enable students to have the required classes and sufficient skills to transfer to another college or university and work while obtaining their bachelor's degree.

The Office Systems Management department offers service courses for other career programs at Ricks College and provides an opportunity for career exploration for General Studies students with an interest in computers and the use of word processing and desktop publishing. Students who include these courses as electives while pursuing other majors have an added advantage when seeking employment. A background in word processing and business writing is a definite asset to almost every other major.
