INTRODUCTION: The objective of this class is that you gain both an appreciation for and new insights into the Book of Mormon. The goal is for you to know that the Book of Mormon truly is another witness for Jesus Christ.





TEXT: Book of Mormon and Student Manual





ATTENDANCE: Attendance will be taken in each class. (1) Students with excused absences (school field trip, illness under doctor's care, etc.) will not lose class credit if an excused absence form is brought to the teacher. (2) Unexcused absences (cut classes, slept in, etc.) will have the following policy: 3 unexcused absences = 100 pts; 4 = 50 pts; 5 = 40 pts; 6 = 30 pts; 7 - 20 pts; 8 - 10 pts; 9 = F. Perfect attendance will increase your grade!





READING: Your homework is to read the chapters assigned each class period and the student manual. Please mark those scriptures which are meaningful to you so you can participate in class discussions. Outline main points covered in the reading assignment and turn in a one page of pondering at the start of class (25 ponderings at 10 points each).





TESTING: Two tests will be given in class and a final exam during Test Week. Once you have been tested on those chapters, you will not be tested on them again; therefore, the final exam is not comprehensive.





PROJECT: 250 Points Due: March 21, 2000

You will have one major paper to write for the class. It will be five pages typewritten or computer printed. The theme for the paper is: "What the gospel means to me."





GRADING: Final grade will be based on points-

1. Ponderings (25 x 10) 250 points

2. Project 250 points

2. Tests 300 points

3. Attendance 100 points

Total 900 points
