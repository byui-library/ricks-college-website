The Department of Art programs are designed to provide a balanced foundation experience and build valuable skills in fine art, graphic design, illustration, and photography. The program will prepare students to successfully transfer to a university, art school, or enter the job market. Classes are also offered which fulfill the Arts requirement of General Education.

NOTE:

1. A minimum GPA of 2.7 in major courses is required for an Art degree. No D credit will be accepted in art or photo classes.

2. Art 290, Art Seminar is required every semester while enrolled as an art major.

3. All Art and Photography majors must select Art 103 as their GE Requirement in the Arts Category

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

Credits

Art 103* Introduction to the Visual Arts 3
No prerequisite

An art appreciation class surveying the ideas, materials, and history of the visual arts including painting, sculpture, design, architecture, and film.

Art History

Art 201* Art History I 3
No prerequisite

A survey of art from Prehistoric to Renaissance. Includes criteria for appreciating ancient art and its relationship to modern civilization.

Art 202* Art History II 3
No prerequisite

A survey of art from Renaissance to Modern, with an emphasis on art in its political, social, and religious context.

Art 211H* Art and Philosophy I 3
No prerequisite

The development of art styles from ancient times to the Renaissance, as those styles relate to the philosophy of each period. Includes criteria for evaluating works of art.

*Gives GE credit in the Arts area of the Arts & Letters requirement

Art 212H* Art and Philosophy II 3
No prerequisite

The development of art styles from the Renaissance to the 20th century, as those styles relate to the philosophy of each period. Includes criteria for evaluating works of art.

Drawing

Art 101 General Art, 2-D 2
No prerequisite
(Non majors only,) Supplies Fee $25

 

An introduction to the visual arts through drawing and painting (2-D). Art elements and design principles will be applied to these areas.

Art 110 Basic Drawing 3
Prerequisite: Enrolled as an Art major or consent of instructor (some Art 110 classes are available for non art majors)

Fundamentals of perspective, structural drawing, light and shade and basic composition.

Art 115 Figure Drawing 3
Prerequisite: Enrolled as an Art major or consent of instructor
**This course may be repeated for an additional three credits

Proportion, structure, composition, and various media will be introduced for interpreting and depicting the human figure.

Art 215 Head Drawing 3
Prerequisite: Art 110 or 115
(Art major only or consent of instructor)
**This course may be repeated for an additional three credits

Concentrated study of the human head, including structure, planes, and features, using various approaches and materials.

Design

Art 120 Design and Color 3
Prerequisite: Art 110 or 115
(Art and Pre Architecture majors only or consent of instructor)

An exploration of the elements and principles of design, with emphasis on color theory.Estimated cost for materials: $100

Estimated cost for materials: $160

Art 235 Watercolor 3
Prerequisite: Art 110 and 120
(Art major only or consent of instructor)
Travel Fee: $10
**This course may be repeated for an additional three credits

Survey and application of various tech-niques of traditional transparent watercolor painting with an introduction to other materials, such as egg tempera and acrylic.
Estimated cost for materials: $160

No prerequisite
