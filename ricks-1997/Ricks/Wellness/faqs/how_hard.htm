How hard should I exercise?

Your maximum heart rate is the fastest your heart can beat. Exercise above 75% of your maximum may be too strenuous unless you are in excellent physical condition. Exercising below 60% gives your heart and lungs little conditioning.

Therefore, the best activity level is 60 to 75% of this maximum rate. This is called your target zone.

When you begin your exercise program aim for the lower limit of your target zone. As you gradually get into better shape build up your to the higher part of your target zone.

To find your target zone, look for your age category closest to your age and read across.
