You will need the following if you are staying in men's on-campus non-cooking style housing:

 
  • Bedding - extra long twin sheets, blankets, pillow, pillowcases
  • Towels/wash cloths
  • Personal items
  • Soap and other toiletries
  • Hangers
  • Alarm clock
  • Flashlight - use of matches or candles are prohibited
  • Microwave dishes if desired
  • Cleaning supplies for cleaning the sink in the room.  (Bathrooms are cleaned daily by a custodial crew.)
  • Sink scrubber
  • Laundry soap
  • You may bring a toaster and a hot-air popcorn popper; no hot plates please.
