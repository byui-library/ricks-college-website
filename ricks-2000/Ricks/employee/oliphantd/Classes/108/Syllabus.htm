Course Requirements:  There will be 12 labs in the semester with one makeup lab at the end.  You may only miss one of the 12 regularly scheduled labs because of sickness, college sponsored activities or other instructor approved absence. Come talk with me if you need to miss more than one lab.  The makeup lab at the end of the semester is only for those who missed a lab.  It is not intended for those who want to redo a lab with a poor grade.

Write-up

As a part of each lab you will be asked to turn in a write-up.  This will consist of an introduction or Abstract, the body and a conclusion.  The write-up should be detailed enough so that someone reading it would be able to repeat the lab.

 

Introduction or Abstract:

-         Brief description of the physics principle that the lab will cover

-         A few sentences about the experimental set-up, equipment, etc.

Body:

-         Algebra and other math notes.

-         Results of analysis of data.

-     Graphs, diagrams, etc.

-     Main points of the lab.

Conclusion:

-         How the lab taught you the physics principles.

-         Any new insights about the physics involved.

-         What you have learned in this lab.

-         Applications of principles learned to other situations.

Quiz:

At the end of each lab there will be a short quiz.  It will usually consist of about 5 questions.  The questions will be related to the physics principles covered in the lab.

 

Grading:  Each lab will be worth 50 points. With 12 labs there are 600 total possible points.  Each lab will have a written part worth 40 points and a short quiz worth 5 points. The other 5 points will be determined by your participation within your group.  You will grade yourself on these five points.  If you feel you have participated in and contributed to your group give yourself a 5.  If you come late or leave early or if you have not done your part in helping the group give yourself less.
