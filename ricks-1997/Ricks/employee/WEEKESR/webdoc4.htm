Course Overview:

As the course title implies, this class is designed to help the student become familiar with the
tools, techniques, and usage skills for basic TV production. The instructor assumes that the
student has no (or little) prior experience in the use of the equipment or in performing in
professional TV settings.

Communication 256 is a prerequisite for this course.
Communication 256 and Communication 259 cannot be taken concurrently.

Materials Needed for this Class:
You need to purchase one (1) SVHS (T-120) video cassette. You will only be able to find this

The general layout of the class is described below:

1. The first segment of the class is reserved for learning to use the basic tools of television
production. This includes the camcorder, videotape editor, character generator, switcher, audio board, teleprompter, overhead camera, and studio cameras.

2. The second segment is designed to enhance your skills with these tools by filling assignments
of shooting and editing videotape into usable packages such as news stories and commercials.
You will be working on your various projects as small production teams.

3. The third segment helps the student further develop presentation skills by working in a
simulated newscast environment in the technical area or by actually performing in anchor
positions.

4. At various times during the semester, you will be assigned different responsibilities. After you have learned to use the equipment and are able to produce various packages, you will be given assignments that may be different from your classmates. For instance, you may be assigned to produce a news story while your classmate may be assigned to run a studio camera during the
in-class activity. Be prepared to respond to these assignments.

Lab Assignments:
(All lab assignments will be completed by a small production team of two or three  team members)

1.  Your team will produce a one- to two-minute video postcard of the Ricks College campus.  All shots must be exterior shots of the campus, students, and campus activities.  Be creative regardless of the weather conditions.  You will present a storyboard in class of what you plan on videotaping.  Your video postcard must contain a variety of wide, medium, close-up, and extreme close-up shots.  You will not be able to use the videotape editor to complete this project.  Thus, all scenes must be shot in sequence and at one time.  Points will be deducted from videos that have a break (“snow”) between any shots.  All scenes must be stable else points will be deducted.  Remember: the purpose of this project is to create a promotional video to send to prospective Ricks College students.  What did you want to know about Ricks
before you came here?

2.  This is a continuation of your first assignment.  You will be able to correct any mistakes or bad scenes in your first assignment by reshooting them, etc.  This project will be able to be edited on the linear videotape editing system.  Your team’s completed project should last sixty seconds exactly, fading up from back and down to black and the video’s beginning and end.  You will also need to add an appropriate musical sound bed.  Remember: the purpose of this project is to create a sixty second promotional video to send to prospective Ricks College students.  What did you want to know about Ricks before you came here?

3.  Your team’s assignment is to create a 30-second video open with narration and music for our simulated newscasts.  This project will go together easier if you first create your narration/music sound be in one of the two audio production labs and then edit your video to audio in the video lab.  You will be allowed to use up to fifty percent of your video from
a network newscast for such things as international/national news events.  The remainder of your video must be videotaped by your team to depict local scenes.  Your video must also open and close with a “KWBH-TV News” logo that you will create using Toaster CG.  This logo will be keyed over your video at the beginning and end.

4.  Your team’s assignment is to create a 30- or 60-second commercial selling a real product.  This should be done on location.  If you are going to videotape in a place of business, make sure you get permission from the manager several days in advance.  Don’t just show up planning to create a commercial without permission.  If permission isn’t granted, you’ll have to revert to Plan B.  To create a properly produced commercial, you must use the wireless microphones if
there are speaking parts on-camera.  Make sure the scenes in the commercial are properly lit with our portable lights if you are shooting indoors.  Again, use a tripod.  Shaky or jiggley video is not acceptable.

5.  Your team’s assignment is to create a 1 ½ to 2 minute news or sports story.  Again, this should be of some real news or sports happenings in the Ricks College/Rexburg community.  The same requirements and suggestions of assignment four pertain to this assignment.  If your team is having a difficult time determining your subject matter, meet with your instructor.  There are plenty of topics of student interest to be found within our community.

6.  This is the only assignment that you will do as an individual.  You will create a final production tape (on VHS not SVHS tape) that will include assignments 2 through 5.  At the beginning and end of the tape, you will need to create a CG graphic that includes your name and your permanent mailing address.  These two graphics should be on the screen for ten seconds.  After the first graphic, insert two seconds of black and then create another graphic that introduces assignment two and what your responsibilities were.  Insert two more seconds of black and then insert your assignment two onto your tape.  After the assignment is over, insert another two seconds of black before inserting your graphic that identifies assignment three.  This is the format you should follow until all assignments are on your tape.  This tape will
be viewed during the final exam period.  As I have stated earlier, no early exams will be allowed.  So please do not plan on leaving town until the school’s final exam period is done.  In the long run, this tape will be something you can show potential employers and other colleges/universities for entrance into their program or scholarship consideration.
