1. Locate your chapter in the LDS Collectors Library Standard Works and print the entire chapter. In order to do this, you must block out the entire chapter. This is done in the following manner. (1) Place the curses at the beginning of the chapter. Then hold down the left button on the mouse. Move the curser down until you have blocked the entire chapter. Then hit the print button on the tool bar. Then hit the OK button.
 

2. If there is a JST change, locate one and print it. If your chapter does not have a JST change, find one in another chapter and print it.
 

3. Click on one of the verse numbers. A little box will appear. Click on "commentary." If there is a statement made by someone, block it and print it.
 

4. Click on "search" at the top of the screen. Do a search in "Search the entire library." Type in a phrase from one of the verses in your chapter. Try to locate a statement made by someone about that phrase or word that is not found in the statements located under the "commentary." On the top of the sheet you hand in, write down the phrase you queried. (To query a phrase, you must put it in quote marks.)
 

5. Print a bibliographic reference of any book from the LDS Collectors Library
