Perfectly situated next to Yellowstone National Park, West Yellowstone is the ideal Elderhostel location.  Enjoy Rocky Mountain wilderness forests in this small alpine resort community nestled in the pines, with easy access to the Park.  The resort area, on the border of Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming is 90 miles northeast of Ricks College.  Lodging, meals, and classrooms are in a comfortable motel with double occupancy, private bath, limited singles, extra charge -- all just minutes from field study locations for courses and wildlife viewing.  No alcohol permitted on site.
