Saturday Conferences

Saturday Conferences are large retreats (100+ students) designed to give students instruction in leadership or personal development principles and skills. The Gospel is the fundamental framework by which all instruction is given.

We usually leave at 8:30 a.m. and return about 5:00 p.m. The usual format for these retreats includes a keynote speaker, lunch, activities, and afternoon workshops. Come join us for a day of spiritual feasting, new insights, personal growth, and fun.

Registration

You can sign up for the conference in person at our office in the Manwaring Center, room # 380, starting two weeks before the scheduled date. Registration is limited and is handled on a first-come, first-served basis. The cost is $3 and includes lunch and transportation.
