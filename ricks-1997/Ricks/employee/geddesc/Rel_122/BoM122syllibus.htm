Course Objectives


l. To increase your knowledge, love, and testimony of The Book of Mormon by:

a. Learning the story line of the second half of The Book of Mormon.

b. Understanding the life-changing doctrines of The Book of Mormon.

c. Searching for and finding many of the lessons Mormon wanted us to learn.

d. Studying and utilizing these lessons in order to liken them unto ourselves

e. Focusing on the invitation of The Book of Mormon to "Come Unto Christ."



Course Policies and Procedure



1. PREPARATION CHECKS: You should be prepared each class time for a reading preparation quiz over the reading assignment for that day. All quizzes will be averaged to be worth 100 points total. No makeup quizzes.



2. ATTENDANCE AND PARTICIPATION: Crucial and calls down the power of mercy to temper the law of justice. If you must be absent, get notes and assignments from a class mate. Each absence causes a loss of points unless cleared by the instructor. Roll will be taken each time.



3. APPLICATION PAPERS: You will be required to write two papers ( each 2-3 pages in length) during the semester, taking a scripture block or passage that we have studied and apply a principle or doctrine from the scripture to your life. You will write it in the form of a talk that you could use in a sacrament meeting, for example. You will be graded on presentation ( appearance and neatness), depth of thought, and content. The first paper will be due Feb. 21. The second will be due April 9. Each paper will be worth 100 points.



4. ARTICLES: You will read six articles that you will obtain from my web page at http://www.byui.edu/Ricks/employee/GEDDESC/default.htm and write a brief summary of article to be handed in on the days listed on the reading schedule. Each article summary will be worth 50 points, 300 total. To receive the full 50 points, the summary must be type-written, on time, and show that you have thoughtfully read the article and understood the main points.



5. PORTFOLIO: On the day of the final you will bring your Book of Mormon 122 Portfolio. This portfolio is intended to be of lasting spiritual and practical value. It will include your article summaries, your two application papers and your class notes. Make it neat and nicely presented, something that you will want to keep and refer to for years to come. This will be worth 200 points.
