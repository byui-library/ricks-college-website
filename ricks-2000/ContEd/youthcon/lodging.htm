LODGING
 

We have camping areas where the participants will stay in

tents or tepees provided by BYU-Idaho. They will use

outhouses located near the camping areas. Meals will be

prepared at our kitchen facility and served in

one of the pavilions.

 

At your request, your group can also be assigned to ten

sleeping cabins available for groups. Each cabin contains three bunk

beds and a small heating stove.  Outhouses are located near the cabins.
