Ricks College exists to provide an education consistent with the religious and family values taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The college seeks to maintain a balanced and diversified student body representative of worldwide Church membership.

The mission of the college is to build testimonies of the restored gospel of Jesus Christ, provide a quality education in a wholesome environment, and prepare students for further education, employment and their roles as citizens and parents.

In selecting a class of about 4,000 new students, the Admissions Committee considers the following: church, seminary and institute activity (LDS); personal commitment to living Ricks College Standards; academic preparation; out-of-class activities; geographic location; gender and ethnicity.

Admissions requirements for New Freshmen, Transfer Students, Former Students and International students are reviewed below.

To be considered for admission, the following materials must be submitted before the deadline (see deadlines).

New Freshmen

  • Application -- The application can either be submitted on-line or by requesting a paper application (please refer to the links to the on-line application or application request form below). Information provided includes personal data, educational plans, high school information, and personal essay/out-of-class activities.
  • Personal Recommendations -- Recommendations are required from an applicant's current ecclesiastical leader and seminary/institute teacher (LDS). The required forms are provided in the application and will be sent to those applying on-line.
  • Application Fee -- $15 for on-line applicants and $25 for application sent by mail (non-refundable).
  • American College Test (ACT) or Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) Scores -- All applicants must take the ACT or SAT. For specific test dates, check with your high school counselor. Be sure to request your scores be sent to Ricks College.
  • High School Transcript -- Have an official transcript sent to the Admissions Office.

Transfer Students

  • Application -- The application can either be submitted on-line or by requesting a paper application (please refer to the links to the on-line application or application request form below). Information provided includes personal data, educational plans, high school/college information, and personal essay/out-of-class activities.
  • Personal Recommendations -- Recommendations are required from an applicant's current ecclesiastical leader and seminary/institute teacher (LDS). The required forms are provided in the application and will be sent to those applying on-line.
  • Application Fee -- $15 for on-line applicants and $25 for application sent by mail (non-refundable).
  • Transfer Credits -- Transfer students must have the Registrars Office of each school attended mail an official transcript to the Admissions Office. If you have taken courses at an LDS Institute of Religion, an institute transcript must also be sent. All transcripts must be received before admission consideration is given. In cases where applicants are applying with less than 12 semester (18 quarter) hours, a high school transcript and ACT/SAT scores must sent to Ricks College.

  • Personal Recommendation -- Recommendations are required from an applicant's current ecclesiastical leader. The required form will be provided in the application and will be sent to those applying on-line.
  • Transfer Credits -- Former students who have attended another school since Ricks transcript to the must have the Registrars Office of each school mail an official Admissions Office. If the student has taken courses at an LDS Institute of Religion, an institute transcript must also be sent.

International Students

In addition to the requirements listed for U.S. citizens international students must submit the following:

  1. International Student Deposit of $1,200 U.S. dollars (not required of Canadian students).
  2. TOEFL (Test of English as a Foreign Language) results required of all students from non-English speaking countries (a minimum score of 500 is required).
  3. ACT (American College Test) scores are required of all students from English speaking countries.
  4. Financial Certification and Affidavit of Support -- A supplemental form for international students containing the Financial Certification forms are available on request (see directory) These forms will be provided for those applying on-line.
  5. U.S. High School Equivalency -- each international student must submit proof that he/she has completed the equivalent of a U.S. high school education. The following list will serve as a guideline for various countries:
  • CANADA -- Submit official transcript listing all high school credit completed at the time of application. However, students are required to complete 12 years of a secondary program and complete all graduation requirements. Official transcript showing completion of all graduation requirements must then be submitted before students can matriculate at Ricks.
  • CENTRAL AND SOUTH AMERICA -- Complete 12 years of secondary school with graduation. Minimum of Sobresaliente, Excelente or Muy Bueno is required.
  • BRITISH patterned education (United Kingdom, East and West Africa, Caribbean area, East Asia) -- General Certificate of Education showing five "O" (ordinary) levels. The subjects must be passed with a satisfactory grade.
  • INDIA, BANGLADESH, PAKISTAN -- Complete the Higher Secondary School Certificate.
  • AUSTRALIA, NEW ZEALAND -- Higher School Certificate with University Entrance Certificate.
  • FRENCH patterned education (France, Middle East, Africa, Caribbean area) -- The baccalaureate with a minimum standard of "BIEN."
  • JAPAN, KOREA, PEOPLES REPUBLIC OF CHINA, REPUBLIC OF CHINA -- Complete 12 years of secondary school with graduation or General Certificate of Education.
  • EUROPE -- Complete the academic secondary school program with evidence of a leaving school certificate or examination certificate. (Example: Getuigschrift, Student-examenbetyg, Examin Artium, Arbitur, Apolitirion).
  • MIDDLE EAST (Israel, Iran, Jordan, etc.) -- Complete the national secondary school certificate examinations.
  • PHILIPPINES -- Complete secondary program with leaving certificate.
  • HONG KONG -- Complete the General Certificate of Education showing at least five passes with no more than two "E" grades.

If you have any questions, please contact the International Student Admissions International Student Admissions Office.
