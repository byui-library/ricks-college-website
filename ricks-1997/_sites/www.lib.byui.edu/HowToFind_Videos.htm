How to Find Videos

Videos are located on the third floor in the Media Services area. You may view a video next door in the Video Viewing area at an individual station or in one of the group viewing rooms. To find a video in the McKay Library:

1. Click on C CATALOG OF BOOKS AND MORE (WHAT WE OWN) in the top left hand corner of the library's homepage.

2. This will bring up the iPAC Search page. Select Expanded Search from the top bar.

3. Before you begin your search, scroll down until the limiting fields section is displayed. Limit the collection field by selecting Media Services (Videos). Return to the top of the screen. Use general keywords for your search. Type any word(s) from the title or subject or description in the general keyword box. This will bring up any videos that have those keywords in the title, subject, description, etc.

4. The title of the video will be highlighted in blue and underlined. If you would like to see a description of this video, just click on the blue title. If there is only one response to the search the entire bib record will be displayed, including description, length, year produced, any specific actors, etc.

5. Faculty may request delivery of a video tape, you will need to either call the scheduling office at 2356, or email Judy Steiner at steinerjl@byui.edu.
