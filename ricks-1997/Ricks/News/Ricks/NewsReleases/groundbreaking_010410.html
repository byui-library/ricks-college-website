Ricks College News Release
April 10, 2001 
Ricks to break ground for new building

REXBURG, Idaho — Ground breaking services for a 54,000 square-foot, multi-use building on the Ricks College campus will be held Wednesday, April 18, at 2 p.m. in the Taylor Building chapel.

Elder F. Melvin Hammond of the First Quorum of the Seventy of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints will preside at the event. Students and employees of Ricks College and the general public are invited to attend.

Following the services participants may join at the building site south of the parking lot adjacent to the Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building for the formal ground breaking ceremony.

The two-story building, which will not be named at this time, "will primarily serve a campus stake and eight wards," says James Smyth, administrative vice president at Ricks College. The building’s secondary use will be for general academic purposes, Smyth says. It will contain approximately 15 classrooms and 25 offices. A permanent chapel, coupled with an adjoining cultural hall and stage area, will seat about 1,600.

"This building fits our needs very well," Smyth says in relation to the continuing transition of Ricks College to Brigham Young University-Idaho. "We chose to use a ‘standard plan’ design used by the Institutes of Religion of the Church because it can be constructed very quickly."

Completion date for the building is projected for August 2002 when the expected enrollment will be about 9,800 students, some 800 above the current enrollment.

Jacobsen Construction of Salt Lake City is the contractor for the project. The architectural firm that adapt the standard plan is Jensen Haslem Architects of Logan, Utah.

The area around the new building will be landscaped to tie in with the college’s garden area to the west. Parking will be placed east of the building adjacent to South Second East Street. Some site preparation work has preceded the ground breaking.
