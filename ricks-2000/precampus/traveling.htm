Traveling to BYU-Idaho

The office of Public Relations has a website with detailed information on student travel options to Rexburg.  

You can also visit the Rexburg City website and the Chamber of Commerce website for additional community information.  

Follow this MapQuest Link for Rexburg City maps or to calculate the precise road travel directions from wherever you live in the Continental U.S. to any street address in Rexburg.  Once you have submitted your address, you will want to select "driving directions" option at the top of the new screen.  In case you don't know your apartment street address in Rexburg, you may enter the college address (525 South Center) along with the 83440 Zip Code, which is the Zip Code for all of Rexburg, except for the college, which is 83460.
