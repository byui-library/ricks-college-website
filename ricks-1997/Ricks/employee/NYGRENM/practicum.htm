Course Purpose

The purpose of the Entrepreneurship Practicum is to allow students to integrate all of their learned business skills into teaching and sharing the principles of free enterprise, supporting S.I.F.E., and engage in experiential based, hands on practical experiences.  You will also assist local businesses in a consulting capacity, depending upon the needs of the local business community.

Objectives

Upon completing the entrepreneurship practicum, you should be able to:

  • Achieve a solid foundation in core subjects and a thorough understanding of the entrepreneurial enterprise

  • Commit to continuous professional development and lifelong learning through deeper understanding of life goals, self-management of learning skills, and deeper understanding of the entrepreneurial process

  • Develop an understanding of the forces of change, such as technology and globalization, to enhance your managerial and decision-making skills.

  • Extend your logical and critical thinking skills, including the capacity to think beyond boundaries and to engage in integrating analysis across disciplines.

  • Strengthen your interpersonal skills, including team participation, team building, and leadership.

  • Increase your commitment to integrity and ethical conduct in personal and professional pursuits.

Grading Components

Evaluation:  Grades will be based on the following point system.  700 possible

  1. ENTREPRENEURIAL BOOK REPORT..................................................................200 Points
    You will be responsible for reading the book entitled, "The Effective Entrepreneur."  Provide a one to two page typed summary of the book.  You will also be assigned a chapter to share an oral presentation with your team.  This should be a professional presentation with power point, and additional resources that would supplement your assigned chapter.

  2. SELECTED READINGS.............................................................................................140 Points
    You will be given an assignment to complete several selected readings. This assignment will include a single or double spaced summary of the article: what you liked and did not like, and how you plan to apply the information contained therein.  Please come prepared to also share your comments and thoughts regarding these articles. Each article will be worth 20 points.  

  3. SIFE PROJECTS/SERVICE LEARNING.................................................................300 Points
    For this component of your grade, you will be participating or assisting in creating three SIFE Projects.  These will consist of projects here on Ricks Campus, into the local schools, assisting businesses, or simply providing a community service.  Information on SIFE projects can be found at www.byui.edu/clubs/sife or at www.sife.org.  This will also give you the opportunity to gain hours towards becoming a SIFE scholar and having a resume submitted to a database of several fortune five hundred.  To become a SIFE Scholar you need to earn 50 hours or more on SIFE related projects or activities.  This database will serve as a great networking tool for you in the near future.

  4. GUEST SPEAKERS.....................................................................................................100 Points
    Throughout the semester we will have various guest speakers. Please take notes and write up a one page summary.

  5. EXECUTIVE SEMINARS, ENTREPRENEUR CONF., BUSINESS SUMMIT...............60 Points
    Each semester there will be various executives coming to speak on campus.  You will be required to attend these executive seminars and submit a one page, double or single-spaced, typed summary of what you learned and plan to apply from the presentation.

Class Policies and Important Points

1. No assignment will be accepted late. You need to learn to be responsible and complete your assignments on time. If an assignment is late due to a medical emergency or family tragedy, leave a message on my telephone and I will work with you. Trips home, etc. are not excuses for late work unless emergencies exist, so plan accordingly. You may turn work in early if you are planning to miss a class.

2. The Code of Honor and Dress & Grooming standards apply and will be enforced.

3. Plan on taking the final exam at the date and time indicated on the schedule. Check travel plans now. I cannot accommodate everyone who wants to leave early.

4. If you have a diagnosed disability, or believe you have a disability that may require reasonable accommodation on the part of Ricks College, please call 1-800-we-care to make an appointment with Red Taylor, Director at the center for students with disabilities.  "In compliance with the applicable disability law, qualified students with a disability may be entitled to 'reasonable accommodation.'  It is the student's responsibility to disclose to the SSD office any special needs he/she may have and to provide the appropriate documentation of the disability.  The SSD office is located inside the Learning Assistance Center, McKay Library 1st floor."

5. The emphasis in this course will be on material discussed in class; therefore, attendance is very important. Because the course will be conducted primarily on a discussion basis, it is very important that the student read the text and other materials so that he or she is prepared to participate in class discussions.

6. Learning outside the classroom: What happens out of the class can be as important, or more important than what happens in the class. For every hour of class, you should spend 3 to 6 hours a week in out-of-class learning activities. Learn to Love Learning!

7. Attendance Policy: One of the most important parts of your grade and certainly what you get out of the class will be your attendance. We both have the responsibility to be active participants in the classroom. If your not here, we all miss out!! Attendance will effect your grade as follows:

1-2 absences = no grade drop 6-7 absences=2 grade drop
3-4 absences = l grade drop 8 or more = F grade

8. Learning Styles: We all have different learning styles. It is challenging to hit every ones Hot Button every time we meet. As a teacher, you can interest all of your students some of the time; you can interest some of your students all of the time; but you can't interest all of your students all of the time.
