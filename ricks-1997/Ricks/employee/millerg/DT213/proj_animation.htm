Basic Animation Project

AThey say unto him, Caesar's. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's.@, Matthew 22:21.

Use 3D Studio to create a simple animation of Project #2 (Shock Assembly) as discussed below and demonstrated in class. Begin with an exploded assemble of Project #2. Animated each item in succession as listed:

FRAME

DESCRIPTION OF EVENTS
0-60 CAMERA PAN - Pan camera around entire assembly in circular fashion, returning to starting point
60-90 PIVOT - Scale pivot to twice its size, then return to normal size
90-120 BUSHINGS - Move/rotate the bushings simultaneously in a semi-circular path 180 degrees to the opposite side of the assembly (i.e. bushings change places with each other), then move in a straight line motion to their assembled position in the shaft
120-150 U-SUPPORT, sequential motion - Rotate the u-support 180 degrees, then move to assembled position in-line with bushings
150-180 SHAFT - stretch the shaft (approximately three times its length and one third its diameter) while moving to the bottom of the u-support then return the shaft to its normal size
180-210 BRACKET, simultaneous motion - rotate 360 degrees about horizontal axis while moving to assembled position
210-240 CAMERA MOVE - Move camera to a position Aunderneath@ so that lower end of the washer and nut may be clearly seen during their assembly operation
240-270 WASHER - Move the washer very close to the camera while rotating about 1 axis, then about a 2nd axis, and then return the washer to the assembly position and slide the washer on the end of the shaft until it comes in contact with the bracket
270-300 NUT - move the nut to the beginning of the threads on the shaft, then rotate (threading motion) several complete revolutions until the nut rests against the washer
300-330 CAMERA MOVE - Move the camera to a different isometric position Aabove@ so that the bolt, nut, and cotter key may be clearly seen during their assembly operation.
330-360 BOLT - slide the bolt into the assembly into its proper position
360-390 NUT - move the nut to the beginning of the threads on the bolt, then rotate (threading motion) several complete revolutions until the nut rests against the assembly.
390-420 COTTER KEY - move the cotter key in 2 sequential moves (above the hole, then insert into the hole) to its final assembled position

Extra Credit: Zoom in tight on the cotter key and Abend@ it so that it won=t fall out. Use as many frames as needed
420-500 CAMERA PAN - zoom in closer on completed assembly and completely pan around part.

Create an avi file of your animation at a resolution of 320x240. Use appropriate lighting effects and materials. Turn in an electronic copy of the avi file in the DropBox.
