Pinewood Derby Simulator Requirements
=====================================

Nonfunctional requirements (constraints)
----------------------------------------
- Provide instructions as a readme.txt file including, but not limited to,
  how to compile and start the Pinewood Derby Simulator.
- The simulator shall be written in C++.

Functional requirements
-----------------------
   Interface
01 - Provide a menu interface; at a minimum, the menu shall provide the
     following options:
     - Display credits
     - Display basic instructions
     - Design and save cars
     - Setup tournament
     - Execute tournament
02 - The "display credits" option shall output credits (i.e., software
     designers and programmers)
03 - The "display basic instructions" option shall output basic instructions
     regarding how to run the simulator
04 - The "design and save cars" option shall allow the user to design and
     save multiple cars
05 - The "setup tournament" option shall provide two options: Select cars
     and Select race schedule 
06   - The "select cars" suboption shall allow the user to select the cars
       to compete for the best of show and races
07     - A list of previously designed and saved cars shall be presented
         to the user
08     - The user shall select from this list
09   - The "select race schedule" shall allow the user to select either
       single elimination or double elimination
10   - The simulator shall not allow the execution of the tournament
       until after the user has selected cars and a race schedule
11 - The "execute tournament" option shall display tournament results;
     two options shall be provided: Display best of show and Display
     race results
12   - The "display best of show" suboption shall display the best of
       show winners
13   - The "display race results" option shall display the results of
       each race and the race tournament winner

   Design and save multiple cars
14 - The user shall be able to design multiple cars
15 - The simulator shall provide eight pre-designed cars for the user to
     choose from; the characteristics of the pre-designed cars are left to
     the designer
     - The user shall be able to modify the following characteristics:
16     - Wheel type
17     - Axle type
18     - Extra weight
19     - Weight location
20     - Color
21     - Paint job
22     - Who helped
23     - Lubrication
24     - Extras (e.g., spoiler, fins, decals)
25 - Each characteristic shall have three options (for example, three
     quality levels of poor, average, and excellent)
26 - Each option for each characteristic shall be assigned values to be
     used in determining best of show and success in the race; the actual
     values are left to the designer
27 - Each option of each characteristic shall have an associated cost in
     points
28 - The user shall have a limited quantity of points to "spend" on the
     modifications; the actual amount is to be determined by the designer
29 - The user shall not have enough points to build the "perfect" car
30 - Each option of each characteristic shall have an associated weight
     (in ounces); the actual weights are to be determined by the designer
31 - The simulator shall not allow any car to exceed 5.0 ounces
32 - The user shall be able to save each car using a user-specified name
33 - The user-specified name for a car shall not exceed 16 characters.
34 - The user shall be able to retrieve any saved car in subsequent
     executions of the simulator by selecting a name from a software-
     generated list of saved cars; there shall be a maximum of 64 cars
35 - The user shall be able to delete any previously-designed car by
     selecting its name from a software-generated list of saved cars
36 - Deleting a car removes both the car in memory and the car saved to
     disk

   Determine and award "best of show"
37 - The best of show shall be determined by summing the best of show points
     of each user-selected option of each characteristic
38 - The best of show sum shall be further modified by "judge bias" - there
     shall be a 10% chance that additional points are added to the sum; the
     actual quantity of points is left to the designer
39 - Ties shall be broken by random selection
40 - The simulator shall award first, second, and third places for best of
     show

   Schedule races in a tournament
   - A tournament is one or more rounds of races. A tournament is complete
     when there is only one car with no losses.
41 - The last car in the tournament without any losses is the winner of the
     tournament.
   - A round is one or more races. A round is complete when each car (still)
     in the tournament has raced at least once.
   - A race is a single match between two cars.
42 - The simulator shall perform all scheduling; no user intervention shall
     be permitted
43 - Only user-designed (modified) cars shall compete in a tournament
44 - Only user-selected cars shall compete in a tournament
45 - There shall be a minimum of four user-selected cars to race in a
     tournament and a maximum of 64 cars
46 - Races shall be executed using exactly two lanes
47 - There shall be exactly two cars for each race
48 - No car shall race alone
49 - Single elimination race schedule: winners move on, losers are out
   - Double Elimination race schedule
50   - Cars without a loss compete only with other cars that have no losses
51   - Cars with one loss compete only with other cars that have one loss
52   - Cars with two losses are out

   Execute scheduled races
53 - Each race shall consist of summing the race success points of each
     user-selected option of each characteristic; the car with the larger
     sum after factoring in random mishaps wins
54 - Ties shall be broken with the toss of a virtual coin
55 - Each car in every race shall be subject to a randomly selected mishap
56   - The probability of the occurence of a random mishap shall be 10%
57   - The random mishap shall be selected from the following list:
       - negative mishaps
         - spontaneous combustion
         - lightning strike
         - wheels fall off
         - explosion
         - paint peels
       - positive mishaps
         - good luck
         - burst of speed
         - push from Brother Twitchell
58   - A negative mishap shall decrease the sum of race success points
       by up to 10%
59   - A positive mishap shall increase the sum of race success points
       by up to 10%

   Output
   - For the best of show output
60   - the car names of the first-, second-, and third-place winners (in
       that order)
61   - the sum of best of show points for each winning car
   - For each race output
62   - the round number (1 for the first round, 2 for the second round,
       etc.)
63   - the race number (1 for the first race, 2 for the second race, etc.)
64   - random mishaps and the cars impacted
65   - the race results consisting of
       - car names
       - sum of race success points for each car
       - an indication if the car was the winner or loser
66 - After the final race, output the name of the tournament winner
