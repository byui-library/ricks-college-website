Ricks College Winter Semester 2001 Devotionals

Devotionals are held each Tuesday at  2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium with live transmission to both the Taylor Chapel and the Kirkham Auditorium. Broadcast of the devotionals can be heard on KRIC, 100.5 FM at 2 and 9:00 p.m.

Devotionals are not available in written form. However, most are available on cassette tape from the Ricks Collage Bookstore. For more information, call the bookstore at (208) 356-2211 or email Bookstore@ricks.edu. Cost is $3.75 (tax and shipping and handling are also added).
