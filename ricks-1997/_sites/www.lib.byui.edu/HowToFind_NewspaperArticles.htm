How to Find Newspaper Articles

Newspaper articles can be accessed from the Library Home Page. Click on Reference Online and select News & Weather from the Quick Jump Menu on the left side of the page. Select one of the newspapers listed on the right, OR, choose NewsBank Online for access to more than 500 newspapers worldwide. If you are using NewsBank Online enter your topic into the search box and click on Search. This will bring up a list of articles matching your search topic. Click on the article title to go to the full text of the newspaper article. The article can be read online, printed out, or emailed to yourself following the directions on the screen.
