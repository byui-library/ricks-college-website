Ricks College News Release, April 22, 1998

Ricks to hold first Campus Education Week

REXBURG, Idaho — Ricks College will be opening up its campus to people of all ages during the first Campus Education Week.
The three-day event running from June 4 to 6 on the Rexburg campus is modeled in part after the successful Education Week that is held at Brigham Young University each August.
Doug Stutz, who is organizing the event through the Division of Continuing Education, hopes to draw 2,000 to 3,000 people.
The college has lined up 47 speakers from among the faculty at Ricks and other Latter-day Saint speakers. They will address more than 300 topics on gospel subjects, self-improvement and family relations in classes running from 8:30 a.m. to 8:40 p.m. each day.
He hopes its three-day format from Thursday to Saturday will make it easier for people to get away from responsibilities at home or work. Timed between the first and second summer terms at Ricks, it's an ideal time to visit campus.
Stutz says Campus Education Week is ideal for people who have enjoyed coming to Women's Week but found it too crowded in recent years, along with those who attended the discontinued Creative Homemaking Conference.
But the new event is designed to include not only homemakers, but men, women and youth 14 and older.
"The successful homemaking conference really paved the path," he says. "It showed us we could do it."
In choosing the theme "Every Home Is a House of Learning," Stutz says, "We wanted to stress the importance of home and education. This statement by Elder (Joseph) Wirthlin reminds us of our important role as parents and leaders to educate our families in spiritual, emotional, physical and secular issues."
Morning devotional speakers will include LDS Church General Relief Society President Mary Ellen Smoot, Elder Wirthlin of the LDS Church Quorum of the Twelve and Ricks President David Bednar.
Other speakers include former Young Women's president Ardeth Kapp, John Covey from Franklin-Covey, artist Del Parkinson, religion teacher Bruce Satterfield, author Jack Weyland, speaker and humorist John Bytheway and composer Darwin Wolford.
Call the Division of Continuing Education at 356-1040 for a booklet or check the Ricks homepage at www.rick.edu under Continuing Education. Preregistration is $30 and housing is available in college dorms.
