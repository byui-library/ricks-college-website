Syllabus

Teachings of the Living Prophets

Summer term

Robert Marcum, Instructor.

Taylor 202B/ext. 1508

Teachings of the Living Prophets is a unique class in which we study the lives and teachings of present general authorities, with emphasis on those who now lead the church as members of the First Presidency and Council of the Twelve. You will read "modern scripture" and understand the Lord's will for us today on such important subjects as Same Gender attraction, Sabbath Day, Drug abuse and Working Moms, while also discussing established doctrines, such as the Abrahamic Covenant, why Temple Marriage, and Atonement of Jesus Christ. Hopefully you will feel free to ask questions and discuss in further detail these important issues.

Academic Requirements: This will not be an easy class. Although it is a religion class it is also academic here at Ricks and requirements to determine a grade are essential.

Report: A report on ONE present day member of the First Presidency or Council of the Twelve Apostles will be required. You will sign up for the authority, and ONE from each group will be assigned to give an oral report in class. The others will be written reports and will be turned in on the day the oral report on the authority is given. At least one article on the life of each one of these men has been written for the Ensign magazine. I would start your search there. The report is worth 100 points.

Attendance: Your participation is necessary to accomplishment of the discussions of doctrines read about. There will be a minus 10 points for each class missed. In addition, if you miss more than 8 class periods you will not receive credit for the courseat all. Be here!!

Daily reading report: Write down at least five principles discussed in the articles assigned each day and your view and feelings about them. Be prepared to discuss them in class. These will be handed in daily and each days report will be given from 0-10 points for completion.

Quizzes: There will be random quizzes on who the leaders of the Church are, important events in their lives, and what they discuss in their articles. Worth from 1-15 points depending on the size of the quiz.

Materials needed: Copy of the "Teachings of the Living Prophets and other General Authorities" Manual, for my class, and a binder to put it in. You can purchase the manual at "Quick Ship and Copy" at 20 west 1st South in Rexburg.

Dress and grooming standards: These were clearly stated in the application to Ricks but if you don't remember what they are, read about them in your class schedule or catalog. Ignorance will not be accepted as an excuse for inappropriate appearance. First offense, a warning. Second, you lose class privileges until you've met with a counselor in student life and rectified the problem. It is not just a matter of looks, but of honesty. You signed your name to a document in which you gave your word to abide by the standards established here. I expect you to be honest and keep your word or accept the consequences of decisions made that are contrary to your commitment.
