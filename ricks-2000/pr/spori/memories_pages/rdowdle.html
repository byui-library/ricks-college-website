Ruth Thomas Dowdle (45)

I have many fond memories of classes attended (in the Spori.) The ‘student union’ was in a corner room where some of us ate our lunch each day and relaxed together for a bit of socializing.

These were the World War II years and men were very scarce. I might mention that there were 26 students in my graduating class, all women, in 1945.

Everyone knew everyone and this great old building brought us all together as a family. We had good times there. I loved this particular building and Ricks College in general.

Ruth Thomas Dowdle (45)
Provo, Utah
