How to Find Journal Articles

These are generally considered to be more scholarly than magazine articles because of their content, their inclusion of author's credentials, and the bibliography or works cited page found at the end of articles. On the Library Home Page, click on Index to Articles, select a database and begin searching. The name of the source itself such as Journal of Education may indicate that it is a scholarly journal, or you may look for other attributes of a scholarly journal. When in doubt, ask a librarian
