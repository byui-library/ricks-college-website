GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.

DESCRIPTION: Equivalent to English 495 but taught in a computer classroom. Keyboarding skill is required.
TOPICS: Theory relevant to reading, writing and interpreting literature topics specific to the chosen theme or historical period; literary research; cultural contexts of literary works.
OBJECTIVES: (1) reviewing in depth a literary theme or period. (2) Writing papers that apply English major course work. (3) Composing a researched, senior paper written to professional standards. (4) Developing oral presentation skills.
REQUIREMENTS: In-depth reading of assigned texts. Synthesizing and applying previous English major course work. Preparing a research project customized to the student's major emphasis. Presenting orally a review of research projects.
