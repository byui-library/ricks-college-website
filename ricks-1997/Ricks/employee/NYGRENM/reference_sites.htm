Government Pages

  • U.S. Small Business Administration    www.sbaonline.sba.gov
    • Find tips on financing, SBA services and more.  This site is a must-bookmark for any entrepreneur.
  • U.S. Patent and Trademark Office    www.uspto.gov
    • Want to file for a patent or trademark?  Find general information as well as rules and regulation; download the necessary forms; plus get guidance on the how-to's of filing.
  • U.S. Government Information    www.access.gpo.gov/su_docs/
  • Equal Employment Opportunity Commission    www.eeoc.gov/small/index.html
    • Federal laws protect employees from age, sex and race discrimination; sexual harassment; and much more.  You need to know the facts, and this EEOC page - tailored for small businesses - provides the scoop.
  • Fed World    www.fedworld.gov
    • This site is an intelligent gateway that helps users gain better access to the tons of stats and information compiled each year by the U.S. government.
  • EDGAR Online    www.edgar-online.com
    • Retrieve all government filing (10ks, 10qa and more) made by public companies and general SEC information at no cost.

Competitive Intelligence

  • Hoover's Online    www.hoovers.com
    • Research the competition at this site.  Some reports have price tags, but most of what you'll need you can find for free.
  • Company Lookup    www.companylink.com/companylookup.cfm
    • News, research and contacts for 100,000 companies are what you'll find here.
  • Companies Online     www.companiesonline.com
    • From Dun & Bradstreet, this site offers information on just about every public and sizable private business on the Web.
  • BBB Online    www.bbbonline.rog   
    • Find information on customers and vendors quickly.
  • Company Sleuth    www.companysleuth.com
    • An absolutely terrific deal-it's free.  Give Company Sleuth the names of up to 10 public companies, and it'll track news, stock prices, analyst reports and much more.  You'll also get e-mail alerts when big news about the companies breaks.
  • KnowX.com    www.knowx.com
    • This site is the place to search through public records-bankruptcies, lawsuits, judgments and others, and it offers a fast way to do your research on prospective partners and big customers.  Searches aren't free and neither is reading the detailed results they turn up, but between $3.95 and $15.00 will get you the scoop on just about anybody or any business.
  • CreditFYI    www.creditfyi.com
    • You can find full reports on how even small companies handle their debts, and much of the info derives from Experience (see TRW Credit).  The cost per report $14.95

Get Cash

  • Money Hunter    www.moneyhunter.com
    • The online kin of the TV series MoneyHunt TV, this is a good place to start your search for financing.
  • Bankrate.com    www.bankrate.com
    • Compare credit card deals with a few mouse clicks.
  • Garage.com    www.garage.com
    • Hunt for venture capital and angel financing here.
  • Lending Tree    www.lendingtree.com
    • Shop online for credit cards, even car-loans, by filling out an application at this site.  The app gets forwarded to participating banks, which get back to you with an approval and terms.  It's a simple way to comparison shop for borrowed cash.
  • Vfinance.com    www.vfinance.com
    • Here is a rich venture capital library, with plenty of links to venture capitalists with cash to invest.

    • General Electric lends millions yearly to entrepreneurs.  Find out how to get your share.
