Grade:

While the real goal of this class is to help you become better prepared to enter the mission field by learning more of the gospel there still must be a grade assigned. The grade will be determined according to the following areas:

Daily Preparation:

There is no substitute to being in class and letting the Holy Ghost testify of the things that we are discussing in class, especially when they coincide with what you have already prepared through your personal study. It is my responsibility to provide you with a class that is both spiritually and mentally stimulating and it is your responsibility to attend. For coming to class you earn five points, five more for reading your assignments and ten more points if you read the Book of Mormon as assigned for a total of 20 points (20 x 30 classes = 600 points) Attendance: School excused or illnesses verified by a doctor may be made up within three days of the absence. Missing more than eight times overrides all other considerations and a failing grade will be given.

Reading the Book of Mormon:

One of the most important things that a person can do to prepare for missionary service is to read the Book of Mormon and while serving a mission it remains one of the most important parts of your day. As part of this class it will be expected that you read one half an hour in the Book of Mormon daily. At the conclusion of the semester you will report on how well you did in your reading as part of your Book of Mormon paper which will be worth up to 100 points.

Tests:

This semester you will be tested on the missionary mastery scriptures in addition to a few other things. There will be three tests of 50 points apiece which will be administered in the testing center. The dates for the tests are given on the course calendar and will be in the testing center for only a limited time. Please make sure that you take the test during the allotted time frame as there will be no make up tests. Please notice the time for the final test to be given in class according to the test schedule.

Papers:

This semester you will be expected to write four papers that relate to the class and the material that we have covered. These papers will be at least two pages in length and the main purpose of the paper is to allow you to share some of your personal feelings and thoughts as they relate to the topics at hand. The due dates and topics for the papers are also listed on the course calendar. Each paper is worth 100 points for a total of 400 points.
