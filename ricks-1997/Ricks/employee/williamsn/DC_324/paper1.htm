Doctrine and Covenants Doctrinal Paper

I. Choose a doctrine of the Doctrine and Covenants you desire a deepened understanding of. (Faith, Repentance, Baptism, Holy Ghost, Resurrection, Revelation, Prayer, etc.) Make your doctrinal selection meaningful and confirm your selection with the reading of and reference to section 1, The Preface to the D&C.

II. As you read take note of all references to that subject and a brief summary of what that reference teaches you about that particular doctrine.


III. Find 5 Quotes from prophets and apostles that testify of and/or give insight to that particular doctrine.


IV. Reflect on how you can better live the principles of that doctrine in your own personal life.
