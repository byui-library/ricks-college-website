Satisfactory Academic Progress

Each time the Financial Aid Office awards a Federal Pell Grant, certifies a federal loan application, or disburses any federal monies, the Financial Aid Office will certify that the student is making Satisfactory Academic Progress according to federal guidelines.

The student's status for Satisfactory Academic Progress will be determined after the grades are posted at the end of the fall and winter semesters.

The U.S. Department of Education (ED) requires that the student's entire academic history be considered in the evaluation of Satisfactory Academic Progress.

The Department of Education also requires a student to progress qualitatively and quantitatively within a predetermined time frame in order to be eligible for federal funds.

QUALITATIVE: The student has established credit with a minimum 2.0 cumulative GPA.

QUANTITATIVE: The student has earned a minimum of 80% of the number of credits attempted at Ricks College and a minimum of 75% during his/her most recent semester at Ricks College. Credits attempted will consist of all course work displayed on the student's academic transcript except for Special Exam credits. Repeated, F, and W courses are counted as unearned credits.

PREDETERMINED TIME FRAME: The student has earned less than 80 cumulative credits or has not received a degree or certificate from Ricks College. Exceptions to this rule are:

1. Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree in Paramedicine (Major Code 228) the time frame is 150 cumulative credits.

2. Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree in Interior Design (Major Code 158) the time frame is 150 cumulative credits.

3. Associates of Arts and Sciences Degree in Nursing (Major Code 093) the time frame is 104 cumulative credits.

4. Specialized Associates Degree in Paramedicine (Major Code 225) the time frame is 134 cumulative credits.

5. For One Year Certificates in Agronomy/Ag Mechanization (Major Code 306), Paramedicine (Major Code 227), Piano Pedagogy (Major Code 305), Office Education (Major Code 174), or Floral Design (Major Code 235), the predetermined time frame is 40 cumulative credits.

TRANSFER STUDENTS

A newly admitted student (either an entering freshman or a transfer student) is eligible for financial aid during his/her first semester at Ricks College.

PROBATION

A student will be placed on probation when:

1. His/her cumulative GPA (including transfer credits) is between 1.00 and 1.99.

2. He/she has earned less than 80% of the cumulative credits attempted at Ricks College.

3. He/she has earned less than 75% of the credits attempted in the most recent semester at Ricks College.

A student may continue to receive financial aid on probation if he/she earns at least a minimum 2.0 GPA and at least 80% of the credits attempted during the most recent semester at Ricks College.

SUSPENSION

A student who has meet one or more of the following conditions will be suspended from participation in federal financial aid at Ricks College:

1. Earns a cumulative GPA below 1.0.

2. Earns less than a 1.0 GPA during his/her most recent semester at Ricks College.

3. While on probation, a student fails to earn at least a minimum 2.0 GPA and at least 80% of the credits attempted during the most recent semester at Ricks College.

4. Exceeds the predetermined time frame.

5. Receives a certificate or degree from Ricks College.

 

REINSTATEMENT

A student may be fully reinstated by meeting the standards of Satisfactory Academic Progress.

A student may be reinstated on probation when he/she earns a minimum 2.0 GPA and at least 80% of the credits attempted during the most recent semester at Ricks College.

APPEALS

A student determined to be ineligible for financial aid may appeal to receive aid when there are unusual or extenuating circumstances. Appeals should be directed to the Financial Aid Committee and delivered to the Financial Aid Office.

If a student has exceeded the predetermined time frame or has received a certificate or degree from Ricks College, an exception may be granted when the committee determines that the student is sufficiently pursuing a degree or certificate at Ricks College. Generally the student will demonstrate that at least 75 percent of his/her present class load is required for graduation in the student’s declared major. Also, an exception may be granted when graduation requirements are being completed during the current semester.

A student with mitigating or unusual circumstances may petition for an exception. Mitigating or unusual circumstances may be, but are not limited to:

1. The death of a near family member or other similar tragedy.

2. A severe or extended illness or injury.

3. A learning or functional disability.

4. An unusual circumstance that has been disruptive to the student's academic performance as determined by the Financial Aid Committee.

FINANCIAL AID COMMITTEE

The Director of Student Financial Aid is the Chairman. All other Financial Aid Administrators are committee members. The Assistant Director of Financial Aid will serve as Chairman in the absence of the Director. The Assistant Academic Vice President for Support Services may serve in the place of the Chairman or any committee member.
