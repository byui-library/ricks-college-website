Women's Non-Cooking Style On-Campus Housing (Fall/Winter Only)

Chapman Hall
Chapman Hall (dorm 6), the only women's non-cooking style apartment is located on the east side of campus, right across the parking lot from the student center.  See map #6.
Enclosed Courtyard
A large courtyard provides secure exterior entrances into each resident apartment.
Your Own Desk With Lofted Bed
Each 3 bedroom apartment is shared by 6 women.  Lofted beds provide desk and floor space for each student.
High-Efficiency Closets
These high-efficiency closets have 3 large drawers, 2 small drawers, 1 mirror and a closet with plenty of hanging room and shelf space for shoes per person.
Lounge
Each building has a main lounge with a TV/VCR, snack and beverage machines, and plenty of comfortable chairs and couches for social gatherings.
Laundry Facility
There is also a coin-operated laundry room with Maytag washers and dryers.  One washer load is 75¢ and a dryer will run for 30 minutes for just 25¢.
Daily Chef Show in the Galley Cafeteria
Two different meal plans are offered for the Non-Cooking Style Apartments.  The Gold meal plan offers students up to19 meals a week, and the Silver meal plan offers 14.  Click here for info!
