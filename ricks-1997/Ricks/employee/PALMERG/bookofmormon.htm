Book of Mormon 122
We will come unto Christ, through the words of his prophets and the experiences, from Alma's mission and interviews with his sons, through the war chapters, the battles with secret combinations, on to the heart of the Book of Mormon: the visit of Christ to the children of Lehi. Then we will see the faithfulness of a lone disciple of Christ.

Book of Mormon 122 syllabus:GRADING

YOUR GRADE WILL BE DETERMINED BY:

  • ATTENDANCE 40%
  • TESTS AND QUIZZES 25%
  • INSIGHT AND APPLICATION 15%
  • SCHOLARLY PROJECT & RESERVE 10%
  • READING 10%
  • ATTENDANCE:

THIS MAKES UP THE MOST PART OF YOUR GRADE. MORE IMPORTANTLY, YOU WILL HAVE A MUCH BETTER EXPERIENCE IF YOU ATTEND EACH CLASS. THE ATTENDANCE GRADE IS BROKEN DOWN AS FOLLOWS:

  • MISS NO MORE THAN 3 DAYS—A
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 6 DAYS—B
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 9 DAYS—-C
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 12 DAYS—Woe!

READING:

PERHAPS READING SHOULD CONSTITUTE A LARGER PORTION OF YOUR GRADE, BUT IT DOESN'T SEEM RIGHT TO GIVE YOU MORE CREDIT FOR SOMETHING A PROPHET HAS TOLD YOU TO DO ANYWAY! IF YOU READ 2.7 PAGES DAILY THROUGHOUT THE SEMESTER YOU WILL HAVE READ ALMA 30 THROUGH MORONI 10—PIECE OF CAKE. SO FEAST ON IT!

  • READ 227 OR MORE PAGES—A
  • READ 201 OR MORE PAGES—-B
  • READ 176 OR MORE PAGES—-C

IT IS ALSO CONSIDERED "A" WORK IF YOU READ A SIGNIFICANT AMOUNT FROM THE BOOK OF MORMON DAILY (which means without missing a day at all for any reason from the first day of class to the last).

THE BOOK OF MORMON MANUAL IS HIGHLY RECOMMENDED.

QUIZZES AND TESTS:

THERE WILL BE A MID-TERM AND A FINAL EXAM. THERE WILL ALSO BE PERIODIC QUIZZES WHICH, IF YOU ATTEND FAITHFULLY, YOU WILL EASILY MASTER.
