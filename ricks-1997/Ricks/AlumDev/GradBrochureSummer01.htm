Summer Graduation June 6, 2001

Basic Information Schedule of Events

Congratulations!! Ricks College Summer Graduation will be Wednesday, June 6, 2001. We hope the Spirit of Ricks will continue with you wherever you may go. Upon graduation, you will join with over 100,000 alumni that have been a part of creating this great Spirit of Ricks.

We hope the information provided will be helpful while you plan for a memorable graduation day. Again, congratulations and best wishes for a bright future!
Checklist
A checklist for summer graduation candidates will be available at the Registrar’s Office beginning May 21. Check to be sure that your name is included with proper spelling, mailing address, etc. If you are graduating but not marching, pick up your diploma cover before you leave campus.
Graduation Announcements
Check with the Ricks College Bookstore at 356-2211.
Caps and Gowns
Summer graduates may pick up their cap and gown Tuesday, June 5 in the Alumni Office, Kimball 220, between 10 a.m. and 3 p.m. Please allow a few extra minutes to fill out an alumni biographical survey so that Ricks College, friends, and roommates can keep in touch with you. The cap, gown, and tassel are yours to keep.
Graduation Breakfast
The graduation breakfast is FREE to all summer graduates. Parents, relatives, children, and friends of the graduates will need to pay $2 per person. Tickets are required. Graduates may pick up tickets in the Ticket Office beginning May 21 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., Monday through Friday. Graduates will need to bring their ID card.

This will be a full buffet breakfast with no planned program. It will be held Wednesday, June 6. You may come to enjoy the food and mingle anytime between 7:30 a.m. and 9 a.m. in the cafeteria area of the Manwaring Center.
Academic March
Graduates will need to line up on the north side of the Manwaring Center by 9:40 a.m. You will then march into the Hart Auditorium, after which Commencement will begin. It will last approximately 1½ to 2 hours. When you pick up your cap and gown, you will receive a name card and instructions on how to line up for the academic march. Please observe Ricks College dress and grooming standards.
Commencement
Graduates are honored together and the degrees are conferred upon the graduates. The speaker has not been announced yet. No separate Convocation is held. Each graduate is recognized individually while walking across the stage to receive his/her diploma cover.
