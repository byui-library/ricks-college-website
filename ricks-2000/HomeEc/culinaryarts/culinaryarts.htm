Culinary Arts

 
 
The mission of the Culinary Arts program is to provide theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience for students interested in becoming caterers, apprentice chefs, or managers.  Students will have the opportunity to do the following: 
  • Learn and effectively practice skills in food preparation and service
  • Understand culinary nutrition, food safety and food identification
  • Gain an appreciation for foods of various cultures
  • Acquire supervisory skills and develop a personal sense of professionalism 
  • Necessary for working success in the food service industry
  • Gain experience in the proper use and maintenance of professional food service equipment
