Performance Tours  coordinates regional and national shows and  tours which give students a chance to further develop their talents and opportunities to perform. At the same time the students become goodwill ambassadors. BYU-Idaho Performance Tours are approved by the Church Educational System.

Locations of Performance Tours

Folk Dance USA
   BYU-Idaho is proud to announce the twenty-fourth European tour of their well-known American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band.
   The troupe is composed of 24 dancers and a six-member bluegrass band. The students range in age from 18 to 25 years.  They are highly skilled and artistically disciplined.  Their program reflects their interest in preserving the folk heritage of the American people.

Dance Alliance
   Dance Alliance is a fast-paced show designed to entertain the entire family.   Since the early 1970's, they  have performed such dance selections as the waltz, cha cha, samba, West Coast swing, clogging, character dances, show themes, jazz numbers and novelty routines.
   When touring, the Ballroom Dance Company and Contemporary Dance Theatre (CDT) combine to become Extravadance.

Collegiate Singers/Vocal Union   

Performers are selected by audition and represent some of the most outstanding talent on campus. Each member brings experience and enthusiasm to the stage and adheres to an ambitious rehearsal and performance schedule.

Touring Production: 2002 show is entitled "Doors."  This production will showcase the music of film and stage in a spirited presentation of the wonders in life’s travels and opportunities. Music Department.
Sacred Music Series

BYU-Idaho is proud of fifth tour of the Sacred Music Series under the name of Ricks College. Every other year, the university commissions a significant new composition of sacred music. This music is masterfully written to bring together the richness of melodies, the harmony of full orchestration, the heavenly fullness of collegiate choirs, and the spirit of a sacred message.

Each presentation results from a synergetic effort of the composer, the directors, and the musicians. The 150 students who tour are a combination of the Collegiate Singers and Symphony Orchestra. An additional 180 Ricks vocalists from and the Men’s and Women’s Choirs will join for special concerts on campus and at the Tabernacle on Temple Square in Salt Lake City, UT.

In the spring of 2001, the series will tour in the Washington DC area with the commissioned work by  Daniel E. Gawthrop entitled The Passion and the Promise of Our Lord Jesus Christ.
