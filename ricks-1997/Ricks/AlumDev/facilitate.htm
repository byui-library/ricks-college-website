Direct Mail is used to initiate annual support of Ricks College from alumni and friends like you.   Via the postal service, you'll probably be invited to sustain and advance the college's goal and priorities. You may find this is a very convenient way to renew your support on an annual basis. When you receive this communication we hope you’ll accept the invitation.

Telefund is also used to solicit support for Ricks College on an annual basis. You may receive a periodic call from a Ricks College student in conjunction with this effort. Be assured the students will properly identify themselves, explain the purpose of their call, and invite you to make a pledge to Ricks. We hope you’ll appreciate this friendly call and respond affirmatively.
