BEGINNING TELEVISION PRODUCTION
BEG TV PROD
CLASS CODE: COMM 265 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: COMMUNICATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Study and practice of television production techniques, with particular emphasis on news and commercial material. Involves operation of equipment as well as performance opportunities.
DESCRIPTION: Study and practice of television production techniques, with special emphasis on news and commercial material. Involves operation of equipment as well as performance opportunites.
TOPICS: Production Planning.
Portable Video Camera.
Video Recording.
Editing Aesthetics.
Linear.
Cuts-Only.
Videotape Editing.
Graphics and Design.
Sound.
Creating a television commercial.
Lighting.
A/B-Roll Editing and SMPTE Time Code.
Digital-nonlinear Video Editing.
Creating a News Package.
Newscast Preparation.
Television in the real world.
OBJECTIVES: This class is designed to help the student become familiar with the tools, techniques, and usage skills for basic TV production. The Professor assumes that the student has no (or little) prior experience in the use of equipment or in performing in professional TV settings.
REQUIREMENTS: Assignments = 600 points.
Tests = 300 points.
Attendance = 300 points.
Newscasts = 50 points.
Total = 1,250 points
PREREQUISITES: Comm 260 or Comm 130 for Broadcast emphasis minors.

This course cannot be taken concurrently with Comm 260.
