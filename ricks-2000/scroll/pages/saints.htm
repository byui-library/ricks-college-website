TREK TO HONOR CALIFORNIA SETTLERS AND PIONEER HERITAGE

The cry of "Wagons-Ho" will be heard again as 20 covered wagons will make an 800-mile trek to Southern California.

The trek was organized to honor the pioneer heritage that began 150 years ago when the first settlers entered the San Bernandino Valley from Utah, according to Deseret News.

The re-enactment will begin April 25 in Provo, Utah, by the Heritage Trails Celebration group. The trek will take 69 days and will end in San Bernandino Valley, Calif., according to www.heritagetrails.cc.

PRESIDING BISHOPRIC ASKS MEMBERS TO DISCONTINUE LOCAL CHURCH WEB SITES

n a letter sent to stake, mission and district presidents of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints March 15, the Presiding Bishopric asked for the discontinuance of all local unit Web sites until a policy is established to govern them.

Local units were also asked not to create or sponsor any additional sites until they were notified that it was okay to do so.

"As the Church grows, it is very important that information presented to the world be accurate and dignified and that it represent a single, unified church voice. In addition, it is imperative that the rights of third parties be protected and respected through strict compliance with applicable laws," the letter stated.
