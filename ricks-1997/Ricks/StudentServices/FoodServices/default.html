Food Services

Where food, friends, and fun get together

Our professional staff of 65 full- and part-time employees eagerly awaits the opportunity to serve you. We consistently observe that students feel better, are healthier, have more energy, and do better in school when the principles of proper nutrition are followed.

Food Services is conveniently located in the Manwaring Student Center. It features a full-service, all-you-care-to-eat cafeteria; two restaurants; a bagel kiosk; and a convenience store. Funds can be deposited to your student ID card account and used at any of the above locations.

The Galley (Student Cafeteria)

The Galley is the pride of Food Services. Our buffet-style meals, featuring all-you-care-to-eat entrees, specialty bars, salad bar, fresh produce, drinks, and desserts, provide proper nutrition while satisfying the student with a streamlined appetite or the one with a hollow leg.

The Galley offers gold, silver, and bronze meal plans.

Gold Plan:

The gold plan offers the best value for your food dollar. It offers up to19 meals a week for as little as $3.85 per meal. 

Silver Plan:

 The silver plan is our mid-range option.  It offers up to14 meals a week at a cost of $4.70 per meal.

Bronze Plan:

 The bronze plan is our most flexible option but provides the least amount of value for your food dollar.  It offers up to10 meals a week at a cost of $5.95 per meal.                             

Regardless of the plan, the student determines which meals to eat in the cafeteria.
