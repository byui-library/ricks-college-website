Image


An image is a word picture
that appeals to the senses,

for example,
"blue eyes crying in the rain."

We see the color blue;
we hear the sobs;
we feel the wet rain and tears.

Images are formed using well-chosen
adjectives, nouns, verbs, and adverbs.

Blue Eyes
Crying in the Rain
byWilly Nelson

In the twilight glow I see her
Blue eyes crying in the rain
As we kissed goodbye and parted
I knew we'd never meet again.

Love is like a dying ember
Only memories remain
Through the ages I'll remember
Blue eyes crying in the rain.

Now my hair has turned to silver
All my life I've loved in vain
I can see her star in heaven
Blue eyes crying in the rain.

Someday when we meet up yonder
We'll stroll hand in hand again
In a land that knows no parting
Blue eyes crying in the rain.
