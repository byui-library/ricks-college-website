Sample Citations - APA Style

 

Journal article, one author

     Bekerian, D. A. (1993). In search of the typical eyewitness.

American Psychologist, 48, 574-576.

     (Note: underline American Psychologist, 48,)

Magazine article

     Posner, M. I. (1993, October 29). Seeing the mind.

Science, 262, 673-674.

     (Note: underline Science, 262,)

Daily newspaper article, no author

     New drug appears to sharply cut risk of death from heart

failure. (1993, July 15). The Washington Post, pp. A1, A4.

     (Note: underline The Washington Post)

Books, one author

     Mitchell, T. R., & Larson, J. R. (1987). People in

organizations: An introduction to organizational behavior.

New York: McGraw-Hill.

     (Note: underline People in organizations: An

introduction to organizational behavior.)

Edited Book

     Gibbs, J. T., & Huang, L. N. (Eds.). (1991). Children of

color: Psychological interventions with minority youth. San

Francisco: Jossey-Bass.

     (Note: underline Children of color: Psychological

interventions with minority youth.)

Brochure, corporate author

      Research and Training Center on Independent Living. (1993).

Guidelines for reporting and writing about people with

disabilities (4th ed.) [Brochure]. Lawrence, KS: Author.

     (Note: underline Guidelines for reporting and writing

about people with disabilities)

Article or chapter in an edited book, two editors

      Bjork, R. A. (1989). Retrieval inhibition as an adaptive

mechanism in human memory. In H. L. Roediger III & F. I. M. Craik

(Eds.), Varieties of memory & consciousness (pp. 309-330).

Hillsdale, NJ: Erlbaum.

     (Note: underline Varieties of memory & consciousness)
(Citation formats based on: Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association (4th ed.). (1994). Washington, DC: American Psychological Society.)
