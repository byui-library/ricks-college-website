FORENSICS TEAM WINS TOP AWARDS AT NATIONAL MEET

The Ricks College Forensics Team showed up at the national forensics tournament for four-year colleges and universities and talked their way into earning many of the top awards. The two-year junior college surprised a lot of four-year schools by winning the individual events sweepstakes award and placing second overall behind Rice University.

The Pi Kappa Delta National Tournament was held in Boise March 29-31 with 77 colleges and universities from across the nation competing, says Ricks coach Eric Embree. He says it was the first time Ricks had ever competed at the national competition designed mainly for four-year schools. Because the competition was in Boise, the Ricks students decided to see how they would stack up against other schools.

Embree says he was even surprised, but says Ricks has built up a strong reputation and attracts many high quality competitors, particularly students who want to attend a Latter-day Saint school.

This is the highest showing by a two-year program in the 84-year history of the organization, he says. "I think it's pretty amazing. I knew we had individual students who would do well, but I just didn't think the entire team would do as well in the overall rankings. We beat a lot of really high-powered programs."

Ricks will compete later this week at the national competition for two-year schools in Florida.

REGISTRATION LATER THAN NORMAL AS RICKS GEARS UP FOR NEW CLASSES

Registration for Fall Semester classes will be a bit later than normal as Ricks College changes to Brigham Young University-Idaho and begins offering upper-division classes. Registrar Kevin Miyasaki says registration is a couple of weeks later because departments have been busy developing courses for new majors leading to bachelor's degrees.

April 16 is the opening date for students with 70 or more credits to register; April 18, for 64 credits or more; April 20, for 54 credits or more; April 23, for 44 credits or more; April 25, for 36 credits or more; April 27, for 32 credits or more; April 30, for 24 credits or more; May 2, for 16 credits or more; May 4, for 12 credits or more; May 7, for 1 credit or more; and May 31, for new students.

Because of the changes, the college catalog also will be printed later. It should be available online later this month and on CD-ROM in May. A printed version will be available at a later date.

Beginning in the fall, BYU-Idaho anticipates offering upper-division classes leading to integrated bachelor's degrees in accounting, English, history and recreation education, along with specialized degrees in business management, elementary education, interior design and nursing. The university also expects to begin offering upper-division, secondary education programs in the areas of biology, earth/physical science, English, family and consumer education, health, history, math and social studies.

RICKS TO PERFORM CLASSIC STORY OF 'LITTLE WOMEN'

The Ricks College Theatre Department will present the classic story "Little Women" as its final show of the season. The performance will be presented in the Snow Drama Theatre April 12-14 and April 17-21. All performances will begin at 7:30 p.m. Tickets for the event are $6 for the general public and available from the Ricks College Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230.

Louisa May Alcott's novel "Little Women" was first published in 1868. This production was adapted from the novel by Marion de Forest. "Little Women" is directed by Rebecca M. Foster, a Seattle native on a one-year appointment with the Theatre Department. Set design is by Robert Nelson, lighting design by Gary Benson, costume design by Susan Whitfield and sound design by Hyrum Conrad.

SACRED MUSIC SERIES ON THE ROAD IN WASHINGTON, D.C., VIRGINIA, UTAH

The location for the performance of The Passion and the Promise of Our Lord Jesus Christ on Thursday, April 5, at 8 p.m. has been rescheduled to the National City Christian Church at Five Thomas Circle in Washington, D.C. Specially invited guest include ambassadors and congressmen. For complimentary tickets, please call 1-800-71-RICKS or visit the web site at www.byui.edu/tickets.

The Passion and The Promise is a commissioned oratorio by Daniel E. Gawthrop written specifically for Ricks College's Sacred Music Series. This oratorio utilizes scriptures in a musical setting to portray events in the Savior's life. Beginning with His entry into Jerusalem and continuing through the crucifixion, the story extends to majestic accounts of his resurrection, his post-mortal ministries, and to Peter's powerful testimony at Pentecost. The oratorio is a serious work of art, composed for professional soloists, large chorus, and full symphony orchestra. It has been written in a style which is melodic, rich, colorful, appealing and approachable for general audiences.

Performances will also be Friday, April 6 at 8 p.m., at Fairfax High School, 3500 Old Lee Highway, Fairfax, Va.; and Saturday, April 7 at 7:30 p.m. in the Tabernacle on Temple Square in Salt Lake City. The oratorio will be performed again in the Barrus Concert Hall on April 10, 11, and 12 at 7:30 p.m. and in the Hart Auditorium on April 15 and 27. (Please note: Tickets are not required for the Tabernacle or Easter Sunday, April 15, in the Hart.)
