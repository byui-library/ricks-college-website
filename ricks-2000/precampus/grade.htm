BYU-Idaho Grade Report

This is a practice trial to assist you in determining your major and familiarizing yourself with the Graduation Report form and General Education Requirements.  Be sure to verify all General Education Requirement information with the BYU-Idaho Online Catalog, (be sure to select the General Education Requirements link).

Access the BYU-Idaho Website, select the current students link located at the top of your screen.   Three columns of links should have appeared.  Look at the first column labeled "Academics".  Here you will located the link for the "Grade Report".

  1. Select the Grade Report link.
    You will need to input your Student ID # (your social security #) and Pin # (the pin number is the number you typed if you enrolled online, or is usually your birth month and year.  For practice purposes enter the following: 

    666-66-6666 (without the hyphens) for the Student ID #
    66666 for the Pin #
    enter the current year
    Enter any major or leave it in "default"

  2. The BYU-Idaho Student Web Services menu should appear.  Select the Graduation Report.  

  3. Rebbeca S. Student's grad report should appear on your screen.  Her report closely resembles what your Grad Report will look like as she has not yet completed any classes for credit.  You may wish to print a copy of this report to help familiarize yourself with all the information contained in the graduation report.
  4. Click "back" on your browser,  and using the scroll down menu, select a major of interest to you.  See how this information differs from the default option listed on the graduation report.

  5. The BYU-Idaho Grade Report lists the following information:


    Academic Advisor:
    Once you register for classes and access your grad report, it will list the name of your academic advisor at the top of the form.

    Credit Section:
    A.  Total credits required for graduation - 64 credits are required to graduate from BYU-Idaho.
    B.  Earned credit - 0
    C.  Deficient Credit - 64, the amount of credits still needed to complete graduation.

    GPA Section:
    A.  GPA Required for graduation (2.0 minimum G.P.A.)
    B.  Cumulative GPA - your current GPA for all semesters completed.

    Residency Requirement:
    At least 24 credit hours taken at the BYU-Idaho campus are required to receive an Associates Degree.  This requirement applies mainly to students transferring to BYU-I.
    A.  Residency Requirement - 24 hours.
    B.  Earned BYU-Idaho Credit - this is the total cumulative hours earned at BYU-Idaho

    Religion Requirements
    A.  4 religion classes are required (most students take one religion class/semester).
    B.  Once you have completed a course which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course.

    Reading & Writing
    A.  Two classes are required to fulfill the Reading/Writing requirement.
    B.  The grad report lists the available classes which would fulfill this requirement.
    C.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Math Proficiency Requirement
    A.  Depends on your ACT  Math Score, a 22 or higher on the math section waives the math requirement.  Take the Math placement exam located in the Assignment Section of Blackboard to see whether or not you should enroll in Math 110, or take refresher math courses.

    Basic Skills Requirement
    A.  Complete one of the following options (A, B, C, or D) listed on the grad report.
    B.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Arts and Letters Requirement
    A.  Six credits are required, three credits from the arts and three from letters.
    B.  FA 100 is required for all students and does not fulfill the 3 credit arts requirement.  FA 100 requires students to attend 7 campus arts events.   Additional information on the FA 100 requirement or current scheduled events may be found by calling the Arts Hotline (356-1251) or on the Fine Arts Homepage.
    C.  Three credits from the Letters classes are needed to fulfill this requirement.  
    D.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Natural Sciences
    A.  Eight to nine credits are needed to fulfill the Natural Sciences Requirement.
    B.  Students choosing to take only eight credits must select courses with labs.   The labs must be related to the lecture class.
    C.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Social Science Requirement
    A.  Six credits are needed to fulfill the Social Science requirement; at least two disciplines must be represented.
    B.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Online Learning Requirement
    A new course requirement has been added for students who enroll for Fall 2000 and beyond.  The online learning requirement specifies that students are required to have one online learning experience to graduate.  Approved online courses are designated in the class schedule with section numbers between 801 and 899.

    Major Requirements
    A.  The classes needed to complete the major of your choice are listed in this section.
    B.  Once you have completed a course(s) which helps fulfill this requirement, it will be listed in this section of your graduation report, along with the credit hour numbers earned from completing that course(s).

    Recommended Electives
    The Grad report will list various elective courses you could take, depending on your choice of major.  This can be of help to you in determining classes you should register for. 

    Unofficial Transcript
    The Last section is your unofficial transcript for each semester enrolled at Ricks.   It includes: the Department name (Dept), Course Number (Crs), Section (Sec), Honors (Hon) if taken, Course Title, Semester Credit (Sem Cr.), Grade (Gr.) and courses that fulfill the General Education Requirements (GE/Rpt)
  6. For comparison reasons, go back to the Grad Report section again (under Student Resources).  This time you will type in the number of a mock student who has almost  completed all of his courses at  BYU-Idaho.  This is how your grad report will eventually look like.  To see this report enter the following information.

    777-77-7777 (without the hyphens) for the Student ID #
    11111 for the Pin #
    enter current year
    Enter Psychology for the Major


  7. Rick S. Student's grad report should appear on your screen.  Review this grad report by going to item number 5 listed above and reviewing each section.

  8. Do you see any differences between the first and second grad reports?  After seeing both  reports, can you now better understand, and/or read your grad report?

  9. Now enter your own personal information (Student ID # and PIN # and the year 00/01) in the Grad Report menu.  If you are undecided in terms of choosing a major, enter each major that interests you and see what classes are required for that major.  Take notes on this assignment  in your New Student Journal as you will most likely need to refer to them for the "Choosing A Major" section of this course.
