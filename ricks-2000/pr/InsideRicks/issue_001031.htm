ALEXANDER PESKANOV WILL PERFORM AT RICKS

Internationally acclaimed pianist and composer Alexander Peskanov will perform Thursday and Friday, Nov. 2-3, at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall at Ricks College. Tickets for the Center Stage Entertainment Series event are $6 for the general public. Peskanov will perform some of his own compositions as well as works by Beethoven, Schubert, Rachmaninoff, Prokofiev and Schumann. The public is also invited to a free lecture demonstration entitled "The Russian Technical Regimen and Its Implementation into Repertoire" Thursday from 2-3 p.m. in the Snow Recital Hall followed by a master class from 3-3:50 p.m.

Peskanov graduated from the Stoliarsky School of Music in Odessa, Ukraine, and received his master's and bachelor's degrees from the Juilliard School in New York City. He is also the author of the series of six books entitled "The Russian Technical Regimen for the Piano," published by Willis Music Company. "This brilliant pianist can create incredible excitement and knows how to please a crowd," reported The Washington Post when reviewing Alexander Peskanov's Kennedy Center performance of Rachmaninoff's Third Concerto. Peskanov's American debut as orchestral soloist came with the National Symphony under Rostropovich, and he has since appeared internationally. He was the featured soloist with the Polish Chamber Orchestra's on their six-week tour in the U.S. Peskanov has performed in the prestigious Van Cliburn Foundation Series in Fort Worth and the Gina Bachauer Piano Festival in Salt Lake City. He is also a successful composer of classical works and the recipient of three special ASCAP awards.

VOCAL UNION AND SOUND ALLIANCE JOIN FOR FIRST CONCERT

The newly formed Vocal Union performing group along with the Sound Alliance Jazz Band will perform Friday, Nov. 10, at 7:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium. Tickets are $2 for the general public and $1 for Ricks students and employees and may be obtained by calling the Ticket Office at 356-2230 or online at www.byui.edu/tickets.

Vocal Union with its 13 performers is new to the Music Department this year and is headed by artistic director Nori Brower and her husband Kevin serves as vocal director. Nori Brower says the freshmen and sophomore students have backgrounds in music, theater and dance. The goal of the group is to be both entertaining to all ages as well as to be challenging to the student performers. "It's entertaining, but there is a message too. The concert is quite heartfelt and very stirring," Nori Brower says. Vocal Union will present vocal selections from the Broadway stage, the big band era and vocal jazz repertoire. Vocal Union intends to give performances both on and off campus.

As an added dimension, Vocal Union will combine with Sound Alliance, which is directed by Wilson Brown. The 23-piece jazz band performs jazz styles from the swing era through contemporary rock and fusion. Sound Alliance will be featured in the concert with big band arrangements of standards. The two music ensembles plan to tour together beginning with a two-week spring trip through the upper western and midwestern states in 2001.

STUDENT LEGACY FUND SEEKS RESCUERS

"Rescuers wanted . . . to help a student in need." The campaign for students to come to the aid of fellow students is under way at Ricks College. The Student Alumni Association has set a goal of raising $20,000 for the Student Legacy Fund by December 31. Even though there may be times when they feel they are "poor college students," the Ricks College students are encouraged to take part in rescuing a fellow student in need. They may never know the names of those they have helped. Many enrolled students have felt the blessings of being recipients and later have been able to turn back and give so that others may attend Ricks. Thus the charitable experience becomes perpetuated. For more information, visit us at http://www.byui.edu/slf or http://www.byui.edu/saa.

FAMILY HISTORY CONFERENCE WILL BE HELD ON CAMPUS

The Fall Family History Conference will be held Saturday, Nov. 4. The biannual conferences are sponsored by the Ricks College Family History Center and the Division of Continuing Education. A variety of topics will be presented in twenty-five classes. There is something for all who have genealogical interests -- those who need a gentle nudge to get started and those who are ready for Internet adventures or a hands-on experience in the PAF corner. Pre-registrations are being taken through Wed., Nov. 1. The cost of the conference is $13 per person if pre-registered. Registration will be taken in the foyer of the Joseph Fielding Smith Building beginning at 7:30 a.m. for $15 per person on a space-available basis. For more information or a brochure, call Continuing Education at (208) 356-2336 or visit the web at www.byui.edu/ContEd.
