This will assist you in saving and inserting an image.

Saving an Image
  1. If you wish to save an image you have found on the Internet such as the Ricks College logo, open up a browser such as Netscape or Internet Explorer; browse and find the image you want; right-click on the image and choose Save Image As... or Save Picture As...
  2. In the Save In: box make sure it has your joseph share located in it, then save it in the approriate place in your share.
  3. If you wish to rename the image, type an appropriate name in the File name: box.
  4. Click Save.
Inserting an Image
  1. Click where you want the image to be placed either on the page or in a cell of a table.
  2. Click the Insert menu, choose Picture, click From File. (FrontPage 98 - Insert menu and click Image.)
  3. Find the image you wish to insert and click OK.  (Remember that the image MUST be saved in your share on joseph.  If it is saved anywhere else such as your hard drive, it will not be seen by other viewers.
Applying Alternate Text to an Image
Alternate text is text that appears when a user rests their mouse on an image.  You can choose to have what text to be shown or no text to be shown.
  1. Right-click on the image you wish to change the alternate text on.  Click Picture Properties. (FrontPage 98 - click Image Properties)
  2. In the Alternative representations Text: box, type the text you wish to appear when the mouse is rested on that image.
  3. Click OK.
  4. To check that your changes have been made, save the current page, and either use the Preview button in the lower left hand corner of your FrontPage screen or view your page in a browser such as Netscape Navigator or Internet Explorer.
  5. Rest your mouse on top of the image you applied alternate text to, and your text should appear.
Applying an Image as a Background
  1. Right-click on the page you wish to alter the background, choose Page Properties.
     
  2. Click on the Background tab.
  3. Click in the Background Picture check box. (FrontPage 98 - Background Image)
  4. Click Browse and locate the image you wish to be the background.
  5. Click OK. (Remember that the image MUST be saved in your joseph folder or other viewers will not be able to view the background.)
