Phi Theta Kappa Newsletter
April 1999


Happy Spring Phi Theta Kappans! We hope you are enjoying the last few weeks of the semester! We, the 1998-1999 Phi Theta Kappa Officers, want to thank you for your participation in our activities this year. We hope that you will continue to be involved next year!

Recent Activities:
Easter Fun Day with Lincoln Elementary Children- Over 100 children enjoyed a magic show, Easter Egg Hunt, and Story Time with the Easter Bunny. It was an Egg-stra special event for both the children and Phi Theta Kappa members.

College Bowl-Utah Valley State College and Ricks College students participated in College Bowl sponsored by Phi Theta Kappa.

Three New Agencies To Offer 
Phi Theta Kappa Summer Internships

USA TODAY, the United States Information Agency and the National Association of Counties have joined five other prestigious Washington, D.C., agencies offering Phi Theta Kappa summer internships. The U.S. Department of Education, the National Science Foundation, the American Association of Community Colleges, the Association of Community College Trustees and Phi Beta Kappa Society Headquarters also participate in the Phi Theta Kappa Summer Intern Program.

Nominees for the 1999 All-USA Academic Team are eligible to apply for the internships, which begin June 20 and conclude July 30. Applications were mailed to all Academic Team nominees last week. Completed applications must be received at Phi Theta Kappa Headquarters by April 2. A total of 17 internships, some carrying stipends, are available. Activities and projects associated with this intern program are substantive, with significant opportunities and responsibilities provided by the sponsoring agencies. All travel, housing and living costs are paid by the students. Phi Theta Kappa Program interns will be housed at George Washington University. For information, contact Executive Assistant Wendy Taylor, wendy.taylor@ptk.org or ext. 518.

New Scholarship Information:


(Boston University) This scholarship was developed to recognize the outstanding academic and leadership achievements among Phi Theta Kappa members seeking to transfer to Boston University. Each Phi Theta Kappa scholar receives a Boston University scholarship in the amount of $6,000.00. Scholarship recipients will be chosen from among students nominated by Phi Theta Kappa chapter advisors. Nominees must be prepared to enter their program of admission as juniors; a minimum of sixty transferable credits is required. Interested students may request an application by calling Boston University at (617) 353-2300. An application can also be requested at the university website -- www.bu.edu/admissions.

Reminder:

For PTK members that will be graduating this semester--Upon graduation, PTK members wear blue and gold cords around their neck as part of the graduation uniform. The cost of the cords is included in your membership fee, and cords are available for pickup in the Austin Building, room 106. Be sure to stop by to pick yours up.

Dave is looking for interesting fund-raiser ideas for next year's activities. If you have any ideas, give him a call.

The deadline for the submission of applications for new Phi Theta Kappa members is February 10, 1999. Completed applications may be turned in along with the $50.00 one time membership fee at the Austin Building, Room 106. It is possible to submit applications after the deadline, but in order to receive membership certificates at the induction ceremony, your application must be turned in by 2/10/99. If you have questions, contact Amanda Baker.

Did you know that over 400 4-year institutions offer transfer scholarships exclusively dedicated to PTK members going to four-year schools? Members can easily access these senior institution scholarships by using the web, www.ptk.org , and following the web links for scholarships, where a search engine lists state-by-state and institution-by-institution scholarships which are available
