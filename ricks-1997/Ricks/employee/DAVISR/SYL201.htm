3. GRADES: A weekly printout showing your FINAL GRADE TO DATE will be posted in the hall. Grades are based on total points, with a 90, 80, 70, etc. cut-off. On the printout your lowest quiz score will be automatically dropped (all your scores will still appear on the printout but the lowest number will not be included in the total).

 

4. EXTRA CREDIT: There is none.

 

5. WANTS:  Please bear with me on three things: no food in class, no baseball caps, and I like to start on time, not five minutes after the bell.

 

6. PAPER: (50 points)   This is NOT a research paper with hours spent in the library. Rather, it’s an opinion paper, based on your own life and observations vis-à-vis The Cave.

TOPIC:

Discuss The Cave. Show me you understand it by citing examples which you have seen in today's world, preferably one or more situations which happened to you.

RULES:

1. Your paper is due at the start of class on the date indicated, and will be on the topic below. Late papers, even 10 minutes late, lose a full letter grade. Remember, you can always hand it in the day before.

2. The paper must meet the following:

a. 1,300-1,500 words (that’s about two pages, single spaced). No more. No less. Use the word count to stay within these limits.,

b. In two columns, justified left and right, so it looks and reads like a magazine (like this page),

c. Single-spaced,

d. In 12 or 13 point TimesRoman (this is TimesRoman, 13pt), or Arial (this is Arial, 13pt) or a similar font (no weird fonts please—especially script—and no Courier),

e. Printed on a laser or ink-jet. No dot-matrix will be accepted.

f. Read items a through e again. You lose points for anything which doesn’t meet these criteria.

3. Use the spell checker and, although you may be tempted, avoid the thesaurus unless you can show me you use the word in normal conversation. In other words, the paper should sound like you.

4. All direct quotes must be so noted, with quotation marks and a reference. Put titles in italics. (In the days of typewriters titles were underlined only because you couldn’t print an italics font. Now that you do, please use it instead.)

5. You will graded on content, grammar, spelling, presentation, etc.  EVERYTHING counts, so be neat, no typos, no last minute corrections in ink, etc.

6. Omit fancy covers, plastic covers, etc. Just a single plain page with your name, section, and row will be sufficient. Please include your row. With over a hundred papers to return it helps me to quickly organize them.

7. If you have the need, you are encouraged to use the Writing Lab in CLK 327. (It's easy; just walk in and ask, or call 1158.).
