INTRODUCTORY APPLIED PHYSICS LAB
APPL PHYS LAB
CLASS CODE: PH 107 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Designed to give practical experience with Physics topics using abstract ideas of applications in reality,help developo analysis, problem solving and technical communication skills.
DESCRIPTION: A three-hour per week physics lab course.
TOPICS: Experimental technique,Data,Projectile Motion,Newton's Laws,Rotational dynamics,Waves, Fluid mechanics,Thermodynamics, Momentum,Torque,Angular momentum,Elasticity, Waves,Sound,Temperature,Calorimetery.Archimedies and Bernoulli's principle.
OBJECTIVES: At the end of the class the students will be able to plot graphs, do computer operations, and have hands on experience in a physics lab.
REQUIREMENTS: No text required. The text from PH 105 should be brought to each lab.A 3-ring binder is needed.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: Due to the high demand for this laboratory, attendance at the first class period is required to secure your place unless prior arrangements are made with the instructor.
