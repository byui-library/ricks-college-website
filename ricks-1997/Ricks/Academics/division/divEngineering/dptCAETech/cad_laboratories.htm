Cad Laboratories

The Cad Laboratories used by the Computer Aided Engineering Technologies Department are modern facilities with industry standards in both software and hardware.  State-of-the-art overhead projection systems enhance the learning process allowing faculty to project CAD/CAM drawings or multi-media presentations.  Students are able to follow along at their individual workstation as faculty demonstrate difficult command sequences and construction techniques.
CAD/CAM software used in the classes includes AutoCAD, Pro/Engineer, 3D Studio Max, and MasterCAM.  Students also learn and are required to use word processors, spreadsheets, programming languages, and other software applications in many of their classes.
The Cad Laboratories are equipped with pentium-based computers which are updated on a regular basis.  The computers are connected to the campus network and use the Windows operating system.
