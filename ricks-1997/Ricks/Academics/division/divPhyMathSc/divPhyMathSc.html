   The Physical and Mathematical Science Division of Ricks College is made up of four departments, ie. Chemistry, Geology, Physics, and Math and Computer Science.
   The Chemistry Department has ten full-time faculty of which seven have doctorates.  General chemistry required for many science major programs is offered as well as general education options for the nonscience majors.
   The Geology Department has five faculty members.  Three of whom have Ph.Ds.  Courses in introductory geology, historical goelogy, environmental geology, mineralogy and petrology are offered to majors and nonmajors.  Field trips include excursions to nearby Yellowstone and Teton National parks as well as Idaho National Engineering Laboratory.  A 5-week summer Natural Science Field Expedition visits Yellowstone, Tetons, Salmon River Canyon and other areas of geological interest in the Intermountain West.  Up to six credits may be taken with this program to fill the G.E. Science requirement.
   The Mathematics and Computer Science Department has 20 faculty who teach math courses in beginning through college algebra, agricultural mathematics, quantitative literacy, trigonometry, Calculus, math for elementary education majors, statistics, linear algebra, differential equations, and topics in pure math.  Computer science students will find classes in computer basics, introductory programming, C++, procedural and object oriented languages, computer architecture, and special topics.  Ricks College does have a general education math graduation requirement of either intermediate algebra or the quantitative literacy course.
   The Physics Department has ten full-time faculty who teach introductory general physics courses, astronomy, weather and climate and acoustics.  Five of the ten faculty have doctorates.  ThePhysics Department maintains an excellent planetarium and observatory as well as solid laboratory experiences.
