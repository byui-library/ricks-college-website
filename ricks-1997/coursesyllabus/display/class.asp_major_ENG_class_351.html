FICTION
CLASS CODE: ENG 351 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Explores the development and characteristics of short fiction and/or the novel.
DESCRIPTION: Explores the development and characteristics of short fiction and/or the novel.
TOPICS: Major literary movements; literary history; major authors; craft and aesthetics; technique.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Developing an appreciation for fiction as a medium of artistic expression. (2) Fostering empathy for people of different cultures and time periods. (3) Finding beauty in the power of language.
REQUIREMENTS: Reading load will include 4-5 major novels, 30-35 short stories, or a combination of the two. Three analytical papers (5-10 pages) and shorter in-class discussion and writing assignments.
