Golden C. Snell has been teaching Spanish at Ricks College since he came in 1969. Previous to that, he had taught in the public school system in a small high school in Byron, Wyoming. His duties there were a little more varied, as there he was assigned to teach Spanish, French, and English in high school, junior high school, and elementary school. He enjoys teaching Spanish, his favorite language, but, also, enjoys teaching some intermediate algebra from time to time.

He graduated with a B.A. degree from the University of Wyoming, and, after obtaining a teaching position here at Ricks College, went on to receive his M.A. in Spanish pedagogy at Brigham Young University.

In July of 1959 he married his sweetheart, Ina Olsen, and they have five children, all boys, who have themselves married and established families in various parts of the U.S.A. They have provided them with 18 grandchildren, who are their pride and joy.
