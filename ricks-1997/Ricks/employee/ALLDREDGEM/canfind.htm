Can You Find It?

25 Library Scavenger Hunts to Sharpen Your Research Skills
by Randall McCutcheon
Minneapolis, MN: Free Spirit Publishing, 1989 (Z 710.M18 1989) 

Periodical

Question #4: p.30 In describing the world's largest shopping mall, William Severini Kowinski wrote, "[It] contains the world's largest indoor amusement park and the largest indoor water park. It has the planet's biggest parking lot. According to Nader Ghermezian, one of the four brothers who devised and built it, it is the most complete tourist attraction in the Universe." Name the mall.

My Clue: 1986 periodical

Newspaper

Question #2: p.23 President John F. Kennedy, on the night of April 29, 1962, gave a White House dinner to honor Nobel Prize Winners. Present were Nobel laureates and 124 other scientists, writers, editors, and educators. On the front page of the New York Times, the President is quoted as describing the dinner as "probably the greatest concentration of talent and genius in this house..." since when?

Clue #2: p.116
All the news that's fit to print often fits into the next day's paper. 

Literary Indexes

Question #10: p.52 "Knowing I lov'd my books, he furnish'd me,
From mine own library with volumes that
I prize above my dukedom."
This quotation is taken from what famous play?

Clue #10: p.129-130
Shakespeare and Barlett's ... what a pear.

Dictionaries

Question #7: p.42 In what year did Johann Sebastian Bach compose the Coffee Cantata?

Clue #7: p.126 "...you need a reference that lists all of J.S. Bach's works. "

Encyclopedias

Question #19: p.84 "Vincent Van Gogh left almost 600 paintings in nine years of intense productivity. Among these works, ‘The Red Orchard' was unique. Why?"

Clue #19: p. 146 "...look for a reference that indexes paintings by title..."

Biographies

Question #9: p.48 Whoopi [Goldberg] later revealed that her mortuary job was "great work" because she was "tired of working on living people who all wanted to" what?

Clue #9: p.128
Find a current reference with well-documented biographical articles.

Yearbooks, Handbooks, Manuals, etc.

Question #15: p.68 "Known as the ‘Mardi Gras of the North,' the Anchorage Fur Rendezvous is a 10-day, community-wide celebration. Highlights of the festival include the annual World Championship Sled Dog Races, a Miners and Trappers Ball, and the famous Eskimo Blanket Toss. In what month is the Anchorage Fur Rendezvous celebrated?"

Clue #15: p.139 "...months have days, and some days have books."

Government Publications

Question #12: p.58 "On December 8, 1941, an outspoken and courageous woman voiced the single ‘Nay' vote against the joint resolution that would declare a state of war between the United States and the Imperial Government of Japan. What was her name?

Clue #12: p.134 "The day after the day that lives on in infamy. Congressional records were made to be broken."
