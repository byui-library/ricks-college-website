Chat Room Coming
Hold your breath on this one. Right now (April 99) we're in the process of setting up a chat room for anyone who wants to talk about Honors. During Spring finals more than a few students voiced a desire to be able to talk with their peers over the web. This was further expanded with the current plans to set up the server on the off-campus side. This would allow former students now on distant campuses to keep in touch with those still here.

The idea has merit and is currently being evaluated. Check this site later this summer for details.
