Manwaring Center Scheduling Policies

FOOD:  All food eaten in the Manwaring Center must be purchased from Ricks College Food Services, call 356-2246 to place your order.  No exceptions.

DANCES: Music and D.J.'s for Dances must also be scheduled with the Dance Office, call 356-2280.

DECORATIONS:  Masking tape, scotch tape, duct tape, tacks, and nails CANNOT be used on walls or glass.  Fire code prohibits use of candles, hay, straw, corn stalks, and leaves in buildings.

CANCELLATIONS MUST BE RECEIVED AT LEAST 24 HOURS IN ADVANCE
PLEASE CALL 256-2248, OR E-MAIL SCHEDULING FOR ALL CANCELLATIONS.
