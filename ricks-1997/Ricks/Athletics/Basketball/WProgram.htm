2000-2001 SEASON-LADY VIKINGS REVIEW

    Another banner year for Lady Viking basketball, posting 26 wins against only 4 losses.  After an early season non-conference loss at Western Wyoming, Ricks went on a 14 game winning streak, posting a 16-1 record and being ranked as high as seventh nationally.  Ricks lost only twice in conference play, and went into the post season tournament as one of the favorites before cold shooting led to an upset at the hands of CSI.  Lori Woodland was named Region 18 Coach of the year while Mandie Little earned First Team All Region 18 honors and All American Honorable Mention.
