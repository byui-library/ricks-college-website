DEFERMENT

To accommodate the needs of Ricks students, a deferment policy has been implemented.  Students can apply for either of two types of deferments: (1) LDS Missionary Deferment and (2) Continuing Student Deferment.  Information on both types of deferments is listed below.  

LDS Missionary Deferment

A completed LDS Missionary Deferment Application and a copy of the mission call must be submitted to the Ricks College Office of Admissions after receiving your call and before entering the Missionary Training Center (MTC).  

To defer enrollment without re-applying, the following conditions should be met:

  • Be admitted as a regular day school, degree seeking student at Ricks College;

  • If you are a current Ricks student, enter the MTC before the end of the semester to which you have been admitted;

  • Do not attend or enroll at any college or university between leaving and returning to Ricks;

  • Must not be on academic suspension nor dismissal from Ricks;

  • Complete a full mission and receive an ecclesiastical endorsement from your mission president.  

Following your mission you must enroll at Ricks College within a given time period.  If you return from your mission from October to April, you must enroll in the next Fall semester.  If you return home from May to September, you must enroll in the next Winter Semester.  

Continuing Student Deferment

All deferment applications must be approved by the Admissions Committee.  Submitting a deferment application does not guarantee its approval.  Notification of approval or denial will be given by the Admissions Committee.  

To defer enrollment without re-applying, the following conditions should be met:

  • Be admitted as a regular day school student the semester prior to requesting deferment;

  • Be admissible to Ricks College;

  • Submit a Continuing Student Deferment Application to the Ricks College Office of Admissions before the deadline of the semester you are wanting to return.   

Deferment for continuing students is valid for one semester only.  The student must re-enroll at the college the semester immediately following the semester of absence.

Deferment Applications

Applications for deferment can be completed and printed from this Web site.  Adobe Acrobat Reader is required to complete and print the deferment forms.  If you do not already have Acrobat Reader, click on the button below to download and install the free software program.  If you do not wish to use Adobe Acrobat Reader, you may obtain a deferment application from the Admissions Office by calling at 208-356-1026. 

The first page of the Continuing Student Deferment Application will be completed by you and the second page will be completed by your current Ricks College campus ward bishop.  You will complete the LDS Missionary Deferment application in its entirety.
