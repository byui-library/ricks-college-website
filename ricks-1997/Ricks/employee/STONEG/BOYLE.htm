ROBERT BOYLE (1627-1691)

(Irish)


I. In 1643 Evangelista Torricelli (1608-1647) was able to prove that air exerted pressure.

A. He showed that air could support a column of mercury thirty inches high, and in so doing, he invented the Barometer.

B. Gases became less mysterious. They were matter, having weight like liquids and solids (lower density).

II. Otto Von Guericke (1602-1686) invented an air pump that could pull air out of containers (1654).

A. Put two hemispheres together, removed the air. Air pressure held the hemispheres together.

B. Teams of horses attached to each hemisphere could not pull them apart.

(a) When air allowed to enter, they fall apart themselves.

III. Robert Boyle did more than any other attempted to produce mechanistic interpretations of chemical phenomena.

A. He used sound logic and repeated reference to experiment.

B. He published the "Skeptical Chemist" in 1661 and scorned the idea of all compounds made from the same three principles or the same four elements.

C. He believed all matter was made up of the same kind of ultimate particles, and that the elements were composed of different groupings or primitive coalition of these particles.

1. He gave no examples of elements, but believed their number might be much larger than the three or four assumed by the alchemists.

2. Boyle's criticism of the old ideas had little immediate effect. He helped to liberate chemistry from the old modes of thought.

IV. Boyle devised a new and better air pump.

A. Then he tried the opposite procedure of compressing gasses.

B. Boyle's Law (Published in 1622). The law was established by using mercury and glass tubes.















V. Boyle's experiments offered a focus for the gathering numbers of atomists. Boyle became an atomist.

A. Liquids and Solids can not be compressed in significant amounts. If they consist of atoms, these atoms must be in contact and can't be pushed closer together.

B. How can gasses be compressed unless they consist of tiny atoms separated by empty space.

1. Water evaporates. How can this be unless it disappears tiny bit by tiny bit (turns to vapor atom by atom).

2. If water is atoms in a gaseous state, why not in a liquid state also - impressive argument. Atomism began to win converts. Newton became an atomist.
