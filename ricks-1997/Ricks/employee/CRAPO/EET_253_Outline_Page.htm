PREREQUISITES: EET 151, 152, 153, and 154.

TEXT: OPERATIONAL AMPLIFIERS and LINEAR INTEGRATED CIRCUITS, Fifth Edition. Coughlin, Robert F. and Driscoll, Frederick F. 1998. Prentice-Hall, Inc. Upper Saddle River, New Jersey.

COURSE MATERIALS: 1. HP48 calculator or equivalent or better.  2. Engineering Graph Paper for problem assignments.

COURSE DESCRIPTION: Operational amplifiers, comparators, active filters, timers, and programmable timer/counters are studied. DACs and ADCs are introduced. An introduction to power control circuits and special measurement techniques is included. A student project is required. Laboratory exercises are required. Oral student presentations are required. Computers are used to simulate and solve linear integrated circuits and write research reports. (Fall)


STUDENT OBJECTIVES:

I. To be able to analyze circuits and solve problems dealing with selected linear integrated circuits.
II. To be able to determine proper component values for the external components used with linear integrated circuits.
III. To be able to use the computer to simulate and solve linear integrated circuits.
