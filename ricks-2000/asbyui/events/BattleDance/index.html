Battle of the Dance

The battle for the best is about to begin as students participate in the first Battle of the Dance at BYU-Idaho, Thursday, Feb. 7 at 7 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium.

Many students have prepared various dance routines, including hip hop, country, ballet, ballroom, folk, and clogging. Tryouts were held beforehand and have been narrowed down to fourteen groups that will perform in front of the judges.

The purposes of the Battle of the Dance are to provide an environment where students are given the opportunity to perform their dance abilities and talents in front of their friends and peers, provide a wholesome entertaining evening for faculty and students and provide an event where every form of dancing is invited and can be seen.

"This is an opportunity for the student body to put together dances and take the lead," Kevin Swiss, Student Body Officer Respresentative of Battle of the Dance, said.

Winners will be announced after the performance and prizes will be given to the winners. All students are invited to attend. Battle of the Dance Chairman is Charisa Fullmer and Student Body Officer representative is Kevin Swiss.
