FRANÇAIS 201 - COURSE OUTLINE



Mes amis, rebonjour et soyez les bienvenus dans notre cours de

français! ! Ce semestre nous allons beaucoup apprendre et nous allons

bien nous amuser. Vous avez bien choisi puisque (comme vous le savez

bien déjà) le français c'est la langue céleste et la plus belle langue

du monde. J'en suis passionnée et je vous souhaite une très bonne

expérience!



Course Objectives:

1. To solidify what you have already learned in French 101, French

102 and/or other courses. We will practice basic French grammar

and structures that you may already know but are uncertain about

using.

2. To expand on basic grammar concepts and structures that are essential

to proficiency in the French language as well as broaden

your vocabulary base.

3. To give you ample opportunity to develop your skills in listening,

reading, writing and speaking.

4. To give you further insight into French culture and the Francophone

world.

5. To deepen your love and appreciation for the French language.



Policies and Procedures



Text Books:

1. Les Aventures du Petit Nicolas by Sempé and Goscinny.

2. Histoires du Livre de Mormon- Pour Lecteurs Débutants.

3. Ma Grammaire Française-grammar packet

4. If you don't have one, a French dictionary is a good idea.



Les Devoirs:

1. You will read the stories from Les Aventures du Petit Nicolas as listed on the schedule. Come to class having read the story the first day it is listed on the schedule. For example, you will have read La Bonne Surprise the first time by Jan. 11. Be prepared

to discuss the story in depth. You will want to read each story several times before we

are finished with it. We will work on it together in class, and you may also be given exercises from the back of the story to do at home.



2. You will read approximately one chapter from Histoires du Livre de Mormon each

night. ( Ne vous inquiétez pas. Elles sont courtes). Read them out loud to practice

pronunciation and be prepared to take a written dictée and/or discuss them the next

day in class. You will need to be ready with chapter 1, Jan. 11.



3. You will be responsible to master a specified section of the grammar packet by the end

of each story from Les Aventures du Petit Nicolas. (see schedule) Any exercises that

we don't cover in class from that section are still your responsibility to complete and

master. You will turn in your grammar packet each test day to get credit for having

completed it. Each section is worth 20 points.



4. You will read a children's story to the class sometime during the second half of the semester.



5. You will participate in a group effort to create an original children's story in French.



Examens:

1. You will have a test at the end of each Nicolas story which will be on the story, and on

the grammar segment listed with that story.

2. You will have a written mid-term exam which will cover the grammar packet from

pages 1-98 and an oral exam over Les Histoires du Livre de Mormon and the Pronun-

ciation Guide from the Grammar Packet.

3. You will have a final written exam which will cover the grammar packet p. 99 to the

end and a Nicolas story (to be announced). There will be an oral final based on the

the Pronunciation Guide and to test your oral proficiency.



You will be graded on the following:

1. Exams- 100 points each, including story tests, mid-term and final tests.

2. Preparation -including participation and contribution to group work and discussions,

dictées etc. 100 points

3. Grammaire Packet completion - 120 points

4. Attendance- You may miss four class periods unexcused before your grade drops one-

half letter grade. Medical and school excuses will be accepted with

appropriate notes only.

5. Stories-individual and group- 100 points each



Extra Credit: bear your testimony in French, go to department tutor 2 hours a week, memorize the poem, "Sourire" located in the grammar packet.

De Bons Conseils:

Stay caught up-don't procrastinate or get behind. It's too hard on you! Don't wait until

the night before the test to start that section. Study a certain amount each night. Get in

a good habit starting now. Hang in there. Don't give up. You can do it!



An "A" student will:

1. Never miss class (few exceptions)

2. Pay attention in class and do what we're doing. Don't work on other classes, write letters, practice verbs etc. We focus on what you will be tested on.

3. Not skip tests. Be there ready unless you have made special arrangements with the

instructor in advance. You will loose points for late tests unless there are extenuating

circumstances.

4. Always complete the grammar packet and other assignments for the designated dates

and bring on time.

5. Be prepared each day and participate enthusiastically.

6. Amusez-vous bien!
