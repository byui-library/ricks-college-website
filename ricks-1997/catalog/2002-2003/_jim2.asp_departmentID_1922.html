    The  Department  of  Dance  offers  a  minor  in  Dance.    This  degree  is  designed  to  allow  a  student  to  open  a  dance  studio,  as  well  as  preparation  to  be  a  dance  specialist  in  a  church  and  community  setting. 

     
    The Mission of the Department of Dance is:
    1.    To  teach  students  that  they  will  become  better  dancers  through  living  the  restored  Gospel  of  Jesus  Christ  by  enhancing  their  roles  as  indiviudals,  Church  members,  citizens  and  parents.
    2.    To  teach  students  how  to  make  moral  and  value  judgments  about  their  choices  in  quality  of  movement,  music,  costume  and  lighting.
    3.    To  give  students  a  solid  technical  foundation  and  provide  an  opportunity  to  develop  creativity  on  all  levels  through  performance,  production  and  service  opportunities.
     


    Minor in  Modern Dance (108)
    This minor is designed to allow a student to open a dance studio, as well as preparation to be a dance specialist in a church and community setting.


    Minor Requirements

    Modern Dance Minors must select 17 credits in the core and must take 3 additional credits of dance classes with at least a "B" average.

    Minor in  World & Ballroom Dance Teaching (148)
    This degree is designed to allow a student to open a dance studio, as well as preparation to be a dance specialist in a church and community setting.


    Minor Requirements

    World and Ballroom Dance Teaching minor must select 11 to 13 credits in core classes and take 9 to 7 additional credits of dance classes with at least a "B" average. Number of credits in the intership will determine the number of electives. Upper division classes can always be substituted for lower division classes.
     Take all of these courses

     DANCE 140
     DANCE 190
     DANCE 258
     DANCE 259
     DANCE 299R
     DANCE 458R
     DANCE 498
     
    Course Descriptions

    DANCE 131 Jazz Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    A movement class where rhythms, style, and technique of jazz dancing are introduced to the student, including basic jazz terminology.
     
    DANCE 135 Tap Dance (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Tap Experience or consent of instructor.
    This is an accelerated beginning class for students with experience; or consent of instructor.
     
    DANCE 140 Modern Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    The course is designed to give the student experience in movement emphasizing locomotor skills, movement progressions, and dance techniques in ballet, modern, and jazz.
     
    DANCE 170 World Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    Dances from around the world.
     
    DANCE 171 Clog Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    This is a movement class where rhythms, styles, and techniques of clog dance are introduced to the student; including basic clog and buck terminology, style and steps. Set routines taught as well as basic skills.
     
    DANCE 172 Country Western and Square Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    This course is designed to teach country western line dance and the twenty-five basics of square dance.
     
    DANCE 180M Social Dance, Beginning (1:0:0)
    For beginners with an emphasis on partner dances in a social setting (waltz, swing, cha cha, foxtrot, polka).
     
    DANCE 180W Social Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    For beginners with an emphasis on partner dances in a social setting (waltz, swing, cha cha, foxtrot, polka).
     
    DANCE 181M Country Western Social Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    A course designed to develop skills of counry western social dancing.
     
    DANCE 181W Country Western Social Dance, Beginning (1:0:2)
    A course designed to develop skills of counry western social dancing.
     
    DANCE 184M Ballroom Dance, International Style-Standard (1:0:0)
    Prerequisite: Social Dance 180, or consent of instructor
    Bronze level technical instruction in the following dances: waltz, tango, foxtrot, quickstep, Viennese waltz.
     
    DANCE 184W Ballroom Dance, International Style-Standard (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Social Dance 180, or consent of instructor.
    Bronze level technical instruction in the following dances: waltz, tango, foxtrot, quickstep, Viennese waltz.
     
    DANCE 185M Ballroom Dance, International Style-Latin (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Social Dance 180, or consent of instructor.
    Bronze level technical instruction in the following dances: cha cha, rumba samba paso doble, jive.
     
    DANCE 185W Ballroom Dance, International Style-Latin (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Social Dance 180, or consent of instructor.
    Bronze level technical instruction in the following dances: cha cha, rumba samba paso doble, jive.
     
    DANCE 190 Ballet Beginning (1:0:2)
    Introducting the fundamentals of ballet technique including terminology, a brief history and ballet as a foundation for all dance styles.
     
    DANCE 231 Jazz Dance, Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: DA 131
    A movement class where intermediate levels of rhythms, style, and technique of jazz dance are explored, including basic jazz terminology.
     
    DANCE 240A Dance Technique IA (2:0:4)
    Prerequisite: This class is for dance minors or consent of instructor.
    Fundamental dance techniques emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time and energy awareness.
     
    DANCE 240B Dance Technique 240B I (2:0:4)
    Fundamental dance techniques emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time and energy awareness.
     
    DANCE 241A Dance Technique II A (2:0:4)
    Prerequisite: DA 140, 240A and B or equivalent.
    Fundamental dance techniques emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time and energy awareness.
     
    DANCE 241B Dance Technique II B (1-2:0:4)
    Prerequisite: DA 140, 240A and B or equivalent.
    Fundamental dance techniques in ballet and modern emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time and energy awareness.
     
    DANCE 242 Improvisaton and Choreography (2:0:4)
    Prerequisite: DA 240
    A practical course providing students with fundamentals of improvisation and choreography.
     
    DANCE 258 Teaching Techniques of World and Ballroom Dance (2:0:0)
    Prerequisite: Dance 170, 172, 180 or consent of instructor.
    A course designed to train a person to teach all forms of Social Dance: Ballroom, World, Square, etc., with an emphasis on Recreational Dancing.
     
    DANCE 259 Leadership in Music and Dance (2:0:0)
    Prerequisite: DA 180 or DA 172
    This course is designed specifically for those in charge of school, church, or club dances. The purpose of the class is to develop the skills necessary to handle all aspects of a dance.

    DANCE 271 Clog Dance, Intermediate (1:0:2)
    Prerequisite: Dance 171 or be an intermediate clogger and know pitter-patter buck steps.
    This is a movement class where rhythms, styles, and techniques of clog dance on an intermediate-advanced level will be introduced to the student.
     
    DANCE 272 Country Western and Square Dance, Intermediate (1:0:2)
