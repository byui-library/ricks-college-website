For several years Ricks College has been involved in men's soccer.  Although it has only been at the club level, the program has struggled  through hard times to become a prominent contender in the men's arena.  In the early 80's a man by the name Oscar Bustos, a former Chilean professional soccer player came to the Rexburg community and began coaching the men's team.  Thanks to Oscar, the program enjoyed several successful winning seasons.  In 1995 a student by the name of Peter Stilling came to Ricks College and played under the direction of Coach Bustos.  Stilling left after one season and later returned to Ricks as an employee, only to find that Oscar had left and the program had gone by the way side.  Stilling immediately took over as the men's soccer advisor in the winter of 1997.  After having been employed by the college for only 4 days, he held tryouts for their first ever indoor team.  Two men's teams were formed and competed very well in the local league.  The following spring of 98 resulted in Stilling coaching the men's team.  The men played their first ever game with their older "brothers" at BYU and were invited back the following spring to play them again.   Stilling continued in his trend and formed a men's team for the summer outdoor play out of the city of Idaho Falls. 

In the fall of 1998, he held the school's biggest men's tryout ever - 126.  He raised the standard of play to an all time maximum.  Normally the men's team was lucky to play 12-14 games in the fall.  This time they were treated to a 26 game season.   After the fall season ended, the indoor season greeted us again.  This time Stilling formed 3 men's teams.  His men's "A" team went 26-2, thereby winning 2 of 3 league titles.  After indoor was done, they again had a great spring season.  The men played their 2nd annual game against BYU.  Spring season also saw the men play their first ever alumni game.   Currently the program is at the end of fall 1999.  The men's team finished with an overall record of   7 - 10 - 3.  This was good enough to place them 6th in the ICSA and 7th in NIRSA Region 6.   Stilling is now the ICSA Men's President.  He oversees the administration of 15 men's teams, as well as maintaining his duties as advisor and coach of Ricks College men's program.
