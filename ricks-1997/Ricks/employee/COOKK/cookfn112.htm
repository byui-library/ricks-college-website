COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 112
Title: Nutrition and Young Children Credit: 2
Text: Health, Safety, and Nutrition for the Young Child, 4th Edition, Marotz, 1998
     Meals without Squeals: Childcare Feeding Guide and Cookbook, 2nd Edition, Berman, 1997

Course Objectives:
At the completion of this course the student should be able to:
- Discuss the role of  nutrition as it applies to the growth and development of a young child from infant to preschooler.
- Define the roles of the adult and the child in the feeding process.
- Describe how to establish an appropriate and safe feeding environment for the young child.
- Plan menus and snacks for feeding young children in group settings following the guidelines of the Child and Adult Care Food Program
- State guidelines for teaching young children nutrition, cultural food awareness and food safety concepts
- Apply principles of food safety and sanitation in food purchasing, storage, preparation and service.

Description:
Concepts of human nutrition, nutrition education, menu planning, sanitation and food safety with emphasis on preschool children.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Develop a Nutrition and Food Safety Document which is an orderly compilation of the nutrition guidelines, nutrition education strategies, menu planning, sanitation and food safety concepts reviewed during the semester.
Plan menus following the Child and Adult Care Food Program dietary guidelines.
