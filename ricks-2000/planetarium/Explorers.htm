"Explorers”  

(3th grade and up)

 

 

Astronomy played a pivotal role in the amazing navigation feats of the early Hawaiians and other Polynesians. These feats are being relived today as modern explorers using the stars, birds, ocean currents repeat the legendary voyages of the early Polynesians. This programs is excellent for third grade or higher. It entertains and educates. Come join us as we sail 1000's of miles of open oceans using the stars as our guide.
