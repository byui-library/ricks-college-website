Mission of the BYU-I/Ricks College Alumni Council
  1. Maintain and strengthen testimonies, standards and ethics of the LDS Church in our families and communities.
  2. Build and reinforce a worthwhile bond between BYU - Idaho and its alumni.
  3. Inform alumni of academic, cultural, social and opportunities provided by BYU - Idaho.
  4. Inform alumni of the accomplishments of the students and alumni.
  5. Encourage a continuing and stimulating contact between the alumni and BYU - Idaho.
  6. Encourage alumni to voluntarily share their resources with BYU - Idaho to impact the future of young people, their families and the growth and stability of Heavenly Father's Kingdom.
