DRAMA
CLASS CODE: ENG 353 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Explores literary study of plays, encompassing their historical, critical, and social contexts.
DESCRIPTION: Explores literary study of plays, encompassing their historical, critical, and social contexts.
TOPICS: Study of central works and major writers; drama in its historical, critical, and social contexts; drama as an art form and its impact on the human condition; language and imagination.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Reading, analyzing, and criticizing dramatic literature. (2) Learning distinctive characteristics and literary terms of drama. (3) Integrating dramatic literature and dramatic criticism within the larger body of literature. (4) Integrating drama with other studies such as philosophy, psychology, sociology, and religion. (5) Perceiving how drama influences individuals and institutions.
REQUIREMENTS: Primary and secondary reading drama and criticism. Papers, e.g. interpretive, analytical, and critical. Presentations; Attendance at dramatic performances. Research. Eight or more plays.
