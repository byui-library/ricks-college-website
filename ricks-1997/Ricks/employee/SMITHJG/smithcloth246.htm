COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 246
Title: Draping Credit: 3
Text: The Art of Fashion Draping

Course Objective:
The major goal of this course is to help students acquire a working knowledge of designing by the draping method.  A collection of draped pattern sloper will be created.
1.  Demonstrate draping skills for Knit & woven fabrics
2.  Apply specific principles to transfer draped patterns to hard copies
3.  Derive solutions to grainline problems

Description:
Concepts of draping 5 basic sloper on dress forms to create various fashion designs.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Customize a basic dress form
2.  Drape 5 basic sloper
3.  Transfer 5 basic draped sloper to hard copies
4.  Drape 2 apparel garments from sketches
