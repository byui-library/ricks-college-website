April Graduation 2001

Basic Information Schedule of Events

Congratulations!! Ricks College Graduation will be Saturday, April 28, 2001. We hope the Spirit of Ricks will continue with you wherever you may go. Upon graduation, you will join with over 100,000 alumni that have been a part of creating this great Spirit of Ricks.

We hope the information provided will be helpful while you plan for a memorable graduation day. Again, congratulations and best wishes for a bright future!

 
Checklist
A checklist for April graduation candidates will be available at the Registrars Office beginning April 9th. Please check to be sure your name is included with the proper spelling, correct mailing address, etc. If you are graduating but not marching in commencement, pick up your diploma cover at the Registrars Office before you leave campus.
Caps and Gowns
Caps and gowns may be picked up in the Hart Auxiliary Gym on Thursday and Friday, April 26 and 27. Please allow a few extra minutes to fill out an Alumni Biographical Record so that the Alumni Association and former classmates can keep in touch with you. If you are unable to pick up your cap and gown yourself, a roommate or friend may pick it up for you.

The representative from Herff Jones will be on campus during that time to assist with any problems you may have concerning your cap and gown.

The gown, cap, and tassel are yours to keep.
Graduation Banquet

The graduation banquet will be held on Friday, April 27th in the East and West Ballrooms of the Manwaring Center. Seating will begin at 5 p.m., the program and banquet will begin at 5:30 p.m. The live program will be in the West Ballroom. Church attire is appropriate.

Parents, relatives, and friends of the graduates are invited to attend. Tickets are available at the Ticket Office beginning April 9th. Each graduate will receive one complimentary ticket by showing his or her I.D. at the Ticket Office; additional guest tickets can be purchased for $10.50 each, with no limit.

(The Ticket Office is open from 9:30 to 3 p.m. Monday - Friday, except for Tuesday, close at 2 p.m.)
Graduation Ball
The Graduation Ball will be held Friday, April 27th from 9 - Midnight in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom. Free Admission, church attire. For more information, contact the ASRC Office (356-2270).
Academic March
When you pick up your cap and gown, you will receive instructions concerning the Academic March. Be sure to read through these instructions carefully.
Commencement
Graduates are honored together and the degrees are conferred on the graduates. The speaker has not been announced. Guests are required to have a ticket to attend this event. Graduates DO NOT need a ticket. There will be only 3,080 available seats in the Hart Auditorium, and 1,500 graduates will be attending. Tickets are required so two members from each graduates family are able to attend. When you pick up your cap and gown, you will receive your 2 tickets (3 for married students). The Taylor Chapel and MC West Ballroom will serve as overflow areas for those without tickets.

Questions concerning Commencement or Convocations, please contact the Graduation Office, ext. 1005 or e-mail calderwoodc@ricks.edu.
Convocations
Graduates are divided into divisions, each graduate is recognized individually while walking across the stage to receive his/her diploma cover. No ticket is required for this event. When you pick up your cap and gown, you will also receive a seating card for your convocation. This card will state which division you are in, therefore, which convocation to attend. If you would like to know now which division you are in, please check with your advisor.
