HONORS PROGRAM

The Honors Program is designed for students who do well academically and who have a positive attitude toward learning. Honors classes and activities can be beneficial to students of all majors as well as those who are undecided about their major.

To be admitted to the program, a student must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.3 (high school GPA if just entering college; otherwise college GPA). Anyone who meets that requirement will be admitted automatically upon submission of an application to the Director in Smith 294B. Once admitted, a student is not required to take any classes, but should do so in order to benefit from membership. Except as explained below regarding graduation and financial benefits, a student's GPA is not monitored after admission.

Because classes are small in size and taught by excellent teachers, a greater opportunity is available for students to develop their capacities. In addition, honors classes are viewed positively by university admission and evaluation personnel.

Financial assistance is available. A $50 scholarship is awarded each semester to any student who takes an honors class, has a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.5, and pays a fee of $5. When the same requirements are met, a student may also register for more than 21 credits without paying the extra fee that would otherwise be required.

Students who graduate in the Honors Program are named in the graduation program, wear a distinctive yellow tassel at graduation, and have their Honors Program graduation noted on their transcript and graduation certificate.

Additional advantages of membership include a lounge located at Smith 294, retreats (Saturday, evening, or overnight excursions for intellectual development and fellowship), and other activities.

For more information or to submit an application, contact the Honors Program Director, Smith 294B, Ricks College, Rexburg, ID, 83460-0835, or call 208-356-1438.
