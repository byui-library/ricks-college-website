      BYU-Idaho Safety Overview

The Mission of the  BYU-Idaho Safety Office is to reduce loss of life and property, and to protect the university and its staff, faculty, and students from all types of hazards through a comprehensive risk-based emergency management program of mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery.

The Safety Office has three main areas of focus:  (1) Fire Protection, (2) Occupational and Student Safety, and (3) Hazardous Materials Management. 

We are located in the Physical Plant building, Room 136.  We can be reached at (356-2457) or by e-mail at Safety@ricks.edu
