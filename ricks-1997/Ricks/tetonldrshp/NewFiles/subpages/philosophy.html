Philosophy

The Leadership Institute is open to any interested student at Ricks College. Our goal is to develop the righteous capacities of all students, not just the selected student leaders or other exclusive groups. We value the development of people and attempt to fashion our activities in a way that promotes individual growth.

The Teton Lodge and beautiful ranch property offer an inspiring setting for leadership training. We feel that theoretical training alone, however, is empty without its partner--action. Many of our programs provide opportunities for students to develop leadership skills through service to others. Our leadership training and service involvement seeks to further the College’s mission to develop testimonies of the gospel and prepare students for future roles as citizens and parents.
