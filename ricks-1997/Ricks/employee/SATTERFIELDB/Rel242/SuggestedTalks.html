Suggested Talks or Writings




Brigham Young
" Salvation." A discourse delivered by President Brigham Young, in the Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City, January 16, 1853. (Journal of Discourses, 1:1-6)

"The Kingdom of God." Remarks by President Brigham Young, July 13, 1862 (Journal of Discourses, 9:308-317) [A very interesting talk about the spiritual and political nature and future of the Kingdom of God on earth.]

"Celestial Marriage." A discourse delivered by Elder Orson Pratt, in the Tabernacle, Great Salt Lake City, August 29, 1852 (Journal of Discourses, 1:53-66) [Brigham Young assigned Orson Pratt to give this discourse in which he was introduce the doctrine of plural marriage to the Church. The discourse is historically interesting but Elder Pratt gives much of his own opinion on the subject.]

"Necessity of Building Temples--The Endowment" Talk given by President Brigham Young at the laying of the foundation stones of the Salt Lake Temple [Journal of Discourses 2:29ff].

"Heirship"   Remarks by President Brigham Young  on the "Right of Heirship in the Priesthood" given at the beginning of General Conference 8 April 1853 [Journal of Discourses, 6:306ff]  Brigham Young asked others of the General Authorities to address this same issue.  The next talk is a response to President Young's request.

"Heirship and Piresthood." A discourse delivered by Elder Parley P. Pratt, at the General Conference, April 10, 1853.

John Taylor
"The Establishment of the Kingdom of God upon the Earth," Chapter 11 in The Government of God, (Liverpool: S. W. Richards, 1852). [Can be found in the book stacks on the second floor of the library as well as in the Infobases.]

Journal of Discourses 23:47-68.  Talk given by President John Taylor in response to the Edmund-Tucker Act against plural marriage.

Wilford Woodruff
"The Administration of Angels" Collected Discourses, Vol.5, October 19, 1896 [A great talk about the necessity of having the Holy Ghost.]

"The Written Word--Joseph and Brigham In Kirtland--Present Inspiration" Conference Report, October 1897, pp.22-24.

"Progression--the Fatherhood of God--the Perfect Man--the Gifts of the Spirit" in Journal of Discourses 14:300ff.

"Glory Awaiting the Saints" Collected Discourses, Vol. 4, Lorenzo Snow, October 5th, 1894.

Conference Report, October 1901, pp. 60-62. (President Snow's last talk)

Joseph F. Smith
"First Presidency Reoganization," Conference Report, November 1901, p.69-73

"Family Government By Love" in Gospel Doctrine, pp. 314-318.

"Status of Children in the Resurrection,"  Messages of the First Presidency, 5:90-98.

"In the Presence of the Divine," Messages of the First Presidency, 5:4-11; Conference Report April 1916, 1-8. [Thousands felt the Divine inspiration that accompanied the delivery of this remarkable sermon, given at the opening session of April 1916 General Conference.]

 A sermon on the literalness of the Sonship of Jesus Christ - Messages of the First Presidency, 4:327-332. [An important addition ot LDS doctrine and should be studied along with the "Origin of Man" statement.]

"The Spiritual and Physical Redemption of Fallen Man,"  Journal of Discourses, 23:169-175.

"Melchizedek Priesthood and its offices," Conference Report, October 1903, p.86-87.
