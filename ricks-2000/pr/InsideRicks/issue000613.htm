RICKS ANNOUNCES RECORD SECOND TERM ENROLLMENT

Ricks College has a record 1,994 students attending its second five-week term of summer school. This is a slight increase from the 1,974 students who attended last year at the same time. First summer term had 2,342 students attending, which was also a new record.

For the first time, Ricks is introducing an eight-week summer session that will begin June 19. Assistant Academic Vice President Jim Gee expects the session to have about 450 students. He explains the session is designed mainly to accommodate students who just graduated from high school, but who were unable to attend the second summer term because high school was not quite finished.

Statistics for the second term show students are taking an average load of 4.8 credits. There are 985 freshmen and 1,009 sophomores; 786 males and 1,208 females; 915 from Idaho, 263 from Utah, 129 from California and 106 from Washington. There are 108 international students currently enrolled with more anticipated for the upcoming summer terms.

RICKS MUSICIANS TO BE FEATURED IN ANNUAL FACULTY SHOWCASE

Four Ricks College music faculty members will be featured in the second annual Music Faculty Showcase set for Thursday, June 22, at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall.. Those performing on the showcase include violinist Ted Ashton, soprano Eda Ashby, pianist Steve Allen and tenor saxophonist Mark Watkins. Tickets for this Summer in the Snow presentation are available from the Ricks College Ticket Office. Call 356-2230 or online at www.byui.edu/tickets. General admission is $3. A reception will be held immediately following the showcase in the foyer of the Snow Center for the Performing Arts and refreshments will be served.

Allen, director of piano studies at Ricks, will play two pieces by composer Claude Debussy. A native of Ogden, Utah, he received degrees in piano performance from the Eastman School of Music and San Jose State University. He earned his doctoral degree in piano pedagogy and literature from the University of Indiana, where he attained additional degrees in music education and musicology. He has taught at Ricks since 1986.

Ashby, a soprano, came to Ricks in 1999 where she is director of vocal studies. She holds a bachelor's degrees from Brigham Young University, a master's degree from the University of Southern California and doctorate in music arts from the USC. She taught for nine years at Utah Valley State College in Orem, Utah, and has been heavily involved with the Church Music Workshop at BYU.

Ashton will perform Sonata in A Major, Op. 12, No. 2, by Ludwig van Beethoven on violin, accompanied by Allen on piano. Ashton serves as director of the Symphony Orchestra, coordinator of chamber music, is a member of the Ricks College faculty string ensemble Serenata and teaches applied violin. Prior to coming to Ricks in 1998, he was the orchestra coordinator for the Logan City Schools in Logan, Utah, where he also directed the orchestras at Logan High School. He received his bachelor of music degree from BYU and master of education from Utah State University. In 1992 he was the recipient of the National School Educator Award from the American String Teachers Association.

Watkins has been director of woodwind studies at Ricks since 1999. He will perform three movements to Concerto for Bassoon and Orchestra, K. 191, by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, accompanied on piano by Nori Brower, an adjunct faculty member at Ricks. The music was transcribed for tenor saxophone and piano by Watkins. He received his bachelor's degree from BYU and his master's and doctorate degrees in five woodwind instruments from Indiana University's School of Music with secondary areas in music history and jazz studies. He has been an associate instructor at Indiana University, instructor at BYU and assistant professor of music at North Dakota State University.
