Motor Pool (7-1) 

I.                PURPOSE 

To describe the guidelines applicable for use of BYU-Idaho Motor Pool vehicles.

II.       REFERENCES

Financial Services Travel Policies.

III.          POLICY

A.      Vehicle use will be charged to departments at current rental rates.  For rental schedules,  
      please contact the Motor Pool office.

B.    A minimum daily charge will be assessed on all vehicle rentals.  For minimum daily charges,  
       please contact the motor pool office.    

C.    University Motor Pool vehicles are to be used only for official University business.                
        University vehicles are not to be used for regular transportation between the campus and the
        employee’s residence, except as approved for on-call duty.

       When personal travel is incurred in connection with an official business trip, these miles will be
        accounted for and the department reimbursed from personal funds.  The rate charged for
        personal miles will be one-half of the current government reimbursement rates.

D.    All University vehicles must be maintained to acceptable standards for safety and appearance
       (cleanliness, paint, damage repair within 90 days, and general upkeep).

E.    All student drivers must be cleared through Campus Police.  A campus-driving permit will be
        issued upon clearance.  All drivers must have valid Idaho drivers' licenses, except for
        non-resident students, who may operate vehicles with valid licenses from their home states.

F.    The University is not responsible for moving violations by drivers of Motor Pool vehicles.

IV.    RESERVING VEHICLES 

A.      A vehicle rental order accessed, through the BYU-Idaho Intranet home page employee        
       section, must be completed and sent to the Motor Pool office at least 24 hours before the
       scheduled departure.  The department name, and account number and travel authorization
       number must be on all reservations.  An e-mail confirming the reservation will be sent to the
       requestor.

B.      If a vehicle reservation is canceled, the minimum daily charge will be assessed to the
      department if it fails to cancel a vehicle reservation by 9:00 A.M. on the working day prior to
      the scheduled departure day.

C.      All vehicles going out before 8:00 A.M. should be scheduled for that morning, not the night
       before.  The keys should be picked up the day before at the dispatching office and the car
       picked up the morning of the trip.  The vehicle should be returned immediately upon arrival
       back from the trip, not the following morning.

V.           REPORTING VEHICLE ACCIDENTS

A.    On Campus

  • Do not move either vehicle.

  • Call Campus Police immediately, regardless of the amount of damage.

B.    Off Campus

  • Call appropriate police agency.  If close to campus, call Campus Police.

  • Report all accidents within 24 hours to the Campus Police, regardless of the amount of damage.  They will assure that the necessary reports are prepared.

  • If injury or death occurs from an accident, please call the Vice-President of Administrative Services, or the Physical Plant Director as soon as possible.

Scheduling of University Busses (7-2)

I. PURPOSE

To outline the policy regarding scheduling of university busses.

II. POLICY

A. Priority for scheduling of busses is as follows:

1. Academic areas and performing groups.
2. Student government and other university-sponsored groups.
3. University stakes and wards.

B. Reservations which have been confirmed cannot be preempted by any other group without approval from the President's Council.

C. A group canceling a trip less than one week prior to the scheduled date will be charged a ten dollar cancellation fee.

III. PROCEDURE

A. Within the same priority status, groups will be scheduled on a first-come first-served basis.

B. Reservations will be held until two weeks prior to the scheduled event. If the reservation is not confirmed by this time, the vehicle will be released for scheduling to the next lower priority status.

Office Furnishings (7-3)

I. PURPOSE

To explain the limitations placed upon those who place personal items in their offices.

II. POLICY

A. The buildings and standard issue office furniture are the property of BYU-Idaho. No changes to the buildings or furnishings can be made without approval of the President’s Council.

B. Employees in offices can choose office paint color from among the approved color schemes available in Physical Plant. Requests for other colors cannot be honored.

C. Employees are not authorized to redecorate their offices by painting, wallpapering, paneling, or changing the ceilings.

D. Carpets may be installed at the personal expense of the employee under the following conditions:

1. The material must meet university standards for flammability.
2. The university will be responsible only to vacuum the carpets.
3. Carpets cannot be permanently installed in the offices. The university will not be responsible for shampooing or for any damage to the carpet. If the employee moves to a new office, the carpet must be removed from the old office.

E. Overstuffed furnishings which are not part of the standard office issue are allowed in offices under the following conditions:

1. The furnishings must be purchased with the employee’s own funds.
2. The university will not be responsible for any damage to the furnishings.
3. The furniture must meet university standards for flammability, safety, and appearance.
4. The furnishings will not be cleaned by the university.

F. Stereos, radios, refrigerators, and other small appliances can be used in offices provided they do not overload the available circuits. The university will not be responsible for maintenance of any of these appliances. If any of these appliances are found to be in poor repair, the university reserves the right to order their immediate removal from the premises.

G. Violations of this policy will be corrected by the university and charged to the personal account of the responsible party.

Keys to Buildings and Offices (7-4)

I. PURPOSE

To outline the policies and procedures for maintaining accountability for the keys to buildings and offices on campus.

II. PHILOSOPHY

A. Because of the need to account for the thousands of keys which have been issued and the need for security on campus, BYU-Idaho has formed a Key Committee consisting of representatives of faculty and staff who meet regularly to formulate rules and standardize policy relating to locks and keys on campus.

B. The building keying policy has been outlined so that all areas are accessible in cases of emergency while security to any given area is still maintained. This policy minimizes the number of individual keys but still does not sacrifice security. The levels of keying are as follows:

1. All buildings have three levels of keying: master, submaster, and individual doors.
2. All doors in a given building will be keyed to that building master key system.

III. POLICY

A. Key Inventory

Since all administrative and academic leadership are responsible for keys issued to their personnel, they will complete an annual key inventory for every key issued to personnel in their particular area. This inventory will be conducted through division chairmen or their administrative equivalent by the Physical Plant locks and key clerk.

B. Desks, Files, and Cabinets

The key office will not keep records of desks, files, and filing cabinet keys. However, keys will still be kept on file and can be made and issued at the key office.

C. Transfer of Keys

There should be no transferring of any key from one person to another without going through the key office to change the record and have a new key card filled out.

D. Terminations from BYU-Idaho

To eliminate the problem of employees retaining their keys or transferring their keys to their successors when they terminate, all university personnel will be cleared through the Personnel Office by means of the BYU-Idaho Employee Exit Form.

E. Housing Policy

Physical Plant will furnish all the keys for Ensign Halls, and Pioneer Halls. Those keys made for the housing department will be issued only to head residents and under no circumstances will Physical Plant issue those keys to the students.

F. Personnel on Leave

When a person goes on leave for more than one semester or when they vacate the office, they are to return all keys. Upon their return to the campus, the keys will be reissued to the individual. Any variance to this policy needs to be coordinated by the department coordinator of the Key Office.

G. Campus Stakes and Wards

Master keys will be issued only to stake presidents and bishops. Other needs will be met by issuing individual door keys. These individual keys will be issued only to the stake president who will be responsible for the annual key inventory as well. Upon his receipt of the keys, he will issue to bishops according to their needs.

IV. PROCEDURES

A. An employee will fill out a Request for Key (key card) and obtain the proper signatures as follows:

1. All key cards must be signed by the person to whom the key is issued and his department coordinator.

2. All master key cards must be signed for by (1) division chairman, (2) Physical Plant Director, or (3) the Vice President of Administration.

B. After the key card has been properly filled out and signed, it may be mailed or presented in person at the Physical Plant Key Office.

C. The key will not be issued immediately upon receipt of the properly signed card, but the key will be available at the Key Office 24 hours after receiving the card. In cases of emergencies the custodian of that building or the Campus Police have keys to allow access to the desired area.

D. No keys will be issued through campus mail. The person to whom they key will be issued must pick up the key at the Key Office. He or she cannot send a friend, spouse, roommate, secretary, etc. to get the key.

E. In the event that a key is lost there will be a $5.00 replacement charge for each lost key or $25.00 for each master key. No new key will be issued until this fee has been paid. However, if at a later day the key is found, upon surrender of the key the imposed fine will be refunded.
