TRANSFERRING GENERAL EDUCATION TO BYU
Although Ricks College and BYU have different General Education programs, it is possible to fill all BYU G.E. requirements while at Ricks. Students graduating from Ricks with an Associate Degree in Arts and Sciences can now waive the lower division General Education courses at BYU. However, to graduate from BYU, students must fill BYU's Advanced Writing and Foreign Language or Mathematic Requirements and also the University Requirements. These requirement are reviewed below and matched with Ricks College courses. Because many majors at BYU specify which courses to use in filling G.E. groups, students should carefully review the requirements of their intended major at BYU.
