Suggested Articles on Temple and Temple Topics

  • Marion G. Romney, "Temples - The Gates to Heaven," Ensign (March 1971), 12-16.
  • Carlos E. Asay, "The Temple Garment," Ensign (August 1997)18-23.
  • Blake Ostler, "Clothed Upon: A Unique Apect of Christian Antiquity," in BYU Studies Vol. 22, No. 1 (Fall 1982), 31-45.
  • Dallin H. Oaks, "Taking Upon Us the Name of Jesus Christ," Ensign (May 1985), 80-83.
  • Lynn H. Hilton, "The Hand as a Cup in Ancient Temple Worshp" (A paper presented at the Thirtieth Annual Symposium on the Archaeology of the Scriptures, held at BYU on 26 September 1981.)
  • Gordon B. Hinkley, "Keeping the Temple Holy," Ensign (May 1990), 49-52. (About temple recommend questions)
  • Marvin J. Ashton, "On Being Worthy," Ensign (May 1989) 20-22.
  • David O. McKay, "An Address on the Temple Ceremony"
  • John A. Widstoe, "Temple Worship"
  • Ezra Taft Benson, "What I Hope You Will Teach Your Children About the Temple," Ensign (August 1985), 7-10.
  • Hugh W. Nibley, "Meanings and Functions of Temples," Encyclopedia of Mormonism, Vol. 4, Temples
  • Alma P. Burton, "Endowment," Encyclopedia of Mormonism, Vol 2, Endowment.
  • Donald W. Parry, "Washings and Anointings," Encyclopedia of Mormonism, Vol 4, Washings and Anointings.
  • Delbert L. Stapley, Strait and Narrow Path" Conference Report, April 1955, 65-68.
  • Bruce R. McConkie, "Obedience, Consecration, and Sacrifice," Conference Report, Apr. 1975, 74; or Ensign, May 1975, 50
