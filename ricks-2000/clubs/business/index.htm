Welcome to the Business Club Home Page!

Meetings for the business club are every Thursday at 8:00 a.m. in Smith 240.  Please feel free to stop by and join the fun.

Mission Statement:

The purpose of the Business Club is to:
1. Create business ethics that exemplify the teachings of Christ.
2.  Cultivate leadership and communication skills that will provide a solid foundation for future responsibilities.
3.  Provide each student with information and experience that will better prepare them for the constantly changing world of business.
4.  Build lasting friendships and college memories through serving one another.
