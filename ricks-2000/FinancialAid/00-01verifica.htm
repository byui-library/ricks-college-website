Verification

In order to ensure accuracy, approximately one-third of all federal aid applicants will be selected by the Dept. of Education to complete a verification process. In this process, the financial aid staff at BYU-Idaho will compare the information reported on the initial application (SAR) with the verification documents that are submitted by the student.  These documents may include such documents as:

* Verification worksheet (will be sent to student from BYU-Idaho),

* Student, Parent (if Independent) tax returns, etc.

* If there are discrepancies, BYU-Idaho will make corrections.

Students selected for verification will receive notification on the Student Aid Report and/or from the BYU-Idaho Financial Aid Office. The student should carefully follow the instructions. The student may be required to submit copies of his/her tax return (and spouses’s return, if married) as well as the tax returns for the parents (if student is a dependent).

If you need tax account information, call the IRS at 1-800-TAX-1040 and ask for the 1721 form or a no-cost recap of the year that is being verified.
