Course Assignment # 3

Reading Assignment:  

Please read the following files located in "Preparing for BYU-Idaho" section of Pathway.   

  1. A Privilege And a Responsibility - Please invite your parents to read both devotional addresses listed in this section and discuss them together.
  2. Find out your campus ward and stake (must know your campus housing address).
  3. Religion Courses at BYU-Idaho.
  4. Weekly Campus Devotionals

Journal Assignment:

Record a journal entry in your "New Student Journal." At the top of the page write Journal
Entry # 3 along with today's date or the date in which you write the entry.

  1. After reading the two devotional addresses, what do you feel your privileges are in attending BYU-Idaho?  What are your personal responsibilities? 

  2. What thoughts, feelings, or insights did  you and your parents have from reading these devotional addresses? 

  3. What effect, if any, did reading these addresses have on the goals you established in the previous activity?  Have your goals changed from participating in this assignment?  Were your previous goals reinforced from this assignment?  

  4. What is the role of BYU-Idaho in terms of spiritual matters?   What, if anything have you learned about yourself? 
  5. What one thing do you hope to accomplish spiritually during your time at Ricks?

  6. Feel free to record any other personal thoughts or feelings you had from this assignment in your New Student Journal.
