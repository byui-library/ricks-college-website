Program Music (narrative music). Music which tells a story, and is usually written on the program so everyone gets it right, thus the name. (i.e.,Hansel and Gretel, Romeo and Juliet)

Rondo: ABACADA. (has one repeating theme, the A)

Theme and Variation (A1A2A3A4) has one melody played from start to finish, over and over, changing it slightly each time.

Sonata form is ABA, with an Exposition, Development and Recapitulation.

Expo usually has two themes. Is repeated so you can memorize the themes.

Dev then works with those two themes.

Recap sounds like expo but does not repeat. Ends with a coda (Latin for tail)

Is found in the first movement only; the other two movements can follow several different forms.

Sonatas are for solo instrument, not for voice. 5-10 minutes long.

Orchestra is a group of performers. (A symphony is a composition played by an orchestra.)

Symphony is a sonata for orchestra. Has 4 movements. Third (minuet) was added by Haydn and later modified by Beethoven (scherzo or "joke"). Is most popular form of classical music today. 20-60 minutes long.

Concerto is a sonata for orchestra and soloist. Three movements. 30-40 minutes. Very popular, even today.

Fugue (flight/tempus fugit/ "fugitive") is AB, has only Expo and Dev. Was invented before the sonata. Favorite form of Bach and others of his day. The single theme flees all over the work, is heard in all the voices, and is played in as many ways as possible: upside down, backwards, only the first part, only the last few notes, etc. Only a few minutes long, but can also appear mixed into many other forms, in symphonies, etc.

This form was the genesis of Sonata Form.

Opera (i.e., Madame Butterfly) is a musical form with soloists and chorus. Has scenery and staging. Serious, dramatic. Every word is sung. Is sung in native language. Several hours long.

aria - solo, where music is more important than the words

recitative - solo, where words are more important than the music

chorus - speaks for the people/the masses

Operetta (Gilbert and Sullivan wrote some of the best, i.e., Mikado) is a smaller version of opera, usually funny, light. Has both singing and speaking lines. Several hours long.

Oratorio (Messiah) is a religious opera, but without staging or costumes. Has chorus, arias and recitatives, like opera. Several hours long.

The two most popular Forms in music are AB (binary) and ABA (ternary)

ABCDEF would be through-composed, because nothing repeats.

Elements are essential to a work of art (you can’t have a line without line)

lines come in 6 types: horizontal, vertical, diagonal, slow curve, quick curve, S-curve

2 types of perspective: aerial, makes things in the distance look lighter in color; fuzzy

linear, makes distant objects look smaller

value: its cost, or the amount of light and dark

chiaroscuro: extreme light and dark, as in Rembrandt or Caravaggio

texture: painting it to look like it would feel to the touch

color: color wheel, primary, secondary, opposites are complimentary; red is seen first, and blue last. In pigments, black is all color; white is none. (Light is just the opposite.)
