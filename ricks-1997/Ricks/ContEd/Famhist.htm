Saturday, March 14, 1998

This conference is presented biannually by the Ricks College Family History Center and the Division of Continuing Education and is open to all persons who have a genealogical interest. The topics are varied enough to meet the needs of the beginner as well as the experienced genealogist.

Deadline for Pre-registration is March 9, 1998. Pre-registration fees for the conference will be $13 per person. Registration will be taken at the door in the Smith Lobby beginning at 7:30 a.m. for $15 per person on a space-available basis.
