Course Description:

Introduction to electronic control systems, feedback control theory, and Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs).  Electromechanical components: sensors, motors, and driver circuits.  Mechanics: gears, springs, friction, and inertia.  Interface circuits between mechanics and electronics.   Student presentations covering some of the class topics are required.   Computers are used to program PLCs, create graphics for class presentations, and write reports.  Laboratory exercises are included.

At the conclusion of this course students will understand the functional blocks of process control systems and servomechanisms.  They will be able to analyze and describe the advantages and disadvantages of different implementation details.
