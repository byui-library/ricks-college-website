COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 270R
Title: Client Experience Credit: 1-2
Text: No Text required   – price list booklets

General Course Objectives:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire the knowledge , skills, and experience necessary to work with clients and earn a profit.  Upon completion of this course students should be able to:

  1.  Demonstrate proper construction techniques on client garments and projects.
  2.  Apply specific principles of altering and fitting garments for clients.
  3.  Derive solutions to problems encountered while working with clients.
  4.  Keep the appropriate records, work orders, and  inventory (computerized).
  5.  Keep appropriate deadlines.

Description:
Client experience in custom sewing including pattern making, fitting problems, measurements, taking orders, and working with deadlines, etc.  Designed to allow practical application of skills and principles learned in other courses to increase speed, ability and confidence.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Design an apparel project
2.  Flat-pattern work
3.  Construction of a total garment
4.  Non apparel project
5.  Mass production project
6.  Hems
6.  Replace a Zipper
7.  Client Measurements
8.  Monogramming or applique work
9.  Take work Orders
10.  Collect Money
11.  Return Projects to Clients
12.  Computer Entered bookkeeping
