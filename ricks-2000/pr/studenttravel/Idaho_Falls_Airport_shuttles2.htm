Until the end of the third summer term 2001, Public Relations will continue to arrange for shuttles to and from the Idaho Falls Municipal Airport located approximately 35 miles from campus. If needed, the college will provide a driver and vehicle to pick up students for a fee of $25 per trip. If more than one student is taking advantage of the offer, the fee is split between them with a minimum fee being $8. Advance arrangements for this individualized service are required at least two business days before the shuttle is needed.  Reservations may be made by contacting the Public Relations office during business hours (Monday-Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.) at (208) 356-1150.

Students should meet at the baggage area of the Idaho Falls Municipal Airport at the designated times. The shuttle bus/van will bring you to your housing unit.  Depending requests, the shuttle leaving from Rexburg meet at the  Manwaring Center circular drive or from individual housing units to go to the Idaho Falls airport.  

Students needing rides from the Idaho Falls Greyhound bus station can make special arrangements with BYU-Idaho Public Relations until the end of the third summer term.

Several travel options are available to those in the Rexburg community.

 EQUIPMENT

Vehicles used may not be owned by the college but may be owned by private, licensed and insured operators hired by the college. If reservations do not warrant use of a bus, the college reserves the right to use vans with luggage trailers operated by college employees.
