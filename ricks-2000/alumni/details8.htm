Details and Criteria

Purpose

To Honor the memory of Ricks Colleges' finest athletes and to recognize outstanding athletic performance at Ricks College and continued athletic achievement at a four-year college or university.

Criteria

  1. Nominees must have participated in at least one varsity sport for two years while attending Ricks College.  Both male and female athletes are eligible.
  2. The nominee must continue his/her athletic prowess in their junior and senior years and be in good academic standing.
  3. They must exemplify the highest standards of the LDS Church and be active in their ward.
