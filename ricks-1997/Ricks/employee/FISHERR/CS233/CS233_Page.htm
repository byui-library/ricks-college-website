Course Description:

Introduction to digital electronics (combinational and sequential logic), computer organization (datapath, ALU, control unit, memory, etc.), instruction sets, I/O, and data communication.  Students must complete a research project.  Laboratory exercises are included.

Required Software: 

WinCUPL: Compiler for Programmable Logic Devices (Freeware version).  The serial number you should enter is 66999998.  Download WinZip if you do not have it already.  Read the CUPL Tutorial.

Digital Works: Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software to design and simulate logic circuits (Freeware version).  Read a short tutorial called Getting Started with Digital Works.

CircuitMaker: This is similar to Digital Works (above), but also lets you work with analog circuits.  This program makes it easy to include schematic diagrams in your lab reports.
