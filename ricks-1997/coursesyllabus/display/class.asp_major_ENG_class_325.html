Theory and Practice of Language - Grammar and Usage
THEORY LANG
CLASS CODE: ENG 325 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Introduces the study of the English language with an overview of grammar, history, usage, and semantics. (Required for English majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Introduces the study of the English language with an overview of grammar, history, usage, and semantics. (Required for English majors.)
TOPICS: Grammar; history of English language; usage; semantics.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Becoming proficient copy editors of their own written work as well as the written work of others.
(2) Understanding principles of English sentence structure and syntax. (3) Understanding word origin, meaning, and structure and their cultural influences. (4) Understanding linguistic categories (parts of speech) of tradition and non-traditional grammars. (5)Understanding how transformational grammar identifies the deep structure of language. (6) Becoming familiar with and understand other grammars. (7) Understanding how language influences thinking.
REQUIREMENTS: Become conversant with the terminology of traditional grammar. Be able to explain grammatical and usage problems to others. Edit and proofread own writing and the writing of others. Be tested on principles of grammar and usage and on the application of those principles. Be tested on their ability to analyze language. Write at least 2,500 words of edited prose which will include: resource reports on various aspects of language, usage and semantics and essays on how language shapes meaning.
