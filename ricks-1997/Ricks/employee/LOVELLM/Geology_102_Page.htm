Geology 102:  Introduction to Geology Lab Syllabus

Instructor: Mark D. Lovell

Office: Romney 142
E-mail: lovellm@ricks.edu
Lecture Room: Romney 145
Phone: office 356-1903
             home 538-7935

Sections: 1, 7, 190 & 901

Class Time: Winter 2000

Prerequisite: Current or previous enrollment in Geology 101, or Geology 104.

Objective: Help students gain an understanding of mineral and rock properties and to use those properties to allow rock and mineral identification. After recognizing the different rock types, we help you learn that rocks tell us the history of the environments in which they were formed. Thus, the student should recognize that any given rock represents a specific geologic environment with a unique history. We introduce maps and show what kind of information can be obtained from the maps. As earthquakes are common in this area we will also work with seismic data in determining epicenter locations.
