CHEM INTERN
CLASS CODE: CHEM 498 CREDITS:  1 - 3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: CHEMISTRY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION:
DESCRIPTION: All chemistry majors must find and experience a work intership. This would generally occur during their junior year, or the semester after their junior year. The experience could involve working for government agencies, industry, an academic institution or any organization that requires chemists on their staff. The credit for the intership would count as the capstone experience required for graduation. The student will have a contract agreement with the employer and be responsible to a faculty supervisor. Upon completion of the intership, a short presentation will be made to the chemistry department here as part of the requirement.
TOPICS: TBA
OBJECTIVES: The objective of the intership is to give the student a real world experience in a environment that employs chemistry. This experience should help a student decide on a career pathway.
REQUIREMENTS: It is the student's reponsibility to find an intership. The intership must then be approved through a faculty supervisor. The student must then complete the intership and give a short presentation to the BYU-I chemistry department.
PREREQUISITES: 2 years of chemistry courses
