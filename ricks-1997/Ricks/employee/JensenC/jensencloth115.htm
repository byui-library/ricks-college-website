COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#:   Cloth 115
Title:  Construction Skill Building Techniques Credit: 4
Text: Reader’s Digests, Complete Guide to Sewing

Course Objective:
1. Develop ability to make appropriate choices among various options after considering all variables, alternative options and results.
2. Strengthen and broaden experience in knowing a variety of apparel construction techniques.
3. Develop construction terminology needed for communication accurately and knowledgeably.
4. Recognize and describe quality characteristics associated with each assigned construction technique and understand the processes conducive to producing them.
5. Create superior-quality examples of construction techniques and accurately evaluate end-product quality.  Portfolio material for job application.
6. Construct two apparel garments from commercial patterns with accuracy using learned skills and techniques for use as portfolio material for job application.

Description: Intermediate sewing course involving construction, skill and technique improvement, discussion, writing, reading and research that explores the how and why of construction techniques.  Lab experiences in comparing and evaluation equipment and construction processes.  Students develop maturity, independence and competence in construction skills.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Students prepare a portfolio of experiments, sample techniques and will also construct 2 full scale garments.
