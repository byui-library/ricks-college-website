College Budgeting Information

a       
                  
                    
   



College provides many new experiences for incoming students.  One of these experiences is the feeling of independence which comes from living away from home for the first time.  For many, it is also their first experience in having autonomy over their finances.  

Students need must be wise stewards over their finances.  Many students too easily become ensnared in debt through unwise spending, excessive borrowing, or credit card purchases.  We challenge you to closely examine your finances for college by calculating your expenses as a student and establishing a monthly budget
