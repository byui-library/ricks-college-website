Applied Animal Health
ANIMAL HEALTH
CLASS CODE: AS 315 CREDITS:  3 FEE:  $10.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: ANIMAL SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Instruction and application of animal health practices to prepare livestock workers to raise healthy animals. Priority is given to disease prevention. Lab classes will allow hands-on training in various health management techniques.
DESCRIPTION: Instruction and application of animal health practices to prepare livestock workers to raise healthy animals. Priority is given to disease prevention. Lab classes will allow hands-on training in various health management techniques.
TOPICS: Nature of disease, nutrition and health, sanitation, disinfectants, immunization, housing, hereditary factors, basic husbandry practices, diseases associated with body systems, digestive system, genitourinary system, respiratory system, circulatory system, nervous system, miscellaneous diseases of farm animals, generalized diseases, metabolic diseases, diseases of skin and extremities, poisonings, parasitology, parasites and their control. Lab sessions focus on various health applications such as needle injections, vaccinations, parturition assistance, cattle herd reproductive exams, animal handling skills, dehorning, castration, etc.
