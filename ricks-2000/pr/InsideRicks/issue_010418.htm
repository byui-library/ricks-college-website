SITE OF NEW MULTI-USE BUILDING DEDICATED IN GROUND-BREAKING CEREMONY

There are "new beginnings springing forth from the foundations of the old," said Elder F. Melvin Hammond of the Quorum of the Seventy as he dedicated the site for the new 54,000 square-foot multi-use building at Ricks College. Elder Hammond was assigned by the First Presidency to preside over the ground-breaking ceremonies. As he reflected on his days spent on campus as a student and a teacher, Elder Hammond said he had witnessed nearly every significant occasion at Ricks College of the last half century. "The wonder of this campus is built into the lives of each of us," he said.

The audience first gathered in the Taylor Chapel. Community Services Vice President Brent Kinghorn conducted the gathering and explained the ground-breaking ceremony symbolizes a proper preparation for that which will now go forth. The audience walked to the building site south of the parking lot adjacent to the Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building for the formal turning of the soil. Some site preparation work preceded the ground breaking. (http://www.byui.edu/Ricks/News/Ricks/NewsReleases/groundbreaking_010410.html)

NEW VICE PRESIDENT OF ADVANCEMENT NAMED

Athletic Director Garth Hall was appointed Wednesday to be the new vice president of advancement by Ricks College President David A. Bednar. Hall will assume the new position June 1. He replaces Brent Kinghorn, who will be leaving the college to serve as a mission president in Albania beginning July 1. Kinghorn served as vice president of community services since the position was created in 1978.

In introducing the new vice president, President Bednar said "Garth brings a rich combination of vision and perspective, skill, desire, and experience that will bless our students and this institution during this time of transition." Hall was appointed athletic director at Ricks in September 1997 and also has been serving as the new director of the Student Activities Program since December. He has worked with athletic programs at Idaho State University, BYU, Tulane University, Wake Forest, and Oregon State.

President Bednar also explained "advancement" will replace the term "community services" because it more clearly defines Hall's role as Ricks becomes Brigham Young University-Idaho. "Advancement" is commonly used by colleges and universities as a term to describe efforts to advance their mission. The goal of advancement is to enhance an institution by building support-be it in the form of public understanding, financial, alumni loyalty or new students. "As we become Brigham Young University-Idaho, this is a time when we must advance and articulate our mission to a variety of publics," the president said.

(More information: http://www.byui.edu/Ricks/News/Ricks/NewsReleases/hall_010418.html)

NEW STUDENT ACTIVITIES PROGRAM DIRECTOR NAMED

Devin Shaum has been named the new student activities program director at Ricks College. He replaces Garth Hall, who was appointed Wednesday to be the new advancement vice president at Ricks College. Hall had served in the newly created position since December.

The activities program opens sports, the arts and service to everyone, Shaum says. When fully operational, the program will have an array of year-round activities in four areas: social and recreational, visual and performing arts, sports and fitness, and humanitarian service and leadership. Each area is structured to give students opportunities for active involvement at various levels of interest--including leisure, instructional, recreational and competitive.

Shaum has served as director of the Ricks College Alumni Association for the past 2½ years. (See details: http://www.byui.edu/Ricks/News/Ricks/NewsReleases/shaum_010418.html)

NEW PRESIDENT NAMED FOR FACULTY ASSOCIATION

Steve McGary is the new president of the Faculty Association with Van Christman as the president elect. Outgoing president Jerry Jex had served for six years on the Association Board, the last two as president. McGary had served as the president elect for the past two years. He said, "I am excited about the future and also concerned. The concern and the excitement both come from knowing we are becoming BYU-Idaho. Becoming BYU-Idaho has many challenges, but working with the great faculty that we have will make it possible to overcome those challenges."

ALUMNI ASSOCIATION RECEIVES NEW DIRECTOR

Ricks College alumnus Steve Davis has been named the new director of alumni relations. He replaces Devin Shaum who recently was asked to serve as the student activities director at the college. Davis will work closely with alumni, including the Alumni Association, Emeritus Club, homecomings, reunions and other activities.

Alumni Relations strives to maintain contact with former Ricks College students through distribution of publications such as the Summit alumni magazine and Inside Ricks e-mail newsletter. The organization is also responsible for recognizing alumni who have made significant contributions and accomplishments. The director oversees Alumni Councils established in various areas of the United States that become involved when a Ricks College event is in their area, such as firesides and performing groups.

Davis has worked at Ricks since 1986, first in the Admissions and Scholarships Office and most recently in the Development Office.
