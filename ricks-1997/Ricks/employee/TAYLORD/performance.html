BRIGHAM YOUNG UNIVERISTY-IDAHO

Mallet Ensemble
 The mallet ensemble is a select group of students invited by the director who perform the classic ragtime arrangements of Green, Joplin, Breuer, classic transciptions, traditional marimba music as well as contemporary marimba literature.

Percussion Ensemble
 This ensemble provides a challenging performance experience for percussion students regardless of playing ability. Focusing on a wide variety of musical selections including standard repertoire, transcriptions of popular music and works written or arranged by students, the Percussion Ensemble offers at least one concert a semester and often appears as a special guest on concerts of other performing groups.

Viking Percussion
 Under the leadership of David Taylor, the Pep Band Drumline uses five snares, five basses, three quints, and at least fifteen auxilary percussionits. Performances include various activities programs. These are auditioned groups, and scholarships are available for each sport.
