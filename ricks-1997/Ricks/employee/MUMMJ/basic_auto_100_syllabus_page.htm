Basic Auto 100

Text: THE CAR CARE BOOK by Barbarossa/Bono

Materials: Worksheet packet

Course description: This class is for those who have little or no knowledge about cars.  No car is needed to
take this class.  It is an informational class covering the various systems of the automobile and maintenance.  How to avoid rip-off repairs, to purchase a new or used automobile, financing and insurance will also be a part of the class. The course is not designed as hands on, but a few maintenance demonstrations will be given.  The labs are for the major classes so there is no opportunity for you to work on your own car.

The lectures are not based strictly on the text, but the test will be a combination of questions from the chapter worksheets and lecture.  The work sheet questions can be answered as you read the chapters and you are responsible for the answers since the answers are not given in class. 

GRADING: Grade will based on chapter quizzes, engine component identification, and final exam. The grade will be calculated on percentage of accumulative points. The chapter work sheets are in the packets and are due the start of the period the day they are due. 

OTHER COURSE POLICIES: Extra Credit: 2% for perfect attendance.  There is no provision for makeup. It is important to just keep current.

Attendance: You are expected to be here for each class and attendance will be taken. After two absences 1% of your total grade will be deducted for each day you miss after the two.

In this class you are expected to observe the Honor Code.
