Private-Family Use Stewardship
Materials belong to the family, not to the state, church, or community. Materials belong to the steward, not to the state, Church or the community. Private property.
Tithing not paid on family funds. Tithing paid on profits or surplus. Amounts above needs and wants of family go into central fund (common) from which family & stewardship funds come for the next year.
Based on wants (a lack--ought to have) and needs (have to have). Negotiated with the bishop.
