Spanish Grammar Review
SPAN GRAM REV
CLASS CODE: SPAN 201M CREDITS:  4
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A thorough grammar review for fluent, non-native speakers. Entry level for most Spanish-speaking returned missionaries.
DESCRIPTION: Review and continuation of first year grammar, conversation, reading and writing.
TOPICS: Review of grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening and speaking.
OBJECTIVES: Spanish 201M is a review of basic grammar, an excursion into intermediate grammar, and an exposure to new vocabulary.
REQUIREMENTS: Workbook, exams, and attendance.
PREREQUISITES: To register, students must be a Spanish-speaking returned missionary or have consent of instructor.
