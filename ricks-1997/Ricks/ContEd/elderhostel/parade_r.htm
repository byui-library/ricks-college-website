Minutes from Yellowstone National Park, Parade Rest Ranch lies nestled in a large grove of aspen and cottonwood trees in the pine-clad foothills of the Gallatin Mountain Range. The ranch is located on the old Bannock Indian Trail at 6,500 feet. Views of beautiful Hebgen Lake and the meandering Grayling Creek add to the tranquility of this mountain hideaway. Log cabins, decorated in western decor. Multiple occupancy cabins (double and twin beds) occupied by a maximum of 1 couple or 3 singles with single men and women housed separately; one bath in each cabin shared by up to 3 people. Meals and classes a short walk from cabins. Enjoy an old-fashioned cookout while basking in the beautiful scenery. No alcohol allowed.

FLY FISHING: THE GREATER YELLOWSTONE AREA EXPERIENCE 
Capture the romance of fly fishing in one of the world’s most pristine areas. Hands on experience for women and men, beginning to advanced. Instruction includes casting, fly selection, and water reading, etc. Bring your own rods, some rentals available. 

EARLY HISTORY OF YELLOWSTONE PARK: A UNIQUE GLIMPSE OF THE PAST 
Investigate the complexities of the dynamic people from the past. Rediscover the Indians, mountain men (including John Colter and Jim Bridger) and explorers who lived and traveled the region of Yellowstone National Park. 

VOLCANOS, EARTHQUAKES, HOT SPOTS: REMARKABLE DISTURBANCES IN THE EARTH'S CRUST 
A close analysis of Yellowstone’s geology, an explosive volcanic geothermal area. Explore Quake Lake, formed as a result of an earthquake in 1959. A field trip into Yellowstone Park will include a tour of the Old Faithful geyser.
