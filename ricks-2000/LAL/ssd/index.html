Services for Students with a Disability

Counseling, advocacy, and coordination of services for students with a disability are available through the Office for Students with a Disability (SSD), located on the 1st floor of the McKay Library, East Wing in room 158. The purpose of the office is to help students overcome physical, educational, or attitudinal barriers which may prevent them from reaching their full educational potential. Staff members coordinate college support services and accommodations, thus aiding students with a disability to become integrated into the campus.
