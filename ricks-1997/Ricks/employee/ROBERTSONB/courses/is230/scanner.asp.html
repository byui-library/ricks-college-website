Scanning in the IS Labs

All IS department labs are equipped with HP ScanJet 5370C scanners. These directions are for use with these scanners and their accompanying HP PrecisionScan Pro software.

  1. Raise the lid and place the object to be scanned face down and butted agains the slightly raised curbing surrounding the glass (this will assist in obtaining a straight scan).
  2. Lower the lid to press the object firmly against the glass. If scanning an object that protrudes from the sides of the scanner, support it so that it does not move.
  3. Press the button on the front left of the scanner (it has an image of a scanner above it).
  4. The HP PrecisionScan Pro software should automatically launch. Wait while it warms up, this process should take about 60 seconds.
  5. The software should produce a preview of the scan (see image below)

  6. You will then use your mouse cursor to select all or a portion of the image (left click and drag to form a selection area).
  7. Once selected, you can either save the image or send it to another application.
    1. To save, click the Save (Disk) icon and save just as any other document ( File type should be .jpg)
    2. To send to another application, click the Send To... (scanner with arrow above) icon.
      1. A popup window will open, select the application and click Scan.
      2. The image will then be transferred to the new program to be further modified.
  8. To scan another object, replace the current object with a new one.
  9. Click the Preview button (scanner with raised lid, far left of toolbar in the PrecisionScan software).
  10. Repeat steps 6 and 7.
