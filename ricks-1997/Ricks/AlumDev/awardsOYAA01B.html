Outstanding Young Alumni Award
Debra Bingham

"I feel blessed and simply lucky that my basic nursing education was with such dynamic teachers and nurses (at Ricks College)," says Debra Bingham, a registered nurse and perinatal nurse specialist at St. Luke’s-Roosevelt Hospital Center on Tenth Avenue in New York City, N.Y. "Over the years I have compared my nursing education to that of other nurses around the country and I have never been able to find a program that even came close," she says of her time at Ricks.

A native of Brigham City, Utah, Debra graduated from Ricks in 1978. After about six years as a nurse at hospitals in Salt Lake City, Provo, and Orem, Utah, she became a research nurse clinician at Columbia University and a staff nurse at Columbia-Presbyterian Medical Center in New York. She obtained her undergraduate degree from the University of the State of New York in 1987 and added a Master’s degree in perinatal nursing from Columbia University in 1990. Debra has been at St. Luke’s-Roosevelt since late 1995 and is presently the clinical administration liaison nurse for maternal-child health, in addition to her perinatal nursing duties.

"Another big part of my education at Ricks was my growth in the Gospel through my student ward, the Sunday night firesides and the weekly devotionals," Debra says. "I remember when Henry B. Eyring was made president of Ricks and how articulate, spiritual, and powerful his messages were."

Debra is married to Kent Hamilton, but uses her maiden name for professional reasons. She met her husband in New York City in 1995. She is the mother of a son, and the stepmother to three daughters. The couple also have four grandchildren. Debra is currently the Young Women’s president in her ward. She lists visiting museums and historical sites, crocheting and reading as her hobbies.
