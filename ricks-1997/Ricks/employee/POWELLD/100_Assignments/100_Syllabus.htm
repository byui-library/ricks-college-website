Auto 100

Course Syllabus - Auto 100

The purpose of this class is to give you a basic understanding of the operation of your car. Whether or not you currently own or drive a car, the time will come when you will. If you learn some basic things about your car it can save you thousands of dollars in your lifetime, and maybe at some specific time when you look into purchasing or repairing a car. Unfortunately, some repair shops are less than honorable, and if you have some knowledge, you should know if you are being taken for a ride. If you have had quite a bit of experience working on cars in the past, you probably need to take a more advanced class than this one, which is designed for those with a very limited knowledge about cars.

This class can be a lot of fun, and I hope you will feel free to ask any question about cars that you think of as it comes up. It is an elective course, so you will get out of it exactly what you put into it.

It is important that you read the text as assigned on the daily schedule. The information given in the lectures will compliment the chapters, but your are responsible to get the information from both the chapters and the lectures. It is the information that will be covered on the tests. Since there are no study questions at the ends of the chapters, a Handout Packet is available in the Secretaries’ office in Room 106 of this building. You should obtain one and fill it out as you read the chapters. They should be turned in to the instructor on the day they are to be discussed. If you have questions about the chapters, ask before the test opens and we will discuss them in class. Late assignments will receive ½ credit or less!

You will be given quizzes during the semester covering class discussions and reading assignments. They are generally given in the testing center and open as indicated on your daily schedule. They are usually open for at least two days. If you fail to take any of the tests, they cannot be made up. There are a total of 201 points possible on the chapter tests, 75 points possible on the mid-term parts test, and 100 points possible on the final exam. Your grade will be calculated on the total number of points you have earned from the combined tests.

You will be graded on your attendance. Each day you miss, 10 points will be deducted from your overall grade. Each day you’re late, 5 points is deducted. If you don’t have any tardies or absences the entire semester, you will be given extra credit!

This class does not have a scheduled lab. You don't need a car to learn about them. If you do have a car, we may invite you to bring it in for demonstrations and parts identification. Please don't count on bringing it in to work on it yourself.
