Civil & Environmental Engineering

Civil engineers design and supervise the construction of large buildings, roads, airports, tunnels, bridges, dams, offshore structures, space launching facilities, water supply and sewage systems. They employ the latest concepts in computer-aided design (CAD) during design, construction, project scheduling and cost control. Civil and environmental engineers are involved in community development, city planning, traffic congestion, energy needs, floods, earthquakes, meeting the challenges of pollution, and how projects impact the environment.
