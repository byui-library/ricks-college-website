6 Day Program at Morningside Farm on Vashon Island, WA

in the Puget Sound between Seattle and Tacoma.

 

JUNE 25-30, 2001

 

YFE is a more scripture based, gospel study skills program than Especially for Youth (EFY). We require that an applicant have at least one experience with the EFY program before applying for YFE. You will have a chance to learn how to "study" the scriptures and how to apply them in your life. We will mix a variety of learning opportunities throughout the week, with a field trip into Seattle and on Vashon Island. Participants will have the chance to spend time at the beach, in campfire settings, providing service to others, and in the classroom. Optional Ricks College/BYU-Idaho credit will be available.
