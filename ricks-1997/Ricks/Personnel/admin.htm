POSITION TITLE:  Communications & Marketing Specialist

CLOSING DATE:  Extended to July 13, 2001, at 3 p.m.

QUALIFICATIONS:   Requires a bachelor's degree in communications, journalism, marketing, or another comparable discipline, with a master's degree preferred.  A minimum of five years of successful experience in either advertising, marketing, journalism, broadcasting, public relations, or another related field is required; experience supervising a production team is also required.  Excellent writing skills and an understanding of media marketing is required.

POSITION DESCRIPTION:  The position is responsible for conceptualizing, writing, editing, and implementing projects related to Advancement.  Coordinates and supervises all institutional publications.  Supervises a graphic design coordinator, public relations photographer, and production supervisor.  Creates, maintains, and oversees the University's visual identity.  Develops and manages Advancement's marketing campaigns and strategies. 

STATUS:   Administrative, full-time.  Start date will be arranged.

POSITION TITLE:  Internal Auditor

CLOSING DATE:  August 8, 2001, at 3 p.m.

QUALIFICATIONS:  CPA/CIA certification is required.  A minimum of seven years auditing-related education or experience is strongly preferred, with a master’s degree in accounting or a business-related field highly desirable.  Demonstrated administrative skills, excellent interpersonal skills, and the ability to write clearly and succinctly are required.  A proven ability to work independently is also required.

POSITION DESCRIPTION:  This position is responsible for planning and performing primarily operational audits at Ricks College (Becoming BYU-Idaho).  This position reports to the Church Educational System (CES) Director of Auditing.  Keeps Ricks College administration and the CES Director informed of audit findings through timely and appropriate reports.  Is responsible for reviewing and monitoring administrative controls in the accounting, financial, and operating functions of the college and should make recommendations for improving the quality of the college’s system of controls wherever possible.  Coordinates audits at the CES level.  Provides assistance to church and external independent auditors for the year-end financial statement and A-133 financial aid audits.  Develops annual risk-based audit approach in conjunction with the CES Director and the BYU-Provo Audit Director.

STATUS:   Administrative, full-time.  Start date to be arranged.
