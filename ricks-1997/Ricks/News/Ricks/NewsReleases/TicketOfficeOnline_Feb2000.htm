Ricks College News Release
February 22, 2000

Ricks Ticket Office goes online

REXBURG, Idaho — The Ricks College Ticket Office is now offering online service. Students and the general public can purchase tickets to Ricks events through their personal computers.

The Ricks College Ticket Office can be accessed at www.ricks.edu/tickets.

"To my knowledge Ricks College is the first college in the nation to put their ticket office online," says Jim Terry, manager of the Ricks College Ticket Office. "We are sure that things will be a little hectic at first but once we become familiar with the online service it will be a great advantage to us and the students."

The online service should eliminate some past problems with buying tickets for popular events such as Women’s Week. These tickets go on sale Monday, Feb. 28, at 7 a.m.

"Students will try for hours to phone the ticket office to purchase tickets but will only find busy signals, or they will sometimes wait in lines all night to get tickets," Terry says of Women’s Week events. "Now they can buy tickets from the comfort of wherever they are and whenever they want."
