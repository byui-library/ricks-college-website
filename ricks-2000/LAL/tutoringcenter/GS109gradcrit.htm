Grading Criteria for GS 109A/Ed. 209

 

 

           Attendance to all tutor-training classes is very important.  Each Class missed, permanently drops your final course grade 1/3 of a letter (i.e. A to A-).  If you miss two or more class meetings you automatically fail GS 109/Ed 209 and will be terminated as an employee of the BYU-Idaho Tutoring Center.  Class will be held Wednesday from 4:00 – 4:50.  GS 109A/ED 209 has 150 total possible points and the criteria for achieving these points is as follows:

 

1.       Attend all tutor-training classes listed in the Semester Outline.

 

2.      Throughout the semester you will be required to complete 10 “Application of Tutor Training Cards”, describing how you applied the previous week’s training to your tutoring. 10 @ 5 pts. each = 50 pts.)

 

3.      Read The ABC’s of Tutoring and turn in the accompanying ABC’s Worksheet, to be completed within the first week of the semester. (25 pts.)

                                               

4.  Attend an end of the semester interview with Brother Teichert. (10 pts.) 

 

5.  Prepare a two page typed evaluation of successes experienced, areas for improvement and insights gained as a tutor this semester.  Also type any suggestions for improving the tutoring program. (25 pts.)

 

6.  Have four of your students complete Tutor Evaluation forms. (10 pts.)

 

7.      Complete a Tutor Self-Assessment Sheet.  (10 pts.)

 

8.   Prepare a presentation to be given during the last class meeting on successes experienced as a tutor and methods or techniques used to bring about those successes. (10 pts.)

 

9.      Twice during the course of the semester, observe a less experienced tutor in an actual tutoring session.  You will be paid for the time spent observing the tutoring session and an observation handout will be provided to assist you in the observation.  (10 pts.)

 

** During the semester there will be three paid General Tutor In-service meetings.  While these In-services are NOT mandatory, it is encouraged that you attend each of these informative meetings pertaining to your job as a tutor.  (The dates and times of these meetings should not conflict with other class times and will be announced throughout the semester.)                     

 

*** In order to certify as a Level III tutor with the College Reading & Learning Association you must complete everything listed above and have tutored a minimum of 75 hours.
