Boys and Girls Cross Country Camp (grades 7-12) 
July 13-17, 1998
 
Youth will learn what it takes to be a successful distance runner. Ricks College Coach Doug Stutz, the 1995, 1996, and 1997  NJCAA National Coach of the Year, will be the primary instructor. The camp includes the following special features: 
  • Theory and practice of distance running 
  • Camp t-shirt for all participants 
  • Nutrition, training theory, mental training and holistic approach to training 
Tuition: $140 
Meals and Housing: $89 
Lunch and Dinner Meals Only: $38
