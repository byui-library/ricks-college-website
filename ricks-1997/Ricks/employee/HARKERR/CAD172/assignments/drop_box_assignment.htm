DropBox Assignment

The purpose of this assignment is to familiarize you with the computer lab environment and the process for submitting electronic assignments.  Please follow the steps listed below closely:

  1. Successfully log on to the a computer in one of the Austin building computer labs (currently AUS 5 and 9, rooms 110 and 208).  It is important that this exercise is done in one of the Austin labs to ensure that your access to the labs has been enabled.
  2. Retrieve the welcome document from your personalized outbox.  The path is: 
    engr on Jupiter\\OutBox\Harker\YourName
  3. Rename the document to your name followed by welcome, i.e. LastName FirstName Welcome.
  4. Answer the quesitons and place the renamed document in the DropBox.  The Path is:
    engr on Jupiter\\DropBox\Harker.  You will not be able to list, view, or edit documents in this directory.
