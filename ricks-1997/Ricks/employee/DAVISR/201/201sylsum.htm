This class is 10 weeks, not 5, which means your final is due the first week of July.

 

I’m often asked if this is the same class I teach on campus. It’s not. This course was written at BYU (1995) and I have been asked to oversee it until our own 201/202 courses can be placed online. Because texts have changed since this course was written, the page numbers will not always match. I’ll try to get you close to the correct spot. For each chapter I’d advise jumping to the end and reading the Chapter Summary first; it will give you a quick overview of where you’re headed in your reading.

 

You have three weeks for the first seven lessons, but you can easily do them in two. Deadlines are there to keep you moving so you don’t suddenly find yourself with one week before the final and 10 chapters still due.  But the converse is also true – you can move as rapidly as you like, ignoring all the dates and handing in material as quickly as you complete it. Midterms and finals are not fixed times; they are ready as quickly as you are; the dates given are the last possible time you can take them, not the only times. You can easily finish this class in four weeks, so why not set youself some deadlines and work toward them.
