Student E-mail Setup Help
This will help students setup their email for a PC or Mac.

Help for Setup for Student E-mail on a PC
  1. You must have your own disk and it needs to be formatted. You will probably want it to be a high density disk because all of your E-mail will be put on this disk. You will only need to do the configuration once and if the machine is set up right it will store this information on your disk. From then on you will simply stick your disk in any P.C. that is set up with the Eudora e-mail program and you will be ready to run.

    Setup Instructions:

    1. Put your disk in A: drive
    2. Go into windows and click on the Eudora E-Mail icon
    3. In Eudora choose "Special" from the menus at the top and go down to "Configuration"
    4. In the POP Account line put in your user name followed by "@neptune.ricks.edu" For example if my username was written as "student1" I would put on this line: "student1@neptune.ricks.edu"
    5. In the "Real Name" line put your name as you want people to know you like: "John James"
    6. In the line "SMTP Server" put "neptune.ricks.edu"

    This is all you need to configure your disk so you can choose "OK". I would suggest you have your password be something that you can remember but no one else can guess. A good way to do a password is to choose a phrase like "The Snow In Rexburg Is White" and use the beginning letters "siriw" as the password. This will make it quite easy to remember, and not being a word, will make it hard for someone to guess.

    Getting your mail:

    1. Choose "File" from the menu in Eudora E-Mail and go to "Check Mail"
    2. It will ask for your password and then it will go get it.

    A few other things.

    1. The mail is deleted off of the server when you get it by using "Check Mail" unless you have "Leave on Server" checked. Don't check this because we don't have much disk space and if you are seen to abuse it you may loose your privileges.
    2. Mail left on the server more than one week is automatically deleted.
    3. You only have 1 Meg of disk space to hold your mail. If you receive mail or files that will take you beyond that amount the server will simply reject it and throw it away.
    4. If a faculty member says they have e-mail and it is "doej" then you would put "doej@ricks.edu"
Help for Student E-Mail Setup for the Mac
You must have your own disk and it needs to be formatted. You will probably want it to be a high density disk because all of your E-mail will be put on this disk. You will only need to do the configuration once and if the machine is set up right it will store this information on your disk in the Mac and open the appropriate files.

Setup Instructions:

  1. Put your disk in the drive.
  2. Go to the systems folder.
  3. Drag the icon that says Eudora Folder to your floppy disk to copy it there.
  4. Close the system folder.
  5. Once it is copied open the folder that is on your disk.
  6. There should be an icon file there called Eudora Settings. This is the one you click on to run Eudora with your settings. You may want to change it's name to something like "Eudora Mail".
  7. Once the name is changed click on that icon and it will bring up Eudora mail.
  8. In Eudora choose Special from the menus at the top and go down to Settings
  9. In the POP Account line put in your user name followed by @neptune.ricks.edu For example if my username was written as student1 I would put on this line: student1@neptune.ricks.edu
  10. Click on the Personal Information> icon on the left.
  11. Fill in the POP account with your username followed by @neptune.ricks.edu as above.
  12. In the Real Name line put your name as you want people to know you like: John Smith
  13. Click on the hosts icon on the left.
  14. Fill in the POP account with your username followed by @neptune.ricks.edu as above.
  15. On the SMTP line put neptune.ricks.edu
  16. This is all the information you should need to fill in. Click ,b>OK to continue.

I would suggest you have your password be something that you can remember but no one else can guess. A good way to do a password is to choose a phrase like "The Snow In Rexburg Is White" and use the beginning letters "siriw" as the password. This will make it quite easy to remember and not being a word will make it hard for someone to guess.

To Change the password:

  1. Choose Special from the menu options in Eudora and go to Change Password
  2. It will ask for your current password and then your new password twice (to verify)

One thing you will not want to do is check the box for the program to record your password in the options. If you do and you lose your disk someone else could get into your E-mail if they found your disk. You will only have to give it the first time you go to get mail each time you run the program.

Getting your mail:

  1. Choose "File" from the menu in Eudora E-Mail and go to Check Mail
  2. It will ask for your password and then it will go get it.

A few other things.

  1. The mail is deleted off of the server when you get it by using "Check Mail" unless you have "Leave on Server" checked. Don't check this because we don't have much disk space and if you are seen to abuse it you may loose your privileges.
  2. Mail left on the server more than one week is automatically deleted.
  3. You only have 1 meg of disk space to hold your mail. If you receive mail or files that will take you beyond that amount the server will simply reject it and throw it away.
  4. If a faculty member says they have e-mail and it is "doej" then you would put "doej@ricks.edu"
