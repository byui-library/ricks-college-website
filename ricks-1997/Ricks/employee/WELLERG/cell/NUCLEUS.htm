The purpose of the Nucleus is to contain the DNA of an eukaryotic cell and to limit access to the DNA of the cell

This is a view from a light microscope of a cell from a stamenal hair of a flower. Notice the nucleus located at the center of the cell.

This is a view of a nucleus with a scanning electron microscope (SEM).  This microscope only shows the outside structures.  The dents near the number 3 are the nuclear pores which are in the nuclear membrane

This view of a nucleus is with a trasmission electron microscope (TEM).   This electron microscope shows internal features.  The large dark area in the nucleus is the nucleolus, where ribosomes are assembled.  Notice the mitochondria at the top of the picture.

Here is another view of a nucleus with a light microscope. The dark purple area inside of the nucleus is the nucleolus. The box like orange structure is the cell wall.
