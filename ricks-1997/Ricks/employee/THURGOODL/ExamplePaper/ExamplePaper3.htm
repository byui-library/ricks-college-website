Purposes of Charity





Scriptural Quotations

1 Corinthians 8:1

"...Knowledge puffeth up, but charity edifieth."
 

1 Corinthians 13:13

"And now abideth faith, hope, charity, these three; but the greatest of these is charity."
 

1 Corinthians 16:14

"Let all your things be done with charity."
 

Colossians 3:14

"And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness."
 

1 Peter 4:8

"And above all things have fervent charity among yourselves: for charity preventeth a
multitude of sins."
 

2 Nephi 26:30

"...Wherefore, the Lord God hath given a commandment that all men should have charity, which charity is love. And except they should have charity they were nothing. Wherefore, if they should have charity they would not suffer the laborer in Zion to perish."
 

Alma 34:29

"Therefore, if ye do not remember to be charitable, ye are as dross, which the refiners do cast out, (it being of no worth) and is trodden under foot of men."
 

Moroni 7:43&47

"And again, behold I say unto you that he cannot have faith and hope, save he shall be meek, and lowly of heart."

"But charity is the pure love of Christ, and it endureth forever; and whoso is found possessed of it at the last day, it shall be well with him."
 

General Authority Quotations

Mark E. Petersen. Ensign. May 1977. p.75.

"And what is charity in its true sense? It is the pure love of Christ which helps us to love both God and our fellowmen."
 

Mary Ellen Edmunds. Quoted by Barbara W. Winder, Ensign. November 1985. p.96.

"For me I feel close to the Savior when I can do in a small way for someone else, what He would do if He were there. In a way, thats what being an instrument is all about... to make it possible for His love to reach more of His children."
 

Joy F. Evans. Ensign. May 1989. p.713.75.

"Having compassion on those who are hurting for whatever reason and then translating the response of the heart into the needed act is truly ministering as God would have us do...

We must recognize that life is a precious gift, that trust and tenderness are fragile, that we must love and serve one another, must encourage one another, forgive one another- all this not once, but over and over again. Then perhaps we shall be remembered among those on the right hand of the Lord when he shall come in his glory."
 

Spencer W. Kimball. Ensign. May 1978. p. 81.

"Let us unite and pray with all the energy of heart, that we may be sealed by [the] bond of charity; that we may build up this latter-day Zion, that the kingdom of God may go forth, so that the kingdom of heaven by come."
 

Hugh Nibley. Of All Things. p.89.

"The final analysis of Mormon and Moroni was that the fatal weakness of the Nephites was lack of charity."
 

Ezra Taft Benson. Ensign. November 1986. p.47.

"The world today speaks a great deal about love, and it is sought for by many. But the pure love of Christ differs greatly from what the world thinks of love. Charity never seeks selfish gratification. The pure love of Christ seeks only the eternal growth and joy of others."
 

Summary

In the New Testament Paul talks a lot about the importance of charity in our lives. In 1 Corinthians 8:1 we learn that charity edifies us. It strengthens us. Also in 1 Corinthians 13:13 it tells us that out of faith, hope and charity, charity is the most important of these. With the knowledge of how important charity is we should have charity in all that we do, according to 1 Corinthians 16:14. In Colossians 3:14 we learn what by having charity we posses a bond that entitles us to be perfect. With this bond we will prevent ourselves from having a multitude of sins according to 1 Peter 4:8. 2 Nephi 26:39 compares charity to love. To love our fellow men we know is a great commandment of God. If we cannot show love or charity to our fellow men we are nothing and will not be able to build Zion in the last day. Alma 34:29 mentions this nothingness that comes from not having charity. We will be cast out and trodden under the feet of men. Charity sounds pretty important to me. In the last scripture verses Moroni 7:43&47 we learn how charity is the true love of Christ and it is an eternal love. We also learn that faith and hope cannot exist with out charity. Plus we learn that with charity we will be found wonderful in the sight of God in the last day.
 

The general authority quotations teach us a lot about the value of charity as well. Mark E. Petersen teaches us what charity really is. He tells us once again that charity is the true love of Christ but also he expands upon it and tells us that it helps us love both God and our fellowmen. By being charitable we are being an instrument in the hands of God. We are doing in little ways what he would do if he were here with us right now. We are following his footsteps in loving our fellowmen, as Mary Ellen Edmunds taught us. Joy F. Evans tells us that we must forgive one another, serve one another, encourage one another, and much more. We should do this not once but over and over again, just like the Savior did. If we do these things we will be found precious in the sight of God in the last day. We must be united with charity so that the kingdom of Zion will come forth according to Spencer W. Kimball. Hugh Nibley gives us insight to why the Nephites were destroyed. He tells us that it was because of their lack of charity that caused their fatal weakness. Lastly, Ezra Taft Benson contrast the love of the world verses the true love of God, which is charity. He tells us, that the true love of God never seeks for selfish gratification it seeks only for the eternal growth and joy of those around us.

Consecration





Scriptures:

1. D&C 42:30 And behold, thou wilt remember the poor, and consecrate of thy properties for their support that which thou hast to impart unto them, with a covenant and a deed which cannot be broken. 31 And inasmuch as ye impart of your substance unto the poor, ye will do it unto me; and they shall be laid before the bishop of my church and his counselors, two of the elders, or high priests, such as he shall appoint or has appointed and set apart for that purpose. 32 And it shall come to pass, that after they are laid before the bishop of my church, and after that he has received these testimonies concerning the consecration of the properties of my church, that they cannot be taken from the church, agreeable to my commandments, every man shall be made accountable unto me, a steward over his own property, or that which he has received by consecration, as much as is sufficient for himself and family.
 

2. D&C 49:20 But it is not given that one man should possess that which is above another, wherefore the world lieth in sin.
 

3. D&C 51:3 Wherefore, let my servant Edward Partridge, and those whom he has chosen, in whom I am well pleased, appoint unto this people their portions, every man equal according to his family, according to his circumstances and his wants and needs.
 

4. D&C 51:5 And if he shall transgress and is not accounted worthy to belong to the church, he shall not have power to claim that portion which he has consecrated unto the bishop for the poor and needy of my church; therefore, he shall not retain the gift, but shall only have claim on that portion that is deeded unto him.
 

5. 2 Corinthians 8:13 For [I mean] not that other men be eased, and ye burdened: 14 But by an equality, [that] now at this time your abundance [may be a supply] for their want, that their abundance also may be [a supply] for your want: that there may be equality: 15 As it is written, He that [had gathered] much had nothing over; and he that [had gathered] little had no lack.

D&C 105:3 But behold, they have not learned to be obedient to the things which I required at their hands, but are full of all manner of evil, and do not impart of their substance, as becometh saints, to the poor and afflicted among them; 4 And are not united according to the union required by the law of the celestial kingdom; 5 And Zion cannot be built up unless it is by the principles of the law of the celestial kingdom; otherwise I cannot receive her unto myself. 6 And my people must needs be chastened until they learn obedience, if it must needs be, by the things which they suffer.

D&C 78:4 For a permanent and everlasting establishment and order unto my church, to advance the cause, which ye have espoused, to the salvation of man, and to the glory of your Father who is in heaven; 5 That you may be equal in the bonds of heavenly things, yea, and earthly things also, for the obtaining of heavenly things. 6 For if ye are not equal in earthly things ye cannot be equal in obtaining heavenly things; 7 For if you will that I give unto you a place in the celestial world, you must prepare yourselves by doing the things which I have commanded you and required of you.
 

Quotes:

1.  The earthly system of Zion is a reflection, or type, of the heavenly society of Gods. As men faithfully consecrate all they possess to God, under Zions law, they become heirs to all that God possesses. However, man must demonstrate that he possesses the character of a God before he can have access to the "Grand Heavenly Storehouse of Divine Powers and Gifts." As the faithful steward in Zion may have access to the earthly wealth of the earthly storehouse, his faithfulness will also give him access to heavenly treasures. However, while he is clothed in the imperfection of mortality his access to heavenly treasures must be limited.

- James R Hams, BYU Studies, VoL 8, No. 1, p.44
 

2.  In order that there might be means with which to accomplish every temporal good for the people, the Lord instituted this law of tithing in the place of the greater and more perfect law of Zion. The Lord revealed to his people in the incipiency of His work a law which was more perfect than the law of tithing. It comprehended larger things, greater power and a more speedy accomplishment of the purposes of the Lord. But the people were unprepared to live by it, and the Lord, out of mercy to the people, suspended the more perfect law, and gave the law of tithing, in order that there might be means in the storehouse of the Lord for the carrying out of the purposes He had in view; for the gathering of the poor, for the spreading of the Gospel to the nations of the earth, for the maintenance of those who were required to give their constant attention, day in and day out, to the work of the Lord, and for whom it was necessary to make some provision. Without this law these things could not be done, neither could Temples be built and maintained, nor the poor fed and clothed. Therefore the law of tithing is necessary for the Church....

- Joseph F. Smith, Conference Report, April 1900, p. 47
 

3.  The payment of tithing also brings the individual tithe payer unique spiritual blessings. Tithe paying is evidence that we accept the law of sacrifice. It also prepares us for the law of consecration and the other higher laws of the celestial kingdom. The Lectures on Faith [teach]: ... "A religion that does not require the sacrifice of all things never has power sufficient to produce the faith necessary unto life and salvation"

- Elder Dallin H. Oaks, Tithing, Ensign, May 1994, 34
 

4.  Young men, prepare to serve as missionaries. Going on a mission teaches you to live the law of consecration. He will be close to you and strengthen you.

The rich, by consecration and the imparting of their surplus for the benefit of the poor, not by constraint, but willingly as an act of free will, evidence that charity for their fellowmen characterized by Mormon as "the pure love of Christ." Summary:
 

What exactly is the law of consecration? Ezra Taft Benson described the law as a necessary part of getting into the celestial kingdom. All holy beings follow this eternal law and it was given to the early church to help them prosper in their new land. We believe, says Joseph F. Smith, that if a community was to follow the law of consecration through tithing, and fasting, and were faithful and followed all the commandments they would be prosperous and no one would be poor and they would all be able to find a job to support themselves. In 4 Nephi 1:3 we read about how the Lamanites and the Nephites had been converted and began following this "law" and had "all things common" and there was no one who was considered rich or poor because they were all free and were receiving of the "heavenly gift." Another example of when this occurred in the Book of Mormon is in 3 Nephi 26:19. We read of the people on the American continent that were taught from Christ after he was crucified. They then taught amongst each other and had "all things common" and each of them dealt fairly. In the New Testament in Acts 2:44 we read about how on the Day of Pentecost Peter begins to teach the people about this gift of the Holy Ghost and about Christs resurrection and many begin to believe and come together and have "all things common. Even more were taught by Peter in Acts 4:32 that came to believe on their words and came together with "one heart and one mind" and didnt feel that any of them had more than each other. Joseph Fielding Smith also tells us that in the Pearl of Great Price, Enoch was also told to tell everyone to repent. All those that did repent came together and followed the celestial law. In Moses 7:18 the Lord decides to call this people Zion because of their goodness and willingness to serve. They were so willing to do anything for the Lord that they became as one with God and then God brought the entire city unto Him in heaven. Brigham Young tells us that the Lord also spoke to Joseph in this dispensation to reveal unto him the law of consecration to see if the people would really follow Him like they had said in their continuous prayers. In Doctrine and Covenants 42:30 they are told to remember the poor and to give of their property equal to all because this is a covenant they have made and it can not be broken. Unfortunately they were not as faithful as the city of Enoch. Loreuzo Snow speaks of how after they had broken this law the Lord gave them another commandment, the law of tithing. In Doctrine and Covenants 85:3 the people of Jackson County were told that they given this law because they needed to be prepared. They were not able to follow His celestial law so now they must follow this law of tithing instead. In these days we are following a portion of this celestial law and Ezra Taft Benson tells us that we are doing that by giving of our fast offerings, paying our tithing, going on mission, and giving other kinds of money service and time. Lorenzo Snow also reminds us that we will all one day have to follow this celestial law. We need to be ready and willing then to follow it. For then we will be prepared to live in Zion.
