              Campus Programs

   

        

 
 

Rexburg, recently named one of the best small communities in America, lies thirty miles northeast of Idaho Falls in the historic Snake River Valley. Apartment style housing adjacent to campus or on campus. Each has three double occupancy bedrooms with shared common living room. Maximum of one couple or three singles, (unlimited singles, no charge) in each apartment. Private bath for couple or three singles share one bath. First and second story. Classrooms and cafeteria are a moderate walk from rooms. Two nice, low-cost golf courses nearby. BYU-Idaho standards require abstinence from coffee, tea, alcohol, and tobacco  while on campus.  Bus: Y (limited)  Plane: Y  Train: N  Ferry: N
