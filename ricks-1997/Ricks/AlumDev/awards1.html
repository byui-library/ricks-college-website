Distinguished Alumni Award for
Professional Accomplishments
William R. "Bill" Walker

After moving from stockbroker to securities wholesaler to head of sales and marketing for the largest real estate syndicator in the United States, Bill Walker was at the peak of his profession in the late 1980s. His consequent retirement, however, was short-lived when the call to preside over the Japan Tokyo South Mission came in 1990. Three years later Bill decided to return to the financial world. He was named vice president for Citibank with responsibility for sales and marketing promotion, and investment and sales training. He is currently national sales manager for proprietary mutual funds for the U.S. Division of Citibank Global Asset Management.

Bill is a native of Raymond, Alberta, Canada. He served in the Northern Far East (Japan) Mission and graduated on the Dean's List from Brigham Young University with a degree in international relations. After stints as a stockbroker in Salt Lake City, he moved to Walnut Creek, California, where he was a securities wholesaler and, later, managed the sales force as vice president of marketing for a realty company. With a partner he also operated his own real estate company for a timer. In 1978 he joined The Balcor Company, an American Express company, rising from regional to national sales manager, to director of national business relations, national marketing director and, finally, from executive to senior vice president of sales and marketing and president of Balcor Securities.

Bill and his wife, Vicki, are the parents of six children and have seven grandchildren. Two of their children are graduates of Ricks College. Bill has been a bishop twice and is currently president of the Sandy Utah Cottonwood Creek Stake.
