Ricks College News Release
June 26, 2001

Longtime Ricks employee 

named Food Services director

    
REXBURG, Idaho – Longtime employee Dan Black has been named director of Food Services at Ricks College.

Black, who has been serving as interim director since April, has worked for Food Services for 35 years.

He began his career working part time at Quality Bakery in downtown Burley while in high school. At age 23, he came to Ricks to be the baker when the Manwaring Center first opened in 1966. At the time, it was the only bakery in Rexburg.

"I was planning on getting my associate degree and going on," he says of coming to Ricks. "I liked it well enough that I’m still here."

He worked as the baker for about 15 years before moving into management where he managed the upstairs operation, including the student cafeteria and bakery. He also served as assistant director for several years, working both with past directors Sherrel Davis and John Soules.

Food Services employs about 45 full-time people and approximately 250 students each semester. During the school year, it serves about 2,500 meals a day. It operates a student cafeteria called The Galley, Nordic Landing, Viking Place, Thor’s Court, Traditions Catering and vending services.

In the future he plans to work on developing a food court where students, employees and visitors to campus can select from a variety of different types of foods.

Black and his wife, Kay, have six children and live in Sugar City.
