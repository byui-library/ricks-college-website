English 311 - Advanced Writing and Critical Reading - 3 credits

Prerequisite: English 111 or 111C and 22 credit hours

Exercises in logic, argument, textual analysis, summary, critique, and synthesis will lead to a substantive, documented, research paper.  Success in the class depends upon an understanding that research is a process of finding out information that is not presently known or of reinterpreting known facts.  Each student will be expected to do scholarly research on a college level.  While some concepts need to be learned in English 311, the main purpose of the class is to build skills in reasoning, research, and writing and not only to memorize concepts.
