Ricks College Health Fair ‘99

The Ricks College Health Fair is almost here. Next Thursday, November 4, the Wellness Committee will sponsor the annual Health Fair. It will be held from 9:00 to 4:00 in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom. This is an opportunity for you and your family to learn more about many things that may affect your health. It is also an opportunity to have your children immunized, to have some blood tests done, to have your blood pressure checked, and to have a massage. Bleed tests and their costs are as follows:

Cholesterol testing (cardiac risk or lipid profile) - 12 hour fast recommended $15 if drawn with a chemistry screen $18 if drawn alone

Basic Chemistry Profile )measures 14 items related to general health) - 12 hour fast recommended $12

                    Complete blood count (indicates infection or anemia) $10

                    Prostate Specific Antigen (screening recommended in men over 40 years) $29

If you plan to have a skin fold test done, come dressed in clothes which are easy to move so we can get to your skin.

The Health Fair is for faculty, staff and their families only in the morning, but after 1:00 students will be admitted. You may want to come early to avoid the crush.

Door prizes will be awarded very hour, with a special drawing for the grand prize at 2:00. The grand prize is a gourmet dinner for four, served to you in your own home!

Mark you calendar, and pan to join us November 4 at the Ricks College Health Fair. Your body will thank you.
