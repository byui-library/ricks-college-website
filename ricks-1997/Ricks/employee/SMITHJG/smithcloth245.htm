COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 245
Title: Pattern Making II Credit: 3
Text: Pattern making by the Flat-Pattern Method

Course Objective:
The major goal of this course is to help students acquire a working knowledge of designing by  the flat-pattern method for Men, Children, Knits, Maternity and advanced woven designs.
1.  Demonstrate flat-pattern skills for Knit patterns
2.  Apply specific flat–pattern principles from fashion sketches and photos
3.  Derive solutions to problems in flat-Pattern
4.  Plot patterns generated from CAD programs
5.  Grade patterns up and down
6.  Draft 5 basic pattern sloper

Description:
Fundamental principles of apparel pattern-making.  Methods include drafting, grading, CAD, flat-pattern knits, children’s men’s and women’s at an intermediate level.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Half scale patterns
2.   Grade and nest 3 pattern sizes
3.   Draft 5 basic sloper
4.   Plot customized sloper
