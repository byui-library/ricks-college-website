A Proprietor's Card, or a Prop Card, is simply a card, similar to a business card
but larger, that the owner of a press can use to circulate among friends, peers,
prospective customers, etc.

It is usually 3 inches by 5 inches in size. The kinds of information found on it
includes things like:

Proprietor's Name                    Date press was founded
Address/ Phone                        Press device (graphic or symbol
Creed or Philosophy of                representing the press)
    the press or its prop.            Name of press
