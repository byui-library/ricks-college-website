PER VIS ART LB
CLASS CODE: FA 100 CREDITS:  0 - 1
DIVISION: PERFORMING AND VISUAL ARTS
DEPARTMENT: ART
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION:
DESCRIPTION: FA 100 is the lab section of the arts requirement for Arts and Letters. It is a cultural arts event attendance lab
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: The following seven events are required: 1) an art exhibit 2) a play 3) a choir concert 4) a dance concert 5) a recital 6) an opera or oratorio 7) a symphonic band or orchestra concert. Completion of the lab requirement is based only on attendance of the seven events noted above.
REQUIREMENTS: To fulfill the Arts GE requirement a student must: 1) take any three credit class from the approved GE Arts classes listed in the college catalog and 2) complete the zero credit FA 100 lab. Both must be completed for graduation. It is recommended that when possible, a student enroll for both requirements in the same semester.
