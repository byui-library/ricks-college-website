President Hinckley to speak at commencement
Gordon B. Hinckley, president of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, will be the commencement speaker at the first graduation ceremonies of Brigham Young University-Idaho April 27.

President Hinckley, who also serves as chairman of the Board of Trustees of BYU-Idaho, will speak at the 9 a.m. commencement in the Hart Auditorium.

Elder Henry B. Eyring, Church Commissioner of Education and former president of Ricks College, will also be attendance.

In June 2000 President Hinckley announced that Ricks College would become a four-year institution known as Brigham Young University-Idaho and begin offering bachelor’s degrees.  

This is the first year that upper division classes have been offer offered and a few students will actually receive bachelor’s degrees during commencement. However, most graduates will be awarded associate degrees.

Seating in the Hart will be ticketed, but overflow will be provided in the Taylor Chapel, Kirkham Auditorium and Manwaring Center West Ballroom. Graduates who are marching will be allowed two guest tickets (three for married students) in the Hart.

President Hinckley was last on campus Sept. 7, 1999 to dedicate the Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building and to speak at devotional.
