Homecoming 2001 -- Extending the Legacy
September 17-22, 2001

An All-Alumni Reception with President and Sister David A. Bednar and former Ricks College Teachers and Staff will be held on September 22 from 5:00 to 6:30 pm in the Taylor Building Cultural Hall.  After the football game, come and meet President Bednar and his wife Susan and mingle with your former classmates, teachers and advisors.

A Student Leadership Reunion Dinner will be hosted by the Alumni Association for all former student body officers and their advisors.  This invitation only dinner will be held after the reception on September 22 at 7:00 pm in the Taylor Building.   If you are a former leader and would like more information please contact us by calling 1-800-437-2586 or send an e-mail to alumni@byui.edu.
