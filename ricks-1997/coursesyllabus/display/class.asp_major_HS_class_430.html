SCHOOL HEALTH PROGRAMS
SCH HLTH PROG
CLASS CODE: HS 430 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HEALTH SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Must declare Health Education as a major or minor and have completed the majority of freshman and sophomore core work. This class is designed to inform the future teacher about the comprehensive school health environment including both the school and the community health programs and procedures. Introduction to needs assessment, program development, finances evaluation, and implementation of school health programs will be covered.

OBJECTIVES: Students will be able to provide a:
1. Concise knowledge of environmental health practice in industry.
2. Concise knowledge of safety practice in industry.
3. Concise knowledge of the food safety inspection of food manufacturers and establishments.
4. Concise knowledge of the inspection process for hospitals, nursing home, and instutionalized people.
5. Concise knowledge of the process and OSHA rules for the inspection of industrial and business sites.
