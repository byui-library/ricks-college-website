I Danced in the Morning
(From a Protestant Hymn)

(Poetic Adaptations by David L. Ward)

I danced in the morning
when the world was begun,
I danced in the moon,
in the stars, and in the sun.
I came down from heaven
and I danced on earth;
At Bethlehem
I had My birth.

I danced for the scribes
and for all the Pharisees,
But they would not dance
and they would not follow Me;
I danced for the fishermen,
for James and for John;
They both followed me,
and the dance went on.

I danced on the Sabbath
while I cured the sick and lame;
And all the holy people
said that it was such a shame.
They whipped and they stripped,
and they hung me up high,
And left Me to Myself
on a cross to die.

I danced on a Friday
when the sky turned black;
It's hard to dance
with a beam upon your back.
They buried My body,
and they thought that I had gone:
But I am the Dance,
and the dance lives on.

They cut Me down,
and I leapt up oh so high;
I am the life
that will never, never die;
I'll live in you
if you'll live in Me:
I am the Lord
of the Dance, said He.


Refrain

Dance, then, wherever you may be,
I am the Lord of the Dance, said He,
I'll lead you all, wherever you may be,
I will lead you all
in the dance, said He.
