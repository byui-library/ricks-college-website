Keys to Teaching

Ideas from Elder Rex D. Pinegar

of the Seventy

August 24, 2000

Ricks College Faculty

1. Elder P gives his life to the Lord out of his knowledge of his own inadequacy (he could not as a new mission president make new companion, transfer assignments).

He prays, AIf you will take the nights, I will give you the days.@

(Trans: Quicken my subconscious, sleeping mind, so that when I am awake I know what to do.)

2. You are building a temple in the souls of every student who comes here.

3. Elder Sterling W Sill=s AWorth of a Soul@ Elder Sill calculated it in financial terms.

You are leading/teaching someone more valuable than that.

4. We are here (teaching at Ricks College) to save the souls of men and women.

5. Jacob de Jaeger=s story, ADid you save him?@ In a grave storm, the rowboat goes out to save the men on the foundered vessel but must leave one there. Who will go back out? A 19 year old volunteers. Mother: ADon=t. You father died at sea. Your brother Pete has been missing for 3 months.@ AI feel I must.@ From the shore coming back, ADid you save him?@ AYes, and its my brother Pete.@

There are those in the storms of life; you are their rescuers.

6. We need the truth because our task is not for mortality alone. We must keep the eternal perspective in mind, for we are educating for the eternities.

7. In the Korean War on the dock before going back to the ship. A comrade who was in a really tough place in the war, said, AI=m not going back.@ (Lives were at that station 7 days long; he had beat the odds so far). They hear a splash. Another splash. A Rescue. ARex, you had all the lifesaving training. What were you doing on the pier?@ Have you ever seen a student floundering, drowning? We think, Asomeone will help.@

We need to save our brothers and sisters by teaching them the truth.

8. Story: Joseph into Egypt: Judah says about leaving Benjamin. AHow can I return and the lad be not with me.@

Who are we going to take back? Who are we going to leave behind?

9. All who labor in the kingdom are in the soul-saving business.

10. Those you are teaching must not just be taught and have a specialty. They must have general skills because they have special souls, and must be equipt to find other souls they can help save.

11. He was a balanced person because he was never out of balance with the Lord.

12. Brigham Young: ATake pride in educating your mind until you can control your lives in everything.@

13. Would God feel comfortable in your class?

14. AThe gospel should be studied more intently than any other subject@ John A. Widstoe

15. Unapologetically use the scriptures. Take them to class. Have them on your desk.

16. The story of his learning to teach 6th graders and the sage advice he received from a master teacher:

Find out first where your students are and take them from there.

17. I had to teach to the students= understanding. To do that I needed to learn the students= needs.

18. Show them how what you teach them develops their relationship to God. President Faust: AThe Church values education because education equates to our eternal well being.@

19. Tie your teaching to eternal life; tie your teaching to the temple. Life is eternal and we must remember to treat it that way. There is in man a divine nature; we respond to eternal principles when we are taught them.

20. To overcome selfishness, focus on the abundant life of someone else.

21. Psalms 127:1 Except the Lord build the house, they that build it build in vain.

22. AYou must stand for something or you'll fall for anything. Lyrics to a CW song.
