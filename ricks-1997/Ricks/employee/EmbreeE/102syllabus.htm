Syllabus - Communication 102

Public Speaking

The grade you receive in this public speaking class will be based on 4 different things: Speeches you give in class, Written assignments, Tests over the text material, and Evaluations you do of other speakers.

You will give a total of 7 speeches in class. These are listed below. There are five written assignments. There will be 4 tests over the chapters in the text. You will also evaluate of your class mates in their speeches. You will receive points for each evaluation you do. The Text is "Speaking with a Purpose" by Arthur Koch

WRITTEN ASSIGNMENTS

Number Weight
1. An analysis of yourself as a public speaker. 50 points

What experience have you had in public speaking situations. What are some of your weaknesses, what are some of your strengths as far as public speaking is concerned. Look at the evaluation handout for some ideas on areas to evaluate your self on.

2. An evaluation of a devotional speaker. 100 Points

Use the 12 categories on the handout sheet. Do not just mark the handout and turn it in. This is to be a fully written paper. Use the 12 evaluation items to give you ideas. You will be graded on how comprehensive your evaluation is. If you are unable to get to a devotional you can use another public speaking situation, check with me.

3. An evaluation of a written speech. 100 Points

Use the same format as the #2 assignment above. The difference will be that you are reading the speech rather than hearing it, so you can not evaluate the delivery portion of the speech. You can use issues of the Ensign which have all the conference addresses in them, or any other source with a written speech in it. Don't use a speech that you also heard.

4. An evaluation of your inform speech. 100 Points

After watching the video tape of that speech, Use the 12 evaluation items and I would like you to be very comprehensive and in depth on the analysis. This should be 2 or 3 typewritten pages. This counts the same number of points as the speech.

5. An evaluation of your self at end of class. 50 Points

This should be written right at the end of the semester. Use a similar format to #1 above, however, you will have what you have learned in class to add to it. I would appreciate it if you would include your evaluation of the class as well.

Your papers will be graded on the following criteria:

  • Completeness -- Did you give me all the information I asked for?
  • Understanding -- Does your paper show you have analyzed and understood the material.
  • Organization and intelligibility -- I can only understand you to the degree that you express yourself clearly. If you have a difficult time writing you can request the help of the writing lab on campus. You should use a thesis, topic sentence, and support material.

* N O T E *

On your written evaluations 2, 3, and 4 listed above, you should use the twelve items for evaluating speeches.
