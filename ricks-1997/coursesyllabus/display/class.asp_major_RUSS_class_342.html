Russian Culture
RUSSIAN CUL
CLASS CODE: RUSS 342 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An introduction to Russian culture, literature, art, music, and history. Taught in English. Cross-listed as Anthropology 342.
DESCRIPTION: This course will give the student a basic overview of more than 1,000 years of Russian history and culture, from the formation of the Kievan state to the fall of Communism. Special emphasis will be placed on the "Golden Age" of Russian culture during the 19th Century.
TOPICS: Russian history and geography and Russian culture, including art, music, literature, and dance.
OBJECTIVES: By the end of the semester, students will be able to: 1)Better appreciate what makes a Russian "Russian;" 2)More fully understand the influene of Russian history and geography on the Russian character; and 3)How that Russian character has helped to define Russian art, music, literature, and dance.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, reading and writing homework, quizzes, papers, midterm and final exams.
PREREQUISITES: English 111 or consent of Instructor.
