CREDIT CLASSES

Who May Enroll
Persons who have graduated from high school or who are nineteen years of age or older may enroll for credit. High school seniors may also enroll for credit with prior approval from Continuing Education officials and their high school principal. BYU/Ricks Continuing Education programs admit qualified handicapped persons and persons of any race, creed, religion, ethnic or national origin who agree to abide by Ricks College standards.
Transferring Credit
Continuing Education is not responsible for determining the applicability of any course or credit toward a degree, certification, or transferring to another institution. This is the student's responsibility. However, Continuing Education administrative personnel can advise you concerning these matters.
Scholarships
Scholarships are available to Continuing Education students registering for BYU or Ricks College Credit at Idaho Falls or Rexburg. To apply, contact either the Division of Continuing Education Ricks College, 356-1040, or the BYU/Ricks Center at Idaho Falls, 523-4682.
Career Guidance and Counseling Services
Career counseling services are available to all Ricks College students and community patrons who are considering further education. This service is free of charge and can be provided at the Ricks College Counseling Center, 356-1167. Call for an appointment.
INEL Employees
Employees desiring tuition reimbursement should consult with their employer.

NON-CREDIT CLASSES

Who May Enroll
All community patrons are invited to take advantage of our non-credit class programs. Some classes are set up specifically for children or youth. Please note age requirements listed in the class schedule with each individual class.
Registration
Enroll by pre-registration only--by mail or in person--no later than 12:00 p.m. the day the class begins. Registration is taken on a first-come basis until the class fills and will NOT be taken at the class.
Tuition
Tuition and any extra fees must be paid in order to finalize registration and guarantee a place in the class. Non-Credit class tuition varies. The cost of the class is listed with its description in the class schedule.
