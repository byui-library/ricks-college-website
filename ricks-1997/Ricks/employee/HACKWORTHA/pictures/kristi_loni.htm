I have two lovely daughter and an intelligent wife.  Here is my second daughter, Kristi, and my wife, Loni.  Kristi recently finished her schooling, internship, and national boards to become a registered dietitian.  She is currently working full time in that profession while her husband, Dan, finishes college. Loni is also an educated woman, a college graduate with many additional graduate credits.  She is currently a licensed librarian and works for our local school district.
