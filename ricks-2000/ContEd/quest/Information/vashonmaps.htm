VASHON MAPS AND DIRECTIONS

 

To get to the ferry at Point Defiance coming from I-5 South into Tacoma you 

should:  Once you pass the Tacoma Dome, work your way into the right lane 

and follow the signs for Hwy. 16. Continue on Hwy 16 until you get to the 6th  

Ave. exit. Go 3/4 of a block, merging into the right lane and turn right on Pearl 

St. (Pearl St. is also known as State Hwy 163.) Follow Pearl St. to the ferry 

dock. Once on Vashon, you get off the ferry at Tahlequah. At the end of the 

dock, turn right (east) on Bachelor SW and drive 9/10 of one mile. While 

driving, you will go up a hill keeping left past the fire station (the road changes at 

this point to 31 Ave SW, keep going past 297 Way SW until you see a white rail 

fence. Turn right in front of the fence by a sign "Morningside Farm."  We also 

plan to have a BYU-Idaho sign on the driveway.

      If you are coming from the North please follow these directions.  Start

following directions at 148th Ave. SE exit in Bellevue on I-90 which is the

Seattle Temple exit.  Take the I-90 West towards Seattle. Merge onto I-90

West.  Take the I-5 South exit, (exit #2B, on the left towards Tacoma/Portland. 

Merge onto I-5 South.  Take the West Seattle BR/Columbian Way exit (exit

#163A).  Keep right at the fork in the ramp then merge onto West Seattle

Bridge.  Continue onto Fauntleroy Way SW.  Turn Left on 35th Ave. SW,

continue on 35th Ave. SW. From the turn onto 35th Ave. SW the road will

curve back and forth to the west and eventually turn into 48th Ave. SW, there is

a Texaco station on the right.  The ferry lane begins on the right on the street. 

Once you get on Vashon Island follow Vashon Highway to Tahlequah, then

follow the directions above to get to Morningside Farm.
