Biography

      I began my career as an "English Pre-Law" major at B.Y.U.  I think I was just looking for an excuse to stay in English because when the time came to study law, I couldn't make myself do it.  "So, what I do I do next?"  That question sent me trekking through M.A. and Ph.D. programs, first at B.Y.U. then at Washington State University.  Along the way, I married an English major, Christen Brog*and we've had great fun collaborating on writing projects ever since.  We have four children, who, by the way, have made it perfectly clear they will not major in English.  I have been at Ricks since 1982 and continue to find the classroom experience here a source of growth and pleasure.
