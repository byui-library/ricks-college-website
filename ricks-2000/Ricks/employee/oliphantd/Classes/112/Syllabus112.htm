Course Description and Objectives

Physics 112 is a lab course designed to give you practical experience with a broad range of physics topics. Physics 112 should show you how abstract ideas have useful applications in reality and help you develop analytical, problem solving, and technical communication skills.

 

Course Requirements

There will be twelve labs in the term with one make-up lab at the end. You may only miss one of the twelve regularly scheduled labs because of sickness, college sponsored activities, or other instructor-approved absence. The make-up lab at the end of the semester is only for those who missed a lab. It is not intended for those who want to redo a lab with a poor grade.

 

Write-up:

  • Pre-report findings. These will be due the day of the lab and consist of a summary of the introductory material and the completion of any work that was supposed to be done prior to the lab. The summary should not be more than a paragraph or two. I will collect the summaries and check off any work.
  • Post-report findings. These are the more formal write-ups and are due in lab the week following. Most of the write-up will be done on the lab manual papers themselves, but if you have extra data or calculations you may turn in extra papers. In addition, I want a conclusion handed in. The conclusion should tie together the important themes of the lab and convey to me what you have learned in the lab. This conclusion will need to be typed on a separate piece of paper and should not be longer than a paragraph or two.

 

As stated previously, pre-report findings are due at the beginning of the lab. Post-report findings are due at the beginning of the next lab. Late write-ups are not generally accepted.

 

Final:

There will be a short final at the end of the last lab (lab 12).

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Grading

Each lab will be worth 50 points. With 12 labs there are 600 points possible. For each lab, pre-reports will be worth 10 points and post-reports will be worth 40 points. The final is required to pass the class.  It is worth 50 points and will take the place of your lowest lab score.  Grades will not be curved.
