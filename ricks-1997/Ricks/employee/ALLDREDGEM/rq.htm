  6. A student is looking for a list of fashion magazines with telephone numbers. Who is her teacher? Is it on reserve? (9/2/99) Answer: A student was not satisfied by any of the following. She was only happy with a list that her teacher had placed on reserve which listed catalogs not magazines.
    • Our serials list has three magazines under FASHION; the front of the magazines have telephone #
    • Ulrich's International Periodicals Directory (Ref Z6941.U5) had listings with phone #
    • Standard Periodical Directory (Ref Z 6951.S78) had listings with phone #
    • Magazines for Libraries (Ref Z6941.K2) has a subject arrangement but NO telephone numbers.
    • Free Magazines for Libraries (Ref Z 692.S5L36-1994) NO telephone numbers.
    • The moral of this search is that patrons do not always know what they want. They are looking for something that matches what they have heard. If the information is not in a red book... or small list, it must not be what they wanted. (9/16/99)
