Mission Statement

We believe that students are best served by teachers that:

1. Possess and demonstrate comprehension, communication, and
creativity.

2. Value and implement an integrated liberal arts curriculum.

3. Conceive and structure experiences that allow students to become
self-directed learners.

4. Use and adapt technology as a tool to meet student needs and
further self-directed learning.

5. Develop and maintain balance through self-renewal.
