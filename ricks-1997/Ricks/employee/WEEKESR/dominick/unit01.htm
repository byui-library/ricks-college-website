How Audio Works: Unit 1


Basic Principles of Audio Technology

•Transduction:
-The process of changing one form of energy into another.

•Signal and Noise:
-Signal is the message.
-Noise is unwanted interference.
-The higher the signal to noise ratio, the better the quality of the message.

•Analog:
- A transduction from physical to electrical impulses or signals.

•Digital:
-A transduction from physical to digital impulses or signals. Binary code is "on-off", "yes-no", "open-shut", or "0-1".

•Frequency:
-The number of [sound] waves that pass a given point in a given time. Frequency is used to describe a sound's pitch.

•Amplitude:
-The height or depth of a [sound] wave. Amplitude is used to describe a sound's loudness.
