Chapter 22

MUSIC — BAROQUE, CLASSICAL

 

 

CHARACTERISTICS

1. no more modes

2. well-tempered system

the start of "keys"

start of 12 notes with 1/2 step intervals

3. shift of interest from a texture of several independent parts of equal importance to music in which a single melody predominated; from poly to homophonic

4. figured bass (as in jazz)

5. terraced dynamics (Four Seasons, Nigel version)

6. Doctrine of Affections

from Camerata (chamber) in Florence (salon in Paris)

invents opera, first performed in 1600, in France

revive the musical dramas of the Greeks/combining of poetry and sound
importance of single melody—not texture

"music must heighten the emotional power of the text"
opera (and Top 10)

word painting

crucifixion - chromatic down

resurrection - major fifth up

crooked in Messiah Tr. 3

7. continuous melody - leave no undecorated space in the music

everything is to be moving, in the act of becoming
Bach Harpsichord CD Tr 9

8. steady rhythm (driving, non-stop, addictive)
Brandenberg #1 Tr 5,7,8 all same tempo

 

Music was for the aristocracy, with opera being their favorite. (because it always portrayed them as gods)

The common man found music at home or at church.

 

BACH 1685-1750

came from musical family

had supplied musicians for 150 years

was an orphan at 10, stayed with his brother, an organist

brother didn’t want him to be a musician

locked scores in cabinet

Bach copied by candle light

starts career at age 18, as an organist

called before bishopric for "curious variations"

and for making eyes at a girl in the choir (whom he later marries.)

they have 7 children, then she dies

age 23 works for Duke of Weimar

wants to leave for 9 years

Bach refuses/Duke refuses

Bach jailed for 30 days, then fired

age 32 he remarries and has another 13 (but only half survive infancy)

age 38 most of his career is at Leipzig

Telemann was offered the job, but refused

"Since the best man could not be obtained, lesser ones would have to be accepted."

becomes the best organist and best composer in Germany

goes blind in old age, but dictates revisions anyway

his primary medium was the organ

he was a virtuoso (only Handel was in his class)

"I had to work hard."

"Anyone who works as hard will get just as far."

"There is nothing remarkable about it. All you have to do is hit the right notes at the right time and the instrument plays itself."

over 200 cantatas and 300 chorales (hymns)

wrote at least one per month, with all parts, etc.

4 hour church service

devout Lutheran

Well Tempered Clavier (48 preludes and fugues) major/minor, two each. (Only three keys before this time. c.1680)

the "Old Testament" of piano (Beethoven’s sonatas are "New Test")

Brandenburg Concertos, for Margrave of Brandenburg, a customs inspector who collected concertos

had Bach write a set of 6

never performed

estate sold them for 10 cents each

are considered the best ever written

wrote all his music in groups of 6

Little Fugue in Gm

fugue = "flight" (of melody from one spot to another)

unity of mood single affection or mood (subject)

unity of rhythm, only one tempo

Switched on Bach Invention

Dm on CD

Passacaglia in Cm

 

Handel 1685-1759

if Bach=subjective mysticism, then Handel=wordly pomp

they never met/Bach was too low for Handel

born in Germany, son of rich doctor (barber/surgeon)

became musician, against father’s wishes

first composition at 20, an opera, instant success

went to Italy for 5 years study, returns a hero

appointed conductor to Elector of Hanover and paid 20 times Bach’s Salary

trip to England in 1710, returns 2 years later and stays

the Queen grants him a pension

he doesn’t go back to his boss in Germany

in 1714 the Queen dies and Elector of Hanover becomes George 1 of England (1714-27)

Royal Water Music (1715)

played by 50 musicians on royal barge

played 3 times each way

writes mostly operas, usually in fits of work 2-3 weeks

(wrote 40 in 30 years)

opera was for the rich and flattered then

John Gay’s Beggar’s Opera was a satire on the form

was in English, not Italian

was about middle class, not rich

Handel couldn’t see the new trend, and wrote even more.

Bombshell: church outlawed religious opera and costumes

so Handel "created" the oratorio (religious opera)

had a stroke at age 52

enemies though he was done, but he recovered

went blind in old age, but dictated, like Bach/Milton

1759 he conducted 10 major works in just over one month,

collapsed backstage after Messiah, and died a few days later.

MESSIAH (not the) (written at age 57)

1742, for a boy’s hospital in Dublin, Ireland.

written in 24 days

(he immediately went right on with Sampson, and had the first of it ready in two weeks. Truly this was the age of giants!)

 

CLASSICAL ERA -- COMPOSERS

HAYDN 1732-1809

came from poor parents

had a good voice, so choir in Vienna until 16

make living by teaching and accompanying

played in streets for pennies

spotted b y aristocrats, and worked in their home

age 29 goes to work for Count Esterhazy (30 years)

wrote for orchestra, opera, chapel, puppets

patronage system

invents symphony "I was cut off from the world. I was forced to become original."

goes to England (Esterhazy dies)

Creation is very popular (second only to Messiah)

dies rich and famous

His music

creates symphony

creates 3rd movement

preferred string quartet (wrote 83)

104 symphonies

14 masses

 

MOZART 1756-1791

child prodigy

"the one moment in musical history when it all came together."

public concerts at age 5, composing even before that

first symphony at age 7, had written 3 by age 10

Haydn copied his music

perfect music, wrote it only once, no revisions

his wife didn’t help his inability to manage

"Stanze" spent them out of the house and home

he dies writing his own Requiem

dies at 35, poor funeral, pauper’s grave

41 symphonies

23 string quartets

22 operas

50 concertos

17 sonatas

perfects the Classical style

 

BEETHOVEN: 1770-1827

from Bonn, Germany/father is town drunk

had to practice late at night/beatings for missed notes

raised in Mozart’s shadow/must be like him

concerts while child, but billed at half his age

ugly/big head/teased/becomes a loner

takes long walks in Vienna Woods

age 17 goes to Vienna/studies under Mozart

age 19 returns home to dying mother

arrives only to find her dead

returns to Vienna to Mozart

arrives only to find him dead

slow learner/revise 20 times/short motives

made piano sound like the orchestra

hated class discrimination, and kings

Eroica originally to Napoleon/tore up title page

expands definition of what a symphony is to be

sym 1 and 2 sound like Haydn (1795-1803)

starts hearing loss 1803

sym 3 to 8 (1803-1815)

5th/same motif in all 4 movts/extremes in emotion

almost deaf by 6th. Could only hear loud passages

deaf by 1809

9th in old age/his best/shut off the world

radical/uses chorus in 5th movt

twice as long as Eroica

ode to Joy by Schiller
