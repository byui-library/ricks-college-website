Advanced Writing for Business
ADVWRTG/BUS
CLASS CODE: ENG 312 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - English requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes rhetorical strategies, patterns, and aims characteristic of professional business writing with a focus on purpose and audience. Requires final drafts totaling 8,000-10,000 words of edited and documented prose. (Required for Business majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes rhetorical strategies, patterns, and aims characteristic of professional business writing with a focus on purpose and audience. Requires final drafts totaling 8,000-10,000 words of edited and documented prose. (Required for Business majors.)
TOPICS: Professional business writing modes and practices, such as correspondence, proposals, summaries, job-search materials, business plans, promotional literature, research reports; critical thinking skills to successfully synthesize, analyze, and evaluate information within business fields; strategies for research and documentation specific to business; professional prose style; collaborative learning and teamwork in a professional environment.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Understanding rhetorical strategies, genres, and pattern characteristic of business writing. (2) Writing with an awareness of prewriting, drafting, and revising drafts. (3) Adapting discourse to particular audiences and purposes. (4) Writing clearly, accurately, and precisely. (5) Using arrangement strategies for organizing and synthesizing information. (6) Applying critical thinking skills to problems typical of business communications. (7) Learning research techniques to find, evaluate, and use sources appropriate to business. (8) Using an appropriate documentation style. (9) Making effective oral presentations.
REQUIREMENTS: Requires final drafts totaling 8,000-10,000 words of edited and documented prose.
PREREQUISITES: Completion of English 111 or 111C and Business 220, and at least 22 credit hours or sophomore standing.
