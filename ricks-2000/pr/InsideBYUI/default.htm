Inside BYU-Idaho, previously Inside Ricks, is a weekly e-mail newsletter sent to alumni, former employees and other interested parties all over the world. Suggestions for future topics may be submitted to PublicRelations@byui.edu.

To add your e-mail address to the mailing list or to unsubscribe, please contact Inside@byui.edu.
