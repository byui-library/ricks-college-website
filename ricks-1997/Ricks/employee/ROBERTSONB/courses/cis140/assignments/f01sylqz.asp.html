IS 140 Syllabus Quiz - Fall 2001

Directions: Open Word 2000. Enter your name and section number at the top of the document and record your answers into the Word document. When done, save it to your H: drive using the same name as recorded for answer #1 of the quiz.

1. What name should be given this Word document? (refer to the Submitting Work section of the syllabus if needed)

2. Briefly summarize the late work policy of the class.

3. All assignments are due at what time of day?

4. What are the values of exams in the course?

5. What two ways can a student submit assignments?

6. What must be in the subject line of emails that have assignments attached to them?

7. What must you do if you submit assignments to the dropbox?

8. What is the URL (web address) for the class CIS 140 Index page, so you can access course materials online?

9. What is the class policy concerning use of Internet, email, instant messaging and other non-class software?

10. When are assignments due for any absence other than school excused?

When you are finished with this quiz submit it using either of the two approved methods.
