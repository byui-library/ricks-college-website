Scope and Content

The Stringham Papers consist mainly of photocopies of the journals of Miranda Campbell Stringham. The journals extend from 1920 to 1984, a period of sixty-four years, and provide a fascinating glimpse into the every-day life of a woman throughout this tumultuous period.

Stringham was a writer on local history and manuscripts of some of her published and unpublished works are contained in the collection. Some correspondence relating to her books is also included.

Biographical Sketch of Miranda Cambell Stringham

Miranda Campbell Stringham was born at Ammon, Idaho on July 17, 1904. She lived there until about 1921. Stringham had an abiding interest in history and took classes in history and journalism.

She belonged to the Idaho Writers and Poets Guild for twelve years and served as president of that organization.

Stringham was the author of several books on local history. These include: Old Ammon: the first fifty years 1885-1935, Basalt-Firth since 1900, History of the Cedar Point, The People of the Hills and other shorter articles. She contributed to the Shelley Pioneer and the Blackfoot News. She penned several county histories for the Idaho History, and wrote a book of poetry as well
