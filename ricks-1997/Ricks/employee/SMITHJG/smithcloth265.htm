COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics Course#: Cloth 265
Title: Line Collection & Evaluation Credit: 4
Text:  “Ready-to-Wear Apparel Analysis” & “Inside Fashion Design”

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire the knowledge and skills necessary for creative application of creating a complete apparel line collection
Upon complete of this course a student should be able to:

 1.  Demonstrate skills in construction an apparel line
 2.  Apply specific principles of ready-to-wear analysis
 3.  Apply principles of designing a fashion line collection
 4.  Recognize seams and quality in respect to price ranges

Description:
Fundamental principles of creating and producing an apparel line collection; evaluation of apparel quality, categories, and price ranges.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Construction of an apparel line
2.  Boards for line collection
3.  Forecasting
4.  Presentation of a line collection
