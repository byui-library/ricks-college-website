Greetings. My name is Brian L. Merrill. I've been a faculty member at Ricks College since 1988, with three years' hiatus for doctoral work. Until 1998 I taught in the English Department. Since then, I have been teaching in the Department of Humanities & Philosophy. My wife Barbara and I have two children and a Labrador named Jewel, whom we attempt to confine to the back yard.

Apart from my family and the Lord, I have one driving passion: learning. My boundless ignorance drives me to read and study whatever and whenever I can. For relaxation I am rather partial to early and classical music, jazz, thoughtful conversation, and occasionally a good film. Finally I enjoy traveling--anywhere will do, but I prefer Europe.
