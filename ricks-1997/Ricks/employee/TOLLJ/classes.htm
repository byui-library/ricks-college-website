My Classes for 2001

Hort 122 - Plant Materials - Evergreen

The identification, landscape value, and special cultural requirements of evergreen trees, shrubs and ground covers. (Winter)

Hort 135 : Flower Arranging

A beginning level course in floral design including: instruction in care and handling of flowers, historical and current application of principles and
elements of design, identification and use of floral equipment and materials,
and bouquet and corsage preparation.

Hort 220 : Plant Propagation

The principles and practices used in commercial propagation of plants with emphasis on landscape nursery stock. Includes tissue culture, seed germination, grafting, layering, budding, cuttings and other specialized techniques.
