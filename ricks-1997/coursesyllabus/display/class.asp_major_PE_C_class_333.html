CLASS CODE: PE*C 333 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Teaching methods of football and wrestling.
DESCRIPTION: Teaching methods of football and wrestling will be taught two hours a week with an additional hands-on lab for a block class.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: The student will be able to:
1. coach and teach football and wrestling. Organize practices, tournaments, and drills.
3. Teach skills and game structures.
4. Understand and exhibit skill of team selection.
5. Assess management and instructional strategies to maximuze activity time and success.
6. Apply rules and skills for team sports
REQUIREMENTS: To teach the correct methods of football and wrestling to students to enable them to use skills learned to teach their own children, classroom students, community sports, etc. for a lifetime.
PREREQUISITES: Must have knowledge and understanding of football and wrestling.
