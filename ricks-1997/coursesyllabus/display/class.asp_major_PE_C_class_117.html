Racquetball Beginning
RACQUET BEG
CLASS CODE: PE*C 117 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Class designed to teach the basic skills, fundamentals, and techniques required to play racquetball effectively.
DESCRIPTION: This class is designed to teach the basic skills, fundamentals, and techniques required to play racquetball effectively. To develop a sufficient level of physical fitness and skill level in each participant to allow them to engage in the activity with proficiency.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. Understand knowledge and skill of fundamentals, rules, and strategies of the game of racquetball.
2. Become more physically fit to participate in more proficient manner
3. Improve cardiovascular development
4. Control emotions and temper by playing sport
6. Learn to deal with people, unite in a team effort, share responsibilities
7. Develop self-esteem and respect for others
8. Develop skills to teach serving, rebounds, strokes, and shots
9. Ability to organize a tournament
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Exhibit ability to organize a tournament
2. Exhibit knowledge and skill of fundamentals, rules and strategies of racquetball.
3. Attend class and participate.
4. BYU-I clothing is required.
5. Racquetball goggles.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences alowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated for one time for credit.
5. P.E. clothing fee: $4.
6. Students must provide their own goggles.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
