History of the Spori Building

Alumni & Friends | Current Students | Employees | Prospective Students | Directories | Search | Index

Jan. 22, 1900 First load of rock hauled to building site.
June 25, 1900 Cornerstone laid by President George Q. Cannon.
Oct. 1903 Building first used at start of 1903-1904 school year.
Nov. 12, 1906 Building dedicated by Apostle John Henry Smith.
1945 Remodeling - Open stairway replaced.  Large lecture room opened.
1956 Remodeling - Auditorium on third floor converted to library space.
1963 Remodeling - 3rd floor converted into offices, classroom and dining room.
Dec. 11, 1963 Building named Jacob Spori Administration Building.
1968 Remodeling - south annex, new offices and freight entrance built.
1976 Remodeling - Administrative offices moved, classroom space built.
1980 Remodeling - student newspaper, communication department move in.
1989 Building is placed on National Register of Historic Places.
Winter 1995 Building re-roofed and north side dormers removed.
March 1999 Future of building in doubt due to safety concerns.
June 22, 1999 Future of Spori Decided
November 30, 1999 Architectural Firm Selected to Design Spori Replacement
