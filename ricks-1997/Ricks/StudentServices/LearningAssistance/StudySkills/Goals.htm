GOALS

 

“What the mind of man (or woman) can conceive-and believe-it can achieve.”                                                                                                  -Earl Nightingale

 

“We often think we have goals, but after honest examination, we find that what we thought was a goal was simply a wish, or desire, or hope. The difference between hopes and goals is that the latter are actualized into practical plans.”

                                                                                      -Wayne R. Herlin

 

Goals are the results toward which effort is directed. A goal can be seen as an endpoint or a checkpoint. They are the means by which we can measure how we are doing in the important areas of our lives. Goals help us to achieve our fullest potential and become better people.

 

Setting goals can sometimes be frustrating and hard. Here are a few steps we can take to help set good goals.

 

The first step in effective goal setting is to know what it is that you would like to accomplish. You need to have a vision or an idea of what you want to take place. Take time out to brainstorm your goals and desires. List all the things that come to your mind. After filling out your list, select the ten most important goals that you will work on and categorize them in terms of how long each will take to accomplish:

long-term goals 3-5+ years; mid-term goals 1-3 years and; short-term goals now-1 year.

 

After you have categorized your list and know which goals you will be working towards realize that goals need to:

1.       Be specific

2.       Be realistic

3.       Be measurable

4.       Be compatible

5.         Have due dates

6.       Be written

 

You can then ask yourself a few questions.

1.       Is this goal consistent with my personal values?

2.       Am I willing to commit myself to a specific timetable for completion?

3.       Can I measure my progress in increments while working toward finishing this goal?

4.                 Have I thought through the commitment necessary and the consequences of

          setting this goal? Am I willing to sacrifice and pay the price?

5.                 Can I visualize this goal clearly and see myself at the endpoint that it

          represents? Is it reasonable to start and complete the project?

6.                 Have I thought through the short-term objectives or the specific activities

          necessary for accomplishing this goal?

 

The final step in effective goal setting is to break the goals down into parts that are easy to follow and accomplish. Breaking the goals down simply means setting up all the short-term and mid-term goals needed to accomplish the long-term goal. Mid­term goals should be broken into short-term goals and short-term goals into weekly or daily tasks to get things done.
