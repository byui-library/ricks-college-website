Services and Information for New Employees and Their Families

Please Note:

  • Fees and restrictions apply to some services.

  • Spouses and dependents of employees are encouraged to observe the BYU-Idaho Honor Code (includes Dress and Grooming Standards) while on campus.

  • It is a good idea to call before coming to campus. Some offices have abbreviated hours.

  • Key Codes:

E-FT = employee (full-time)
E-PT = employee (part-time)
S-FT = spouse of a full-time employee
S-PT = spouse of a part-time employee
F-FT = family member of a full-time employee
F-PT = family member of a part-time employee

DMBA (insurance and retirement benefits) (E-FT, S-FT, F-FT)

  • DMBA toll-free telephone number: 1-800-777-3622. Web site: www.dmba.com

  • Insurance benefits include: medical (three choices), dental, life, disability, and occupational accidental death and dismemberment.

  • Supplemental insurance benefits include: supplemental life, universal life, and 24 hour accidental death.

  • The university provides a retirement benefit. An employee becomes vested after 5 years.

  • Supplemental retirement programs are also available (i.e. 401K). Employees can invest in DMBA programs (After-Tax and Before-Tax) or TIAA-CREF.

  • Part-time employees may participate in the Supplemental Retirement program.

Faculty Association (E-FT, E-PT)

  • Open to employees who teach credit classes or who have eligibility for faculty status.

  • The Faculty Association Mission Statement focuses on three goals:

  1. To Improve the Faculty (The association sponsors various programs.)

  2. To Represent the Faculty (Faculty can share concerns with their division representatives and association officers. These concerns can become agenda items in the Faculty Association Council, the Academic Council, or the president's monthly meeting with the University President.)

  3. To Honor the Faculty (Faculty Association Banquet and awards ceremony each year)

  • See web page for more information (http://www.byui.edu/FacAssn/)
