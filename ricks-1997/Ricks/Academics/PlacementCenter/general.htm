The purpose of the center is to assist students and alumni in marketing themselves to potential employers. The staff at the center help train students and alumni in job search techniques, resume writing, cover letter writing, and interviewing. We also provide job listings and assist students in obtaining full-time career jobs or summer internships. The center is equipped with MS Word, WordPerfect 9 and laser printers to assist students and alumni in producing top quality documents.

Resources at the center include:
 Employment and career advising.
Job listings (various openings from employers that are posted in the center).
Free job search literature (resume booklets, career surveys, etc.).
Critiques of resumes and cover letters.
Contacts and leads (from our database of contacts).
Business directories and information.
Job search success books.
Computers and a laser printer to print out quality documents.

Additional job hunting resources:
Your own personal network of contacts (This is the most effective method).
Libraries (which contain newspapers, directories, phone books, etc.).
State employment agencies.
LDS Employment agencies.
Career resource centers at other colleges.
Temporary and other employment agencies.
Internet -- Web.
Newspapers.
Job fairs.

We will give you all the assistance that we can, but ultimately the responsibility for finding a job is yours, not ours. If we can be of assistance to you, please let us know. Good luck in your job search!
