The mission of the Student Alumni Association is to strengthen the links between past, present and future Ricks College Students by:
  • Fostering a sense of tradition and loyalty to Ricks College that will motivate students to become active alumni.
  • Cultivating future alumni leadership in current students.
  • Supporting the Alumni Association in their goals and activities.
  • Serving as student ambassadors who reflect the Spirit of Ricks College and the values of the Church.
  • Teaching charitable giving and service by encouraging all students to participate in the Student Legacy Fund.

If you are interested in this worthy cause and would like to be actively involved with our association, please call us at 208-356-1128, send us an e-mail or visit us at Kimball 220, in the Student Services Building.
