Course Description

This course focuses on understanding the doctrine, teachings, and structure of the "most correct book" and "keystone of our religion," the Book of Mormon Another Testament of Jesus Christ.

Value of the Course

Four inspired statements highlight the value and importance of this course.

"I told the brethren that the Book of Mormon was the most correct of any book on earth, and the keystone of our religion, and a man would get nearer to God by abiding its precepts, than by any other book." (Joseph Smith, HC 4:461)

"No member of this Church can stand approved in the presence of God who has not seriously and carefully read the Book of Mormon..." (Joseph Fielding Smith, CR, October 1961)

"Every Latter-day Saint should make the study of this book a lifetime pursuit. Otherwise he is placing his soul in jeopardy and neglecting that which could give spiritual and intellectual to his whole life. There is a difference between a convert who is built on the rock of Christ through the Book of Mormon and stays hold of that iron rod, and one who is not." (Ezra Taft Benson, Ensign, May 1975, p. 65)

"There is a power in the book [of Mormon] which will begin to flow into your lives the moment you begin a serious study of the book. You will find greater power to resist temptation. You will find the power to avoid deception. You will find the power to stay on the strait and narrow path. The scriptures are called 'the words of life' (see D&C 84:85), and nowhere is that more true than it is of the Book of Mormon. When you begin to hunger and thirst after those words, you will find life in greater and greater abundance." (Ezra Taft Benson, Ensign, Nov. 1986, p. 7)

Course Objectives

  • Create a learning environment wherein the spirit of the Holy Ghost is present and can teach and testify (D&C 50:13-14).
    •  
  • Deepen our love and testimony for the Book of Mormon and our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
    •  
  • Read, ponder, and feast upon the words of Christ as found in the Book of Mormon.
  •  
  • More fully understand the doctrines of the Gospel as taught in the Book of Mormon and apply them in our daily lives.
    •  
  • Increase our knowledge of the people, places, and organization of the Book of Mormon to the extent it helps us more fully understand the doctrines therein.
    •  
  • Increase our ability to truly study and understand the scriptures.

Course Procedures

Students are expected to l) complete reading and writing assignments before class sessions; 2) come to class spiritually and mentally prepared to ask questions and participate in class discussions; and 3) be receptive to the promptings of the Holy Ghost and "liken all scriptures unto themselves" (1 Ne. 19:23).

Class attendance and your participation in and contributions to class discussions are expected. Note that the quality of your contribution is important, not the amount of "airtime."

Some Thoughts on Grading and Evaluation

1. You are adults-I will treat you like it.

2. Consequences are real-good and bad.

3. We do not come to understand gospel doctrines by reading and discussing alone-we must use them, apply them, go and do them.

4. "Understanding" is a gift from God-a process of revelation-not only intellectual.

5. Only you can decide to take responsibility for your higher education-to "act...and not be acted upon."

6. Do not assume that the only important learning activities are the ones that get graded.

7. Remember that the grades I assign do not reflect your spiritual stature or growth, but how well you learn the course materials and fulfill the course assignments. There is another, more important grade-between you and your Heavenly Father-that is recorded elsewhere.

8. If you believe you have a disability that may require reasonable accommodation on the part of BYU-Idaho, contact "Red" Taylor, Director, Learning Assistance Labs, 356-1158.

9. Remember it is a privilege not a right to attend BYU-Idaho. You are here in part because of the payments of the "widow's mite" from members from all over the world. Therefore, on your third unexcused absence I will subtract 100 points, and on your sixth you will automatically fail the class! Absences are excused ONLY for the following reasons: 1) an official BYU-Idaho outing (fieldtrip, etc.); 2) very serious illness; and 3) a death in the family. When at all possible, inform me you will miss class BEFORE time.

10. Heavenly Father does not grade on a curve and neither do I. As of day 1 you all have A's, and you will have to work very hard to show me you don't deserve it. I am on your side! Your grade will be based on the following criteria:
