SPORI PHOTO

In the early 1900's a historic photo was taken of students and faculty in front of what was then their administration building and later became known as the Jacob Spori Building.

At the Homecoming 2000 festivities, a replay of the scene developed and a photo was again taken in celebration of the heritage and strong foundation for education established nearly a century ago. President's Council gathered in the upper balcony while students, community friends, and alumni spread before the building in a grand tribute and photo-opportune moment. The historical moment was captured by the college photographer, Mike Lewis. Prints of the Spori Celebration photo are available through Alumni Relations for $5 each. Please call 1-800-537-2586 or e-mail alumni@ricks.edu to request your copy.

 

SEARCH BEGINS FOR NEW FACULTY MEMBERS FOR BYU-IDAHO

Ricks College has begun its search for 25 new faculty members as it gears up to offer new baccalaureate programs. The junior college is transitioning to become a four-year university to be known as Brigham Young University-Idaho and it plans to begin offering eight baccalaureate programs beginning in the fall of 2001. Another 13 positions need to be filled because of present faculty members who will be retiring or leaving, bringing the total new faculty hires to 38.

The faculty positions are in accounting, agronomy/ag business, animal science, art, biology, business management, communications, chemistry, economics, early childhood/special education, elementary education, English, foreign language (Spanish), geography, geology, history, instructional design, math learning assistance, math, music, nursing and recreation education.

Ricks will accept applications until Jan. 29. The hiring process will then continue until May 10 and the jobs will start Aug. 16 just prior to the opening of Fall Semester. Master's degrees are required and doctorates are preferred for these positions.

Beginning in 2001 the university anticipates offering upper-division classes leading to integrated baccalaureate degrees in accounting, English, history and recreation education, along with specialized degrees in business management, elementary education, interior design and nursing.

Additional faculty members will be hired during the next several years. It is estimated that approximately 20 new faculty members will be needed for the 2002-03 year, 5 for the 2003-04 year, 25 for the 2004-05 year, and 25 for the 2005-06 year. More information is available by calling the Faculty Hotline at (208) 356-1188 or on the Internet at www.edu/Ricks/Personnel.

 

NETWORK WILL BE SHUT DOWN

Ricks College's network will be shut down at 5 p.m. on Thursday, Dec. 21 and will continue to be down Dec. 22 until the work is complete. The goal is to correct problems that have bogged down the college's network system which has been operating on two separate cores. The modifications will move all processing to a Cisco network core. Information Technology hopes to complete their task sometime on Dec. 22. However, they are prepared to continue through the weekend if unforeseen problems prolong the project. While the changes are being made, a telephone hotline (208) 356-2060 will have a recorded message on the status of the network.

The shift to the network core will disrupt many of the computer oriented day-to-day operations on campus. Those trying to access services from off campus need to be aware of the situation as responses may be delayed due to the following:

Computers: The shutdown will have a mixed effect. Users with Windows 95 , will be unable to log on to their computers and thus have no access. Those with MAC or Windows NT and 2000 systems will be able to log in and work on files stored on their own local machines.

File Access: All users on campus will be unable to access on-line files (those files which are stored on shared servers).

Printers: Employees will not be able to operate their network printers (printers that are shared within offices or at the press).

E-mail: No campus access to e-mail.

Internet: No on-campus access to Internet. No off-campus access to the college's web site. Students will be unable to access their fall semester grades. Students will be unable to register for classes.

Phone Information System (208-356-1006): The phone information system whereby students access their grades will not be available until the project to shift the network core is completed.
