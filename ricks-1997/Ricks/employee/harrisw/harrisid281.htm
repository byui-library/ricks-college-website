COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 281
Title: Contemporary Furniture and Architecture Credit: 3
Text: Modern Furniture Classics, Stimpson

Course Objectives:
 1. To examine the cultural legacy of Western civilization in the area of contemporary architecture, interior furnishings, and decorative arts.
 2. To define specific styles of contemporary architecture, decorative motifs and interior furniture classics through lecture, reading, research and graphic illustration.
3. To provide the student with an understanding of major contemporary design theories.
4. To provide an opportunity for team approach to design research and visual display.
5. To further develop the student’s oral, written, presentation, and graphic skills.

Description:
A course consisting of lecture, viewing of slides and discussion related to the study of contemporary architecture, interior statements, and furnishings which reflect specific cultures and their time periods.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Completion of six major examinations.
2. Completion of an original illustrative book of contemporary design studies.
3. Research and presentation of a contemporary architect/designer, complemented by visuals and class handout.
4. Completion of a creative three-dimensional showcase display portraying a specific contemporary period.
