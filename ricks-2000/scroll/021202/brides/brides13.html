Set the wedding mood through announcements

by Sarah Lunt
LUN99013@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Among the many concerns that come with a wedding, one of the most pressing and important concerns is the official announcement. These publicize the couple’s engagement, let friends and family get a “sneak preview” of the lucky fiance and tell when and where the wedding will take place.
Because announcements give all these vital details, they must be taken care of as carefully as possible.
Two companies in the area that print announcements, Typewriter Ink and Printing and Fairy Tale Weddings, gave some tips on choosing the perfect announcement.

Jason Watson of Idaho Falls took over his family’s printing business, Typewriter Ink and Printing, about a year ago. As a student at BYU-Idaho, he is in touch with what engaged students want in an announcement.

Jennie Scott from Fairy Tale Weddings also has some ideas for couples.

Announcements that are “postcard” style, with the writing directly placed on the picture, are the most popular, Scott said.

This announcement tends to be cheaper and easier to mail because the couple needs only to place the announcement in the envelope and mail it, Watson said.

“They [the design mentioned] are the most popular because they are the most inexpensive [compared to the traditional card and picture announcement],” Watson said.

The previously mentioned style tends to run about $375 for 500 announcements, Watson said.

The traditional announcement, with the separate card and photograph, runs about the same price, although couples must pay the additional money to get the photographs reprinted, Watson said.

When selecting announcements, “look for something that is [you], that is [your] style,” Scott said.

Though some may suggest getting the photo reprinted to have more of the engagement photograph, the quality will not be as good as from a professional photographer, Scott said.

The most important thing in choosing announcements seems to be that the couple find a “happy medium” in price and style, so they will be able to look at their announcement years later with joy that is unaffected by the memory of large bills.
