COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 110
Title: Introductory Foods Credit: 2
Text: On Cooking by Labensky and Hause

Course Objective:
1.  Students will be exposed to a variety of foods and their preparation techniques.

2.  Students will prepare and evaluate standard products for the foods discussed.

Description:
This class is designed to help students develop skill in food preparation.  There is a lecture and a lab each week to assist students in acquiring accepted techniques in food preparation, safety, and presentation.  A variety of foods and techniques are covered throughout the semester.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Hands-on experience in the lab each week.

2.  There is a $30 fee required to cover the food expenses.
