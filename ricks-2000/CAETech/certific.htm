Certificate Programs Available

Certificate programs are designed to provide students with intensive hands-on training in a short period of time to prepare them for immediate employment in the job market.   The following is a brief description of certificate programs offered within the Department of Computer Aided Engineering Technologies.  For a full catalog description of each program and required courses, please follow the appropriate link.

Certificate Name Degree Program Description
Certificate in Welding Technology 1 year certificate This is a one year program designed for the student who wants to enter the welding industry well trained and ready for a challenging, rewarding career. This course is hands-on and labor intensive. You will receive instruction and training using the latest welding techniques on modern equipment. You will be trained in SMAW, GTAW, GMAW, and FCAW. Instruction also includes robotic welding, automatic thermal cutting, repair and maintenance welding.

For the student going on a mission, this may be the course for them. It can be completed in one year enabling them to leave on a mission with a certificate of graduation and return job ready.

Our goals include having you certified in pipe and plate and able to fit and weld in all positions.

This is a new program which will start in September and end in May. For more specific information, please contact the department head.
