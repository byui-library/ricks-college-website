Ricks College News Release, October 1, 1997

Ricks announces 1998-99 tuition fees

Students planning to attend Ricks College during the 1998- 99 school year will encounter a $35 per semester (3.6 percent) increase in tuition.

Beginning in the fall semester of 1998, tuition will raise from $975 to $1,010 per semester. Students who are not members of the LDS Church will pay $1,500 per semester, up from $1,450.

The college is operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Tuition pays slightly less than 30 percent of the actual operating costs of the college while the LDS Church funds the remainder, says James R. Smyth, administrative vice president at the two-year private college.
