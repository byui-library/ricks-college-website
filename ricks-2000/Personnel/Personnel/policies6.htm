Equipment and Supply Purchases (6-2)

I. PURPOSE

To establish guidelines for purchasing, coding, and accounting for capital equipment, operating equipment, and general supplies.

II. DEFINITIONS

A. Capital equipment is defined as equipment, other than computer related equipment, having a life expectancy exceeding one year and costing $2,500 or more per unit.

B. Operating equipment is defined as equipment, other than computer related equipment, having a life expectancy exceeding one year and costing between $100 and $2,500 per unit.

C. General supplies are defined as supplies and equipment having a life expectancy of one year.

III. POLICY

A. Capital and Operating Equipment

1. General

All new items of capital and operating equipment should be requested in the budget proposal for the new year by division and department heads. These will then be published on an "Approved Capital and Operating Equipment List." This list will be available for reference by division and department heads. Any additions, deletions, substitutions, or other changes to this list must be made according to the following procedures:

a. Additions

1) Items may be added to the approved equipment lists if sufficient funds are available. Capital equipment additions must be made on the "Capital Equipment Change" form. This form is available from the Budget Officer. It must be approved by the department head, division dean, and appropriate vice president. This form is processed by the Budget Officer.

2) Budgets from Personnel Services (i.e. faculty salaries, administrative salaries, staff salaries, etc.) may not be transferred to purchase equipment items.

b. Substitutions and/or deletions

Substitutions or deletions to the approved capital equipment list made by using the "Capital Equipment Change" form.

c. Additions

Substitutions and/or deletions to the approved  operating equipment list can be made by department heads or division deans.

2. Computer Equipment

The request to purchase computer equipment is made through the Director of Information Services.  Computer equipment is funded out of the ITI (Information Technology Infrastructure) funds.

B. Operating Equipment

Operating equipment should be coded on the purchasing document with a standard code within the equipment area provided by the Accounting Office. No special approval is necessary to purchase these items.

C. Supplies

1. Supply Code

All supply expenditures should be coded on the purchasing document with a supply code appropriate to the department.

2. Personal Purchases

Acquisition of any items through the school purchasing system for personal use will not be permitted. Such items as pictures, vases, clocks, pen sets, etc., which are not a permanent part of the decor and which would not remain in the event of an employee move, are considered personal.

3. Nonstandard Furniture and Accessories

Purchase of nonstandard furniture, equipment, and accessories such as refrigerators, vending machines, rugs, carpets, and drapes will require special approval by the Administrative Vice President.

Printing and Graphic Services Policy (6-3)

I. PURPOSE

To outline the University’s policy for responding to the printing needs of the campus.

II. POLICY

A. The BYU-Idaho Press and the Graphic Services Department exist to provide professional printing services for all campus departments and programs. In order to properly provide such service, all printing projects should be submitted to the University Press for an initial review.

B. All departments with printing needs have the right to seek a competitive bid through Central Purchasing. Printing projects with a cost estimate in excess of $500 must go through Central Purchasing for competitive bidding.

C. All printed information representing BYU-Idaho to the public shall be approved for content and design by Graphic Services.

D. All printing projects not done by the University Press must be submitted to purchasing with a purchasing requisition.

III. IMPLEMENTATION

A. Customer

It shall be the customer’s responsibility to provide a project description rough layout, photographs*, and a typewritten copy of the text to the University Press for the initial review. (*Note: Graphic Services may be able to assist with any photography needs.)

B. University Press

The University Press shall determine if the project cost estimate exceeds $500 and if it will require Graphic Services.

1. If the project requires editorial or design service, it shall be forwarded to the Graphics Services Department.

2. If the project does not require editorial or design service, or a competitive bid, the University Press will help the customer with the project’s requirements and with a production schedule designed to meet the customer’s needs most efficiently.

3. When the University Press is asked for a competitive bid, specifications shall be supplied by Central Purchasing.

C. Graphic Services

For projects requiring design or editorial assistance, Graphic Services shall prepare a mock up, including general layout and content, for the customer’s review. When the mock up is acceptable to the customer, it shall be reviewed by a printing control committee composed of the printing advisor, a purchasing representative, a graphic services representative, and the customer. After this meeting, the following steps shall occur:

1. Graphic Services submits the mock up to the University Press or Central Purchasing for a cost evaluation or a competitive bid.

2. Upon receipt of the total cost evaluation, the customer is notified and asked to authorize Graphic Services to prepare the project for camera-ready art. (If the cost is unacceptable, Graphic Services will attempt to redesign or modify to reduce costs, whereupon C-1 and C-2 will be repeated.)

3. The customer then proofs the camera-ready materials before it is sent by Graphic Services to the Press.

a. If any changes are needed, the customer is asked to re-check the final version before printing.

b. If such changes require additional costs, the customer must authorize before printing may proceed.

c. When a project is for the purpose of student recruitment, it must be reviewed by the Admissions Office prior to submission to the Press for printing. (Note: Any sharing of recruitment printing costs shall be negotiated between the Admissions Office and the department concerned.)

D. Central Purchasing

All printing projects whose cost estimates exceed $500 must be submitted to Central purchasing for competitive bidding. Central Purchasing is authorized to submit printing materials for bid from both the University Press and/or Graphic Services.

1. The customer may suggest printing firms for Central Purchasing to contact for competitive bids. In the absence of any recommendations Central Purchasing shall use its discretion in submitting printing projects to reliable firms for bid.

2. In all cases, Central Purchasing shall submit printing requests to the University Press for competitive bidding.

3. Customers shall normally accept low bids unless extenuating circumstances (i.e. timing, materials, etc.) are unacceptable for the project concerned.
