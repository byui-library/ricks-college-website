Unit #3 - CHEMICAL EQUILIBRIUM

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Explain the notion of activity to a non-chemist, and specify (for a given solid, liquid, gas, or solute) which numerical quantity (molar concentration, partial pressure, or unity) can be substituted for activity.
2. Write the equilibrium constant expression for a given equation in terms of activities or their substitutes.
3. Calculate, for a particular reaction, any one of the following, given the others: the equilibrium constant, the activity of each reactant, or the activity of each product.
4. Calculate Kp from Kc and vice versa.
5. Describe the effects on a given reaction and its equilibrium constant of changes in (a) temperature (given the sign of DH), (b) the amounts of reactants and products, and/or (c) the total pressure or volume.
6. Predict quantitatively the extent of a reaction, given its equilibrium constant and the initial conditions.
7. Interconvert the equilibrium constant and the Gibbs free energy for a given reaction.
8. Evaluate equilibrium constants at temperatures other than 298K.
