The A Student - Excellence

1. Few absences, virtually no tardies.
2. Alert during class; responds to comments given to class as a whole as well as to comments given individually.
3. Performs work with good alignment. (While no one is perfect and we all are working on alignment, the important thing is that a dancer is always becoming more aware and improving.)
4. Is observant and picks up on movement nuances which cannot be explained or defined.
5. Creative assignments are well prepared and well performed; the movement is unique and original, yet it answers the problem given.
6. Assignments are completed on time.
7. The student takes risks and learns to extend self in movement improvisation.
8. Written work is succinct and organized; journals are kept up daily and not thrown together at end of semester.
9. Motivates self; practices and searches out movement when there are no ready answers; evaluates self and takes responsibility to make improvement where needed.

The B Student - Good and Commendable

1. Absences and tardies may take toll on understanding and performing with depth of dance material given.
2. Usually attentive during class; responds to most comments given to class, responds to most comments given individually.
3. Has some minor alignment problems that do not seem to be improving.
4. Is usually observant but misses subtleties in phrasing and nuances.
5. Creative assignments are essentially well prepared, but the student may have a problem of forgetting due to lack of rehearsal; the movement answers the problem but not as fresh and original as it should be, or movement is exciting and unique but does not answer the problem given.
6. Assignments are usually on time.
7. The student does not completely involve self in improvisation.
8. Written work is neat but lacks depth, or it is messy though it includes some good ideas; journal thrown together in spurts rather than recorded consistently.
9. Relies too much on teacher for feedback; does not make personal decisions as to what to work on.

The C Student - Average

1. Absences and tardies are such that the student’s work is adequate but probably missing energy, clarity, excitement.
2. Phases out now and then, is weary and sometimes inattentive; responds adequately to comments given, but movement remains in the "adequate" level and does not extend to the "exciting and memorable" level.
3. Has some alignment problems that do not seem to be improving.
4. Is observant enough to meet basic movement criteria, but again, lacks excitement that comes from probing, searching, and exploring the undefinable.
5. Creative assignments are prepared and complete but do not "shed new light" on the problem given.
