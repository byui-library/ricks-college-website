Beginning Chinese Reading and Writing
BEG RDG WRTG
CLASS CODE: CHIN 112 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Reading and basic composition using Chinese characters.
DESCRIPTION: A Chinese course for students with at least one year of spoken Mandrin skills, such as Mandarin-speaking returned missionaries. This course emphasizes reading and writing.
TOPICS: Reading, writing and grammar covered in Chinese 101 and 102.
OBJECTIVES: Students will bring reading and writing skills up to a level commensurate with their oral language skills.
REQUIREMENTS: Completion of all homework assignements, quizzes, exams.
PREREQUISITES: Equivalent of at least one year spoken Mandarin skills.
