Browser Cache configuration

  • The web pages used for student access are dynamic. Sometimes browsers are configured to remember pages to speed up access. This works fine for static or unchangeable pages, but not for dynamic pages that are computer generated. To change your settings to use our student web access system the following instructions may be helpful.
  • Netscape Settings- To change your settings to work with our web pages click on "Edit" and then "Preferences". Then click on the + sign next to "Advanced". Next click on "Cache". Under "Document in cache is compared to document on network" check "Every time". Then click on OK to save your settings.
  • Internet Explorer Settings- To change your settings to work with our web pages click on "View" and then "Options". Then click on the "Advanced" Tab. In the middle there is a section on temporary internet files. Click on "Settings". Under "Check for newer versions of stored pages" check "Every visit to page." Click on OK to save your settings.
  • Ricks College uses a secured server (SSL) for applications. If your server does not allow SSL access you will get an error telling you so. You will need to use the unsecured access method if this occurs, however your data will be unprotected as is travels the internet. To register without secured access go to UNSECURED Access
