Showtime Company

About the Group:

The summer tour 2000 was the final episode of Showtime Company.  Beginning with the fall semester, a new program was introduced under the direction of the Music Department and the Division of Performing and Fine Arts. The new tour program will combine the talents of Vocal Union and  Sound Alliance.
