Who are my advisees?

     I advise three different groups of students: 2-year IS majors (244), 4-year IS majors (680), and General Business (297) majors. To assist you in selecting the classes that you need to take to graduate I have advising sheets for each of these majors in PDF format so that you can print them. To view these sheets you need Acrobat Reader which can be obtained free from Adobe. Click the appropriate link that pertains to you:

  • 2-year (A.A.S.) IS Planning Sheet
  • 4-year (B.S.) IS Planning Sheet
  • 2-year General Business Planning Sheet
 
How often do I visit with my advisees?
     There are at least 2 meeting times during the year that we must meet face-to-face: October and March. During these months the school has an advising week (check the Scroll and your mail for actual dates). During these weeks all students are to meet with their advisor. During the meeting we review your academic plan which includes the length of time you plan to attend BYU-Idaho, your graduation and transfer plans (if in an A.A.S. program) and the schools you are considering for transfer (if any). In addition, we can discuss classes that you need for the following semester. Attendance at these meetings is mandatory. A student who does not attend this meeting cannot register for courses the following semester. Filling out one of the planning sheets (available above) will greatly help with your planning of what courses to take.
     Beyond these meetings we can meet whenever you have a question. If you want or need to, you can also call me at (208) 496-1356 or email me at robertsonb@byui.edu. You will also need to meet with me after you have applied for graduation to cover your application and identify any classes that need to be taken prior to graduating.

How do I update my Educational Plan?

     During the October and March Advising weeks, all students (except those who have applied for graduation) need to update the Educational Plan. Simply drop by my office (Smith 410) and we can take care of it in a few minutes.

What if I decide to change my major?
     To change your major is relatively simple, but should not be done on a spur of the moment decision. To change your major go to the Career Advising Center, Room 250 in the Kimball Building. Tell them you want to change your major and what you want to change it to. They will make the change and assign you to an advisor in the new major. Make sure you go and visit with your new advisor.
However, make sure that your new major is one that you have considered carefully. If you have questions about a major visit with a faculty member in that area. Ask questions! Remember that each area has specific requirements. If you change your major too often you lessen your chances to actually graduate and it will cost extra tuition and fees (not to mention housing and living expenses) for every extra semester you stay at BYU-Idaho.
