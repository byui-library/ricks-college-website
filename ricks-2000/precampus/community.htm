Eastern Idaho

Things to Do

BYU-Idaho, nestled in the community of Rexburg, Idaho, is close to many outdoor recreational areas including West Yellowstone, Grand Targhee ski resort, Jackson Hole, Wyoming, and Island Park, Idaho.  Neighboring Idaho Falls (30 minutes south of Rexburg) is also home to the Idaho Falls Temple.


There is much to do in this region, especially if you are an outdoor enthusiast.
