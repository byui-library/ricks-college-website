Elder Russell M. Nelson: Build a firm foundation in the Church, as individuals

by Melissa Merrill
MER00006@BYUI.EDU
Religion editor

ELDER NELSON
Elder Russell M. Nelson of the Quorum of the Twelve encouraged Latter-day Saints to secure their foundations in the gospel.

He demonstrated the importance of having a solid foundation through the construction of the Mexico City Temple. Because the city is situated on a basin over water, some buildings settle and become tilted with time.

“[To combat this problem in building the temple] 221 large reinforced concrete piles were driven more than 100 feet deep into the ground. Onto those pilings, steel straps were anchored and fastened to a unit that can be adjusted if need be to keep the building level. With this unseen but sure foundation, that temple today stands steady and straight,” Elder Nelson said.

He then listed and expounded upon several “premortal pillars” that undergird the Church’s foundation:

1. The plan of redemption and possibility of an inheritance in the kingdom of God

2. The Atonement of Jesus Christ

3. Opposition from the adversary

4. Foreordination for and conferral of the Priesthood

5. Sisters empowered to glorify God

6. Blessings predicated to obedience to the law

7. Sacred things to be revealed in the latter days

8. The redemption of the dead

Elder Nelson proceeded to explain principles that secure the foundations of individual members of the Church.

“We can waffle ‘like the wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed’ (James 1:6), or we can stand on a firm foundation and anchor ourselves with straps of spiritual steel, rooted and grounded to the timeless pillars of the gospel,” Elder Nelson said.
