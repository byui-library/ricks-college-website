CAD 238
Menu & Toolbar Creation

 

"The test, then, of our soul's greatness is rather to be sought in our ability to comfort and console, our ability to help others, rather than in our ability to help ourselves and crowd others down in the struggle of life.", Joseph Fielding Smith.


Create a menu for AutoCAD which includes the following sections as described:


BUTTONS/AUX

Design to support both 2 and 3 button mouse. Right button should be a carriage return. Middle button on 3 button mouse and shift+right button on 2 button mouse should display POP0.

POP0 Use for object snap menu. Include endpoint, intersection, center, and 1 object snap of your choice.
POP1 Label as Draw. Include line->, circle, circle TTR and 2 construction commands of your choice. The line entry should be a cascading menu which includes line, line endpoint endpoint (closed), line qua qua line qua qua (for centerline creation).
POP2 Label as Edit. Include move, move endpoint endpoint, move relative, repeating fillet, and 2 editing commands of your choice.
POP3 Label as Display. Include zoom, regen, rtpan, and 2 display commands of your choice.
POP4 Label as 3D. Include 3D dynamic view, automatic setup of front/top/side/iso views, vports single, vports retrieve, and 1 3D command of your choice.


ACCELERATORS

Include accelerator keys for undo, file save, zoom ext zoom .95x, and 2 commands of your choice.


TOOLBARS

Create a custom toolbar named DT238 which includes the following commands/macros:
line erase zoom endpoint
circle copy zoom w center
circle diameter rotate zoom - intersection
circle ttr stretch zoom ext
dtext trim zoom ext zoom .95x
dtext ht .125, ang 0 extend zoom previous
offset rt pan
fillet
fillet rad 0

If you add flyouts or modify icons, make certain the appropriate files are included when handing in your assignment. You may add additional buttons if space permits, but all buttons should be visible when displayed at a resolution of 640x480 and docked in a horizontal position.

All commands should automatically cancel any active commands where appropriate (may not want to cancel when using display commands)
