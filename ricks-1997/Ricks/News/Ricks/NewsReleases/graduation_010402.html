Ricks College News Release
April 2, 2001 

Ricks College's last April graduation scheduled for April 28

REXBURG, Idaho — Nearly 2,900 students will receive diplomas from Ricks College during the school’s 112th graduation Saturday, April 28.

This will be the last April commencement before the school becomes Brigham Young University–Idaho later in the year.

Elder Russell M. Nelson of the Quorum of the Twelve of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints will speak at the commencement exercises at 9 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium.

Commencement will be preceded by the traditional academic march at 8:40 a.m. by faculty and graduates. Diplomas will be handed out at the various division convocations, all scheduled for 11:30 a.m. at various locations.

Graduation activities begin Thursday with the paramedic graduates pinning ceremony at 2 p.m. in the Manwaring Center Little Theater.

Friday will include a banquet for all graduates and their parents at 5:30 p.m. in the Manwaring Center Ballroom, the nurses pinning in the Taylor Chapel at 7:30 p.m., an American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band performance at 8 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium, and a sacred music performance of "The Passion and the Promise of Our Lord Jesus Christ" by combined choirs and symphony orchestra at 8 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall. The night will conclude with a Graduation Ball in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom from 9 p.m. to midnight.

Saturday will begin with an Emeritus Club breakfast at 7 a.m. in the Manwaring Center for alumni from 1951 or earlier and retired Ricks employees.

Ricks College, with an enrollment of approximately 9,000 students, is affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
