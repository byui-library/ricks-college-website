British Literature - Medieval and Renaissance
BRITLIT/MD&RN
CLASS CODE: ENG 331 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Examines the influence of historical events, philosophical ideas, and literary trends on works and authors of the Medieval and Renaissance periods from Beowulf to Milton.
DESCRIPTION: Examines the influence of historical events, philsophical ideas, and literary trends on works and authors of the Medieval and Renaissance periods from Beowulf to Milton.
TOPICS: Development of the Medieval and Renaissance periods, including its major movements, genres, and authors; placement of this period literature in a relevant cultural and historical context; interrelations between these two historical periods and types of literature; usage of the fundamental tools of literary interpretation in the discussion, analysis, and evaluation of literature of these periods; usage of a variety of critical and theoretical approaches to the literature of these periods; broad context for further study and specialization.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Studying, analyzing, and critically evaluating works, distinctive characteristics and themes within the Medieval and Renaissance periods. (2) Knowing and using the fundamental tools and vocabulary of literature study (i.e., symbolic language, characterization, conflict, theme, point of view, form and style) in the discussion, analysis, and evaluation of literature of these periods. (3) Identifying (both orally and in writing) the interrelationships of works and ideas within and across these two periods. (4) Using various critical approaches (as introduced in English 251 and English 314) to appreciate and analyze works from different genres and periods. (5) Effectively researching and discussing works of this period using reliable sources and MLA style.
REQUIREMENTS: Research and discuss works of the Medieval and Renaissance periods using reliable sources and MLA style. Extensive reading, discussion and oral presentations, interpretive, analytical and/or critical evaluation papers and journals.
PREREQUISITES: Completion of English 251. Concurrent registration with English 252 is permissible.
