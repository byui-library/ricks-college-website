Ricks College News Release
April 2, 2001 

Devotional: Bryan Weston

REXBURG, Idaho — Bryan Weston spoke on "the Lord making bare his arm" Tuesday at the Ricks College weekly devotional.

Weston, an administrator in the Church Educational System of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, started his talk by quoting a scripture in the Book of Mormon (1 Nephi 22: 10-12) about the Lord making bare his arm among the nations of the earth. From these passages, he addressed three areas in which the "Lord would make bare his arm" in personal lives as well as the nations of the world.

His first area centered around the serving of missions for the church. "The Lord makes bare his arm in the calling of missionaries (to certain places) or in the blessings that come after," Weston said. "The Lord’s blessings are evident in so many ways."

The second area concentrated on the seeking of a solid education. "Getting an education can be a marvelous blessing," he said. "I have seen the arm of the Lord manifest in the educational experiences of students many times. If you are diligent and work hard, he will bless you."

In his final point, Weston talked about making preparations for going to the temple and raising a family in today’s world.

"The Lord will bless you as you move ahead (with getting married and raising a family)," he said. "The love and the blessings of the Lord will help you to make it as a family."

In conclusion, Weston said, "The Lord will make bare his arm and establish his purposes and work in the nations of the world. I am convinced that he will do this in your behalf as long as we listen to the spirit and keep the commandments and do our part."

Next Tuesday’s speaker will be E. Dale LeBaron, a BYU Church History and Doctrine faculty member. He will speak at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Devotionals are broadcast live and repeated at 9 p.m. on KRIC-FM.
