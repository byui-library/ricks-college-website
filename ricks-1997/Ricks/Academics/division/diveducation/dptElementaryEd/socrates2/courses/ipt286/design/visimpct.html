How We Learn

Student ability to recall information from a lesson given three day ago, when:

Verbal teaching used alone 10%

Visual teaching used alone 20%

Visual and verbal teaching used together 65%

Time Required to Teach

The time required to present a concept can be reduced by 40% when visual aids accompany a verbal presentation.


Research Findings

Studies show that using visual aids in a presentation:

  • Makes your presentation 43% more persuasive
  • Shortens meeting times by 28%
  • Makes your audience perceive you as being more professional and better prepared
  • Aids in helping participants understand complex or detailed information
  • Speeds along group decisions
