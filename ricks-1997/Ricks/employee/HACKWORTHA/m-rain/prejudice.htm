Field of Green
                             audio
Why does it happen?
I certainly didn't want it.
Against my will
Black oil spilled,
Seeped on my field of green.

The caustic crude
Turned a verdant field
Into rude wilting grass.

I desired a higher harvest,
Fresh-water splashes of green,
Plashes of growing goodwill.

One day in Jerusalem,
I wove my way through a forest,
A thousand tourists
Who found their way to the Wailing Wall,
A reverent spot where a lot of men
Recall ancient prayers.

A million scribbled invocations,
Crushed into cracks,
Enriched the weathered rocks.

Jews galore, facing the wall,
Let their heads fall,
Up and down,
Bobbing incessantly
To their rhythmic prayers.

Then without warning
A man appeared.
Starring benignly, he asked,
“May I pray for you?”

Being touch by his creamy kindness,
I quickly said,  “Of course.”

He began,
And I was thrilled by his milk:
The Hebrew sounds that wound
Their succulent way
Across his honeyed tongue.

He finished his abundant prayer.
Then again without warning,
To make his point he thrust his hand
Like a knife to my belly, and said,
“Pay me.”

I would not.

Most religious people,
Motivated by concern for others,
Offer generous prayers daily.
A prayer for me is always free.

He repeated again --
Again stabbing like a piston,
“Pay me!”

I would not.
He insisted
Again and again,
Stabbing, stabbing,
“Pay me!
Pay me!
Pay me!”

I wanted to spit in his face,
And I thought of a chosen race,
Who in temple space,
Two thousand years ago,
Merchandised religion.

Another time at a Russian airport,
When the lines were long
And waiting seemed
The wrong thing to do,
A family, (the father Hasidic),
Forced its way to the front
And demanded service.

Again, dark oil surfaced.
I wanted to hit the man
Right where his curly, hair locks began.

Time passed.
Now I clean less pollution
From my field of green.
