 DESCRIPTION:

   A theoretical, academically oriented course focusing on the physical, cognitive, emotional, and social development of the child from conception through adolescence. Influences of family, peers, and social institutions on the child's development will be discussed.

  Proclamation on the Family   The Proclamation on the Family was given through the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve Apostles. It is revelation for our times. There are nine paragraphs in the document. You will be required to: 1) select one quotation from each of the nine sections that seem to be particularly important statements about the nature of man and families; 2) find two quotations from A child's World which either support or contradict each of the statements you have selected; 3) after reviewing what you have discovered, respond to this question-- "Do the views of modern social scientists agree or disagree with the Lord's "Proclamation on the Family"? Your response should be about 500 words, two pages typed and double spaced. As you share your impressions, please include some of the evidence that led you to your conclusion. In evaluating this assignment, I will be looking for: 1) meaningful quotations from the Proclamation and A Child's World (50%); and 2) focus, evidence, coherence and mechanics in the two-page response (50%).
