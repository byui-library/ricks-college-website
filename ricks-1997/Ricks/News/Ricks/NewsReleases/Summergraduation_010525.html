Ricks College News Release
May 25, 2001

Thomas E. Ricks descendants
invited to final Ricks graduation

REXBURG — Descendants of Rexburg pioneer Thomas E. Ricks are invited to attend the final commencement ceremony at Ricks College before it becomes Brigham Young University–Idaho.

Summer graduation will be Wednesday, June 6, at 10 a.m. with the academic march beginning at 9:50 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Tickets are not required and family members will be seated in a special reserved area. A reception for family members will follow.

Thomas E. Ricks, who lived from 1828 to 1901, was a polygamist with five wives, 43 children and 238 grandchildren (ten grandchildren are still living). There are about 775 known great-grandchildren and it’s estimated there are as many as 10,000 descendants.

He was the founder of a Latter-day Saint settlement in 1883 that was named Rexburg and the stake president when Bannock Stake Academy was founded in 1888. In 1902, the academy was officially named after Ricks who had died a year earlier. 

“Thomas E. Ricks was a pioneer who helped to build a foundation that has lasted for many generations,” Ricks President David A. Bednar says. “I’m sure that he and the other individuals who were instrumental in establishing Bannock Academy would be pleased and gratified to see how this institution has blessed scores of thousands of young people from all over the world.”

A new academic building that will be constructed in the near future will bear the name of Thomas E. Ricks. The college also will create theatrical and musical remembrances of Thomas E. Ricks and the history and spirit of Ricks College.

President Bednar says, “We will continue to have and refer to the ‘Spirit of Ricks’ at Brigham Young University-Idaho.” He describes the “Spirit of Ricks” as the supportive, caring and spiritual atmosphere of the institution.

According to Ricks Registrar Kevin Miyasaki, 623 students have applied to graduate. Statistics show there are 416 females, 207 males, 89 married students, 7 married couples and 21 foreign students. There are 216 graduates from Idaho, 116 from Utah, 61 from California and 43 from Washington. 

The traditional summer commencement follows the first five-week summer term that ends June 5 and includes students who will be completing graduation requirements during any one of the three summer terms.

A graduation breakfast for graduates and their parents and friends will be served from 7:30 to 9 a.m. in the Manwaring Center prior to graduation. The breakfast is free for graduates and $2 for family and friends. Tickets for the breakfast are required and available from the Ticket Office weekdays from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m.
