Ricks College News Release
May 2, 2001

Ricks College Education Week
set for early June in Rexburg 

REXBURG, Idaho — More than 60 speakers and 270 classes will highlight the fourth annual Education Week at Ricks College set for June 6-9.

The three-day even is expected to draw nearly 3,000 people to the Rexburg campus to participate in classes focusing on such topics as the gospel, family, self improvement, homemaking and fine arts.

Although most classes are aimed at adults, specific classes are held for youths between the ages of 14 and 18. 

Vickey Pahnke will be the keynote speaker at a pre-conference session  Wednesday, June 6, at 6 p.m. in the Taylor Chapel. Her talk will be centered on the theme of Education Week: “What Is Man that Art Mindful of Him.”

A concert featuring Michael McLean will be held on Thursday, June 7, in the Hart Auditorium at 7:30 p.m. and is open to the community.

Two special evening events will be held for those who are registered for Education Week. The musical group Heartbound will perform on Thursday at 7 p.m. in the Snow Recital Hall, and Marvin Goldstein, Janice Kapp Perry and Johanne Freschette Perry will unite in a musical journey on Friday at 7 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall.

“I’d like to extend an invitation to the entire community, regardless of religious affiliation,” says Kristen Foster, who coordinates the annual event. “There are many classes and events that will help families and enrich personal lives.” 

Applications are currently being taken for the event through the Division of Continuing Education and are due by May 29 for mail-in, fax and online applications. In-person registration deadline is June 1 and needs to be turned in to the Division of Continuing Education at the Auxiliary Services Building on campus or at the BYU/Ricks Center in Idaho Falls. At-the-door registration will be available. Cost is $38 for the full three days or $16 for one-day or evening passes. 

Each morning throughout the event, a free devotional that is open to the public will be held at 9:45 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Speakers will be Ricks President David Bednar and his wife, Susan Bednar, June 7; Elder Dallin H. Oaks, a member of the Quorum of the Twelve of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, June 8; and Elder F. Enzio Busche, an emeritus member of the church’s Quorum of the Seventy, June 9.

Any questions can be directed to the Continuing Education Department at 356-1040. Information can be accessed on the Internet at www.byui.edu/educationweek.
