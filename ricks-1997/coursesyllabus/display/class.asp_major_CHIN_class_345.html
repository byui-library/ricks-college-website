Chinese Culture
CHIN CULTURE
CLASS CODE: CHIN 345 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An introduction to Chinese culture, ancient and modern. All readings and instruction in English. Cross-listed as Anthropology 345.
Offered Fall.
DESCRIPTION: Cross listed as Anth 345. An examination of Chinese culture from ancient to modern times.
TOPICS: Topics vary, but may include: geography, history, politics, family, social issues, religion, language, literature, thought
OBJECTIVES: A better understanding of China and her people, and a desire to study China in greater depth, either formally or informally, in any applicable discipline.
