MAKE A GIFT

How to Join with Us in this Great Cause.

We invite you to talk about your charitable desires with us. We will be happy to discuss the methods, joys, and rewards of giving to help Ricks College succeed.

  • Free Gift Planning Services Are Provided. The Ricks College Development Office has assembled a staff of professionals to help satisfy your desires to make a voluntary gift. We will also assist your attorneys, accountants, financial planners, and other professionals in developing a proposed gift. These wide range of services are provided without cost.
  • Strict Confidentiality Is Honored. When you make a contribution to Ricks College you can be certain that all conversations, transactions, and dealings are held in the strictest confidence.
  • One Hundred Percent of Your Gift Goes Directly to Ricks College. Your entire gift goes to the priority or program of your choice. None of it is used for salary, administrative, or other costs. By sharing of your means to advance Ricks College educational programs, you are able to help bless the lives of students and faculty who, in return, will bless others.
  • A Restricted gift is one that you may direct to a specific program or priority of your choice, knowing exactly how and where it will be used.
  • An Unrestricted gift is given to Ricks College without specific designation, placing your confidence in the President and administration to use the money where the need is greatest.
  • An Endowed gift is desireable for those who seek a more permanent and perpetual funding source to create an endowment in a particular department and named in memory of a loved one.

To make a contribution or pledge, please contact us at Rcfriends@ricks.edu or call 208-356-1128 or 800-227-4257.
