Course Description: This course is designed to meet the general chemistry requirements in technical fields such as engineering, life sciences, agriculture, pre-professional, etc. The course will provide students with technical knowledge and background that will help prepare them for a variety of classes in chemistry and other sciences. The class will also provide a lab experience that will give hands on experience that will allow students to see and understand the true nature of chemistry.

LAB & LECTURE POLICIES

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES






Students are expected to be in class or lab (on time) when class or lab is in session.

Excess unexcused absences or tardies may result in a grade cut or a loss of points.

Students that have these problems must meet with the instructor and work out a solution to prevent the loss of points or a grade cut.
 

All assigned labs must be completed when scheduled andturned in on time.

Role is taken at the start of each lab.

Those absent will be given a grade of -25 for that lab.

Those who are late must notify the lab assistant so that the absence can be changed to a tardy.

Students have four days to complete and turn in each lab report.

Late labs will not receive full credit.

A -25 will be recorded if labs are over two weeks late.
 

When a lab is missed, that lab must be made up as soon as possible.

The lab should be made up the same week that is was missed because the chemicals will still be out.

A make up slip must be completed by the student and signed by the instructor that is in charge of the make up lab.

The make up slip must be stapled to the back of the lab report and turned in at the regular time.

If the completed make up slip is stapled to the lab report, the -25 that was recorded will be replaced with the grade that is given on the lab report.
 

Quizzes will be given periodically during class time.

The three lowest quizzes will be dropped at the end of the semester.

If a student misses a quiz, that quiz will be counted as one of the quizzes that is dropped.

Missed quizzes can not be made up at a later date.

Quizzes are normally announced before they are given, however, there may be a few that are not announced.
