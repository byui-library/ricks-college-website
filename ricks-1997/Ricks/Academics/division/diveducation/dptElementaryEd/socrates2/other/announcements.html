Beginning Winter Semester 2000 no portion of the PPST masy be waived for BYU. All students must have completed all sections of the PPST and passed them to gain admittance to the education program at BYU.

Beginning Winter Semester 2000 the BYU EEE packet to be submitted differs from the current packet. All current packet materials will not be accepted. Check with the ELED Department office after approximately March 10th to pick up new packets to be used for BYU's admissions to the ELED program.

Summer 1999 ElEd 310R

Due to program changes, there will be NO sections of 310R offered during the summer of 1999. Plan to register for this course during the regular academic semesters of 1999.

Fine Arts 100

Anyone who is enrolled in FA 100 must finish the events before the semester ends in December. Events do not carry over to next semester. If you do not finish by December, you will have to start over attending all seven during winter semester of 1999. There are still enough events left for you to attend the required seven, but if you procrastinate much longer, the opportunity will be gone.

Utah State University Update

Elementary Dance 326 and PE will no longer fill the Utah State PE requirement.

Brigham Young University

Beginning in August 1999 all students must complete the Foreign Language, Statistic or Calculus requirement to graduate. The Math for the Elementary Teacher sequence (Math 205/206 or 305/306) will no longer meet the university requirement.
