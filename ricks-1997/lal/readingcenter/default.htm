Mission Statement

                               The Mission of the BYU-Idaho Reading Center is to:

1. Provide placement, diagnostic testing, and advising.

2. Improve basic reading skills through individual tutoring.

3. Teach college reading skills and learning strategies through classes and individual tutoring.

4. Maintain an environment where students feel comfortable   acquiring and practicing college reading skills and strategies.

5. Train college reading tutors.

6. Support the BYU-Idaho and Administration. 

Overview  The Reading Center helps students with basic reading skills and skills to study college textbooks. Each student receives individual diagnosis, advising, and specific instruction. Most instruction is one-on-one with a tutor, supplemented by small group meetings and regular class work. Students may choose to register for credit or to work without credit. 
Skills  Some of the topics for study are phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, grammar, critical thinking, reading rate, and learning strategies. Reading classes are listed under English in the catalog.
Reading Center Courses English 100, Reading Comprehension: emphasizes essential skills, i.e. phonics, vocabulary, comprehension, and fluency in oral reading. 2..0 credits.

English 101, Reading and Learning Strategies - An individual tutorial for college textbook reading skills, i.e. vocabulary, rate, grammar, spelling, and learning strategies (marking, flashcards, textbook notetaking, mapping, and summarizing). .5 - 4 (Repeatable credit, may be taken subsequent semesters, 4.0 credits maximum).

English 107, College Reading: emphasizes the reading skills necessary to learn from college textbooks in the natural sciences, social sciences, humanities, and religion.

English 108, English Language Development - Oral: emphasizes improving speaking, listening, reading, and writing skills of non-native English speaking students with an emphasis on the oral.

English 109, English Language Development - Written: a course for non-native speakers of English to help improve college-level English capabilities with an emphasis on oral as well as written discourses.
