Ricks Issues - BYU-Idaho Transition

KRIC Radio Report, October 2000

The following was prepared by Mark Bailey, KRIC Radio news director. It includes sound bites that you can click on and hear audio recordings:

No proposals have been approved yet as Ricks College continues to make a transition from a junior college to a university.  School president David Bednar reiterated that at a recent question-and-answer session with faculty and staff.  Since August, deans and department heads have been proposing ideas on developing bachelor degree programs.  That process is continuing, and the transition won't be done even when the prospectus is written.  That will be on-going for a number of months. 

The question of duplication was asked, and that is part of the process of developing programs at BYU-Idaho.  There are excellent programs being offered at BYU-Provo and BYU-Hawaii where it is even hard for some students to get into. Duplication at Idaho may be needed because students would be able to graduate and find jobs.  What BYU-Idaho does not want to replicate is where programs are under-enrolled at one campus or the other, or at all three locations.  Consolidation of programs is also being looked at and discussed as part of the transition.  President Bednar was asked when will initial announcements be made on what majors will be brought on line for bachelor degrees.  

[Click here to listen]
"We have no deadline that says we are going to do it November 15.  What people really need to get a sense of is that there are a lot of people who will put eyes on what is being talked about.  This is not a decision made in isolation in Rexburg, Idaho.  We will do our best work with everybody on campus.  Then it will be coordinated with folks in Provo, and we will have ideas and suggestions with them.  Then the Commissioner [of Education] will put his eyes on it.  Then the Executive Committee will put its eyes on it.  Then the Board will put its eyes on it.  Now we are not going to tell them when the announcement is going to be.  We may get to a point where they say, 'You know, let's go back; and let's re-examine this; and let's take some time to do it.'" 

He says the first stages MIGHT be announced at the end of the year.  But again, the process of developing bachelor degree programs is going to take time.
