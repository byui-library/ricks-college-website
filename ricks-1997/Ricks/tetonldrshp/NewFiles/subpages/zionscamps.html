Zion’s Camp

Zion’s Camps are designed to teach principles and skills of teambuilding, cooperation, and service and give students a chance to apply their learning by performing a meaningful service project.

A Zion’s Camp begins with an overnight retreat at the Teton Lodge. We leave campus on Friday at 5:00 p.m. and return around 1:00 p.m. Saturday. Participants go through a series of ice-breaker and team-building activities from which they learn the importance of working together in accomplishing a task. Students then apply their newly acquired skills by planning and organizing a service project to be performed the following week. During the week, students meet in committees to follow through with their assignments in preparation for the service project.

Registration

You can sign up for a Zion’s Camp in person at our office in the Manwaring Center, room 380, starting two weeks before the scheduled date. Registration is limited and is handled on a first-come, first-served basis. The cost is $3, which includes transportation, dinner, and breakfast.
