COACH JERRY SCHELGELMILCH
            Jerry Schlegelmilch arrived at Ricks College in 1990 as the new head baseball coach.  He faced the challenge of building a successful program in a hostile weather environment.  It didn't take long.  In 1992, the Vikings posted a 21-21 record, relying on a couple of athletes that were the envy of many coaches, Kyle Anderson and Garrett Stephenson.  Stephenson became an 18th round draft choice of the Baltimore Orioles.  He remains in the major leagues as a starting pitcher with the Philadelphia Phillies.

            The following year, the Vikings won over thirty games, going 31-18.  The club finished second in the Northern Division and fourth in Region 18.  Out of that group came Chris McInness, a ninth round draft choice of the Milwaukee Brewers.  The winning continued in 1994, then reached a new high in 1995 when Ricks College won the Northern Division of the SWAC and hosted the Region 18 tournament.  Ricks finished second in the tournament, but firmly established themselves as a program to be reckoned with in the SWAC.  The tradition continues under Coach Schlegelmilch, using Ricks College's indoor fieldhouse as his off-season training facility.  The Vikings will move to a brand new baseball facility in the fall of 1998.

            "Schleg" is a graduate of Whittier High School, in Whittier, California.  He was an All-Confernce player on the school's baseball team.  He moved on to Cal-Poly University where he was a three year letterman and an All- Player in 1967.  After graduating in 1968, Jerry signed a contract with the Minesota twins in 1969.  Schleg spent five years in professional baseball and was named to the Carolina League All-Star Team in 1971.

            After his professional playing career ended, Jerry began his coaching career at Rio Hondo College in California.  He was threre from 1973 to 1990.  He spent a year at BYU as an assistant coach in 1982, before coming to Ricks College.

            Jerry and his wife Donna are the parents of seven children.
