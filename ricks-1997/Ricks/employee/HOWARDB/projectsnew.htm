Extra Credit Projects

You can earn a total of 30 extra credit points for the term by completing any two of the following projects.



1. Compile a personal Top 25 listing of excellent internet sites. You may want to include sites you use for school, to find out about your potential career, mass media sites, or sites that you just think are cool. Cite the URL (web address) and provide a brief summary (short paragraph) of what content is available and why you like the site. 15 pts.



2. Create your own website. Include information about yourself and at least five hyperlinks to sites that interest you. 15 pts.

3. Keep an accurate journal of all your media use (radio, tv, magazines, newspapers) for seven consecutive days. Turn in journal and a one-page summary of your reaction to what you discovered. 15pts.



4. Attending a speech, seminar or symposium related to the mass media, or watching a relevant TV or video presentation. Submit a one-page critical analysis of the activity. Opportunities for extra credit activities may be presented to you in class, or you can propose your own ideas to the instructor. 15 pts



5. Writing short "thought" papers on media topics of your choice. The only standard is that you must show original thought or analyze a current mass media challenge, issue or trend. This can be as simple as a response to a story you read, a show you watch or a song you listen to. 15pts.
