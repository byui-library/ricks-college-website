Sermon Summary
 
REMEMBER:
    *The power of a sermon is in it's setting, it's outcome, and it's content.
    *Every sermon in scripture was included for a purpose. The themes that each prophet discussed were chosen and presented through inspiration. When one discovers this organization, the sermon becomes meaningful and powerful in one's life.
    *Understanding the doctrine of the Gospel is usually more important than remembering stories. Pound for pound, the sermons are more doctrinally valuable to us than the stories.
    *Within each sermon are many favorite scriptures. These scriptures become more meaningful when we understand their context in the sermon.
