Inside Ricks - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

February 23, 1999

 

WRESTLING

Four Ricks College wrestlers were named All Americans by the National Junior College Athletic Association at the NJCAA Wrestling Championships last weekend in Rochester, Minn. Heavyweight James Huml won the national championship upsetting his hometown opponent from Rochester. At 149 pounds, BJ Wright finished in third place. At 157 Josh Wright placed sixth. At 184 pounds, Steve Babcock finished seventh. Ricks College took 6th place overall.

The team competed without Aaron Polkowske, the 141-pound 1998 All-American who was out of the competition due to a neck injury. "The NJCAA tournament is always tough. We knew that without Aaron, it was going to be even tougher to win," said Ricks coach Bob Christensen. "We were hoping to place as high as we could, and we wrestled well. We were glad to place four of our guys."
