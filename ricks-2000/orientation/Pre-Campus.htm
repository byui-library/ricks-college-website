Welcome to the BYU-Idaho Pathway Pre-Campus Orientation program. First, congratulations on being accepted to BYU-I.  We wish you every success in your academic, personal, and spiritual endeavors. 

Success, as with many other things in life, is achieved by hard work and preparation, and so it is with college.  Your success will largely be based upon your efforts and preparation for a Ricks College learning experience.  By preparing now, you will have greater success in all aspects of college life.

Pathway

To help you succeed, we have developed an online Pre-Campus Orientation web site entitled Pathway.  By participating in Pathway you can learn vital information about a BYU-Idaho learning experience including campus information, student services, academic services, ways to get involved, help in course registration, academic advising, and more.  The course features several video clip segments that can be viewed through QuickTime Player, as well as an online discussion board.  Plan now to participate in Pathway and get a jump start on your college success.

 

Online Course Requirement


Beginning with the summer of 2000, each student who is admitted to BYU-Idaho must graduate with a minimum of one online learning experience. During a transition period the one-credit, Pre-Campus Orientation Course associated with Pathway will meet this requirement.
