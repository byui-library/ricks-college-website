2001 Athlete of the Year
Melanie Clark Steere '98 

Melanie led her BYU women’s cross country team to a second-place national team finish at the women’s cross country NCAA Championships last fall. She paced BYU with a 17th-place finish for All-American honors. During the track season at BYU last year, Melanie placed sixth in the 800-meters in the NCAA finals, running the second fastest 800-meters ever by a BYU runner. She also qualified for and competed in the U.S. Olympic Trials. In her junior year at BYU she was a member of the indoor distance medley relay team that placed second nationally. She sat out last year’s indoor season, but placed fifth nationally this year in the NCAA’s indoor 1,500-meter finals. She is majoring in dietetics and her 3.91 GPA qualified her for academic honors by the College Sports Information Directors of America.
