Summer in the Snow 2001
Ricks College Summer Entertainment Series

For tickets, contact the Ricks College Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230.
Ticket office hours:  9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. most weekdays
Tuesday 9:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.  

Free Organ/ Choir Recitals

Because of an open-seating policy for events in the Barrus Concert Hall, the audience needs to be seated ten minutes prior to curtain time to guarantee seating. Seats not occupied at 7:20 p.m. will be released to those waiting for tickets. The same seating policy applies in the Snow Drama Theater, even though seats are reserved. 

Proper dress in the Barrus Concert Hall is more informal in the summer and ties for men and dresses for women are not required. However, standard Ricks College classroom attire is required, including no shorts. Children under six years of age, including babies, are nto admitted to Summer in the Snow events.

Michael McLean and Company
Thursday, June 7, 7:30 p.m.
Hart Auditorium

Composer and singer Michael McLean will entertain during Education Week at Ricks College. The concert is open to the public and you do not need to be registered for Education Week to attend this special evening of music. Michael has been entertaining audiences for more than two decades with his uplifting, contemporary music that touches the soul. His annual "Forgotten Carols" plays to sellout crowds at Christmastime. Come hear some of his old standbys, as well as some of his latest works. Cost is $8 and $6.

"Mystery Manor" Dinner Theater
Thursday, June 14, 7 p.m.
Manwaring Center West Ballroom

The Vigilante Theatre Company of Bozeman, Mont., will present a dinner theater show entitled "Mystery Manor." At this very special dinner at Mulrooney Hall, everyrone is an honored guest . . . but someone has unsavory intentions. Your hosts, Charles and Victoria, serve a frightfully elegant plum pudding, but when the topic of inheritance comes up, the proof is in the murder. Cost is $13 (which includes dinner provided by Ricks College Food Services).

Swingset (Ricks Faculty Jazz Ensemble)
Friday, June 22, 7:30 p.m.
Kirkham Auditorium

Swingset, the Ricks College faculty jazz ensemble includes Ricks employees Mark Watkins and Mat Miles on saxophone, Brian Pyper on trombone, Wilson Brown on piano, David Taylor on percussion, vocalist Missy Babcock and Ricks student Adam Pyper on bass. Cost is $3 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

Dick Clark and the Flashbacks
Thursday, July 26, 7:30 p.m.
Barrus Concert Hall

Join us for this annual Rexburg concert featuring Sunbird Dick Clark from Sun City West and his musical friends. Cost is $3 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

"West of the Moon"
July 5-7 and 12-14
Snow Drama Theater, 7:30 p.m.

"West of the Moon" will be presented by the Ricks College Theatre Department as this year’s summer production. Written and directed by Omar Hansen, this new original musical is set in mythical Ireland. It is a night for the whole family with dragons, giants, selkies and a whole Irish fairy tale world. Cost is $6 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

Opus IV – Encore Performance
Thursday, July 12, 7:30 p.m.
Barrus Concert Hall

Last summer’s biggest hit is back by popular demand! Opus IV is Montana’s only professional vocal quartet. Composed of four talented singers and a pianist, Opus IV combines the best of classical music and musical comedy into one exciting program. Their summer program, "From Bach to Broadway," features a blend of music, comedy and scenes from Broadway’s greatest musicals. Cost is $3 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

American Folk Dancers Tour Show
Friday, July 20, 2 p.m.
Kirkham Auditorium

You won’t want to miss this always popular show of all-American talent! The Ricks College American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band will treat you to their tour show before they embark on their month-long summer tour of Poland, Hungary and Austria. Cost is $6 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

South Family "Legacy of Music"
Saturday, July 21, 7:30 p.m.
Barrus Concert Hall

"Legacy of Music" will be presented by the South family of Idaho and Utah featuring violin, cello and piano pieces; as well as old time fiddling, Broadway tunes and music for the July patriotic and pioneer holidays. Music has played an important role in the South Family from the log cabin days at the sawmill in Island to the present with family members having performed for thousands, in concert halls on three continents. They are descendants of Barney and Marjorie South, including the families of David, Barry, M’Jean (Lund), Myrna, Susan (Crandall), and Randy. Cost is $3 general admission; $1 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

InsideOut – Men’s A Cappella
Saturday, August 11, 7:30 p.m.
Kirkham Auditorium

InsideOut, a six-man, all-vocal group from Provo, Utah, has been a big hit at Ricks. With their musical capability, vocal percussion and invigorating performance, InsideOut has produced show that entertains audiences of all ages. Their show consists of original music and cover tunes, many of which can be found of their CDs, "Innocence" and "Experience." Cost is $5 general admission; $3 for currently enrolled Ricks students.

Free Organ Recitals

As has been a tradition each summer, Ricks College will be featuring free organ recitals by Darwin Wolford, director of organ studies at Ricks, and other faculty organists. The recitals are at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall on June 28, July 27 and August 9.

Free Summer Choir Recitals

Ricks College also will offer several free choir recitals at 7:30 p.m. by summer students at Ricks College on May 30, July 11 and August 15.
