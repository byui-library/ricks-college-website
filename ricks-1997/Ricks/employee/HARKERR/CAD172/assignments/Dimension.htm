Dimensioning

"Patience is composure under stress.", Ezra Taft Benson.

 

Construct a solids model of the guide stop below, using basic primitives and boolean operations. Use paperspace to create front, top, right side and isometric views. The isometric view should be shown with hidden lines removed.  Modify the existing guide stop as follows:

  • Fillet the front corner of the stop with a 1/2 inch radius
  • Drill a 3/4" diameter hole through the "triangular" section, located 1-1/2 inches in and up from the lower left corner of the block.

Dimension the drawing appropriately as discussed in class. Be sure to use good dimensioning practices. Also include hidden and centerlines as appropriate. Place in a B-size title block. Plot at full scale. Be sure to adjust line thicknesses for plotting as in previous assignments.
