COLLEGE WRITING RESEARCH PAPER

GUIDELINES / LENGTH

Your paper should be no shorter than 5 pages and no longer than 8 pages. This will require you to have a limited and controlled thesis.

Your research paper may be informative, providing general background on a subject. However, your thesis should clearly state your position on your topic. Your paper may also be an argument on a question where people can reasonably disagree, where you attempt to move the reader toward your position. Your goal should be to persuade the audience to consider your position more seriously than it had before. Argue from a deep understanding of everyone's perspective on the issue. See p. 282 in Aims and Options for the five parts of formal persuasive organization.

SUBJECTS

Most subjects are acceptable. You are encouraged to explore a topic or question that may have occurred to you in another class, or in your major field. The following subjects are not acceptable: witchcraft, capital punishment, gun control, UFO's, abortion, euthanasia, child abuse, eating disorders, or subjects dealing with Mormonism.

SOURCES

You should have a minimum of five different sources from which you take your research. Remember the quality of your paper is directly related to the quality of your sources. All materials you use in the development of this research paper must be turned in when you submit your paper. You must turn in either a marked copy of each article you cite, OR written notes on the material. If you type directly from a book to your paper, you must turn in the book. All notes, xerox copies, and rough drafts should be clearly labeled and put into a large envelope.

FORMAT

Your paper will use the APA style based on the Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association. Examples of the APA citation style are found in Research Writing Simplified. Your paper does not need a title page or an abstract. All papers must be typewritten, with the page number and title in the top right hand corner (see pp. 55-56 in RWS). Margins are 1 1/4 inch on top, 1" on right and bottom, and 1 2 inch on left. Double space and use a 10-12 font. This should be a scholarly effort, so your paper should be finished far enough in advance of the due date that you are able to find all typographical errors and misspellings and eliminate them. Print your paper on a laser printer.

DOCUMENTATION

The APA style does not use footnotes. References to quoted, paraphrased, or cited material are included in the body of the paper itself and enclosed in parentheses. Your citations should look exactly like this: (Johnson, 1996, p. 44). Your reference page must have a centered title of References, and entries are alphabetized, double spaced, and indented five spaces on the first line. Only sources cited in your paper are listed on your References page. Your References page is included at the end of your paper, but does not count as one of the pages of your paper.
