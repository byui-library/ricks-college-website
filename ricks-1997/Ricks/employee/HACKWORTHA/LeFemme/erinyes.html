Erinyes • Furies • Eunimides • The Vengeful Ones

Any of the three terrible, winged deities of Greek mythology, namely, Alecto (unceasing in anger), Megaera (jealous one), and Tisiphone (avenger of murder). Born of the blood of the castrated Uranus, they had serpentine hair, heads of dogs, and wings of bats. They could not perish while sin exists, and they punish those who killed their own kin. They were also known as the Eunimides, but it is considered unlucky to call them that.
