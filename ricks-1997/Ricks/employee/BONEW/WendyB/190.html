COURSE DESCRIPTION

    This course is designed to introduce the student to beginning ballet technique emphasizing strength, alignment, and clarity of skills.

OBJECTIVES

    1. To develop flexibility, strength, endurance, balance coordination, phrasing, and dance performance skills.
    2. To develop a kinesthetic awareness of body alignment.
    3. To develop a degree of self-mastery from ballet discipline.
    4. To develop a keen awareness and appreciation for dance as an art form.
    5. To become knowledgeable of basic ballet terminology.

REQUIREMENTS

    Attendance/participation

      Two (2) absences are allowed without penalty. Three to four (3-4) absences will result in lowing of grade. The fifth absence will result in automatic failing of course. Three (3) tardies equal one absence. Three (3) observations equal one absence. To receive credit for observing you must take in-depth notes on your observation and turn them in to the instructor.

      Professionalism is stressed:

        1. Hair pulled back and up off neck, secured so as not to distract from dancing.
        2. Proper and modest attire (leotard and tights)
        3. Ballet Slippers (optional)
        4. No jewelry
        5. Proper underclothing
        6. No chewing gum

    Journals:

      A daily journal will be kept of movement combinations, vocabulary, individual goals, progression and involvement in class. The instructor periodically throughout the semester will collect these journals for review.

    Practical and Written Evaluations:

      Practical and written evaluations will be held periodically in order to determine the progress of each student. These exams can not be made up. The score of a zero will be averaged in to the total score.

    Final Exam:

      This is your responsibility!! The final will be held during finals week and will be a percentage of achievement and skill. It is up to you to be there at the right day and time. This can not be made up or rescheduled.

    Grading:

      Attendance 30%
      Achievement/skill 40%
      Written assignments 30%

    Extra Credit:

      Extra credit must be pre-approved by the instructor. This will be a critique of the instructor’s choice.

    The above schedule, procedures, and requirements in this course are subject to change in the event of extenuation circumstances.

    Students are encouraged to meet with the instructor at any time they feel the need for feedback or other assistance. Periodic feedback will be given, solicited or not, but the student must ensure that it is sufficient for his or her individual needs. It is hoped that this will be a rewarding semester in which the student begins to feel the exciting dynamics that can exist in skillfully relating body and spirit. Come ready to work hard and enjoy.
