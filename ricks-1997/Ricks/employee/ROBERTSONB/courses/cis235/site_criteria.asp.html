Site Criteria

Personal Web Site

  1. Multiple pages (all connected together via links)
  2. Follow proper design principles
  3. Follow naming rules
  4. Be content rich (doesn't just look pretty)
  5. Contain graphic images (none over 40k, as small as possible)
  6. Be HTML 4.0 compliant (no 3.2 or older tags)
  7. Implement at least 1 external style sheet (CSS).
  8. Be fully operational (NO broken links, broken graphics)

Remember that your personal websites must be loaded to Mars by the syllabus specified date. Be sure to fully test them after loading them!

Corporate Web site

  1. Meet all criteria listed above for the personal site and:
  2. Have a Site Analysis Sheet and Diagram submitted by the syllabus specified date.
  3. Contain at least one PDF file
  4. Include an interactive form (a Perl script provides the interactivity - it may not be the one done in class!)
  5. Contain a viable privacy and use statement
  6. Contain a contact page
  7. Demonstrate the ability to market your product
  8. Provide credits for the design team including what each team member did

The site must be loaded to the Mars server to your team folder by midnight, the syllabus specified date.

All corporate sites will be demonstrated in class by the design team. The presentation is formal as if you were a design team presenting to us, the client.
