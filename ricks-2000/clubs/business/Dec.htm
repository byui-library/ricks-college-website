What is DEC?

Simply, Delta Epsilon Chi (DEC) is a college version of DECA, an association of marketing students. It provides an extension of the awesome opportunities that marketing students can have. It provides an extension of the awesome opportunities that DECA, a renowned organization, has to offer. DEC in not a fraternity or sorority – just an organization of students with a common goal: a career in marketing, management or entrepreneurship.

How Can I Benefit?

Delta Epsilon Chi members participate in competition geared toward and applicable to the business world. Also, students participate in conferences, and further develop ties with the business community through civic consciousness projects and internships.

Where Do I Get Started?

Talk to Brother Holt in the business club office in the Smith building, room 269, or talk to any of the club officers or members.
