Geology Seminar
GEO SEMINAR
CLASS CODE: GEOL 497 CREDITS:  0
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Seminars on various geologic topics by invited speakers.
DESCRIPTION: This course exposes students to geologists, their careers, and the results of their work. It broadens their experience in what geologists do and how they do it. It also introduces them to new ideas and provides important contacts with professional geologists.
TOPICS: TBA (A seminar schedule with dates and topics is developed prior to each semester)
OBJECTIVES: To expose students to geologists, careers in geology, and new ideas.
REQUIREMENTS: All sophomore-, junior-, and senior-level students should register for this class each semester. Students are required to turn in a brief report following each seminar to the faculty member acting as seminar coordinator.
PREREQUISITES: Geol 111 & 112.
