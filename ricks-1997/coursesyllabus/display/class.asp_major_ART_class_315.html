Life Drawing II
LIFE DRAW II
CLASS CODE: ART 315 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PERFORMING AND VISUAL ARTS
DEPARTMENT: ART
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Continuation of Life Drawing I with emphasis on expression and developing personal style.
DESCRIPTION: A course intended to help the student develop greater sensitivity in expressing the beauty of the human form through drawing.
TOPICS: Anatomy, design, composition, expression, mediums, and anatomical accuracy.
OBJECTIVES: Development of a personal style.
REQUIREMENTS: Enrolled as an Art major or consent of instructor.
PREREQUISITES: Art 110, Art 215.
OTHER:
EFFECTIVE DATE: January 2002
