H.S. 298

Clinical Experience Observation

Credits 1-3

Fred R. Pearson, Ph.D.



This is the first of two semesters working in the clinical setting for health science majors. This semester will be spent in observing the procedures and daily duties of professionals in the health care areas. The instructor will help the student to design an observation program that will allow the student to understand the responsibilities and duties of health professionals.



For each credit taken the student will be expected to observe for at least 20 hours.

Example: Three credits= 60 hours of observation



The grade is based on how well the student performed with the health professional.

Grading criteria will be based on either an oral or a written report from the health professional. Some criteria will be such things as: dress, arriving at work assignments on time, communication with a patients and health professional. The instructor will communicate with the supervising health professionals about the grade.



The student should keep track of all the hours they work or volunteer and what they are doing during the work time. This report should be submitted to the instructor at the end of your observation hours.



It is recommended that the student obtain a letter of recommendation from the health professional for applying to schools and employers.
