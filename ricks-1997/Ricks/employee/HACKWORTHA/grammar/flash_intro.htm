Print page one and then use that sheet to print page two.  Print page two on the back of page one.  Print page four on the back of page fhree.

Experiment to find the proper orientation for your sheets; however, for my HP printer, after printing page one, I insert page one in the paper holder with the page one text up (showing) and the top of the page is pointing at me.

After printing, cut the cards into 15 cards per sheet.

Arrange the cards in groups.  For example, put all the SVDO together.  Put all the CLAUSES together, etc.  Then use the card to drill when you are learning that particular lesson.
