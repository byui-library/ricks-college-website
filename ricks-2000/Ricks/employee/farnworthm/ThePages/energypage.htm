Energy will be a synonym for feeling, emotion and states of psychological agitation, both positive (energies like love, concern, joy) and negative (energies like anger, sadness, fear).  This page will attempt to explain how fields are created when energy is experienced in thinking and feeling-  it is these fields which  may become problematic in our lives when our energies overwhelm us or incapacitate us.

Roger Callihan, the founder of Thought Field Therapy, accidentally discovered the dramatic  breakdown of energy fields when tapping on acupressure points as he was working with a woman who had a phobia of the water.  The woman's phobia disappeared so completely and suddenly that Roger realized he had stumbled onto a great healing technique that utilized  systems of the body to break down and remove disturbing energy patterns and systems.

Roger Callahan developed and marketed his discovery and was one of the pioneers of the field of Energy Psychology.  Other adaptations and techniques were developed by Roger's Students.  Gary Craig developed a repeatable sequence of tapping that utilized multiple spots on the face, torso and hands that created an easy and effective technique which he called Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT).  Gary made the training available to anyone interested and did not charge the high prices associated with early Energy Psychology training.

The tapping technique is very successful in breaking down energy fields associated with fears, trauma, phobia, loss, addictions, depressions, anxiety, stress, sadness and anger.  It does take some practice to effectively engage the sequence of tapping and the thought process to  dissipate the energy field  but can easily be mastered with a little bit of effort.

One of the interesting paradoxes of the tapping techniques is that they are often so effective that once engaged the person involved will attempt to create other scenarios that explain away their obvious relief.  To counter this tendency, the person is often asked to gage their degree of agitated-intense energy on a scale from one to ten, as a measuring device to compare before and after states.  The measuring is called a Subjective Unit of Distress or SUDs.  With out it the person can easily say it really didn't do anything of substance as the relief is subtle and almost non-perceptible.

My personal theory regarding this phenomenon is that the body's natural state of health is balance and equilibrium and we only notice a problem when the energy is blocked, out of whack and causing stress.  As soon as the balance is restored via the acupressure tapping then there is nothing to experience except the memory of the problem and that can be easily questioned since the energy no longer exists.  Said another way: "when the shoes fits they're not noticed".  The restored energy balance is so subtle and natural that there is little conscious awareness of the restoration... it all seems so normal that there is little to feel.   And since the energy field causing the problem is gone there is no substantial evidence of it ever being.

The balance of energy is successful in approximately 80% of the people who engage the process with less effectiveness  in those trying it for the first time.

Another interesting aspect of the tapping is that it is not psychological in nature and the person doing it can be as skeptical and cynical as they may want to be.  You do not have to believe in it for it to be effective.  You can believe it is just a bunch  of "crap".  You evaluation of the tapping is a non issue in its effectiveness.

Some Frequently Asked Questions

What is Emotional Freedom Technique?

EFT is a universal healing aid that is in use worldwide by those in the healing professions.  The method is based on traditional Chinese medicine's energy system, the same system on which acupuncture is based.  It rebalances the energy systems.  EFT allows individuals to let go of such difficult feelings as fears, anxiety, depression, guilt, jealousy, and anger.  It is also effective with core beliefs.  It was developed by Gary Craig as an outgrowth of Thought Field Therapy™ which was developed by Dr. Roger Callahan, Ph.D.

How is it practiced?

EFT is a psychological version of acupuncture, except that we don't use needles.  Instead the body's energy system is balanced by tapping with our fingertips on selected points along the energy meridians.

Do treatment effects last?

When the problem feelings are no longer able to be accessed in the office, then the effectiveness of the treatment needs to be tested in the real world.  Sometimes the client reports no more fear, sometimes a greatly reduced fear, and (very rarely) the same level of fear as before treatment. The treatment is never considered successful until the individual reports lasting effects in the real life situations.

How effective is EFT ?

The effectiveness of EFT depends on the nature of the problem treated and the number of problems with which the person struggles.  The highest rate of effectiveness seems to be with a single, uncomplicated phobia, such as fear of heights, snakes, or claustrophobia.  It appears to be about 85% effective in just one or a few sessions.  Other problems can also be very rapidly changed as well.  As you might expect, some problems are more complicated to diagnose and treat.  Individuals with a broad number of issues tend to require more complicated treatments. 

Does EFT resolve all problems? 

No.  EFT is often highly effective in removing blocks in the system.  It removes blockages.  It does not add anything - knowledge, insight skills, emotional growth or anything else.  However, the impact of removing blockages often results in startling changes in the person's ability to think clearly, act effectively, and feel differently so that it often looks as though the person's abilities, knowledge, and behavior have been transformed in certain ways or in certain areas.

Is EFT safe to use?

Yes.  Unlike many other methods of treatment, EFT rarely has any unwanted side effects such as causing a temporary increase in emotional pain.  Occasionally some individuals report uncomfortable sensations while doing the tapping and I will work in other ways with that individual.  Unlike most treatments, it does not require toleration of the unwanted emotion for long periods of time.  Only rarely is there any discomfort associated with the treatment itself, other than to bring up the thought and rate your feelings.  Generally, the tapping begins to alleviate the feelings (or have no effect). The one way in which painful feelings can seem to increase through treatment is when the first layer of feelings is released and layers of feelings underneath are uncovered and emerge.  EFT does not cause the loss of any memory, and will never remove anything you need.  For example, EFT treatment will never remove a necessary fear.  It only removes unrealistic and excessive fears which are interfering with the quality of life.
