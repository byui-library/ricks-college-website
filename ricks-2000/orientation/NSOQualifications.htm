NSO Team Leader Qualifications:

In order to be considered for an NSO Team position, you must:

1.  Be a sophomore or returning student for the 2002-2003 school year.

2.  Have a minimum of a 2.25 cumulative GPA to participate.

3.  Maintain weekly officer hours on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.

4.  Attend weekly NSO Team meetings.

5.  Remain in Rexburg during the summer months. (Three weeks vacation is allowed per team member and must be scheduled in advance with the NSO Coordinator).

6.  Attend the NSO Team training retreat in January/February.

7.  Organize and participate in the R-Team Leader Conference(s).

9.  Conduct R-Team Leader applicant interviews.

10.  Complete the NSO Team application.

NSO Team Benefits:

1.  Increase your wardrobe!   NSO Team Leaders will receive several embroidered/printed NSO uniforms to be worn during the orientation. 

2.  Experience the thrills of camping!  NSO Team Leaders will participate in an overnight training retreat at Teton Lodge.  Leadership, training, and team-building activities will be included, as well as meals.

3.   Spruce up your resume!  Serving as an NSO Team leader demonstrates that you have exceptional leadership skills and abilities.  Listing your NSO Team Leader involvement experience on your resume will be an added plus at employment interview times.

4.  Free Meals!  You'll get to eat for free at at least two major orientations (Fall/Winter), in addition to several other times including an NSO Appreciation Dinner, etc.

5.  Meet new people, make new friends, and help new students feel welcomed.

6.  As this is a major leadership experience, certain school benefits apply.
