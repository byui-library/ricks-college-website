FRENCH 201



INTRODUCTION

Welcome to French 201, an intermediate level course for students with 2 semesters of college French or equivalent experience. In this class you've arrived at a satisfyingly high level of competence. Although you may feel a little nervous about expressing yourself in French, you're confident about entering into and exploring the French mind by way of its literature and culture. We do just that in this class. Along the way, we also plunge deeper into the mysteries of French grammar. Reviewing what you have already learned (that's a confidence builder), we go beyond to introduce new grammar concepts. The language of class, naturally, is French. As we step across the threshold into the francophone world, we leave English behind, and French, with all its beauty and precision, becomes our language of choice.



CLASS OBJECTIVES:

A. We have a SPEAKING goal:

We speak only French in class, thus polishing our speaking abilities. All discussion of literature and grammar are exclusively in French. Conducting class in French enhances our ability to use this world class language with skill and confidence.



B. A Reading and Cultural Goal: to experience the pleasure of reading genuine French literature. We will read and discuss in French (with increasing ease and pleasure) six stories in Les aventures du petit Nicolas and a small number of class handouts. More, we will view and discuss a French video in class.

C. A Grammar Goal, to master French grammar, while exploring its beauty and architecture. In pursuit of this goal we will master the accompanying grammar text, Ma Grammaire Française, in its entirety.

D. A Vocabulary Goal: To practice and learn the vocabulary for Les aventures du petit Nicolas.

E. A pronunciation goal: To master French pronunciation, using a cassette available from the library and a study guide that will be given to you in class.

Materials for class:
