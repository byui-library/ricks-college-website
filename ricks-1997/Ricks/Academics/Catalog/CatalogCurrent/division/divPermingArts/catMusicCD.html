General Education

Fine Arts 100 Fine Arts Attendance 0
Required for graduation with Associate in Arts and Sciences Degree. To fulfill the requiremelnts of this course the student attends ten (10) approved fine arts events. One or two orientation meetings will be held at the beginning of each term.

Music 100* Introduction to Music 3
Two lecture hours per week plus one of the following lab’s:
Music 100L, Sec 1, 2, 3, 4 --College Choir (Fee: $ 2)
Music 100L, Sec 5 -- Women’s Choir (Fee: $ 7.50)
Music 100L, Sec 6 -- Collegiate Singers (Fee: $12)
Music 100L, Sec 7 -- Concert Chorale (Fee: $10)
Music 100L, Sec 8 -- Symphonic Band (Fee: $10)
Music 100L, Sec 9 -- Symphony Orchestra (Fee: $10)
Music 100L, Sec 10 --Chamber Winds
Music 100L, Sec 11 -- Women's Glee
Music 100L, Sec 12 -- Concert Percussion
Music 100L, Sec 13 -- Men’s Choir (Fee: $5)
Music 100L, Sec 14 -- Concert Band
Music 100L, Sec 15 -- String Orchestra
An introduction to the history of musical style, period, and form. Special emphasis is placed upon analyzing compositions performed in the respective lab’s. The lab for this class is membership in one of the performing ensembles.

Music 101* Music and the Humanities 3
Three hours per week
A course designed to acquaint non-music majors with our musical heritage and its relationship to culture and the other arts.

Music 137 Concert Band 1
Prerequisite: Basic skill on a band instrument
Three lecture hours per week

Music 138 Symphonic Band 1
Prerequisite: audition (September)
Five hours per week
May be repeated for credit
Fee: $10
A 62-72 member performing wind and percussion ensemble (Percussionists assigned from Concert Percussion-Music 138A). Three programs feature diverse and difficult wind band literature, including a Thanksgiving “Pops” Concert, a February “Patriotic” Concert and a year-end “Great Band Literature” Concert. Guest artists and a biennial tour included. (See M100L for the Arts registration option for ensembles).

Music 138A Concert Percussion .5-2
Five Class hours per week
May be repeated for credit
Concert Percussion is for percussionists of all concert organizations. Training on organizing effective percussion sections and on all standard percussion instruments is given. Assignments are made from this class for members to perform on specific numbers with various instrumental and vocal ensembles. (See M100L for the Arts registration option for ensembles).

Music 139 Chamber Orchestra .5
Prerequisite: Audition
Four hours per week
A select group of 22-27 advanced string players. Concert programs include standard repertory as well as choral/orchestral, chamber music (quartets), Broadway musicals and full-scale operas. Winds and Brass are added from the Chamber Winds & Brass as needed. Biennial performance tour in conjunction with Collegiate singers.

Music 140 String Orchestra 1
Prerequisite: Basic skill on an orchestral string instrument
Three lecture hours per week
Designed to provide a challenging experience for string students who possess basic technique while focusing on the improvement of orchestral playing abilities. Combines with the Symphony Orchestra and combines choirs for annual Fall semester presentation of Handel’s “Messiah.” (See M100L for the Arts registration option for ensembles).

Music 146 Jazz Improvisation .5

One hour per week
May be repeated for credit

Music 147 Symphony Orchestra 1-2
Prerequisite: Strings, basic string techniques; winds, audition
Five hours per week

Fee: $10 uniform fee for men; women must supply own black concert dress.

(See M100L for the Arts registration option for ensembles).

Music 148 Chamber Winds 1

Music 149 Concert Attendance 0

Music 150 Chamber Music Ensemble (Instrumental or Voice) .5-l
Prerequisite: Consent of director
Two to three hours per week
May be repeated for credit
Select groups of musicians organized to study and perform chamber music literature. Groups using this number include the following:

A Wind ensembles such as woodwind quintets and brass choirs.

B. Vocal ensembles specializing in the performances of madrigals and other choral chamber music.

C. Percussion Ensembles. The following groups are traditionally offered:

Percussion Ensemble: This ensemble provides a challenging performance experience for percussion students regardless of playing ability. Focusing on music written solely for percussion instruments, this ensemble combines with the Marimba Ensemble in several concerts each year. Membership in the Percussion Ensemble is open to all interested percussionists although enrollment in Concert Percussion is strongly encouraged.

Marimba Ensemble: Comprised of 4 to 6 players, the Marimba Ensemble performs music from the Choral Preludes of J.S. Bach through the Rags of Scott Joplin, to the sounds of contemporary artists. The Marimba Ensemble provides incidental music for several music events each year as well as appearing in concert with the Percussion Ensemble each semester. Membership is by audition only.

RIXSTIX: An indoor drum line and percussion ensemble developed to provide percussionists with a “drum corp-like” experience. One show is produced each year and performed at the half-time of the men’s basketball game each February. Percussionists playing snares, quads, bass drums and cymbals are encouraged to also be in the Football Band. Other members are encouraged to sign up for Concert Percussion and/or Percussion Ensemble.

Two hours per week

Music 190 Basic Music Theory 2
Two class hours plus one lab hour per week
Practical experience in fundamentals through creative activities in music reading, writing, singing, and functional piano. Music 194 Aural Skills II 2
Prerequisite: Music 195 and Concurrent enrollment in Music 196
Four hours per week
Development of sight-reading and sight-singing skills using melodic solfeggio symbols. Also includes development of rhythmic, melodic and harmonic dictation skills through study of rhythm, interval and chord qualities.

Music 195 Music Theory I 3
Three hours per week
Review of fundamentals and theory terminology and introduction to diatonic harmony through part-writing and analysis.

Music 196 Music Theory II 3
Prerequisite: C- or higher in M195, concurrent enrollment in M194
Three hours per week
Further studies in diatonic harmony including non-chord tones, secondary chords, and modulation. (Winter, First Summer Term)
