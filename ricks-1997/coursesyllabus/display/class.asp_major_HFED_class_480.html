Senior Seminar
SENIOR SEMINAR
CLASS CODE: HFED 480 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A course which facilitates the transition of the Family and Consumer Science Educator into the public school system.
DESCRIPTION: This course is designed to increase students' awareness of the opportunities for those with a degree in Family and Consumer Sciences. Much of this course is directed at the graduating senior, with practical information about identifying alternative career paths, job-seeking ideas, steps in resume writing, and job descriptions of specific careers.
TOPICS: Professional development for careers; Lifelong plan of education and professional development; Internships and other preprofessional employment; Developing job seeking strategies; Professional resume and portfolio; Finding desirable employers; Interviewing and employment offers; Career Development; Transitions; Managing a successful career; Professional associations, organizations and memberships; Future directions for Family and Consumer Science professions
OBJECTIVES: The student will become aware of the opportunities for those with a degree in Family and Consumer Sciences. He will become familiar with alternative career paths, job-seeking ideas, steps in resume writing and job descriptions of specific careers.
REQUIREMENTS: Department Approval
