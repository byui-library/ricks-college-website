Former Student Search


In order to locate a former student, roommate, classmate, please send us an e-mail. 

First, please list your name, address and the years you attended, if you have been married, list both maiden and married names, your spouse and whether they attended Ricks or BYU.

Second, include the full name and any other information you have on the person you would like to locate.   Some things that are helpful to include are:  where they were from, class year, or major.  We will  look the person(s) up for  you and e-mail you back as   quickly as possible.  We are only allowed to give out addresses,   no phone  numbers.
