"Something's Afoot"

Oct. 10-13 and 16-20, 2001
7:30 p.m., Kirkham Arena Theater

A zany, entertaining show that takes a strong satirical poke at Agatha Christie mysteries and musical styles of past years. Ten people, stranded in an isolated English country house, frantically race to discover the identity and motivation of the fiendish culprit picking them off one by one.

Tickets go on sale Sept. 24. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Play.
