Testing Services

The majority of classes have their tests administered in the BYU-Idaho Testing Center.  Religion classes also administer their tests at the Religion Testing Center located in the Taylor Building.  The BYU-Idaho Testing Center has on online web site which can inform you about the following:

  1. Testing Center Hours

  2. Current Conditions - a detailed report of available seating, as well as a chart detailing the amount of students entering and exiting the center, as well as net/gain loss of students.  This can be of tremendous value to you to know when to go and take your tests.

  3. Contact Information

  4. Student Scores - you can look up your scores from various tests via the web.

The Testing Services web site requires you to login using your Student ID # and Pin #.
