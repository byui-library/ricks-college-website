EMPLOYEE CARDS

 

  • All employees are entitled to an employee ID card.  The Personnel Office issues a green card to all new employees, which should be brought to the ID Center.  This card is needed before an ID card can be processed
  • A spouse and  family members of a full-time employee are entitled to have an ID card. 
  • Dependent children from 8 to 26 will be issued an ID card free of charge.
  • Full-time seminary teachers can request an ID card (spouse and dependents not eligible). There is a $10 charge.
  • Part-time Ricks employees are entitled to an ID card (spouse and dependents not eligible).
