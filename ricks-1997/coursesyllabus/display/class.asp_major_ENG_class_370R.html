MAJOR AUTHORS
CLASS CODE: ENG 370R CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Focuses on the life and works of one major writer or the life and works of a few related authors. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
DESCRIPTION: Focuses on the life and works of one major writer or the life and works of a few related authors. (May be repeated for credit. See schedule for specific offerings.)
TOPICS: The life and works of the author(s); the distinctive characteristics of the author's writing; the literary merit of the author's works; the cultural contexts of the author's works.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Analyzing the literary themes of the author's works. (2) Explaining the distinctive characteristics of the author's writing. (3) Discussing and writing about the author. (4) Understanding the author's life and times.
REQUIREMENTS: Read representative examples of the author's works. Summarize or review primary texts and secondary sources. Write analytical, interpretive, and evaluative papers.
