2000 Ricks College
Merit Badge Pow Wow
October  21 & 28
New two-week format!!

The 2000 Scout Merit Badge Pow Wow is for all Scouts eleven years of age and older. Instruction is available to Scouts in one or two merit badges of their choice. Participants will work on each merit badge for one hour and 20 minutes each Saturday of the Pow Wow (a few merit badges require the Scouts to meet for the full 2 hours and 50 minutes each Saturday). The new two-week format should make it easier for more scouts, leaders, and counselors to participate in the Merit Badge Pow Wow.

Adult Leaders
Adult leaders will not be required to pay the registration fee. They may purchase a 2000 patch after the Pow Wow begins. In accordance with National Council policy, two adult leaders should attend the Pow Wow with each unit. Adult leaders are strongly encouraged to attend merit badge classes with their boys.
    • All Scouts and leaders are asked to wear their scout uniforms at the Pow Wow.
    • An opening ceremony will be held the first Saturday from 8:30-8:45 a.m.  Scouts are encouraged to attend.  They will receive their 2000 Pow Wow patch at the ceremony.
Standards
Participants of any race, color, creed, ethnic or national origin may participant in the Merit Badge Pow Wow provided they maintain the Ricks College standards of modesty in dress and conduct and abstain from alcohol, tobacco, coffee and tea while on campus.
