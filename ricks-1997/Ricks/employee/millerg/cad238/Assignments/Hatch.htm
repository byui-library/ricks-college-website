1.  Create the following linetypes

A.  Simple Linetype

"SOS"        __ __ __ . . . __ __ __ . . . __ __ __

B.  Complex Linetype

"CUT"        ---  CUT  ---   ---  CUT  ---  ---  CUT  ---
Embedded Text:            CUT
Use standard text font style, height=0
Text should be centered in gap, both horz. & vert.
Dash length         .5
Space length        .25

Note:
    Space on both sides of text
    2 dashes between words, 1 dash on ends
