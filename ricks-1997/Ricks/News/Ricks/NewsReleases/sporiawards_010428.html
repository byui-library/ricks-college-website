Ricks College News Release
April 28, 2001

Ricks College graduates named
Spori Scholars  

REXBURG, Idaho — Eleven Ricks College graduates were honored as Spori Scholars during graduation ceremonies April 28.

The Spori Scholarship is the highest award offered to Ricks students for academic excellence. The scholarship is named after the eastern Idaho school’s founder, Jacob Spori. The award includes a two-year scholarship that is offered by the Utah and Idaho university to which the graduate is transferring.

Spori Scholars are listed below:

• Tonya Alleman, daughter of Gerald and Jolene Alleman of Thatcher, Idaho, graduated from Grace High School in 1999. She majored at Ricks in Office Systems Management and plans to continue her education at Idaho State University in business education. 

• Michael A. Asher, son of Mike and Vonnie Asher of Vancouver, Wash., graduated from Columbia River High School in 1991. He majored in Health Science and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Colby Blair Curtis, son of Blair and Julie Curtis of Rock Springs, Wyo., graduated from Rocks Springs High School in 1997. He majored in Pre-Optometry and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• David Marshall Duerden, son of David S. and Donna Duerden of Rexburg, Idaho, graduated from Madison High School in 1996. He majored in Humanities and plans to continue his education at the University of Utah in Salt Lake City or at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Caesar R. Gonzales II, son of Caesar Gonzales Sr. of Shelby Township, Michigan, and Bernadette Gonzales of Novi, Mich.,graduated from Henry Ford II High School in 1996. He majored in Pre-Medicine and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Gerald Edwin Hansen III, son of Gerald and Sharlene Hansen Jr. of Rexburg, Idaho, graduated from Madison High School in 1997. He majored in French and International Studies and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Stephen Hunt, son of Murray and Maryjo Hunt of Rexburg, Idaho, graduated from Madison High School. He majored in Russian and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Hojin Kang of Kwangju, South Korea, majored in International Studies and plans to continue his education at Brigham Young University in Provo,

• Adrienne Paulsen, daughter of Stewart and Annette Paulsen of Salt Lake City, Utah, graduated from Olympus High School in 1999. She majored in Print Journalism and plans to continue her education at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Cassie Ann Searle, daughter of Gerald and Jolynn Searle of Idaho Falls and Anna Marie and Ben Coronado of Shelley, graduated from Shelley High School in 1999. She majored in Communications (print journalism), and plans to continue her education at the University of Idaho in Moscow.

• Suzanne N. Wray, daughter of Richard and Kathy Wray of Rexburg, Idaho, graduated from Madison High School in 1998. She majored in nursing and plans to continue her education at Utah State University in Logan, Utah.
