Associate in Arts and Sciences
CAD/CAM Technology (325)

The major in CAD/CAM Technology, (325), gives graduates an introductory education into engineering technology relating to Computer-Aided Design (CAD), Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM), and Advanced Welding Processes Technology while completing the general education course requirements for the Associate of Arts and Sciences degree. The program is designed for students who have an interest in discovering what technology is about and how it is applied in today's world. This major will help focus a student's interest in the area of technology.
