Background:  Steven D. Hay, Ph.D.
Academic :Bachelors degree, Psychology, Idaho State University, 1981.
                 Masters of Science, Marriage and Family therapy, BYU, 1983.
                 Masters of Social Work, Clinical Social Work, BYU, 1983.
                 Doctor of Philosophy, Family Studies, BYU, 1995.
Dr. Hay has taught at BYU-I (formerly Rick's College) since 1986, teaching in the Department of Sociology, Social Work & Criminal Justice.  He was Department chairman from 1990 to April 1999.  Classes typically taught include:  Soc 111 Introduction to Sociology,  SW 360 Introduction to Social Work, SW 367 Human Behavior in the Social Environment, and occasionally Soc 112 Modern Social Problems, SW 362 Social Work Practice 1 and Sociology 451.  Dr. Hay emphasizes both content and process education.  He will do all he can to help students help themselves reach their goals.
