Bliss

       Gordon B. Hinckley said,

"Anyone who imagines that bliss is normal is going to waste a lot of time running around shouting that he’s been robbed.

The fact is that most putts don’t drop, most beef is tough, most children grow up to be just people, most successful marriages require a high degree of mutual toleration, most jobs are often more dull than otherwise.

Life is like an old-time rail journey: delays, side tracks, smoke, dust, cinders, and jolts, interspersed only occasionally by beautiful vistas, and thrilling bursts of speed. The trick is to thank the Lord for letting you have the ride."
