COACH CLYDE NELSON
    Brigham Young University
    Ninth Season (2001-2002)  

      Entering his ninth year at the helm of the Viking basketball program, Coach Clyde Nelson has posted an outstanding winning percentage of 67%.  The Vikings have won twenty or more games in six of Nelson's eight seasons.   Only once under Nelson has the program posted a losing record.

       Nelson's career at Ricks is highlighted by the 1997-98 season when the Vikings, led by  Shawn Moore, Ty Church, Seth Dahle, and Ricky Bower, recorded a, 12-6 record in the tightly balanced Scenic West Athletic Conference.  On their home floor the Vikings went on to win the Region 18 tournament on a buzzer-beating shot by Ricky Bower.  Nelson was named Region 18 Coach of the Year.

       The Vikings went on to the NJCAA tournament where they  finished 8th in the nation. Bower was named to the All-Tournament team while Ty Church won the tournaments ‘Small Man' award.  The 1997-98 Vikes posted an impressive 28-10 overall mark.  The 28 wins were the most in Coach Nelsons six seasons at Ricks.

       Prior to coming to Ricks, Coach Nelson enjoyed an extremely successful prep coaching career at Viewmont High School in Bountiful, Utah. In his last two seasons at Viewmont, Nelson posted a 47-1 record and went 24-0 in 1993, winning the Utah State 4-A Championship. His 1992-93 team was the first Utah high school team to be ranked nationally when the "USA Today"  Newspaper listed them 22nd in the nation. Nelson, who was selected as the 4-A Coach of the Year in 1991, 1992, and 1993, won four regional championships over his eight-year stint at Viewmont.
       Previous to his appointment at Viewmont, Clyde coached for three seasons at Skyview High School in Smithfield, Utah.  In April of 1994, Nelson took an all-state team from Utah to the national Basketball Congress International tournament in Phoenix, Arizona, and coached them to a 9-0 record and a BCI national championship.
       Coach Nelson was a starting point guard for the Vikings during the 1972-73 and 1975-76 campaigns and was also a baseball standout for Ricks. He earned all-conference and all-region honors and was ranked fifth nationally with a .486 batting average. He was drafted out of high school by the San Diego Padres but instead elected to play basketball and baseball for Ricks.
       He is a graduate of Ricks College and Brigham Young University and served an LDS mission to Melbourne, Australia. Clyde was born in Cody, Wyoming, but was raised in Bountiful.
