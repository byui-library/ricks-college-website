Temple Square sister missionaries greet 30,000 visitors daily throughout the Salt Lake City Olympic Games

Chelsea Summers
SUM00007@BYUI.EDU
and Kelly Smurthwaite
SMU01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

A sister missionary talks with visitors on Temple Square. Missionaries serve as guides for the 300,000 to 400,000 visitors per month who tour Temple Square, the Conference Center, the Beehive House and other nearby sites. According to the Church’s web site, over 30,000 people will walk through Temple Square during the Olympics. photo courtesy of lds.org
Because of the increase of people visiting Temple Square during the 2002 Winter Olympic Games, life for the sister missionaries assigned to this area is busy.

Temple Square has over 30,000 visitors per day, according to the Church’s web site, lds.org,.

But many of the sister missionaries are prepared to answer the questions of Olympic visitors. They will also be giving tours and introducing Church movies such as The Testaments and Legacy.

“Really, we aren’t doing much to prepare the Sisters, just making sure they know their stuff an have smiles on their faces,” Sister Larson, secretary for the Salt Lake Temple Square Mission, said.

Although missionaries will be available to answers questions of visitors, no missionaries will be proselyting, Church officials said.

“We’re as anxious as anyone to see that these Games are really the Games of the Salt Lake Olympics, the Games of the United States Olympics,” Bruce L. Olsen, the Church’s managing director of public affairs, told The Salt Lake Tribune.

“If people come to us, we’ll answer their questions. We will seize the opportunity to tell about the history and culture of the area, which the Olympics has always featured in one way or another,” he said.

There are many activities going on during the Olympics that all missionaries will be encouraging Olympic spectators to attend. The Mormon Tabernacle Choir’s four concerts and “The Light of the World” musical presentation are just some of the events occurring during the weeks of the Winter Games.

About 185 sister missionaries are assigned to the Temple Square Mission in downtown Salt Lake City, according to The Salt Lake Tribune.

Most of the Sisters are bilingual. They work 10-hour shifts. During this time they give tours, greet people at the gates surrounding Temple Square and help with family searches at the Church’s genealogy center, located near the temple.
