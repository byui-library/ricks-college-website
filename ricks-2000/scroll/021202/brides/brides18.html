Shoes can be a bride’s sole mate

by Rebecca Long
LON01004@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

While shoes may seem to be a minor detail on the big day, the bride may realize their importance when she considers that she will be standing for a full day. Fortunately, many styles are available now to match the bride’s preference and need for comfort.

Although every bride has her own style, many choose to wear platform sandals — even in the winter, Karen Monik of Hart’s Tux and Gown Shop in Idaho Falls, said. These platforms are the most popular of shoe styles in the city and average about a two and a half inch heel. They are “really comfortable,” Monik said.

Another reason for wearing platforms is that they take up some of the length of the skirt, Margene Hansen of Margene’s Decorating in Idaho Falls, said.

Another popular style is the “clear shoe” with rhinestones on the toe, a style that is a similitude of Cinderella’s glass slipper.

Some brides opt for tennis shoes. These may also have a platform sole and are often chosen for comfort’s sake. The brides who wear sneakers often decorate them, much like the shoes worn in Father of the Bride, Hansen said.

Shoes with a low heel are also a big hit because the brides are “going to be standing for a long time,” Monik said.

Most brides spend around $69 on shoes for the wedding, Monik said.

Make sure your shoes are broken in before your wedding day.

“Never wear a pair of shoes brand new for the day,” Hansen said.
