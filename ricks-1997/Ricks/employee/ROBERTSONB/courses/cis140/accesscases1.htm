Case 3 (page 3.41 #4) – Lopez Lexus Dealerships

 

Step 1 - Open the lastf.section.case1 database from your AccessSave folder.

Step 3 – Do not print. [The Car Info query should produce 25 records.]

Step 4 – Do not delete the year field. Only hide it. Save the revised query as “2000 Cars with

Year Hid.” [Note: Both queries will produce the same results except the year field will not be visible in the “2000 Cars with Year Hid” query.]

Step 5 – Do not print either query. [Note: Location or Trans should produce 12 records while the

Location and Trans query should produce 2 records.]

Step 6 – Remember to separate the name of the field from the calculation with a colon “:”. Do

Not Print the query results.

Step 7 – Only use the Cost and SellingPrice fields initially. [Hint: This will be a different type of

query.] Do not print any of the query results.

Step 8 – Ask for “Top Values Query”. Do not print any query results. The Query Properties sheet

can be found in View: Properties.
