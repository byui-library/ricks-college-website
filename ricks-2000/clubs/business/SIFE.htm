What Is SIFE?

SIFE’S VISION:

Helping people achieve their dreams through free enterprise education.

SIFE’S MISSION:

To provide college students the best opportunity to make a difference and to develop leadership, teamwork, and communication skills through learning, practicing, and teaching the principles of free enterprise.

During SIFE’s 1997-98 academic year, the number of active SIFE teams grew to encompass students on over 600 college campuses across the country and beyond US borders. Students reached more than 102 million people through their free enterprise outreach programs. SIFE’s philosophy is, "tell me and I will forget, show me and I might remember, involve me and I will understand." By putting students in the role fo teaching others, they get a first-hand understanding of economics, management, marketing and education. Through their outreach programs, SIFE teams teach current economic issues and basic economic concepts to all ages through creative vehicles that students invent themselves. By generating computer simulations, seminars, news articles, and television and radio commercials—students get the kind of experience they can’t find in the classroom. SIFE students are not only involved in their community, they gain invaluable experience while helping to raise their own funding for their outreach programs. Students also receive academic credit for their SIFE work at more than 70% of participating colleges and universities.

"People support what they help to create" is the cornerstone of SIFE leadership training. Students tackle issues like illiteracy and the undereducated work force, the dangers of deficit spending and the consequences of excessive government regulation. This hands-on experience helps students improve their team-building, communication and leadership skills—important advantages when the job hunt begins.

Each year, all colleges and universities in the country with a SIFE team participate in SIFE regional competitions to meet and compete against their fellow schools in a stimulating presentation that will decide which teams go on to the international competition. These competitions, conducted by SIFE Headquarters, give the students a forum to present their outreach programs to a panel of national business leaders and entrepreneurs who rate the overall effectiveness of their efforts. The winning teams gain national recognition, and over $280,000 is awarded annually in prize money and trophies to winning teams. Moreover, every student who competes gains valuable experience and insight into the business world and are exposed to career opportunities available only to SIFE students.
