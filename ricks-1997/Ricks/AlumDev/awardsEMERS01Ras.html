Distinguished Emeritus Service Award 2001

Richard C. and Barbara Hegsted Rasmussen
of Battle Creek, Michigan

Richard and Barbara met at Ricks College in the 1935 production of "The Mikado." They were also in a German class together. Richard graduated from Ricks in 1937.

Richard is a native of the Rexburg area and says "I had hardly been outside Madison County, except to punch cows in Kilgore." His father, Aaron, or "Happy" as he was more commonly known, was a basketball coach and teacher at Ricks Academy in 1912 and 1913.

Barbara Hegsted is from the Salem area.

Richard received his Bachelor of Arts degree and a teaching certificate from the University of Utah. At the time he was an aspiring actor and appeared in many student productions.

He taught in the Rigby High School for two years.

They have been married for 63 years and are in the process of moving from Battle Creek, Michigan, to the Orem, Utah, area.

Richard joined the Navy and has lived in Nebraska, Tennessee, Florida, California, Missouri, Washington (where they lived for 10 years in the Seattle area) and Michigan in his duties in the Navy and, later, in his civil service career. He has also been to Europe, the Orient, Philippines, North Africa and Canada. His patriarchal blessing said that he would travel widely.

Last month, Richard and Barbara were named Honorary Mayor of Battle Creek for one day, on March 23, 2001.
