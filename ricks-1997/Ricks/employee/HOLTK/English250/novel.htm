NOVEL SUMMARY ASSIGNMENT

 

THE ASSIGNMENT

Read a good work of literature (one you have not previously read), 300+ pages, and write a three-four page paper. Your paper should be at least 750 words and no more than 1000. It must be typed, double spaced, with one inch margins. Include all of the following headings. Use quotations, followed by the page number in parentheses, to support your statements. Your answers should be concise, yet thorough and well-formulated. At the bottom of your paper, sign your name to verify you read the entire book and the summary work is your own. Staple this Assignment Sheet to your paper and turn it in to your instructor. Do not put your summary in a folder.

 

HEADINGS

_____/4 pts Bibliography Author's last name, author's first name. Novel Title. Original publication date. City published in: Publisher.

_____/5 pts Setting Describe the setting. Remember to include time, place, and social environment.

_____/8 pts Characters Briefly list major characters first, then any minor characters that have a significant impact on the story.

_____/8 pts Plot Briefly discuss major plot events. Summarize, but tell the basic chronology.

_____/10 pts Conflict Describe the major conflicts, and explain their resolution.

_____/20 pts Theme State a theme of the novel, and explain why it is a theme of the novel. Give examples from the text (this is where you must use page numbers in parentheses). Demonstrate a personal understanding of this theme.

_____/10 pts Quotation Quote at least one important passage from the novel and explain its significance. Provide background information (who says it and when). You may quote more than one passage, and/or you can quote parts of passages.

_____/10 pts Style Describe at least one element of literature and how it adds meaning to the novel. Give examples from the text (be sure to include page numbers). You may discuss more than one.

_____/25 pts Response Write a reader response to the novel. How did you like it? How did you relate it to your personal life? What was the most profound lesson you learned from the novel? Use illustrations from the novel, and include page numbers. (Make certain you aren't just restating your description of the theme.)
