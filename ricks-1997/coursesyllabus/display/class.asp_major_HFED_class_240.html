Meal Management
MEAL MANAGE
CLASS CODE: HFED 240 CREDITS:  2 FEE:  $40.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: The practice and implementation of meal management principles as they relate to controlling cost, providing satisfying meals, entertaining, conserving time and energy, using modern kitchen technology, and ensuring a safe food supply. Students will plan, prepare, and serve meals for several occasions.
DESCRIPTION: The practice and implementation of meal management principles as they relate to controlling cost, providing satisfying meals, entertaining, conserving time and energy, using modern kitchen technology, and ensuring a safe food supply. Students will plan, prepare, and serve meals for several occasions. Lab Fee: $40
TOPICS: Food Sanitation; Methods of Service; Nutrition; Menu Planning; Food Buying and Storage; Milk/Dairy Products; Meats/Alternatives; Fruits/Vegetables; Breads/Cereals; Organizing the Kitchen; Hospitality; Setting the Table; Microwave Cooking; Time/Energy Management; Menus for Special Occasions
OBJECTIVES: You should be able to demonstrate in your questions, applications, lab experiences, and exams:
1. the development of a personal philosophy of meal management and the basic knowledge required to translate this philosophy into practice.
2. a familiarity of the food marketplace and integrate acquired knowledge with the ability to prepare and serve nutritious food attractively to meet nutritional, psychological, and social needs of families and individuals.
3. an ability to react to the challenges of conserving resources with a variety of solutions.
4. practical, individual decision-making and planning as keystones of successful meal management today.
REQUIREMENTS: Sanitation Assignment; Cost of Staples Worksheet; Menu Planning Worksheet; Supermarket Worksheet; Market Comparison; Meat Lab Comparison; Fruits and Vegetable Comparison; Pricing Worksheet; Kitchen Plan; Final Project - a week's menus with pricing
PREREQUISITES: Cooking Experience
