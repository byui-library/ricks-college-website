     COMING UP:          NJCAA CHAMPIONSHIPS, March 19-23, 2002; Salina, KS
                                             FIRST ROUND:  March 19 - 8:15 PM;  Ricks College vs Arizona Western.
    The Lady Vikings open play Tuesday, March 19, at 8:15 pm against Arizona Western.  Ricks is ranked 14th on the final NJCAA poll and seeded ninth in the tournament. The Lady Matadors are ranked 8th on the final poll and seeded eighth in the tournament.  Ricks finished the year as SWAC Co-champions, then won the Region 18 tournament.  The Lady Vikings are 27-4 overall.  Arizona Western is champion of the Arizona Community College Conference and Region 1.  They are 30-1 on the year, having lost only to Central Arizona.  This is only the second year of women's basketball at Arizona Western, and their first appearance in the NJCAA Sweet Sixteen.  For Ricks, this will be the sixth time they have played at nationals, the fifth under Coach Lori Woodland.

    RADIO: Radio broadcast of Lady Viking games will begin at 10 minutes before tip off on KBYI FM 100.5.  Bryce Rydalch will carry the play-by-play with Brian Howard anchoring at KBYI Sports.

    THE COACH: Ricks College is coached by Lori Woodland. Woodland is in her 14th season as head coach at Ricks. Her career mark with the Vikings stands at 303-118 going into this weeks games. She is the winningness basketball coach ever at Ricks College. Under Woodland, Ricks has won the SWAC championship twice and the Region 18 title five times, participating in the NJCAA Tournament four times, finishing as high as third in 1992-93.

    HISTORY: Ricks won the SWAC crown in 1994-95 with Trent Shippen as head Coach. The Lady Vikings also won the Region 18 title that year and finished 5th at nationals. In 1993 and 1994, Lori Woodland coached Ricks to Region 18 Crowns and finished third nationally in 1993. The 1994 team did not place at nationals. Woodland returned as head coach in 1997-98, won the SWAC and Region 18 crown and finished fifth at nationals. This season, Ricks shares the Conference championship with Dixie State College.
