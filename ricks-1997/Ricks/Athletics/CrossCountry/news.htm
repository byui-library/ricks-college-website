Press Release, Sep. 23, 1997

        THIS WEEK: Ricks College travels to Oregon City, OR, for the Clackamas College Invitational. The race is Saturday. This meet features the best teams in the Northwest Association of Community Colleges (Oregon and Washington). One of the top male runners in the northwest will be competing, Billy Harper of Lane CC. Coach Doug Stutz says, "I think we’ll be in the thick of things to win the team title in both the men’s and women’s divisions. We will see how we stack up against the best of the NWACC schools. We’ll also be running at sea-level which is similar to the elevation of our national meet. This is a big meet and should help us gain confidence." The first Cross Country polls of the season are out and listed in the next column.

        LAST WEEK: The Vikings did not compete last weekend. The off-week has given a number of athletes some time to heal and improve conditioning, especially on the women’s side. "Katie Williams health is getting better and she will compete this weekend. Tarrah Monson and Natalie Griffeth both are running stronger in practice and should have much better meet performances in Oregon. Our men are in good health and should run faster on the sea-level Clackamas course. Daniel Kemp is from Bend, OR, so this will be a homecoming of sorts for him. He’s sure to have a good race."
