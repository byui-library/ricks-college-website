Giving in support of Ricks College with an Outright or Deferred Gift Brings a feeling of satisfaction and accomplishment that can only come through sharing part of yourself with promising youth. You allow many students to reach their potential and you help to build the kingdom, of which Ricks College is a significant part.

Outright gifts include cash, real estate, and/or negotiable securities. Many people, even those of modest means, find they can give in this way. It is the most common, direct, and simple type of gift.

Deferred gifts can take several different forms, all of which allow long-term benefits for the beneficiary as well as the benefactor. With most, you can have the benefit of a lifetime income, and significant tax advantages. Benefits come to you while making a considerable contribution to Ricks College.

Following are among the many good things that often come to those who give generously of themselves:

  1. Putting your financial house in order,
  2. Turning your non-income producing holdings into income-producing assets,
  3. Increasing your disposable income,
  4. Avoiding tax on capital gain,
  5. Reducing your income tax,
  6. Having your assets managed by a professional trustee,
  7. Receiving a lifetime income,
  8. Securing financial peace of mind for spouse and family,
  9. Ensuring that your estate is settled the way you choose,
  10. Guiding the use of your assets after death; and
  11. Reducing gift and estate taxes for your spouse and children.

A Will has two general options available in designating gifts to Ricks College:

  • Outright Specific Bequests designate a specified amount or percentage of cash or other assets to the college; the remainder goes to beneficiaries.

  • Residual Will Instructions allow you to specify that after certain payments have been made for your estate, the balance goes to the college as designated.

Although wills do not provide an income for the donor as a trust might, they do offer other advantages, including distribution of your assets to your satisfaction. [up]

A Life Insurance Policy allows you to name Ricks College as the beneficiary and ultimately make a gift larger than your current circumstances might allow. This option also allows you to tender the gift without restriction, or to designate the priority or program you wish to support. [up]

A Charitable Remainder Unitrust is rapidly becoming one of the most popular options among people making planned, deferred gifts because of inherent benefits to Ricks. [up]

  • This type of trust might allow you to receive an income for your lifetime, and if you desire, for the life of your spouse.
  • Payments might vary depending on fluctuations in interest rates.
  • At the end of the trust term, any remaining assets and income go to Ricks College as you designate.

A Revocable Living Trust allows you complete flexibility to administer your assets during your lifetime.

  • At your death, your trust results in a generous gift to Ricks College.
  • It distributes the remaining assets according to your wishes. [up]

A Charitable Gift Annuity, similar to a remainder trust, differs in that payments are based on a predetermined earnings percentage guaranteed by annuity owner.  In this case, Ricks College is the owner.

  • Income payments can begin when the annuity is established.
  • Or payments can begin in the future, as you request. [up]

A Charitable Lead Trust functions in reverse of a charitable remainder trust.

  • The income from assets you place in trust is directed to Ricks at the beginning rather than at the end of the trust term.
  • Using this option, you can choose a specific term of years or the duration of your life for the income to be given to the college.
  • At the end of the trust term, the asset reverts to you or goes to the beneficiary you have designated.  [up]

A Remainder Interest Deed on a mortgage-free home or farm can allow you to retain use of the property during your lifetime while making it a gift to Ricks College.

  • At your death, the property passes on to the college.
  • This type of deed allows an immediate tax deduction when the interest deed is executed. [up]

A Bargain Sale transaction permits Rick College to purchase a property or asset you elect to sell at a bargain price. The difference between the property’s fair market value and the sale price constitutes the amount of your gift. [up]

Gifts-In-Kind are particularly appealing to corporations and to small businesses. They can elect to contribute equipment, machinery, tools, hardware and software rather than cash.

  • The gift can go to specific colleges, departments, and programs.
  • In this way, the donors are able to substantially support students and faculty while creating awareness for their products.
  • At the same time, they receive charitable tax deductions equivalent to the fair market value of their gifts. [up]
