"Together Again for the First Time"

Jan. 23-26, 29-31 and Feb. 1-2, 2002
7:30 p.m., Kirkham Arena Theater

This original play written by Ricks College graduate Reed McColm tells the story of a mixed family whose grown children encounter each other for the first time for a challenging Christmas vacation.

Tickets go on sale Jan. 7. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Play.
