ON-LINE APPLICATION

Ricks College, BYU, BYU-Hawaii and LDS Business College all use a common admissions application.  The on-line version of this application is available at www.ldsces.edu or by the selecting Start Your Application or Continue Your Application buttons below.  To check your status and verify which parts of your application Ricks College has received, select the Check Your Status button below (this information applies to those items received by Ricks College only, not the other CES schools).  

Majors Requiring Additional Application

In addition to being admitted to Ricks College through the Admissions Office, the following majors require that an additional application be submitted directly to the department.  Please contact the individual department for an application and more information.  

  • Nursing (208) 356-1325

  • Paramedicine (208) 356-1333

  • Interior Design (208) 356-1340
