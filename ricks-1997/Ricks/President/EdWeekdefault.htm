Campus Education Week
Devotional Messages 

Campus Education Week is held annually at Ricks College and is normally scheduled between the first two summer terms.  Patterned after the successful program at Brigham Young University, the week offers daily devotionals, nightly presentations, and over 300 daytime classes.  Topics include home, family, gospel teachings, dealing with challenges, self-improvement, and fine arts.  Education Week is under the administration of Continuing Education.  Enrollment is open to adults and youth ages 14 and older.
