Thanksgiving in Juvenile Books

ARL = Accelerated Reader Level

Juv PZ7.B151Ste
Bailey, Carolyn Sherwin
Stories for Every Holiday
New York: Abingdon Press, 1918
    Thanksgiving contents: Turning the Thanksgiving Table -- Captain Christy's Thanksgiving -- The Country Mouse's Thanksgiving.

Juv PZ7.B81618Art-1983
Brown, Marc Tolon.
Arthur's Thanksgiving/
Boston : Little, Brown, 1983
    Arthur finds his role as director of the Thanksgiving play a difficult one especially since no one will agree to play the turkey.  ARL 2.5

Juv PZ7.B912Jn
Bulla, Clyde Robert
John Billington, Friend of Squanto
New York : Crowell, 1956
    Chapter book for middle to older readers.

Juv PZ7.B912Sq-1982
Bulla, Clyde Robert.
Squanto: Friend of the Pilgrims/
New York : Scholastic, 1954, 1982.
    Chapter book for middle to older readers.

Juv PZ7.D49875Ct-1980
Devlin, Wende. & Devlin, Harry
Cranberry Thanksgiving
New York : Four winds Press, 1980, 1971
    Grandmother almost loses her secret recipe for cranberry bread to one of the guests she and Maggie invite for Thanksgiving dinner. ARL 3.9

Juv F68.D65
Donovan, Frank R.
The Mayflower Compact
New York : Grosset & Dunlap, 1968
    Chapter book for older readers.

Juv PZ5.O44G
Good Stories for Great Holidays
Boston: Houghton Mifflin, 1942
    Contents: The First Harvest Home in Plymouth by W. De Loss Love -- The Master of the Harvest by Mrs. Alfred Gatty -- Saint Cuthbert's Eagle -- How Indian Corn Came Into the World by Henry R. Schoolcraft -- The Nutcracker Dwarf by Count Franz Pocci -- The Pumpkin Pirates, A Tale From Lucian -- The Spirit of the Corn by Harriet Maxwell Convers -- The Horn of Plenty by Ovid.

Juv PS595.T53M47-1993
Hopkins, Lee Bennett. & Shecter, Ben.
Merrily Comes Our Harvest In : Poems for Thanksgiving/
Honesdale, PA : Word song, Boyds Mills Press, 1993, 1978
    Contains 20 poems with Thanksgiving themes: Harvest by Anonymous -- I Like Fall by Aileen Fisher -- November's Gift by Alice Crowell Hoffman -- First Thanksgiving by Myra Cohn Livingston -- First Thanksgiving by Margaret Hillert -- Thanksgiving Time by Anonymous -- The Pumpkin by Robert Graves -- Thanksgiving Thought by Bobbi Katz -- Thanksgiving Magic by Rowena Bastin Bennett -- Thanksgiving Wishes by Arthur Guiterman -- Apple Pie by Else Holmelund Minarik -- What the Little girl Said by Lee Blair -- Turkey Time by Dean Hughes -- The Little Girl and the Turkey by Dorothy Aldis -- Thanksgiving by Margaret Hillert -- Thanksgiving by Marchette Chute -- Day is Done by Lee Bennett Hopkins -- Thanksgiving by Ivy O. Eastwick -- Thanksgiving Day by Annette Wynne -- All in a Word by Aileen Fisher [acrostic].

JUV F68.L9-1964
Lowitz, Sadyebeth & Lowitz, Anson
The Pilgrim's Party; a Really Truly Story
New York : Stein and Day, 1964,C1959

Juv E99.W2S49-1990
Sewall, Marcia.
People of the Breaking Day/
New York : Atheneum, 1990
    A poetic description of the lifestyle and traditional beliefs of the Wampanoag Indians at the time of Massasoit.

JUV F68.S49-1986
Sewall, Marcia
The Pilgrims of Plimoth /
New York : Atheneum, 1986
    Summary: Chronicles the day to day life of the early Pilgrims in the Plimoth Colony. ARL 5.6

JUV F68.W33-1989
Waters, Kate & Kendall, Russ
Sarah Morton's Day : a Day in the Life of a Pilgrim Girl /
New York : Scholastic Inc., 1989
    Summary: A pilgrim girl is followed through a typical day as she milks the goats, cooks and serves meals, learns her letters, and adjusts to her new stepfather. AR RL 3.4

JUV F68.Z5
Ziner, Feenie
The Pilgrims and Plymouth Colony
American Heritage Pub. Co.,1961
