Ricks College News Release
April 28, 2001

Ricks College graduates presented
Leadership Awards 

REXBURG, Idaho — Three Ricks College graduates were presented John L. Clarke Outstanding Student Leadership Awards during graduation ceremonies April 28.

The award recognizes students who have excelled in areas of leadership, service, scholarship and campus citizenship. It is named after the late John L. Clarke who served as president of Ricks College for 27 years. The scholarship includes a two-year, full-tuition scholarship from either Utah State University, Idaho State University or Brigham Young University.

Leadership Awards were presented to the following:

• Lance Martin Taylor, son of Von and Karen Taylor of Newport, Ore., who graduated from Newport High School in 1996. During the past year he has served as student body president at Ricks. His scholarship is for Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah.

• Brady Alan Apostolo, son of Leonard and Penny Apostola of Crestview, Florida. He formerly lived in Randleman, N.C., and graduated from Northwest Guilford High School in 1991. While at Ricks we was new student orientation spirit coordinator, vice president of the Native American Club and vice president of education for Students of Free Enterprise. His scholarship is for Utah State University in Logan, Utah.

• Kimberly Anne Clinger, daughter of Dallas and Brenda Clinger of American Falls, who graduated from American Falls High School in 1999. Her scholarship is for Idaho State University in Pocatello where she will study organizational communications.

Ricks College is the largest private junior college in the United States with 9,000 students. It will become Brigham Young University-Idaho in the Fall Semester when it begins offering bachelor’s degrees.
