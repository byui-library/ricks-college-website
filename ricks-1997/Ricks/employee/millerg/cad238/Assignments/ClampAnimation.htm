Clamp Animation with 3D Studio

 

They say unto him, Caesar's. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's.@, Matthew 22:21.


Use 3D Studio to create a simple animation of the clamp created earlier this semester (see Slide Script assignment) as discussed below and demonstrated in class.  Begin with an exploded assembly of the clamp.   Animate each item in succession as described below:

FRAME

DESCRIPTION OF EVENTS
0-60 CAMERA PAN - Pan camera around entire assembly in circular fashion, returning to starting point
60-90 BODY - Scale body to twice its size, then return to normal size
90-120 SCREW - Move screw to beginning of threads on body, then rotate (threading motion) several complete revolutions until screw protrudes about half way through opening
120-150 CAMERA MOVE - Move camera about 90 degrees to a different isometric view
150-180 PIN - move the pin to the beginning of the hole at the top of the screw, then scale the pin twice its length and half its diameter while inserting it in the hole, finally returning the pin to normal size
180-210 CAMERA MOVE - Move camera to a position Aunderneath@ so that the lower end of the screw and swivel may be clearly seen during their assembly operation
210-240 SWIVEL - Move the swivel very close to the camera while rotating about 1 axis, then about a 2nd axis, and then return the swivel to the assembly position and slide the swivel on the end of the screw
240-300 CAMERA PAN - zoom in closer on completed assembly and then pan completely around part.

Create an avi file of your animation at a resolution of 320x240. Use appropriate lighting effects and materials. Turn in an electronic copy of the max and avi files in the DropBox, use the keyword Clamp.
