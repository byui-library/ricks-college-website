MISSION STATEMENT

The mission of the

BYU-Idaho Reading Center is to:

1. Provide placement, diagnostic testing, and advising.

2. Improve basic reading skills through individual tutoring.

3. Teach university reading skills and learning strategies through classes and individual tutoring.

4. Maintain an environment where students feel comfortable acquiring and practicing college reading skills and strategies.

5. Train university reading tutors.

6. Support the BYU-Idaho Faculty and Administration.

ENGLISH AS A SECOND LANGUAGE (ESL)

ESL is an English development program for students with English as a second language who have met the TOEFL requirements for BYU-Idaho. Its mission is to:

1. Build confidence in oral and written communication.

2. Increase proficiency in oral and written communication.

3. Support the cultural and academic adjustment process.

4. Introduce students to the English development resources available at BYU-Idaho.

5. Prepare students for success in English 111.
