FOLK DANCE FESTIVAL IN FULL SWING

Ricks College and Rexburg have been transformed into an international community this week as the annual Idaho International Folk Dance Festival continues. Teams from across the world have come to Rexburg for this international exchange that is sponsored by the Rexburg Chamber of Commerce. Many of the performances and activities are held on campus, including opening ceremonies that were held last Saturday evening and several full performances during the week.

This year's teams have come from Bulgaria, China, Croatia, Israel, Mexico, Germany, Slovakia and Hawaii. While in Rexburg, they are housed by host families.

Upcoming shows include the International Folk Dance Party at 7 p.m. Wednesday, Aug. 2, in the Hart Auditorium; full evening performances on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights at 7:30 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium; a matinee show Friday at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium; and a community picnic and free band concert on Saturday beginning at 11 a.m. in Smith Park. For ticket information, call the Rexburg Chamber of Commerce at 356-5700.

INSIDEOUT A CAPELLA TO PERFORM

InsideOut, which is made up of six current and former students at Brigham Young University, will perform a matinee concert Saturday at 3 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall of the Snow Center for the Performing Arts. Tickets are $3 for Ricks student and $5 for the general public. They may be purchased from the Ricks College Ticket Office, by calling 356-2230 or over the Internet at www.byui.edu/tickets. They also will be available at the door. InsideOut also will provide the preshow at the Idaho International Folk Dance Festival at 7 p.m. on both Friday and Saturday nights. They will also make a guest appearance at Ricks College Guitars Unplugged on Friday night.
