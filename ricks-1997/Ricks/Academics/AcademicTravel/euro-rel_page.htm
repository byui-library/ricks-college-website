The European Religious History tour explores the wonders of some of the greatest European sites. Each summer we visit 10-13 great cities such as London, Paris, Avignon, Istanbul, Ephesus, Athens, Samos, Rome, Florence, Venice, Geneva, Zurich, Amsterdam, and Munich. We concentrate on the religious sites and history of those great places. A small sampling of the great churches and mosques we examine includes St. Peter's and St. John's Lateran in Rome, Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, and Suleimaniye Mosque in Istanbul, Chartres, Notre Dame, and Sainte Chappelle in Paris, Westminster Abbey and St. Paul's in London, St. John's in Ephesus, the Cathedral of Athens, the Duomo and San Croce in Florence, St. Mark's in Venice, and St. Peter's in Geneva. We see other religious sites such as a monastery and the Papal Palace in Avignon, the old city of Geneva, Mary's house in Ephesus, Vatican City, the square where Savanarola was burned in Florence, and Santa Scala in Rome.

Besides religious sites we examine many of the other wonders the cities of Europe have to offer. We visit some of the best museums in the world, including the Louvre, the Musee d'Orsay, Vatican Museum, the Uffizi, the British Museum, and the National Gallery in London. we visit castles and palaces such as the Doge's palace in Venice, Fort St. Andre in Avignon, the Topkapi and Dolmabahce in Istanbul, Buckingham Palace in London, Versailles near Paris, Pitti Palace in Florence, and Nymphenburg Palace in Munich. We have seen the Roman ruins of Ephesus and Rome. We also visit bazaars, ancient temples, mausoleums, prisons, Roman aqueducts, ancient and modern theaters, and beautiful squares. Finally we have enjoyed breathtaking gardens, lakes, mountains, rivers, and beaches.

When the tour is all done we will have studied Islam and Eastern Orthodoxy. We will have learned about the Avignon papacy, the Great Schism, how the architecture of cathedrals compares to temples, the development of Catholicism, a little art history, the Reformation, the Renaissance, relics, and tons of European history and culture.
