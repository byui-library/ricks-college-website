This allows you to add and drop classes under the same restrictions imposed by the Ricks College telephone registration system. To continue just follow these simple steps. After you are done adding and dropping classes you may click on the Display Class Schedule to obtain a full listing of your classes.

  • First enter your Ricks College ID number. For most US students this is your Social Security number. For others this is the Ricks College assigned number.
  • Next enter your Personal Identification Number (PIN). This is your month and year of your birth, unless you have changed it. This is a 4 digit number, so March, 1978 would be keyed 0378. If you have forgotten your PIN, please contact the Registrars Office at (208)356-1007 for instructions. The Registrars Office hours are:
    • 8:00am to 5:00pm - Monday through Friday
  • Choose a term and year for which you wish to register.
  • Click on ADD, DROP, or Display Class Schedule.
  • Some classes are offered for variable credit. You must choose how many credits you wish for these classes. You may increase this value after classes start, but you cannot decrease it.
  • Click on SUBMIT to process your request.
  • If there are any problems processing your request, the computer will inform you. Simply push BACK on your browser to correct the problem.
