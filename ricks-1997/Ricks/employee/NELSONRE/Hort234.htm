Course Description:  Greenhouse construction, environmental control and plant culture.  Emphasis will also be given to crop selection and scheduling, site selection, and management, as well as the development, scope, and future of the greenhouse industry. 

 

Objectives for Course:

1.      Provide insight into the field of greenhouse management sufficient to enable the student to decide if this is the appropriate field of endeavor for him or her.

2.      Identify different types of greenhouse construction, equipment and supplies. 

3.      Be able to manipulate the greenhouse environment to provide favorable conditions for plant growth and development.

4.      Practice good cultural practices, e.g. media selection, watering, fertilizing, lighting, growth regulators, pest control, etc.

5.      Be able to make management decisions about climate control systems, crop selection and rotation, site selection, marketing, etc. 

6.      Be able to grow common greenhouse crops including cultural practices, rotation, schedules, and marketing.

7.      Instill an awareness of the rapid evolution of production and marketing technology in the greenhouse industry, the global shifts occurring in production and marketing, the constant need for new knowledge to prosper with in these changes, and train the student in the techniques of accessing the required information over time.

LAB TOPICS

1.      Tour and evaluate structure, layout, covering materials, benching systems, etc. in the Ricks College greenhouses.

2.      Greenhouse layout and construction.

3.      Schedule, plant, transplant, and assume total responsibility for greenhouse crop.

4.      Heating systems and heat requirement calculation.

5.      Cooling and ventilating greenhouse structures.

6.      Media—mixing, sterilization, testing, and amending.

7.      Fertilization and CO2 enrichment-calculating parts per million, fertilizer injector

8.      Light and temperature control and system integration.

9.      Disease, pest and insect control.

10.  Pots, containers, and benches.

11.  Crop production and rotation schedule for greenhouse crop(s).

12.  Research paper and condensed growers guide (2 page maximum) for two greenhouse crops as assigned by instructor.
