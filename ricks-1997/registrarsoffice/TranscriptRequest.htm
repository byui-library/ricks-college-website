TRANSCRIPT REQUEST

 

A transcript can be requested by filling out the *Transcript Request Form, and then sending it to the address list at the top of the form.

 

There is no charge for BYU-I transcripts.

 

To Mail… Send completed Transcript Request Form to:

BYU-Idaho

Registrar’s Office

Rexburg, ID 83460-0870

 

To Fax… Fax completed Transcript Request Form to:

BYU-Idaho: Registrars Office

(208) 496-1035

 

To Go Online…Follow the Instructions below.

1. Go to the BYU-Idaho home page (www.byui.edu).

2. Click on the Registration link at the bottom of the page (www.byui.edu/spencer/LOCAL/WARN.htm).

3. Then go through the warning page and enter your ID and PIN.

                                           4. Click on the “Transcript Request” link.

                                           5. Fill out form and click “Request Transcripts”.
