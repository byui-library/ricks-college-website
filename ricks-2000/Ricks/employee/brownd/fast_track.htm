If a student enrolls in Ricks College with some college credit already earned, it may be possible for that student to graduate in one calendar year (for two-year degrees) or in three calendar years (for four year degrees) or less.  For a more complete description of the Fast Track program, and how to enroll in it, consult the Admission Office’s Fast Track web page. 

If you are interested in becoming a Fast Track student in Mathematics or in Mathematics Education at Ricks College, then please e-mail me at the address above.  Tell me as much of the following as may apply: 

1)       your name

2)       what your educational goals are

3)       whether you have been accepted to Ricks, and if so, when you will arrive

4)       which AP exams you have already taken and passed

5)       which AP exams you plan to take, or have taken but haven’t received word on

6)       what college-level courses you already have credit for, and by what means (example: High school Calculus, Political Science 105 (Liberty Community College), etc.)

7)       how I can reach you (telephone number, e-mail address, home address, etc.) 

I will respond as soon as I can, and give you a rough idea of the courses you would need to take, as well as how many semesters and summer terms would likely be required for you to graduate under the Fast Track program. 

When school is in session, please allow five business days for a response.  If you e-mail me during the summer or between terms, please understand that I may be away from campus.  I will check my e-mail as often as I can, but it may be a couple of weeks before I can get back to you.
