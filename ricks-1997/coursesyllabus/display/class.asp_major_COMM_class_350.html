SMALL GROUP COMMUNICATION
SMALL GRP COMM
CLASS CODE: COMM 350 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: COMMUNICATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Analysis of theory and practice of human communication in a small group setting, focusing on leadership, participation and evaluation, with emphasis in group norms, roles, cohesion and conflict.
DESCRIPTION: How to organize, lead, participate in, and evaluate small group discussions, conferences, and meetings.
TOPICS: Group Work (Problem Solving).
Group Work (Service).
Group Work (Scripture Study).
Group Work (Social).
Group Work (Discussion).
OBJECTIVES: This course will provide basic facets of small group communication. Use of the textbook, guest speakers, and active participation of small group opportunities will give the student chances to discover more about the principles and processes of the communication in small groups.
REQUIREMENTS: Academic Group Presentation (no more than 15 minutes).
Portfolio (half of your grade).
Group Debrief.
PREREQUISITES: Comm 120
