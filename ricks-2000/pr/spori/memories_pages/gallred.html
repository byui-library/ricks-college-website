Grant Allred (72)

My physics instructor (at Ricks) spoke of the Spori building being made out of a sandstone that should last at least 200 years. I remember going to look at the building’s exterior rock and wondering about the cold winters, and how such predictions could be made. For odd reasons in 1971 there were a number of us students in the dorm (Rigby Hall) that figured Jesus Christ would come in 1976, or at least about the year 2000, and it seemed to me that Spori Hall would last into the millennium.

Grant Allred (72)
Arnold, Md.
