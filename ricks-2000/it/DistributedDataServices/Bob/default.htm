Bob is the Internet Services Manager.  He has been at Ricks College since April 1998.  Prior to coming to Ricks, he was employed at the Idaho National Engineering Laboratory and the Army Communications Command at Fort Huachuca, Arizona.  He graduated from Idaho State University in Computer Science and taught part time at the University of Idaho for 15 years.  Responsibilities at Ricks include management of the web servers, and the Intranet and External web sites.  Bob and Beverly have five children and enjoy camping, family history, and their grandchildren.  One of Bob’s favorite activities at Ricks is working with the student helpers who assist faculty and staff build their home pages.
