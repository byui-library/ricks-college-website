COURSE DESCRIPTION

    A dance class where social dance steps are taught, social etiquette developed, and an understanding of social dance skills is refined. The main goal of the class is to teach students the skills of participating at a Church sponsored dance, and understanding their responsibility in bringing the spirit of the Lord to the dance. D&C 88:36-39.

OBJECTIVES

1. Dance and verbally explain the following basics:

    6 directions--forward, backward, left turn, right turn, sideways left, sideways right.

    8 positions--closed, semi-open (promenade), left-side, right-side, open (RL & LR), full open, two hand, and sweetheart.

    8 components that can be done in any rhythm--walk, rock, arch (loop), wheel, pivot, hesitate, chasse, grapevine.

    proper closed position
    rules of walking
    how the upper body moves in relation to the feet

2. Dance these couple dances:

    Walk, Cha Cha, Waltz, Swing (single and triple rhythm), Foxtrot, Country two step, Polka. Other couple dances are; Teton Mt. Stomp, Elvira, Bingo Waltz, Jump Polka, Broken Ankle, Salty Dog Rag.

3. Dance 3 line dances.

4. Recognize and explain the criteria of choosing music in the following areas:

    Danceable rhythms--Why a Cha Cha is a Cha Cha, etc. How to hear the rhythm.
    Moral judgment of music--What’s in the music that leads away from the spirit

5. Dance at least 3 non-partner folk dances: MaNavu, Little Bird, Hashual, etc.

6. Recognize and explain the proper etiquette and responsibilities of an uplifting dance:
Asking to dance, escorting on and off the floor, floor is for dancing and sides are for standing, proper lighting, no hats, proper behavior, etc.
