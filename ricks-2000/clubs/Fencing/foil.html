Foil Fencing
Target: The foil target is restricted to the torso; it extend in front from the upper limit of the collar out to the arm seams and down to the lines of the groin, and in back form the upper limit of the collar out to the arm seams and down to a horizontal line that passes across the top of the hip bones.
Scoring:A point is scored when the tip of the attacker lands on the target of the defender. The attacker has to have the right of way when the tip of his foil lands on the target. He can gain the right of way by extending his arm first and attacking or by taking the right of way the one attacking.
Types of touches:There are really two types of touches: on target and off target. When a touch is on target and the owner of the weapon has the right of way there is a point scored. If the blade slaps the body of the person being touched the touch is invalid. Also when the tip of the weapon touches the arms, legs, head, or other extremities the touch is invalid and the action stops.
Defense and offense:Offensive actions include lunges, jumping lunges, thrust, and disengage. The thrust is the simplest of the attacks and can be very fast. The lunge and the jumping lunge when combined with either the disengage or simple thrust can be a fast attack that covers a large amount of distance.

The main defense is the parry. There are two main parries, the simple parry and circular parries. The simple parries can be used against simple attacks to avoid being touched as well gain the opportunity to get a point. The circular parries move in a circular pattern to protect your target area.
