Materials Needed for an Online Class

1) A Computer

On-campus computer access
- Computers are available on-campus for you to use for your online course.

McKay West wing 3rd floor (approx 200)
East wing, room 190 (approx 25)
East wing 2nd floor (approx 16)
East wing 1st floor (approx 10)

Off-campus computer access
- You can access your online course from any computer that has Internet access.

 
2) Textbook(s)

Visit the Ricks College Bookstore and get the book(s) for your section.

Textbooks can be picked up at the Bookstore – or mailed to you (call 208-356-2211)

All courses (except HS 131 and CS 100) have at least one textbook. The list of textbooks required for each class can be found under the "Textbooks" section (see "Textbooks" button on the left).

 
3) CD-ROM

Pick up the CD-ROM for your course in McKay 321 – Cost = $3.
To have a CD mailed, call(208) 356-1200 or e-mail onlinehelp@ricks.edu. Cost for CD and mailing is $5.
All courses (except CS100) have a CD-ROM that supplements the online course materials.
Occasionally there are video or audio clips associated with the course that are too large to be downloaded over the Internet. These are found on the CD-ROM. Just put the disc in the CD-ROM drive when you are accessing your course. When there is a video or other item to access, the course will automatically pull it off of the CD-ROM. You will not need to install anything from this CD.
