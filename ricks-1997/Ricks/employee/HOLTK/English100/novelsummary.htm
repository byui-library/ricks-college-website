NOVEL SUMMARY ASSIGNMENT

THE ASSIGNMENT

Write a one page summary of the novel you read. It should be at least 250 words and no more than 375. It must be typed, double spaced, with one inch margins. Label your summary with the title and author, and at the bottom of your paper, sign your name to verify you read the entire book and the summary work is your own. Staple this Assignment Sheet to your paper and turn it in to your instructor. Do not put your summary in a folder.

 

THE GUIDELINES

_____ 1. 10 pts. Summary demonstrates an understanding of major characters.

_____ 2. 5 pts. The summary includes a brief mention of setting.

_____3. 10 pts. Major plot events are discussed.

_____4. 5 pts. Resolutions to major conflicts and problems are explained.

_____5. 15 pts. A personal understanding of the theme (a generalization you can make about life, based on the story) is demonstrated. Include at least one quotation from an important passage and explain its significance to you. Provide background information (who says it and when). Include page numbers in parentheses.

_____6. 5 pts. The summary does not merely list topics or report chronological events. The summary is specific and shows that the reader has read the book and digested the content; it is concise, objective, and complete.
