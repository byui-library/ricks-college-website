Advanced Grammar and Composition
ADV GRAM/COMP
CLASS CODE: SPAN 321 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Intensive study and practice with grammar, vocabulary, and writing.
DESCRIPTION: Intensive study and practice with grammar, vocabulary, and writing.
TOPICS: Grammar topics that are difficult for the non-native; basics of good composition; Spanish syntax
OBJECTIVES: 1. To prepare students to represent the Church and BYU-I well; 2. To develop an understanding of and the ability to express how the grammar works; 3. to speak with confidence; 4. to write correctly; 5. to read with comprehension.
REQUIREMENTS: Homework, quizzes, compostions, and exams; peer teaching, tutoring, and editing.
PREREQUISITES: Spanish 202 or 302. Also open to native speakers.
