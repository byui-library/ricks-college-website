FOOD AND HOUSING

Food and housing will be provided by the Quest program while the student is at a remote site such as Badger Creek and Vashon Island.  Food and housing will be the responsibility of the student while studying at  campus.

For more information about on-campus housing click here. When you call the On-Campus Housing office mention you are a Quest participant for a special discount.  For more information about off-campus housing click here.  Inform the personnel in the office of the Ricks College Housing Department or Rexburg Housing that you are seeking to rent an apartment on a block.  This will allow them enough information to aid you in finding just the right living situation for you.
