1. MISSION STATEMENT FOR RELIGION 130:

Mission Preparation includes 2 main areas of emphasis:

1.The Spirit (D&C 42:14) where we will work to:

A. Strengthen Testimonies

B. Increase Worthiness

C. Learn to recognize and respond to the Spirit

D. Teach the value of Faith and hard work

E. Understand the divine attributes & try to acquire them.

F. Understand Covenants and the relationship of obedience and closeness to Christ

2.The Word (D&C 11:21)understanding what God has given us, we will

A. Read entire Book of Mormon this semester (with class focus)

B. Understand and memorize the 42 discussion scriptures.

C. Read & understand entire Student Manual.

D. Learn 6 Discussions & deepen understanding of the doctrines taught in discussions.

2. EXAMS - 10% of the Final Grade - Exams will be handed out in the classroom, and taken in Testing center. Dates are indicated on the accompanying reading schedule.

3. READING - 50 % of final grade Daily read for 30 minutes in the Book of Mormon, then study missionary information & scriptures for 30 minutes. Keep a spiritual journal daily (and bring to each class) of the things of your soul from this reading time. READING AND JOURNAL TOGETHER GET THE 50%.

4. SCRIPTURE MEMORIZATION- 10% of the final grade. Memorize one scripture every day during this semester. This means 7 days per week and does not include those we will learn in class. To show accomplishment of this task you will pass the quiz on the scriptures given at the beginning of each class.

5. PERSONAL IMPROVEMENT PROJECT - 10% of your final grade. Prayerfully pick a particular area of your life that you wish to improve. Look for an area that would most help your missionary preparation. Write a one page explanation of what you hope to accomplish and how you wish to do it. You may wish to hand this in as an daily plan outline which will include some time each day working on an improvement in your desired areas. Turn this in by OCT.1, 1997. Make this a measurable goal. At the end of the term write another one page summary explaining how you did

6. MISSIONARY EFFORT 10% OF FINAL GRADE. Try for a Missionary experience every day with an absolute minimum of 1 per week. Ask the G.Q.'s to someone, send a letter, send a Book of Mormon, bear your testimony, help a "less active", remember one meaning of L.D.S. is "LETS DO SOMETHING"!

7. DAILY SERVICE 10% OF FINAL GRADE. Absolutely, every single day, find a service to give. Always be looking, don't stop with one, try for those where no one will know you did it. Remember, where there is no service, there is no spirituality.

ABSENCES "For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them" Matt. 18:20 -0 or 1 absence raises earned grade 1 notch. 2-3 absences grade remains as earned. With 4th absence grade drops one notch with each additional absence. It is most important that you attend. .

DAILY READING - The scriptures bring guidance, peace and strength. If you read at least 30 minutes in the Book of Mormon every single day this semester, (or repent by reading 1 hour and 15 minutes the next day if you miss) you will receive the chance to earn a full "A" grade for this course. This is the only way to get an "A" this semester. You must do all the other work and read daily to qualify.

CLASS PARTICIPATION - Plan to make at least 1 significant contribution to the class each week, sharing ideas, insights and conclusions found in your study. This will keep your grade from dropping a notch.
