Materials Needed for your Online Class

 
1) A Computer

On-campus computer access
- Computers are available in the library on-campus for you to use for your online course.

Off-campus computer access
- You can access your online course from any computer that has Internet access.

 
2) Textbook(s)

Visit the Ricks College Bookstore and get the book(s) for your section.

Textbooks can be picked up at the Bookstore – or mailed to you (call 208-356-2211)

 
3) CD-ROM

Pick up the CD-ROM for your course in McKay 321 – Cost = $3.
To have a CD mailed, call(208) 356-1200 or e-mail onlinehelp@ricks.edu. Cost for CD and mailing is $5.

Occasionally there are video or audio clips associated with the course that are too large to be downloaded over the Internet. These are found on the CD-ROM. Just put the disc in the CD-ROM drive when you are accessing your course. When there is a media item to access, the course will automatically pull it off of the CD-ROM. You will not need to install anything from this CD.
