Looking beautiful on the big day

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Makeup can not only enhance beauty, but can add to the confidence of the bride. Here are 10 steps to a beautiful bride:

Step One: Concealer
• Use a concealer under the eyes to hide darkness. Also, at the sides of the nose and under the bottom lip, right above the chin. Put a little on the top of the forehead. Doing this, will cause the highlighted areas to glow a little bit and gives the face more dimension and radiance.

Step two: Foundation
• Use a cream foundation, but apply it very lightly with a sponge in a downward motion to prevent make-up from entering the pores. Use just enough to go over the concealer so the skin tone looks even and natural. No white should show now from the concealer — just a glow or a lightness. Also be sure to apply some base on top of your eyelids.

Step three: Powder
• To set the foundation, take a powder puff and wrap it around the index finger on the the writing hand. Next, take some loose powder and put it in the palm of the other hand. Using the puff, swirl it into the powder. Apply the puff to just-foundationed face. Press lightly into skin with a rolling motion.

Step four: Blush
• Gently sweep a brush into a pot of soft color. Smile and apply the blush in the apple of the cheeks. If the face shape tends to be more round than angular, apply a little more blush at an angle in an upsweep along the cheekbones. This sculpts the face a little more than applying the blush on the apples of the cheeks.

Step five: Eyeshadow
• First, put a light eyeshadow color over the entire eye. This gives a neutral palette to work on. Then, add a soft brown in the crease of the eye (the brow bone), and on the side of the outer eyelid so it makes a sideways V. Imagine taking a pencil and lining it up to the outside of the right eye (where eyebrow ends) and turning it on a diagonal so the other side of the pencil lines up with the outside of the right nostril. Look at the eye with the pencil lined up against it on a diagonal. No eyeshadow should go beyond this line. Be sure that when brown shadow is applied, it is blended and kept within the correct area.

This V shading creates a nice, subtle look for the eyes. To add a little more depth to the eye, put a goldish color on the lid and a slight bit of shell pink under the brow as a highlighter. This helps to soften the whole eye.

Step six: Eyeliner
• After the shadow is in place, apply the eye pencil at the base of the bottom lashes, going half-way in (stopping at the pupil). Line the base of the top lashes in the same way, stopping at the pupil. If the liner is brought all the way in, it may make the eyes look smaller. By lining the eye half-way, it creates a more open look. After using the pencil, take a thin square-tipped brush and blend, blend, blend the pencil. This creates a more natural look. To increase the drama, go over the blended pencil with a little bit of brown eye shadow. This sets the blended pencil and adds a little more depth to the eye.

Step seven: Eyelashes
• Open the eyelash curler and put it at the base of the top lash. Be sure not to grab any skin. When using mascara, don’t pump the wand — it only makes the mascara clump. When putting the mascara on, start at the base of the lash and brush outward. With the mascara brush, press the lashes toward the eye, mimicking the eyelash curler. Always use a lash comb to remove clumps while the mascara is still wet. Start at the base of the lashes and comb outward.

Step eight: Lip liner
• Use small feathery strokes — not one continuous line. Softly shape the lips. If a mistake is made, just wipe the liner off with a tissue and start again. Whatever shape the lips take, make sure it’s a natural one. The liner should not be drawn beyond the natural lip line.

Step nine: Lipstick
• Blend the pencil on the lips and fill in with a matching color.

Step ten: Finished
• Put on that gorgeous dress, touch up your hair, and go kiss your groom.
