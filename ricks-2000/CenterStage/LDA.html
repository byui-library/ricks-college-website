Lex De Azevedo's "Hosanna"

Women's Week Concert
Saturday, March 23, 2002
3 and 7 p.m., Hart Auditorium

Noted composer Lex de Azevedo returns with his oratorio “Hosanna, The Life of Christ, Part 3” as part of Women's Week. The concert will feature Jenny Oaks Baker on violin, soloist George Dyer, the Millennium Choir, and a full orchestra.

Tickets go on sale as part of Women's Week. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Opera. Concert dress suggested.
