THE PLUMBING OF A GEYSER: GEOLOGY IN YELLOWSTONE PARK 
Study and observe the formation of geysers (including Old Faithful), fumaroles, hot springs, and mud pots, within Yellowstone, a setting that surpasses all the world’s other geothermal areas in sheer number of spouting displays. 

IN THE BEGINNING: EARLY HISTORY OF YELLOWSTONE PARK 
Study the history of the country’s first national park, focusing on our earliest knowledge of the area from Indians, mountain men, and fur trappers of the 1800's to its position today as the destination for visitors from around the world. 

CAUGHT IN THE MIDDLE: WILD ANIMALS IN YELLOWSTONE PARK 
Known for wildlife and recreation, Yellowstone is an important case study of modern conflict between animals and people. Some wildlife are endangered, some dangerous, and others controversial. Study the issues; visit the park to observe its wildlife.
