FAST TRACK

Fast Track is a program designed to assist students who earn college credit while in high school to graduate with an Associate Degree or attain junior level status in one calendar year, including summer enrollements. Upon completing the Fast Track program by earning an Associate Degree or reaching junior level status, students may continue their education on the track for which they were originally admitted.


How Does Fast Track Work?

Fast Track students enroll consecutively for one calendar year and earn their Associate Degree or attain junior level status in one calendar year. Because Fast Track is an accelerated program, it is generally most feasible for students who earn college credit in high school.

How to Prepare for Fast Track

High school students can best prepare for Fast Track by earning college credit while in high school. This can be done in several ways, including:

  • Attend BYU-Idaho during the summer following your junior year in high school
  • Take Advanced Placement (AP) test and score a 3 or higher
  • Participate in concurrent enrollment programs with your local college/university


How Do I Enroll in Fast Track?

Students interested in Fast Track should follow the process outlined below to enroll.

  • Be admitted as a full-time day student before signing up for Fast Track
  • Select a major from the Fast Track Majors List
  • Contact the Fast Track advisor for your Major
  • With the assistance of your advisor, complete and submit a signed contract to the Fast Track Coordinator in the Admissions Office.


What are the Benefits of Fast Track?

Contracted Fast Track students automatically qualify for a one-half tuition scholarship for all summer terms attended. The scholarship is redeemable by submitting a copy of your summer schedule to the Scholarship Office.

Also, because Fast Track students will complete their education more quickly, non-tuition costs (i.e., room and board) can be reduced by up to 25%.
