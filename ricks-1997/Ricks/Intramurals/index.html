Welcome to the Ricks College Intramural Sports Page

The Intramural Sports Office, located in Room 227 on the Hart Physical Education Building, is the Administration Center for all men's, women's, and co-ed Intramural Sports Activities.

Office Hours for Winter 2001: The Intramural Office will be open generally from 9:00 A.M.- 5:00 P.M. Monday thru Thursday.  If you can not find us or these hours don't fit your needs be sure to email us.

Schedules:  Schedules and other pertinent intramural information is posted on the intramural bulletin board located on either side of the intramural office entrance as well as on this web page.

Registration:  Entry forms for all intramural activities are available in the intramural office.  Entries must be submitted by 5 pm on the deadline date for each activity.
Team Competition is carried on through the Wards on campus as well as through Independently organized groups. For further information contact student staff in the Intramural office or call ext. 2115.  (off campus 356-2115)  Or reach us by e-mail intramural@ricks.edu

Gaining that "freshman fifteen?" Come participate in  kickboxing.   We offer kickboxing in the dance studio. Kickboxing starts January 2 to April 26.   Mon-Thur from  6:30 to 7:30 AM and  from 5:00 - 6:00 PM and 6:00 - 7:00 PM, Come get in shape!
