BYU-IDAHO CELLULAR TELEPHONE CONTRACT

Ricks College has entered into a new agreement for cellular telephone service with Edge Wireless Communications Co. The new contract is for digital cellular service and provides significant improvements in equipment, price and features over our previous contract.

BASIC GUIDELINES OF RICKS COLLEGE CELLULAR TELEPHONE PLAN

Top of Page

BYU-Idaho has a contract for cellular telephone service with Edge Wireless Communications. In order to increase the number of minutes used under the contract and therefore to obtain the lowest possible overall price and as a service to its employees, the college makes the service available to its employees at cost. Also, the College handles all billings and collections for this program. Some of the major provisions of the cellular contract are:

All non-temporary employees and retirees of BYU-Idaho are eligible to obtain cellular service under the contract. All applications for cellular service are subject to credit approval by the Accounting Office.

Employees receive a base model phone at no cost for each service line activated. The phone becomes the property of the employee after 12 months of continuous service under the plan. In the event of early termination, the employee must return the phone in good working order or pay the cost of the phone which is $99.99.

Eligible employees may obtain cellular phones for use by immediate family members. However, the responsibility for payment of charges incurred remains with the employee. For purposes of the cellular service agreement, immediate family includes: The employee’s spouse and the employee and spouse’s children, parents and siblings.

There is an initial limit of 4 phones per employee. More phones may be obtained with the approval of the Director of Accounting Services.

Details of charges for each phone in service will appear on the employee’s personal account statement. Charges are billed to the account monthly. Billing period ends on 25th of month.

Applications to obtain a cellular phone are available at the Accounting Office (KIM 130)

 

***Important Note*** Be sure to fully charge your battery (overnight) before using your phone for the first time. This will help assure maximum battery life.***

 

BUSINESS USE PHONES:

Top of Page

The primary purpose of the BYU-Idaho Cellular phone contract is to provide phones for College Employees to use while conducting College business. A specific application form is necessary to obtain a business phone. All business phone applications must be approved by the user’s department head and by the appropriate vice president. There are two types of business phones:

100% business use phone – The phone is intended to be used exclusively for College business and the user’s department pays the entire amount of the bill each month. Personal use of these phones is expected to be minimal or non-existent. In the event of personal use not approved by a supervisor, the user is expected to reimburse the College for the cost of the personal use.

Subsidized personal phones – All charges incurred for the phone are billed to the personal account of the user. However, the user’s department pays a subsidy each month towards the user’s bill. The subsidy will not exceed the greater of the maximum amount approved on the application form or the total cell phone bill for the month.

 

BASIC PROVISIONS OF RATE PLAN:

Top of Page

 

Access line charge $5.99 per month, plus applicable taxes. The access charge provides the user with a cellular telephone handset and a phone number. No air time minutes are included.

Calls made from within the 208 area code plus the Jackson Hole area to anywhere in that area will be charged $.08 per minute.

Calls made from Idaho or the expanded calling area (Montana, Wyoming, Utah, Nevada, California, Oregon, and Washington) to any location within any of those states will be charged $.10 per minute. There will be no additional roaming or long distance charges on these calls.

Calls made from Idaho or the expanded calling area to any state outside the expanded calling area will be charged $.10 per minute plus $.17 per minute for long distance.

Local calls made outside the expanded calling area will be charged $.60 per minute. There will be no daily roaming fees or other charges.

Long distance calls made outside the expanded calling area will be charged $.60 per minute plus $.17 per minute long distance.

All calls received within the 208 area code plus the Jackson Hole area will be charged $.08 per minute.

Calls received in the expanded calling area  will be charged $.10 per minute. There will be no call delivery (long distance) charge for these calls..

Calls received outside the expanded calling area will be billed $.60 per minute plus a $.17 per minute call delivery (long distance) charge.

In service areas where the technologies are available, this plan also includes: caller ID, message waiting, call waiting, 3 way calling, call forwarding, voice mail, 2 way short messaging service (phone to phone, phone to computer, computer to phone - 250 messages / month - there after $.10 / message), no other service or line charges, no activation or disconnect charges, no roaming or access charges in all 50 states

A "Free Use Zone" option is available for an additional $9.99 per month. This option allows the user to make unlimited calls from within a designated free use zone to all of the 208 area code and the Jackson Hole area. There will also be no air time charge for any call received in the free use zone. Collect calls do not apply.

The free use zone consists of specific antennas that cover the BYUI campus, most of Rexburg, and some of the surrounding area. See "Free Use Zone" Billing Explanation for additional explanation.

 

EXPLANATION OF PLAN FEATURES

Top of Page

Conference Calling – Allows you to talk with more than one person at the same time. Air time and any applicable long distance tolls will be charged to each call.

Call Waiting – Similar to call waiting on conventional phones. A tone will notify you if you receive an incoming call while you are on another call.

Call Forwarding – allows you to send all your incoming calls to a different phone number. Air time is charged the duration of any forwarded calls.

Caller ID – Allows you to see the name or phone number of all incoming calls. Screen your calls easily with Caller ID. This feature will work in all areas where the local provider has the technology to provide it. Combine Caller ID with Call Waiting and you'll know if you should click over and answer the other call.

Voice Mail – You may select this option at no extra charge. If you do not answer your phone by the 4th ring, the incoming call is routed to your voice mail box. You can receive your messages by pressing and holding the "1" key on your phone. If you did not select this feature when you signed up for service under the Edge contract, you may add it by calling Johnnie Cahoon at ext. 1048. Once activated by Edge, the feature may be turned on and off from the cell phone.

You will not be charged for air time for the time it takes a caller to leave a voice mail message.  Calls to retrieve voice mail messages with your cell phone will be charged air time at the same rates as other calls.   You may also retrieve your messages from a regular phone at no charge.   Those who have the "Unlimited Air Time Zone" feature will not be charged air time for the call to retrieve messages if the call is made from Rexburg.

Message Waiting Indicator – In home area and other areas where technology is available. Lets you know the minute you get a new voice mail message. Consists of an icon that appears on the top line of the phone’s display when a new voice mail message has been received.

Short Messaging Service (SMS) – This feature allows the user to send and receive short e-mail messages up to 150 characters in length. Text messages can also be sent directly between Edge Wireless phones. With this feature your phone can essentially function as an alphanumeric pager. The base model (Nokia 5165) phone and all Nokia upgrade phones support these features. Other available phones may support this option.  Check specifications for the phone you are interested in at the manufacturers' website (Website links below). 

PHONES

Top of Page

The base model cellular telephone handset provided with conversion from US Cellular to Edge Wireless is a Nokia model 5165. This is a tri-mode/dual band phone, which means it operates on both the 1900 Mhz and 800 MHz bands and it can access both TDMA digital and analog networks. The standard battery provides standby and talk time capacities that are many times better than the analog phones that were previously used under the BYU-Idaho plan.

There is no charge at time of sign-up for the base model phone. However the employee must sign an agreement to maintain service under the plan for a minimum of 12 months. In the event of early termination, the employee must return the phone in good working order to the Accounting office or pay the cost of the phone which is $99.99.

All new phones carry a one year manufacturer’s warranty. Should the phone fail after the warranty has expired, Edge has agreed that they will ensure that all active service lines have a working phone available. Replacement phones may be used equipment at Edge’s option, but will be comparable in quality and level of features to the base model phone. Employees who upgrade to more expensive phones will receive the same assurance of working phone availability after warranty expiration, but the replacement will be comparable to the base model unit only. The guarantee of a working replacement does not extend to phones that have obviously been abused or neglected.

The BYU-Idaho contract with Edge provides for upgrades to other phones at a significant discount. Information about pricing for phone upgrades and accessories is available at:  BYU-Idaho Prices on Upgrades and Accessories  

ACCESSORIES

Top of Page

The most commonly purchased accessory is a vehicle adaptor that allows you to connect your phone to your vehicle’s cigarette lighter adapter to recharge the phone’s battery. The phone can be used to make calls while it is charging.

Those who are obtaining a cell phone for safety reasons, such as to call for help in the event of car trouble on the highway, are urged to obtain a vehicle adaptor. Cell phone batteries gradually loose their charge over time, even if the phone is never turned on. A phone left unused in a vehicle glove box for several weeks may not have sufficient remaining battery life to make a call when it is most urgently needed. With a vehicle adaptor, the phone can be used as long as the vehicle battery has power.

If you have a vehicle adaptor for a Nokia model 252 or newer phone, it will work with the Nokia digital phones provided by Edge Wireless.
