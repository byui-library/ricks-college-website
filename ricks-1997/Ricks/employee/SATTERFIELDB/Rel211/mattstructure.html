The Narratives and Discourses
of the Gospel of Matthew

Bruce Satterfield
Department of Religious Education,
Ricks College


 


        Matthew structured his Gospel in a careful and skillful manner. The book is divided into three main segments: (1) the birth of Christ narrative (ch.s 1-2); (2) the ministry of Christ (ch.s 3-25); and the Passion of Christ (ch.s 26-28). The first segment, the birth of Christ, acts as the prologue to the ministry of Christ while the last segment, the Passion of Christ, serves as the climax to his ministry. The majority of the Gospel focuses on the ministry of Christ among the Jews. Matthew has clearly divided this segment into five large blocks of narrative followed by discourse. Each section of discourse concludes with a formula found nowhere else in the Gospel: "And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings . . ." (7:28-29; cf. 11:1, 13:53; 19:1-2; 26:1-2).
