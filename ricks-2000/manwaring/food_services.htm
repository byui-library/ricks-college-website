Our professional staff of 65 full and part time employees eagerly await  the opportunity to serve you. We consistently observe that students feel better, are healthier, have more energy, and do better in school when the principles of proper nutrition are followed.

Food Services is conveniently located in the Manwaring Student Center. It features a full-service, all-you-care-to-eat cafeteria, The Galley; two restaurants, The Nordic Landing & The Viking Place; a bagel kiosk, JoLynn's Bakery & Bagel Shoppe; and a convenience store, Thor's Court. Funds can be deposited to your student ID card account and used at any of the above locations.

If you have any questions about the Galley plans or any area within Food Services, please call the Ricks College Food Services Administrative Office at (208) 356-2251 Monday through Friday from 10:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m. or e-mail us at FoodServices@ricks.edu. We would be happy to respond to your questions.
