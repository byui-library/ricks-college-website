If you love Ricks and are committed to helping the college and its alumni "continue the tradition, strengthening links between former students, Ricks Alumni, and the college's growing legacy,  We would love to have you join the Student Alumni Association.  The only requirement is that you be a current student of Ricks College.

To become involved with our association or if you have questions about the Student Legacy Fund (SLF), just call 208-356-1128 or send us an e-mail.  We would also love to see you in person.  We are in the Kimball Building room 220.  We look forward to hearing from you!
