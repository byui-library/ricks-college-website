Given your target market, describe each of the following aspects of product strategy thoroughly.  As you write your detailed product strategy, write each section in complete sentences, with rationale for every part of your strategy.  

Product
In one or two paragraphs, describe your client's product (include the want it satisfies, guarantees, level of service)  Distinguish betweens goods and services

Product Classification
Describe which product classification your product or service would most likely fall into for the majority of your target consumers.

Product Line
Discuss the depth of your product line in terms of styles, sizes, colors, etc  

Product Life Cycle
At what stage of the industry life cycle is your product competing in?  What implications does this have for distribution, promotion and pricing strategy?

Line Extensions
Recommend future line extensions that make sense given your target, positioning and stage in the product life cycle.

New-Product Development
Write a sentence about each phase of the new-product development process.  The sentence should address what you have done or could do for each in regards to your products (you can skip idea generation if it is not applicable)

Positioning
Write a one-sentence positioning statement that concisely states how you want your product or service to be perceived in the mind of the target consumer.

Use data from your market research and your recent marketing experiences to draw a perceptual map to show how you are positioned against competitors on two important factors affecting buyer behavior.  Discuss the strategy implications of the perceptual map you have created.

Product/Service Differentiation
Describe perceptible and/or hidden differences that exist between your product/service and your competitors'.

Discuss any additional differences that could be created to effectively differentiate your product in the eyes of the target consumer.

Product/Service Modifications/Improvements
Take a step back and look at you product or service offering.  Are there any modifications that could be made to the current product design, product line or service offering to better fit the chosen positioning or to address the current stage of the product life cycle?  Describe any recommended changes, indicating whether they are quality, functional or aesthetic changes.

Brand Name

Packaging
If applicable, describe your packaging and the functions that the packaging will perform
