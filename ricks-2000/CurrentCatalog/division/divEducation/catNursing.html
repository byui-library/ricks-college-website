The Nursing Department offers the following programs:

 

Associate IN Arts and Sciences Degree with a major in Nursing

This program has been designed to prepare the Graduate to write the licensing examination for Registered Nurses.

 

Upon completion of the Nursing curriculum, the Graduate will be prepared to:

1. Use the conceptual model as a guide for holistic nursing care.

2. Administer the variety of health care required for patients with identified health problems across the life span.

3. Be accountable for their individual nursing practice.

4. Assume responsibility for personal and professional stewardship goals.

HIGH SCHOOL RECOMMENDATIONS

 

The Ricks College Nursing Program is intended to integrate students immediately out of high school and complete the course of study within two years.

 

High School Courses Recommended:

-Algebra II

-Nutrition

-Anatomy and Physiology

-Chemistry (one full year with a grade of “B” or better will enable the student to waive the one semester of College Chemistry required as a prerequisite to the program.)

 

Entrance Requirements

 

Students are accepted into the Nursing program following admission to Ricks College and review and selection by the Nursing Admissions Committee. The evaluation is based upon:

 

1. Scholastic record

2. American College Test (ACT) Score

3. TOEFL (see below)

 

Two separate applications are required; one for Ricks College, and one for the nursing program.

 

The completed nursing application must be on file with the Department of Nursing prior to February 15.

 

All students with English as a second language will be required to pass the TOEFL Examination with a score of at least 500. Check your local college/university for availability. Ricks College offers the exam by request only.

 

Clinical experiences are in the communities of Rexburg, Idaho Falls and Blackfoot. To achieve the best possible clinical education, students are assigned to various facilities throughout these communities. Students are responsible for, and assume the risks of, their own transportation to and from the clinical areas.

Students will be required to participate in two trips; one during their Freshman year,and one in the Sophomore year. Transportation will be provided.

Freshman Travel Fee: $15

Sophomore Travel Fee: $25

 

Program Requirements

 

Students must:

1. Receive confirmation of acceptance to the Nursing program by formal letter in order to register for any Nursing course.

2. Provide documentation of current “Provider CPR” certification

3. Submit completed health questionnaire with immunization documentation.

4. Provide own transportation to clinicals (see above).

5. NCLEX Review Course, total cost-$242 (or $30/selected modules)

 

All nursing students are required to carry student liability insurance against malpractice suits. The insurance fee is $10 per semester and is paid for by Ricks College.

 

LPN’s accepted into the program may choose to take the LPN Mobility Exam I and II at their own expense. If they pass with a 90% composite or better, they can waive Nursing 100 and 100A. They are also required to pass a clinical case scenario exam. to waive Fundamentals, student must contact the Department Chair, (208) 356-1325 to arrange approval prior to Fall Semester.

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

 

Nurs 100 Fundamentals    6

Prerequisite: Acceptance into nursing program

Completion of prerequisites as stated in catalog

Two hours core-lecture, and nine hours clinical/lab per week

Lab Fee: $5

Syllabus: $45

Nursing Kit: $125

 

This course offers the student experience in basic nursing assessment and care of the hospitalized patient in acute care settings.

 

Nurs 100 A Professionalism; Interpersonal Skills    1

Prerequisite: Acceptance into nursing program

This class will meet one hour per week

 

Introduction of concepts related to professionalism including values, ethics, legal implications, cultural, and spiritual aspects. Communications and interpersonal skills will be emphasized.

 

Nurs 101 Gerontology     2

Prerequisites/Concurrent: Nurs 100 BS 221 Gen Micro, Faml 210 Child Development

Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week

Lab Fee: $5

 

This course relates to the assessment and care of the problems of aging.

(5 week module)

Nurs 102 Metabolic/GU    2

Prerequisite/Concurrent: Nurs 100 Fundamentals, BS 221 Gen Micro, Faml 210 Child Development

Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week

Lab fee $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course incorporates nursing assessment and management of disorders involving the kidney and

related structures, liver, gall bladder, and pancreas.

(5 week module)

 

Nurs 103 Psych Nursing     3

Prerequisite/Concurrent: Nurs 100, Psych 111, BS 221 Gen Micro, Faml 210 Child Development

Three hours lecture, and 12 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course is designed to aid the student in applying the nursing process to the psychological needs of the patient, as well as providing the student with a growing self understanding through the study of the principles of psychiatric nursing. (5 week module)

 

Nurs 104 GI and Cancer    3

Prerequisites/Concurrent: Nurs 100 Fundamentals, BS 221 Gen Micro Three hours lecture and 12 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course focuses on problems of the gastrointestinal tract, and introduces the student nurse to the care of a person with cancer.

(5 week module)

 

 

Nurs 200 Neuro/Endocrine    4

Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference and 14 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course focuses on the assessment and care of the person with problems relating to neurological, sensory and endocrine systems and immobility. (Block class)

 

Nurs 201 Cardiovascular and Respiratory    4

Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference, and 14 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course focuses on prevention of illness and patient teaching, as well as covering assessment and nursing

care of the person with problems relating to respiratory and cardiovascular systems.

(Block class)

 

Nurs 202 Obstetric    4

Three hours lecture, one hour clinical conference, and 14 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course offers the student experience in the hospital and community settings in assessing and caring for the child bearing family. (Block class)

 

Nurs 203 Pediatric     4

Prerequisites: Nurs 202 or permission of instructor

Three hours lecture, and 14 hours clinical per week

Lab fee: $5

NCLEX Review Fee: $30

 

This course focuses on the assessment and care of the well and sick infant, child, and adolescent in hospital and community settings. (Block class)

 

Nurs 204 Law and Ethics     1

Two hours of lecture per week

Syllabus: $50

 

This course focuses on professional accountability and legal aspects. (Fall Semester)

 

Nurs 205 Trends and Professionalism     1

Two hours of lecture per week

Graduation Pin Fee: $35

 

This class is a continuation of Nurs 204 with a focus on licensure, professional and educational opportunities. (Winter Semester)

 

Nurs 290 Special Problems    1-3

 

This course provides the opportunity for study of pertinent topics not included in the Nursing curriculum. May be repeated for credit.
