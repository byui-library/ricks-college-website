What is Fast Track?

Each year more students are earning college credit while in high school. They earn credit by taking college credits concurrently during their senior year or by passing an Advanced Placement (AP) exam.

Fast Track is a program designed to help energetic high school students begin their college career early. Even more exciting, Fast Track will allow graduation from Ricks College after only one year, including summer enrollments.

How Fast Track Works:

  • Earn college credit while in high school. This can be done by:
    1. Attending Ricks summer school between your junior and senior year of high school.
    2. Enroll in BYU or Ricks correspondence classes.
    3. Earn concurrent credit from your local college.
    4. Pass an Advanced Placement (AP) exam (with a 3, 4, or 5).

  • Attend Ricks for one consecutive year
    Your enrollment at Ricks would begin the summer after your graduation from high school and continue through the next summer. At that point you would graduate from Ricks with your associates degree.

Many college credits earned while in high school will fill both college and high school requirements. Students participating in Fast Track should work with both a Ricks advisor and high school counselor in planning their program.

What are the Benefits?

The benefits of Fast Track are many, here are just a few:

  • Graduate with an associates degree before an LDS mission.

  • Be eligible for earlier admission consideration to Brigham Young or another university.

  • Reduce overall cost of college education. Non-tuition costs (for example room and board) would be cut by as much as 25% for a four-year program.

  • Receive admission benefits by qualifying for Fast Track.

  • Possible eligibility for one-half tuition scholarship.

Who Can Participate?

  • Students who have completed college credit while in high school, and
