World class guitarist to perform

by Peter Jones
sel00001@byui.edu
Scroll Staff

World-known classical guitarist Denis Azabagic will perform at BYU-Idaho Thursday at 7:30 p.m. in the Ruth H. Barrus Concert Hall. Tickets are $6 for the public and $1 for students.

Azabagic has proven himself to be one of the best guitarists of his generation by winning more than 20 awards from international guitar competitions. These awards include one of the most prestigious among guitarists “Jacinto e Inocencio Guerro.” He won this award in 1993 at the age of 20.

Other awards Azabagic has won include first prize at the international Guitar Foundation of America Competition, first prize at the International Guitar Festival Germany and first prize at the International Guitar Competition, “Rene Bartoli.”

Throughout the competitions he had attended, Azabagic consistently maintained a prize winning position in all the competitions.

“Azabagic was playing with four hands, not just two,” The Los Angeles Star wrote of his performances.

“The sound was glorious; he is a superb technician,” The Herald Times of south central Indiana wrote. “The brilliance of Azabagic’s playing brought an easy grace to the music.”
