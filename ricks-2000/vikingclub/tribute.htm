The final season of intercollegiate athletics at Ricks College starting this fall promises to be one of spirited action as well as one of reflection and paying tribute to former athletes and coaches, says athletic director Garth Hall. Hall, recently appointed vice president of advancement at the college, will continue as athletic director in addition to his new assignment. Also, by the start of the final athletic season in early September, Ricks College will be known as BYU-Idaho, and will have changed to a four-year institution of higher learning. It will continue, however, to compete athletically against two-year junior colleges for one final season.

A gala evening of paying tribute to athletics at Ricks College is planned for Friday, March 1, 2002, in the Hart Auditorium. Each sport will be honored and current and former athletes, coaches, administrators, and staff will be recognized for their contributions. There will be awards and entertainment and the general public will be invited to participate.

"March 1 of next year will be an evening you won’t forget," says Hall. "We’ll honor each sport that has been a part of Ricks College athletics. We’ll reminisce, share stories and pictures from the past...in short, it will be a gala night to enjoy."
