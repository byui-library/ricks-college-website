Archery Beginning
ARCHERY BEG
CLASS CODE: PE*C 126 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Designed to introduce the sport of archer through hands-on application of using the bow and arrow through lecture, demonstration, and practice of archery skills.
DESCRIPTION: This class is designed to introduce the student to the sport of archery through hands-on application of using the bow and arrow through lecture, demonstration, and practice of archery skills.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. Learn fundamentals and elementary skills of safe archery.
2. Exhibit knowledge and skill of fundamentals, rules, and strategies of archery.
3. Learn to interact with other class members
4. Knowledge of history, rules, etiquette and courtesy
5 Develop skill that can be used for a lifetime
6. Develop skills to teach fundamentals and elementary skills of archery
7. Abide by rules and be more self-disciplined
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Exhibit knowledge and skill of basic archery
2. Attend class and participate.
3. Students must furnish six arrows and a set of finger tabs. Approximate cost is $30. Meet with instructor the first day of class before purchasing arrows.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absence cllowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
