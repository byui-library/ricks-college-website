Q1: Can I do extra credit work to bring my grade up? A1: No, I don't usually give extra credit for extra projects. Maybe I'm rationalizing to defend my "nice guy" presenting self...but considering that I have over 100 students per semester and that each is required to turn in 3 projects per semester and each project takes an average of 8 minutes to read...I already spend about 40 hours reading and grading projects. One more wouldn't be that big of a deal, but I wouldn't feel right about doing it for one student and not for another one.  Instead, I try to make my grading flexible by allowing you to have projects graded or not, and in my interpersonal classes by allowing you to take optional tests.   I also give some extra credit on tests and assignments for exceptional learning.
Q2:  I notice that the majority of my grade in your class is based on tests.  I'm not a good test taker.  Do you have any suggestions to help me do well in your class? A2:  Maybe.  I am willing to do anything I can to help you get a good grade in my class.  Please come talk with me about things like altering the testing environment, or building further flexibility into my current grading system.  But please don't wait until the end of the semester to do so.
Q3:  Are we doing anything important in class today? A3:  Honestly, I prefer you don't ask this question, but if you must know...I certainly hope so!  If we're not, then you or I or both of us need to repent of our sins and come to class more prepared next time.
