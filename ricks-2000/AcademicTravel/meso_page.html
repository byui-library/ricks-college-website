Meso-America Tour

$2,495 + tuition
  This includes:
All transportation
All meals in MesoAmerica
Lodging in MesoAmerica
Entrancew fees/tickets to attractions

  Does not include:
Passport
Spending money

Scholarships and financial aid apply

First summer term, tentatively April 29 - June 5, 2002
We will stay on campus one week (April 29-May 4) and travel in MesoAmerica for 4 weeks (returning by June 5). Exact dates will be determined by plane ticket availability.
Five credits are required, and up to ten can be taken.

  • Anthropology 350: Latin American Culture (3 credits, Fills G.E. elective requirements)
  • Anthropology 340: Special Topics (1 credit, Fills G.E. elective requirements) 
  • Biology 118: Field Biology (4 credits, Fills Science requirements)
  • Communications 102: Public Speaking (3 credits, Fills G.E. basic skills requirements)
  • FA 100: Fine Arts Attendance (0 credits, required for graduation)
  • Religion 122: Book of Mormon (2 credits, Fills GE religion credit)
  • Sociology 112: Modern Social Problems (3 credits, Fills Social Science requirements)
  • Spanish 211: Second-year Conversation (2 credits, Fills G.E. Elective requirements)
  • Spanish 290: Directed Studies (1 credits, Fills G.E. Elective requirements)
Highlights:
Snorkeling the blue Caribbean
Spotting monkeys and crocodiles
Climbing ancient pyramids
Hiking through a rain forest
Visiting prospective Book of mormon sites
Staying with an LDS family
Exploring interesting cities, including Mexico City - one of the largest in the world.
Interacting with people of different cultures
Having free time
Making some of the best friends of your life.
