Facilities for the BYU-Idaho Photo Department are located on the ground floor of the George S. Romney building.  The photography suite is #55.  The general student and office area opens into the hallway containing the sophomore darkrooms and student storage area, the film loading and developing area, the community darkroom, the studio/classroom facility, and the supply checkout room.  Other rooms include areas for model dressing and makeup, studio storage, and a computer imaging station..

All areas are fully equipped for student use.  Studio lighting and cameras are supplied.   Small and large format equipment is available for check-out.  Supplies are either included in the modest lab supply fees or available at reasonable cost.   Digital imaging is available in the Art Department Imaging Lab and in the Photo Department Imaging Station.  Available are imaging, scanning, printing, and processing computer equipment.
