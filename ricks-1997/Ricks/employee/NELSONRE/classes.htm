Class Description

Hort 124 - Flower Identification

    The identification , landscape value, and special cultural requirements of annual and perennial flowers.  This class is offered in the fall as a block class and also in the summer time.  Students are exposed to various types of flowers that grow in the Rexburg area and other places around the country.  It includes three hours of instruction a week.

Hort 219 - Landscape Management

    This class involves learning about various types of landscape care.  It will include the maintenance of trees, scrubs and turf in landscape, park, golf, and athletic areas.  Students who are pursuing a career in the golf industry or in landscape maintenance will benefit from taking this class.  It is valuable also for general information for the management of grounds on any scale.  It is only offered in the fall semester.  It includes two hours lecture and three hours of small group lab per week

Hort 234 - Greenhouse Operations

    Students who participate in this class learn important techniques about greenhouse construction, environmental control and plant culture including production of greenhouse floral crops.  It is a strongly recommended class for those pursuing a career in the greenhouse, nursery, or floral areas.  It includes two hours lecture and three hours of small group lab per week.

Hort 299 - Horticulture Seminar

    Students participating in this class will have the opportunity of hearing each week from guest speakers from the Green Industry.  There are a range of topics and subjects covered throughout the class.  It involves one class hour per week.  

Hort 297 - Practicum

    This class involves a combination of classroom discussion and supervised practical experiences for the development and improvement of horticultural skills.  It is coordinated with related and laboratory studies.  It involves three hours per week per semester credit hour.
