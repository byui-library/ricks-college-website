E-Business Model
Team Project

Description:  Find a business Web site in which the Web site represents a significant portion of the company's business, and prepare a power point presentation that details its E-business model(s).

Rules:

  • It can't be any of the Web sites mentioned on the Digital Enterprise article.
  • The presentation will be evaluated by class mates and me based upon the following metrics

Grading Metrics

Technical Marks
  Points (highest # is best)
Profitability Does the Web site appear to contribute significantly to the company's profits? 1-5
Uniqueness Is the Web site unique and/or interesting 1-5
Artistic Marks
Identifying models Has the team correctly and completely identified all business models? 1-10
Explanation How clearly did the team explain how the company makes the model(s) work?  Does it make sense? Was the explanation complete? 1-10
