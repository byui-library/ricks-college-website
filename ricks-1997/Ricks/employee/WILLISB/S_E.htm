The World of Soils and Ecology is becoming larger and larger all the time.  The following is a list of wesites that are involved with the ever changing world of soils and ecology.  It will give the student a chance to keep up on the latest methods and news.  It will also give them an opportunity to search for career opportunities, and to fufill their educational goals.

The Ecology WWW Web Page

      As a volunteer service to students, teachers, researchers, and others interested in the science of ecology, this list was compiled by Anthony R. Brach (Missouri Botanical Garden & Harvard University Herbaria, U.S.A.), and was originally posted on ECOLOG-L in March 1995. Jean Thioulouse (University of Lyon, France) created and maintained the HTML and search script from 1995-1998, and maintains a mirror site in France.

     Ecology.About.com         

    This website offers the student current news and events.  Also it offers a search engine to search for other things related to soils and ecology.

    The University of Georgia

    For more than thirty years, ecologists at the University of Georgia have worked together to promote interdisciplinary team research and to deal holistically with humans and the environment. As a result of their efforts, the University of Georgia has emerged as a leading center for ecological research and training.

    Ecology Communications

    This website is filled with news and current events.  You may join an ecology club and check out new advances in technology.  Peter's Garden features tips on gardening with all types of plants and fertilizers.  A real treat for the garden lover.

    The Utah State University Ecology Center

    It offers students a chance to check out the faculty, degrees offered, curriculum, and other things associated at the Ecology center at Utah State University.

    The Need to Know Library - Ecology and Environmental Page

    Ecological topics are listed under three headings: 1) Ecology, 2) Conservation Biology, and 3) Botany and Systematics. It offers links to home pages of botanical and ecological societies.  It offers links to sites describing software applications for analyzing or organizing ecological data.  It also links to home pages of government agencies that conduct ecological research or that are responsible for the management of various ecosystems, and links to sites that feature information about natural areas, LTER sites, national parks, and wilderness areas.

    The Marsh Ecology Research Program 

    The Marsh Ecology Research Program (MERP) is a non-profit grants program which supports high quality scientific research on understudied aspects of the ecology of freshwater and saltwater tidal marshes.

    The Journal Of Ecology

    The Journal of Ecology is the premier journal in the field of plant ecology and is edited by the British Ecological Society. It is published by Blackwell Science in Oxford, their WWW J.Ecol page also includes subscription details.

 

    The Universtiy of Washington Soils Glossary

    This site provides a glossary of soil terms for those who wish to either polish up on old terms, or to find  a word that they are just stumped about.  It has everything from Abrasion to Yardwaste.  It is provided by the Universtiy of Washington

    The University of Florida - EDIS 

    This site proviodes fertilization recommendations for trees and shrubs in home and commercial landscapes,  plant nutrients and fertilizers for the non-farmer, also information on  soil pH and landscape plants that can be used in different parts of the country, and also soils and plant nutrition information help.

    The Soil Science Society of America

    The Soil Science Society of America (SSSA) is the professional home for over 6,000 professionals throughout the world dedicated to the advancement of soil science. The primary purpose of the Society is to advance the discipline and practice of soil science by acquiring and disseminating information about soils in relation to crop production, environmental quality, ecosystem sustainability, bioremediation, waste management and recycling, and wise land use.
