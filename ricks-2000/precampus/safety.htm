Safety

Although BYU-Idaho and the Rexburg area have the feel of a "small-town" atmosphere, it doesn't mean that crime is non-existent.  No campus is 100% free of crime and danger, however, BYU-I is notorious for having a relatively small crime rate.  Students should be familiar with the campus safety services and safety support available on campus.  Not all dangers come from crime. Students should also be familiar with winter weather conditions, winter driving conditions as well as other general safety concerns
