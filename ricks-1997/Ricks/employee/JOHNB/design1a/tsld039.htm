Types of Mulch

  • Organic

    • Decomposes - Improves Soil
    • Wood/Bark Chips, Grass/Straw Clippings

  • Inorganic

    • Does Not Decompose Quickly
    • Rock, Gravel, Boulders

  • Groundcovers

    • Defines Space, Prevents Soil Erosion
    • Creates Texture - “Carpet Effect”
