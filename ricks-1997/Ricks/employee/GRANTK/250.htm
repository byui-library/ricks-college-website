Who I Am/Who I Want To Be


 I believe that Introduction to Literature should be a fun class while it changes our lives.  You might want to understand my definition of fun--fun to me is hard work with laughter, tears, and meaningful conversation.  I define fun as walking away from this class with meaningful artifacts that students spend many hours perfecting.  Students defy Rexburg weather and unruly roommates to protect their artifacts by any means possible.  The laughter, the tears, the conversation, the hard work, the artifacts--all of this we do with the objective of leaving from this class different than when we started.  We leave with a deeper understanding of principles that help us to be better followers of Jesus Christ which means that we will be better spouses, parents, and citizens. 

Does this sound idealistic.  I hope so!  The day I find that my students are not walking out of my classroom changed is the day that I will hunt for another line of work.  Let's change the world!

Helpful note for students:  We will look into truths and doctrines that will inspire thoughts about principles and laws.  If we can begin to master the principles and laws, then we will try to find ways to apply those principles and laws outside of the classroom.  If we master the principles and laws, then we become truly powerful and can change the world.
