Course Description: This course provides a basic introduction to physical geology by focusing on the active processes that continue to shape and modify the surface of the earth.

Course Objectives: First, we introduce the class to the concepts that are included in Physical Geology. Second, we focus on  minerals and the three major rock types. This part of the class supports many of the exercises performed in Geology 102 lab. Third, we will learn about different geologic processes that shape the earths features including volcanoes, streams, faults, and glaciers. We will also discuss surface water and ground water issues. Fourth, a more detailed discussion of plate tectonics and geologic resources rounds out the class.

This course should provide you with a greater awareness of how active the earth is. It should also make any camping or hiking adventures more enjoyable as you couple your appreciation of nature with an understanding of the materials and processes that produce the natural wonders that we see. Also, we try to provide you information and understanding that could help to minimize the risks associated with earthquakes, volcanoes, floods, landslides, etc. to you as an individual.
