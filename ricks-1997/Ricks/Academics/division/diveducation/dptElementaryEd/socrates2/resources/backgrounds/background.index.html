All of the backgrounds in this index are .GIF or .JPG format. Most of the images are larger than they appear.

To view an image in its full size

Hold down the right mouse button while the cursor is positioned over the image.
When the menu appears, select "View Image" from the menu choices.
When you are finised viewing the image, click the "Back" button.

To copy an image to your disk

Hold down the right mouse button while the cursor is positioned over the image
When the menu appears, select "Save Image as..." from the choices.
A dialog box will appear, indicate the destination, and any changes to the name, click "Save"
