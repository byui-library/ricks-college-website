Event Calendar

Don't sit at home at night. Get out there! The only way to really get good at dancing is to get out there and do it. Here's a list of events to keep you on your toes.

Swing Night
Wednesday nights swing hard!  We are dancing in the East Ballroom.  The dance costs $2 for students (free w/membership card) and instruction starts at 7:30pm with dancing from 8-10pm.  
Don't try this on Wednesday nights!

Oktober Swing Fest - Sat, October 13th
Tonya Surface and Joon Chang, just took 1st in the Lindy at one of the biggest national competitions around.  What's their next challenge?  They're coming to Rexburg to teach us how to swing like them! The workshops will take place October 13th. Class sizes are limited, and the event will surely sell out, so sign up now!  The full workshop will cost only $10 for club members, $15 with BYU-I ID, or $20 for non-students.  Reserve your spot today by emailing David at morte100@hotmail.com.  The schedule is as follows:
