Tutor Recommendation/Approval Form

     A primary concern of the BYU-Idaho Tutoring Center is to attract the most skilled and qualified students available as tutors.

     We welcome your input as to how we can help your students achieve the academic success they desire. Since a number of your students do come to the Tutoring Center for help, your recommendation or approval of students who might be qualified to tutor would be greatly appreciated.

     Could you please refer two or three returning students, who have done well in your class(es), or would you approve of the applicant in question to tutor for the course(s) you teach?

     Please be aware that while the Tutoring Center seriously considers your recommendations, there is no guarantee that the student(s) that you recommend will be hired.  Listed below are some considerations for candidate tutors.

      Some Considerations for Tutors

  1. Tutors should be qualified and prepared in every aspect to give assistance to students who seek their help.
    Being qualified includes:
    • Previously taken the class.
    • Received an "A-" or better.
    • Is presently a full-time student?
    • Does not already hold a campus job.
    • Can dedicate at least 10-12 hrs per week to tutoring.
    • Cannot be a dependent of an employee.
  2. Tutors should be persons who will be dependable in keeping their commitments and appointments.
  3. Tutors should be fully living the standards of the Church.
  4. Tutors should be willing to communicate with the teacher in question on an ongoing basis, to keep abreast of the class homework, tests, expectations, etc.
  5. Tutors should recognize that they are accountable to the Director of Tutoring and that their employment is conditional upon their willingness to abide by the Tutoring Center’s policies. Tutors must be willing to complete a one-credit tutor-training course.
