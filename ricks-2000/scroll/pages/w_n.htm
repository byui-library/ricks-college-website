PRESIDENT BUSH CREATES NEW WHITE HOUSE OFFICE

To release more tax dollars for faith-based groups across the country, President Bush issued an executive order to create the new White House office of Faith-based and Community Initiatives Jan. 29.

"Church and charity, synagogue and mosque lend our communities their humanity, and they will have an honored place in our plan and in our laws," President Bush said during his inaugural address on Jan. 20.

The new office is headed by Princeton University professor of politics and public affairs John J. DiIulio Jr., who will report directly to the president. The legislation portion of the president's plan will make government tax dollars available to faith-based and other community organizations.
