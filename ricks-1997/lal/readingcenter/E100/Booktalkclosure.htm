The Assignment

 In 1 Nephi 19:23 Nephi says he read the scriptures to his people and then (after reading) he says AI did liken all scriptures unto us, that it  might be for our profit and learning.@  Even though the book you read is not scripture, and the setting of the book you read may have been in a different place at a different time, it probably had something that touched your life or connected to an experience you have had in your life. 

 

For this assignment, liken the book  you have chosen to read Aunto your own life, that it might be for your profit and learning.@  Write a one page report connecting an idea that touched you, from the essay  to your own life. The connection you write about may be positive or negative, but it must be real.  This paper will not be read in class.  Only you and I will read it.  This assignment is not a Abook report.@  It is designed to help you look inside and learn about yourself, and recognize that reading can help you do this.  Use detailed examples from both the essay and your life to make connections.

 

Examples

AThe Pearl by John Steinbeck made me realize that I was spending too much time focusing on money, and not enough time focusing on my family.  Because of this, I changed the way I spent my free time.@

AThe Firm by John Grisham helped me to understand that things are not always what they appear to be.  This helped me to not compare myself to others.  I recognized that what seemed to be true, might not be reality.  I would then use detailed examples from the book and my life to support my connections.@

 

Purpose

In this class, we have gone from Adecoding@ to Areading@ to Acomprehending@ and now to Alikening and learning.@  Have fun with this assignment, and remember the most important things we learn are the things we learn about ourselves.  Our friends can help us do this, and as Bernadete Piassa points out in A Love Affair With Books, books can be our friends.

 

Format

Your paper must be typed.  It should have your name, the date, and class in the upper right hand corner.  It should be one page, 12 pt font, double spaced, with one inch margins.  The title should be centered.

 

Due Date                                                                                                     

The day you give your Booktalk.
