March 21-24, 2002


General Information

Mothers' Weekend, sponsored by the Associated Women Students, is an annual event held at BYU-I where students' mothers are invited to visit campus. The purpose of Mothers' Weekend is to provide an opportunity for students to strengthen relationships with their mothers by experiencing a weekend of college life together. Various activities are provided to help enrich this relationship, but, are not intended be the main focus of Mothers' Weekend, nor is it expected that everyone would attend all events.

During Mothers' Weekend regular class work will continue and some professors may allow mothers to attend classes. There will also be some events provided for mothers to enjoy while students are in class. Activities are scheduled from Thursday evening through Sunday evening. Mothers are invited to come any time during that period and see for themselves what the "Spirit of Ricks" is all about!

(Because of limited facilities and the students' academic responsibilities, bringing other family members, including children, as well as extended visits beyond the weekend, are discouraged.)

Registration

There is no formal registration or fees required to visit during the weekend. There are, however, various events you may wish to attend. Tickets for those events should be purchased in advance from the BYU-I Ticket Office.

Ticket Information

Tickets will be available for purchase beginning Feb. 27, at 7 a.m. M.S.T. They may be purchased on the internet at www.byui.edu/tickets or by phone at 1-800-717-4257 (local calls 496-2230). Tickets may also be purchased at the Ticket Office window. In order to get the student price, the student's I.D. Number and PIN Number will need to be given. All tickets are limited. For questions concerning tickets, call the above phone numbers.

Transportation

There are local carriers that make daily trips from Salt Lake airport and Idaho Falls airport.  More information is available at:(http://www.byui.edu/pr/studenttravel/options.htm

Lodging

Mothers are allowed to stay with their daughters in college apartments. Mothers of sons, or those preferring motels, will find the following accommodations available in the Rexburg area: Comfort Inn 359-1311; Cottontree 356-4646; Day's Inn 356-9222; Super 8 356-8888; Weston Inn (St. Anthony) 624-3711; South Fork Inn (Rigby) 745-8700; There are also many motels in the Idaho Falls area.

Food Services

The campus cafeteria will have special rates for mothers. Two campus snack bars and a convenience store, as well as vending machines, provide reasonably priced meals and snacks.

Dress

Because of Idaho's changeable weather, warm clothing is usually needed in March. Dress slacks are appropriate for most of the events unless otherwise indicated. Sunday dress, of course, is appropriate for all Sunday meetings. Ricks College dress standards apply to all visitors.

Bookstore

The Bookstore is located in the Manwaring Center where many LDS books, tapes and a variety of other items are available for purchase. Check brochure upon arrival for times when various authors and musicians will be available for autograph sessions.

Pictures

Mother/daughter or mother/son pictures will be taken Friday and Saturday on the second floor of the Manwaring Center for a minimal fee.

Endowment Session

There will be a bus available for those wishing to attend an endowment session in the Idaho Falls Temple on Friday morning, March 22, from 7:30 a.m. to noon. Interested participants should sign up at the M.C. Information Desk before 5 p.m. Mar. 21. There is a $2 fee for the bus.

Mother-Daughter/Son Look-Alike Contest

Come and see the look-alikes! Friday, March 22, at noon in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom. Prizes and fun! Sign up at door by 11:30 a.m. to be in the contest.

Displays

Some of the campus academic departments may have various student displays in their buildings. Mothers are encouraged to check for more information upon arrival to campus.

For More Information

A Mothers' Weekend information table will be located in the lower lobby of the Manwaring Center where more detailed brochures listing the various workshops, speakers and events will be available upon your arrival. These will not be distributed in advance. If you have further questions concerning Mothers' Weekend call the BYU-I College ABYU-I Office at 208-496-2270 or check the BYU-I College website at www.byui.edu.
