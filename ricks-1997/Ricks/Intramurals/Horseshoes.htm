Horseshoes

Pre determine the number of points or the number of shoes pitched.

     1. Two types of Games:

          a. Count-all

          b. Cancellation

     2. Fun games = 21 pts     or     Tournament games = 40 pts/ shoes

Note: Flip a coin or shoe to see who will pitch first.

Types of Throws

     1. Flip-hold the shoes between thumb and two fingers inside/out.  Have it turn 1 or 2 turns.  End over end.

     2. 1 1/4 turn-hold so the opening is away and throw so it turns clockwise.

     3. 1 3/4 turn-hold so the opening is toward and throw so it turns clockwise.
