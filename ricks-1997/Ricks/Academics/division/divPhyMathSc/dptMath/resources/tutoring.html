Tutoring for Math and CS

The Tutoring Center offers free individual, group, and walk-in tutoring to all students who need help beyond their regular classroom instruction.

Trained student tutors are proficient in a variety of subjects and are prepared to help sudents strengthen their academic background, understand class material, comprehend the textbook, organize assignments and improve general learning and study skills.

Emphasis is placed on the needs of each individual student and on flexibility in meeting those needs.

Students desiring to be tutored individually should come to the Tutoring Center (MC 164) where they will receive the name and phone number of a tutor who is qualified to help them.

Drop in tutoring times for math are posted in the halls of the Romney building. Times for CS students are posted in the Computer Lab, Rom 271
