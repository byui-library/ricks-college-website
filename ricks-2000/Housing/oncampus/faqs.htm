Are freshman required to live on campus? [Top]
No.  Students may live in any on or off campus housing as long as it is BYU-Idaho approved housing.

 
Is there a deadline to reserve on-campus housing? [Top]
No.  However the sooner you reserve housing, the easier it is to get a reservation.  If you wait to long, the housing option you want may fill leaving you only the option of being placed on a waiting list.

 
Are private rooms available? [Top]
Yes, but there is only a limited number.

 
Are there ADA rooms available? [Top]
Yes, but there is a limited number.

 
Is there a quiet study housing option available? [Top]
Yes.  You will need to request this option online, or by checking this option on your personal data sheet.

 
When can I move in and what should I bring? [Top]
Read our Move-in Guide for more information.

 
What information is available to plan my travel to Rexburg? [Top]
Please visit the student travel web page for more information.

 
When will I find out my room assignment? [Top]
Room assignments are currently made about a week before the upcoming contract period begins due to changes from cancellations and a working waiting list.

 
When will I know who my roommates are going to be? [Top]
Roommates are currently placed together based on the Head Residents discretion.  Assignments are made based on data returned from the students personal data sheet.

 
Can I request a roommate? [Top]
Yes.  You can request a roommate online, or by contacting the Housing Office.  In order for the request to be granted, both students desiring to become roommates will need to submit a request with the other students social security number.

 
What ward will I be in and who will my Bishop be? [Top]
Head Residents are informed each year of ward boundaries relative to their dorm or hall.  You will find out what ward you are in when you check in.  Visit the Stake/Ward Locator online for more information.

Can I make changes to my upcoming housing reservation? [Top]
Yes.  Changes to upcoming housing reservations are currently handled by the Housing Office.  Stop by the office or call to make changes.  You can also make these changes online.

 
Is it possible to change to a different room? [Top]
If you wish to move to a different room within your on-campus apartment or residence hall, just talk to your Head Resident.  She will help you arrange a move if possible.

 
Where do I send my housing payments? [Top]
Send them to the Housing Office.  Please do not send cash.  Payments can also be made via phone or online with a credit card.

 
How can I get replacement payment coupons? [Top]
Just contact the Housing Office.  We will be glad to send you a new copy.  You can also print the coupons of our website.

 
Will my parents receive billing information? [Top]
All billing information will be sent to you, as you are the individual who has entered into the housing agreement.  You will initially be sent coupons detailing the amounts and due dates of your payments.  Further billing correspondence will be sent to you from the Accounting Office.  If your parents will be making these housing payments, please make sure you give this information to them.

 
What if I am leaving during my reserved contract period? [Top]
Let your Head Resident and the Housing Office know that you are leaving as soon as possible.  For some situations (missions, graduation, or marriage) you may be released from your agreement automatically.  For other situations, you will be charged a termination fee.

 
When will I get my deposit back? [Top]
If no future on-campus housing reservations exist, deposits will be mailed to you by the Accounting Office after you have checked-out.  Amounts from reported damages will be taken out of your deposit amount automatically.  If future on-campus housing reservations do exist, your deposit will be carried over and used for that contract period.

 
How do I use my meal plan? [Top]
See the Food Services website for more information.

 
Do I need to have a meal plan? [Top]
Residents living in Non-Cooking Style Housing are required to participate in either the Gold, Silver, or Bronze meal plan.  If you do not wish to have a meal plan, you might consider living in Cooking Style Housing-(available to women only.)

 
How do I change my meal plan? [Top]
You can change your meal plan from Gold to Silver or Silver to Gold during the first week of your contract period.  After the first week, only changes from Silver to Gold will be allowed.  Contact the Housing Office to make these changes or do it online.

 
Can I have guests stay overnight? [Top]
No overnight guests of the opposite sex are permitted.  All overnight guests (two or three days maximum) should have the permission of roommates and register with the Head Resident.  Immediate family members and other guests of the same sex may stay at no charge (other than at check-in and -out times when no guests may stay).  All guests are expected to abide by BYU-Idaho standards and residence hall guidelines.
