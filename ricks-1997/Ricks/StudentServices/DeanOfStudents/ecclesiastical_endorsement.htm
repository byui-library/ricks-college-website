Ecclesiastical Endorsement

LDS Students

Annually, each student planning on continuing at Brigham Young University-Idaho  beyond the winter semester will be required to obtain a continuing endorsement from the bishop of the ward the student resided in during the winter semester before registering for any subsequent term or semester. Campus bishops will have the forms necessary to complete the endorsement. Students attending wards off campus or at home will need to obtain a form at the Dean of Students Office. The bishop certifies that the student is abiding by the Honor Code, the dress and grooming standards, and is fulfilling his/her duty in the Church by attending Church meetings and abiding by the rules and standards of the Church. Frequent absences from Sunday meetings in the student’s campus ward to attend farewells or other meetings may jeopardize the student’s attendance requirements.

Non-LDS Students

An endorsement may be obtained from the LDS bishop of the ward the student resides in during the winter semester, his/her own ecclesiastical officer, or the Dean of Students.

Ecclesiastical Endorsement - Withdrawal

Any student who is denied a continuing ecclesiastical endorsement may request a review by the stake president over the ward he/she resided in during the winter semester. An administrative review by the Dean of Students Office may be made in the event the decision by the stake president is adverse. There are two grounds for administrative review by the Dean of Students Office:

1. The student claims proof of compliance with the applicable standard of conduct in question.

2. The student believes there exists mitigating factors which, in fairness, should allow for continued enrollment at the college in the absence of a continuing ecclesiastical endorsement, i.e., close to the end of the semester or very near completion of an academic program.

Contact the Dean of Students Office for procedural information.
