      Robyn Bergstrom has been teaching at Ricks since 1994 in the Communication Department. She attended Ricks College, and moved on to Brigham Young University for her Bachelors Degree in Communication with a minor in Psychology and English. She later received a Resource Specialty from the University of Utah, and her Masters Degree is in Communication from the University of North Dakota. She loves teaching both Interpersonal Communication and Small Group Communication. She is the Assistant Coach to the Forensics team She has worked with the Chicago Urban Studies program, the Humanities tour of Europe, the Connection program for incoming freshman and the Quest program . She enjoys spending time with her family and friends. She is married to Scott Bergstrom and have four children. Mark, Angie, Alison, and Erik. She loves the statement made by Nelson Mandela. "Our deepest fear is not that we are inadequate. Our deepest fear is that we are powerful beyond measure. It is our light not our darkness, that most frightens us. We ask ourselves, 'Who am I to be brilliant, gorgeous, talented, fabulous?' Actually, who are you not to be? You are a child of God.”
