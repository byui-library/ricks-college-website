The distance from Jerusalem to the Red Sea (the Gulf of Aqaba) is about 180 miles through hot, barren country infested anciently by many marauders. Lehi and-his family traveled three days'journey beyond this point (see I Nephi 2:5-6). This meant at least a twelve-to-fourteen-day trip one way, which gives added meaning to Nephi's response in 1 Nephi 3:7.
