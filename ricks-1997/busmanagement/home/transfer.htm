Transfer Student Information

Business students intending to transfer to BYU-Idaho in Business Management should first seek admittance to the University by completing the following steps:
  1. Apply to BYU-Idaho through the admissions office.
  2. Send official transcripts from all previously attended institutions.

The admissions office can be contacted at (208) 496-1026 or admissions@byui.edu.

Business Management at BYU-Idaho requires completion of a Pre-Business Core.  Transfer equivalencies do not guarantee acceptance to the major.  After completing pre-business core requirements, students must make formal application and be accepted into full junior status.

All management transfer courses must be evaluated through the Management Advising Center.  If you have any questions regarding courses taken at other colleges, visit the College of Business and Communications Advisement Center in 225 SMI or contact them at   (208) 496-1451 or labaugha@byui.edu

Many courses have already been evaluated.  You will need to provide a course description and a syllabus to the Advisement Center for evaluation of a specific course that has not been evaluated.

Potential students who have more than 89 credits  should contact the Admissions Office for further information.  Those with 90 or more credits are generally not admissible and any exceptions need to go through the Admissions Committee. 

Transfer Policies

Students are responsible to verify that their transfer courses will satisfy BYU-Idaho requirements.  Upper-division transfer credit is granted on an individual basis.  A limit of 12 hours of upper-division credit will be accepted.  Please contact the Business Management Department to receive credit for upper-division transfer courses.
