ASSURE Instructional Model


ASSURE, as an instructional model has been widely implemented, especially when media resources are being used in the instruction. For a detailed overview of this model see Heinich, R., Molenda, M. & Russell, J. (1994). Instructional media and the new technologies of instruction. (4th edition). New York: Macmillian.

ASSURE is an acronymn that represents the six steps in the model. They are:

  • Analyze the audience
  • State the objective
  • Select the media and resources needed
  • Utilize the resources in instruction
  • Require learner performance
  • Evaluate & Revise instruction as needed
