Warwick Castle

Warwick Castle, the perhaps the finest Mediaeval Castle in England, rises majestically from the banks of the River Avon, just a few miles from Shakespeare's Stratford, on a site chosen by William the Conqueror.

The Castle is often called one of the finest Medieval castles in England. It was originally built by William the Conqueror in 1068. to fill the conquered Saxon population with awe and respect.  Four hundred years later it fell into the hands of the Earl of Warwick The personal belonging of the Earls of Warwick mostly remain in the Castle, and these include a superb baronial Armory, portraits and other paintings, furniture and other works of art. There is also a huge Great Hall, a haunted Tower, and a dungeon where countless prisoners have been held captive through the years.
