On-Campus Student Family Housing

Newly remodeled, two-bedroom fully-furnished apartments are available for families on campus.  Rent ($475/mo.) includes utilities, heat, and local telephone.  Free high-speed Internet access and basic cable TV service are provided by the Housing Office at no additional charge.  A laundry room is available as well as two lounges to provide open space for children to play (when attended by an adult) or to hold group and ward gatherings.

To place your name on our waiting list, print a waiting list form from your Internet browser and mail it in with your $25 application fee.  You will need Adobe Acrobat Reader to view and print this form.
