Ricks College News Release
April 18, 2001 
New student activities director named at Ricks College

REXBURG, Idaho — Devin Shaum has been named the new student activities program director at Ricks College.

He replaces Garth Hall, who was appointed Wednesday to be the new advancement vice president at Ricks College. Hall had served in the newly created position since December.

The student activities program will replace intercollegiate athletics as it is phased out during the next year as the college becomes Brigham Young University-Idaho.

The activities program opens sports, the arts and service to everyone, Shaum says. When fully operational, the program will have an array of year-round activities in four areas: social and recreational, visual and performing arts, sports and fitness, and humanitarian service and leadership. Each area is structured to give students numerous opportunities for active involvement at various levels of interest—including leisure, instructional, recreational and competitive.

Although Ricks will discontinue intercollegiate athletics by the end of the 2001-2002 school year, highly competitive athletics will continue within the institution and will involve a higher percentage of students than ever before. This new program goes beyond "intramurals" through which a group of friends play together on teams. The competitive athletics program will require practicing, training, coaching, and skill development—a level formerly characteristic of only varsity athletics.

Shaum has served as director of the Ricks College Alumni Association for the past 2½ years. His replacement was not announced.

He graduated from Ricks in 1979 and served as executive vice president of the Associate Students of Ricks College in his sophomore year. After his graduation from Ricks, he was employed at the college’s Outdoor Learning Center at Badger Creek for three summers.

Following graduation from Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah, he returned to Ricks in 1983 as an admissions counselor. In 1987 he joined the Development Office as its director of annual giving. In that position he was responsible for the annual telefund, the Viking Club, the Thomas E. Ricks Association and other fund-raising projects. In 1991 he joined the major gifts team in the Development Office and later was named associate director of donor relations.

Shaum, a native of northern Utah, is married to the former Kimberly Stoker and they are the parents of six children.
