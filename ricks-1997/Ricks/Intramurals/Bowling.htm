Coed-Bowling

1. We will play one game per week. The team with the highest three game total will win the championship.

2. We will play with teams of four. Two males and two females will play at all times. You may have as many alternates as you want. If you play short you may not count the missing game by having someone bowl twice. The score will be entered as played.

3. General bowling rules apply.
