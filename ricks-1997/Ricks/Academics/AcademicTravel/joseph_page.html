The spirit and determination of Chief Joseph and his people on their flight toward Canada serves as the backdrop of this outdoor educational experience. Highlights include -camping at beautiful Wallowa Lake, near Nez Perce ancestral grounds; Idaho's mountains: horseback riding in the Salmon River area; and camping in and exploring Yellowstone National Park.

An appreciation of the trial and struggles of these tribal people will help students understand principles in Biology, Literature, Religion and Horsemanship. All elements of this DISCOVERY excursion will combine to make this a fun, educational experience.

The team will be guided by Van Christman and Tate Carter of biology and Greg Hazard of English.

Enrollment limited to 30 participants
