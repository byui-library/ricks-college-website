A.F.Y. is a high adventure program for youth designed to promote personal and spiritual growth while developing lifelong friendships. A.F.Y. is held on the Ricks College campus in Rexburg, Idaho. Many exciting activities are offered in A.F.Y. including: a white water raft trip, a nature hike, dances, an overnight camp at the Ricks College Outdoor Learning Center, initiative training, canoeing, climbing, rappelling, and many other features designed for the adventurous youth. The spiritual aspect is enhanced by excellent devotional and workshop speakers that have been carefully selected to share their enthusiasm, humor and testimonies. Nightly counselor devotionals and a testimony meeting will also be part of the agenda.
