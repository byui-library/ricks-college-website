Clothing Construction II
CLOTH CONST II
CLASS CODE: HFED 307 CREDITS:  3 FEE:  $20.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Continued comparison of techniques of construction skills. Portfolio is completed along with practical application projects.
DESCRIPTION: Continued comparison of techniques of construction skills. Portfolio is completed along with practical application projects.
TOPICS: Using decorative stitches and gathering techniques to construct a pillow; Sew a dress, joining a bodice to a skirt; Knit shirt using the serger and learning various neck treatments; Fitted jacket with lining; Selecting the appropriate methods for different fabrics
OBJECTIVES: 1. Demonstrate construction skills on specialty fabrics.
2. Apply specific construction techniques for various design principles.
3. Derive solutions to construction problems.
4. Select the appropriate needle, thread, interfacing, and stitch for different fabrics.
5. Use decorative stitches in creating a design.
REQUIREMENTS: Dress Project; Pillow Project; Jacket Project; Knit Project; Mid-Term and Final; Attendance
PREREQUISITES: HFED 207
