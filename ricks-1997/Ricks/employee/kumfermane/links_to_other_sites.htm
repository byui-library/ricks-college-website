Links to Other Sites

Russian Language, Teaching, and Culture Sites

Russian Culture.  A great website with lots of information about Russian history, culture, and language. 

Bucknell University Russian Site.  Information on the Russian program at Bucknell.  Excellent resource for information on many aspects of Russian life including news, art, music, literature, and business.  Also includes on-line resources and links to other sites.

AATSEEL.  Homepage of the American Association of Teachers of Slavic and East European Languages.

ACTR.  Homepage of the American Council of Teachers of Russian.  This Russnet website has a series of resources for learning, teaching, and working with the Russian language.

ACTFL.  Homepage of the American Council on the Teaching of Foreign Languages.  Good link for jobs, information, and links for teachers of ALL foreign languages
