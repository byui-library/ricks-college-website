Steps for Ordering

(1) Schedule a room. Room reservations must be made with the scheduling office at 356-2242 prior to placing your order with the Catering Coordinator.

(2) We encourage as much lead time as possible in the booking of your reservation. When placing your order submit an approximate number of guests.

(3) Call, Fax, or e-mail your menu selections.
          Phone: 208-356-2247
          Fax: 208-356-2196
          E-mail: iviem@ricks.edu

(4) We ask for your guaranteed number of guests two days prior to your event. The guarantee number is what you are charged for. If you have more guests than planned, we will accommodate them. However, we can not assure the same menu for the extra guests as for your guaranteed guests. There will be an additional 15% emergency charge for the extra guest.

(5) Payment is required at the time of services. You may pay by any of the following terms: Account with the College (you must provide account number) - Cash or Personal Check - Credit Card - All Major Credit Cards

Please do not hesitate to contact our Coordinators to answer any questions
or assist you in your next campus catering event.
