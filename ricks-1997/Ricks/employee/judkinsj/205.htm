  • Course Description:  In narrow terms, this is a class about getting you ready for your first clinical experience in communicative disorders. In that setting, your goal will be to make a difference in someone’s life. As if that weren’t enough, this class will attempt a larger objective relating to the broad concept of change, ". . . the process whereby an individual, behavior, emotion, thought, or perception moves from one point to another (Goldberg, 35)." It sounds simple enough, but it is one of the most complex and incredible processes you’ll ever encounter. The objective of this class is for you to become a master of this process–a master of change--or at least for you to start down that road. Please note that being a master of change is vital not only in making a clinical difference in an individual’s "communication life," but has more immediate and farther-reaching applications as well. Note also, that it is a lofty goal, requiring a change in you that will involve learning not only in your head, but also in your heart.
