COW 101 T W Th

Intro to Cow Tipping

 It is a strange fact of nature that cows sleep standing up. Several theories have been proposed as to why this is so, but the prevailing idea is that sleeping upright is an evolved defense mechanism that provides the cow with a better opportunity to escape predators. Whatever the reason, you can use this natural anomaly for your recreational pleasure!
COW 110 T W Th

Intermediate Cow Tipping

 Cow tipping is a tradition among rural youths that has been around since humans first domesticated the bovine species. The practice is a great way to increase self-confidence at an early age. Also, with the increasing separation of humans from the natural world, cow tipping is
an easy way to get in "touch" with nature.
COW 111 T W Th

Advanced Cow Tipping

 However, the urbanization of rural areas worldwide has begun to threaten this long-standing and important custom. It is therefore imperative that cow tipping be kept alive so future generations at Ricks (and everywhere else) can enjoy this activity,which has been so important to rural adolescent development.
COW 162 T W Th

Fundamentals of Cow Tipping

 This course deals with the philosophy behind cow tipping and why it has become such a great recreational past-time of students all over the world.
COW 243 T W Th

Intermediate Virtual Cow Tipping

 Ok, a few matters to sort out: cow tipping is the act of clandestinely sneaking up on a sleeping cow and slamming it with all your strength until it tips over and falls. This is particularly funny since cows are inherently clumsy and have a bad sense of balance. It is usually not advisable to attempt this since there's usually a bull hanging around the pasture- and besides, it hurts the cows when they fall over. If that wasn't enough, it's also a felony! There really isn't anything overly cyberpunk about cow tipping (minus the element of anarchy) however, when the cow is a wireframe reconstruction in a virtual environment... well, you get the idea. By the way, these happy heifers can not only be tipped, but you can even try flipping them around and spinning them on their heads...something you've always wanted to do but were afraid to try.
