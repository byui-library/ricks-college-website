COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 104
Title: Surface Ornamentation Credit: 3
 Text: No required text

Course Objective:
To strengthen the student’s understanding of two dimensional design fundamentals by producing motif and pattern repeat for surface design on fabric.  To expose students to faux and painted finishes as an innovative and creative finishing method for interiors and furnishings in the design market.  To have each student produce a working set of faux finish sample boards for use in the job market.  To stimulate creativity.

Description:
Motif and pattern development for applied design on two and three dimensional surfaces.  Emphasis on screen printing, stenciling, and fabric painting, and/or dyeing, on fabric for interiors; pater making and mask making.  Also, faux finish and paint finish techniques, i.e., marbling, granite sponging, combing, and metallic leafing will be addressed.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
By using paint and a few simple tools, the student will simulate White Italian Marble, Serpentine Marble, and Granite, referred to as “faux finishes”.  With the use of paint and a variety of tools the student will produce 16"X16"X1/4" tempered masonite sample boards, including a crackle finish technique, rag rolling technique, spattering technique, feathering technique, plastic bag technique, sponging technique, and a creative board.  Students may choose from one of the following assignments to be completed on fabric: block print wax resist, block printing, linoleum block printing, screen printing, and a mixed media project.  Students are also required to complete a reference file with photographic examples, class notes, design inspiration, and any additional information gathered.
