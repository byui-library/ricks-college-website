Food and Housing Information

All participants will stay on the BYU-Idaho campus, and will be housed in dorms or an adjacent apartment complex. One night, the participants will stay at the Outdoor Learning Center and sleep in tents. After you are accepted into the program, information will be sent of what to bring (i.e., sleeping bag, personal items, etc.). The fees include meals starting with dinner on Monday through lunch on Friday.
