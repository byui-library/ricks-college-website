Eliza R. Snow Award for the Performing and Fine Arts
Mary Beth Cooper Cheney

Music has been a driving force in Mary Beth's life. At age 11 she was called as her ward's Sunday School organist. Today, as a homemaker in Livermore, California, she has been the organist for nearly every wedding and funeral in her ward and stake for over 25 years. She also heads Valley Voices, a private children's chorus, and now her own children provide the piano accompaniment. Additionally, she regularly accompanies and performs solos in church, city, school and other programs. About four years ago Mary Beth and two others founded the Blankenship Memorial Project in honor of an amateur pianist and fellow piano instructor in the Bay Area of California. The project, sponsored by the Music Teachers' Association, Alameda County East Branch, provides piano lessons and singing experiences to children from low income families who would not be able to afford pianos or lessons. Mary Beth, a former president of the association, currently serves as its membership secretary and treasurer.

An alumna of Ricks College in 1971, Mary Beth graduated Summa Cum Laude in music piano performance from Brigham Young University. In 1991 she obtained a Masters degree in piano pedadogy from Holy Names College in Oakland, California, again graduating Summa Cum Laude. She currently studies with Ray Bogas, a concert pianist and music professor. Mary Beth married Ricks alumnus Nolan Dean Cheney, a dentist practicing in Livermore, in 1973. Although at Ricks at the same time, they did not meet until both attended BYU. Two of their four children are currently attending Ricks College. Blair will graduate in April and Cami is majoring in piano performance.

She is currently the ward organist, choir director, and music chairman and directs the stake choir. She has also served in the Primary and the Relief Society.
