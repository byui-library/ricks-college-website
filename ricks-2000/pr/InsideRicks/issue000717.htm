RICKS ART DEPARTMENT MOVING DOWNTOWN

The Art Department at Ricks College will be moving downtown this summer as the college vacates the Spori Building in anticipation for it being torn down later in the year. Because there was no room on campus, the Art Department will be based at 16 E. Main St.

The college is leasing the building until a new structure is built where the Spori Building now stands. Administrative Vice President James Smyth hopes design work can be completed in early 2001 on the new building with construction starting in the spring. It is estimated that the new building will take from 20 to 24 months to complete.

The Spori Building has housed the Communication and Art departments; along with KRIC and KWBH radio stations, Graphic Services; and the campus newspaper, the Scroll. Communication classes will be moved to other locations on campus, the Scroll has been moved to the Manwaring Center and a new building currently is being constructed for the radio stations and Graphics Services.

PARSON FAMILY ART EXHIBIT IN SPRINGVILLE, UTAH

"The Parsons: Oliver, Del, Leon and Nolan" art exhibit will be on display at the Springville Museum of Art in Springville, Utah, through Aug. 27. Oliver Parson, now in his 84th year, taught art at Ricks from 1954-79. Three sons, Del, Leon and Nolan, also taught or are currently teaching at Ricks.

ACCIDENT CLAIMS LIVES OF AWARD RECIPIENTS

Richard "Rick" and Peg Young of Washington, Utah, recipients of this year's Presidential Service Award, were killed in an accident July 2 while traveling on Interstate 15 north of Idaho Falls. They had donated property to the Church on Vashon Island in Washington State. The property is used by Ricks students who participate in the college's Quest program. They also generously donated scholarships to single mothers and funds for the organ in the chapel of the John Taylor Religion Building.
