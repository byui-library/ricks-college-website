Joseph Smith’s journeys – winter results                                                                                                                 February 19, 2001

 

 


On April 8, we completed the Wellness Committee challenge program following the Journey’s of Joseph Smith.  A special thanks goes to the following individuals from the Religion Department who researched and wrote the weekly articles.  They include Steve Clark, Jerry Glenn, Doug Ladle, John Thomas, Roy Huff, Brian Kinghorn, and Nathan Williams.  Through their efforts we have learned many important and interesting facts pertaining to the early history of the Church.

      As we have done with the last couple of challenge programs, “bonus minutes” were given for those who ate at least five fruits and vegetables as well as drinking eight to ten glasses of water on a daily basis.  Many of you have expressed how much better you feel with the increase in energy and the improvement in the overall fitness.  We are delighted to hear of your successes.

      This past semester we had twenty-three teams involved during the twelve-week program.  We are happy to report the team with the overall most minutes was the “Hart Stompers”, with captain Judie Hood.  They had a total of 55,281 minutes!  Next to them was the “Butter Burners” led by Jean Hardy with 44,665 minutes.  After this there were several teams with over 30,000 minutes.

      Jim Lamph had the most individual minutes with 11,240.  Our hats are off to Jim for his devoted efforts.  Following Jim were Marie Parkinson with 8822 and Rick Stowers who had 7110.  There were a number of people who had over 5000 minutes.  All of you set an example for the rest of us to follow.  Everyone who participated received a gift compliments of the Wellness Committee.

      We will have another challenge program in the upcoming Fall/Sinter semesters.  The next one will follow the Mormon Trail and the footsteps of Brigham Young.  Now would be a good time to starting thinking about being on a team.  If you need help, please contact the Wellness Committee at Ext. 1040.

      Three members of the Wellness Committee have completed their term of service.  They include Jean Hardy, John Soules and Kim Andersen.  New to the committee are Kathy Cook, Ed Kinghorn and Vern Kaaiakamanu.
