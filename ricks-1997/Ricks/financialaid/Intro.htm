Most students at Ricks are eligible for some form of financial aid.  Federal Pell Grants, GSL and PLUS loans are available.  Ricks does not participate in Perkins loans, SEOG grants, work study, or any other campus-based programs.   Scholarships are awarded through the Admissions office.
