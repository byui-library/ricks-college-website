Senior Writing/Critical Thinking Seminar
SRWRTGSEM
CLASS CODE: ENG 495 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Provides a capstone experience for all English majors. Requires the preparation of a senior portfolio including the student's best work and the writing of a major paper relevant to the student's emphasis. (Required for English majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Provides a capstone experience for all English majors. Requires the preparation of a senior portfolio including the student's best work and the writing of a major paper relevant to the student's emphasis. (Required for English majors.)
TOPICS: Theory relevant to reading, writing and interpreting literature topics specific to the chosen theme or historic period; literary research; cultural contexts of literary works.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Reviewing in depth a literary theme or period. (2) Writing papers that apply English major course work. (3) Composing a researched, senior paper written to professional standards. (4) Developing oral presentation skills.
REQUIREMENTS: In-depth reading of assigned texts. Synthesizing and applying previous English major course work. Preparing a research project customized to the student's major emphasis. Presenting orally a review of research projects.
