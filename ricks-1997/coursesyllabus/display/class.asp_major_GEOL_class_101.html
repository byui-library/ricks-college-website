Introduction to Geology
INTRO TO GEOL
CLASS CODE: GEOL 101 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: General non-technical course for the non-science student who desires a broad introduction to Geology. Students who take Geology 101 may not also receive credit for Geology 111. This class is also taught on Discovery and Natural Science Field Expedition. Contact directors of these programs for special fees and other information.
DESCRIPTION: The study of rocks and minerals; landforms and environments; the processes such as erosion, earthquakes, vulcanism, and plate tectonics which have shaped our planet; as well as the importance and impact of geologic resources and hazards to our society.
TOPICS: 1. Introduction to earth systems
2. Atoms, elements, and minerals
3. Igneous rocks and volcanoes
4. Weathering and sedimentary rocks
5. Metamorphic rocks
6. Mineral resources
7. Downslope movement of surface material
8. Work of rivers and development of landscapes
9. Geologic processes in arid regions and the work of the wind
10. Geologic work of ice: glaciers
11. Shorelines
12. Ground water and water resources
13. Earthquakes and the earth’s interior
14. The oceans and the continents
15. Origins of crustal structures: sea floor spreading, continental drift, and plate tectonics
16. Climate change
17. Geologic time, relative and radioactive dating
OBJECTIVES: 1. To introduce the student to the basic concepts of Geology and provide a general education course for the non-science major.
2. To help students recognize the geologic forces which operate on our planet, and know how the basic land forms we see were formed. The role of time in all geologic processes will be presented to the student, and class members will be taught the importance of geologic processes in everyday life activities.

REQUIREMENTS: Required Lab: Take Geology 102
