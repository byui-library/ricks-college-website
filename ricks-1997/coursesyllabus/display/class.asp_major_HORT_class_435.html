Advanced Floral Design
ADV FLORAL DES
CLASS CODE: HORT 435 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Professional floral design with emphasis on retail flower shop operation, products, and materials. Particular attention is given to developing speed, proficiency, and quality after students have completed a floral internship experience.
DESCRIPTION: Professional floral design with emphasis on retail flower shop operation, products, and materials. Particular attention is given to developing speed, proficiency, and quality after students have completed a floral internship experience.
TOPICS: Floral products
Advanced design techniques
Retail presentation; improving speech and communication
OBJECTIVES: To help students improve their level of technical competence and gain confidence in their skills.
REQUIREMENTS: Floral tools, some materials may be required. Students may be required to purchase additional design material.
PREREQUISITES: Hort 335, 298B or consent of instructor.
Students may be required to purchase additional design materials
