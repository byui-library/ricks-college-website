Text Sizes

  • Computerized text is measured in points.
  • There are 72 points to the inch.
  • Standard text size is 12 point.


When projecting text, such as in a computer presentation, anticipate 1/4" of text height for every 8 feet of classroom depth (example: If my room is 40 feet deep, the text on the screen should project a minimum of 1 1/4 inches in height. Realistically, larger lettering will increase readability and reduce eye strain.

Posted lettering, such as on a bulletin board, should be 1/2" for each 10 feet of viewing distance.

Color and Readability

The legibility of text is not only a result of size, but also of case use and color combinations. The proper use of colored foregrounds and backgrounds can greatly enhance readability of the text message.

TEXT THAT IS ALL CAPITALS IS HARD TO READ. USE SMALLER CASE INTERMIXED WITH CAPITALS TO IMPROVE READABILITY. (WHEN USED ON THE INTERNET OR WITH E-MAIL, ALL CAPITALS IMPLIES THAT YOU ARE YELLING!)
