CAREER AND ACADEMIC ADVISING CENTER (CAC)

The CAC has full-time specialists available to help students with concerns about academic and career decisions.


An excellent career library containing extensive career and college transfer information is available in the CAC for student use. This center also contains a helpful computerized career decision making program to assist in making career choices.
ACADEMIC ADVISING

Majors Advising


Students who have selected a major are assigned a faculty advisor who assists them in individualizing their education. The advisor helps students (1) choose courses to prepare them for graduation from Ricks, (2) understand the lifetime
opportunities in their particular major, (3) adapt to the college environment and its challenges, and (4) reach their potential in accepting the challenges and realizing the opportunities at Ricks College.


Undecided Majors Advising


Students who have not chosen a major are assigned to a faculty advisor that can help them fill General Education requirements and select a major.
