Village of Haworth

Haworth is a small village in West Yorkshire. The Village is famous world wide for its connection with the Bronte sisters. Although the Sisters were actually born in Thornton, they wrote most of their famous works while living at the Haworth Parsonage while their father was a parson in the Haworth church.

There are many small hikes to take in and around the village, among which: hikes to the picturesque Bronte Falls, the Bronte Bridge, and the Bronte Stone Chair in which the sisters took turns to sit and write their first stories. This path forms part of the 40 mile long "Bronte Way."
