Talent Awards

Talent awards in music are given to those students whether music majors or not who present evidence of high talent and achievement in music and who are interested in continuing to develop their talent through participation in the Music Department programs.  Talent awards are not automatically renewed.  Recipients of talent awards who demonstrate continued growth and actively support departmental programs are encouraged to reapply for continuing student talent awards.

Recipients of talent awards are expected to participate in one or more major ensembles. It is expected that the student will continue his or her musical development through applied music lessons and individual practice.   Scholarship students are also often selected for leadership positions in various departmental programs.

The awards will be by a competition held on Saturday, February 9, 2002, at the Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts.  Audition appointments are limited, and students are encouraged to contact the department early to reserve a time (call [208]356-1259).  Applicants who live more than 500 miles from Rexburg may audition by video tapes (voice, winds, strings) or by audio tape (keyboard) submitted before February 1.

The audition requirements are as follows for each competition.
 

PIANO:  Ten to twenty minutes of your best memorized playing.  An "Allegro" first or final movement from a sonata, a piece by Bach, and a significant selection from the 19th and/or 20th centuries are strongly recommended.  Sight reading may also be included in the audition.   Talent awards are limited to piano majors.  A few small scholarships are available for  nonmajors for which a taped audition is requested.                                 

VOCAL:  A minimum of a ten-minute memorized program of song and/or aria demonstrating proficiency in a variety of styles and languages including: 
1.  At least one song or aria from the 18th or 19th centuries. 
2.  Additional songs or arias from either 18, 19 or 20th century to complete the ten-minute program. 
No music in any "popular" style is acceptable for this audition. 

STRINGS:  A ten minute program consisting of three contrasting numbers. 

WINDS:  At least five minutes of slow and fast movements of significant solo concert pieces or etudes.  Two major and two minor scales, from keys with two or more sharps or flats, once slowly, and once in 16th notes. 

PERCUSSION:  Audition requirements available by request from the Music Department.

 
We invite all who participate in these auditions and anyone coming with them to join us at a no-host luncheon where you may meet the music faculty.  More information will be given at the time you make your audition appointment. Winners of these awards will be notified by approximately May 1.
