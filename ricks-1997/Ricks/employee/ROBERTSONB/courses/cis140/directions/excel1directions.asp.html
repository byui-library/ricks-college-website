Excel Case 1: Page 4.42 – Internet Assignment

Open your web browser and type in the following URL exactly as it appears:

http://www.course.com/downloads/NewPerspectives/office2000/excel_main.html

Once you have arrived at the Excel Internet Assignments page, click 4.

The page should read: Excel 2000 Tutorial 4 Internet Assignments – Weather Research.

Print this page so you can refer to it as you complete this assignment.
Step 1 Do Not Print. Instead, write the information to a notebook, be sure to include the days and dates for each of the 5 days shown. To find the information you will have to do a European Region Search, Italy and find Venezia (this is the actual name of the city, Venice is the English translation).
Step 2 Enter the Fahrenheit temperatures for all 5 days. The weather page will provide the data for the current day and the next four days.
Step 3 Save the worksheet as lastf.section.xlcase1.xls to your ExcelSave subfolder inside the CIS 140 Completed Assignment folder on H:.
Step 5 Rename series 1 “High” and series 2 “Low”. The chart title should be Venice’s Five- Day Forecast.
Step 6 In addition to the assignment directions, change the data markers for the Highs to red and Lows to light blue.
Step 7 Ignore the online directions. Instead do the following:
 
  1. Insert a new column b. Enter the dates associated with your days.
  2. Label column E, Average
  3. Enter a function that will calculate the average temperature for each of the five days.
  4. Create a Clustered Column with 3-D visual effects chart that displays the average temp, the day and date.
  5. Change the name of series 1 to Average Temp.
  6. Name the chart, “Venice’s Average Temperature”. Label the X axis “Days” and the Z axis “Temperatures”. Hide the Legend.
  7. Save the new chart to sheet3. Rename the sheet “Average Temp Chart.”

Adjust the size of the chart so that all data is clearly legible.
Step 8 Be thorough in your purpose statement and worksheet description. Remember the scenario that you are working within. If needed, reread the introductory paragraph to the assignment.
Step 10 Ignore. Do not print.
