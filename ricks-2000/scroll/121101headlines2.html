BYU-Idaho student dies unexpectedly

by Andrea Scott
sco00003@byui.edu
Arts Staff

Hilliard
Police are still investigating the unexpected death of a 22-year-old BYU-Idaho student found dead in his apartment on 2nd West Sunday evening. An autopsy is scheduled for today to determine the exact cause of death.

Robert Allen Hilliard, a nursing student from Aurora, Colo., died unexpectedly in his bed after reportedly feeling ill since Thursday, Captain Randy Lewis of the Rexburg Police Department said. He was found by his roommates at approximately 7:54 p.m.

“We don’t suspect foul play, but we still have to investigate all unattended deaths,” Lewis said. “We have to investigate the cause of death, take interviews, gather evidence and eliminate possibilities. We have to treat it like a crime scene.”

A roommate reportedly called friends who owned a vehicle to take Hilliard to the hospital, but he had already died by the time his friends arrived at the apartment, Captain Garth Gunderson of the BYU-Idaho Campus Police said. He then called 911.

Greg Heiner, a local doctor and a member of the bishopric in Hilliard’s student ward, contacted Hilliard’s father and informed him of the death.

The Rexburg City Police are working with the assistance of the BYU-Idaho campus police to investigate the death.

Hilliard worked at the help desk on the third floor of the library. He was scheduled to graduate from the nursing program at the end of this semester.

Hilliard’s mother passed away approximately a year ago from cancer, Gunderson said.
