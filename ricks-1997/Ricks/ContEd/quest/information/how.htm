HOW IS QUEST DIFFERENT FROM BEING ON CAMPUS?

Foremost it is smaller. During your time at Ricks College you will experience the good and the bad of attending school with 8600 other students. At Quest, you will be in a cohort of 26 students in your program. There isn't competition with 8600 other students for classes or 30-minute lines in the bookstore, etc. An opportunity to grow and develop academically and socially with a small cohort of students is a hallmark of all Quest programs.

Secondly, hands-on learning or experiential learning is stressed.  The strengths of the areas or locations that the Quest program visits are utilized in the presentation of course work. As an example, at Vashon Island, Washington "Modern Social Problems" (Sociology 112) is taught. As part of this class, students have the opportunity to render service as part of their course work at homeless shelters, food banks and other charitable social organizations. Utilizing the natural existing strengths of Seattle and Tacoma gives students  a laboratory unlike anything else that could be offered from campus in Rexburg, Idaho.
