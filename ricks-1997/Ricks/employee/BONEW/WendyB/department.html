Ricks College Dance Department - General Information

The Dance Department at Ricks College offers an Associate in Arts and Sciences Degree with a major in Dance. This degree is designed for transfer to a college or university dance program, or to a professional training school. The dance courses are taught in two dance studios, an auditorium, and two ballrooms. Talent Awards are available from the Department of Dance. A variety of dance courses is offered to the general student body and are listed in the Ricks College Catalogue.

The dance performing groups at Ricks College produce shows throughout the year. During the Fall, Ballroom, CDT, and the Folk/Clog teams perform ‘Extravadance". There are two groups that may tour together for 3-4 weeks at the end of spring semester in an "Extravadance’ tour. The Folk/Clog Company may tour Europe for 5-6 weeks each summer. Style travels on weekends throughout the academic year to conduct dance workshops and firesides for wards and stakes throughout the northwestern states.

The faculty for the dance area of the department consists of Wendy Bone (Contemporary Dance Theater -modern, ballet), Shawn Fisher (Ballroom), Noelle Hammond (Modern), Robert Oliphant (Style), Charles West (Folk, Clog).

Those applicants with high scholastic achievement are encouraged to apply for an academic scholarship as well as a talent award. In the event an applicant is chosen as a recipient of both a talent and an academic award, they will be considered for awards to total no more than the full amount of tuition. Talents at Ricks College are not automatically renewable; however, recipients may reapply for subsequent years.

Recipients of talent awards in dance are not required to be dance majors. However, they are required to audition for performance teams and take at least one class per semester in the area in which they receive their talent award.

Auditions for the dance companies are held the first week of Fall Semester each year. There will be a mandatory meeting held for all who are interested in auditioning for any dance company. Each company will hold its own audition. More detailed audition notices will be posted in the Kirkham Building as you arrive on campus in the Fall. Check them for further information.

Ballroom Company Auditions - Be prepared to dance an American Style waltz and cha cha. Partner provided for audition. Street clothes.

Contemporary Dance Theater Audition - Modest leotard and tights, bare feet.
No prepared routine required for audition

Folk and Clog Company Audition - Know buck step and other clog step.
No prepared routine required for audition. Street clothes.

Style Company Audition - Basic knowledge of social dances. Street clothes
