A. David Allen

Phone: (208) 356-1926

Email: allend@ricks.edu

Brother Allen recieved his BS degree from Utah StateUniversity in 1964 and his MS from Oregon State University in 1965. He has been a U.S. Army Lieutenant, in the Signal Corp. and served in Vietnam from 1965-1967. He worked for the Reynolds School District in Portland, OR from 1967-1968. Brother Allen has been at Ricks College since 1968. Brother Allen has been a member of the American Association of Physics Teachers since 1963, and served on its board of directors from 1985-1988. He also serves on Selective Service Local Board 12 since 1981.
