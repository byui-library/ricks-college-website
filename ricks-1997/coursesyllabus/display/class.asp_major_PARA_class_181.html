EMT-AMBULANCE
EMT-AMBULANCE
CLASS CODE: PARA 181 CREDITS:  6 FEE:  $280.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HEALTH SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emergency Medical Technician training designed to qualify student to become ambulance attendants at the Basic Level upon passing the National Registry of Emergency Technicians - Basic written and practical exams.
DESCRIPTION: Emergency Medical Technician training designed to qualify student to become ambulance attendants at the Basic Level upon passing the National Registry of Emergency Technicians - Basic written and practical exams.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: To provide training and understanding that will prepare the student to be able to pass the National Registry Emergency Medical Technician Examination and to perform as an EMT-Basic with an ambulance service.
REQUIREMENTS: Fee $280 for disposable supplies, uniform, equipment, MOCK accident, and National Exam
PREREQUISITES: Concurrent enrollment in PARA 182 - consent of instructor.
OTHER:
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1992
