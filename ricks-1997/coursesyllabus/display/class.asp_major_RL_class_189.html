Careers in Recreation
CAREERS
CLASS CODE: RL 189 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: RECREATION LEADERSHIP
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Introduction to career opportunities in Recreation
DESCRIPTION: Discover new and different careers in the growing field of Recreation and Leisure. Students will be introduced to different careers by visiting different recreation facilities and by having guest speakers. Students will learn what opportunities for recreation careers really exist.
TOPICS: Explore careers in recreation and leisure.
Learn what is required in education and xperience for a career in different areas.
Learn what is required of individuals in particular careers.
Expose students to procedures for finding jobs in the field.
Help students to locate colleges, universities or schools they can attend after BYU-Idaho.
Expose students to different people in the recreation field.
OBJECTIVES:
REQUIREMENTS: Be involved in ALL class meetings.
Evaluation of guest speakers and field trips. (1 page reaction paper)
Career research paper. Present a paper on a particular career in recreation of your choice. Write about educational and experience requirements for the job. Include a total job description i.e. responsibilities, wages, benefits, opportunities for advancement, etc.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: Exams
Final - last day of class

Grading
Exams and Quizzes - 30%
Research paper - 30%
Speaker evaluations - 30%
Class participation - 10%
