Purpose

This course is designed for non-science majors and fills the physical science general education requirement. The purpose of the course is to give the student an introduction to the materials that make up the earth, i.e. rocks and minerals as well as internal processes (volcanoes, earthquakes, plate tectonics) and external processes (rivers, glaciers, erosion) that continue to modify and produce the physical world on which we live. The student should expect to master the terms and concepts that describe the physical world.

This course can be taken with Geology 102 which is the laboratory part of the class. Together these courses will provide 4 credits of physical science toward the 8 credit natural science option.
