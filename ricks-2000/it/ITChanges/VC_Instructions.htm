BYU-Idaho Video Conference Instructions

December 20, 2001

 

BYU-Idaho has videoconference capabilities that run over the Internet.  We can setup conferences with other institutions that provide videoconference over the Internet.  At this time we have setup the ability to conference with BYU-Provo, BYU-Hawaii, and LDS Business College.  Church Office personnel are welcome to use the LDS Business College facilities for conferences.  Following are instructions to initiate a videoconference.

 

1.      Schedule one of the “videoconference” capable conference rooms.

3.      At the scheduled time or several minutes before, go to the scheduled conference room.  Open the cabinet and turn on the TV.

 

4.      Use the (TV/PC/AV) button on the TV remote to select NTSC S-VHS.

 

5.      Take the microphones (they look like a computer mouse) attached to the Polycom camera unit and string them out and locate them apart from each other on the table.

 

6.      Turn on the power to the Polycom camera unit (facing the camera the power switch is on the back side upper left corner).

 

7.      After it comes up, use the address book to select the conference location(s) you wish to dial/connect with or wait for the other end to connect to you.
