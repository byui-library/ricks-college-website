HORSE PACKING IN THE TETON MOUNTAINS
Explore the spectacular western slope of the Teton Mountains by horseback; travel some to the areas that Jedediah Smith, John Colter, Jim Bridger, and other mountain men traveled. Learn fundamentals of horseback travel; study Teton Mountain history, especially the fur trade era, visiting Pierre’s Hole, a major historical mountain man encampment. Study environmental features such as local botany. Food/ camping gear transported by pack horses. Participants will learn proper care and safety with horses and minimal impact camping. Hostelers should be in good physical condition and able to ride horseback in mountain terrain for several miles per day going from elevations of 6,000 up to 8,000 feet and be comfortable with camping.
