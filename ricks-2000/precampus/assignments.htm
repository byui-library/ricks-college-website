FINAL EXAMINATION:

1. Develop a four-year course schedule that has been well thought out. Students should use online BYU-Idaho internet resources such as the recommended course sequence for your program of interest. Students should make three copies of this class schedule, these copies are for:   (1) the New Student Journal, (2) to review with your Academic Advisor during academic advising week, or before, and (3) for your own records.  

2. Register for classes.

3. Write a final New Student Journal Entry describing your experience in participating in this course.

5. Participate in the Fall, Winter, or Summer "Departmental Orientation Meetings" as part of the campus orientation program.  Students admitted to fall block, winter block, or first summer term are not required to participate in this activity, but are highly encouraged to do so at the next orientation (Fall, Winter, 2nd Summer Term).

6. Submit your New Student Journal, or declare your intent to register by coming to 250 MC by no later than the third Friday after classes have started (until August 1st, at which time the NSO office will be relocated to Rigby Hall).

7.  Pick up your journal within three weeks after dropping it off.
