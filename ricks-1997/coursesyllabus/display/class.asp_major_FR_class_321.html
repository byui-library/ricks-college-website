Advanced Grammar and Composition
ADV GRM & COMP
CLASS CODE: FR 321 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A course leading to greater mastery of French Grammar.
DESCRIPTION: Through speaking, listening, reading and writing exercises students practice and master a wide varitey of grammatical forms in modern French.
TOPICS: French grammar.
OBJECTIVES: Students finishing this course should be able to speak at intermediate high level with realtively few errors and write carefully revised, three page essay with few grammatical errors.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, oral participation in class, a textbook, listening CD's and handouts, and a pronunciation guide.
PREREQUISITES: French 201 with a grade of C or higher or consent of instructor
