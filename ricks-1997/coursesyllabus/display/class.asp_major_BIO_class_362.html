Wetland & Riparian Ecology
WET/RIP ECOLOG
CLASS CODE: BIO 362 CREDITS:  3 FEE:  $35.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An in depth examination of Wetland and Riparian Ecology. Emphasis is placed on stream channel morphology and function and ripariaan plant succession. Links to terrestrial and riparian components of applicable ecosystems will be investigated.
DESCRIPTION: An in depth examination of Wetland and Riparian Ecology. Emphasis is placed on stream channel morphology and function and ripariaan plant succession. Links to terrestrial and riparian components of applicable ecosystems will be investigated.
TOPICS: Stream morphology, stream function, properly functioning condition, riparian plant succession, plant/herbivore interactions, aquatic vertebrates and invertebrates.
OBJECTIVES: Introduce students to the principles of Wetland and Riparian Ecology.

Facilitate internships for students who are majoring in Natural Resource Management.

Provide a solid knowledge base for students taking course as a requirement for Natural Resources minor.
REQUIREMENTS: Proper dress for field trips
PREREQUISITES: General Ecology
OTHER: $35.00 Lab Fee
