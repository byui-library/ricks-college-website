Student Life and the Manwaring Center now offer an Online Bulletin Board where students can place ads or search for the following online:

Calculators / Computers
General Sale / Want Ads
Housing / Jobs / Ride Board

There is also a ride board posted on the bottom floor of the Manwaring Center by the Ticket Office.

The Ride Board is a way for students to find a ride, or look for riders to help pay for gas to various destinations.  Most students use the ride board for weekend travel from Rexburg to Utah or other locales.

The Online Bulletin Board web site requires students to login using their username and password.
