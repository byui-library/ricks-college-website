 Welcome to the Business Expedition 2001 webpage!  Want to earn college credits and visit major businesses and sites in major cities such as; New York, Boston, and London England?  Join us for the eight-week summer term and learn from top executives from 21 different major corporations, establish connections for future internships and jobs, and develop a lasting relationships with fellow students.
This unique opportunity allows student to earn up to 11 credits. Experience church history sites such as Hill Cumorah, Palmyra Temple, and Preston, London Temple.   Visit Wall Street, climb the Statue of Liberty, and tour Harvard Business School.  See a Broadway play in New York, Les Miserable in London, and Shakespeare's Julius Caesar in Stratford-on-Avon.
During the 5 weeks on campus, visits will be to Playmill, Sun Valley, and Jackson Hole.
