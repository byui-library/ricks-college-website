Do you want to serve the students of Ricks College?  This is your chance to make a difference!   With a plethora of positions to be filled, we want you to come in and apply.   Time commitments are flexible, and there are a variety of programs, clubs, activities, and causes to get involved with.  You can even run for an office yourself!  Come to the ASRC Offices, on the 3rd floor of the Manwaring Center, and pick up an application.  Here are just some of the duties and responsiblities of the officers and their committees:
