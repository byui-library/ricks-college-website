SWOT Analysis

Team Exercise #2--(30 points)

Do a SWOT analysis on the your assigned business idea which demonstrates the merit of the idea and helps determine where the companies should allocate its resources (see pages 44 & 45).  In doing the analysis your team should do the following:

Strengths and Weaknesses
  • Outline your company's internal strength
  • Outline your company's internal weaknesses or limitations.
    Note: Identify all assumptions
Marketing Environment

Identify all relevant elements of the marketing environment.  Go through each one--think hard)

Opportunities and Threats

Identify all relevant factors from the external environment that seem to indicate that this is a market opportunity

Identify all relevant threats from the external environment that might pose a challenge or minimize your success.

Discuss how you can match opportunities to your company's strengths (leverage)

Discuss how you might turn threats into opportunities, or at least how you could minimize any vulnerabilities that exist where weaknesses match up with threats.

Discuss how you can overcome weaknesses that may be constraints that prevent the company from pursuing opportunities.

 

Strengths:

Definition:  "Competitive advantages or core competencies that give the firm an advantage in meeting the needs of its target markets."  "...should be customer-focused."

Examples:

  • The company possess a highly trained and capable sales force.
  • Service record, experience, reputation
  • Access to a certain market
  • Knowledge about a distribution channel and/or good relationships with channel members
  • In expensive labor
  • Experienced management
  • etc.

Weaknesses:

Definition: "Limitations a company might face in developing or implementing a marketing strategy.

Examples:

  • Limited resources
  • Inexperience in this market
  • Opposites of above
