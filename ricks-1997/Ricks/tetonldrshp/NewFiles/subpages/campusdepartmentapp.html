The Leadership and Service Institute (LSI) seeks to develop and prepare students for leadership and service in the family, Church, workplace, and community. Our threefold mission is to:

o Promote Leadership and Personal Development
o Instill Life-long Service Values through Participation
o Strengthen Families through Marriage Enrichment

The Institute’s primary objective is to serve the students of Ricks College. However, the Teton Lodge facility is available for campus departments to use for in-service training or planning sessions for which the outcomes obtained will be used to serve the Ricks College community. The Teton Lodge is not to be used for social or recreational activities. This facility is made available through the generosity of its owner and Ricks College donors.

Application

To schedule the lodge return the completed application to Van Crawford in MC 380 (+0790). Reservations are granted on a first-come, first-served basis. Upon approval, a letter of confirmation will be sent to the applicant.
