REMINDER FOR MORE EFFECTIVE ADVISING

Care about advisee by showing empathy, understanding, and respect.

Establish a warm, genuine, and open relationship; evidence interest, helpful intent, and involvement.

Be a good listener.

Establish rapport by remembering personal information about advisee.

Be available; keep office hours and appointments.

Provide accurate information.

When in doubt, refer to catalog, advisor's handbook, etc.

Know how and when to make referrals, and be familiar with referral sources.

Don't refer too hastily; on the other hand, don't attempt to handle situations for which you are not qualified.

Have students contact referral sources in your presence.

Don't make decisions for students; help them make their own decisions.

Focus on advisee' strengths and potentials rather than limitations.

Seek out advisee in informal settings.

Determine reasons for poor academic performance and direct advisee to appropriate support services.

Clearly outline advisee' responsibilities.

Follow up on commitments made to advisee.

Encourage advisee to consider and develop career alternatives when appropriate.

Keep an anecdotal record of significant conversations for future reference.

Evaluate the effectiveness of your advising.

Don't be critical of other faculty or staff to advisee.

Be knowledgeable about career opportunities and job outlook for various majors.

Encourage advisee to talk by asking open-ended questions.

Don't betray confidential information.

Categorize advisee' questions: are they seeking action, information, or involvement and understanding?

Be yourself and allow advisee to be themselves.
