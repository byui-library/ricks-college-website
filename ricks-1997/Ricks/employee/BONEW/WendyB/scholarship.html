Ricks College Talent Award in Dance - General Information

Applicants must take the following steps to apply for a talent award in dance:

1) Apply for admission to Ricks College. You may apply for a Talent Award before admission. However, Talent Awards will not be granted until applicant has been admitted to Ricks College.

2) Apply for a Talent Award in Dance. The applicant should complete the Dance Talent Award application. There is also a dance evaluation form to be completed by instructor/director. A personal letter of recommendation from a school or church teacher or other leader should be sent to the address below.

3) Auditions. This requirement may be satisfied by a taped audition. Tapes should be in a V.H.S. video format, and must be labeled with your name, address, and title of selections. Use fresh tapes only. (Maximum of 10 minute audition)
Submit tapes and Talent Application to:

Ricks College Department of Dance
Talent Award Committee
Krikham 211
Rexburg, ID 83460-0300


You may Download the Talent Application form below. It can be opened and printed using any word processor.

    Download Application for Dance Talent Award

Application materials must be mailed to Ricks College between January 1 to February 1.

Awards will be granted on the basis of funds available and the talent qualifications of the applicant.

If you need additional help completing your application, please feel free to write or call the Department at (208) 356-2073.
