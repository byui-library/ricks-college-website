Ricks College News Release, September 8, 1997

Ricks admissions application now online

Power up your home computer, link to the Ricks College home page and you can now apply for admission.

While it's not required, applicants have the option of applying electronically over the internet for admission to the largest private junior college in the country. Of course, they may also apply using standard paper applications.

Steve Davis, assistant director of admissions, believes the electronic process will be easier for both applicants and college workers.

The process is not a 100 percent electronic at this point. Endorsements from religious leaders and seminary teachers are still required and must be filled out and sent in. High school and college transcripts must also be mailed.

Davis says it took about a year for a team of Ricks computer specialists to get the new application online. The main challenge was to develop a system that protects the confidentiality of personal information.

He says it's the most advanced online application of any college in the state. Unlike many other online college applications, once information is submitted to Ricks, it does not have to be re-keyed by workers in the admissions office. Instead, the information is immediately downloaded to the college's main computer system.

Considering the admissions office received about 13,000 applications last year, Davis believes this will lead to considerable savings in such costs as manpower, postage, and printing of materials. Less mail will have to be opened and sorted and fewer applications will have to be electronically scanned or re- keyed once they're received.

Applicants also have the ability to check the status of their admissions. Through the internet, they can see if their application is complete or what parts are missing.

Davis hopes at least 20 percent of the applicants will apply online for the coming school year.

Applications for the winter semester are due by Oct. 1 and for fall semester by Feb. 15.

For more information, see the Ricks internet site at www.ricks.edu and follow the links to admissions.
