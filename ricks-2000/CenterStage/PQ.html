Pacifica Quartet

Wednesday-Thursday, Jan. 16-17, 2002
7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall

The Pacifica Quartet has quickly built an enviable reputation for its dazzling ensemble work and exuberant performances. They are winners of the 1998 Naumburg Chamber Music Award and 1996 Coleman Chamber Music Competition.

Tickets go on sale Jan. 7. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Recital. Concert dress required for BYU-Idaho students.
