Course Description:

This introduction to management information systems focuses on the use of computers in business and society with emphasis on managements understanding of the role and use of information technology in the digital economy.  The topics covered include concepts and management of information technologies for competitive advantage, communications systems infrastructure in the web revolution, managerial and decision support systems, data security/control, and implementing and managing information systems in organizations.

 

Information technology involves computers but is more than just computers.  No matter what role you play in your organization – founder entrepreneur, manager, production worker, supervisor, front line staff, salesperson, or accountant – using information technology effectively will help you and your organization survive and thrive in today’s competitive, globalized marketplace.  The course focuses more on understanding the broad implications of information technologies such as, competitive advantage, organizational redesign, and business process reengineering rather than gaining specific technology skills. 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Course Objectives:

After completing this course the student should be able to: Understand and apply information systems that will be helpful in supporting the information needs of businesses and organizations in a global society. 

1.                    Appreciate the pivotal role of information in management. 

2.                    Understand basic information systems technology and terminology. 

3.                    Gain an appreciation of the systems development life cycle. 

4.                    Understand the role of a non-IS person in shaping the development, management, and use of information systems in organizations.

5.                    Make effective use of computers, communications technologies, and the Internet for research, communications, and other college/university and career activities. 

6.                    Identify and locate sources of information about new developments in information systems.
