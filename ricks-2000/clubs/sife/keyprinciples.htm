Key Principles

 

1.  To teach the importance of individual integrity and the social concept
     of being closely connected as the elements to prosperity in today's global
     economy.

2.  To teach the advantage of continually improving upon one's
     interpersonal, technical and monetary skills, which are vital to success.

3.  To teach how supply and demand drive the marketplace, and how
     businesses can use that model to fulfill the consumers needs as well as their own.
