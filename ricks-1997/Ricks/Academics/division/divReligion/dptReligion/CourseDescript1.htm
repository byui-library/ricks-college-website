Teachings for Life

Genealogy of the Church and other Church

Rel 251* 
The Gospel and World Religions		 Two hours per week:
A study of religions of the world such as Buddhism, Islam, Hinduism, Confucianism, Judaism, Sikhism, Jainism, and Zoroastrianism.  This course is designed not only to introduce the basic belief and practices of the world's religions, but also to show these religions in comparison with each other, with the ancient religions of Greece and Eqypt, and with christianity.		 2
Rel 252* 
The Gospel and Christian History		 Two hours per week:
A study of the history and doctrine of Christianity from the Roman period through the early development of the Catholic and Orthodox churches, to the middle ages, the Reformation, the period of the enlightenment, and up to modern times. 		 2
Rel 261* 
Introduction to Family History (Genealogy)		 Two hours per week:
An introduction to the Family History program of the Church; emphasis is on identifying ancestors up through four generations and submitting needed names for temple work; use of Church Family Search (computer) program, including the PAF; use of other secondary resources to identify ancestors; writing personal history.		 2
Rel 264* 
Family History Research		 Two hours per week:
Prerequisite: Rel 261 or competency with PAF & Fmaily Search programs or consent of instructor            Lab Fee: $5
          A research orientated course, with emphasis on using the origianl records of vaious countries.  Results of research will be pointed toward doing temple ordinance work for ancestors and the compiling of family histories.		 2
