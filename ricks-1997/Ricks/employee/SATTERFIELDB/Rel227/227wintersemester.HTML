RELIGION 227 - The Pearl of Great Price


Instructor: Bruce Satterfield Office: Taylor 216 Phone: 356-1566

COURSE OBJECTIVES

1. To view the Pearl of Great Price as a Temple Text.

2. To study the major aspects of the temple endowment as outlined by Elder James E. Talmage

3. To collect several articles that will aid in the study of the temple.

4. To come to a clearer understanding of the doctrine of faith.

5. To understand the purposes and differences between the Aaronic and Melchizedek priesthoods.

6. To become fully acquainted with the Abrahamic Covenant (Marriage covenant) and its promises.

Texts

1. The scriptures of the LDS church (Bible, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, Pearl of Great Price). Bring all four standard works to class every time!

2. Selected quotes. (You will find these on my home page)

Grading

A.  Scripture Reading Assignment Outlines On the following page you will find listed under the Daily Reading Assignments what will be studied each day. On the days that have a reading assignment marked with an asterisk a summary outline of that reading assignment will be due. These outlines must be typewritten. Handwritten outlines will result in a lower grade. If the outline is more than one page it MUST be stapled or you will lose points. The outlines must be turned in on the due date found in the Daily Reading Assignment section to receive full credit. You will receive ½ credit if turned in after the due date. Any exception must be approved by me. No late outlines will be accepted after April 1. The scripture reading assignments will be worth 15% of the overall grade.

B.  Article Summaries During the course you will be required to summarize 7 articles dealing with various topics. You will find the title of the articles and the dates they are due in the Daily Reading Assignments section. These summaries should be brief enough to fit on one page but in-depth enough to cover the major points of the article. These assignments will be graded on the depth of coverage and observations made. Summaries must be typewritten. Single space is allowed. No late summaries will be accepted after April 1. The article summaries are worth 25% of the overall grade.

C.  Paper Towards the end of the course, you will hand in a typewritten paper concerning these Pearl of Great Price subjects: (1) faith, (2) premortality, (3) the creation of the earth and man, (4) the fall of Adam and its results upon mankind, (5) the atonement of Jesus Christ, (6) the preparatory gospel, (7) the everlasting gospel (the holy order of God), (8) the laws of obedience, sacrifice, and consecration, (9) the Abrahamic covenant, and (10) the role of man and woman. This paper should be no more than 8-10 pages. Single spacing is allowed! Your audience is a member of the church who knows little about the Pearl of Great Price. Begin the paper with an introduction and then explain each of the ten areas. You should use scriptures, class notes, and other sources to help you in this paper. Use footnotes, endnotes, or text notes with a bibliography at the end. The paper must be divided into the ten divisions listed above with a heading that describes each division. Please hand in stapled with NO COVER!!

This paper must be written during March 23 - April 13 and not before!! It will be due April 13 AT THE BEGINNING OF CLASS! Any paper handed in after the beginning of class on April 13 will be considered late resulting in lower grade!

I will grade the paper by checking to see that you have all ten areas covered and then I will read one or two areas carefully and grade the paper on those areas. The paper will be worth 40% of the overall grade.

D.  Collection of Temple Articles You will be required to compile a collection of 30 articles that relate to temple subjects (Temple worship in general, creation, fall, atonement, marriage, role of men and women, ordinances, etc.). These articles do not need to be read! This collection must have a your name and a table of contents at the beginning to receive full credit. The only time this assignment will be accepted is on April 18, the final day of class! You will show it to me but not hand it in. Any collection handed in at my office will not receive a grade. The article collection will be worth 20% of the overall grade.Disability
