Ricks College News Release
March 5, 2001

Women's Week at Ricks College expected to draw visitors to campus

REXBURG, Idaho — An estimated 2,000 visitors are expected to attend the annual Women’s Week at Ricks College from Thursday to Sunday, March 15-18.

While at Ricks, the visitors, who are mainly mothers of current students, will have the opportunity to spend time with their children and attend some activities especially planned for visitors and students.

"The purpose of Women’s week isn’t to have an educational week, but to build relationships between mothers and their students," says Kris Fillmore, organizer of the event that is sponsored by the Associated Women Students.

Regular classes will continue Thursday and Friday and some instructors will allow mothers to attend class with their students.

There will also be a wide variety activities during the four-day event. Tickets for activities are limited, and some are already sold out including the Performing Arts Showcase, Extravadance, Osmond Brothers concert and mother-daughter buffet.

Activities on Thursday include announcement of the Woman of the Year at 2 p.m. in the Taylor Cultural Hall, a Fashion Show at both 4 and 6 p.m. in the Manwaring Center West Ballroom, Extravadance performances at 6 and 8 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium and Symphony Orchestra concert at 8 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall.

For the first time, Women’s Week will include a service project for students and their mothers Friday from 9 a.m. to noon. The Humanitarian Service Center of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints has provided material for participants to cut out fleece vests. The cut fleece then will be sent to Salt Lake City to be finished and then distributed to needy people throughout the world.

The official welcome will be at 2 p.m. in the Taylor Chapel by President David A. Bednar and his wife, Susan Bednar. The Spring Garden, Flower and Bridal Open House will be held in the Benson Building from 3:30 to 8:30 p.m. and a recital by Orpheus Winds in the Barrus Concert Hall will be held at 4 p.m. Following the Performing Arts Showcase in the Hart at 7:30 p.m., a lip sync dance will be held at 9:30 p.m.

Workshops will be held from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. on Saturday. Fillmore emphasizes that the purpose of the workshops is to involve mothers and their children who are attending Ricks. Area residents are reminded that many mothers are visiting from out of state and that space is limited to each workshop.

The mother-daughter buffet will be at 12:45 p.m. and mother-son buffet at 5:30 p.m.

The Osmond Brothers from Branson, Mo., will perform concerts at 3 and 7:30 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. The show will feature Jay, Merrill and Wayne Osmond performing everything from country to pop music.

Sunday will feature a fireside at 5 p.m. by Virginia H. Pearce, a daughter of LDS Church President Gordon B. Hinckley. She was scheduled to speak last year, but bad weather prevented her from arriving at the airport. She will speak on "Glimpses into the Life and Heart of Her Mother, Marjorie P. Hinckley." Admission is free, but the fireside will be ticketed. Tickets for the Hart Auditorium are already distributed, but overflow will be available in the Taylor and Kirkham buildings.

For ticket information, contact the Ricks College Ticket Office at 356-2230 or online at www.ricks.edu/tickets.
