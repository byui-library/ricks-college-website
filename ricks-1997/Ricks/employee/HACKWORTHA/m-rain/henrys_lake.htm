Henrys Lake
              audio
Nutrient rich as Grandmother's vegetable stew,
Henrys Lake sweetens her mint-green shores with dew
And implores all who sip her beauty to drink freely.
Her banks rise into bread-soft, grassy meadows
Which stretch gently over the hills.
.
In the clean, mountain soup,
Large, hybrid trout thrive
And profoundly teach the meaning of design.
 
Non-stop through the summer,
These silk shadows glide and gorge themselves
On nymphs, slugs, and the right amount of minnows.
 
For these lunkers, it's a smorgasbord delight.
Henrys Lake, a fertile, mountain womb,
Never stops giving birth from June to June.
 
Bugs, moss, and aquatic vines,
Seagulls, sand-hill cranes, and lines of pelicans
Form a vibrant, thriving world.
 
Henrys lake is never the same.
Her ever-changing face connects
With the hills, peaks, and trees,
With the clouds and south breeze,
With the dancing winds and soft rains,
With the emerging, mid-day, or fading light,
With the bright moon glow and quiet nights.
 
Sometimes when the summer sun
Shines brightly through white, puffy clouds,
Thunder crashes like a loud argument
Southwest of Jefferson Peak.
 
Then, taking its time,
A black shroud spreads across the sky,
And someone cries,  "Take cover."
 
Like new water flowing down an empty ditch,
A storm rolls north on its way to Montana.
Rain stretches its long, liquid fingers to the ground
And white caps pound the shore.
 
I love it all:
The changing waters,
The rugged, nearby lands,
The demands the weather makes on campers,
The low-flying birds
Which drift and float with the ease of a smile.
 
With a grateful heart,
When I start a prayer of thanks,
I won't forget the taste of fresh trout,
Fried in butter and lemon pepper,
Wild Rose Ranch, Staley Springs,
Professional-guide Bill Schiess,
Who brings success to any fishing trip,
Power boats of all sizes,
Float tubes, fishermen galore,
And fertile Henrys Lake
With its hatchery on the north shore.
