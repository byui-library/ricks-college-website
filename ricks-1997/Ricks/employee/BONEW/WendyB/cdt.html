The Mission of
Contemporary Dance Theater

But behold, that which is of God inviteth and enticeth to do good continually; wherefore, everything which inviteth and enticeth to do good, and to love god, and serve him, is inspired of God. —Moroni 7:13

As Selected members of the Ricks College Contemporary Dance Theater we focus to glorify God through our love and passion for the art of dance. We understand the intense level of commitment, dedication, and sacrifice that is required in order to obtain such a focus. Our primary responsibility and calling as members of this company is to stand as "witnesses of God at all times, and in all things, and in all places." (Mosiah 18:9)

By doing this we aspire to portray the Light of Christ, becoming missionaries for this gospel. As such we desire to share our testimonies of what we believe to be true, of principles that have changed our lives, and of happiness that is exquisite. We accept this calling on an individual level and recognize the personal obligation to worthiness and constant self-improvement that accompanies such a privilege. We yearn to discover Heavenly father’s purpose and goals for us as a team and as individuals. A higher level of respect, cooperation, and unity is expected. Above all these things, there must be a constant expression of gratitude for the divine blessing we receive. It is our privilege, our responsibility, and our desire to share that which "inviteth and enticeth to do good, to love God, and to serve him…

We seek to inspire.
Auditions for the Contemporary Dance Theater (CDT) will be held August 31, 2000 from 5-9 in the KRK dance studio.

Women - please wear modest dance attire, tights and leotard. No baggy shirts or pants.

Men - please wear modest and comfortable clothes, sweat pants and t-shirts are fine.

You will not need to prepare a dance for the auditions. A few short combinations will be taught from which the judges will make cuts, choosing the team before the end of the evening.

If you are selected to be a member of CDT you will be required to meet the following obligations.

1.) Commit to one full year of participation.
2.) Enrollment in DA 241A
3.) Mandatory attendance to rehearsals (T TH 8-10:5Oam and most Sat. 8am-12noon)
4.) Attendance at all concerts and performances.
5.) Participate in the end of the year tour.
