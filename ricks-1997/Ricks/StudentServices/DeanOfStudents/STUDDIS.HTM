Grounds for Dismissal of Students

Over the years, students have been dismissed from Ricks College for some 
of the following violations:

 1.	Academic dishonesty
 2.	Alcohol and tobacco violations
 3.	Assault and battery
 4.	Destructive behavior
 5.	Drug involvement, i.e., use, possession, sale, and being present 
	where used
 6.	Immorality
 7.	Overnight with opposite sex
 8.	Severe violations of the Code of Honor
 9.	Criminal violation which constitutes a felony

In the past there has been confusion regarding the college's stand on 
members of the opposite sex staying overnight together.  The position of 
the college is that overnight activities such as camping or staying in 
motels or individual apartments with members of the opposite sex is 
prohibited and grounds for dismissal.  The following excerpts from 
devotional addresses by President Bennion have been included as 
guidelines:

	Now I could well spend the remainder of my time to elaborate on 
	each important part of the code.  I will not, but I strongly urge 
	you to take a few minutes to read and reflect on them.  I will 
	add, however, two cautions or reminders of related rules of the 
	college:

	The first is to remember that the curfew hours are for both men 
	and women students:  11 p.m. on weeknights and 1 a.m. on Friday 
	and Saturday evenings.  A majority of past problems and violations
	of standards have occurred after hours.  We have given the clear 
	signal and expectation to your dorm and apartment managers that 
	housing hours are to be enforced.  They are for your benefit and 
	protection!  Again, this vital rule will be enforced!

	Now, please beware that there are powers and influences, even 
	subtle forms of deception, that would have you demean or 
	rationalize the code or parts of it as not being important.  Each 
	part is important to sustaining the light and spirit of this 
	college.  Perhaps you might be tempted to think that some rule is 
	for others  but not you, or you may hear someone say that the 
	Code of Honor violates your freedom of choice.  Each of you read 
	the code before coming and signed your word, your commitment, to 
	sustain and uphold it.  You chose to come here under the 
	conditions outlined.  We expect you to honor your word, to keep 
	your integrity.  There are too many students waiting in the wings 
	who would love to be here, and no space was left.  There has been 
	too much toil and sacrifice throughout the Church to expect less.


	The second caution is that visitors are NOT (and I emphasize NOT) 
	to enter the bedrooms of anyone of the opposite sex.  Visits to 
	apartments are to occur ONLY when three people are present and 
	with the approval of roommates!  (From September 8, 1992, 
	devotional by President Steven D. Bennion)

	Another tendency might be staying out too late with your date or 
	staying too long in the car with your girlfriend or boyfriend.  I 
	probably do not need to elaborate on this, only to clearly state 
	that housing hours at Ricks are there for a purpose and a 
	protection to you and others.  The same is true for visitors of 
	the opposite sex.  They are to leave an hour before the regular 
	curfew hours--which means 10 p.m. on school nights and 12 midnight
	on Fridays and Saturdays.  There are to be no visits by members 
	of the opposite sex in your bedrooms--at any time!  Again, 
	remember to avoid the edge of the cliff.  (From September 11, 
	1991, devotional by President Steven D. Bennion)

	I also want to make this absolutely clear (as Tevye, alias John 
	Bidwell, and LaRae Clark did so well in the orientation assembly 
	last week).  I'm sure this only needs repeating for a small 
	number.  "There is no excuse--I repeat--no excuse or reason for 
	anyone of the opposite sex to be in your bedroom or that of one 
	of your roommates.  That is private domain, and anyone who does 
	not follow this counsel brings into serious question his or her 
	privilege to remain at Ricks."  --President Joe J. Christensen  
	(From September 5, 1989, devotional by President Steven D. 
	Bennion)
