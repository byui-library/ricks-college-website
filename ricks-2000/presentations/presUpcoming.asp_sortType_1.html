Weekly devotionals are held in the Hart Auditorium on Tuesdays at 2 p.m. Featured speakers include General Authorities and other Church leaders. Additional overflow seating is located in the Taylor Chapel and Kirkham Auditorium.

Devotionals are broadcast on KBYI-FM 100.5 at 2 and 9 p.m.

Audio cassettes of most devotionals can be ordered through the BYU-Idaho Bookstore by e-mailing Bookstore@byui.edu or by calling (208) 496-2211.
