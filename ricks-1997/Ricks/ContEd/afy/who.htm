Who May Attend?

Adventure for Youth is open to young men and women ages fourteen to eighteen. Ricks College does not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, sex, ethnic or national origin. Participants accepted into the program agree to maintain the standards of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and the Ricks College code of dress and conduct. The code includes neat and modest dress, abstinence from tobacco, alcohol, coffee, tea, and harmful drugs; high principles of integrity and decency; and respect for the personal rights of others.
