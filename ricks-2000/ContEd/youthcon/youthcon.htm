The BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center (OLC) also

known as Badger Creek Ranch is available for scheduling of 

Youth Conferences, Young Women Camps, Scouting activities, etc.

This facility is situated 45 miles east of Rexburg on the western

slope of the Teton Mountain Range (about an hours drive).

 

For more information on scheduling our facilities and the availability of them, you 

can e-mail RobisonC@byui.edu or BadgerCreek@Ricks.edu

 

The cost for each program will vary depending on the

length of stay, activities, food events, and so forth.
