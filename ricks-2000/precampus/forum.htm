New Student Forum

Discussion Board

The New Student Forum, an online discussion board, allows admitted students and selected current students to participate in an open forum discussion on issues of importance to incoming students.  

The New Student Forum requires a username and password to login into the Pre-Campus Orientation online discussion board.  

1) Click on the New Student Forum link to enter the discussion board.
2) Enter username and password below.
3) Click on "Pathway Pre-Campus Orientation" located on the right side of the screen.
4) The discussion board will show up.
