Hello!  My name is Lance Taylor and I am your ASRC Student Body President  and I am looking forward to spending the upcoming school year together.  What does ASRC stand for?  It stands for Associated Students of Ricks College, and every student at Ricks is part of ASRC.  It's really just another way to say Student Body.  As I have been among you recently, I am so impressed with the number of quality individuals we have comprising our student body.  You truly impress me and I am humbled to work with you.  I along with other student body officers are looking forward to serving, and spending time, and getting to know you.  Please, I invite you to find ways to get involved.  One by one we can make a difference.
