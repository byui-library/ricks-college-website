Always Rethinking
BYU-Idaho is rethinking education. Creating a two-tiered institution that allows students to choose between associate or bachelor's degrees. Developing integrated degrees that are interesting, relevant, and marketable. Expanding our student activities program to open sports, the arts, and service to more students. Preparing for the expected increases in enrollment by developing an innovative year-round track system. Focusing our faculty on learning and teaching. While Preserving and enhancing the "Spirit of Ricks."
