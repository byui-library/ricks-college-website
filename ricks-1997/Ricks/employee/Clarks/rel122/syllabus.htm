1.MISSION STATEMENT: WE WILL STRIVE TO: Build testimonies of the restored Gospel by teaching the scriptures and writings of modern day apostles and prophets. Measurable outcomes should include a better understanding of history, places, peoples and doctrines as given in the inspired revelations. It is also desired that commitment to God will flourish as individuals deepen their knowledge, prepare themselves for the sacred ordinances of the temple and determine to serve the Church through missions and day to day activity in the Lord's Kingdom.



DAILY READING - The scriptures bring guidance, peace and strength. 1 hour every day all semester with weekly written update, take tests, write in daily scripture journal, contribute to class, etc. gets an "A". You have yourself in a situation ,where,(if you do not fall from grace) your day of judgement has been moved up and your calling and election is made sure!



ABSENCES "For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them" Matt. 18:20 -0 absences raises earned grade 1 notch. 1 or 2 absences grade remains as earned. With 3rd absence grade drops one additional notch with each additional absence. It is most important that you attend. These absences are for any reason except school excused which do not count as absences. (A notch is the next grade: A to A-)



2. EXAMS - FOR REVIEW PURPOSES ONLY. 3 Exams will be given in the Testing center. Dates are indicated on the accompanying reading schedule. These will require only 70% to not affect your grade. If you drop below that we can give you opportunity to retake. These must be taken at the times scheduled. A 25% penalty for late takes.

3. READING - THIS IS WHERE YOU WILL GAIN YOUR GRADE.50% reading 50% journal. Read the indicated references before the class during which they will be discussed. You can keep track of your own reading score by giving yourself 1 point for the COMPLETE study of the lesson including student manual and writing in your journal (one hour average per day, 7 hours per week)

4. CONCLUSIONS (JOURNAL)- IS TO BE DONE WITH THE READING.50% of your grade. Write at least 2 lessons for your life. Where possible identify Doctrines, Principles and Appropriate applications from each days readings. give the reference and the meaning you saw for you. Try to average a page once you get going. Journals need to be brought to class daily to be checked for credit on the reading-journal effort.

5. CLASS PARTICIPATION - Plan to make at least 10 significant contributions to the class sharing ideas, insights and conclusions found in your study. This will keep your grade from dropping a notch.

EXTRA CREDIT OPTIONS:
