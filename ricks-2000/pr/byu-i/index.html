TRANSITION SURVEY

On June 21, 2000, Gordon B. Hinckley, President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and Chairman of the Board of Trustees for Ricks College, announced that Ricks College would become a four-year institution known as Brigham Young University-Idaho. 

The official change in name took place August 10, 2001, in preparation for the beginning of the Fall Semester. During the Fall Semester, the new university will begin offering classes leading to bachelor's degrees in several disciplines. Other majors will be phased in during the next four to five years.
