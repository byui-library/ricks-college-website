Fall Campus Orientation Overview

Fall Orientation Schedule -subject to change


Monday, September 3, 2001:

Info Fair
10 a.m. - 2 p.m. - Manwaring Center West Lounge

Representatives from various offices and campus services will be available to answer questions and disseminate information to interested parents and students.


Parent Orientation
10 - 11 a.m. - Manwaring Center West Ballroom

Parents can learn about the BYU-Idaho experience from President and Sister Bednar, as well as other university administrators.  This event typically features a question and answer session with the President.


Presidents Reception For Parents
11 a.m. - 1 p.m. - Manwaring Center East Ballroom

New students and their parents are invited to visit with President and Sister Bednar and other members of the University administration during the President's Reception.  Light refreshments will be served.  Representatives from various areas on campus will also be adjacent to the ballroom in the Manwaring Center West Lounge as part of the Info Fair to answer questions and distribute information to interested parents and students.


Meet Your Y-Team/New Student Barbecue
3:30 - 5:30 p.m. - Intramural Fields

The New Student Barbecue provides new students the opportunity to meet other students, make friends, and catch the "Spirit of Ricks" at BYU-Idaho.  Here, new students will meet other members of their Y-Team as well as their Y-Team Leaders.


New Student Commencement
6:00 - 7:00 p.m. - Taylor Chapel

New Student Commencement serves as the official welcome to BYU-Idaho by President and Sister Bednar.  The commencement also explores the nature of education at BYU-I, including the principle of learning by "study and by faith."


Campus Tours
7 - 8:00 p.m. - Campus

Following the New Student Commencement your R-Team group will take you on a walking tour of campus.  Here you will learn more about the various buildings, campus services and the history and traditions of BYU-Idaho.


Monday Night Extravaganza
8:00 - 11:00 p.m. - Manwaring Center/Taylor Quad

The Monday Night Extravaganza is a welcome back activity sponsored by ASBYUI and NSO for new and returning students to BYU-Idaho.  The Cost is $4.00 per student and includes food, dancing, inflatable rides, games, prizes and giveaways, concerts and much more.  You won't want to miss this exciting event.



Tuesday, September 4, 2001:

Morningside: AArise for Honor"
8 - 9 a.m. - Taylor Chapel

New students with their Y-Teams are encouraged to arise early and attend the "Arise For Honor" Morningside which focuses on the tradition of honor at BYU-Idaho.  A continental breakfast will be served to all who are in attendance following the event.


Y-Team Breakout Discussions
9:30 - 10:00 a.m. - Various Locations

Y-Teams will conduct a breakout discussion regarding the Morningside presentation.


Academic Department Orientations & LUNCH
10 a.m. - 12:00 p.m. - Various Locations

The Academic Department Orientation meetings provides you the opportunity to meet with faculty and advisors regarding your chosen discipline.  Ask your Y-Team Leader where your specific Academic Department Orientation meeting will take place.  FREE LUNCH included.


Service Project/Involvement Fair
2 - 2:20 p.m. - Taylor Chapel
2:20 - 4:30 p.m.- Intramural Fields

New students, with their Y-Team, will learn about the importance of service as well as participate in a meaningful service project which will benefit the lives of others around the world.  We will be assembling hygiene kits, painting toys, and cutting Delta Airline seat covers  for LDS Humanitarian Services.  There will also be an involvement fair as part of the rotations.  Students can see what student organizations are available to them to get involved in on campus.


Y-Team Breakout Discussions
4:30 - 5:30 p.m.

Y-Teams will hold a Y-Team Breakout discussions following the Service Project.  Here Y-Team Leaders and members can share their thoughts and feelings on the service project, the day's events, orientation, the gospel, BYU-Idaho, or other topics of choice.  


New Student Orientation Concert/ASBYU-I Welcome Back Dance
7 - 8 p.m. - Intramurals Field - New Student Concert 
8 - 10:15 p.m. - Intramurals Field ASBYU-I Dance.
10:15 - 10:30 p.m. - BYU-Idaho Kickoff Celebration - Fireworks presented by NSO.

An outdoor concert and dance for all students will take place on the intramural fields.  There is a $4.00 fee to participate in the concert and dance.  A fireworks display to celebrate the first semester as BYU-Idaho will follow the dance.


Sunday, September 9, 2001:

CES Young Adult Fireside
7 - 8 p.m. - Hart Auditorium, Taylor Chapel, Kirkham Auditorium.

Y-Team Breakout Discussions
8 - 9 p.m. - Various Locations

Y-Teams will hold their final Y-Team Breakout Discussion.  Here Y-Team Leaders and members can share their thoughts and feelings on the Fireside they just participated in, the gospel, Ricks or other topics of choice.  This is the last formal opportunity for Y-Teams and Y-Team Leaders to meet together as a group as part of campus orientation.

Tuesday, September 11, 2001:

First fall semester Campus Devotional with President & Sister Bednar. 
2 - 3 p.m. - Hart Auditorium (also broadcast to the Kirkham,  and Taylor buildings).
