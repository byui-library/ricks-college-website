Fencing
FENCING
CLASS CODE: PE*C 110 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: To introduce the student to the sport of fencing through experience with the French foil and introduction to the 'Epee.'
DESCRIPTION: To introduce the student to the sport of fencing through experience with the French foil and introduction to the ‘Epee.'
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. Acquire a sense of self-control and self-discipline
2. Increase courage and dedication
3. Knowledge of physical conditioning through fencing
4. Knowledge of terms and muscles used
5. Improve flexibility of arms, legs, and back
6. Develop skills to teach fundamentals of fencing
7. Ability to organize a tournament
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Exhibit knowledge and skill of fundamentals, rules, and strategies of fencing.
2. Attend class and participate
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences allowed
2. four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Failure grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
