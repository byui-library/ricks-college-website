COURSE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

This course will help each student to:

* have fun and learn a lot!!!

* gain interest in and appreciation for human anatomy and physiology,

* understand basic biochemistry,

* understand cell structure and function,

* understand the structure and function of the skeletal, muscular and nervous systems.
