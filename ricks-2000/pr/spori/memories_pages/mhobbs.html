Michael L. Hobbs (79)

In the fall semester of 1977, my new roomate from Burley, Idaho, begged me to go with him to the Spori building to audition for a singing group on campus. At that audition I learned the meaning of tone deaf. My roommate couldn’t carry a tune, yet sang with gusto. Luckily for us, he didn’t get the part.

Michael L. Hobbs (79)
Albuquerque, N.M.
