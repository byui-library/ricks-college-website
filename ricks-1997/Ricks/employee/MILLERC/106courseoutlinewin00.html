OSM 106 BUSINESS ENGLISH

 

COURSE

OSM 106 Business English is a 3-credit course meeting three hours per week on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. This course includes the rules of spelling, punctuation, usage of numbers and abbreviations, grammar, and proofreading. This course has no prerequisites and does not transfer to any school.

TEXTS

Business English at Work by Jaderstrom and Miller, Study Guide for Business English 106 by Miller and Anderson, and The Gregg Reference Manual by Sabin.

A three-ring binder, note paper, texts, and a red pen or red pencil.
