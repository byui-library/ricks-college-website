TRANSFER STUDENT ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Application -- The application can be submitted on-line or by requesting a paper application by calling the Admissions Office.  Information provided includes personal data, educational plans, high school/college information,   personal essays, and out-of-class activities.

Personal Recommendations -- Recommendations are required from an applicant's current ecclesiastical leader and seminary or institute teacher (LDS). The required forms are provided in both the paper and on-line applications.  

Application Fee -- $25 non-refundable application fee is required for all applicants--both on-line and paper.  

Transfer Credits -- Transfer students must have the Registrars Office of each school attended mail an official transcript to the Admissions Office. If you have taken courses at an LDS Institute of Religion, an institute transcript must also be sent. All transcripts must be received before admission consideration is given. In cases where applicants are applying with less than 12 semester (18 quarter) hours, a high school transcript and ACT/SAT scores must sent to Ricks College.
