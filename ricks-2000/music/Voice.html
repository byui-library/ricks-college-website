THE VOCAL ENSEMBLES

Collegiate Singers     (conducted by Kevin Brower) is the premier off-campus performing choir at Brigham Young University-Idaho. Comprised of 40-42 of some of the best singers on campus, this group provides both singers and audience members an exciting experience of a wide variety of musical styles and periods.  Collegiate Singers rehearse daily from 12:00-12:50 in the Recital Hall.  In addition, one hour of rehearsal with an assigned small group or quartet will be required each week. This choir generally tours each year in addition to many on- and off-campus performances. Non-music majors who are members of this choir need to enroll concurrently in Applied Voice Lessons (M104 or M158D).  Music majors also need to enroll concurrently in Applied Voice Lessons (M155, M159D, M160D, or M260D) as a part of their music major requirements.   Acceptance into this group is by audition (see requirements below).

Concert Chorale     (conducted by Kevin Brower) is an auditioned ensemble of mixed voices for those whose schedule otherwise limits their activity during the day. Concert Chorale meets one night a week in cooperation with the Upper Valley Civic Chorale.  Performances include major works ranging from the standard repertoire of choral/instrumental literature to more intimate unaccompanied selections.  Concurrent enrollment in voice lessons is encouraged, but not required.  Acceptance into this group is by audition (see requirements below).

Men's Choir    (conducted by Randall Kempton) is an auditioned all-male chorus of 90 - 100 singers which is sure to thrill audiences of all ages.  The Men's Choir repertoire ranges from barbershop to Gregorian chant, from sacred hymns and spirituals to the best of Broadway and Hollywood, and from serious classical music to popular a cappella.   In addition to rehearsing and performing independently, Men's Choir combines regularly with Women's Choir for special rehearsals and performances to be announced at the beginning of each semester.   During the 2000-2001 year, the Men's Choir joined other campus choirs and instrumental ensembles to present Handel's Messiah, the Ricks College Sacred Music Project (an oratorio composed by Daniel Gawthrop), and the music for the Saturday afternoon session of April General Conference.  Men's Choir rehearses daily, 1:00-1:50. Concurrent enrollment in voice lessons is encouraged, but not required. Acceptance into this group is by audition (see requirements below).

Women's Choir     (conducted by Eda Ashby) is an auditioned all-ladies chorus of 90 - 100 singers.  The purposes of the Women's Choir are:   to provide an equal opportunity for women at Brigham Young Universtiy-Idaho to participate in a quality choir, to develop the vocal and musical talents of each member, to provide opportunities for church, community, and school service and leadership through class, special rehearsals, and public performance of high quality choral literature. The Women's Choir rehearses daily, 1:00-1:50 in the Fourth Ward Church.   Women's Choir will also combine with Men's Choir for special  performances such as Handel's Messiah.  Concurrent enrollment in voice lessons is encouraged, but not required. Acceptance into this group is by audition (see requirements below).

Women's Glee    conducted by Marybeth Jones is an open-enrollment, all ladies chorus.  This choir provides its members with the opportunity to learn to blend their voices in close three and four part harmonies.   Music performed is challenging, yet not as advanced as that performed by the Women's Choir. Women's Glee rehearses MWF, 11:00-11:50.  No audition is necessary for this class.

University Choir      (directed by Randall Kempton) is an open-enrollment choir designed for all students interested in developing basic vocal and musical skills through group singing.   Members will develop an appreciation for traditional and modern choral literature as they participate in rehearsals and on-campus performances.   University Choir rehearses MWF from 10:00-10:50 in the Recital Hall.  No audition is necessary.

Opera Workshop    Opera Workshop provides the opportunity for vocalists to become familiar with standard opera literature, dramatic techniques, and the production procedures of staging a complete opera.  This is accomplished through regular concert performances and the biennial production of a complete opera in the Snow Drama Theater.  In addition to performance opportunities, the history and tradition of opera are presented through lectures and multimedia presentations.

Auditions   Audition dates for Collegiate Singers, Concert Chorale, Men's Choir, and Women's Choir are September 1, 3, and 4, 2001, in the Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts Building (SNOW).  A sign-up sheet will be available on the above dates. It would be very helpful if you could plan to audition on the first or second day of auditions. Only one audition is required for all of these groups. The directors will then meet together to decide in which group you will be most successful.  Prepare a two-minute excerpt of one art song or aria for the auditions which best showcases your voice, or men may sing a prepared hymn. An accompanist will be provided or you may want to provide your own  (our accompanist will be sight-reading).  

Vocal Union  (Kevin and Nori Brower, artistic directors),  is an ensemble of mixed voices specializing in popular styles of vocal jazz, musical theatre, and "swing era" tunes.  Vocal Union rehearses five hours a week over a three day period: M W from 2:00-3:50 and F from 2:00-2:50. On many occasions, Vocal Union  combines with Sound Alliance Jazz Band in entertaining performances of contemporary music. Each year this ensemble participates in many on- and off-campus performances.  Tours are planned for each year with  extended travel on a biennial basis. Concurrent vocal instruction is recommended.  Music majors should consult with their academic advisor or area coordinator before considering this ensemble.
