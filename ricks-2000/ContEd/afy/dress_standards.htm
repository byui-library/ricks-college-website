BYU-Idaho

Dress & Grooming Standards

DRESS AND GROOMING FOR

WOMEN

Women are encouraged to wear modest

attire in good taste, refraining from

excessive or extreme styles.  Wardrobe

selection should reflect modesty and

femininity.

DRESS AND GROOMING FOR

MEN

Men are encouraged to wear modest

attire in good taste, refraining from

excessive or extreme styles.  Wardrobe

selection should reflect good taste and

masculinity.

APPROPRIATE

Nice Jeans and shirts

Modest pants or slacks

Dresses, skirt with blouses or sweaters

Hair is neat, clean, and feminine

Nice Jeans and shirts

Shoes and socks

Slacks or pants, sport coats and ties

Hair is near, clean and trimmed
