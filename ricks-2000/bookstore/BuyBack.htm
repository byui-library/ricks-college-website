Ricks College Bookstore       Buy Back

During the finals week of each term, we conduct a used book "Buyback."   During this Buyback period we pay 60% of the current retail price for books that meet the following criteria:

  1. An instructor has submitted a Textbook Request for a future term prior to Buyback.

  2. The book is the correct edition, volume, etc., and is in good condition.

  3. The book is not a workbook with fill-in or tear-outs or a packet/shrink-wrap with unbound pages.

  4. The book is not overstocked from prior terms.

If a book does not fit the above criteria for Ricks Buyback, it is considered for a wholesale buyback.  Wholesale means that the book is shipped out of state to a national wholesaler who will, in turn, sell it to another bookstore.

The price you receive for a wholesale book is determined by the national demand.  It generally is between 10% and 30% of new retail value.
