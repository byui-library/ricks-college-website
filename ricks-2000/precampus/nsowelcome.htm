Welcome to BYU-Idaho.  We look forward to your arrival to campus.  As members of the 2001 New Student Orientation Team, we look forward to the opportunity to assist you in your transition to college life, and hopefully make it easier for you.  

Our first suggestion to you is to prepare now to come to BYU-Idaho.  You can start your preparations by reviewing all information contained in the Pathway program.  This award-winning program will help you prepare yourself for a BYU-Idaho learning experience.  Second, plan to participate in all New Student Orientation Activities with your Y-Team.  When you come to college you will be assigned to a Y-Team.  Y-Teams are an essential part of New Student Orientation and will help you  become familiar with campus, help you meet new people and make new friends, and learn how to get involved in all that college has to offer.

Do not hesitate to contact us with any questions you or your parents may have.  You may reach us by calling (208) 356-2393, or by sending an e-mail to orientation@ricks.edu.

We look forward to the opportunity of serving you.

Sincerely,


2001 NSO Team
