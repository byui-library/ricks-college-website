Become acquainted with this syllabus and know what is expected of you and how the class will operate.

Textbook
Algebra and Trigonometry by Bittinger, Beecher, et. al

  • This textbook is mandatory.
  • Any additional books, student manual, calculator helps, etc. while helpful, are not required material for the class.
  • A TI-85 or TI-86  calculator is required for the class. 


Tuesday and Thursday are lecture days for Math 111

  • Lectures will be given to introduce textbook topics.
  • Assignments/quizzes will be given. Check the Class Journal to stay updated on weekly class proceedings.
  • Attendance to class is strongly recommended.


Keep all of your returned and graded papers in a folder. These will serve to verify the accuracy of midterm and final grades.

To calculate your grade:

  1. Take the number of points you have earned
  2. Divide this number by the total number of points possible
  3. Multiply by 100
  4. Locate this number(your percentage) in the grading scale to determine your current grade. Keep proper track of your points and you should know your grade for each class period.

Important Note:

Late assignments, missed quizzes and tests will not be made up, unless prior approval is granted by the instructor and the reason for the lateness must be extraordinary. It is your responsibility to get things done on time.

Suggested out-of-class study time for this class is 1 hour per day(Monday through Saturday)

I suggest the first-half hour be devoted to reading, pondering, working through examples, etc. in  the assigned section in the textbook  I suggest that notetaking and comments be written in your textbook. The second - halfhour of the of the study period should be used to work on assigned practice problems. All practice problems assigned in a textbook section should be done in a notebook neatly and completely.  Practice problems are not to be turned in. However, it is important that you do all of them.  On those problems you don't understand, you must  clarify by means of study groups, tutors, or the professor.


This time schedule, while strongly suggested, may need to be altered depending on the individual.situation.

Tutoring Help

If you feel the need for one to one math tutoring for this class, go to the counseling office in the  and they will set you up with a tutor free of charge.
Don't wait until you are totally lost in the class to seek help. A little help daily, early in the semester, can make a world of difference in course.   Department provided tutors will also be available.  Calculator help is also available from department tutors. Studying in groups with other students in the class can also be helpful.
