Hierarchal Links

"Being of good cheer may not halt or deflect the downpour of discouragement which is drenching others, but it is the unfolding of an umbrella and walking together under it.", Neal A. Maxwell.

 

Use 3D Studio to create an animation of the robot shown below using hierarchal links.  Create the robot using either AutoCAD or 3D Studio. Keep the construction simple, as shown, using cylinders or boxes for most of the construction.   Inside 3D Studio add the appropriate hierarchal links as shown in the graphic and described in the table below.

Linkage Name

Motion Required
V-Shaft Vertical movement, full length
Hinge Horizontal rotation, 360 degrees
H-Arm Vertical rotation, approximately 120 degrees
H-Shaft Horizontal movement, full length
Hand Vertical rotation, 360 degrees plus grasping action

 

FRAME

DESCRIPTION OF EVENTS
0-100 CAMERA PAN - Begin with a camera pan camera around entire robot in circular fashion, returning to starting point
100-400 JOINT ANIMATION - Show full articulation of each joint (sequential not simultaneous motion) in the robot, always returning to the starting position
400-600 TASK - Next the robot should pick up an item, move it to a new location, release it gently, and then move back to its starting position.

Note: the item to be moved should not be visible through the first 400 frames
600-700 CAMERA PAN/MOVE - Lastly, zoom in for a close-up on the base of the robot to reveal the Ricks College logo and your name

Create an avi file of your animation at a resolution of 320x240. Use appropriate background scenery, lighting effects and materials. Turn in an electronic copy of the max and avi files in the DropBox, use the keyword Robot.
