Elder Joseph B. Wirthlin: Follow Jesus, get rid of entangling nets

by Jenna-Leigh Tracy
TRA99010@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

ELDER WIRTHLIN
“Peter and Andrew stopped as Jesus of Nazareth approached, looking into their eyes, and spoke the simple words, ‘Follow me’,” Elder Joseph B. Wirthlin of the Quorum of the Twelve said. “The two fisherman ‘straightway’ left their nets and followed Him.”

“Have you ever wondered what it must have been like to have lived in the days of the Savior?” he said. “If you had been there, would you have heeded his call, ‘Follow me?’”

“Perhaps a more realistic question might be, ‘If the Savior were to call you today, would you be just willing to leave your nets and follow Him?” Elder Wirthlin said.

“Some have to discover that nets, by their very nature, are sometimes not easy to leave,” he said. “Nets come in many sizes and shapes.”

“Nets are generally defined as devices for capturing something,” Elder Wirthlin said. “We might define a net as anything that entices or prevents us from following the call of Jesus Christ.”

“Nets can be our work, our hobbies, our pleasure and above all else, our temptations and sins,” he said. “In short, a net can be anything that pulls us away from our relationship with our Heavenly Father or from his restored church.”

“Many of us enjoy watching athletic contests, but if we can recite the statistics of our favorite players and at the same time forget birthdays or anniversaries, neglect our families, or we may ignore the opportunity to render acts of Christlike service,” he said.

“If we are sincere in our desire to follow him, we must straightway leave the world’s entangling nets and follow him,” Elder Wirthlin said.

“Sometimes we feel that the busier we are, the more important we are — as though our ‘busyness’ defines our worth,” he said. “We can spend a lifetime checking off list after list of things that, in the end, really don’t matter.”

“We have nets that must be tended and nets that must be mended,” he said. “But when the Master of the ocean, earth, and sky calls to us, we should leave the entangling, worldly nets behind and follow his footsteps,” he said.
