PREREQUISITES: EET 152.





TEXT: Boylestad, Robert L. 2000. INTRODUCTORY CIRCUIT ANALYSIS, Ninth Edition. Prentice-Hall, Inc. Upper Saddle River, New Jersey.





COURSE MATERIALS: 1. HP48 calculator or equivalent or better.

2. Engineering Graph Paper for problem assignments.





COURSE DESCRIPTION: Sine waves are introduced. Phasor Algebra is used in solving AC circuits. Network theorems are applied to the solving of complex AC circuits. Resonant circuits and harmonics are studied. The analysis and generation of non-sinusoidal waveforms is included. Lab included. Oral student presentations are required. Computers are used to simulate AC circuits and write research reports. (Fall, Winter, and Summer)





STUDENT OBJECTIVES:



1. To be able to analyze circuits and solve problems dealing with ac circuit principles.
2. To be able to determine proper component values for ac circuits.
3. To be able to apply ac circuit principles which are necessary for success in later electronics courses.
4. To be able to use computer simulation to solve AC circuit problems.
