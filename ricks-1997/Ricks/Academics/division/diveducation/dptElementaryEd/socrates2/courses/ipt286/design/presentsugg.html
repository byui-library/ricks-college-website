Computer Presentation Suggestions


When using text on computer screens for presentations

  • Use brief, concise textual phrases (5 + or - 2 words per line; 15-20 words per screen)
  • Avoid long quotes
  • Don't read the slides to the audience -- paraphrase or provide examples


When you have an abudance of information to convey

  • Break it down into small components
  • Cover the components individually first, then build connections
  • Cover complex material more than once


When you have very little visual information to convey

  • Don't use single words as prompts on presentation screens
  • Do provide brief statements of the point to be conveyed
  • When showing a graphic, provide a label or brief explanation
  • Use verb phrases to clearly state the point being made

Bulleted text lists

  • Confusion in bulleted text messages can result from grammatical differences
  • Start bullet points with the same grammar element
  • Maintain titles, subtitles and headings in the same location

Puctuation and capitalization

  • Inconsistent use of punctuation and capitalization make presentation appear amatuer
  • Be consistent -- use punctuation to clarify your points
  • Use capitals for all important words in a title
  • Don't use periods in titles or subtitles

Many times periods can or should also be avoided in bulleted text
