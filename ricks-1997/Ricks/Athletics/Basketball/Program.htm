2001-2002 SEASON PREVIEW

    It seems that Coach Clyde Nelson is always starting from scratch with each new basketball season. It’s close to that this year. One starter returns from last season, and a number of players with limited experience.

    Back from last season is guard Adam Hiatt who will take over at the point. Hiatt was the second leading scorer on last years team with a 13.4 points per game average. He was the second leading three point shooter on the team. "I think he’s going to be pretty good. He’ll be our leader and make us go," says Coach Nelson. Hiatt could play the ‘two’ spot as well. Behind Hiatt is Zach Jacobs and Joe Green. "Jacobs is probably our fiercest competitor. He’ll pick up the tough defensive assignments. Joe is a great shooter."

    Aaron Bowman holds down the ‘two guard’ position right now, and will be tough to move out. "Aaron is a Ben Caton type player. He can jump, shoot, and has great heart. He’ll play at the next level." Chris Howard returns for his sophomore year and will back up Bowman, along with Ryan Moats.

    Todd Amberry has waited patiently for his opportunity to play at Ricks. He had some experience with the team that went to nationals. He has size and is a very good shooter. Kelly Nead of Driggs is back at Ricks, this time to play basketball. "Kelly is hard nosed, a good shooter. He’ll play a lot." Allen Whitehead could also contribute here.

    At power forward stands Tyler May. "He’s this teams Dan Beus or Seth Dahle." Meaning Tyler May doesn’t mind mixing it up inside. He can shoot, he’s very athletic, very tough. "I’m expecting a lot from Tyler May." Luke Chatwin saw playing time at this position last year, but knee problems could limit Chatwins time on the court.

    Rexburg’s Kyle Stevens has been impressive at the post spot. "Kyle is bigger and stronger than when he was in high school. He can move outside and hit the long jumper." Stevens is very mobile, can run the floor well, and understands the game. Backing Stevens up will be Aaron McKee and Jeremy Thomas. McKee is a transfer from Big Bend Community College. Thomas saw playing time last season for the Vikings. Both need to step up.

    Overall, "I like the chemistry of the team. I like how hard they work. I like their intensity. I like how they communicate. We’re better in all those areas than we were last year." Nelson is concerned about the inside game. "We need someone that can post up and do some good things for us inside. We don’t have that right now, but we have to find it or create something."

    The league is as strong as ever. Dixie and Southern Idaho are the teams to beat. Snow, Utah Valley, and North Idaho are teams that can challenge. "Dixie has great talent coming back to go with great recruits. CSI has a new coach, but he’s been in the league before and won, and he has talent waiting. Snow, North Idaho, and Utah Valley are always close. It will be a tough league once again."
