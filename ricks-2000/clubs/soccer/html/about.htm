For several years Ricks College has been involved in men's soccer. Although it has only been at the club level, the program has struggled through hard times to become a prominent contender in the men's arena.

The founder of Ricks College Soccer is Oscar Bustos who volunteered the time and equipment necessary to run the program for many years. In the early 80's Oscar Bustos, a former Chilean professional soccer player came to the Rexburg community and began coaching the men's team. Thanks to Oscar, the program enjoyed several successful winning seasons. Oscar deserves tremendous credit for such service.

In order to receive recognition from the college administration, however, the club needed a college employee to be an advisor. Robert Bird, who for three years played varsity soccer at Olympus High School in Salt Lake City, offered to advise the club. In the Fall of 1995, Oscar continued to coach the team, while Robert worked for more official support of the team from the College and increased the club's active participation in the league and tournaments of the Intermountain Collegiate Soccer Association.

In the Fall of 1996 and 1997, Robert and Roy Paynter coached the team and coordinated some official support from Don Schiess, Intramural Director, and Preston Hailey, Club Sports Director. Don planned and oversaw the completion of a new lighted soccer field, and Preston obtained and coordinated some college funds to help the club cover travel expenses. Because of the intensive time commitment required, Robert was relieved to have Peter Stilling become the club advisor in 1997. Peter --dedicating tremendous time, energy and money-- has made the program strong and progressive.

Stilling immediately took over as the men's soccer advisor in the winter of 1997. After having been employed by the college for only 4 days, he held tryouts for their first ever indoor team. Two men's teams were formed and competed very well in the local league. The following spring of 98 resulted in Stilling coaching the men's team. The men played their first ever game with their older "brothers" at BYU and were invited back the following spring to play them again. Stilling continued in his trend and formed a men's team for the summer outdoor play out of the city of Idaho Falls. In the fall of 1998, he held the school's biggest men's tryout ever - 126. He raised the standard of play to an all time maximum.

Normally the men's team was lucky to play 12-14 games in the fall. This time they were treated to a 26 game season. After the fall season ended, the indoor season greeted us again. This time Stilling formed 3 men's teams. His men's "A" team went 26-2, thereby winning 2 of 3 league titles. After indoor was done, they again had a great spring season. The men played their 2nd annual game against BYU. Spring season also saw the men play their first ever alumni game. Currently the program is in the middle of the 2000 fall season.

Stilling is now the ICSA Men's President. He oversees the administration of 15 men's teams, as well as maintaining his duties as advisor and coach of Ricks College men's program.

A special thanks to Robert Bird for his contribution to this page.
