Folk Dance USA


About Folk Dance USA:

The award-winning Folk Dance USA strives to preserve the folk heritage of the American people through dance. Since 1972, this team of talented dancers and musicians has appeared yearly at international folk festivals on five continents. They have won the audience appeal award in Germany, the best traditional dance award in Poland, and the hearts of admirers worldwide.

Prior to 2002, the group toured as the Ricks College American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band.  In August, 2001, Ricks College became Brigham Young University-Idaho and several performing groups changed names also.

With a beautiful array of costumes, this exciting group of 24 dancers are accompanied by a six-member Bluegrass Band and travel with four directors/managers. During the past four years the group has traveled to Poland, Hungary, the Czech Republic, Portugal, Spain, Austria, Germany, Belgium and Denmark. As an emissary of the United States and American folklore, the group has achieved international acclaim at the most prestigious folk dance festivals abroad where they perform with many of the finest dance companies from throughout the world.

"An enormous amount of talent, rehearsal and resources go into a Folk Dance USA production," commented Charles West, artistic director since 1970. "Each performance, however, is more than just great entertainment. It is designed to reflect the traditions of the hard working and energetic people of America, and to preserve fragments of this noble country that may otherwise be forgotten." West received a Masters of Recreation Education at BYU in Provo. He is chairman of the Dance Department at BYU-Idaho, instructor of folk and clog dance, as well as past president of the National Folk Organization.

The Bluegrass Band, under the direction of Eileen Wilcox, a member of the BYU-Idaho adjunct music faculty, has six members. The band consists of two fiddles, banjo, mandolin and two guitars.  It specializes in country and American folk tunes.

Program Description

Festival programs run from 15 to 90 minutes in length and showcase many stylized dances and bluegrass instrumental tunes. A few titles from their repertoire include:

Grand Square Medley (1800)
Virginia Reel (1850)
Grand Square Medley (1850)
Ashokan Farewell (Civil War)
Golden Slippers (1900)
Charleston (1920)
Swing Kids (1940)
Rockin' Robin Swing (1960)
White Water Clog
Alabama Jubilee Clog
Orange Blossom Special (bluegrass)
Jambalaya (bluegrass)  

The versatility of the program allows the group to perform in auditoriums, theaters, church halls, on gymnasium floors, or even outdoors. Surfaces to be avoided are carpet, splintered wood, or rough concrete. The group provides their own sound system or they can use an existing system. With over twelve separate  costumes for each dancer, every show fills the stage with color and energy. Folk Dance USA is acclaimed by thousands as they project enthusiasm and patriotism with their exciting portrayal of the American spirit through dance.

About Scheduling:

Schedules permitting, Folk Dance USA is prepared to visit schools, nursing homes, hospitals, etc., in an effort to support church and community relations. Advance notice of these visits is necessary to coordinate the itinerary.
