Resources - Coming Soon
The Math Testing Center in the Romney building has been closed to provide additional class room space. Tests are now taken in the McKay Testing Center. ROM 276, home of computer science classes, is being upgraded to become a multimedia classroom. An overhead projection system has replaced the LCD panel and overhead projector. While we will miss having only half the screen in focus at any given time, we are confident we will adjust. We are hoping that 25 computers will be installed in ROM 276 by January 2000. Computers in the classroom will enable in-class lab exercises. In addition, computer science tutoring will be offered in ROM 276 rather than ROM 272.
