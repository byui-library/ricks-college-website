COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 280
Title: History of Furniture Textiles, and Architecture Credit: 3
Text: Elements of Interior Design and Decoration, Whiton

Course Objectives:
 1. To examine the cultural legacy of Western civilization in the area of historical decorative arts, architecture and interior furnishings.
 2. To define specific styles of historical architecture, decorative motifs and interior furniture classics through lecture and individual reading and research.
 3. To provide the student with an understanding of major historical events, geography and people which effected the development of reoccurring design styles.
4. To provide an opportunity for team approach to three-dimensional design application.

Description:
A course consisting of lecture, viewing of slides and discussion related to the study of furniture, textiles, and architecture from ancient times to 1900.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Multiple reading assigments with short, written summaries.
2. Original historical “book” consisting of visual and written information describing each period discussed during the course.
3. Completion of a three-dimensional display creatively “teaching” a specific historical period.
4. One quiz and five major exams.
