COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 240    (Culinary Arts Majors)
Title: Meal Management Credit: 2
Text:  Professional Table Service, Van Nostrand Reinhold, New York, 1991.

Course Objective:  The student will learn the practice and implementation of meal management principles as they relate specifically to the front of the house operations.  The goals of the student should be:  to learn how to control costs, perform the various service styles, learn different sales techniques, understand beverage management, and understand the basic principles of dining room design and decor.  In addition, the student will be asked to memorize certain French terms and participate in out-of-class service functions.

Description: The practice and implementation of meal management principles as they relate specifically to front-of-the-house operations.  Emphasis is placed on cost control, service styles, sales techniques, beverage management, and dining room design and decor.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
In addition to class assignments and reading the students will participate in at least two banquets in the function of server.  The student will be evaluated by peers and the instructor.
