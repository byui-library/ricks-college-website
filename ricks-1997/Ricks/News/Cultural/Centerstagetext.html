Center Stage Entertainment Series

For tickets, contact the Ricks College Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230.
Ticket office hours:  9:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. most weekdays
Tuesday 9:30 a.m. to 2 p.m.

 

Richard Morris
Wednesday, Feb. 7, 2001, 7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students Jan. 22 and general public Jan. 24. Cost $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

Atlanta native Richard Morris will return to play the Ruffatti organ in the Barrus Concert Hall. He is one a very small number of organists to ever have appeared as a soloist in Carnegie Hall.

Trio Voronezh
Saturday, Feb. 24, 2001, 7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students Feb. 5 and general public Feb. 7. Cost $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

Discovered playing Bach in a Frankfurt, Germany, subway station, Trio Voronezh made its U.S. debut at the 1996 Oregon Bach Festival. The classically trained Russians perform classical works on traditional Russian folk instruments. A pre-show Russian dinner will be available at 6 p.m. in the Manwaring Center East Ballroom for $10 extra.

Behind the Broken Words
Thursday and Friday, March 1 and 2, 2001, 7:30 p.m, Kirkham Auditorium
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students Feb. 12 and general public Feb. 14. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

Emmy-winning actors Roscoe Lee Browne and Anthony Zerbe will present a performance of 20th century poetry and drama. "Behind the Broken Words sings; Brown and Zerbe are a two-part harmony," writes The New York Times.

Gilbert and Sullivan's "The Pirates of Penzance"
Feb. 22-24, 27-28, March 1-3 2001, 7:30 p.m, Snow Drama Theatre
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students Feb. 5 and general public Feb. 7. Cost $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

This delightful musical, loved by young and old, will be presented jointly by the Ricks College Theater and Music Departments. A fanciful tale of romance and duty with noble pirates and beautiful maidenswill warm even the most winterized heart.

Ricks College Jazz Festival/Kevin Mahogany
Saturday, March 10, 2001, 7:30 p.m., Hart Auditorium
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students Feb. 20 and general public Feb. 21. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $3 (Ricks students).

The third annual Ricks College Jazz Festival will feature internationally acclaimed jazz vocalist Kevin Mahogany with the Sound Alliance jazz band. The recording artist blends blues, soul, gospel and jazz into his music.

The Osmond Brothers
Saturday, March 17, 2001, 3 and 7:30 p.m., Hart Auditorium
Tickets go on sale as part of Women's Week. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

The Women's Week concert will feature the Osmond Brothers, who perform nearly year-round at their theater in Branson, Missouri. The Osmonds are among the most celebrated families in the music industry. Music is their medium, joy is their message, and integrity is their bottom line. (Children under six will be admitted.)

Lousia May Alcott's "Little Women"
April 12-14, 17-21, 7:30 p.m., Snow Drama Theatre
Tickets go on sale to Ricks students March 26 and to the public March 28. Cost $8 and $6 (public), $1 (Ricks students).

Presented by the Ricks College Theatre Department, you will be captured in this heart-warming dramatization of Louisa May Alcott's novel "Little Women."
