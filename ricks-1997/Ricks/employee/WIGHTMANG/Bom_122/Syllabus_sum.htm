Required Materials:

Grading: (480 points possible)
As this is an academic course for 2 credits, you must be graded on your efforts and your mastery of the content. An "A" will not be given simply because someone is "nice" or has a testimony of the gospel. These two things are of most importance for you but they do not meet the academic standard to receive college credit. Therefore, the "A" grade will be reserved for those who excel far above the average level to make this class more meaningful not only for themselves, but for others. The standard is as follows:

Scripture Reading Summaries --- 37% of overall grade
You will be required to submit a typewritten reading summary at the beginning of each scripture block studied. GO READ THE GUIDELINES to and an example of the summary on my web page under "Class Information". There will be 18 summaries (each worth 10 pts) and they will be due at the beginning of class.

Attendance/Participation --- 21% of overall grade
Not only are you expected to attend, you are expected to be a positive influence on the learning environment through your participation in classroom discussions. My definition of participation is remaining alert and attentive by taking notes, and asking a question or making a comment now and then. If you are shy, it is time to become comfortable being an active participant in society. There are a possible 100 points for attendance and participation.

You will be allowed three excused absences this block which means you either call or email to determine if your absence is excused.   I will determine if the absence is excused, not you.  Use these wisely as each absence thereafter will result in the loss of 10 points no matter what the excuse!   Any un-excused absence results in the loss of 10 points.  Those who choose to attend but not participate will also lose 10 points.
I reserve the right in the end to remove points for block long silence. I get to know those who participate. If I don't know you by the end of the block, you will most likely lose points.

Content Mastery --- 21% of overall grade
You are expected to try and master the material you will learn this semester.  I feel this is best accomplished through note taking.
Take good notes while in class, then learn how to transcribe those notes into your scriptures. Come test time, you will be allowed to use your scriptures on the test. There will be a comprehensive "take-home" final worth 100 pts.  TAKE GOOD NOTES!!

Book of Ether Application Project --- 21% of overall grade
Instead of meeting M WTF for 75 minutes, we will meet for 50 minutes on the required days and you will use the remaining 25 minutes each day, in addition to 1 hour of your "two hours out of class for every hour in class" towards a self-directed project.  Thus, you will spend around 8 hours of work per week outside of class WHILE you are performing your normal "in class" work during the 5 weeks.  You can find the guidelines for this project on the web page under "Class Information."  This application project will be worth 100 pts.
