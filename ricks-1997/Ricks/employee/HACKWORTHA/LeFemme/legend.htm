Herein lies the story of Queen Rhiannon....

In the hills above Arbeth is a large stone in the shape of a chair. This is known as "The Throne of Arbeth".  According to local legend, if a person of noble birth or decent sits on the throne, they will either see a wonderous vision or suffer a terrible fate. One day, Pwyll, Prince of Dyfed, sits on the throne. As if by magic, a woman appears, wearing a cloak of gold and riding a gray and white horse. She appears once everyday for the next three days, but not even the fastest horse in all the land could catch up to her. On the third day, Pwyll gives chase on his own horse. Faster and faster they ride until Pwyll can go no faster. At last he calls out to this beauty on horseback. Hearing his voice, she stops, and reveals herself to be Rhiannon, Queen of the Otherworld, come to declare her love for him.
