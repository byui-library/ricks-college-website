    Region 18 Tournament
    March 8, 2001
    Southern Idaho 71 Ricks College 67
    The second seeded Lady Vikings came into the tournament with the leagues top offense, but found it tough to get the shots to go against CSI. The Lady Eagles jumped out to an 11-4 lead in the first six minutes. Ricks fought their way back to take a 30-29 lead with 1:20 left in the half, but CSI outscored Ricks 5-0 in the final 1:20 and lead 34-30 at the half. Ricks went cold in the second half, shooting only 34% from the floor to CSI's 55%, yet the Lady Vikes came back to take a 54-52 lead with 8:40 left. CSI came right back with five points to take the lead. Ricks recovered and tied the game at 60-60 on a Candis Wilcox three point shot with 4:25 to go. Another Wilcox three pointer gave Ricks a 63-62 lead. Mandie Little's field goal put Ricks on top 65-62 at the 3 minute mark. CSI came back to tie, but Wilcox put Ricks back on top 67-65 with 1:35 left. In that final minute and a half, CSI scored six points, the game winner being a three pointer from Natalia Kortisova, while the Vikings committed two turnovers and missed two free throws that could have sent the game into overtime. Wilcox led all scorers with 21. Mandie Little had 17 points and 16 rebounds. Ricks was 14 of 32 from the free throw line.

    March 3, 2001
    Ricks College 94 Eastern Utah 67
    The regular season ended with a dominating second half performance by the Lady Vikings. Leading by as many as fourteen in the first half, Ricks went cold and watched CEU battle back to take a 39-38 lead at halftime. Ricks scored the first six points of the second half and went on to outscore CEU 56-28 over the final twenty minutes to post their 26th win of the season. CEU shot just 22% from the floor in the second half. Ricks out rebounded Eastern Utah 51-39. Sarah Fister and Lindsey Johnson scored 14 each, with Merilee Udy, Mandie Little, and Annie Anderson also in double figures.

    March 1, 2001
    Ricks College 96 Colorado NW 77
    The Lady Vikings jumped out to a 7-2 lead, held off an early charge by CNCC, then began to pull away. Ricks led 49-36 at the half. Candis Wilcox of Rexburg hit 5 of 5 from the floor, including 2 of 2 from the three point line to total 12 first half points. After being held to four points in the first half, Mandie Little took over in the final 20 minutes, totaling 18 points and 7 rebounds for the night. Ricks shot 58% from the floor in the second half. Angie Colf led CNCC with 18 as Ricks improved to 15-2 in league play, 25-3 overall.

Twice the Lady Vikings held thirteen point margins in the second half, but the Lady Chukars came back, cutting the lead to six with 7:10 to go in the game, and again with 2:10 left it was a six point Viking lead.     February 10, 2001
