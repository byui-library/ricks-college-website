ACTIVITIES

 

 

YFE participants will devote the majority of their time to exciting presentations from a seasoned director, researching their chosen gospel topic in small group and individual study.  On Friday each participant will make a presentation of their research findings to the group.

 

On Wednesday we will do baptisms for the dead at the Idaho Falls, Seattle or Nauvoo Temples, depending on your session.  We will also perform various acts of service for the community in which you will be staying.

 

Participants will also have the opportunity to present a talent and skit with other participants during our Friday activity night.
