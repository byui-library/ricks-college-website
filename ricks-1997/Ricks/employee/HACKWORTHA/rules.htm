Class Rules
and Grading


1. Create an up-to-date portfolio using a word processor. The appearance should be as professional as possible. Keep your portfolio at the end of your three-ring binder that also contains your textbook.

2. Never miss class and never be late.

3. Do your best work when completing all the assignments.

4. Completely read the assigned novel before the end of this term.

5. Meet all of your appointments with Brother Hackworth and be strictly on time.

6. Always bring your portfolio with you for your appointments with Brother Hackworth.

7. Keep strict records of your attendance and study time.    Spend six hours per week studying out of class.   If you do extra mile work, write it down.

8. Make paper revisions on all suggestions by Brother Hackworth. Make the revisions before the next class. Suggestions will be given when you meet during your appointments.

9. When papers are due, always bring two, typed copies.  Brother Hackworth will use the second copy to edit as you read your paper to the class.

10. In class, always be a good listener.

Rules

Take off 10 points each time you break one of the rules.  For example, each time you miss an apointment, take off 10 points.   100 points are possible in this area.  If you talk to your friends while the teacher is teaching, take off 10 points, etc., etc.  If you don't keep an up-to-date portfolio, take off 10 points.

Grading:

Help Brother Hackworth calculate your points for effort.   You will answer questions which will determine this portion of your grade.  These questions are listed below.

Grades will breakdown as follows:  Tests - 55%,  Rules - 10%,  Attendance - 10%, Effort - 20%,  Extra Mile - 5%.

94%  A,    90%  A-,    88%  B+,     84%  B,    80%  B-,    78%   C+,
74%  C,    70%  C-,    68%  D+,     64%  D,    60%  D-,    59%   F

To earn an A you must:

1.  Follow all the rules and consistently show an extra mile effort throughout the term.  An extra-mile effort means putting in extra study time (beyond six hours per week).

These activities count for extra mile efforts: extra time with research, writing, and revision, extra time with grammar studies, reading carefully Elements of Style, learning all the vocabulary words (see MLG), learning all the affixes (see MLG), and getting 100 percent on every MLG drill.

2.  And get a 94% or better average on the five tests.

Effort Questions

1 and 2.  How many hours did you spent with your English studies?
For an A, you need 92 hours plus extra mile points.
92 = 40 points,  82 = 30 points,  72 = 20 points,  62 = 10 points.

3.  Did you properly complete all of your writing assignments on time?
Take off five points per assignment if you are late.  Take off five points per assignment if you do follow all of the requirements.

4.  Did you always bring two copies of your papers when they were due?

5.  Did you always complete the grammar assignments on time?

Each question is worth 20 points.  Each week you must say "no,"   reduce the points possible by 5%.
