President's Welcome

  a       
             
             
     

On behalf of the faculty, staff, and administration of this great institution, I welcome you to BYU-Idaho.  Your decision to join us is an expression of trust which we value and take most seriously.  Our primary desire is to assist you in making your experience at BYU-Idaho both meaningful and memorable.  We are confident you will contribute to and benefit from the “Spirit of Ricks.”

Brigham Young University-Idaho exists to provide an education consistent with the religious and family values taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  The Church leaders who comprise the Board of Trustees of BYU-I have provided a beautiful campus where you can develop spiritually, intellectually, socially, and physically.  A wealth of resources exists at BYU-Idaho, particularly a competent and caring faculty, to help you achieve your personal and career goals.  I encourage you to take full advantage of and magnify the many opportunities for learning provided at this unique institution.

The Honor Code and related standards are essential elements in the learning environment at BYU-I.  The decision you made to comply with and adhere to these guidelines will be a great blessing in your life and for the entire campus community.

Attending devotionals, studying for exams, meeting new people and developing lifelong friendships, serving in church assignments, participating in campus organizations, discussing an issue or a concern with a faculty member who truly cares---all of these activities will be important parts of your BYU-Idaho experience.

We are delighted you chose to attend BYU-Idaho and look forward to working and serving with you this year.  We wish you the very best as you join us at this special school.

 Sincerely,


David A. Bednar
President
