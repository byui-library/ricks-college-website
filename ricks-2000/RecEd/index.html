Purposes & Outcomes
The purpose of the Recreation Education Department is to teach, through experience, the life enhancing skill of effective use of leisure time. The Department utilizes the unique natural resources of this region in teaching outdoor recreation activity skills. These lifelong recreational pursuits will provide opportunities for students to experience personal growth, enhance self esteem, and to develop leadership skills in a positive environment. The development of a strong code of ethics toward the protection of our natural resources and toward responsible Risk management are central to all that we teach.
