HOW TO REGISTER FOR KNOW YOUR RELIGION

PREREGISTRATION will be handled through participating wards and stakes, or individually by completing and mailing a registration form. If registering by mail, please allow enough time for your ticket(s) to be returned by mail.

AT-THE-DOOR registrations will be accepted before each lecture.  Please arrive a few minutes early.  Season passes will also be available at the door.  A season pass will be a savings if you plan to attend three or more lectures.

TUITION:

Individual Full Series...........................................$16.00

Full-time Student Full Series...............................$  9.00

At-the-door Single Lecture..................................$  5.00

A SEASON PASS IS VALID AT ANY "KNOW YOUR RELIGION" PROGRAM in the US and Canada.

The Church Educational System Continuing Education programs do not discriminate on the basis of race, sex, color and ethnic or national origin, but all registrants must meet and maintain the ideals and standards of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints while in attendance at the program.
