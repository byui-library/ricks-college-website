Welcome to the Department of Engineering

Engineering is an exciting major which offers professional job satisfaction, good starting compensation, and excellent placement potential. The Department of Engineering has designed a program for students to complete the first two years of their engineering education at Ricks College and then transfer to a four-year university to complete their B.S. degree. A wide variety of fields are open to engineers including:

  • Aerospace
  • Agricultural
  • Biomedical
  • Chemical
  • Civil
  • Computer
  • Electrical/Electronics

  • Environmental
  • Industrial
  • Materials
  • Mechanical
  • Manufacturing
  • Nuclear
  • General Engineering

Students in engineering programs receive a strong foundation in science, mathematics, computers, design, materials, and engineering science courses. Through practical experience in engineering analysis and design projects, students are prepared for continued study at a four-year university.
