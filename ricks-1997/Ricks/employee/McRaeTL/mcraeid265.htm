COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 265
Title: Portfolio Credit: 2
 Text: No text required

Course Objective:
To review student portfolios and have the student contract with the instructor all necessary changes.  To require the student to produce a professional portfolio of at least six major and six minor projects.  These are to include specification books, color and finish boards, and blueprints.  Photographs of 3-D work, Design Basics projects, Color projects, Surface Ornamentation, Furniture Design, and drafting portfolios can be included in the six minor projects required.  To update and finalize a resume for employment purposes.  To require the student to evaluate their previous work.  To evaluate the student’s competency by assessing the completed portfolio work in respect to employment seeking qualitites.
 
Description:
This course is designed to aid the third year student in preparation of the portfolio.  Emphasis is placed on quality portfolio pieces and finalized resume in preparation for the job search.
 
Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
 The student will review their portfolio pieces and select at least six major projects to rework for their portfolio.  The student will review and rework at least six minor projects for the portfolio, i.e. drafting portfolio, landscape architecture, color, design basics, furniture design, 3-D, etc.  The student will produce a professional resume and have it printed.  The student will write a contract stating the work that they intend to complete during the semester.
