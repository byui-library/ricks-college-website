Journal Assignment:

  1. Record a journal entry in your "New Student Journal." At the top of the page write Journal
    Entry # 2 along with today's date or the date in which you write the entry.

  2. Respond to the following questions and record your answers in your new student journal. 

              (1)  Write down your top five fears in coming to BYU-Idaho.

    (2)  Why are these five fears concerning to you? 

    (3)  What can be done to eliminate or lessen these concerns?

  3. Reflect on what you hope to accomplish during your time here at BYU-I.  Take some time to reflect on each of the six areas listed below and what it is that you would like to change or accomplish in each of these areas in your life.   Remember that in setting goals, they should be things you truly hope to accomplish, be realistic and achievable.  Another helpful hint in setting goals is to develop immediate, short-term, and long-term goals.

    Six Areas of Personal Development:
    1.  Spiritual
    2.  Intellectual
    3.  Occupational
    4.  Physical
    5.  Emotional
    6.  Social
  4. Record your immediate, short-term and long-term goals for each of the six areas of personal development listed above in your New Student Journal.
  5. Write down what specific steps or actions you will need to take in order to achieve these goals.  Also write down what steps or areas you may require assistance from others to help you achieve these goals?
