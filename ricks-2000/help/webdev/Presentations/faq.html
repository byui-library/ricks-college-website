Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I determine what version of Power Point I have?
  • How do I determine if I already have "Web Authoring" components?
To determine what version of Power Point you have:
  1. Start the program
  2. Click "Help" on the menu bar
  3. Click "About Microsoft Power Point"
          (Hint: You may have to click the double arrows at the bottom of the menu to find this)
  4. The version will be at the top of the pop-up window:

To determine if you already have "Web Authoring" components:

  1. Open a Presentation
  2. Select FILE menu
  3. If the "Save as Web Page..." is selectable, you have the needed components.
    If the "Save as Web Page..." is not selectable, you need to have the componenets installed.
