Oriental Floral Design
ORIENTAL FL DS
CLASS CODE: HORT 336 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Principles of oriental design and their influence on modern American floral design.
DESCRIPTION: This course helps students understand the theories of Asian design influence, with particular emphasis on the use of line
TOPICS: Historical review; Ikebana schools, line in art and architecture; observaton of nature.
OBJECTIVES: To help develop a broad base of understanding through analogy of cultral influence.
REQUIREMENTS: Students must have floral tools and maybe asked to provide some of the required design materials. Web access.
PREREQUISITES: Hort 335 and 435, FDM major or consent of instructor
