Electronic Mail using Eudora for Macintosh
(version 3.0)

These directions are specific to students accessing Eudora through the Elementary Education Computer lab at Ricks College. However, general use guidelines should be universally applicable.

General Information

The student mail server at Ricks College is named Gordon. The domain name is ricks.edu. Ricks, of course, is the name of the school and .edu specifies that it is an educational institution. Thus, to send mail to a student at Ricks, who has an account, the proper address would be [username]@www.byui.edu. There are no spaces and no capitals.

The mail servers for administrators, staff, and faculty are centrally fed by a mail router. The name of the specific server being used by the employee is not needed. To send electronic mail to an employee at Ricks requires only the username and the domain name. Thus, a message sent to the administrator of this page is robertsonb@ricks.edu.

Please note that Eudora requires that all addresses are in small case. If you use capitals, it may not work!

Creating an E-mail Account

An E-mail account created anywhere on campus is good for use anywhere on the campus regardless of what machine you are using. To create an E-mail account from the Elementary Education Computer lab:

1) Launch Netscape 4.0 (this will take you to the Ricks College Intranet).
2) You will find yourself on the Socrates homepage, locate the "Utilities" link from the Navigation menu.
3) Click once on the first link on the Utilities page labeled "Set up your E-mail account."
4) Read and follow the directions you will find as you progress throught he setup screens.
5) Pay particular attention to and write down your account name (it will look something like "rob76001" which is the first three letters of your last name, the year you first enrolled at Ricks, and the order of the student you are who enrolled that year with the same or similar last name).

Creating an E-mail Disk


You must have your own 1.4 meg, HD disk, formatted for Macintosh. All mail and the Eudora settings will be stored on your disk. You will have to configure your E-mail settings once, and thereafter, you can use any Mac which is on the network, and has the Eudora application, to access your E-mail account.

Copying Instructions
1) Make sure the TEd icon appears on the desktop.
2) Place your disk in the computer.
3) Open the TEd icon, then locate and open the Eudora folder inside the Internet Resources folder.
4) Drag the settings folder to your disk. When it is done copying close the TEd windows.

Create an Alias of Eudora Settings file

1) Open the Eudora Settings folder on your disk.
2) Single-click the Eudora Settings file.
3) From the File menu, select "Make alias...".
4) Drag the alias from the Eudora Settings folder window to your disk window.
5) Click the name "Eudora Settings alias" and rename it something that tells you to click this icon to launch your email.
6) Once you have renamed the icon, press the "Enter" key or click elsewhere in the window.

Configuring Eudora Settings on your Personal Disk

1) Double click the renamed E-mail icon.
2) From the Special menu, choose Settings, if it does not take you there automatically.
3) Click the "Getting Started" icon.
4) Enter your username in the POP account line (myname@www.byui.edu)
NOTE: Everything must be in small letters, DO NOT USE CAPITALS.
5) In the Real Name area put your real name (real complicated I know).
6) Click the "Personal Information" icon.
7) The POP account and Real name should have been copied from the Getting Started window, type the POP account information into the Return address area.
8) Click the Hosts icon, make sure the SMTP, Ph, and Finger servers all say "www.byui.edu".
9) When you are done, click "OK."

Resetting Your Account Password

When your account was created a password was assigned to it. For security reasons you will want to change it. Make it something you can and will remember, but that others cannot guess. To change your password:
1) From the Special Menu, select "Change Password"
2) Enter the old password.
3) Enter the new password.
4) Reenter the new password.
5) The password will be changed and a dialog box will tell you that it is done.

Sending Mail
1) From the Message menu, select "New Message."
2) Type the address of the person you want to send a message to (e.g... rob76001@www.byui.edu)
3) If you don't know the address of the person, you may want to contact them some other way to get the correct address.
Getting your mail
Mail is stored on Gordon until you get it. It is then transferred to your disk.
1) From the File menu, select "Check mail."
2) The system will ask for your password, when entered correctly it will then show a list of your mail. Double click the mail you want to read.
Other Information
1) Check your mail often, mail left on Gordon more than 7 days is deleted.
2) You mailbox in Gordon is only 1 mg in size. Anything larger than that may "plug" the mail box and not allow you to use it. After 7 days it is automatically deleted.
3) To send e-mail to a faculty member type their last name, first initial then @ricks.edu.
