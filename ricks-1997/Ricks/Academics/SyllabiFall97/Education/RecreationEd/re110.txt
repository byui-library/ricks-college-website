I. 	Course description:  Introduction to lake and river canoeing.
	equipment, rescue, river reading, and paddling skills will be 
	covered.
II.  	Statement of Purpose:  Provide instruction and practical 
	experience in maneuvering a canoe safely in a variety
	of situations.
III. 	Prerequisite:  Swim Test - be able to swim, tread water, or 
	float for 3 minutes in deep water.
IV. 	Objectives:
	A. Provide instruction and skill practice in safe canoe 
	handling for a variety of conditions.
        B. Develop a foundation of a life long recreational activity.
        C. Develop an appreciation and respect for the lake and river 
	environment.
        D. Knowledge of care and maintenance of Aquatic Equipment
V.   	Fees and Equipment:
        A. Lab fee $30.00 to cover transportation, food and group gear.
        B. Textbook: class handouts only
        C. Clothing:  Come to class dressed in comfortable outdoor 
	clothing - long pants, modest shirt, tennis shoes.  Rain or 
	wind gear may be appropriate depending on the weather.  Visors, 
	sunglasses and sun screen are highly recommended.
VI.  	Grading Criteria:
        A. Class attendance.  We meet only 6 times.  Important skills 
	will be covered each class period. Attendance is critical to 
	your success in this course. (30%)
        B. Written final - Equip. 1st Aid,  transportation, reading a 
	river. (10%)
        C. Practical Final - Tandem demonstration of the following 
	canoeing techniques. (60%)

        Carrying a canoe              Pry            Launching
	Boarding                      Draw           Forward Stroke
        Back Stroke                   Cross Draw     J Stroke
        Forward Sweep                 Throw Rope     
        Reverse Sweep                 Push Away
