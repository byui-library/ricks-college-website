BACKGROUND:
In this class students will study leadership principles that they may begin to develop the qualities and skills that Jesus Christ demonstrated in his perfect leadership. As President Spencer W. Kimball explained: “We will find it very difficult to be significant leaders unless we recognize the reality of the perfect leader, Jesus Christ, and let Him be the light by which we see the way!” (“Jesus: The Perfect Leader,” Ensign, August 1979, p. 7).

RICKS COLLEGE GUIDING PRINCIPLES:

“These guiding principles should be at the heart of all we are and do at Ricks College. Our planning and decisions and programs and activities should be focused upon and give emphasis to these basic principles” (President David A. Bednar).

1. True teaching is done by and with the Spirit of the Holy Ghost.
2. Every person at Ricks College is a teacher.
3. The most effective teaching and learning experiences occur “one by one.”
4. More students must be blessed.
5. Righteous sociality (D&C 130:2) is essential to individual development.
6. Service provides opportunities for growth.

COURSE VALUE:

President Kimball said, “Membership in the Church is a call to leadership” and that leadership is, “the ability to encourage the best efforts of others in working toward a desirable goal” (The Teachings of Spencer W. Kimball, p. 322). Elder Boyd K. Packer emphasized that all Church leaders serve as teachers: "The prophet is a teacher; his counselors are teachers; the General Authorities are teachers. Stake presidents and mission presidents are teachers; high councilors and quorum presidents are teachers; bishops are teachers; and so through all of the organizations of the Church. The Church moves forward sustained by the power of the teaching that is accomplished" (Teaching, No Greater Call, p 150). Student Leadership Development at Ricks College is “Leadership with Life” and “Life with Discipleship.” Students receive training and experiences to help chart a leadership course of true Discipleship in the Church, Family, Community, and Workplace.

Students teaching students and students helping students to know, understand and apply the doctrines and principles of the gospel, this is “Divine Centered Leadership.” This leadership emphasis is one of many initiatives at Ricks College to help overcome the “Moral Decline” of the world.


INTRODUCTION:

Members of the Church can learn to be leaders. "Contrary to the old saying that leaders are born not made, the art of leading can be taught and it can be mastered" (President Gordon B. Hinckley quoting General Mark W. Clark, cited in TOGBH, p. 306). The scriptures serve as the text for this course. President Spencer W. Kimball taught that, "The scriptures are the handbook of instructions for the would-be Leader" (Jesus: "The Perfect Leader," Ensign, August 1979, p. 7). President N. Eldon Tanner said: " . . . Christ is a model of correct leadership; and, as the scriptures record his life and teachings, they become case studies of divine leadership" (The Message, New Era, June 1997, p. 4. cited in Eric Stephan, R. Wayne Pace, The Perfect Leader, p. 3). (NOTE: Authorized by CES to pilot this syllabus)

COURSE REQUIREMENTS:

  1. Attendance is expected on Monday and Wednesday with no class on Friday (this hour spent teaching others during the week).

  2. Weekly reports on your progress, teaching, and leadership to be posted on the Message Board (on this home page)

  3. Grade will be determined my attendance, contribution to class discussions, lesson preparation and presentation, and weekly "student teaching student" presentations and your weekly reports posted on internet.

Because "Effective teaching is the very essence of leadership in the Church (President Gordon B. Hinckley), we will each have the chance to develop our teaching/leadership skills by preparing a leadership lesson and presenting it in class. These lessons will be prepared in such a way that each student will "go and teach" others. Each lesson will be prepared using the following guidelines:

  1. Teaches the Doctrine/Principles of the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

  2. Prepare a Flip Chart consisting of no more than 15 pages, and

  3. Prepare a sound track CD with at least two quotes using the voice of our living prophets and apostles (from recent General Conference talks).

  4. Lesson should be fifteen to twenty (15-20) minutes in length.
