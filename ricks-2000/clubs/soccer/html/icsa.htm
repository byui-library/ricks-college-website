The ICSA is made up of 16 Men's teams in 2 Divisions.  A Northern Division and a Southern Division that are administrated by Peter Stilling of BYU-Idaho, Rexburg, Idaho.   Special thanks to Carl Johnson - UVSC, Henry Ibarguen - Weber State, and James Hampton - SLCC for all the pioneer work they did to bring this league to a reality.  Also, special thanks to the administration of BYU-Idaho to host this page for the benefit of the ICSA.

Special thanks to Udine Falabella, THE REAL WEBMASTER,  for doing such a great job on the new web page.
