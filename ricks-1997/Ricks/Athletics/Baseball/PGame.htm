    April 13-14    Ricks hosts CEU
    April 13, 2001
    Ricks College 6 Eastern Utah 0
    Viking pitcher Aaron Ott followed up his no-hitter last week, with a 5 hit shutout Friday, beating Eastern Utah 6-0. The Vikings took advantage of two CEU errors in the first inning to score four unearned runs, and taking a 4-0 lead. In the second inning, with two out, Jared Chappell tripled then scored on a double by Joseph Barber. Ricks added their final run in the fifth. Ott struck out nine and got out of a bases loaded jam in the third inning to get the win.

    Ricks College 12 Eastern Utah 4
    Eastern Utah staked starting pitcher Joldy Watts to a 1-0 lead in the top of the fourth. Watts former American Legion team mate James Schlegelmilch then turned the game around with a grand slam home run in the bottom half of the inning. Watts was hurt by a CEU error on a double play ball, in the inning, that led to seven Viking runs. Ricks added five more runs in the fifth inning off three different CEU pitchers. Josh Kennedy had two hits, two runs, and four rbi’s. Rexburg’s Tyson Perkes got the win, going five innings.
