This is the process to create a new page in FrontPage 2000.
There are three ways of creating a new page:

Click the button method
  1. Click on the white new page icon at the top left of the screen.
File -> New Method
  1. To create a new page click File, click New, click Page.

  2. It's recommended that you choose Normal Page.  Click OK.
HotKey Method
  1. Type <CTRL> +  N.
