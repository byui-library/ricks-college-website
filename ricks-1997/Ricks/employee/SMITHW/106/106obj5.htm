Unit #5 - IONIC EQUILIBRIA II: HYDROLYSIS AND TITRATIONS

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Write net ionic equations for the following reactions in aqueous solutions: a) the hydrolysis of the conjugate acid of a weak base, and b) the hydrolysis of the conjugate base of a weak acid.
2. Calculate the equilibrium constant for the hydrolysis of an ion from Kw and the appropriate Ka or Kb.
3. Find the equilibrium concentrations of all species in a hydrolysis reaction, given appropriate initial conditions and equilibrium constants.
4. Carry out calculations on the stoichiometry of acid-base reactions in titrations.
