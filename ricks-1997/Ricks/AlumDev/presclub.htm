President's Club is an opportunity for you to tie yourself more closely with the college, its programs, and with the President. By giving $1,000 or more annually you automatically become a member.

Membership Options Include:

  • Sustaining Member—Make an initial gift of $1,000 or more to any Ricks College fund within a 12-month period (which can be paid in monthly installments). Continued membership requires a gift of at least $1,000 annually.
  • Heritage Member—Make a gift of $25,000 or more (cash or gift-in-kind). Or, pledge to give $25,000 (paid in five years). Or, give a deferred gift of $100,000 (present value). And continue to give at least $500 a year thereafter.
  • Legacy Member—Give a one-time gift of $10,000 or more in your will to Ricks College and, if possible, make an annual donation.
  • President’s Associate—Make a minimum donation of $3,000 per year as a business, foundation, professional corporation, or other organization.

As part of your membership, you’ll:

  • Enjoy association with others who feel passionately about assisting Ricks College,
  • Have opportunities to interact directly with the president,
  • Learn firsthand about the priorities and progress of Ricks College.
