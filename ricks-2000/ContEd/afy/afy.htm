AFY is a high adventure program for youth designed to promote personal and spiritual growth while developing lifelong friendships. AFY is held on the BYU-Idaho campus in Rexburg, Idaho. Many exciting activities are offered in AFY including: a white water raft trip, dances, an overnight camp at the BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center, initiative training, canoeing, climbing, rappelling, and many other features designed for the adventurous youth. The spiritual aspect is enhanced by excellent devotional and workshop speakers that have been carefully selected to share their enthusiasm, humor and testimonies. Nightly counselor devotionals and a testimony meeting will also be part of the agenda.
