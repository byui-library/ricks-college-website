Research Paper Proposal

 

The purpose of this project is to get you to determine early in the term what you are planning to research. It's to give yourself direction now. A research proposal is a formal paper which simply proposes a question you would like to answer, a project you would like to do, or an area you'd like to do more research in. What makes this proposal more than saying you'd like to research in the fields of engineering or computers, it forces you to determine what type of research you'll be coming up against. From this you can determine whether or not your subject is suitable for research or whether or not there are materials available for research. Believe me, this is a good, time-saving idea.

 

Here's how you organize a research proposal.

I. Introduction

A. What is this proposal report?
B. What is the purpose of the proposal?
C. What is the scope of the proposal?
D. What is the plan of development of the proposal?

II. Define the problem

A. State briefly the general subject being investigated.
B. Indicate your basis for restricting and focusing that general subject.
C. State clearly the restricted question or problem you plan to research--the best single sentence question you can state that identifies what it is you really want to find out.
D. Indicate the purpose for your paper--what it should do for you, to whom it is written (your intended audience), and what use or uses you feel the paper will have for your audience.

III. Analyze the research problem and procedure


A. Establish a clear relationship between the whole question or problem and its logical parts.


1. Suggest a tentative division (outline) of the whole into its parts by listing the main question or problem and the subordinate questions you will try to answer to solve the problem or answer the main question.
2. Consider alternatives within your problem and formulate standards of judgment you will use in determining the best alternative.


B. Establish clearly your commitment to following the procedure below to solve your research problem and lead you to a quality finished product (schedule).


1. The preliminary work
2. Reading and note taking
3. Evaluating and interpreting the data
4. Outlining the defense of the thesis and the plan of the paper
5. Writing the drafts
6. Documenting the paper
7. Composing the final paper
8. Presenting the supplements to the paper
9. Presenting the paper appropriately on time.

IV. Submit a preliminary bibliography of at least ten sources representing the honest search you have conducted using the library catalogs and indexes most appropriate for your subject and problem.

Summary


1. Have a strong, developed introduction.

2. Have at least one paragraph telling me in detail what topic you want to research and why.

3. Have a least one paragraph telling me of your background experience and your preliminary research.

4. Have at least one paragraph telling me about your limitations or problems you expect.

5. Have at least one paragraph telling me the direction you want your paper to take.

6. Include a tentative outline.

7. Include a tentative schedule.

8. Include a bibliography of at least ten sources (follow Pauley/Riordan Appendix B, 471-474).

We are submitting this proposal to receive your approval for our research paper. We plan to research the relationship between carbohydrates and the emotional state of the brain. The purpose of this proposal is to define the topic, discuss our limitations, evaluate our background experience, and provide a tentative outline, schedule, and bibliography.

The demand for this type of research is becoming greater and greater as the general public gains more knowledge about food and how it affects the body. As one gains knowledge in this area, one comes to realize the far reaching impact of food choices on health, both physical and mental. There has been a great deal of research done on the effects of diet and the physical well-being of the body. For the most part, the general public is well informed in this area, but just as important and less publicized is the effect of food on our emotional well-being. Therefore, the purpose of our research is to determine what relationship exists between carbohydrates and the emotional state of the brain.

We have limited our topic to the essential nutrient: carbohydrates. However, one of our greatest disadvantages will be the limited amount of information available to us. Furthermore, we only have approximately 2 months in which to gather information, formulate ideas, and put them into a 15 page paper. A research project like this requires a lot of time and effort, and we may not have all the time necessary to do a complete research of the available material, due to our other courses. Moreover, due to our contrasting schedules, we also have a limitation as far as when we can work on the paper together.

One of our goals in this project is to gain research experience in our major fields of study: dietetics and medicine. As second year students in these fields, we have limited experience and basic knowledge. Yet, we feel confident that we have the understanding necessary to successfully complete this assignment. The preliminary research that we have conducted in the library and in interviews has resulted in new ideas and information. We feel that this initial research has increased our interest and excitement for this research assignment.

We would like to direct this paper to all interested individuals. We do not want to limit the audience of this paper to a particular group. Our approach will be to share the results of our research in a nontechnical manner due to our audience=s limited knowledge on this topic.
