Elder Jeffrey R. Holland: God’s love equal for each of his children

by Kelly Smurthwaite
SMU01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

ELDER HOLLAND
Elder Jeffrey R. Holland spoke about how Heavenly Father loves his children equally.

Elder Holland began his remarks by telling the story of the prodigal son. He told of how the elder brother was jealous because of the attention and praise his brother was receiving. The elder brother was angry with his parents because they were happy that his once wayward brother, had come home.

Elder Holland used this parable to illustrate that though the younger brother had sinned, the older brother had sinned just as much because of the resentment and jealousy he had felt toward his brother.

“Who is it that whispers so subtly in our ear that a gift given to another somehow diminishes the blessings we have received? Who makes us feel that if God is smiling upon another, then he surely must somehow be frowning upon us? You and I both know who does this — it is the father of all lies. It is Lucifer ...,” he said.

Elder Holland also went on to talk about the pressure the world places on people to be in competition with each other.

“Someone or something is forever telling us we need to be more handsome or more wealthy, more applauded or more admired than we see ourselves as being ...,” he said.

He went on to explain that this way of thinking is not God’s way of thinking. God does not compare his children nor their intelligence, attractiveness or success, he said.

People can overcome these misconceptions by counting their blessings and by serving others, Elder Holland said.

He concluded his remarks when he said he knows God loves all of his children.

“I testify that he loves each of us. ... He cheers on every runner, calling out that the race is against sin not against each other,” he said.
