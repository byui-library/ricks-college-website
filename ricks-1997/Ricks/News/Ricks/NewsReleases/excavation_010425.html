Ricks College News Release
April 25, 2001 
Excavation work under way
for new Spori building

REXBURG, Idaho — Excavation on the foundation of the proposed new Jacob Spori Building at Ricks College has been undertaken even before all aspects of the building’s final design have been completed.

The early excavation is part of an effort to speed up the time table for construction of the proposed classroom building that will sit on the site of the recently demolished Spori Building, says Physical Plant Director Chuck Frost. He estimates that early ground work will speed up construction by about two months.

Edstrom Construction Inc. of Rexburg has been awarded the contract to excavate the site that already had a partial basement from the prior building. The excavation site is about 110 by 140 feet and will be 15 feet at the deepest point to allow for construction of a full basement.

Because lava rock lays just 2 to 3 feet below the surface, some drilling and blasting is necessary. It’s estimated that about 5,000 cubic years of rock and soil will need to be hauled away.

Frost says the college hopes to finalize the design of the new building within the next month with a bid opening in early June. Construction will start as soon the project receives final approval from the college’s Board of Trustees and completion is projected in March 2003.

Ricks officials selected the architectural firm of FFKR of Salt Lake City to design the new building because they believe it has the vision and capability to design a building that will reflect the heritage and legacy of the former historic structure.
