PROFESSIONAL INTERESTS

MY PROFESSIONAL INTERESTS REVOLVE AROUND UNDERSTANDING THE ROLE OF CULTURAL INFLUENCE AND THE CULTURAL PARADIGMS THAT DOMINATE OUR CONSCIENCE MIND.   I AM INTERESTED IN CHILDHOOD TRAUMA AND HOW THAT PLAYS ITSELF OUT IN OUR ADULT LIVES.  I AM INTERESTED IN THE GOSPEL OF CHRIST AND HOW IT RELATES TO THE HEALING OF OUR HEARTS AND MINDS.  I AM INTERESTED IN ADDICTIONS AND HOW THEY SPIRITUALLY BANKRUPT US AND SEPARATE US FROM OURSELVES, OTHERS AND OUR GOD.   I AM INTERESTED IN OUR CONFUSION OF RIGID RELIGIOUS BEHAVIOR WITH A SPIRITUAL LIFE.  I AM INTERESTED IN OUR DENIAL SYSTEMS OF OUR FALLEN NATURES AND THE SOCIETY OF BABYLON THAT WE SEEM TO HAVE EMBRACED AND ABSORBED INTO OUR BEINGS.  I AM INTERESTED IN OUR GOD GIVEN ENERGIES AND BODIES AND HOW OUR CULTURE HAS TURNED US AGAINST THEM.  I AM INTERESTED IN THE SPIRITUAL ADVENTURE OF OUR LIVES AND THE COURAGE TO GO ON THE JOURNEY OF SELFHOOD AND THE FEAR THAT WE CONFRONT ONCE WE HAVE BEGUN.  I AM INTERESTED IN THE ENERGY FIELDS THAT ARE CREATED WHEN WE THINK THOUGHTS AND ENGAGE OTHERS IN RELATIONSHIPS.  I AM INTERESTED IN THE NEW SCIENCES OF CHAOS THEORY AND QUANTUM THEORY AND THE RADICAL INVITATIONS THAT THEY BRING.  I AM INTERESTED IN THE HARMONY AND WISDOM OF NATURE AS IT IS MANIFESTED IN OUR LIVES.
