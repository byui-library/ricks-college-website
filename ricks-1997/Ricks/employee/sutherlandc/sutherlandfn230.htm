COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 230
Title: Advanced Bakery Credit: 2
Text: The Professional Chef, Friberg
          Professional Baking,   Grisslen

Course Objective:
The student will learn to prepare a sampling of the classical desserts, the role of ingredients, and stylized presentation techniques.  Cake decorating will be emphasized.  Management of “R” Bakery will also be conducted as necessary to insure that it runs smoothly.

Description:
A study of the preparation and construction of classical desserts.  Advanced cake decorating is taught.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Participation in a cake show which is open to the public.

Other:
Students must pass identified competencies.
