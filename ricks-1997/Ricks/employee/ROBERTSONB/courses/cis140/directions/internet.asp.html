IS 140 Internet Assignment

If you have not read the general directions for all assignments, please be sure to do so prior to completing this assignment.

Many people (you, for instance) use the World Wide Web and E-mail regularly. However, using it and being aware of issues of legality and proper use are sometimes two different things. This assignment is intended to assist you become better informed on issues of importance: Security, Netiquette, Copyright, and Privacy. As you read these online articles you will be asked to answer a series of questions pertaining to each. Record your answers.

E-mail Security

Almost all university students use email. However, many students get spamed at one time or another or, knowingly or unknowingly, spam someone else. It is important that you understand some security issues that accompany e-mail use. Do the following and answer the accompanying questions, you will submit them at the conclusion of this assignment:

Go to http://www.hotmail.com. This will take you to the Hotmail homepage.

Locate the Policy and Member Conduct link, click it and read the information found there. Based on the information found there answer this question:

  1. What is spam when using E-mail?
  2. If you use Hotmail, What should you do if you receive spam e-mail?

Then return to the Hotmail home page. Locate and click on the Email Safety link. Based on the information found on that page answer these question:

  1. Why should you never open attachments from an unknown source?
  2. What are the first and last recommendations for staying safe and secure online? Why are these important?

Netiquette: The Golden Rule of Web Use

Go to http://www.albion.com/netiquette/introduction.html. Read the document you find there. Record your answers to the following questions for submission at the conclusion of this assignment.

  1. What is Netiquette?
  2. Summarize the 10 rules of netiquette.
  3. Ethically, what should you do if you use a shareware application?
  4. Define Bandwidth.
  5. What should you ask yourself before sending or forwarding copies of messages?
  6. What are you judged by when sending e-mail?
  7. What is meant by Flaming?
  8. Why is it unethical to read someone elses e-mail?
  9. If you discover that someone made a mistake, how should you inform them?

Copyright: Honoring the Rights of Others

Go to http://whatiscopyright.org/ . Read the document What is Copyright Protection? Record your answers to the following questions that will be submitted at the conlcusion of this assignment.

  1. What is copyright?
  2. When does copyright begin?
  3. What is the proper way to place a copyright notice?
  4. What are the three reasons information from the Internet can be copied?
  5. What does the copyright symbol © at the bottom of a home page apply to?
  6. What is fair use and how can a student use it when researching?

Web Privacy (or lack thereof)

In your web browser go to http://www.seattletimes.com/news/technology/html98/bugs_19991113.html. Read the article entitled Web bugs nibbling at computer privacy. Based on the article record your answers to the following questions, your answers will be submitted at the conlcusion of this assignment.

  1. What is a web bug?
  2. What is a cookie?
  3. What happens if a cookie is set?
  4. Why do some companies use web bugs?
  5. What do web bugs represent to consumer watchdog groups?

Submitting your work

Create a new Word document. Type the questions and answers that you have recorded based upon the readings into the document. Label the document lastf.section.WWW.doc. Attach the document to an email message, addressed to me at robertsonb@byui.edu. The subject line should be WWW Assignment. When it has been send the assignment is finished.
