Requirements for your Research Paper

Research papers should be done on a subject that is of particular interest to you that relates to this class. The purpose of the paper is to make you an expert on one aspect of the Drive Mechanism class. Your paper should be done professionally and cover about ten pages typed and double-spaced. This doesn't include the title page, table of contents, appendix or footnote pages. You should use several different sources for your information.

The appendix is optional. If you have some large pictures that are hard to fit into the body of text, you may use an appendix. However, it is generally preferable to put the pictures on the page where they apply to what is being talked about.

Any pictures used that you do not personally take should be identified as to source. They should say "Courtesy of ..." with the picture. You can also identify them with a number and put a footnote page just before the bibliography at the end. Either way is acceptable.

Watch your papers for neatness and spelling. You need to get used to being able to express yourself on paper, as it will help you throughout the remainder of your college career as well as throughout life. Start on your paper early so you don't have to rush it and get a crummy grade.

If you haven't had much experience writing, turn in your paper a week or so before it's final due date and I'll read it and make suggestions as to what you can do to improve it. If you make the corrections and resubmit it by it's due date, you will get a better grade on it.

I have some papers that are excellent examples of how a research paper should be written. They have a title page, table of contents, body of text, summary, footnotes, and bibliography. You are welcome to look at them if you would like some ideas.

There are several computers in the library for student use that use a word-processing program that is easy to learn and use. If you don't know how to use it, let me know and I'll be glad to spend some time and teach you. It has a spelling program to help those of us that haven't taught our keyboards how to spell yet, and it makes it very easy to edit and move words around without having to retype the whole thing. Computers have made the typewriter obsolete!

Don't put off your paper till the last minute. It's worth 100 points! If you want extra credit, you can do your paper, then present a class presentation on the subject for an extra 50 points. Clear it at least 2 weeks in advance, though.
