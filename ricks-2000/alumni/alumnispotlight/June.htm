June Spotlight -- Sue Anderson

Of her Ricks experience Sue Anderson stated, "At Ricks I was blessed with good experiences, good friends, a good education, and a GREAT husband."  Sue is married to Neil Anderson.  She met Neil when he came to her apartment to pick her roommate up for a date.  Fortunately, her roommate was already out with another young man.  

Sue and Neil are the parents of four sons, who have four lively wives.  They have been blessed with five grandchildren with more on the way.  Sue serves as the Relief Society secretary and visiting teacher in her ward.

After Ricks College Sue completed here studies at Idaho State University earning a bachelor of arts in 1973.  She currently teaches first grade at Groveland Elementary in Blackfoot.  She has previously taught in Bonneville, Pocatello, and Madison School Districts and has served as the head teacher and acting principal at Lyman Elementary in Rexburg.
Sue has received various honors throughout her career, including the Outstanding Service Award for the Madison School District in 1986, Groveland Teacher of the Year in 1988, and Blackfoot teacher of the year in 2001.  Sue's favorite award is the Teacher of the Millennium-2001, an informal award presented to her by the parents of her students.  Sue's passion for education has led to her success.  She recently said:  "There is only one contribution I can claim, one thing that I continue to find exciting.  What I do is important-I teach children!"

Sue recently received the Outstanding Educator Award.
