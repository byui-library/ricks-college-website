Unit #10 - ORGANIC CHEMISTRY

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Cite or identify examples of alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, and aromatics.
2. Obtain the molecular formula of an organic molecule from its structural formula.
3. Tell whether a given pair of structural formulas represents different molecules, the same molecule, or structural isomers.
4. Give the IUPAC name of any hydrocarbon or organic halide from its structure, and vice versa.
5. Cite or identify examples of alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amines, and amides.
6. Give the IUPAC name for any one of these from its structure, and vice versa (simple examples only).
