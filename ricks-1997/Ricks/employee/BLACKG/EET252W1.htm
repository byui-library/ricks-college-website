Electronic Communications Circuits

EET - 252 SECTION 1 11:00 - 11:50 M,W and 11:00 - 12:50 T,Th

Prerequisites: EET - 251, EET- 250 (concurrent)

Faculty Name: GORDON BLACK

Text: Electronic Communications Systems; Frank R. Dungan; Delmar; 1998

Materials: None

Course Description: Oscillator circuits: Colpitts, Hartley, Phase Shift oscillators, etc., are constructed and tested. Communications circuits found in transmitting equipment are studied: transmitters, amplitude modulation, frequency modulation, pulse and digital modulation, antennas and transmission lines.

Course Objectives: To provide the Student with an understanding of basic communications systems.  To give the student hands on experience with practical communications circuits through laboratory experimentation.
