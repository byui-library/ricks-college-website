Canote Brothers

Thursday, June 15
Dinner - 6 p.m., Manwaring Center West Ballroom
Concert - 7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall

A delicious dinner will be served by Ricks College Food Services prior to a concert by the Canote Brothers of Seattle, Wash. The twin brothers demonstrate their love and mastery of vintage styles from old-time fiddle tunes and country songs to novelty numbers and swing. Garrison Keiler of "Prairie Home Companion" said, "It was a great time! Wonderful stuff!" Cost is $13 for dinner and show; $3 for show only. $1 admission to show for currently enrolled Ricks students.
