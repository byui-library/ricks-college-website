Scholarships
 
Scholarships are only awarded to students who are planning to attend a four-year institution.  Students may apply during their sophomore year for a two-year scholarship that they would use at their follow-on university.  For more information call Toll Free in Idaho 1-800-632-6586 ext. 4175 Toll Free Nationwide 1-800-824-7017 ext. 4175 or visit scholarships
