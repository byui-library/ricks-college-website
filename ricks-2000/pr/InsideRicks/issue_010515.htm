RECAP OF FINAL TRACK MEET: NJCAA CHAMPIONSHIPS

Seven Ricks athletes gained All American status at the National Junior College Athletic Association championships held in May 10-12 in Odessa, Texas.

Top place finishers were Shalene Liddle in the heptathlon, Audrey Golightly in the 10,000 meter run and Ryan Jenks in the 3000 meter steeplechase. Each placed second.

Liddle entered the competition after nearly three weeks of recuperation from a stress fracture. The rest seemed to pay off as she scored a personal best 4826 points. She won the long jump portion of that event with a big personal record leap of 18' 4.75"--her first time over 18 feet.

Golightly was more than 30 meters behind the second place runner in the 10,000 meter race with less than a lap to go. Not to be denied, Golightly put her legs into high gear and blew by the other runner with 100 meters left, to finish in second place. Coach Doug Stutz said, "The other runner did not slow down in the final lap...in fact, she picked up the pace. Audrey just picked it up that much more."

Jenks picked up his pace to come from behind on the final lap of the 3000 meter steeplechase closing in on the eventual winner, Michael Langat of Butler County. Coach Stutz reports, "Ryan made his move with a 100 meters to go, passing two runners, but ran out of track before he could pass the leader. He lost by a foot."

Other All-American performances came from Aubry Wood in the high jump (tied for 3rd with a jump of 5' 6"), Mandy Kelly in the pole vault (3rd), David Woodbury in the 10,000 meters (3rd), and the biggest surprise of the meet, Kathrine Anderson in her debut 3000 meter steeplechase race (3rd). Kathrine had qualified to go to nationals as part of the women's 4x800 meter relay team (which finished 4th). Being allowed one wild card entry, Coach Stutz entered her in the steeplechase although she had never competed in it previously. Coach Stutz explained, "With nothing to lose, Kathrine went out in the race just to have fun. She was even heard to exclaim 'Whee!' while going over the water jump. She moved from 5th to 3rd place in the final two laps to claim the final All-American spot."

POST SEASON HONORS GIVEN FOR BASEBALL

Ricks College baseball coach Jerry Schlegelmilch was named Region 18 Coach of the Year at the just concluded SWAC Baseball Tournament held in St. George, Utah. "Schleg" has just concluded his eleventh season as head coach of Viking baseball. His career mark at Ricks is 289 wins against 258 losses. The Vikings were 18-18 in league play this season, finishing sixth in the SWAC and qualifying for the Region 18 post season tournament where the Vikings were 1-2. Highlighting the season was a home stand against SWAC Champion and second ranked Dixie in which the Vikings won two of four games. Only one other team in the league was able to beat Dixie twice in a four game series during the season. Additionally, third baseman Jacob Stubblefield of San Diego and James Schlegelmilch of Sugar City were named to the All Region team. Stubblefield earned First Team All Region Honors and was named Region 18 Defensive Player of the Year. Stubblefield finished with a .358 batting average for the entire season, but an even more impressive .386 average in league play, and a 91% fielding percentage. James Schlegelmilch was named Second Team All Region and finished league play hitting .315.
