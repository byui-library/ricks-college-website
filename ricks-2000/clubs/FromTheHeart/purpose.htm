Speaking From the Heart

From the Heart is truely one of the most spiritually oriented groups here on campus although our music is not limited to religious songs. However there is a great need for uplifting music, music that inspires the soul to rejoice in family, friendhsip, love, and all the beautiful things in this life. In order to spread this joy to those who may find themselves lost from it, we put on musical firesides and performances with the hope that at least one soul will be touched by the greatest power God gave us, the power of music.

What is our purpose?

  1. To create quality musical firesides and small-scale entertainment revues using students with talent in popular music.
  2. To offer these firesides and shows AS A SERVICE to campus and off-campus organizations requesting inspirational programs or popular entertainment.
  3. To magnify talents through weekly club rehearsals and performance experiences
  4. To build character and gospel commitment within club members especially through performances/visits at nursing homes, treatment centers, and like organizations.
  5. To spiritually uplift audiences at all performances.
