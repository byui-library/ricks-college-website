Ricks College News Release
May 19, 2000

Ricks begins construction
on radio/graphics building 

REXBURG, Idaho —  ground has been broken on a new building at Ricks College that will house the studios of two college-operated radio stations and the graphic services office.

The 4,225 square foot building will be located between the Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building and the tennis courts along East Viking Drive.

The one-story, steel-frame building will have a brick veneer with stone quoins, says campus architect Rulon Nielsen. It was designed by Nielsen and is being constructed by Vern Clark and Sons Construction of Idaho Falls.

The building will house KRIC-FM, a public radio station owned and operated by Ricks; KWBH-FM, a low-power, student-operated station; and Graphic Services.

The building is scheduled to be completed Sept. 1 in time for the radio stations and graphic services to move out of the aging Jacob Spori Building that will torn down later this year.

Although a new classroom building will be constructed on the location of the Spori Building starting in 2001, college officials decided to relocate these services in a separate building for several reasons. A higher location on the hill will give KRIC-FM a direct signal to its transmitter on the South Menan Butte, the radio stations will have a facility built specifically to their needs and the offices will be close to the administration building.

Jim Clark, KRIC station manager, says, "We are very fortunate to be able to design a space from the ground up. It isn't often that a public radio station is afforded the opportunity and we are grateful to our sponsoring institution."

Clark adds, "This facility will help us carry on our mission that we represent Ricks College to the public, along with present classical music and National Public Radio news."
