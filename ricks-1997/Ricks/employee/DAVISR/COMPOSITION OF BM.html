COMPOSITION OF THE

BOOK OF MORMON

Brass Plates of Laban (brought from Jerusalem; contained most of the Old Testament from Genesis through Jeremiah)

Large Plates (made by Nephi 590BC; contain secular history)

Book of Lehi (first 116 pages of book; lost by Martin Harris)

Small Plates (first 143 pages of B/M)

(a second set of plates made by Nephi 20 years later, about 570BC, and containing only religious writings)

1st, 2nd Nephi, Jacob, Enos, Jarom, Omni

Words of Mormon (385AD; a few words from Mormon to join the Small plates to the Large ones. Note that the Small plates end in the early reign of King Benjamin.)

Mosiah (we now switch back to the Large Plates; they begin late in the reign of King Benjamin. From now on the text contains both sacred and secular events.

Alma

Helaman

3rd/4th Nephi

Mormon (About 380AD Mormon abridges the Large Plates; remember that we have only his summary of each book, not the original. He then added the Small Plates, unabridged, to the end of his record.)

(Last 44 pages are all summarized by Moroni, not his father Mormon.)

Ether (Moroni, about 421AD, abridges this book and adds it to his father’s record.)

Moroni

Summary Page (This is the last page written by Moroni. Today it is the Title Page of your Book of Mormon.)

Joseph Smith described the plates as being about 6"x8" and 6" thick. About two thirds of the book was sealed, and thus not translated. We also lost the first 116 pages (Book of Lehi) when the pages were given to Martin Harris, who lost them.
