Evening Retreats

Evening retreats are designed to provide students with a meaningful learning experience that will foster personal growth and/or leadership development in a small group setting.

These retreats are a great way to learn more about yourself and how to improve, meet new people, and have some fun. Each retreat has a different theme such as "Reaching Your Divine Potential" and "Seek Learning by Study and also by Faith." Learning is accomplished through gospel teachings, structured activities and discussion. We leave at 4:30 p.m. and return about 10:30 p.m.

Registration

You can sign up for the retreats in person at our office in the Manwaring Center, room # 380, starting two weeks before the scheduled date. Registration is limited and is handled on a first-come, first-served basis. The cost is $1.50 that includes refreshments and transportation.
