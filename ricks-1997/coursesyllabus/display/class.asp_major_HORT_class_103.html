Home Gardening
HOME GARDENING
CLASS CODE: HORT 103 CREDITS:  3 FEE:  $10.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Learn basic principles, culture and production of vegetables, fruits, flowers, trees shrubs, and turf, as it relates to the home garden and self-reliance. (For non-Hort majors only)
DESCRIPTION: Two hours lecture
Two hours lab per week
TOPICS: Soils, Fertility, Vegetables, Irrigation, Mulching, Pruning, Insects, Disease, Weeds, Harvesting, Storing Produce, Hydroponics
OBJECTIVES: This is a beginning course of practical, down to earth gardening. Participants will gain a broad knowledge base including, garden planning, production, pruning, maintenance, harvesting, storage, variety selection, cultural practices, composing, small space gardening, integrated pest management and the efficient use of natural resources. It is designed for majors other than horticulture.
REQUIREMENTS: Required Text: Intermountain Vegetable Gardening, Allen Wilson and Intermountain Flower Gardening, Allen Wilson.
PREREQUISITES: Designed for non-horticulture majors
OTHER: $10 fee
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2000
