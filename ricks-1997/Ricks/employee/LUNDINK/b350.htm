Course Description --Basis For Class

 We are living in a time of “fast history”.  The World Wide Web is less than 3000 days old.  Globalization fuel by technological advances is introducing changes to the business world at an accelerating rate.  The biggest challenge facing business mangers today is effectively adapting to these changes.

 Students graduating with a degree in Business Management must be familiar with and be able to make decisions regarding technology with a comfortable level of confidence.  This class is not designed to give all the answers, but to expose students to an area of business that is rapidly evolving.  Knowing where to find answers and asking the right questions is extremely valuable in today’s business world.

Objectives

  1. To better understand and appreciate the impact technology has upon businesses and organizations worldwide.  
  2. To enable future business managers and owners to effectively communicate and work with technical experts.
  3. Be able to analyze the potential benefits of technology relative to the costs.
  4. Appreciate the limitations of technology.
  5. Understand the role of technology in supporting the strategy of an organization.

 Areas of Focus:  

  1. Digitization:  A major focus in business today is moving physical products to a digital format (i.e. Reports, Training, Brochures, audio, video, and text).
  2. Customer retention and satisfaction:  The course will address ways in which technology can assist companies in adding-value in the eyes of the customer.
  3. Supply Chain Management: Improving the supplier and distributor interaction with the company.  (B2B)   extranets, logistics, supply chain management.
  4. Improving internal process between employees, departments, divisions, companies, etc.
