EET 151 Point-To-Point Soldering

 

Purpose:

1. Practice laying out components and wires on a blank, perforated board.  (This skill is necessary for completing the HW Design Project for this class.)

2. Continue soldering (and perhaps, desoldering) practice.

3. Be able it identify and explain the basic operation of some common electronic components.

 

Procedure:

Build this simple oscillator circuit and make it work.

Notes:

The resistors and capacitors in your parts kit may have slightly different values than those shown in the schematic diagram.

There is no formal lab report for this lab exercise!  Simply make it work and present it for grading.  You will be scored on the quality of your solder connections, and whether both LEDs actually blink.
