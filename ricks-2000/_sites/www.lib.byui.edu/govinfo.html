Federal Government:
Legislative and Regulatory Information

  • GPOAccess -- Search and retrieve congressional information from the US Government Printing Office, with mirror, or Gateway, sites at over 20 universities, each with their own strengths.
  • FindLaw -- Internet Legal Resources, including Supreme Court decisions since 1893.
  • Library of Congress -- guide to the U.S. Legislative Branch
  • Thomas -- search & retrieve legislation from the 103rd to current congresses
  • U.S. House of Representatives Internet Law Library -- laws and regulations
