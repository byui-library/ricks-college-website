WHERE IS BADGER CREEK?

The Outdoor Learning Center (OLC) or commonly referred to as the Badger Creek ranch is a 160- acre property at the northern end of the Teton Valley crossed by Badger Creek. To the northeast is Yellowstone National Park. A few miles east of the ranch is Grand Teton National Park. Just southeast of the ranch is Driggs, Idaho. Forty-five miles to the west is Rexburg and the Ricks College campus.

The Badger Creek Ranch is nestled in the scenic Teton Valley of Idaho at the foot of the majestic Teton Mountains where students in the Quest program participate in recreational and scholastic activities regularly. The valley is rich in history. It was formerly called Pierre’s Hole by the mountain men and was thought  by some to be the most beautiful valley in the world. Pierre’s Hole was one of the sites of the legendary mountain man rendezvous.
The majestic beauty of the valley that the mountain men loved remains for students to enjoy.  The ranch has been used for student recreation classes, clubs, ward activities, Outdoor Especially For Youth, youth conferences and family reunions during the spring and summer. Now during the fall semester, the Quest program at Ricks College is sponsoring an educational experience with adventure in mind. A strong focus of the Badger Creek Ranch staff is to help students develop self- confidence and leadership skills through participating in its famous ropes and initiative courses.  Unlike some outdoor programs, Quest is not for remedial education or "troubled youth."  It is for the student interested in quality education which offers an outdoor experience.

Starting in 1998, Ricks College began to offer a resident academic program at the Badger Creek ranch that was later named Quest. Several upgrades have been made to the accommodations for the Quest students. Improvements in lodging, classroom facilities, and computer lab, as well as other additions, have been completed without changing the nature of the facility.
By design, the ranch is rustic. It is not intended to be a "city slicker mountain chateau." Young women will be housed dorm style in the Lodge. Sixteen young women will share two large bay style rooms. 
Young men will be housed in the guest cabins with two or three students per cabin.
The Quest programs of Ricks College are where life changing experiences are gained... and where life long friends are made.
