Airport Shuttles
The Public Relations Department offers shuttle service to and from the Salt Lake City and Idaho Falls airports at the beginning and end of each semester, Christmas and Thanksgiving.  Service can also be provided, if requested in advance, from the Idaho Falls bus station.  A nominal fee is charged for this service.

ASRC (Associated Students of Ricks College)--Officers
ASRC officers are the recognized student leaders on campus.  The ASRC officers  and ASRC Committees sponsor most of the extra-curricular activities scheduled on campus, such as academic competitions, academic lectures, leadership development, service projects, clubs and organizations, Multi-cultural Week, Women's Week, homecoming activities, dances and other activities and special events.  Students who are interested in participating on one of the numerous committees may apply at the ASRC Office located on the third floor of the Manwaring Student Center, Room 394.  Elections for ASRC Officers are held in March.

Athletics
Ricks provides a diversified sports program which includes:  men's football, basketball, wrestling, baseball, track, and cross country.  Women's sports include:  basketball, softball, volleyball, track, and cross country.  For additional information about intercollegiate sports call the coaches' secretary, 356-2124.

Center and also in the Kimball Building.  All machines provide “no surcharge” transactions.  For more information, contact Beehive Federal Credit Union, 356-3955.

Attendance at Class
You are responsible for attendance in class.  No absences, whether approved by the college for participation in college-sponsored activities or due to illness or other personal emergencies, are "excusable" in the sense of relieving you of the responsibility for work assigned or participated in by the class during your absence.  In most cases, repeated absences may cause you to receive a lower grade or even fail a class.

Bicycles
All bicycles ridden on campus must be registered with the Campus Police.  Registration helps prevent theft and facilitates the recovery of stolen bicycles.  There is no cost for this service.  Bicycles can be registered at the Campus Police Office in Kimball 150.  Bicycle riders must yield to pedestrians on sidewalks and ride at walking speeds or dismount, if necessary, so as not to constitute a hazard to any pedestrian on campus.  Non-compliance will be enforced by Campus Police under Rexburg City Ordinance #759.  Racks are provided near the entrances to most buildings and near the on campus residence halls.  To prevent theft, bicycles should be locked to racks.  They should not be locked to trees, shrubbery, buildings, or building apparatus.  Improperly parked and/or unregistered bicycles are subject to impoundment and a fine.

Board of Trustees
Ricks College is owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  Members of the First Presidency and of the Quorum of Twelve and other selected church leaders constitute the Board of Trustees.  Direct responsibility for the operation of the school is under the direction of the president of Ricks College.

Body Piercing
Piercing of the body, with exception of conservative pierced women’s ears (not more than one per ear), is not appropriate under any circumstances.

Bookstore
The Ricks College Bookstore offers a wide selection of merchandise to meet your academic and personal needs.  The Bookstore is located on the first floor of the Manwaring Student Center and the hours of operation are posted.

Bookstore Textbook Buyback 
Book buyback is held at the end of each semester.  Quantities purchased are determined by instructor requests.  A textbook wholesaler will purchase used books that are not being bought by the bookstore.  Wholesale prices they offer are based on national demand for the books.  It is recommended that students hold their books until the buyback for best pricing.

Bookstore Textbook  Returns
Textbooks purchased from the bookstore at the beginning of a semester may be returned for full refund until the last day of the no penalty add/drop period.  Returned books must be in resalable condition and a receipt must be provided as proof of purchase.  Books purchased after the add/drop period may also be returned within two business days.  There are NO refunds on textbooks purchased the last week of any semester or term or during final exams unless they are purchased for class use during the next semester or term.

Caps and Gowns
The Alumni Office makes arrangements for the graduation banquet and for all cap and gown rentals.  When you fill out your Application for Graduation, there is a small section for you to list whether you are marching in graduation or not.  There is also a place for you to fill in your height (the cap is one size fits all).  When you do this, your cap and gown are automatically ordered and the rental fee is charged to your student account.  Please check The Scroll and the Marquee for deadlines, or call the Alumni Office at ext. 2234.  The gowns are to be returned immediately after individual convocations to the Hart Auxiliary Gym.

Career and Advising Center--CAC
The staff in the Career and Advising Center(CAC) provides two types of services.  They do academic advising which includes class selection and changing majors.  Secondly, they help students explore career information and college majors. The CAC contains extensive information on careers and colleges.  An excellent computerized career decision-making program is available in the CAC located on the 2nd floor of the Kimball Bldg.

Cashier's Office
The Cashier's Office is open from 8:15 a.m. until 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday and closed Tuesday (1:50 p.m. to 3:00 p.m.) for devotional.  You may cash checks up to $200 by displaying your Ricks College I.D. card.  All payments for loans, registration, tuition and fees, and housing may be made at the Cashier's Office located in the Kimball Building.

Catalog
The Ricks College Catalog is the official publication listing all requirements for entrance into Ricks College as well as detailed information on all class offerings and associate degrees available.  Since graduation requirements change periodically, you should keep a copy of the catalog for the first year of your attendance at Ricks.  You will be held to the graduation requirements of that catalog.

Check Cashing
The Ricks College Cashier's Office and the Bookstore, as well as many downtown banks and businesses, will cash checks when proper identification is shown.  Insufficient fund checks are subject to additional charges and may result in criminal prosecution.  Avoid such problems by keeping your check register balanced and sufficient funds in your account.

College Skills Seminar
There are two sessions of the College Skills Seminar offered each summer, with students having the option of attending either the June or  July session.  Those who attend are instructed in the basic skills of reading, writing, and arithmetic and studying.  Brushing up on these basics provides growth in self confidence and a more successful orientation to college life.  Additional information is available through the Summer School Office or the Learning Assistance Labs.

Commencement/Convocations
Division convocations are held after baccalaureate/commencement ceremonies during April graduation.  You will be assigned to one of these convocations according to the type of degree you receive and your major area of study.  It is during your convocation that you actually receive your diploma cover.  Your diploma is mailed to you upon final completion and certification of your work.
 
Computer Fraud
Computer Fraud is a Criminal Offense.  Any person who knowingly and without permission accesses or attempts to access any campus computer, computer system or computer program without prior authorization is committing Computer Fraud.  You may be charged with a misdemeanor or felony and/or be dismissed from Ricks College should you become involved in any form of computer fraud.

Copyright
The copyright law of  the United States governs the making of photocopies or other reproduction of copyrighted material.  Photocopies or other reproductions can be furnished only under certain conditions, if they will be used solely for private study, scholarship, or research.  Use of the reproduction for other purposes may make the user liable for copyright infringement.  Ricks College reserves the right to refuse to accept a copying order if, in its judgment, fulfillment of the order would involve violation of copyright laws.

Counseling Center
College students frequently experience stress and other emotional discomforts.  These may range from challenges adjusting to college life to severe feelings of depression or anxiety; from concerns about careers to feelings of low self-esteem.  Help in dealing with these problems is available at the Counseling Center located in the Kimball Building, 356-1100.  Students will receive reliable, professional assistance in an atmosphere of warmth and understanding.  Complete confidentiality is maintained by the counselors.

Credit by Exam
Several courses at Ricks may be challenged by special examination by permission of the appropriate Department Chairman.  Credit by examination must be done by application.  These forms are available at the Registrar's Office and must be signed and approved by the Department Chairman concerned.  Prior to taking the exam, a fee for the purchase of the credit must be paid at the Cashier's Office.  The fee is $5 per credit, with a minimum charge of $15 and a maximum charge of $25.  After the exam has been taken, the instructor records the grade on the application.  That is forwarded to the Registrar's Office where it is recorded on your transcript.

Crime Reporting
Ricks College encourages the prompt and accurate reporting of crimes to the Campus Police.  Students who wish to report crimes occurring off campus are to contact the Rexburg City Police.  All crimes reported will be investigated and disposed of in a professional and appropriate manner.  Any suspicious or emergency situation should be quickly reported by dialing 911 from any phone or by activating any of the emergency telephones located on campus.  Non-emergencies can be reported to the Campus Police by calling 356-2448.  (See also, Police, Emergency-General and Emergency Telephones.)

Curfew
Hours are the same for off-campus and on-campus housing units and apply equally to single male and female students.  All students must be in their apartment or housing unit by 11 p.m. on nights preceding a school day, and by 1 a.m. on nights not preceding a school day.  Arrangements may be made for a late or overnight pass.
