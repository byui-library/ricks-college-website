CC = Community college (all students)

CC-T = Community college (transfer students) -- includes only those students who intend to go to a 4-year school after completing their community college degree

CC-V = Community college (vocational students) -- includes only those students who are pursuing a 1- or 2-year terminal degree at the community college

Some of the community colleges which were included in the norms:

  • Antelop Valley CC
  • Central Arizona College
  • Cleveland State CC
  • Irvine Valley CC
  • Jamestown CC
  • Napa Valley CC
  • Pasadena City College
  • Northern Nevada CC
  • Saddleback CC
  • South Seattle CC
  • Walla Walla CC

Note.  The norms for community colleges were obtained from the CCSEQ [Community College Student Experiences Questionnaire] norm booklet where items were equivalent.   Where norms are blank, there were no CCSEQ items corresponding to the CSEQ.
