Occupational Internship
OCCUP INTERN
CLASS CODE: HORT 298A CREDITS:  0.5
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Work experience in the florist, landscape or nursery industry. A comprehensive experience under supervision of th employer and faculty specialist. A florist, nursery or landscape operation is selected whose practices conform closely to the needs and training objectives of the student.
DESCRIPTION: Class preparation for work experience in the florist, landscape or nursery industry.
TOPICS: A survey of horticulture job opportunities; personal evaluation techniques; introduction to forms required for an internship.
OBJECTIVES: Find an Employer and sign a cooperative contract for the student's Internship and learn the necessary paperwork to be completed during an internship.
REQUIREMENTS: Horticulture, landscape, floral majors must enroll in this course as preparation for Hort 298B.
PREREQUISITES: Horticulture Management or Floral Design Management Major or consent of instructor, two weeks work experience for each hour of credit. Repeatable to a maximum of eight credit hours.
