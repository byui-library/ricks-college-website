GRADING

Attendance: Each day present you are given five points for a total of (approx) one hundred fifty points. If you are absent for school excused reasons or serious illness you can make up the discussion work missed that day by doing the questions and summazing the chapters assigned and turning them in WITHIN THREE SCHOOL DAYS OF THE ABSENCE. The maximum days you can miss is EIGHT. After that you lose your credit. Be careful.

Tests and quizzes: Quizzes are given periodically. There will be a midterm and a final. If you use the study guide well--complete it and use it to prepare for your midterm and final--you will do fine. If you don't it could be difficult. All questions on quizzes and tests are taken from questions IN THE STUDY GUIDE.

The Study Guide can be found on my faculty homepage. Go to the Ricks College External Home Page; Click on Directories; Click on Departments/Divisions; click on Religious Education; Click on faculty. You will see my ugly mug. Just below it click on Homepage. Once there, Under Book of Mormon 122 click on Study Guide, then print it. It is ten pages or more, depending on format, etc. Be prepared with the answers each day of class.

Reading: You are given five points for each reading assignment completed ON TIME. If it is not done ON TIME you lose the five points.

MATERIALS NEEDED

Book of Mormon, note paper, study guide, and pens and pencils. You will NOT need the Book of Mormon Student manual, though you may purchase it if you want. It is a good resource.
