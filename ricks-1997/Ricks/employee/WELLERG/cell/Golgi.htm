The Golgi body (also known as a Dictyosome) are involved with the production of chemicals and the packaging of these chemicals often for excretion from the cell.  Many of the chemicals which are packaged in Golgi body are sent to the Golgi from the ER.  The chemicals received from the ER can be modified and then repackaged.

This is a transmission electron microscope (TEM) view of Golgi Body.  A Golgi Body can be seen running diagonally through this image from upper left to lower right.
