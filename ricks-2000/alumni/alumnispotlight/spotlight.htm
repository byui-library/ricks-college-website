October Spotlight -- Tamra Norton

     Tamra Norton, married to Dennis Norton 91’, is the author of an LDS youth marketed novel, entitled "Molly Mormon?" This book is about a sixteen year old girl, Molly Chambers, who lives in a small LDS community. Molly is a girl who has a desire to do what is right, even if it brings ridicule to herself. Through some very tough situations she and her friends would go through, she comes to realize that her honest desire to do what she knows is right has made the difference for many others.

     Tamra decided that she wanted to become a writer because of a composition class she took here at Ricks. She wrote "Molly Mormon?" with the purpose of sharing a "good message" with the youth in the church. She has two teenage daughters and can see the struggles that youth deal with. Some of the messages conveyed in this novel are to "Always stay true to yourself and the gospel, because that’s where true happiness comes from," and that we’ll never know the influence that we can make in another’s life.

     At Rick’s, Tamra was on the Folk Dance team, were she enjoyed clogging ext.. She loves to do crafts, bake, and loves to read; "…it’s something I do everyday." She is a mother of 7 children, and lives in Spring, Texas. She is currently working on a sequel to "Molly Mormon?," that will be released next March. This book continues the story of Molly at BYU- Idaho two years after the first book takes place.

You can find Tamra Norton’s book at most LDS stores, or by going to www.cedarfort.com.
