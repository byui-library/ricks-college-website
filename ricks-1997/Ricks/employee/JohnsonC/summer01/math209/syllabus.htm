Prerequisite:  Math 110, completed with a grade of B or better

Required Materials:  Textbook:  Calculus With Technology, by Barnett & Ziegler.
Calculator:  A TI-86 calculator is required.

Course Description:  This is a terminal course of single and multivariable calculus with application to business, biology, agriculture and other sciences.  The computation and application of derivatives and integrals will be studied.  Calculus principles will be applied in various situations.

Additional Help:  If you need additional help, please come see me during my scheduled office hours.   If your schedule precludes this, I will gladly make an appointment with you.  If you are struggling, come immediately for help.  Tutors are available from 3:00-5:00 p.m. in ROM 260.

Collaboration:  Students are strongly encouraged to study in small groups to do homework and to prepare for exams.   Group study facilitates a more complete learning experience than can be achieved individually.   Even though students may collude on homework, each person must contribute ideas and generate their own write-up, showing work as needed.
