Readings in Hispanic Literature
READ HISP LIT
CLASS CODE: SPAN 202 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An exploration of Spanish-language literature across the ages.
DESCRIPTION: A study of Spanish-language poems, short stories, plays, novels, and culture. Practice of principles leading to understanding and appreciation of literary genres.
TOPICS: Principles for analyzing literature, study of symbols, culture, word histories, historical background, etc.
OBJECTIVES: To give students tools and abilities to analyze and enjoy literature on their own. Students completing this course should be able to speak at intermediate mid-level and appreciate varied literary forms.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, oral participation in groups, papers, extensive readings, exams (including short essay).
PREREQUISITES: Spanish 201 with a grade of "C" or higher or consent of instructor. No Spanish-speaking returned missionaries or native speakers. Spanish-speaking RM's should enroll in 302. Native speakers should enroll in Spanish 321.
