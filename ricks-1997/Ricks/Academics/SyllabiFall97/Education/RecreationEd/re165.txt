I. COURSE DESCRIPTION - Emergency preparedness techniques used in a 
	lost or disoriented situation.

II. STATEMENT OF PURPOSE - In today's world of gadgets and conveniences, 
	few people are skillful in the use of their hands or imaginations. 
	It seems that manual dexterity is dwindling, even to the extent 
	where people are becoming virtually  immobile without the aid of 
	some form of mechanization. Along with this decline in 
	self-reliance, comes a lack in the amount of self-confidence 
	and initiative needed to be a survivor.

	The purpose of this course is to help improve one's self-reliance 
	and build up the valuable attribute of "A Positive Mental
	Attitude."  Attitude is the key to survival.  But attitude, 
	unfortunately, is not easily taught; it is something that must be
	acquired through personal action.  So, as an alternative we teach 
	skills to the novice in hopes that he too can accept the
	challenge of being a survivor.

III. REQUIRED PREREQUISITE - RE 165 is a Level II course, but because of 
	its basic nature requires no previous experience.

IV. COURSE OBJECTIVES:
	A. To develop a working knowledge of primitive and contemporary 
	survival principles.
	B. To develop the ability to adapt to a varied set of terrain, 
	weather, and other environment situations.
	C. To enhance the students initiative and self confidence 
	through the completion of established survival experiences.
	D. To develop a respect for the neutrality of mother nature 
	and learn the skills needed to live with and not conquer.
