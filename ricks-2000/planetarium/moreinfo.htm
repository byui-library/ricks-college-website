How to get to the Planetarium:

  • Enter Rexburg using the Main Street Exit.
  • Turn right on 1st West Street
  • Turn left onto 2nd South Street
  • Turn right into parking lot east of football stadium.
  • Romney Science Building is second building East from Parking lot.
  • Enter East doors!

  • Planetarium is located in room 107, down the stairs by the weather instruments
