Instructional Strategies

The four teaching/learning strategies described in this section are meant to be a primer for the beginning teacher. Teaching and learning are dynamic processes that truly exemplify the notion of the Gestalt theory - the whole is greater than the sum of its parts. As preservice teachers explore the realm of instructional design, teaching strategies, and learning theory it should be come obvious that all are inter-related and extremely complex.

To learn more about these instructional strategies the materials packet entitled Teaching, Learning & Technology is available from Apple Computer, Inc., and the same packet can be used in the Teacher Education Dept. computer lab, in Smith 148 on the Ricks College Campus.
