CLOTHING & EQUIPMENT LIST

 

BYU-Idaho Campus Attire:

         " Pants / Shirts (casual)

         " Shoes / Socks

         " Sunday Dress for: (Women - dresses /  Men - shirts, ties, dress pants)

               Thursday Evening Activities

               Friday Devotional & Testimony Meeting

         " Scriptures

         " Note pad and pencil

Off Campus Activities:

 

 " Coat

   " Sweater / Jacket

   " Rain Poncho or equivalent (MANDATORY: must be shown at 

     check-in.  Please note that having quality rain gear is very 

     important! Cheap items such as garbage bags do not stand up 

     to the activities you will be involved in.)

   " Good Hiking Boots (broken in) and/or tennis shoes

   " Modest one-piece swim suit for river rafting 

  (Shorts may be worn for river rafting only!)

   " Change of dry clothes for after river rafting

   " Hat (for sun)

   " Water Bottle (at least 32 oz.) MANDATORY

   " Insect Repellent

   " *Sleeping Bag

   " *Sleeping Pad

   " Towel & washcloth (for river rafting and at OLC - towels & 

      washcloths from dorms may not be used for these activities)

   " Day Pack (used to carry personal items to workshops - put your 

      name and address on it.)

   " Flashlight - including extra batteries

   " Suntan Lotion / Sun Block (waterproof)

 

Optional:

         " Sunglasses                       " Gloves

         " Chapstick                        " Camera

         " Journal                             " Pocket Knife

        

*These items can be rented (very limited supply) if you contact our office by a week 

before our program starts.

  This clothing and equipment list is not designed to encourage participants to go out 

and buy new things.  Please try to use existing clothing, then try to borrow from family 

or friends, etc.  Arm/Navy surplus, DI (Deseret Industries) and other thrift stores are 

excellent places to buy outdoor type clothing.

 

Please bring only the items on this list.  Bare feet, midriffs, tank tops (boys and girls), 

shorts, etc.  are NOT ALLOWED.  Shirts must be worn at all times.
