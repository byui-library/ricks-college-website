Every Thursday night from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m in the BYU-IDAHO pool.  Learn the basics of scuba diving in a 2 hour class/experience in the pool taught by qualified instructors.  The cost is only $12.  Certified divers can dive for only $5.  Call Doug Conway at 1146 or Garth Nelson at 2321 for more information. 

 

  Take a Continuing Education Class!

523, ENRICHED AIR NITROX CLASS - (3/14/2002 - 3/28/2002)

Instructor: Inland Scuba

Enriched Air Nitrox is a SSI speciality allowing the diver to dive with gasses containing a higher concentration of oxygen than compressed air. Diving with Enriched Air Nitrox allows the diver to have longer down times and shorter surface intervals. It has also been reported to cause less fatigue. The class includes books and dive tables. Instruction includes special equipment needs, dive tables, and oxygen analysis.

 

524, 525, SCUBA DIVING - BEGINNING  03/07/2002 - 04/18/2002)

Instructor: Inland Scuba

Course includes 6, three-hour sessions, with one hour class time and two hours pool time. There will be an open-water diving at Belmont Hot Springs (dates to be determined in class). Upon successful completion, SSI lifetime certificate will be issued. Tuition includes use of equipment and pool, but not transportation or open water diving entry fee of $30. 525 excludes April 4th.
