About Twitchell

Kevin E. Twitchell has a BA in History and Asian Studies from Brigham Young University and an MS in Computer Science from the University of Idaho. He has 20 years of industrial software engineering experience including
  • real-time systems,
  • statistical process control,
  • machine vision and other manufacturing systems,
  • system administration,
  • software quality,
  • software engineering,
  • Web development, and
  • software project management.
He currently teaches computer science at Brigham Young University-Idaho.
