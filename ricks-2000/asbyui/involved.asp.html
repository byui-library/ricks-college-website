Get Involved

Do you want to serve the students of BYU-Idaho?  This is your chance to make a difference! With a plethora of positions to be filled, we want you to come in and apply.   Time commitments are flexible, and there are a variety of programs, clubs, activities, and causes to get involved with.  You can even run for an office yourself!  Come to the ASBYUI Offices, MC394, and pick up an application, fill out an online application, or Contact Us!
