COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 299R
Title: Special Studies Credit: 1-3
Text: None

Course Objective:
To provide the student with experience in the Culinary Arts vocation.

Description:
Culinary Arts internships or approved projects.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Students must get a summer job in food service or work during the semester while taking classes.

This course is also used for special projects such as serving as an officer in the Culinary Arts Club or working at Badger Creek as a chef.
