Philosophy

Questions were more important than the answers

Questions of Life always the same, but answers change with new knowledge

Thales - basic component of the universe is water

Anaximander - all is separating out or going back into the boundless

Heracleitos - Logos guides all change/is in all things “nothing IS, everything is BECOMING”, “can't put foot in same river twice”

Pythagorus - only that which is permanent or unchanging is real

        reality can exist apart from substance/ideas are real, need no substance

        trust the mind, not the senses - use math, it's always correct.

        - there is a permanent Pattern for each Type / forerunner of Plato

Democritus 390BC - all matter is composed of atoms

        - we are in a blizzard of atoms. They make us do things, thus we are not in control of ourselves. This leads to Fatalism.

        - atoms are controlled by Fate (or Gods) so you're not in control

        - leads to today's existentialism

Socrates c.450BC - wrote nothing/all we have is from Plato

        - loved any discussion (dialectic) to show that truth is hard to define (“Truth” is usually what men choose to call it, but not what it “really” is.)

        - taught rhetoric, the art of persuasion

        “The unexamined life is not worth living.”

        - the establishment doesn't like him/trial on phony charges

        - condemned to death/forced to drink cup of poison

 

Plato 427BC - student of Socrates / nickname, means “wide”

        - in the beginning we started with 1. God, or the Creator, 2. world of Ideas or Patterns, and 3. natural things

        - he divides all into two realms: perfect: (world of Ideas) and imperfect:(this world)

        - God molds things in shape of patterns. Since things change, none is perfect like its pattern.

        - Before this life, Man lived as a soul in the world of Ideas/he had a divine origin

        - at birth he was made to forget/soul was imprisoned in the body. We can remember, somewhat, of the beauty which surrounded us, and this life is the search for that beauty and truth we once knew.

        - it can be found through education

        - at death the soul will return to the realm of perfect ideas (heaven)

        - the soul consists of 3 parts: appetite (which must be confined), courage (to make us honorable), and reason (to govern the body and make it perfect). He elaborates on these in The Republic, giving a picture of an ideal, socialist state, which defines the nature of Justice. Three groups in the state 1) philo. kings 2) soldiers 3) workers

 

Aristotle 384BC - student of Plato

        - lived in northern Greece/son of a physician, so he had an empirical, scientific background

        - is personal tutor to Alexander the Great

        - tries to reconcile the duality of Plato

                “idea” cannot be separated from the “object”

                substance + idea = reality

                both are of equal importance

        - enteleche “that which has purpose”

                is in all things/light of Christ/all move toward perfection

        - God is “unmoved Mover”

        - each object works for its own good summum bonum

        Man's “good” is to live in harmony with others, which can be done in two ways:

                1) life of virtue. Achieve Golden Mean/work for balance in all you do

                2) life of contemplation. Thought is better than action. (will lead to monasteries in middle ages)
