Included in the $175 deposit is a $25 non-refundable processing fee and a $150 security deposit (of which $100 will be applied to the contract amount after the student moves in). The $150 security deposit will be refunded if the Housing Office is notified of cancellation by June 30. One hundred dollars of the security deposit will be refunded if notification is received between July 1 and 31. After that time, the security deposit is non-refundable.
