Transferring General Education to University of Utah
The following agreement is designed for students who will transfer to the U of U without their Associates of Arts and Science Degree. Students with the Associate of Arts and Sceinces waive their GE at the U of U. A minimum of eight semester credit hours in each of the General Education areas (taken from the following list) will satisfy the Liberal Education requirements at the University of Utah. Completion of PolSci 110 or AmHer 100 will satisfy the American Institutions requirement. Completion of Eng111 or 114 will satisfy the writing requirements. Candidates for a B.S. degree must complete College Algebra. Candidates for a B.A. degree must complete (or demonstrate equivalent of ) five quarters in one of the classical or modern languages taught at the University of Utah.
