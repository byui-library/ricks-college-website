CLASS POLICIES:

Assignments:

1. Note on the schedule that assignments are due on the class date listed.

2. Late work will lose 10% for each school day late up to a maximum of 50%.

3. Absent students are responsible to find out details for each assignment.

4. Missed assignments due to illness are due at the next class meeting. Please phone prior to class to let me know if you will be absent due to illness.

QUIZZES:

1. Quizzes will be given at the start of class.

2. Unscheduled quizzes may also be given so review your notes from the previous class lecture.

CURRICULUM:

A daily schedule of homework due dates, daily topics, and assignments are enclosed in this packet. This schedule will be closely followed, however, the instructor reserves the right to make changes to the curriculum at any time.

CHALLENGES:

In compliance with applicable disability law, qualified students with a disability may be entitled to "reasonable accommodation." It is the student’s responsibility to disclose to the teacher any special need she/he may have before the end of the first week of class.
