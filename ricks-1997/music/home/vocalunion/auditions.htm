Auditions...

    When: 

    Tuesday Sep 3, 2002

    Where: 

    BYU-Idaho Music Department 

    Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts (SNO)

What:

    • Prepare two songs of contrasting style (one fast and one slow ballad-like) you may be asked to only sing a portion

    • Choreograph one of your songs to demonstrate good stage movement

    • Prepare a 90 second autobiographical monologue (a story all about you) 

Other:

  • Signup for an audition time in the Snow Building Main Lobby Area on Aug 31, Sep 2 or Sep 3, 2002

  • You may bring your own accompanist or sing to a tape/CD

  • Call-back auditions will be held on Wed Sep 4, 2002, time and location to be announced

  • We are looking for good people - tell your friends
