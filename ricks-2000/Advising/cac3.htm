The CAC offers a series of computer modules you can take which will evaluate your strengths
and will suggest a major best suited for you. This program is free of charge. Undecided
majors are called General Studies. Many students graduate with an Associate in Arts and
Sciences in General Studies. If you have not declared a major, select General Studies in the
division you would like to be assigned to for advising purposes. A student may change his or her
major at most any time by visiting the CAC.
