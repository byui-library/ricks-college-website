COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 181
Title: Culinary Food Preparation II Credit: 2
Text:   On Cooking, Labensky & Hause.  Prentice Hall, New Jersey, 1998.

Course Objective: To teach the students basic cooking techniques and practices for every meal.  To ensure that the student understands and can perform various cooking techniques.

Description:  This course is designed to teach students some of the basic kitchen skills necessary for proper and efficient food production.  Skills taught include: breakfast preparation; egg cookery; soup, salad, and sandwich preparation; and cheese making and identification.  The class also includes a six week cycle menu during which the students will practice the knife skills, cooking methods, and various cooking techniques learned in FN 180.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
A.  Competencies: A list of cooking competencies will be signed off by the instructor to ensure that each student has performed and learned the assigned task.
B.  A practical cooking examination will follow the cycle menu at the end of the class.  The student will be asked to perform a specific knife cut and specific cooking methods, as well as various other techniques learned during the past two semesters.
