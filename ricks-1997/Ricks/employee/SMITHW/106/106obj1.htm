Unit #1 - CHEMICAL THERMODYNAMICS

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Obtain DH (or DE) for a reaction from appropriate calorimetric data and vice versa.
2. Write a balanced equation for the standard formation of any compound from its elements.
3. Use Hess' Law to calculate DH or DS  or DG  for an overall reaction given data for its intermediate reactions.
4. Calculate DH or DS  or DG for a reaction given the data in Appendix K.
5. Calculate DG at temperatures other than 298K using the formula DH - TDS .
6. State in your own words the three laws of thermodynamics.
7. Explain, in terms of enthalpy, entropy, and Gibbs' free energy, the driving forces of a chemical reaction.
8. Determine qualitatively which of two given systems has the higher entropy.
