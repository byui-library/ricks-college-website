Ricks College News Release
March 13, 2001

Stephen G. Hanks

Boise man to be honored
as Business Leader of the Year

REXBURG, Idaho — Boise resident and Rexburg native Stephen G. Hanks, president of Washington Group International (formerly Morrison Knudsen Corporation), will be honored as Business Leader of the Year during Ricks College’s annual Business Summit Thursday, March 22.

The Boise resident will speak at a forum in the Taylor Chapel at 2 p.m. The address will be carried live by KRIC-FM. Immediately following the forum he will participate in a question/answer session.

Hanks, a 1968 graduate of Madison High School in Rexburg, attended Ricks College and received a bachelor’s degree in accounting from Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. He received an MBA from the University of Utah and a law degree from the University of Idaho. He is a member of the American Bar Association, Idaho State Bar Association and American Institute of Certified Public Accountants.

He is a 23-year veteran of Washington Group International, Inc. He joined the company in 1978 as an attorney in the legal department. In 1991, Hanks was promoted to vice president and corporate secretary and was named senior vice president and general counsel in 1992.

He is a member of the board of St. Alphonsus Regional Medical Center Foundation; the Danny Thompson Memorial Leukemia Foundation; and the Ore-Ida Council of the Boy Scouts of America. He has served as a board member and president of the Boise Public Schools Education Foundation and the University of Idaho College of Business and Economics Advisory Board.

He has been active in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, serving as a stake executive secretary, stake mission president and stake high councilor. He currently serves as the high priest group leader in his ward.

Hanks and wife, Debra, are the parents of four children.

The summit will also feature a full-day schedule of events. The day will start at 8:30 a.m. when business leaders and seminar speakers will hold a panel discussion.
