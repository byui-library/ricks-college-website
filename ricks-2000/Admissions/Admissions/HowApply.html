HOW DO I APPLY?

BYU-Idaho, BYU, BYU-Hawaii and LDS Business College use a common application. The online application is available at www.ldsces.edu. Use the Application Status link to the left to view which parts of the application have been received. The Admissions Decision link can be used to view what decision BYU-Idaho has made regarding your application.


Majors Requiring Additional Application

In addition to being admitted to BYU-Idaho through the Admissions Office, the following majors require that an additional application be submitted directly to the department. Please contact the individual department for an application and more information.

  • Nursing (208) 496-1325
  • Paramedicine (208) 496-1333
  • Interior Design (208) 496-1340
  • Culinary Arts (208) 496-1340
