Kilgore
                                                                                            audio
Near Kilgore,
Flying low to the ground,
I rounded a corner
And knew I had come home.
 
Coasting to a stop,
I cut my motor,
stood serenely,
And listened
To the dew-drenched meadow.
 
Radiant blue hung high
In a cloudless sky
Where two hawks soared
O'er the gray, weathered boards
of a fallen barn.

Kicking their heels,
Horses pounded
The ground.
Their rhythmic hooves
Sounded like drums
As their mane and tails
Flowed like flags.
 
Never wanting
Another meeting,
I thought,
"I could forever stay
In this out-of-the-way spot."
 
This day was for picking fruit
From Camus Creek,
So I rolled northwest
To the best spot for fishing.
 
After a few miles and turns,
After passing
Through barb-wire gates,
I snaked my way
Through willows and brush
To some lush, open water.
 
A brook trout
Beat his friends to my bait.
Soon, six more late fish
Wiggled their way into my creel.
 

Here life blended
Like a German-chocolate cake:
A leisure sky,
A motorcycle on which to fly,
Meandering horses and cattle,
Birds chirping like women in love,
Salted-and-peppered trout
Sizzling in yellow butter.

Oh, busy riders
Of the city range,
Take your turn.
Find your place
Where you too can forget
The "getting and spending" race.

Ah, Kilgore.
