STUDENT HEALTH CARE SERVICES

Ricks College maintains a modern Health Care Center located in the John L. Clarke Building. This facility is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 to 5:00, and help is available for after-hours emergencies. The Medical Center is equipped to treat day-to-day health needs, medical referrals and emergencies. It has a full-time staff, including three medical doctors, physician assistant, nurses, and lab and x-ray technicians.

The Center includes examination rooms, outpatient surgery and a well-supplied pharmacy with most prescription medications and many over-the-counter medications.
A van is maintained on campus for emergency transportation, and Madison Memorial Hospital is only minutes away. Most medical specialties are available either in Rexburg or nearby Idaho Falls, just 30 minutes away.

All students attending Ricks College must have hospital and accident insurance. Those who cannot show proof of coverage from another source must buy Ricks College insurance. Details are provided each student at the time of registration.
