August 17, 2001

BYU-Idaho athletic teams 

to remain Ricks College Vikings

    
REXBURG, Idaho – The school may be known as Brigham Young University-Idaho, but its athletic teams will continue to be known as the Ricks College Vikings during their last season of play.

Athletic Director Garth Hall says BYU-Idaho will retain the Ricks College Viking name as a tribute to the college, coaches and players that produced successful athletic teams.

He says, "Student athletes and coaches felt it was important to retain the name for one last year. They wanted to compete as Ricks College."

Besides the emotional attachment to Ricks, it would have been expensive to change names on uniforms for one final year of play, Hall adds.

The football season will kick off Sept. 1 at 7 p.m. at Viking Stadium against Pima Community College from Tucson, Ariz. The volleyball season will begin Sept. 5 at home at 7 p.m. against Colorado Northwestern Community College.

A gala evening of paying tribute to athletics at Ricks College is planned for Friday, March 1, in the Hart Auditorium. Each sport will be honored and current and former athletes, coaches, administrators and staff will be recognized for their contributions. There will be awards and entertainment and the general public will be invited to participate.

"March 1 of next year will be an evening you won’t forget," Hall says. "We’ll honor each sport that has been a part of Ricks College athletics. We’ll reminisce, share stories and pictures from the past . . . in short, it will be a gala night to enjoy."

Additionally, as each sport concludes competition, former athletes and coaches will be invited to return to campus and will be recognized at the last home game or meet.

Volleyball: The final women’s volleyball game will be Oct. 20 against Snow College.

Football: The final home football game will be Oct. 27 against Arizona Western College.

Cross Country: The regional finals in cross country will be Oct. 27 in Rexburg and former cross country runners will be invited to return to campus. Additionally, track and field athletes will be invited to participate.

Wrestling: The regional wrestling tourney Feb. 1 will bring former wrestlers to campus.

Basketball: Both men’s and women’s basketball will be celebrated with the final home games March 2.

Softball: The final women’s fast-pitch softball game will be April 20.

Baseball: The final men’s baseball game will take place May 4.
