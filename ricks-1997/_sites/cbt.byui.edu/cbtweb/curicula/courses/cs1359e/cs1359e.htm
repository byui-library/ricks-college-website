Cisco IMCR: Installing and Maintaining the 12000 Series II

General Information

Course Duration: 3 hours

Audience: Technicians, support analysts, and maintenance managers with responsibility for installation, troubleshooting, and maintenance of Cisco router products

Prerequisites: The Cisco IOS 11.3: ICRC curriculum and the courses Cisco IMCR: Fundamentals of Cisco Router Configuration and Cisco IMCR: Installation and Maintenance Procedures; a working knowledge of basic networking concepts, terminology, standards, and network components

Topics Covered

  • Introduction to the 12004
  • Power supplies and distribution
  • Cooling and management
  • 12000 series line cards
  • Startup problems
  • Subsystem troubleshooting
  • Running diagnostics

Course Aim

To provide an overview of the Cisco 12004 and the 12000 series line cards, and to outline the troubleshooting procedures for the 12000 series


Learning Objectives

After taking this course, the user should be able to
  • identify the features and components of the Cisco 12004 router

  • discuss the chassis installation procedures for the 12004

  • describe the components of the 12000 series POS and ATM line cards

  • interpret the various line-card LEDs

  • identify the 12000 series subsystems

  • troubleshoot the 12000 series by subsystem
