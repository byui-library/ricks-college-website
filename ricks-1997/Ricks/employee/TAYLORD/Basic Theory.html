Course Information
COURSE DESCRIPTION: An online course designed for the prospective music major covering theory concepts such as rhythm, keys, scales, intervals, triads, and seventh chords.

OBJECTIVES: Successful completion of this course prepares the student for the Music Theory entrance exam required of all potential music majors.
REQUIREMENTS: This course is pass-fail with the minimum passing grade of 90%. Students desirng to take this course must have access to a computer with available internet connection.
