Ricks College News Release
May 30, 2001

Ricks to pay tribute to athletics 

during final year

    
REXBURG, Idaho – The final season of intercollegiate athletics at Ricks College starting this fall promises to be one of spirited action as well as one of reflection and tribute to former athletes and coaches, says athletic director Garth Hall.

Hall, recently appointed vice president of advancement at the college, will continue as athletic director in addition to his new assignment.

By the start of the final athletic season in early September, Ricks College will be known as BYU-Idaho, and will have changed to a four-year institution offering bachelor’s degrees. It will continue, however, to compete athletically against two-year junior colleges for one final season.

A gala evening of paying tribute to athletics at Ricks College is planned for Friday, March 1, in the Hart Auditorium. Each sport will be honored and current and former athletes, coaches, administrators and staff will be recognized for their contributions. There will be awards and entertainment and the general public will be invited to participate.

Additionally, as each sport concludes competition, former athletes and coaches will be invited to return to campus and will be recognized at the last home game or meet. The final women’s volleyball game on Oct. 20 against Snow College will kick off the celebrations.

The final home football game will be a week later on Oct. 27 against Arizona Western College. That same day the regional finals in cross country will be held in Rexburg and former cross country runners will be invited to return to campus. Additionally, track and field athletes will be invited to participate.

The regional wrestling tourney on Feb. 1 will bring former wrestlers to campus, and both men’s and women’s basketball will be celebrated with the final home games March 2. The final women’s fast-pitch softball game will be held April 20, and the final baseball game will take place May 4.

"March 1 of next year will be an evening you won’t forget," Hall says. "We’ll honor each sport that has been a part of Ricks College athletics. We’ll reminisce, share stories and pictures from the past . . . in short, it will be a gala night to enjoy."
