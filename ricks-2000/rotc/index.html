Military Science classes are elective courses that use a combination of academics and hands-on training to teach students leadership, responsibility, and teamwork.   These classes add management training, practical experience, and personal growth opportunities to a student's college education. 
 
First and second-year course are open to any college student with no military obligation required.
 
This program is a partnership program of the Military Science Department at Boise State University .  For additional information about Military Science you can go to Military Science
