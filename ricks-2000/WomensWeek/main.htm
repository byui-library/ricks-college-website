BYU-I
Mothers' Weekend
"Stand Ye in Holy Places"
March 21-24, 2002

Mothers' Weekend, sponsored by the Associated Women Students, is an annual event held at BYU-I where students' mothers are invited to visit campus. The purpose of the Mothers' Weekend is to provide an opportunity for students to strengthen relationships with their mothers by experiencing a weekend of college life together.
