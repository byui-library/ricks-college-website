COST

Cost of $345 includes tuition fees, transportation to and from airport to YFE location, housing, food, field trips, T-shirt,  and Temple trip. Enrollment is limited to 35-40 applicants per session. 

 

The program is recommended for youth who have attended at least one session of EFY, AFY or OEFY and are between the ages of 16 to 19.

 

Optional BYU-Idaho Religion 190 credit is available at an additional cost of $104.  To register  for the Religion credit call (208) 496-2339.

 

REFUND POLICY

No refunds will be granted after a session begins.  There will be a $25 processing fee for each refund.  A $100 processing fee will be withheld from refunds made within two weeks of the start of the program.

 

SESSION CHANGE FEE

There will be a $25 fee assessed for changing sessions or programs.
