Course Description:  Through communication we discover and rediscover who we are. Each person is born with distinctive qualities and unlimited potential. Each has a "voice" to contribute. Through communication, we relay our unique "voices" or identities to others.  Unfortunately, many people who yearn to communicate, struggle and often fail to do so.  Speech pathologists and audiologists are people who, with unique perspective and understanding, devote themselves to serving individuals with "unheard voices."  The goal of this course is for you to begin to think like a speech pathologist / audiologist.   In order to accomplish this goal, you will be responsible for the learning and sacrifice that necessarily accompany new ways of thinking. To support you, the course will be divided into three units. In each unit, we will explore one of the following questions:  (1) What exactly is a speech pathologist and an audiologist?  (2) What is normal communication?   (3) Who is the "communicatively disordered" individual?   By answering these questions and exploring the issues related to them, we will build a foundation upon which you can build your knowledge. But whether you choose to continue in this major or not, thinking like a speech pathologist / audiologist can help you to understand, value, and serve all people as communicators.
