WHO DETERMINES MORALS?

Kant —  Morality Through Moral Laws

          What determines moral law?

          1. moral law exists in Nature; some laws are just right. You don’t have to argue them.

          2. Good Will is when we have a choice and choose the right.

          His Conclusion:  The Categorical Imperative

          “Always act so that your action could be a universal law.” (Do it because it’s right.) Act so that anything you do could be taken as a law for all men.

          a. Your action must always be the same/consistent.

          b. Your action must be reversible. (Golden Rule)

This leads him to 4 conclusions:

          1. the “ends” principle.  Always treat others as ends, not means.

          2. always act as if you are free, and that what you do and say will make a difference.

          3. autonomy.  You must agree to let others act as you do. (So you keep your nose clean and do only good actions.)

          4. unconditional worth. Because each man acts morally he will feel good about himself, will have dignity.

If we all acted this way society would be perfect.
