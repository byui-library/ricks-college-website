Ricks College News Release
May 2, 2001

Michael McLean to perform
as part of Education Week concert

REXBURG, ID – Michael McLean and his musical friends will perform at Ricks College Thursday, June 7, at 7:30 p.m. as part of Education Week activities.

The concert is open to the public and concert-goers do not need to be registered for Education week to attend. Tickets are $8 and $6 and currently on sale at the Ricks College Ticket Office at 356-2230.

McLean is known as a performer, composer, songwriter, producer and director, with a list of accomplishments reflecting well in each of those areas. 

He has written music and lyrics for 21 albums, most of which have been released by Deseret Book. They include “Stay with Me,” “You’re Not Alone,” “Celebrating the Light,” “A New Kind of Love Song,” “One Heart in the Right Place,” “You’ve Always Been There for Me,” “Father and Son,” and the best-selling “The Forgotten Carols.” For the past nine Christmas seasons, he has performed The Forgotten Carols to sold-out audiences throughout the western United States. 

McLean was writer, producer and director of “Nora’s Christmas Gift” starring Celeste Holm and “Mr. Krueger’s Christmas” with Jimmy Stewart. Other well-known works include “Together Forever,” “A Labor of Love,” “What Is Real?” and “The Prodigal Son.”

He has written and directed films for Bonneville International where he was active in the creation of the award-winning Home Front television commercial series. During his 17 years with Bonneville, he received the prestigious Cleo Award and Addy award as well as the Bronze Lion at the Cannes Film Festival.

In September 2000 his stage musical “The Ark,” written with Kevin Kelly, was featured in the Festival of New Musicals on Broadway in New York City. Participation in the festival is by invitation only. 

His 1999 album, “Safe Harbors,” served as the inspiration for the “Safe Harbors – Service under Cover” project to benefit the Travelers Aid Society of Utah. Several hundred blankets were collected, along with a cash donation, during the album’s debut weekend in support of the society’s winter shelter program.

His latest album from Shadow Mountain/Deseret Book, “Michael Sings McLean,” the first recording on which he sings, was released Sept. 19.

Michael and his wife, Lynn, have three children. They live in Heber City, Utah.
