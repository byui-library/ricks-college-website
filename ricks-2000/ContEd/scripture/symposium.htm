Keynote Speaker 9 a.m.

Taylor Chapel

Brother Robert L. Millet "Creating a Covenant Community"  This presentation will focus on the nature of covenant, what it means to be a people of covenant, and the proximate and ultimate blessings that flow from loyalty and devotion to the covenant.

 

Robert L. Millet is Richard L. Evans Professor of Religious Understanding at Brigham Young University.  Before joining the BYU faculty in 1983, he worked with LDS Social Services as a marriage and family counselor and with the Church Educational System as a seminary instructor, institute director, and teaching support consultant.  Brother Millet received his bachelors and masters degrees from BYU in Psychology and his Ph.D. from Florida State University in Religious Studies and Psychology.

 

Since being at BYU he has served as chairman of the department of Ancient Scripture, dean of Religious Education, and director of the Religious Studies Center.  He is the author of many books and numerous articles, dealing mostly with the doctrine and history of the Church.

 

In the Church, Brother Millet has served as high councilor, bishop, stake president, and a member of the Church Material Evaluation Committee.  He and his wife Shauna are the parents of six children and reside in Orem, Utah.

Gerald E. Hansen, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

"Pleasant Memories of the ‘Land of Jerusalem’"

Douglas S. Ladle, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

"The Fall of Adam and Eve: Insights from Latter-day Scriptures and Prophets" What conditions existed in the Garden before the Fall? How were the doctrines of agency and accountability part of the conditions necessary for the Fall? What roles did each participant play in the Fall?  Top of Page

 

Robert Marrott, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

"Hinge-point in Scriptural History: The Two Ways: Moses’ Instructions to Joshua for the House of Israel" - (morning) The promises and warning of a prophet of God set before the people and the choice they made, and the effects of their choice on the doctrine of the scattering and gathering. The symbolism of "north".

"The Sons of Levi" (afternoon) An examination of D&C 13 in conjunction with other sections in the Doctrine and Covenants and teaching of Joseph Smith relative to the offering of the sons of Levi.

 

D. Kelly Ogden, Faculty, BYU-Provo

"LDS Concept of Messiah" (morning) Definition, purpose, and prophetic foreshadowings of the Messiah. How Jesus was the Messiah and different reactions to Jesus as the Messiah.

"Jerusalem, the Eternal City" (afternoon) A carefully documented study of Jerusalem from 2, 000 BC to AD 2,000. From Melchizedek to the present, forty centuries of the greatest events in the history of the world.

Gregory G. Palmer, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

Gary Purse, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

Lon Pyper, Faculty, BYU-Idaho

Joseph B. Romney, Faculty, BYU-Idaho
