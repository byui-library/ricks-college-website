New Student Journal:

A reflective journal  is used as a way for students to reflect on individual learning that takes place from course assignments, activities, and experiences associated with coming to BYU-I. These learning experiences can focus on the insights students have gained from taking the course, or other experiences outside the class which may have a correlation to the topic of discussion.  

Reflective journals are typically spontaneous accounts of one’s thoughts, feelings, ideas, and concerns. The most frequent mistake that students make is having the reflective journal become a log of completed activities rather than a consideration of the lessons learned. In order to avoid this problem, students should use the following guidelines when writing a reflective journal:

Consistent - Journal entries should be made for each assignment and always dated. Typically one-page entries are appropriate

Thoughtful - Journal entries consist of thoughts, feelings, and insights from the experience. Students should describe what they are learning or have learned. Sometimes, it is helpful to identify a "critical incident" that occurs during these learning experiences, and students would be asked to reflect upon what they have learned as a result of that incident.

Your New Student Journal - Students' journal entries are to be done offline, using your own notebook, binder, and/or paper.  This journal is to be submitted to room 144 of Rigby Hall by the third Friday, after school has started.
