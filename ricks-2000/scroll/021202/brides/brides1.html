Entertainment at weddings

Elegant

For a more classy styled reception, pianists, string quartets and vocalists are elegant forms of entertainment.

Casual

For a more spunky styled reception, live bands and disc jockeys are casual forms of entertainment.

Music

Music sets the tone for your wedding. Two sets of performers may be required, one for the ceremony and another for the reception. You may, for example, choose a live band, disc jockey, solo singer, pianist/organist, harpist or combination of two or more. Wedding bands usually have audition nights at which you can hear them in person before making your final decision.

Photographers and Videographers

Every wedding experience makes a memory. Such memories should be captured to reflect upon in years to come. Most couples spend their wedding day anxious and nervous and do not have time to capture every special moment on film.

Couples, do not worry. Photographers and videographers are available to help capture such a memorable occasion. Hiring a professional photographer or videographer can make a huge difference.

Tips:

• Decide on the type of photography you want and hire accordingly; book early.
• Do not underbudget. Remember to discuss a payment plan.
• Be aware of available packages.
• Present the photographer or videographer with a list of people to photograph or record.
• Ask to view samples of work.
• Remember to ask to see negatives and proofs.
