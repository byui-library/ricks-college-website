Building an E-business

Assumptions:  You and your group are interested in starting an e-business now--while you are still in school.  After graduation all or some of you will then work full time at the business.

Logistics:  You will need to create a "Web site" where you will deliver the following information to me.  It will most likely be a part of one your Catalog.com sites.  The main page of the site should have, at the top, a listing of all your team members with links to their e-mail.  Each individual in the group should have a link to the groups site on their individual site.

Phase I--Deciding on an E-business

Idea Generation
You should have a 20 minute idea brainstorming session.  Think of all the ideas you possible can and list all the ideas on you teams Web site on a page in in a section entitled Ideas from Brainstorming.  Also, state at the bottom of the page, the number of minutes your teamed actively brainstormed for an idea.  I will grade mainly on the number of quality ideas.  (10 points)

Screening
Do a SWOT analysis to determine which idea to choose.  The strength and weaknesses should be strength and weaknesses of the group members (what do you bring to the business).  The opportunities and threats should be derived from the elements of the business environment.  You should choose the idea that has the greatest opportunities and the least serious threat.

I will grade the screening portion based on the following:

  • Was there a complete list of relevant strengths and weaknesses?
  • Was there a complete list of relevant opportunities and threats?
  • Was there an analysis of what this means your company should do (mention risks)?
  • Was it presented on the Web site in a professional manner?

Label this page or section Screening (use headings).  It will be worth 20 points.

 

Phase II--Main part of the plan

Phase II should address the following:

Primary purpose Define the purpose(s) of your business (it is assumed that one purpose is to make money--don't mention that).  Tell what need you intend to fulfill and what good/services you plan to provide and/or sell.
Target market Who is your primary target market and why?  You can also mention a secondary target market also
Strategy Describe how your product fits into the market.  Tell about its positioning and how you differentiate it from the competitors.
Support Provide any evidence to support your e-business concept.  You may want to cite some secondary marketing research.
Business model(s) Tell which e-business model(s) your e-business will use.  Be very explicit on how you will make money.

You should present this information on a page or section of a page entitled Phase II, and you should use headings.  I will grade this phase on how clear you present each section above, on if they make sense, and on how well you support your thoughts (30 points)

Phase III--Implementation

In this phase you will need to answer the following questions:

  • Where will you get financing?
  • What will your domain name be and why?
  • How will you integrate with suppliers, and, if possible, how would you like to integrate with suppliers (if applicable)?
  • Where will the site be hosted and why?
  • What will the home page look like (do a mock up of it)?
  • Who will design the site?
  • What "technologies" are you doing to need to make it work (mention e-commerce, data bases, asp, etc)?  Create of mock of some of these main pages.
  • How will the site be organized?  Show a flowchart of the site.

You should title this section Phase III.  I will grade this based on whether you completely and concisely answer each question and on how professional the mock-ups look.  This phase will be worth 40 points.
