Project 3:  Detailed Object Creation: Using Materials & Lighting

"And if a person gains more knowledge and intelligence in this life through his diligence and obedience than another, he will have so much the advantage in the world to come.", DC 130:19.

The purpose of this project is to help you gain familiarity with the creation of small, detailed objects commonly found in an office environment and the application of lighting and materials available in 3D Studio Max. Another goal of this assignment is to build a library of mesh models which will be available for your use in the next project.

Listed below are several pieces of office equipment. Please note the point value allowed for each object. Select enough objects such that your point total is a minimum of 20 points. Extra credit may be earned by completing additional objects up to a maximum of 30 points. Point values are assigned based on object complexity.

Models do not have to match the pictures shown below in every detail, but should be comparable in the level of complexity and material textures. Pay special attention to the selection of materials and lighting to make the object look as realistic as possible. The higher the realism, the higher your grade! The best of the models turned in will be put in a library for use on the next project. As an extra incentive, extra credit points will be awarded to those whose models are selected for inclusion in the library.

For each object that you create, include a minimum of 2 jpg files at a resolution of 640x480. Also create 2 versions of the 3D Studio model for each object. One object should include minimal background so you will be able to cast shadows and see the details of your object better. In the other model, simply remove the background objects. If your model is selected for inclusion in the library, this will make merging it into other models much easier. Use descriptive names for the models and renderings to make them easy to identify. Place the renderings and the models of each object inside a folder in the DropBox when complete using the keyword of Office before your name.

If you have another idea for an office item that you do not see listed that you would like to create, talk to Br. Miller to negotiate a point value before proceeding.
