MEN’S BASKETBALL

    Coach: Clyde Nelson (9th season 188-92)  2000-2001   Record:  15-8  / SWAC: 6-6

    THIS WEEK:       

    Thursday, February 14; Ricks hosts Utah Valley, 7:30pm, Hart Gym
    Saturday, February 16; Ricks hosts Salt Lake CC, 7:30pm, Hart Gym

    Dixie State College is back in first place in the SWAC with weekend wins over Southern Idaho and Treasure Valley. CSI was also beaten by Snow College, dropping the Eagles into second place, with Snow College a close third.

    The Vikings moved into a tie for sixth place in the league with a win over visiting North Idaho. Adam Hiatt and Aaron Bowman continue to lead the Vikings in scoring, averaging 17 points per game. Kyle Stevens is the teams leading rebounder. Ricks is outscoring opponents 75-73.

    The Vikings are back home this week for two games, hosting Utah Valley on Thursday, and Salt Lake CC on Saturday. Tip off is set for 7:30 pm.

    Utah Valley is tied with CEU for fourth place in the league after beating CEU and CNCC over the weekend. CNCC came from behind and almost upset Utah Valley, before the Wolverines pulled away in the final minutes. UVSC has a big and balanced attack, led by Seth Scott. The Wolverines are outscoring opponents 91-76.

    Salt Lake is the eighth place team in the SWAC with a 5-8 record after beating CNCC last weekend, but losing to CEU. The Bruins have been struggling since losing to Ricks and North Idaho back in mid-January. They are led by Jeff Blackett.

    RADIO: Radio broadcast will begin at 7:20 PM on KBYI FM 100.5. Bryce Rydalch will carry the play-by-play with Brian Howard anchoring at KBYI Sports.

    THE COACHES: Ricks College is coached by Clyde Nelson. Nelson is in his ninth season as head coach at Ricks. His career mark with the Vikings stands at 188-92 going into this weeks games. Under Nelson, Ricks has won the Region 18 championship once, and participated in the NJCAA Finals in 1997-98.

    Utah Valley is coached by Jeff Reinert who is in his 8th year as head coach of the Wolverines. He is assisted by Quincy Lewis, Ray Stewart, and Derrick Elliot.

    Salt Lake CC is coached by former Viking player Norm Parrish. Parrish is in his 11th year as head coach of the Bruins. He is assisted by Andy Ward, Jose Chavez, and Matt Logan.

    HISTORY: Ricks first played UVSC in 1972-73 when Glen Dalling was head coach. Ricks won the first six games played, and has gone on to lead the series, 38-26, including a loss earlier this season in Orem. Clyde Nelson is 11-8 against UVSC, 5-3 at home against the Wolverines.

    Ricks first played Salt Lake CC in 1986-87 when Gary Gardner was head coach. Ricks won 5 of the first 6 games played and leads the series 20-15, including a win earlier this season in Salt Lake. Clyde Nelson is 10-8 against Salt Lake, 7-1 at home against the Bruins.

    COACH NELSON: "We played well against NIC. Aaron Bowman got back on track. I hope he can stay there. Aaron McKee’s defense was big in the second half. Two more great teams come into our gym this week. Salt Lake is struggling. That scares me. They’re a hungry, wounded bear. Utah Valley dominated us in Orem. We’ve got to improve to compete with their size and talent. We’ve been playing well. We’ve got to keep it up."
