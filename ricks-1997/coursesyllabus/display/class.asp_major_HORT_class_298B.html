Occupational Internship
OCCUP INTERN
CLASS CODE: HORT 298B CREDITS:  1 - 5
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Class preparation for work experience in the florist, landscape or nursery industry. Work experience in the florist, landscape or nursery industry. A comprehensive experience under supervision of the employer and faculty specialist. A florist, nursery or landscape operation is selected whose practices conform closely to the needs and training objectives of the student.
DESCRIPTION: Internship work experience
TOPICS: Based on individual work experience
OBJECTIVES: To gain industry experience
REQUIREMENTS: Horticulture majors, internships must be approved by the supervising faculty.
PREREQUISITES: Hort 298A
