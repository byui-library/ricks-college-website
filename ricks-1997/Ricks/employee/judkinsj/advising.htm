Scholarship Information

The Communication Department frequently has funds set aside for scholarships. Usually, they go to students with a high GPA, who are not receiving other financial assistance, and who are majoring in one of the degrees offered by the Department. 

Go to MC 243, OR call  (208) 356-4929, OR email our department secretary to request an application.

Application deadline for Fall is typically in mid-March.

 

                                  

To Major or Not To Major?

The benefits of graduating in the major are (1) you can tell people something besides "generals" when they ask what your major is, (2) you can take specific classes designed to prepare you for further studies in Speech Pathology & Audiology, and (3) your advisor will be better able to help you with questions about specific classes and transfer schools.

The costs of graduating in the major are (1) Requirements are not easy, and demand hard work & dedication to complete successfully, and (2) if you have chosen this major late in your timeline at Ricks, extra time and money might be required for you to graduate.

In other words, it may be to your advantage to graduate in "Generals" or another major if (1) Taking courses required for graduating in the major brings your GPA down below a 3.0, (2) graduating in the major prevents you from taking specific courses required by the University to which you are transferring, (3) graduating in the major would require extra semesters beyond an associates degree in another major, or (4) you are positive that you do not want to continue studying Speech-language pathology or audiology after you leave Ricks College.

Please feel free to use me in your decision-making process. I will be happy to be a sounding board, and to answer any questions you might have. I encourage you to do your part to make a good decision regarding your major and the classes you should take, and to then have faith that the Lord will direct your paths. I know He loves you and wants you to learn, progress, and find happiness.

 

Program Description
Welcome to the greatest major on campus!  As the advisor of the Communicative Disorders major, my goal is to prepare you for further studies in speech-language pathology and audiology.  As a future professional dedicated to assisting individuals with communication difficulties, I hope you're ready to work hard and learn a  lot!  The requirements for this major are designed not only to enhance your understanding of human communication and its disorders, but also to help you become more effective communicators yourselves.  A 3.0 GPA is required for graduation in this major.

Fast Track Option
