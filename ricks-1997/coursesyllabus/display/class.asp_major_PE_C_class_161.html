Swimming Intermediate
SWIMMING INT
CLASS CODE: PE*C 161 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An intermediate swimming class designed to instruct students beyond the basic swimming skills and strokes.
DESCRIPTION: For swimmers that want stroke improvement. The course teaches the six main strokes with an emphasis on distance and endurance. It includes basic springboard diving, safety, and rescue skills.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. Reach for individual potential by improving knowledge and skill of swimming.
2. Share ideas while working toward common goals in a spirit of unselfishness.
3. Be able to exercise enough to get a cardiovascular work-out and to relieve stress.
4. Knowledge of the terminology, rules, safety practices, and correct fundamentals of intermediate swimming strokes and diving.
5. To gain a testimony of the divine origins and sacredness of our bodies.
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Pass all requirements as outlined in the intermediate course of American Red Cross
2. Show swimming proficienty in scissor kick, flutter kick, ship kick, elementary back stroke, optional butterfly, and underwater swimming.
3. Non-stop swim with a variety of strokes, tread water for 15 minutes, and turns.
4. Pass written exam
5. Pass one mile swim
6. Pass underwater swim 15 feet
7. Perform water rescue procedures (reach, rescue and CPR)
8. Attend class and participate.
9. BYU-I clothing required.
PREREQUISITES: Pass Beginning Swimming class or exhibit basic skills of swimming.
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences allowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
5. P.E. clothing fee: $4.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
