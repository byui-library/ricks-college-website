6. An oath, in ancient times, was more binding than any contract. It was made by using the phrase "as the Lord liveth" or "as I live". Knowing this, please explain why Nephi was so determined to get the brass plates from Laban, even after the first attempt failed (see 1 Nephi 3: 14-15). Also, please explain the use of a covenant when Zoram was about to flee from before Nephi and yet went into the wilderness with Lehi's family (1 Nephi 4: 30-37)

* Nephi used an oath in 1 Nephi 3:14-15 to help his brothers (and maybe even himself) realize that he truly would accomplish the thing which the Lord had commanded him.
When Zoram was about to flee, Nephi held him, promised him, in the name of the Lord, or "as the Lord liveth and as I live" that if he went into the wilderness with them, he would be a free man and have an inheritance with the rest of the family. Zoram, in turn, made and oath to the brothers that he would do so and they didn't have to worry about him after that.
