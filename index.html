Ricks College Website Archive

www.ricks.edu, 1997–2001

Ricks College, 1997 The original Ricks College home page, with academics, admissions, college services, news and student life.
Becoming BYU-Idaho, 2000–2001 The site from the year Ricks College announced it would become Brigham Young University–Idaho, with departmental sites.

This is a restored copy of the Ricks College website (ricks.edu) as it stood between 1997 and 2001, before Ricks College became BYU-Idaho in 2001.

It was rebuilt from a backup of the college’s web server. About 1,060 files missing from the backup were recovered from the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine, and links to other websites open the Wayback Machine’s copy from that time.

Many images and pages were never preserved, so some links and images are broken.
