Computer Science Program

Computer Science is "the study of computers, including their design, operation, and use in processing information. Computer Science combines both theoretical and practical aspects of engineering, electronics, information theory, mathematics, logic, and human behavior. Aspects of Computer Science range from programming and computer architecture to artificial intelligence and robotics." (Microsoft Press Computer and Internet Dictionary) The Computer Science curriculum teaches students how to develop software applications but not how to use applications such as word processors and spreadsheets.

Students graduating with a bachelors degree in Computer Science are prepared to enter the work force and develop applications as a software engineer. These applications impact all aspects of our lives. Students may also choose to continue their education and research some of the many facets of Computer Science. Regardless, Computer Science majors will have a challenging and rewarding career. The Computer Science major provides a solid background in Computer Science that facilitates transfer to a four-year university by providing experience in:

  • algorithm development
  • procedural and object oriented design and programming
  • software engineering practices
  • data structures
  • computer organization
  • theoretical foundations

Associate in Arts and Sciences with a major in Computer Science

The curriculum for the Computer Science major is designed for the student planning to transfer to a four year school following graduation from Ricks. Students should plan their individual curricula based on the requirements of schools to which they plan to transfer. In addition to the General Education requirements, a student graduating with a major in Computer Science is required to complete the following with a grade of "C" or better:

  • CS102, CS170, CS173, CS233, CS235, CS236, and CS246
  • Two of the following math courses: Math 162, Math 209, Math 243, Math 263
  • Two of the following physical science courses: Chem 105, Chem 106, Ph 121, Ph 122
  • Eng 216 (Technical Writing)

Course Descriptions

CS102 Introduction to Computer Science (1)
One hour of lecture per week
(Fall, Winter, and Summer)

Introduction to Computer Science is designed to help a student determine if he/she would like to major in this field. Basic concepts of Computer Science, software engineering, and software development. Review of curricula at Ricks and selected transfer schools.

CS144 Introduction to Programming (3)
Prerequisite: Math 101 or high school equivalent
Three hours of lecture per week
(Fall, Winter, and Summer)

Introduction to programming using an object oriented programming language. Algorithm development, problem solving, testing and debugging, language features, and classes. All programming is in a UNIX environment. For non-Computer Science majors.

CS148 Programming Languages (2)
Prerequisite: CS144 or CS170
Two hours of lecture per week
(Fall and Winter)

Introduction to a selected programming language. Emphasis on learning a new language and not on basic programming concepts.

CS165 Object Oriented Programming (3)
Prerequisite: CS144 and instructor permission
Three to four hours of lecture per week
(Fall, Winter, and Summer)

Software design and development using an object oriented language. Algorithm formulation, an introduction to software engineering, and object oriented programming.

CS170 Introduction to Software Development (4)
Prerequisite: CS102 or CS144, Math 110
Five hours of lecture per week
(Fall, Winter, and Summer)

Introduction to software design and development. Algorithm formulation, introduction to software engineering and structured programming, procedural and object oriented programming.

CS173 Web Programming (2)
Prerequisite: CS144 or CS170
Two hours of lecture per week
(Fall, Winter, and Summer)

Introduction to Web programming in a client-server environment. Web site design, HTML forms, and CGI programming with Perl.

CS233 Introduction to Computer Organization (3)
Prerequisite: CS144 or CS170
Three hours of lecture per week
(Fall only)

Fundamentals of computer organization and operation. Memory structure, registers, arithmetic and logical functions, instruction formats, addressing modes, machine and assembly languages, and internal-external data representation.

CS235 Foundations of Computer Science I (4)
Prerequisites: CS170 and Math 162
Five hours of lecture per week
(Fall only)

Iteration, induction, recursion, lists, trees, sets, relations, functions; mathematical analysis of algorithms and data models; object-oriented implementation of abstract data types.

CS236 Foundations of Computer Science II (4)
Prerequisite: CS235
Five hours of lecture per week
(Winter only)

Continuation of CS 235; relations, graphs, automata, grammars, propositional and predicate logic. Implementation of object-oriented algorithms.

CS246 Advanced Object Oriented Programming (3)
Prerequisite: CS235
Three hours of lecture per week
(Winter only)

Continuation of study of object oriented programming. Advanced software development and object oriented design. Code larger programs. Utilizes the Java language.

CS290 Special Topics 1-3
Prerequisite: consent of instructor

Faculty/student consultation determines an area of research study beyond the subject matter covered in standard courses. Content and credit are approved by the instructor.
