THIRD PLACE FINISH NATIONALLY

REXBURG, ID -The Ricks College wrestling team rolled to a third place finish at the NJCAA finals in Bismarck, ND, over the weekend. It's the second highest finish ever for Ricks wrestling at the national meet. Seven of eight Vikings wrestlers scored team points, while five of those eight attained All-American status. 118 pounder Shawn Jensen of West Jordan, UT, led Ricks with a second place finish. He won four matches before being losing by decision in the finals. At 134 pounds, Jeff Ireland of Martin, SD, won 5 out of seven matches to finish fifth in the nation. Shane Wasden of Sugar City, ID, matched Ireland with 5 wins on his way to All-American status in the 167 pound class. Rexburg's Jed Zirker repeated as an All-American at 190 pounds with four wins in six matches. The fifth All American is Dave Anderton of Blackfoot, ID. The 275 pounder came home in third place in the heavyweight division. He won four matches, three of them by pins. Wild card entrants Steve Gulbrandson (150) and Darren Phillipy (177) also contributed vital points for the Vikings.

The two day meet saw Ricks stand in ninth place after the first day of competition. It was in Saturday's 'wrestle-backs' that the Vikings fought back to move from ninth to third in the final standings, behind National Champion Lassen College of California, and runner-up North Idaho.

Coach Bob Christensen was thrilled with his teams performance, especially on the final day. "The kids were motivated to finish strong. They're conditioning played a major roll on the final day. Some of our kids wrestled seven times, and were noticeably stronger down the stretch than their opponents. I'm really happy. We did very well." #
