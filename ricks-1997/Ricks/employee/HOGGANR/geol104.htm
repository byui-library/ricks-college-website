Purpose:
This course is designated for non-science majors and fills the physical science general education requirement.  It's purpose is to help students become aware of their relationship with the geologic environment and covers such topics as:  geologic hazards (earthquakes, volcanoes, landslides, floods, etc.), mineral and energy resources, land use planning, waste management, and other pertinent topics.  It is expected that this course would prepare students to be better informed citizens in making important economic, social, and political decision that affect their lives.
