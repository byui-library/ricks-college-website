All registered students are required to provide local address information. The local address is a Rexburg address (or within driving distance of the university).

A student can notify the Registrar's Office of their new local address information by one of the following three ways:
     1. Go to the BYU-Idaho home page (www.byui.edu), click on the Registration link
         at the bottom of the page (www.byui.edu/spencer/LOCAL/WARN.htm). Then
         after going through the warning page and entering ID and PIN, click on the Local
         address update link.
     2. Call the registrar's office at (208) 496-1003.
     3. Send an e-mail message to orrr@byui.edu

If the local address information is not provided by the first day of class, a restriction is activated which prohibits all registration activity. The restriction is removed when the local address is entered. It is also important to notify the Registrar's Office if there are address changes during the school year.
