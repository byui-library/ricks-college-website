LORI WOODLAND
    BrighamYoung University
    Fourteenth Season (2001-2002)

       Lori Woodland returned as Head Coach of Lady Viking Basketball in 1997-98.  Without missing a beat, Woodland roared through the season with a school record-tying 33-3 season.  The Lady Vikes won the SWAC regular season crown, the Region 18 tournament, and finished fifth at the NJCAA tournament, led by first team All-American Cady Williams.  For all that, Woodland was named Region 18 Coach of the Year.  She earned that honor again last season as the Lady Vikings finished second in the SWAC, won the Region 18 tournament, and went on to finish eighth at the NJCAA tournament.  In the just concluded thirteenth  season as head coach of Lady Viking basketball, Woodland was once again honored as Region 18 Coach of the Year.

         In thirteen seasons, Woodlands' Lady Vikings have won 279 and lost 114, a winning percentage of 71%.  1993 was a banner year for Woodland and Lady Viking basketball.  That season, Ricks posted a 29-4 record and won the Region 18 championship, qualifying for the NJCAA tournament.  At the nationals in Tyler, Texas, Ricks took third place, the best national finish ever recorded by a women's team in Region 18 history.   Woodland was voted the 1993 Region 18 Coach of the Year and was chosen by her peers as the "Coaches Coach of the Year".
     
          Coach Woodland was born and raised in Winnemucca, NV, where she graduated from Lowry High School in 1970.  After graduation she entered school at the University of Nevada-Reno where she earned her bachelor's degree in 1975.  While at UNR, Lori played basketball for the Lady Wolf Pack for four years and volleyball and softball as a freshman. She returned to Winnemucca after her graduation to coach basketball and teach physical education at her alma mater.  In five seasons as Lowry's head coach, Lori's teams posted a 65-15 overall record and won two state championships.  She was voted Nevada AA Coach of the Year twice.
     
        In 1980, Lori received her master's degree from BYU.  From there she went on to perform sports information duties at the College of Eastern Utah in Price.  Coach Woodland and her husband Richard live in Rexburg.
