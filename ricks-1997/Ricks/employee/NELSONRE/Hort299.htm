Instructor: Reese Nelson

Office: ETB 263

Phone: 356-2014

Lecture: Tuesday 1:00 to 1:50, Taylor 120

Purpose:

Become acquainted with horticulture careers, work opportunities, businesses and fields of interest.

Procedures:

The course will consist mainly of a series of lectures by guest speakers from the horticulture industry and faculty in the landscape horticulture and related departments. A form like the one attached will be provided to prepare a one page abstract for each speaker. Speakers will occasionally be scheduled at times other than normal class time.

top

Assignments

One page hand written abstract or summary of at least 8 speakers on the form provided. This will normally be turned in at the end of each presentation.

No exams will be given
