NETWORK DESIGN
CLASS CODE: IS 340 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: INFORMATION SYSTEMS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Principles of network design standards and architectures. Configuration and use of networking devices including repeaters, hubs, bridges, switches and routers to create enterprise networks.
DESCRIPTION: Principles of network design standards and architectures. Configuration and use of networking devices including repeaters, hubs, bridges, switches and routers to create enterprise networks.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: At the conclusion of this course the students should understand: Principles of good network design. Advantages and disadvantages of various networking topologies, architectures and protocols. How to create a working enterprise network.
