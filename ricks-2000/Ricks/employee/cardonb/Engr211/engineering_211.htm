 ENGINEERING 211

This is an elementary surveying course that will introduce the student to the theory and practice of plane surveying. We will measure angles, distances, vertical elevations in the labs. We will make a record of survey drawing(plot plan) that includes a property description and basic topography of the survey area. In addition we will study the rectangular system of surveys,and the theory of errors.  This course is designed for majors in civil engineering, land surveying, architecture, and building construction. A lab is required in this course.
