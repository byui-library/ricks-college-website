One Time Service Programs

The Institute helps sponsor a number of single or "stand alone" service activities in which students can participate. A brief description outlines these activities below. More specific details can be gathered by contacting the program director or by visiting our office


Serving with Smiles

All students are welcome to assist with a monthly service project sponsored by Serving with Smiles. Previous projects have included a highway clean-up, helping elderly with yard work, and a trip to the welfare cannery.
Program Co-Directors:
Julie Wilding, Email: wil99021@ricks.edu
Jeremy Field, Email: fie97004@ricks.edu

Red Cross Blood Drive

Help save a life? Donors are needed to give blood at periodic drives held by the Red Cross. You can sign up at the Manwaring Center information desk one week prior to the upcoming drives
Program Director: Kory Godfrey
Email: god97004@ricks.edu.

Zion’s Camp

These overnight training experiences at the Teton Lodge involve a group of around twenty students in planning and performing a service project. (Click here to find out more)

We hope that these selections will offer you a positive service experience. As you serve others, you will not only help them, but will gain valuable skills and grow in your understanding of yourself and the community.

While Ricks College encourages its students to perform community service, it does not endorse or investigate the listed providers of service opportunities that are not directly affiliated with the college. Rather, it is up to the student to determine for himself or herself as to whether or not to volunteer service through these listed community agencies or campus organizations. As a result, Ricks College does not take responsibility for the acts or omissions of either the service providers (not supervised by Ricks) or of the student volunteers.

 Ongoing Volunteer Programs

The Institute has the following programs to involve students in service on a weekly basis with local community agencies. A brief description of each activity and the type of commitment involved outlined below.

If you would like to participate in one of these programs or need more information contact the student program director through email or visit our office.

Adopt-A-Grandparent

Elderly residents in the community would love groups of students to come visit on a regular basis. This program matches groups of students with "grandparents" and sponsors monthly group activities.
Commitment: 2-3 hrs./wk.
Program Director: Sara Thomson
Email: tho99032@ricks.edu

Big Brothers/Big Sisters

Children (6-14 yrs old) from single-parent homes need an adult mentor--someone to spend time with--someone to have fun with playing sports, going to movies, doing chores, etc.
Commitment: 2-3 hrs./wk. all year
Program Director: Amanda Crane
Email: cra99006@ricks.edu

Family Crisis Center

Make a difference in the life of a child. Volunteers are needed to help participate in group activities with children to teach self-esteem and teamwork while their parents participate in Thursday night classes
Commitment:.1-2 hrs./wk.
Program Director: Daylene West
Email: wes99004@ricks.edu

Friendship/Homework Tutoring

Tutors needed to assist school children that have difficulty with basic reading and writing skills. Training and transportation to the schools during the day are provided. Sign up at the beginning of the semester.
Commitment: 1-4 hrs/wk.
Friendship Program Director: Kimberly Tietjen
Email: kymberly00@hotmail.com
Homework Program Director: Heather Henkel
Email: hen99010@ricks.edu

Humanitarian Service Organization

Twice a month students meet to perform humanitarian service projects in conjuction with the Church Humanitarian Services through Deseret Industries. An outreach program permits students who attend these projects to take these projects to other campus groups.
Commitment: 2 hrs/ every other wk.
Program Director: Adrienne Paulsen
Email: Sbpap@aol.com

Merit Badge Pow Wow

Counselors are needed to teach merit badge classes to boy scouts on three consecutive Saturday mornings (October 21st, & 28th) Instructors needed for various topics to teach young men ages 12-18.
Commitment: 2 Saturday mornings.
Program Director: Jacob Sumsion
Email: sum990009@ricks.edu


Radio Reading Service

Sight impaired individuals would like to have newspaper articles read to them over a special radio channel. Volunteers sign-up to read local newspapers during the evening hours.
Commitment: 1/2 hr. each week
Program Director: Kyle Brown
Email: bro99012@ricks.edu

Rexburg Nursing Center

Volunteers need to bring cheer and hope to some of Heavenly Father’s "more experienced" children. Visiting, helping with arts & crafts, and playing the piano (or other instruments) would be appreciated.
Commitment: 1hr./wk.
Program Director: Kristin Jones
Email: jon99022@ricks.edu

Special Olympics

Athletic events for mentally challenged athletes are sponsored by this program. Volunteers are needed to assist with events at periodic large games and with weekly leagues and planning.
Commitment: varies
Volunteer Coordinator: Charla Dietzel
Email: die99004@ricks.edu

Tator Tots

Students assist in reading stories and planning activities for preschool children at the Madison County Library. The program is held Monday mornings between 10-11 a.m.
Commitment: 1 hr./wk.
Program Director: Brenda Bates
Email: bat99003@ricks.edu
