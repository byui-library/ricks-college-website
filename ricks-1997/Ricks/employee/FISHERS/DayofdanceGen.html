Day of Dance information

Day of Dance is an event held on campus each semester. Instructors are invited to campus to teach' often times new dances, and share their skills and love of dance.

The day consists of 6 hours of workshops and an evening dance. The cost usually is around $15.00 for the entire day.

About 450 Students attend. Many come from nothern Utah as well as the Boise area.
