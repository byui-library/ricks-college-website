COURSE

OSM 220, Business Communication, is a three-credit course which meets twice a week for 75 minutes. This course does transfer to other universities and serves as GE Credit for the 200 level English course for some majors. This course provides students the opportunity to improve communication skills through analyzing and composing business letters, memos, and reports; public speaking, creating a job application cover letter and resume, interviewing, and listening. PREREQUISITE for OSM majors--Successful completion of OSM 106.
