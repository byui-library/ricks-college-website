GRADES

Your grade will be determined by 2 criteria. If at the end of the semester (or block) you would like to challenge the grade earned by these 2 criteria, this will be an option, provided you meet the requirements stated below.

1. Attendance. Roll will be taken at the beginning of each class. If you arrive late it will be your responsibility to have my roll corrected on that day.(not later) Campus or excuses given by me, must be taken care of previous to the day missed. Trying to get absences excused at the end of the semester or block will not work. I reserve the right to mark anyone absent who is not attentive in class. You have the right to give a rebuttal to this absence. It must be done in writing. All absences will be marked on the roll, and can be checked at the beginning of class or end of class each day!

0-2 abs. = A, ,3-4 abs. = B, ,5-6 abs. = C, ,7-8 abs. = D,, 9 or more abs. = F

2. Term Writing You will find a listing of terms by course number on this web page. They are listed in the order they will be discussed in class, and for the most part as they appear in the scriptural text. You are to write on every term. Terms papers are to be e-mailed  to my office. Handwritten papers will not be accepted!

An "A" for the Term writing assignment will include 100% of the terms. This  will include all the major points discussed in class, in the text, in the Bible Dictionary, and for Doctrine and Covenants Students in the History Of The Church.

To Receive an  "A"  you must send to me a minimum of 15 Terms each week of class. Please send them via e-mail.  Remember, at least 20 terms each week of the semester, block or term or you cannot receive an "A" in the class

A "B" for the term writing assignment will include 80% to 99% of the terms. Of the  randomly selected terms, most of the major points will be given, but not all.

A "C" for the term writing assignment will include 60% to 79% of the terms. Of the  randomly selected terms, they will be 1 to 2 lines statements found in most dictionaries/commentaries.

A "D" for the terms writing assignment will include 50% to 59% of the terms. Of the  randomly selected terms, they will be mostly 1 or 2 line statements.

An "F" will be given for effort less that the above.

COPIES OF PAST STUDENT TERMS PAPERS CAN BE SEEN IN MY OFFICE DURING MY OFFICE HOURS. THIS WILL GIVE YOU AN IDEA OF WHAT AN A PAPER IS LIKE.

The grades of these two criteria will then be combined to reflect the final grade!

A,A=A A,B=B+ A,C= C+ A,D=D+ A,F=D-

B,B=B B,C,=C+ B,D=D+ B,F=D-

C,C=C C,D=D+ C,F=F

D,D=D D,F=F

IMPORTANT NOTE******************************

If you desire to challenge the grade earned by this criteria, you may challenge this grade by a test. The test can be taken for block students, the Friday before the final day of class. For Semester classes, you may take the test 2 Fridays before the final day of class. In order to qualify for this test your terms papers must be turned in the Monday before the test.  The Test is not an option for someone that has not completed any terms.
