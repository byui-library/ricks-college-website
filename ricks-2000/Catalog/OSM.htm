Office Systems Management

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

OSM 272 Office Equipment 3
Prerequisite: OSM 106, OSM 203, OSM 231
The development of speed and accuracy in the transcription of documents in the fields of legal, medical, travel, small business, etc. The development of speed and accuracy using the ten-key calculator and other office machines. (F)

OSM 273 Records Management 3
An overview of records systems and basic files maintenance. (F)

OSM 276 Executive Office Procedures and Systems 3
 Designed to prepare the student for job responsibilities requiring specific skills, understanding and knowledge, motivation and initiative. Areas covered are making travel, meeting, and conference arrangements; mail handling; administrative support; office etiquette; and career planning. (W)
