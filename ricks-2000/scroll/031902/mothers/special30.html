Pantyhose pull their weight around the house

by Sarah Lunt
LUN99013@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

A rite of passage into the women’s world is that first pair of pantyhose.

These leg-enhancing wonders cost a few dollars and maybe last a few days. However, there are ways to prolong the life of pantyhose and then recycle them after they are not fit to be worn.

After bringing home a pair, wet them down, wring them dry and put them in a plastic bag in the freezer. After they are frozen, remove them from the plastic bag and allow them to thaw. Then let them air dry, according to www.spots.ab.ca/~ics/hse.html.

With pantyhose mishaps, such as a small run in the toe, paint clear nail polish on it to prevent further running.

Once the runs finally take over the pair of pantyhose, there are several ways of recycling this remarkable product.

Clean hose can be used as breathable bags. These are excellent for a number of things. One might put cinnamon cloves in these bags and place the bag in a pot of cider that is being prepared. One pair of pantyhose should usually yield from four to six breathable bags, according to www.aanpi.com/book.htm.

Pantyhose may also be used to keep static cling away from clothing. Simply rub an old pair of pantyhose over the clothes, according to www.pioneerthinking.com.

That used-up pair may also be used as a brush for suede to make it look nicer, according to www.spots.ab.ca/~ics/hse.html.

If one is worried about onions lying out and spreading fumes, storing them in an old pair of pantyhose may help, according to www.geocities.com.

The good news is, if a car’s fan belt snaps, one can use a nice pair of pantyhose to substitute for a while. Cut them to fit.

Among other random uses for pantyhose are deer repellent sachets for fruit trees, a soap holder and possibly a jelly bag, according to www.canadianparents.com.

So, to those people who abhor waste, there are many creative ways to extend the life of a pair of pantyhose and even resurrect them.
