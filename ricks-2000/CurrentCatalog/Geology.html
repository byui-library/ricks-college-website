Students interested in Physical or Life Science may find a satisfying and well paying career in geology. Domestic employment opportunities will be primarily in geological engineering, environmental geology, water resources, computer application to geology, mineral or energy exploration and development. Careers also exist in education, and with local and federal government agencies. The geological sciences often appeal to those who enjoy outdoor field studies as well as indoor laboratory investigation. Majors may transfer into a four year baccalaureate program with the essential preparation necessary to succeed in undergraduate as well as post graduate study.

The study of Geology also offers the non-science majors an opportunity to fill Physical Science requirements as well as to gain a better understanding of their natural environment.

COURSE DESCRIPTIONS

Credits

Geol 101* Introduction to Geology 3
Three hours lecture per week
Optional field trip with fee of approximately $12
Students selecting the lab option take Geol 102

General non-technical course for the non-science student who desires a broad introduction to Geology. Students who take Geol 101 may not also receive General Education credit for Geog 101, Geol 104, Geol 110, or Geol 111

This class is also taught on Discovery and Natural Science Field Expedition. Contact directors of these programs for special fees and other information.

Geol 102* Introduction to Geology Lab 1
1.5 hours of lab per week
Field trip may be required with fee of approx $7

General experience with mineral and rock identification. Along with map and photo study and interpretation with application to the environment.

Previous or concurrent enrollment in Geol 101, 104, or 110 required

Geol 103* Life of the Past 3
Three hours lecture per week
Optional field trip with fee of approximately $7

A non-technical study of the development of life and major events of earth history.
For non-science students.
No prior Geology class is required.

Geol 103L* Life of the Past Lab 1
One two hour lab per week Field trip may be required with fee of approx $7

Lecture/lab experience with rock and fossil identification as well as application of basic geologic principles.

Previous or concurrent enrollment in Geol 103 required.

Geol 104* Natural Disasters and Resources 3
Three hours lecture per week
Required field trip with fee of approximately $12
Students selecting the lab option take Geology 102

For the non-science majors emphasizing physical geology related to geologic hazards, mineral and energy resources, and the environmental consequences of urban and rural development.

Students who take Geol 104 may not also receive General Education credit for Geog 101, Geol 101, Geol 110, or Geol 111.

Geol 110* Earth Science 3
Three hours of lecture per week
Students selecting the lab option take Geol 102
Field trips may be required
Field trip fee will be approximately $12

This course is designed to enhance the student's understanding of his natural environment and how to relate it to his life, and covers basic concepts and methods used in geology, astronomy, and meteorology. It also provides good preparation for Education majors with knowledge and skills necessary to teach these concepts in the classroom.

Students who take Geol 110 may not also receive credit for PhS 100, Geol 101, Geol 104, or Geol 111.

Geol 110L* Earth Science Lab 1
(Note: Geol 102 Lab is currently being substituted for Geol 110L)
Prerequisite: Geol 110 concurrent registration required
One two hour lab per week

Lab studies, field trips, and home projects emphasize rock, mineral, and fossil classification and interpretation, as well as map and photo interpretation.

Geol 111* Physical Geology 3
Three hours lecture per week
Concurrent registration in Geol 111L required
One or more field trips may be required

Field trip fee will be approximately $35

For majors in Geology, Engineering, Forestry, and other sciences. Includes a study of our earth and the processes that formed its features. Students who take Geol 111 may not receive credit for Geol 101 or Geol 110. (Fall Only)

Geol 111L* Physical Geology Lab 1
Two hour lecture/lab per week
Concurrent registration in Geol 111 or instructors permission required
Mineral and rock identification in hand samples and large specimens are studied. Also extensive map and photo exercises are included.
(Fall Only)

Geol 112* Historical Geology 3
Prerequisite: Geol 101, 104, 110 or 111
Three hours lecture per week
Concurrent registration in Geol 112L required
One or more field trips may be required
Field trip fee will be approximately $50

A study of the geological history of the earth and the evolution of its life forms. Students who take Geol 112 may not receive credit for Geol 103.
(Winter Only)

Geol 112L* Historical Geology Lab 1
Concurrent registration in Geol 112 required
Two hour lecture/lab per week

Examination of the principles of historic geology, along with the study of fossils, special attention given to identification, type of preservation and environments of fossils.
Geologic map studies are included.
(Winter Only)

Geol 251 Mineralogy 4
Prerequisite: Geol 101 and 102, 110 and 102 or 111, and Chem 105 Concurrent registration accepted
Three hours of lecture and 2 hours of lab per week
One or more field trips may be required
Field trip fee approximately $40

The study of crystallography and other important physical, optical and chemical properties of minerals, with emphasis on hand specimen and microscope identification of rock-forming minerals. (Fall Only)

Geol 252 Petrology 4
Prerequisite: Geol 251
Three hours of lecture and 2 hours of lab per week
One or more field trips may be required
Field trip fee will be approximately $50

A detailed hand specimen and microscopic study of the genesis and classification of important igneous, and metamorphic rocks. (Winter Only)

Geol 290 Directed Study 1-3
Prerequisite: Consent of instructor

Faculty student consultation will determine a special area of study and/or research problems that will give students greater preparation for advanced work in geology and related fields. Term of enrollment, credit, and other details will be arranged with instructor. Recommended for students in Field Expedition or Discovery who have taken Geology 101 previously.
