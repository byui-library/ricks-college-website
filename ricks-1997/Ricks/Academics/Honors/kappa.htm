PHI THETA KAPPA NEWS -

Ricks was selected to be the site for the regional Phi Theta Kappa leadership conference, which will take place this weekend, Saturday 26 September, from 10AM - 4PM in SMI 240. Speakers include Dean Sorensen, Boyd Cardon, Bill Torngren and Dave Ward. Last year's Ricks chapter president Jen Leonardson was elected regional president [northwest US].
Student Phi Theta Kappa leaders and advisors from UVSC, SLCC, Snow, CSI, NIC [?], Clackamas and who knows where else will be in attendance.

Did you know that over 400 4-year institutions offer transfer scholarships exclusively dedicated to PTK members going to four-year schools? Members can easily access these senior institution scholarships by using the web, http://www.ptk.org , and following the web links for scholarships, where a search engine lists state-by-state and institution-by-institution scholarships which are available.

Also, fall invitations for induction into Phi Theta Kappa are in the works [should be mailed or hand-delivered next week]. Qualifications are 3.8 gpa for most recent semester and maintenance of a cumulative 3.6. Contact the Phi Theta advisor Vic Forsnes at 356-1864 or email forsnesv@ricks.edu.
