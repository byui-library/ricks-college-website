Athens

for N. Gage

Home Class News Grammar Literature Writing Syllabus Library Essay Menu

 

Today,
Chipped, chiseled rocks
And artifacts galore
Still flicker with the glory
That flashed 500 years before
Christ.

On earth’s floor,
Like massive bones,
Broken stones
Ignore their decay
And implore us to remember
Civilization's university.

Temples, literature,
Recorded history, and art,
Precious spokes of light,
Dart through the night,
Back 2,500 years ago
Back to the white center,
To the bright, bedrock days
When to Athena Socrates prayed
And asked,
“How should men live?”

Yesterday vibrant Greeks,
With their Acropolis-like voices,
Started an eruption, a Vesuvius,
And the world has never been the same.
