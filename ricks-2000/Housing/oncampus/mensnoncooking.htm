Men's Non-Cooking Style On-Campus Housing 

(Available Fall & Winter Only)

Rigby and Biddulph Halls
The men's Residence Halls are located right on the campus.  There are two dorms - Rigby Hall and Biddulph Hall.  See map #26 & #27.
Daily Chef Show in the Galley Cafeteria
Two different meal plans are offered for the Non-Cooking Style Apartments.  The Gold meal plan offers students up to19 meals a week, and the Silver meal plan offers 14.   Click here for info!
Residence Hall Bedrooms
There are two occupants per room.  Each room has two beds, two high-efficiency closets, two desks, a microwave, and a microfridge®.  Showers and restrooms are located on each floor, and are cleaned daily.
Laundry Facilities

 

 

 

 

There is also a coin-operated laundry room in the basement with Maytag washers and dryers.  One washer load is 75¢ and a dryer will run for 30 minutes for just 25¢.
Lounge
The main lounge is located on the first floor of each hall.  Features available to occupants include a big screen TV, pool table, foosball, an upright piano, and a nearby basketball court.
Basketball Court
A basketball court is conveniently located between the men's residence halls.
