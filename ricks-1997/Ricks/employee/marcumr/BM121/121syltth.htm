Introduction to the Course
In this class we will study the history, doctrine, places, and peoples of the BOOK OF MORMON. Through this study it is hoped that you will get closer to your heavenly Father and our Savior, Jesus Christ. You should come to know that the Book of Mormon is another witness of Christ, and your personal testimony should be strengthened.

The BOOK OF MORMON cannot be read like any other book. It is not a novel, nor a textbook. Nor is it a history book, or an archaeologists manual of place origins. It is scripture and must be read as scripture, prayerfully and carefully.

We will study the BOOK OF MORMON sequentially from I Nephi 1 to Alma chapter 13. We will draw from the book those stories, truths, and principles that, through the inspired editing of Mormon and Moroni, were included for us in these latter days.
 

Class Requirements
Although we study the book as scripture the course is also given FOR CREDIT at Ricks College. The grade you receive, therefore, is given, not to show how much your testimony has grown from your study of scripture, but on how well you met the stated requirements for grade and credit given below.
 

Grading

Attendance:
MISSING MORE THAN EIGHT TIMES, OVERRIDES ALL OTHER CONSIDERATIONS AND YOU WILL RECEIVE A FAILING GRADE.
Minus points will be given for each missed lecture or presentation of materials. When lectures are missed and material not receive for absences beyond your control (such as school excused absences or an illness verified by a doctor) you can MAKE UP THE WORK within three school days. This is done by coming to my office and taking the quiz, and by writing a one page summary of the reading assignment for that day.

Tests:
There will be DAILY quizzes on the reading assignment. The quiz will be taken from the study guide that can be copied from the original now on library reserve. If you read the assignments and answer those questions prior to coming to class you will have no trouble with the quizzes. If you do not, things may prove difficult. MAKE A COPY OF THE STUDY GUIDE FOR YOUR OWN USE, AND USE IT!! As noted it can be found on my homepage.

A midterm and a final will be given directly from the study guide questions! The midterm will be given at the end of second Nephi. The final at the end of Alma 16.

For the most part this class is a discussion based class. I will ask you questions about the reading, and you can ask me questions as well. In addition we will share insights and discuss doctrines taught, people and their problems, history, and perspective. Please come prepared to share what you have learned and to ask questions about what you read.
