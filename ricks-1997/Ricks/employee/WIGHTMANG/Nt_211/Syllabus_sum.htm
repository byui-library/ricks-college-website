Course Objective:
1. TAKE RESPONSIBILITY FOR YOUR OWN LEARNING by:
            * abiding the attendance and punctuality requirements established
            * completing assignments, exercises, and tests as required ON TIME.
            * intensively studying from the New Testament in and out of class
            * being a positive influence on the learning environment which includes:
                       * actively participating in classroom discussions
                       * living life so as to not offend the presence of the spirit (honor code and dress standards).
2. Study in depth the testimonies of Matthew and John, including structure, purpose, and message.

Required Materials:
    All four standard works of LDS footnoted scriptures.

Grading: (460 points possible)
As this is an academic course for 2 credits, you must be graded on your efforts and your mastery of the content. An "A" will not be given simply because someone is "nice" or has a testimony of the gospel. These two things are of most importance for you but they do not meet the academic standard to receive college credit. Therefore, the "A" grade will be reserved for those who excel far above the average level to make this class more meaningful not only for themselves, but for others. The standard is as follows:

Scripture Reading Summaries --- 45% of overall grade
You will be required to submit a typewritten summary at the beginning of each scripture block studied. GO READ THE GUIDELINES to and the example of the summary on my web page under "class information". There will be 21 summaries (each worth 10 points) and they will be due at the beginning of class.

Attendance/Participation --- 22% of overall grade
Not only are you expected to attend, you are expected to be a positive influence on the learning environment through your participation in classroom discussions. My definition of participation is remaining alert and attentive, taking notes, and asking a question or making a comment now and then. If you are shy, it is time to become comfortable being an active participant in society. There are a possible 100 points for attendance and participation.

You will be allowed three excused absences this semester which means you either call or email to determine if your absence is excused.   I will determine if the absence is excused, not you.  Use these wisely as each absence thereafter will result in the loss of 10 points no matter what the excuse!   Any un-excused absence results in the loss of 10 points.  Those who choose to attend but not participate will also lose 10 points.
I reserve the right in the end to remove points for semester long silence. I get to know those who participate. If I don't know you by the end of the semester, you will most likely lose points.

Content Mastery --- 33% of overall grade
You are expected to try and master the material you will learn this semester.  I feel this is best accomplished through note taking. Take good notes while in class, then learn how to transcribe those notes into your scriptures. Come test time, you will be allowed to use your scriptures on the test. There will be 2 tests, each worth 25 pts, and a comprehensive final worth 100 pts.  (150 total points) (see dates below)

Application
Although you will not be graded in the area of application, you cannot fully take responsibility for your own learning without participating in some form of application.  There is a recommended process on my web page under Application Guidelines that past students have suggested changed their life.  I would invite all to participate, even though most will not have the spiritual inclination to do so, perhaps, only seeing the grade as the end instead of a means to the REAL end.  I will make myself available to anyone who wishes to discuss the results of their application process.  However, you will need to begin immeditely leaving me plenty of time to respond.
