Friday and Saturday, October 10-11, 1997

    Friday, October 10

    6:15-7:45 p.m. Spaghetti dinner for alumni & spouses in the Manwaring Center cafeteria..

    8:00-9:45 p.m. Team meeting in the new Taylor Religion Building. A chance to hear from your coaches one more time. At the close of this meeting you’ll receive something to take with you to eat for breakfast and a t-shirt to wear at the meet on Saturday.

    Saturday, October 11

    9:00-11:30 a.m. Ricks College Invitational Cross Country Meet at the Rexburg Municipal Golf Course. Women’s 5k race at 10:00 a.m. followed by the men’s 8k race at 10:40 a.m. If you plan to run and would like to preview the course, you may do so between 4:00 and 5:30 p.m. Friday. The course in very flat and mostly grass.

    12:15 - 1:45 p.m. Picnic lunch for alumni & families at Porter Park.

    Does that sound like an exciting weekend?!!

    Now to answer a few questions you might have.

    Who is invited? Everyone who has been on the cross country teams at Ricks College since 1987, and their spouses (if they have one). Children are welcome at the events on Saturday.

    How much will it cost? For everything listed in the above itinerary of the Ricks College Cross Country Alumni Reunion you will pay $10/person! What a deal! Dinner on Friday, breakfast and lunch on Saturday, and a keepsake t-shirt to boot!

    What about a place to stay Friday night? Refer to the bottom of this page for a list of Rexburg motels and their current rates. You are welcome to make a reservation and stay there. Or, if you’re scraping for money, bring a sleeping bag or blanket and a pillow and plan to sleep on the floor in the Stutz house or outside in a tent in their backyard (Bring a tent if you want to).

    Do I have to run in the race? No. About half of your former teammates will be. Showers and towels will be available after the race in the Hart Building locker rooms.

    Now, here’s what we’d like from you before the Reunion. Please e-mail to let him know you are coming. This is the first time we’ll do this and who knows if, or when, it will happen again. Let’s make it a great experience this fall!
