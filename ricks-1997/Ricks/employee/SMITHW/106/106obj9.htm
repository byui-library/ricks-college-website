Unit #9 - NUCLEAR CHEMISTRY

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Write balanced nuclear equations.
2. Use first-order kinetics to determine rates of decay and half lives of radioactive isotopes, including those used to esimate the ages of rocks and artifacts.
3. Calculate mass losses for nuclei and for nuclear reactions, and use the Einstein relation to convert these to binding energies and reaction energies, respectively.
