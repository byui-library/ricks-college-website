Masterpieces of Spanish Literature
MP SPAN LIT
CLASS CODE: SPAN 441 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Literary masterpieces of Spain.
DESCRIPTION: Survey of texts from the Medieval Period through the contemporary scene.
TOPICS: Major authors, periods, and issues in the history of Spanish literature.
OBJECTIVES: Students will become familiar with the principal writers, themes, and questions of Spanish literature. Students will develop both an appreciation for the development of that literature through time and an increased ability to deal with a given text as literature per se.
REQUIREMENTS: Requirements will vary from section to section, but attendance, consistent and lively participation, exams and written work may be required.
PREREQUISITES: Spanish 339 with grade of "C" or higher or consent of instructor.
