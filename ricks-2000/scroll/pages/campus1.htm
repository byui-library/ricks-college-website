NEW ASRC OFFICERS SWORN IN AT BANQUET

 The 2001-2002 ASRC officers are sworn in Wednesday night. From left: Ryan Fleming, Kevin Swiss, Cassie Golie. Lori Parker, Hannah Irvine, Kalli Kronmiller, Crystal Goodwin, and Spencer Haacke. Photo by THOMAS GROVER, scroll staff.

by Thomas Grover
campus editor

The Associated Students of Ricks College held its annual banquet Thursday, honoring and thanking individuals and organizations who have contributed to ASRC during the past year.

"This is an evening of appreciation and recognition," Robert Wilkes, student life vice president, said.

The new ASRC officers for 2001-2002 were sworn in to office. President David A. Bednar also gave a short address.

In addition to the swearing-in, the banquet recognized faculty and staff, Scroll, the campus newspaper, Rixida, the college yearbook, the New Student Organization, Tami Burton and Hutch Swan, Woman and Man of the Year, the eight ASRC boards, and the 2000-2001 ASRC officers.

"It was a wonderful night. It was a night of gratitude and reflection," Autumn Southam, women's vice president, said.

One hundred eighty-seven individuals attended the banquet. Dinner was served and then individuals and groups were recognized. Advisers spoke about their organizations, Burton and Swan were awarded $300 scholarships and each ASRC board reviewed its accomplishments.

A video compiled of pictures and video footage from the past year was shown. Afterward, Student Life Vice President Bob Wilkes gave his remarks, followed by Sister Susan Bednar and then President Bednar.

"The experiences you are having are crucial," President Bednar said.

President Bednar encouraged the audience, as they reflected on the past, to focus on the future.

Bednar said the important thing is to focus on the leadership, teaching and service the audience has learned the past year and use it to focus on home.

After the banquet, Lance Taylor, ASRC president, talked about his experience.

"The most important thing I would share is the love I have for the students of Ricks College. ... I am humbled and appreciative [of them]," Taylor said.

The current officers said they were happy for the new ones.

"I am excited for the new officers and have a lot of confidence in them," Southam said.

The new officers were pleased with the banquet.

"It just makes me really excited for next year," Cassey Golie, 2001-2002 social vice president, said.

"I feel the whole weight of the stewardship on my shoulders. I'm a little nervous, but I'm excited," Spencer Haacke, 2001-2001 ASRC president, said.
