Health Teaching Methods
HLTH TCH METH
CLASS CODE: HS 405 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HEALTH SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Health for the secondary teacher, emphasizing his or her role as a health educator, appraisal of typical student health problems.
DESCRIPTION: Health for the secondary teacher, emphasizing his or her role as a health educator, appraisal of typical student health problems
TOPICS: All forms of health education.
OBJECTIVES: Each student will leave the class with:
1. A complete background of health education.
2. A complete understanding of teaching strategies and understand which ones work for the different types of learners.
3. The ability to teach health principles in a manner that will positively effect their students in decision making choices.
4. A complete understanding of all aspects of being an organized teacher.
REQUIREMENTS:
PREREQUISITES: HS 131 or 131W, BIO 264, BIO 265. Must be a declared Health Education Major or Minor in junior or senior year.
