Israel Tour Quotes Masada- Suicide- President Spencer W. Kimball and M. Russell Ballard
Shortening life is a sin. This temple of God is the body that the Lord has given us. It has been given to us to last a long time. It is a terrible criminal act for a person to go out and shorten his life by suicide or by any other method if it is intentional, by shortening it with the things that will create an early death. That isn‘t the way the Lord arranged it. He intended that men should live to the age of a tree. (Teachings of Spencer W. Kimball p. 187.)
I am confident that there is a time to die. I am not a fatalist. I believe that many people die before "their time" because they are careless, abuse their bodies, take unnecessary chances, or expose themselves to hazards, accidents, and sickness...God can control our lives...I am positive in my mind that the Lord has planned our destiny. We can shorten our lives but I think we cannot lengthen them very much. (Pres. Kimball, Faith Precedes the Miracle, p. 103.)
Obviously we do not know the full circumstances surrounding every suicide. Only the Lord knows all the details, and He it is who will judge our actions here on earth...When the Lord does judge us, I feel that he will take all things into consideration: our genetic and chemical make-up, our mental state, our intellectual capacity, the teachings we have received, the traditions of our fathers, our health and so forth. (M. Russell Ballard, Ensign, Oct. 1987.)
