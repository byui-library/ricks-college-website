Test Week Schedule
Winter 2002

1. Instructors are required to give a final exam or meet the class according to the schedule listed below. NOTE: For classes which do not start on the hour, schedule tests for the full hour in which the class meets. For example, if a class meets M W 9:35 - 10:50, schedule the test for the time reserved for classes meeting M W 10:00 - 10:50.

2. In cases where you have conflicting examinations, or more than three examinations in one day, individual arrangements for alternative tests times should be made with the Instructor.

3. Final exams for any classes not fitting this schedule, including those which meet one day per week, should be given during the last class period or at another time arranged by the Instructor.

4. Travel plans are not considered to be extenuating circumstances for adjusting your test schedule any exceptions must be approved by Max Checketts, Assistant Academic Vice President.
