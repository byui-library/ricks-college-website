Martha Stewart cooks up a fortune

by Sarah Lunt
LUN99013@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

The name of Martha Stewart has become synonymous with the all-American home.

Martha Kostyrn was born in 1941 in Jersey City, New Jersey.

Stewart’s mother was a schoolteacher and an active homemaker. She taught Stewart the secrets of baking, canning and sewing, while her father taught her the art of gardening, according to www.foodtv.com.

Stewart attended Barnard College and got her B.A. in history. Her sophomore year she married Andrew Stewart. She worked as a model to put herself through school and continued until her first child was born. She also had a successful stint as a stockbroker until a recession hit in 1972.

After her time in the stock market, Stewart decided to start her own catering business. The business expanded until it was worth $1 million. She soon began to publish books and launched her own magazine, Martha Stewart Living, according to www.myprimetime.com.

In 1998 and 1999, Fortune magazine named Stewart as one of the “50 Most Powerful Women.”
