February Spotlight -- Matt and Jeanette Bennett
When Matt Bennett '95 and Jeanette Waite '94 got married in 1995, they had a lot to talk about. They had both graduated from Bonneville High School in Idaho Falls, and they had both graduated from Ricks (although they weren't there at the same time). They also shared a passion for journalism.

After following each other around at the Daily Universe at BYU, and then at the Deseret News, the couple started dreaming up how they could start their own publishing company.

Their first publication under the "Bennett Communications" label was
Scholastic News Service, which is a newsletter for high school journalists.

Matt's father, Ron Bennett (Scroll adviser), works with them to publish this newsletter each month.


This first newsletter gave the Bennetts the confidence and knowledge to 
start a bigger publication.

Matt and Jeanette Bennett started Utah Valley Magazine in September 2000. They've
interviewed Larry King, Donny Osmond and Rulon Gardner, as well as other
local artists and musicians, for this bi-monthly glossy magazine.

The couple also started Utah Valley Bride magazine, which is an annual
publication.

The Bennetts have also worked the New Era Magazine, Potato Grower magazine
and Sugar Producer magazine.

Both Matt and Jeanette were editors of the Scroll newspaper at Ricks College
and the Daily Universe at BYU.

Jeanette graduated from Ricks in 1994, and Matt graduated from Ricks in
1995.
