Individual Speech Therapy

Assessment, diagnostic, & therapy services are provided in Articulation/Phonology/ Accent Reduction, Stuttering, Voice, Auditory Processing, & Language Expression.

As a speech-language pathologist, I provide therapy for up to three students a semester.  Students who would like to enhance their communication abilities can register for Comm. 105 for 1 credit, and get a chance to work one-on-one in a supportive environment and in a goal-driven way on their communication skills.  Because of the limited time available for these services, and because therapy may not be necessary in many cases, students must see me prior to registering for Comm 105 (see also Comm. 105 under Classes).

Hearing Screening Services

Along with speech therapy services, I also supervise the provision of hearing screenings for Fall and Winter Semesters.  These are sponsored by the Communication department and are offered FREE of charge to all current & retired faculty and staff, as well as to all current students.  If you would like a hearing screening, either come in at the scheduled time (see Announcements to the right), call or e-mail me to set up an appointment (Phone: 356-2929; e-mail).
