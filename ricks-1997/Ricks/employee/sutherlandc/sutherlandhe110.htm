COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: HE 110
Title: Practical Homemaking Credit: 3
Text: Lab Manual

Course Objective:
Students will develop the skills needed to establish and maintain an effective LDS home.  The Proclamation of the Family will be the basis for the course.

Description:
A course designed to teach principles relating to various aspects of homemaking, including: food preservation, food preparation, nutrition, meal planning, home nursing and health, home management and organization, financial management, basic sewing skills, and emergency food storage all within the context of the LDS home.  Emphasis is on practical hands-on learning experiences.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Students have projects in each of the subject areas: foods, sewing, organization.
