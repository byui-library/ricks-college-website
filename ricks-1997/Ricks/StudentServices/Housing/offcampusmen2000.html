Warning!

Choosing your housing without being aware of all the contract terms and conditions may be HAZARDOUS to your happiness.
Being approved merely means the unit meets several basic minimums in physical facilities, management, and has agreed to enforce the Ricks College Honor Code. The Ricks College Housing Office, (208) 356-1045, will answer any questions you may have, but the student assumes full responsibility to read and understand contracts before signing. For off-campus housing reservations, contact housing units directly.
