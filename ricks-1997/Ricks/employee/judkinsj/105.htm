     Personal

  • Course Description: As a speech-language pathologist, I provide individualized speech-language therapy for up to three students a semester.  Students with communication difficulties, such as voice problems, stuttering, dysarthria, etc., can register for Comm. 105 for 1 credit, and get a chance to work in a supportive environment and in a goal-driven way on their communication skills.  I also work with English pronunciation for students learning English as a second language.  Because of the limited time available for these services, and because therapy may not be necessary in many cases, students must see me prior to registering for Comm 105.

  • Course Fee: To help offset costs of therapy materials, and to encourage students to take personal responsibility for therapy, a $50.00 fee is assessed each semester.
