Ricks College News Release
March 22, 2001 

 

Rexburg Woman honored as Exemplary Woman of the Year

REXBURG, Idaho —Gail Jensen Seely, a Rexburg mother of eight children, was honored as the Exemplary Woman of the Year by Ricks College Sunday night.

In presenting the award, college President David A. Bednar read a tribute from her husband, Sylvan, who described his wife as a woman "with no pretense. She lives what she believes. She is a role model as a wife, mother and grandmother."

She responded by paying tribute to her own mother, 88-year-old Edith Jensen of Rexburg, who she said "is a woman of grace and beauty who loves the Lord."

After meeting her future husband at Ricks College, she and her husband were married in 1958 and started raising a family. "Those years of childbearing at times were a little overbearing, but so rewarding. Our children have filled our lives with joy," she told the audience assembled in the Hart Auditorium for the Women’s Week fireside.

She was raised in both Idaho Falls and Ashton. During her teen-age years, she helped on the family farm and worked in the family’s restaurant. She also taught violin and guitar lessons to many other children.

Her interest in music, specifically the violin, has remained strong throughout her adult life. She continues to play and teach, and over the years, has organized and participated in many musical groups to entertain at various functions.

She has been a member of the Madison Hospital Board of Trustees since 1997 and is presently serving as chairperson of the board’s Beautification Committee.

She has been active in church work and has served as a Primary worker, ward chorister, Young Women president, Relief Society president and Relief Society advisor in the Ricks College LDS Fifth Stake.
