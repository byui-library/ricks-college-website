Project #2 - Shock Assembly

AFor our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding [and] eternal weight of glory@, 2 Corinthians 4:17.

Using 3D Studio Max (no AutoCAD allowed!!) construct a 3-dimension model of the shock assembly shown below. You may use any of the techniques discussed thus far in class.

This project consists of 4 main objects [Bracket, Pivot, U-support, Bushing (2)] and several support pieces. Create the 4 main objects to exacting dimensions as shown in the diagrams and explained in class. Create the support pieces as explained below. Place the finished assemblies inside a simple room. Choose several different metallic materials for the different parts of the assembly. Place one or two spotlights to illuminate the assemblies with shadow casting and add a couple of omni lights to balance out the lighting effect.

Bolts & Nuts:
Create bolts the same diameter as the hole through which they protrude. Threads are not required but may be included for extra credit if done well. The nuts and heads on bolts are typically 1.5 times the diameter of the bolt shank when measured across the flats with a thickness of about 2/3 the diameter. Note the Aheadless@ bolt shank which is to be welded to the U-support.

Washers:
The inside diameter of the washer should be the same or slightly larger then the bolt through which it protrudes. The outside diameter should be 1.5 to 2 times the diameter of the bolt. Use any reasonable thickness.

Cotter Pin:
The cotter pin may be easily created using a lofting technique as discussed in class.

Spacer:
The piece between the U-support and the Bracket is called a Spacer. Please omit this from your assembly so that the U-support rests directly upon the Bracket.

Turn in:

  • One close-up, perspective rendering (jpg format) of each of the 4 main objects

  • Two close-up2 perspective renderings (jpg format) of the fully assembled assembly from diametrically opposing viewpoints. Be sure to include support pieces.

  • 1 perspective rendering (jpg format) of the exploded exploded assembly. Be sure to include support pieces.

Place all required files inside a folder in the DropBox with the key word Mech in front of you name.

Hint: After completing the assembled version of the model, copy the model before creating the exploded view. Do not try to place both assemblies in a single 3D Studio Model!
