Academic Behavior Survey

The purpose of the ABS is to aid you in identifying the areas where your study habits need to be changed or improved for you to be successful in college.  Read each statement on the questionnaire carefully and indicate your study behavior.  There are no "right or wrong" answers; this is only a survey to aid you in determining your academic needs. 

Read each statement of academic behavior, then enter the answer which most closely describes how often you do this behavior according to the scale below.  Be honest in your response.   Fill in only one blank for each question.  After completing the survey fill in the results form.
