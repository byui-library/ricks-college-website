Bob Christensen
    18th season  (2001-2002)  202-62 Career Record at Ricks

    Coach Bob Christensen, one of the most successful wrestling coaches in the nation, enters his eighteenth year at the helm of the Ricks College wrestling team. Christensen's successes are many, but among them was 1998's 17-3 dual meet record, the best ever at Ricks.  The Vikings were ranked number one on the NJCAA Coaches poll the entire year before finishing sixth at the National Junior College Championships. That was followed by a 13-4 mark in 1999, a year that saw the Vikings win the Region 18 Championships for the second time in Christensen's career at Ricks.  In 2000-2001, Christensen's team recorded an 18-5 record, the most dual meet wins ever in a single season for the coach.  The 18 wins also pushed Christensen over the 200 win mark for his career.

    During his tenure, 40 Viking wrestlers have won Region 18 championships. Sixty four of Coach Christensen’s wrestlers have won All-American honors. Eight of Bob’s teams have finished in the top four at the NJCAA finals. He won Coach of the Year honors in 1988 and Region 18 Coach of the Year in 1999.   Christensen's career dual meet record at Ricks College now stands at 202-62-2.

    Bob was a state heavyweight wrestling champion at Missoula County High School in Montana. He won an NJCAA championship as a heavyweight at Ricks College in 1964. He finished third in the Western Athletic Conference in 1967 and 1968 as a heavyweight at Brigham Young University.

    Christensen first became a head wrestling coach in 1968 at American Falls High School in Idaho. He moved to North Fremont High School in Ashton in 1971 where he won two Idaho state championships in 1978 and 1980.

    He moved to Ricks College in 1984. In 1996 under Coach Christensen, the Vikings were ranked #1 among junior college wrestling teams for three weeks at mid-season. The Vikings went on to finish third at the NJCAA finals.  In 1998, Ricks was ranked #1 on the NJCAA poll the entire season, finishing sixth at nationals.  In 1999, after a slow start, the Vikings went on to win the Region 18 title and finish third in the nation with all ten Viking wrestlers scoring points.

    Bob is married. He and his wife Marcy have five children.
