1999 Ricks College
Merit Badge Pow Wow
October 16, 23, 30

The 1999 Scout Merit Badge Pow Wow is for all Scouts eleven years of age and older. Instruction is available to Scouts in three merit badges of their choice. Participants will work on each merit badge for fifty minutes each Saturday of the Pow Wow.
Adult Leaders
Adult leaders will not be required to pay the registration fee. They may purchase a 1999 patch after the Pow Wow begins. In accordance with National Council policy, two adult leaders should attend the Pow Wow with each unit. Adult leaders are strongly encouraged to attend merit badge classes with their boys.
    • All Scouts and leaders are asked to wear their scout uniforms at the Pow Wow.
    • An opening ceremony will be held the first Saturday from 8:30-8:45 a.m.  Scouts are encouraged to attend.  They will receive their 1999 patch at the ceremony.
Standards
Participants of any race, color, creed, ethnic or national origin may participant in the Merit Badge Pow Wow provided they maintain the Ricks College standards of modesty in dress and conduct and abstain from alcohol, tobacco, coffee and tea while on campus.
Registration Information
