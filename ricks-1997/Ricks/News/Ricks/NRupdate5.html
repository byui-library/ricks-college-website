Ricks College News Release, May 19, 1998

Ricks Update - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

May 19, 1998

Ten faculty members will retire by the end of the summer. They are Lee Gifford, teaching communications since 1962; Roger McPheeters, who handles information services in the library and has been at Ricks since 1964; Vance Hendricks, director of the Counseling Center and here since 1966; Marlene Hatton, home economics instructor since 1967; Jerald Oldham, chairman of the Department of Biology and an instructor since 1968; Arlin Bartschi, instructor in technology education and construction management since 1969; Lyle Anderson, health science instructor since 1969; Nathan Larsen, business management instructor since 1969; Paul Nye, history instructor since 1971; and George Quarez, a nursing instructor since 1980.

The Collegiate Singers and Chamber Orchestra just completed a very successful tour to Denver and surrounding areas presenting a program entitled "Expressions of the Spirit." Music selections were performed from the past six years of sacred music works that were commissioned by Ricks. The works were applauded with standing ovations at every performance. The Saints of Denver and surrounding areas were blessed by the spirit that was brought to these concerts and our students were greatly blessed by the testimonies and love that was shown them by the Saints of those areas.

Our first Campus Education Week that will be June 4-6 and will feature such speakers as Elder Joseph L. Wirthlin of the Council of the Twelve and Sister Mary Ellen Smoot, General President of the Relief Society. For more information, you can check the Ricks home page at www.ricks.edu under Continuing Education or call (208) 356-1040 for a booklet.

Starting Tuesday, the north lane of Viking Drive was permanently closed to begin preparations for a new outdoor quad. By eliminating parking along the block between the Manwaring Center and John Taylor Building, two-way traffic will be possible in the south lane until 7th South Street is completed. The partial street closure will allow work to begin on a large underground storm water detention vault in the north lane of Viking Drive. The street is expected to close permanently in late June as soon as construction is completed on 7th South that skirts the south side of campus.

President David Bednar spoke to a large group of Ricks alumni in Mesa, Arizona, May 12 organized by Steven and Cecilia Flint, regional alumni directors for the Phoenix Area. President Bednar will speak at a devotional at the Institute of Religion in Logan, Utah, Tuesday, May 26, at 12:30 p.m.
