How to Find Internet Resources

Click on the Internet Resources icon on any computer in the library and it will take you to the McKay Library Home Page. Internet Resources on that page include Reference Online, Index to Articles, Show all Databases, and Site Overview. These are considered to be high quality resources that should be examined first as you begin your research. If after consulting these resources, you need access to search engines on the Internet such as Yahoo, Google, etc., please ask for assistance at one of the reference or information desks.
