Microsoft Windows, Beginning
Organizing and backing up your computer files
- Learn how to create a system for organizing your computer files (no more hour long searches for that document you created last month)
- Find out how the Windows Explorer can let you be in control of the information on your computer
- The Y2K scare is over, I don’t need to back anything up - - right? Learn how to backup your files on a regular basis, and how to retrieve those files if you need to.
This workshop is intended especially for those that use Windows on a regular basis, but do not utilize its features.
