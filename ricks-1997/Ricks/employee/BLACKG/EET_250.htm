Text: Perozzo, James; The Complete Guide to Electronic Troubleshooting;                                                                         Delmar Publishers Inc.; 1994

================================================================================ Course Description: Observing and repairing trouble symptoms in TV, tape recorders, radios, and cassette tape units. Using proper instrumentation to isolate the malfunction to a particular part of the circuit or section involved. Replacing the faulty part and restoring normal operation.
