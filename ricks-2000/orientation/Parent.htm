Parent Orientation

Greetings from the office of New Student Orientation!  We extend an invitation to all parents to participate with their son or daughter as  they prepare for their BYU-Idaho experience.  As a parent we encourage you to take an active role with your child as they participate in the Pathway Pre-Campus Orientation program. 

 

Winter  2002 Parent Orientation - We will be holding a Parent Orientation for all parents of new students who are admitted for 2nd Summer Term on Friday, June 28, 2002.  Please take the opportunity to review the Parent Orientation schedule listed below.  We hope you will join us.  

Parent Orientation Track:
 11-12 a.m.         Parent Orientation Assembly - Manwaring Center W. Ballroom.
10 a.m.-2 p.m.   Info Fair - Manwaring Center West Lounge
12 p.m.-2 p.m.   Presidents Reception - Manwaring Center East Ballroom
12 p.m.-1 p.m.   Parent Tours of Campus - Manwaring Center Information Desk

Parent Orientation - This is an opportunity for parents to learn about the BYU-Idaho experience from President and Sister Bednar.  Includes a question and answer session.

Info Fair - Come and learn about the many campus services available to assist students during their time at BYU-Idaho.

President's Reception - Parents and new students are invited to stop by and visit with President and Sister Bednar, as well as other members of the President's Council.  Light refreshments will be served.  Information booths outside the reception, in the Manwaring Center West Lounge will be available to both parents and students.  Be sure to stop by and discover more about the various programs and services available to you and your son or daughter.

Parent Tours  - Guided tours of campus will be made available to parents.

Campus Services & Hours during Campus Orientation 

Any questions regarding New Student Orientation or Parent Orientation should be referred to  the Office of New Student Orientation at 208-496-2393 or by sending an e-mail to orientation@byui.edu.  We look forward to seeing you on campus. 


Parents are our best advisors!!!           Plan now to attend 2nd Summer Term, Fall, or Winter Parent Orientation
