Business Management Orientation

The Business Management Department will host an orientation meeting for students interested in the Bachelor Degree program. The meeting will be Thursday, April 5th @ 2:00 pm in Smith 240.

CIS 258 RPG IV Class Notice

  • The CIS 258 RPG IV class will not be taught during the Fall 2001 semester. It will be taught Winter 2002.

Univ. of Idaho - Business & Economics Scholarships

  • The U of I offers a number of scholarships for transferring Ricks College students. They include the Graue and Folz scholarships equaling $11,000. The Ross Butler scholarships equaling $3,000 and general transfer scholarships equaling $2,000. For more information contact Dana Wekerle at 1-800-960-3033 or danaw@uidaho.edu .

 

BYU-Idaho and CIS
  • As many of you are already aware the Board of Trustees announced that Ricks College would become a 4-year school and would undergo a name change to Brigham Young University - Idaho. While no final decisions have been made as to what programs will offer Bachelor's degrees there is a tentative list and timeline for the transition. As you review the list you will find that the CIS department is planning on a name change to the Information Systems Department with junior level classes tentatively scheduled to begin fall of 2003.
