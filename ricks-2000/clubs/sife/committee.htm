Business-
    Vice President: Peter Buchan
        Business Committee tries to involved local business owners in serving the needs of the community.  Right now we are planning educational seminars for 'at risk' or 'underprivileged' people to teach them benefits of the free enterprise system.  We hope to get local business owners to come and speak for a short while on topics such as resumes, interviews, and entrepreneurial skills in these seminars.
 

Questions contact Peter Buchan at bigpete70@hotmail


Community-
    Vice President: Julie Rees
        As Students In Free Enterprise one of our responsibilities is to reach out and influence the community. Helping young businesses mature or working with volunteer agencies are things that a community needs help with in order to survive.
 

Questions contact Julie Rees at juliannerees@hotmail.com


Educational-
    Vice Presidents: Brady Apostolo
        The Educational Committee's main priority is to teach children. Introducing the concept of free enterprise at a young age helps younger children set and achieve goals. We also introduce basic business ethics and ideals with simulations that teach them the importance of things like a savings account.
 

Questions contact Brady Apostlo at apo99001@ricks.edu


Financial-
    Vice Presidents: Trevor Smith
        Being financially stable and independent is a goal of everybody in SIFE. The financial committee holds fairs and seminars that teach students and the community how to get out of debt, how to use credit wisely, and how to be financially stable.
 

Questions contact Trevor Smith at sowegaboy@hotmail.com
