    Grading and Evaluation criteria

  • Assignment points are based on
    • the number of sources used,
    the difficulty of use,
    steps necessary to find information
    100 point maximum on one assignment
  • Reading points measure preparation & comprehension.
    • CourseInfo allows retakes of reading quizzes.
  • Take home examination points awarded for using
    • a variety of research tools
    steps taken to reach information
    difficulty of use
  • Portfolios provide opportunities to
    • demonstrate computer use,
    create a game to teach a skill, such as alphabetical order,
    do another student initiated learning activity.
    perform individual research,
    gain experience with specialized research, (Legal Research) or
    render service in the community
  • Point accumulation sheets will be available weekly in class.

    Course Policies

  1. Attendance Requirements:
    2. Participation enhances learning.
    E-mail or office visits can supplement or replace class attendance.
    Student portfolio experiences provide opportunities to evaluate, teach, and personalize instruction.
    If you plan to be gone, hand in the assignment and reading notes early.
  2. All work must be completed by the final examination time.
    • Hand papers in each class!
    • Feedback is essential to both teacher and learner!
    • Deadlines will be within a week of the assignment unless illness or other unforeseen events arise.
  3. Academic dishonesty is not tolerated.
    4. If a question arises about honesty, you may be asked to replicate your work in a proctored setting.
    The goal is to complete the assignment without help.
    Help is available to LEARN skills in class and at the Reference Desk.
  4. Papers must be legible & in proper form
    5. Put your name and the unit for which credit is sought on each page.
    Include basic descriptive elements of common research styles.
  5. Dress and Grooming Standards for classroom appearance are expected.
  6. Code of Honor is the expected level of behavior.
