I. Assignments

    A. Services: 50 points

    1. Take library tour (attach testing center printout)
    2. Find Education Index. Locate an article on a current topic. Copy entry.
    3. On second floor find Rixida in oversize books. Copy down the call number. Does 1997 or 1998 have faculty pictures?
    4. On third floor find the juvenile books. Copy down the call number, title, and author of one book.
    5. On third floor find the Media lab. Calculate the price for one media lab project.
For example, what does a single sheet of construction paper cost?
What does laminating it cost?
In room 328, what would it cost for one page scanned?
    Would a scanned picture be quicker and less expensive than making figures out of construction paper and laminating them?
Portfolio option: make something and record your expenditure of time and money

    B. Research Material: 50 points

        1.Find a style manual for writers of term papers @LB2369.. Describe the book.
            a. the complete call number
            b. editor or author, if given

        2. Use an example of periodical article to create a footnote for an article in the Ensign.
        3. Use an example of newspaper entry to create a footnote for an article in the Scroll or local newspaper.
        4. Use an example for a book to create a footnote for children's book in our collection.

Portfolio option for hands on: repair or protect a paperback book.

    1. Look in the Library of Congress or Sears, Subject Headings under Education.
        a. What is the [general call number] assigned to Education?
        b. What is a related (RT) or see also topic?
        c. What is a narrower (NT) or broader xx topic?
        d. What is a subdivision( -- )?
        e. What does the see also note (SA) say about education of groups? Give an example.
    2. Use the subject search of the catalog to find a periodical on education. Print or copy the information: title, group publishing the periodical, oldest and newest dates and format. Example, Ensign published by the Church of Jesus Christ of Latterday Saints. 1971 to ?
    3. Use the author search to find a publication by the Department of Education or another group.
    4. Use the call number search to find a book with the call number, LB2369 .

 Portfolio option Using CIP and Sears catalog five books that could be used for a classroom library [wish list].

    D. Periodicals: 50 points

    1. Use ProQuest to find an article on an educational topic. Copy the citation.
    2. Use Education Index to find another article on the topic. Copy the entry and translate abbreviations.
    3. Use Academic Abstracts to find an article on the educational topic. Copy the citation.
    4. Use ERIC or CIJE to find an article on the educational topic.
    5. Use the Index to Church Periodicals to find an article for a church assignment.
 

    E. Newspapers: 50 points

        1. Use NewsBank  to find a newspaper article on education. Give:
            Last name of author, First name. "Article title." Newspaper Source. Day Month Year of publication: NewsBank. CD-ROM. [NewsBank, Inc.]. [Electronic publication date from top line].
        2. Find an article on education in the New York Times. Record information found and citation for either microfilm or electronic product. For example:
            a. the author of the article,
            b. title of the article,
            c. name of the newspaper,
            d. date, page, and column.
        3. Use Internet to find a campus newspaper. Print or describe what you found out about schools in another state.
        4. Use Gale Directory to find the editor of your hometown or Idaho newspaper.
        5. Find an article on Newspapers in Education (NIE) in a periodical or newspaper.
 

    F. Literary Sources: 50 points

        1. Use Something About the Author to find a biography of a favorite author.
        2. Use Children Literature Review to find criticism about a favorite author.
        3. Find a book review for a children's book in Book Review Digest or current periodicals.
        4. Find a poem or quote that could be used for a bulletin board or program.
        5. Use a concordance or Infobase to find a scripture or quote from a prophet for a church assignment.
Portfolio option volunteer to read to children for 50 minutes.
 

    G. Dictionary: 50 points

        1-3. Use three different dictionaries to find a word. Which dictionary had the best definition? Why was the definition better? Record call numbers, titles, and pages so that someone else could see the same information.
        4-5. Find two reviews to compare dictionaries.
            Which dictionary is recommended as best for your home or futureclassroom?  Record your review sources information so that someone else could verify price or order information.
 

    H. Encyclopedia: 50 points

        1-3 Use three different encyclopedias.
                One should be shelved with subject resources for education with signed articles by authors with credentials.
                One could be for a young child. Record call numbers, titles, volume, and page numbers so that someone else could see the same information.
        4-5 Use two review sources to compare encyclopedias. Which encyclopedia has the best recommendation? Record your review sources information so that someone else could verify price or order information.
