Summer in the Snow
Ricks College Entertainment Series 
Performance information:

Open-seating policy: The audience needs to be seated ten minutes prior to curtain time to guarantee seating. Seats not occupied at 7:20 p.m. will be released to those waiting for tickets. The same policy applies in the Snow Drama Theater, even though seats are reserved. 

Dress: More informal in the summer and ties for men and dresses for women are not required. However, standard Ricks College classroom attire is required, including no shorts. Children under six years of age, including babies, are not admitted.

Free Organ Recitals:

As a tradition each summer, Ricks College will feature a number of free organ recitals by Darwin Wolford, director of organ studies at Ricks College, and other faculty organists. Recitals will be held at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall June 28 (Darwin Wolford), July 27 (Marsha Bjornn) and August 10 (Rebecca Parkinson). 

Free Summer Choir Recitals:

 Ricks College will also offer several free choir recitals performed by summer students. Performances will be held at 7:30 p.m. June 2, July 11 and August 15.
