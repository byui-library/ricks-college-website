BYU-Idaho Approved Off-Campus Housing

The BYU-Idaho Housing Office provides information for approved off-campus housing units.  All single students must live in approved housing.  Designating a housing unit as approved does not mean BYU-Idaho endorses or approves contractual and financial arrangements or that the apartment will meet your particular needs or preferences.  Being approved merely means that unit meets several basic minimums in physical facilities, management, and has agreed to enforce the BYU-Idaho Honor Code. 

The Housing Office, (208) 496-1045, will answer any questions you may have, but the student assumes full responsibility to read and understand the contract before signing.  For off-campus housing reservations, contact units directly.

  BYU-Idaho Approved Off-Campus Units Are Privately Owned And Operated
