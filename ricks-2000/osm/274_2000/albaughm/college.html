FIRST YEAR

My first year of college was pretty hard to get things going the way I wanted them to go. I was kind of a book worm and learned that I do really enjoy reading and can't stop once I start a book. I don't have a lot of pictures because I just kind of kept to myself throughout the year. The first semester I can't remember a whole lot about it. I didn't grow very close to my ward or my roommates. They say that college is where you find yourself but my first year I think I lost myself. In my second semester I had tons of fun, I grew really close to my roommates, and got to know more people in my ward. I guess I was slowly breaking out of my shell which I had formed throughout my first semester.
