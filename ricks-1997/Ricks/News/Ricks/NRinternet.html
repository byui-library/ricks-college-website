Ricks College News Release, May 11, 1998

Ricks students can now register for classes over Internet 

Thanks to the Internet, students around the world can now bypass the telephones to register for classes at Ricks College. 

College Registrar Kelly Hymas says the new system will help this week when a rush of more than 3,000 freshmen begin to register on May 8 for fall classes. 
In the past, registration has been limited to 24 incoming phone lines, which has led to some frustrated students who can't get through because of busy signals. 

Using the Internet, an unlimited number of students may register at one time. 
While students may still register by calling 356-1006, Hymas says the Internet option will likely become more popular. 

The system was developed by Terry Silva, a college communications programmer. Silva says the system allows a display of the class schedule, class registration information, financial data and the ability to change PIN numbers.  
An online catalog is under construction and other services will be added as time goes on. 

Silva explains the service is routed off-campus via a secured server so student ID numbers, credit card numbers and other data are encrypted and no one else can see the data.  

The path to get to the Student Web Access page begins at the Ricks home page at www.ricks.edu . From there go to "College Services" and then to "Registration." 

To get directly to the page from off campus, the address is http://www.ricks.edu:8086/LOCAL/WARN.MBR . From on campus, the address is ht tp://as400/LOCAL/WARN.MBR .
