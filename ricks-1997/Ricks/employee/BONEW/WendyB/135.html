Tap Dance, Beginning

Instructor: Wendy Bone
Office: Kirkham Building 209
Phone: 356-2074
Secretary: 356-2073

Text: None Required

Course Description: This is a movement class where rhythms, styles, and techniques of tap dancing are introduced to the student, including basic tap terminology.

Course Objectives:

    1. To introduce tap dancing as a stylized dance form with an introduction to the basics of dance technique as a foundation.
    2. To demonstrate an understanding of basic dance technique and tap skills.
    3. To help each student experience the fun of tap rhythms and motion.
    4. To heighten physical health and well being through activity and correct principles of training.

Course Requirements:

    Professionalism is stressed:
    1. Punctuality
    2. Proper and modest attire
    3. Hair out of face
    4. No food or gum
    5. Total involvement and attention during class

Attendance/Participation

    Two (2) absences are allowed without penalty. Three to four (3-4) absences will result in lowering of grade. The fifth absence will result in automatic failing of course. Three (3) tardies equal one absence. Three observations equal one absence. To receive credit for observing you must take in-depth notes on your observation and turn them in to the instructor.

Movement and Technique

    Combinations will be performed periodically throughout the semester for evaluation by the instructor. Evaluations will be directly related to demonstration of tap rhythms, style, and technique.

Paper

    A 2-page paper regarding dance history, theology, or a biography is required. This paper must be grammatically correct, cleverly written, and interesting to read. You may elect to do a project instead, but it must be pre-approved by the instructor.

Grading

    Attendance 40%
    Achievement/Skill 40%
    Written assignments 20%

Final

    This is your responsibility! The final will be held during finals week and will be a percentage of achievement and skill. It is up to you to be there on the right day and time. This cannot be rescheduled.

    Students are encouraged to meet with the instructor anytime special feedback or other assistance is desired. Periodic feedback will be given, solicited or not, but the student must ensure that it is sufficient for his or her individual needs.
