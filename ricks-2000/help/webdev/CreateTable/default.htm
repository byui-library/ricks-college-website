This will assist you in creating or editing a table either by inserting or drawing the table.

Inserting a Table
  1. Click on the page where you want the table to be place.
  2. Click the Table menu, click Insert, click Table. (FrontPage 98 - Click Table menu, click Insert Table.)

  3. Choose the appropriate number of rows, columns, border size.  Click OK.   You can always edit the table later.

  4. The table will appear in your page.

Drawing a Table
  1. Click on the Table menu.  Click on Draw Table.  Your cursor will now be a pencil and not an arrow.

  2. With the pencil, click, hold, and drag the length of the table you want.




  3. To draw rows/columns, click inside the table (with your pencil) and drag a line from one side of the table to the other.

  4. To erase a line, click on the eraser.  Then click on one side of the line you wish to erase, drag across the unwanted line (it will turn red), and then let go of the mouse.   Remember, whatever lines turn red will be erased.

Editing a Table/Cell/Row/Column
  1. To edit a table such as the alignment, table size, border size/color, or background, right-click inside the table you wish to alter.  Click Table Properties.

  2. To edit a specific cell within a table, right-click inside the cell you wish to edit.  Click Cell Properties.  Now you can alter the alignment within that cell, backgrounds, and so forth.

  3. To even up a group of cells within a row or column, highlight the cells wanted to be equal, and click on the Table menu and  Distribute Rows Evenly or Distribute Columns Evenly.
