RICKS COLLEGE VOLLEYBALL
    2001 Review

    Another great year of volleyball at Ricks College as the Lady Vikings finish with a 40-10 record and a number ten ranking on the final regular season NJCAA poll.  Ricks got off to a great start, winning fifteen of their first sixteen games, including victories over national powers Utah Valley and College of Southern Idaho.   With a 20-2 record, the Lady Vikings jumped onto the national poll at the number five spot.  With league play underway, Ricks moved into first place with a 7-0 record before losing at Southern Idaho.  By then the Lady Vikes were ranked number three in the nation.  Ricks went on to finish league play with an 11-3 mark, then beat North Idaho in the opening round of the Region 18 tournament before falling to Southern Idaho in the Northern Division championship game.  Trish Pettingill and Brittany Ahlstrom were named First Team All Region 18.  Malia Koelliker was a Second Team pick, and Jessica Jackson was Honorable Mention.  Pettingill, Ahlstrom, and Koelliker were All Tournament Team selections as well.

     

    Topping the honor roll,
    Trish Pettingill was named First Team All American by the NJCAA. 
    Pettingill led the team in sets, assists, and defense.
