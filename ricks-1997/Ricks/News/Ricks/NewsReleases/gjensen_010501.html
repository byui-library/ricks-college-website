Ricks College News Release
May 1, 2001
Gaylen Jensen named director
of Telecommunication Network Services

REXBURG, ID –  Rexburg native Gaylen Jensen has been named director of Telecommunication Network Services at Ricks College.

The new position was created following the recent retirement of Ron Moss, who served as director of Telecommunication Services. Moss retired after 38 years of service to the college.

For the past eight years Jensen has been the director of network operations and been responsible for computer network connectivity of more than 2,400 employees, students, systems and servers. He was also responsible for campus Internet connectivity, including firewall security and filtering.

The new position combines that organization with Telecommunication Services, which includes the telephone system and the fiber optic and copper cable plant system.

Jensen holds a master of science degree in engineering management from Southern Methodist University in Dallas, a bachelor’s degree in design engineering technology from Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah, and an associate degree from Ricks College. He and his wife, Lisa, have seven children and live in Rexburg.
