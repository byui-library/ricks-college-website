Course Description: A course designed to develop skills of social dancing. Four of the following dances will be taught each semester:

waltz, foxtrot, cha cha, swing (various styles), west coast swing, mambo, tango, rumba.

Course Objectives: Through Dance 280 students should:

1. Develop a proficiency in intermediate level American style ballroom dancing.

2. Increase self-confidence and enjoyment in a dance environment.

3. Learn to discriminate the different dances according to the music.

4. Learn and practice proper social dance etiquette.

5. Become more aware of the positive and negative effects that music and dance can have on the spirit.
