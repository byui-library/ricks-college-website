Why Do I Need A Disclaimer Page?
BYU-Idaho is a privately owned college that provides space on their Web Site for faculty and college-related pages. These pages are subject to the BYU-Idaho Honor Code and are insured to provide information that is both compliant with the Honor Code and endorsed by BYU-Idaho.
Besides Honor Code issues, off-campus links may contain material not endorsed by either BYU-Idaho or the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and/or may contain materials violating copyright laws.
A Disclaimer Page is needed to bridge the link between on and off-campus links, ensuring that the off-campus links are not affiliated with BYU-Idaho or the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
