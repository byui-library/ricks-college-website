Godzilla to take over Halloween concert

by Patricia Selman
sel00001@byui.edu
Scroll Staff

Screams of horror may be heard throughout the Hart Auditorium at 7 p.m. today and Wednesday as Godzilla visits BYU-Idaho for the annual Halloween Concert.

The theme of the concert is “Godzilla Eats Las Vegas.”

“The reason this theme was chosen is because it is going to be a very theatrical show and very alive,” Jennifer O’Farrell, director of the Contemporary Dance Theater II that will be performing in the concert, said.

The BYU-Idaho Symphonic Band will accompany CDT’s dances, along with other classical and modern music that will be “fun and spooky,” Kendell Nielsen, director of the symphonic band, said.

The songs that will be featured come from movies such as The Patriot, Gladiator, and Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon.

There is a change to this year’s program.

“Although much of the concert will be directed toward having fun,” Nielsen said. “The last half of the concert will be a tribute to those who have lost their lives and those who will lose their lives in the war on terrorism.”

The concert will be a visually exciting program. The band got a grant to bring in a laser show from a company in Indiana called Laser Image. The laser show will be in sync with the music and videos, and may remind the audience of the feelings they felt on Sept. 11, Nielsen said.

Nielsen invites everyone to wear a costume. He and every member of the band will be wearing one. The concert, which costs $1 for BYU-I students and $3 for the general public, is FA-100 approved.
