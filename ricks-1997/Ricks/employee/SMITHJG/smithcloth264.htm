COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 264
Title: Fashion Manufacturing Credit: 5
Text:  “Apparel Manufacturing Handbook” Solinger
 
 

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire a working knowledge of basic steps in the manufacturing process of clothing.  Upon completion of this course the student should be able to:
  1.  Demonstrate skills in operating different industrial machines
  2.  Identify the various stitch classes
  3.  Set-up piece rates for different construction operations
  4.  Set up a line flow for different types of apparel garments (using a budget)
  5.  Ticket and bundle garment pieces for assembly lines
  6.  Construct proto samples
  7.  Identify various quality levels and the different price rages
  8.  Produce markers for given styles and sizes
  9.  Apply specific principles making 1st and production patterns
  10.  Derive solutions to problems occurred in the flow process

Description:
Stitch identification, machinery, line flow, proto samples and production concepts as related to the fashion industry.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Stitch classification file
2.  Bundling flowcharts
3.  Proto samples
4.  Time Study
5.  Set up piece rate system
