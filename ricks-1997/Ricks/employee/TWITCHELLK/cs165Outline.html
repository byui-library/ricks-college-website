Class
Preparation 		Brigham Young said, "We are prepared for some things, and we receive just as fast as we prepare ourselves.” You are expected to read assigned material prior to class.
Homework Homework assignments allow you to demonstrate that you have mastered the implementation details of what we discuss in class. Point values for each homework assignment vary based on the perceived difficulty of the assignment. Programming assignments shall compile and execute using the Linux systems in ROM 272. Late homework is penalized 10% the first school day, 30% the second school day, and 50% the third school day. Homework is not accepted after three school days late or after the CS computer labs close for finals, whichever comes first. It is your responsibility to submit homework correctly, including, but not limited to, the correct location, the correct format, and the correct file name. Incorrect homework submissions are subject to late penalties, including not being accepted. You may not resubmit homework that has already been graded. The final homework grade is the ratio of total earned points and total possible points.
Assignments 00, 01, 08, 09, and 13 through 25 of the CS170 homework assignments are required for CS165 students. Please note that some of these assignments are due when you are not required to be in class. Also note that assignments 17 and 22 were assigned previously as procedural programs (11 and 07) for CS170 students. The schedule reflects the fact that basic algorithms were previously developed. You may want to develop the algorithms for these assignments ahead of time.
Section
Tests 		There shall be two section tests to allow you to demonstrate that you have mastered the concepts discussed in class. Point values for each test vary based on the amount of material covered. Tests shall be administered in the testing center on the scheduled dates; late tests shall not be accepted. The final section tests grade is the ratio of total earned points and total possible points.
Final Exam The comprehensive final examination shall be administered in ROM 276 at the times specified in the anticipated schedule. Make your end-of-semester plans accordingly.
Grading 70%   Homework
20%   Section Tests
10%   Final Examination
Computer
Labs 		Our computer labs are located in ROM 271 (Windows) and ROM 272 (Linux). You are expected to comply with lab rules. Lab assistants are available but may be of limited assistance. "Walk-in" lab tutors are available from at the times posted in the lab. You may also request a private tutor at Learning Assistance in the McKay Library.
Justice
and
Mercy 		Homework assignments and tests are to be completed as scheduled. Do not procrastinate! (See Alma 34:33.) However, mercy is available for a limited number of emergency situations if you discuss your situation with me before (not on and especially not after) the scheduled due date. Otherwise, justice prevails. There will be no routine extensions of due dates.
Disclaimer The instructor reserves the right to make changes to this course outline, including the anticipated schedule, as well as to homework assignments. You will be notified in class when changes are made.
