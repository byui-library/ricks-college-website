The purpose of the chloroplasts is to perform the process called photosynthesis.  Photosynthesis is the process where cells of plants and alga (plural of algae) capture the energy of light and store that energy in organic molecules usually sugar.

This is a light microscope view of a cell from a water plant called elodea.  The green circular structure are chloroplasts.  You can also see the cell walls in this picture.  With a light microscope you can not see a plasma membrane.

Here is a view of a chloroplast with a  electron microscope (EM).  This view is prepared with a transmission electron microscope (TEM). With a TEM you can see internal structures of a cell.  With an electron microscope you can see plasma membranes.  You can not see a plasma membrane with a light microscope.  In this view you can see the inner membrane folded in to stacks called grana.  The two large potato-shaped structures are starch grains.
