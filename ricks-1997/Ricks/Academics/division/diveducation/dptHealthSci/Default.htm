WELLNESS COMMITTEE

 

"Joseph Smith taught '...it is the will of God that man should repent and serve him in health,and in the strength and power of his mind, in order to secure his blessings....'(Teachings, page 197).  Ordinarily, a healthy, vigorous person is in far better position to work out his own salvation and to be an effective instrument in rolling forth the great purpose of Deity."

Bruce R. McConkie, Mormon Doctrine, page 346
