Elder Marlin K. Jensen teaches that friendship never faileth
By Jennifer Warnick, Scroll Staff




A friend in need is a friend indeed.

In his address at the Sunday morning session of general conference, Elder Marlin K. Jensen named friendship as one of the more subtle but most important of the gospel principles.

"Perhaps one reason the scriptures make little specific mention of the principle of friendship is because it should be manifest quite naturally as we live the gospel," he said. "In fact, if the consummate Christian attribute of charity has a first cousin, it is friendship."

Elder Jensen outlined the importance of having friendship between brothers and sisters, and also between parents and their children.

"Our needs for friendship are often best met in the home," he said. "If our children feel friendship within the family, with each other, and with parents, they will not be desperate for acceptance outside the family."

Also important is the relationship of friendship to courtship and marriage, Elder Jensen said. "A relationship between a man and a woman that begins with friendship and then ripens into romance and marriage will usually become an enduring, eternal friendship."

There is an overwhelming need as Latter-day Saints to, befriend fellow members in sincerity. To illustrate this, he related a story about a new member of his ward who was struggling with activity and his neighbors. When Elder Jensen asked the member what he could do to help, the member responded, "Whatever you do, please don't assign me a friend."

"No one wants to become a 'project;' we all just want spontaneously to be loved," Elder Jensen said. "And, if we are to have friends, we want them to be genuine and sincere, not assigned."
Elder Jensen encouraged everyone to practice being a good friend, which, he said, in itself makes a "significant contribution to God's work and the happiness and progress of his children.

"The power is in each of us to be a friend," he said. "Old and young, rich and poor, educated and humble, in every language and country, we all have the capacity to be a friend."
