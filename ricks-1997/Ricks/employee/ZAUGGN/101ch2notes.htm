Chemistry 101

Chapter 2 notes and comments

Section 2.2

Know Table 2.1, except for giga-, nano-, and pico-. Know the name of the prefix, its symbol, and its mathematical meaning. For example, know that mega- has M as its symbol and that it means one million.

Be able to think metric. In other words, can you decide whether a pencil would be 15 m or 15 cm or 15 mm long? Be able to do the same for metric units of mass and volume.

Section 2.6

You do not need to memorize conversion factors between systems of units, for example 2.54 cm = 1 inch. I will supply those if and when you need them.

Section 2.7

Body Mass Index (BMI) is calculated by dividing your mass in kilograms by the square of your height in meters. If the result is greater than 25, you are considered overweight. This is the current way of determining whether a person is overweight. (See the Chemical Connections box in this section.)

Section 2.8

Be able to think Celsius. How would you dress for a 25 oC day? Be able to convert between Celsius and Kelvin temperatures, but not between Fahrenheit and Celsius temperatures (as long as you can think in Celsius).
