The text on the left says:

"This is perhaps the most famous of all cuneiform tablets.  It is the eleventh tablet of the Gilgamesh Epic, and describes how the gods sent a flood to destroy the world.  Like Noah, Utnapishtim was forewarned and built an ark to house and preserve living things.  After the flood he sent out birds to look for dry land."    WA K 3375

The tablet is small and the print is tiny.  As you you look at the picture, realize that the artifact is around 4,000 years old.
