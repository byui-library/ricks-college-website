Melvyn Sanford, born, I think, many years ago. Came to Ricks College English Department, 1969. Has a masters degree in English from BYU. In 1990 he earned a masters degree in French from UC Santa Barbara (after study at Ricks, BYU and Middlebury College, Vermont.) Did post-graduate study at Université de Bourgogne, Dijon, France with mention très bien.* Has explored France many times and has led two tour groups there. French is his passion. He aims to create a love for it in the hearts of his students.

A family man, he has a wife and five children. Wife is an expert kindergarten teacher. His oldest daughter served a mission in France, his youngest son in Georgia. His children are married now and raising a generation of grand children, probably the smartest on the planet, certainly the best looking.
