Ruth Jones Payne Nelson (32)

I was fortunate to be able to attend Ricks during the depression years of the early thirties.

When we entered the Spori building the two back rooms on the south were the locker rooms--girls to the left and boys to the right.

Some of the offices of the teachers were the little rooms on the landings of the stairways going to the top floor.

But the room I remember best was the southwest room on the top floor--Miss Edna Ricks’ English class. I had to take bonehead English, but she soon straightened me out, and I thoroughly enjoyed all the other English classes I took.

Ruth Jones Payne Nelson (32)
Idaho Falls, Idaho
