Where will you be in 15 years?



In this assignment students will evaluate where they will be in fifteen years from today. We will base all financial estimates on the economic standard of today. Tell me what your income will be? How many people working to achieve that goal. How many children will you have?

What will they look like, be like act like, academic standards achieved, hardships faced, etc? What will your spouse look like, be like, act like, physical description, attitudes etc?

What will your home be like?

What will your spiritual life be like? (FHE, Temple Attendance, Callings, Service, Scripture Study, Personal and Family Prayer, Sunday Observance, etc.)

Talk about Jobs and cars, and yards, and neighbors, etc.



Your paper is more than a fact sheet answering these questions. You need to include a lot of detail so as to evaluate the real from ideal in 15 years.
