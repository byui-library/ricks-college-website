MAP AND DIRECTIONS TO BADGER CREEK

From Rexburg:

Travel North on Hwy 20 for 4 miles.  At the Hwy 33 Junction turn right.  Travel east for 39

miles.  As you approach Tetonia, the road will curve to the right.  Turn off the highway (left)

about midway through the curve. Pass by the Rammels Valley Pack and continue straight

north about 3 miles.  At the "T" junction, turn right. Continue down the road until you come

to the marked entrance of BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center. Take the second

entrance next to the large building with the light blue roof.

 

From Ashton:

Traveling South from West Yellowstone, MT on Hwy 20, you will come to the city of

Ashton, ID.  Turn left at the controlled intersection with the high school on the left. Drive all

the way through town. You will approach the junction of Hwy 32.  Turn right (south).  Travel

approximately 29 miles until you reach the junction of Hwy 33. Turn left.  As you approach

Tetonia, the road will curve to the right.  Turn off the highway (left) about midway through

the curve. Pass by the Rammels Valley Pack and continue straight north about 3 miles.  At

the "T" junction, turn right. Continue down the road until you come to the marked entrance

of BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center. Take the second entrance next to the large

building with the light blue roof.

 
From Driggs:

From Driggs continue North on Hwy 33, following it to Tetonia. Pass through the town.

Approximately .6 miles past the church the road will curve left.  Turn off the highway (right)

about midway through the curve.  Pass by the Rammels Valley Pack and continue straight

north about 3 miles.  At the "T" junction, turn right. Continue down the road until you come

to the marked entrance of BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center.  Take the second

entrance next to the large building with the light blue roof.
