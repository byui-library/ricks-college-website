English 250 block Class

Instructor Ron Messer

Introduction to film: This class will use film as its primary teaching tool. This is an introduction to the techniques used in achieving dramatic effects in poetry, stories, novels, drama, and film. Eleven books are required reading for this class. One novel, Sense and Sensibility, is for 25 points extra credit to be counted at the final.

Requirements:

1. Attendance  Over five absences equal failure.

2. Class notes (Will help on tests-Handouts are on the Homepage, including syllabus.)

3. Fulfill reading assignments for quizzes and tests

4. Books: (Note: the following books are all Dover editions)

  1. Doyle, Hound of the Baskervilles
  2. Hawthorn, Young Goodman Brown
  3. Shelley, Frankenstein
  4. Turgenev, First Love
  5. Twain, Mysterious Stranger
  6. Voltaire, Candide
  7. Shakespeare, Hamlet
  8. Shaw, Pygmalion
  9. Synge, Playboy of the Western World
  10. Coleridge, Rime of the Ancient Mariner
  11. Austen, Sense and Sensibility

All tests will be in the testing center. Check the schedule. In most instances the tests will be in at least five days. The exceptions are toward the end of the semester. Please carefully follow your syllabus. If you fail to take a test during that period of time, you forfeit that test. Such points can be costly. The only extra credit will be Sense and Sensibility. It is worth 25 extra points. All test make-ups are 50 percent of highest score in class. The tests will cover both the novels and in-class lectures.
