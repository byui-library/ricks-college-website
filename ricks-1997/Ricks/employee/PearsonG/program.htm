The photography programs at BYU-Idaho are designed to help students develop the skills needed to start their own photographic career after earning either a specialized associate or bachelor of fine art degree.

After an introduction to the many facets of photography at BYU-Idaho, each student chooses a personal or professional direction that is suited to their interests and skills.

The following photography courses are required for the specialized associate degree:

Photo 100 - Principles of Photography The basic preparatory course for all incoming freshmen
Photo 110 - Studio Lighting Study of lighting in the photo studio environment
Photo 120 - Portraiture I Posing and lighting with models
Photo 210 - Photo Communication Architecture and studio still life photography
Photo 220 - Portraiture Advanced lighting, posing and printing 
Photo 260 - Professional Studio Portfolio Advanced portfolio production
Photo 270 - Personal Portfolio Production of an expressive portfolio
Photo 290 - Internship Final preparation working with a professional photographer
