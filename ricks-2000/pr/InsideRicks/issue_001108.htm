SPORTS ROUNDUP

Competition is winding down for the three fall sports at Ricks College. At the same time, the three winter sports, men's and women's basketball and wrestling, have just gotten under way.

Cross country: In region finals in cross country, held Saturday at the Northern Pines Golf Course in Kalispell, Mont., both the Ricks men's and women's teams won. For the women it was the seventh consecutive Region 18 title, dating back to 1994; and for the men it was the fourth consecutive. Individually, Ricks' Jeff Davidson was first in the region for the men in 25:44, some 11 seconds ahead of the next runner on the 8,000-meter course. Both the Ricks men's and women's teams have been ranked first nationally throughout the season.

Football: Ricks' come-from-behind 42-28 home victory against Dixie College has assured the Vikings of a winning season record for a 21st consecutive season. The Vikings have one regular season game remaining. They will be in Colorado Springs, Colo., to face Air Force Prep on Friday, November 10.

Last weekend's win is expected to garner Ricks an invitation to play in the Real Dairy Bowl in Pocatello on December 1. However, an announcement is not expected to be made until November 14. On that day the Real Dairy Bowl Committee will announce the bowl participants.

Women's volleyball: The nationally 10th -ranked Lady Vikings fell to Utah Valley State College in three games Saturday in Orem, Utah, and ended any hopes of hosting the Region 18 finals November 10-11. Instead, nationally fifth-ranked Utah Valley State will provide the host site. The Ricks women are 39-9 on the season, their best season since 1997 when they placed second in the region.

CONCERT TO FEATURE PATRIOTIC MUSIC

A special invitation is extended to all veterans to join the Ricks College community as they celebrate Veterans Day with a patriotic concert of music written by U.S. Sen. Orrin Hatch and composer Janice Kapp Perry. The concert will be held Saturday, Nov. 11, at 7:30 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Veterans may arrange for a free ticket by calling the Ricks College Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230. Tickets are $8 and $6 for the general public.

The show will be hosted by Perry and Hatch and feature pianist Marvin Goldstein, a combined Ricks choir and professional vocalists Wintley Phipps, Jenny Jordan and Paul Engemann. The show is being produced by Greg Hansen, a Ricks College alumnus who works as a professional producer and arranger in Provo, Utah.

Hatch and Perry have written and produced seven albums together in recent years. Hatch says he has had a love of music since an early age when his parents saved scarce money to buy him a season pass to the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra.

'I REMEMBER MAMA' OPENS IN SNOW DRAMA THEATER

The heart-warming comedy "I Remember Mama" opens Thursday in the Snow Drama Theater. The play runs Nov. 9-11 and 14-18 at 7:30 each night. Cost is $6 for the general public. Tickets are available from the Ricks College Ticket Office at (208) 356-2230. Directed by Robert Nelson, the Theatre Department's production promises to be an audience pleaser. "It's a feel-good type of comedy," Nelson says. "It's the kind of story that could happen to any family. There is a crotchety aunt, a grumpy great-uncle and a mother who is the epitome of the good, warm-hearted, stay-at-home mother whose only concern is her children."

He says it was last performed at Ricks in 1968 when he designed the set and Lyle Watson directed the show. Nelson, who has taught at Ricks for 37 years, says he thought of doing the play as he reflected on his time at Ricks. "As my career kind of winds down, I've been kind of looking at things we've done in the past," he says.
