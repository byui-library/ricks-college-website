The Planetarium of Ricks College

Many of the groups that come to campus enjoy visiting the Planetarium in addition to the campus tours.  For large groups, a visit to the Planetarium gives additional options on rotating groups through the various display locations.

The Planetarium is scheduled through the Manwaring Center Scheduling Office.   Please call (208) 356-2248 to make your reservation.
