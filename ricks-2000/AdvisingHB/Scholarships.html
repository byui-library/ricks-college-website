SCHOLARSHIPS, AWARDS & FAST TRACK

Scholarships and awards provide incentives and assistance to students enrolling at Ricks College. They recognize outstanding performance academically, in the talent areas, and for departmental achievement. Applicants must complete all admissions requirements before the scholarship deadline. Scholarships vary in amount and duration based upon the individual's achievement. They are awarded in the following areas:

1. ACADEMIC SCHOLARSHIPS: Based upon grades and ACT scores. Students with AP or Honors classes will receive special consideration.
2. LEADERSHIP AWARDS: Consideration is given to students who have held various school, seminary, and civic leadership positions. To receive full consideration it is anticipated that grades be above average.
3. DEPARTMENTAL ACHIEVEMENT AWARDS: Available to students to acknowledge outstanding achievement in a particular area. Available in select departments only. Please write specific departments for application materials.
4. TALENT AWARDS: Awards are given in art, dance, debate, drama, journalism, and music. Supplementary scholarship application materials are required. Please write specific departments for application materials.
5. SOPHOMORE AND CONTINUING STUDENT AWARDS: Based upon performance and review by the Scholarship. Committee. Students should carry a minimum of 12 credits each semester of their freshman year to be considered for a sophomore scholarship. Applicants who have completed 60 credits or more must request special
consideration.Fast Track: Each year more students are earning college credit while still in high school. They earn this credit by taking college courses concurrently during their senior year or by passing an Advanced Placement (AP) exam.
Fast Track is a program designed to help energetic high school students begin their college career early. Even more exciting, Fast Track will allow graduation from Ricks College after only one year, including summer enrollments.
