Textbook: Introductory Algebra (5th edition)

GRADING CRITERIA:

  • Homework
  • Quizzes
  • Four Tests  -- Calculators are not allowed on Tests.
  • Common Final Exam (comprehensive) given by the Department.  Calculators are not allowed on the Final.

The Final Exam is about 25 % of your grade.

Keep all returned-graded papers in a folder. These papers will verify accuracy of your midterm and final grade.

To determine your grade:

1. Keep a running total of points you earn.

2. Divide this number by the total number of points possible.

3. Multiply by 100 (this will be your percent).

4.  This is a Pass/Fail class.  You percent in step 3 must be  at least 70%  to receive credit in the course..

TOPICS:

Selected sections from the textbook chapters 1 - 10 will be discussed in detail.

Expectations:

Class meets each Monday and Wednesday , 5:30 - 6:45 p.m., in room 219. Failure to attend class usually reflects in a lower grade. Make up work, including tests, will not be allowed unless prior approval is granted by the instructor or the university. You will be expected to critically read the section of the textbook to be discussed. Highlighting and questioning topics and concepts in the reading will be important for the class.

Tutoring Help:

It often becomes necessary to get out-of-class help for this course from someone other than the instructor. This help may come from a variety of sources, however, be aware that help is available from the Math Lab here on campus. Posting of days and hours of the Math Lab can usually be found in the student services area. Also, instructional videos for each section of the textbook can be found in the library.  There is also computer aided software (related to your textbook) in the computer lab that will provide additional interactive practice.
