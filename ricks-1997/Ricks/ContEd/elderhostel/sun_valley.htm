Located in the heart of Idaho, Sun Valley/ Ketchum is a year-round recreational resort. Because of its picturesque setting and mild climate, movie stars and celebrities frequently visit; the late Ernest Hemingway made the area his home. At 5,900 feet, Sun Valley lies 175 miles west of Ricks College. Lodge provides double occupancy, limited singles, extra charge. Private bath. Classes and meals are at the lodge. No alcohol allowed.

INVESTIGATING THE GRAY WOLF:  SYMBOL OF THE WILD
Starting with only 15 wolves translocated from Canada, the Idaho Recovery Program now has approximately 115 wolves and 12 known wolf packs in Idaho.  Learn about the gray wolf's struggles and success for existence in today's society.

GEOLOGY:  CENTRAL IDAHO'S ROCKS AND NUGGETS
Study geology of Central Idaho including volcanoes, lava flows, hot spots, craters, and mountains.  Trace the history of mining for gold in the famous Yankee Fork area. 

THE LIFE AND WRITINGS OF ERNEST HEMINGWAY
In the beautiful setting he chose for his final home, explore Hemingway’s famous writing style; probe his fascinating tales of love and war; and see him in a new light.
