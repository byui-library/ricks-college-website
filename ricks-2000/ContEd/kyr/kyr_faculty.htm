KNOW YOUR RELIGION FACULTY & TOPICS

 

STEVE ADAMS, Institute Director, Wichita State University; THE POWER OF THE WORD - Following Nephi's advice (1 Nephi 19:23,) we will use likening techniques and scriptural example to bring spiritual power into our lives in the 21st Century.

MICHAEL W. ALLRED, Seminary Faculty, Roy, UT; LIGHTEN YOUR LOAD:  LEARNING TO FORGIVE - Learning why we should forgive and how to forgive.

 PHIL ALLRED, Religion Instructor, BYU-Idaho; ALMA TO CORIANTON: A PATTERN FOR MODERN PARENTS - Alma's doctrinal counsel to his wayward son provides a timeless template for latter-day families.

SCOTT L. ANDERSON, Institute Faculty, Orem Institute; Marriage Counselor; HIS HEALING HAND - This lecture will include the Savior's desire to be one with us, His desire to "succor" us in our infirmities, and activating the Atonement in our thoughts, desires and actions.

TERRY B. BALL, Associate Chair, Associate Professor, Ancient Scripture, BYU-Provo - ISAIAH AND THE MESSIAH - An introduction to understanding Isaiah with special emphasis on his teachings concerning the Messiah.

RONALD E. BARTHOLOMEW, Seminary Principal, Orem, UT, Part-time Faculty Ancient Scripture, BYU-Provo - UNDERSTANDING THE DOCTRINE OF FORE-ORDINATION - Elder Neal A. Maxwell wrote, "The doctrine of fore-ordination is one of the doctrinal roads least traveled by, yet it clearly underlines how very long and perfectly God has loved each of us." 

K. DOUGLAS BASSETT, Institute Faculty, Orem Institute - THE BOOK OF MORMON: "DRAWING US CLOSER TO GOD THAN ANY OTHER BOOK" - Discussing reasons why this statement by the Prophet Joseph Smith is literally true.

ALEXANDER L. BAUGH, Assistant Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - THE QUORUM OF THE TWELVE: SPECIAL WITNESSES - This presentation will trace the early historical development of the Quorum of the Twelve through 1844.  Additional instruction will be given concerning the responsibilities governing the Twelve Apostles today.

RANDALL C. BIRD, Institute Faculty, Ogden Institute; THE FAMILY - A PLACE OF REFUGE FROM THE STORM - The family is the basic unit of the Kingdom of God on earth.  The Church can be no healthier than its families.

RANDY BOTT, Associate Teaching Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - BELIEVING THE PROMISES OF THE DOCTRINE AND COVENANTS - A sampling of the many promises made by the Lord to His saints to cheer and comfort them in the days before His Second Coming.

DOUGLAS E. BRINLEY, Associate Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - GOSPEL PERSPECTIVES ON MARRIAGE - The gospel is the plan that we came to earth to live.  Marriage is the highest ordinance of the gospel and essential to exaltation.  Here are some gospel helps to improve your marriage. 

BERNELL CHRISTENSEN, Independent Consultant, Family Relations/Research - THE SIX MINUTE SOLUTION TO HEALTHY RELATIONSHIP - How to handle touchy, controversial, and stressful conversations well.  Resolving differences in significant relationships - strengthening and building deep and lasting relationships.

JACK R. CHRISTIANSON, Institute Faculty, Orem Institute - THE BOOK OF MORMON IN OUR MODERN WORLD - The Book of Mormon was written for us today.  Out challenges, our victories, our families are all addressed in this glorious book of scripture.

PAUL CLAYSON, CES Special Faculty, Salt Lake City, UT  - THE VOICE OF THE SPIRIT - The "still small voice" of the Spirit can only be heard when we are calm, peaceful and spiritually and mentally in tune with the Lord.

RICHARD O. COWAN, Professor of Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - ANSWERS TO YOUR QUESTIONS FROM THE DOCTRINE AND COVENANTS - Called the "capstone of our religion" by President Ezra Taft Benson, the Doctrine and covenants provides the most information among the scriptures concerning  Church doctrine and organization.  This presentation provides keys to gaining the most from studying this book of modern scripture.

MARY ELLEN EDMUNDS, Author and lecturer, former director of training, MTC

Twin Falls ID - A MIGHTY CHANGE OF HEART- Mosiah 5:2, We will explore how we might achieve a mighty change of heart, becoming more Christ-like, more holy and pure, more "fit for the kingdom" (see also Moroni 10:32.)

Hailey ID and Bozeman MT - THE SOUL'S SINCERE DESIRE - Prayer is the soul's sincere desire and is critical to so much that we yearn for, dream for, and hope for.  

Lovell WY - GRATITUDE:  ENJOY, APPRECIATE AND SHARE - Peace comes in part from learning to enjoy, appreciate and be content with present blessings.  

Billings MT - STRENGTHENING HOME AND FAMILY - The basic unit of the Church is the family.  Ideas will be shared on specific ways to strengthen homes and families and experience greater enjoyment.

LAWRENCE FLAKE, Teaching Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - JOSEPH SMITH'S VISION OF THE KINGDOMS OF GLORY - Wilford Woodruss called "The Vision" (D&C 76) the "greatest revelation ever recorded by man."  We will discuss its historical background and a parallel poem the Prophet Joseph Smith wrote concerning it.  

RICHARD NEITZEL HOLZAPFEL, Associate Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - JOSEPH F. SMITH: THE MAN AND HIS MESSAGE - Using previously unpublished photographs from private and institutional sources, this presentation highlights the life of the sixth president of the Church.

GERALD L. HOMER, Institute Faculty, Pocatello ID - SECURING THE LORD'S PROTECTION IN PERILOUS TIMES - Presentation on the perilous times we live in, our need for the Lord's protection, specific promises of the Lord to protect his Saints, and doing our part.

CURTIS L. JACOBS, Institute Faculty, Logan UT - THE SECOND COMING AND YOU - What must still happen to the Church before the Second Coming of the Savior?  What are the signs?  Two words usually describe the Second Coming, "great" or "dreadful."  A look at both and how to be prepared for the events of the last days.

JERALD L. JEX - Recreation Ed/Religion Instructor, BYU-Idaho - WHAT THINK YE OF CHRIST?  WHO'S SON IS HE? - To know that God loves us, and that his love is found in the gift of his Son will bring joy and hope to our hearts, and assist in bringing our behavior in tune with our beliefs.

STANLEY A. JOHNSON, Assistant Professor, Ancient Scriptures, BYU-Provo - THE MIGHTY CHANGE OF HEART - How to obtain and keep the spirit of the Lord.  The process is presented in the scriptures and by the modern prophets.

TODD A. KNOWLES, Institute Faculty, Boise ID Institute - "I'M TRYING TO BE LIKE JESUS":  BECOMING MEN AND WOMEN OF CHRIST - The words of this simple Primary song become the quest of a lifetime.  We'll look at how the Savior's example in His premortal, mortal, and post-mortal life teaches us what manner of people we ought to be.

JEFFREY MARSH, Associate Professor, Ancient Scripture, BYU-Provo - PLAIN AND PRECIOUS TRUTHS REVEALED:  UNDERSTANDING AND USING THE JOSEPH SMITH TRANSLATION - This lecture can help members understand what changes were made by the Prophet Joseph Smith, where they can find these changes, and how to use them.

JACK S. MARSHALL, Institute Faculty, University of Utah - KEEPING PERSPECTIVE WHEN A CHILD STRAYS - Many families anguish over the inactivity of their loved ones.  Where can we find peace and hope?  Joseph Smith taught that the everlasting gospel is mightier in power to save than our anrrow finite minds can comprehend.

ROBERT J. MATTHEWS, Professor Emeritus, Ancient Scriptures, BYU-Provo - THE GREAT MILLENNIUM SHALL COME - The great millennium will begin with the glorious return of Jesus Christ to earth.  Many changes will take place, many purposes of the Lord will be fulfilled.  Ancient and latter-day prophecies will be cited.

MARK L. McCONKIE, Professor of Public Management, University of Colorado, Colorado Springs - JOSEPH SMITH, THE PROPHET AND SEER - An examination of the First Vision and coming forth of the Book of Mormon, illustrating eternal principles that have play in our lives, even as they did in the life of Joseph Smith.

 ELDER V. DALLAS MERRELL, General Authority, Emeritus - WILL YOU? IMPLEMENTING THE MISSION OF THE CHURCH EFFECTIVELY - Doctrines, principles, and experiences related to successfully, "inviting all to come unto Christ."

 ROBERT L. MILLET, Professor, Ancient Scriptures, BYU-Provo - REDEMPTION THROUGH THE HOLY MESSIAH - Some things simply matter more than others.  An understanding of the Atonement--what it is and how we can apply its principles--is fundamental to all spiritual growth.

ROBERT J. NORMAN, Institute Faculty, University of Utah - LATTER-DAY SCRIPTURES REVEAL THE PLAN OF SALVATION - This lecture will survey the scriptures that fill out the Plan of Salvation as known only by those who believe in the Restoration.

RICHARD OPENSHAW, Institute Director, Pocatello ID - LDS HYMNS: MY SOUL DELIGHTETH IN THE SONG OF THE HEART - The developmental history, power and beauty of LDS Hymns as they teach us the doctrines of the Restored Gospel of Jesus Christ.  Selected Hymns will be examined to illustrate doctrinal development in the Church.

VICKEY PAHNKE, Author, Lecturer, Vocalist -  THE PEACE THAT PASSETH ALL UNDERSTANDING - Discussing how trials of our faith come in ways that allow us to grow closer to the Lord as we stand fast in Him.

TODD B. PARKER, Associate Professor, Ancient Scripture, BYU-Provo  - TRUE DOCTRINE, UNDERSTOOD, CHANGES ATTITUDES AND BEHAVIOR - This presentation will discuss selected doctrines from the standard works and statements of the brethren on how an understanding and implementation of the Doctrines of the Gospel can improve one's life.

KIM M. PETERSON, CES Coordinator, Boulder CO - MADE WHOLE - This talk will examine the accounts of some who were healed by Christ.

O. CURTIS PETERSON, Institute Faculty, Pocatello ID - SCHOOL OF THE PROPHETS - A SCHOOL FOR THE SPIRIT - Historical background, purpose, instructions concerning doctrine and ordinances, and how each purpose of the School of the Prophets are fulfilled through the organizations and temples of the Church today.

GARY L. POLL, Institute Faculty, University of Utah - HELPING YOUTH AVOID TEMPTATION - Some suggestions for helping our children and grandchildren hold fast to the rod of iron.  special attention is given to teaching the sacredness of chastity.

KEITH F. RASMUSSEN, Institute Faculty, Pocatello ID - IF THE SOUL IS OF WORTH, THEN WHAT IS THE PRICE? - Ultimately we choose to be chosen.  With the help of family, scriptures, leaders and others we see that everyday principles help blow away clouds of forgetfulness and help to bring to remembrance the worth of souls, beginning with us.

GLEN J. RAWSON, Seminary Faculty, Blackfoot ID - THE LAW OF SACRIFICE - Sacrifice is a fundamental law still in effect today.  This lecture will explore what the law means and how it is to be applied in our time.  We have some unique challenges today as it relates to the law.  These will be discussed.

CLIFFORD J. RHOADES, Institute Faculty, Pocatello ID - THE ABRAHAMIC FACSIMILIES AND THE PLAN OF SALVATION - A review of the three facsimilies and the doctrines that they teach in relationship to the plan of salvation, covenants and the progressive doctrine of our Father in Heaven.

MATTHEW RICHARDSON, Assistant Professor, Church History and Doctrine, BYU-Provo - THERE IS HOPE SMILING BRIGHTLY BEFORE US  - In a world with so many problems, Saints stand boldly and declare "There is hope!"  Understanding the gospel based principle of hope is critical for peace and happiness.

DAVID J. RIDGES, Associate Director/Faculty, Orem Institute - FROM MORTALITY TO EXALTATION - THE PLAN OF SALVATION - Doctrinal details of our premortal life, the war in heaven, mortality, the postmortal spirit world, the Second Coming, the Millennium, Resurrection, final Judgement Day, the three degrees of glory and outer darkness, exaltation and then what?  Please bring your scriptures.

BRUCE K. SATTERFIELD, Religion Faculty, BYU-Idaho - PRIESTHOOD POWER AND KEYS - A discussion of the purpose and importance of priesthood authority or power, the purpose and importance of priesthood keys, and the history of the restoration of priesthood authority and keys to Joseph Smith.

JEFFREY L. SERMON, CES Special Faculty, Provo UT - BUILDING BRIDGES TO THE HEART - Through music, scripture, and stories we examine those Christlike traits that, when acquired, will lead us step by step to our Heavenly home.  We learn to understand ourselves, the gospel plan, and other principles that help us achieve happiness.

CALVIN R. STEPHENS, Institute Faculty, Ogden Institute - MISSION AND WORK OF THE SAVIOR - Covers five assignments the Savior has from His Father.

SCOTT STRONG, Faculty, Ancient Scripture; Administrator in Admissions Counseling, BYU-Provo - WHY THE FAMILY IS AT THE HEART OF THE GOSPEL - Doctrinal look at the Proclamation on the Family.

PAUL WARNER, Director/Chaplain, Student Athletic Center, BYU-Provo - GOSPEL POWER IN MY LIFE - Discussion of principles that bring power into a person's life enabling happiness and hope.

A. BRYAN WESTON, CES Zone Administrator - PROVIDING FOR MY SAINTS - ENOUGH AND TO SPARE - The Lord in the scriptures, speaks of providing blessings for both the rich and the poor.  President Gordon B. Hinckley, in announcing the new Perpetual Education Fund, introduced an opportunity for members to assist one another in sharing their blessings.  We will discuss opportunities and needs and the Lord's invitation to be blessed.

S. MICHAEL WILCOX, Institute Faculty, University of Utah - THE BLESSINGS OF THE HOUSE OF THE LORD - A discussion focusing on the great and redeeming power of the house of the Lord for both the living and the dead.
