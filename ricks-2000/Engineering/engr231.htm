Engr 231 Engineering Thermodynamics   3
Prerequisite: Math 263
Fundamentals of classical thermodynamics. Energy and entropy concepts in power and
refrigeration cycles; ideal gas mixtures; psychrometrics; combustion; irreversibility; availability.
For general engineering, civil engineering, mechanical engineering majors and other interested
engineering majors.
