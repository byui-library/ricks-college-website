Presidential Service Award
Lee S. and Shirley Carlson

Lee is a native of Adams County which is north of Denver, Colorado. He was a member of the U.S. Army Occupation Forces in Korea and attended Colorado State University (majoring in general agriculture) on the G.I. Bill. Lee graduated in 1951 and worked on the family farm.

Shirley was born in Southern Colorado but was raised in Denver where she attended Cathedral High School, a parochial school. She graduated from the University of Colorado in 1950 with a degree in elementary education and taught Kindergarten for three years.

Lee and Shirley met through friends in Boulder, Colorado. They were married in 1951, and Shirley, though not a member of the Church, served with Lee as stake dance directors. She was baptized and became a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in 1956.

Following a disastrous drought, Lee quit farming and operated a dairy for ten years. He bought and sold cattle and then obtained his real estate license. In the early 1970s he bought a real estate agency, renaming it Carlson Associates a few years later, and remaining active in its operation.

Shirley, meanwhile, was a mother to seven children, five boys and two girls. The family now includes 30 grandchildren and two great-grandchildren. Four of their sons are involved with their father in the real estate business.

Lee has been patriarch of the Denver North Colorado Stake for 27 years. He has been a bishop, served in a bishopric, taught in the Sunday School, and now teaches the high priests in his home ward.

Shirley has been a volunteer with LDS Family Services for 28 years. She was a stake Young Women’s president for 12 years, a ward Relief Society president three times, and now serves as Public Affairs Director of her stake.

Lee and Shirley make their home on a small farm in Thornton, Colorado.
