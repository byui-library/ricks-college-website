MATH STUDY CENTER

Drop-in: Open 53 hours a week where a staff of tutors is available to answer math questions.  In the math study center you may work on your homework and ask questions.  Textbooks, solution manuals, and math strategies are on hand for many of the math classes. 

Test Review:  Students may also review their Math 100 and 101 tests at the Math Study Center during all center hours (except Saturdays).  Students will need the test printout and a picture ID for test help.

One-on-one Tutors:  Students who need more individualized customized help may sign up for a personal tutor.  Tutors are available for all math classes offered at Ricks and are arranged for at the Learning Assistance desk from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday.  Students may meet with tutors three times a week.  There is no charge for tutors.

Lecture-based College-prep Math Classes:  Some students need additional background before taking college level math courses.  We offer three courses taught in a lecture based setting.  Students should meet with one of the faculty who teach these courses before they enroll to determine which level will be best.
