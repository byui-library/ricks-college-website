Married at Ricks
We were unsure of what it was going to be like to be married at Ricks College--where the primary population is single. To our surprise and great satisfaction, we have found married life to be wonderful so far. We don't feel like outcasts or anything; in fact, we are in a wonderful Married Ward. We have also found that Ricks sponsors activities for married students. One of these activities is a Marriage Enrichment Retreat. Glenn and I had the priviledge of attending the first one of the school year on September 24 and 25. We were taught many important values on communication and how to build a successful marriage. The speakers were Brother and Sister MacBeth. These are some of the things we saw and experienced.
