Eliza R. Snow Award for the Performing and Fine Arts
Gary L. Price

There’s a soft spot in sculptor Gary Price’s heart for Ricks College. "I never dreamed of going to college. Nobody in my family had gone to college. I barely made it through high school," Gary says. But after arriving at Ricks in the fall of 1973, ". . . Because of the environment, the individual attention, the devotionals, etc., I was on the honor roll!"

Art has been both an escape and a salvation to Gary. A native of Montpelier, Gary was a young boy of six when he tragically lost both of his parents. Gary came through a difficult childhood that many would find crushing, but he instead decided to take that experience and do all he could to add something positive to life.

Through a childhood friend and a fellow convert to the Church, Gary’s lifelong interest in art was nurtured by enrolling at Ricks. There, instructors like Oliver Parson and fellow students like Leon Parson, served as role models. After leaving Ricks Gary served in the Germany South Mission, studied sculpture at what is now Utah Valley State College and graduated in 1981 from the University of Utah with a fine arts degree.

To support himself he worked in a foundry in Mapleton, Utah, and met his wife, the former Lanea Richards. They were married in 1981 and are the parents of five boys.

Gary sold the rights to his first sculpture to a Provo art gallery in 1980. His early work was western-themed. In 1986 he began sculpting game birds, and, later, exotic birds. Since then he has concentrated on contemporary themes. In July, Gary will be presented with the Governor’s Mansion Artist Award in the state of Utah. Gary’s motto is, "uplifting the human spirit," and that motif runs throughout his art.

His works can be found throughout the United States, and in Hong Kong and Iceland, among other divergent places. In 1996 Gary sold three of his pieces to Ricks at a greatly reduced cost. They are located near the entrance to the library, in front of the Austin Building, and in the Landscape Horticulture Gardens.
