NSO Team

What is the NSO Team?
The NSO Team is a group of eight student directors who work together in planning and implementing the various New Student Orientation programs and events.

Applications for the 2002-2003 NSO Team will be accepted through September 20, 2002.  

Interviews will be conducted from September 23-26.  

Those selected to the team must attend a mandatory retreat on the 27-28th of September.    

NSO Team Application.

Recruitment Coordinator:
Oversees the recruitment, screening, selection, and pairing of Y-Team leaders, Y-Team modifications (splitting/pairing, etc.), Y-Team Boundaries, Y-Team correspondence and all other matters relating to Y-Team recruitment and management.  Would oversee focus groups and surveys regarding Y-Team Leaders.  Will work closely with the New Student Coordinator.  Is responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation.  

New Student Coordinator:
Oversees new student mailouts, dial-a freshmen program, new student check-in, and coordination of special student groups (married students, transfer students, non-traditional students, etc.).  Will work with apartment managers in determining new student tenants at each complex in forming Y-Team Boundaries (in conjunction with the Recruitment Coordinator).  Would oversee focus groups and surveys regarding new students.  Is responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation. 

Training Coordinator:
Oversees the planning and implementation of the annual Y-Team Conference and Winter/Summer/block Y-Team Leader trainings.  Updates and prepares Y-Team Leader Handbooks and Mentor/Assistant Mentor training materials, etc.  Coordinates training for NSO Team and NSO Board.  May be responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation.

Publicity Coordinators (2 positions):
Oversees all aspects of marketing/publicity for NSO events including logo development, posters, flyers, billboards, website management, event programs, photos, video footage, etc.  Also updates and manages the printing of the Student Handbook, Y-Team Passports, Y-Team Posters, NSO Mailings, and photo and video footage for NSO history, presentations, etc.  Will produce the annual New Student Magazine (formerly a tabloid newspaper).  Is responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation.  

Involvement Coordinator:
Oversees recruitment, selection, training, and management of the NSO Board and event day-of volunteers.  Will coordinate weekly NSO Board meetings, board training, etc.  Will oversee on-going involvement with Y-Team Association, and Y-Team Mentor Groups.  Is responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation.

NSO Spirit Competition Coordinator:
Oversees all aspects of the Orientation Spirit Competition including:  Y-Team Passports (works with Publicity Coordinators), stamps, stickers, judges, spirit competition tables, Y-Team Flags, and Spirit Competition activity for winning Y-Teams.  Oversees all prize recruitment and selections for Orientation activities.  

On-Going Orientation Coordinator:
Assists NSO Director in coordinating Pre-Campus, and On-Going Orientation initiatives such as Passport Program, Grad Fair, and more.  Will work with video, internet, and audio productions as part of Pre-Campus Orientation, and event programming with On-Going Orientation.  Is responsible for at least one major NSO event/activity during each orientation.
