Irrigation
IRRIGATION
CLASS CODE: HORT 329 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Irrigation of turf and landscape plants. Design of irrigation systems and selection of components.
DESCRIPTION: A course to familiarize the student with irrigation design and installation procedures.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1-Working knowledge of frequently used irrigation components
2-Principles of basic hydraulics, precipitation, rates, pipe sizing, water pressure
3-Wiring and operation of electric values and controllers
4-Sprinkler repairs
5-Drip irrigation
REQUIREMENTS:
PREREQUISITES: Hort 230
