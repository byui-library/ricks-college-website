The Eliza R. Snow Society for the Arts has been established to support artistic expression that, as President Kimball urged, edifies us and prepares us for heaven.  It is a society of friends who wish to share their means to make it possible for Ricks College students to develop their talents and achieve their full potential in the areas of theatre, dance, music and the visual arts.

Scholarships to the Snow Society will be used for the following:

  • Scholarships for talented and deserving students who, without financial assistance, could not fully participate.
  • Specialized equipment, such as lighting systems, dance floor surfaces, sound systems, and painting/sculpture tools and equipment.
  • Additional and replacement instruments for the symphony orchestra, the chamber orchestra, and the several ensemble groups.
  • Performing and visual-arts productions held during the summer months.

"Our art must be the kind which edifies man, which takes into account his immortal nature, and which prepares for heaven."

Spencer W. Kimball
