Courses I Teach

Auto 100 - Basic Automotive - 2 credits  
(Click on title)

      This class is for those who have little or no experience working on cars.  You don't have to have a car to take this class.  It is mostly lecture/discussion, with some demonstrations in the shop.  We learn automotive terms, what the parts of the car are, and what they do.  We also learn basic maintenance procedures and when they should be performed.  It is taught for one hour twice a week.  Chapter assignments are due as each chapter is discussed, and quizzes are given regularly covering the information in the chapters.  A test is also given covering some basic service tools and common parts on the car.

                                                               

 


Auto 118 -Automotive Maintenance - 3 credits
(Click on title)

      This class is for those who have had either Auto 100 or quite a bit of high-school automotive experience.  You DO need a car to take this class!  We learn about the basic maintenance procedures cars need and then you do them to your car in the lab.  We have classroom discussions and demonstrations during our Tuesday two hours, and on Thursdays you work on selected jobs in the shop.  The procedures we do are the kind that you can do on your own cars after the class is over, and will help you save money on repairs and maintenance for as long as you own or drive a car.

                                                                    

 

Auto 165 - Drive Mechanisms - 4 credits
(Click on title)

     Drive Mechanisms is intended primarily for Automotive majors.  It deals extensively with clutches, manual transmissions, drive lines, axle shafts, differentials and drive axles.  The work in the lab is limited to these areas.  The class meets at 7:00 a.m. for an hour, then we have two sections of hands-on lab, each section lasting three hours.  You are expected to replace at least one clutch, rebuild a transmission or transaxle, and a differential to pass the class.  There are other jobs you will need to do to learn more and get full benefit from the class.  You do NOT work on your own car in this class.  If your car needs this type of work, it can be assigned to another student in the class.  

                                                                         


                              

  Auto 265 - Automatic Transmissions - 5 credits

     Automatic Transmissions class is limited to Automotive Majors.  It is a challenging class that deals exclusively with the design, operation, diagnosis and repair of automatic transmissions and transaxles used in today's cars.   Students spend 4 hours per week in class work and 8 hours per week working on transmissions in the lab.   Students remove transmissions from cars, repair them, re-install them, and test them for proper operation.
