Team Sports
TEACH METH
CLASS CODE: PE*C 334 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Teaching methods of golf and tennis. Teaching methods of basketball.
DESCRIPTION: Teaching methods of golf and tennis will be taught two hours a week with an additional hands-on lab for a block class.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: The student will be able to:
1. coach and teach team sports such as football, volleyball, soccer, and basketball.
2. Organize practices, tournaments, and drills.
3. Teach skills and game structures.
4. Understand and exhibit skill of team selection.
5. Assess management and instructional strategies to maximuze activity time and success.
6. Apply rules and skills for team sports.
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Class meets three days a week for lecture and lab.
2. Attendance and participation.
3. Written tests and quizzes.
4. Written paper(s) approved or assigned by instructor.
5. Project(s) approved or assigned by instructor.
PREREQUISITES: Volleyball prerequisite: Must have PEC 145.
Basketball prerequisite: Must have PEC 141.
OTHER: 1. No unexcused absences allowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2002
