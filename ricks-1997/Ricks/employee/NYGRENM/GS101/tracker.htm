TEACHING-SHARING TRACKER

 

Instructions:

  • You will commit to 20-30 minutes each week talking to your 'Partner' about what you are learning.  If you choose to communicate with a partner via a distance medium (i.e. e-mail, v-mail, telephone, etc) 10-15 minutes may be a more appropriate time frame to complete this exercise/process.  Ideal 'Distance-Partners' may be a parent, family member, or special friend.

  • Make a commitment with your partner to be religious about this exercise.  Set a specific time when you will do it.  If you chooses to do this with a 'distance partner', you will want to arrange for them to have access to a book.  They will read the chapter we are studying in class and complete the exercise of creating their own Creed Document.

  • This exercise/process is designed to last for twelve consecutive weeks.  The objective is to discuss concepts that each of you are learning in class and/or from the text.

  • Each week 'log' the tracker below and make appropriate notations.  Each visit MUST take place during the week noted.  At the conclusion of the (12) week period you will turn in this log, certified by your signature that the information is both accurate and truthful
