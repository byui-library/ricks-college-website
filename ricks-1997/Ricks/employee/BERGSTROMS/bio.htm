Summary

Scott J. Bergstrom currently serves as the Director of Institutional Research at Ricks College. His two primary duties include outcomes assessment and teacher evaluation. Prior to assuming these duties in September 1997, he was a Visiting Professor at Ricks College, working with faculty and administrators on instructional technology initiatives and other special projects in the improvement of teaching and learning.

He is also currently serving as the Executive Administrator of Aviation Industry Computer-Based Training Committee (AICC), an international consortium of experts in aviation computer-based training who set guidelines for the use of technology in commercial aviation training.

Previously, he served as the director of the Airway Science Distance Education Center at the University of North Dakota's Center for Aerospace Sciences where he managed the development and operation of a satellite-based distance education center and numerous multimedia development projects. Prior to that, he was a Training System Analyst with CAE-Link where he designed and developed training systems for military aviators.

Dr. Bergstrom received his Ph.D. from Brigham Young University in Instructional Science and Technology.
