Introduction to Family & Consumer Science
INTRO FCS
CLASS CODE: HFED 101 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Basic concepts, philosophy, career exploration and professional development in Family and Consumer Science Education.
DESCRIPTION: In this course you will be introduced to the future direction for FCS oriented professionals and discover a host of opportunities for students interested in the profession. Throughout the semester, we will concentrate on professional careers in related fields of FCS and focus on developing each student's own personal career path.
TOPICS: Future Directions for FCS Oriented Professions; Your Plan of Education and Professional Development; Internships and Other Pre-professional Employment; Professions Serving Families and Consumer; The Growth and Status of Our Profession; The First Steps of Professional Career Planning; The Career market of Today - What to Expect; Careers in the Arts and Sciences; Careers in Business; Careers in Education and Cooperative Extension; Careers in Human Services; Careers in Government and Public Policy; Developing an Innovative Career Path
OBJECTIVES: The student should be able to demonstrate in their questions, papers, presentations and exams:
1. Consideration for career preferences and identify special interests and values regarding career choices in FCS, set goals, and formulate a plan for educational professional development.
2. Recognition of the importance of and make a commitment to continued professional development through membership and involvement in related professional organizations, reading of research and related publications and continued learning.
3. An ability to examine the career market of tomorrow, including the qualities employers will be looking for and the types of jobs that are expected to be in plentiful supply.
REQUIREMENTS: Personal Goal Statement; Interview of Professional; Journal Entries; Resource File
PREREQUISITES: For Family and Consumer Education majors only or those very interested in becoming majors.
