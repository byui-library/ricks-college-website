Group Project Overview

Your team have been hired by a wealthy Rexburg resident, Sharon Bucks, to put together a marketing plan for a restaurant.  Sharon has pretty much given your team free rein on this project with the following exceptions:

  • It must be located in the Rexburg area (Rexburg address)
  • The restaurant must produce at least one product (e.g. salsa, corn bread, salad dressing, etc.) that will be marketed through out, at least, Idaho and Utah. 
  • The restaurant must have a grand opening on September 15th.

Good Luck.  Sharon and all of Rexburg is counting on you.
