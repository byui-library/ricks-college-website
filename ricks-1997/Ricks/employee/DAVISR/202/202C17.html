RENAISSANCE ART CH 17

 

MAN: Put more emphasis on Man than on God

a. Principle aim of Western man was fame, success

b. Middle class grew in power through banking and trade, then commissioned portraits of themselves

Individual portraits had not been done since Roman times

c. New emphasis on youth and children

Favorite subject is David (youth/vitality=Greek, thinker=Roman)

d. human dignity and worth now emphasized

e. Christ is now shown as baby, not little man

f. Artists begin to include themselves in paintings

 

NATURE: medieval art had nature as make-believe, a symbol of heaven

a. Now man is in love with life, studies all around him.

study of Nature promotes study of Man

Man is a microcosm

b. anatomy studies begin; medicine begins

c. Perspective imposes reason and order on the art world; it shows Man in his logical place and surroundings

 

STUDY: becomes all important

a. The best pose is a Ren Man with book in hand. (Surely Man can solve all by his reason.)

b. Ideal man is possessed of virtu, is a philosopher.

(Shakespeare's Prospero in The Tempest/and Leonardo)

c. Study Greeks in thought and deed. Copy it all.

d. Books for everyone.

 

ARCHITECTURE: starts with Pazzi chapel, Brunelleschi

has no forerunner, copies no style

is light, airy, no weight

doesn't try to impress you with its size.
