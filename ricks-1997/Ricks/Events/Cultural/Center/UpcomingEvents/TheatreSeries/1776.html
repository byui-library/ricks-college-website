"1776"

November 18-22, 1997
Matinees: November 22, 2 p.m.; November 24, 4 p.m.

Snow Drama Theatre

.....It's that long, hot, yet eventful summer of '76 in Philadelphia. John Hancock presides over the Continental Congress. John Adams endeavors to convince his colleagues to "Vote Yes! Vote for independancy!" The action commences in May and climaxes in July. The Declaration of Independance is passed and signed by the members. As the New York Times says, "You don't have to be a historian to love 1776."

music and lyrics by Herman Edwards, book by Peter Stone
