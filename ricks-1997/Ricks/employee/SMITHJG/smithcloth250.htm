COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 250
Title: Fashion Design Credit: 2
Text: Inside Fashion Design   (TATE)

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire the knowledge and skills necessary for creative application of designing for apparel garments.  Upon completion of this course the student should be able to:
   1.  Demonstrate skills in identifying apparel styles
  2.  Apply specific design principles
  3.  Derive solutions to design problems
   4.  Design apparel garment from given criteria
  5.  Locate designs for apparel scrap

Description:
Designing apparel clothing, & identification of basic component parts such as sleeves, necklines, collars, pants, etc.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Reference file system containing apparel clothing component parts
2.  Scrap
3.  3 apparel design projects  with cost sheets, design plans, and sketches
