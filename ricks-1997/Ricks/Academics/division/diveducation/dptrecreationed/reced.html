The Department of Recreation Education offers degrees designed to address the requirements of those who intend to go to a university. Career options for those who complete a four year degree in this field include choices in the areas of Outdoor Recreation, Therapeutic Recreation, Municipal Administration, Youth Agencies and Commercial Recreation.

In addition, the Department offers an elective program of instruction in outdoor leadership. Special emphasis is placed on the experimental teaching and leadership of one's own family in the outdoors. The department is a strong resource to the general student body and community.

Facilities

The Ricks College Outdoor Learning Center at Badger Creek

The Outdoor Learning Center is owned and operated by Ricks College. Located on the north end of the beautiful Teton Valley, the ranch spreads across 200 acres of rolling hills and valleys. The Teton Mountains and Targhee National Forest are present in panoramic view. Facilities include a campground, 10 small cabins, lodge, resident director's cabin and a logistics center. A high adventure ropes course with seventeen elements, horseback riding center, cross country ski trails, rope tow for sledding and skiing and a canoeing pond are available for programmed activities.

Facilities are available for rental between September and May when not in use for academic programs. Cabins sleep 6, with propane light and heat. Restroom facilities for the cabins consist of two outhouses. No running water, kitchen or shower facilities are available for cabin occupants. The lodge sleeps 20 with kitchen, bathroom and shower facilities. Bedding and utensils must be provided by individual groups. For more information or to schedule the facility, contact the Recreation Education Dept.

Special Programs

 

Family and Youth Programs:

Scheduled programs include: Family Reunions, Youth Conferences, Outdoor Especially for Youth and special workshops for grandparents and grandchildren. Programs can also be scheduled for special group gatherings. Program activities include: canoeing, horseback riding, ropes course, black powder rifles, outdoor survival workshops, crafts, handcart treks, and initiative course. For information on scheduling and cost, call Continuing Education, (208) 356-1040 or Continuing Education, ASB, Ricks College, Rexburg, ID 83460-8011.

Summits:

The Department of Recreation Education offers extended expedition experiences.

Spring Summit:

Three to four week trip to the Canyonlands area of southern Utah. Students spend a week mountain biking, a week of backpacking, and a week of canoeing and white water rafting on the Green and Colorado Rivers. Participants travel through geological wonders and visit ancient Native American sites. Students need to be in excellent physical condition to participate. Courses offered include: Mountain biking, Backpacking, Outdoor leadership, Camp Skills, Personal Heath and First Aid.

Winter Summit:

A two part trip held during Christmas break in the Tetons. The first trip is four day long before Christmas. The second part is six days long between New Year's Day and the beginning of Winter Semester. Students travel on back country skis, carry packs and pull gear sleds. Shelters include snow caves and tents. Students need to be in excellent physical condition and provide their on warm, functional winter outdoor clothing. Courses offered include: Winter Camping, Backcountry sking and Outdoor leadership.
