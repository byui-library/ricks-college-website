Horticulture Orientation is required of all Hort departmental majors, and should be completed
during the fall semester of the freshman year, or the
second year if you were not enrolled in the major at that time.
You are expected to complete the following assignments:

1.   Read one of the following motivational books:
          "The Magic of Thinking Big" by David Schwartz
          "How to Win Friends and Influence People" by Dale Carnegie
          "The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People" by Steven R Covey
          "A Passion for Excellence" by Tom Peters and Nancy Austin
          "The Things that Matter Most" by Lowell L. Bennion
There are copies of these books at either the bookstore or the library.  Prepare a report (minimum
3 pages typewritten) on how the principles discussed
in the book you read can be applied to your life.  Try some of the suggestions or ideas and report
on the results.  (If you know of a book that you believe
is of equal caliber, ask the professor for approval to use it instead of the above references.) This
report is due by 16 October.

2.   Learning Assistance Lab individual needs assessment survey (in class 18 Sept.)

3.   Attend University Transfer Day on 16 October--MC West Ballroom.  Fill out report form and
turn it in to me there, or in my mail box.

     4.   After viewing the video "The Greening of the Planet," in the library viewing area, please
write an essay regarding your feelings about the
     role of Horticulture and Agriculture in the global perspective.  What do you feel is our
stewardship, and how will it influence your career
     choices?  Due by 16 October.  (you may turn it in sooner if you would like to)

     5.   On your own time, take the self-guided tour (available at the McKay library information
desk), then turn in the signed completion form to me. 
     You may be required to do this for another class.  If you are, then you can count it toward this
requirement.  Just turn in a photocopy, or have
     the library sign two of them for you.   Due by 16 October
