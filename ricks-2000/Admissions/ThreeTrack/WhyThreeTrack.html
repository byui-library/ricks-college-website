WHY THREE-TRACK?

Prior to Three-Track, just under 9,000 students were enrolled during the fall and winter semesters. With the implementation of a year-round track system, the total enrollment for any one semester, fall, winter or summer, is expected to reach 11,600 in 2005.

By operating on an expanded year-round track system, the total number of students who will actually spend time on campus during a calendar year will rise to 14,500 by 2005, although only 11,600 will be enrolled at one time.

Because of Three-Track, we are able to accommodate significantly more students with minimal additional resources.
