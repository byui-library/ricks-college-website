Instructor:  Reese Nelson

Office: ETB 162

Phone:  356-2014

Class Meeting times:  11:00 to 11:50 Monday and 3:00 to 4:50 Tuesday and Thursday

Purpose:  Learn the identification and landscape value of herbaceous annual and perennial flowers, including bulbs, ground covers, ornamental grasses, ferns and water plants.

 

Results Which You Can Expect:  At the end of this course you should be able to correctly identify up to 200 of the most popular flowers.  You will be able to think about flowers as a horticulturist--that is, visualize how they fit into a landscape setting.  In order to do this you will learn uses and characteristics such as hardiness, height, flower color, flowering period and shade tolerance.

 

Required Text:  Manual of Herbaceous Ornamental Plants by Steven M. Still.  Many additional books and visual materials will also be available for study in BEN 144.

 

Class Procedures:   This will be an informal class with most time spent outside observing and learning by seeing the plants as they grow.  Books, slides and videos will be available for further study individually or in groups.  Group study is recommended.  You may be assigned to a study group.  A lab assistant will be available for group tutoring and to give individualized makeup quizzes.  You will be introduced to about 12 new plants each class period (36 plants per week).  Each student will be assigned two plants to research the information contained on the attached Landscape Horticulture Flower Scoop Sheet.  Students will report this information to the class during the next class period.

 top

Quizzes and Exams:  Since this course requires memorization, quizzes will be used to motivate you to learn the names and characteristics of plants.  A quiz will be given each Tuesday and Thursday.  Quizzes will be comprehensive.  Emphasis will be given to the most recently learned plants, but any plant previously given can be included.  You will be asked to identify plants by scientific and common names and give one additional identifying or use characteristic such as family name, height, hardiness zone, and shade tolerance.  A comprehensive final exam will be given on Oct. 19.  You may request an individualized makeup quiz on the plants you miss on regular quizzes.  Simply give the list of missed plants to the lab assistant and schedule a testing time (minimum of 10 plants per makeup quiz).

 top

Grading:  (1) You will receive one point toward your grade for each plant name learned (correctly spelled genus and species).  Keep a master list of plants correctly identified on quizzes.  If you miss a plant on your list on a subsequent quiz, you must remove it from your list.  You will turn in a copy of your list the last week of the semester.  (2) You will receive up to 5 points for each scoop sheet written and presented to the class.  (3) The highest 10 to 20 % of test scores for each quiz will receive one extra bonus point.
