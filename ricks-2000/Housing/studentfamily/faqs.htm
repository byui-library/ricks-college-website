Are BYU-Idaho student families required to live in approved housing?
Of course not.  Housing units that rent to families are privately owned and do not need to be approved.  However, make sure you understand the contract terms and personally inspect the apartment you will be renting to insure it will meet your needs.

 
How often is the web page that lists all off-campus student family housing updated? 
The family housing advertisements are updated on a daily basis.

 
What do I do if all of the numbers on the list seem to be full?
Check to local papers to find houses for rent.  Continue to call ads posted on this website as well.  

 
What is subsidized housing and how does one qualify?  
Subsidized housing is housing where rent is based on income.  There are a few different subsidized housing programs.  Check with the units listed on our site to find out what qualifications are required.

 
Is there a need for student family housing at BYU-Idaho?  
Yes.  Developers are encouraged to contact the Housing Office to get information on the need for student family housing in Rexburg.

 
What is the average price of rent for student families?  
Rent prices vary between $250 per month to $600.  The average rent price is right at $400 per month.

 
Do single student approved units rent to married students?  
If approved single student units do not fill, they may rent a few apartments to married students.  One of the renters has to be a BYU-Idaho student.
