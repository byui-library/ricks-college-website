Ricks College News Release
April 3, 2000

Contract awarded for renovation of
Smith Building at Ricks

REXBURG, Idaho — A large classroom building at Ricks College will get a major overhaul this summer following the end of Winter Semester.

Work will begin April 24 on the first phase of a major renovation project in the four-story Joseph Field Smith Building.

The contract has been awarded to R.E. Beck Construction of Idaho Falls. Design work is already being done by JHS Architects of Pocatello.

Physical Plant Director Chuck Frost says the building was constructed during the 1960s during the age of typewriters. Inadequate ventilation has become a significant problem with between 400 and 500 computers in use throughout the classroom building.

"There have been revolutionary changes in the way they are teaching business and English," Frost says. "With so many PCS, it gets hot and stuffy in the computer labs."

The building will be closed during the summer and will reopen in mid-August just prior to the start of the Fall Semester. Initial work will include asbestos abatement in sections of the first floor and in mechanical chaseways throughout the building. The asbestos is found in sprayed-on, fire-proofing insulation.

During the first phase of a project that may take several years to complete, much of the work will be confined to the first floor. The chiller and ductwork will be replaced on the first floor. Because of the ductwork, the ceilings on the first floor will also be replaced.

Electrical service to the building will be replaced. An economics computer lab on the north end of the first floor will be doubled in size.

All windows throughout the entire building will be replaced during the summer. And the two stairwells on the east side of the building will be enclosed to comply with current fire codes.
