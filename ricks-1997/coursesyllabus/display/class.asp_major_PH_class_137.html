WEATHER AND CLIMATE
WEATHER-CLIM
CLASS CODE: PH 137 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An introductory study of the atmospher including weather,climate, methods of weather forecasting and the environmental impact of man.
DESCRIPTION: An introductory study of the atmosphere inclding weather, climate, methods of weather forecasting, and the environmental impact of man.
TOPICS: Measurements, Powers of 10,
Motion, Speed,Velocity,
Acceleration,Circular Motion
Newton's 1st & 2nd Laws
Optics
Electricity

Nuclear Physics
Volcanism, Mineral, Rocks, Solar System,Moon Phases,Universe,Chemical Bonding, Solutions,Acids,bases
OBJECTIVES: To help students learn about the physics of the lower atmosphere and about the energy relationships as weather occurs. To learn the basic cloud formations and how storms form and their characteristics.
REQUIREMENTS: Text: An Introduction to Physical Science. A quiz each class period to enhance overall score. No graded on attendance but performance of overall participation of class.
