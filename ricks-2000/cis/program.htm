Graduating in CIS

The Associate in Arts and Sciences in Computer Information Systems (major code 244) allows a student to enter the workforce as an information professional upon graduation and/or transfer to a four-year college or university. The program is designed to meet the requirements for the American Assembly of Collegiate Schools of Business (ASCSB). Transfer students should check the program requirements of the school to which they are transferring and with a CIS advisor to assure that core requirements for transfer are met.

A Program Planning Worksheet is available to print (Acrobat Reader required).

Note:

  • No "D" credit is allowed in computer courses for a CIS major.
  • A keyboarding skill of 35 words or more per minute is strongly suggested.
  • All CIS majors must enroll in B193 their first semester as a freshman.
  • Economics 111 and 112 should be taken the freshman year as part of the college's Social Science general education requirement.
  • Before taking a math class, see your advisor.
  • Students interested in an internship should register for B298a.
