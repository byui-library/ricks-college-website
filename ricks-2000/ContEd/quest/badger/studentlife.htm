STUDENT LIFE

The Logistics Center at Badger Creek makes a perfect high mountain valley classroom.

 

 

 Room and board are provided while students reside at Badger Creek.
While staying at Badger Creek, laundry facilities are provided at no charge to the students of Quest programs.  Soap and other laundry supplies are also provided.   Dormitory life is supported by a resident assistant member of the staff.

 
 

 

 A computer lab with computers networked to the Internet provides a way of completing homework assignments as well as staying in touch with family and friends via e-mail.
 

 

After the camp-fire burns down in the evening from the fireside discussions, young women retire to the main lodge dorm and  young men return to their accommodations in the guest cabins situated in the aspen trees.
