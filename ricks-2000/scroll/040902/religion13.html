Elder Robert D. Hales: Dispelling darkness by light of Christ

by Kelly Smurthwaite
SMU01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

ELDER HALES
Elder Robert D. Hales spoke about having the Light of Christ.

He began by remarking about how the world got to know more about the Church through the Salt Lake City Olympic and Paralympic Games.

“The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints continues to be ‘brought ‘forth out of obscurity and out of the darkness,’” he said.

He mentioned the theme of the Olympics, “Light the Fire Within,” and used it to illustrate how Church members can share their light and have it always within them.

“Each of us brings a light to the earth, the Light of Christ,” he said.

If members of the Church are righteous, they can receive another great light: the gift of the Holy Ghost, he said.

“It [the gift of the Holy Ghost] is received by those who have followed the principles of faith and repentance and have received the ordinance of baptism by immersion for the remission of sins,” he said.

Elder Hales also talked about how to generate the Light of Christ. By reading the scriptures along with prayer, fasting and serving others can all help generate the “light from within,” he said.

The world is full of darkness, Elder Hales said. He spoke of the importance of not letting the light of Christ decrease.

“If we let the Spirit flicker or fade by failing to keep the commandments or by not partaking of the sacrament or praying or studying the scriptures, the darkness of the adversary will surely come in,” he said.

“We who have the gift of the Holy Ghost must be true to its promptings so we can be a light to others,” he said.
