Honor Code Block
Ricks College has chosen to block certain categories of Internet sites for ethical and/or business reasons. Following is a brief description of each category that is being blocked:
Adult Entertainment Illegal Sexuality/Lifestyles Violence
Gambling Personal/Dating Tasteless Web Chat
Hacking Pornography

Adult Entertainment
Sites that have full or partial nudity, strip clubs, lingerie, adult-oriented chat rooms, erotica, sex toys, light adult humor and literature.

Gambling
Any site that promotes gambling or allows on-line gambling.

Hacking
Any site promoting questionable or illegal use of equipment and/or software to hack passwords, create viruses, gain access to other computers, etc.

Illegal
Sites that promote illegal activity such as murder, rape, drunk driving, prostitution and child pornography.

Personal/Dating
People meeting other people, personal ads, mail-order brides, escort services.

Pornography
Heterosexual activity involving one or two persons, hard core adult humor and literature, etc.
"Pornography, with its sleazy filth, sweeps over the earth like a horrible, engulfing tide.  It is poison.  Do not watch it or read it.  It will destroy you if you do.  It will take from you your self-respect.  It will rob you of a sense of the beauties of life.  It will tear you down and pull you into a slough of evil thoughts and possibly of evil actions.  Stay away from it.   Shun it as you would a foul disease, for it is just as deadly.  Be virtuous in thought and deed."  (President Gordon B. Hinckley, October 1997 General Conference Priesthood Session).

Sexuality/Lifestyles
Heterosexual acts involving more than two people; homosexual and bisexual acts; orgies, swinging, bestiality, sadism and masochism, fetishes, etc. and related hard core adult humor and literature.

Tasteless
Subjectively tasteless or inappropriate content, especially humor.

Violence
Any site promoting/containing information on violent acts, criminal activity, gangs, gross depictions, excess profanity.

Web Chat
Chat sites via http protocol.
