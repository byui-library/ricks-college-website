Tentative Syllabus
Hours Available:
I am available for help during the day, except when I am doing other class orientations and library meetings. I also work Wednesday evenings every other week (1:30-10:30pm), and  sometimes on a Saturday. On the weekends that I work, I will not be available on the Friday before.

Course description:
This class is designed to help you understand how/why information is collected, gathered, and organized, in order that you can research and retrieve it when needed for college classwork.

Text: Required.
Using the David O. McKay Library by Marcia Alldredge. These can be purchased in the bookstore, or copies are available, for reading or duplication, on Reserve at the McKay Library Circulation desk if needed. I do have some copies of the text also. Assigned reading sections are to be read before coming to class.

Some assignments may require printouts for which you may be charged by the library at the current rate per page.

Attendance:
I expect you to attend class. There are a few extenuating circumstances, but without attending class you may not know how to proceed, and will miss out on important matters. You may also miss a short quiz on either reading material or on class discussions. You should contact me and maybe also some of your classmates if you need to miss a class.

Final Exam:
The final exam may be handed out in class, likely Dec. 16, or we may make other arrangements. It may require some hands-on searching within the library, mini-essay questions and other questions arising from the class. A portion of the exam may be in the testing center.

Bibliography:
During the course of the class assignments you will be gathering citations about a topic of your choice. You may use this as an opportunity to work on getting a paper researched for another class, but what you hand in to me must conform to the requirements for this class. My requirements are these:
 • You need at least one citation from each major source.
 • You must specify at the start of the bibliography which style/format you will use for the citations, and stick with it.
 • The bibliography needs to be typed.

You can have more than these minimums, but they won’t necessarily count towards your grade. The due date will be discussed in class.
