Keys
When you need keys for campus buildings, you must present a key card with the proper signatures to the Locks and Keys Department in the Physical Plant building.  Handicapped students who need keys apply at the Housing Office or Key Office.

KRIC-FM (100.5)
The Ricks College - owned radio station broadcasts NPR and other public radio programs 24 hours per day to Southeastern Idaho and by way of 8 translators, to Southwestern Montana, Western Wyoming and Southern Idaho.  The weekly Tuesday devotionals are broadcast live at 2 p.m. and rebroadcast Tuesday evenings at 8:30 p.m.  Mens football and basketball games are also aired.

Learning Assistance Labs
The goal of the Learning Assistance Labs is to help students master basic academic skills and to supplement classroom instruction.  The LAL offers free assistance to students with the Reading Lab, Writing Center, Math Learning Lab, Study Skills Lab, and Tutoring Center.
Because of construction, our labs will be located in the following areas for the 1999/2000 school year:  Reading Lab and Writing Center - 3rd floor Clarke Building, Tutoring Center - MC 164, Math Learning Lab - 1st floor McKay Library (former TV viewing area), Study Skills -  2nd floor of the McKay Library.

Library
The David O. McKay Library is available for study as well as for checkout of books, records, tapes, videos, and any necessary equipment for viewing or listening of these materials.  The Library also provides many services to the students of Ricks College, including personal computers.  You may become familiar with these services by visiting with the library staff.  The library is open from 7 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. daily, Monday through Friday, Saturdays from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.  Closed Sundays, holidays and every Tuesday during devotionals.

Lockers
Lockers in the Hart Building can be rented on a semester basis by paying a fee at the Cashier's Office in the Kimball Building.  The receipt must then be taken to Hart 152 where the locker will be issued.  You may rent lockers in all academic buildings for $5.00 per year.  A table will be set up across from the Bookstore the first week of Fall semester to rent lockers, thereafter, go to the Key Office.

Lost and Found
The Lost and Found Department is located on the first floor of the Manwaring Student Center behind the Game Room, and is the best place to check for missing items.  Items will be held at least 60 days before being sold.

Manwaring Center
The Manwaring Center is the student activities center on campus and houses a bowling center, game room, snack bars, movie theater, ticket office, bookstore, ballrooms, conference rooms, ASRC offices, and other facilities.  Rooms may be scheduled for club or organization meetings in the Scheduling Office (call 356-2248)  behind the information desk.  Building hours are Monday through Thursday from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturdays from 8 a.m. - 12 midnight.

Manwaring Center Movies
The campus movie theater is located on the third floor of the Manwaring Student Center and provides full-length movies each Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday evenings.  Tickets are sold beginning at 8:30 a.m. the day of the movie.  Titles, show times, and dates are listed on the Campus Activity Calendar.

Married Student Association
Under the leadership of the Married Student Council, the MSA provides services and activities designed for married students.  The Married Student Council is made up of married students and plans activities sponsored by the Ricks College Marriage Enrichment Committee.  Activities include firesides, dances, socials, and overnight marriage enrichment retreats.  For more information contact the advisor, David Macbeth at 356-1120.

Married Student Housing
The Ricks College Housing Office acts as a referral source for married student housing in the Rexburg area, but does not endorse or require Ricks College approval of any housing unit for married students.

Masks, Painted Faces, or Tattoos
Masks or facial coverings of any type, including full or partial painting of the face, are inappropriate at any event sponsored by the college.  Tattoos, as a desecration of the body are inappropriate even if not visible.

Name Changes
Names of currently enrolled students can be changed only for the following reasons:  marriage, divorce, legal adoption, legal name changes, and misspelling. Certified documentation is required for all name changes, except misspellings.  International students cannot anglicize their names without permission of the International Student Advisor.  Name changes are made at the Registrar's Office.

Overnight Activities
Overnight co-ed activities such as spending the night at the dunes, camping, staying in motels or cabins and similar activities are prohibited.  No overnight guests of the opposite sex are permitted at any time in unmarried student housing.  Violations of this rule may result in immediate dismissal from the college.
