President David A. Bednar,
Charting a Course

Much of my life, professionally and personally, has been devoted to education. Over the course of my career, I have been associated with a number of educational institutions and have been greatly blessed by the training and experience I received within their halls and from many of their highly regarded professors. Little of my previous work, however, prepared me for the unique spirit and dedication I have encountered at Ricks College.

Ricks is extraordinary in many ways. On this campus is a deep, penetrating commitment to learning-spiritually and academically. Even though there is an almost epic history associated with Ricks College, everyone seems to sense there is also a bright, even more important future. No one is resting on past laurels.

Students who come to Ricks to begin their university education, or to prepare for the workplace, are not coming solely for the purpose of improving their future income possibilities. Their goals are more Christ-centered. Certainly they want to provide for themselves and their families and become financially successful. But more importantly, they want to follow the Savior. They want to be prepared for whatever He would have them do in life. These students want to serve and be useful. They want to make a difference. Indeed, they want to do all they can to make certain their journey through life is firmly rooted in the things that matter most: faith, family, and service.

And service is what gives substance to the Spirit of Ricks. That spirit, the love of the Savior, is prevalent among the students, faculty, staff, and administration at Ricks College. We will work hard to nurture and enhance this precious, refreshing spirit.

In addition to great students and caring faculty, Ricks has a beautiful campus. The facilities are magnificent. This is a wonderful environment that fosters learning and prepares students for a lifetime of work and service.

As I walk around campus, I am mindful of the many wise and dedicated people who have given much to create and preserve this great educational endeavor. They have established a solid foundation, and many have benefitted from their work and sacrifice.

Now, however, we must turn to the future. Our challenge is to prepare for the opportunities that the Twenty-first Century will surely bring. We need to preserve and enhance the Spirit of Ricks and help the young men and women who come here increase their understanding of all truths-both temporal and spiritual.

The future will bring unforeseen challenges. The world will change. New technological developments will improve our ability to communicate with one another. We will develop better ways to travel, improve medical treatments, and create products and materials that we have yet to dream about. The world will become a global community. Our understanding of diverse languages and cultures will, of necessity, be enhanced. With all of this will come new educational challenges and opportunities. Indeed, this new period in the history of the world will be exciting!

To prepare for these unprecedented changes, while still meeting the basic needs of our students, the administration and the Board of Trustees have identified four basic areas of focus for your philanthropic support in the years immediately ahead. They are:

  • 1. Expand the doors of educational opportunity for deserving and needy students,
  • 2. Enhance the academic environment,
  • 3. Support selected building and beautification projects, and
  • 4. Enhance existing programs and prepare for future opportunities.

The assistance we enjoy from you, our alumni and friends, will help us accomplish the primary mission of the school as stated by the Board of Trustees.

As we succeed in carrying out these initiatives, we will sustain the hopes and dreams of generations of students yet to come. We will add our part to the legacy that others began, we will encourage future students to continue their eternal quest, and we will be doing our part to preserve and enhance the unique Spirit of Ricks.

For your inspired and generous support, past and present, we thank you.

Sincerely,

David A. Bednar

President
