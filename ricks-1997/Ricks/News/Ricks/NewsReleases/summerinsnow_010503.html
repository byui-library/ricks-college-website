Ricks College News Release
May 3, 2001

Ricks College to feature weekly
entertainment series during summer

REXBURG, ID — Ricks College is gearing up for another series of summer shows as part of its Summer in the Snow entertainment series in Rexburg.

This year’s series will include a variety of summertime concerts, several dinners, folk dancers and a theatrical production.

Tickets for paid events will go on sale beginning May 29 at the Ricks College Ticket Office (tickets for Michael McLean are currently on sale as part of Education Week.) The ticket office is open weekdays while school is in session from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. For more information or to order tickets, call 356-2230 or go online at www.ricks.edu/tickets. Costs range from free to $15, depending on the show.

The Ricks College Choir will perform three times during the summer at the end of each term. The free concerts will be on Wednesdays, May 30, July 11 and Aug. 15, at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall.

Three free organ recitals will also be presented during the summer in the Taylor Chapel at 7:30 p.m. They include Darwin Wolford Thursday, June 28; Marsha Bjornn, Friday, July 27; and Rebecca Parkinson, Friday, Aug. 10.

Songwriter Michael McLean will return to the Hart Auditorium Thursday, June 7, at 7:30 p.m. to entertain during Education Week. McLean has been entertaining audiences for more than two decades with his music that touches the soul. The concert is open to the public and concert-goers do not need to be registered for Education Week to attend. Tickets are $8 and $6. 

The Vigilante Theatre Company of Bozeman, Mont., will present their ever popular dinner theater show “Mystery Manor” Thursday, June 14, at 7 p.m. At this very special dinner at Mulrooney Hall, everyone is an honored guest . . . but someone has unsavory intentions. The hosts, Charles and Victoria, serve a frightfully elegant plum pudding, but when the topic of inheritance comes up, the proof is in the murder. Tickets for this dinner show are $15.

The Ricks College faculty jazz ensemble Swingset will present an evening of summertime jazz Friday, June 22, at 7:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium. Cost is $3.

The annual concert featuring Sunbird Dick Clark from Sun City West and his musical friends will be held Friday, June 29, at 7:30 p.m. in the Barrus Concert Hall. The concert has been a tradition in Rexburg for several years. Cost is $3.

The Ricks College theater department will present “This Castle Needs a Good Scouring” from July 5-7 and 12-14 in the Snow Drama Theater at 7:30 p.m. Written and directed by Ricks faculty member Omar Hansen, this summer’s theatrical production is a new twist on the Cinderella story. Cost is $6.

Last summer’s biggest hit of the season is returning Thursday, July 12, at 7:30 p.m. to the Barrus Concert Hall. Consisting of four talented singers and a pianist from Montana, Opus IV combines the best of classical music and musical comedy into one program. Their summer program features a blend of music, comedy and scenes from Broadway’s greatest musicals. Cost is $3.

The Ricks College American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band will present their annual tour show Friday, July 20, at a 2 p.m. matinee in the Kirkham Auditorium. The group will be leaving on a month-long summer tour of Austria, Poland and Hungary. Cost is $6.

The South family of Idaho and Utah will perform in the Barrus Concert Hall Saturday, July 21, at 7:30 p.m. “Legacy of Music” will be presented by the South family featuring everything from cello and piano to old time fiddling and Broadway tunes. Music has played an important role in the South family from the log cabin days at the sawmill in Island Park to the present. Cost is $3. A pre-show dinner is also offered for an extra $10.

InsideOut, a six-man, all-vocal group from Provo, Utah, will return to Ricks with a show Saturday, Aug. 11, in the Kirkham Auditorium. With their musical capability, vocal percussion and invigorating performance, InsideOut has produced show that entertains audiences of all ages. Their show consists of original music and cover tunes, many of which can be found of their CDs, “Innocence” and “Experience.” Cost is $5.
