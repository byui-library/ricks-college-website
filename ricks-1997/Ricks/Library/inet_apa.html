Sample Internet Citations - APA Style

 

World Wide Web Site

Basic Format
     Author.  (Publication or update date).  Title. 
Internet Address [access date].
     (Note: underline Title.)
Example
     Walker, J.R. (1995).  MLA-style citations of electronic 
sources. http://www.cas.usf.edu/english/walker/mla.html [26
October 1995].
     (Note: underline MLA-style citations of electronic
sources.)

E-Mail

Basic Format
     Author.  (year, month day).  Subject of message.
Recipient's e-mail address [access date].
     (Note: underline Subject of message.)
Example
     Bruckman, A.S. (1994, Dec.20). MOOSE crossing
proposal. Mediamoo@media.mit.edu [10 May 1996].
     (Note: underline MOOSE crossing proposal.)

Listserv

Basic Format
      Author.  (year, month day).  Topic of discussion.
Listname@e-mail address  [access date].
     (Note: underline Topic of discussion.)
Example
      Smith, J. (1994, Nov. 9).  Daily schedules.
LIBREF-L@Kent.edu. [17 March 1993].
     (Note: underline Daily schedules.)

Online Journal

Basic Format
     Author.  (year). "Article Title."  Journal Title, volume
(issue), paging.  Internet Address  [access date].
     (Note: underline Journal Title, volume)
Example
     Inada, K. (1995).  "A buddhist response to the nature of
human rights."   Journal of Buddhist Ethics 2, 55-66.     
http://www.cac.psu.edu/jbel/twocont.html  [23 July 1994].
     (Note: underline Journal of Buddhist Ethics 2)
Based on:
     Brown, H.  Citations of electronic documents in an
electronic document;
     Li, X. and N.B. Crane.  Electronic style:  A guide to
citing electronic information;      
     Publication manual of the American Psychological
Association, 4th edition and
     Walker, J. MLA style citations of electronic
sources.

Source:

      Mabee Library, Washburn University. "Sample Citations - 
            APA Style." http://www.wuacc.edu/mabee/inet_apa.html (10 Feb 1998)
