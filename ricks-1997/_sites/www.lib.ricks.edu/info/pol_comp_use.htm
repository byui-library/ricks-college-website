David O. McKay Library Computer Use Policy

Mission Statement

The computers in the Library are specifically pre-installed with software which provides a consistent working environment for all users. They are set up either as a AReference Workstation@ or a AResearch Workstation.@ The Reference Workstation provides access to electronic research tools. The Research Workstation provides access to these research tools, and to writing tools and electronic mail. The installed programs have been specifically chosen to support the mission of the Library and Ricks College.

Library-Approved Computer Programs

Library-approved programs are those pre-installed programs which are readily accessible from the standard desktop environment (the Start menu or desktop icons). All other computer applications are unauthorized. No alterations, customization or other changes to library-approved programs, including the desktop, are allowed. Downloading any form of software is expressly forbidden. Text documents may only be downloaded from research or Internet databases to a personal 3-2@ floppy disk.

Session Time Limitation

The Library reserves the right to limit patron use of computer equipment to one hour when other patrons are waiting. A patron may be asked at any time to relinquish their use of any given computer. The Library also reserves the right to limit or restrict access to computer programs and/or equipment.

Email

Email is accessible from any Research Workstation. Access is granted to Ricks College email accounts only. To use a campus email account, students agree to the following: Not to share their password; not to send chain mail or any inappropriate material, including messages of a harassing or salacious nature; not to use their account for commercial purposes; to keep all email within the college Code of Honor. The Library reserves the right to monitor email to verify compliance with these policies.

Internet

The Library uses a research-directed Web browser, which is designed first and foremost to serve as a research tool for class work and other activities that support the mission statement of the Library and Ricks College. This browser focuses research to selected Internet sites and their associated links. When a legitimate research need is identified which requires more extensive Internet access, and is approved by a Library employee, such access can be granted on a limited basis. Regardless of the form of Internet access, usage must be research-oriented. Activities such as chatting forums, Web-based email, games, Netmeeting, and other non-research or recreational activities are expressly forbidden.

Copyright Compliance

Patrons must stay within compliance of U.S. copyright law when using text or multimedia data (including pictures, sounds, etc.) obtained in the Library, whether in electronic form, including the Internet, or in another form. Questions about copyright issues should be directed to a member of the Library staff.

Security Violations

Violations of computer use policies will be viewed as computer fraud as described in the AR-Book,@ and will be treated as such. They will be reported to Campus Security, and may result in legal charges and/or dismissal from Ricks College. Logging in to any computer as an unauthorized user (using lab accounts, personal accounts, and the like) will not be tolerated. Other security violations include: attempting to gain unauthorized access to any computer program, system, or network; installing any form of software; changing any installed software (including the desktop), hardware, or other computer equipment.

Problems

If any type of problem with the computer equipment is encountered, or if violations of this policy are observed, the patron is to notify a Library worker immediately. Patrons are not to fix computer problems.
