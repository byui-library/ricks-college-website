Education Conferences for the Year 2000

Representatives from Ricks College, BYU, LDS Business College and BYU-Hawaii will be presenting Education Conferences throughout the United States.  These firesides will provide important information regarding admissions, scholarships, costs and deadlines for the Church schools.  In addition, representatives from the Institute of Religion in your area will be in attendance to answer questions regarding local CES options.  All high school students (grades 9-12) and their parents are invited to attend.
