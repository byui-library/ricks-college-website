Cross country teams take first at Region 18 meet in Rexburg

Amanda Hansen
han98007@byui.edu
scroll staff

For the fourteenth time since 1982, the Ricks College women’s cross country team took first place at its Region 18 meet.

In its last and only home meet of the season ,with an unbelievable 30 second spread between its top five runners, the women’s team won by a margin of 18 points.

Angie Benson led all runners with a time of 18:09 in the 8000-meter race.

Benson let Utah Valley runner Amanda Archuleta take the lead until the two mile mark. Benson then took control of the race and won by ten seconds.

Lida Clapier was Ricks’ No. 2 runner; she finished four seconds behind Archuleta and 14 behind Benson. Kathrine Anderson, Emily Prestwich and Audrey Golightly rounded out Ricks’ top five runners.

Ricks women finished with 21 points Utah Valley came in second with 39 points and Flathead Valley was third with 85 points.

In every men’s race so far this year, Ryan Jenks came in first, followed closely by David Woodbury. The Region 18 meet was no exception.

Jenks came in first overall with a time of 15:20. Woodbury was only a second behind him.

Jeff Davidson, Brett Larsen and Barry Jenks were the next three runners for Ricks.

The two teammates ran nearly head to head the entire race until Jenks sneaked by Woodbury at the end to win.

Ricks’ men’s team won its tenth first-place title since 1982 with 23 points, followed by Utah Valley with 39 points and Flathead Valley was third with 84.

The Cross country team’s final meet of the season will be the national meet Nov. 10, in Lansing, Mich.

Both teams will be defending their first-place titles from last year. Davidson will be defending his first-place finish overall.

Davidson is still recovering from an injury sustained earlier in the year and has been finishing as the third man for Ricks.
