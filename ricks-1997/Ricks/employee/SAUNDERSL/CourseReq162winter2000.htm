Math 162 - Course Requirements – Fall 2002

Bro Larry Saunders, Romney 242  Office Hours 10 MWF 11 TT

 

Text:       Calculus, 7th; 2002, Larson, Hostetler, Edwards; Houghton Mifflin

                The TI-86 Graphing Calculator is required

 

Course Description:           College Algebra and Trigonometry are reviewed, and then the Limit is developed and from it differentiation with its rules and applications.  Applications include related rates, graphing functions, and optimization problems.  The anti-derivatives are developed, the definite integral is defined and the First Fundamental Theorem of Integral Calculus with other properties used.  Definite integrals are used to find area, volume and other applications. 

 

Course Outline:  The chapters covered in the text are:  one through six.

Course Requirements:

1.    A loose-leaf notebook containing:

a.    100% Capture of the Principles and Structure given by your Instructor. (45 pts)

b.    Insert within a rewrite of your Instructor's notes THREE (or more) of your own examples for each item.   Identify your examples, with an asterisk (*) in the left margin.  Use your text for examples. (35 pts)

c.     In a separate section write and keep your Lesson Plans (LP’s).  Each LP must be two pages or less (one side of page only) and include a title, a written description of the process, and at least three examples completely worked out.  On the back of the first page of each LP you must include FOUR teaching sentences.  Each teaching sentence is written after you teach the lesson to another person and must include the date and one math insight you had when you taught or prepared.  Each LP is worth 6 pts plus 8 pts for the four teaching sentences.

d.     In a separate section prepare FIVE Real-life applications.  You may model your real-life applications after those shared by the Instructor or developed with other students or from the text, but your numbers must be different and the solutions must be correct. (40 pts)

e.     In a separate section keep your homework.  You must work a minimum of 5 problems from any text each day.  Use the Index of your text to find appropriate problems. (30 pts)

Note:  always keep your Notebook up to date and always bring it to class.  It must be complete for an A or B.
