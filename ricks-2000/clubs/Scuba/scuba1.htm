Scuba Certification

    Are You interested in becoming a diver? Have you ever seen divers on television, and thought to yourself, "I could do that"?  Learning to dive is easy, fun and exciting.  The adventure begins!!

    Do you have to be near the ocean to go diving?  No!  Some of the best diving spots are rivers,    lakes, dams and quarries.  Check out our "Upcoming Dives."

    The "Open Water Diver" certification course is offered every semester.  It provides the   fundamental knowledge and skills to scuba dive.  Upon successful completion of this course, graduates are considered competent to engage in open water diving activities.    Further certifications are available: Underwater Photography, Wreck Diving, Night Diving, Underwater Archeology, etc.            E-Mail us for class registration information or call 356-2300 during business hours and ask for Les.

       We would like to acknowledge our gratitude to Keith and Patty Christiansen of Inland Scuba and their professional staff for providing the instruction and equipment needed to make our scuba instruction possible.
