Distinguished Alumni Award for
Professional Accomplishments
Thomas C. Timmreck, PhD

"My time at Ricks was the most enjoyable period of my life," says Dr. Thomas Timmreck of San Bernardino, Calif. "This is where I found out who I was." He is a professor of public health and health administration at California State University where he has taught since 1985. He is a native of Cokeville, Wyo., where he attended high school. He served in the U.S. Army in Vietnam with a National Guard unit from Rexburg.

Tom graduated from Ricks in communications and broadcasting in 1968. Three years later he obtained his undergraduate degree in health science from Brigham Young University. He earned Masters Degrees from Oregon State in health and health education, and from Northern Arizona University in counseling psychology. In 1976 Tom was awarded his Ph.D. in public health and health administration from the University of Utah.

He has taught at Texas Tech University, Idaho State University and Northern Arizona University and part-time in the MBA programs at Western International in Phoenix, at University of Colorado-Denver, University of Denver, USC, and University of Redlands (California), and at Loretto Heights College (Denver) in its health promotion program.

Tom has been involved in gerontology since 1971 and is the former Administrator of a Health and Social Services program for the elderly in Cache County, Utah. He has been a licensed nursing home administrator in Arizona and Colorado.

Tom is the author of the first major reference book on health care administration, now in its third edition. He has published over 55 professional articles on topics related to health care administration, public health, behavioral health and gerontology.

Tom is married to the former Ellen Prusse. They are the parents of three children, and have two grandchildren. A former member of a stake high council and a High Priest group leader, he is currently the Sunday School President of his ward.
