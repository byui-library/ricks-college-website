Thanksgiving/End of School
Things to check on your car before you leave:

1. Check to make sure you have a jack and know where it is stored.

2. Make sure you have a tire wrench that fits your lug bolts.

3. Make sure your spare tire is pumped up.

4. Check your engine oil, and have it changed if it needs it.

5. Have your engine coolant checked to make sure it's full.

6. Have your anti-freeze level checked. Most service stations will do this for free.

7. Have your fan belts and hoses checked to make sure they aren't cracked or worn.

8. Have your tire air pressure checked and inflation corrected if needed.

9. Check your tire tread. Replace tires that are worn or damaged.

10. Have your exhaust system checked. A leaking exhaust system can leak deadly carbon monoxide gas into the passenger compartment.

11. Check your windshield washer reservoir to make sure it is full. It can keep your windshield clean so you can see out of it.

12. Replace your windshield wipers if they streak the window when you turn them on. Most of them will streak right at first, but after three or four swipes they should clean the window good.

13. Tell someone when you are leaving and which route you plan to follow. Also tell them what time you plan to arrive at your destination and who you are taking with you. If you make major changes in your plans, call your family and let them know so they won't worry about you.

14. Observe all speed laws and traffic rules. They are there for your safety and the safety of others on the road. Be especially careful and drive slower if it's stormy.

15. If you get tired, let someone else drive, or stop and walk around or rest. Get some peanuts or raisins and eat them one at a time to keep you alert. Get a BIG soft drink to sip on. It will help keep you awake, and give you a reason to stop more often.

16. Drive safely. Remember Christmas break is only three weeks away, and you want to have a fun Christmas with your family.  And if it's the end of the school year, you won't want to be laid up during what should be a great summer!
