Substitutions?
lQ:
–Can the 200-level business elective classes previously  taken at Ricks College fulfill any 300 or 400-level requirements for the Bachelor’s degree?
lA:
–Students who took 200-level business classes BEFORE FALL 2001 may be able to use them to fulfill certain 300 or 400 level classes, but with certain stipulations.
–Do not take B201, 247 or 270 now and expect them to replace 301, 341 or 321.  They won’t.
