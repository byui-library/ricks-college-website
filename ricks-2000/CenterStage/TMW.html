"The Merry Wives of Windsor"

Feb. 22-23, 26-27 and March 1-2, 2002
7:30 p.m., Snow Drama Theater

This story tells of return of Falstaff, Shakespeare's most endearing buffoon, in an opera setting. It's a story of his belated and misguided attempts at romance.

Tickets go on sale Feb. 4. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Play.
