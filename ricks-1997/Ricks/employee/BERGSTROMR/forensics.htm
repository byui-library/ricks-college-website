THE FORENSICS TEAM


Forensics Team wins top awards at national meet

     The Ricks College Forensics Team showed up at the national forensics tournament for four-year colleges and universities and talked their way into earning many of the top awards. The two-year junior college surprised a lot of four-year schools by winning the individual events sweepstakes award and placing second overall behind Rice University. 
     The Pi Kappa Delta National Tournament was held in Boise March 29-31 with 77 colleges and universities from across the nation competing, says Ricks coach Eric Embree. He says it was the first time Ricks had ever competed at the national competition designed mainly, for four-year schools. Because the competition was in Boise, the Ricks students decided to see how they would stack up against other schools.
     Embree says he was even surprised, but says Ricks has built up a strong reputation and attracts many high quality competitors, particularly students who want to attend a Latter—day Saint school.
This is the highest showing by a two-yea r program in the 84-year history of the organization, he says. "I think it’s pretty amazing. I knew we had individual students who would do well, but I just didn’t think the entire team would do as well in the overall rankings. We beat a lot of really high-powered programs."
     Ricks will compete later this week at the national competition for two-year schools in Florida.

The FAN Mail

Greetings!
    I just received via e-mail the latest edition of Inside Ricks, and was delighted at the lead story regarding the success of the Ricks College Forensics Team.  As a Ricks debator/impromtpu/extemp participant from "way, way back," it's always a joy to hear of the continued success of the program.  By far and away, the highlight of my two years at Ricks College was being a member of the "traveling squad," under the direction of Bro. Alan Bossard.  Again, back in the 'old, OLD days,' we took on 4-year schools during my two year tenure (at the intermountain level) and, much to the 4-year programs shock and dismay, defeated them...often!
    However, your success at the national level is to be applauded!  It does an alumni's heart good to see Ricks College gain the respect and admiration so richly deserved.
    Again, congratulations on a job well done, and here's wishing you and your students the best of success in Florida!  One of the benefits of our travel (we guestimated we traveled 60,000 miles in the two years I spent on "the squad")...everyone knew we were from Ricks College...that 'Mormon' school somewhere in Idaho.  Everyone looked at us carefully, waiting for one of us to sneak a cigarette, or a cup of coffee, or to hear a less-than-appropriate word come from our group.  We disappointed them at every turn!  We were able to turn confrontation into cooperation; adversity into advantage, and our efforts provided many missionary opportunities.  I'm sure you and your students have had similar challenges and opportunities.  As the reputation of Ricks College, and I hope BYU-Idaho circulates, you and your students will have more and more opportunities; not only to participate in Forensics, but to represent our faith and beliefs to the world.
    Well done, and "keep up the good work!"
Best Regards,
