Presentation of Proposed By-Law and Constitution Changes

President Springfield turned the time over to Past President Jim Moore to present the proposed changes to the Constitution and BY-Laws.  Jim mentioned the various methods used, including the special forum at this conference, to inform the membership of the proposed changes.  The proposed changes would include the modification of the Executive Board and the creation of standing committees.  The Executive Board would consist of:

  • President
  • President-Elect
  • Past President
  • Vice President for Professional Development
  • Vice President for Membership
  • Vice President for Publications and Development
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer
  • Diversity Development Advocate

In addition, all elected officers, with the exception of the president, would serve two-year terms and the terms would be staggered.

The proposed standing committees included:

  • General Conference Committees -- Local Arrangements and Program
  • Diversity Development Committee
  • Professional Development Committee
  • Membership Development Committee
  • Publications and Information Technology Committee

It was also proposed that the Diversity Development Advocate become a voting member of the board.

Jim called for questions from the floor. Hearing none, Jim asked Ron Heath to act as parliamentarian.  The vote was conducted and the proposed changes were approved.
