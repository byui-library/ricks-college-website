Photoshop Lessons
1. Introduction to tools -- get an overview of Photoshop's tools and pallettes.
2. Photoshop Tour--Learn some basic photoshop tricks.
3. Text effects--Make a photo appear in your text.
4.Outer Glow--Bring light to the outside of your text.
5. Drop Shadow--Learn about shadows, borders and duotones in this lesson.
6. MasksLearn about feathering and masking in this lesson.
7. Photoshop 5 Many of the earlier lessons are much easier thanks to Photoshop 5.
 
In most of these tutorials, you will find an image to follow along. Click on that image, then drag and drop it to your desk top, then open the file in Photoshop that you've copied over to your desktop or to your disk.
