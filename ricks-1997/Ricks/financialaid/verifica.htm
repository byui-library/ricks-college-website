Verification

Approximately one-third of all federal aid applicants will be selected to complete a verification process. In this process, the financial aid administrator will compare the information that was reported on the initial application with the verification documents that are submitted such as: Verification worksheet, tax returns, etc. If there are discrepancies, Ricks College will make corrections.

Students selected for verification will receive notification on the Student Aid Report and/or from the Ricks College Financial Aid Office. The student should carefully follow the instructions. The student may be required to submit copies of his/her tax return (and spouses’s return, if married) as well as the tax returns for the parents (if student is a dependent).

To be eligible for a Pell Grant payment, the student must submit all verification documents within 45 days after the date of his/her last day of attendance and before August 29, in the academic year the student is enrolled.

If you need tax account information, call the IRS at 1-800-TAX-1040 and ask for the 1721 form or a no-cost recap of the year that is being verified.
