Course Description and Objectives

* Learn the basic principles of public speaking

* Practice creating, organizing, and presenting 7 effective public speeches

* Analyze communication situations, audiences, and purposes of public speaking

* Become a more critical thinker, listener, and writer

* Understand your relationship and ethical responsibilities to others involved in communication     transactions

Class Requirements

40% Speeches- 7 speeches will be presented to the class during the semester. This process will begin with a simple introduction speech and progress through more complex speeches. The speeches presented and their point value is as follows:

1. Introduction (25 points) 2. Oral reading (50) 3. Primary Purpose (50) 4. Informative Speech (75)

5. Persuasive Speech (75) 6. Debate (75) 7. Impromptu (50)

30% Exams- 3 exams will be given in the testing center (T1=100 points;T2=100points;T3=100 points). Each test will consist of true and false, multiple choice, and essay questions. 

10% Reaction Papers- 5 reaction papers (no more than 3 pages in length: Typed: Double spaced) will be required throughout the semester. Each paper will evaluate a public speaking experience. Each paper will be worth 20 points. Late reaction papers will not be accepted.

10%Attendance/Participation- Because of the nature of the material and the importance of class discussion, students are required to attend each class. Students are allowed 2 (I repeat Two!!!!) absences before their grade will be affected. Each absence thereafter will result in a 50% loss of attendance grade. Any student who misses on the day of their presentation (or quiz) will not be allowed to makeup their presentation (or quiz), hence they will lose the points for that presentation (or quiz).

5%Chapter Readings/Quizzes- students are required to read each chapter by the assigned dates and be prepared for a 10/5 point quiz.

5% Portfolio- each student will be asked to keep a portfolio during the semester. The portfolio will contain critical thinking questions and assignments pertaining to public speaking, which are to be completed on a weekly basis. Portfolio's will be collected on December 4th. Late portfolios will not be accepted.

100%  =  1000 Points
