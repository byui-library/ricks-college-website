Household tips uncover cleaning mysteries

by Mikie K. Hayhurst
HAY01001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Carpet spills can be cleaned easily with household items. James Ricks / Scroll
From “Hints from Heloise” to Martha Stewart, bachelors and homemakers alike receive household tips ranging from cooking to cleaning and back again.

The following is a sample list of hints:

• Removing stains from the carpet:

Nail polish — Apply nail polish remover with an eyedropper, let sit for one to two minutes and wipe up with a clean, dry cloth.

Chewing gum — Rub carpet with an ice cube until gum is cold and hard. Scrape up with a spatula or butter knife.

Spills — First, blot carefully with a clean towel to remove as much excess liquid as possible. Second, lightly spray area with water and blot again. Finally, cover damp area with a dry towel and apply a hot steam iron.

General carpet care — Vacuum at least once a week because dust and dirt get ground into the carpet and slash fibers. Never use ammonia or soap; they contain alkalis that will fade colors.

• Dusting:

Dust objects in room from top to bottom in order to capture falling dust.

Due to static charge, a used fabric softener dryer sheet can be used to dust electronics.

To clean a feather duster, submerge in cold soapy water, rinse, shape and line dry.

• In the kitchen:

To get rid of a lingering smell, bake orange peels in the oven at 350 degrees for about 15 minutes.

For a clogged drain, pour one cup of baking soda and one cup vinegar over drain. Once it foams, run hot water down the drain.

To get rid of odors in plastic food containers and/or lunch boxes, put a piece of white bread that has been soaked in vinegar in the container overnight. Or place a lemon in the container and put it in the freezer overnight.

• Laundry:

Fabric softener dryer sheets placed in dresser drawers keep clothes smelling clean.

For ink stains, try aerosol hair spray and gentle blotting.

To clean a handmade quilt, vacuum fabric with a brush attachment. To clean stains or soils, ask a fabric restoration expert for advice specific to your quilt.

•Miscellaneous:

Crayons on the wall — Sprinkle baking soda on a damp cloth and gently rub.

Musty smell in old furniture — Fill plastic containers with white vinegar, seal and punch holes in lid. Place containers in drawers or furniture overnight. For extreme cases, clean with vinegar-dampened cloth.

To remove frost, wash off with ammonia.

Desk lights should have a pale-colored shade that is wide at the bottom; for best overall light, a lamp should be placed at both ends of the desk.

Foggy windshields — Keep a chalkboard eraser in the glove box to wipe interior of windshield when foggy.
