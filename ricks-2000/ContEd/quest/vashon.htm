WHERE IS VASHON?

Vashon Island is an approximately 90-square-mile island located in the Puget Sound two miles west of Seattle and two miles north of Tacoma. Residents and visitors access the island by one of two ferry dock terminals. The 110-acre campus at Morningside Farms is situated one mile from the southern tip of the island nearest Tacoma. The campus property was once one of the premiere horse ranches in the Northwestern United States. Now the acres of forest, grassy fields, orchards and water front are home to a dormitory, classroom and dining facility for a cohort of students. Other facilities include a recreational arena, amphitheater, stocked salmon pond and indoor heated swimming pool. The membership of the local branch provides support for the administrators and students.

Marks the location of the campus on the south end of Vashon Island, Washington.

Fifty miles away rests Mount Rainier. This 14,000-foot dormant volcano is highly visible from Morningside Farms. Geographically the mountain dominates other features in the region.  The best time to view the massive glacier fields from Morningside Farms is early evening as the sun sets on the crest of the mountain.

The island is accessible by two ferry terminals.  Boats make the fifteen- to-twenty minute run each way to Seattle and Tacoma several times an hour.  The commute on the ferries allows time to view the scenery of the Pacific Northwest at its best.  

The campus property was a generous gift by a donor whose intent was to "bless the lives of the youth of the church."   Morningside extends from the beachfront of the Puget Sound to the rolling hillside where the dormitory and classroom are situated.  Between the beach and the dormitory are acres of forest paths and manicured pasture lands.
The view of the Puget Sound from the beach at Morningside gives a view of Maury Island, Federal Way and the Port of Tacoma.  Students manage their free time enjoying the marine environment.
There are quiet and tranquil areas around the property that lend themselves to a  student's need to study and contemplate. The gazebo at the pond is just such a place. The wide open grassy fields  provide ample space for those wanting to participate in recreational activities.
