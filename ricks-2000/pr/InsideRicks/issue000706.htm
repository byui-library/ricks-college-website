THE NAME CHANGE TO BYU-IDAHO IS NOT IMMEDIATE

Perhaps the most frequently asked question since the announcement by President Hinckley June 21 has been, "When do we begin using the name Brigham Young University-Idaho?" For the time being, we will continue as Ricks College. We will not begin using the name Brigham Young University-Idaho in the immediate future.

A letter has been mailed to the Northwest Accreditation Commission informing them of its intent to change to a four-year institution. We also are required to file a prospectus outlining our strategic plan and programs. Obviously, the prospectus will take time to develop. After the prospectus is filed, the Northwest Commission will respond to our request and either accept or reject us as a candidate for accreditation at the four-year level. Only at the point of being accepted as a candidate will it be appropriate for us to begin using the name of Brigham Young University-Idaho.

Therefore, please continue to use the name of Ricks College for letterheads, business cards, correspondence, etc. Any materials that need to be printed at this time should continue to use the authorized Ricks College logo.

MONTANA GROUP TO PERFORM "FROM BACH TO BROADWAY"

"From Bach to Broadway" will be performed by the Montana singing group Opus IV Thursday, July 13, at Ricks College. The Summer in the Snow Entertainment Series at Ricks will present the group in the Barrus Concert Hall of the Snow Center for the Performing Arts at 7 p.m. Tickets are $3 for general admission and available from the Ricks College Ticket Office or at the door. Call the ticket office at 356-2230 or order online at www.byui.edu/tickets.

The program features music, comedy and scenes from Broadway’s great musicals, interwoven with classical selections by the world’s great composers. The two married couples met while singing together with the Montana Chorale and now are Montana’s only professional vocal quartet. James Aiken and Nita Hamilton live in Missoula while Robert Worobec and Linda Terry live in Livingston. They are joined accompanist Kenneth Christensen, a piano instructor at Montana State University in Bozeman.
