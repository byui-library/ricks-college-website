Angela Cheng

Wednesday, Nov. 28, 2001
6 p.m., Dinner in Manwaring Center
7:30 p.m., Barrus Concert Hall

Hailed by critics for her remarkable technique, tonal beauty and musicianship, Angela Cheng is one of Canada's most distinguished and respected pianists and a gold medal winner of the Arthur Rubinstein International Piano Masters Competition.

Tickets go on sale Nov. 12. Cost $6 (public), $1 (BYU-Idaho students). FA 100 - Recital. Concert dress required for BYU-Idaho students.

A pre-show holiday dinner will be served in the Manwaring Center Special Events Room at 6 p.m. for $10 extra.
