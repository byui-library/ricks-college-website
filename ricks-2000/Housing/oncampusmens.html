On-Campus Men's Housing

The Ricks College men's housing units are Rigby and Biddulph Halls
Each one houses 232 men, with a coin-operated laundry room and facilities for disabled students in each building.



  Each building has a main lounge with a big-screen TV and vending machines.



  Student rooms are 19' X 10' and contain two beds, two wardrobe/dresser units, two desks, and one sink and medicine cabinet for the two occupants. Each room also has air conditioning, a MicrofridgeÆ unit with microwave and refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls.
