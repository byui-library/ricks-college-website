Graphic Services

Graphic design of college publications adds a professional touch while supervised student designers gain hands-on experience. It also ensures uniformity in layout and quality in design.  Contact Brian Memmott at memmottB@byui.edu

Internal Communications

Distribution of e-mail messages to all employees and a weekly printed newsletter, News & Notes, are key elements of the college's internal communication. A weekly e-mail newsletter, Inside BYU-Idaho, is sent to alumni, former employees, and other interested parties all over the world.

KBYI-FM 100.5

The BYU-Idaho-owned radio station broadcasts NPR, PRI, and local originating classical music programming and news 24 hours a day to southeastern Idaho and by way of eight translators to southwestern Montana, western Wyoming and southern Idaho.

PR Photography


Professional photography captures the activities at BYU-Idaho that are featured in publications, with press releases, and on the school's Web site.  Photos are stored and used for archival purposes.
