The Electronics Engineering Technology Department offers two degree programs to satisfy a wide variety of needs:  1) Specialized Associate Degree in Electronics Engineering Technology and 2) Specialized Associate  Degree in Computer Systems Technology.    Students are taught how to combine scientific knowledge and methods with technical abilities to solve problems and design electronic circuits in support of engineering activities.  

Extensive laboratory and computer experience is included with all course work. The facilities include two classrooms, two large and well equipped general electronics laboratories, one computer lab, and an amateur radio lab.   

Instructors include, Rex Fisher, department chairman, Gordon Black, and Tom Crapo.  Employment opportunities for electronics and computer technicians in these areas are excellent.  Graduates find work in such areas as:  failure analysis, electronic manufacturing, electronic design, field technician service, instrumentation and calibration, computer repair, and computer networking.
