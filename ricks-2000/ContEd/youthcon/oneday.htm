ONE-DAY OUTDOOR ADVENTURE

 

This itinerary reflects the standard Outdoor Adventure program

which begins at 7:30 a.m. and ends at 6:00 p.m. Times shown are

approximate.  We reserve the right to make adjustments in the itinerary

due to bad weather or other circumstances beyond our control.

 

7:15 a.m. Arrive at the BYU-Idaho Outdoor Learning Center. Please adjust your

schedule in order to be on time. There will be time for the staff to get acquainted

with the participants. We will also collect an "Assumption of Risk" form

from those in attendance.

 

8:00-10:00 a.m. Workshops

 

10:00-12 noon Workshops

 

12 noon Lunch

 

1:00-3:00 p.m. Workshops

 

3:00-5:00 p.m. Workshops

 

6:00 p.m. Approximate time to depart

We can make arrangements for dinner, at an additional cost.
