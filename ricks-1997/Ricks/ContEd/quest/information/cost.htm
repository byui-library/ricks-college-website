HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO PARTICIPATE IN A QUEST PROGRAM?

The costs of the Quest programs are similar to a semester on-campus. The tuition for the Quest program includes room and board, transportation* and admissions to events and activities sponsored by the Quest program.  The posted prices do not include the costs of room and board while Quest students are campus-based. To offer students the choice of where they stay during the campus-based portion of the program, room and board during that time have not been included in the total cost.  For more information about on-campus housing click here.   For more information about off-campus housing click here.
