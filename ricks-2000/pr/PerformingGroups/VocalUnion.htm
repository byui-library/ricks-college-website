About Vocal Union and Sound Alliance

The division of Performing and Fine Arts and the Department of Music at Ricks College are pleased to announce the creation of a new contemporary performing arts ensemble. This new ensemble, ready for touring during the spring of 2001, will combine the talents of Vocal Union (vocal ensemble) and Sound Alliance (stage band).

 About the Directors: 

Kevin Brower, the director, is a graduate of Ricks College, Utah State University, Brigham Young University and a DMA candidate at Arizona State University. In his ninth year at Ricks College, Mr. Brower serves as Director of Choral Activities, conducts Collegiate Singers, and acts as vocal coach for Vocal Union. Before his appointment at Ricks, he taught music in the public schools in Wyoming including, junior high and high school choir, band, musical theatre, and general music.

Nori Brower is the assistant director of Vocal Union.  She is a graduate of Utah State University with a Bachelor of Music Education in choral and general music. In addition to her assignment with Vocal Union at Ricks College, Mrs. Brower teaches music theory, aural skills, and keyboard harmony. She previously taught in the public school system in Wyoming and Utah and has been an active participant in amateur and professional theatre.

Wilson Brown, former keyboard player and arranger for The Lettermen, is the musical director for Sound Alliance. He is a graduate of the University of Utah and Brigham Young University. He worked professionally for 20 years and began teaching at Ricks College in 1977 specializing in jazz.  He and his students have been part of the the performance tour program at Ricks since 1979.
