I.              INTRODUCTION

 

Welcome to German 102. This course is designed for those students who have either completed German 101 or have had at least two or more years of German in high school. At the end of this course, you will have completed a fundamental introduction to the basics of German grammar, and you should be able to understand, read, and, in a limited fashion, speak about those themes introduced in the course. If you are consistent in your study habits and homework assignments, this course can be one of your most rewarding college experiences.

 

II.            COURSE OBJECTIVES

 

The emphasis in this course will be to continue your development of listening and reading skills, with an increasing focus on speaking. Although points of grammar will be briefly explained in class, you will be expected to have studied the grammar and attempted or completed all assigned homework prior to coming to class. Assignments will be periodically collected, reviewed, and evaluated.

 

III.           COURSE MATERIALS

 

The following materials will be required to successfully complete this course:

 

1.     Kontakte -- A Communicative Approach, 3rd ed.

2.     Kontakte: Arbeitsbuch, 3rd ed.

3.        A loose-leaf notebook in which you will write all assigned grammar exercises. Each exercise (Übung) must be identified at the top by Lektion number, and exercise (Übung) number.

4.        Listening comprehension tapes (see below).

 

IV.           ABBREVIATIONS

 

Kon                        Kontakte (textbook)

Ab                          Arbeitsbuch (workbook)

Ha                           Hausaufgabe (homework)

S                              Seite (page)

 

V.            STUDY/HOMEWORK

 

This study guide contains all homework assignments for each class period.

·         Answers to most textbook grammar exercises are found in Appendix F of the textbook beginning on page A-31.

·         Answers to most assigned workbook exercises are located in the back of the workbook beginning on page 297.

All assigned homework should be attempted prior to looking up the answers. Consistent homework is extremely important in learning a foreign language. You should plan on studying somewhere between one and two hours between each class period.

 

Consistent preparation + active participation in class = maximum fun!

 

VI.           TAPES

 

All listening comprehension exercises in your workbook have been recorded onto four cassette tapes. These may be in Room 325 of the McKay Library at a cost of $2.25 per tape. A set of tapes will also be placed on Reserve in the McKay Library. This is an excellent opportunity to listen to native speakers.

 

VII.         GRADING

 

Your grade will be based on seven tests, a final exam, and homework assignments. The following table shows the evaluation percentage weights:

 

Seven Tests....................50%

Final Exam.......................25%

Homework.......................25%

 

VIII.        EXAMS

 

There will be an exam after each of the seven chapters. Tested material will be comprehensive and focus on vocabulary, grammar, and listening and reading comprehension exercises. If you are ill, please contact me by the next day so we can schedule a make-up test.

 

IX.           CLASS ATTENDANCE

 

Consistent attendance is essential to learning a foreign language. If you must be absent, please discuss the matter with me before the absence, unless it is a medical situation or other emergency. Each unexcused absence will lower your final grade by one percentage point. I reserve the right to count excessive tardies as absences. I will add two percentage points to your final grade if you have no unexcused absences during the entire semester.
