Course Description: Personal Health and Wellness is a course designed to help you gain a better understanding of the principles of health and wellness and the impact that lifestyle choices have on your health, quality of life, and risk for developing disease. The course will provide information and activities that will help you incorporate positive lifestyle behaviors into everyday living. The ultimate goal is that through the learning, understanding and implementing of wise and healthy lifestyle choices you will enjoy good health and a greater quality of life through maximization of your personal potential.



Course Objectives: Each student will gain an understanding of the five areas of health and wellness (mental, emotional, social, physical, and spiritual) and how each area affects his or her quality of life and personal potential. Each student will increase their level of awareness and understanding of how their current lifestyle habits are affecting their health and future risk for developing disease. Each student will be pro-active in taking personal responsibility for his or her own health through implementation of healthy lifestyle choices. Each student will find fun and enjoyment in learning and living a "Wellness" oriented lifestyle.
