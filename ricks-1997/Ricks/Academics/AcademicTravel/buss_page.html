Business Expedition 2001

"To provide a unique educational and spiritual experience for business division majors by conducting coordinated visits and class material at various business and church history sites across the United States. "
$2600( plus tuition, books, food, housing for first five weeks on campus)
Eight-Week Summer Term (June 25 - August 15) First five weeks on campus and last three weeks traveling to major businesses and church history sites in New York, NY, Boston, MA, and London, England.
up to 11 credits may be taken
  • B 1O1: Introduction to Business (3 credits)
  • B 193:  Advising Seminar (0.5 credits)
  • B 201:  Finance(3 credits)
  • B 275: Business Law (3 credits)
  • Econ 111:  Macroeconomics (3 social science credits)
  • Econ 112: Microeconomics (3 social science credits)
  • Rel 224:   Doctrine & Covenants (1st half) (2 credits)
  • Eng 111:   College Writing (3 credits)
  • Eng 211:   *Advanced Writing & Critical Reading (3 credits)
      *Prerequisite - Eng 111 & 22 credit hrs or sophomore standing.
  • Eng 250:   Introduction to Literature (3 credits)
  • FA I00: Fine Arts (Required for graduation, 0 credit)
Business Expedition is a program combining academic with the real business world in the United States.  Travel through parts of the continental United States visiting some of the largest businesses in the world.  Supplement classroom teaching with visits to major businesses in the United States and London, England.  Visit various kinds of business enterprises -- retail, finance, manufacturing, human resource management, entrpreneurship, travel, marketing, hospitality, and government.  Provide an experience for international businesses.  Learn what makes big business tick and strategies of management.  Meet CEO's and management (many of whom are L.D.S.) of top business firms across the country, create business connections, and network with them. Earn up to eleven credits of business and general education credits.
