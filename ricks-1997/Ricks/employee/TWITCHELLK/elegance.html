An Elegant Solution...

  1. works correctly
  2. demonstrates an understanding of the problem
  3. comes from a knowledge and understanding of the programming language
  4. is the result of careful thought (and is not hacked out)
  5. takes time to develop
  6. is succinct but not necessarily terse
  7. is robust
  8. is readable and understandable (implies simplicity)
  9. is efficient
