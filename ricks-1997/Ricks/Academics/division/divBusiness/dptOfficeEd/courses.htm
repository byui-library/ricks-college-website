OSM Courses

OSM 101 (3 Cr) Fundamentals of Keyboarding Semester class with emphasis on developing speed and accuracy in basic keyboarding. Designed for beginning typist or student who has not exceeded 25 net words a minute in keyboarding. Taught on computers.
Five class hours per week


OSM 105 (1 Cr) Brush-up Keyboarding
Block class for students who are typing from straight-copy material between 25-40 words a minute on a 5-minute timed writing. Class will emphasize building accuracy and speed on straight copy and numbers. Introduction to correspondence, tabulation, and manuscripts. Class may be repeated for a maximum of two credits.


OSM 106 (3 Cr) Business English*
The study of basic grammar; parts of speech; number, abbreviation, spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and proofreading rules.
Three class hours per week
*Students may test out of this class. For further information contact teacher first day of class.

OSM 131 (1 Cr) Beginning Word Processing
This block class meets two days a week and is designed to provide students with a working knowledge and application of the basic capabilities of the current release of Word Processing software.
Prerequisite: 20 wpm required or have consent of instructor. Students not meeting this requirement, take OSM 101 Fundamentals of Keyboarding.


OSM 203 (3 Cr) Production Keyboarding*
Emphasis on accuracy, format and speed in production work, letters, manuscripts, tables, and business forms using computers.
Prerequisite: 40 wpm typing speed on 5-minute timed writing with an average of one error or less a minute. Five class hours per week
*Students may test out of this class. For further information contact teacher first day of class.


OSM 220** (3 Cr) Business Communication
This course includes analyzing, composing, and dictating business letters, job application documents and reports. Students will also give oral presentations.
Prerequisite: Majors 167, 242, 244 and 297 must have completed Eng 111 and 22 credit hours.
**Gives GE credit in the Reading & Writing area of the Basic Skills Requirement for 167, 242, 244, 297 and 314 majors. Some transfer institutions may still require that you take their Advanced Writing English class.

OSM 231 (3 Cr) Word Processing*
Designed to provide students with a working knowledge and application of the basic and advanced capabilities of the current release of Word Processing software.
Prerequisite: Students must be able to type at least 35 words a minute


OSM 271 (3 Cr) Desktop Publishing
Documents and graphics applications of design and layout principles. Word processing software and HP Laserjet technology emphasized.
Prerequisite: OSM 231 or concurrent registration


OSM 272 (3 Cr) Office Equipment
The development of speed and accuracy in the transcription of documents in the fields of legal, medical, travel, small business, etc. The development of speed and accuracy using the ten-key calculator and other office machines.
Prerequisite: OSM 106, OSM 203 and OSM 231


OSM 273 (3 Cr) Records Management
An overview of records systems and basic files maintenance.


OSM 274 (3 Cr) Advanced Office Systems
 Emphasizes software applications used in the office environment. Microsoft Office User Certification (MOUS) is available for MS Word, Excel, Access, and PowerPoint at the proficiency and expert level.
Prerequisite: OSM 220, OSM 231, CIS 140


OSM 275 (3 Cr) Advanced Software Applications
An advanced course in software applications used in the computerized office environment emphasizing Windows 95/98 Operating System, QuickBooks, PageMaker, and other software programs.
Prerequisite: Acct 200 or 201, OSM 271, and 274 (OSM 271 and OSM 274 may be taken concurrently with OSM 275)


OSM 276 (3 Cr) Executive Office Procedures and Systems
Designed to prepare the student for job responsibilities requiring specific skills, understanding and knowledge, motivation and initiative. Areas covered are making travel, meeting and conference arrangements; mail handling, administrative support, office etiquette and career planning.
Prerequisite: OSM 231, Three class hours per week


OSM 278 (2 Cr) Introduction to Legal Assistantship
Class will familiarize student with basic vocabulary and substantive knowledge useful in working as a legal assistant. Will provide opportunity to develop skills in law office communication, legal research, people skills, and professionalism.


OSM 295 (.5 Cr) Practicum
An advising/communication class to allow students opportunities to develop professionalism by participating in symposiums, business lecture series, study skills, career vision activities, job seeking exercises, field trips, competitive events, and leadership activities.
Required of majors 174, 175 and 314 if not registered for OSM 298 or 299
Activity/Lecture Fee: $15, Member of Business Professionals of America


OSM 297 (1-3 Cr) Summer Office Internship
A "summer only" program for OSM majors who have completed at least one semester in their program. Jobs are secured by student with help of program director in any part of the world. Each credit requires 90 hours of work and two written reports.


OSM 298 (2 Cr) Beginning Cooperative Work Experience
A course providing part-time practical experience under the direction of the coordinator in an approved campus office, business firm, or governmental position.

Arrangements for the work experience program must be made during the semester before enrollment. Applications can be obtained from the co-op coordinator prior to registration.
Prerequisite: Must be accepted Office Systems Management major, Consent of Instructor
Activity/Lecture Fee: $15, Member of Business Professionals of America
Work 6-20 hours per week; one classroom hour per week


OSM 299 (.5 Cr) Advanced Cooperative Work Experience
For students who have completed one semester of OSM 298 Activity/Lecture Fee: $15, Member of Business Professionals of America
May be repeated for a total of 1.5 credits.
