 ASSIGNMENTS:  Your major assignments for this course include the following:
  Document*: Collect all materials used during the semester. The objective of this assignment is to document your efforts and to create a resource manual for your future teaching experiences.
           
  + Lessons and Plans: Teach lessons as needed, probably four at the first of the semester and
two in the middle of the semester. You may use plans and materials
from a previous semester.
           
  + Lab Information: Collect your lab's rules and expectations and lists of staff and children.
           
  + Reading Notes: Read and carefully consider each assignment. Write five (5) important ideas you learned from each reading.
           
  + Home Visits: Schedule home visits with those parents who desire it. Coordinate home visits with your team and arrange your schedule so that you can attend each home visit. Type a one-half page report for each visit in which you express what you learned about the child and his/her family.
           
  +Observations:        
    Formative      
      Four-fold -- Daily on each student Using NCR papers, write observation notes on each co-supervisor, support teacher, and trainer. Use the ideas on the four-fold form (teacher example, designing and presenting instruction, and use of resources) as a guideline in these observations.
           
      Four-fold -- Weekly and monthly on each student Summarize daily observations. Give a copy of this summary to the
student and appropriate faculty. Briefly discuss your summarized
observations with individual students. Emphasize specific strengths and
areas where you feel they need to improve. Discuss significant
concerns with appropriate faculty.
           
      Circle time -- Two times on each student Using the "Circle-time evaluation form", observe student's performance in
closing circle and gathering time.
           
      Tally Event Sampling -- Five times during the semester on each student Develop a tally sheet for a specific teaching skill and use it during child selected activities. Briefly discuss your summarized observations with student staff. Emphasize specific strengths and areas where you feel they need to improve. Discuss significant concerns with appropriate faculty.
           
    + Summative· Midterm and Final* Complete an evaluation form for each support teacher and trainer.
You should use evidence collected in your formative evaluations. Briefly discuss your summarized observations with student staff. Emphasize specific strengths and areas where you feel they need to improve. Discuss significant concerns with appropriate faculty.
  + Inservice Lessons: Prepare and present five inservice lessons. The lessons should pertain to
teaching skills which you and your team members decide are needed to
make the experience better for the children in your lab. You will use the
same lesson plan format for writing these lessons as you did with your lessons for the children. NOTE: As a part of your inservice lesson, you will introduce your staff to the "Event-tally sheet" that you have designed and will use as an observation tool during the next week.
           
  + Resources: Collect copies of other student's lesson plans, and learning cards, letters to parents, inservice materials, etc.
