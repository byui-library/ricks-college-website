Ricks College, an equal opportunity employer, is owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  Adherence to personal behavioral principles and standards of the Church is required of each employee of the College.

Employees must also observe the Ricks College Code of Honor including its dress and grooming standards.  All employees accept, as a condition of employment, an annual eligibility review process which confirms compliance with the required Church and College standards.
