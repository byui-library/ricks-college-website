Debra Haas sacrifices all for son Robbie in sports

by Johnny Gonzales
GON00001@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Behind every good man, there is a better woman. For the majority of the male students at BYU-Idaho, that woman is their mothers.

For sophomore quarterback Robbie Haas, the woman behind his success is Debra Haas, his mother.

Robbie Haas was selected as the WSFL First team Quarterback. His accomplishments and excellent play on the field are the main reasons that the Ricks College Vikings won the last Real Dairy Bowl. Attributes of selflessness and sacrifice have helped him succeed, and Robbie credits his having these attributes to his mom.

Debra Haas grew up in the Logan, Utah area where she now lives with her husband. She is the mother of four children, three of which are still living at home.

“She will sacrifice whatever it takes to give us every opportunity,” Robbie said.

Debra has always done her best to support all of the members of her family in all they do. Whether it is her husband’s calling, Matthew’s hockey games, Haley’s drill team performances, Kristin’s choir recitals or Robbie’s football games, she is always there, he said.

Debra, has always supported Robbie and made it to every game she could. Robbie’s fondest memory of his mom’s support was when Debra took it upon herself to rally all of the available relatives for the 2001 Real Dairy Bowl. She got tickets and organized all of the relatives that could make it, he said.

Robbie’s mother has been the most important spiritual influence in his life. She always set a standard of righteousness and encouraged Robbie to set his standards high, he said.

“She is very big on reaching potential, and helps me to realize my spiritual potential,” Robbie said.

Debra is a great influence in Robbie’s life. He has even decided to enter the same field that she works in. Debra is a nurse, and she is the reason why Robbie is a biology major.

“She is a major influence in my life, and I am eternally grateful for her,” Robbie said.
