Ricks Update - A weekly newsletter for friends of Ricks College

May 4, 1998

We will be opening up our campus to adults and youths 14 and older during the first Campus Education Week. The three-day event from June 4 to 6 will feature 47 speakers from among the faculty at Ricks and other Latter-day Saint speakers. They will address more than 300 topics on gospel subjects, self-improvement and family relations in classes running from 8:30 a.m. to 8:40 p.m. each day. Call the Division of Continuing Education at (208) 356-1040 or e-mail wyliel@ricks.edu for a booklet. You can also check the Ricks homepage at www.ricks.edu under Continuing Education. Preregistration is $30 and housing is available in college dorms.

The center of campus is expected to shift southward upon completion of a new five-acre quad. Slated for completion in 1999, it is hoped the project will commence sometime in late May upon closure of a portion of Viking Drive (4th South) that dissects the campus from east and west. The through street will close upon the completion of Seventh South, a new east-west roadway that skirts the southernmost portion of the Ricks campus. The new quad will encompass the present street between the Manwaring Center on the north and the new John Taylor Building and the Spencer W. Kimball Student and Administrative Services Building on the south. The construction project will require some 20,000 yards of fill to build up and level the area. The project was designed by the Salt Lake City architectural firm of FFKR and general contractor is Pacific West Construction.

The Ricks College Alumni Association recently honored Wilma Smith Livsey (1962) of Salt Lake City with the Distinguished Alumni Award for Humanitarian Services, Bradley Smith (1966) of Salt Lake City with the Outstanding Teacher Award, Phil Allsen (1954) of Provo with the Distinguished Alumni Award for Professional Accomplishments; and Kriss Nielsen (1974), a former Rexburg resident now stationed with the U.S. Army in California with the Outstanding Young Alumni Award. Three awards were bestowed posthumously. The late Chester Hill, a former Ricks music teacher, was honored with the Eliza R. Snow Award for the Performing Arts, and two sisters, Gladys Walz (1930) and Grace Walz Jackson (1952), were given Presidential Service Awards.

Recently inducted into the Ricks Athletic Hall of Fame were Linnea "Linn" Pearson Belnap, the first woman athletic All-American at Ricks who played volleyball at Ricks in 1979 and 1980, and Jody A. Smith, an All-American in wrestling who competed at Ricks in 1970 and 1971. Honored as Alumni Athlete of the Year was Ben Cahoon, who played football at BYU in 1996 and '97.

President David A. Bednar will be in Arizona May 11 and 12 to met with alumni and friends of the college. A fireside with alumni and friends will be Tuesday, May 12, at 7 p.m. in the Mesa Central Stake Center, 925 N. Harris.
