Brigham Young University - Idaho  Travel Study

  BIRTH OF A NATION and THE RESTORATION OF THE KINGDOM TOUR

May 6-20, 2002 --Visit sites and explore in depth the birth of our nation as foretold by prophets of old.  (1 Nephi 13 and 3 Nephi 21.)  Sites will include Plymouth Rock, the Mayflower, Boston historical sites, Gloucester MA, Old Sturbridge Village, the birthplaces of Joseph Smith and Brigham Young, Fort Ticonderoga, Saratoga National Historical Park, Palmyra, the Sacred Grove, Hill Cumorah, Philadelphia historical sites, Monticello, Jamestown, Williamsburg , Yorktown, Washington, D.C., Mount Vernon, plus the Pennsylvania Amish Country.

  SPRING CHURCH HISTORY and AMERICAN HERITAGE TOUR

May 22-June 6, 2002 --This sixteen-day tour will take you to such sites as Plymouth Rock, Plimoth Plantation, Boston historical sites, Gloucester MA, Old Sturbridge Village (where time stood still since 1830,) Joseph Smith's and Brigham Young's birthplaces, Fort Ticonderoga, Palmyra, the Sacred Grove, and Hill Cumorah.  Make stops in Kirtland, Nauvoo, Liberty Jail, Winter Quarters, Independence, Adam - ondi - Ahman, Far West, plus an extra treat to experience the Ohio Amish people and country near Kirtland. 

***WE HAVE TICKETS TO THE NAUVOO TEMPLE OPEN HOUSE!!!

     HISTORIC MORMON AMERICA and HILL CUMORAH PAGEANT TOURS

Tour #1: June 25-July 9, 2002 and Tour #2:  July 10-24, 2002--These fifteen-day tours will guide you through the principal sites in Church history.  You will visit Nauvoo, Independence, Liberty Jail, Adam -ondi-Aham, Carthage Jail, Kirtland, Ohio Amish country, Palmyra, the Sacred Grove, the Hill Cumorah Pageant, Martin Harris home, Whitmer farm, Niagara Falls, Hannibal, MO, Boston historical sites, plus many more interesting and sacred sites.
