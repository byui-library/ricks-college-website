COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#:  Cloth 105
Title:   Beginning Clothing Construction Credit:   2
Text: Sewing Essentials --  Singer’s Sewing Reference Library .

Course Objective: Students will learn basic clothing construction principles and develop basic sewing construction skills.  Emphasis will be on beginning and basic sewing techniques, pattern interpretation, and fundamental fitting problems.

Description: Application of construction principles from pattern to completed garment. Emphasis will be on unit method of sewing basic construction techniques and fundamental fitting problems.  Four projects will be completed during the semester plus seven sets of samples.  We will start with very easy projects and continue on to more difficult projects.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)  The projects must be completed and include a pillowcase with continuous bias trim, a skirt with waistband, slack with elastic waist and side pockets,  a dress with set in sleeves, facings and continuous buttonholes. The samples are to be professionally mounted on card stock paper then covered with plastic cover sheets for continued reference.
