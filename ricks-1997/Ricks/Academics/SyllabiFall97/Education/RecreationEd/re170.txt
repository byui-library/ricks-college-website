Purpose: The purpose of this class is to give the student a basic 
	skill and a comprehensive understanding of all aspects of 
	Whitewater as it applies to canoeing, kayaking, and rafting. 
	The class will center on safety as its primary concern and 
	to develop a good judgement by the user at all times as he 
	participates in Whitewater activities by himself or with others.
  
- River Safety: The student will be familiar with the safety in using 
	the river, dealing with emergencies, hazards, both natural and 
	man-made.
- The student will be familiar with all the strokes necessary for 
	Whitewater canoeing and kayaking, such as the draw, sweep, pry, 
	pushover, bow, skulling, etc.
- The student will be familiar with how to do braces, both low and high as 
	it applies to canoeing and kayaking.
- The student will be taught and will be required to do the eskimo roll 
	which is a way of rolling a kayak back up after capsizing.
- Rafting techniques with the use of paddles will be taught.
- The student should be able to execute forward and back ferries in all 
	three types of crafts.
- The student should be completely familiar with how to read the water, 
	the hydraulics and how they affect the type of craft he is using.
- The student should be familiar with difficult rating scales.  The student 
	will also learn how to determine the average, gradient of the river.  
	They should also become familiar with river flow volume as it 
	pertains to cubic feet per second CFS.
- The student will become familiar with tripping, where to obtain permits 
	and how. This will include shuttles, maps, foods, logistics, etc.
- The student will become fully aware of how to do emergency repairs on 
	canoes, kayaks, and rafts.
- The student will become fully aware of environmental concerns with using
	Whitewater and how to protect the environment so the water and 
	environment surrounding the river trail will not be degraded in 
	any way.
