The success of the Annual Giving program at Ricks College depends upon gifts from many different individuals and is welcome in any size. Annual Giving helps students continue their education while improving the quality of that education. It also:

  • Creates an important, ongoing partnership to bless future generations
  • And in some instances, can qualify you for admission to one of our Recognition Societies

You have an opportunity to financially help:

  • Cover unbudgeted, but meaningful contingencies;
  • Create additional, ever-important scholarships and grants-in-aid;
  • Make needed, but non Church-funded opportunities become a reality.

Annual Giving Can Be:

  • A Restricted Gift that you designate to a specific Ricks College program or priority of your choice, knowing exactly how and where it will be used.
  • An Unrestricted Gift to Ricks College without restriction, placing your confidence in the President and administration to use the money where the need is greatest, putting vital tools in their hands so that critical opportunities can be taken advantage of in a timely manner.
  • An Endowed Gift which establishes a more permanent and perpetual funding source, is established in a department of your choice, and is named for a loved one whose memory you wish to perpetuate.

If you have any questions about making an annual gift to Ricks College, please e-mail or call.  We will be happy to help you and your legal or tax advisors at no charge, and with complete confidentiality.
