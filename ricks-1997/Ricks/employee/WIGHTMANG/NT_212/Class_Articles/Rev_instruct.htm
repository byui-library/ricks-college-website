Instructions for Revelation 2-3 Assignment
The most important thing you need to do when it comes time to print out the chart is make sure that you print this chart out in landscape format.  In other words, print it so it appears horizontally on the page and not vertical.  Go into print properties and somewhere there should be a place where you can either choose "landscape" or "portrait".  The words may vary between the different software.  What ever the lingo is, make sure it is set to print out the chart horizontally (landscape) instead of vertically (portrait).  Ask for help if you need it.

The rest is fairly simple.  As you did your summary (you did do your summary first, right?), you probably noticed some phrases that were often repeated.  That is because there is a format that is being used to bring about a certain point.  You want to pay attention to what comes after these "phrases" for each city because they will vary from city to city.

Statements of Authority   These are statement of Christ's authority to the different cities, and usually are preceded by the phrase "these things saith he . . . ."  Again, they will differ for each city and that is what you will write down in the boxes across the first row.

"I know thy works"  is the statement that will usually precede the things that the various cities are doing that the Lord is pleased with.  The "works" statements for some cities are not all together, so pay close attention.  One of the cities has nothing said about them in this area.

Chastisement  In these area, write down the things that the Lord is not pleased with.  Two of the cities have nothing said about them in this area.

Instructions    Then will come some general or specific instructions for each city for you to write down in the appropriate box.

"He that hath an ear" is the phrase that will precede the blessings mentioned to those who overcome.  Don't forget that this is the heart of the assignment.  Pay attention to the various statements.  What do they sound like?  In what way do they relate to the theme of Revelation?  What to they have to do with Revelation 1:6?

Enjoy this assignment and may the Lord enrich your understanding of these chapters as you willingly put effort into understanding "one of the plainest books God ever caused to be written" (HC 5:342).

Now go print out the chart and get started!!!
