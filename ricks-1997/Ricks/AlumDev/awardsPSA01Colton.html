Presidential Service Award 2001

Sterling and 
Eleanor Ricks Colton

Sterling is a native of Vernal, Utah, graduating from high school there in 1947. He obtained his degree from the University of Utah with honors in banking and finance. He went on to Stanford Law School in California and earned his law degree in 1953.

Ellie was born in Hibbard just west of Rexburg. Her paternal grandfather, Thomas E. Ricks, colonized this area and established Bannock Stake Academy, the forerunner of Ricks College which bears his name. Ellie’s father died when she was only six years old and the family moved to Idaho Falls where she graduated from high school in 1949. She then obtained a degree in elementary education from the University of Utah and taught fifth grade in Salt Lake City for one year.

Sterling and Ellie met at the University of Utah. They were married in the Idaho Falls Temple in 1954 while Sterling was on leave from the Army. They spent the early years of their marriage in

Germany where Sterling was stationed with the Judge Advocate Division. The first two of their children were born in Germany.

Sterling started practicing law in Salt Lake City in 1956. Ten years later he joined the Marriott Corporation in Washington, D.C., eventually becoming senior vice president and general counsel of the company. Upon his retirement some 30 years later he was called to preside over the Vancouver British Columbia Mission.

Sterling and Ellie raised a family of four children and have 12 grandchildren. Sterling has been a bishop twice. He was a branch president in Germany and has served as a stake counselor. Ellie has been a stake Relief Society and Young Women’s president, in addition to teaching and leadership positions in various home wards. About a year after returning as the mission president, Sterling was called to serve as president of the Washington D.C. Temple. Ellie serves as matron of the temple. They presently make their home in Bethesda, Maryland.
