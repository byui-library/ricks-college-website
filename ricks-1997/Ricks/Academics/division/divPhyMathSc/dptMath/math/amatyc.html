AMATYC Student Mathematics League Test
What is the AMATYC Student Mathematics League Test?
It is a national test given to two-year college students by The American Mathematical Association of Two-Year Colleges (AMATYC). Tests are administered three times each year. Students can take one, two or three of these tests. Over 130 two-year colleges participate in this competition.

What’s in it for me?
Ricks College: Recognition will be given to the top three students at Ricks College. Cash scholarship awards will be awarded to the students with the top three averages of those who took at least two tests. The prizes are as follows: first prize $100, second prize $50 and third prize $25.

Northwest Region: Prizes will also be given for placing in the Northwest Region of AMATYC.

Nationally: The grand prize nationally is a $3,000.00 scholarship. Other cash awards are given for placing nationally.

Satisfaction: Aside from the awards, students who participate will have the thrill of trying something new and flexing their gray matter.

Who is eligible to participate?
Anyone can take the test. To have their score included in the national competition, a student must (1) not have a college degree and (2) be registered for a class at Ricks College.

What is the test like?
The test consists of 20 multiple-choice questions. The test covers material up to (but not including) calculus. Each question generally has 5 possible choices. Scores are earned as follows: 2 points for each correct answer, –½ point for each incorrect response and 0 points for items left blank. Guessing is strongly discouraged unless the choices can be narrowed to two or three possible answers. Since points are subtracted for wrong answers, a negative score is possible. Do not feel discouraged if your score is negative. On the first trial, a positive score is a good score.
