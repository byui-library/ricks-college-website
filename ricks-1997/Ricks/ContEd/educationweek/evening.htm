Celebrate The Passover
Dr. Victor and V-Ann Ludlow
Friday, June 4, 6:30 - 10 p.m.
Manwaring Center West Ballroom

Would you like to know what the Seder plate is? Afikomen? Maror, Haroset, and Matzahs? Have you ever recited the Dayenu? Experience first hand the Jewish Passover commemoration (meal with its attendant observances, symbolisms, songs, customs, and recitations). A fascinating experience you won't forget. This evening includes a full-course meal.

COST: $15 per person (note: this program has a cost in addition to Education Week Registration.) If you have already registered, complete only the name and address information on the registration form, and how many tickets you want. Fill in charge card information , or pay by check or money order.

Community are Invited to Attend.**
