1999-2000 AWARDS

 
Ricks College- SIFE  
Recently on April 17, 2000, Ricks college students along with advisior Mark Nygren travelled to Seattle, WA to participate in the Annual SIFE regional competition.  
May17, 2000, Ricks College was privileged to attend as a Rookie school for the highly touted International competition in Kansas City, MO.  Anticipation was high with the new experience.  Ricks College dominated the Regional events and now was prepared for the Internetional competition.
