COURSE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

This course will help each student to:

* actually have fun in a science class!!!

* inspire interest in and appreciation for biology,

* gain an appreciation for science and how scientific information is acquired,

* understand cell structure and function,

* understand the structure and function of several human organ systems such as the digestive, immune, circulatory, and reproductive systems,

* understand human genetics as well as current genetic techniques such as genetic engineering and DNA fingerprinting,

* understand evolution and ecosystems and how they involve humans
