I am convinced that without exception everyone of Father’s children on this earth who will read this book with a sincere heart and who will honestly ask God if it is true will feel the witness of the Holy Ghost confirming that it is. No exceptions, every person on this earth will know; every Russian, every Chinese, every American, everyone. Think of it - not one exception!

Read it with an open mind. Read as though it is true and then kneel and ask. Ask for a confirmation. Ask for a warmth in your heart. I promise you that you will receive it. I stake my reputation as a disciple of Christ on that promise.
