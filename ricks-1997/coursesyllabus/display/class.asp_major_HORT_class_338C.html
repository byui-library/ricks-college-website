Wedding Flowers Lab C
WEDDING LAB
CLASS CODE: HORT 338C CREDITS:  0
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This section is for third and fourth year participants.
DESCRIPTION: This course is designed to provide the student with an understanding of the requirements for wedding and entertainment designing.
TOPICS: Bouquet construction; supportive skills; consultation; portfolio development.
OBJECTIVES: To prepare floral majors for opportunities to plan and design floral products for weddings.
REQUIREMENTS: Tools and supplies as indicated by the instructor.
PREREQUISITES: Hort 335, FDM major or consent of instructor.
