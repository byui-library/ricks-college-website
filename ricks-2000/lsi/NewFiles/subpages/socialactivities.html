Social Activities

Each semester the Married Student Association cosponsors a social activity with the married student wards. This brings off campus and on campus married students together in a social network. Advisor Dave Macbeth said that past activities have included a Fall "theme" dance, Christmas break game night, a Valentines dinner/dance that has involved over 225 couples, and a summer picnic in the park. These activities are a great way for married students to get together and share information like housing openings, doctors, places to get your car fixed, parenting ideas, and etc.
