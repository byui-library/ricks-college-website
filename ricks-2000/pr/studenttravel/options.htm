The school sponsored student travel program offered by Ricks College was phased out effective at the end of summer 2001.  Thus BYU-Idaho does not provide or sponsor any transportation for its students unrelated to approved academic classes. There are, however, several travel options offered by companies in the community. The following is a partial list of companies offering travel options. This list is provided for your convenience and to help you weigh the options and make decisions based on your own special needs and preferences. These listings are informational only. BYU-Idaho does not endorsement any of these companies nor has BYU-Idaho investigated any of these companies to determine their safety record, their compliance with state or federal regulations, or their business standing. Before using any of these carriers, BYU-Idaho recommends that you determine that they are appropriate for your needs.

Because service may be limited, the best plan for travel is to make plans early. Planning helps students and their families have the reassuring knowledge that things are taken care of in advance. It also helps the companies offer services to fill the students’ needs. (See travel tips and regional map for basic travel routes.)

Special charters:  Charters may be offered by the commercial bus lines listed below at peak times of student travel such as beginning and end of semesters and holidays. Routes will be determined by these carriers based on expressed student interest and needs. Planning ahead by students and making early requests will help the companies see the needs and plan routes accordingly. Please check individual options for travel and contact the bus companies for specific details as early as possible.

Flights:  The closest commercial airport is in Idaho Falls, 25 miles south of Rexburg. SkyWest, a Delta connection, and Horizon Air are the major providers. Salt Lake City, about 260 miles south of Rexburg, has the closest international airport.

ABC Cab offers service between Rexburg and Idaho Falls.  Because the needs of Ricks College students is sporadic, it is advised to plan ahead when using a taxi service.  Call (208) 535-8394.

The Bulletin Board is located on the college's web site.  This electronic bulletin board was created to enable enrolled students to list their need for a ride or offer a ride to others.

C.A.R.T. (Community and Rural Transportation) is a private non-profit corporation which offers services between Idaho Falls and Rexburg and also within the two cities. They offer semi-fixed routes, dial-a-ride, and subscription service. CART buses do not normally operate on Saturday, Sunday, or holidays. Contact them at (208) 356-9033 or (208) 522-2278, (800) 657-7439 or E-mail to cartbus@ida.net.  C.A.R.T. and Greyhound Bus Line cooperate for extended services options.  

Easy-Way Taxi and Deliver offers service between Rexburg and Idaho Falls.  Because the needs of Ricks College students is sporadic, it is advised to plan ahead when using taxi service.  Call (208) 525-8344.

Greyhound Bus Lines has more than 3,700 locations in North America (excluding Alaska and Hawaii), Greyhound offers a lot of choices in planning travel. They are also willing to do charters exclusively for Ricks College students, if the demand is high enough (26 students going to the same destination). Call (208) 522-0912 or charter (800) 454-2487. The Idaho Falls operation of C.A.R.T. and Greyhound Bus Line cooperate for extended services options. 

Holiday Motor Coach, Inc. offers charter service at peak travel times for students.  For specific details of schedules and prices, call toll free (800) 249-4539 or call (208) 745-0005 or e-mail wade@holidaymotorcoach.com.

Morris Murdock Travel is a local agency offering assistance with travel arrangements. They have an office conveniently located on Main Street in Rexburg.  Call  (208) 356-4506, (800) 933-4506, or  fax (208) 356-8236 or e-mail morrismurdock@morrismurdock.com. 

Rexburg Downtown began service in May of 2001 and operates Monday through Saturday.  Transportation is offered to the public from the campus to downtown locations for shopping convenience.  Schedule runs hourly, Monday-Friday 4-11 p.m., Saturday, 10-4 p.m.  Special requests are accepted.  Payment of $1 fare upon boarding.  For more information, call (208) 656-9066 or (208) 313-2509.

Ride Board is located on the first floor of the Hyrum Manwaring Student Center.  This is a self-serve area where students offer or search for a ride with each other. More information can be obtained once on campus at the Information Desk on the Manwaring Center second floor.

Rocky Mountain Trailways:  Salt Lake Express offers daily ground transportation to and from Salt Lake City (airport & downtown).  As part of the National Trailways Transportation System, they offer convenient round trips daily leaving Rexburg in the morning and arriving back in late afternoon or evening. The Salt Lake Express provides service to and from the Salt Lake City International Airport and the following communities: BYU- Idaho, Rexburg, Rigby, Idaho Falls, Blackfoot, Pocatello, Brigham City, Ogden, Layton, Kaysville, Farmington, Centerville, Bountiful. Call (208) 656-8824, (208) 356-9796 or (800) 356-9796 or visit online. http://www.saltlakeexpress.com or http://www.gotrailways.com.

SLASH (Salt Lake Airport Shuttle Hop) provides daily shuttle van service between Salt Lake City and and southeastern Idaho.  In operation since 1993, SLASH normally has two shuttles to the Salt Lake City International Airport with a third operating during the summer months and holidays. Seats are guaranteed by paid reservation. SLASH  can be reached at (208) 522-4743, (800) 359-6826 or by e-mail to slash@srv.net.

Sunrise Travel, Inc. is a full service travel agency with offices located in Idaho Falls and Pocatello.  Contact them at (208) 522-2974, (208) 234-2386 or (800) 627-2662.  

Teton Stage Lines has been serving Eastern Idaho since 1974.  Their motorcoaches are equipped with air conditioning, restrooms, VCR and a stereo system.  They also have 15 passenger and mini vans available.  Operating from a location adjacent to the Idaho Falls airport, transportation is also offered between Rexburg and Idaho Falls seven days a week, 24 hours a day (advance arrangements requested).  Charters to selected locations are offered at times of peak travel for the students.  For reservations or information, call (208) 529-8036 or (800) 285-8036, fax (208) 529-2364, or E-mail tsl@nstep.net.

UNI Discount Travel is a no fee or service charge agency offering discounts on major airline carriers both domestic and international.  You can contact UNI Discount Travel by and the the following methods -- just have your travel destinations and date ready:  (866) 435-9864 toll free,  (208) 542-1206, fax (208) 542-1507, e-mail  uni.travel@usana.com
