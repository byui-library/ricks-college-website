Ticket Office

 

The BYU-Idaho Ticket Office is located on the lower floor of the Manwaring Center.

It is open weekdays when school is in session from 9:30 A.M. to 3 P.M.
(Except on Tuesdays when the office closes at 2 P.M.

A limited number of season tickets are available from the BYU-Idaho Ticket Office.

To order tickets

Online:

Purchase tickets online

By phone:

Local: (208) 496-2230
Long distance: (800) 717-4257
Tickets may be ordered by phone and may be purchased with a MasterCard, Visa, Discover, American Express, or by internal requisition (employees only).

There is a $2 phone ordering fee.

When picking up tickets paid by credit card you must show picture identification.
