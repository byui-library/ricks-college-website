Great Basin Discovery

 

DISCOVERY is a program in, for, and about learning in the great outdoors.  Each five-week session may offer explorations in the wilderness areas of Yellowstone, the Tetons, the Great Basin, the Pacific Coast, or other back-country locations of special natural history interest.  DISCOVERY teams travel by diverse means in order to see and learn about the great outdoors.  Members of the DISCOVERY teams can take classes in a variety of fields including biology, botany, english, geology, health sciences, history, humanities, religion and zoology.  Most of these classes can assist students in completing the general education requirements of Ricks College.

April 30 to May 30, 2001
  Explore the wild and beautiful Great Basin of the Western United States with Gene Weller of Biology, and Gene Thompson and David Peck of History from April 24 to May. 
   This discovery group will spend the first three weeks camping, hiking and exploring in central and southern Utah and Nevada.  The last two weeks will be spent in the Salmon country of central Idaho. Some of the highlights of this trip include swimming in the Great Salt Lake, viewing the beautiful red rock country of southern Utah, visiting church historic sites and exploring Leman Caves in Nevada.  The objective of this program is a quest for knowledge and fun using the wilderness as a classroom. 

Enrollment limited to 30 participants
