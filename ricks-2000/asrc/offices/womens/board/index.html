Fall Focus on Women
"Your Voyage to Self Discovery"

Fall Focus on Women is a new event that AWS is starting for Fall semester 2001. Comparable to Women's Week, but on a smaller scale, FFW's purpose is to help women discover who they are. During Fall Focus on Women, AWS has planned activities centered around safety, service, social, and the spiritual aspects of a woman. October 17-20.

Wednesday, October 17
Self defense class. Taylor cultural hall, 6-8 PM.

Thursday, October 18
Come see our table in the MC to join us in our commitment to serving others.

Friday, October 19
Julie de Azevedo in concert and Preference Dance. Tickets available at Ticket Office for $15 for both events.

Saturday, October 20
Self-improvement workshops. Taylor Chapel, 10-12 A.M.
