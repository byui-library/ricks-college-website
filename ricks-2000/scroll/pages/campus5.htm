MEET JACK


Photo by BECCA PASSEY, scroll.

by JOSHUA GIGUIERRE
scroll staff

While the house is still and quiet, Jack Weyland awakens and looks at the clock.

It reads 4:30 a.m.

He arises from his bed and walks silently toward his computer where he begins his morning ritual.

For the next three hours, Weyland will write.

Weyland is a physics teacher on campus, as well as an accomplished author of more than 50 publications. Popular titles he has written include Emily, A New Dawn and Sam.

He became interested in writing during his ninth grade year when he became a staff member of the school paper.

"I wrote a story which ran for several weeks. I can't even remember what the story was about, but I do remember how much I enjoyed writing and being read," Weyland said.

The New Era magazine gave Weyland his first opportunity to be published when they accepted an article he had submitted.

"I wrote a story and sent it to the New Era. They accepted it and sent me money. I thought it was great!" Weyland stated.

Weyland's writings fall under the category of fiction and are sold in most Deseret Book stores. Some of the experiences mentioned in his writings, however, are based on actual events. Books with such experiences include: Sara, Nicole, Kimberly, Brittany, Stephanie and Emily.

The characters and topics are oriented toward the youth of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in an effort to make a difference in their lives, according to Weyland's Web site, jackweyland.com.

"I write about what I know. I grew up in the church. Because of that, I can relate and depict a Latter-day Saint better than anything," Weyland said.

Along with writing and teaching, Weyland enjoys racquetball, running and hiking.

"I'm always eager to learn new sports or talents. After all, life is a pursuit of knowledge," Weyland said.
