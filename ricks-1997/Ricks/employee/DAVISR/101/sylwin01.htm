CLASS REQUIREMENTS

 

1. QUIZZES: There will be preparation quizzes (10 pts each) in class. No make-ups unless for a school activity, cleared with me in advance.

 

2. EXAMS: You will have two major exams plus the final, 100 points each. All are comprehensive. Review sheets for each are on the web.

 

3. GRADES: A weekly printout showing your final grade to date will be posted in the hall. Grades are based on total points to date, with a 90, 80, 70, etc. cut-off. Your lowest quiz score is dropped. (Please note that it will not be included in the total even though all your scores appear on the printout. Computers are smart. They can do that kind of stuff.)

 

4. REVIEWING: All the paintings are found on my web site. Click on any painting to have it enlarge to full screen size. You can even save it to your own computer or disk. Give it a try.

 

Don’t wait until the last of the semester to get your music CD. Start enjoying it now. By the end of the semester and your final listening quiz you will be very comfortable with the selections.

 

5. EXTRA CREDIT: There is none.

 

6. WANTS:  Please bear with me on three things: no food in class, no baseball caps, and I like to start on time, not five minutes after the bell.

 

 

 

I’ll try to adhere to this schedule, but life being what it is, some things may change. You’ll be informed if they do.
