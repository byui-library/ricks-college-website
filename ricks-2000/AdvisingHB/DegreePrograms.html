II. ARTS AND LETTERS
Six credits are required: Three credits from the Arts classes listed below AND completion of the FA 100 (required non- credit lab), and three credits from the Letters category listed below.

Exposure
Credits
FA 100 Fine Arts Attendance 0
Every Arts class requires the accompanying lab, FA 100. The lab does not have to be taken the same semester as the Arts class, but the Arts requirement for graduation is not fulfilled until FA 100 is completed. Students needing Arts credit to graduate should sign up for FA 100 their first semester at Ricks.approved fine arts events.

Information Fine Arts 100
1. An Arts course (such as Humanities 101) is required of every student who receives an Associate in Arts and Sciences degree.

2. FA 100 is the lab for all Arts courses and is also required for the A.A.S. degree.

3. Only those who have an Arts course on their transcript dated prior to Fall Semester 1992 will have the requirement waived.

4. FA 100 students are taught concert etiquette and must pass an examination on concepts and vocabulary relating to the fine arts.
5. Each student must attend ten fine arts events.

6. Information on upcoming events and FA 100 concerns can be obtained by calling 1251.

7.Student files will be retained for several years, so that students returning after an extended absence can still count events attended before they left Ricks.
