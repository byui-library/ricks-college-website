COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 104
Title: Fashion Illustration  Credit: 3
Text: 

Course Objective:

The objective of this course is to give the fashion design student the tools to be able to design on paper.  Introduce the student to the form, movement, and limitations of the human body so they have a concept of the parameters of fashion design.  Give the student practical experience in drawing and rendering material and clothes.

Description: (Topics Covered)

Principles and Elements of  Design
Design and the Human Body
Proportions and Movement of the human body
Seven Laws of the Fold
Interpretation of Types of Cloth with Line
Rendering Patterns
Quick Sketching

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)

Students are required to design a portfolio of six different fashion designs, prepare them in a formal presentation complete with: name, line drawings, full-figure illustrated rendering, and material samples.

Other:

Required complete one sketch book of sketches.
