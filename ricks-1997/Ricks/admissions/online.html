Admissions and Scholarships On-Line Services


Apply to Ricks as a Summer-Only Student.

If you have already applied, you may check your admissions status by clicking here.

We are now accepting applications for the following terms:

  • Winter Semester (October 1 Deadline)
  • Fall Semester (February 15 Deadline)
  • May, June and July Summer Terms (February 15 Deadline)

Note:  The Taxpayer Relief Act of 1998 requires that we collect tax information for all applying students.   It will be helpful if you have the name, social security number and address of the person who will claim you for federal tax purposes.  This will usually be your father.

To apply on-line for admissions and scholarships, just follow the following simple steps.

  1. Click on the Continue button below.  The Honor Code   and Dress and Grooming Standards will appear.  Please read these standards carefully.  You will be asked to sign a commitment to abide these standards later in the application process.  Once you have read this form, click on the Continue button at the top of the page.
  2. The Student Commitment and Confidential Report will then appear.  This form must be printed and signed by you.  After you have signed this form, please give it to your LDS bishop or current ecclesiastical leader (non-LDS).   He will complete the form and send it to Ricks College.  Once you have printed this form, press the Continue button again.  The Seminary/Institute Evaluation will appear.
  3. After you have printed the Seminary/Institute Evaluation form, please click on the appropriate icon and the On-Line Application will appear.  The seminary form must be completed and sent by your current or most recent seminary/Institute instructor.
  4. Complete the On-line Application and submit it to Ricks College.
