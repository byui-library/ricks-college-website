Ricks College
ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS POLICY
Honor Code Block

The intended use of Ricks College's electronic communication systems, i.e. e-mail, voice mail, network, the Intranet and the Internet, etc., is for business-related purposes only. Incidental and occasional use of these systems for personal communications within Ricks College is permitted, but such messages will be treated no differently from other business messages. Personal use should be minimal and should not occur under circumstances that interfere with work responsibilities or that inappropriately consume College resources or materials. Ricks College will monitor employee use of College-owned electronic communications equipment. Examples of inappropriate use include, but are not limited to:

1. Viewing, storing, or transmitting pornographic, erotic, indecent, or other offensive material.

2. Using work time to look up information for personal use or to play games.

3. Using communication systems for personal business purposes or for personal gain.

4. Using communications systems for advertising, selling, or soliciting.

5. Printing information for personal use without reimbursing the College for supplies. Incidental and occasional printing of information such as e-mail from family members is permitted.

6. Engaging in any activity that causes harm to communication systems including creating or propagating viruses, sending chain or junk e-mail, using e-mail to send frivolous messages or games, etc.

7. Communicating with others using a false name or an unauthorized assumed identity.

8. Using profane, lewd, or indecent language in electronic communications.

9. Using e-mail to harass, intimidate, or annoy another person.

Inappropriate use of electronic communication systems shall be subject to disciplinary action as outlined under the College's discipline and discharge policy. Employees who access inappropriate material via the Internet, using College-owned equipment, are in violation of the Code of Honor as well as the intended use of the Internet, and shall be subject to immediate termination.
