COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 285
Title: Business Principles Credit: 3
Text: Professional Practice for Interior Designers, Piotrowski
          Rhinoceros Success, Scott Alexander

Course Objective: 
To have each student develop a professional, creative resume.  To expose the student to proper interview techniques.  To present business forms and procedures and emphasize the importance of their use without exception.  To establish an understanding of the terminology used in interior design business practice.  To expose the student to various interior design business formations and their requirements.  To introduce methods of business promotion and advertisement.  To expose the student to the reality of the interior design business and how to cope.  To provide methods of compensations for services rendered.  To evaluate the student’s understanding of presented information by written exam.  To require the student to arrange two interview and report in written format.
 
Description:
This course is designed to introduce the student to the business aspect of Interior Design.  Information will be presented regarding procedural forms and professional practices for the interior designer, i.e., contracts, fee bases, purchase orders, letters of agreement, business formations, and terminology relating to the interior design business world.  Resume writing and interviewing for the job market will also be introduced.
 
Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
 
 Each student will develop a creative resume in preparation for the job search and have it printed professionally.  Each student will read RHINOCEROS SUCCESS and be prepared to discuss his/her relationship to the basic premise.  Each student will be required to take five exams to evaluate the understanding and comprehension of materials presented.  Each student will arrange two interviews and prepare a written report of each according to information presented in class.  Each student will complete homework assignments as given.  Each student should be prepared for quizzes at the beginning of each lecture.
