Applets:

Central Limit Theorem (David Lane, Rice Virtual Lab)
Draw a sample of size n = 2, 5, 10, 16, 20 or 25 from various distributions
Illustrates CLT graphically
 
Confidence Intervals (Webster West)
Choose an a-level and observe 50 confidence intervals
Repeat several times to obtain thousands of confidence intervals
 
Venn Diagram (Webster West)
Click on various events and see them highlighted on a Venn Diagram
 
Linear Regression (V. Phaniraj)
Add, move and delete points on a scatterplot
Regression line(s) updated dynamically
 
Correlation (Juha Puranen)
Choose a correlation and observe the associated graph
 
Guessing Correlations (John Marden)
Match scatterplots and their correlation coefficients
Compete against the world or request a group contest for your students
 
Histogram Bin Width Demo:  Old Faithful Data  (Webster West)
Illustrates effect of changing histogram bin widths
 
WebStat (Webster West)
Online statistical analysis tool
