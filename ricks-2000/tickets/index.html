Ordering by Phone:

Tickets may be ordered by phone and may be purchased with a MasterCard, Visa, Discover, American Express, or by internal requisition (employees only). There is a $2 phone ordering fee. When picking up tickets paid by credit card you must show picture identification.

Group Sales:

Groups of 20 or more may qualify for special discounts.  For information on discounts, please contact the Ticket Office.

Special Needs:

Please call in advance. When ordering please indicate if special seating is required ( i.e. hearing, sight, or wheelchair).

Picking up Tickets:

Tickets for all performances may be picked up prior to the performance at the Ticket Office in the Manwaring Center or one hour before curtain time at the location of the performance.

Seating:

You must be in your seats no later than 10 minutes before curtain time at which time seating is released. We will make every effort to seat latecomers depending on seating availability.
All events in the Hart Building are reserved seating and are not released unless indicated on the ticket.

Electronic Equipment:

Cameras and Tape Recorders are not permitted for any Concert events.  Before the performance begins, please turn off watches, pagers and phones.

Children:

For most concert events, children under 6 and babies are not admitted.   When ordering tickets for children please ask to see if children or babies are admitted.
