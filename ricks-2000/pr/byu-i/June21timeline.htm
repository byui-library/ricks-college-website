News release

Timeline: June 21, 2000 to June 21, 2001

June 21, 2000 Gordon B. Hinckley, President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, announces that Ricks College will become a four-year institution known as Brigham Young University–Idaho.

November 2000 Ricks College outlines framework for becoming baccalaureate-granting university, introducing the plan for integrated degrees designed to be customized to students’ specific postgraduate interests.

November 2000 Baccalaureate and associate degrees are announced for new BYU–Idaho. 51 baccalaureate programs by 2004, including 17 in 2001-2002.

December 2001 Ricks College begins search to fill 25 new faculty positions, receives 650 applications.

January 2001 Ricks College announces new joint-use facility to serve religious and academic needs.

March 2001 Ricks College receives candidacy status for bachelor’s degree programs.

April 25, 2001 Ground broken for new, 54,000 square foot, multi-use facility.

April 28, 2001 New BYU–Idaho building and gardens to be named after Thomas E. Ricks.

May 29, 2001 Ground broken for new, 44,000 square foot Spori Building.

June 6, 2001 Last Ricks College graduation.

. . . and Beyond

August 10, 2001 Officially change school name to BYU–Idaho.

September 4, 2001 First day of classes at BYU–Idaho.

September 18-22, 2001 BYU–Idaho celebration and homecoming.

October 27, 2001 Last home football game.

March 1, 2002 Tribute to Ricks College Athletics.
