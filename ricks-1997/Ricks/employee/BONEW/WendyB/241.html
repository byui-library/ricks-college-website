COURSE DESCRIPTION

    Fundamental dance techniques in ballet and modern emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time and energy awareness.

OBJECTIVES

    1. To understand the relationship between dance and the gospel and our stewardship as dancers in the Lord’s kingdom.
    2. To develop flexibility, strength, endurance, balance, coordination, phrasing, and dance performance skills.
    3. To develop a kinesthetic awareness of body alignment.
    4. To demonstrate a knowledge of and control over the elements of dance (time, space, and energy).
    5. To develop fundamental techniques in improvisation and composition.
    6. To develop a keen awareness and appreciation for dance as an art form.
    7. To become knowledgeable of basic modern dance history.
