OBJECTIVE: To strengthen our individual testimonies of the restored gospel.

It is hoped that our study of Church History might help us see and appreciate the hardships and joys of the early Church members in order that our own appreciation for the Church might grow. We can learn much by watching the successes and failures of our forefathers.





TEXT REQUIRED: Church History in the Fullness of Times
