The Galley, where food, friends, and fun get together.

The Galley, an "All you care to eat" cafeteria style dining facility, is located on the second floor of the Manwaring Center. The Galley is for contract students that desire a great meal and cash customers are welcome at door prices. The Galley is the pride of Food Services.

Contract Meal Plans
The Galley offers gold, silver, and 1 meal a day contract meal plans.
Regardless of the plan, the student determines which meals to eat in the cafeteria.

Gold Plan:
The gold plan offers the best value for your food dollar. It offers 19 meals a week for as little as $3.87 per meal. 

Silver Plan:

 The silver plan is our mid-range option.  It offers up to14 meals a week at a cost of $5.04 per meal.

1 Meal A Day:

 The 1 meal a day plan is our lowest option at $320 and offers 80 meals a semester for $4.00 per meal.    This plan is for any time and any day.  Use them any way you want.  Those living in cafeteria style dorms are not eligible for this plan..
