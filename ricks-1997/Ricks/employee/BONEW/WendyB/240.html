COURSE DESCRIPTION

    Fundamental techniques in ballet and modem dance emphasizing development in body, motion, space, time, and energy awareness.

OBJECTIVES

    1. To incorporate gospel principles in developing flexibility, strength, endurance, balance coordination, phrasing, and dance performance skills.
    2. To develop a kinesthetic awareness of body alignment.
    3. To learn, develop, and perform axial and loco-motor movement progressions
    4. To demonstrate a knowledge of and control over the elements of dance (time, space and energy).
    5. To experience fundamental dance techniques in improvisation and composition.
    6. To develop a keen awareness and appreciation for dance as an art form.
    7. To become knowledgeable of basic modern dance history.

REQUIREMENTS

Professionalism is stressed:

    1. Punctuality
    2. Appropriate and modest attire
    3. Hair out of face
    4. No chewing gum
    5. Total involvement and attention during class

Attendance/Participation:

    Three (3) absences are allowed without penalty. Four to six (4-6) absences will result in lowering of grade. The seventh absence will result in automatic failing of course. Three (3) tardies equal one absence. Three observations equal one absence. To receive credit for observing you must take in-depth notes on your observations and turn them in to the instructor.

Critique:

    A two-page, typewritten critique (not a review) of a live dance performance will be submitted by each student. Late papers are not accepted.

Movement Combinations:

    Will be performed periodically throughout the semester for evaluation by the instructor. These exams can not be made up. You will be given a zero for that evaluation.

Final Exam:

Grading:

Students are encouraged to meet with the instructor at any time they feel the need for feedback or other assistance. It is hoped that this will be a rewarding semester in which the student begins to feel the exciting dynamics which can exist in skillfully relating body and spirit. Come ready to work hard and enjoy.
