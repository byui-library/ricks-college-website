GRADING -
Preparation:
For each class meeting, one hour of individual study of  the teachings of the prophets. (Scripture or other writings, including all assigned reading for the day) 27 hours of individual study during the semester.
Be prepared for a quiz over the reading each class meeting
Write about things you thought or felt while reading (scripture margins, journal, etc.)
Consider questions that could be asked during class
Be doers of the word, not hearers only.
Participation:
Attendance is required. Each absence deducts 5%; Each Tardy deducts 3 %.
Contribute your ideas, thoughts and questions; listen and keep notes of things you think, feel and learn.
Honor Code observance will have a positive effect on your grade and life.
Quality: Time invested does not necessarily result in excellence. Performance (quality) = effort + competence. Write  paper which focuses on historical events or prophetic teachings that you have studied this semester in the Old Testament. Some of you will cover more pages of each assignment than others during a reading session.  At the end of the class, I will ask you to report the number of hours you have spent studying, searching, and cross-referencing the scriptures, focusing on the principles, doctrines and history of the daily suggested references for class discussion.  Hopefully, many of you will study a majority  of the doctrinally significant passages of the first half of the  Old Testament this semester.  During the final exam period each of you will be asked to report the percentage of 27 hours (1 hour for each class session) that you have spent studying the scriptures.  As you read, prepare to write of some historical event or doctrine as follows:
Explain the doctrine (s) /principle (s) associated with an historical event which you choose
Personalize your paper by describing how the application of the principles of the selected doctrine has had a positive effect in your life or the life of someone you know.
Length - This paper should demonstrate your understanding of the doctrines, principles, and history - minimum 5 pages Double Spaced and typed.
Due - beginning of class near the end of the semester.
Complete each of  three open book worksheets
