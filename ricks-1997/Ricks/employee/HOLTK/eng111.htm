RATIONALE. A Rationale must be included with each essay. The Rationale is a two paragraph response to the idea you have written. It details the thoughts you have thought, the concerns you feel, the insights enjoyed, or the confusions that still press you about class discussions. Consider including in your Rationale principles we have discussed or misgivings or questions you have. Write your Rationale in the form of a letter to me. In it write about whatever you think I should know in order to evaluate your writing and thinking appropriately.

Use the first paragraph of your rationale to keep me in touch with your life and attitudes in general. How is the course going for you so far? Are you adjusting to Ricks? Are you happy about what you are learning? Any problems that are keeping you from doing your best work? Use the second paragraph to talk with me about successes you are having in your writing, failures you are having difficulty overcoming, worries you have about what we are studying, and notions and concepts that just don't make sense yet.

In one way or another, what you discuss in these two paragraphs should amount to a rationale for the grade you think you deserve for the particular assignment. At the conclusion of your second paragraph, identify your purpose audience, then grade your work A-F, following the guidelines we'll discuss in class. Note: All written pieces submitted without a Rationale and a grade will be returned unread and cannot be used for the Portfolio. Note this policy well. There will be no exceptions.
