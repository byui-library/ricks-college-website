Geology 104:  Natural Disasters and Resources Syllabus

Instructor: Mark D. Lovell
Office: Romney 142                 E-mail: lovellm@ricks.edu
Lecture Room: Romney 145    Phone: office 356-1903   home 538-7935
                                           
Section: 1  Class Time: Tues. & Thurs. @ 12:30-1:45 pm
Prerequisite: None              Credits: 3   
Text: Geology and the Environment - Bernard Pipkin
Field Trips: Idaho National Engineering Lab - Cost approximately $5

Objectives: 1)To increase individual awareness of the interrelationships that exist between the human population and the earth. This course emphasizes understanding physical geology as it relates to geologic hazards (earthquakes, volcanoes, landslides, floods, etc.) and geologic resources (minerals, energy resources, building materials, water, etc.).  2)To help prepare individuals as they participate in decision making regarding disposal of all kinds of wastes, and urban and rural development by better understanding the geologic environment in which they live.
