Summer Business Development Project

Standards:  Computer generated, doubled-spaced, <= 4 pages
Grading:  Content, Application to the text, grammar, spelling, and punctuation

 Phase I--Vision/Blueprint

1)    Deciding on a Business (Pre-business plan preparation)

  1. Go to the Web site for the Small Business Administration and answer the applicable questions on the First Steps How to Start a Small Business (1 to 2 pages)

2)      Over-all vision and mission (1  to page)

  1. Give the over-all vision of your business
  2. Mission statement
  3. One paragraph of what your business will do (product, who is most likely to buy etc.)
  4. Three objectives (measurable, time frame)

3)      Assessment of Competitive position (1 page)

  1. Who are your competitors (restate answer to question in the First Steps Section)

  2. SWOT

  3. Competitive differentiation (one way you will better serve the needs of your target than your competitors)

4)      How much money do you think you will need to start the business and how will you raise it? (1 or 2 paragraphs)

5)      Organizational structure, management/leadership, human resource management.  If you are the only employee, talk about time and resource management. (1/2 to 1 page)
