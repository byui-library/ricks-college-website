Ricks College News Release, April 22, 1998

Ricks to hold 109th gradution

REXBURG, Idaho—Elder Henry B. Eyring of the LDS Church's Quorum of the Twelve will speak at Ricks College's 109th graduation ceremonies Saturday, April 25, as the college graduates its largest class in the history of the school.
Elder Eyring, who served as president of Ricks from 1971-77, will speak at commencement at 9 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Some 2,946 degrees will be awarded to 2,874 graduates at the annual commencement ceremonies. This year's graduating class is an increase of 119 students compared to the 2,755 who graduated in 1997.
The Class of 1998 has 1,153 males, 1,721 females, 1,100 returned missionaries, 458 married students and 78 foreign students. The states with the most graduates are Idaho with 1,100; Utah with 447, California with 231 and Washington with 195.
Commencement will be preceded by the traditional academic march at 8:40 a.m. by faculty and graduates. Diplomas will be handed out at the various division convocations, all scheduled for 11:30 a.m. at various locations. The Spori Scholar luncheon, which honors the top graduates for their academic achievements, follows at 1 p.m.
Graduation activities begin Thursday, April 23, with the paramedic graduates pinning ceremony.
Friday, April 24, will include an alumni award luncheon, a banquet for all graduates and their parents, the nurses pinning, a performance by Ricks College Extradancers and the graduation ball.
Ricks College is the largest private junior college in the United States. Owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, it has an enrollment of 8,350 students.
