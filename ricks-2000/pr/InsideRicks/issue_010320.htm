ORIGINAL THOMAS E. RICKS ORGAN BACK AT COLLEGE ONE MORE TIME

A trip to a local antique shop ended in the retrieval of a piece of Ricks College history. Two Ricks art teachers, Gerald Griffin and Matthew Geddes, found the organ that belonged to Rexburg founder Thomas E. Ricks in the Main Street Antique Mall in Rexburg. The two teachers knew it was part of local history when they found documentation that it had been on campus for a number of years, Geddes says. "It is meaningful to the area and to the college," Geddes says. "We felt like it should be part of Ricks College."

As a result, the antique was bought through money from the college's Performing and Fine Arts Division budget. Currently, the organ is on display on the second floor of the Eliza R. Snow Center for the Performing Arts.

REXBURG MOTHER HONORED AS THE EXEMPLARY WOMAN OF THE YEAR

Gail Jensen Seely, a Rexburg mother of eight children, was honored as the Exemplary Woman of the Year by Ricks College Sunday night. In presenting the award, college President David A. Bednar read a tribute from her husband Sylvan who described his wife as a woman "with no pretense. She lives what she believes. She is a role model as a wife, mother and grandmother."

She was raised in both Idaho Falls and Ashton. During her teen-age years, she helped on the family farm and worked in the family's restaurant. She also taught violin and guitar lessons to many other children. Her interest in music, specifically the violin, has remained strong throughout her adult life. She has been a member of the Madison Hospital Board of Trustees since 1997 and is presently serving as chair of the board's Beautification Committee. She has been active in church work and has served as a Primary worker, ward chorister, Young Women president, Relief Society president and Relief Society advisor in the Ricks College LDS Fifth Stake.

UTAH STUDENT SELECTED AS WOMAN OF THE YEAR

Tamara Burton, a sophomore English major from Hyde Park, Utah, was named Woman of the Year at Ricks College on Thursday. Always known to have a smile, Burton says she came to Ricks because she was attracted by "the spirit on campus and the unique environment. I felt comfortable here." She says the key to enjoying Ricks is "getting involved and serving, whether it's your next door neighbor, roommates or through clubs on campus. The more you serve and love other people, the more you grow yourself." While at Ricks, she has served as an executive officer for the Associated Women Students, been an R Team leader, sung in the Women's Choir member, joined Phi Theta Kappa honor society and performed in Guitars Unplugged. She will graduate in April and plans to continue her studies at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. She is the daughter of Robert and Dixie Burton of Hyde Park and graduated from Skyview High School in Smithfield, Utah, in 1999.

 

REXBURG NATIVE NAMED RICKS BUSINESS LEADER OF THE YEAR

Rexburg native Stephen G. Hanks, president of Washington Group International (formerly Morrison Knudsen Corporation), will be honored as Business Leader of the Year during Ricks College's annual Business Summit Thursday, March 22. The Boise resident will speak at a forum in the Taylor Chapel at 2 p.m. The address will be carried live by KRIC-FM.

Hanks, a 1968 graduate of Madison High School, attended Ricks and received a bachelor's degree in accounting from BYU. He received an MBA from the University of Utah and a law degree from the University of Idaho. He is a member of the American Bar Association, Idaho State Bar Association and American Institute of Certified Public Accountants. He is a 23-year veteran of Washington Group International, Inc. He joined the company in 1978 as an attorney in the legal department. In 1991, Hanks was promoted to vice president and corporate secretary and was named senior vice president and general counsel in 1992. He has been active in community affairs and has served in the church as a stake executive secretary, stake mission president and stake high councilor. He currently serves as the high priest group leader. Hanks and wife, Debra, have four children.
