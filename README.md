# Ricks College Website Archive, 1997–2001

**Visit the archive: https://byui-library.github.io/ricks-college-website/**

A restored copy of the Ricks College website (www.ricks.edu) as it stood between 1997 and 2001, before Ricks College became Brigham Young University–Idaho. It's maintained by the BYU-Idaho David O. McKay Library.

## What's here

| Folder | Contents | Start page |
|---|---|---|
| `ricks-1997/` | The original Ricks College site, including academic departments, admissions, news, and faculty and staff pages | [`ricks-1997/Ricks/Ricks.html`](https://byui-library.github.io/ricks-college-website/ricks-1997/Ricks/Ricks.html) |
| `ricks-2000/` | The 2000–2001 "Ricks College becoming BYU-Idaho" site and departmental sites | [`ricks-2000/default.html`](https://byui-library.github.io/ricks-college-website/ricks-2000/default.html) |
| `index.html` | The landing page that links to both | [Open](https://byui-library.github.io/ricks-college-website/) |

## Where it came from

- **A backup of the college's web server**, about 11,000 files.
- **About 5,600 files recovered from the Internet Archive's [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/)**. These are pages and images the backup's pages linked to but the backup didn't contain, plus the images and pages that those recovered pages link to, followed up to four links away from the backup.

Pages keep their original late-1990s look. The restoration changed only what modern browsers can't handle:
- Links were made to work offline from their original server.
- Image maps were kept, and old Internet Explorer–only menus were made to work in today's browsers.
- Text was converted to UTF-8, so punctuation displays correctly.
- Hit counters and server-side search and login boxes were removed. Forms that had real content are kept but don't submit.
- Links to other websites open the Wayback Machine's copy from that time.

## Known gaps

The backup was incomplete. Many images and pages, including most faculty and staff pages and many per-folder copies of shared images, weren't preserved anywhere. Some links and images in the archive are therefore broken.

A faculty art-history slide collection (`ricks-1997/Ricks/employee/DAVISR/HumPix/`) is only partly restored: about 1,500 of its 2,500 slides are included so far.

## How it's built

The archive is generated by a separate build tool, which restores the pages, recovers missing files from the Wayback Machine, and copies the output into this repository. **Don't edit files here by hand.** Changes are made in the build tool and synced in. GitHub Pages serves this repository's `main` branch.
