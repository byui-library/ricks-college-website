BYU-Idaho Home Page Policies

  1. Work under the direction of your department and division.
    The University provides links from its home page to various areas on campus. Viewers will assume that pages linked to the home page are official. To make your pages official, you must have an approval form signed by your department and division chairmen; and if it is an off-campus page, it also must be approved by the Internet Home Page Committee.
  2. Confine yourself to providing information from your own area.
    BYU-Idaho leaders have assigned areas such as Public Relations to speak officially for the university. Other areas such as Sports Information and the Alumni Association also speak officially to audiences but in their fields of specialization. Divisions and departments offer information to students and others from their own foundation of expertise. No one at the university is authorized to speak for the Church. If you are putting up information on the web, please be conscious of your boundaries and remain within them.
  3. Maintain the highest level of quality in your communication.
    Make sure that your information is accurate and presented in proper grammatical form. Your pages should be graphically pleasing and proofread just as if they were a brochure representing BYU-Idaho.
  4. BYU-Idaho pages are for BYU-Idaho information.

    Please be sure that BYU-Idaho pages are tied firmly to the mission of the university.

  5. Comply with existing policy regarding political activity.

    The essential function for the university requires strict institutional neutrality, integrity, and independence regarding political activities.

  6. Comply with existing policy regarding pornography.

    Do not engage in any activity that does not comply with the Code of Honor or the principles of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

  7. Do not use copyrighted material on your pages without permission.

    You must comply with state and federal copyright laws. Copyright violations create potential legal liability for both yourself and the university. For more detailed information regarding copyright laws, please refer to "The United States Copyright Office" Web Site.  It is located at: lcweb.loc.gov/copyright/

Rules for Building Web Pages at BYU-Idaho

  1. The employee who is given responsibility for the web page is accountable for its security. Students may work on the web pages but the employee is the only one who is allowed to upload the web material onto the server. Passwords should not be given to the students under any circumstances.

  2. Normally, an employee, department or division web page should not exceed 200 mb of disk space without approval of the Internet Services Manager.

  3. Icons, pictures, and background elements that can be used by more than one person exist in a shared directory. Unless specifically exempted, materials of this type that you use will become "college domain" and will be available in this directory.

  4. Web pages must be free from copyright violations. You are responsible to verify permission for any copied materials.

  5. Include a copyright notice in the bottom of your documents.

  6. When using a person's name or picture, you must obtain written permission from the individual before the material is made public. Failure to do so is a violation of their privacy.

  7. The BYU-Idaho masthead is required on the first page of the document and may be used on following pages if desired. This will help maintain an official image and give the impression of consistency for all programs.

  8. To make a major change to an existing off-campus web page, rewrite the document to meet your needs. Upon completion, replace the original file and notify the Internet Home Page Committee (through Public Relations) for review. This review process is not used to critique information per se. It is used to keep home page offerings within BYU-Idaho guidelines of style and policy.

  9. University policy prohibits the use of any commercial advertising.

  10. Each web page requires a link to the BYU-Idaho home page. Construct your document so that the top banner and/or the standardized footer is a link back to the home page. Departmental pages should also include a link to catalog information.

  11. For generic communication, you should consider using an alias that will provide a measure of privacy.

  12. Links in off-campus pages to other non-BYU-Idaho home pages must receive prior approval from the Internet Home Page Committee.

  13. Personal links and references to topics unrelated to the sponsoring department are inappropriate.

  14. Statements regarding LDS Church doctrine are issued only by the Church, not the university. Discussion of specific Church doctrine should not be included.

  15. Catalog information will be provided by the university. It is not necessary for departments to duplicate catalog information.

Steps for Building Web Pages at BYU-Idaho

  1. First, decide on your intended audience. Are you planning to build a web page for viewing only by people on the BYU-Idaho campus (on-campus web page) or are you planning to build a web page that can be viewed both by those on or off campus (off-campus web page)? An example of an on-campus web page would be some information by a support department specifically direct to employees or students.

  2. Fill out a form (available from Public Relations) and have your division chairman's signature if your web page is for your division, and your division and department chairmen's signatures if it is for your department.

  3. Submit the signed request form to the Internet Home Page Committee (in care of Public Relations). If your web page is for on-campus use, the BYU-I Internet Services Manager (currently Robert Reed) will be notified and asked to create a directory for you to upload your information. (You may want to contact him once you know you have permission.) If it is for off-campus use, the request must first be considered by the Internet Home Page Committee before a directory is created.

  4. Once you have uploaded the information, make sure that all links work.

  5. If the information is for off-campus use, you must notify a designated member of the Internet Home Page Committee to review your work (currently Public Relations). Once all modifications are made, your web page(s) will be linked to the official off-campus BYU-Idaho home page.
