PUBLIC SPEAKING
PUBLIC SPEAK
CLASS CODE: COMM 101 CREDITS:  2
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: COMMUNICATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Basic Skills requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Study and practice of public speaking skills and verbal ability, public self-confidence, message structure, presentation strategies and audience adaptation.
DESCRIPTION: Improving public speaking skills and verbal ability, self-confidence, message structure, presentation strategies and audience adaptation.
TOPICS: Fundamentals of communicating through effective public speaking.
OBJECTIVES: 1. Overcoming apprehensions
2. Selecting effective topics
3. Organizing a basic speech
4. Presenting five-seven speeches, including informative speaking and persuasive speaking
5. Incorporating speaking dynamics--pauses, enthusiasm, rate of speech, etc.
6. Developing effective introductions and conclusions to talks
7. Evaluating the success of other's speeches
REQUIREMENTS: Students are required to prepare and deliver speeches to audiences in public speaking settings.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: Fills General Education requirement under Basic Skills area.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2002
