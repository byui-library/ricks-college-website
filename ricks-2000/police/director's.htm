Director's Message
 
 

Garth Gunderson Director

    Welcome to our Web Page. Most of your questions concerning Police services that we
provide can be found at this site. It is our goal to be one of the most service oriented
departments on the Ricks Campus. Please call me at any time if you have questions or
concerns. Stop by the office and I will provide a tour of our facility and familiarize you with
services that we provide.

    The officers at Campus Police work hard to prevent crime, thereby reducing the number
of victims and time needed to solve crimes.   We provide many educational programs and
are always looking for opportunities to make presentations.  We are open 24 hours a day
365 days a year.  Please call 356-2448  if we can be of Service.

Garth Gunderson
Captain-Rexburg Police Department
Director- College Division
