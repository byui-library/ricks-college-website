What is the NSO Board?

The NSO Board is a student leadership group consisting of approximately 20 or more student leaders who have been selected to assist in the planning and implementation of Orientation activities and events.  NSO Board members volunteer their time and talents in serving the new students who come to BYU-Idaho.   

As a member of the NSO Board you will have the opportunity to:

  • Receive significant event planning and leadership training experiences while working with members of the NSO Team, in planning and implementing various orientation activities and programs.   

  • Attend a leadership retreat.

  • Assist in the planning and implementation of major NSO Events.

  • Have the possibility of attending the Y-Team Conference.

  • Work out of the NSO offices in Rigby Hall.

  • Gain significant experience which may prepare you for a position on the 
    NSO Team.

 


Like to help plan activities?  Want to get involved?   Join the NSO Board today!
