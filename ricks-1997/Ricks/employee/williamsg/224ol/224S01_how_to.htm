Welcome to the Summer Online version of the Doctrine and Covenants 224! I'm sure you have a few questions on HOW and WHERE to begin. I hope this will be of help. Below are few steps that will help you get going. . .

Step #1: READ the Course Syllabus

It is extremely important that you read the Course Syllabus! The Course Syllabus is designed to give you an introduction, overview, and outline of the basic structure and requirements of this course. Click here to view Course Syllabus.

Step #2: Orientation

Live orientation sessions are available for all students taking Ricks College online courses. These workshops are available for any students taking online courses -- Summer or Fall. Each session is the same, so pick one that fits your schedule.

The 45 minute sessions will include a live explanation and demonstration of your online course - - including information on how to access the course, how to submit assignments and take tests, materials you will need, and strategies for doing well in an online course. If you are not going to be on-campus when you take your online course, be sure to attend one of the sessions before you leave.

For those who are not on campus or those who missed the Live Orientation Workshops, the ITC (Instructional Technology Center) has posted an On-line Orientation designed to familiarize you with the On-line courses. Click here for the online tutorial.

Step #3: Purchase CD

You will need a CD as part of this course. These can be purchased at McKay Library 321. The cost is $3.00. If not on campus, send email to onlinehelp@ricks.edu or call (208) 356-1200 and Ricks College will send you a copy. The cost is $5.00 to cover CD plus shipping and handling.

Step #4: Accessing the course

1) Go to the Ricks Home page (www.byui.edu)
2) Click "Online Courses"
3) Click "Blackboard Courseinfo"
4) Login with Ricks College User Name and Password.

Step #5: Accessing Course Material

1) Click "Course Documents"
2) Click "Religion-Course Content Pages"
3) Click "Begin Course" [Be sure to visit the other links, such as Welcome, before you begin the course].
4) Complete Lesson #1

I hope you have a great and successful experience!!!
