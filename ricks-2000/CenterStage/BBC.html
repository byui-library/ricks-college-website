Big Band Cabaret

(Dinner, Show and Dancing)
Friday, April 19, 2002
Hart Auditorium
6:45-7:45 p.m., buffet
8 p.m. show, followed by dancing till 11:30 p.m.

The university will present two nights of Cabaret. The annual student cabaret will be held on Saturday, April 20, sponsored by the Associated Students of BYU-Idaho (exact details, times and cost will be announced at a later date).

However, Center Stage will present an additional Cabaret on Friday night that will be geared to area residents, alumni and employees, as well as interested students. The evening will begin with an elegant buffet in the Hart Auditorium with seating at round tables on the floor. It will be followed by a 1940s-style musical show performed by the Sunburst Dancers and Singers from Utah State University and the popular Crest Mark Orchestra from Logan, Utah. Dancing to big band music will follow until midnight.

Tickets go on sale to general public March 25. Cost for the buffet with seating at tables on the floor, along with show and dancing is $30 (or $25 for BYU-Idaho students). Cost for just the show and dancing is $10 (general public), $5 (BYU-Idaho students). Semi-formal dress is suggested. FA 100 - Orchestra/Band.
