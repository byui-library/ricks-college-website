Athlete of the Year
Dan Robinson
University of Hawaii Football

Dan knows a thing or two about facing adversity. As a freshman third-string quarterback at Ricks in 1995 he watched the Vikings rebound from a 2-2 early-season start to finish 8-4. A year later, as the starting quarterback he led the Vikings from a 1-3 start to a 6-4 finish. He placed third nationally in passing with a 255.9 yards-per-game average and fourth in total offense with 2,398 yards that year. Adversity continued at the University of Hawaii where a stress fracture in both legs caused him to reshirt in 1997. The following year, as the starting quarterback, Dan saw the Rainbow Warriors shut out without a win at 0-12. Then came last year's 9-4 season that included an Oahu Bowl victory against Oregon State. The wins are the most ever for a football team in NCAA history after coming off a winless season the previous year. "He's been the heart and soul of our offense," a grateful Hawaii Coach June Jones says of Dan.

Has just moved stateside to Utah after spending the last three years in Hawaii as a student at the University of Hawaii.

Dan is married to Jill Glade of Highland, Utah. They met in a 9th grade yearbook class and have been best friends ever since. They have two girls, two-year-old Malia and seven-week-old Nadelyn.

Dan served a Church mission to Hong Kong.

While living in Hawaii he has been a Young Men's president in his ward; also a Sunday School teacher to 15-year-olds; scout committee chairman, Elder's Quorum secretary and ward executive secretary.

For fun Dan likes to work on his old 86 Honda, horseback riding, snorkeling, throwing a football, fishing, or just being with his family.
