BIOGRAPHY

I love teaching Ancient World 200 and Modern World 201. We try to build pigeon boxes--a chronology--into the two courses, but we are more interested in the unfolding of ideas which impact us. Lecture-discussion along with student presentations works best in my classes. Finally, I'm not into guesswork in my classes: I let you know what I want you to know. Remember what I tell you and you're home free with an A.
