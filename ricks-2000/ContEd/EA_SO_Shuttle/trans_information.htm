Reservations must be made by no later than the Thursday preceding the start of your program by 3:00 p.m.  Any received after this time will be handled on a time and space available.  

If a cancellation is necessary, please call and notify our office at (208) 496-2339 so that we can accommodate your needs as well as ours. IF YOU MUST CANCEL, REFUNDS ARE ONLY AVAILABLE MINUS A $5.00 SERVICE FEE UNTIL TWO WEEKS PRIOR TO YOUR PROGRAM START DATE AFTER THAT NO REFUNDS ARE AVAILABLE. 

Please follow the directions below to make reservations for the Shuttle.  Please list your airline, flight number, and arrival time. Without this information we will be unable to track you down if your flight is late. The airlines will not give us any information on a passenger.

Please read through the agreement carefully to ensure you understand the requirements in order to ride our Shuttle.  We expect those arriving by BYU-Idaho transportation to observe the dress and conduct standards of BYU-Idaho.  Shorts, capris, tank tops, and grubby attire are not allowed on campus. 

If you have any questions feel free to call us (208) 496-1040.
