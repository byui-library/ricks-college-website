  Welcome   

On behalf of the faculty, staff, and administration of this great institution, I welcome you to Brigham Young University-Idaho. Your decision to join us is an expression of trust which we value and take most seriously. Our primary desire is to assist you in making your experience at BYU-Idaho both meaningful and memorable. We are confident you will contribute to and benefit from the unique environment found on this campus, often referred to as the "Spirit of Ricks."

BYU-Idaho exists to provide an education consistent with the religious and family values taught by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. The Church leaders who comprise the Board of Trustees of BYU-Idaho have provided a beautiful campus where you can develop spiritually, intellectually, socially, and physically. A wealth of resources exists at BYU-Idaho, particularly a competent and caring faculty, to help you achieve your personal and career goals. I encourage you to take full advantage of and magnify the many opportunities for learning provided at this unique institution.

The Honor Code and related standards are essential elements in the learning environment at BYU-Idaho. The decision you made to comply with and adhere to these guidelines will be a great blessing in your life and for the entire campus community.

Attending devotionals, studying for exams, meeting new people and developing lifelong friendships, serving in church assignments, participating in campus organizations, discussing an issue or a concern with a faculty member who truly cares---all of these activities will be important parts of your BYU-Idaho experience.

We are delighted you chose to attend Brigham Young University-Idaho and look forward to working and serving with you this year. We wish you the very best as you join us at this special school.

Sincerely,

David A. Bednar
President

Dear Student:

Thank you for choosing to enroll at Brigham Young University-Idaho. We welcome you and express appreciation for the strength you will bring to our campus.

Your experience at BYU-Idaho will be significantly enhanced as you become acquainted with our expectations of you and with what you may expect of us. Our new student orientation will make you aware of information and services that will be very helpful in this regard. The policies and procedures outlined in this booklet are also designed to provide you with the background you will need to succeed. In addition, your advisor, faculty members, and any BYU-Idaho employee will be eager to assist you in finding the answers and services you seek.

You are encouraged to quickly become involved in some of the many leadership and service opportunities that will be available to you. Student government, clubs, service organizations, performance groups, extra-curricular activities, and full participation in your ward and stake will help you create a well-balanced and exciting educational experience for yourself and others.

One of the life enriching characteristics you will have the opportunity to establish or strengthen while at BYU-Idaho is the ability to make and keep commitments. This capacity is becoming increasingly rare in the self indulgent world we live in; and being able to do so, will serve you well as a student and beyond.

We are confident that as you keep yourself informed, become involved, and keep the commitments you have made you will have a unique and very worthwhile BYU-Idaho experience. I would welcome the opportunity to become personally acquainted with you.

Good Luck!
Sincerely,

Robert M. Wilkes
Student Life Vice President
