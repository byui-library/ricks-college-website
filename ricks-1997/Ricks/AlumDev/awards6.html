Presidential Service Award
Richard "Rick" & Margaret "Peg" Young

In June of this year, Rick and Peg Young can look back at ten years of Church membership. Introduced to the Gospel by one of their employees, the Young's have embraced the Church. Although they are grateful for the success they are enjoying in their business venture, life seems to have taken on a new meaning. They have been stalwart members of the Ricks College President's Club for many years, taking an active interest in the college and its students. Their gifting, which has been of immeasurable value to the Church, has blessed, and continues to bless, the lives of many.

It was an interest in photography while still a teenager that ultimately led Rick to start Image Control Systems, a privately-held company. ICS represents major manufacturers and maintains industrial photo equipment, imagine conversion and transfer systems used in the graphics communications industry. The company primarily functions in the Pacific Northwest and Alaska with Rick as president and major owner.

Rick, a native of Idaho's panhandle, attended school in Rathdrum. He enrolled at Gonzaga University in Spokane, Wash., in early 1948, studying business administration, accounting, and working as the school's photographer. About this time he often assisted, and traveled on assignment with one of Time and Life magazine's major photographers in the Pacific Northwest. Drafted into the U.S. Army, Rick served overseas in France, Holland and West Germany and attended photo schools. In 1957 he obtained a degree in photo science from New York state's Rochester Institute of Technology. He was employed by Kodak and DuPont, demonstrating new photo imaging products, before moving to Chicago to work for a photo equipment manufacturer. In 1965 he started up ICS in Seattle, Wash., and, ten years later, created branch offices in Portland, Ore., and Spokane, Wash., and, later, in Anchorage, Alaska, and Vancouver, Canada. The latter office is no longer in operation.

Peg is a native of New Orleans, La. She attended St. Louis University, receiving a degree in childhood development from the Jesuit college. She taught school for a short time, eventually moved to Milwaukee, Wis., where she worked in a residential adolescent treatment center. Some time later she obtained a similar position in Vancouver, Canada. There she met Rick and they were married in 1974. Peg then worked for ICS at the order desk and in purchasing in the Seattle office.

In the early 1990s Rick and Peg stepped back from the daily operation of their company. They bought property and built a retirement home near St. George, Utah, and, in 1997 and 1998 served for 18-months in the Tokyo South Mission. Since joining the Church they've both been active with Rick serving in his ward and stake's Sunday School and Peg having served as Relief Society secretary.
The Youngs enjoy traveling and entertaining friends. They participate in civic affairs in the St. George area, taking a particular interest in an area brass band. They are also noted horse breeders and operated a Morgan horse farm on Vashon Island, in Washington's Puget Sound.
