COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 241
Title:  Space Planning II: Residential and Contract Fundamentals Credit: 4
Text: Designing Interiors, Kilmer
         Estimating For Interior Designers, Sampson
         A Complete Guide to Barrier-Free Housing, Branson

Course Objective:
 
 To build and add to the student’s knowledge of varied aspects of residential design, i.e. codes, specifications, budget analysis, programming, space planning, construction documents, and finish material selection.  To provide the student with an opportunity for further research skill development in relationship to design process and problem solution in residential design.  To expose the student to “special population” considerations in residential design, i.e., disabled.  To introduce the student to ecological concerns and energy conservation.  To provide an opportunity for team approach to design conceptualization, process and solution.  To further develop the student’s oral, written, and presentation skills.  To increase the student’s technical knowledge of HVAC, lighting, electrical plans, and detail drafting for residential design.  To provide the student with program requirements for individual research and development for an adaptive reuse project.  To introduce and provide calculation exercises for floor coverings, wall coverings, and window coverings.  To evaluate the student’s knowledge by exam and project development.
 
Description: A course designed to give students experience in research development, conceptualization, problem solving, space planning, furniture layout, fabric and finish material selection and calculation, and practical competencies in residential design.
 
 Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
 
 Each student will complete two assigned projects with requirements as listed.  Each student will complete homework assignments as given. i.e., carpet, tile, window, vinyl, wallcovering calculation assignments and compile in a notebook.  Students will be required to read from the text in preparation for lectures, quizzes, and exams.  Each student will be required to research assigned topics and present the information to the class in written and oral format.
