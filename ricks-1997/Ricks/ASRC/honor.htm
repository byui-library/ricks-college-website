The Associated Students of Ricks College

Mission, Principle & Function

Mission Statement

        The organization of the Associated Students of Ricks College (ASRC) is sponsored, authorized, and sanctioned under the auspices (protection and support) of the office of the President of Ricks College and the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.  Its mission is to:

       1. Harmonize in both purpose and principle with the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and its mission.

        2. Promote, support, and uphold the mission of Ricks College and its institutional traditions and standards.

        3.   Prepare and strengthen students in their social relationships, civic responsibilities, school spirit, and service to mankind by sponsoring meaningful and wholesome programs and activities.

        4. Provide for student growth and development through leadership training opportunities and guidance.

 

Stewardship

The Governing Principle of ASRC

        The ASRC is established of the principle of stewardship.  The principle of stewardship suggests that "...the earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof" (1 Cor. 10:26).  And "...it is required of the Lord, at the hand of every steward, to render an account of his stewardship, both in time and in eternity" (D&C 72:3).  "And whoso is found a faithful, a just, and a wise steward shall enter into the joy of his Lord..." (D&C 51:19).

 

Service

The Operative Function of the ASRC

        The ASRC function is one of service.  "Whenever we reach outside ourselves in the Lord's pattern to serve another, we communicate value to him, and receive value in return.  Genuine involvement ("with real intent")...yields a sense of individual worth and personal security" (Stephen R. Covey).  "Indeed, the very usefulness of our lives depends upon our willingness to serve others:...Surely Jesus has given us the model of the leader-servant.  '...whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant'" (Neal A. Maxwell, 20:25-28).

        Latter-day revelation underscores this standard of service: "For of him unto whom much is given much is required."  Each student should be encouraged to offer a gift of service in return for the countless gifts and educational opportunities provided by the church and Ricks College.

        "And in doing these things thou wilt promote the glory of him who is your Lord" (D&C 81:4).
