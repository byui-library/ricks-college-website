Ricks College Standards

Ricks College is sponsored by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints to provide an educational opportunity for students desiring to obtain their academic education in a spiritual setting. Gospel principles and secular teachings are blended together in a unique spiritual atmosphere where learning is enhanced by the continual presence of the spirit. Through this blending, every student has the opportunity to grow and develop into well rounded citizens prepared in a diverse campus community to face the challenges of a global community.

The Honor Code and dress and grooming standards adopted by Ricks College are endorsed by the Board of Trustees of the institution and are reviewed from time to time to ensure that they meet the mission and challenge given to the institution by the Board.
