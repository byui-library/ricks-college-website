COURSE OBJECTIVES FOR MACHINE TRANSCRIPTION:
 After completing this course, the students will be able to:

 1. Demonstrate the ability to manipulate the machine controls properly.

 2. Demonstrate ear-finger-foot coordination in transcribing material from the transcription equipment.

 3. Demonstrate the ability to keep the word processor in constant motion while transcribing material from the transcription equipment.

 4. Demonstrate the ability to transcribe mailable keyboarded copy on the first draft from the transcription equipment.

 5. Demonstrate the ability to supply appropriate words for each document.

 6. Demonstrate the ability to supply the proper punctuation in dictated copy.

COURSE OBJECTIVES FOR 10-KEY APPLICATIONS:
 After completing this course, the students will be able to:

 1. To learn to finger rapidly and accurately the 10-key calculator by touch through addition and subtraction drills.

 2. To learn simple multiplication and division.

 3. To learn simple interest calculations.

 4. To learn percentage calculations.
