Community Involvement Project

Entrepreneurship and Small Business Management B283 

During the course of the semester, you must complete 8 hours of volunteer service for an educational or charitable organization of your choosing (other than religious organizations).  I must approve in advance the work you will do and the organization for which you will work.  Your are responsible for finding a suitable organization and agreeing with that organization as to the nature of your service.  A responsible executive within the organization must agree to write a brief letter to me at the conclusion of your service setting forth the dates you worked, the number of hours and the nature of the assignment.

Deliverables:

Plan.  No later than the _______, provide me with a one page plan of how you intend to carry out this responsibility.  Your plan should include the organization, the executive who has agreed to write the letter for you, the work you will do, and what impact you expect your work to have upon the organization and/or its clients.  I will return your summary at the next class meeting with either my approval or disapproval.

Paper.  No later than the ________ provide me with a short paper (not to exceed three pages) discussing the work you actually accomplished.  Please attach to the paper the letter from the executive supporting your work.  Please include in your paper a discussion of how your work was of value to the organization and what you learned from the experience.  You may also make a recommendation as to whether I should continue this requirement in future classes.  As time permits, you may also be called upon to share a 5 minute oral presentation.  Please provide pictures or a video clip of your service or learning activity.

Why this assignment is required.  I believe citizens and business organizations have a responsibility to give back to the community in which they live and work.  There are many opportunities for doing this.  I hope through this exercise to expose you to at least one.  it is my hope that this service will be personally rewarding to you, and will begin a lifelong habit of community service.

One of the most useful things a businessperson can do is develop a personal network of acquaintances.  During the semester I will relate to you some of my own experiences with my network.  Networks are frequently built through community involvement.  This assignment will give you an opportunity to start building that network.

While you are working for your chosen organization, you will have an opportunity to see yet another organization in  operation.  Be observant.  It is just possible you may learn something new about how organizations work or a new skill based upon your work experience.

Finding a service opportunity.  You may have your own idea as to where you would like to donate your time, or you may already be working for one of our many fine community organizations.  If you need help finding a suitable project, please feel free to discuss this with me.  I maybe able to suggest ideas for you based upon the experiences of former students and your own interests.  If you would like other help finding an organization that needs your services, contact the Leadership & Service Institution at 356-2596.
