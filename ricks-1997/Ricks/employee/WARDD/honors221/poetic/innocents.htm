Innocents

Finally . . .
to realize
we are all
innocents-

willing what hurts
and
breaks us
apart
from all
we
hope to have
hope to love-

sets us free
from
puny purposes
of blind insistence
and
welcomes us
into
the life of One,
Who,
knowing all
may
forgive all,
bearing all,
that all
may be
light.

David L. Ward
September 30, 1993
