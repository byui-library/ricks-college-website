PHILOSOPHY AND GUIDELINES

THE FIVE BASIC OBJECTIVES OF ACADEMIC ADVISING AT RICKS COLLEGE

  • 1.Provide students with infor- mation on pertinent college procedures and policies.
    All students should be made aware of and have easy access to information on the policies and procedures regarding admissions, registration, adding and dropping classes, changing advisors, class attendance, credits and grades, academic probation, tuition and fees, financial aid and scholarships, academic honesty, standards, insurance, health care, and student rights.
  • 2.Help students understand their talents, interests, aptitudes, and capabilities.
    All students should be helped to review their past accomplishments and to assess their talents, interests, aptitudes, and capabilities. They should understand the meaning of the ACT data. They should be made aware of and given ready access to interest, aptitude, and talent measuring instruments available on campus as they desire to use them.
  • 3.Acquaint students with services and resources on campus designed to help them.
    Students should be made aware of the following resource and services and should be referred to them as needed: career choice, clubs, counseling services, devotionals, entertainment and fine arts programs, field programs, foreign and native American student programs, forums, handicapped assistance, health services, honors program, learning assistance programs, night classes, outdoor recreation, recreational programs, special education programs, specialized advising, student activities, study skills class, and tutoring.
  • 4.Help students determine career goals and plan an appropriate educational program.
    All students should be assigned in making career decisions. Career goals may include: advanced degrees, associate degrees, vocational training, preparation for marriage, etc. Once a career choice is made, then goals may be determined for one semester or several years.students should receive careful guidance in planning the best possible educational program to achieve the desired goals.
  • 5.Ensure regular contact, support, and follow-up for students through completion of education programs.
    Students should feel a warm, personal, caring concern from all aspects of the advising program. Students should be given feedback on their academic progress before the semester drop date and at the end of each semester. Each student should have the assurance of regular and helpful visits
