COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 255
Title: Contract Design I Credit: 3
Text: Space Planning Basics, Karlen
          The Codes Guidebook for Interiors, Harmon
          Time-Saver Standards for Interior Design and Space Planning, Chiara, Panero & Zelnik

Course Objectives:
 1. To provide the student with the experience in contract design that creates an awareness of the current state of the design profession.
 2. To build and add to the student’s knowledge of the varied aspects of contract design: specifications, codes, budget analysis, programming, space planning, construction drawings, and finish materials selection.
 3. To provide the student with an opportunity for further research skill development in relationship to design process and problem solution in contract design.
4. To expose the student to “special population” considerations in contract design.
 5. To provide an opportunity for team approach to design conceptualization, process and solution.
6. To further develop the student’s oral, written and presentation skills.

Description:
A course designed to provide students with experience in research development, building codes, conceptualization, specification and practical competencies in contract design.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1. Multiple small assignments to implement application of text and lecture principles.
2. Multiple open book quizzes/worksheets to facilitate reading comprehension.
3. Three major projects in contract space planning and design with professional, oral presentations.
