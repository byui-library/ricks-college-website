Ricks College News Release
August 18, 2000

Ricks expecting larger enrollment as college opens for fall classes

REXBURG, Idaho — Nearly 9,000 students are expected to begin classes at Ricks College in Rexburg when the fall semester officially begins Aug. 29.

Administrators at the largest private junior college in the country are gearing up for 8,900 to 9,000 by adding some extra classes, says assistant academic vice president Jim Gee. Last year's fall enrollment was 8,628 and winter enrollment was 8,840.

Students will discover several changes on campus caused mainly by the pending permanent closure of the Jacob Spori Building. Classrooms and faculty offices that were in the Spori have been moved to other locations on and off campus until a new building is constructed at the site.

Because there was no room on campus, the art department will be based at 16 E. Main St. Many art classes will be taught on the main level and five faculty members and the department secretary will be housed in the basement. Communication classes have been moved to other locations on campus; the student newspaper, The Scroll, has been moved to the Manwaring Center; and a new building currently is being constructed for the college's two radio stations and Graphics Services.

Twenty-three new full-time faculty members will be working at Ricks when fall semester classes begin. Max Checketts, assistant academic vice president, says the new employees have been hired to fill vacancies mainly caused by retirements. The overall number of full-time faculty members remains steady at approximately 370.

Most returning students are expected to arrive in Rexburg during the weekend of Aug. 26 and 27 and many activities are planned in an effort to orient new students with the campus. Many student services on campus will be open Saturday to serve incoming students.

Expanded new student orientation activities will span three days, says Allen Jones, the new student orientation coordinator. "We want students to feel more welcomed and comfortable at Ricks and to have parents get a better feel for campus and what their son or daughter will experience here," he says.

Saturday's expanded parent orientation will include the President's Reception from 11-11:50 a.m. in the Manwaring Center East Ballroom, parent information booths from 11-2 p.m. in the Manwaring Center West Lounge, a parent orientation assembly from 12-12:50 p.m. in the Taylor Chapel, parent seminars from 1-1:20 p.m. and 1:30-1:50 p.m. in the Manwaring Center and parent tours from 2-2:30 p.m.

All new freshmen students will be assigned to an R Team to make a smaller, more personal learning experience, Jones says. There will be about 100 groups of from 15-20 students. They will led by fellow students who will serve as peer mentors.

Saturday's new student orientation activities include the Meet Your R-Team Barbecue from 4-5:45 p.m. at the intramural play fields; new student commencement from 6-6:50 p.m. in the Hart; and campus tours from 7-8 p.m. An ASRC dance will be held in the Manwaring Center ballrooms from 9-12 p.m.

Church activities will get under way Sunday with the start of the 56 LDS Church wards on campus. Students are encouraged to check with apartment managers for time and locations. A student fireside will be held at 7 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium featuring Ricks President David Bednar and his wife, Susan.

Monday will begin with a student morningside focusing on the honor code and a continental breakfast in the Taylor Quad at 8 a.m. followed by 42 academic departmental advising meetings at 10 a.m.

A student humanitarian service project will be held from 1-4 p.m. Monday. This activity will start in the Hart Auditorium and then move to the intramural playing fields where more than 8,000 hygiene kits will be assembled for the LDS Church Humanitarian Service Center. The activity also will include the making of 100 quilts and cutting out of fleece toys and jackets. Displays will encourage students to get involved with service during their time at Ricks.

The Monday Night Extravaganza will run from 6:30-10:30 p.m. at the intramural playing field. Peter Breinholt and the Big Parade from Salt Lake City will perform. The ASRC will hold a dance and ice cream bash. The cost will be $2 for Ricks students or $3 without student ID.

Classes will begin Tuesday morning. Tuesday's opening devotional by the Bednars will be at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. It also will be carried live on KRIC-FM and rebroadcast at 8:30 p.m.
