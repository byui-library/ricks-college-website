ACADEMIC ADVISING

Majors Advising

Students who have selected a major are assigned a faculty advisor who assists them in individualizing their education. The advisor helps students
(1) choose courses to prepare them for graduation from Ricks,
(2) understand the lifetime opportunities in their particular major,
(3) adapt to the college environment and its challenges, and
(4) reach their potential in accepting the challenges and realizing the opportunities at Ricks College.

Undecided Majors Advising

Students who have not chosen a major are assigned to a faculty advisor that can help them fill General Education requirements and select a major.
