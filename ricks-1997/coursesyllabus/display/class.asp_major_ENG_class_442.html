Literature and Culture
LIT & CULTURE
CLASS CODE: ENG 442 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Examines the interdisciplinary relationship between literature and one of the following significant cultural areas: myth, philosophy, humanities, psychology, economics, or politics. (Required for English Literary Studies Emphasis.)
DESCRIPTION: Examines the interdisciplinary relationship between literature and one of the following significant cultural areas: myth, philosophy, humanities, psychology, economics, or politics. (Required for English Literary Studies Emphasis.)
TOPICS: The interdisciplinary relationship between literature and culture, e.g., (determined by the focus of the course) mythologies narrated in; philosophical or ethical positions explored through; psychological theories derived from; and art, economics, or politics related to literature.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Increased awareness of the cultural complexities of literature creation. (2) Substantial reading in and analysis of a significant interdisciplinary cultural area.
(3) Improved critical reading and writing skills.
REQUIREMENTS: Readings in primary literature; readings in secondary historical, cultural, and theoretical sources; short analysis and summary papers; a significant senior paper modeled after published examples of literary criticism.
PREREQUISITES: English major core classes.
