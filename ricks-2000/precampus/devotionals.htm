Weekly Campus Devotionals
It is hard to overemphasize the importance of attending the weekly campus devotionals.  For most students, this is a time to take a break from classes and come together with friends to receive spiritual guidance and instruction from prophets, apostles, and other leaders within the church.  Plan now to attend every devotional during your time at BYU-Idaho.  Here you can come and feel of the spirit and be instructed on spiritual matters and eternal principles.   Attending campus devotionals is as much a part of your BYU-Idaho education as is completing your general education requirements, perhaps even more so.

When and Where?
Devotionals are held each Tuesday at 2:00 p.m. in the Hart Building except for fall semester 2002 due to remodeling.  Overflow sessions for fall 2002 will originate from the Taylor Chapel and be broadcast live to the Kirkham Auditorium, Manwaring Center Little Theatre, and the New Multi-Use Building.  Summer devotionals will also be held each Tuesday at 2:00 p.m. in the Taylor Chapel.    

KBYI FM 100.5 broadcasts all devotionals live at 2:00 p.m.  You can see who will be speaking at upcoming devotionals by accessing the devotional speakers schedule.

Learning by Study and by Faith

Elder David A. Bednar of the Quorum of Seventy and President of BYU-Idaho has invited students to prepare themselves to learn and be taught by the spirit at devotionals by:

(1) Dressing up (Sunday Best) each Tuesday for devotionals.
(2) Bringing your scriptures with you to each week's devotional. 

 

President Bednar has promised students that faithful application of these two principles can have a strong influence on the spirit that is felt by participants as well as the message which is presented.

Doctrine & Covenants 130:18-19
Whatever principle of intelligence we attain unto in this life, it will rise with us in the resurrection.  And if a person gains more intelligence in this life through his diligence and obedience than another, he will have so much the advantage in the world to come.
