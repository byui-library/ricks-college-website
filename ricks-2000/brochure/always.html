Always Rethinking
At BYU-Idaho, we will always be rethinking education. We will continue to encourage reassessment by everyone who cares about the future of higher education. University employees, faculty members, students, parents, community residents, alumni and Church members are encouraged to give us ideas on how to provide even better educational offerings at BYU-Idaho.

For more information contact BYU-Idaho at (208) 356-1150 or look on our website at www.byui.edu.
