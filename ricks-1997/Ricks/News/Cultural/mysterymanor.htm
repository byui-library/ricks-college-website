"Mystery Manor" Dinner Theater
Thursday, June 14, 7 p.m.
Manwaring Center West Ballroom

The Vigilante Theatre Company of Bozeman, Mont., will present a dinner theater show entitled "Mystery Manor." At this very special dinner at Mulrooney Hall, everyone is an honored guest . . . but someone has unsavory intentions. Your hosts, Charles and Victoria, serve a frightfully elegant plum pudding, but when the topic of inheritance comes up, the proof is in the murder. Cost is $13 (which includes dinner provided by Ricks College Food Services).
