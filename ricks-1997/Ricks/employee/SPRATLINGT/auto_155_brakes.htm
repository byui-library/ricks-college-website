Text:  Automotive Chassis Systems, Brakes, Steering, Suspension   and Alignment. James D.   Halderman,
                2000. Prentice Hall Publishers, ISBN# 0-13-052317-8.

Materials:  Basic set of Mechanics Tools (a must) and the ability to apply yourself.

Course Objective/Description: This class is meant to give students a working knowledge of automobile/light truck braking, suspension and steering systems. Emphasis will be placed on obtaining hands-on service experience. Students will perform automotive repairs such as: alignments, disc and drum brake service, ball joints, shocks, springs, bushings, and other steering/suspension components. Each day that class is held there will be one hour of lecture and three hours of lab. Students will for the most part work on customer vehicles.  Working on "live" vehicles will provide real life experience in that students will learn how to work with customers and how to commit and finish a job.
