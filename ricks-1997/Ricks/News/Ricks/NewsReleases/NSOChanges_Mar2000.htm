Ricks College News Release
March 3, 2000

Changes coming to Ricks student orientation

REXBURG, Idaho — Beginning in the fall semester, incoming students attending Ricks College will have the chance to participate in an expanded orientation program spread out over three days from August 26-28.

The expanded fall orientation is one of several initiatives that the newly created Office of New Student Orientation is planning on implementing this year.

A second project, called Pre-Campus Orientation, should be available this summer for new students admitted to Ricks for fall. This program will be offered online over the Internet and will allow students the chance to become familiar with campus and the many services available to them. It will also provide students with helpful information on choosing a major, registering for classes and where to go to receive answers to their questions.

Ricks administrators say the new program will greatly benefit incoming students and believe that the positive outcomes from this program will extend to other areas as well.

"Pre-campus orientation will not only benefit incoming students, but will be of great benefit to other departments and to the institution as a whole," says Bob Wilkes, vice president of Student Life. "We are taking advantage of modern technology to better prepare students for Ricks before they ever arrive on campus."

Administrators say that Pre-Campus Orientation will serve as the foundation for incoming students, while campus orientation will build upon that foundation.

"When the new students arrive on campus, they will find an extensive array of orientation events and activities designed to welcome them, integrate them into campus life and help them feel of the ‘spirit of Ricks,’ " says Ric Page, assistant vice president of Student Life.

One of the goals associated with campus orientation is to provide a more personable experience to incoming students. With more than 4,500 new students admitted into Ricks each fall, this will be a large task.

For this reason, the NSO office has expanded the R-Team program and is seeking 300 or more R-Team leaders to serve as peer mentors to incoming students. R-Team leaders will serve in pairs and oversee a group of about 30 freshmen who constitute an R-Team. New students will participate in their group and attend each orientation activity as an R-Team.

Students who will be sophomores for the 2000-01 school year are invited to apply for an R-Team leader position. "They are the key element in reaching out to new students and making them feel welcome and a part of Ricks College," says Allen Jones, coordinator of New Student Orientation.

Several R-Team leader open houses will take place during March to secure the 300-plus R-Team leaders. The first open house is scheduled for Wednesday, March 15, in Manwaring Center Room 272 from 3-6 p.m. Students may come by anytime during that time to find out more about R-Team and NSO. Applications are also available at the information desk in the Manwaring Center.

In addition to R-Team leaders, the NSO office is also looking for returning students with exceptional organizational and leadership skills to serve on the NSO Team. The NSO Team will oversee the training of R-Team leaders, as well as plan and implement the various orientation activities that will take place in the fall and throughout the year. Students interested in participating on the NSO Team should contact Allen Jones at 356-2393 before March 1.

"Any student who is outgoing, is a people-person, likes to be involved and is willing to serve new students should get involved in this program and help build a new tradition at Ricks," Jones says.

Jones was hired in October as the first coordinator of new student orientation at Ricks. He also works half time as the assistant campus safety officer. Jones, who has a master’s degree from Seattle University in student development administration, worked for three summers on new student orientation at Brigham Young University.
