In 1966, I came to Ricks with a BA in Spanish from Utah State University and an MA in Spanish from The University of Missouri at Columbia. Teaching and traveling have stimulated interests in history, anthropology, mythology, political science, geography, sociology, art, music, literature, theater and other related and unrelated fields. My travels have been to rain forests, concrete jungles, museums, historical sites, ruins and hamlets of Europe, Mexico, Central and South America and the USA.

Although I am committed to my discipline, I believe there are more important things to learn in a Spanish classroom than Spanish.
