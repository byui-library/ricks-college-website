Extravadance

About Extravadance:

They consist of 24-36 dancers from two different dance companies-- the Ballroom Dance Company and the Contemporary Dance Theatre.

With the artistic director and spouse, tour manager and spouse, and possibly additional college administrators, there is generally a total of 28-42 people traveling. 

Members are selected by audition and represent some of the most outstanding dancers on campus. Each dancer brings many years of previous experience, and adheres to an ambitious weekly rehearsal and performance schedule.
