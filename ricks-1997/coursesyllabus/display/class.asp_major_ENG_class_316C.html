CLASS CODE: ENG 316C CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - English requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Equivalent to English 316 but taught in a computer classroom. Keyboarding skill is required. (Required for Engineering, Computer Science and Biology majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Equivalent to English 316 but taught in a computer classroom. Keyboarding skill is required. (Required for Engineering, Computer Science and Biology majors.)
TOPICS: All aspects of the writing process: prewriting, drafting, responding, revising, editing, postwriting, audience awareness, collaborative writing, research, and documentation.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Organizing ideas logically and presenting them objectively. (2) Writing clearly, concisely, and precisely. (3) Using writing strategies, such as researching, summarizing, defining, describing, analyzing, classifying, proposing, revising, and editing. (4) Employing appropriate formats and conventions of various technical and professional documentation.
REQUIREMENTS: Requires final drafts totaling 8,000-10,000 words of edited and documented prose.
PREREQUISITES: Completion of English 111 or 111C and 22 credits or sophomore standing.
