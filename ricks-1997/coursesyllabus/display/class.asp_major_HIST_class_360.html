Idaho & The Northwest
IDAHO & THE NW
CLASS CODE: HIST 360 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: RELIGION AND SOCIAL SCIENCE
DEPARTMENT: HISTORY, GEOGRAPHY, POLITICAL SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Studies the exploration, settlement, and development of Idaho within the Pacific Northwest as well as the nation.
DESCRIPTION: Studies the exploration, settlement, and development of Idaho within the Pacific Northwest as well as the nation.
TOPICS: Exploration, fur trapping, mining, colonization, contention with native americans, territorial era and statehood.
OBJECTIVES: Understand the development of the Pacific Northwest within the context of the larger story of national and international events.
REQUIREMENTS: Oral presentation, ten page paper, reading log, answering questions on the readings, mid term and final examinations.
