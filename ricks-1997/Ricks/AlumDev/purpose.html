Helping to Change and Save Lives

The purpose of the Ricks College Development Office is to advance the unique mission of Ricks college by encouraging and facilitating gifts that will assure the college's success.

The mission of Ricks College is to provide students with a stellar educational experience:  academically, culturally, socially, and spiritually.

This exhaustive endeavor cannot simply be taken for granted. Your interest and financial support will change and save lives.

In preparation for the next century and its unprecedented educational challenges and opportunities, we continue to seek funds to help successfully cope with those future challenges and unforeseen opportunities.

Now is the time to renew our dedication in support of this remarkable institution, it's students, and the eternal principles associated with the pursuit of truth, light, and knowledge.

Now is the time to meet the future straight on; time to make an explicit difference in helping to build Heavenly Father's kingdom on earth, of which Ricks College is significant.
