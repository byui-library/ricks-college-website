As each sport concludes competition, former athletes and coaches will be invited to return to campus and will be recognized at the last home game or meet. The final women’s volleyball game on October 20, 2001, against Snow College will kick off the celebrations. The final home football game will be a week later, on October 27 against Arizona Western College. That same day the regional finals in cross country will also be held in Rexburg and former cross country runners will be invited to return to campus. Additionally, track and field athletes will be invited to participate. The regional wrestling tourney on February 1, 2002, will bring former wrestlers to campus, and both men’s and women’s basketball will be celebrated with the final home games on March 2, 2002. The final women’s fast-pitch softball game will be held April 20, 2002, and the final baseball game will take place May 4, 2002.
ATTENTION FORMER ATHLETES:  
To ensure you receive a personal invite to your reunion, please contact us by e-mail reminding us of your current mailing address and the sport you participated in.
