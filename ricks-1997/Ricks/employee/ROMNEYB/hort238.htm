Course Description:
Theories, methods, and materials involved in wedding and special occasion arrangements in preparation for the Annual Spring Bridal Flower & Garden Show.
 The purpose of this course is to provide opportunities for the student to learn the basic technical skills involved with wedding, display, and other specialty arrangements.  There is also some emphasis placed on the relationship of the designer with the client as each individual works closely with the bride in coordinating the flowers that will be used to complement the bridal gown.  There will be periods of instruction provided, as well as time spent in individual practice.

Topics covered in the preparations for the Flower & Bridal Open House include:

 Bridal designs--where do ideas come from?
 Terminology of bridal flowers.
 Setting up the wedding and reception lines.
 Consultations--what are the right questions to ask?
 Body flowers
 Developing creative designs--from thought to paper to reality
 Bouquets--style, construction technique
 Display arrangement--what are the limits?
 Consultation notebook--creating your own selection guide
 Show preparations--pulling it all together.
 The Show Must Go On! (keeping a level head when things go wrong)

  Please note:  Grades will be based primarily on participation, attendance, and your actual design work for the Flower and Bridal Show.  Your timely completion of assignments, and your level of committment will determine the letter grade that    you receive.
 

The Main Event
  During Women’s Week, our Department plays host to a huge open house and reception where those attending will be able to browse through several  demonstrations and displays.  We will have the opportunity to mingle and interact with interested people, explaining how we have used the materials in the designs, what flowers were selected, and how they could communicate with a floral designer for their own wedding flowers.  During the open house, all students will be dressed in “Sunday best” attire, along with their choice of floral adornment.

 The core event for the floral designers will be the bridal flower workshop that will be held prior to the open house.  Each student receives personal instruction in constructing a fresh flower bridal bouquet during the workshop.  Each student in the class must also be enrolled in Hort 95 (Lab fee)

There will be some out of class time involved in inventory, ordering, flower preparation, and other organizational committees involved in the open house.  The open house will be Friday, March 21st.   It is the responsibility of each student to participate in the decoration, setting up, and cleaning up for the open house.

Students wishing to take this course for only one credit must receive approval from the instructor.
