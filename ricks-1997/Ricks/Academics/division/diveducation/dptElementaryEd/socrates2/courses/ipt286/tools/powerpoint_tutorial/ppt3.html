3. Customizing Your PowerPoint Environment

[ Contents ] [ Previous ] [ Next ]

The PowerPoint interface can be customized to a large extent through the choice of toolbars. Toolbars are collections of useful features that are accessed by clicking on icons. When PowerPoint is first started up, a set of toolbars is placed onto the interface automatically. Choosing the type and location of toolbars in PowerPoint will help to customize your environment to your needs.

To choose the toolbars that you will have open, select the "Toolbars" option from the "View" menu. This will produce the box shown below:


Our initial screen displayed only the Standard and Formatting toolbar. Another very useful toolbar is the "Drawing" toolbar. To select the drawing toolbar, place an checkmark in the box next to Drawing by clicking on it with a mouse. After hitting the OK button the following set of icons (the Drawing toolbar) will appear on your screen. The features of this toolbar are shown below::

The position of the toolbars can also be changed. To do this, simply "click and hold" on any part of the toolbar that does not have an icon. Then, while holding the mouse button down, drag the toolbar around the screen and watch the shape change. To drop the toolbar, simply let go of the button.
