Basic Principles of Video Technology

•Signal and Noise:
–Signal is the message.
–Noise is unwanted interference.
–The higher the signal to noise ratio, the better the quality of the message.

•Analog:
– A transduction from physical to electrical impulses or signals.

•Digital:
–A transduction from physical to digital impulses or signals. Binary code is "on-off", "yes-no", "open-shut", or "0-1".

Video Signal Generation
•Scanning:
–The process of tracing (scanning) the video signal onto a television screen.

•Standardization:
–Different countries use different standardizations

•NTSC:
–National Television Standards Committee
–525 scan line system used in North America and Japan

•PAL:
–625 scan line system used in Europe

•HDTV:
–High Definition Television
– Uses up to 1080 scan lines for higher resolution

•Field:
–The odd or even scan lines. Each field is 1/60th of a second.

•Frame:
–One odd and one even field combine into one frame. There are 30 frames of video in one second.

•Inside the Video Camera:
–Pickup Tubes
–Charge-coupled Device (CCD)
