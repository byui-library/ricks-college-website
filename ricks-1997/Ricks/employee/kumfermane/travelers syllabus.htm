Course Description

This course focuses on the rich history, culture (art, literature, music) and people of Russia/the former Soviet Union during the millennium of its existence from 988 A.D. up until the present day.

Course Procedures

Students are expected to l) complete reading assignments before class sessions; 2) come to class spiritually and mentally prepared to ask questions and participate in class discussions; and 3) keep an open mind about this different culture and people. Remember: Their culture is not better or worse than ours--just different!

Class attendance and your participation in and contributions to class discussions are expected. Note that the quality of your contribution is important, not the amount of "airtime."
