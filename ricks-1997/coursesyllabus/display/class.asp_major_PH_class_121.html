MECHANICS - PRINCIPLES OF PHYSICS
PRIN PHYSICS
CLASS CODE: PH 121 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Recommended for students majoring in physics, engineering, chemistry,math or geology.
DESCRIPTION: Training your mind to combine intuition and past experience with mathematics to understand the fundamental laws of nature. The goal is to combine what the body understand aboaut force, acceleration with higher levels of thinking involving equations based on the laws of physics.
TOPICS: Beginning topics of mechanics and wave motion.
Graphs, equations, Newtons's Laws of Motion, conservation of mechanical energy, conservation of momentum, conservation of angular mementum.
OBJECTIVES: Learn to work together, working in small groups, discussing concepts and working problems. Work hard, work smart.
REQUIREMENTS: Four "hour" exams, lowest "hour" will be dropped. "Hour" exams take 1-3 hours.
Required to turn in an assignment or take quiz each class period.
PREREQUISITES: Math 110 and Math 111 or high school equivalent.
Concurrent Registration: (or completion of)Math 112
Math Level: Extensive use of college Algebra (Math 110 equivalent), Trigonometry and Elementary Calculus.
