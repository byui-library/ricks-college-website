Oct 19 A question was brought to my attention on how to plot a solid with hidden lines remove.  With the isometric viewport active, go into the plot command and check the box that says Hide-Lines.  You do NOT have to do the HIDE command first. If you do the hide command and DON'T check the Hide-Lines box your hidden lines will NOT be removed on the plot.  When hidden lines are removed using this technique, you will see at lot of zig-zag (triangular) pieces.  That is correct.   Later we will learn how to get better looking hidden line plots.
