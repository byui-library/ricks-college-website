The Transition: Time line
Baccalaureate degrees will be phased in over the next few years. Space limitations will require some majors to be delayed until additional classrooms and faculty office space become available. Officials are working on plans for a new ecclesiastical building that will be used for both religious and academic activities. 

BYU-Idaho may see "a modest increase" in the student body size during the next few years. To accommodate the most students, the university will utilize its facilities year round offering a complete array of courses during the summer. Students will be admitted to one of four enrollment tracks: fall-winter, winter-summer, summer-fall, or the year round Fast Track program.
