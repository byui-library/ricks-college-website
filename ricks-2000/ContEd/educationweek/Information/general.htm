HOUSING

CAMPUS HOUSING
All housing registrations must be mailed or faxed to us.  No internet or phone registrations are available.  To be housed with a specific roommate or as a group, family, or couple submit all housing registrations in the same envelope.  

 

click here

Housing Registration Form (print and mail copy)

 

Housing Assignments:

Education Week guests will be assigned to one of the following:

  • Three-bedroom apartments (six twin beds) in off-campus housing complexes, with bathroom and living  area.

  • Two or three bedroom apartments (four or six twin beds) in on-campus housing with bathroom and living area.

  • Dormitory-style rooms (two twin beds) with shared bathroom facilities on each floor and shared lounge.

Items Provided:

The following items are provided (per person):  soap, washcloth, 1 towel, a flat sheet and a fitted sheet, 1 blanket, pillow, pillowcase, and paper towels. Microwaves and micro refrigerators are available in most rooms.

 

Assignment of Roommates:

Campus housing is available on a first-come, first-served basis.  Participants who come alone will be assigned a roommate of their own sex.  Couples and families will be housed in their own unit. Groups of more than six will be assigned in adjoining apartments.  All registrations must be sent in the same envelope to be housed together.  Please request which type of housing you will need on the registration application. 

 

Children and Babies:

Students ages 14-18 must be accompanied by an adult.  No babies in arms or anyone else under 14 will be allowed in campus housing.  

 

Deadline and Cost:  We must receive your registration by Tuesday, May 29.  

Cost is $39 (Wed, Thurs, and Fri nights).

Housing Check-in:         4:30-10 p.m., Wednesday, June 6, at the lounge area of the complex you are assigned to.
Housing Check-out:       By 9:30 a.m., Saturday, June 9

NOTE: We cannot accommodate early check-ins or late check-outs.

Campus housing is available but may not be able to accommodate all those who apply.  You will be notified of your housing status within a week of your registration by a confirmation letter and a receipt.  If housing is unavailable, you will be notified and advised to seek other accommodations.

 MEALS

MEAL TICKETS
Meals are served at the Manwaring Center Cafeteria (The Galley).  Meal tickets may be purchased when you register.  All meal ticket registrations must be in our office by May 29.  There are two options for meal tickets this year.  Option 1 provides you with any six meals (Wednesday Dinner - Saturday Dinner) and costs $30.  Option 2 provides you with any nine meals (Wednesday Dinner - Saturday Dinner) and costs $42.  These meal passes may be shared with participants if desired.  Tentative meal times are shown below:

Times
  Breakfast:   7 - 9:15 a.m.
  Lunch:       11:00 a.m.- 1:45 p.m.
  Dinner:      4:30 - 6:30 p.m.

NOTE: Serving lines will close at these times, but you can stay as long as needed to finish eating.

 

OTHER MEAL OPTIONS
A variety of food items are also available at the Viking, JoLynn's Bakery,  and from vending machines and snack bars.  There are also plenty of restaurants within the Rexburg area.  To view a list a restaurants in the Rexburg area see www.rexcc.com/rest.htm.
