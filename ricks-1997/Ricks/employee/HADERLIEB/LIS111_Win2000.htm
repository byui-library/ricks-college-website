Text: Using the David O. McKay Library (1994) by Marcia Alldredge (updated version also available online).

Objective: The students will become familiar with information resources in print, electronic and other formats, and will be able to apply this information by producing an annotated bibliography on a subject of their choice.

Grading: Based on assignments (40%), an exam (20%), an annotated bibliography (20%), and class attendance and participation (20%).

Policies: The instructor will determine whether or not an absence is excused. Assignments are due 1 week after they are given, unless other arrangements are made. Late assignments will have 10% deducted each additional week they are late. Standards specified in the current R-Book (including dress and grooming, code of honor, etc.) apply.
