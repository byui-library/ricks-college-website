Bus & Comm College Page

 

  

    The Information Systems major prepares individuals for positions in Information Technology such as applications programmer, network manager, and Internet developer. Technologies covered within our department include programming, networking, analysis and design, Internet-based development and database design and management.  This site is designed to provide you with contact and program information that will help you to be better informed and complete a program of study more smoothly. Use the navigation menus to the left or below to locate information that you need. If you cannot find what you are looking for please do not hesitate to contact a member of the department using information found on the Contacts page.
