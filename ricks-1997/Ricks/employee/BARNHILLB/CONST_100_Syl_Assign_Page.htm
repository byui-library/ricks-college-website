I. CATALOG DESCRIPTION

A survey course designed to acquaint students with the professional aspects of architecture and the construction industry. A special focus on employability and personal skills needed for the profession. For majors and non-majors.

II. COURSE OBJECTIVE

To introduce the architecture and construction students to the functions of the construction industry, its characteristics and terminology, its history, the people, materials, methods, and the management necessary to construct a structure.

Outline potential employment prospects, job descriptions, salaries, career tracks, and educational requirements.

Each student will have a better understanding of what learning and working in the profession will be like to assist them in planning for future careers.

Seek to understand the future of architecture and construction in a world economy and the growing number of opportunities available.

Seek to understand new technologies and environmental issues and how they will effect the architecture and construction industry in the future.

Stimulate an attitude toward lifetime learning, continued education, and participation in professional organizations.

III. COURSE POLICIES

This course is designed to create, simulate, and/or paint the picture of the real working environment for architecture & construction. On the job, employees are expected to be to work each day, on time. In the construction industry, bids are not accepted even one minute late. Fifteen percent of your grade will be on promptness and attendance. We have guest speakers from the industry and students are not to come in late.

All assignments are due on the assigned date, at the beginning of class. No late assignments will be accepted. All assignments are to be professionally typed and presented. No handwritten assignments will be accepted and if turned in, a score of "0" will be given.

It is expected that each student will abide by the Ricks College dress, grooming, and honor standards. Feel free to consult with the instructor at anytime you may have difficulty or have questions about these standards. Office hours are posted outside office 134.
