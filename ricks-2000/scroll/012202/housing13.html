Students learn of leases

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

When renting or leasing an apartment or home, there are a few things that need to be considered for a safe agreement.

Renters generally either have rent that is paid from month to month or a lease that is a contract for a specific period of time.

The lease is either oral or written. Each has its own benefits that should be considered.

An oral agreement can be beneficial because there is the option of negotiation and more understanding. There is also the chance that each side memory may result in disagreements and misunderstandings between owners and tenants, according to www.lawguru.com.

“... Neither party should rely on oral statements that are contrary to the terms of the written rental agreement,” according to the BYU-Idaho off-campus Housing Guidebook for 2001.

A written agreement can be more useful for setting a firm contract on issues regarding the lease. Although everything is outlined in writing, many do not read between the lines. Sometimes a lease agreement will mention another paper with house rules. The agreement should not be signed until all documents regarding it have been read.

Make sure that if there are any changes needed on a written agreement, they are altered before the signing. Changes are allowed prior to signing, but when a signature has been given, the contract is set in stone. The written document is a legal agreement that is binding.

Avoid misunderstandings between tenant and owner about deposits. Many students do not realize where their deposits go, according to the BYU-I Housing Office.

Many owners keep deposits while others give the deposits back after tenants move out.

Benefits of renting or leasing may include the possibility that providing furniture or appliances is not necessary in some apartments or homes. Lease contracts for students generally provide such accommodations.

As well as being exempt from the appliance or furniture hassle, renting or leasing also leaves the responsibility of repairs for the landowner or manager. This allows the tenant to have time for other worries in life as well as the expenses that may be associated with those worries.

When renting an apartment or house there is the chance of rent increasing or decreasing, whereas in a lease the rent remains the same for the time period of the lease.

If leasing, there are precautions that can be taken to make the process easier. Be sure to be educated about housing and the contract before the decision is made to sign a lease.
