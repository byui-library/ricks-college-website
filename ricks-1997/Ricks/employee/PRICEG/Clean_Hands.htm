Helping Hands - (Social)

 

"...and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger" (Job 17:9).

     As you seek to live the standards of the Church, you will be able to reach out and lift and build your brothers and sisters.

     Everyone needs good friends... Treat everyone with kindness and dignity... Help them feel welcome and wanted... Set a positive example for your friends and others by using clean language.

     Joy and happiness come from living the way the Lord wants you to live and from service to God and others.... You can render service more effectively if you maintain high standards.  Always take a stand for the Lord's way.
