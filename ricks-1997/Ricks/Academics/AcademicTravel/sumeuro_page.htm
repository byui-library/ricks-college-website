Summer in Europe Humanities Tour


$3300 (plus tuition)
Third summer term 

The European Summer Tour begins in July, 2000 and explores the wonders of great European sites.  Each summer we visit 10-13 famous cities such as Rome, Florence, Venice, Munich, Paris, Brussels, and London.  We explore the religious symbolism of cathedrals, including St. Paul's  and Westminster Abbey in London, San Croce in Florence, and St. Mark's in Venice.  Highlights include the following:

  • Stroll down a Paris boulevard
  • Take a boat ride on the Seine 
  • Wander through Roman ruins
  • Visit The Vatican City
  • Sistine Chapel
  • Eiffel Tower
  • Trevi Fountain
  • Trafalgar Square
  • Luxembourg Gardens
  • The Louvre and d'Orsay Museums
  • Listen to a concert of Mozart's music
  • Les Miserables in London.
