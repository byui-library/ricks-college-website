Environmental Health
ENVIRON HEALTH
CLASS CODE: HS 300 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HEALTH SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Introduction to the field of environmental health and the regulatory aspects of local, state and federal laws. This course investigates the impact on our environment of water pollution, air pollution, solid and hazardous waste, resource recovery and reuse, toxic substances, pesticides, herbicides, occupational safety and health and radiation.

OBJECTIVES: The student will be able to understand:
1. The principles of monitoring air, water, radiation, and noise pollution.
2. The principles and practices of preventing and reducing air, water, radiation, and noise pollution.
3. The principle of occupation exposure to environmental chemicals and physical factors.
REQUIREMENTS: Four exams about every 4 weeks, textbook assignments, research papers
