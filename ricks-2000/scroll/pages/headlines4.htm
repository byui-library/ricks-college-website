OPEN HOUSE TO BE HELD FOR BRENT KINGHORN

KINGHORN.


Employees, students and community friends will have a chance to say goodbye to Community Services Vice President Brent Kinghorn and his wife, Donna Jean, on his last day of work, May 31, from 2-4 p.m., in the executive offices reception area located on the second floor of the Kimball Building.

The Kinghorns will soon be leaving for Albania, where he has been called to serve as president of the Albania Tirana Mission for the next three years. He will take a three-year leave of absence from the college.

Kinghorn is in his 34th year of working at Ricks. For the first 11 years he worked for the BYU-Ricks Center for Continuing Education and eventually became its director.

In 1978 he was appointed to serve as vice president of the newly created Community Services area by Ricks President Bruce Hafen. He will have been the only person to ever serve in this capacity, because the Community Services area is being reorganized and transitioned into a new area called Advancement.
