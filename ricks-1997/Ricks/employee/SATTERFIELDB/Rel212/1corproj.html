1 Corinthian Project

 


Project Goal

In 1 Corinthians, Paul responds to a variety of problems in the Corinthian church. Your goal in this project is to identify each of the problems and Paul's various responses to each.
 

Evidence of completion

You will hand in a typewritten report listing each problem and Paul's responses to each problem. You will include scripture references from 1 Corinthians.  Format is up to the student.  A good way to format this is in column form.
 

Criteria of Judgment

This project will be judged by how well it would help someone who has not read 1 Corinthians understand why and what Paul is saying to the people of Corinth.
