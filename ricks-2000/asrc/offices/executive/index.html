Executive Vice President's Mission

Hello, hello, and welcome to the Executive Vice President's page. My name is Charley Jenkins and I am serving in the current EVP position. I grew up on a farm in Roosevelt, Utah. In high school I loved being active. I was involved in football, wrestling, team-roping, calf-roping, and performing. Becoming involved in high school was a little easier for me than at the college level. But with a few jogs out of my comfort zone, I now love serving Ricks College. The office of the Executive Vice President supervises over 100 clubs and organizations at Ricks. This is done through the Executive Board, Inter-Organizational Council, and the Organizational Finance Ward. Throughout these boards, and with the help of the clubs and organizations, ASRC puts on major campus events. The biggest of these events is the Halloween carnival and the Multicultural Festival. The clubs and organizations are one of the main reasons Ricks College is so awesome, and will play an even larger role in the future.
