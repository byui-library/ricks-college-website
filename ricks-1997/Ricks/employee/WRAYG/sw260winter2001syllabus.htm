Course objective:

Throughout the semester you will become familiar with the profession of social work while understanding various dynamics that created the need for social welfare. We will discuss the history of social welfare and some of the problems that relate to how services are delivered. You will become familiar with some basic philosophies, values, and ethics in social work, along with problems and services available to deal with them. Finally, you will be introduced to some of the skills and areas of practice important to the social work profession.

Self-evaluation paper:

A self-evaluation paper will be turned in on April 7 and should address the following:

  1. An area of social work that you would like to pursue, (ie. Child protection, adult chronically mentally ill, juvenile delinquency, etc.)
  2. Reasons for choosing the above
  3. Personality factors you are aware of about yourself that would help in your field of choice and those that would hinder you in your choice.
  4. Five pages
  5. List references, authors, etc.
  6. Cover page
  7. This paper is worth 75 points

Exams:

You will be given a total of four exams during the semester, including the final. All tests will be given in class on the assigned dates. The exams will cover lecture material and material from the assigned chapters. Each test will be worth 100 points.

 

Attendance:

You will be allowed three absences. For each absence thereafter, five points will be subtracted from the possible 100 points. Attendance is important.

 

Field Trips:

I will make arrangements for the class to travel to four different agencies in the surrounding communities that work with various populations in the field of social work. At the conclusion of our final field trip each student will write a two page paper discussing your thoughts and feelings about the agencies we visited. This paper will be worth a maximum of 25 points and will be due on April 17.
