FRESHMAN AWARD OF EXCELLENCE

The Award of Excellence Scholarships are for new freshmen and are based on a combination of academics, leadership, service, church activity, seminary activity, extra-curricular activities, and essays.

The awards range from one year quarter tuition to one year full tuition plus $500 for books and fees. All applicants who meet the BYU-Idaho admission deadline will automatically be considered. No separate application is required for this scholarship.

Those who receive an Award of Excellence Scholarship for fall or winter semesters are eligible for same tuition award (i.e., full, half or quarter tuition) for the preceding summer terms. Recipients should submit a summer class schedule to the Scholarship Office to receive this award.


Thomas E. Ricks Award

The Thomas E. Ricks Award is the most prestigious award offered by BYU-Idaho. The top 200 students will receive a one-year, full-tuition scholarship, plus $500 for books.


Award of Excellence -- Full Tuition

Recipients of this award will receive one year full tuition. This full-tuition scholarship will be awarded to 200 students.


Award of Excellence -- Half Tuition

Recipients of this award will receive one year half tuition. This half-tuition scholarship will be awarded to 50 students.


Award of Excellence -- Quarter Tuition

Recipients of this award will receive one year quarter tuition. This quarter-tuition scholarship will be awarded to 800 students.
