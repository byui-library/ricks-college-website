CRLA Certification Process

 

The BYU-Idaho tutor-training program is accredited through the College Reading and Learning Association.  Tutors receive nationally recognized levels of certification.

 

The purpose of establishing a series of tutoring certificates is twofold.  First, it allows tutors to receive recognition and positive reinforcement for their successful work from an international organization, CRLA.  Secondly, the certificates help set up a standard for the minimum skills and training a tutor needs to be successful.

 

Certification is offered at three levels-Regular, Advanced, and Master.  To obtain all three levels requires a minimum of thirty hours of training and seventy-five cumulative hours of actual tutoring.

 

Certification at all three levels can be achieved over two semesters by completing GS108A and GS109A, or Education 208 and 209 for students going in to an education field.  Tutors will be heavily recruited from the education programs.
