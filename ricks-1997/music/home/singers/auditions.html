Auditions

Each fall auditions are held for all the choirs at BYU-Idaho.  Being selected to participate in Collegiate Singers involves a serious commitment to practice and performance.  Those selected to sing in Collegiate Singers are among some of the finest singers on campus.  While some exceptions are allowed, members of Collegiate Singers are  expected to participate during both fall and winter semesters.

Auditions will be held Aug 31, Sep 2 & 3, 2002.  A sign-up table is setup in the main lobby of the Snow building while auditions are being held.

What to Prepare:

    One memorized song showing your best voice in a classical or artistic style.  We are specifically looking for qualified singers who demonstrate a positive attitude of teamwork and desire to learn.

Those who audition may be required to sing before a panel of BYU-Idaho choral directors.  Other items such as: sight-singing; melodic and rhythmic memory; and scales may be checked.  Auditions are recorded to aid the directors in selecting members for their choirs. Those auditioning may declare which choir they wish to sing in, but everyone auditions for all the choirs. This means that if a student is not selected to sing in Collegiate Singers, he or she may be invited to sing in one of the other choirs.
