A five-day spiritual program of adventure, campfires, special speakers, initiative training, horseback riding, canoeing, handcart trek, and many other features designed JUST FOR YOUTH. The program will be held at the Ricks College Outdoor Learning Center, located near the Teton Mountain Range. Outdoor EFY will provide a unique opportunity for youth to develop lasting friendships, grow spiritually, and gain self-confidence in a caring and trusting setting. Outstanding speakers and staff will be on hand to make this a special experience.
