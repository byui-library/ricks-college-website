 Course Objectives:
  • Seek learning in the Doctrine and Covenants by study and by faith
  • Build faith through the personal application of principles and doctrines learned through personal and class scripture study

Requirements:

1. Attendance-Important, enjoyable, mandatory. Edifying discussions is essential to the religious experience. 20 points will be deducted for every absence over 2. Only illness and school excused activities will not be penalized. More than 9 absences will result in a loss of credit and failing the class.

2. Scripture Study-Read the assignment before class. You will report on this each class period. 10 points will be deducted for each reading assignment not completed before class.

3. Journal / Sharing-Write a one page entry for each reading assignment. Write another one page entry for class notes and insights.

4. Papers-4 two-page, stapled, type-written, doctrinal-research papers are required on the due date.

5. Exams-Two exams, a mid term and a final, will be experienced.

Required Texts: Bible and Triple Combination
