General Eligibility

In general, the would-be financial aid recipient must meet the following eligibility standards.

1. Must have a high school diploma, G.E.D. certificate, or a certificate issued by the state.

2. Must be admitted and enrolled as a regular student in an eligible program.

3. Must be a U.S. citizen or an eligible non-citizen.

4. The student must be making satisfactory academic progress in his or her course of study as defined in the Policy of Satisfactory Academic Progress

5. Must not owe a repayment on any federal grant, and must not be in default on any federal loan for attendance at any school.

If required, a male student must be registered with Selective Service.

Deadlines

Priority Deadlines

In order to award students money and make disbursements on time, the Ricks College Financial Aid Office will need the Student Aid Report (SAR) data, any loan applications and/or all verification documents at least
2 to 3 months prior to the date the student will attend Ricks College. Students not meeting these deadlines will not be denied aid, however, funding may not be available until later in the school year.  The Financial Aid office will not be responsible for any late fees the student may receive.

Mandatory Deadlines

FAFSA  Whether the Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is done by mail or done electronically it must be received by the application processor by JUNE 30, 1999 for the 1998-99 School year.  When the FAFSA is received by the processor they will produce a SAR (Student Aid Report).

SAR Submission The correct results of the SAR MUST be received at Ricks College by your last day of enrollment during that school year  or August 31, 1999. whichever is earlier.

Verification The Verification process must be completed within 60 days after the last day of attendance or August 31st, whichever is earlier.  Contact the Financial aid office for more information on deadlines for Verification.
