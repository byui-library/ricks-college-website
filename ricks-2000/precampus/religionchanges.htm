Changes in Religion Requirements

 

The department of Religion and its faculty is looking forward to your arrival. Because of the transition to BYU-Idaho we need to make all students aware of some important changes in our policies. A complete statement of policy is found at the front of your class schedule and in your BYU-Idaho catalog. Please make note that all students are required to be in Religion class each semester until they have completed 14 credits.

CHANGES TO NOTE:

1. Modular classes versus regular classes. Modular classes run three days a week for eight weeks. Regular classes run two days a week for the entire semester. Modular classes require the same amount of work as a regular class but in a shorter period of time. They also require more out-of-class time. However, the shortened period allows you the flexibility to take another class on campus in the second eight week period or use the time to do internships, projects, student teaching, work, leave for a mission at block, etc.

When you register remember, three days a week (MWF or TTF) indicates modular classes. If you take one of these note the date the class begins and ends so that you know which module you are signed up for and can then be in the class at the appropriate time!

2. ATTENTION PROSPECTIVE MISSIONARIES! There are two options that will help you prepare for your missions. The first is Religion 130–Missionary Preparation. It is a single semester course and offers two credits. The other is Religion 121–BOOK OF MORMON FOR PROSPECTIVE MISSIONARIES. This is a three-day-a-week class that runs the entire semester, will include 122 (the second half of the Book of Mormon), will give you four credits and fulfill the Book of Mormon requirement to graduate from BYU-Idaho or any other Church School. The Book of Mormon will be taught with preparation for a mission in mind and will include many the important principles taught in Rel. 130. The intent is to help you prepare to teach the Book of Mormon as you enter the mission field while deepening your own understanding of this "keystone" scripture. Book of Mormon for Prospective Missionaries is found in the class schedule under Book of Mormon classes but designated for Prospective Missionaries only. There are only two sections taught by Brother Robert Marcum and Brother Greg Palmer and space is very limited.

RETURNED MISSIONARIES: Religion 130, AND 121-122 For Prospective Missionaries, are not for those who have returned from a mission and should you sign up for them, you will be required to make a change.

3. All Students! Note that some classes( both modular and regular) may be for majors only. Do not sign up for these classes if you are not majoring in that discipline. The only exception is if you have special permission from the teacher!

4. Because of the increasing number of students and space limitations we no longer allow students to take more than one religion class in a single semester. Exceptions will only include the following:

a. Students who have been attending Ricks College and, under the old policy, missed a semester and need to make it up in order to meet graduation requirements (this exception will be made only this coming year).

b. students in specialized programs that may require two religion classes in a single semester in order to meet graduation or program requirements.

These exceptions will be granted only after the first day of classes and only if seats are available. We would encourage new students to avoid any later complications by making sure they are in a religion class each term.
