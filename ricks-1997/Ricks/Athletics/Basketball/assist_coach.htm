Assistant Mens

Basketball Coach:

Ken Jackson

Ken has been employed at Ricks College since 1993, however he is in his rookie season as the Viking's assistant coach. Jackson was hired four years ago as a financial aid administrator after completing an eight-month administrative internship under Ricks College President Steve Bennion. Prior to his internship, he graduated from Southern Utah University where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration.

Jackson played for Ricks from 1988-1990 and went on to complete his eligibility at SUU.

He served an LDS mission in Mexico. Ken is married to the former Christy Stembridge of Rexburg, who was a Viking volleyball standout. The Jacksons have two children.
