IS 140 General Directions

By reading and following these directions you should find success in completing assignments for this course.

1. All assignments, when submitted, must follow a naming scheme: Lastf.section.assignmentname.extention

  • Last indicates your own last name.
  • F indicates your own first initial.
  • Section indicates the section in which you are enrolled. Check your schedule.
  • Assignmentname is a brief description of what is being submitted.
  • Extention indicates the proper file type of the document being submitted.

           For example: smithj.1.win2000.doc

2. All files, except for some Internet Assignments, are located in the J: drive, in the folder named CT140files. Usually the J: drive will be accessed through an icon on the desktop of computers in the Information Systems Department computer labs named IS on Jupiter. The folders containing these files should be copied to your individual workspace on a campus server. This server will be designated as H: Student. At no time will you create a data disk as the book explains. Instead you will always copy the appropriate module folder to your H: drive. This will become your data disk.

3. You should create a folder on your H: drive named IS140 Assignments. All work from the class should be saved to this folder and within subfolders that correspond to the class modules: Win2000, Web, Excel and Access. This will make it easy to find your work when submitting it or going back to check previous work.

4. The text will frequently give directions to print a file or document. DO NOT PRINT unless directed to do so on a directions sheet (like this one) or verbally in class by Br. Robertson. However, do follow directions to Print Preview. The ability to manipulate print instructions for files is valuable; when finished click Cancel instead of Print.

5. Assignments canl be submitted via email attachment. When submitting work via email it is vital that you always double check three things:

  • Send the work to robertsonb@byui.edu (No other address is correct).
  • The word “Assignment” must appear in the subject line. It should also be preceeded by a single word description of which assignment it is (e.g. win2000, excelcase4, etc…).
  • The file being attached is the correct one and has been named properly (see #1 above).
      Violation of any of these three rules will result in a penalty of up to 100% for the assignment.

6. Assignments may also be submitted via the 140 Dropbox. The 140 Dropbox is located inside the robertsonb folder on the IS on Jupiter (J:) drive. To submit work via the 140 Dropbox, simply drag the file being submitted from the source destination (usually your H: drive) to the 140 Dropbox folder. You will be able to open the dropbox folder and see that the file has been copied, but you will not be able to open it or remove it from the dropbox once it is submitted. Remember that if you submit assignments to the dropbox, you should send me an email indicating that the assignment was submitted in that manner.

7. The lab hours are posted outside of the respective rooms (Smith 440, 442, 453). Consult the posted operating hours cards outside the doors to these labs. During these hours there are lab assistants who are familiar with the programs and assignments and can assist you when you have questions. They are there to help, not to do the work for you. They also enforce the lab rules and BYU-Idaho standards. Please assist them by following both.

8. Working at home. The files needed for the class are available online as well as in the lab (see #2 above). You are welcome to work on the assignments at home provided you have Office 2000. However, if you work at home you must realize that you are on your own.
