1997 Ricks College
Cross Country Meet Previews
Southern Idaho Invitational

Friday, September 12, Twin Falls, ID. Run on the Twin Falls Municipal Golf Course. Ricks, Utah Valley, Southern Idaho and Brigham Young University (women "B" team only) will compete. The men's 5 mile race starts at 3:00 p.m.; women's 5k at 3:45 p.m. Course is mostly grass, mostly flat.
Clackamas Open
Saturday, September 27, Oregon City, OR. Run on Clackamas Community College campus; begins and finishes on the track. The women's 5k race starts at 11:00 a.m.; Men's 8k at 11:45 a.m. Course is grass, dirt, and barkdust with moderate hills. This is the same course that will be used at the North West Athletic Association of Community Colleges championship meet in November. Many of its member colleges will be participating in the race.
Ricks College Cross Country Classic
Saturday, October 11, Rexburg, ID. Run on the Municipal Golf Course. Ricks, Flathead Valley, Utah Valley, Montana State, Idaho State, Utah, Utah State, and Weber State to compete. Women to run 5k at 10:00 a.m.; Men 8k at 10:45 a.m. Course is a very flat combination of grass and dirt trails.
Brigham Young University/Reebok Invitational
Saturday, October 18, Provo, UT. Run on the East Bay Golf Course. Part of the USA/Reebok Cross Country Grand Prix Circuit. Eight to ten top universities, Ricks, Utah Valley, and 12-15 top U.S. class runners to compete. Women's 5k race to start at 10:00 a.m.; Men's 8k race to follow at 10:30 a.m. Course is all grass and flat.
Region 18 Championships
Saturday, November 1, Twin Falls, ID. Run on the Twin Falls Municipal Golf Course. Men to run 5 mile race at 11:00 a.m.; women's 5k at 11:45 a.m. Course is all grass and mostly flat. Region 18 schools Ricks, Flathead Valley, Southern Idaho, North Idaho, and Utah Valley to compete. Winning team and top 10 individuals automatically qualify for the NJCAA National Championship Meet.
NJCAA Championships
Saturday, November 15, Levelland, TX. South Plains Community College hosts the meet at City Park. Top NJCAA teams and individuals from across the nation to compete. Course is mostly grass with several pavement crossings. Men's 8k race to start at 9:30 a.m.; women's 5k race will follow at 11:00 a.m.
