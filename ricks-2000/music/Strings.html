Strings


In combination with an outstanding wind and brass program, string students at Brigham Young University-Idaho are provided with a rich variety of orchestra opportunities.  These include the Symphony Orchestra, Chamber Orchestra, and String Orchestra.   Private lessons are available for all students, regardless of their major or level of ability, who participate in one of the above ensembles.

ORCHESTRA


Symphony Orchestra    Under the direction of Edward (Ted) Ashton, the Symphony Orchestra annually presents four different concerts.  Programs feature major works from the 19th and 20th century standard repertoire as well as the biennial premiere of newly commissioned sacred LDS oratorios.  Six performances to standing-room-only audiences of the annual Halloween Concert begin each year's season.  In following concerts, featured soloists include our own students in an annual Concerto Concert as well as a tradition of guest international soloists such as Benny Kim, Dmitri Berlinsky, and Steven Honigberg.  "Popular" performing artists with Ricks orchestras have included Kurt Bestor, the Bar-J Wranglers, and even the likes of the outrageous PDQ Bach!  Following the premiere of his oratorio Visions of Eternity in 1993, composer Crawford Gates told members of the press, "It's absolutely stupendous to have a junior college that produces that kind of symphony orchestra . . . I don't know of a junior college anywhere that I've ever come in contact with that's even close to this, in terms of the quality and maturity of the performance of its students."

Chamber Orchestra    Twenty-five select string players, chosen from the members of the Symphony Orchestra, form the core of the Chamber Orchestra under the direction of  Robert Tueller,.  Designed to give advanced students a "near professional" experience, this ensemble combines with members of the select Chamber Winds and Chamber Brass in presenting at least four additional concerts each year.  Regular concerts feature orchestral literature of the 17th through early 19th centuries.  Additionally, these ensembles participate in operas, musicals, "live" stage shows, and chamber music performances.  A biennial concert tour takes place during the winter semester.

String Orchestra    Designed to provide a challenging ensemble experience for many string players, the String Orchestra class focuses on the improvement of orchestral playing skills needed for participation in auditioned ensembles such as the Symphony and Chamber Orchestra.    The String Orchestra focuses primarily on improving students' individual and ensemble skills rather than on preparing concert repertoire.  A final "demonstration performance" at the end of the semester provides an opportunity to apply, in a concert setting, that which has been learned.

Auditions    Auditions for the Symphony and Chamber Orchestras are held at the beginning of the Fall Semester.  No audition is required for participation in the String Orchestra.
