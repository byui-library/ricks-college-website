Culinary Arts


 The mission of the Culinary Arts program is to provide theoretical knowledge and hands-on
experience for students interested in working as culinarians.  Students will have the opportunity to
     •    learn and effectively practice skills in food preparation and service
     •    understand culinary nutrition, food safety and food identification
     •    gain an appreciation for foods of various cultures
     •    acquire supervisory skills and develop a personal sense of professionalism 
          necessary for working success in the food service industry
     •    gain experience in the proper use and maintenance of professional food service
          equipment
