Orientation Services

Future Campus Orientation Dates

New Student Guide
The New Student Guide is a tabloid publication produced by the Office of New Student Orientation.  More than 6,000 copies of the New Student Guide are printed annually.  The publication is distributed three times a year by mail along with University registration materials to newly admitted students and their parents.  Advertising opportunities are available for both on-campus and off-campus businesses and organizations.  Ad submission deadlines for the 2003 New Student Guide is January 10, 2003.  is a group of eight student

Info Fair
The Info Fair is held three times a year during Winter, 2nd Summer, and Fall Orientations.  The Info Fair allows campus departments and organizations to inform students of available services, opportunities, etc.  Departments and organizations interested in participating in the Info Fair must complete an Info Fair Application for each Info Fair.  Applications are now available online

Grad Fair
The Grad Fair is a new event which will be held twice a year:  January for Spring Graduation, and May for Summer Graduation.  Grad Fair services will include such things as graduation announcements, class rings, graduation photos, cap & gowns, graduation applications, alumni placement, etc.  Check back here for updates as they become available.
