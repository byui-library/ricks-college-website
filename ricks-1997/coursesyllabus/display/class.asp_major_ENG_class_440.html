Studies in Literary Theory
LIT THEORY
CLASS CODE: ENG 440 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Surveys predominant critical theories for literary study and interpretation, such as historical, psychoanalytical, archetypal, deconstructionist, reader-response, multicultural, Marxist, feminist, New Criticism, and others. (Required for English Literary Studies Emphasis.)
DESCRIPTION: Surveys predominant critical theories for literary study and interpretation, such as historical, psychoanalytical, archetypal, deconstructionist, reader-response, multicultural, Marxist, feminist, New Criticism, and others. (Required for English Literary Studies Emphasis.)
TOPICS: Critical theories, including historical, psychoanalytical, archetypal, deconstructionist, reader-response, multicultural, Marxist, feminist, New Criticism, and others; impact of theory on reading, interpretation, and canon formation; application of critical theory to literary texts.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Studying several critical theories to understand their tenets, applications, and influences. (2) Writing analytical papers demonstrating an understanding and application of critical theory. (3) Improving critical thinking and analytical skills. (4) Understanding the interdisciplinary nature of literary criticism.
REQUIREMENTS: Readings, oral presentations, and analytical papers to improve critical thinking and interpretive skills, and increase knowledge of interpretive and literary theories.
PREREQUISITES: Completion of English 251.
