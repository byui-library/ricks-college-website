ES 140 Environmental Science 4


Three class hours per week

Two lab hours per week

Travel Fee: $30

Prerequisite: Must be registered as a major in one of the Environmental Science programs.

Emphasis is on the relationship between human population demands and the environment. Students are provided a broad background of problems and issues associated with the management and conservation of ecosystems in preparation for a career in environmental science.

ES 240 Environmental Science Seminar 1-3

Hours arranged with instructor

The student in conjunction with a faculty supervisor, will select a problem or area of environmental concern. Arrangements will be made to investigate the concern, write up the findings and present the material to a panel of peers and faculty.

IS 293 International Studies Advising Seminar 1

One hour per week or equivalent activity.

This course is an introduction to International Studies. It will emphasize employment requirements and opportunities, academic requirements, travel opportunities and campus resources.
