April Spotlight -- Rebecca Rasmussen

Rebecca Waite Rasmussen' 74 is the founder and director of Mothers for the Children of Russia.  This group was started in 1999 after Rebecca heard stories from her uncle, a humanitarian missionary in Vladivostok, Russia, about the destitute state of Russian orphanages.  Rebecca went to work, Recruiting five of her friends, and Mothers for the Children of Russia was born.  Under her direction, many communities in Cache Valley, Utah, raised money to send to Russia.  By February of 2000 they had raised $50,000.  In March of that year the women went to Vladivostok in Eastern Russia with the money, toys, candy bars, and a rocking chair.  Once there, they purchased paint, formula, light fixtures, and baby food that they then contributed to orphans in Vladivostok.  Since their first trip, the organization has raised about $300,000 and returned 7 times to Russia to bless the lives of the children there.
     
Rebecca attended Ricks in 1974, which she remembers as a time of spiritual growth.  She thanks "wonderful professors that emulated Christ's light."  She further states, "The greatest asset of attending Ricks was that the doctrines of eternal salvation could be discussed and applied openly in a classroom environment creating a greater atmosphere of learning!"
     
Rebecca is the proud mother of six beautiful children and two grand children.  She currently serves as the Gospel Doctrine teacher in here Hyde Park, Utah, Ward.

Rebecca was recently honored by BYU-Idaho for her humanitarian service with the Distinguished Alumni Award for Humanitarian Service.
