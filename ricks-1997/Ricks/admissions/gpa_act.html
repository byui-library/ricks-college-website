Question: 

Are there GPA and ACT/SAT minimums? [click here]

 

Answer: 

Ricks does not have a GPA or ACT/SAT minimum.  However, because enrollment is limited, demand for admissions spaces may cause the required GPA/ACT to increase.   It also depends on the term applied for.  The fall semester is the most comptetive, while it is easier to be accepted for the summer or winter.  The GPA and ACT/SAT needed to be comptetive will vary by term.  The average for fall 1998 was a 3.5 GPA and a 24 ACT.  Ther average for winter 1999 was a 3.0 GPA and a 21 ACT.
