*Technology Reviews (3 papers total):

This assignment is designed to have you become aware of what is happening in the field of computers and technology. You are to read a print or Internet source that reviews current trends, break throughs, or gadgets that are changing the way technology works or may be used by humans. After reading the article or web reference you are to write a 1 page (maximum) paper about what you read. The paper should consist of 3 main parts: 1) The title and source of the information (see referencing your source, below), 2) A paragraph that reviews what the sources said (main points) and 3) how this technology will affect humans or why it is a change from how things are now or were in the past.

The paper is to be word processed, double-spaced, spell checked, grammar checked and proofread prior to submission as an email attachment.

Referencing your sources: Students should use MLA or APA referencing styles in documenting the source for each  paper.

Class Policies

Computer Ethics

The use of the Ricks College network, systems and data impose responsibilities and obligations upon the students to use these computer components in an ethical manner and in accordance with the guidelines found in the Ricks Honor Code. Students are to access only data and files that are owned by them, or the instructor for their use has given explicit authorization. Copyrighted material must be used in accordance with copyright law and applicable license agreements followed.

Attempting to circumvent or subvert system security measures or engaging in any activity that causes harm to systems or to any information stored thereon, such as creating or propagating viruses, disrupting services, or damaging files are considered inappropriate use. Violators are subject to disciplinary actions as prescribed by Ricks Policy and may also be prosecuted under applicable local, state, or federal civil or criminal law.

NOTE: A more detailed listing of Appropriate Use Guidelines will be posted in poster form in each of the computer labs (Smith 440, 442). You will want to review these posters.
