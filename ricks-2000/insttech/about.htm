Mission

The Department of Instructional Technology supports the Mission of Ricks College by:

  • Promoting and implementing the use of new educational technologies.
  • Providing training on educational technologies
  • Providing resources that help faculty enhance the educational experience
  • Ensuring the instructional quality of materials produced
Services

Online Courses
The Instructional Design staff facilitates the development of online classes.

Technology Training (TechSmart)
The Technology Training staff keep College faculty and staff up-to-date with educational technology skills.

Instructional Technology Center(ITC)
The ITC is a lab where faculty can use some of the newest software and hardware to create educational materials.

Photo Services
The photo lab is a convenient place to get quality wedding, graduation, or mission pictures taken.

Media Lab
A do-it-yourself instrucional materials lab where students may laminate, dry mount, do die-cutting, binding, etc.

Location

The Department of Instructional Technology is currently located on the 3rd floor of the David O. McKay library Room 355.
