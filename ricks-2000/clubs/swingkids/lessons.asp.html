Did you enjoy the "day of dance" instruction?  Great!  We promised the Advanced Lindy class a list of the moves that Eliza and David taught, so here you go.    The moves we taught, we put together in a short routine as follows:

Advanced Lindy Routine
Basic
Inside turn w/freespin
Double outside turn with quickstop
Switches
Push-catch-spin out of switchesM
Texas Tommy to side-by-side
Basic Charleston
Tuck out shoulder catch
Sugar Push
Cuddle with reverse turn
That was it! And that was probably enough for most of you. If you have any questions about any of the moves, talk to us at one of the dances, and we'll gladly help you out.

Caroline Spark is our V.P. of Instruction.  She's spent a lot of time taking lessons over the summer, and she's ready to share what she has learned with the rest of us!  First of all, lessons are for members only, and class sizes are limited.  You don't need a partner, but you should plan
There will be 4 class types: Beginner, Intermediate 1, Intermediate 2, and Advanced. In each case the previous class is the prerequisite. The advanced class requires instructor approval. The location will be announced soon. Sign up at the Swing Club on Wednesday!

The first session starts Saturday, September 15th.  The next session will begin October 27th.

Class Description
Beginner
9:00-10:00am		 Just getting started with the Lindy Hop?  Need a refresher?  Want to build on a solid foundation?  This class is for you.  Includes basic lead/follow, lindy/charleston basics, and more.
Intermediate 1
10:00-11:00am		 Ready to move on?  People in this class should have successfully completed the beginner class or have a firm grasp on the lindy/charleston basics.
Intermediate 2
11:00am-Noon		 This class is for those that have completed Intermediate 1.  We'll continue to add to your repertoire of fancy steps and introduce playing/improvisation.  Good times!
Advanced
8:00-9:00am		 If you're dumb enough to want to get up and dance at 8am on a Saturday then talk to Caroline about this one. Primarily team members, instructors and those who have been dancing the longest will benefit from this class.
