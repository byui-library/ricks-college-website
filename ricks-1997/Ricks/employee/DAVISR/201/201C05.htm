Chapter 5

Theater   was for (1) entertainment, and (2) education;  Started in Greece, as part of a worship service (Dionysus)

            Thespis changed church service to drama in 600's; Only instrument was the aulos, all music is lost

orchestra (to dance) was center, front, used by chorus for dancing and singing/had statue or alter in center stage was the skene/used for changing

            Theater at Epidarus seated 13,000/50 rows

                        actors wore masks/too far to see facial expressions;  also wore platform shoes/larger than life

                        one stage setting

                                    forced audience to use their minds. Today? is realism better? (horror movies)

            Plays were in a series of three, all with same theme, and ended with a satyr (bawdy) play

            Wealthy citizens would fund the play;  best method of putting across an idea to the public

AGAMEMNON  theme: the old way, Fate, tradition, revenge

            What is justice? is revenge justice?

            Is “blood will have blood” civilized?

                        At what point is it not?

            Why does Cassandra accept her fate?

            Discuss Agamemnon, the man.

                        How much do you give up for a cause? A daughter?

            Role of the chorus is to show middle ground/common man.

                        The “silent majority” of today

                        Slow to change our attitudes.

THE LIBATION BEARERS  theme: middle play to create conflict between old way (revenge) and new way (justice).

            story: Orestes and Electra his sister plot and kill their mother and her lover, but they have pangs of conscience about it.

            Orestes is now pursued by the Old Hags (conscience or guilt) and finally runs to Apollo, the god who commanded him to kill his mother, for help. (Man cannot solve all things, and must appeal to the gods).

EUMENIDES  theme: new form of justice

            retaliation is poor form of justice

            create a jury/Would a reasonable man do it?

                        What about times when a group of men is not reasonable? (It is normal for the majority to choose . . .)

            Create laws which seek the average. neither too strict nor too liberal

            Why do the Old Hags change? What about remorse? Guilt? Repentance? Do we have cartharsis?

EURIPIDES    “The Pacifist of Athens”

            even today he would be called a liberal

            Trojan Women shows his view of women; almost all his plays are anti-war; most showed the suffering of war

                        war is a no-win scenario

            his views finally get him run out of town

                        (plays were not government controlled—you could say what you wanted)

            he had more vision than most men/perceived more

                        Isaiah called it “the burden of the valley of vision”  sees “what might be” if only men would change; takes a moral stance: “If gods do evil, they are not gods.” it is wrong to kill (a very “modern” idea)

SOPHOCLES   took the stoic point of view

            face facts and accept them

            bear the burden of being a human being

            “Nothing is wrong which the gods command.”
