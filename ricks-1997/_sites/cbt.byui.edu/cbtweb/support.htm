Technical Support

If you are experiencing a technical problem, you may find your answer in this list of frequently asked questions.

SmartForce provides free telephone and e-mail support to all eligible customers on any of the products developed or distributed by SmartForce.

There are three Technical Support Centers—one at the Corporate Headquarters in Silicon Valley, one at the Development Centre in Dublin, Ireland, and one in Sydney, Australia.
