Ricks College News Release
May 26, 2000

Slovakian musician among graduates
at May 31 Ricks summer commencement

REXBURG, Idaho —  A Slovakian who first came to Rexburg as a member of a folk dance band will be one of 593 students graduating from Ricks College during summer commencement.

After spending two years at Ricks, 22-year-old physics major Frantisek Plasil of Trencin, Slovakia, will receive his associate degree at Wednesday's ceremony set for 10 a.m. in the Hart Auditorium.

Plasil was 16 when he came to the Idaho International Folk Dance Festival in the summer of 1994. The clarinet player is the son of Olga Plasilova, who returned again with the Radost folk dance group and established friendships with several Rexburg families.

Plasil says his mother coaxed him into coming to Ricks, even though he had already finished two years at a university in Slovakia. He agreed to come for one semester. "I didn't want to disappoint her," he says. "I thought as soon as it was over, I would run home."

But Plasil found life at Ricks very much to his liking. During the first year he lived at the home of Ed and Shauna Strobel and later in a college apartment complex. Besides the academics, he liked the social and religious aspects of college life, including playing his clarinet in the symphonic band and competing on intramural soccer teams.

He says, "I really liked the teachers. I had no idea it made such a difference when you put the gospel into teaching – even in physics."

He adds that many students at Ricks "have no idea what they have here – the labs, the library, the free computers, email and Internet access."

While friends back in Slovakia have encouraged him to continue his studies in the United States, he is drawn home. "I miss my family," he says. "There is a cost not to be with my family." Plasil will continue studying physics when he returns to Slovakia.

The traditional summer commencement follows the first five-week summer term that ends May 30 and includes students who will be completing graduation requirements during one of the three summer terms.

Ricks President David A. Bednar will conduct the commencement exercises. Dean Sorensen, a religion teacher and former academic vice president who is retiring from Ricks after 33 years, will be the featured graduation speaker.

A graduation breakfast for graduates and their parents and friends will be served at 7:30 a.m. in the Manwaring Center prior to graduation.
