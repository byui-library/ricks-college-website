Getting Onto Your Online Course

Area 1)
Information ABOUT your online course - - -

After clicking on the name of the course you want to access, navigate through the different buttons on the screen to find information about the course.

  - The button links to a page with course updates and other current information from your instructor. Check this section daily.
  - The button links to a page with a welcome message from the instructor, a list of assignments, grading information, and a syllabus. The information in this section is your guide and "contract" for completing the course.
  - The button will link you to information about your instructor, and others assisting with your online course.
  - The button will link you to the COURSE MATERIALS (where you will spend most of your studying time . . . ) - - Click "Next" to read more about Course Materials.
