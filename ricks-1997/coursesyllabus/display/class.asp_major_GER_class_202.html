Introduction to German Literature
INTRO GER LIT
CLASS CODE: GER 202 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An exploration of German literature covering various time periods.
DESCRIPTION: A study of German poems and excerpts from plays, autobiography and novels. Practice of principles leading to understanding and appreciation of literary genres.
TOPICS: A variety: idioms, grammar application, culture, etc.
OBJECTIVES: Improve ability in all language skills. Strive for 100% German usage in the class by instructor as well as the students. Help all students to internalize vocabulary and grammatical structure.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, participation in class and timely completion of assignments and exercises.
PREREQUISITES: German 201 with grade of C or higher or consent of instructor.
