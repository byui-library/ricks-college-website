Our History From the Heart

In August of 2000, Whitney Lent, a sophomore from Idaho Falls and former member of Showtime Company, spoke with Russell Bice (former director of Showtime), about her desire to be involved with a musical fireside group which would draw from the vocal talents of Ricks students. She had heard that none of the vocal groups operated by the Music Department would be performing LDS contemporary music in fireside settings. Because this was her primary interest and she knew of no other campus performing groups presenting this type of inspirational music, Russell Bice suggested exploring the formation of a Ricks College club - a club which would not require extensive rehearsal, but which could utilize many of the excellent voices on campus.

After carefully studying the "Club Handbook," Whitney drafted a final constitution to submit to the ASRC for approval. Before doing so, Whitney and Brother Bice agreed to add a provision to prepare small scale entertainment revues using solos, duets, etc. These revues could be scheduled through Entertainment Management in Public Relations in no academic performing group was available for, or interested in program requests from local wards and businesses. These small scale popular music programs would allow the college to respond to more entertainment requests and give more students the opportunity to share and develop their talents.

Because of the limitations, Russell Bice invited Elizabeth Bossard, an adjunct vocal instructor, to serve as a second advisor. Both advisors have many years of experience in preparing and presenting musical productions. This would be crucial in making sure the quality of all presentations are the kind both Ricks College and club members could be proud of.

The constitution was approved by the ASRC Executive Vice President just as school began in late August of 2000. Audition posters were prepared and ten days after school began, auditions were held in the Manwaring Center - third floor. Whitney was scared to death that no one would show up. To our surprise nearly 100 students applied - many with strong voices. The constitution called for fifteen members, so Whitney, Brother Bice and Sister Bossard met to ammend it and expand initial membership to 20. Call backs were scheduled which included short interviews, prayers were offered for guidance, and a final list was posted. Five or six members will leave in January so further auditions will be held again in December of 2000.

Along with the final list, the following notice was posted:

AN IMPORTANT NOTE TO ALL WHO AUDITIONED
from Officers and Advisors of "From the Heart"
Our sincere thanks to ALL of you for your patience and perserverance in waiting until we finished call backs last night. We were so impressed with not only your talent, but your depth of insight and dedication. As you know we are not set up to operate with a large membership - but we have determined to expand as much as possible to include what we hope will be a workable number.

To those not on the list, we hope you have made new friendships and will continue to take every opportunity to share your talents.
