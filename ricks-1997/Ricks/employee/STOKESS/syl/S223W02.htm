EXAMINATIONS: All exams carry the same weight in the final grade and includes only the material covered in reading assignments and in class since the last exam.  These must be taken at the scheduled time unless arranged for under emergence situations. 

BOOK REVIEW: A three to five page book review must be written on one of the four additional reading books.  Reviews must be typed/double spaced/stapled in the top left corner.  A panel made up of all the students that have read and reviewed the same book as you, will present a report and review the book and your arguments made in your individual reviews.  Reviews are due April 3 and panels will present the last weeks of the semester.

Choose one of the following books:

Mary Crow Dog:  Lakota Women- A Lakota Sioux tells her story of growing up on a reservation in South Dakota and her participation in the AIM and  Native American

Religion. 

Ted Conover:  Coyotes- A journalist’s experiences as he travels and lives with migrant workers from Mexico.

Mitchell Duneier: Slim’s Table- Black Americans of south Chicago and their attitudes towards and their interaction with White Americans and other groups of blacks.

Ben Fong Torres: The Rice Room- Growing up Chinese American and caught between the pressures of two cultures.

Reviews should include 1) the basic theme or thesis of the book. 2) the points of arguments that are used to back the theme or thesis. 3) and your opinion of the argument and the thesis.
