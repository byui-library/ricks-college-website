MILLENNIAL HOMECOMING PLANS IN THE WORKS

We hope you are keeping the weekend of Oct. 14-16 open for our Millennial Homecoming celebration. Plans are still be finalized, but here is what we know so far: A Homecoming Extravaganza will be held Friday night in Hart Auditorium featuring Ricks performing groups and alumni performers. Saturday will feature the emeritus breakfast, a parade at 10 a.m., special events and reunions on campus during the day, a football game versus Scottsdale, and a concert by Reunion (including former members of the Lettermen) at 7 p.m., and homecoming dance. Saturday will also be the community's Huckleberry/Potato Festival and feature a potato bar on or near campus. We'll keep you posted about details, but keep the weekend open!
