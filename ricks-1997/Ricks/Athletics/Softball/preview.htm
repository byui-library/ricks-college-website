Ricks College Softball Season Preview

A LEAGUE OF THEIR OWN
It's hard to comprehend the amount of success the Lady Viking softball team had last season. It was a new program full of freshman faces with a coach with no head coaching experience. A fifty-fifty year was probably a high expectation going in, but the end result was unbelievable. The Vikings finished up with a 28-6 overall record and won the Scenic West Athletic Conference with a 14-2 mark, which gave them hosting rights to the Region 18 outright in some of the worst weather imaginable, and Coach Larry Stocking took his team of freshmen to the area playoffs where they finally lost out to the defending National Champions, Central Arizona college. There's more. Pitching ace, Monika Hurley, went 21-1 on the year, was All Region 18 Tournament's Most Valuable Player, and was the League's number one All-American candidate. Returning with Hurley are sophomores Leli'a Roberts playing first base, Kacy Hone at short stop, Sarah Bryner at third base, Lindsay Carlen in Right field, an Patience Parker in center field. Adding to the lethal line-up are freshmen Cassie Alvey catching, Stacy Holmes at second base, Stephanie Kirklan in left, an Kim Burton pitching for the Vikings.
