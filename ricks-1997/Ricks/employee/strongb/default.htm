Brent M. Strong joined the Ricks College Foreign Language Department in August 1989 as an instructor of German. Born and raised in Idaho Falls, Idaho, he graduated from Brigham Young University with a B.A. degree in German. He subsequently launched a military career as an officer in the United States Air Force. During his twenty-two year military career, both he and his family lived and traveled throughout much of the world. Although originally trained in the Military Intelligence career field with assignments in Germany, at the Pentagon, and in Southeast Asia, where he completed forty-two combat missions as an airborne crew member, much of his later military career was spent in education on the faculties of the United States Air Force Academy in Colorado Springs, Colorado and at the German Air Force Academy just outside Munich, West Germany. In addition to his B.A. degree, he holds an M.S. degree in German from Georgetown University and a Ph.D. degree in Foreign Language Education from The Ohio State University. He is married to the former Karolyn Ashby of Provo, Utah, and they are the parents of six children.
