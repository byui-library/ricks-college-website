HOW TO SUCCEED: This is not an easy class, nor is it intended to be, but you’ll learn a great deal, and will use it the rest of your life. (1) Plan to work and you will be amazed at how much you will learn. Miss several times, skip your reading, fail several quizzes, and you will be amazed how quickly your grade will drop. (2) Study in groups of 2 or 3 individuals. Meet on a regular basis and review the material with each other. Verbalize the concepts, asking each other questions. Making the chapter make sense to another person is the best way to have it make sense to you. (3) Please do not quit. Ten years from now you’ll have noticed that those who make it, in class or anywhere else, are those who simply settle down and get to work.
