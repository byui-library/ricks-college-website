When combined with the contributions of others, gifts made by Ricks College Employees make a tremendous impact on the college and individual student's lives.  Your gift may provide:
  • one more scholarship
  • one more computer
  • one more tutor for a student in need
  • one more Badger Creek experience
  • one more opportunity for a student to stay in school
  • or many other experiences

In the past, Ricks College employees have contributed to more than 75 different campus programs.  Whatever program you choose to enhance, you can assist our students make their experience here one that will have life-changing results.

How much should your gift be?  Only you know your circumstances and how many lives you can bless.  Turn in your contribution or return the payroll deduction form today.
