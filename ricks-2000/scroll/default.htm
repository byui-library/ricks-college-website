Celebrating a century of sports

Students wave pennants as a final goodbye at the close of the Ricks Athletics Tribute Friday night in the Hart Auditorium. The night’s activities were prepared to honor those coaches and athletes who participated in Viking Athletics throughout the years. Photos by James Ricks
The last 100 years of athletics at Ricks College include 15 national championships, 61 individual national champions, 102 first-team All-Americans and one NJCAA Player of the Year.

Friday night, Ricks honored every athlete, coach and participant in the athletic program and celebrated a century of Viking traditions on the playing field as the final year of athletics comes to a close... [more] March 5
