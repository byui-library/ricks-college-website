What is Physics? Physics is an on-going search for an explanation of the behavior of objects in the Universe.  Physics tries to answer how things behave, and why things behave as they do.

What is Chemistry? Chemistry is the study of the structure of matter and the changes it undergoes.

 

Goal of Physical Science 110:

         To gain an appreciation of the basic concepts and

         principles of physics and chemistry and show how

         they come into play over and over again in our lives.
