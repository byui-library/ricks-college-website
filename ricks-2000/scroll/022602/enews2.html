Miss Idaho Falls

by Ashley Andrus
AND01049@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Freshman Elisha Richardson’s crowning as Miss Idaho Falls in the Miss Idaho Falls pageant did not quite happen in a way that was normally expected.

After two years of being involved with the pageant, Richardson of Idaho Falls was selected as the first runner-up. However, the position only lasted for a few months until it was discovered that the winner was too old to continue further in the other events leading up to the Miss America pageant.

“She [former Miss Idaho Falls] called me up in December and told me that she was going to renounce her crown and that if I wanted it, it was mine,” Richardson said. “I felt it was my obligation to do this and I saw it as a big opportunity to present my platform [specific cause] to the community. I was shocked and excited all at the same time.”

Ever since Richardson was a tiny girl, it has been her dream to be in the pageant. She recalled watching her aunt compete for the crown and decided to make it a goal as well.

In the months leading up to the pageant, Richardson was busy presenting her platform to the community as part of her pageant requirements. She chose to focus on service in Idaho Falls and spent much of her time visiting local schools, giving speeches, organizing projects and attending public events to include others in her cause.

“It was a really fun opportunity,” Richardson said. “I performed my talent at the schools [singing] and gave them ideas of what they can do to get involved at a young age.”

Richardson is now working towards entering in the Miss Idaho pageant held on June 8th in hopes of ultimately winning her way to the Miss America pageant. She anticipates that there will be approximately twenty girls in the Miss Idaho pageant. She is also majoring in vocal performance and hopes to someday be involved with studio recording.

“It’s a great opportunity to get to know who you are,” Richardson said regarding her pageant experience. “You get to know a lot of really neat people and learn a lot of things ... It’s more than looks, it’s much more than that. You’ve gotta go out there and do what you say you’re going to do.”
