Outstanding Young Alumni Award
Michael W. "Mike" Mosman

Mike attended Ricks prior to and after a Church mission to Spain. He met his wife, Suzanne Hogan, at a training meeting for dorm assistants at Ricks. He left here in 1979, graduated from Utah State University, and obtained his law degree from BYU's J. Reuben Clark Law School in 1984. He is a native of West Linn, Ore., where he now lives, but was raised in Lewiston and Moscow, Idaho. Mike is a former bishop and Primary teacher, and is now first counselor in his stake's Young Men's presidency. He and his wife are the parents of five children, ranging in age from two to 19.

We asked Mike to tell us about his job. To rewrite or attempt to capsulize his comments would be a disservice. He writes: "My official job title is Assistant United States Attorney. I tell people I am a federal prosecutor. I have had a wide variety of cases and am in court all the time. I handled a marijuana smuggling case involving the shipment of over twenty towns of marijuana from Thailand to California, and its eventual distribution across the U.S. and Canada. Coincidentally, some of that marijuana ended up in Rexburg. It also involved laundering millions of dollars through financial institutions in Hong Kong, New York, Toronto, and Switzerland. I also prosecute felony crimes on the three major Indian Reservations in Oregon. As a results, I have handled many cases of murder, rape, and child abuse. The latter are unusually difficult and discouraging cases. My current assignment involves prosecuting organized crime cases, with the usual panoply of electronic surveillance, and the added twist of sophistication from the computer era. I consider it a tremendous honor to serve as an Assistant United States Attorney. I have no illusion that this job alone can keep the streets safe, but I believe that it is a very important part of that effort."
