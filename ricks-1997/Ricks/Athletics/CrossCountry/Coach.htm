DOUG STUTZ
    Brigham Young University   -   Ten Years (2001)

    Doug Stutz arrived at Ricks College in 1988 as an assistant coach in cross country and track and field. His specialty was distance running, a sport he excelled in in high school and college. His competitive skills quickly translated into coaching skills which translated into success for Ricks College runners.

    In 1992, Stutz was named head coach of the men’s and women’s cross country program. A dynasty had begun. The men’s team showed immediate improvement, winning the Region 18 crown seven times in the next ten years, and finishing second twice. At the national level, the men returned to prominence, placing in the top eight at the national finals on eight occasions, topped by  national championships in 1999, 2000, and 2001. He has coached seventeen young men to All-American status, including 2000 NJCAA champion Jeff Davidson of Burly, Idaho.

    The performance of the women’s team under Coach Stutz’s guidance has been remarkable. In ten years they have won the Region 18 title seven times, finishing second Twice and third on the other occasion. In 1992 the team took third at the NJCAA meet. The Lady Vikings improved to second in 1993 and 1994. Their progress continued, reaching the top spot at the national meet in 1995, then stunning the competition by repeating as NJCAA champions in 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, and 2001. During that run, Stutz coached four individual champions, Kara Ormond in 1996, Alycia Boyer in 1998, Kristen Ogden in 2000, and Angie Benson in 2001. In all, 41 young women have attained All-American status with Stutz as their head coach. The NJCAA acknowledged Doug’s success by naming him national Coach of the Year seven times. Five times his teams have won the award for best combined men’s and women’s scores at the NJCAA finals.

    Stutz is now the head coach of the track and field team, where his distance runners do equally as well. He uses a wholistic approach to training distance runners. He emphasizes not only the workouts, but also adequate rest, nutrition, proper mental preparation, and academic, social, and spiritual habits as well. Coach Stutz utilizes the unique environment of Ricks College to cultivate the abilities of the athletes he works with.

    Doug ran competitively at Brighton High in Salt Lake City where he finished in the top ten twice in the Utah State High School cross country finals. He continued at BYU where he was a part of two District 7 championship teams and competed in the NCAA Cross Country finals three times. In track and field, Stutz competed in the 5000 meters and 3000 meter steeplechase. He continues to actively train, often running with his team during practices.

    Doug’s wife Keysha was also a distance runner. The two now make their home in Rexburg with a young and growing family. Doug served a mission for the LDS church in Argentina from 1980-1982.
