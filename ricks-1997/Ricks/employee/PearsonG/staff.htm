Two full-time photographers, with assistance from other art and professional photography faculty, provide instruction in the two photography programs at BYU-Idaho.  Darren Clark and Gary Pearson work out of the Romney Photo Facilities and supervise the teaching of the Specialized Associate Degree and the Bachelor of Fine Arts Degree course.  Also taught are evening photojournalism and community education classes. Courses are taught based on the experience of the instructor.

Darren Clark earned a photography associates from Ricks College, a BFA in photography from Utah State University and a Master of Fine arts degree in photography from Louisiana State University.  Gary Pearson earned his MFA in photography from Utah State University.

Other professional photographers teach courses where their expertise expands personal vision and enriches the student experience.
