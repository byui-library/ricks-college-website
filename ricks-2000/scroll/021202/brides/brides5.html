Types of Bouquets: The Traditional Bridal Accessory

Nosegay: A small cluster of flowers in a holder or wrapped with ribbon that is held in the hands.

Biedermeier: A small nosegay with rings of various colors and usually contain small blossoms.

Tussie Mussie: Hand-tied bouquet with stems tied together and trimmed with a ribbon.

Wired: Each individual bloom is wired to stand upright and is flexible enough to be bent into the shape of a bouquet.

 Cascade: Blooms and greenery that gracefully fall downward along the front of the gown.

Composite: Individual petals of a flower or a cluster of blooms wired and glued together for a full look.

Pomander: A blossom-covered globe held together by a ribbon. This is good for children to carry, as are baskets.

Boa: A luxurious length of wired flowers to be worn as a stole.
