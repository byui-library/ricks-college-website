Mission

The mission of the Associated Students of Brigham Young University-Idaho (ASBYUI) Student Government is to:

  1. Support and uphold the mission of Brigham Young University-Idaho and its institutional traditions and standards.
  2. Represent the student perspective and be an effective liason between students and the administration.
  3. Enrich the lives of students by providing significant experiences and opportunities in leadership, service, and enrichment programs and activities.
