Financial Aid
Most students are eligible for some form of financial aid.  Financial aid at Ricks primarily consists of Federal Pell Grants, Stafford Loans, and a limited number of other types of aid.  Ricks does not participate in Perkins loans, SEOG grants, work study, or any other campus-based programs.  Students applying for Federal Student Financial Aid must first complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).  This form is available from high school counselors, a college or university near the student’s home, or the Ricks College Financial Aid Office.  Students may also complete the FAFSA on the Web which can be linked from our web site (see below).  If a student is planning to get married within the year, contact the Financial Aid Office.  Getting married may affect the  eligibility of the student.  Our office can advise the student on when he/she should apply for financial aid.   The Ricks College Title IV school code which is required on the FAFSA is:  001625.

Federal Pell Grants
Under the Federal Pell Grant Program students may be eligible for a non-repayable government grant.  Grants are awarded on the basis of financial need.

Federal Family Education Loans
Federal Subsidized, Unsubsidized and Parent Loans for Undergraduate Students (PLUS) are available.  First year students may borrow up to $2625 per school year.  Students who have earned 32 credits or more may borrow up to $3500 per school year.

Federal Subsidized Stafford Loan
Principal and interest payments are postponed while students are enrolled in school at least half time and during their 6 month grace period.  The federal government pays the interest for the student during this time.

Federal Unsubsidized Stafford Loan
Monthly principal payments are postponed while students are enrolled in school at least half time and during their 6 month grace period.  Students are responsible for paying the interest that accrues on their loan.  Students can either make interest payments or request that the lender add the accrued interest to the principal balance.  This is called capitalization and increases the total amount students will pay.

Parent Loans For Undergraduate Students (PLUS)
Parents of dependent undergraduate students may borrow up to the student’s annual cost of attendance.

For more detailed information about financial aid, deadlines and other general information, visit our web site at:  http://www.byui.edu/Ricks/Financial Aid or visit the Financial Aid Office in the Kimball Building #100 or call us at (208)356-1013.

Financial Holds
Financial Holds will be placed on student records when any debt owing to the College is past due.  This will stop any registration and the release of transcripts until the debt is paid.  Make payments at the Cashiers Office.  If there are any questions, contact the Accounting Office.

Fire
If fire or smoke is discovered, (1) activate the nearest fire alarm, (2) leave the building immediately, staying close to the floor where smoke is less dense and (3) follow staff, police, and fire personnel directions.  If time permits in the residence hall, (1) turn on the light in your room, (2) put on a coat and shoes during inclement weather, (3) open the drapes and/or the blinds, (4) close the windows, (5) pick up a towel to put over your face in case smoke is encountered, (6) close your door leaving the lights on, and (7) leave the building quickly.  TAMPERING WITH FIRE EXTINGUISHERS AND/OR FIRE ALARMS IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED.  ANY VIOLATIONS WILL BE DEALT WITH CRIMINALLY AND WITH SCHOOL DISCIPLINARY ACTION.  Proper conduct during fire evacuations is the most important contribution you can make toward your own safety and that of your friends.

See Student ID cards.
Proper Care of the ID Card:
Treat your ID card with care, as you would your driver’s license, credit cards, or cash.
1.  Don't bend or wash the ID card.  Card readers cannot read bent or cracked cards reliably.
2.  Keep the back of the card away from the back of other cards with magnetic stripes, the magnetic fields erase each other.  Also, keep your card away from any magnetic fields such as VCR’s or stereo speakers.
3.  Don’t use the card as a toothpick or an ice scraper to clean your car windows.  They will break.

Incomplete Grades
An incomplete is a conditional grade and is given only in exceptional cases--never to extend time to obtain a better grade.  It is not a substitute for a failing grade and is given only when you can complete the prescribed coursework without further attendance in the classroom.  To receive an incomplete grade in a course, you must meet with your instructor and request an incomplete.  The instructor and you determine what work needs to be completed and the time given in which to do it.  Both you and the instructor must sign the contract.  A $5 fee will be charged at the time you pick up the Incomplete Contract at the Registrar’s Office.  A copy of the contract is given to the Registrar's Office by your instructor at the time final grades are submitted.  To remove the incomplete grade, you must complete the work required.  The instructor will notify the Registrar's Office, who will change the grade on the transcript.  If the work has not been completed by the end of the next major semester, the "I" will be changed to "F."

Insurance
Ricks College requires that EACH STUDENT MUST BE COVERED IN THE REXBURG AREA BY HEALTH AND ACCIDENT INSURANCE.  Therefore, each student who registers as a half time student or more is automatically charged for the Ricks College Student Insurance, (Continuing Education students must apply for insurance).  A student may purchase insurance for his dependents during the first week of Fall Semester or the first term of attendance during the school year.  Also a dependent can be added within 30 days after a new dependent is acquired.  Insurance coverage will continue for students and /or their dependents as long as there is no break in the students enrollment.  Dependent insurance is not available unless the student is also covered by Ricks insurance.  You and  your dependents may also continue insurance coverage  during a break from school for up to one semester at one time as long as the student is returning to Ricks.  HOWEVER, the student must make application in the Accounting Office before the break to have insurance coverage continued.  Payment is due at the beginning of each semester or term.  For insurance available after graduating or leaving Ricks, check with the accounting office.
A STUDENT MAY HAVE RICKS INSURANCE WAIVED if (1) he or she is covered in the Rexburg area by another health and accident insurance plan, AND (2) a completed Waiver Form has been submitted to the Ricks Accounting Office BEFORE the deadline (first week of a Semester or block or term).  Once a Waiver Form has been submitted, the student will not be charged for insurance in subsequent semesters or terms unless the student notifies the Accounting Office that he no longer has private insurance.  Failure to do this is grounds for disciplinary action.  Waiver Forms are mailed to new and former students with the registration packets.  A waiver form will also be mailed prior to Fall Semester to each student who has been charged.  The student may also pick up a waiver form in the Accounting Office.  If a student has still been charged when he arrives at school, he should bring his private insurance information (insurance company, address, policy number, group number, and policy holder’s name) to the Accounting Office and fill out the waiver card before the deadline (first week).
