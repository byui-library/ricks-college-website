Minutes from Yellowstone National Park, Parade Rest Ranch lies nestled in a large grove of aspen and cottonwood trees I the pine-clad foothills of the Gallatin Mountain Range. The ranch is located on the old Bannock Indian Trail at 6,500 feet. Views of beautiful Hebgen Lake and the meandering Grayling Creek add to the tranquility of this mountain hideaway. Log cabins, decorated in western decor. Multiple occupancy cabins (double and twin beds) occupied by a maximum of 1 couple or 3 singles with single men and women housed separately; one bath in each cabin shared by up to 3 people. Meals and classes a short walk from cabins. Enjoy an old-fashioned cookout while basking in the beautiful scenery. No alcohol allowed.

HORSE PACKING IN YELLOWSTONE NATIONAL PARK: GALLATIN MOUNTAINS
Explore the spectacular Gallatin Mountains of Yellowstone Park. Study the flora and fauna including trees, flowers, and other vegetation. Animals include the ray wolf, bears (grizzly and black), elk, and others. Nature and wildlife discussions cover the results of the Forest Fires of 1988 and the Big Freeze of 1989. Learn philosophies concerning land use an ethics. Classes review history and expeditions of the park. Learn how the park received its name, Indians that traveled the area, and early mountain men. Participants will learn care of horses, saddling, bridling, safety, etiquette, grooming, and packing. Field work will illustrate basic equestrian, setting up low impact horse camps and other camping skills.
