Religion Requirement
lTwo credits required for every 16 hours of Ricks/BYU-Idaho credit earned until you have 14 credits.
lTransfer credits for LDS religion from other colleges accepted, but you still need two credits for every 16 hours at this campus.
lStudents are discouraged from taking more than one religion course in a semester.  (The computer won’t let you) A possible exception is Missionary Preparation.  The Religion Department Chairman must approve exceptions.
