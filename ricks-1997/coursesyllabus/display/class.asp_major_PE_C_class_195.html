Skiing
SKIING
CLASS CODE: PE*C 195 CREDITS:  1 FEE:  $240.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Recreational skiing and telemarking, one day a week for ten weeks at Grand targhee Resort with professional instructors.
DESCRIPTION: Skiing for participants of varying abilities. Sections formed on basis of ability with instruction suited to varying skill levels in fundamentals of skiing. Instruction will be provided in three areas: alpine and telemarking provided in a safe and supervised environment.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: The purpose of this program is to teach the student a lifetime sport that will provide learning opportunities in other areas as well. The student will:
1. Learn patience and control while performing or competing
2. Form a support group with common interests.
3. Mentally learn correct fundamentals, courtesy, and motor learning.
4. Physically get a cardiovascular workout and discover an activity for a lifetime.
5. Learn natural laws and consequences
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Skiers will participate in one of seven skill level groups and attend five classes.
2. Skiers will ride bus.
3. Skiers will be offered ten class times - six being mandatory for a pass grade.
4. Skiers will follow safety rules on bus, at ski lifts and on ski hills.
5. Skiers will provide their own insurance.
6. Attendance/participation.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences allowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
5. Ski fee: $240 (fee varies according to transportation and left pass).
6. Skis and boots can be rented at resort for $25 fee.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
