How to Find Microfiche/Film Sources

Materials available on microfilm or microfiche can be located using the Library Catalog. Using either the General Key Word, Subject Key Word, Author, Title or Series searches, enter your topic into the box and press enter or click on Search. A list of materials that match the search topic will be displayed. Materials that are recorded on microfilm will be designated as such in the Call Number column of the display. Magazines, journals and newspapers that are available in microfilm or microfiche will be designated as Periodicals (mf) on the full catalog display. Government documents that are available on microfiche will have the initials GDM or the words Government Document Micro listed as the collection code. The periodicals and government documents on microfiche/film are located in the Periodical room on the first floor of the West Wing.
