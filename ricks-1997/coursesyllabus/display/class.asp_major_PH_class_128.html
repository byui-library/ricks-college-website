ELEMENTARY ASTRONOMY LAB
ASTRONOMY LAB
CLASS CODE: PH 128 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Physical Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Astronomy lab is designed to give you experience in observing/analyzing basic astronomical phenomena. Consists of 14 three hour lab sessions.
DESCRIPTION: Observational astronomy designed to give the student experience in observing and analyzing basic astronomical phenomena.
TOPICS: Physical Properties/Relationships
Predicting Star/Plant Locations
Observing Stars, Planets and the Moon
Optics and Telescopes
Magnitude Scale
Spectroscopy
Physical Properties of stars
Hubble's Constant
Mass of Jupiter
Average Density and the Planets
Radioactivity and Age Dating
Photogeology
OBJECTIVES: To observe motions of the celestial objects in the sky and demonstrate techniques used by scientists to discover information about the stars and planets.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance is mandatory. Labs cannot be done outside of class. Each lab is 25 points (lab #3 is 40 points), reading quizzes 5 points, (60 points total) total 400 points.
