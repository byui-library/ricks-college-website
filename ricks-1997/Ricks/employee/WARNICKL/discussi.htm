Let's talk about it

 

Now, please choose a discussion group with which to share your ideas about the future of newspapers and how you would help or save them. Click on one of the buttons below representing the group you wish to join. You should contribute at least one original entry of 200 words or more, respond to at least four other students' postings, and maintain the discussion threads with those who respond to you. Feel free to monitor the other groups, as well, and contribute to their discussions when the urge strikes. Let's see what kind of monstrous ideas you can generate!

Note: you do not need to navigate clear through the site for your return trips to the discussion groups. Just click on the discussion button on the home page to return directly to this page.
