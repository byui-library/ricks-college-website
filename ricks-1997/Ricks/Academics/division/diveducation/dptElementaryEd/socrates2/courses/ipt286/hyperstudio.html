Your Hyperstudio Assignment

This project is not intended to be long nor cumbersome. It is intended to allow you to use Hyperstudio to build a sample stack that would be appropriate as a learning tool in an elementary classroom. The criteria are:

  1. Choose a curricular area within the grade level of your web site
  2. Choose a specific skill, concept, principle or law within the the curriculuar area (not just facts) - remember the KISS principle (Keep it specific and simple)!
  3. Write an appropriate lesson plan that uses Hyperstudio as the teaching tool or "U" under the ASSURE model.

Then build the hyperstudio stack including:

  • A title card
  • A credits card that describes you (you may link to your personal web page to replace this card)
  • A learning objective card
    • A full lesson plan card should be linked to the objective card*
  • An introduction card
  • An appropriate number of instruction cards as needed
  • A conclusion card
  • A checkup or "Require Learner Performance" card
  • And any other appropriate cards

* This will probably require a scrolling field on the card

Operational criteria include:

  • Proper navigation throughout
  • Consistency of colors, buttons and transitions
  • Use of graphics, text, sound and video (if included)
  • Instructionally sound (it should follow the lesson plan and be aligned).
