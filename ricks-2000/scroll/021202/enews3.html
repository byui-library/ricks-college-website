Major forum held by SIFE

by Seth Moncur
MON98005@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff

Students in Free Enterprise (SIFE) held a executive seminar Thursday for a full Taylor Chapel.

Jay Price, president and co-founder of AMX International, an Idaho Falls based consulting company. AMX has 150 employees and 400 client companies, such as Coke, Toyota, Gateway and UTA in Utah.

Amex specializes in the implementation and support of enterprise resource planning, software for large corporations and public sector organizations.

Price compared consulting with a drummer in a rock band.

“A consultant is an expert, an ordinary guy from out of town. A rock star is always from out of town,” Price said.

A consultant can make more money the farther away from home you are, he said. He could charge $10 an hour in Rexburg, but he could charge $180 an hour in Brazil.

Price outlined the growth of his business and discussed some principles of good business.

AMX started in 1989 with two employees and made $150,000 in revenue that year. AMX steadily grew and now makes $25-30 million per year and employs 150 people.

Price listed some good business practices he got from his book Everything I Learned About Consulting I Learned In a Rock and Roll Band. The ideas include: doing what is right for the customer, putting everything in writing, get out of town for better business, learn the language and tools of the business, pay your dues and get a four year degree, never partnership with a family member, and be a values based business.

All classes, such as science and geology and humanities, will make you a well rounded person and will enable you to talk to other people in business, Price said.
