Swimming Advanced
SWIMMING ADV
CLASS CODE: PE*C 162 CREDITS:  1
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: PHYSICAL EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: An advanced swimming class designed to instruct students beyond intermediate swimming skills and strokes.
DESCRIPTION: This is an advanced class for fitness swimming and water sports. The course is for those students who want to swim laps with sections on water polo, springb oard diving, and water aerobics.
TOPICS:
OBJECTIVES: 1. Reach for individual potential by improving knowledge and skill of swimming.
2. Share ideas while working toward common goals in a spirit of unselfishness.
3. Be able to exercise enough to get a cardiovascular work-out and to relieve stress.
4. Knowledge of terminology, rules, safety practices, and correct fundamentals of intermediate swimming strokes and diving.
5. To gain a testimony of the divine origins and sacredness of our bodies.
REQUIREMENTS: 1. Pass all requirments as outlined in the advanced course of the American Red Cross.
2. Show swimming proficiency in
3. Do non-stop swimming with a variety of strokes
4. Pass a written exam
5. Attend class and participate
6. BYU-I clothing required.
PREREQUISITES: Pass Intermediate Swim Class or exhibit skills of intermediate swimmer.
OTHER: 1. Two unexcused absences allowed.
2. Four or more absences will result in automatic failure.
3. Pass/Fail grade is available with less than four absences.
4. May be repeated one time for credit.
5. P.E. clothing fee: $4.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 1986
