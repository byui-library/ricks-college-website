You may be able to double or even triple your gift to Ricks College with a simple call. Thousands of employers in the U.S. have established "matching gift" programs. They will match the gifts their employees make to institutions of higher education. In some instances employee gifts are matched two- and even three-to-one. In effect, your gift to Ricks College could be worth twice as much, or more.

To learn if your company has a matching gift program and find out how you can take advantage of a matching gift at no additional cost to you, see or call your personnel, human resources, or community relations office. If your employer participates, they can give you the appropriate form. Simply fill the form out and send in to Ricks along with your gift.
