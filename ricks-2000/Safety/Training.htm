  Employee Safety Training

 

Ricks College is committed to providing a safe work place for individuals working in college facilities, whether they be full-time, part-time, faculty, staff, or students.  A major component of providing a safe work place lies in training.  The College Safety Office will do all that it can to assist departments with training.  However, the responsibility to provide employee training lies primarily with individual supervisors.  Departmental supervisors also have a responsibility to ensure that all employees within their area have been adequately trained in regards to work and personal safety.

The Safety Office is conducting monthly safety training seminars for faculty/staff, and students.  Topics vary month to month.  If you or your department has a specific training topic you would like presented, please contact the safety office with your suggestion.

In addition, the Safety Office has a wide array of Safety Training Videos which can be checked out for Safety Training Workshops and Presentations.
