New Testament 211 Syllabus: GRADING

YOUR GRADE WILL BE DETERMINED BY:

  • ATTENDANCE 40%
  • TESTS AND QUIZZES 25%
  • INSIGHT AND APPLICATION 15%
  • SCHOLARLY PROJECT & RESERVE 10%
  • READING 10%
  • ATTENDANCE:

THIS MAKES UP THE MOST PART OF YOUR GRADE. MORE IMPORTANTLY, YOU WILL HAVE A MUCH BETTER EXPERIENCE IF YOU ATTEND EACH CLASS. THE ATTENDANCE GRADE IS BROKEN DOWN AS FOLLOWS:

  • MISS NO MORE THAN 3 DAYS—A
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 6 DAYS—B
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 9 DAYS—-C
  • MISS NO MORE THAN 12 DAYS—Woe!

READING:

DURING THE COURSE OF THE SEMESTER YOU WILL BE EXPECTED TO READ THE FOUR GOSPELS (178 PAGES). IF YOU READ 1.7 PAGES PER DAY, YOU WILL COMPLETE THE GOSPELS BY THE END OF CLASS.

THE NEW TESTAMENT MANUAL IS HIGHLY RECOMMENDED.

QUIZZES AND TESTS:

THERE WILL BE A MID-TERM AND A FINAL EXAM. THERE WILL ALSO BE PERIODIC QUIZZES WHICH, IF YOU ATTEND FAITHFULLY, YOU WILL EASILY MASTER.
