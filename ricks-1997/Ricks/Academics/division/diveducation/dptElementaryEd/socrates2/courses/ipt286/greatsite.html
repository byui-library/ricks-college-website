The World Wide Web is a lot like a flea market with a vast selection of sites of varying quality. Sometimes it's hard to tel what's worth looking at, what's junk and what's a hidden treasur. The best sites taka advantage of the Web's unique features to enrich the user's experience.

Here are some basic guidelines to help you select the best for children-or yourself.

1. The purpose of the site is clear whether it's to entertain, persuade, educate or sell.

2. A source is clearly identified.

3. Advertising should not overshadow content.

4. The graphic design should be appealing with text that is easy to read.

5. The content should be well organized and easy to understand.

6. The information should be current and updated regularly.

7. Users should be able to get around the site easily.

8. The page should be consistently available and load in a reasonable amount of time.

9. A user should not have to pay a fee or type in personal information.

10. The skills required to use this site should be appropriate for its intended age group.
