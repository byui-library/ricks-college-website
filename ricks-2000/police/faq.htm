Frequently Asked Questions

Are you Real Cops or Security Guards?
We have both!   All Police officers of our division have to successfully complete and maintain Certification through Idaho Peace Officers  Standards and Training. We write real tickets and put people in jail (its what we live for). We also hire Student Security guards. Security Guard training mandates  that they recieve 40 hours of various training including Liability training, First Aid, Community Relations, Shoplifting apprehension techniques, Patrol Theory etc,. Our Security Guards are certified by the International Association Chief of Police.

Are you Campus Police or Rexburg Police?
Yes.  We are a division of the Rexburg City Police Department. All of our paperwork goes through the City of Rexburg.  We are assigned to the Campus area to work. We have jurisdiction of the entire City of Rexburg.
 

What Kind of Gun do you Carry?
Currently we are carrying the Glock model 22. This firearm is chambered to .40 S&W.

Have you ever killed anyone?
Currently no officers in our division have had to take a life in the line of duty.

Are you going to turn me over to student life for breaking College rules.
No. We follow the Lemon law. (A separation of church and state). Most action that is taken is a matter of public record and it seems that Student Life always finds out.
 

Is Skateboarding Really a Crime?
Rexburg City Ordinance #759  prohibits Roller blades and Skateboarding on campus. If you are caught, You will probably be Cited. Use the Skateboarding Park instead. It is located near the Madison County Fairgrounds North West of town.

How about Snowballs?
Rexburg City ordinance also prohibits throwing snowballs, rocks, water balloons etc. The officers really don't care if you have a snowball fight so long as wayward snowballs don't find their way to any unwilling participants.

Why do you write parking tickets or "work" on Sunday?
Most of us don't even like working on Sundays! We would rather be with our families. We don't enjoy writing parking tickets but we need to maintain the public order. We have to keep access to the buildings open so that Ambulances and Fire trucks can access the campus as well as our handicapped patrons if need be. Of course the criminal element has never paid homage to the "Sunday" issue and as Municipal Police officers we patrol the college area to make the area safer for you. Here's an interesting side note, out of the 3466 incidents that have occurred on campus during 1999, roughly 8% happened on Sunday. This is interesting  because school is not in session and the average 1200 to 1500 faculty and staff people are not around. The bigger question that should be asked is why do those who profess to be such good christians act so thoughtlessly in regards to their fellowmen and act like they own the whole parking lot?
Until their are no medical, fire or other emergencies and the Criminal element disappears, We will be out in full force.
