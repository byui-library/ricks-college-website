College Student Experience Questionnaire (CSEQ)

Developed at Indiana University, the CSEQ assesses student involvement in college activities and the degree to which students have engaged in behaviors that contribute to their learning and development in college. It assesses how college students spend their time on such activities as course work, visiting the library, and interacting with faculty. It assesses their involvement in extracurricular, social, and cultural activities as well as other opportunities that exist on college campuses.

The CSEQ was administered by mail to a random sample of 550 students enrolled at Ricks College in the Winter Semester 1997.  Of this sample, 240 students completed and returned surveys for a return rate of 44%.  The completed surveys were scored, analyzed, and normed by the test publisher.  Results include national norms for different types of higher education institutions.
