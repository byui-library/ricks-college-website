ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Admissions requirements for New Freshmen, Transfer Students, Former Students and International Students can be reviewed by following the link to the desired student type. All materials must be received by the Admissions Office before the appropriate deadline.

  • New Freshman
  • Transfer Students
  • Former Students
  • International Students
  • Concurrent Enrollment Students




New Freshman

New Freshman applying to BYU-Idaho with fewer than 30 semester hours (45 quarter) of college credit must submit the following:

  • Part 1: Student Information
  • Part 2: School Selection/Scholarships
  • Part 3: Confidential Report
  • Part 4: Seminary/Institute Evaluation
  • Part 5: High School Information including an Official Transcript
  • Part 6: Extracurricular Activities
  • Part 7: Student Essays
  • $25 non-refundable application fee per school
  • American College Test (ACT) scores or Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT) scores
  • Official Transcripts from each college or university you are attending or have attended


Transfer Students

Transfer Students applying to BYU-Idaho with more than 30 semester hours (45 quarter) of college credit must submit the following. Applicants with fewer than 30 semester hours (45 quarter) should follow requirements for New Freshman.

  • Part 1: Student Information
  • Part 2: School Selection/Scholarships
  • Part 3: Confidential Report
  • Part 4: Seminary/Institute Evaluation
  • $25 non-refundable application fee per school
  • Official Transcripts from each college or university you are attending or have attended


Former Students

Fomer Students must submit the following.

  • Part 1: Student Information
  • Part 2: School Selection/Scholarships
  • Part 3: Confidential Report
  • Official Transcripts from each college or university attended since last attending BYU-Idaho


International Students

International Students must complete and submit the following:

  • Part 1: Student Information
  • Part 2: School Selection/Scholarships
  • Part 3: Confidential Report
  • Part 4: Seminary/Institute Evaluation
  • Part 5: High School Information including an Official Transcript
  • Part 6: Extracurricular Activities
  • Part 7: Student Essays
  • Part 8: Confidential Statement of Finances
  • $25 non-refundable application fee
  • Official Transcript in English from each college or university attended


Concurrent Enrollment Students

Concurrent Enrollment Students (a high school student who wishes to be considered for admission as a part-time student while enrolled in high school) must submit the following:

  • Part 1: Student Information
  • Part 2: School Selection/Scholarships
  • Part 3: Confidential Report
  • $25 non-refundable application fee per school
  • Concurrent enrollment voucher (available from admissions office)
  • Have a cumulative high school GPA of 3.0 or higher
  • Have completed their junior (grade 11) year of high school
