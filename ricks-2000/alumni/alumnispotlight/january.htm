January Spotlight -- Artist Wade Huntsman

Wade Huntsman '92 from Shelley, Idaho currently works as a background artist for Dreamworks SKG.  After graduating from Ricks he completed his studies at The Art Center College of Design in Pasadena, California.  For Dreamworks, Wade has worked on the following movies:

* The Prince of Egypt
* Road to El Dorado
* Joseph - King of Dreams
* Spirit - Stallion of the Cimmaron (soon to be released)

 

In addition, Wade has done freelance work for the following clients:

* 20th Century Fox (Titanic movie)
* Warner Brothers (Harry Potter)
* Harper Collins
* Harcourt Brace
* Disney Interactive
* Disney Animation
* Turner Feature Animation
* Ricks College Summit Magazine
* Children's Bureau of Los Angeles
* Field and Stream
* Petersons Publishing

Additionally, Wade has done fine art work for various individual clients.

Wade is married to the former Mary Jo Davis '91 and they are the parents
of 2 children.

Wade is an avid hunter and fisherman and spends much of his spare time in
the mountains and along the streams of Idaho.
