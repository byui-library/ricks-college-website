ACTIVITIES

The Ranch at Badger Creek provides high-adventure learning opportunities for Quest students.  Confidence courses, canoeing, high altitude hikes and horsemanship opportunities will broaden the student's experience and increase their appreciation of the outdoors.
Field trips center around experiential learning.   Technical skills as well as good citizenship in the out-of-doors are emphasized.
As Quest students enjoy the beautiful vistas of the Teton range they will come to understand the unique pleasure of playing on a glacier in early September as they climb Table Rock Mountain.

With four different ropes and confidence

courses on the ranch, Quest students will

be challenged to leave their comfort zones

to experience the thrill of accomplishment

Quest students learn about what groups are capable of accomplishing when they are focused into a team.
