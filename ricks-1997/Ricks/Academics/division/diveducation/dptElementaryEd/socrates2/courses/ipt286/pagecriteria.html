Minimum Requirements for your personal Web Page

  • Include a minimum of 3 Internet links (these must be to sites that you did not create).
  • Include an Email link to your personal email account.
  • Include a picture taken with the digital camera or scanned (be sure to label the picture as having been taken with the digital camera or scanned).
  • Follow Color and Design guidelines.
