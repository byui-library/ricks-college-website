May Spotlight -- David J. Thueson

David Thueson '83 is the founder and CEO of Great Northern Bark Company out of Columbia Falls, Montana.  David grew up in Rexburg, graduating from Madison High School.  Later, he served in the England Manchester and Tempe Arizona missions. 

While attending Ricks College, he had many opportunities to serve in campus wards, clubs and in student government.  He met his wife Jenelle Ihler ’83 in a family home evening group.  She later served as his campaign manager helping David in his successful bid to be student body president.  In addition to this leadership role, David was also selected by his fellow students and college administrators as Man of the Year.  After graduating from Ricks, David received his bachelor’s degree from BYU.  The Thuesons have five children.  David is currently serving as bishop of his ward in Columbia Falls. 

Of his Ricks experience, David has said:  "There are so many opportunities to serve others at Ricks.  Student government helped teach me the skills and confidence to succeed in business.  The spirit and hope and positive attitudes at Ricks helped me believe I could accomplish whatever I put my heart into.  Ricks supported and strengthened my standards and values that I have carried with me throughout the country."

David recently received the Outstanding Young Alumni Award from BYU-Idaho.
