PATHOPHYSIOLOGY -  BIO 281
Text: Pathophysiology, by Price and Wilson

Michael D. Groesbeck, Ph.D. BEN 224  Ex: 2035

Course Description: This course is designed for students interested in careers in nursing or other allied health professions. Its intent is to apply the basic principles of physiology to pathological conditions. Prerequisites for the class are BIO264 and BIO265 (Anatomy and Physiology I and II) and Chemistry 101 or equivalent (Introductory General Chemistry). It is expected that you already have a basic understanding of the physiology of the organs systems to be covered. We will not have time to review this material in detail.
