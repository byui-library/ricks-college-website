THREE-DAY OUTDOOR ADVENTURE

 

This itinerary reflects the standard Outdoor Adventure program which starts on the

first day at 10:00 a.m. and ends at noon on the third day. Times shown are

approximate. We reserve the right to make adjustments in this itinerary due to 

bad weather or other circumstances beyond our control.

 

Day 1

10:00 a.m. Arrive at the BYU-Idaho OLC. Please adjust your schedule in order to be on

time. There will be time for the staff to get acquainted with the participants. We will also

collect the "Assumption of Risk" forms for those in attendance.

 

11:00 a.m. Move to campsites. You will be separated in to six equal groups.

 

12 noon Lunch prepared by BYU-Idaho.

 

1:00-3:00 p.m. Workshops

 

3:00-5:00 p.m. Workshops

 

5:00 p.m. Open time

 

6:00 p.m. Dinner prepared by BYU-Idaho

 

7:00 p.m. Open time

 

8:00 p.m. Campfire program presented by BYU-Idaho staff

 

9:00 p.m. Night Walk. A guided walk at night with activities to help the participants gain a

better awareness of the night environment and themselves.

 

10:00 p.m. Approximate time to retire for the evening.

 

Day 2

6:00 a.m. Arise and prepare for breakfast

 

7:00 a.m. Breakfast prepared by BYU-Idaho

 

8:00-10:00 a.m. Workshops

 

10:00-12 noon Workshops

 

12 noon Lunch prepared by BYU-Idaho

 

1:00-3:00 p.m. Workshops

 

3:00-5:00 p.m. Workshops

 

5:00 p.m. Open time

 

6:00 p.m. Dinner prepared by BYU-Idaho

 

7:00 p.m. Open time

 

8:00 p.m. Campfire program presented by participants. Come prepared with skits, songs, etc.

 

9:00 p.m. Fireside speaker. Usually someone from the ward/stake leadership. This will be

followed by quiet games and activities.

 

10:00 p.m. Approximate time to retire for the evening.

 

Day 3

6:00 a.m. Arise and prepare for breakfast

 

7:00 a.m. Breakfast prepared by BYU-Idaho

 

8:00-10:00 a.m. Solo experience. This is a time when the participants will be by themselves to

write in their journals, read scriptures, pray, meditate, and other things of an uplifting nature.

 

10:00-12 noon Testimony meeting conducted by ward/stake leaders

 

12 noon  Break camp, clean up area, and board buses. A sack lunch will be provided for the

return trip.
