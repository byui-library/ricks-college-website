On-Campus Housing

The BYU-Idaho Housing Office operates and maintains all of the on-campus housing units for men and women.  A professional, full-time maintenance and custodial staff help ensure that units will be clean and in operation. 

Women can choose between cooking or non-cooking style residence halls in the fall and winter semesters.  Tenants residing in non-cooking style housing can choose between three different meal plan options.  Only cooking style residences exist for women during the summer semester.

Men are housed in non-cooking dorms during the fall and winter semesters and in cooking style housing during the summer semester.  Tenants residing in non-cooking style housing can choose between three different meal plan options.
