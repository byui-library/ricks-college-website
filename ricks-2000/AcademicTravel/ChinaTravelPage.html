First summer term
Six credits are required, and up to nine can be taken.

Course offerings will be determined in consultation with students.  Students will be able to choose from courses offered in Chinese culture/anthropology, English, business management and communication.

 

The China Study Tour offers students an opportunity to acquaint themselves with a culture and people that have been influencing the world for centuries.  We will get a glimpse of why two authors have referred to China as a "Waking Giant."

Once the tour group is defined, we will meet together once a month in preparation for our grand adventure! Check your e-mail frequently
