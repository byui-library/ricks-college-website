COURSE SYLLABUS

 Department: Home Economics
 Course#:ID 103
 Title: Three-Dimensional Design  Credit: 2
 Text:

 Course Objective:

Introduce the design student to the principles and elements of design and how they apply to three dimensional shapes and spaces.
Give the design student to design model making

Description: Topics Covered:

Elements and principles of design and three dimensional design
Basic model construction building.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)

There are three projects required:
A conceptual three design
A model of a bus stop for their home town
And a full model of a home, studio, or business
