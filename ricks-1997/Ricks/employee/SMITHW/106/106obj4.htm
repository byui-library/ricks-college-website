Unit #4 - IONIC EQUILIBRIA I: ACIDS AND BASES

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Write net ionic equations for the following reactions in aqueous solutions: a) the ionization of a weak acid, b) the ionization of a weak base.
2. Calculate Ka and/or Kb values from given concentration data.
3. Calculate the equilibrium concentrations of a substances in an acid-base reaction, given appropriate initial conditions and equilibrium constants.
4. Calculate the pH and/or pOH of an aqueous solution given either [H3O+] or [OH-], and vice versa.
5. Find the ratio of concentrations of weak acid (or base) and its conjugate that yields a buffer solution with a specified pH.
6. Calculate the change in equilibrium concentrations when a specified amount of either acid or base is added to a given buffer solution.
