ACCOUNT BALANCES

 

Funds deposited to the ID card are accessible only through Food Services at this time. Information regarding account balances can be obtained by contacting Tom Luke at 356-1143 or at his office in Room 160A of the Manwaring Center. Information about account balances can also be obtained by contacting a Food Service cashier at the Nordic Landing, Viking Place, or Thor's Court. Tom Luke can also assist in solving problems.

 

FOOD SERVICES DECLINING CARD

A person who wants to eat at the Nordic Landing, Viking Place, or Thor's Court can put money on his/her account by going to any Food Services cashier. A minimum deposit of $10 is required. A 5 percent discount is given when food purchases are made. When purchasing food, an ID card is required to access the account. If a student/employee is using the meal plan or declining food card and loses his/her ID card, it is important to report the lost card as soon as possible to the ID Center. This will prevent someone else from accessing those food dollars.
