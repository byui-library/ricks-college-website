COURSE DESCRIPTION: The Doctrine and Covenants is a significant and powerful collection of revelations and instructions given to the Prophet Joseph Smith and others. A thorough study of this great book of scripture will bless each student with an increased testimony and knowledge of the gospel of Jesus Christ. It will also assist each student (in connection with the proper application of the other standard works and teachings of the living prophets) to resolve most, if not all, of life's dilemmas.

CLASS OBJECTIVES:
1. To assist students to better understand and appreciate the background, doctrines and covenants contained in this great book.
2. To assist students to strengthen and deepen their testimonies of the Savior and His atonement.
3. To assist students in applying into their own lives, today, the principles taught in the gospels.

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Attendance: This is NOT a home study class. Regular attendance is essential in order to receive credit. 200 points are possible for perfect attendance. 40 points will be deducted for each absence over 2. Only school excused (and approved) absences will not be penalized. More than 8 absences will result in a loss of credit and in failing the class.

2. Scripture Study: Each student is to read the assigned text BEFORE class. You will report on the completion of this assignment each day. 150 points are possible. 15 points will be deducted for each assignment NOT completed BEFORE class. Your scripture study is not complete WITHOUT HAVING WRITTEN YOUR JOURNAL ENTRIES!

3. Scripture Study Journal: In connection with your personal study, you are to keep a journal. This journal should contain examples of the knowledge, understanding and feelings about the gospel principles you have discovered, or rediscovered during your personal scripture study. This journal can be handwritten and is personal, but will be evaluated. At least 2 gospel principles should be identified from EACH reading assignment during the course. At least 1 significant paragraph should be written for each principle selected. These paragraphs should include WHAT the principle or idea is and the scriptural REFERENCE that brought it to your mind; WHY it is important to you, and HOW the principle can help you in your life. You may also want to set goals and plan for the implementation of these principles. This journal should be written in a small binder or even a spiral notebook. This project is worth 150 points. It will be evaluated three times during the semester. Additional ideas for journal entries can be found on my web page.

4. Worksheets: Two worksheets will be given, a midterm and a final. Each will be worth 100 points.

5. Doctrinal Research Papers: Four, 2-3 page, (stapled) typewritten papers are required ON OR BEFORE the due date. A doctrine, phrase, verse or block of verses should be selected from any of the reading assignments given prior to the due date of the paper. This topic should be one that you would like to better understand. This is NOT to be written as a talk. It is a research paper. You should use cross references, footnotes, topical guide/index, bible dictionary, and extracts from General Conferences and quotes from The Brethren as primary sources. Secondary sources such as commentaries should be limited. You will find that a great amount of understanding can come from the scriptures themselves. These papers should include the following:
A. Student name, course (i.e. Doctrine and Covenants, Religion 224) and class time (i.e. 8:00, 10:00, etc.)
B. Identification of the topic you selected.
C. Inclusion of at least 7-8 scriptural clarifications of the topic (from Standard Works)
D. Inclusion of at least 5 General Conference or other quotes from The Brethren. You must include the NAME of the authority, and the REFERENCE or source of the quote. For example, Elder Boyd K. Packer, "Our Moral Environment", Ensign, May 1992, p. 31; or, James E. Talmage, Jesus the Christ, p. 357.
E. A ½ page summary (minimum) of what you have learned about the topic and your feelings about it. Important!

Papers MUST be written in the form shown in class and are worth 75 points each. Late papers/assignments are penalized by 50%.

6. Textbook and Materials: You are to have the Standard Works with you at each class. The New Testament Student Manual 211-212, "The Life and Teachings of Jesus & His Apostles", is highly recommended, but not required.
