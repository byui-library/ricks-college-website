In addition to online testing, you can download Mastery Learning Grammar (MLG).

Each drill has approximately 200 questions in the database, and each quiz presents a random selection from the pool of questions.  

A teaching response shows if the question is missed, thus speeding up the learning process.  Plus, a report card keeps a record of your most recent scores.

MLG drills the 200 vocabulary words and the 160 affixes.  MLG also provides tutorials for each category of study.  These tutorials teach what is needed in order to do well on the quizzes.
