Electronic Funds Transfer

Through Electronic Funds Transfer or (EFT), you can authorize your bank to automatically transfer a specified amount from your checking account to Ricks College on a monthly basis.

Gifts of $10, $25, $50, $100 or whatever you choose may be easier for your monthly budget than one, large lump sum, but it adds up to considerable support over time.
