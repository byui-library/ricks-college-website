WHO MAY ENROLL: Persons who have graduated from high school or who are nineteen years of age or older may enroll for credit. High school seniors may also enroll for credit with prior approval from Continuing Education officials and their high school principal.

TRANSFERRING CREDIT: Continuing Education is not responsible for determining the applicability of any course or credit toward a degree, certification, or transferring to another institution. This is the student's responsibility. Lower division courses, Independent Study, and 514R courses cannot be applied toward a graduate degree at BYU. Please refer to the current BYU or Ricks College catalog for additional information.

SCHOLARSHIPS: Scholarships are available to Continuing Education students registering for BYU or Ricks College credit at Idaho Falls or Rexburg. To apply, contact either the Division of Continuing Education Ricks College, 356-1040, or the BYU-Ricks Center at Idaho Falls, 523-4682.

Application deadline is Tuesday, August 15 for Fall Semester 2000.

CAREER GUIDANCE AND COUNSELING SERVICES: Career counseling services are available to all Ricks College students and community patrons who are considering further education. This service is free of charge and can be provided at the Ricks College Counseling Center, 356-1167. Call for an appointment.

INEEL EMPLOYEES: Employees desiring tuition reimbursement should consult with their employer.

INDEPENDENT STUDY COURSES FOR INEEL EMPLOYEES: Tuition payment policies governing regular courses have now been extended to BYU Independent Study classes by several INEEL companies. Check with your company to determine eligibility. Catalogs are available at no charge at the BYU-Ricks Center in Idaho Falls, 523-4682 or Continuing Education in Rexburg, 356-1040.

BENEFIT FOR SENIOR CITIZENS: Senior Citizens 65 years or older may audit Ricks College evening classes and classes offered through the BYU-Ricks Center in Idaho Falls. Cost is $20 per credit. Register at the Division of Continuing Education in Rexburg, or BYU-Ricks Center in Idaho Falls.
