Mystic

The ship they built is, unexpectedly, magically, in port

I saw rib and beam, plank and joint

now I see mast, spar, rope, gild and keel.

I launch this image in two dimensions.

I engage and see through these lines.

 

The nine year old girl is sent alone of her family

from the old world because she has most chance

of admission, survival.

Lying on the brink of entry to the transcendent world,

she drew freedom, the welcoming woman

of gold and green.

 

The family has the drawing somewhere.

She was said to be the captain’s favorite,

his vision of hope, embodiment of

and alternative to his long view

of the ever rolling sea.
