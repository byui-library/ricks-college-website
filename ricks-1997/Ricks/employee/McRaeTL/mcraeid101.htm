COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics
Course#: ID 101
Title: Design Basics Credit: 3
 Text: Art Fundamentals, Ocvirk

Course Objective:
 To define the elements and principles of two dimensional design.  To introduce the student to the concept of research in the design process and problem solution.  Expose the student to a criteria for judging good and poor design.  Expose the student to oral and visual presentation techniques as a means of “selling” good design.  To introduce ergonomics as a design consideration.  To expose the student to the creative design process and require the student to use this process in the development of assigned projects.  To evaluate the student’s understanding of information presented by application of design fundamentals to projects, visual media, and by written analysis and exam.  To introduce the student to the use of graphic presentation as a means of communication.
 
Description:
An introduction to the elements and principles of design; their use and meaning in the environment; development of qualitative judgement; emphasis on two-dimensional design and creative problem solution.
 
Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Each student will complete 3 written assignments as outlined in the Course Outline and the individual assignment sheets.  Each student will complete two design analysis projects for formal classroom presentation as outlined in the individual assignment sheets.  Each student will complete 7 “hands-on” projects as outlined in the individual assignment sheets.  Each student will organize a design file as explained in the individual assignment sheet.  Each student will be required to take three exams to test their comprehension and understanding of information presented.  Each student will be required to complete a final slide presentation.  Each student will be required to dress professionally when making formal presentations in the classroom.
