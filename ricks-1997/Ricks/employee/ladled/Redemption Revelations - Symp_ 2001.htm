7. Development of the Doctrine and Ordinances of the Temple from 1842 - 1894:

- The endowment was revealed to Joseph in the Nauvoo era, and introduced to a few trusted latter-day saints in the upper room of the red brick store on 4 May 1842. He had arranged the office and assembly room to represent the "interior of a temple as much as the circumstances would permit." CES manual, p. 254.

- Shortly after the introduction of the endowment, the prophet revealed that a married couple could be sealed together by the power of the priesthood for time and all eternity. Many of the people endowed were also sealed by the prophet. These ordinances were for the temple and would be available to the greater church membership as soon as the temple was completed. (CES Manual, p. 25

- DC 131, May 16-17, 1843 Highest order of the Celestial Kingdom requires temple marriage.

- DC 132, July 12, 1843 Revelation on Celestial Marriage and Plural Marriage written down by the prophet which had been revealed to him as early as 1831.

- December, 1845, Temple dedicated and the Saints begin to receive their temple ordinances.
 

8. The Doctrine of Adoption or Sealing Children to Parents.

- The biological family of God is linked from God to Adam to the last human to be born at the end of the millennium. That linkage gives them certain rights and privileges (i.e. potential to become a God, etc.)

- The future Celestial family of God must be also organized and linked to God and Adam through temple ordinances and covenants. We are building that organization through priesthood ordinances in the temple. That organization will consist of couples, sealed together, who are likewise linked to others in a great priesthood organization of the Family of God.

* Through our personal and proxy temple work, we are uniting everyone together by ordinance and covenant, but whether or not individuals qualify for this right to receive exaltation and live as God lives will depend upon righteousness in keeping the covenants of the temple.

* Those Who Are Born in the Covenant

-George Q. Cannon, Excerpts from CR, April 8, 1894.

"We have been taught...that the children begotten in these [temple] marriages are born, as we phrase it, in the covenant, that is, they are recognized by the Lord as legitimate children of the covenant....In this way the Latter-day Saints are being bound together in the new and everlasting covenant...and the children, the offspring of these marriages, being born in the covenant, under the blessings and the recognition by the Almighty of the bond that exists between their parents."

"They are legitimate heirs of the Priesthood and the blessings....But not so with those who have been born outside of this covenant. There has to be some ordinance performed in order to make them legitimate...we call sealing."

"We must all be united to our father Adam; that there must be a bond of union between us and our great parent; and that that will be done through the ordinance of adoption [sealing] where children are not born in the covenant."

"It is the duty of every man to be sealed to his father, where his father is not a man that has proved entirely unworthy. And when such a case arises--which will be very seldom--we have the man in our midst who has the keys of the Priesthood and unto whom this question can be submitted for him to decide what should be done."
 

9. The Practice of Adoption or Sealing Children to Parents.

- Source: George Irving, "The Law of Adoption: One Phase of he Development of the Mormon Concept of Salvation, 1830-1900. BYU Studies, Vol. 14--1973-74, Number 3, Spring 1974.

- Since most church members of the church at this time were first generation converts, the idea of being sealed to non-member parents caused a concern: If my parents don't accept the gospel in the spirit world, I could be left out of the linkage in the family of God.

- To avoid this gamble, it became the practice for individuals to be adopted by (or sealed to) church leaders.

- There is no consensus with regard to the date when the first adoptions (sealings) were performed; it is certainly possible, perhaps probable, that Joseph Smith did initiate certain trusted leaders into the adoptionary order as early as 1842.

- During the few months (Dec 1945-Feb. 1946) when temple ordinances were being given to some of the saints in the completed Nauvoo Temple, the forced departure of the Church leaders from Nauvoo prevented the general membership from being adopted or having their children sealed to them.

- Details of the Adoption practice: page 4.

- In theory, the importance of Adoption lay in the validation of one's sonship in the family of God.

- From 1846 to 1848, the practice of Adoption was important to the Church as a social experiment, but for various reasons, it did not achieve the goal of bringing the people together in this spiritual family life as they made their new lives in Mormon communities n the West.

- From 1848 to 1877, the practice of Adoption was abandoned as a social experiment. No temple was yet standing among the Saints and the ordinance of sealing of children to parents could not be solemnized in the Endowment House since it was an "advanced ordinance" of the priesthood. Therefore, until the dedication of the St. George Temple in January 1877, adoptions or sealings were not being performed.

- With the dedication of the three Utah temples, beginning with the St. George temple in 1877, adoptions or sealings were again performed.

- Certain guidelines governed the practice of Adoptions at this time. (p. 12.) The most interesting of these guidelines was that adoptions could be performed back only one generation for dead relatives. These adopted grandparents were often adopted into the same family (i.e. church leader's family) as the members performing the work, so that they could all be in the same family.

12. Missionary Work In The Spirit World.

- Lorenzo Snow, Far Greater Success Preaching In The Spirit World.
I believe . . . that when the gospel is preached to the spirits in prison, the success attending that preaching will be far greater than that attending the preaching of our elders in this life. I believe there will be very few indeed of those spirits who will not gladly receive the gospel when it is carried to them. The circumstances there will be a thousand times more favorable.
* Lorenzo Snow, Millennial Star 56:60
 

- Joseph F. Smith, Family Teaching Family In The Spirit World
As the primary responsibility for the teaching of the gospel rests with the family here, so it rests with the family there, the difference being that in the world of the spirits there is a greatly extended family. In large measure it will be the responsibility of husband and wife to search out their own progenitors and teach them the gospel. As the primary responsibility for ordinance work rests with the family here, so it will be with the teaching of the gospel there.
*Joseph F. Smith, JD, Vol .1, p. 12.
 

- Wilford Woodruff, Some of Your Own Seed Are Needed in the Spirit World
After losing a son in a drowning accident, Wilford Woodruff learned the following: "I asked the Lord why he was taken from me. The answer was, 'You are doing a great deal for the redemption of your dead; but the law of redemption requires some of your own seed in the spirit world to attend to work connected with this.' That was a new principle to me; but it satisfied me why he was taken away."
* Wilford Woodruff, Millennium Star, Vol. 58, p. 742.
 

- Melvin J. Ballard, Fathers in the Spirit World Help their Children Receive the Gospel
" Why is it that sometimes only one of a city or household received the Gospel? It was made known to me that it is because of the righteous dead who had received the Gospel in the spirit world exercising themselves, and in answer to their prayers, elders of the Church were sent to the home of their posterity that the Gospel might be taught to them and through their righteous-ness they might be privileged to have a descendant in the flesh do the work for their dead kindred. I want to say to you that it is with greater intensity that the hearts of the fathers and mothers in the spirit world are turned to their children than that our hearts are turned to them. ...the spirit and influence of your dead will guide those who are interested in finding those records. If there is anywhere on the earth anything concerning them, you will find it."
Sermons and Missionary Services of Melvin J. Ballard, p. 249
 

- Bruce R. McConkie, Leaving the Spirit Prison of Ignorance and Darkness.

As rapidly as they can overcome these obstacles--gain light, believe truth, acquire intelligence, cast off sin, and break the chains of hell--they can leave the hell that imprisons them and dwell with the righteous in the peace of paradise.
