PRIORITIES 2000-2005

At Ricks College there is a deep, penetrating commitment to learning—academically and spiritually. And even though there is an almost epic history associated with Ricks College, everyone seems to sense there is also a bright, even more important future.

As we turn to the future, our challenge is to prepare for the opportunities that the twenty-first century will surely bring. In the process of the world becoming a global community, there will be unforeseen challenges. Along with new technological developments, communications, better travel, and improved medical treatments, there will come new educational challenges and opportunities.

To prepare for these unprecedented changes, while still meeting the basic needs of our students, the administration and the Board of Trustees have identified four basic areas of focus for  philanthropic support in the period immediately ahead. They are:

  1. Expand the doors of educational opportunity for deserving and needy students
  2. Enhance the academic environment
  3. Support selected building and beautification projects
  4. Extend the influence of Ricks College

The assistance we enjoy from you -- our alumni and friends -- will help us sustain the hopes and dreams of generations of students yet to come. Your generosity will help Ricks College accomplish its kingdom building role by advancing our fund-raising goals in preparation for the twenty-first century.

We want . . . to stretch the souls of the students who come [to Ricks College], both spiritually and academically, so they can be increased in their appreciation of the great blessings of life which our Father in Heaven has bestowed upon us all and at the same time be increased in appreciation of their own talents and abilities with which that same God has endowed them.

President Marion G. Romney, 1978
