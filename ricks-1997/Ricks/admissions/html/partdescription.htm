DESCRIPTION OF PARTS OF ON-LINE APPLICATION

Following is a list of the various parts of the application which need to be submitted.  Please note that parts 3, 4 and 5 need to be submitted by someone else on your behalf.  Please allow sufficient time before the deadlines for those parts to be received by the Admissions Office.  

Part 1: Student Information

This section asks about your biographical information, such as name, high school, birth date, etc.  

Part 2: School Selection and Scholarships

In this section you will need to provide information about when you want to attend and which scholarships, if any, you are interested in.  

Part 3: Student Commitment and Confidential Report

You will commit to abide by the Honor Code and Dress and Grooming Standards in this section.  This section must be submitted in hard copy form by your ecclesiastical leader. 

Part 4: Seminary/Institute Recommendation

You must provide this form to your seminary/institute instructor who will then complete and submit the form on your behalf.  

Part 5: High School Information and Courses

You are required to complete most of this form.  You will then need to give it to your high school counselor and he or she will submit it with a copy of your high school transcripts.  

Part 6: Extracurricular Activities

We ask you about your extracurricular-activities which have been categorized into ten different sections.  

Part 7: Student Essays

We ask that you write two essays so we can get to know you better.  We suggest you write your essays in a word processor and then copy and paste them into the appropriate place in the application.  

Please note that the maximum length of the essays is 200 words.  

The topics for the two essays are:

  • What additional information regarding your qualifications would you like the Admissions Committee to consider?

  • Describe a significant experience in your life that has strengthened your character.  

Part 8: Confidential Statement of Finances

This section is to be completed by international students only. 

Application Fee

A non-refundable $25 application fee (payable in US dollars) is required.  The fee can be submitted on-line using a credit card or by check or money order.
