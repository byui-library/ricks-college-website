Class
Preparation 		Brigham Young said, "We are prepared for some things, and we receive just as fast as we prepare ourselves.” You are expected to read assigned material prior to class.
Homework Homework assignments allow you to demonstrate that you have mastered the implementation details of what we discuss in class. Point values for each homework assignment vary based on the perceived difficulty of the assignment. Programming assignments shall compile and execute using the Linux systems in ROM 272. Late homework is penalized 10% the first school day, 30% the second school day, and 50% the third school day. Homework is not accepted after three school days late or after the CS computer labs close for finals, whichever comes first. It is your responsibility to submit homework correctly, including, but not limited to, the correct location, the correct format, and the correct file name. Incorrect homework submissions are subject to late penalties, including not being accepted. You may not resubmit homework that has already been graded. The final homework grade is the ratio of total earned points and total possible points.
Tests There shall be a midterm and a final examination. These tests allow you to demonstrate that you have mastered the concepts discussed in class. The midterm shall cover object oriented design and shall be administered in the testing center. The final shall cover object oriented programming using Java and shall be administered in ROM 276. Late tests shall not be accepted; plan accordingly.

Mercy 		Homework assignments and tests are to be completed as scheduled. Do not procrastinate! (See Alma 34:33.) Otherwise, justice prevails. There will be no routine extensions of due dates.
