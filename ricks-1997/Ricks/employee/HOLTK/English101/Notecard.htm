ASSIGNMENT

Read the assigned material. Remember that active reading includes a preview, accessing prior knowledge, and correct marking techniques. To help you prepare for a student discussion covering the assigned reading, prepare a 5 x 7 index card. You will share your note card in class with a partner, small group, and/or the entire class. Staple this sheet to your card and bring it to class on the due date.

GUIDELINES

On the card you are required to record at least five complete thoughts, ideas, reflections, or ideas from the reading you would be willing to share in class. Your statements cannot be repetitions of what is already stated in the reading. For full credit, you must reflect upon what you have read. You might consider including on your card:

/ Statements linking the reading to your personal philosophy of life.

/ Ideas in the reading that are tied to previously learned material.

/ Descriptions of how the practices in the reading will be used in your future.

/ Opinions on ideas presented in the reading.

/ Reflections on experiences with any of the ideas contained in the readings.

Your card does not need to be typed, but should be neatly written and the handwriting legible. Clearly number each statement. Do not use more space than the front and back of the card.

SCORING

Each statement on your card is worth five points. Points will be awarded based on the following criteria:

5 pts. Sentence is a strong main idea statement that expresses an opinion, comment or point of view about the reading. Statement demonstrates a clear understanding of the reading and reflects a personal application, i.e. opinion, thought, reflection, or idea. Details are provided to explain and support the main idea statement.

4 pts. Sentence is awkward and fails to make a personal connection with the reading. Details provided do not support the statement.

3 pts. Sentence is unclear, shows a lack of comprehension of the reading and no details are provided for support.

2 pt. Sentence is incomplete; no clear point is expressed. Sentence is a restatement from reading.
