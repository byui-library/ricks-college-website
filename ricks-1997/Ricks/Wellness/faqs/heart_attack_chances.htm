Can exercise reduce my chances of getting a heart attack?

Coronary artery disease is the major cause of heart attacks in America. It develops when fatty deposits build up on the inner walls of the blood vessels feeding the heart (coronary arteries). Eventually, one of the major coronary arteries may become blocked- either by buildup of deposits or by a blood clot forming in the artery's narrow passageway. The result is a heart attack.

We know that there are risks for developing coronary artery heart disease- and thus the chances of a heart attack. Fortunately, many of these risks factors can be reduced or eliminated.

The most important of these risk s factors.

  • Cigarette smoking, High blood pressure, High blood cholesterol
  • Diabetes
  • Overweight
  • Low levels of HDL's.
  • Physical inactivity
