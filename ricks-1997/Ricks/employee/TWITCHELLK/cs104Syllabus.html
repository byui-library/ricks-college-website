CS104 - Ethics I
Syllabus

Survey of issues regarding ethics in Computer Science.

This is the first in a series of four Computer Science ethics courses. The objective of the series of courses is to increase awareness of the Computer Science major regarding ethics associated with

  • computing
  • peers
  • the profession
  • society in general

After completing this course, the student should understand that he/she will be making ethical decisions throughout his/her career. The student should also realize that frequently there is no clear-cut, simple answer to an ethical dilemma.

Topics

  • Contribute to society and human well-being
  • Avoid harm to others
  • Be honest and trustworthy
  • Be fair and take action not to discriminate
  • Honor property rights including copyrights and patent
  • Give proper credit for intellectual property
  • Respect the privacy of others
  • Honor confidentiality

Prerequisite(s)

  • none

Text(s)

  • ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct at http://www.acm.org/constitution/code.html
