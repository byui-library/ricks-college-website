 The purpose of this course is to enrich and increase your exposure to different design influences.  It is an indepth investigation into the basis of oriental cultural influences in floral design theory.  The class includes discussion and application of the historical and contemporary techniques learned particularly from studying the Japanese schools of Ikebana.

NOTEBOOK:
Each student is required to keep a notebook containing information presented in class, along with sketches, drawings, and ideas that arise from class discussions.  An example of a notebook will be shown on the first day of class.  You should create
your own notebook to express your personality and interpretations appropriately.

The following topics will be discussed during the course:

Section 1          Introduction--oriental influence
   Japanese design schools
    Ikenobu
    Sogetsu
    Ohara
   Principles of oriental design
    observations of nature

Section 2          Styles, influences, and language
   Rikka
   Nageire & Seika
   Moribana
   Contemporary styles:
   Jiyu-bana, Zenei-bana

Section 3                              Practical applications
   Shaping exercise
   Discussion and practice
   Contemplation
   Influences in western design

Section 4                                Final design project
