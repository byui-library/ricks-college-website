COURSE DESCRIPTION

English 107, College Reading, teaches the learning strategies and reading skills necessary to read college texts. The course emphasizes vocabulary, comprehension, and fluency in oral reading, and concentrates on reading and thinking skills through group tutoring. An A or B in this class is an indication you may be ready to take E-111 Fall Semester and E-211 Spring Semester (you must have 22 credits to take E-211). All CSS students are encouraged to continued their preparation by taking E-101 Fall Semester.

 

TEXT and MATERIALS

Self-Discovery through College Reading, and All I Ever Needed to Know I Learned in Kindergarten by Robert Fulghum can be purchased at the Bookstore. The Learning Guide for Self-Discovery, a packet of handouts to accompany Self-Discovery, must be purchased at Zippy Ship-N-Copy, 20 West 100 South, Rexburg. Materials needed for class include: a wireless notebook, one package 3x5 cards, and a double-sided, high density, computer disk. The first week of class you will be assigned a novel to read. DO NOT buy a novel at the Bookstore until assigned by your instructor.

 

 

ATTENDANCE

You will meet in class and with a tutor 24 times. Three points are earned for every class session, and 6 points for each tutor session (3 points preparation/3 points participation) for a total of 9 points daily.

 

 

ASSIGNMENTS

Homework and reading assignments are given daily. The preparation points are determined by your tutor and checked each tutoring session.

1. Homework Assignments written in your Reading Journal (100 points).

2. Frontier Word Cards (1 point each/maximum 30 points).

3. Booktalk (25 points).

4. Maps (25 points each).

5. I-Search (50 points).

6. Group movie/essay presentation (60 points).

7. Group Novel Portfolio (100 points).

 

TESTS

1. Classroom Preparation Quizzes (5 points each).

2. Main idea quiz (50 points).

3. Psychology Exam (100 points).

4. Phonics Review (70 points).

5. Take-home final (100 points).

 

GRADES

Grades will be determined on a strict percentage basis: 90-100%=A, 80-89%=B, 70-79%=C, 65-69%=C-, 55-64%=D, and below 55%=F. Your grade will be determined from your attendance, homework, participate/preparation score, exams, Reading Journal, Library I-Search, Novel Portfolio, reading posttest improvement, and an evaluation from your tutor. You should score around 10th grade reading level on the Woodcock Posttest to earn an A grade in the class. Late work will only be accepted for 24 hours after the class it is due, at a 50% deduction. If you have any questions on your points or scores, discuss them with the instructor, not your tutor.
