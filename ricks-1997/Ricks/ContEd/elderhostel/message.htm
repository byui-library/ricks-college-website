Greetings! 

Welcome to the Ricks College Elderhostel programs. Ricks College has been involved with the Elderhostel organization since 1986 and currently offers a wide variety of programs from which to choose. Our goal is to provide a highly educational experience that is reasonably priced and filled with a sense of adventure. It does not matter if your desire is to attend a program on campus, in Yellowstone National Park, Sun Valley, Idaho, a back country adventure (canoeing and llama packing) or one of our Intergenerational weeks designed for both grandparents and grandchildren--we have the program for you. We hope you will take a minute to read through our various selections. 

Our on-campus housing facilities are designed to house one couple or 2-3 singles per apartment. Each single participant would have his or her own bedroom. Each apartment contains a private bathroom and a living room to relax, read or visit. The programs held in West Yellowstone, Stanley, and Sun Valley are housed in modern, comfortable motels. A more complete description is contained in our packets sent to each enrollee.
