Although capitalization rules exist, they are sometimes arbitrary.  However, the rules which follow are generally agreed upon. If you are in doubt about a word and if you can not check your style sheet, use the lowercase letter.  Be conservative on the side of not capitalizing words.  
 
1.  For a title or chapter heading, capitalize the first and last words and all other words except articles, prepositions, and coordinating conjunctions.
Book title:  Letters from the Earth  
Essay title: "A Tale of Two Moralities"
Chapter Heading:  The Conditions Essential to Human Happiness
 
2.  Proper names and adjectives formed from proper names
Russia                                 Russian
England                               English
Orient                                  Oriental
 
3.  References to Deity, names of holy books, and specific religions
Mormon                              Catholic
Book of Mormon                  Koran
Apollo                                 Zeus
Christ                                  God the Father
the Godhead                        the Holy Ghost
 
4.  Titles of relatives but not when used with my, his, her, your, etc.
Grandfather Swenson            my grandfather
Give it to Mother.                   her mother
Aunt Thelma                         your aunt
5.  Titles of officials
Bishop Ronald K. Messer       the bishop
President Brent Strong           the stake president
Captain Jones                        the captain
Mayor Porter                          the mayor
 
6.  Days of the week and months but not seasons
Monday                                  fall
April                                       summer
 
7.  Streets, avenues, parks, rivers, mountains, cities, states,  provinces, nations, continents, oceans, lakes, and specific  geographical regions but not directions
Steiner Avenue                      Utah
Porter Park                            the Snake River
the Teton Peaks                     Provo
British Columbia                     Africa
Henrys Lake                          the Pacific Ocean
the West Coast                      the Midwest
the West                                west (a direction)
 
8.  Buildings
Smith Building                        the Snow Fine Arts Center
the First Ward                        the Stake Center
the Romance Theater              the Empire State Building
 
9.  Private organizations
the Lions Club                        Kiwanis
the Booster Club                     the American Legion
 
10.  Government organizations
Congress                               Supreme Court
the Senate                             the Cabinet
the Navy                                the Air Force
the Peace Corps                    Farm Home Administration  
11.  Historical documen ts, events, and periods
the Bill of Rights                    the Constitution
Korean War                           the Renaissance
 
12.  Specific school courses
English 111C                        Group Guitar 109
Music 109                             History 170
 
13.  Brand names but not the names of products
a Ford truck                        Goodyear tires
Tide soap                            Old Spice deodorant
Martin guitars                      an IBM computer
 
 
Capital Practice
 
Study the following sentences and make the appropriate corrections on a piece of paper.  Change the lower-case letters to upper-case when they are needed.  Click here to see the answers.

1.  After talking to Mr. miller, we decided to take organic chemistry 252 in addition to a course in biology.
 
2.  We drove to the food center to buy some grapes.
 
3.  One block away from college avenue is the holiday theater.
 
4.  Being a member of the united states air force is a good deal for most students.
 
5.  To cure her malaria, dr. packer prescribed dolin tablets, made by the pfizer company.
 
6.  I take history 170 in the smith building, and then i eat lunch in the manwaring center.
 
7.  My mother is a mormon, but father is a member of the catholic church.
 
8.  We live at the somerset apartments which are west of the physical plant building.
 
9.  Sue favors platonic philosophy because it is non materialistic.
 
10.  A course in freshman composition can help you succeed in introduction to literature 220.
 
11.  In the orient there are 900 million buddhists and 100 million taoists.
 
12.  Henry's lake is located in the western region of the united states.
 
13.  After you study history with brother coates, you may agree that the bill of rights is the most important part of the constitution.
 
14.  If you want to walk on water, try the great salt lake; if you want to water ski, try willard bay.
 
15.  In the second world war the germans were allied with the Italians.
 
16.  The book we use for a text is entitled, mastery learning: composition.
 
17.  Part of a poem by william wordsworth, intimations of immortality, is used in the text.
 
18.  The relief society is an organization that complements the melchizedek priesthood.
