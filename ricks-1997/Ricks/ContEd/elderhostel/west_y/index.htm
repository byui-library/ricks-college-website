Perfectly situated next to Yellowstone National Park, West Yellowstone (6,600 elevation) is the ideal Elderhostel location. Capture the experience of the Park with several field trips to view wildlife, thermal activity, and geological features. Enjoy Rocky Mountain wilderness forests in the small alpine resort community nestled in the pines. The resort area, in the borders of Idaho, Montana and Wyoming is 90 miles northeast of Ricks College. Lodging, meals and classrooms are in a comfortable motel with double occupancy, private both, limited singles for extra charge-- all just minutes from field study locations for courses and wildlife viewing. No alcohol permitted. For PHYSICALLY ACTIVE HOSTELERS.
