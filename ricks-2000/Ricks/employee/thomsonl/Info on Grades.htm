INFORMATION ABOUT GRADES

  1. Your attendance in class is important each day.  Daily quizzes will be given to check roll and test your knowledge of the reading assignment.  You will not have the opportunity to make up missed quizzes.  If you are absent due to an assignment from another college class or illness, your missed quiz score will be determined from the average of your other quizzes later on in the semester.  Please don't come to class when you are sick and expose the others to what you have.  A good night's sleep, balanced meals, and a strict study schedule will make you "healthy, wise, and eventually wealthy."  Going home early on Friday or not returning on time Monday is NOT an excuse for make up quiz adjustment.
  2. Missing an exam because you are not ready is NOT an acceptable reason.  If you are having trouble with the course, see the instructor before the exam - not afterwards.  Make up exams are reduced by 10% of your score each day after the test has been given unless you have a doctor's care or are on a college assignment in another class.  If a college assignment in another class requires that you miss an exam, then you should make arrangements with the instructor to take the exam prior to being gone.  Missed tests must be made up within TWO days after returning to school.
  3. In addition to the daily quizzes, 4 or 5 100 point exams plus a final will be given.  No quizzes or exam scores will be dropped except as announced by the instructor.  A schedule of the course will be handed out.  The scores and grades will be printed periodically by SSN and posted.  A standard score has been set for each grade level with a grade of 'C' being average.
  4. It is your responsibility to keep track of your own quiz and exam scores in case an error is made so you can show the error was made.  During the course, several lab and homework assignments will be assigned.  These will NOT BE ACCEPTED AFTER THE DUE DATE.  You are encouraged to get them in early.  All assignments will be turned in at the proper box marked PH.S 137 or PH.S 100 for the instructor in Romney 126.  NO assignments will be accepted in class or at the instructor's office.
  5. "Should I read the text or just take notes?"  Test questions come from the reading, lectures, labs, and homework.  You are expected to obtain most of your learning from your own reading and studying.  The instructor will spend class time expounding on the more difficult concepts and answering questions.  If there is some concept that is not covered in class that you do not understand, please ask about it in class (for your classmates benefit also), or see the instructor in his office.  Office hours are posted outside your instructor's door.
  6. The important thing to remember is that "YOU EARN YOUR GRADE EACH DAY YOU COME TO CLASS".  It is difficult to improve your grade as the semester approaches the end.  "Extra credit projects" such as writing a report at the end of the semester just to improve your grade is not possible.  This is not fair to those who turn their work in on time daily.  There are tutors provided in the CAC in the administration building 2nd floor.  Many students do well until the last week or two before the semester ends, then they get tired of the course and give up the final week.  I want you to succeed.  Please come in and get help when you need it and do not wait until after a problem develops.  Many students fear science, but if you come to class every day, take all the quizzes and exams, hand in all the labs and homework assignments, then at the least a passing grade is guaranteed.
  7. Science is exciting.  It can strengthen your testimony of a Divine Creator, as well as help prepare you for work in a highly technological and changing world.
