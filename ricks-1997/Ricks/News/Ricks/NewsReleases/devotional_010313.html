Ricks College News Release
March 13, 2001

Devotional: Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf

By Trent Toone
Ricks College News Bureau

REXBURG, Idaho — Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf, a member of the First Quorum of the Seventy of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, spoke about the transitions of earthly life Tuesday at the weekly Ricks College devotional.

"You are privileged to be part of this historic transition (of Ricks to BYU-Idaho)," he said. "As we look at our individual lives, aren’t we all in a transition phase during our earthly existence?"

Elder Uchtdorf, who was introduced by his wife Harriet, talked about life as a transition from the presence of Heavenly Father to new phases of life such as birth, death and resurrection.

"Let us be aware of this great transition in which we are engaged throughout our whole lives," he said. "Transition is healthy."

Elder Uchtdorf shared experiences of his youth as he grew up in East Germany and West Germany, and how he struggled in school with learning foreign languages. Even though he didn’t want to learn English, he was motivated to achieve because it was necessary for him to fulfill his dream of becoming an airline pilot.

"I was able to learn English. Why? Because of a strong motive," he said. "Our motives and thoughts ultimately influence our actions. . . . If we apply these principles to our lives, with a desire to succeed in this great and marvelous transition and make significant contributions to the world, we will have right, true, proper and pure motives."

He taught that a major part of our earthly transition would be a testimony.

"This testimony, the firm belief and assurance, the feeling in your heart will be a solid foundation, an overarching motive in all you do and accomplish in your lives," Elder Uchtdorf said. "It will be your true and faithful companion during good and challenging times in your lifelong transition. Your testimony will be the strongest motive to select the path and direction in your education and vocational endeavors. Your testimony will even help you . . . in the transition from single to married (life)."

Elder Uchtdorf concluded by sharing his "See’s," intended to help one pass through this earthly transition and lift oneself to higher grounds of academic and spiritual achievement.

"See who you are. See your supernatural roots. See the purpose of your being. See your potential. See the divine role of the Savior and his atonement. See and follow the living prophet. See your wonderful and exciting future," he said.

Next Tuesday’s speaker will be Dale Murphy, a recently released president of the Massachusetts Boston Mission and former professional baseball player. He will speak at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Devotionals are broadcast live and repeated at 9 p.m. on KRIC-FM.
