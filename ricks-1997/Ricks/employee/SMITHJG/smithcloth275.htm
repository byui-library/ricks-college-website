COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 275
Title: Evening & Bridal Wear Credit: 3
Text:     Bridal Couture

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire the knowledge and skills necessary to design, fit and construct Bridal Apparel.  Upon completion of the course the students should be able to:

  1.  Demonstrate skills in designing bridal patterns
  2.  Apply specific fitting principles (computerized & manually)
  3.  Derive solution for problems which occur with lace and bridal fabrics
  4.  Construct a bridal dress using appropriate techniques
  5.  Construct a bridal head piece using correct techniques

Description:
A construction course for evening and bridal gowns and fabrics. Speciality fabrics and lace techniques are emphasized.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Bridal or evening Gown Designed and Constructed (with appropriate boards and sketches)
2.  Head Piece
