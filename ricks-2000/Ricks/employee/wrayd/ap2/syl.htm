COURSE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES

This course will help each student to:

* have fun and learn a lot!!!

* gain interest in and appreciation for human anatomy and physiology,

* understand the functions and mechanisms of the special senses

* understand the structure and function of the endocrine, cardiovascular, lymphatic, respiratory, digestive, urinary, and reproductive systems
