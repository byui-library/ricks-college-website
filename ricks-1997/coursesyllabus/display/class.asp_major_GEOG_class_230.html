Introduction to Geographic Information Systems
GEOG INFO SYS
CLASS CODE: GEOG 230 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: RELIGION AND SOCIAL SCIENCE
DEPARTMENT: HISTORY, GEOGRAPHY, POLITICAL SCIENCE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This introductory course is designed to provide a general overview of Geographic Information Systems.
DESCRIPTION: A general overview of Geographic Information Systems including background, development, trends and prospects for the use of the rapidly developing technology in a variety of professions and academic fields.
TOPICS: Basic components, analytical functions of GIS, spatial and geographic concepts, cartographic communication, automated mapping characteristics, map projections and map scale, geocoding, coordinate systems, and nature of spatial area.
