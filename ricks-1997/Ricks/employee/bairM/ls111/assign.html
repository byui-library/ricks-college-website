4. CATALOG / ARRANGEMENT ASSIGNMENT 50 points

1. Use the Library of Congress, Subject Headings or Sears List of Subject Headings or Library of Congress catalog to find 5 terms or subdivisions for a field of study.

2. Look up one or more terms  in our catalog using the subject search.
    Describe: Author, Title, Place of publication, Publisher, Date, Call # (5 point description)
    Find the material on the shelf or in its collection.
    Find information in the material using the table of contents, index or other tools.
    Write a note or copy a quote from this source. Give page number. (5 points information)

3. Look up an organization or individual author using the author search of the catalog.
    Describe: Author, Title, Place of publication, Publisher, Date, Call # (5 points)
    Find the material. Use the table of contents or index to find information.
    Write a note or copy a quote from this source. Give page number. (5 points information)

4. Find a popular magazine (periodical) title using the title searches of the catalog.
    What is the latest issue of the periodical?
    Find an  issue of the periodical.
    Look at the table of contents and choose one article:
    Describe: Author, "Title of article," Periodical, volume (date): pages. (5 point)
    Use italics or underline for periodical title.
    Write a note or copy a quote from this source. (5 points information)

5. Browse the call number found using one of the above searches. 
[A call number search is available only inside library]
    Describe: Author, Title, Place of publication, Publisher, Date, Call # (5 point description)
    In the actual item use the table of contents or index to find information.
    Write a note or copy a quote from this source. Give page number. (5 points information)

1. Use an index by H.W. Wilson to find periodical articles on a topic from your field of study. 
        (a) Describe the index by title, date or volume, and page. (5 pt)
        (b) Copy the entry. Translate abbreviations. (5 pt)

  • Reader's Guide to Periodical Literature
  • Education Index
  • Social Science Index
  • Business Periodicals Index
  • General Science Index
  • Applied Science and Technology Index
  • Biological and Agricultural Index
  • Humanities Index
  • Art Index

            (a) Describe the index by title, date or volume, and page. (5 pt)

    Index to Periodicals of the Church of Jesus Christ of LDS...
