Organization
One way to find the connections in your essay material you find is to look for those ideas or themes that coordinate with one another--are of equal importance or significance--and those ideas or themes that are subordinate to others--are of lesser importance or serve to explain or develop a more important or significant theme.

Think of coordinating and subordinating ideas in terms of a family tree.

Notice that everyone on the tree is interconnected or related, but not all are interconnected on the same level. Some coordinate with each other (husbands and wives, brothers and sisters). They are of the same importance. Others are subordinate to each other (children to their parents, grandchildren to their grandparents). They are offshoots of a bigger branch--parts of a greater whole. Finding such relationships between the various ideas and bits of information you've gathered will help you better organize your essay. Look at how the family tree can be used to outline the hierarchy between different parts of the same topic.
