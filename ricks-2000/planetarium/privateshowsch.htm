Private Show Scheduling

The Planetarium may be scheduled for private shows by school groups, scout groups, family, church, and civic groups.  The Planetarium will comfortably hold 60 adults.  You may schedule a show Tuesday through Friday between 9:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.  Private showings cost a minimum of $25 (if your group has less than 25 students or 20 adults) or $1 per student and $1.25 for adults (if your group is larger).  Saturday shows may be scheduled on occasion for a minimum of $40.

To make your visit more enjoyable, please follow the steps below.

  • Look over the list of planetarium shows.  Choose one that best fits your group.
  • Choose two dates and times for your show (in case one time is already taken)
  • Call the Scheduling Office at (208) 356-2248 between 8 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.
  • You must schedule at least four days prior to the desired date.
  • You may schedule as much as three months in advance to guarantee your choice of date.
  • Please be aware that our staff has other obligations to fulfill--teaching, committees, classes, etc.
