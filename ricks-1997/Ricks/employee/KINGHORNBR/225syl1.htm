Daily Preparation: There is no substitute to being in class and letting the Holy Ghost testify of the things that we are discussing in class, especially when they coincide with what you have already prepared through your personal study. Each class period I will have you report to me the level of your preparation which will be broken down into the following categories: attendance, your scripture journal and assigned reading. For coming to class you earn five points, five points for your journal entry and ten more points if you read the Doctrine & Covenants assignment for a total of 20 points per day.

Journal: This scripture journal will provide you with an opportunity to record any of the impressions that you might receive while studying the scriptures. As you record your journal entries it would be good to record questions that you have, insights that you gain or doctrines that you don't understand and then refer to these in class as part of our discussion. I would recommend that you take your class notes in the journal as well.

Historical Papers: The Doctrine and Covenants is unique among our scripture because we know the history surrounding the revelations it contains. Since we won't spend much time on the historical stuff I would like you to pick one of the sections that we are studying for that week and explore its historical setting and then write a brief (1-2 page single spaced) paper on it. There will be one due roughly every other week and each will be worth 20 points.

Tests: I realize that some people do better than others on tests and so I am giving you the option to take both tests (worth 100 points) or to write a paper in place of the midterm test. The test option will be given in the testing center according to the schedule that is included on this syllabus and will be multiple choice, true/false and matching. All students will take the final which will be an essay based test. The paper will be due the day that the test closes and must be 4 pages in length and will be worth 100 points. I will provide information concerning this option in class.

Personal Project: This semester you will be completing a project that relates to the Doctrine and Covenants or the history surrounding it. You have a lot of freedom concerning the final product so long as it relates to the course. The following are some suggestions, but you are not limited to these. If you would like to express yourself in some other way (art, poetry, etc...)then you are welcome to, provided you clear it with me.

Doctrinal development paper

Who's who paper

My ancestor paper

Conference talk

Historical research paper

etc...

If you choose to do a paper I would expect that the length be at least three full pages (1.5 line spacing) and that you would make every effort to use the scriptures and the words of the prophets to develop the paper. All sources would need to be cited and full references given of course. My hope is that this would be something that you would not only learn from but that you would really enjoy doing. If you decide to do something other than a paper it would be expected that you would spend at least as much time doing it as a paper would require.
