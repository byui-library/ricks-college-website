Application for Student Clubs

The Teton Lodge facility is also available for officially sponsored ASBYU-I clubs and organizations for training and development purposes. Various days are available--Wednesday through Saturday--for student groups and organizations to use the lodge.

The lodge is not for entertainment or recreational activities. The lodge can only be used for training purposes that are consistent with the user’s mission and with the Institute’s mission statement. For example, a Lambda Delta Sigma president may wish to plan a retreat to train her officers for the coming school year, or an ASBYU-I service committee may wish to develop and train volunteers before a service project. The lodge is not available for club parties, dances, or firesides. Advisors must be present on the retreat at all times.

The facility can be used free of charge because of the generosity of its owner and other private donors. The Institute can help with transportation for student groups, if notified in advance. Groups must provide their own food. The Institute expects groups to leave the lodge as clean as they found it and bring back any trash they acquired while at the lodge.

To assist you in developing your retreat, the Institute has a resource library of training, team-building, and icebreaker ideas and activities. Institute staff members can also be of assistance when planning a retreat. Student leaders are trained and available to assist in conducting team-building and icebreaker activities on the retreat. The actual training of the group should be conducted by the advisor and/or club officers.

To schedule the lodge return the signed application to Van Crawford in MC 380 (+0790). Approval will be given on first-come, first-served basis. Upon approval, a letter of confirmation will be sent to the advisor listed on the application form with related information regarding transportation, resources, etc.
