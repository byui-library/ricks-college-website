Purpose

To more effectively prepare students to acquire the knowledge, skills, and experiences necessary to make an immediate contribution in the workplace and to rapidly grow into leadership positions in their organizations by have them manage an ongoing business.

Course / Program Descriptions

B450       3 Credits
Students fill assignments in student-run companies using innovative business models to address marketplace needs. Students will take roles in planning, organizing, leading and controlling the marketing, finance and operations of an actual ongoing business. Business models pursued will include utilization of the Internet, technology or innovative business practices to increase productivity and customer satisfaction. Student admitted to the class via application. Any BYU-Idaho student may apply.


B499C       3 Credits
A senior capstone experience wherein students act in leadership roles in student-run companies using innovative business models to address marketplace needs. (same as above) Except: Requires successful completion of B450 and instructor approval.

Principles / Guidelines

  • Focus on integration of disciplines within College of Business and Communications and beyond.
  • Create an environment for students to apply and to act.
  • Grading to focus more upon goals met and results vs. activity.
  • Marketplace to extend beyond campus.
  • Board of Directors comprised of faculty, staff, and outside professionals.
  • Profits used to expand the educational opportunities for students.
  • Focus on becoming self-funded.
  • Companies to operate within guidelines established by BYU-Idaho administration.
  • Become a natural sequence to a student’s educational experience.
  • Provide an opportunity for ongoing case study / projects to used by faculty for other classes.

Course Content

Preliminary Organization:  The class will form a preliminary organization/team structure for preparing the business plan/proposal and presentation.  This should be e-mailed to me by September 10th.

Business Plan/proposal and Presentation:  The class will prepare a business plan/proposal that will outline situation analysis, objectives, a work schedule, milestones, required resources, outputs, organization structure, etc.  This should be e-mailed to me and the board of directors by September of 24th.  The business plan should be formally presented to the board of directors on September the 26th or thereabout depending on the schedules of the board members and/or other stakeholders (the class will need to make these arrangements.).

Initial Performance Appraisals:  Managers need to have an interview with everyone who reports to him/her to outline expectations and goals.  A record of these interviews should be e-mailed to me by October 8th.

Value Added Reports:  Each person needs to e-mail to their manager a brief report of how they are adding value to the company.  These are due by the Friday at 10:00 each week beginning on October 11th (cc me).

Progress Report(s):  You will need to deliver at least one progress report via e-mail to the board of directors and stakeholders and me by November 21st.  The progress report should concisely and accurately detail how the company and is doing especially in regards to meeting its milestones.

Final Report and Presentation:  You will need to submit a final report (like an annual report) and a formal presentation of your semester's work on December 12th or thereabout depending on the board members schedule (you need to arrange this).  You should pay special attention to how well you did in meeting your goals.  Please include financial statements.   

Final Performance Appraisals:  Managers need to have an interview with everyone who reports to him/her to discuss that persons performance through the semester--especially how well they met their goals and added value to the company.  A record of this interview should be e-mailed to me by December 20th.

Peer Evaluations:  Members of each team should evaluate all the other member his/her team by December 20th.  I will provide some forms for doing this evaluation.
