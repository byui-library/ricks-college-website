COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 145
Title: Flat Pattern Design Credit: 2
Text: Pattern Making by the Flat-Pattern Method (Hollen)

COURSE OBJECTIVES:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire a working knowledge of designing by the flat-pattern method.  Upon completion of this course the student should be able to:
  1.  Demonstrate flat-pattern skills
  2.  Apply specific flat-pattern principles from fashion sketches and photos
  3.  Derive solutions to problems in flat-pattern

Description:
Techniques and principles of flat-pattern design, application in half and full size, and original design pattern principles

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  50 half scale patterns including bodice, skirts, pants, sleeves, dresses etc.
2.  2 complete garment articles designs and patterns
