Exams:

Six unit exams will be given in the testing center.  No make-up exams are allowed.  Each unit test is worth 100 points.  The final is comprehensive and mandatory.  If you don’t take it you automatically fail the course.  It is worth 200 points.  One half of it is over unit 7; the second half is a comprehensive review of material from previous chapters.  The final exam cannot be retaken and must be taken in class during the indicated time.

Case Analyses:

You will be required to write 2 brief case summaries, by analyzing subjects taken from film, according to two of the eight most influential models of psychopathology:  biogenic, psychoanalytic, humanistic, existential, behavioral, cognitive, family systems, and multicultural.  You are free to select from the list (which will be given to you later) and analyze according to any model you wish.  However, each character can only be analyzed from one viewpoint.  Therefore, two different characters can be examined.  Each case analysis is worth a maximum of 50 points.

Participation:

You will receive points through participating in the class.  The participation will take the form of individual and group teaching and responding to questions that I will pose to you.  As the semester progresses we may come up with more ideas of how to encourage participation.
