Getting Onto Your Online Course

Follow these steps to access your online course:

1)       Go to http://www.byui.edu - click on “Online Courses”

2)       Click Login

3)       Enter your username
This will be something like you91063
If you do not know your username, you can find it out by looking yourself up in the online student directory (http://www.byui.edu/spencer/local/directo.htm)

4)       Enter your password
Unless you have changed it, this will be the PIN number you used to register for classes.  If you do not know your password, or would like to change it, call the Student Help Desk at (208) 356-2361

5)       This will take you into BlackBoard, the portal for your online courses and other online course materials used by Ricks instructors.  A list of your online courses and some other courses will show up on the screen.  Click on the name of the course to enter it.  In BlackBoard you will notice a “course” called – “Online Student Information.”  This is an online help area to assist you in succeeding in your courses.  If you have questions or experience difficulties, check this area first.

NOTE: You will not be able to access your online course materials until the first day of the term, unless you have made special arrangements with your instructor.

 
Your online course is divided into three areas:

1) Information ABOUT the course - syllabus, contact information, what you need to do to succeed, etc.

2) The COURSE MATERIALS - Textbook reading assignments, supplemental readings and web pages, etc.

3) ASSESSMENT - assignments, quizzes, tests

The three segments below contain step-by-step instructions for accessing each of these areas.

 
1) Information ABOUT your online course - - -

The course syllabus and other information about your online course are found in the program called BlackBoard, referred to above.

1) Click on the name of the course you want to access

2) Navigate through the different buttons on the screen to find information about the course.

- The button will have course updates and other information from your instructor. Check this section daily.

- The button links to a page with a welcome message from the instructor, a list of assignments, grading information, and a syllabus.

- The button will link you to information about your instructor.

- The button will link you to the COURSE MATERIALS (the "meat" of the course) - See explanation below.

There are other buttons, but this is just a "getting started" tutorial. Look for further information about BlackBoard in the other sections of the orientation within BlackBoard.

 
2) The COURSE MATERIALS

The COURSE MATERIALS section is the heart of the course.  This is where you find the information to study.

It is not found in this BlackBoard program.

When you click the "Course Documents" button on the left, you will see a link titled - "click here to access course content".

When you click it, a new browser window will open. BlackBoard will still be open in the background.

In the COURSE MATERIALS page, you will find some introductory materials. These materials will give you additional information about online learning. At the bottom of this list, you will find a button called "Begin Course." Click this to start your course.

The COURSE MATERIALS are divided into Lessons and Pages. Taking your online course consists of working your way through these lessons.

At the end of some of the lessons, you will find a link to an assignments or ASSESSMENT page. This will open another browser window. The ASSESSMENT pages will be discussed next.

 
3) ASSESSMENT

The assignments and tests for your online course are taken through a program called PERCEPTION.

Depending on the course you are taking, all or some of the lessons will have an assignment/quiz/test at the end. To access it, click the link titled "Begin Assignment" - this will open Perception.

The first thing you will see is a screen asking for a username and password. This is the same username and password you used to access BlackBoard.
Note: There is not a direct connection between the Ricks College computer system, BlackBoard, and Perception. If you call and change your password, it will change only the Ricks system password.

The second thing you will see is a list of all the assignments/quizzes/tests that are available to you - in all of your online courses.

Click on the arrow next to the assignment you want to work on.
After you have completed the assignment, click the "Submit" button. You will receive a score and some feedback regarding your assignment. If the assignment is an essay, it will give you a score of 0. After the instructor reads and grades the essay, this score will be updated.
