All assignments will be due at the beginning of the class hour and must be stapled together if more than one page. Assignments which are not stapled or which are turned in late will not be accepted. Please bring your assignment to class already stapled.  (The only late papers that will be accepted are those that are arranged for previous to when it is due.)

Daily assignments must be neat and legible. Spelling, punctuation and grammar will count on all quizzes and assignments.

Plan to be quizzed on every reading assignment.

d. The specific date due will be decided later but plan for around midterm.

COURSE PHILOSOPHY:

            This course is designed to enhance college level reading and writing skills. It assumes that students have basic reading skills. Emphasis is placed on reading& writing for literal, critical, and interpretive comprehension. If you think you have basic reading problems, see me for a diagnostic reading assessment. English 311 is a required general education course. It is designed to train students to read non-fiction prose analytically, critically, and confidently. It is not a remedial nor a speed reading course. It is a course to fine tune college-level reading skills while emphasizing the close relationships among reading, writing, and thinking. English 311 compliments English 111, Composition, which deals primarily with the development of writing skills.

PURPOSE:

        The ability to read well is essential to acquiring information and to continuous learning. As we have access to more and more information, we also have access to more and more misinformation and nonsense. Those who read critically can understand, analyze, and synthesize what they read to filter out that which is valuable from that which is not. Writing is a medium for expressing critical thinking.

OBJECTIVES:

English 311 will focus on four basic objectives:

I. To comprehending complex ideas through

    A. recognizing the author's purpose
    B. recognizing the main and supporting ideas
    C. recognizing the organization of the essay

II. To Evaluate and write critically about ideas:

    A. recognizing premises, conclusions, assumptions and implications
    B. recognizing validity, truth, and soundness
    C. recognizing common induction errors--inadequate sampling, representativeness of sample, improper inference
    D. recognizing alternative explanations

III. To develop additional vocabulary through

    A. recognizing unfamiliar words and using context clues
    B. the dictionary

IV. To use language appropriately and persuasively with

    A. clarity
    B. Accuracy
    C. Precision
    D. Relevance
    E. Depth
    F. Breadth
    G. Logic
