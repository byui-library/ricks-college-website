COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: ID 140
Title: Introduction to Architecture: Interior Design Credit: 3
Text: Beginnings of Interior Environment, by Allen and Stimpston

Course Objective:
1.  Students will learn to identify and appreciate historical lifestyles, furniture and architecture from ancient times to the 1900's and recognize their relevance and application in today’s world.

2.  Students will acquire a foundation of the principles and elements of design and apply them in the decorating and furnishing of a home.

Description:

The course is an introductory historical perspective of lifestyles, architecture, and furnishings from ancient times to the 1900's.  Its goal is to develop a lifelong appreciation and understanding of the arts.  In addition, students will acquire a foundation of the principles and elements of design and apply them in critiquing floor plans, arranging furniture and accessories.  Consumer issues will discussed in relation to furniture, window and wall treatments and floor coverings.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Three historical projects of the student’s choice (within class guidelines) are required as well as designing a room that reflects their personality.
