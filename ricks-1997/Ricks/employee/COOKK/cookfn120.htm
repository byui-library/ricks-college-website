COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics Course#: FN 120
Title: Introduction to Food Service Sanitation and Safety Credit: 2
Text: Applied Foodservice Sanitation, A Certification Coursebook,
              4th Edition, 1995

Course Objective:
At the completion of this class the student should be able to:
? Implement a food safety program using the HACCP method in food purchases, preparation and service
? Describe guidelines for hiring and procedures for orienting foodservice employees in foodservice sanitation and safety.
? Assess the needs and develop an on-going training program for foodservice employees in the area of sanitation and food safety.
? Develop and implement a cleaning program for a foodservice establishment to maintain sanitary facilities.
? Identify and describe federal and state agencies responsible for food safety in the United States.

Description:
Introductory course into the principles of food microbiology, food borne disease and food safety from a foodservice management perspective. The HACCP procedure is used for developing food safety measures to prevent food borne disease. Cleaning programs, pest control and safety for food service workers is also reviewed.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Develop a HACCP flowchart
Complete a foodservice sanitation and food safety survey
Teach an inservice based on a sanitation and food safety topic
