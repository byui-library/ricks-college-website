COURSE SYLLABUS

Department: Home Economics Course#: Cloth 232
Title: Specialty Techniques Credit: 2
Text: Fine Machine Sewing - Carol Laflin Akles

Course Objective:
l. To recognize and extensive variety of specialty techniques that can add creativity, distinction and elegance to clothing and accessories.
2. To become aware of the fabrics, supplies and other materials needed to accomplish the assigned techniques.
3. To develop skill in adapting clothing designs, as needed, so that specialty techniques can be incorporated as aesthetic focal points or major structural details.
4. To accomplish perfection in both hand and machine stitching.

Description: Creative use of decorative and structural techniques applied to a variety of specialty fabrics to add creativity, distinction and elegance to clothing and accessories.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Port folio and specialty techniques samples, and the construction of a lined velvet dress.
