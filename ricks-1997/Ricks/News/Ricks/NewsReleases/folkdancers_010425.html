Ricks College News Release
April 25, 2001 
American Folk Dancers Ensemble to perform graduation show

REXBURG, Idaho — The Ricks College American Folk Dancers and Bluegrass Band will perform a graduation show on Friday, April 27, at 8 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium.

Tickets are $6 for the general public and available from the Ricks College Ticket Office at 356-2230.

The group will perform this summer in Brusy, Poland, July 23-28; Zywiec, Poland, July 29-Aug. 5; Vienna, Austria, Aug 6-9; and Szazhalombatta, Hungary, Aug. 10-22.

The students depict dances that have been enjoyed throughout the history of the United States. They will feature traditional folk dances and fun-filled features that will leave the audience tapping their toes.

The team is directed by Charles West, an instructor for the Ricks College dance department and past president of the National Folk Organization of the United States.

For the past 23 years, the American Folk Dancers have been performing in folk dance festivals in Europe representing the United States, Ricks College and The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

The dance group was first organized in 1972 after receiving an invitation to perform in Europe. In 1999, the group was awarded the "Audience Appeal Award" at the festival in Ribnitz-Damgarten, Germany.

The goal of the traveling group performance groups from Ricks is to edify and entertain audiences with uplifting, excellent performances and provide favorable publicity for the college, the church and the United States.
