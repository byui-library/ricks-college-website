SUGGESTED OLD TESTAMENT BOOKS

What books would be good to have in your own personal library that will help you understand the Old Testament?

The following is a BRIEF list of books that will help in understanding the Old Testament. All these books present ideas and opinions of the authors and are not the Church! Nonetheless, they are helpful. Books beginning with an asterisk indicates the author is LDS.
