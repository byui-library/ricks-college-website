Interpretive Design
INTERP DES
CLASS CODE: HORT 437 CREDITS:  1 FEE:  $20.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: HORTICULTURE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This course is a final evaluation of the student's ability to assess proper design interpretations, artistic dimensions of the student's preparation and training, fluency with the language of flowers, and product knowledge
DESCRIPTION: This is a capstone course allowing the student to complete the learning experience in the FDM program.
TOPICS: Flower fluency; assessories; techniques of design and presentation; telling a story with flowers; designing out of the frame.
OBJECTIVES: To polish the skills of FDM students as a final review and evaluation.
REQUIREMENTS: Tools, portfolio.
PREREQUISITES: Hort 335, 338, Hort 435, Hort 436, FDM major or consent of instructor
