Frequently Asked Questions

Can we play more than one sport at a time?

Yes, you can play in as many sports at the same time, but you can only play on one team in the same sport. 

Do we need ID's to check in?

No, you don't need ID cards to check in anymore.  Just go to the court you are assigned to and sign in.

What if we are scheduled the same day we have a temple trip or a ward activity?

We will try to schedule around your other activities, but you have to let us know by Tuesday of the week before.  We post schedules by friday at 2:00

Can we add players to rosters?

Yes, if they aren't already on another team and are either full time students for independent teams or ward members for ward teams.
