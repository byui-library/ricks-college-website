 Begger Maid.    A poem by Tennyson.

Birth of English Law.   Looks at a novel by T. H. White entitled The Once and Future King.  The focus is on King Arthur's contributions to the birth of English law.

Bliss.  An insightful statement by Gordon B. Hinckley.

Ender's Game.  An explanation.

Jonathan Livingston Seagull.  Explains the meaning of this inspired tale.

Joseph Campbell.   Lists Campbell's ten commandments for reading and understanding myths.

Lady of Shalott.  Includes Tennyson's poem with pictures.

Les Femme Fatale.   Moves briefly from the third millenium B.C. and The Epic of Gilgamesh to our own day.  Includes pictures by John Waterhouse and other pre-Raphael English painters, plus a 16th century English ballad, "Lord Randall," and Keat's poem, "La Bella Dame sans Merci." 

Mother.   A reaction to a novel by Maxim Gorki entitled, Mother.

One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest.  This brief essay makes observations about the  nature of women in Ken Kesey's novel.

Revision.  Here is an fascinating statement by the painter Sir Edward Burne-Jones.  He tells of working on a painting for 20 years.

Saluting the Robot.  Traces real and imagined developments in robotics and artificial intelligence, and raises the question, "What losses will we experience as computers become more sophisticated and as their uses become more intense, pervasive, and indispensable?"  Although certain developments are presented as good, the opposite is actually true.

Shakespeare's Wisdom.   Includes short quotes from Shakespeare's plays.  The quotes are grouped according to play.

Star Wars.   A short essay which examines Star Wars Episode One - The Phantom Menace.  Discussed are nine principles  which can add enrichment to our own lives.

Steppenwolf.   A short explanation of Hermann Hesse's novel.
