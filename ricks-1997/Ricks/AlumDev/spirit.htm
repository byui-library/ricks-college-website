A MESSAGE FROM PRESIDENT DAVID A. BEDNAR

Preserving and Enhancing the Spirit of Ricks: A Living Legacy
Before 1902, what was later to become known as College Hill was simply a large, bleak knoll on the south side of Rexburg, Idaho. Despite the hardships faced by the LDS pioneers who settled the area in the early 1880s, the education of their children was a leading priority.

Ricks College had its humble beginning in an unpretentious log structure mortared together with mud to keep out the wind and cold. This building was later attached to a larger structure made of rough-sawn lumber with desks and seats made of the same unfinished material.

An extraordinary spirit was born in those early classrooms. The first students resolved to be successful in their quest to obtain knowledge and in their efforts to serve others. Truly, for the time and place, it was an extraordinary spirit.

With the support and sacrifice of many, Ricks College’s first major building was begun in 1902 and completed in 1903. It stood as a single educational beacon on College Hill. Inside that structure was that extraordinary, exceptional spirit.

It was quite some time before the campus began to expand. When it did, the dynamic spirit that had been felt all along intensified—the indomitable Spirit of Ricks.

Today, Ricks College is a far cry from the single building of its beginning. In addition to dedicated students and caring faculty, Ricks is blessed to have a beautiful campus with magnificent facilities. A wonderful environment fosters rigorous learning and helps prepare students for a lifetime of work and service.

Remarkably, the Spirit of Ricks has survived more than a century and continues to flourish. It permeates every crevice of this campus. It crowns learning by both reason and faith and impacts and changes lives in important ways. It resides in the hearts of all those who become exposed to it. The Spirit of Ricks is a living legacy that must be preserved and enhanced.

I have found that Ricks College students want to be prepared for whatever the Savior would have them do in life. They want to be useful. They want to make a difference, to be firmly rooted in the things that matter most: faith, family, and service. Service, after all, gives substance to the Spirit of Ricks—the spirit of love for the Savior that is prevalent among students, faculty, administrators, and staff employees. And, significantly, that same spirit also resides with Ricks College alumni and friends wherever they may be around the world.

I am mindful of the many people who have given much to create and preserve this great educational endeavor at Ricks College. They have established a solid academic foundation, and many students have benefitted from their work and sacrifice. At the same time, I am keenly aware of the loyal alumni and friends who continue to advance this institution through their generosity.

It is now time to add another chapter to the history of Ricks College. To do so we must turn to the future. Our challenge is to successfully take advantage of the educational opportunities of the twenty-first century. Those opportunities and related demands are very real, and they are coming much faster than we might imagine. We must be prepared to appropriately meet them. As we set challenging goals and successfully carry out the unique mission of Ricks College, we will sustain the hopes and dreams of the students here now as well as those yet to come. Through the involvement and support of alumni and friends, the dreams of today will become the realities of tomorrow.

Certainly, we will add our part to the living legacy begun by others and thereby preserve and enhance the unique Spirit of Ricks.
