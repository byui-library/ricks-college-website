Intermediate French I
2ND YR FRENCH
CLASS CODE: FR 201 CREDITS:  4
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: FOREIGN LANGUAGE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - no GECode requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes speaking and mastery of intermediate grammar concepts through the study of stories, essays, poems, videos, and a grammar text.
DESCRIPTION: This class concentrates on perfecting a student's speaking abilities and on instilling a greater mastery of French grammar leading to increased confidence in speaking, reading and writing.
TOPICS: French literature, grammar, oral reports, group study.
OBJECTIVES: Students who finisht this course should be able to speak at intermediate mid to high level, demonstrate mastery of intermediate grammar concepts and write a two page essay in French using most grammar concepts studied in the semester.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance, oral participation in class, text book, pronunciation guide.
PREREQUISITES: French 102 with grade of "C" or better or three or more years of high school French or consent of instructor.
