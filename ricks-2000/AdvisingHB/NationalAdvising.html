NATIONAL ADVISING GOALS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

The primary purpose of advising is to support students in the development of an academic career supporting individual life goals. Students are not just recipients of advice, they should be treated as partners in the advising process. Students are expected to initiate regular appointments with their advisors, participate in decision making, and take responsibility for satisfactory progress toward a degree. Faculty members mentor students, serve as role models, and provide primary advising (by providing information and suggesting alternatives) in the major, minor and graduate programs when necessary.

NACADA, the National Academic Advising Association, recommends the following goals for academic advising:

-development of life goals/career goals
-development of suitable educational goals
-selection of appropriate courses and other educational experiences
-communication and interpretation of institutional requirements
-increasing student awareness of education resources available
-evaluation of student progress toward established goals
-development of decisions making skills
-reinforcement of student self-direction
-referral to and use of other institutional and community support services, where appropriate.

Based on the NACADA recommendations and the findings from the Art & Sciences Advising Committee, they recommend the following specific roles and responsibilities for advisors in Arts and Sciences:

1.Clearly communicate advisor / advisee roles, responsibilities, and legitimate expectations.

2. Initiate contact at least once per semester for the purpose of reviewing students' academic standing (i.e. mid-term grades) and course planning.

3. Recognize when problems are occurring that could interfere with academic progress and make appropriate referrals when necessary.

4.Clearly publish/post office hours and location, phone extension, and ways that your advisee can be in contact with you.

5.Reply to your advisee via phone or e-mail or in person with 2 working days of their contact with your office. If this is not possible, arrange for alternative assistance (e.g.the Career and Advising Center).

6.Keep current your understanding of all academic requirements (including core) for students in the College of
arts and Sciences, accurately communicate those requirements to students and monitor progress toward meeting graduation requirements in a timely manner. At the same time you should make sure your advisee clearly understand the implications / consequences of the alternatives they are considering.
