Phase II--Marketing Plan

Standards:  Computer generated, doubled-spaced, 6 pages
Grading:  Content, Application to the text, grammar, spelling, and punctuation

Cover Page - One-page color advertisement on your product--as though you were going to put the Ad in a newspaper or a magazine.  Please include your name in the lower right-hand corner. Note: do this last.

Page #1:  Target Market--Define in detail your primary target market in terms of the market segmentation data given in the text.  Give your primary target market a catch-name and tell me the potential number of customers you could have in a summer

Page #2: Product--Describe your product, the needs/want of your target market that is will satisfy, and the customer service or warrantee that go along with it (1 paragraph).  You will also need to complete one primary research exercise to show support or lack of support for your basic business idea or hypothesis.  Your primary research should include a minimum of 10 different responses (click here or here or here for examples) with the use of one of the following research instruments?

    Personal interview
    Written questionnaire

You will need to include in your report, a sample copy of the interview outline or written questionnaire along with the data gathered--including all written questionnaires or names of people interviewed in your research (in a appendix).  Summarize your finding in your findings in table form and write-up your conclusions based on the data gathered (click here for an example).

Page #3: Place--How are you going to distribute your product/service?  Regardless of how simple or complex your distribution channel you need to answer this question.  How can I use an intermediary to help me distribute my business?  You will need to make up a plan on how to use another organization to help you expand your business.  Look to chapter 15 for ideas and definitions.

Page #4: Promotion--In plain English (no ad or sales hype), explain your message--what you hope your target market will learn from your promotion. 

Using Chapter 16 to help you come up with ideas for promotion, you are required to do one of the following:

  1. Decide on a type and media of advertising (pages 529-537) to promote your business and give a detailed write-up of the exact lay-out and words your ad would say with a stick figure cartoon drawing if you will be using a visual ad (The ad will be on the coversheet).
  2. If you are going to advertise with a flyer, in paragraph form explain the flyer, its objectives, and where it will be distributed.  Make a mock copy of what your flyer will look like and include it on your coversheet instead of a newspaper ad.
  3. Decide on a type of  "sales promotion" you will use and complete a detailed write up of what your promotion will offer an how you will get the offer to the public.
  4. Write up an outline of what you would say on a door-to-door sales approach and presentation.
  5. If you are going to use publicity, write-up a simple press/news release and tell what type of media you will send it to.
