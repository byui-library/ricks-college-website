October 11, 2001

Emeritus president of the Seventy 

speaks at BYU-Idaho devotional


REXBURG, Idaho – Elder L. Aldin Porter, an emeritus member of the Quorum of the Seventy of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, spoke about individual preparation during Tuesday’s devotional.

The recently released senior president of the Seventy said, "I am here to raise a warning voice. You have each lived during the past 30 days of tumultuous times. It is not the end."

He then explained that much can be determined about a culture or a nation by looking at who they praise. Using examples of a famous basketball star and a pop singer, Elder Porter said athletic or musical skill does not make up for a lack of virtue.

"Our moral environment is more polluted than our physical environment. This is the world you live in," he commented. "Samuel the Lamanite said, ‘How long will ye suffer yourselves to be led by foolish and blind guides?’ What about us? To whom do we give our honor?"

"There is only one plan, and that is the plan of happiness, the plan of salvation," he continued. "Now more than ever the world needs order. Order requires a plan, and our Father has given us one."

Elder Porter explained that through the plan of salvation preparations can be made for the future, especially preparations to deal with temptation. "Do we comprehend how the devil operates? The devil uses mocking or derision. We know his playbook, and so we should prepare and defend."

One of the greatest tools we can use to prepare ourselves is the Book of Mormon. "God, who knows the beginning and the end, told Mormon what to put in the Book of Mormon that we would need today. When we read, we should ask why that was put in for us today."

Another important tool in preparing for what is to come "is to listen to the words of the prophet," Elder Porter advised. "Feel the words of the Lord, don’t just listen."

He quoted Church President Gordon B. Hinckley by saying, "Believe in yourselves as sons and daughters of God, men and women with unlimited potential to do good in the world. Believe in your power to resist the evils that would surround. Believe in one another as the greatest generation yet to live upon the earth."

He then closed by saying, "I testify of the fifteen prophets who lead the church. God will not leave us alone, if we prepare in advance."

Elder Porter was sustained a member of the First Quorum of the Seventy on April 6, 1991. He recently was made an emeritus member of the Seventy. He also served as executive director of the church missionary department, as well as a member of the presidency of the South America South Area and president of the Utah South Area.

Next week’s devotional speaker will be Patricia A. Pinegar, former General Primary President. She will speak in the Hart Auditorium at 2 p.m. The devotional will be broadcast live on KBYI, 100.5 FM and rebroadcast at 9 p.m.
