COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 272
Title: Advance Presentation Credit: 3
Text:  The Professional Garde Manger. John Wiley & Sons, Inc., New York, 1996.
           Garnishing.  Lynch.  Berkley: 1987.

Course Objective:  To gain a basic understanding of the following areas of cold cuisine: salads, charcuterie, basic garniture, visual composition, cold food flavoring, centerpieces, hors d’ oeuvres, cold sauces and dressings, and ice preparations.

Description: The art of garde manger will be practiced including cold food presentation and beginning ice carving.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Each student will plan and document a buffet for 50 people.  This manuscript must be presented in, at least, a three-ring binder including the following:
 A.  Introduction - name of project, name of class, your name.
 B.  Theme description - a detailed description of your buffet including style, setting, and clientele.
 C.  Table of contents (including page numbers).
 D.  Recipes and relative costing - twelve items (recipes).  Costing must be included for each recipe on the same page as the recipe.  Individual costing must include the cost for each ingredient and a total cost.  Unit cost is not required.
 E.  Three centerpiece descriptions - an in depth paragraph must be written for each of the three centerpieces to be displayed with the buffet (be specific).
 F.  Cost compilation sheet - all recipes will be listed and totaled on a final costing sheet.  In addition, the following will be factored and completed: Q-factor (if applicable), cost per person, price per person based on a 30% food cost.
