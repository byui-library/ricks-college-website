March 20, 2001 

Devotional: Dale Murphy

By TRENT TOONE
Ricks College News Service

REXBURG, Idaho — Living gospel fundamentals was the subject of retired professional baseball player Dale Murphy’s devotional talk Tuesday at Ricks College.

Murphy, a recently released president of the Massachusetts Boston Mission of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, elaborated on his experiences as a mission president and stressed to listeners that fundamentals are the key to staying strong in their faith.

"The good missionaries understood the basics of missionary work," he said. "The basic fundamentals of the sport ensure success, and those basic fundamentals need to be practiced again and again and again. Fundamentals need to be second nature. In our lives and in the gospel, the basic fundamentals need to be second nature."

Among others, Murphy outlined some of these fundamentals are magnifying callings, reading the scriptures, prayer and regular temple worship.

"These need to be second nature to us," he said. "The fruits of the working on the fundamentals will bring us to (the Lord)."

Murphy said living these fundamentals are continually looked down upon by the world, especially in marriage relationships.

"Keep your relationships proper," he said. "Marriage is the greatest blessing, but it’s constantly shown in a negative light in the world. It’s all about families. Take your roles as husbands, fathers, mothers and wives seriously."

He also encouraged young people able to serve full-time missions.

"Serving in a full-time capacity helps missionaries to develop a relationship with the Savior that they wouldn’t normally be able to develop," he said.

Next week’s speaker will be Bryan Weston, who works for the Church Educational System. He will speak at 2 p.m. in the Hart Auditorium. Devotionals are broadcast live and repeated at 9 p.m. on KRIC-FM.
