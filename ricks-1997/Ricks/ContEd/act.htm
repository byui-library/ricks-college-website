For Juniors and Seniors applying to take the ACT Test on Saturday, April 4, 1998. "The Act Score is one of the major considerations in college admission." Juniors are encouraged to take the class during prior to the April test and Seniors are encouraged to take the class prior to the October test. This course includes:

  • Sixteen hours of instruction covering each content area:
    • Test taking strategies ............................Monday, March 9
    • Reading ........................................Wednesday, March 11
    • English ...............Monday & Wednesday, March 16 & 18
    • Physical Science .................................Monday, March 23
    • Biological Science .........................Wednesday, March 25
    • Math ...........Monday & Wednesday, March 30 & April 1
  • A practice timed test in each content area.
  • Instruction provided by high school faculty.
  • A chance to feel confident and prepared.
  • An opportunity to improve your score.
