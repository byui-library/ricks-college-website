Ricks College Health Fair

Health concerns and questions? Receive help by coming to the Ricks College Health Fair on Thursday, November 4th in the MC West Ballroom. You and your family can benefit from the following: 12-hour fasting blood draw (lipid profile) - paid for by DMBA every three years, health appraisal, physical fitness appraisal, skin folding to determine body fat (private testing), nutrition analysis, hearing screening. Information on organ donation, emotional wellness, stress reduction, family crisis, safety precautions, diabetes, heart disease, cancer, arthritis, aging, nursing homes and assisted living. There will be free massages, chiropractic evaluations, vision screening and other medical help. Dr. Eric Barton will be doing foot screenings from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and Dr. Garland, a neurologist, will be here from 9:00 a.m. to 12 noon. District Seven Health Department will be here to give children’s immunizations, missionary shots, etc. This is a real service to us? There is a charge for the shots. Food Services will have a tasting table with many low fat, nutritious snacks along with yummy frozen yogurt.

We have incorporated suggestions on the Health Fair thru our E-mail survey and encourage your support. The Health Fair will be open to Ricks College employees and their families from 9-4. College students will be allowed to attend from 1-4. In an effort to promote attendance, we will have several door prizes each hour. Gifts such as food certificates, t-shirts, Swiss army knives, sweatshirts, bookstore certificates, drinking mugs, and a grand prize of a gourmet dinner for four will be given away. The grand prize will be drawn at 3:00. You need not be present to win any prize but you must attend the Health Fair in order to put your name in for the drawings.

Many people from our medical community have graciously donated their time to be here to help us. Let us support them by attending this worthwhile Health Fair. See you there! (Submitted by Jean A. Hardy, Wellness Committee
