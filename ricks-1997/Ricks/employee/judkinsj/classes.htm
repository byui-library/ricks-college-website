Comm 105: Individual Speech Therapy
Course Description As an ASHA certified SLP, I provide individualized therapy for up to three students a semester.  Students with communication difficulties, such as voice problems, stuttering, dysarthria, etc., can register for Comm. 105 for 1 credit, and get a chance to work in a supportive environment and in a goal-driven way on their communication skills.  I also work with English pronunciation for students learning English as a second language.  Because of the limited time available for these services, and because therapy may not be necessary in many cases, students must see me prior to registering for Comm 105.

Comm 202:  Introduction to Comm Disorders
Course Description Each person is born with distinctive qualities and unlimited potential. Each has a "voice" to contribute. Through communication, we relay our unique "voices" or identities to others.  Unfortunately, many people who yearn to communicate, struggle and often fail to do so.  Speech pathologists and audiologists are people who, with unique perspective and understanding, devote themselves to serving individuals with "unheard voices."  The goal of this course is for you to begin to think like a speech pathologist / audiologist.   To support you, the course will be divided into three units. In each unit, we will explore one of the following questions:  (1) What exactly is a speech pathologist and an audiologist?  (2) What is normal communication?   (3) What is disordered communication?   By answering these questions and exploring the issues related to them, we will build a foundation upon which you can build your knowledge as future comm. disorders professionals.

Comm 203: Clinical Phonetics
Course Description In this class, you will learn how to accurately write things as they are pronounced using the International Phonetic Alphabet and diacritic symbols.   Hopefully, however, more than that will occur. Hopefully you will learn to think phonetically.  This is the objective of this course.  Thinking phonetically is not easy--especially when dealing with phonology which is disordered, foreign, or unfamiliar to you. It requires a solid knowledge and understanding of phonetics and related concepts, and the development of a finely-tuned auditory perception mechanism.  Furthermore, thinking phonetically ought to have a "point."  We will practice applying your knowledge in many ways, but most importantly, we will practice applying it clinically, for those who struggle to produce understandable speech.

Comm 205: SLP Clinic Preparation
Course Description In narrow terms, this is a class about preparing you for your first clinical experience in communicative disorders. In that setting, your goal will be to make a difference in someone’s life. As if that weren’t enough, this class will attempt a larger objective relating to the broad concept of change. It sounds simple enough, but it is one of the most complex and incredible processes you’ll ever encounter. The objective of this class is for you to begin to think like a master of change--or at least for you to start down that road.

Comm 220: Interpersonal Communication
This course is about relationships. The purpose of this course is not only to explore information, but to apply it to relationships. As true communication principles are learned and applied, they become an integrated part of the heart mind and soul. The heart’s capacity to love will increase; the mind’s capacity to reason will increase; and the soul’s capacity to receive inspiration will increase.   The course objective, then, is to expand the heart mind and soul in order to maximize interpersonal communication competence.   Communication competence is not something with which you are either born or not born, rather, it is skill that can be learned and developed line upon line throughout a lifetime -- a byproduct of awareness, understanding, caring, active & critical thinking, and consistent practice.
