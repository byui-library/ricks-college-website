Ricks College News Release
April 25, 2001 

Steve Davis

Steve Davis named new alumni director

REXBURG, Idaho — Ricks College alumnus Steve Davis has been named the new director of alumni relations.

He replaces Devin Shaum who recently was asked to serve as the student activities director at the college.

Davis will work closely with alumni, including the Alumni Association, Emeritus Club, homecomings, reunions and other activities.

Alumni Relations strives to maintain contact with former Ricks College students through distribution of publications such as the Summit alumni magazine and Inside Ricks e-mail newsletter. The organization is also responsible for recognizing alumni who have made significant contributions and accomplishments.

The director also oversees Alumni Councils established in various areas of the United States that become involved when a Ricks College event is in their area, such as firesides and performing groups.

Davis has worked at Ricks since 1986, first in the Admissions and Scholarships Office and most recently in the Development Office. He earned the Exemplary Employee Award at Ricks in 1995.

He graduated from Madison High School in Rexburg in 1979 and Ricks in 1984. He earned a bachelor’s degree in history from Utah State University in 1986 and a master’s degree in education from Idaho State University in 1992.

He and his wife, Cynthia, are the parents of four children and live in Sugar City.
