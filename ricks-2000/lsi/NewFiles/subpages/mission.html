Mission

The Leadership & Service Institute seeks to develop and prepare students for leadership and service in the family, church, workplace, and community. Our three-fold mission is to:

• Promote Leadership and Personal Development
• Instill Life-long Service Values through Participation
• Strengthen Families through Marriage Enrichment

Vision

For the coming five years the Leadership & Service Institute seeks to improve and expand each of our three program areas with an emphasis on reaching out to positively influence as many students as possible.
