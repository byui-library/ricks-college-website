A Season to Celebrate!
Brigham Young University-Idaho is firmly committed to continuing the Ricks College tradition of presenting some of the finest performing artists of our day.

As we assume our new position as a four-year university, we are calling this "A Season to Celebrate" through the performing arts. From the Mormon Tabernacle Choir to our Big Band Cabaret, we believe we have found performers who will make this a season of celebration. We are grateful for the changes that have come which will serve to build and strengthen the performing arts on our campus and throughout the region.

You also may notice a change in our name from an "entertainment series" to a "performing arts series." We believe this more appropriately reflects our mission to present not just popular entertainment, but the very best in all forms of performing arts.

The Center Stage Performing Arts Series will remain committed to presenting entertaining, uplifting, enlightening and sometimes thought-provoking works of art through music, theater and dance.

We hope you enjoy our season!
