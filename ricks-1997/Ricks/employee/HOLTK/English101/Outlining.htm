INSTRUCTIONS

(2 pts.)1. Read a chapter from a college text (20 page minimum). Remember that reading is an active process that includes previewing, reading, marking main ideas, and reviewing.

2. Outline the chapter using the following steps:

(3 pts.) a. Identify the main ideas with Roman numerals I, II, II, etc. You may want to write the heading by the Roman numeral.

(3 pts.) c. Indent at least five spaces and fill in major supporting details. Identify major supporting details with capital letters A, B, C. These should be written as short phrases or single words.

(3pts.) d. Indent five more spaces and identify minor supporting details with Arabic numberals 1, 2, 3. Look for the five ways writers support main ideas: details, examples, facts, reasons, and anecdotes. Include these in your outline.

(4 pts.)3. Write the main idea by each Roman numeral as a COMPLETE sentence.

4. Your outline should follow this general pattern:

First main idea (written as a complete sentence)

A. Major supporting detail

B. Major supporting detail

C. Major supporting detail

Second main idea (written as a complete sentence)

Major supporting detail

Major supporting detail

Minor supporting detail

Minor supporting detail

Third main idea (written as a complete sentence)

Major supporting detail

Minor supporting detail

(4 pts.) 5. At the end of your outline, write a 1-2 paragraph summary of the chapter you have read.

(1 pt.) 6. Remember to follow notetaking guidelines--skip lines between sections, write on only one side of the paper, and label your notes.
