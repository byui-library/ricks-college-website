RICKS TO HOLD GROUNDBREAKING CEREMONY FOR NEW SPORI BUILDING

The groundbreaking services for the new Jacob Spori Building will be held May 29. The building will be fashioned after the old Spori building, as pictured. James Ricks · Scroll

Groundbreaking services for the new Jacob Spori Building on the Ricks College campus will be held Tuesday, May 29, at 2:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Auditorium.

Elder Henry B. Eyring of the Quorum of the Twelve of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints will preside at the event. The former president of Ricks College currently serves as commissioner of education for the Church Educational System.

Students and employees of Ricks College and the general public are invited to attend. Following the services participants may join at the building site at about 3:30 p.m. for the formal groundbreaking ceremony.

The three-story building is being designed by the Salt Lake City architectural firm of FFKR. The bid opening will be held in June and construction is expected to start in early July and be completed in 2003.

The building will house the Communication and Art departments, an art gallery and the offices of the student newspaper.

Excavation of the foundation is already under way to help speed up the construction schedule. Edstrom Construction Inc. of Rexburg is excavating the site that already had a partial basement from the prior building that was demolished late last year. The site is about 110 by 140 feet and will be 15 feet at the deepest point to allow for construction of a full basement.
