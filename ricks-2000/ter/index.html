     The Thomas E. Ricks Endowment honors the man primarily responsible for the founding of Ricks College supports the faculty in their renewal and teaching efforts.  Funds for this endowment come exclusively from private donations.  All faculty are eligible to apply for research, travel, seminar, and other grants that will directly benefit students. 
     We  request that you join this association and help strengthen teaching through your gift to this Endowment.  By so doing, you will ensure the perpetuation of the "Spirit of Ricks" as we make the transition to BYU-Idaho.
Church education is the best education there is and Ricks College represents Church Education at its best.

Marion G. Romney
