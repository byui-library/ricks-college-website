CONTINUING, RETURNING & TRANSFER STUDENT AWARDS

Applications for the following Ricks College Continuing, Returning, and Transfer Student Awards are available at the Ricks College Scholarship Office located in the Kimball Building, Suite 120. Phone: (208) 356-1087.  Please see scholarship application deadlines.  

Summer Scholarship

This award is for Continuing and Returning Ricks Students or Transfer Students attending one or more Summer Terms. It is based on the student’s cumulative college GPA at the end of Fall Semester. To be considered, students must have attended Ricks or another college or university as a full-time student. Applicants with a 3.65 GPA or higher will be considered. The application deadline is March 1st. In addition to the scholarship application, Transfer Students will need to submit official college transcript(s) by the March 1st. Award amounts vary.

Fall/Winter Academic Scholarship

This award is for Continuing and Returning Ricks Students or Transfer Students attending Fall and Winter Semesters. This award is based on the student’s cumulative college GPA. To be considered, students must have at least 24 semester credits completed by the end of Winter Semester and a 3.7 GPA. The application deadline is April 15th. In addition to the scholarship application, Transfer Students will need to submit official college transcript(s) by May 10th. Award amounts vary from full tuition to quarter tuition.

Leadership/Need Scholarship

This scholarship is for Continuing or Returning Ricks College students attending Fall and Winter Semesters. The award is based on leadership and service activities participated in at Ricks College. Financial need is also considered. The application deadline is April 15th. Award amounts vary.

Winter Semester Academic Scholarship

This scholarship is for Continuing and Returning Ricks Students or Transfer Students attending Winter Semester. This award is based on the student’s cumulative college GPA. To be considered, students must have at least 24 semester credits completed by the end of Fall Semester and a 3.7 GPA. The application deadline is December 15th. In addition to the scholarship application, Transfer students will need to submit official college transcript(s) by January 2nd. Award amounts vary from full tuition to quarter tuition.
