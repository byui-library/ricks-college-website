DRIVING DIRECTIONS TO

BYU-IDAHO CAMPUS

 

If you are coming from the South on

Hwy 20. Take the first Rexburg Exit.

The sign will also say BYU-Idaho.

When you get to the stop sign take a

right.  At the stop light take a left.  You

will be on Old Hwy 191.  You will pass

the Madison Middle School and several

car dealerships.  The road will turn by

the Cottontree Inn, stay in the right lane. 

You will now be on 4th South.  Follow

4th South until you come to a turn

around circle end.  The building in front

of you is the Manwaring Center.  You

park in the parking lot to the West of the

building or in the parking lot next to the

Taylor Building (the large white one). 

Please do not park in the turn around

circle, you may unload there.

If you are coming from the North on

Hwy 20 take the first North Rexburg

Exit.  At the stop sign take a left.  That

will put you on 2nd East.  Continue on

2nd East, you will pass WalMart. 

When you get to Main Street continue

on 2nd East.  As you approach campus

you will pass the girls dorms.  The road

immediately after the dorms is Viking

Drive or 4th South.  You need to turn

right here.  You will come to a turn

around area, by the Manwaring Center. 

You may park in the parking lot to the

East of the Manwaring Center.
