Inroduction to Cultural Anthropology
INTRO CUL ANTH
CLASS CODE: ANTH 101 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: RELIGION AND SOCIAL SCIENCE
DEPARTMENT: SOCIOLOGY, SOCIAL WORK, & CRIMINAL JUSTICE
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Social Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Anthropological approaches and perspectives on humans, their culture, and their society; early and prehistoric humans. Basic concepts for analyzing cultural behavior. Comparison on non Western with Western societies.
DESCRIPTION: A structure/functional approach to the study of human culture.
TOPICS: Family, marriage, religious kinship, government, subsistence patterns kinship, early man
OBJECTIVES: Students will understand other cultures and develop a sympathetic view of cultural diversity.
REQUIREMENTS: 4 exams, 2 book reports, maintain a journal
