Individual Focus Group

Requirements: One page, double spaced, typed

Content:

Answer the following questions in a similar format at the top of your paper. (this part is not double spaced)

Name: (your name)
Team #:
Product: (the product your are doing the focus group about)
Target Market: (description of what you believe the make-up of your target will be)
Date and Time: (date and time of your focus group)
Number of Participants:
Demographics of Participants: (break-down of which segments your participant fit in.  e.g. 3 in 15 to 18 age group, 2 white collar workers, etc. -- mostly demographics

The paper should include, in paragraph form, things likes what did you hope to accomplish by this focus group, what insights you gained from the group, things that went against what you had thought, a hypothesis, etc. (This part is double spaced).
