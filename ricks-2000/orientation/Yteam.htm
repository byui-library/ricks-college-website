Y-Team

Y-Team Leader Qualifications:


In order to apply for Y-Team you must:

1.  Be a returning student for the 2002-2003 school year.

2.  Be willing to work in pairs (two Y-Team Leaders per Y-Team). 

3.  Return to Rexburg before school starts and attend a two day training conference at Teton Lodge on Friday, August 30, and Saturday, August 31, 2002.  

4.  Pay a $15.00 deposit to secure Y-Team membership. 

5.  Be outgoing and willing to serve approximately 15-25 new students over a two-three day period, as well as throughout the semester as part of on-going orientation.

7.  Complete a Y-Team Leader application.  Applications will be  available at the Manwaring Center Information Desk beginning January of 2002.
