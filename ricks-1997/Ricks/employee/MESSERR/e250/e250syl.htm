English 250 (2000 1st Summer Term)

Instructor Ron Messer

Introduction to literature and film: This class will use film as its primary teaching tool. This is an introduction to the techniques used in achieving dramatic effects in poetry, stories, novels, drama, and film. Eleven books are required reading for this class. One novel, Sense and Sensibility, (optional) is for 25 points extra credit to be counted at the final.

Requirements:

1. Attendance (Attendance roll must be signed at beginning of class. Cannot come in 20 minutes after class has started and sign roll. If you forget to sign the roll, it is counted as an absence. It is cheating to have friends sign the roll for you. Cheating, if caught, results in automatic failure for both parties. Four absences equal failure.)
2. Handouts are on the Homepage, including syllabus.)
3. Books: (Note: the following books are all Dover editions)

1. Doyle, Hound of the Baskervilles

9. Synge, Playboy of the Western World
