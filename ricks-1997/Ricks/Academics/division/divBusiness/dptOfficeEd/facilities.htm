OSM Facilities

Classrooms and Labs: The classrooms and labs of the Office Systems Management Department are located in the Smith Building rooms 453, 476 & 478. The classrooms/labs are filled with high tech powerful equipment.

Hardware and Software: The hardware used in the OSM program at Ricks College is top-of-the-line Compaq Pentium II's. Software used in classes includes: (but not limited to) Microsoft Word 97 , Corel WordPerfect 8.0, Pagemaker, Netscape, Microsoft Office products, and Corel Office products.
