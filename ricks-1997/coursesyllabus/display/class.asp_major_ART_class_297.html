Art Seminar
ART SEMINAR
CLASS CODE: ART 297 CREDITS:  0.5 FEE:  $20.00
DIVISION: PERFORMING AND VISUAL ARTS
DEPARTMENT: ART
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Course developed to expose art majors to a variety of contemporary artists and designers.
DESCRIPTION: A guest artist program designed to introduce students to a variety of artists and designers. Students are encouraged to interact with the artist during a question and answer session. Usually four to six guest artists are invited each semester.
TOPICS: Artists from a cross section of the arts and from a variety of locations will present their work to the students.
OBJECTIVES: This course is designed to introduce students to new ideas and methods that will compliment their classroom studies through their interaction with the guest artists.
REQUIREMENTS: Attendance at all seminars and submission of report sheet for each. Make-up work may be arranged for extenuating circumstances.
PREREQUISITES:
OTHER: Four semesters are required. It is recommended that three Art Seminars be taken during the freshman- and sophomore-level years.
EFFECTIVE DATE: August 2002
