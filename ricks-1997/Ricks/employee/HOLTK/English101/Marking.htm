INSTRUCTIONS

(5 pts.) A. Read the attached essay by Mortimer Adler entitled, "How to Mark a Book," and learn Adler's 7 rules for marking texts. They are as follows:

1. Underlining of major points, of important or forceful statements.

2. Vertical lines at the margin to emphasize a statement already underlined (/////).

3. Star, asterisk, or other graphic at the margin to be used sparingly (*, @, #).

4. Numbers in the margin to indicate the sequence of points in a single argument (1, 2, 3,).

5. Numbers of other pages in the margin to indicate where else in the book the author made points relevant to the point marked.

6. Circling of key words or phrases.

7. Writing in the margin, or at the top of bottom of the page, for the sake of recording questions and perhaps answers which a passage raised in your mind.
