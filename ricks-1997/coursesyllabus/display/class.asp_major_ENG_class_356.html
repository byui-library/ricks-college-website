Young Adult Literature
YNGADLTLIT
CLASS CODE: ENG 356 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Focuses on evaluating, promoting, and using young adult literature in the classroom and at home. (Required for Secondary Education English majors.)
DESCRIPTION: Focuses on evaluting, promoting, and using young adult literature in the classroom and at home. (Required for Secondary Education English majors.)
TOPICS: The major periods and "touchstone" authors of adolescent literature; means of reaching adolescents at their intellectual, emotional, moral, and social levels through literature; the cultural traditions of self and others.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Determining reading development so readers move steadily ahead in reading maturity. (2) Recognizing developing trends in literature for adolescents. (3) Distinguishing causes as to why some adolescents dislike reading. (4) Recognizing commonalities between canonical and contemporary young adult literature. (5) Recognizing and reading outstanding books and articles about young adult literature. (6) Evaluating young adult literature as a piece of literature.
REQUIREMENTS: Read at least 20 young adult novels. Write summary/bibliography cards on each novel. Book talks. Three short papers on censorhip/morals, literary standards, and reading history. Thematic unit/young adult literature workshop.
