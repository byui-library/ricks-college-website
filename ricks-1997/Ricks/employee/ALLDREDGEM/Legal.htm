County and municipal legal sources

    Legal notices appear in the local newspapers. Compilations are found at City Hall or County Courthouse
    An example is:
        Rexburg City Ord. 523: Because of snow removal problems the city of Rexburg finds it necesary to forbid overnight parking on any City Street from 2 a.m. to 7 a.m., Nov. 1 to March 1.

    Legal citation forms

    The legal form of citations or references has unique abbreviations and arrangement. The volume or title precede the abbreviation. The section or starting page follow the abbreviation. The date of the legal action may be included in the citation.
