Course Objectives:

       Unit One: Academic Skills

Objective: to learn a method for analyzing and summarizing complex texts

Writing Assignment: two summaries

 

            Unit 2: Synthesis

Objective: to draw inferences from data and to recognize patterns among data

Writing Assignment: a synthesis essay

 

            Unit 3: Critique

Objective: to understand the distinctions between strong and weak arguments

Writing Assignment: a critique analyzing a written argument about which you are interested

 

            Unit 4: Argument

Objective: to become familiar with the form of classical argument and to learn a strategy for major-specific research and documentation

Writing Assignment: an argument exploring an issue related to your major

Specific Assignments and Procedures:

1.  Please attend class each day, even if you occasionally feel only partially prepared.   Should you be absent, check with a classmate for information about missed work and assignments.

 

2.  Read all text assignments.  I especially hope you enjoy our readings from Thinking About Thinking.  These essays about why learning matters have encouraged me to strive for academic and spiritual excellence, a process that never seems to end.  I hope they inspire you too.

 

3.  Complete major writing assignments:

A.  two summaries, 250 words each

B.  a synthesis, 1,250 words

C.  a critique, 1, 250 words

D. an argument, 2,500 words

 

4.  To encourage and reward your preparation, be prepared for frequent unannounced quizzes.  To received credit for these, please be in class on time.  There is no make up for missed quizzes.

 

5.  Major writing assignments are due at the beginning of class on designated dates. Late papers lose ten percent of graded value per day.

 

6.  Points for course work is weighted as follows:

1.                  Summaries (50 points)

2.                  Synthesis (100 points)

3.                  Critique (100 points)

4.                  Argument (125 points)

5.                  Quizzes (100 points)

6.                  Reading Log (50 points)

7.                  Final Exam (75 points)

 

7.  Final grades are based on the total percentage of 600 points earned: 93-100%, A; 90-92%, A-; 87-89%, B+; 83-86% B; 80-82% B-, etc.
