ADMISSION DEFERMENT

Two types of deferments are available to BYU-Idaho students: LDS Missionary Deferment and Continuing Student Deferment. Information on both types of deferments is listed below.


LDS Missionary Deferment

A completed LDS Missionary Deferment Application and a copy of the mission call must be submitted to the BYU-Idaho Office of Admissions after receiving your call and before entering the Missionary Training Center (MTC).

To defer enrollment without reapplying, the following conditions should be met:

  • Be admitted as a regular day school, degree seeking student at BYU-Idaho;
  • If you are a current BYU-Idaho student, enter the MTC before the end of the semester following the semester you are currently attending;
  • Do not attend or enroll at any other college or university between leaving and returning to BYU-Idaho;
  • Must not be on academic suspension nor dismissal from BYU-Idaho;
  • Complete a full mission and receive an ecclesiastical endorsement from your mission president.

Following your mission you must enroll at BYU-Idaho within a given time period. If you return from your mission from October to April, you must enroll in the next Fall semester. If you return home from May to September, you must enroll in the next Winter Semester.


Continuing Student Deferment

All deferment applications must be approved by the Admissions Committee. Submitting a deferment application does not guarantee its approval. Notification of approval or denial will be given by the Admissions Committee.

To defer enrollment without reapplying, the following conditions should be met:

  • Attend as a regular day school student at least one semester prior to the semester you wish to defer;
  • Be admissible to BYU-Idaho;
  • Submit a Continuing Student Deferment Application to the BYU-Idaho Office of Admissions before the deadline of the semester you are wanting to return.

Deferment for continuing students is valid for one semester only. The student must re-enroll at the university the semester immediately following the semester of absence.


Deferment Applications

The first page of the Continuing Student Deferment Application will be completed by you and the second page will be completed by your current BYU-Idaho campus ward bishop. You will complete the LDS Missionary Deferment application in its entirety.

Applications for deferment can be printed from this Web site by clicking on the appropiate application link below (Adobe Acrobat Reader is required). If you do not already have Acrobat Reader, click on the button below to download and install the free software program.

You may also obtain a deferment application from the Admissions Office by calling 208-496-1036.
