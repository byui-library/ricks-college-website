The David O. McKay Library is located in the center of the campus, just north of the Manwaring Center. The Library maintains a well-balanced collection of traditional materials and new technology as it serves the needs of the students, faculty and staff of Ricks College. The core collection includes approximately 150,000 books, 800 periodical subscriptions, government documents, reference CD-ROMs, a large video collection, microfiche and microfilm, and computer databases (CD-ROM and Internet-based). Presently there are over 300 computers in the Library for student use. The Internet-based databases can be accessed from other computers on campus, and some of them can be accessed remotely by employees and students of Ricks College. The Library also houses a very active Family History Center.

The Library is expanding into the former Administration Building. Remodeling of the "East Wing" and of the third floor in the "West Wing" has been completed, and the first and second floors of the West Wing will be remodeled somewhat in the spring of 2001. This remodel will expand Library seating from 700 to some 1,200 study seats, of which 400+ will have computer workstations.

 

Books, cassettes, records, tapes, maps, and posters are items that are normally checked out for two weeks. Books may be renewed two times. Faculty may keep these items for as long as a semester, if needed. Music CD’s check out for one week, with a limit of 2 CD’s per patron. Reference books, newspapers, and periodicals do not circulate.

Pictures circulate for 105 days or one semester. Calculators and headphones can be checked out for a maximum of two hours.

A Ricks College ID card serves as a library card. Off-campus patrons can obtain a library card at the Circulation Desk for $1.

Fines: Fines for overdue items are assessed at a cost of $0.25/item/day. Fines for Reserve items are $0.25/item/hour.

Reserve: Teachers may put materials on reserve for students to read or listen to. These items check out for two hours at a time.

Electronic Reserve: Electronic copies of materials can be placed on "Electronic Reserve", which students can then access on or off campus via the Internet. Faculty can send these materials electronically to Brooks Haderlie (HaderlieB@ricks.edu) or Tom Thornton (ThorntonT@ricks.edu).

Availability: If the online catalog shows the status of an item as "Bindery", "In cataloging", or "No items at any location", this indicates that the item is still being processed and is not available for check-out.

-- How can I access McKay Library resources from my home?

Anyone with Internet access can access the McKay Library’s online catalog, our Internet Resources page, and all other public Web pages sponsored by the various departments in the Library.

In addition, if you are a student or employee of Ricks College, you may access some of our paid subscription databases from home, including the Encyclopedia Britannica Online, the ProQuest periodical index database, and the NewsBank newspaper database. Simply go to the Library "Internet Resources" Web page (http://www.lib.byui.edu/newmain.htm), click on the desired link, and you will be asked to enter your campus network user name and password.

In the "User Name" field, type the first part of your Ricks College email address (up to the @ricks.edu part). Type your usual password in the "Password" field. You may find it necessary to type the Ricks College network domain name before your user name. This depends on whether you are an employee or a student, which Web browser program and which version of that browser you use, and how you have configured your browser. Faculty and staff will need to use the following format for their user name: "ricks_college\smithj", using their own account name in place of "smithj". Students should not need to type a domain name before their user name, but again, this will depend on the brand and version of the browser. If you are a student and you can't get access using only your user ID, try this format: "student\abc99001".

If you are using Internet Explorer 5.0, you will see a pop-up window with three fields. In that case, type only your user name in the "User Name" field, your password in the "Password" field, and either "ricks_college" or "student" in the "Domain Name" field.

Some people are having problems getting the password to work, even if they add the domain name in front of their user name. These users are typically using eMstar.net or CyberHighway for their Internet service. The problem seems to result when you use a browser other than the one you used to set up your Internet service. For example, if you installed a version of Internet Explorer when you created your Internet account, and then you use Netscape to try to access the Library resources, you will probably not be successful, and vice versa. There are two solutions to this problem: 1) Always use the same browser that you used to set up your Internet service when you are trying to access the Library resources. 2) Use the software your Internet provider gave you and install the other browser as well. This will correctly configure that browser to work with the Internet and with the Library resources.

Please note that not all vendors allow us to grant remote access as described above. Therefore, not all links on the "Internet Resources" page will be accessible from home.

Mission Statement

 

Computer Lab

The primary computer lab on campus is located in the McKay Library. There are currently some 150 research workstations on the West Wing third floor, and another 50+ on the East Wing first floor. These computers provide access to word processors, Library research databases, multimedia presentation software, and campus email accounts (but not non-campus email accounts). They are available on a first-come, first-served basis. For more information, contact Paul Jeppson at ext. 2370.

Conference Rooms

Room 140 on the West Wing first floor will accommodate 15-20 people and is available for use during certain days and hours. These rooms need to be reserved through Lana Hepworth at ext. 2350.

Copy Center

The Copy Spot is a branch of the Campus Press. They can print, collate, and staple materials on a choice of white or colored paper. Other services include laser color photocopies, T-shirt transfers, and hot glue binding.

The Copy Center is open the following hours during the regular semester: Mon-Fri 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m., and Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. It is now conveniently located at the North entrance in the East Wing. For more information, contact Deena Larsen at ext. 2384.

Faculty Document Delivery

Interlibrary Loan materials and any circulating items in the McKay Library itself can be delivered to faculty offices on campus. Normal delivery times are 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m. Mon-Fri. Contact Shirley Calder ext. 2357, or email her at CalderS@ricks.edu.

Family History Center

Sources for family history and genealogical research, using the latest computer materials, can be found in the Family History Center. Expert genealogists are also available to help patrons. The Center is open during regular Library hours, as well as 3:00-7:00 p.m. on most Sundays during the regular school year. For more information, contact Verna Hatch at ext. 2377 or Blaine Bake at ext. 2376.

Instructional Technology Center

The ITC is a new facility where faculty can receive training and production assistance as they develop curriculum materials using high-tech methods. At the ITC, one-on-one tutoring and consultation is provided to faculty in the areas of multimedia authoring, online course development, and desktop presentation and publishing. In addition to "help desk"-type assistance, other resources available include computer workstations, scanners, printers, video equipment, software, digitizing, examples, literature, training materials, and CD-ROM production. For consultation and instructional design help, and for help with developing PowerPoint presentations, contact Alan Young at ext. 1217 or Arlen Wilcock at ext. 1204.

Interlibrary Loan

If printed material is not available in the McKay Library, it can be obtained by filling out an Interlibrary Loan form, available at the Information Desk, or by emailing Shirley Calder at CalderS@ricks.edu. On average, it takes 7-10 days or less to receive items. The lending institution usually allows us to use the material for 4 weeks or less. Ordinarily there is little or no cost for this service, unless the item is turned in late or is lost. Materials can be picked up at the Interlibrary Loan office in 119A McKay.

Learning Assistance Lab

Several personnel from the Learning Assistance Lab have offices in the Library. These include Karl Edwards, Carla Labrum, Tom Croasmun, Richard Llewellyn, and Richard "Red" Taylor. The Study Skills Lab is located in the Second Floor corridor connecting to the East Wing.
