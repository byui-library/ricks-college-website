DR. REX E. HEAD

Distinguished Alumni Award
for Humanitarian Service

 

Not very many people would be willing to leave a secure job and uproot their family to move to a developing Third World country to help strangers. After several years of visiting orphanages in Mexico, Peru and Ecuador to provide medical assistance, Rex knew he needed to find help. The plight of orphans in an understaffed facility operated by nuns in Cuenca, Ecuador, caused him to start the non-profit organization Semester of International Service in 1998. Last year 14 volunteers and the Head family spent several weeks in Ecuador. In January Rex resigned as physician at the Ricks College Student Health Center and this month he and his family will return to Ecuador to direct the program personally and bring much-needed medical care to the orphans. His hope is to be able to help abandoned children throughout the world, and he has his sights set on Romania where conditions in orphanages are especially bad.

Rex is a native of Salt Lake City but grew up in the Teton Valley of eastern Idaho and on U.S. Air Force bases throughout the country. He served a Church mission to Catania, Italy, and attended Ricks College in 1980-81. He graduated from Brigham Young University and obtained his Doctorate from the University of Utah. Rex served in the U.S. Air Force for three years and came to Ricks in 1991 as a physician in the Health Center.

He and his wife are the parents of five children ranging in age from four to 12. Most recently, Rex has served as the Elder’s Quorum instructor in his home ward in Lyman.

His wife, Melodie, says "Rex has a profound love for those children in Ecuador and they know it. They swarm around him whenever he visits. He’s been going there almost five years. His strong feeling toward these children and their future has made him want to have this assistance be an on-going year-round program."
