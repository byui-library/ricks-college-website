Protect pores with tender care

by Janielle Gurney
GUR01003@BYUI.EDU
Scroll Staff
Beauty comes naturally through healthy skin. Makeup, tanning beds, hair dressers and even plastic surgery can all be avoided in preparation for a wedding if good skin care is used.

Natural items can be used for skin care prior to the wedding. Bath salts and oils can aid in refreshing the body’s natural beauty.

If a person’s body is dry and irritable, she should take a hot soda bath, which will make the skin feel fresh and soft. Milk can also be used to get rid of dry skin because it has exfoliating properties, according to loveyourskin.com.

When trying to get healthy skin for a wedding, the habit of good skin care should begin prior to the big day.

Cleanse the skin with a mild cleanser at least twice a day. After cleansing, apply a toner that will refresh the skin and remove any traces of dirt.

Next: apply an exfoliant cream to remove any dead skin cells. After the exfoliant, apply a moisturizer to the skin to keep it hydrated and full of nutrients.

A face mask can also remove dead skin and unclog pores and should be applied once a week. This pattern may result in skin becoming overly dry and irritated if done excessively.

Advice that has been heard for years about drinking eight glasses of water per day and it will keep skin healthy is true, according to www.allsands.com.

Women are expected to be silky smooth in America’s culture. There are different ways to rid skin of hair. Shaving is the most common form of hair removal because of the easy, quick access it provides women. The downfall of shaving is that it often makes the hair tips coarser when they grow back.

Women who wish to get rid of hair down to the root can use depilatory cream. Although the hair is removed from the root, many women find that the depilatory cream needs to be used regularly and may irritate the skin.

Waxing removes hair from the root and lasts longer than depilatory cream. Most waxes last up to six weeks. The disadvantage to waxing is that it may irritate the skin, especially the facial area. Waxing also requires quite a bit of regrowth for the wax to be fully effective.

Electrolysis is becoming more common for women’s hair removal. Although it is a permanent form of removal, the treatments may have to be repeated for to guarantee permanent hair removal.

There are many things to do to keep skin healthy and beautiful for a wedding. Planning ahead and acquiring good skin care habits will create naturally healthy skin to make a beautiful bride.
