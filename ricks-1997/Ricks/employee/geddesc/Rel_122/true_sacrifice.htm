The True Sacrifice

   ....Real, personal sacrifice never was placing an animal on the altar. Instead, it is a willingness to put the animal in us upon the altar and letting it be consumed! Such is the "Sacrifice unto the Lord...of a Broken heart and a contrite spirit," (D&C 59: 8). a prerequisite to taking up the cross, while giving "away all [our sins]" in order to "know God." (Alma 22: 18). For the denial of self precedes the full acceptance of Him.

Neal A. Maxwell
(from "Denying Yourselves of All Ungodliness," Ensign, May 1995, 68)
