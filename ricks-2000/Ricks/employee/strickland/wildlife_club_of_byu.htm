The wildlife club is a dual purposed club.  It provides an opportunity for students of BYU-Idaho, who are interested in wildlife and the great outdoors, to associate and interact with other students with similar interests.  The Wildlife club will also strive to educate club members, the student body, and the general public on wildlife and ecological issues.

 The Wildlife Club is open to any student of BYU-Idaho interested in any aspect of wildlife ecology.

 The Wildlife Club’s officers and members will uphold standards consistent with the standards of BYU-Idaho, The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, and the Constitution of the United States of America.

 The Wildlife Club’s officers and members will uphold the standards of BYU-Idaho.

 The wildlife club’s dues are $ 10 per semester.  These funds will be used to cover travel expenses and supplies.
