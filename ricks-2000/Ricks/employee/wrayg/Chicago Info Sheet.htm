URBAN STUDIES

INFORMATION SHEET

CHICAGO 2001

 

OVERVIEW:

The Urban Studies Program is a service experience.  We work with youth from Urban Chicago.  Thy are basically from Afro-American, Hispanic or Polish American ethnic groups.  The program is designed (1) to assist the youth to raise their vision of life; (2) to identify and develop values consistent with life success; (3) to assist the growth of these young people by teaching life skills and reinforcing basic academic skills; (4) to get the youth in a safe environment where they can interact with quality Ricks College students for an enjoyable experience.                              

SUMMER QUARTERS:  A six day camping experience with boys from 9-12 years of age and a ten day camping experience with youth from 13-15.

SUMMER JAM:  A 16 day experience with young ladies from 9-15 years of age held at Hyde Park LDS Chapel in Chicago.

CAREER ADVENTURE:  A program designed to teach the older youth, 14-15, leadership skills and skills needed to secure employments.
