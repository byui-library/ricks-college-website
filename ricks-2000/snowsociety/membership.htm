You can become a partner with Ricks College in supporting the arts and enhancing arts education for our students in music, visual arts, theatre, and dance.  In addition to the knowledge that you are blessing their lives and contributing to the cultural offerings in the Upper Snake River Valley, there are a variety of benefits we are happy to provide to you, based on what membership circle you choose to participate with annually.
  • Friend -- A contribution of $50 per year enables you to purchase tickets to arts productions in advance to their going on sale for the general public.  Friends also receive a handsome certificate acknowledging and expressing gratitude for the support of the arts.
  • Contributor -- For your donation of $100 or more annually, you will be in the Contributor's circle, and will be able to take advantage of advance sales and receive a membership certificate and a Snow Society pin, notices of upcoming shows and performances, and an invitation to participate in the wonderful Celebration of the Arts.
  • Patron -- If you contribute $250 or more each year, you will be a patron of the arts, providing significant underwriting support to our programs.  As a member of the Patrons Circle, in addition to the benefits listed above, you will be entitled to two free tickets to a student production of your choice during the year.
  • Master -- Couples or individual contributing at least $500 will be recognized as being in the Master's Circle of membership.  As such, you will receive all of the benefits of the Patrons' circle, four tickets to campus performances of student productions, an attractive membership plaque, and invitations to private showings, workshops, and receptions for faculty and visiting artists and performers.
  • Benefactor -- For your support of $1,000 or more annually we welcome you to the Benefactor's circle.  In addition to the benefits of the Master's Circle, your gift will qualify you as a member of the Ricks College President's Club, with its own annual banquet, report from the president, and other recognitions for those contributing significantly to the school and its programs.
  • Family Memberships -- For an additional $50 per year at each level, your participation can be acknowledged as a family membership, and you and all members of your household will be welcome to attend the Celebration of the Arts. 

In addition, all members of the Snow Society will be recognized in a list of supporters to be published in programs at arts productions.
