I attended all my classes in the Spori Building--most of them on the first floor. My classes, teachers, and the location of those classes were as follows: English Composition from Ruth Biddulph on the second floor, southeast corner; American Literature from Howard Salisbury on the second floor, northeast corner; Psychology from Arthur Sylvester on the second floor, northeast corner; Economics from Ezra Stucki on the first floor, south side center (now a foyer); Botany from Lorentz Pearson on the first floor, southwest corner; Agriculture from J. Wendell Stucki on the first floor, southeast corner; and Religion from Hyrum Manwaring on the second floor, northeast corner.”

Alyn B. Andrus (55)
Rexburg, Idaho
