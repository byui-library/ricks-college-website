Take your Bible to the Holy Land- President Harold B. Lee
(From Elder Harold B. Lee, spealdng at LDSSA fireside, Utah State University, Oct. 10, 197 1, about his visit to the Holy Land in 1958) "I was in the Holy Land a few years ago with my wife. President McKay had said, as he knew we were going to the Holy Land, 'Now don't do as some others have done. Some people go there and they are disturbed by the shrines that are all around various places. Don't go there to see what now is, but you go to see what once was.' So in preparation for that as we would leave our rooms in the morning, our prayer was, now today as we follow the guides, deafen our ears to the talk of the guides that has no significance as to the real places that we visit, but enliven our spiritual senses to know when we are on sacred ground, and with that prayer in our hearts we went from place to place." President Kimbal, early 70's: Go to the Holy Land, but don't go as tourist; take your Bible and study. It changed my life and it will change yours.....! Elder Faust told us, "The most important reason you're here is to find the Savior. But you won't find Him in the Garden Tomb. You won't find Him on the Mt. of Olives. You won't find Him in the Galilee. You have to find Him in your heart. He said I stand at the door and knock; if any man will open .... I will come in..."'
