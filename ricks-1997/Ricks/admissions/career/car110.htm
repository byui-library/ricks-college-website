  Summer		 Associate in Welding Engineering Technology (110)

The Associate Degree in Welding Engineering Technology provides hands-on training in welding processes and skills leading to Welder Certification. This program provides a solid foundation in welding theory, applications, and metallurgy. The program also fulfills many of the requirements of general education, math, chemistry and physics required at the University level. Thus students are prepared for immediate employment, or to transfer to a university to pursue a four year degree in Welding Engineering, Welding Engineering Technology, Manufacturing Engineering Technology, etc.
