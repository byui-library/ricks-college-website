Library Services

Tour of the library

At the main reference desk on the first floor is a walking tour of the library. After going around the library looking for the services appearing in CAPITAL letters, a quiz is available in the testing center. A proctored quiz is also available to those registered for the Course Info materials.

A virtual tour of the library as it appears after hours when everyone has gone home is available. It is being developed for Freshmen Orientations.

Library personnel

In addition to the link on the Library Information page, a marquee on the main stairs gives office numbers for departments and library personnel. The faculty and staff of the library are listed in the campus directory.  The Internet directory to Ricks College Faculty / Staff Directory has links to e-mail and homepages. Individual home pages and the college catalog will give faculty credentials.
 
Students who wish to work on campus, including the library must register with Student Employment in the Manwaring Center.
