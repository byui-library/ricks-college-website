ADVERTISING POLICIES

Ad Placement
Scroll reserves the right to refuse any advertising which does not conform to the published advertising policy or the standards / policies of Ricks College. All advertising is subject to the approval of the newspaper.

Terms of Payment / Credit
Advertising must be paid in advance until an accounts / receivable account has been established. Cash clients can apply for credit with Ricks College Accounting after three months of good paying record.

Advertisers on credit account agree to pay amount in full within 30 days after receiving their statement. Credit accounts should mail payments directly to Ricks College, Cashiers Office, Rexburg, Idaho 83460-4106.

"Make Goods"
Make goods or credit adjustments will be made for the portion of an ad that is in error. The limit of responsibility is the total ad space in which the error occurs. Scroll assumes no financial responsibility for typographical errors which do not significantly detract from the advertising message. Design will not be a factor in awarding credit or make goods. Scroll will furnish copies of ads to businesses for prior review upon request.

Cancellations
Cancellation of advertising space is accepted without penalty until 5 p.m. the Thursday before publication. Cancellation after that deadline will be charged 50 percent of the full price.

Page Requests
Page and position requests will be honored when possible but cannot be guaranteed.

Protection of Creative Ideas
It is our policy to protect the creative ideas, design and artwork of an advertiser from being used by another advertiser. Any advertisement designed and produced by Scroll cannot be copied or reproduced without the expressed written permission from Scroll.
