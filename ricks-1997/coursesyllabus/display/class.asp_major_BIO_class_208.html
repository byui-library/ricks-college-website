CLASS CODE: BIO 208 CREDITS:  4 FEE:  $15.00
DIVISION: AGRICULTURAL AND LIFE SCIENCES
DEPARTMENT: BIOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Biological Science requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: For students majoring in Forestry, Botany, and Agriculture. An introduction to cell structure, plant anatomy, physiology, reproduction, heredity, evolution and ecology.
DESCRIPTION: This is a basic botany class designed to give a substantial overview of plant chemistry, the cell, genetics, anatomy, physiology, ecology, evolution.
TOPICS: Chemistry, cell theory, genetics, genetic engineering, evolution, reproduction, anatomy, taxonomy and systematics.
OBJECTIVES: To provide students with an introduction to botany and current issues in science and agriculture.
