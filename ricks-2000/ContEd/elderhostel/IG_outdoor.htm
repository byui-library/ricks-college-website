BADGER CREEK.
Horseback riding, rapelling, canoeing , initiative courses, and other activities are in store for participants. This is an excellent setting to spend quality time with grandchildren while learning about and enjoying the outdoors. Classes focus on history (mountain men, Indian conflict) of the area. Geology courses will help explain sites seen on a field trip to Yellowstone Park to observe thermal features (amazing geysers, hot springs, mud pots, and more).
