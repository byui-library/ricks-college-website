Brigham Young University - Idaho 

West Yellowstone Elderhostel

SNOWMOBILING

January 6-11, 2002

 

 

Perfectly situated next to Yellowstone National Park, West Yellowstone is the ideal Elderhostel location.  Enjoy Rocky Mountain wilderness forests in this small alpine resort community nestled in the pines, with easy access to the Park.  The resort area, on the border of Idaho, Montana, and Wyoming is ninety miles northeast of Brigham Young University - Idaho.  Lodging, meals, and classrooms are in a comfortable motel with double occupancy, private bath, limited singles, extra charge -- all just minutes from field study locations for courses and wildlife viewing.  No alcohol permitted on site. FOR PHYSICALLY ACTIVE HOSTELERS.  Elevation:  6,000 ft. 

Plane: Y   Bus: Y (limited)   Train: N 
Please make your travel arrangements so you can arrive in  West Yellowstone at  3 p.m. for check in.
