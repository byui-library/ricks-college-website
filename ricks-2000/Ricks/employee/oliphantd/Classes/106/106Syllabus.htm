Course Description:  This course is the first semester of the Applied Physics sequence. Concurrent registration with Physics 107 is strongly encouraged. This course will cover topics in mechanics, waves, sound, heat, and thermodynamics at the introductory level. These areas are important in a wide range of areas including structures, astronomy, human anatomy, transportation, music, earth science, and philosophy.

Course Objectives: Students should come away from this course with a greater understanding and appreciation of the natural world, and the physical truths that govern it.  In addition students will develop problem solving skills and perspective that will be beneficial to them in every day life as well as their careers, and give them a greater appreciation for the creations of God

Course Requirements:  There will be 6 tests and a comprehensive final.  The final grade will be based on these tests, class participation and homework.  Each test will be worth 5% of your grade.  The Final will be worth 10%.  You are strongly encouraged to work in groups.  However, group participation should not be an excuse for individual incompetence.

 

Grading: Each of the 6 tests is worth 5% of your grade and the final is worth 10%.  The homework is worth 50%. And Class participation (Graded by reading quizzes and other) is worth 10%.
