Ricks College News Release
March 8, 2001

Temple president named Ricks Man of Excellence

REXBURG, Idaho — A Rexburg man who is currently serving as president the Logan Utah Temple of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints was honored as this year’s Ricks College Man of Excellence Feb. 25.

The award was presented to Max Craner at a Sunday night fireside marking the conclusion of Men’s Week, a week-long celebration at the Rexburg college.

A native of the Declo-Burley area, Craner graduated from Ricks College in 1953 when it was a four-year school. He worked for 40 years with the Church Educational System, including supervision of seminaries and institutes.

While employed by CES, Craner organized some 300 seminaries in the northeastern United States and Canada. He has been institute director at Snow College and Idaho State University and was area director for CES for southern Idaho.

After retiring from CES in 1989, he moved to Rexburg and taught for two years as a volunteer part-time instructor in the Religion Department at Ricks. He is a past-president of the Philippines Quezon City Mission. While living in Rexburg, he also served as a church Area Authority for several years before assuming his duties in Logan.

He and his wife, the former Evelyn Parke, are the parents of six daughters and have 47 grandchildren.
