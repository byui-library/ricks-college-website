Making the Deadline . . .

When writing the story in order to make the nightly deadline, here are a few more things to remember:

Use inverted pyramid style, writing the most important information first and then descending to the least important.

Refer often to you AP Style handbook to make sure everything has proper usage.

Double check spelling of names and facts so that everything is accurate before giving the story to the editor.

Don't leave until everything is in order and the editor has given consent.

If you do these things, your story is sure to be a slam-dunking success!
