Creating an E-commerce site
50 points

Create a Web page linked to your Catalog.com site that does the following:

  • List the step of creating an E-commerce site.
  • Explain the most common security risk associated with an E-commerce site
  • Explain how you address these risks
  • Explain the Internet processing system for accepting and managing credit card payments on the Web.  Tell who the participants are and what they do.
  • List and briefly explain other methods of payment over the Internet.  Tell which you prefer and why.
  • Identify a company that will handle the whole credit card process for you (basically a prepackaged online store).
