Unit #2 - CHEMICAL KINETICS

Upon completion of this unit you should be able to:
1. Interpret a plot of the reaction path versus the energy involved.  (Is the reaction spontaneous? exothermic? rapid?)
2. Predict, qualitatively, the effect of changes in temperature, concentration, or volume on the rate of a chemical reaction.
3. Predict the effect of a catalyst and/or inhibitor on (a) the rate of a reaction, (b) DG for the reaction, and (c) the magnitude of a reaction's activation energy.
4. Determine the rate law (a) of a simple collisional reaction from its stoichiometry, or (b) of a more complicated reaction from appropriate experimental data.
5. Write the rate of a specified reaction in terms of the formation of a product or the consumption of a reactant.
6. Determine reaction orders from a given rate law.
7. Interconvert rate constants and half lives for first order reactions, and calculate the concentration of reactants at any time during the course of the reaction.
