Portfolio

    should include at least 110 points from the following options:
  • introduction: what you know and what you want to learn (required 10 pt)
  • self evaluation of your portfolio ( 10 pt)
  • Test score on the Library Tour (required for English courses, study skills, etc.) 20 pt.
  • LC Easy program (required of all student workers who shelve materials using LC ) 25 pt.
    • 100% = 25 pt.
    • 90 %  = 24 pt.
    • retake until perfect source (hint)
  • Can You Find It?  Questions (20 points for each)
  • Complete background study  100 maximum
  • catalog your own books, music, etc 100 maximum
  • demonstration of computer use 25 pt  Example
  • researching an author 10 to 100 pt.
  • legal research reading 10 pt
  • legal research assignment 50 pt
  • other student initiated learning activity

Introduction to Portfolio

due 1st class attended for 10 points
  • Name ________________________________________
  • What places do you like? Hometown; dream vacation location; foreign country (2 pt)
  • Who are favorite persons from past and present (especially those in the news)? (2 pt)
  • What do you want to be able to do during and after this class?
  • Describe your previous library experience.
  • What questions would you like to research?
  • What is your major or what classes are you taking this semester? (2 pt)

Evaluation of Portfolio

due last class for 10 points
  • Name  ________________________________________
  • How would you rate the class on a scale of  0 to 10 ?  ____________
    • with 0 being nothing learned and 10 being learned more than expected
  • What was your favorite topic or unit?
  • What was your least favorite topic or unit?
  • What could have been covered that was not covered?

 Computer Demonstration

  • demonstrate how to use the catalog
  • demonstrate how to use a periodical index
  • demonstrate how to use a CD on demand or other computer program
