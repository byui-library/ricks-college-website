 REGISTRATION INFORMATION

Each person attending Campus Education Week must be registered and have his or her own ticket.  Your ticket represents your registration; thus, the ticket may not be given to another person or shared between students.  Tickets are non-transferable.  You must wear your ticket to be admitted to each class.

PREREGISTRATION
Preregistration will save you time and money!  We encourage you to preregister by mail or in person.  Ticket lines at the door on the first day tend to be long.

Preregistration by Mail

 HOUSING

CAMPUS HOUSING
Campus housing is available on a first-come, first-served basis.  Participants will be housed in campus apartment units which accommodate six people and consist of three bedrooms with twin beds, living area, and a shared bathroom; or dormitory housing units which accommodate two individuals and consist of living area and shared bathroom.  Bedding and towels are provided.  Couples and families will be housed in their own unit.  Participants who come alone will be assigned roommates of their own sex.  Please request which type of housing you will need on the registration application. Students ages 14-18 must be accompanied by an adult.  No babies in arms or anyone else under 14 will be allowed in campus housing.  In order to house as many students as possible we cannot honor requests for specific dorms.  Groups of more than six will be assigned in adjoining apartments if all registrations are sent together.  We must receive your registration by May 23.  Cost is $36 (Wed, Thur, and Fri nights).

Housing Check-in:         6-10 p.m., Wednesday, May 31, at the lounge area of the complex you are assigned to.
Housing Check-out:       By 9:30 a.m., Saturday, June 3

NOTE: We cannot accommodate early check-ins or late check-outs.

Campus housing is available but may not be able to accommodate all those who apply.  You will be notified of your housing status within a week of your registration by a confirmation letter and a receipt.  If housing is unavailable, you will be notified and advised to seek other accommodations.

HOTELS/MOTELS
There are motels in the surrounding area that will offer a discount rate.  A list of these is available upon request from the Continuing Education office.  Please call (208) 356-2344 to have a motel list sent to you.

 MEALS

Meals are served at the Manwaring Center Cafeteria (The Galley).  Meal tickets may be purchased when you register.  All meal ticket registrations must be in our office by May 23.  The cost is $28 per ticket and provides you with any six meals Thursday-Saturday.  Meal times are shown below:

Times
  Breakfast:   7-9 a.m.
  Lunch:       11:30 a.m.-1:45 p.m.
  Dinner:      4:30-6:30 p.m.

NOTE: Serving lines will close at these times, but you can stay as long as needed to finish eating.

OTHER MEAL OPTIONS
A variety of food items are also available at the Viking, Baglebys,  and from vending machines and snack bars.  There are also plenty of restaurants within the Rexburg area.
