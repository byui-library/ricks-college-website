Money Management
MONEY MANAGE
CLASS CODE: HFED 280 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Financial concepts of direct concern to the individual or family. Budgeting, financial institutions and services, consumer buying, use and control of credit, financial records, buying and selling homes, insurance, and basic investments will be discussed.
DESCRIPTION:
TOPICS: LDS philosophy of money, career planning, goals, wants vs. needs, budgeting, budget systems, marriage and money, retirement savings, savings accounts, checking accounts, bank statements, credit, credit bureau reports, revolving credit, installment credit, credit problems, bankruptcy, financial records, buying a home, mortgages, closing costs, selling a home, auto, property, health and life insurance, retirement, investing.
OBJECTIVES: The student will be able to:
1. Set up a workable budget based on past expenditures to achieve financial goals.
2. Make wise consumer decisions.
3. Learn principles relating to: budgeting, credit, checking and savings accounts, record keeping, buying and selling a home, estate planning, consumer buying, insurance, and investing.
REQUIREMENTS: Budget assignment; Class Presentation; Web site evaluations; Exams and quizzes; Final exam
