FINES FOR TRAFFIC VIOLATIONS Are listed below. Misdemeanor Fines are set by the Magistrate Judge in Madison County

Careless or Inattentive Driving-----$ Misdemeanor (Fine and or jail time set by Madison  County Magistrate Judge)
Any person who drives or is in actual physical control of any vehicle upon a highway, or upon public or private property open to  public use, carelessly and heedlessly or without due caution and circumspection, and at a speed or in a manner as to endanger or likely to endanger any person or property, or who passes when there is a sight distance restriction, shall be guilty or reckless driving. Inattentive driving shall be considered a lesser offense than reckless driving and shall be applicable in those circumstances where the conduct of the operator has been inattentive, careless or imprudent.
Idaho Traffic Code Violation 49-1401(3)

Failure to Yield to Pedestrian in Crosswalk-----$53.00
If a pedestrian is in a Crosswalk, and in your half of the roadway, you must wait until he/she exits from your half of the roadway. Also, if a pedestrian is about to enter your half of the roadway, while in a Crosswalk, you must yield while they enter and wait until they exit the Crosswalk before continuing.
