MEET THE PLAYERS NIGHT TO OPEN BASKETBALL SEASON

The annual Meet the Players night will be held Tuesday, October 31, at 6 p.m. in the Hart Main Gym. The women's basketball team will lead out with introductions and an intra-squad scrimmage. They will be followed by the men about 45 minutes later. Admission to Meet the Players night is free.

What is ahead for the men and women cagers this season?

Both squads start play on the road at a tourney in Miles City next week. The home opener will be Tuesday, Nov. 14, against Western Montana.

For the women, Coach Lori Woodland looks for the return of two starters. They are the regional tourney MVP, Mandie Little, and Candis Wilcox. Little led the team in scoring with a 14 points per game last year. She holds the team single game and team season record for rebounding. Also back are six-foot Lindsey Johnson who played in 29 of 37 games; another six-footer in Sara Fister who played in 28 games; and guard Alison Baker who played in 25 games.

For the men, Coach Clyde Nelson returns only Marshal Martinsen from last year's team. At 6-5, he's battling to start at forward. Back from the 1996-97 teams are guards Joshua Griffeth, Collin Johnston and Bryson Vaughn and forward Jared Layton, who hits 6-8. A redshirt ready to play this season is Adam Hiatt, a guard. Back from the regional championship team of 1998 is 6-7 forward Brandt Peterson. Two transfers are 6-7 Tyler May who played at Western Wyoming prior to his mission and Joseph Donaldson from BYU.

PLANETARIUM REOPENS

Sit back, relax and take a journey to the stars. The Ricks College Planetarium is now open after a six-month closure for major renovations. Nearly $100,000 has been spent to upgrade and computerize the program along the addition of a new video projector. With the new system, programs flow more smoothly and contain added special effects.

The planetarium is open for public shows Thursdays at 7 p.m., excluding holidays. Through the month of November, the show will be "Stars and Galaxies." The program for December is "The Christmas Star." The planetarium is located in the basement of the Romney Building. Admission price $1.25 per adult and $1 per student. In addition to the Thursday evening presentations, groups may schedule private shows through the Scheduling Office by calling (208) 356-2248.

RICKS HALLOWEEN CONCERT

The 16th annual Halloween Concert at Ricks College will feature "Great Lovers" during performances Oct. 27, 28 and 31 in the Barrus Concert Hall. Performances on Friday will begin at 7:30 p.m., Saturday at 5 and 7:30 p.m., and on Halloween at 6 p.m. and 8:30 p.m. As of Tuesday, some tickets were still available for Halloween night only. Tickets are $3 for the general public and available from the Ricks College Ticket Office.

Directed by faculty member Kevin "The Count" Call, the Halloween concert has been an annual event since 1984. Up until last year, the concert was always been presented by the Symphony Orchestra. Last year the Symphonic Band presented the concert, and this year it is once again the orchestra. The two groups will continue to alternate years.

"The purpose of the Halloween concerts is to introduce people to the world of classical music in a new and unique way," Call says. "We have a lot of fun doing it and there is a lot of silly business that takes place in the concert, but the music is all first-rate, great classical music."

This year the music that will be performed was taken from many of the great love stories of all time, hence the title "Great Lovers." These include Tchaikovsky's composition of Romeo and Juliet. Contemporary Dance Theatre will be joining the symphony to present this number. Also portrayed are excerpts from three operas and stories of Sleeping Beauty, Beauty and the Beast, Orpheus and Euridice (from Greek mythology).
