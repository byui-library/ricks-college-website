I.	COURSE DESCRIPTION:  Basic outdoor skills including campsite 
	selection, shelter preparation, meal planning, map and compass, 
	and camp sanitation.
II.     STATEMENT OF PURPOSE:  The purpose of this course is to provide 
	students with the principles and practice of core skills for 
	camping or outdoor living in harmony with the environment.  
	The course will focus mainly on the need of the individual 
	or family camper, but will also apply to the volunteer leader 
	(scout master) or professional leader (camp director).
III.    REQUIRED PRE-REQUISITE:  None - this is a Level I course.
IV.     COURSE OBJECTIVES:
        A. To develop an awareness & understanding of the philosophy 
	of skilled outdoorsmanship.
        B. To develop an ability to plan & participate in a short camping 
	experience.
        C. To develop self-reliance and ability to cope with various 
	outdoor situations.
        D. To provide "hands-on" learning activities that may be 
	re-used in personal situations.
V. FEES AND EQUIPMENT:  
        A. Lab Fee $30, to cover transportation, food, camping gear, 
	lab assistance and program materials.
        B. Notebook - A three-ring notebook is to be tab indexed and 
	used for the compilation of course materials and personal 
	resources.
        C. Pocket Knife - A required item.  Obtain a good blade 
	(schrade, case, camulis) and try to avoid "super-cheapos". 
        D. Personal Camping Gear - Your class fee covers group equipment 
	and some individual gear.  You must submit your I.D. to 
	checkout school gear.  Clothing and other gear should consist 
	of anything you can beg, borrow, or otherwise acquire. 
        F.   The most expensive item required is your TIME.  
	Do not miss class.

     	The final grade will be computed according to the students

     	Lab Attendance - A minimum of 1 night and 2 days in a

	should be attended as this will be where requirements are 
	practiced and signed off.  A written description and personal 
	account of each outing is to be included in your notebook.

        Exams - Two major exams will be given; a written final and
