Water Aerobics


I really believe in Water Aerobics as a top work out and especially for those of you rehabiliating from an injury. It is the best muscle work out because the water adds resistance, and it is so good for your because there is no foot strike adding stress to the joints. This is one of my favorites, but plan on getting a little wet. Before the semester ends, there will be an all out water fight!
