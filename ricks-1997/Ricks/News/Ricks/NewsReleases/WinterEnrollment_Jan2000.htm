Ricks College News Release
Jan 13, 2000

Winter enrollment at Ricks climbs to 8,840 

REXBURG, Idaho — Ricks College has unintentionally broken its own enrollment record with 8,840 students enrolled for the winter semester.

This is over the target enrollment of 8,600 and higher than the fall semester’s enrollment of 8,628.

"We are handling the extra students just fine," says Jim Gee, assistant academic vice president. "It is difficult to know exactly how many students are going to show up, but we have not changed our cap of 8,600 students."

Attending Ricks this winter semester are 805 new students. The remaining number includes 7,606 students continuing from the prior semester and 429 students returning after an absence, such as returned missionaries who re-enter Ricks following their missions for the LDS Church.

Winter enrollment statistics show that Ricks College is attended by mostly 18-22 year-old students. The average credit hours per student are 15, showing that most students are attending on a full-time basis.

Ricks represents 51 foreign countries this year with 272 international students. In a breakdown of the United States, statistics show 2,931 from Idaho, 1,312 from Utah, 865 from California, 739 from Washington, and in fifth place, Oregon, with 387 students. 

Fifty-eight percent of the students are women (5,111). LDS returned missionaries number 2,575. Marital status shows 542 Ricks students are married.
