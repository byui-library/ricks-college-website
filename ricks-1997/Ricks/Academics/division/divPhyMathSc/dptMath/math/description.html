Mathematics Major
Students of mathematics at Ricks College are, in the main, well prepared and inquisitive. Many go on to study mathematics, engineering or the sciences, while others enter programs in math education. The Mathematics major (238) is designed to prepare the student for such educational options. The mathematics student is given the usual background in calculus and related subjects, such as linear algebra and differential equations. Our students also have opportunities to study Statistics, to learn about current technological tools for solving problems, and to program computers (usually in the C or C++ languages). Those who plan to teach mathematics are taught by professional math teachers, which allows them to see their discipline modeled daily. One distinctive feature of our program is that we require all mathematics majors to learn the reading and writing of proofs. This provides valuable preparation for subsequent studies in mathematics and related disciplines.
