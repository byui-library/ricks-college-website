COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: FN 101
Title: Culinary Practicum Credit: 1
Text: No text required

Course Objective:
To help students make a successful transition from high school to college and the Culinary Arts major.
To provide a social and professional interaction with the campus community.  A student club charter for “R” Bakery will provide the guidelines for decision making.
To facilitate service and humanitarian efforts in the world through existing organizations, i.e. Partners of the Americans Family Gardens and Inner City Youth Charitable Foundation of Chicago.
To carry out the business of the “R” Bakery
To participate in the Idaho Chapter of the American Culinary Federation and Idaho Chef’s Association.

Description:
Special projects in community and campus related to the field of food service.  Field trip once per semester.  Required each semester for majors.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
Participation in “R” Bakery on production or selling for 4 hours per week.
