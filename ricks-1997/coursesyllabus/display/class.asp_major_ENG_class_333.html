British Literature - Victorian and Modern
BRITLIT/VCT&MD
CLASS CODE: ENG 333 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Examines the influence of historical events, philosophical ideas, and literary trends on works and authors of the British Victorian and Modern periods from Carlyle to Eliot and contemporaries.
DESCRIPTION: Examines the influence of historical events, philosophical ideas, and literary trends on works and authors of the British Victorian and Modern periods from Carlyle to Eliot and contemporaries.
TOPICS: Development of the Victorian and Modern periods, including its major movements, genres, and authors; placement of the literature of this period in a relevant cultural and historical context; examination of how these two historical periods and types of literature interrelate; use of the fundamental tools of literary interpretation in the discussion, analysis, and evaluation of literature of these periods; variety of critical and theoretical approaches to the literature of these periods; broad context for further study and specialization.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Using the fundamental tools and vocabulary of literary study (i.e. symbolic language, characterization, conflict, theme, point of view, form, and style) in the discussion, analysis, and evaluation of literature of these periods. (2) Identifying (both orally and in writing) the interrelationships of works and ideas within and across these two periods. (3) Using various critical approaches (as introduced in English 251 and English 314) to appreciate and analyze works from different genres and periods. (4)Researching and discussing works of this period using reliable sources and MLA style.
REQUIREMENTS: Extensive reading. Discussion and oral presentations. Interpretive, analytical, and/or critical evaluation papers. Journals.
