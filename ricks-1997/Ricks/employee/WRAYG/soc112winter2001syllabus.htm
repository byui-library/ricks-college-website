Course description:

This course is designed to help you become familiar with some basic sociological terms and begin to examine various social problems from a sociological perspective. We will be discussing subjects such as the realities of today’s economy, as well as problems with government, education and family. You are strongly encouraged to keep up with the reading assignments and to keep up on current social problems that are happening around us. You will be learning about origins, sources, effects and possible solutions to the social problems that you will be exposed to throughout the semester.

Course requirements:

Exams:

There will be four exams (including the final) throughout the semester. The final will be comprehensive, using material learned and tested on previously. Each exam will is equal to 100 points. All exams will be in class.

Current events:

At the beginning of each class period we will be discussing current events. You are encouraged to share information about these events. I will keep track of those students who do this. There is a maximum of 50 points for this throughout the semester.

Quizzes:

There may be a 10 question quiz at the end of some chapters. There will be no more than 10 quizzes during the semester. Questions on the quiz will be taken directly from the chapter and will reflect your reading.

Group article reviews:

Four times throughout the semester the class will be divided into groups. Each group will be given an article that they will read together and discuss as a group. Participation is important as part of this process. The instructor will provide the articles for review and will ask some groups to present their findings to the class upon completion.
