Ricks College News Release, October 19, 1998

New vice president named at Ricks

REXBURG, Idaho--A longtime faculty member and recently released LDS Church mission president has been named the new Student Life vice president at Ricks College.

Robert M. Wilkes of Rexburg will replace Vice President Mack G. Shirley who will be retiring in December.

In making the announcement Monday afternoon, Ricks President David A. Bednar stated, "Bob Wilkes is one of the most respected and admired people at Ricks College. He loves students and students love him, and as a President's Council we look forward to serving with him in blessing the lives of the students here at Ricks."

Wilkes returned in July from serving as president of the church's Wisconsin Milwaukee Mission for the past three years. Since returning to Ricks he has been teaching both religion and horsemanship classes.

As Student Life vice president, Wilkes will oversee student activities, the Manwaring Center, Campus Police, Housing Office, Dean of Students Office, Counseling Center, Health Center and Safety Office.

He has extensive service for the LDS Church where he has been a regional representative, president of the Ricks College 2nd Stake, bishop, seminary teacher, seminary area director and an institute instructor.

A native of Afton, Wyo., Wilkes attended the University of Wyoming before serving a church mission to the Central States. He graduated with a bachelor's degree from Brigham Young University and taught church institute for two years in Reno, Nev., where he supervised early morning seminaries. He worked on his master's degree at the University of Nevada and completed the degree at BYU.

He joined the faculty at Ricks College in 1970 and has served as chairman of the Department of Recreational Leadership, chairman for the Division of Religion and Family Life and chairman of the Division of Education. In 1987 he was honored by his associates with the Exemplary Faculty Award.

He and his wife, Estella, are the parents of six children, three girls and three boys, and the grandparents of 11.

Shirley has served for 35 years. He was hired in 1963 as assistant administrator for the BYU-Ricks Continuing Education Center in Idaho Falls. He was named dean of Student Life in 1971 and appointed vice president in 1978.
