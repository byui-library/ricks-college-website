WE ARE A FULL-SERVICE DOCTOR’S OFFICE FACILITY INCLUDING:

  • Lab
  • X-ray and Orthopedic Services.
  • Pharmacy

We do Mission Physicals and Pre-Marital Physicals.

We treat BYU-Idaho Students (carrying at least six credits; or three block credits) and their spouses only. We do not treat children or infants, former students, non-students, employees, or relatives. Please plan to leave your children at home or arrange for a friend to care for them in the waiting room. Thank you for your cooperation in this matter.

We do not do:

  • Regular Physicals, employment physicals, nursing, P.E., or class-required physicals.
  • Maternity care or Pediatric care
  • Immunizations--please call District Seven Health Department (208) 496-3239 for immunizations (except meningococcal, which can be provided by the Student Health Center)
  • Class required height, weight, blood pressure
  • We do not file private insurance claims or bill your parents.

Health Center Personnel

Our professional staff includes two full-time physicians, other part-time physicians and family nurse practitioners, two full-time registered nurses, a pharmacist, an x-ray technician, and a lab technologist.

Confidentiality

Anything you and your health care provider discuss will be held in strict confidence. As a general rule, your physician will release no information concerning you or your treatment to any other party, unless: 1) you grant permission; 2) the information is sought through an appropriate court order; or 3) the release of the information is required by law.
