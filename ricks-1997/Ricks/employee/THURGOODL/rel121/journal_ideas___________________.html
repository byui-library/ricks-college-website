JOURNAL IDEAS 
                                           Remember: "VARIETY IS THE SPICE OF LIFE" 

  • Look for answers to personal questions as you read the scripture block, i.e. What topic shall I speak on in my Sacrament Meeting talk? How can I be a better room mate? How can I be more tolerant? Etc.
  • Write about you learn from the scriptures. 
  • Reflect on past experiences as you read. Write about how the scriptures relate to those experiences and how the scriptural content was illustrated through your experiences. 
  • Look for present day applications with the situations you are currently facing and how the scriptures give added insight and understanding to your life. 
  • Outline the scripture block you have read for better comprehension and understanding. 
  • Write questions that come to your mind during the reading. Then look for answers. 
  • Write the question that the verse or verses seem to be answering. 
  • Write a mini-research paper in your journal as you search to find the meaning to a word or phrase or doctrine. 
  • Search the footnotes and write about what you have discovered.
