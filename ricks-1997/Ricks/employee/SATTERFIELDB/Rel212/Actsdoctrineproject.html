The Book of Acts Project


Part One

In five to seven pages (single spacing) rewrite the story line of The Book of Acts. Include the following criteria:

1. Divide your writing up according to the structure outlined in the Bible Dictionary article concerning the Acts of the Apostles.

I.   "In Jerusalem" (Acts 1-5)
II. "In Judaea and Samaria" (Acts 6-9)
      A. Story of Stephen (Using this basic structure, outline the Book of Acts. Of course, you would look for further divisions under the A, B, C, etc. This project must be between 7 to 10 pages single spaced. Use must include scripture references Acts 6-7)
     B. Philips missionary work in Samaria (Acts 8)
     C. Conversion of Saul - Paul (Acts 9)
III. "To the uttermost parts of the earth" (Acts 10-28)
      A. The revelation commanding the gospel to go to the gentiles (Acts 10-11)
      B. The gospel spreads to Asia minor (12-15)
      C. The gospel spreads to Greece and Rome (16-28)

2. Use headings throughout the rewrite to indicate your divisions.
3. Use scripture references.
 

Part Two

Throughout the Book of Acts, Luke refers to several doctrinal issues either by discussion or example. Choose five different doctrines found in Acts, including the Holy Ghost, and discuss what is learned in Acts concerning those doctrines. Also, for each doctrine you discuss, find at least one comment made by the Brethren the elucidates that doctrine.
