How do I register for a choir?

You can register for a choir at BYU-Idaho one of the following ways (choose only one):

1)     The most painless way to register for a choir at BYU-Idaho is to register under the 300 level course corresponding with the choir of your choice ie; Collegiate Singers is listed in the class schedule under MUSIC as MUS 319 Section 1.  It is available for 1 or 2 credits depending on your desire for additional credit.  These credits are counted as general elective credit toward graduation at BYU-Idaho.

2)    If you would like to have the choir count toward fulfillment of the GE-Arts requirement you should register for the class MUS100 (Intro to Music) and MUS100L Section ? (Whichever section corresponds with choir of your choice).  By looking at the Catalogue and Schedule of Courses you will notice that MUS100 is a three-credit class that only meets twice a week compared with all other Arts classes that meet three days a week.  By registering for MUS100 and MUS100L it is assumed you are putting in as much work as the class that meets three days a week.  This option should not be used if you are declaring music or other Arts-type program as your major.  Because you are in an Arts field already there are probably other required classes that will fill this requirement.

What are the advantages of participating in a Department of Music choral ensemble?

There are many valuable things gained by participating in a choral ensemble in the Department of Music:

1)    Some have said it is important to use your talents or you may lose them.  While this may be true, it is important to understand that a large part of your experience at any institution of higher learning is to develop new skills in addition to magnifying those you already possess.  Under the tutelage of highly qualified and renowned instructors, members of choral ensembles in the Department of Music are continually engaged in learning more about themselves and their ability to contribute to society in artistic expression of developing skills.

2)    As a member of a Department of Music choral ensemble you not only gain from the experience of the instructor but also are sustained by the resources of the entire department.  The focused cooperation among the music faculty at BYU-Idaho is rare.  All are concerned for your welfare and development.  Because of this cooperation, unique opportunities are provided that are often life-changing.

3)    Participating in a Department of Music choral ensemble is much more than showing up to sing each day.  Instructors take great care to plan curriculum that not only prepares for the eventual sharing of skills developed but also helps a student measure their musical and spiritual progress on a frequent basis.  Many members of the ensembles find their experience spiritual refreshment during the day.  Others find answers to questions of life through music that is selected to eliminate trivial or mundane burdens.  At BYU-Idaho, the gospel is the foundation of mission and is found at the core of the choral experience.

4)    In a society that seems to be increasingly focused on self and isolation, singing in a choral ensemble is a way to maintain contact with humanity at its best.  By its very nature, a choir is a community of people working together for the common good.  It provides an immediate social outlet for expression of beauty.  It is a shared feeling of achievement in a wholesome and uplifting way.
