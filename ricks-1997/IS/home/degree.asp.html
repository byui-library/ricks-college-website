Graduating in Information Systems

The Associate in Arts and Sciences in Information Systems (major code 244) allows a student to enter the workforce as an information professional upon graduation and/or transfer to a four-year college or university. The program is designed to meet the requirements for the American Assembly of Collegiate Schools of Business (ASCSB). Transfer students should check the program requirements of the school to which they are transferring and with an IS advisor to assure that core requirements for transfer are met.

An A.A.S. Program Planning Worksheet is available to print (Acrobat Reader required).

A BS Program Planning Worksheet is available to print (Acrobat Reader required).

Please note that this worksheet is for those student planning to graduate with a B.S. degree from BYU-Idaho. It is particularly aimed at new freshman for the Fall Semester of 2001. Those students intending to graduate with the Bachelor's degree should have a major code of 680. Junior and Senior level courses will not be available in Information Systems until Fall of 2003.

Note:

  • No "D" credit is allowed in computer courses for IS majors.
  • A keyboarding skill of 35 words or more per minute is strongly suggested.
  • Economics 111 should be taken as part of the college's American Institutions GE requirement.
  • Before taking a math class, see your advisor.
  • Students interested in an internship should register for B298.

* IS Classes

IS 203 Visual Programming - 3 credits
IS 210 OO Programming I - 3 credits
IS 235 Internet - 3 credits
IS 240 Networking - 3 credits
IS 256 Systems Analysis - 3 credits
IS 258 RPG IV Programming - 3 credits
IS 265 CASE Tools - 3 credits
Students transferring to other institutions may substitute Math 119 (Elementary Calculus) or Math 221 (Statistics) for any of the above IS classes.
