Elementary Education 150
Introduction to Education and Technology
Third Summer Term 1999

This course explores the roles and challenges of the teaching profession. While the course focuses on the Elementary school and children it is general in its approach and includes middle and high school levels as well as general education issues. The technology component of the course is designed to familiarize the student with the application of word processing, spreadsheets, databases and presentation software in the school classroom.

Objectives:

By the conclusion of this course, the student will have:

  1. Made an informed decision to pursue a course of study leading to teaching in the public school system.
  2. Explored a variety of educational issues that influence professional practice and policy in American schools.
  3. Attended school district meetings to become better informed of issues and challenges facing local schools.
  4. Conducted research and written briefs to become better informed personally of educational issues.
  5. Interviewed at least two current public school employees concerning issues and challenges of working in public schools.
  6. Planned a course of study leading to an A.A. degree in a major of her or his choice.
  7. Developed skills with primary computer applications that will be useful privately and in public school use.

Assignments:

Classroom Assignments

School Board Meeting

During the academic year you would visit public school classrooms and work with teachers and students. In as much as summer school has concluded and regular school has not begun, you will be required to attend two school board meetings rather than visiting classrooms. The two school board meetings you are required to attend are:

 July 15th 7:00 p.m. Madison Dist. Board Room 290 N. 1st East, Rexburg

July 21st 8:00 p.m. Sugar-Salem Admin. Bldg. 105 W. Center, Sugar City

While attending the meetings you are to take notes about the issues and discussions that occur. You should then develop and write a personal response to the issues as recorded. All notes and personal responses should be word processed and submitted no later than Monday, July 26th.

Weekly Research Article and Reflection Paper

Each week you are to read an article from a national educational journal. You should photocopy the article and submit it with your reaction paper. The paper should do two things: 1) reflect on the article as it relates to the topic of discussion for that week, and 2) provide a statement of effect. That is, you should say how that issue affects your decision to become a teacher. The reflection paper must be word-processed and be no longer than 1 page single spaced.

Readings and Quizzes

Each week a different topic will be covered. You are responsible to keep up with your reading assignments in the text and take the quizzes. Quizzes will be each Friday. All quizzes are worth 10 points and cannot be made up if missed.

Teacher Interviews

You are responsible to interview two currently practicing public school teachers. Interviews can be conducted face-to-face, via telephone, letter or email. You should ask specific questions about education, issues, work conditions, and anything else that you have questions about as they pertain to being an educator. Submit the questions asked, the individual's responses and a concluding statement of how the interview affected your decision to become a teacher. The interview submission must be word processed and double-spaced.

Course of Study (Two-year Plan)

You will develop a course of study outlining what classes you will need to complete, in what semester and credit loads in order to graduate from Ricks College and transfer to a 4-year institution in the major of your choice.

Video Reviews

Over the course of the class you will be asked to view four videos dealing with American education. These will be viewed on you own time. You will submit a maximum one page, word processed review of the video. Specifically the review should address what the issues are the video highlights, how the issues are influencing American education, and how your understanding of these issues is affecting you decision to be a teacher. Be sure to clearly identify the video being reviewed.

Computer Assignments

There will be a total of 10 computer assignments. Four of these will be papers turned in to meet the assignments already covered by this syllabus.

Word Processing and Graphics

There will be a total of 5 word processing assignments. Four of these accounted for by the school board report, research reviews (all count as 1 WP assignment), teacher interviews reports (both count as 1 WP assignment), and video reviews (all count as 1 WP assignment).

The fifth assignment will be a personal resume developed and submitted during the class. The resume should be professional in appearance and provide an overview of your education, work and volunteer experience, honors and awards, and references by people who can testify of your skills and performance.

Each of these assignments must contain an appropriate graphic element. The key to appropriate placements of graphics is that the graphic is of such a nature and placed such that it enhances the message rather than detracts from it.

Spreadsheets

There will be two spreadsheet assignments. The first will constitute a running tally of your grades for all courses you are taking during this term. The spreadsheet must account for each assignment by name, total value and individual score for each course being taken. In addition, a total, percentage, grade calculation must be calculated. A chart must also be developed for the spreadsheet.

The second spreadsheet assignment will be the course of study (2-year plan) discussed earlier in the syllabus. The means of development and layout will be discussed in class.

Database

You will develop a database containing the names and vital information for a hypothetical class of 30 students. Each record must contain the following data:

Full name
Address
Phone
Emergency Contact
Emergency Phone
Parent(s) Names
Medical conditions
A list of the texts, inventory number and conditions of the texts checked out to the student.
Standard Test Scores for Math, Reading, Social Studies, Science for the current year
Standard Test Scores for Math, Reading, Social Studies, Science for the previous year
Calculation of change in standard scores from previous to current year

The database must also contain two forms in addition to the standard view. Form one is a mailing label and form two is a word processing form letter that captures and uses information from the database to notify the child's parents of an upcoming school event.

Presentations

You will develop two PowerPoint presentations. The first will be autobiographical in nature. The presentation may either highlight a singular event or achievement that you would like to highlight or it may provide a general overview of your life. The second will be a presentation developed around one of the weekly research reviews. It should cover the same general information as the review; however, it should include graphics and be formatted such that it would be appropriate to present to an audience.
