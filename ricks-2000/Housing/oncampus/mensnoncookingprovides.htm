Men's Non-Cooking Style Housing Provides:

 
  • Two twin size beds with stilts per bedroom
  • A vinyl mattress cover for each bed
  • Two closets that can be locked with a padlock if desired
  • Six drawers beneath each closet
  • Two desks per room with holes drilled for computer cords
  • Bulletin board by each desk
  • One telephone per apartment (long distance cards can be purchased at the Cashiers Office)
  • Sink and mirror in the room
  • Microfridge (microwave, small refrigerator/freezer unit)
  • Bathroom facilities on each floor, including three showers and one bathtub
  • Vacuum on each floor
  • TV/VCR in the main lounge and TV in upper lounge
  • Ping Pong table
  • Foosball table
  • Piano in the main lounge and practice room in the basement
  • Laundry facilities - washers 75 cents/load; dryers 25 cents/30 minuets (quarters only)
  • Ironing board and iron in laundry room
  • New kitchenette is available in the basement for occasional cooking.  Dishes and silverware may be checked out from the head resident.
