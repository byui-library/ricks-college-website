Many seem uncomfortable with the idea of nurturing and developing the inner kingdom.  It seems almost selfish.  But as a wise man (Parker Palmer) once said, 

"Self-care is never a selfish act-- it simply is good stewardship of the only gift I have, the gift I was put on the earth to offer to others."  My self.

How many times did Christ retire by himself?  How often did he go off to commune with his Father in prayer, meditation and solitude?  Do we engage in such?  This page is dedicated to the idea that our inner kingdom is what our spirituality is all about.  The scriptures teach  many wonderful truths about the nature of who we are and of our relationship to Christ... but it seems we are limited by our culture's paradigms to see them clearly.

What is your take on the following scripture?

"THEREFORE IT IS GIVEN TO ABIDE IN YOU; THE RECORD OF HEAVEN; THE COMFORTER, THE PEACEABLE THINGS OF IMMORTAL GLORY; THE TRUTH OF ALL THINGS; THAT WHICH QUICKENETH ALL THINGS, WHICH MAKETH ALIVE ALL THINGS; THAT WHICH KNOWETH ALL THINGS AND HATH ALL POWER ACCORDING TO WISDOM, MERCY, TRUTH, JUSTICE AND JUDGMENT." MOSES 6:61

The Indwelling is a gospel reality that acknowledges that the Gospel and Spirit of Christ resides in us and dwells there in a much more substantial way than what is typically understood by the western industrialized mind set.  Thanks to Newtonian Physics- most believe matter is solid and no two things can occupy the same space. The physics that came out of the middle ages isolated us in a dead mechanical world of things.  Even with each other, we feel frustrated with the commandment to love our neighbor as our self, feeling separate and alone.    Quantum Physics, on the other hand, opens up new paradigms of seeing with new eyes.  The potential nature of quantum reality with it's new  intriguing metaphors to explain are exciting. 

Here is one example: 

 D&C 93:3  "And that I am in the Father, and the Father in me, and the Father and I are one."

The mind set of Newtonian Physics would explain this scripture as a oneness of purpose or agreement.  A oneness of goal oriented behavior and endeavors... the language of 'in' and 'one' can only be understood (by a Newtonian mind set) to be symbolic- since no two things can occupy the same space.

The mind set of Quantum Physics would use the example of a Bose- Einstein Condensate to illustrate that parts of an ordered system that not only behave as a whole, they become whole.  Their identities merge or overlap in such a way that they lose their individuality to the wholeness.  The Bose-Einstein Condensate creates a paradigm where in we can start to apprehend the idea of entities sharing  properties and information that, although bizarre and strange to our Newtonian conditioned mind sets, actually vibration-ally merge and become 'one'.

The Divine Nature: The light, life, way, truth and law of Christ

Whereby are given unto us exceeding great  promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the     divine nature... II Peter 1:4                                                       

"And the light which shineth, which giveth you light, is through him who enlighteneth your eyes, which is the same light that quickeneth your understandings; which light proceedeth forth from the presence of God to fill the immensity of space-- the light which is in all things, which giveth life to all things, which is the law by which all things are governed, even the power of God who sitteth upon his throne, who is in the bosom of eternity, who is in the midst of all things."                                         Doctrine and Covenants 88:11-13

This is a marvelous description of the Divine Nature of God, the consciousness that gives life and that governs all.

Isn't it interesting that Christ said: "I give unto you these sayings that you may understand and know how to worship and know what you worship, that you may come unto the Father in my name, and in due time receive of his fullness."    D&C 93:19

Now the interesting implication is that we worship a "what"... a system of consciousness, power, light, life, truth and law that we can receive a fullness of if we but come unto the Giver of that Divine Nature- The Father through Christ.

Light, Life and Law

I want to explore the implication of several ideas in this paragraph, focusing specifically upon the light, life and law:

"And the light which shineth, which giveth you light, is through him who enlighteneth your eyes, which is the same light that quickeneth your understandings; which light proceedeth forth from the presence of God to fill the immensity of space-- the light which is in all things, which giveth life to all things, which is the law by which all things are governed, even the power of God who sitteth upon his throne, who is in the bosom of eternity, who is in the midst of all things."  Doctrine and Covenants 88:11-13

My take on this scripture is that the light and life and law are all connected and are within us ... let me explain. 

I think it is pretty self evident that the 'light' and 'life' of Christ are within us and that we can enjoy more of both if we Come Unto Christ.  The interesting implication is found with the 'law' being inside of us too.

There is a Law... (singular not plural)

"There is a law, irrevocably decreed in heaven before the foundations of this world, upon which all blessings are predicated--" D&C 130:20

There is a Law and the Law is Christ.                                                             "Behold, I am the law, and the light."      3rd Nephi 15:12

Christ was foreordained before the foundations of the world.                                                            "But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot:  Who verily was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you,"    I Peter 1:19-20

The typical mechanistic  interpretation of Doctrine and Covenants 130: 20-21, relies upon the scientific paradigm of Newtonian physics and sees law as eternal principles of mechanical truth- you do this and this will occur in response. 

Another interpretation of Quantum physics would embrace the idea that intelligence is a manifestation of consciousness (light and life) and would see law as simply being true/congruent/indigenous to that 'Christ Nature' that is in all things...

"And behold all things have their likeness, and all things are created and made to bear record of me, both things which are temporal, and things which are spiritual; things which are in the heavens above, and things which are in the earth, and things which are in the earth, and things which are under the earth, both above and beneath: all things bear record of me."   Moses :63

In this paradigm, Christ as Light, Life, Law & Truth would reside in us as an indwelling state.  Thus, fulfilling the promise made to us in Doctrine and Covenants 93:22...

"And all those who are begotten through me are partakers of the glory of the same, and are the church of the Firstborn."

The Inner Kingdom and Garden...

When one awakens to the reality of the Inner Kingdom and what it is that resides there-- the spirit, light, life, truth and law of Christ-- maybe there will be less resistance to nurture and tend it's garden.  We need to explore new paradigms that invite introspection, inspiration, and intuition of the heart.

 "...written not with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God; not in tables of stone, but in fleshy tables of the heart." II Corinthians 3:3

The changes that need to occur in our lives are not found in the outer garment of social acceptability but rather in the inner chambers of the soul where the tensions build between surrendering our will to the light, life and law of Christ.

"Therefore is any man (woman) be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."  II Corinthians 5:17

To nurture and attend to the inner kingdom is the essence of the spiritual life.

 

Why do people stay out of the Inner Kingdom?

Many children become fragmented and lost growing up.  They get pushed off the path of selfhood by well meaning but wounded people who become parents before they learn to heal from their own woundings.  The loss of Sanctuary (access to the inner kingdom) is precipitated by process trauma which desecrates the Sacred and Holy places with in a child's soul, namely their mind and heart.  If a child is made to feel unworthy, shame, fear or contempt for themselves by not measuring up to outside expectations then they will focus more and more energy upon performing for the outer kingdom (parents, adults, significant others).  Over time the child learns to live outside themselves and to focus upon the reactions of others.  Their own realities, spiritual and visceral, will be ignored in the exchange for acceptance and approval.

This lose of self will introduce outer products, sensations, relationships and process that will be used in attempting to fill the void left in their hearts when they abandoned the Sanctuary.  These outer realities will begin the process of addiction where over time the person will use them, more and more to escape their own feelings of anxiety, sadness, pain and disgust.  Moral agency will slowly dissolve and they will find themselves engaging in behaviors and activities that  will mood alter.  Some addictions will be contemptible to them (food, sexual, drugs, pleasing others) and some will receive the support and approval from the culture at large (i.e. work, shopping, success, control).  

Addictions are a state of spiritual bankruptcy and will become the source of a home grown religion with rituals, and gods we turn to in times of trouble.  Addictions will cut you off from your own self, body and spirit.  Addictions  will cut you off from other people, especially those that you say you love the most. Addictions will cut yourself off from your God.   Addictions will reek havoc in your life and eventually, over time, will spiritually and physically lead to death.

Addictions are well enough known and understood by the culture at large to be destructive and hurtful.  How many drug, alcohol, sex, work, eating, shopping, adrenaline, sugar, nicotine, caffeine, religious, romance, relationship junkies exist in the world to day.  All are just trying to survive by pursuing the things that can never satisfy the emptiness and void within.

In the mean time the inner kingdom is growing more and more desolate and barren.  It is time to return home and attend to the Garden.  It is time to suffer the energies of neglect, enmeshment and abandonment.  It is time to grow up and differentiate.  It is past time to confront the self and start on the spiritual adventure of reclaiming the sanctuary and inner kingdom.  The gift of self has too long been unopened.
