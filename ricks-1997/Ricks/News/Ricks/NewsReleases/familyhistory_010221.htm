Ricks College News Release
February 21, 2001

Family History Conference
to be held at Ricks

REXBURG, Idaho — Beginning and advanced genealogists are invited to participate in the Ricks College Spring Family History Conference Saturday, March 10, from 8 to 5 p.m.

"New this conference are four classes that will be hands-on computer classes that deal with the Internet," says Gloria Bradshaw, Continuing Education secretary and registrar for the event. "Some of the classes from last time were popular and are being represented. Others were adjusted after suggestions by patrons and participants."

Some topics include Getting Started, Internet Basic Tools, Personal Ancestral File 4.04, Familysearch, Using and Editing the Ancestral File, My Family Online, LDS Vital Record Indexes, Research Using CD Data Files, Pedigree Resource File, Mormon Emigration Index and Journals, Family Tree Maker, U.S. Research, English Research and Census Searches on the Net.

Class sizes are limited, so pre-registration is recommended, but not required. The pre-registration fee is $14. Registration and fees will be accepted until March 7.

After March 7, participants may sign up at the morning of the conference in the Smith Building foyer beginning at 7:30 a.m. The fee will be $18 at the door. Sign-up on the day of will be on a space available basis.

"Some of the classes are in lecture halls and will have a lot of space, others fill up rather quickly; so we encourage participants to hurry," Bradshaw says.

Lunch will not be provided, but food will be available in the Manwaring Center for those who would like to stay on campus for lunch.

The semi-annual event draws in more than 200 participants and is co-sponsored by the Ricks College Family History Center and Continuing Education Department.

For more information, contact the Continuing Education Department at (208) 356-2336 or 356-1040, or e-mail bradshawg@ricks.edu.
