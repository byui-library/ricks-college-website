Web Site Criteria

Your web site should be a virtual reflection of a working elementary level classroom. What you do in the regular classroom should be reflected on your web site. As such, the following items should be found in your site:

Due Date: Saturday, April 17th!

Parental contact information

  • How and when could a parent contact you?

Personal Page

  • Who you are
  • Some Information about you (Not too much and not too personal)

Homework Information

  • List of assignments
  • Due dates
  • Model assignments or examples?
  • Resources available to assist the student?

Lesson Materials

  • Lesson Plans (Sample Lesson Plan Format in PDF format)
  • Online Presentations (Powerpoint or Hyperstudio)
  • Handouts (PDF files for printing)

Graphics

  • Digital Camera
  • Scanned
  • Self-developed

Curriculum Materials

  • Links to learning resources
  • Resources to a variety of curriculum areas (e.g. math, spelling, science, etc...)
  • Teaching presentations
  • A minimum of two powerpoint presentations
  • A minimum of one Hyperstudio stack.

In addition, design considerations should be followed:

  • Easy navigation
  • Consistent use of color and style
  • Links work
  • Pictures function (no broken graphics)
