FINES


For your convenience, We have listed some of the most common violations that we target while on patrol to make Ricks Campus and the City of Rexburg a safer environment for you. There are three levels of punishment that are given by the State of Idaho for violations of the law. These are Infractions, Misdemeanors, and Felonies. We have divided this page into two sections, Traffic Fines and Misdemeanors. The majority of Traffic fines are infractions.  An infraction is a Civil public offense not constituting a crime punishable by a penalty not exceeding a $100.00  and for which no incarceration may be imposed.  A felony is punishable with  death or imprisonment in a state prison. Every other crime is a misdemeanor. Fines are paid at the Madison County Court house in Rexburg Idaho. It is located on the Northwest corner of Main street and 2nd East Street. We hope that you obey traffic rules and regulations and are exemplary in your behavior so that you never have to visit the Madison County Jail or Court house while in Rexburg.
