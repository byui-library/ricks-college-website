Alumni Chapters
Regional Alumni Chapters offer a local association of alumni and friends who care about preserving the legacy and spirit of Ricks College and support the future of BYU-Idaho.  Our chapters and alumni provide valuable resources for local communities and for the university.  If you are interested in being involved in a local alumni chapter, please contact your Regional Alumni Director.  Please check back here often as more chapters are established.
