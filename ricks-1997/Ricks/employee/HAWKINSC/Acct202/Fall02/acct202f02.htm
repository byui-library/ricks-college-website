Ethics Case:  The grade assigned to the following ethics case will be determined by the student’s recognition of accounting and ethical issues involved, logical development of response, and grammar and presentation skills.  The length should be approximately one type-written page.

 

 

GA Company

 

The top management of GA is well known for “managing by the numbers.”  With an eye on the company’s desired growth in overall net profit, the company’s CEO (chief executive officer) sets target profits at the beginning of the year for each of the company’s divisions.  The CEO has stated her policy as follows:  “I won’t interfere with operations in the divisions.  I am available for advice, but the division vice presidents are free to do anything they want so long as they hit the target profits for the year.”

 

In November, Stan, the vice president of the Cellular Division, saw that making the current year’s target profit for his division was going to be difficult.  Among other actions, he directed that discretionary expenditures be delayed until the beginning of the new year.  On December 30, he was angered to discover that a warehouse clerk had ordered $350,000 of telephone parts earlier in December even though the parts weren’t really needed by the assembly department until January or February.  Contrary to common accounting practice, the GA Accounting Policy Manual states that such parts are to be recorded as an expense when delivered.  To avoid recording the expense, Stan asked that the order be canceled, but the purchasing department reported that the parts had already been delivered and the supplier would not accept returns.  Since the bill had not yet been paid, Stan asked the accounting department to correct the clerk’s mistake by delaying recognition of the delivery until the bill is paid in January.

 

1.                  Are Stan’s actions ethical?  Explain why or why not.

 

2.               Do the general management philosophy and accounting policies at GA encourage or discourage ethical behavior?  Explain.
