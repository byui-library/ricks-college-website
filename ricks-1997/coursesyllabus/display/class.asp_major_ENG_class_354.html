NON-FICTION
CLASS CODE: ENG 354 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: LANGUAGE & LETTERS
DEPARTMENT: ENGLISH
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course fulfills a General Education - Letters requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes intensive reading, writing and study of non-fiction works by major authors. Includes genre such as history, philosophy, speeches, journals, reviews, columns, letters, biographies, etc.
DESCRIPTION: Emphasizes intensive reading, writing and study of non-fiction works by major authors. Includes genre such as history, philosophy, speeches, journals, reviews, columns, letters, biographies, etc.
TOPICS: Analyzing, critiquing, appreciating and enjoying non-fiction writing.
OBJECTIVES: (1) Evaluating good and bad writing. (2) Interpreting symbolism, inferences, assumptions, reasoning. (3) Synthesizing. (4) Drawing from thematic sources to write a paper. (5) Analyzing, critically reading and writing about strength of essays. (6) Reporting/reviewing. (7) Summarizing or paraphrasing- extracting main idea and support.
REQUIREMENTS: Extensive reading of non-fiction authors in various sub-genres. Complete and demonstrate understanding of a significant amount of reading. Complete one research project which will include an oral presentation. May include 1-2 major papers, projects or presentations.
