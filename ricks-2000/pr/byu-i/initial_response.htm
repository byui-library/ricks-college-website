The Transition: Initial response of President Bednar

The official announcement was linked from Salt Lake City to Ricks College via an audio feed to an audience of approximately 2,000 gathered in the Hart Auditorium.  The transcript tells what happened at the conclusion of the broadcast:

President Bednar to audience at Ricks College: Now it would be appropriate to cheer.

(Audience responded with great cheers and applause.)

I know that I am a college president and that I should use good English, but . . . "I never would have thunk it." I would have been less surprised if the announcement would have been that the school would be closed. Those of you who have been here many, many years know that we are well versed and well schooled in articulating all the reasons why Ricks College would not become a four-year school. And we have all just changed our minds very quickly.

I want to make just a few comments before Brother Wilkes offers a benediction and we get you back to classes.

First, today at 5 o'clock in the Taylor Chapel, the members of the President's Council will be available to answer your questions. If you come with great expectations for specific answers, you will be sorely disappointed. You may frequently hear us repeat, "We are not sure yet." Please do not be frustrated with us. But we do want to hear your questions, and they will be of assistance to us.

I think it is important to characterize this announcement as initiating a process of transition. This is not announcing a finished product. Would you please keep in mind that today the President of the Church and Chairman of the Board of Trustees announced the beginning of a transition. We would remind you that this is a Board-directed initiative. This was a directive from the Board.

Now, given some of the very natural questions and concerns that all of us will have as we move forward, please know that the President's Council will continue to be very open and forthright in telling you what we know, when we know it, when we can. Please do not speculate and feed the rumor mill. I would offer a suggestion: If you hear something from someone who heard that thing from someone else, do not believe it. You will get firsthand information from the people involved in this process. We will be, as we have tried to be for a number of years, readily available to answer questions and provide information. Please do not speculate on what this means. We will be up front. We will articulate that process and the criteria that will be used in the evaluations that will be done.

The last thing I would say is that in the excitement of this moment, I hope we can grasp, both spiritually and historically, what we just heard. I do not think it is overly dramatic to suggest that this is one of the biggest steps in the Church Educational System in the last 50 years. This is a very significant event. It should be a detailed journal entry for all of us.

The other thing I would add is, though we cannot see what the members of the Board may see, we can strive to obtain that vision. This move is clearly a move for a new century and a new millennium. This move is oriented toward the future. Please seek spiritual understanding of that vision, and I promise it will be provided for all of us as we move forward. I so testify in the name of Jesus Christ, amen.
