OUTDOOR LEARNING

CENTER WORKSHOPS

FOR AFY

 

Horseback Riding

The basic techniques will be covered. This will include safety, grooming, bridling,

saddling, mounting, and riding. After the group is ready, they will go on a horse ride

around the OLC property.  (2 hours)

 

Canoeing

Under the direction of the staff, the participants will learn how to get in and out of a

canoe, the various strokes, parts of the canoe, safety, water rescue, etc. Toward the

end of the workshop, there may be some free time to paddle or be involved in a

competition course. (2 hours)

 

Ropes Course - A/ Inclined/Horizontal Log

This is a high element ropes activity.  The participants have the opportunity of walking up an inclined log.  Upon reaching the top they step onto the horizontal log and walk across.  The participants are then lowered to the ground using a belay system.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be used. (2 hrs.) 

 

Ropes Course - B/ Climbing Tower

This high element ropes activity consists of climbing the face of a 35-foot high wall. 

The participant will learn climbing techniques to aid in their ascent.  Helmet, harness,

and climbing ropes will be used.

 

Ropes Course - D/ Power Pole

This is a high ropes activity which involve a great deal of commitment.  Participants will

have the challenge of climbing a vertical pole and standing on top, afterwards jumping

out to touch a hanging tennis ball.  Helmet, harness, and climbing ropes will be used.

 

Initiative Course #1

This course has approximately ten events which will challenge the thinking of those

involved.   There are various obstacles, tasks, and problems to solve, sometimes using

equipment, while other times not being able to speak to one another. This is an

excellent workshop for building teamwork, unity, trust, self esteem, communication, etc.

(2 hours)
