STUDENT FINANCIAL AID

Financial aid programs have been established to help students pay the cost of attending college. It is awarded on the basis of financial need.

The process of determining a student's eligibility for financial aid is called need analysis. In a need analysis, income and asset information from both student and parents (or student and spouse, if married) is examined to determine the family's ability to pay educational costs. This figure, called the family contribution, is subtracted from what it will cost the student to attend Ricks College for a specific length of time (typically, one academic year). If the cost of education is more than the family can pay, the student has financial need.

Ricks College uses the government need analysis form, Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), in determining how much the student and family should pay. All students applying for financial aid must complete this form. The application is not complete until the results of the FAFSA have been received by the Financial Aid Office.

The FAFSA forms are available from high school counselors, the Ricks College Financial Aid Office or any other college or university. Continuing students who filed for federal aid during the previous school year will receive a renewal application in late November or early December. In this case, only ONE of the forms should be used, the FAFSA OR the Renewal Application, not both. Do not submit an application to the federal processor prior to January 1.

Applying for financial aid is complicated and time consuming. Considerable delays and frustrations can arise from filling out forms incorrectly or failing to submit the proper documents in a timely manner. Students and their parents are encouraged to read instructions carefully and to complete all forms and documents correctly. Professional counselors in the Financial Aid Office are available and eager to assist in answering questions and helping with the application process.

FINANCIAL AID HANDBOOK
The Financial Aid Handbook, available at the Financial Aid Office, provides additional information concerning student financial aid. Students are encouraged to obtain a copy if they are interested in receiving financial aid.

GENERAL ELIGIBILITY
To be eligible for federal financial aid a student must:

1. Be enrolled as a regular student in an eligible program;
2. Be a U.S. Citizen or an eligible noncitizen;
3. Be making satisfactory academic progress toward a degree or certificate as defined in the Financial Aid Handbook;
4. Not owe a refund on any previous federal grant or be in default on any federal loan received for attendance at any school;
5. Be registered with the Selective Service, if male, over 18 and born on or after January 1, 1960, and not currently in the U.S. Armed Forces;
6. Be a high school graduate or have a G.E.D. certificate;
7. Adhere to the policies of individual programs.
Financial aid recipients who withdraw from Ricks College or drop credits and are due a refund will have the refund returned to the appropriate financial aid program. They may also be required to repay a portion of the aid they received in cash.

FEDERAL PROGRAMS
FEDERAL PELL GRANTS
Federal Pell Grants are non-repayable grants from the federal government. Students eligible for these grants have demonstrated financial need with the use of the FAFSA as described previously.

Pell grant funds will be credited to the student's account after the student is enrolled and attending classes and, if the student is selected for verification, the verification procedure has been completed. The funds will be credited in the following order: tuition, fees, books, supplies and campus housing. Any remaining amount will be available to the student at the Cashier's Office.

If students desire a payment for less than full-time enrollment they must request it at the Financial Aid Office by signing an acknowledgment that this amount is their final payment for the semester/term. Adding classes later (block plan, for example) will not increase an award.

FEDERAL FAMILY EDUCATION LOANS INFORMATION

FEDERAL STAFFORD STUDENT LOANS
First year students meeting federal guidelines may borrow up to $2,625 per academic year and second year students may borrow up to $3,500. Loan proceeds will be delivered to students in a minimum of two disbursements (generally one each semester/ term). Students entering the first year of a program who are first time borrowers will have their initial disbursement delayed 30 days to comply with federal regulations. Late fees which accrue for tuition, fees and books will be reversed.

Students must attend an Entrance Interview before they receive their first Stafford Loan check. They must also attend an Exit Interview as they leave Ricks College. They will be informed of the time and place of these interviews when Ricks College processes the loan application or they may obtain the interview schedule from Financial Aid Office.

Loan application may be obtained from participating lenders or at the Financial Aid Office. A Loan Supplement Form, available at the Financial Aid Office, must accompany the loan application.

SUBSIDIZED STAFFORD LOAN
Principal payments are postponed and the interest is paid by the federal government while students are enrolled in school at least half time and during their grace period.

UNSUBSIDIZED STAFFORD LOAN
Monthly principal payments are postponed while students are enrolled in school at least half time and during their grace period. Students are responsible for the interest that accrues on their loan. Students can either make interest payments or they can request that the lender add the accrued interest to the principal balance. This will result in a higher cost to the student as interest is then charged on the interest added to the balance.

ADDITIONAL UNSUBSIDIZED LOAN
This loan is available to independent students and is similar to the unsubsidized loan.

FEDERAL PARENT LOANS FOR UNDERGRADUATE STUDENTS (PLUS)
Parents of dependent undergraduate students may borrow up to the student's cost of attendance minus any other financial aid awarded for the period of enrollment covered by the loan. Loan proceeds will be delivered to the parents in a minimum of two disbursements (generally one each semester/term). Repayment of principal and interest begins within 60 days of disbursement.

PRIORITY DEADLINES
In order for the Financial Aid Office to award students and make disbursements at the beginning of Fall Semester, the following submission deadlines have been established at Ricks College:
MAY 1 - Federal Pell Grants

The Financial Aid Office must receive the data contained in the Student Aid Report produced by the Department of Education from the FAFSA submitted by the student.

MAY 1 - Federal Family
Education Loans

The Financial Aid Office must receive a loan application in addition to the data contained in the Student Aid Report. A Loan Supplement Form, available at the Financial Aid Office, must accompany the loan application.

JUNE 1 - Verification

The Financial Aid Office must receive all requested documentation for students selected for verification.

Students not meeting the above deadlines will not be denied aid. However, funding may not be available until later in the school year.

BUREAU OF INDIAN AFFAIRS GRANTS
To be eligible, the student must:

1. Be at least 1/4 Indian Blood;
2. Be registered with a tribe that has a treaty with the U.S.;
3. Have a U.S. census number and Certificate of Indian Blood;
4. First apply for all other types of aid and demonstrate a need for assistance.

Interested students should request necessary forms and information from their tribal agency.

When the student has been accepted for admission and the Financial Aid Office has received the data contained in the Student Aid Report AND the other requested documentation, the Financial Aid Office will forward an award recommendation to the tribal agency. All details must be completed prior to the student's arrival on campus.

TRANSFER FROM RICKS COLLEGE
Financial aid awards do NOT automatically transfer from one school to another when a student transfers to a different school during an academic year. Students who transfer, and desire their financial aid awards to move with them, must contact the Financial Aid Office of their new school. Further information is available at the Ricks College Financial Aid Office.
