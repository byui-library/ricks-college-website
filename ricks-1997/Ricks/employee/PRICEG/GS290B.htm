Course Description
Leadership in the “Apartment Home” will be the focus of this class. Subjects will include:
(1) Apartment Mission Statement initiative, (2) Doctrine and Principles of Home, Family, & Leadership as found in the scriptures and family proclamation. (3) Applying true doctrine and principles to change behavior (Hold Fast). (4) L•E•A•D Behavior Dynamics. (5) The Principle of Stewardship.
Students will work together to understand leadership principles that, if used, will invite the spirit of the Holy Ghost into the apartment “home” environment. Students will have the opportunity and responsibility to learn, teach and live these principles.

Objectives
• Understand the doctrines and principle relative to “leadership in the home.” Identify specific behaviors and applications.
• Learn to provide leadership in the apartment “home” and help students articulate and apply their life goals.
• Discover together the various applications of leadership inherent in having the spirit of the Holy Ghost in the apartment home environment.

Course Procedures
This pilot approach requires all students in an apartment to sign up if any students in that apartment register. Registration is with the approval of the instructor. A three-level student contact approach will be used: (1) Meet together with the whole class twice during the semester. (2) On a rotating basis meet with an apartment representative and teach them a leadership concept which in turn will be taught by them to the other members of their apartment (each student will have the opportunity and responsibility to learn and teach at least one concept). (3) Internet contact will be maintained between each student and the instructor. After the designated student has received instruction they will need to go back to the apartment and teach those principles. Students will have one week to post, on the internet message board, a response to the things learned including their application.

Grading Requirements (applies only to the Ricks College Apartments)

  • Grades will depend upon the following:
    • Participation and success in the “Student Teaching Students” approach both as a teacher and learner. The student assigned to teach will be, in part, evaluated on the understanding of the roommates as they respond (via the internet) to the learning experience.
    • Participation on the internet message board.
    • Attendance at the two class gatherings and at the apartment learning experiences.
