INSTRUCTIONS FOR SUMMER CAMP REGISTRATIONS

1.  You have 2 options at the Student Log On Screen:

A-If you have registered for a Continuing Education class or camp within the past year,

just enter your social security number and password and click Log On.

B-If you have not registered through the Continuing Education system within the past year, click on New Student, fill in all the blanks on the 'New Student Log On" page then

click Log On.

 

2.  You will see a "Welcome your name" screen, click on Continue.

 

3.  At "Option Selection" page you will see the name of the camp you are registering for.

Click on Select if you want on-campus housing during the camp.  To request a roommate, you need to enter their social security #.  Then click on Register.

    (If you select housing you will see a Scheduling Messages screen that confirms your housing selection.  Click Continue)

 

4.  This page shows what you have registered for (you may also add the Housing option from this page).  Click in the Pay boxes, it will show a check mark, then scroll down and click on Complete Registration.

 

5.  Select your payment option, enter the information requested, then click on Process.

 

6.  Please print the "Receipt of Payment" screen so you have a record of this transaction.  Then click on Continue.

 

7.  An insurance information page comes up here; it will print out in our office.  This screen must be totally filled out for your child to be registered in any camp.

 

8.  Your Letter of Acceptance, Camp Schedule, and Campus map will appear next; please

print this.

 

You are complete!

 

Continue Registering
