Course Objectives:  Enhance our ability to Come Unto Christ by studying and applying the teachings of the Old Testament.

Texts:  Complete set of LDS standard works.

Grading:  There will be approximately 10 quizzes throughout the semester on the reading, lecture notes and terms to know.  They will be approximately 10 - 15 questions each.  There will also be a midterm and a final both of which comprise of 50 questions your reading, class lectures and terms to know.  They are worth 100 points each.

                                                                                         93% - 100% = A
                                                                                         90% - 92%  = A-
                 Midterm 100 pts. = 100 pts.                             87% - 89%  = B+
            Quizzes 10@10 pts. = 100 pts.                             83% - 86%  = B
                      Final 100 pts. = 100 pts.                             80% - 82%  = B-
                       Total points = 300 pts.                             77% - 79%  = C+, etc.
These points are approximate!

***ABSENCE POLICY
School Excused Absences: Students who miss class due to a school excused absence must clear that absence with the teacher prior to the day(s) missed. A written permission slip signed by the proper instructor or advisor must be brought to the teacher and a plan to make up the missed materials and quizzes will be discussed. It is the student's responsibility to arrange for the missed materials.
Illnesses or Family/Personal Emergencies: If a student misses class because of illness or a family or personal emergency, the quiz that is missed may only be made up if that student calls my voice mail the day they miss or as soon as possible (before the next class day) and clears the absence. Leave your full name, the class and section you are in, and the reason for missing class. The student must find someone to get the notes missed and be prepared to take the quiz missed by the next class period, along with the current class quiz.
