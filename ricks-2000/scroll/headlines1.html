Suspect pleads not guilty to I.F., BYU-Idaho shootings

by Andrea Scott
sco00003@byui.edu
News Editor

Briggs
The man accused of killing his ex-fiancee and shooting a BYU-Idaho student pleaded not guilty to first degree murder, two accounts of aggravated battery and eluding the police Monday at an arraignment at the Bonneville County Courthouse at 1:30 p.m.

Todd Robert Briggs allegedly shot and killed his ex-fiancee Sunday morning in Idaho Falls and is suspected of shooting Maria Ireland, a freshman from Payette, Idaho, in the leg later Sunday. Ireland found him in her Rexburg apartment while Briggs was trying to elude the police.

Briggs, 39, is suspected of hitting Melissa Garcia with a rental car as she was leaving work in Idaho Falls around 9 p.m. Thursday. Garcia reportedly broke off the couple’s three-month engagement recently and had received threatening messages from her ex-fiancee. She suffered a broken leg and cuts and bruises and was released from the hospital later.

Garcia was at her mother’s home Sunday morning when a man entered the unlocked door, found her in the bathroom and shot her once in the heart, killing her. The man then drove away in the rental car.

A 17-year-old sibling found Garcia on the bathroom floor and called the police.

Ireland
A caller contacted police at the scene of the murder from the Grand Teton Mall around 1 p.m. to ask if she was dead yet, Lt. Steve Roos of the Idaho Falls Police Detectives said. Police arrived at the mall in time to see Briggs leave in his vehicle.

Briggs fled Idaho Falls, heading northbound on Highway 20 with state and county police in pursuit, Captain Randy Lewis of the Rexburg Police Department said. He exited at Rexburg and turned down Main Street going “a hundred plus miles per hour,” Lewis said. An officer followed the car, then lost sight of it temporarily.

Briggs entered an off-campus apartment complex on S. Center to escape detection from the pursuing police. Ireland returned shortly before 2 p.m. from a church assignment to find the man hiding in her bedroom.

“Initial investigation indicates that he grabbed the girl, put a gun to her head, and she immediately grabbed his arm,” Lewis said. A struggle ensued, and the small caliber handgun went off, hitting her in the leg.

Ireland’s roommate, playing the flute in another bedroom, heard the commotion, and the two girls fled the apartment. They called 911 from a neighbor’s apartment while Briggs left the scene.

Police arrived and followed the suspect. Members of the state, Fremont and Madison counties, Rexburg and campus police were in pursuit of Briggs at the time of his apprehension, Sergeant Ron Larsen of the Rexburg Police Department said.

Ireland was transported to Madison Memorial Hospital by ambulance. She underwent surgery to remove the bullet 7:30 a.m. Monday.

Briggs was taken into custody after his vehicle clipped a truck and crashed just short of the Montana state line.

A preliminary court hearing at the Bonneville County Courthouse Nov. 5 at 1:30 p.m.
