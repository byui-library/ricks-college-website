Welcome to the KNOW YOUR RELIGION program.  We are excited about the prospects of sharing another outstanding series with you.  Please take time now to review this brochure and schedule the KNOW YOUR RELIGION lectures you would like to attend.  The object of the KNOW YOUR RELIGION program is to provide thought-provoking and informative lectures on religious subjects which will enrich, supplement and strengthen the regular programs of the Church.  The presentations are intended to build and strengthen testimonies of the Savior.
