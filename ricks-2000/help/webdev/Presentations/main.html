Before you start -

Make sure you have:
  • Power Point 2000 (click here to learn how to check)
  • "Web Authoring Tools (HTML)" installed (click here to learn how to check)
If you have an older version of PPT or you are missing the Web Authoring Tools, contact your CCS.    (Click here if you're unsure how to contact your CCS)

 Getting started -

Step One: Saving the file

  1. Open the Presentation you wish to convert.

  2. Click on the FILE Menu and then click "Save as Web Page..."

     


Step Two: Choosing your folder

  1. Choose your Omni directory from the drop down menu.

  2. Choose the folder you wish to save your pages under.
 


Step Three: Linking to your page

  1. Open your the page you want the link on in FrontPage.

  2. Highlight the text you want made into the link.

  3. On your menu bar, click "Insert" then "hyperlink.

     

  4. To find your presentation, click the icon on the pop up window that looks like a folder with a magnifying glass in front of it.

     

  5. A new pop-up window will appear. Find your presentation (saved on your Omni directory), highlight it, and click "OK."
