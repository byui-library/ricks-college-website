L & R Biddulph Scholarship

The English department offers one half-tuition scholarship:

The L & R Biddulph endowment funds one half-tuition scholarship for a sophomore English major

who demonstrates academic achievement and interpretive skills. 

Obtain a scholarship application at the English department office (Smith Annex)

at the beginning of October; a completed application and an interpretive

essay of an assigned poem must be submitted to the English department office

by the first Monday in November. 

The scholarship will be awarded to a student for the following Winter semester.
