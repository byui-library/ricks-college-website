Our Mission
The Business Management Department's Mission is to create a learning environment for students to acquire the knowledge, skills, and experiences necessary to make an immediate contribution in the workplace and to rapidly grow into leadership positions in their organizations.


Our Vision
Graduating students will emerge with an innovative and entrepreneurial spirit with technological and global literacy, as well as key teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Our students will bring honor to the school through their integrity and success.
