COURSE SYLLABUS

Department:  Home Economics
Course#: Cloth 135
Title: Intermediate Clothing Construction Credit: 2
Text: The Complete Book of Sewing

Course Objective:
 The major goal of this course is to help students acquire a working knowledge of sewing skills at an intermediate level on such specialty fabrics as naps, plaids, suedes and bulk.  Upon completion of this course the student should be able to:
 1.  Demonstrate construction skills on specialty fabrics
 2. Apply specific construction techniques for various design principles
 3.  Derive solutions to construction problems
  4.  Select the appropriate needle, thread, interfacing, and stitch for different fabrics

Description:
Intermediate sewing course involving construction, and application of Cloth 115 principles, designed to give additional experience and expertise in clothing construction, emphasizing specialty fabrics and design.

Special Requirements: (Research Papers, Projects, etc.)
1.  Jean Construction
2.  Plaid (Matched) Shirt
3.  Ultrasuede Skirt
4.  Velvet Dress
