COURSE DESCRIPTION

    Improvisation and composition are an integral part of modern dancer’s education.
    A study of the basic elements of dance, Improvisation and composition will lead to the creation of a movement product.

OBJECTIVES

    1.) To explore, experiment, play, form, and create.
    2.) To get out of self and into self-in-movement, the deeper self that is often suppressed or unknown.
    3.) To cull the courage to give up conscious decisions or control and trust intuitive choice making.
    4.) To find identity in movement; to try out different roles an explore a range of responses in order to achieve a large and more flexible self image.
    5.) To become involved in a group process; to yield to the group while maintaining one’s basic responsibility as an individual.
    6.) To become conscious of, and more skilled with, the expressive, communicative nature of movement.

At the end of the semester the student will be able to:

    1.) Respond to a given stimulus in improvised movement.
    2.) Create a basic organic form (beginning, middle, end).
    3.) Utilize the possibilities the body has for making movement statements.
    4.) Articulate the appropriate use of space, time, and energy for the specific experience.
    5.) Be open to a wide range of response: kinesthetic, emotional, intuitive, and intellectual.
    6.) Have the confidence to maintain a solo and the ability to respond to other people in duets and small groups.
    7.) Keep in touch with the overall direction of the improv or compositional study.
    8.) Develop the movement material so that the improv or composition is all about one thing and not a collection of diverse responses.

REQUIREMENTS

Attendance:

    Two (2) absences are allowed without penalty. The fifth absence will result in automatic failing of the course. Three (3) tardies equal one absence. Three (3)
    observations equal one absence. To receive credit for observing you must take in-depth notes on your observation and turn them in to the instructor.

Participation:

    Improvisation and composition problems will be presented daily throughout the course. Students are expected to participate in movement sequences to the best of their ability. Class discussions are a vital part of the learning process. Students are expected to share their comments and feelings.

Attire:

    Proper and modest attire is expected. Hair should be pulled out of the face. No jewelry. No chewing gum.

Journal:

    Record you objective and subjective experiences and observations:

      1.) Record physical sensations and images.
      2.) Describe intriguing movement/moments
      3.) Comment of specific relationships and the identity of the group.
      4.) Identify potential applications.
      5.) Investigate how you felt about the group and the movement experience itself.
      6.) Explore how the experience relates to you and to the rest of your life.
      7.) State problems and possible solutions.

    The journal will be submitted periodically throughout the semester for evaluation by the instructor.

Grading:

    Attendance 30%
    Quality of class participation 50%
    Journal 20%

Final exam:

    This is your responsibility!!! This final will be held during finals week and will be a percentage of class participation. It is up to you to be there at the right day and time. This can not be made up or rescheduled.
