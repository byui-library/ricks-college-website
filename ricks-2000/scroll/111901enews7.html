Last-minute drive helps Vikings squeak by Alumni

by Nathan Hathaway
hat97013@byui.edu
Scroll Staff

There is no feeling like beating your older brother in basketball for the first time.

The men’s basketball team had that rare opportunity Friday, beating the Ricks National Alumni team 93-89.

“That’s a good team to play,” Ricks Coach Clyde Nelson said. “We’re not going to play many teams better than that.”

It took some last-minute scrapping for Ricks to pull out the win.

After leading at the half by one, 45-44, Ricks came out and held a lead until the alumni closed the gap and took a one-point lead 67-66, then again at 70 69. With the game tied at 74, the alumni went on an 8-0 run to extend their lead to 82-74.

Ricks fought back with a run of its own, 16-4, to gain a four-point edge over the former Ricks athletes. 1996-97 standout Danny Bower hit a 3-pointer to bring the alumni to within one. Ricks Adam Hiatt missed the second of his two subsequent free throws, leaving the door wide open for the alumni to tie the game.

Bower made an entry pass to Brandt Wilcox, but it was tipped away from Wilcox. Hiatt tracked down the errant ball and was immediately fouled. Hiatt hit both of his free throws to seal the win.

In the second half alone the teams tied six times and swapped leads another six times.

Alumni forward Seth Dahle brought the crowd to its feet 1:24 into the second half with a rim-rattling fastbreak dunk, the only dunk of the night by either of the teams.

Point guard Ty Church led all scorers with 26 points on 8-11 shooting from the field, including a perfect 4-4 from 3, and hitting six free throws.

“He’s so good,” Nelson said. “He’s one of my favorite guys I’ve ever coached.”

Nelson recalled coaching Church as a 5’4” sophomore in high school when the team went to a tournament in Arizona and Church made seven 3-pointers.

Aaron Bowman led Ricks with 19 points and Joe Green had 18.

Perhaps the most odd statistic of the night was Wilcox, who played center for the 1997-’98 Vikings, going 2-2 from 3-point range.

The Ricks National Alumni team was composed of four of the five starters from the Regional Championship team of 1998. The only starter missing was Ricky Bower, triggerman of “the shot heard ’round Rexburg,” and he was sitting on the bench cheering the team on.

Another member of the 1997-98 team, Brandt Peterson, is now an assistant coach with the Vikings and coached the alumni team. A seventh championship team member, Todd Amberry, plays with this year’s Vikings.
