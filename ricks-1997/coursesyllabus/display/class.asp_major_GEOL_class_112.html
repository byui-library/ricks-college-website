Historical Geology
HISTORIC GEOL
CLASS CODE: GEOL 112 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: PHYSICAL SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
DEPARTMENT: GEOLOGY
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: A study of the geological history of the earth and the evolution of its life forms. For geology and related majors. Students who take Geol 112 may not receive credit for Geol 103.
DESCRIPTION: Foundational topics pertaining to historical geology are studied that include early contribution to the science, geologic time and how it is measured, stratigraphic principles, early life forms, then environments and development, and the impact of the Plate Tectonic Theory. Early earth history, then each geologic period is studied in more detail as applied to regions of North America and world localities.

OBJECTIVES: Geology 112 is designed to prepare geology majors in basic geologic concepts so that they can continue and successfully complete their four-year program at BYU-Idaho.
REQUIREMENTS: Concurrent registration in Geol 112L. Students would be required to attend weekend field trips to Utah or Southeastern Idaho to apply basic principles of historical geology and study fossil localities.
