HEAD TRACK AND FIELD COACH

    Doug Stutz enters his second season as head track and field coach at Ricks College.  He arrived at Ricks College in 1988 as an assistant coach in cross country and track and field. In 1992, he was named head coach of the men’s and women’s cross country program. A dynasty followed.   At the national level, the men returned to prominence, winning the NJCAA Championship in 1999, 2000, and 2001. He has coached seventeen young men to All-American status, including 2000 NJCAA Champion Jeff Davidson.

    The performance of the women’s team under Coach Stutz’s guidance has been remarkable. In nine years they have won the NJCAA Championship in 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, and 2001. During that run, Stutz coached three individual champions, Kara Ormond in 1996 and Alycia Boyer in 1998, Kristen Ogden in 2000, and Angela Benson in 2001. In all, 42 young women have attained All-American status with Stutz as their head coach. The NJCAA acknowledged Doug’s success by naming him national Coach of the Year six times. Four times his teams have won the award for best combined men’s and women’s scores at the NJCAA finals.

    "I’m excited to keep the tradition alive in track and field. We’ll continue to have a good program. We expect to be successful," said Stutz. 

    Stutz will continue as head coach of cross country, and in his position in Continuing Education. Doug and his wife Keysha make their home in Rexburg with a young and growing family.
