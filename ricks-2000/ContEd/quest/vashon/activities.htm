ACTIVITIES

Students in the Quest program at Vashon Island have the opportunity to interact with the branch members.  These interactions consist of visits to their homes for Sunday dinner and at other times for Family Home Evening.   The hospitality of the branch members is exceptional.
Past groups have had the opportunity to tour local industrial attractions such as the Boeing 747 plant, K2 Ski as well as other shops.  Cultural attractions visited include Victoria, British Columbia, The Seattle Museum of Fine Art, The Asian Art Museum, Omni Center, The Pacific Science Center, folk art exhibits and demonstrations of glass blowing.
The students have the opportunity to join field trips that explore the natural resources as well.  These trips have included attractions like Snoqualmie Falls, Mount Rainier, Point Robinson lighthouse, Point Defiance Zoo and Aquarium and Fort Casey.
In addition to touring students have the opportunity to volunteer on the property in valuable service projects sponsored by the local Branch President. "The Widows Wood Pile" is one example of how students can serve the community.  This store of cut and split wood provides for those incapable of providing this resource for themselves.
Morningside is an open campus and students are encouraged to organize and plan trips into Seattle in groups to enjoy the markets, attractions and sporting events.
