If you plan to make up a missed class, do the
makeup work no later than three days after you
miss the class.

Do two things for each day you miss class. 

1. Read a selection from the list on the left.
Pick a story that is not a part of your regular assignments.

2. Type a 300-word summary. At the end of
your essay, include three or four sentences
which explain the meaning of the story.  Email
Brother Hackworth a copy of the summary.

Use a word processor to write the summary.
Then use CTRL C and CTRL V to copy and
paste the essay into your own email program.
Mail the essay to:  HackworthA@byui.edu
