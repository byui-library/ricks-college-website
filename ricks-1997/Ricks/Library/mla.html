A Book by Two or More Authors

     Jakobson, Toman, and Linda R. Waugh. The Sound Shape of
          Language. Bloomington: Indiana UP, 1979.
     (Note: underline The Sound Shape of Language)

A Book by a Corporate Author

     Public Agenda Foundation. The Health Care Crisis:
          Containing Costs, Expanding Coverage. New York:

An Article in a Scholarly Journal

     Scotto, Peter. "Censorship, Reading, and Interpretation: A
          Case Study from the Soviet Union." PMLA 109
          (1994): 61-70.
     (Note: underline PMLA)

An Article in a Scholarly Journal that Pages each Issue Separately

     Hallin, Daniel C. "Sound Bite News" Television Coverage of
          Elections, 1968-1988." Journal of Communication
          42.2 (1992): 5-24.
     (Note: underline Journal of Communication)

An Article in a Newspaper

      Feder, Barnaby J. "For Job Seekers, a Toll-Free Gift of
          Expert Advice." New York Times 30 Dec. 1993,
          late ed.: D1+.
     (Note: underline New York Times)

An Article in a Magazine

      Bazell, Robert. "Science and Society: Growth Industry."
          New Republic 15 Mar. 1993: 13-14.
     (Note: underline New Republic)
