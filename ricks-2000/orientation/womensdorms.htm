In This Building:

 

On-Campus Women's Cooking-Style Housing.  

The BYU-Idaho Women's Cooking-style housing units are Dorms One through Four (dorms 1-3 in the summer) . Each one houses 120 women.

Each apartment houses four women. Bedrooms have two beds, two dressers, an eight-foot closet, one desk, and some wall-mounted shelving.

The newly remodeled bathroom has a tub/shower and toilet, which has a cabinet-mounted sink and a large four-door medicine cabinet.

Each apartment also has a newly remodeled bright-white kitchen with microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls.

There is also a coin-operated laundry room in each building with Maytag® washers and dryers.

 

On-Campus Women's Cafeteria Style Housing.  BYU-Idaho Women's Cafeteria Style housing units are Dorms 5 and 6. Each one houses 210 women with facilities for disabled students.

Each building has a main lounge as with all on campus dorms

The three bedroom apartments are shared by six women, two per bedroom. The bedrooms contain two beds, two dressers, one desk, an eight-foot closet and some

wall-mounted shelving.

The double-sink bathroom has a tub/shower and toilet, with a floor-to-ceiling storage cabinet. Each apartment also has a Microfridge unit with microwave and refrigerator-freezer, and a telephone with free local calls.

There is also a coin-operated laundry room in each building with Maytag® washers and dryers.
