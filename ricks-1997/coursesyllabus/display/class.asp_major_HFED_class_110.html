Introductory Foods
INTRO FOODS
CLASS CODE: HFED 110 CREDITS:  2 FEE:  $30.00
DIVISION: EDUCATION
DEPARTMENT: HOME & FAMILY EDUCATION
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: Open to all students. To develop skill in food preparation. Laboratory experience assists in acquiring accepted techniques in food preparation. Students receive hands-on experience. Food is furnished.
DESCRIPTION: Open to all students. To develop skill in food preparation. Laboratory experience assists in acquiring accepted techniques in food preparation. Students receive hands-on experience. Lab Fee: $30, food is furnished.
TOPICS: Knife use; Sweeteners; Butter demo; Vegetables and fruits; Salads and salad dressings; Stocks and soups; Principles of cooking meat; Beef; Poultry and fish; Milk, cheese and eggs; Starches and sauce, cereal, grains and pasta; Flours, quick breads; Pies and pastries; cakes and frostings.
OBJECTIVES: 1. Students will develop a working knowledge of foods and their preparation techniques.
2. Students will prepare and evaluate foods as they apply preparation.
3. Students will develop a working knowledge of basic nutrition, safety, and sanitation.
REQUIREMENTS: Lecture attendance; Weekly lab participation; Quizzes; Final
