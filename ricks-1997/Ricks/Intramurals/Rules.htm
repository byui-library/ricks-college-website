General Rules

1) Games will be scheduled on the hour starting at 5:00 P.M..

2) You will be required to obey the dress and grooming standards. That means no 5:00 shadow, no jewelry, can't tape earrings.

3) No new rosters will be accepted after the starting date.

4) Independent leagues -must be a full time student with 12 credits or more.

5) Ward leagues -must be a ward member.

                           -don't have to be a full time student.

6) Red shirts can't play in related sports.

    Gray shirts can only play in ward league.
