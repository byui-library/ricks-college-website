ETHER—15 Comments

Hugh Nibley

Ensign Oct 1975 - Feb 77

1. Why isn't the Brother of Jared (and his children) named? because the writer of this book was a descendant of Jared, not his brother, so he didn't care about his geneaolgy. (What if your grandfather was President Kimball’s brother? And his name was Jared?)

2. The words "descendant"(ben) and "son" (ben) are interchanged, and cannot be taken literally. Most of the chronologies in the bible are far too short. (read Ether 1)

3. The book is full of details that only a casual eyewitness could have included.

4. When the Tower of Babel was built the people had already been spread upon the earth for some time. (2200)(Flood 2300) At that time the whole earth was speaking the same language.

5. How can we expect to believe they had steel?

Historians expect a gradual evolution of abilities. Such is not always the case. We assume that since steel is better than iron that it is impossible for an earlier civilization to have had it. Not so. It was a trade secret, and many times it died with the family, or was carried off with them when they left, or it was ignored and not used (i.e., steel vs. bronze in Egypt, where bronze was used because it lasts longer, and doesn't rust).

6. The tower and Nimrod:

Everyone was building towers to get to heaven. But they failed, so they set out to conquer the world instead. The sons of Seth (Adam's successor) wanted to go back to the garden of Eden, so they went up on Mt. Hermon and lived lives of religious asceticism, calling themselves the "Watchers" and "the Sons of Elohim". (Gen. 6)

When it didn't work they came down from the mountain, mad, broke the covenant, and married the daughters of Cain. Determined to possess the earth if they could not possess heaven, they faked the priesthood and the garmet, and forced the people to accept kings over them, rather than prophets.

In another version the leader became the "Mad Hunter," claimed to be the correct ruler of the universe, and challenged God to an archery contest, and built a tower from which he could shoot arrows into heaven. (Some apocryphal writings say that the city of Enoch left the earth, but was still visible in the air above the earth. It was into this city that men tried to shoot their arrows.) This archer was Nimrod, whose name is always associated with rebellion. The Greeks called him Zoroaster, the first king of Persia, the builder of Babylon, the first man to teach the killing and eating of animals. He faked the priesthood, used magic instead, used a fake crown, and was called "god of lawlessness," "god of no government."

Adam's son Seth kept a book called the Book of Life, which he kept hidden from the sons of Cain. Nimrod also kept a book, called the Book of the Dragon (or serpent/snake/worm/Devil), and imitation of the former.

Jared and his party go into the valley of Nimrod, to the north.

7. Garments: Nimrod claims power by virtue of possessing the "garment of Adam." The garments of skin the Lord made for Adam and Eve were given to Enoch after the death of Adam, then to Methuselah, then to Noah, from whom Ham stole them as the people were leaving the ark. This story is so well known that Michelangelo depicted it in the Sistine Chapel. Ham's son Cush then gave them to his son Nimrod.

Other sources say Ham merely took the garment while Noah was asleep, copied it, and returned it. But Noah knew, and cursed Cain. (last of Gen 9)

The Pharaohs of Egypt also wore a garment made of skin. When they died they were clothed in a skin garment (as were also the priests), or a cloth one that looked like the skin version.

8. The Dispersion: Don't read into scripture things that are not there. Nothing is said that every man suddenly spoke a new language. To confound is to "mix up", to "pour together". The Lord "did not confound the language of Jared, . . . and their friends and their families also, that they were not confounded." So, they were not mixed up with other people or languages. The record does not attribute the scattering to the confusion of tongues. Language had little to do with driving them out of the land.

9. Weather: They left due to the winds. The wind blew so hard that it buried entire cities in a matter of days. It was so strong that it blew gravel. All this caused the land to dry up, the water, the grass, so the people were forced to move in order to find food. Note that other peoples record the great wind of 2300-2200 BC, and the world floods of 1300BC. The fugitives were obliged to divide into small groups, since no one water-hole could accommodate them all.

NOTE: Other records say "the Lord laid great blasts upon the tower, and the languages of men were divided into dialects, so that the earth became filled with different kingdoms of men."

10. Direction: They went before the winds, traveling east across Russia, driving their herds before them and taking years for the passage.

11. Travel across Russia: Completely different from the way Lehi left town (in secret, hiding in the desert, no fires). These people left with huge herds, carrying everything from household goods to bees and fish. In his travels during the 1200's Marco Polo saw wheel ruts more than 20 feet wide, and 22 oxen pulling a complete house on wheels.

12. The ships: Why weren't Lehi's and Jared's ships the same? Lehi went probably 3-4 times farther than Jared, and Jared's were driven by the wind, and had no upper decks nor sails. Since the prevailing westerlies blow from the mainland of Asia toward America, and since the Japanese current also sweeps this same direction, Chinese junks have been washed up on the shores of California after drifting a year on the ocean, driven by the currents and the winds. The easterly direction is easy to understand, while to expect them to have crossed in the other direction would make it almost impossible, given the prevailing winds and currents.

13. Prisons: Why did a king capture another king, put him in jail, and keep him there the rest of his life? Because that's the way it was done in Asia, not here. It seems crazy to us, but that's the way they did it. You could even have children, live in a nice house, but were a prisoner just the same.

14. Distances: The type of warfare used by Shiz and Coriantumr was typical of Asia, not anywhere else. To cross huge distances was common. To have they move from the "narrow neck" of land to upstate New York and across the US seems to be stretching things, but it isn't. Look at any map and notice that the US is much smaller than Asia. The great Khan noted in one of his journals that his army was separated from that of his son by only 1200 miles (from Canada to Mexico), and thus was close enough for help. These same tribes thought nothing of wintering in Germany or Poland, and then returning to upper China for the summer. To only go from New York to Mexico was a walk in the park!

15. Chess: The wars of extermination were typically Asiatic. And in numbers, quite small. The 2-4 million dead in Ether seems incredible until compared with two Chinese lords who left 18 million dead in 15 years, or Genghis Khan who left 40 million dead in China alone. Everything was killed, even the dogs and cats, so the land could return to its natural state. The mongols wanted freedom to move, with nothing in their way.
