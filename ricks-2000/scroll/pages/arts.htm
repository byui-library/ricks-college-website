BALLROOM TEAM TAKES FIRST AT COMPETITION

The Ricks College ballroom dance team competed in an international competition Feb. 3.

Photo courtsey Public Relations

 

The Ricks College Ballroom dance team took first place in the formation championships of the International Federation Event held in Vancouver, British Columbia, Feb. 3.

The team competed against several international ballroom dance teams. Six Ricks couples also competed in the individual division, all of whom placed in the semi-finals.

The dance styles consisted of Silver Latin, cha-cha, jive and others. The ballroom dance team sacrificed many hours of practicing to prepare for these events.

CHARACTERS COME TOGETHER IN "THE DINING ROOM" TO REMINISCE ON MEMORIES, LIFE-CHANGING EVENTS

Cliff Reliford a sophomore from Franklin, Tenn., plays Paul in "The Dining Room" which is set to open Thursday in the Kirkham Arena theater. Reliford plays a variety of characters throughout the production. Carol Dittli / Scroll

"The Dining Room," a comedic and entertaining play, will run Thursday through Saturday at 7:30 p.m. in the Kirkham Arena Theatre. The play consists of many different stories shared in a dining room.

"This play is a 'workshop' production not part of the regular Ricks College theater season. This is a chance for regular students who were not cast in the season productions, to practice and improve their acting and technical skills," Becky Foster, "The Dining Room" director, said.

The dining room is a place of conversation where truth is revealed and a person can open the desires of his or her heart.
