Receiving Credit

Registering For The Course

Newly admitted students can receive credit for completing Pathway's Pre-Campus Orientation course. 

Students interested in receiving online credit for this course are encouraged to complete all assigned coursework before they start school.  

For more information please contact the office of New Student Orientation via e-mail at orientation@ricks.edu

To register for the course students should submit their completed assignments (New Student Journal) to the Office of New Student Orientation.  

Upon receiving students' completed journals, the Coordinator of New Student Orientation will officially register them in the course and give them their grade (pass/fail) at the end of the semester or term.
