Bachelor's Degree Planning Worksheet

A BS degree planning worksheet is now available for students interested in graduating from BYU-Idaho in Information Systems. It is primarily intended for freshman students entering Fall 2001. Visit the Degree Page for more detailed information.(Posted 28 August, 2001)
BYU-Idaho and Information Systems

With the transition from Ricks College to Brigham Young University - Idaho a number of Bachelor's degrees will be offered. The Computer Information Systems Department has changed its name to Information Systems effective Fall 2001, and our department is scheduled to begin offering junior level courses beginning Fall semester 2003. As more information becomes available it will be posted on the IS Dept. web site. (Posted Aug. 2001)
