Outstanding Young Alumni Award
Dale M. Johnson

"It was the defining point of my life," says Dale concerning a meeting with John Walker of the Ricks College agriculture department in 1978. "(Agriculture) is where you need to go," Dale recalls John telling him. Agriculture is where Dale has been ever since. Presently he is an agricultural educator at the University of Maryland at College Park.

A native of Shelley, Dale served a mission to Pennsylvania Piitsburgh following high school. He attended Ricks College from 1978 until graduating with honors in 1981 in farm crops management and in arts and science. "I had tremendous advisors and teachers. These fantastic people instilled in me a desire to become an educator," Dale says.

Dale graduated magna cum laude from Utah State University in agribusiness management and obtained his Master’s degree in agricultural economics from Cornell University in 1985. He has been an extension economist and farm management specialist with the University of Maryland since then. In his job he combines his practical farm background with his extensive professional experience in agricultural economics, agri-business and farm management, business planning, financial analysis, and computer applications. Dale has traveled extensively organizing and conducting work shops and seminars, including trips to eastern Europe and former Soviet bloc nations. He teaches, directs leadership and management programs, conducts research and surveys, and publishes and presents his findings on many agricultural economics topics.

"This wonderful career has given me experiences and taken me to places around the world that I could not have imagined when I was at Ricks," Dale says. "I like to think of Ricks as the hand that pushed my boat away from the dock as I began this incredible odyssey."

Dale is married to the former LeAnn Allen of Salt Lake City. The couple have six children and make their home on a small farm at the north end of the Antietam National Battlefield near Sharpsburg, Md. Dale teaches early morning Seminary and is a home teacher in his ward.
