COURSE DESCRIPTION

    This course is designed to give the student experience in movement emphasizing locomotor skills, movement progressions, and dance techniques in ballet, modern and jazz.

OBJECTIVES

    1. To introduce the elements of dance through the technical exploration of ballet modern and jazz.
    2. To experience locomotor skills and progressions and combining them with axial movement combinations.
    3. To increase the technical ability of each student, specifically in strength, endurance, flexibility and alignment.
    4. To develop an understanding of awareness of body alignment, coordination, and balance.
    5. To develop an appreciation for dance as an art form.
    6. To learn to enjoy movement and to become more aware of and appreciative of the body.
