Managerial Economics
MANAGERL ECON
CLASS CODE: ECON 300 CREDITS:  3
DIVISION: BUSINESS & COMMUNICATION
DEPARTMENT: ECONOMICS
GENERAL EDUCATION: This course does not fulfill a General Education requirement.
CATALOG DESCRIPTION: This course is designed to enhance the student's understanding of how economics analysis can be applied to modern business decision making.
DESCRIPTION: The objective of this course is to familiarize the students with the microeconomic tools necessary for sound managerial decision making in private, public, and not-for-profit institutions.
TOPICS: Topics include: demand estimation, forecasting, production, cost, market structure, input pricing, and risk analysis.
