How To Apply

All students applying for federal student aid, must first submit a Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) to the federal processor. This Application may be obtained from your high school or at any college which participates in Federal Financial Aid including Ricks College.  We encourage you to apply through Internet with FAFSA on the Web This process is quicker and more accurate. If you have any questions as you are filling out this application, please call the Ricks College Financial Aid Office at (208) 356-1015 or E-Mail the Financial Aid Office.  Answering questions incorrectly on your application will cause delays in obtaining your financial aid.

SUMMER TERMS - If you will be attending a Summer term you may need to apply using a sepearate application. 

For Example, if you are attending School any time between:
August 29, 2000 to August 17, 2001  - Complete the 2000-2001 FAFSA
September 5, 2001 to August 13, 2002  - Complete the 2001-2002 FAFSA

 

A Renewal Application will be sent to you, by the federal processor, if you have previously received federal aid. The Renewal Application is basically the same as the FAFSA and the student may use either when applying for aid.

One of the questions on the application will ask to list the Title IV code of Ricks College. This number is 001625. Clicke here if you would like Title IV codes for other colleges.

The federal processor will process the application and send a Student Aid Report (SAR) to the student , usually within three to four weeks. If corrections are necessary, follow the instructions on the Student Aid Report. You may make corrections on Part Two of the SAR After the corrected information is processed, the student will be sent a new SAR. This usually takes another three to four weeks.

 

 

Remember to meet all Deadlines.
