March Spotlight -- W.R. Barney and Della Evans Barnett

In the fall of 1978 Barney Barnett and Della Evans were classmates in the recently started landscape nursery management program at Ricks College. In the spring of 2002 Barney and Della will be in their 15th year as owners of Willard Bay Gardens LLC. 

A lot of history has occurred for them in the past 20 plus years since attending Ricks College. Actually, Ricks College is part of that history.
Barney first attended Ricks in the fall of 1974 and was involved in the Natural Sciences Field Expedition. He claims that Ed Williams and Lorentz Pearson (the expedition professors) are keys in his pursuit of a Horticulture Career. Della's father Seth P. Evans is a Rexburg native. He and Della's mother Shirley Hyde, both graduated from Ricks College. After two years of Horticulture study, Della and Barney graduated together in April of 1980 from the Landscape Nursery Program. They continued their education together in Cheyenne Wyoming as managers of a greenhouse operation and as husband and wife.
    
 March of 1988 brought them to Chadwicks plants in Willard, Utah. A name change and a lot of work transformed the 40 year old nursery, located on Brigham City's historic fruit way into what the Barnetts call now Willard Bay Gardens. Willard Bay Gardens is an herbaceous plant nursery. Annuals, herbs, ornamental, grasses and perennials are the primary plants grown and sold there. The Barnett's and their staff have plants that will adapt to the mountains, plains, or desert. They have a wide range of clientele which includes Ricks College demonstration gardens (BYU-Idaho), several LDS Temples, USU, U of U, Thanksgiving Point and Red Butte Gardens. However, their favorite customers are gardeners.  Barney and Della are always ready to send home with you a new or interesting plant.
   
Barney and Della are involved with local and national Horticulture groups. They enjoy speaking and sharing their love of perennials and other garden plants. They live with their son Parker, in Willard, Utah. Barney and Della say thanks to all the things that  Ricks College has done to help shape their lives.  Barney and Della currently serve on the Ogden Utah Chapter Council.
