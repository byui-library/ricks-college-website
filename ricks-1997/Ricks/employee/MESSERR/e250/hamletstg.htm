Some suggestions on studying for the Hamlet Test.

I. Total number of questions-100

II. Break down of questions

Matching - Quotation Identification

  • •Section A Choose the correct speaker for each quotation Questions 1 - 5
  • •Section B Choose the character that is described by each quotation Questions 6 - 10

Matching - Character Identification

  • •Section C & D Choose the character that matches each description Questions 11 - 20

Matching - Descriptions

  • Choose the item that matches each description Questions 21 - 27

True-False Questions 28 - 64

Multiple Choice Question 65 - 100

III. Some hints on true and false

Many are interpretive questions and require a close reading. In each case the evidence is very strong toward right answer. Therefore read Hamlet carefully. Know the characters: Hamlet, Claudius, Polonius, Horatio, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern, the Ghost, Gertrude (the queen), Ophelia, Reynaldo, Marcellus, Fortinbras, Laertes.

IV Some hints on Multiple Choice

Know the relationships among the characters, the setting, the characters, the plot, the major content of Hamlet's soliloquies, the theme.
